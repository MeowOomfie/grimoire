# How Game State Affect Itemization | Phantom Kelvin Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-03-30

## Covers
Midknight reviews one Phantom-rank Kelvin game (grenade build) for a player who asked for midgame macro advice, laning against a Holliday and Shiv.
Free coaching review over a replay. Recorded March 2025, on an early-2025 patch; the game has changed a lot since, so check items, numbers and rank names against the current patch.
Themes: itemizing for a lead versus defensively, using lane pressure for camps and box runs, respecting baits at higher rank, fake melees in the dome, when to take mid boss, rescue items, and solo boss steals.

## Claims
Player 1 (Kelvin, Phantom):
- Be pre-aimed with your crosshair so you get the deny the moment the minion is last hit. [0:30]
- Lead with Arctic Beam as poke, then turn it into farm when they hide; there is no downside, it does a lot of damage. [1:03]
- Identify when the enemy laner (Holliday) has not bought Healing Rite and kill her under tower before she can afford it. [1:34]
- Don't buy defensively just because the lane matchup is bad: at 3 minutes you were up 700 souls, so buy offensive items to push the lead, and only lean defensive once the lane turns neutral. The first Extra Health came out early. [2:05]
- Once you start getting poked down, buying a little defensively (Healing Rite) makes sense. [2:35]
- Respect the enemy wave when low; troopers are your highest damage source at that point, so don't stand in front of the wave. [3:05]
- If the enemy laner doesn't want to commit, don't commit either; diving the Shiv by parrying the tower was possible but very risky. [3:05]
- Kelvin has a lot of healing, so trading into a Shiv who tanks your beam plays in your favor. [3:35]
- Once you have ult and they don't, play in their face; if they ever jump you, press ult (Frozen Shelter). Do it while they are still saving for their ult, around 2.2k to 2.3k souls. [3:35]
- Don't always melee inside your dome; every Kelvin does it and it is predictable. Fake melees in the dome instead. [4:05]
- You had wave pressure all game and did zero camps, and only 30% damage to the Guardian. When the wave is pushed, go do camps, box runs and roams. [5:06]
- Even a minor box run, like walking into their jungle to grab a camp and boxes and walking back, cements and pushes your lead. [5:36]
- Against an enemy with Enchanter's Barrier you can't one-shot him, so respect it and don't go for the grenade one-shot play. [6:07]
- Grab boxes and buffs when you pass them; the buffs despawn if you leave them too long. [6:37]
- Walking into the tower and parrying it puts a lot of pressure on the defending laner even without diving. [6:37]
- When you start a dive, focus the tower first so you can kill it and then pressure the enemy. [7:07]
- Pick up the drops from breakables you break; leaving them is a disservice to yourself. [8:11]
- An enemy with ult up can't be 1v1'd, but you can scare her away; if she pokes you down, drop your dome on top of her ult. [8:41]
- When you see three enemies in a fight elsewhere, you can do about 30% damage to a walker before they rotate over. [9:12]
- When an enemy at 1 HP stays behind a truck next to their walker, at Phantom rank assume it is a bait and a teammate is coming. In Archon you can assume they are just bad; above the top 10% you should respect it and not dive. [9:43]
- Habits from climbing in lower ranks will hurt you more and more as you climb toward Ascendant. [10:45]
- On the grenade build, buy Rapid Recharge and Improved Burst before a 6k item; they put in a ton of work for this build. [10:45]
- Don't walk into the Bebop laser. [11:15]
- Ulting a trapped target just to hold him until help arrives would not hurt, though you could kill him without it. [11:47]
- At 3k souls with many open slots, buy slot fillers (tier twos) next shop instead of another tier three. Fleetfoot was needed at this point. [11:47]
- Once you identify an enemy as the weak link it is fine to target her, but you had two chances to get out and kept going back in; that is overcommitting. [12:49]
- Buy when you are next to the secret shop and rich. [12:49]
- Rushing Mystic Reverb on the full grenade build is fine with Fleetfoot bought, but filling an open slot with a cheap tier one first gives a smaller spike for the next teamfight. [13:19]
- He expected Escalating Exposure on this build because the beam was doing so much damage with nothing built for it. [13:50]
- You probably need armor soon. [13:50]
- Instead of air-domeing when chased, cancel Ice Path and drop behind cover; you could have lived without the dome, though overly safe beats risky. [14:21]
- Kelvin likes running urn with Ice Path, especially on a movement build. [14:51]
- Run urn to force a fight when your team's ults (Abrams, Infernus, Haze) will be up and theirs are weaker. [14:51]
- Use your ult to stop Bebop. [15:21]
- When the fight turns negative and your teammates aren't there, don't go back in; it is a 2v3. [15:51]
- At Phantom, 23 minutes in: a two-player advantage is enough to take mid boss; with three it is 100% mid boss. One player up is good, two is great. [16:22]
- If you can't reach the boss in time to zone with your ult, identify that and go for a parry or melee on the Rejuvenator instead. [16:53]
- Leading the charge on a walker push as Kelvin is a good call. [17:24]
- If you made the right call to push but three teammates stayed farming, notice it and stop pushing; ping them, and if they don't listen, give up the push. Pushing alone or with one teammate won't end well. [17:56]
- When you call fall back and your team won't, leave anyway; your Abrams is at half and they have their ults up and their whole team. [18:27]
- Rescue Beam value depends on how your team plays. A way to get value: in late game, follow an overextending teammate from a little behind, and when they get jumped, beam them out and leave. [19:28]
- Run urn with Ice Path sooner; it is very fast. Without Duration Extender the path is shorter on the grenade build. [19:58]
- Farming urn on that side is risky; when your Vindicta dies next to you, drop the urn and defend the walker. [20:29]
- When alone 1v4, play back at your bridge and mostly look for denies with Arctic Beam instead of trying to hold the walker forward. [21:29]
- Superior Cooldown is good on this build; putting it on the grenade gives about an 11 second cooldown. [21:59]
- Stop going for solo mid boss steals around high Oracle or Phantom; enemies are usually ready with utility and steals need coordination. Go for one only when the game is lost and you need a Hail Mary. [22:29]
- Instead of melee steals at these ranks, jump in and parry on top of the Rejuvenator. [22:59]
- When the enemy has Bebop, be ready for the hook; don't play on the bridge, play super safe. [23:30]
- Summary, laning: you had a ton of lane pressure and never did camps or box runs; roam sooner and more. [24:30]
- Summary, items: you itemized defensively with a lead; itemize to push the lead until it is neutralized. [25:01]
- Summary, midgame: farming and splitting macro were solid; the main fix is respecting the enemy and treating bad-looking plays as baits at your rank. [25:01]
- After you have killed an enemy and confirmed they are weak, you can drop the bait assumption for that player. [26:01]
- Look at your team on the map more before pushing; when players on your team are dead, don't look for fights and expect the Bebop hook. [26:01]
