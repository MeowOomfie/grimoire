# I Made The Perfect Mirage Build
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-09-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pQ6SEpLAmFs

## Covers
Mirage, aquatiq, commentary over one standard match (win, about 60k damage).
A spirit Mirage build with Suppressor, Headhunter, Toxic Bullets, Mercurial Magnum and Diviner's Kevlar, on the 2026-09-11 patch.

## Claims
- He is using builds from Ozus (as captioned), which he rates highly. [1:04]
- Skip Extra Regen first in this lane, there is no point; he buys Monster Rounds first. [1:04]
- Suppressor is his second item, bought after his Fire Scarabs come online. [1:35]
- He thinks Mirage is in a really bad state right now and his win rate is very poor, though his late game may have something left to discover. [1:35]
- Mirage's early game is fantastic, with some of the best early game shove in the game. [2:06]
- Suppressor is strong in games against gun heroes such as Seven. [3:06]
- With a Rem healing you in lane, play more aggressively. [3:36]
- Healbane would be good in this game. [5:39]
- He does not want Cultist; later he says not to use it, since you do not care much about gun damage on Mirage. [5:39]
- Headhunter comes after Suppressor. [6:10]
- He has two points in Djinn's Mark early, then maxes it and starts marking everybody. [6:40]
- Suppressor procs off his 2 and his 3, basically off everything, which is why it is good on him. [6:40]
- Fire Scarabs thrown at long range are hard to land reliably. [7:10]
- Headhunter heals a lot and sustain is very important on Mirage; Scarab healing plus Headhunter is a strong double sustain. [10:51]
- Mirage's early to mid game is very strong, but late game enemies buy items and he stops killing people. [12:54]
- Buy Toxic Bullets against a team with a lot of healing (Mo & Krill, Wraith, Bebop). [13:26]
- Against heavy damage and Seven's stun, buy the debuff remover item (renamed in this patch). [14:58]
- His plan: Toxic Bullets, Mercurial Magnum, then Ricochet. [17:03]
- Mercurial Magnum used to go on Djinn's Mark so you could spam the 3; you need cooldown before doing that. [17:03]
- He puts the imbue items on his 3. [19:45]
- He adds Superior Cooldown. [20:46]
- Against a team with a lot of spirit resist, buy spirit resist shred. [22:19]
- Diviner's Kevlar gives a barrier when you cast your ultimate; with Traveler up about every 37 seconds that is a 1k barrier each time. [26:27]
- Ricochet is needed for team fights. [28:02]
- He sells Healbane later in the game. [28:32]
- Against Arctic Blast he looks to get Escalating Exposure. [29:35]
- A double Tornado (two charges) is a great escape; Echo Shard would make the escape certain. [30:35]
- Clear the minions so they target the Patron. [32:08]
- Traveler can only teleport to the mid boss when it has spawned, landing on top of it. [33:41]
- Verdict: Diviner's Kevlar is really good, since you get a 1k barrier every 40 seconds even when you Traveler to base, and most Traveler uses are aggressive. [35:16]
