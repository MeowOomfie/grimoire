## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream on 5/28/26, the day a small urn patch landed (he reads and reacts to the notes first). The replays are from the patch before it.
He coaches four players: a Mirage (Oracle), an Apollo (Oracle), noob on Dynamo (high lobby) and Henry on Lash. In between he reviews Rem, Holliday, Victor and Dynamo builds.

## Claims
Patch reaction (5/28/26 urn patch) and meta:
- The urn spawn interval got longer (now 6 minutes); he thinks it is a bad change because the biggest complaint at high level is that there is nothing to do while the urn is off the map. [2:34]
- The urn timer now gets frozen by Frozen Shelter. [2:34]
- The urn no longer gives spirit and bullet resist while it is carried or dropped. This makes comeback urn significantly worse, because you can now just fight the enemy team before they get to the cash. [3:04]

- Parrying troopers no longer resets your parry cooldown (it stuns the trooper again), which kills the parry-bait tech. [4:05]

- Tapping the urn is still a thing after the patch. [85:29]
- The urn cash time is 10 seconds; when it flips it adds a few extra seconds. [134:04]
- Shrines went from useless to the most overpowered objective: two guaranteed lane prios and minus 30% souls for the enemy. [50:32]
- Burger Box (mosh-pit, AoE) metas are bad, and he has not met anyone who likes this one; macro players suffer and burger-box heroes win. [6:10]
- On-hits are very strong; he thinks no on-hit hero is fair right now. [20:31]
- Haze's Fixation lasting 12 seconds is absurdly long for keeping someone out of lane. [21:01]
- Infernus has too much damage amp, which is why his afterburn has to stay weak. [22:05]
- Opening Rounds is statistically overpowered on gun heroes; after the spirit power cut you can no longer buy it on pure spirit heroes. [29:47]
