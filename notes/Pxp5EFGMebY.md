## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream, recorded 6/15/26 after the minor patch around June 12 (the reworked AoE urn patch). Four VOD reviews: a Mina (Ascendant), a Lash (Ascendant), an Abrams on the Gumby build (Ascendant) and a Holliday (Eternus 6 lobby).
Themes: playing for urns, converting a lead into towers, guardians and invades, not fighting after objectives, ulting targets in the open, box routes and secret shops, tunnel vision, movement inefficiencies, and AP order debates for Rem, Lash and Holliday. Side Q&A on countering Silver, Dynamo, Victor, Sinclair and Haze.
