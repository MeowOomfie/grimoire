## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (patch of late May 2026, with the new urn and execute changes). He reviews four submitted games: a kidnap-build Doorman (Emissary), a Yamato in an Eternus 6 lobby asking how to convert a lead, a Call Bell Doorman asking why his damage is low, and a gun Ivy (Oracle) on Hidealker's greedy build.
