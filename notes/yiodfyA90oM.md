# How This Patch Will Affect Your Lobbies | Deadlock Patch Notes
channel: Midknight
date: 2024-11-08
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yiodfyA90oM

## Covers
Patch reaction by Midknight to the early November 2024 Deadlock patch, boiled down to what matters for the average player: soul sharing on waves, kill value, camps, items (Soul Rebirth removed, Slowing Hex cross-tree) and hero changes with verdicts.

## Claims
Systems and macro:
- Biggest change: troopers no longer change soul sharing after laning, so two heroes on a wave both get full souls all game instead of splitting. [0:31]
- So it is always better not to take a wave alone; a farming hero like Wraith should bring a teammate like Infernus to a pushed lane or double wave to duplicate the souls. [1:01]
- Splitting the map in pairs now has almost no extra cost besides showing the enemy where two of you are. [1:01]
- Before clearing a wave alone, look at the map; if a teammate is coming, last hit as few minions as you can and wait to clear it together. [1:31]
- Hero kills are worth more, especially late, and neutral creeps a little less, so orient more toward fights than hyper farming. [1:31]
- Corner camps moved toward the middle of the map so the player farming them can join a team fight; Valve seems to want more fighting and quicker games. [2:02]

Items:
- Sharpshooter and Long Range keep damage at range but now get 10% extra weapon damage within 15 m. [2:02]
- Toxic Bullets now tick for 2.5% max health instead of 5% current health; he calls it a strong buff that does more to low HP enemies leaving a fight, good on Infernus. [2:33]
- Small Slowing Bullets buff. [2:33]
- Healbane and healing reduction overall are up; the devs are moving away from a healing meta. [3:34]
- Divine Barrier got a buff and is very strong right now, especially with the extra spirit power; you should be buying it. [3:34]
- Metal Skin no longer blocks melee, so stop shooting and melee someone who pops it. [4:05]
- Veil Walker was buffed again; he recommends trying it. [4:05]
- Soul Rebirth was removed because whole teams spammed it past 30 to 40 minutes; a good change. [4:05]
- Siphon Bullets was buffed though it was already good. [4:36]
- Phantom Strike on a flying target like Vindicta, Grey Talon, Bebop or Seven now drags them to the ground, canceling Seven's ult or knocking snipers out of the air. [4:36]
- Slowing Hex now builds from Enduring Spirit, the first cross-tree item, and gets its stats, but lost spirit power, sprint and damage and costs more; hard to call buff or nerf. [5:07]
- On gun heroes like Mirage who bought Slowing Hex as a must buy, switch to the buffed Slowing Bullets and only buy Slowing Hex against heroes with dashes or things to suppress. [5:38]
- Decay does less damage and instead reduces healing more, a more supportive item. [6:08]

Heroes:
- Abrams changes add up to a slight nerf; he will feel a little squishier. [6:08]
- Bebop's hook gets more base damage and strong spirit scaling, while his bomb's disarm becomes minus 30% damage for 5 seconds. [6:08]
- Dynamo's Singularity does more damage throughout the game, plus a little bullet resist and scaling. [6:39]
- Grey Talon's early game was nerfed but he scales to the same mid and late game; his flight is a bit reduced and he does a little less damage from far away; Grey Talons will still be happy. [6:39]
- Haze Bullet Dance no longer gives a target cap, so expect Haze to buy Ricochet more; it also gives more fire rate. [7:09]
- Haze Sleep Dagger now makes targets wake up with reduced fire rate. [7:09]
- Biggest Haze change: she can use items during her ult, including Warp Stone to stay on targets; he expects her low ELO win rate to skyrocket. [7:09]
- Against Haze, especially one building Warp Stone, build Metal Skin; it pretty much completely counters her. [8:09]
- Infernus Catalyst does a little less damage and afterburn builds slower; nothing big. [8:09]
- Lash Ground Strike now pulls enemies to the ground, so jumping at a Vindicta or Grey Talon and ground striking brings them down; Lash keeps the sniper meta in check. [8:39]
- If the enemy has a Grey Talon, hope you have a Lash; as Grey Talon, hope the enemy has none. [9:10]
- McGinnis turrets do less DPS and have less health; good, they were oppressive. [9:10]
- Mirage Tornado goes less far and lifts 0.1 seconds shorter, and Fire Scarabs shred less bullet resist, the biggest Mirage nerf. [9:40]
- Paradox Pulse Wall no longer has charges and deals no max health damage until upgraded, so less lane burst. [9:40]
- Paradox Kinetic Carbine does 15% more damage. [10:11]
- Paradox Swap is now very fast, which is overall a nerf because the enemy is stunned for less time. [10:11]
- Pocket gets small buffs to light melee and Barrage, his first buffs in many patches. [10:42]
- Vindicta crow tick rate changed so Escalating Exposure works less well on her; Assassinate may start at 25% power instead of 50%, making quick scoping less viable; early game nerfed but same damage at max level. [10:42]
- Viscous Cube health regen now scales 0.2 with spirit, and the Goo Ball scales with range upgrades into a giant ball; he expects a nerf or size cap like the Seven ball meta. [11:13]
- Warden gets spirit resist during his ult channel, so just shoot him when he pops it; abilities like Yamato's 1 do less to him. [11:44]
- Wraith ult cast time and damage slightly nerfed; he thinks she needs far bigger nerfs. [12:14]
- Yamato ult no longer prevents death; it heals and gives bullet and spirit resist, and each kill extends it 2 seconds; less of a panic button, but very snowbally with 1v9 potential in low ELO. [12:14]
- With the Yamato change, Silence Glyph is mainly good against Geist, a slight nerf to the item and a small indirect buff to Geist. [12:45]
