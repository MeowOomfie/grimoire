## Covers
Deathy reviews his own top elo Kelvin game (with Mina in mid lane, vs Warden, Vindicta, Infernus, Haze, The Doorman, Mo & Krill) while learning the hero to widen his pool.
Topics: losing lane and coming back, the 4:30 wave before buffs, playing on the urn spawn side, delaying urn turn-ins, dead time, Rejuvenator usage, why roles are mostly fake in matchmaking, and a damage Kelvin build rushing Mystic Reverb.
For players learning a new hero or recovering from a bad lane. Patch as of early May 2026.

## Claims

- Kelvin has only 1 health regen, so many of his trades are not favorable because he does not heal the health back. [1:02]

- Extra Regen is a great item for Kelvin because it builds into Healing Booster, though he no longer feels it is a must. [3:34]
