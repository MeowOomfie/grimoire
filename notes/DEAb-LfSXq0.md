# Why Your Mid Game Sucks | ETERNUS Macro Guide
channel: Sensu  date: 2026-05-18  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DEAb-LfSXq0

## Covers
Mid to late game macro: comeback mechanics, bounty and respawn timers, Urn (states, locking the state, delaying, orbs), Mid Boss and Rejuvenators, Guardians, Walkers, base Guardians, Shrines, Patron, roles (gun carry, spirit carry/nuker, frontline, support, pick), one more wave, counter jungling, deathballing, teleporters, late game items (Refresher, Unstoppable, Indomitable, Cursed Relic); heroes named: Celeste, Bebop, Dynamo, Lash, Seven, McGinnis, Mo & Krill, Haze

## Claims
- If your team is up to 20% behind in souls, the comeback mechanic gives up to 30% more souls from troopers, jungle camps, Sinner's Sacrifices and objectives like Guardians and Walkers. [0:00]
- Killing an enemy richer than your team's average net worth gives a bounty bonus, even if your team is ahead, so focus the fed hero on the enemy team. [0:31]
- After 10 minutes, killing a player whose souls are well above your team's average gives them a longer respawn, from +6 to +22 seconds depending on match time and net worth gap; it starts at 10 minutes at a 15% gap and caps at 25 minutes at a 30% gap. [0:31]
- Never give up when behind, since comebacks are extremely easy; for the same reason it is extremely easy to throw when ahead. [1:02]
- When really ahead in souls, play with caution and never take one for one trades, because you are punished more for dying. [1:02]
- The soul Urn is arguably the most important objective in Deadlock. [1:02]
- The first Urn spawns at 10 minutes on the yellow lane and takes about 12 seconds to descend; its spawn alternates between yellow and green lane every 5 minutes. [1:02]
- The carrier gets movement speed and resists and delivers the Urn to the opposite side of the map. [1:33]
- Delivery gives the team 3,600 souls for the first Urn and an additional 1,150 souls for each later Urn, and each player a random golden statue buff; the carrier gets 35% more souls and four permanent buffs. [1:33]
- On hand-in, 50% of the souls go to the team instantly and 50% spawn as contestable soul orbs; confirm them by shooting or meleeing, and if you cannot contest an Urn, sit back and deny the orbs. [2:03]
- The Urn becomes a comeback Urn when your team's total souls are 10% or more behind (15% for the first Urn), shown by a green triangle on the minimap and green candles; when your team is winning it is marked red with red candles. [2:03]
- A comeback Urn's drop-off is on your side of the map, making it easier to secure. [2:34]
- Delivering a comeback Urn gives each teammate a comeback bonus of 130 souls per minute times the deficit percentage, instantly secured and undeniable; the carrier gets 35% more (the game says 25%, a visual bug). [2:34]
- A comeback Urn is the single fastest way to close the soul gap and should always be the highest priority objective when behind. [2:34]
- A neutral Urn is harder; often neither team runs it until it favors them. Run it if you are confident of winning the team fight: numbers advantage, lane priority or good team fighting ultimates. [2:34]
- Against an unfavored or enemy comeback Urn you cannot contest, let them secure it while you pressure other objectives: kill their Walkers or steal their jungle and Sinner's Sacrifices. [3:04]
- You can also delay the enemy by grabbing the Urn and holding it until your team can hand it in, or dropping it in your spawn (example: an enemy Bebop did this to their comeback Urn). [3:04]
- Capturing the Urn locks its state until the next Urn spawns: if the enemy is close to 10% behind, grab it quickly so it stays neutral. [3:34]
- If your team is close to 10% behind, have the team stop farming for 5 to 10 seconds so it flips to comeback, then capture it to lock it. [3:34]
- Some heroes like Celeste run Urns faster, but any hero can do it in around 25 seconds or less with the right Urn routes. [4:05]
- Mid Boss spawns at 10 minutes; killing it gives each player 500 souls and two golden statue buffs. [6:42]
- The crystal then takes 6 seconds to descend; heavy meleeing it gives the team one Rejuvenator credit, up to three, and full HP to the first player who melees it. Rejuvenators last 4 minutes and revive each player once after dying. [6:42]
- Mid Boss respawns 7 minutes after its first death, 6 after its second, 5 after the third and later. [6:42]
- Mid Boss should take no more than 15 seconds to kill, and you should always have at least one gun carry on it. [6:42]
- Because of recent skill point changes, heroes scale faster into the mid game and the first Mid Boss can easily be taken at 15 to 20 minutes. [7:13]
- Your team should aim to kill at least the first Walker before going for Mid Boss. [7:13]
- If your team is at least 20% ahead with a strong Mid Boss comp, killing it with every enemy alive is not a bad idea. [7:13]
- If you spot the enemy's most valuable player on the far side of the map clearing a wave, use that window to rush Mid Boss. [7:13]
- In most cases, look for Mid Boss when at least two enemies have more than 20 seconds left on their respawn by the time your team can reach the pit. [7:43]
- A common mistake is coin flipping Mid Boss when already ahead; if you are ahead and it seems iffy, most of the time it is not worth taking. [7:43]
- If you are behind and the enemy has heroes that steal Rejuvenators or fight well in the pit, consider baiting Mid Boss: make it roar at 50% HP (which alerts everyone), then camp the entrances with your team to pick people off. [7:43]
- With three Rejuvenators, stay grouped and take objectives one by one: Urn first if available, then Walkers; you have four minutes, so reset to full HP, spend souls, fix lanes, then group and force. [8:14]
- If the enemy has three Rejuvenators, delay them: secure the Urn while they take Mid Boss if it is uncontestable and drop it in your spawn, then turtle for 4 minutes by pushing lanes out so they cannot siege. [8:45]
- Enemies with three Rejuvenators get overconfident and overextend alone in your jungle, making them easy picks. [8:45]
- In a close game when you are too late to steal the Rejuvenator, it can be worth forcing a fight on the team grouped in the pit to burn a couple of their Rejuvenators; do this in the mid game, when they lack time to siege your base during your death timers. [9:15]
- A Guardian gives the team 1,500 souls, 50% to nearby allies and 50% to the rest; killing it disables the nearby enemy shop. [9:46]
- By 12 minutes Guardians drop to -50% damage resist and are very easy to kill; kill them as early as possible so your troopers push toward Walkers and you can roam the enemy side stealing boxes, camps and Sinner's Sacrifices. [9:46]
- Chip Guardians during laning when the lane is shoved, ideally after kills. [10:16]
- After taking your lane's Guardian, depending on hero, let your lane partner solo the lane while you rotate to three-man other Guardians; that is how you snowball into the mid game. [10:16]
- A Walker gives the team 4,000 souls (50% nearby, the rest to the team) and unlocks one extra item slot for the team. [10:16]
- Walker HP rises as each dies: first 6,000, next 9,000, last 12,000. [10:16]
- Walkers start at 65% damage resist, falling to -65% by 18 minutes; they give nearby allies 15% bullet and spirit resist, their fireball reduces enemy resist by 10%, and they stomp on a 6 second cooldown for damage and a 2 second stun. [10:47]
- Benchmark: take the first Walker around 15 minutes, since its HP is low and you do not want to be item slot locked before the first Mid Boss fight. [10:47]
- A gun carry soloing that Walker gets 2,000 souls, the biggest soul spike possible in the mid game. [10:47]
- Do not mindlessly throw yourself at Walkers and die for chip damage; Walkers die deceptively fast and should be pushed strategically. [11:17]
- Take Walkers after kills (a pick with two or three teammates in the lane), or when all lanes are pushed and enemies must split; when several lanes crash into objectives at once, push the Walker fast, since likely at most one enemy defends it. [11:17]
- Another Walker window is when the enemy is grouped on an objective such as an uncontestable Urn: trade the Urn for the Walker. [11:47]
- With low fire rate, the fastest way to kill a Walker is heavy melee weaved with gun attacks, while playing around the stomp; getting stomped with an enemy nearby is deadly. [11:47]
- Avoid much of the Walker fireball damage by double jumping and wall jumping, though fireballs can still hit you on a wall. [12:17]
- When defending, fight on top of your Walker and never more than 28 m away, or you lose its resists and enemies lose the resist reduction; know when to give it up, especially 1v3 against enemies who play around the stomp. [12:17]
- A base Guardian gives 1,000 souls (50% nearby, 50% to the team) and unlocks a faster zipline for that lane; it melees and knocks back, so attack from range. [12:17]
- The only reason to kill base Guardians is to let troopers into the base to pressure Shrines; there is little incentive and it is often not worth trading other objectives for. [12:48]
- Currently you can skip base Guardians and hit Shrines directly since they do not need troopers to take damage; he expects a patch, but with a small window while pushed up a lane it may be worth it. [12:48]
- A Shrine gives 2,000 souls and lets the enemy Patron be damaged; it turns your troopers into super troopers (+60% damage, +40% HP) in that lane and the blue lane, so one Shrine buffs two of three lanes; super troopers give 15% fewer souls to enemies. [12:48]
- First Shrine has 5,000 HP, the second 10,000; Shrines take reduced bullet resist when enemy heroes are near, and do an AoE knockback that curses and damages on about a 10 second cooldown. [13:18]
- After all three Walkers, Shrines are the highest priority objective besides Mid Boss; the best time is after Mid Boss with three Rejuvenators. [13:48]
- With no Rejuvenators and Mid Boss not yet respawned but a numbers advantage, quickly take one Shrine, since the first is easy and buffs two lanes. [13:48]
- Never greed the second Shrine if risky, since it buffs only one lane and takes a while; it is safer after Mid Boss, when super troopers also pressure the enemy. [13:48]
- To take Shrines fast, put at least three people on one to cut its bullet resist, stand on the platform to avoid its attack, and use the vent to rotate to the other Shrine. [14:18]
- If enemies push your base with three Rejuvenators, fight at your last Shrine (10,000 HP), where they clump up for your AoE. [14:18]
- The Patron's first phase is damageable after the second Shrine falls; both phases have 12k HP scaling per minute. [14:18]
- Killing the first Patron phase cuts the defenders' respawn timers by 20 seconds (down to no less than 10 seconds left), and the second phase takes 10 seconds to form. [14:48]
- So you can leave the first Patron phase up while taking the remaining base Guardians, or while your team guarantees a Mid Boss, keeping enemies on longer respawns and entering the next push with three Rejuvenators. [14:48]
- The weakened Patron does a cursing knockback explosion every 7 to 8 seconds; you can only hit it from the pit, and there is a safe spot that avoids the explosion. [15:18]
- The Patron gets 50% damage resist with one defender in the pit and 100% with two or more; with one low defender and a low Patron, ignore the hero and tunnel the Patron, but with two defenders focus the heroes first. [15:18]
- When defending your Patron, never fight outside the pit; use its attacks and the resist you grant it. [15:49]
- Roles are a guide, not a definitive list; pick heroes can be gun carries, some spirit heroes can be pick, and so on. [15:49]
- Gun carries farm everything: solo side lanes, solo structures, take Sinner's Sacrifices and jungle camps, especially T3 camps; avoid early team fights since they scale with souls, and use fights across the map as split pressure to solo Walkers. [15:49]
- Spirit carries or nukers fight early to mid game while farming T1 and T2 camps and Sinner's Sacrifices; they do a lot of damage with few items. Stick with your team's pick or frontline heroes and rotate for kills. [16:20]
- Frontliners create space and chaos hunting engagements; they are strong in team fights, so avoid 1v1 side lane duels and keep a teammate nearby to follow up. [16:20]
- Supports watch enemy items and threats, buy Knockdown, Rescue Beam, Divine Barrier and Cursed Relic, and shadow the strongest teammate to enable plays and keep them alive. [16:50]
- Pick heroes use mobility and CC to catch enemies out of position, staying off the enemy minimap and targeting overextended rich enemies to cut their farm and force long respawns. [16:50]
- When enemy troopers are on the zipline, ask if you can clear one more wave before leaving the lane; extra waves crash into objectives and force enemies to fix the lane. [17:20]
- Pushed waves act as mobile wards that reveal enemies and create blind spots for them, giving your team a green light to counter jungle or pressure an objective elsewhere. [17:20]
- When lanes are shoved or you have a numbers advantage, counter jungle to widen the soul gap; leave one jungle minion alive to delay the camp's respawn. [17:50]
- Deathballing: group with at least three teammates, ideally one pick hero and one spirit nuker, and move as a unit to run down objectives and catch enemies overextending or solo farming in their jungle; it breaks enemy tempo by forcing defensive rotations. [17:50]
- Deathball after winning a skirmish with a numbers advantage, rather than dispersing to lanes; also after mid game power spikes such as carries getting tier four items, or to break a stalemate when both teams just farm. [18:21]
- Teleporters are rarely used at low ranks but are the fastest way to cross the map and take 4 seconds. [18:21]
- Teleporter use: if your shop is down from a lost Guardian and you hold souls (and unsecured jungle souls), teleport to your secret shop on the other side, spend, and teleport back to lane. [18:52]
- Teleporter use: after taking a Walker deep in one lane, avoid being cut off on the zipline by teleporting from the enemy subway to the other side near the enemy secret shop (at most one, maybe low, enemy there), then pressure the other lane's Walker. [19:22]
- Teleporter use: from a side lane with no objectives, teleport to join a fight your team takes on the opposite side. [19:52]
- In the late game one bad team fight loses the game; watch for fight-swinging items. [19:52]
- Refresher has a 5 minute cooldown and resets ultimate cooldowns; mostly bought by Dynamo and Lash, sometimes Seven, McGinnis and Mo & Krill. [19:52]
- Unstoppable makes you immune to all CC; usually bought by heroes with channeled ultimates like Lash, Dynamo, Haze and Seven; do not waste CC on a player with this effect. [20:23]
- Indomitable is a passive item that stops curse and most CC on a 55 second cooldown, like a passive Counterspell for crowd control; many carries buy it late game. Do not waste high cooldown CC on its holder until it is on cooldown, and call it out when it procs. [20:23]
- Both Unstoppable and Indomitable stop curse, so hold Cursed Relic until those items are on cooldown. [20:54]
