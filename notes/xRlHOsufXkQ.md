channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-05-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRlHOsufXkQ

## Covers
A fast stream tier list of every weapon, vitality and spirit item after the May 2025 major item update (late May 2025 patch era, placements will go stale). For players choosing builds who want one gun-focused player's read on which items are strong, bait or overrated, with reasons for the contested ones (Spellslinger, Armor Piercing Rounds, Fury Trance, Trophy Collector, Counter Spell).
