# The BEST Items In Deadlock Right Now (META Guide)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-03-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RAvOQCYRFMg

## Covers
The strongest items after the March 2026 item patch, drawn from Night Shift pro tournament and ladder stats: Greater Expansion, Healbane, Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Warp Stone, Golden Goose Egg, Improved Spirit and Boundless Spirit, Opening Rounds, Spirit Shredder Bullets and Weighted Shots.
For players of any rank who want to know what to buy in a spirit-heavy meta and when.

## Claims
- In both Night Shift pro stats and ladder stats, gun items are underperforming right now; the meta is very spirit focused and spirit nuking stays strong even in the late game. [0:30]
- Greater Expansion is the best item right now: ability range applies to all your abilities and also to the range of your items. [0:30]
- On Infernus, Greater Expansion makes Flame Dash about twice the size with a big trail that is hard to dodge, and makes the ultimate huge. [1:01]
- Greater Expansion with a maxed Infernus ultimate makes the ultimate cover the entire mid boss pit. [1:32]
- On Ivy, Greater Expansion greatly increases Kudzu Bomb radius, the tether link radius, the stone form radius and the ultimate radius. [1:32]
- Vortex Web is commonly bought on Ivy right now; its base radius is smallish but Greater Expansion makes it huge since item range scales too. [2:02]
- On Apollo, Greater Expansion gives a bigger ultimate radius and a bigger lunge radius so you can catch and hit enemies from very far away. [2:02]
- Greater Expansion also makes Torment Pulse tick from much farther away, so Apollo can keep chasing and damaging enemies with no downside. [2:33]
- Greater Expansion also gives 10 spirit resist; it is bought a lot in pro play and you should include it in builds on many heroes, because hitting abilities more consistently means more consistent damage. [2:33]
- Healbane is the second best item and is overlooked, especially in low rank, because most heroes now have a lot of base healing even before the enemy buys lifesteal items. [3:04]
- Kelvin was recently buffed to heal a lot early from his grenades and Ice Storm, even in lane. [3:34]
- Victor heals from two base abilities he spams: Pain Battery at max level heals for missing HP, and so does Jump Start. [4:04]
- Reducing enemy healing with Healbane usually adds more damage than buying another damage item. [4:04]
- Heroes with base healing that Healbane counters include Abrams (his 1), Mina (her rake) and Wraith (the heart card now heals on base); more than 50% of the roster has some base healing. [4:04]
- Buy an early Healbane if your lane opponents are heroes with base healing. [4:36]
- In pro play three or four Healbanes show up almost every game because it raises damage output; the effect is not obvious in a fight but is large compared with not having it. [4:36]
- Other healing reduction options are not meta: Toxic Bullets and Decay are good situational buys on certain heroes but expensive, and the rest are 6K items only affordable very late. [5:06]
- Counterspell is underbought; it counters many ultimates in a meta full of hard stuns and ults that kill you if you get caught. [5:38]
- Not being stunned for two or three seconds, or not being displaced into the enemy team, is worth more than a 30% spirit resist item, because you die anyway with the resist. [6:10]
- If the enemy has three or more heroes whose ultimates Counterspell answers, plan before the game to buy it. [6:10]
- You can buy Counterspell from roughly 10K to 12K net worth, but you really want it by 15K to 20K net worth in those games. [6:40]
- Decide on Counterspell before the game so you are not caught by multiple ultimates and only realize after three deaths that you needed it. [6:40]
- Dispel Magic got much better this patch; last patch it was weak because there were few damage over time effects to remove and Counterspell was the better option. [6:40]
- Many damage over time effects got buffed: Infernus burn got much stronger and Dispel Magic is the best counter to it. [7:10]
- Shiv knives got much stronger; taking two or three knives basically kills you, and Dispel Magic lets you get out. [7:10]
- Mirage's Djinn's Mark does more damage than before; most Mirage players go full spirit and one shot you once it is maxed, which Dispel Magic answers. [7:40]
- Slowing Hex got buffed and is coming into the meta; it shuts down heroes who rely on movement abilities to get in and out, such as Calico and Apollo. [7:40]
- Under Slowing Hex, Apollo cannot use three of his abilities and Calico cannot use Leaping Slash or her ultimate. [8:11]
- On movement heroes, buy Dispel Magic if the enemy buys more than one Slowing Hex so you can still play the game. [8:11]
- Green (spirit) items are very strong right now. [8:11]
- Warp Stone is underbought; high level streamers and pros buy it almost every game. [8:11]
- Warp Stone is bought for the extra movement, not the bullet resist: it is basically a blink on cooldown to get into or out of a fight, and it saves you in situations where you would otherwise be dead. [8:42]
- You can also pop Warp Stone against gun damage for its bullet resist effect. [8:42]
- Greater Expansion also increases Warp Stone teleport distance. [9:12]
- Golden Goose Egg is good in certain situations: buy it as your first item when you expect a hard lane. [9:12]
- With Golden Goose Egg against stronger enemy laners, play passively, push the wave out and farm boxes so the item accumulates value over time. [9:42]
- Do not buy Golden Goose Egg if you have a chance in lane, since it lowers your chance to kill the enemy and one or two kills are usually worth more than the souls and buffs the egg accumulates. [9:42]
- Rule of thumb for Golden Goose Egg: judge how hard the lane is for you and buy it accordingly. [10:13]
- Sell Golden Goose Egg at around 12 to 20 minutes; keeping it longer is too greedy. Sell it when you are back in base to buy your first big item. [10:13]
- Example: on Victor with Golden Goose Egg first, sell it in base to afford a first 3K item like Berserker or Surge of Power; sell it for a 3K or 6K item in the mid game. [10:44]
- Improved Spirit and Boundless Spirit are very strong on many heroes because most heroes' ability spirit scaling was improved, making raw spirit very valuable. [11:14]
- People are rushing Boundless Spirit or buying it more; it used to be a situational late game item. [11:45]
- Boundless Spirit gives more percentage the more base spirit you have, so it gets stronger the longer the game goes; do not rush it as your first 6K item in lane. [11:45]
- Buy Boundless Spirit to reach a higher spirit investment threshold, around 9.6K spirit investment. [11:45]
- On Apollo the coach buys Boundless Spirit after items like Torment Pulse at around 20K net worth: spirit power goes from 114 to 164. [12:15]
- On Apollo, without Boundless Spirit a perfect hold does 333 damage and the ultimate 450; Boundless Spirit raises both by around 33% or more (more for the ultimate). [12:15]
- In a spirit meta many enemies buy spirit resist; counter it with more damage or with spirit shred later. [12:47]
- There are few good spirit shred options; it usually comes from a gun carry buying Crippling Headshot in the late game, with only some mid game options, so going more spirit (Boundless Spirit) is usually correct. [12:47]
- Several meta gun items also give spirit power right now, notably Opening Rounds and Spirit Shredder Bullets. [13:17]
- Opening Rounds gives more weapon damage against enemies above 50% health plus free extra spirit, the same spirit effect for 800 souls while holding a gun item. [13:17]
- Spirit Shredder Bullets is high value on heroes who apply bullet damage easily or shoot between casts: it shreds enemy spirit resist and gives spirit lifesteal, now 12% after the buff. [13:48]
- Spirit Lifesteal alone gives 16% spirit lifesteal, while Spirit Shredder Bullets gives shred plus 12%, close to it. [14:18]
- Weighted Shots is a strong pick because debuff resist is very hard to get right now; debuff items were nerfed and the old debuff remover was changed to Dispel Magic. [14:18]
- Debuff resist lowers the effect of things over time such as a Mo & Krill grab, a Holliday lasso or Infernus burn, giving a higher chance to live. [14:50]
- Debuff Reducer is a good choice but only upgrades in the late game into Spellbreaker and Unstoppable, which are expensive. [14:50]
- Weighted Shots gives almost the same debuff resist as Debuff Reducer plus slowing bullets and weapon damage, so it gained value on heroes who use their weapon well. [14:50]
- Buy Weighted Shots late game on someone like Victor who does not want to be shut down or stunned, and on hard carries who do not want to be stun locked. [15:20]
- If you cannot afford Unstoppable or want to skip it for more gun damage, Weighted Shots keeps you alive longer while adding bullet damage and a slow that is strong against most heroes. [15:20]
- Classic gun carries like Warden and Venator should still follow the gun-heavy builds of pros and streamers; those gun items are still valuable. [15:51]
- Overall gun is weak right now, at least at higher rank; even at lower rank it is better to invest more into spirit and into making abilities hit. [16:23]
- Give Greater Expansion a chance on many heroes. [16:23]
