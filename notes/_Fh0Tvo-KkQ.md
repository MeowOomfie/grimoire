## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream on the 12/30/25 patch (Yamato, Sinclair and Ivy buffs). Two solo queue reviews: a Victor laning with Warden into Lash and Yamato (rank not given), then an Ascendant Holliday playing Midknight's own build.
Then live scrim coaching for Colossus, a South American pro team he coaches: drafts, lane plans, side laners, farming, Sinner's Sacrifice, spikes and macro, plus Seven and Warden build reviews for chat.
Side opinions: Billy, Lady Geist, Abrams, Shiv and Mirage overtuned, Mo & Krill, Bebop and on-hit effects.

## Claims
General and patch notes:
