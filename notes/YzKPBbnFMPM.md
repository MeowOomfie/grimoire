channel: Midknight
date: 2024-11-16

## Covers
Midknight coaches a Ritualist Lady Geist (about their 100th game, Normals) in a duo lane with Bebop against Vindicta and Warden, then the whole 40-minute game.
Themes: lane positioning and minion damage, Malice before Essence Bomb, Healing Rite when low, overfighting and staggered respawns, flex slots and walkers, mid boss and bridge buffs, spending souls, Mystic Reverb, anti-heal.
Late-2024 video (patch of mid November 2024, Soul Rebirth just removed); the game has changed a lot since, so check items, numbers and map details against the current patch.

## Claims
Player (Lady Geist, Ritualist):
- Walking up to melee a minion tanks two minions plus full poke from Vindicta and Warden; play around cover instead of tanking. [0:31]
- Extra Regen is a fine defensive item when you are above about 40% HP; when you are this low, buy Healing Rite. [1:01]
- Gun players dislike buying Healing Rite because they play for the swap, but keep your health topped off before you get your ult. [1:01]
- You can see a projectile's trajectory; walk to either side of it instead of walking in front of it. [1:01]
- When the enemy is about to kill minions or is shooting them, put your crosshair on the minion's head and get ready to flick to the soul orb for the deny. [1:33]
- While farming, always think about which cover you are behind so enemy laners cannot poke you down. [1:33]
- Throw Malice first, then Essence Bomb: if Malice hits, it gives about 8% extra damage on the bomb, and the bomb does a huge amount of damage. [1:33] [2:35]
- At this MMR players melee minions by default; shoot the minion instead when the enemy can punish you, because you take a chunk even if the melee lands (about 400 damage of poke here). [2:05]
- When very low, reset or buy Healing Rite; use the zipline boost both ways to get back to lane faster. [2:05]
- Leveling the ult mid fight with the tab click or alt click shortcut is good. [2:35]
- At half HP with no ult, do not play far up; after a kill, reset, do camps and come back when they push the wave back. [3:06]
- Taking three minions' fire costs about half your HP; here it was 400 trooper damage in 30 seconds. [3:06]
- At half HP be afraid of a Vindicta tether into a snipe. [3:36]
- When Bebop lands a hook, start meleeing the instant the target arrives. [3:36]
- When a target is tethered or standing still, your crosshair should be on their head. [4:07]
- After the enemy Warden wastes his ult, punish him for it. [4:39]
- When down two players and the enemy pushes two other walkers, decide which walkers to defend and give the third; if the whole team defends one, you lose two. [4:39]
- Use your zipline boost to get to the defense sooner. [5:43]
- In a fight, burst the misplaced backline Yamato first instead of over chasing a low McGinnis into three enemies and leaving two half-HP teammates alone. [5:43]
- If you come to a lane where you cannot kill the tower, it should be for the Urn. [6:15]
- When an enemy is under his tower, crash behind the boxes to poke him without taking tower damage instead of backing off. [6:46]
- Do not shoot the tower when an enemy hero is one shot. [6:46]
- Against double snipers, Knockdown is very good; Bebop should buy it and so should you. [6:46]
- Do not walk back into a fight two enemies hold just to chase the ult. [7:16]
- Take bridge buffs after they spawn and check the map at least every 30 seconds; ideally every couple of seconds at a high level. [7:16]
- Constantly pathing to fights and death balling without getting kills is bad; this was a good farming break. [7:46]
- With three enemies dead (18 minutes in) and no enemy stun up to steal Rejuvenator, call mid boss. [7:46]
- Someone on the team needs Knockdown at minimum; it is very strong and good against their comp. [8:17]
- Once you push past bridge, expect their team to come; chasing into their walker with four people deserved to be punished with a team wipe and a mid boss. [8:47]
- Once you push past about 40% of their lane you run into too many base guardians. [8:47]
- At a decently high rank, call mid boss once one enemy is dead near mid boss. [9:17]
- If the whole team collapses on a caught teammate, join it; otherwise let him die. [9:47]
- Fighting without taking objectives is bad. [9:47]
- With three of yours dead and theirs on shorter respawns, the fight is a 4v3 that becomes a 5v3 when Warden respawns in 10 seconds; catch a side lane instead of pathing to the fight. [10:17]
- When the enemy pushes with no objective in that lane (only base guardians), it matters less than a walker. [10:17]
- Do not fight until your Infernus and Haze respawn in 20 seconds; play in your base. [10:48]
- When your target escapes to his team, call it dead and back off. [11:51]

- Normally a lone Grey Talon means his team is near, but at low rank he is probably just alone. [13:52]
