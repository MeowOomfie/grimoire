## Covers
Box and healing bat runs on the new map: what boxes, tough crates, healing bats and golden statues are worth, when a box run beats staying in lane, how long it should be, and route walkthroughs for yellow, green and blue lanes.
Includes a tunnel loop and ultimate-timing trick for the tunnel heroes Calico, Rem and Rat King. For any player who wants more souls between waves.

## Claims
- Boxes, tough crates and healing bats spawn at 3 minutes and respawn 3 minutes after you take them. [0:31]
- Regular boxes have a 60% chance to contain souls, averaging about 18 souls at 3 minutes and about 20 souls at 5 minutes. [0:31]
- Tough crates (the ones you heavy melee) always drop souls and are worth around 60 souls in laning phase. [0:31]
- A trooper at 3 minutes is worth 106 souls, or 57 each when shared with your lane partner, so one tough crate is worth about a whole shared trooper. [0:31]
- Healing bats heal 10% of max health. [1:01]
- The healing trooper heals 160 plus 13% of current HP (more when lower), divided by two with a lane partner; you can take the solo heal if your teammate leaves. [1:01]
- Example: at 1,200 max health and half HP, a shared healing trooper heals about 160 and a healing bat 120. [1:01]
- Golden statues (now buff containers) have a 50% drop rate of a stat buff, and the buffs increase at 10 minutes. He values a drop at roughly 20 to 80 souls and says to go out of your way for them. [1:32]
- Box run when your lane is not productive, meaning you cannot get positive trades that force enemies under their Guardian, back to base, or kill them. If you can do something productive, the box run is usually not worth it. [2:02]
- Doing a lot of Guardian damage or taking the enemy Guardian is usually worth more than a box run. [2:32]
- If you cannot walk up to the Guardian, for example against a Lash or a Billy who would run you down, go on a box run instead. [2:32]
- Guardians start at 50% damage resist, scaling down linearly to -50% at 12 minutes, so at 3 minutes you may do little Guardian damage even with gun items and a box run can be better. [2:32]
- Many heroes spike hard at about 2.6k souls when they get two ability points into one ability; with that matchup, play for the spike and avoid interacting until you have it. [3:02]
- Always tell your lane partner when you leave for a box run, or they may walk into the enemy Guardian 1v2 and die. [3:32]
- Useful pings: "I'm leaving the area", "Retreat" for heroes without that voice line, the new "Going to farm", or "Going to rat" to catch attention. [3:32]
- Priority means pushing the wave into their Guardian first, so you can leave while they clear under tower, which also allows an aggressive route stealing boxes on their side. [4:02]
- Trooper waves spawn every 30 seconds; the faster you clear, the longer your run can be. To be back for the next wave and keep priority, keep the run under about 30 seconds. [4:32]
- If you do not care about priority on the next wave, take a longer route; then you only need to avoid missing troopers that die under your Guardian. [5:03] [11:12]
- Winning and stronger (you get the push) but lane not productive: take an aggressive or a ganking route. Neutral lane: neutral or ganking route, or aggressive if you can get priority. Losing or weaker: neutral route depending on map control, or passive if they push to your tower and you cannot contest. [5:03]
- A ganking route takes a jump pad toward boxes near another lane (from mid, toward the middle area by the bridge buffs) so you can gank that lane. [5:03]
- Always push your wave before going on a box run. [6:03]
- The trade-off of a long run is losing priority: your partner (his example a Wraith) is left 1v2 and cannot push, so you may lose tower damage or troopers. You can choose to give a healthy partner with wave clear the solo wave, but tell them. [6:03]
- A long absence can also let an enemy (his example a Lash) take priority and rotate to gank mid. [7:04]
- Tunnel heroes really only need one route: two connected tunnels. At the bridge buffs, one entrance is on your left when facing the secret shop, the other is past the railway under the bridge, on the left of the tier two camp. [7:04]
- Boxes inside these tunnels spawn at 5 minutes and respawn after 5 minutes, not 3. [7:35]
- One side of the bridge has the secret shop at Leaning Lake, the other at Chinatown; the entrances are laid out the same on both sides. [8:07]
- The other tunnels are not worth learning unless you one-trick a tunnel hero. [8:37]
