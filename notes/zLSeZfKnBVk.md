## Claims
- Grey Talon hard counters Vindicta. [0:31]
- His charged shot hits for about 80 early, and Mystic Burst roughly doubles that. [0:31]
- He is using Lord NM's build again. [0:31]
- Early items in this game included Extra Charge. [4:15]
- Boxes are worth much more now than before, especially the heavy boxes. [7:24]
- You have to hit that box halfway this time around. [7:24]
- Late in the game Mystic Burst no longer does much. [14:12]
- Doing waves is beneficial; do not throw them away to go mid. [14:12]
- Fighting under your own blue Walker was a terrible idea and cost two Walkers. [15:46]
- Grey Talon does not need high frame rates: drop traps sometimes, fly in the air, aim your 1 and hit birds; it is much easier than an M1 hero that juggles many abilities. [16:18]
- He has a lot of trouble birding on this map because there are so many buildings people can hide in and behind; the old map was much more open. [16:48]
- Planned item order: Rapid Recharge, then Spirit Burn, though he realized he needed Counterspell. [18:48]
- Since enemies cannot remove his trap, putting traps up all the time is good. [21:24]
- He picked up Metal Skin (already had Battle Vest), then planned Spirit Burn and maybe Counterspell. [22:57]
- If you die out there, they take base; keep that in mind before fighting. [22:57]
- He followed Counterspell with a Boundless item next. [25:35]
- He thinks he needs Indom (as captioned) on Grey Talon all the time as a backup for when Counterspell bugs and does not go off. [27:42]
- When behind, the only way to win was contesting mid boss. [29:46]
