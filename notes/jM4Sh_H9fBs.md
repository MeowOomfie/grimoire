## Covers
Graves, played by aquatiq with a gun and debuff build (Tesla Bullets rush) instead of the usual spirit Soul Collector build.
Commentary over one game: lane safety, ult placement, items and macro calls.
Patch of early October 2026 (video dated 2026-10-10).

## Claims
- This game skips the usual spirit and Soul Collector build for a "Farming Bips" (as captioned) Tesla Bullets rush, a debuff build that still does a lot of spirit damage. [0:39]
- With this build you are supposed to play safe until you get Tesla Bullets. [1:09]
- Graves with the normal build is very strong situationally. [1:09]
- In lane, prioritize collecting the souls over fighting for a camp you cannot get. [2:10]
- Play further back than your Pocket lane partner. [2:10]
- Graves is weak really early and gets really good late, so play very safe for a long time and heal instead of going back to spawn. [3:14]
- He considered Suppressor against this lane, but leaned toward rushing Tesla Bullets. [3:14]
- Against Haze, stay away: her Fixation stacks build when she is close to you, and trading gun shots with her only gives her more stacks. [3:44]
- Healing Rite let him stay in lane instead of going back. [4:45]
- The most important thing for Graves is to stay alive; her abilities deal the damage, not her gun. [4:45]
- He found a new spot for the Graves ult and was happy to put it there every time. [5:16]
- Do not punch an enemy who may have Rebuttal; he suspected the Drifter bought it against Pocket's melee. [6:51]
- While the team goes mid, farm the side lane. [7:23]
- Maxing Essence Theft makes all summons apply Essence Theft. [7:23]
- He should have bought Battle Vest. [8:30]
- Capacitor was his next item after losing a bag; bags do not matter that much. [9:33]
- Put the ult down all the time; it is still good. [9:33]
- Having enemies chase you instead of going to the Rift is a good trade. [10:34]
- Do not take a 2v1 fight in a side lane. [11:36]
- Kill the Haze when she has a big bag. [11:36]
- Toxic Bullets is a must against a very high health enemy (one had about 17k). [12:40]
- Put the ult down just to be annoying, forcing enemies to come and clear the wave. [13:10]
- Graves might be the best debuffing character in the game because she auto-applies everything. [13:10]
- He wanted Stamina Mastery. [14:16]
- Capacitor was nuts in a team fight. [15:17]
- Split Shot gives the build multi-target attacks. [16:23]
- Put the ult in a far spot where enemies need a long path to clear it, though an enemy still broke it. [16:55]
- At a mid boss fight Graves is probably useless; put the ult on a side lane instead. [17:29]
- If the enemy has two players at mid boss only when all are there, push a Walker; he took a solo Walker for a lot of souls. [18:00]
- He needed resilience items because he was taking so much damage. [18:32]
- Down 10k souls, sit on the Rift. [18:32]
- He put the ult in the pit where the enemies were fighting. [24:49]
- Plated Armor saved him; with the team down 30k, tanks like Abrams should have Metal Skin against gun damage. [25:19]
- Late items: Leech and Unstoppable. [25:51]
- In a base defense, play around the door in the pit. [29:29]
