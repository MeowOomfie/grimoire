## Covers
Patch reaction by Midknight, a Deadlock coach, to a minor but long update.
Each change with his verdict: urn and bounty values, Opening Rounds, Apollo, Calico, Doorman, Graves, Grey Talon, Holiday, McGinnis, Paige, Pocket, Seven, Silver, Viscous, Victor, Vindicta, Warden.
Patch of 2026-06-12.

## Claims
- Urn: comeback bounty reduced by 10%. [0:00]
- Urn: the give-up timer is now 60 seconds (was 75); if the urn sits that long it explodes into souls you can confirm. [0:00]
- The health buff from breakables scales less. [0:30]
- Kill bounty values increased by 8%; he dislikes it, since it pushes toward a meta where the laning stage does not matter. [0:30]
- Opening Rounds gets its spirit power back in exchange for its conditional weapon damage; he hates the change, since the real problem is spirit and hybrid heroes buying it (he bought the old 7% spirit power version on Rem without really shooting). [1:00]
- Apollo: his well (what he puts in the ground) now launches him further and more horizontally; the maxed upgrade lets him recast it, which adds a lot of damage. [1:00]
- Verdict on Apollo: really strong early and mid game, but he still has no answer to Spirit Resilience, so he should not run lobbies late. [1:32]
- Calico: about 100 extra healing, a bit more ultimate damage and better melee shred on her bombs; insignificant, because melee builds are never real. [1:32]
- Doorman: minor nerf to his bell. [2:02]
- Graves: her 1 is nerfed a bit and is harder to charge; it was dealing too much damage instantly, even when you break the skulls after their first hit. [2:02]
- Haru (as captioned): the grab holds you for less time, but the real issue is its odd hitbox and how far it pulls from. [2:02]
- Gun Grey Talon nerfs: relatively minor on paper but the two changes hurt his DPS a lot; he hopes Grey Talon shifts back to spirit builds. [2:02]
- Holiday: her keg now starts with two charges, but the tier two upgrade only gives a charge, a much weaker spike than the old 58 damage. [2:32]
- Holiday: initial spirit scaling nerfed, so she is weaker in lane, and the charge time between kegs is longer. [2:32]
- Holiday: the tier three keg upgrade now gives more damage than the old tier two, plus 0.5 more spirit scaling and minus 5 seconds charge time; she will actually do something mid to late, and her barrel spike is massive. [3:02]
- Holiday: the stun is back on her bounce pad; he calls it the worst change, it was awful when it was in the game before. [3:32]
- McGinnis: turret DPS is significantly lower but scales better, breaking even at 200 spirit power, which most McGinnis players do not reach; the turret build will feel less oppressive early. [3:32]
- Paige: a decent buff; more spirit scaling on her barrier makes it annoying to play against, but he still thinks she is a worse Viscous. [4:03]
- Pocket: 1 second less on his cloak, which he mocks as far too small ("the hero is balanced now"); less Affliction damage to objectives is a good change, but the hero will still nuke you. [4:03]
- Seven: the bullet damage scaling nerf is significant, minus 15% of his gun damage at max level late game, though much of his damage comes from spirit. [4:33]
- An ultimate captioned "Shovel" is made worse for moving around the map; he thinks if people only use an ultimate to travel, the fix is to buff the ultimate instead. [4:33]
- Silver: big nerfs, her curse tier three no longer heals and her ultimate has 30% more cooldown; a step in the right direction but she is still not close to balanced. [5:03]
- Silver fix he would prefer: make her human form weaker and make it harder for her to get into her ultimate. [5:03]
- Viscous: his ball goes from 30% to 40% resist; still bad, it used to be 60% and actually felt hard to kill, so it needs plus 15 to 20%. [5:34]
- Viscous: Puddle Punch lifesteal buffed; he thinks punch builds are bad to play and play against and should be dropped. [5:34]
- Victor: a bit weaker early, stronger in his ability upgrades and especially his maxes. [6:04]
- Vindicta: Assassinate spirit scaling buffed; he thinks she is already good and only overshadowed by the strongest heroes, so she will be in a really good spot once those are nerfed. [6:04]
- Warden: more bullet falloff, less spirit scaling on his flask, and an ultimate buff; he finds the mix odd and doubts it does much. [6:36]
- He would like Warden pushed back to spirit builds; spirit Warden is more fun and more counterable, since you play around stopping his ultimate or cursing him during its channel. [7:06]
