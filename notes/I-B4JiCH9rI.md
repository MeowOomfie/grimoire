# Is Gun Rat King Good?
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-10-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I-B4JiCH9rI

## Covers
Rat King with a pure gun build (basically the Warden build), aquatiq commentating over his game.
Commentary verdict on gun Rat King vs his earlier tank build, lane feel and ult usage.
Patch of early October 2026 (video 2026-10-05).

## Claims
- He ran essentially the Warden gun build, after using a tank build in his previous game where he just ran at people. [0:31]
- Verdict: Rat King's gun is not good, even with a full gun build; he does very little damage. [1:01]
- He does not recommend the gun build; try it if you want but he suggests against it. [13:11]
- With lots of gun items he still did little damage late game and regretted building gun. [24:15]
- Rat King's lane is very bad; he needed extra healing and regen in lane. [1:31]
- Into an enemy laner with built in Suppressor his lane was rough. [0:31]
- He is good for one thing, his ult; otherwise he is a tank whose job is to eat damage. [2:02]
- A partner ulting first (here Seven) is strong with him, because together you zone the enemy and he just runs at them. [4:06]
- He likes the Swift Striker option in the build. [5:10]
- His strength: put the shield (Royal Pestilence) on and run at people; you deal little damage but take none either. [5:44]
- Royal Pestilence saved him when he was chased across the map. [13:11]
- Always break the boxes you walk past because they have a 100% spawn chance and give good money. [9:58]
- Use the ult to start fights: call it, then run in with the team. [12:40]
- Keep the ult for the Rift; he offered his ult for the Rift fight. [21:41]
- While carrying the banner his turning circle is very bad. [22:44]
- He put an ability targeted item on the wrong ability, called it a bad buy, sold it and put it on Rat Swarm instead. [19:00]
- His huge hitbox makes him a free target that is almost impossible to miss. [21:41]
- He ended the game with the most assists (17) despite low damage, as a tank who enables the team. [25:20]
