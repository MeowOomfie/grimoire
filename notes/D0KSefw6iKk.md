# The Unstable Rift hits the Cursed Apple | Patch Breakdown
channel: Deathy

## Covers
Patch breakdown for the 2026-06-30 patch: the new Unstable Rift king of the hill objective, the reworked solo Urn, net-worth-scaled comeback bonuses, the boon table, economy, respawn and movement changes.
Then the short item list (Mystic Shot, Toxic Bullets, Scourge, Cursed Relic) and hero-by-hero changes with the coach's read on each.
For any player who wants to know what changed and what it means in their games, read before the coach had played the patch.

## Claims
- The Unstable Rift is a new king of the hill objective, essentially the old Urn king of the hill split out; it warns you where it will open before it spawns and shows a capture zone with a progress bar and its current value. [0:00]
- Captured at about 45 seconds into the game (in the coach's demo) the Rift is worth about what the Urn was worth before. [0:31]
- Capturing the Rift spawns rift troopers: bigger, very tanky, and at 1 minute they can probably take down a guardian by themselves despite early guardian resistances. [0:31]
- The Unstable Rift spawns every 6 to 7 minutes with a variable start time and a global announcement 25 seconds before it spawns, meant to make it harder to react to so not every fight turns into a 6v6. [2:33]
- Coach's verdict: unsure about the spawn time randomness, but it will bring more chaos, which may be good short term. [3:03]
- The comeback bonus used to be a flat 35% resists of both types; it now scales linearly with how much net worth the team trails by, so 10% behind gets less than 15% behind. Coach calls this good and long requested. [3:03]
- The Rift sends rift troopers down from its location: 5 troopers when the game is even, up to 12 when the capturing team is behind; they have extra health and damage. [4:06]
- Any player inside the Unstable Rift zone is revealed on the main map. [4:06]
- Rift troopers exist to give momentum to push an objective: a comeback Rift with 12 troopers is basically a guaranteed walker, and even an even Rift gives at minimum a double wave against the walker, so push the walker after you win it. [4:36]
- The Rift gives about the same bounty the Urn king of the hill gave. [5:07]
- The Urn is now its own solo objective: it spawns at either end of the map where the old neutral pickup locations were, is delivered to the opposite end, and there seem to be no comeback locations anymore. [5:07]
- The Urn spawns every 5 minutes starting at 10 minutes (10, 15, 20), back to the old spawn time. [5:39]
- The Urn runner is no longer revealed, disarmed or silenced: you can shoot and use all abilities while carrying it, though a few things still drop it (going through The Doorman's doorway, Mirage teleport). [5:39]
- You cannot manually drop the Urn, so you cannot pick it up and hand it to an Ivy or Kelvin; you carry it the whole way. [6:09]
- The Urn runner can no longer parry, so you can melee them repeatedly; the Urn drops when the runner is hit by a light or heavy melee, stunned or killed. [6:09]
- A dropped Urn stays where it drops and does not walk back home. [6:09]
- The Urn's bounty starts decaying 45 seconds after pickup, drains over another 45 seconds, and the Urn is removed when empty, so you can deny it by picking it up and holding it for 90 seconds. It is unclear whether it drains while lying on the ground. [6:39]
- Instant deposit is back. [7:09]
- The Urn is worth much less: 250 souls plus 70 per minute, so almost 1,000 at 10 minutes, about 1,200 at 15 and about 1,500 at 20; a smaller net worth swing but still important. [7:09]
- The Urn still gives four permanent buffs. [7:39]
- On deposit all the souls are released as orbs into the air; the bounty goes only to the runner unless allies secure orbs, in which case they share it. [7:39]
- The Urn is now louder and easier for nearby enemies to hear. [8:11]
- After 3 minutes from spawn the Urn slowly walks by itself toward the deposit location; the coach expects this to matter in lower ranks where the Urn gets ignored. [8:11]
- The Urn runner's passive bonuses now scale with how far behind the team is instead of being on or off; coach says this is healthier than teams gaming the threshold, for example someone dying to get 9% behind for the comeback Urn. [8:41]
- The boon reward table was reworked: early ability points come slightly earlier (13 AP at 10,000 souls instead of 11, 22 at 20,000 instead of 19), but full AP now needs 48,600 souls instead of 40,000. [9:12]
- Coach's read on the boon table: heroes take longer to reach maximum power, likely aimed at reducing the power of farming heroes. [10:16]
- Health per boon reduced 5% for everyone, so every hero is a little squishier; coach agrees heroes were too tanky. [10:16]
- All ultimate base and upgrade cooldowns nerfed by 15%, rounded to the nearest multiple of 5; this continues the trend of making ultimates feel more special. [10:47]
- The Rejuvenator now lasts 3 minutes instead of 4; coach calls it a great change since map control matters more with the Rift and a 4 minute Rejuvenator would be too strong. [10:47]
- Respawn timers are slightly longer toward the 17 to 20 minute mark, by a few seconds, so deaths there are more punishing. [11:18]
- Walkers give 500 fewer souls, guardians 250 fewer, and first blood and base kill bounty are slightly lower. [11:49]
- Troopers give less before 20 minutes and more after (lower base bounty, higher scaling), so in very late games troopers give a lot. [11:49]
- Coach's read on the economy changes: they target heroes like Seven, Wraith and Venator who sit on side lanes absorbing trooper souls, then push a walker for "flash net worth events". [12:19]
- The minimum unsecured souls that can drop went from 150 to 50 plus 5 per minute, so early in lane you are much more likely to drop unsecured souls if you die on a box run. [12:49]
- The deniable flying orb is now 50% of the trooper bounty instead of 40%; coach is unsure, noting players preferred 40 when it was changed before. [13:20]
- The dash jump input window went from 0.2 to 0.25 seconds, making dash jumps easier and better for newer players. [13:20]
- Dash jump distance increased by 1 meter and vertical impulse slightly; coach calls this the right way to buff it, since dash jumps were inferior and bait for costing two stamina. [13:20]
- The jump pads on top of the double centers building were removed, a massive change that makes invading the enemy double centers much harder because you cannot escape right after killing them. [13:51]
- Hovering an item in the shop now previews your investment bars (quality of life). [14:21]
- Item changes are short and coach thinks items are mostly balanced. [14:21]
- Mystic Shot cooldown nerfed from 8 to 9 seconds. [14:21]
- Toxic Bullets bleed damage buffed from 1.7 to 1.9 base. [14:53]
- Scourge goes from 3.5% max health damage with no spirit scaling to 2.6% plus 0.0055 spirit scaling; you need about 150 spirit to break even, so it is worse for almost every hero who bought it (for example a Silver buying it as her only spirit item). [14:53]
- Scourge is better than before on high spirit backliners such as Grey Talon at around 250 spirit, who can still put it on their front liners, so expect it bought more by spirit backliners and less by low spirit heroes. [15:24]
- Cursed Relic's damage penalty went from -10% to -14%, so do not buy it if you want to deal damage. [15:54]
- Abrams: Infernal Resilience untouched; Siphon Life tier three radius nerfed by 1 meter and Shoulder Charge tier three cooldown 2 seconds longer. Small nerfs, but Abrams' balance is tight so they matter, plus everyone being squishier should hurt him a bit. [16:25]
- Apollo: Riposte tier one buffed, with a longer grace window to target after the channel, cast range up to 35 m and a wider targeting angle, making Riposte far easier to land; Flawless Advance heal on hero hit spirit scaling up; ultimate max AP buffed to +50% damage from +40%. Coach says these make him better at being in people's face. [16:56]
- Billy: bullet damage per boon reduced 10%; Bashdown melee scaling cut from 1.1 to 0.9 so he cannot heal absurd amounts late; tier three heavy melee damage from 60% to 50%. [17:28]
- Billy's Rising Ram tier three was nerfed but its spirit scaling buffed, so above about 111 spirit power it is better than before: rushing it is worse, taking it after Boundless Spirit and heavy spirit investment is stronger. Coach likes that you can still build into it. [17:28]
- Celeste: Dazzling Trick cooldown reduced, Radiant Dagger buff duration and spirit scaling increased, ultimate bounce range and damage increased. Coach thinks she was not super strong and these plus the squishier meta will bring her back. [17:58]
- The Doorman: only change is doorway cooldown nerfed by 5 seconds. Coach is least satisfied with his balance, thinks he needed a slight damage nerf, and expects him to stay insanely strong (Valve likely holds back because he is less of a threat at low ranks). [18:28]
- Drifter: health per boon 41 to 43, faster cast and post cast time on his rend ability, tier two cooldown buffed, Bloodscent tier one and tier two buffed. Coach predicts Drifter becomes decent to pretty good, helped by more split action from more objectives. [18:58]
- Dynamo: only Rejuvenating Aurora base regeneration +5, which helps his laning. [19:28]
- Grey Talon: Rain of Arrows cooldown nerfed another 2 seconds (after a previous 2 second nerf) and Guided Owl permanent spirit per kill cut from +10 to +8. [19:28]
- Holliday: Powder Keg velocity slightly reduced, charge delay +0.5 seconds, spirit scaling 1.4 to 1.2 and tier three from +100 to +80. Coach thinks the charge delay should have gone up more and expects her to stay quite strong. [19:58]
- Haze: Sleep Dagger and Smoke Bomb tier three slightly buffed; coach predicts the overall meta benefits her and she will go up in the rankings. [20:30]
- Ivy: Entangling Thorn spirit scaling slightly up; her tether's replicated healing per boon buffed from +0.5 to +0.85, a big healing buff that helps the build maxing her 2; Air Drop cooldown reduction when used on allies from -25 to -30, so ult your teammates into the enemy team. [21:01]
- Lash: Ground Strike cooldown 21 to 18 seconds and Flog angle 30 to 38. Coach thinks it helps him a bit but the Ground Strike build still will not be good. [21:32]
- Mina: Rake missing health as damage 5% to 6%, Sanguine Retreat tier three adds +3 m range, Nox Nostra damage and its tier one damage up. Pretty significant buffs; coach expects her back to strong, helped by squishier heroes since she is a spirit burst hero. [21:32]
- Mo & Krill: Sand Blast tier two slow from -25% to -30%, tier three now gives -25 seconds cooldown; Combo max health per kill from +30 to +40 plus 2 per boon, a very significant health gain if you get many Combo kills. [22:02]
- Paige: health per boon increased while everyone else lost 5%, so she should not get one shot as much. [22:32]
- Paige: Bookworm tier one changed from duration and radius to cooldown reduction, tier two now +1 charge, +1 radius and trail duration, tier three extra travel range instead of a charge, so taking the first point lets you use Bookworm more often in lane. [23:03]
- Paige: shield fire rate component spirit scaling up; tier three shield now gives 100% barrier value instead of 75%; Rallying Charge now counts as a miss if it only hits non-heroes (so the miss cooldown reduction works); Rallying Charge tier three max amp increased for far range hits. [23:33]
- Paradox: Kinetic Carbine now increases move speed with spirit scaling while charging or charged; its tier three now affects both min and max damage scaling, which might finally make that long-weak upgrade good. Needs testing. [24:04]
- Pocket: bullet damage nerfed again and Flying Cloak tier three nerfed; Pocket has been S tier for many patches, and squishier heroes plus the Urn happening on different sides of the map (fewer huge fights) might finally be enough. [24:35]
- Rem: Tag Along healing spirit scaling nerfed a lot (0.66 to 0.4) and its missing health scaling nerfed; Little Helpers spirit resist nerfed but tier one and tier two buffed, so investing in Little Helpers is slightly better while base is worse. [25:06]
- Rem: Little Helpers tier three changed from +2 helpers and +20% trooper damage and resist to +1 helper and +15% spirit resist; you now need tier three for the old spirit resist (27 total instead of 23, versus 12 without it); each upgrade gives +1 helper, still 4 total. [25:37]
- Seven: Lightning Ball charge delay reduced; Storm Cloud time to expand 1.5 to 3.5 seconds (more time to escape it), damage interval 0.25 to 0.3, tier two adds +5 initial radius. Very slight changes that should not hurt his power much but make counterplay a bit easier. [26:07]
- Shiv: alt fire damage now has its own per boon value, enabling alt fire builds; alt fire ammo per shot up from 3 to 5; alt fire knockback movement is now disabled by Slowing Hex, so Slowing Hex really shuts him down. [26:38]
- Shiv: knives cooldown buffed; Slice and Dice now deals light melee damage instead of spirit damage, so it applies melee items such as Spirit Snatch and Melee Lifesteal, a huge deal; its -6% spirit resist became +4% damage amp, which coach thinks is worse overall because it no longer helps the team. [27:08]
- Shiv: Bloodletting tier two changed from +15% incoming damage deferred to +35% deferred damage cleared, with the deferral returned at tier three; overall an objective Bloodletting nerf. [27:39]
- Shiv: Killing Blow now damages troopers and neutrals along the way, executing a hero instantly fills the rage bar, and recast within 20 seconds on a hero kill is now part of the base ability, so you can execute several heroes without maxing it. [28:10]
- Shiv: full rage damage bonus reduced 4% and base range cut from 18 to 12 m, but one point gets the range back, two points the damage back, and tier three is better. Coach expects his ultimate to be really crazy and his single target damage better, applying melee items without risking a parried light melee. [28:42]

- Warden: another bullet damage per boon reduction (the second in a row), Willpower tier two and tier three spirit scaling buffed; coach reads this as Valve wanting him as a front line disruptor in a Billy-like range rather than a gun carry. [31:15]
