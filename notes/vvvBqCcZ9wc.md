## Covers
A lightly edited review of Deathy's own 40 minute Eternus 6 Yamato game (played 2026-02-06), lane against Silver and Mo & Krill, a mid game lost down nearly 20,000 souls, then a comeback through a mid contest, a waited-out comeback Urn and mid boss.
For players reviewing their own replays: lane trading, rotations, center sacrifices, game state checks, Urn and comeback Urn rules, playing from behind, target choice for an anti-carry and late game risk.
