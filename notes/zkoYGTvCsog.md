# How to Play Deadlock After The NEW UPDATE (Complete Beginner Guide)
channel: Sensu
date: 2026-10-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zkoYGTvCsog

## Covers
Beginner overview after the big map update: win condition, melee and parry, movement tech and stamina, map features (ziplines, steam vents, jump pads, teleporters, veils, districts, tunnels), haunted souls and boons, every timed objective in order, structures, comeback mechanics, hero choice and itemizing.
For new and returning players.

## Claims
- To win, destroy the enemy Guardians, Walkers, base guardians, shrines and finally the Patron. [0:00]
- Every hero has a left click ranged attack and four abilities; melee scales off gun damage. [0:30]
- Light melee is quick and low damage; holding melee does a heavy melee that does more damage and charges you forward. Heavy melee has a 1 second cooldown, slightly longer if you miss. [0:30]
- Every hero can parry light and heavy melee; a successful parry stuns the enemy, who takes extra damage from all sources during the stun, and resets the parry cooldown. [0:30]
- Counterspell and Rebuttal give extra benefits to parries. [1:00]
- The camera sits over the right shoulder, so peek from the right side for more visibility and accuracy, especially in lane. [1:00]
- Movement is the most important mechanic to learn. [1:00]
- Firing your weapon slows your movement speed by 25%. [1:30]
- After 3 seconds out of combat, sprint speed is added on top of movement speed; items and spells can affect either one separately. [1:30]
- Heroes have 2 to 4 stamina. A ground dash costs 1; crouching cancels it into a short dash. An air dash costs 1 and lets you shoot or melee mid-dash; only one air dash unless you have Stamina Mastery. [1:30]
- Down dash (double tap crouch mid-air) returns you to the ground quickly for 0.5 stamina. [2:00]
- Dash jump: ground dash then jump within the 0.3 second window (stamina bar flashes blue); costs 2 stamina, for a big momentum burst. [2:00]
- Double jump costs 1 stamina; Stamina Mastery allows a triple jump. [2:00]
- Crouch slide by holding crouch after a dash on a downward slope or after mantling; it keeps your momentum and should be muscle memory. [2:30]
- Shooting while sliding consumes no ammo. [2:30]
- Wall jumps cost 0 stamina and give a burst of momentum; wall jump into an air dash is a fast way to move for 1 stamina and should be muscle memory. [2:30]
- Each wall jump after the first costs 0.5 stamina; wall jumping again within 1.25 seconds causes fatigue and a weakened jump; wall jumping in combat reduces stamina regen for 5 seconds. [2:30]
- Edge boost: jump as late as possible at the end of a wall for extra horizontal distance and velocity. [3:01]
- Corner boost: precisely timed jump on an edge, corner or curved surface for much more height; beginners need not worry about it, it is not needed to play at a high level. [3:01]
- A heavy melee gives a slight distance boost to reach a ledge or burst around a corner. [3:31]
- Ziplines are the fastest way around the map; there is one each for yellow, blue and green lane, reaching as far as your troopers are pushed. [3:31]
- Bind the zipline latch to a key other than jump for a slightly faster latch. [4:02]
- Get off a zipline with Ctrl instead of jumping off, which preserves momentum for a couple of seconds. [4:02]
- Taking hero damage blocks zipline attachment for 3 seconds; taking enemy damage on a zipline knocks you off, costs HP and stuns, unless you have been on long enough to show the blue knock-off immunity effect. [4:02]
- The zipline speed boost unlocks at 6 minutes, increases zipline speed drastically and has a 6 minute cooldown. [4:02]
- Steam vents (new) hide heroes inside them, make them untargetable and give level-scaling health regen; you are revealed if you deal or take enemy damage or an enemy enters the vent. [4:33]
- Taking damage within 0.6 seconds of using a jump pad (air vent) stuns, adds damage and slows you after landing. [4:33]
- Two pairs of teleporters sit next to the secret shops; they have a channel time and give movement speed after teleporting. [4:33]
- Cosmic veils block vision from one side and can be used to hide or juke; all are one way except those under the shrines, which block both ways. [5:03]
- The map has two asymmetrical sides (Hidden King and Archmother), three lanes (York, Broadway, Greenwich) and four districts (theater, Chinatown, haunted lot, plaza). [5:03]
- The plaza's purple fog holds neutral monsters, boxes, Sinner's Sacrifices and golden statues; you cannot see or hear out of it and your stamina drains slowly and does not regenerate inside. [5:33]
- The Chinatown bell tower (climbed by rope) has Sinner's Sacrifices, boxes and golden statues on top; touching any of them rings the bell and alerts the whole map, and the only way down is through the center, so bring backup. [5:33]
- The underground tunnel network is only usable by Calico, Mo & Krill, Rat King and Rem and holds boxes and golden statues. [6:04]
- Souls are both currency and experience. Most souls are secured and cannot be lost if unspent. [6:04]
- Haunted souls come from haunts (jungle creeps), boxes and Sinner's Sacrifices, count toward your total and convert to secured over time. [6:04]
- If you die with haunted souls they drop in a soul container that any player can claim with a heavy melee. [6:35]
- The shop spends secured souls before haunted souls (example: 2,000 secured plus 1,500 haunted, buy a 3,200 item, left with 300 haunted). [6:35]
- Haunted souls count toward leveling; if you die and lose your bag you keep your last unlocked level but lose progress equal to the bag size. [6:35]
- Boons show your level; max boon level is 35, and each level gives base bullet damage, melee damage, spirit power and base health, different per hero. Some abilities and items scale off boons (green arrow and hero icon). [7:06]
- Levels 0, 2, 4 and 7 grant ability unlocks; every other level grants an ability point, up to level 32. [7:06]
- Ability tiers cost 1, 2 and 5 points, 8 points to max one ability; right click a tier out of combat to undo a misplaced point. [7:36]
- Trooper waves spawn every 30 seconds, more often as the game goes on; types are ranged, melee and medic. They ride in on a zipline vessel that can be shot down to drop them early. [7:36]
- A dying enemy trooper drops 50% of its souls as an orb you collect by being in range and 50% as a floating orb you must shoot or melee to confirm (or wait for it to time out); enemies can deny it first. [8:06]
- Last hitting a trooper with melee confirms 100% of its souls, so it cannot be denied. [8:36]
- Medic troopers drop a medic pack that heals nearby allies based on missing health and also heals allied troopers. [8:36]
- Mid Boss is up from the start, but the first one is usually killed between 15 and 25 minutes; at 50% HP it roars and alerts everyone. [8:36]
- Killing Mid Boss gives souls and permanent buffs and drops a Rejuvenator crystal; heavy melee it once landed to give the team a Rejuvenator credit (up to three). The first player to hit it heals to 100%. [9:07]
- Rejuvenator credits revive a teammate at 100% HP on death, once per player and up to three per team. [9:07]
- Mid Boss respawns 7 minutes after its first death, 6 after the second, and 5 after the third and later deaths. [9:07]
- At 2 minutes tier one haunts spawn (single triangle on the minimap); small haunted souls, easy, respawn in 1 minute 25 seconds. [9:07]
- Healing snacks spawn at 2:30 and restore 10% max health over 4 seconds; there are 36 locations, mostly high between buildings. [9:38]
- Boxes spawn at 3 minutes, drop haunted souls at a 60% chance, and respawn 3 minutes after collection. [9:38]
- Tough boxes (new) need a heavy melee, always drop double a normal box's souls, so one is worth about 3.33 regular boxes. [9:38]
- Golden statues (buff containers) have a 50% chance to drop a random permanent stat buff; the buff level depends on when you get it; respawn 3 minutes after collection. [10:09]
- Boxes and golden statues take 5 minutes to spawn and respawn in the tunnels, 8 minutes on the bell tower and 10 minutes in the room above Mid Boss. [10:09]
- At 5 minutes tier two haunts spawn (one line under the triangle); decent souls, 4 minutes 50 seconds respawn; group them, use heavy melees and hit the critical points (bright yellow damage numbers). [10:09]
- At 5 minutes the two bridge buffs spawn, then every 5 minutes; claim with heavy melee, lasts 160 seconds, and they grow stronger over the game. [10:39]
- Bridge buff types: casting (spirit power and cooldown reduction), gun (fire rate and ammo), movement (stamina, stamina regen, sprint speed, zipline speed), survival (bonus health and health regen). [10:39]
- At 8 minutes tier three haunts spawn (two thick lines); large souls, 5 minutes 35 seconds respawn, high HP and resists, so have enough HP and damage first. [11:10]
- At 8 minutes Sinner's Sacrifices spawn; break them with melee for haunted souls and permanent buffs; 5 minute respawn, delayed if a nearby haunt camp is not also cleared. [11:10]
- A Sinner's Sacrifice deals 80 damage per melee hit to you and takes 8 light or 4 heavy melees (same time), so heavy melee them in 99% of cases. [11:40]
- At three or fewer bulbs the Sinner's Sacrifice enters jackpot mode (white or orange light); hitting it while all lights are on gives random permanent buffs, 1 for a light melee and 4 for a heavy melee. [11:40]
- White jackpot: time your heavy melee at three lights remaining; orange jackpot: charge your heavy melee when the light is a bit above halfway. [12:10]
- At 10 minutes the Urn spawns on yellow lane and alternates sides every 5 minutes; pick it up with melee and deliver it across the map for souls and four permanent stat buffs. [12:10]
- Confirm the Urn delivery souls, enemies can claim them; a meleed carrier drops the Urn. [12:40]
- The Urn carrier gets movement speed, stamina, stamina regen, dash distance and slow immunity, plus sprint speed and bullet, spirit and debuff resist if their team is behind. [12:40]
- If the Urn is not delivered within 45 seconds of pickup it decays in value over another 45 seconds until it expires. [12:40]
- The Rift spawns between 10 and 12 minutes on the green or yellow side and respawns every 6 to 8 minutes; a visual indicator appears 60 seconds before it is announced. [13:10]
- The Rift opens 20 seconds after being announced and takes 12 seconds to capture, king of the hill style; if still contested after 60 seconds it draws and souls drop as orbs for either team. [13:10]
- The Rift winner gets instant souls and large black rift troopers that are tankier, hit harder and push the lane. [13:10]
- The team behind in souls gets a shorter Rift capture time, bullet, spirit and debuff resist, and extra souls and rift troopers if it wins; the team ahead has a longer capture time. [13:40]
- Each team has three Guardians; you must be within 24 m to hit one, and they take headshot damage. [13:40]
- Guardians shoot the closest target, but switch to an enemy who damages an allied player within 15 m. [13:40]
- Parrying a Guardian disables it for 2 seconds. [14:10]
- Guardians have high damage resist early that drops over the first 12 minutes; killing one gives souls split across the team and disables the enemy's nearby lane shop. [14:10]
- Walkers become damageable once that lane's Guardian dies; each Walker death gives the remaining Walkers more HP; standing near your own Walker gives bullet and spirit resist. [14:10]
- Walker attacks: a constant fire beam on the nearest target, an AoE stun stomp and fireballs that reduce resistances; the large white circle is damage range, the small one stomp range. [14:10]
- After 18 minutes Walkers have their lowest resistance and are easy to destroy; killing one gives a lot of souls and an extra item slot for the whole team. [14:41]
- There are six base guardians, two per lane; they deal constant damage and melee nearby players, take more damage the more players are near, and killing both in a lane permanently speeds that lane's zipline. [14:41]
- Each team has two shrines, undamageable until at least one pair of base guardians dies; their bullet resist drops when players are nearby. [15:12]
- Shrines start at 5,000 HP; when one dies the other's HP doubles; they can only be attacked from their platform. [15:12]
- The Patron defends shrines every 5 to 10 seconds with an AoE beam that knocks back, curses and damages players on the platform. [15:12]
- Walkers, base guardians and shrines get backdoor protection (damage resist and health regen) when no enemy troopers are nearby. [15:12]
- Destroying a shrine gives souls and super troopers in that shrine's lane and blue lane; super troopers hit harder, have more health and give fewer souls. [15:42]
- Once both shrines die the Patron is vulnerable. Phase one has a resist-shredding laser that leaves burning ground; destroying it cuts defenders' respawn timers by up to 20 seconds, but not below 10 seconds remaining. [15:42]
- The Patron takes about 10 seconds to transform to phase two, which pulses AoE damage and does a pit explosion every 15 seconds that damages and curses. [16:12]
- To hit the phase two Patron you must stand in the pit; it has 50% damage resist when one defender has been in the pit over 2 seconds and 100% with two or more, so focus defenders standing in the pit. [16:12]
- The Patron has backdoor protection and is the only structure that regenerates health out of combat. [16:12]
- Comeback mechanics: a team behind in net worth gets extra souls for almost everything, scaled by how far behind; killing a hero above the killer team's average net worth gives many more souls. [16:43]
- After 10 minutes, killing a player whose net worth is well above their team's average increases their respawn timer. [16:43]
- Never give up when behind because of the comeback mechanics. [16:43]
- The roster is soon to be 44 heroes. [16:43]
- For beginners, the recommended path is sticking to one or two heroes and learning fundamentals before trying others; test heroes in the pre-game lobby. [17:13]
- Heroes have different base stamina and are in stamina buckets: bucket one has the fastest dashes, bucket three the slowest; if you like fast movement avoid bucket three heroes, except Shiv. [17:13]
- Some passives shape builds: Haze gets more ammo from spirit power and Wraith gets more sprint speed from spirit power. [17:43]
- Roles are not fixed; your items largely decide your responsibility in the game. [17:43]
- You have 12 item slots; the simple start is picking a top build from the public browser and buying items left to right. [18:15]
- Item types are gun, spirit and vitality. Weapon damage raises bullet damage, melee damage and weapon or melee scaling abilities; weapon damage percent raises bullet and melee damage multiplicatively. [18:15]
- Spirit scaling is the number under the purple star; it multiplies your total spirit and adds that on top of the base value; hold Alt to see the base value. [18:45]
- Spending in an item type gives investment breakpoint bonuses; 4,800 souls of spirit items gives +38 spirit power. [18:45]
- The 4.8k breakpoint gives much bigger stat bonuses than the previous ones for every item type (the 4.8k spike), and many builds are built around reaching it. [19:15]
- For builds, use Deftly's weekly updated tier list: copy a hero's top build ID into your shop browser, but ignore the tier ranking itself since it mostly applies to high elo and changes constantly. [19:45]
- The best way to learn is to stop watching videos and play. [19:45]
