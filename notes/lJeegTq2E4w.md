## Covers
Viscous, VOD review coaching by Midknight of an Oracle-rank Viscous (a McGinnis main) laning with Vindicta against Abrams and Infernus. Mid-2025 video, patch around June 2025.
Includes two demos: a faster mid lane roll out and the Skinner combo (punch, right click, Splatter). Other themes: spamming Splatter in lane, defensive early items, parrying Abrams, Urn runs and when not to run, Goo Ball control practice, playing Goo Ball in the mid boss pit, and the fake back.

## Claims
Player 1 (Viscous, Oracle):
- Focus the confirm on that orb because Infernus has an angle on it. [0:32]
- The main Viscous build now looks like full AP Splatter; ball builds are the best. [0:32]
- Never use right click to last hit a minion. [1:02]
- Into this lane go Puddle Punch second, after Splatter, and max the poke. [1:02]
- Spam Splatter off cooldown in lane; it does a strong amount of damage. Every time it comes off cooldown, throw it. [1:02]
- Seeing Abrams walk up was predictable; a double jump and vault up onto the ledge keeps you safe from his charge. [1:32]
- After getting chunked, itemize defensively, for example Extra Regen; Healing Rite with The Cube is also a good combo. Extra Regen is better than another buy here because with it you will not die in lane; with 400 HP and no Cube you are very vulnerable. [2:03]
- Faster mid lane roll out: on the zipline, wait until right before the sign, press crouch, do a diagonal air dash, double jump, slide over, grab the rope. It saves about two more seconds than the under-the-bridge route (a commenter claimed 7). Practice it about 10 minutes; always do it, even on first spawn for practice. [3:05]
- Skinner combo: punch, right click, Splatter, pressed very fast in that order. It guarantees the Splatter, and if the punch hits everything hits. [4:36]
- In the Skinner combo the right click lands first, because the punch is an animation, so Spirit Shredder Bullets apply their life steal and -7% spirit resist to the whole combo. [5:06]
- Right click before Splatter because the right click has gun downtime; doing it first hides that downtime, and you can shoot by the time they lose the vertical momentum from the punch. [6:08]
- A Skinner combo under the bridge would have killed that Abrams; always look for it in lane. [5:37]
- Skinner combos feel amazing with Vindicta because Viscous' abilities are the same size as her tether, so the full combo is guaranteed. [6:38]
- Use The Cube when you are about to die. [6:38]
- When combining Splatter and punch, make sure Splatter connects before the punch, or you punch them out of the Splatter. [7:10]
- Boxing out is fine when Healing Rite is ticking, but tell your Vindicta not to push. [7:40]
- You can parry Abrams' heavy melee off his charge if his charge does not have the T2 upgrade (more stun on wall hit); without it he cannot heavy melee off it, so you get a free parry. [8:42]
- Wait at the corner for Abrams to walk out and Skinner combo him there; if you don't let him in range of your tower, he can't parry, and the combo plus tower laser takes him from 100 to zero. [9:42]
- CC that stops opponents from parrying a tower dive is incredibly powerful; they often throw parry on instinct and get punished, and Viscous is one of the best heroes at this. [10:42]
- Don't melee there; parry. Don't ult into her as she parries. [11:13]
- Save for Rescue Beam. [10:42]
- Rescue Beam on yourself does heal but gives no extra, the double heal is for allies. [11:44]
- The build (Rescue Beam, Extra Stamina, Arcane Surge) is a perfect Viscous support build; only critique is a bit more defensive itemizing early. Arcane Surge and Rescue Beam are overpowered. [12:15]
- If they perma shove and fight, look elsewhere on the map: hard shove wave, then box or camp. [12:45]
- You favor wall punches too much; a floor punch as he walks out would have been perfect extra poke. [14:17]
- The emblem item gives good cooldown reduction but is kind of pointless in this lane since Infernus builds gun and Abrams mostly melees; be careful rushing it into Infernus unless his partner is spirit. [14:47]
- Use Puddle Punch to save teammates by punching them out. The wall nearby is great for punches, it flings them into you if they step up. [15:48]
- Your ult control is bad. Prescription: go into the explore map tool, pick Viscous, turn on infinite cooldowns, and spend an hour in ult. [16:18]
- Don't pick up the first Urn to run it. In Oracle lobbies people do not expect or take the first Urn even on their side, so you don't need to deny it, though denying is good practice for Ascendant lobbies. [17:20]
- Running the Urn into their base when you cannot make it before Krill's ult is up is a bad run; you were playing like an Oracle player. With two teammates farming on the other side of the map, don't run it. [19:54]
- When Krill with ult is near the Urn drop off, walk away. [20:55]
- That Urn run should have been punished; Bebop or Paradox could easily catch it. [21:25]
- Putting a point into Puddle Punch early for two charges is influential; Melee Charge and an early Puddle Punch point give melee sustain, movement and damage. People sleep on how much melee helps you get around the map. [22:57]
- If you run Urns a lot, learn the old trick of ulting and grabbing the Urn at the same time; you run it much faster. [24:27]
- Pulling the Urn for a fight while up 30k was not bad; ninja cashing it was. The other two runs you were not up enough to cash it in their base. [25:58]
- Always double jump when cashing the Urn into a Bebop because of his hook. [26:29]
- In the mid boss pit, melee or parry; there is no reason not to (except heroes like Dynamo waiting to ult). [27:01]
- Good practice after killing mid boss: jump out of the pit and Warp Stone in at the last second to melee it, so you are not hit by an AoE ult in the pit. Any hero with Warp Stone should be leaving the pit. [27:01]
- You looked greedy; back off. [28:34]
- Fake back: everyone comes back and waits hugging a wall for an enemy to walk out and push the wave, then kills whoever pushes forward. Re-engaging in your base after faking back just gives ground. [28:34]
- Viscous in the pit is very strong: bounce around the pit walls in Goo Ball like a blender of goo instead of ping-ponging outside the pit. [29:37]
- Summary: in this lane, take Puddle Punch before The Cube; itemization was good but you could have rushed some regen early. [30:38]
- Summary: as Viscous with Vindicta into Abrams and Infernus, passive is ideal since they have much stronger laning; spam Splatter from the Guardian, you could have Splattered more. [31:10]
- Summary: mid game, learn Skinner combos, learn the roll out, spend an hour in the custom tool with your ult. [31:40]
- Summary: with a ball build, be in the pit; any closed corridor or circular area is where Goo Ball does the most. Also use Splatter while in Goo Ball, not only punches. [32:41]
