## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream, recorded 5/5/26 just after a patch that lowered squishy heroes' HP pools and reworked Trophy Collector. It opens with him reading YouTube comments, then he coaches four players: a Calico (Ascendant, reviewed before), an Ivy (Eternus, on Hidocker's build, game played before the patch), a Paradox (Ascendant) and a Lash.
Themes: respecting trooper damage, box routes and dead time, urn control and comeback urn, picks with a strong hero, mid boss timing, flex slots and tier 2 items, Fortitude when ahead, Reactive Barrier nerf, Ground Strike use, movement (sliding while shooting, diagonal dashes).
