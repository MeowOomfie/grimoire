## Claims
- He left a lane against a Vindicta, because as Graves he would have just fed that lane. [0:31]
- On Graves you have to be really aggressive to get any damage out; playing passive gets you nothing. [1:31]
- Graves's early game is one of the worst in the game, close to Billy's, but her late game is insane. [2:01]
- Once Graves has her ultimate, enemies cannot move forward in the lane, which is what you want. [2:31]
- He buys an item captioned as "Reso Shot" first every game on Graves, because her attack auto aims so you cannot miss its proc. [3:01]
- Mystic Shot is the same: you cannot miss it, since her attack has no bullets to miss. [3:31]
- The weakness is range: Graves has basically zero range. [4:01]
- He follows a build named Mayonnaise and puts two points in the ultimate early. [4:01]
- Graves has decent wave clear. [4:32]
- An enemy with Disarming Hex (as captioned "disarming axe") stopped him from shooting, so he backed off. [5:04]
- Early buys he goes through: Extra Regen, Improved Spirit, then Surge of Power. [5:04]
- He wants the church camp for its skulls, not just its souls. [5:34]
- Do not place skulls where an enemy will just shoot them; when playing aggressive, place your skulls late. [5:34]
- Place the ultimate where enemies cannot reach it, for example upstairs. [6:05]
- Place the ultimate every time it is up; it is really good for pushing. [8:43]
- Graves has no movement abilities, so she cannot escape a dive. [6:36]
- He guesses weapon shielding (as captioned) is good when he cannot tell what is damaging him. [9:51]
- Just shooting enemies takes their weapon damage and spirit resist, which helps a teammate's Urn run and is not a bad idea. [11:21]
- He thinks you do not need Reactive Barrier for Ivy's stomp and Billy's Bash Down; he takes the two barrier items instead, since the enemy has no Bebop and he dies very fast. [12:25]
- Put the ultimate down, push the wave, and let the zombies go up so the enemy has to react. [13:25]
- A strength of Graves: walk around, put the ultimate down and run away. [13:56]
- Do not walk into the enemy side of the map for farm when there is none on yours. [15:32]
- He normally places the wall vertically, not horizontally. [18:06]
- He takes spirit resilience from enemies by hitting them. [19:08]
- He goes for Echo Shard to get a double wall, and wants Heroic Aura. [19:40]
- Enemies who punched his gravestone took about a thousand damage each. [21:42]
- Graves does not care about getting kills. [22:45]
- Her ghouls cleared the enemy waves and jungle. [23:45]
- He considers Toxic Bullets; since he is procing effects on enemies all the time, it is not a bad buy. [24:50]
- Graves does not need to be with the team to do damage: he ended with 31k damage and 16 assists while often away from fights. [26:27]
