# The New URN Changes Just RUINED Deadlock…
channel: Sensu
date: 2026-05-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fM2V11-6d9E

## Covers
Opinion piece on the melee-claim Soul Urn from the patch released 2026-05-23 (deposit on the mid bridge, heavy melee to flip): why it hurts split pushing, comebacks and team comp variety, and suggested fixes.
For players trying to understand how the mid urn changed macro and what the community wanted instead.

## Claims
- Because the urn is always deposited in the middle, there is a big team fight about every 5 minutes from the 10-minute mark. [0:00]
- If your team is outnumbered you will most likely lose the deposit, so every player, carries included, must drop what they are doing and run to the blue lane. [0:00]
- Split pushing is essentially gutted: it takes under 20 seconds to rotate from the urn to a side lane to defend the Walker or kill the split pusher, so in most cases you would rather join the urn fight than trade an objective. [0:30]
- Urn fights are decided by team comp because the area is open and claiming needs a heavy melee; without great AoE or ultimates (Abrams, Kelvin, Dynamo, Lash) your team loses the urn almost every time. [0:30]
- Running a comeback urn to the mid bridge while far behind is nearly a death sentence despite the 50% damage resist, because the enemy has more souls and lane priority. [1:00]
- The winning team usually has lane priority, making it harder for the losing team to group mid, and after winning the urn fight they can rotate straight to the mid boss under the urn while the losers are dead. [1:30]
- The urn forces constant deathballing for numbers advantage, and fights devolve into heavy melee and parry spam, with people building Rebuttal to lower parry cooldown for urn fights. [1:30]
- Suggestion: put the losing team's drop-off closer to their base and near the side lane, so the winning team has to split up, with more lenient timers. [2:01]
- Suggestion: if the deposit stays in mid, put it under the mid bridge and add pillars or cover so you can break line of sight from heroes like Seven or Bebop. [2:31]
- Suggestion: replace heavy melee with King of the Hill rules where enemies in the area pause the timer and one team must wipe or push out the other. [2:31]
- Deathy's idea: spawn a mini patron that teams fight or defend to secure the souls. [3:01]
- Suggestion: pay souls out incrementally by control time (hold three of five seconds, keep 60%) so a last-second flip from an ult or heavy melee is less punishing and the snowball is smaller. [3:01]
- Deadlock is in playtest and Valve experimenting with the urn is good; the coach expects most issues fixed within a week and asks for Graves and Yamato nerfs. [3:32]
