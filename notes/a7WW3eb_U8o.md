## Covers
Solo queue advice from Tonydadof4: why to play hyper carries (Seven, Wraith), how to farm, where to stand in fights, and the mindset for losing.
A short guide, no one coached. Patch date 2026-08-01.

## Claims
- In solo queue you cannot rely on your team, so you have to be the one to change the game. [1:02]
- He dropped every hero except hyper carries for solo queue and calls it mandatory. [1:02]
- He plays Wraith for raw DPS, because she can solo walkers and has good escape utility. [1:02]
- He plays Seven for team fights, using the ultimate to clean up or push enemies out of an area. [1:32]
- On Seven he uses the balls (Lightning Ball) and his 3 to clear waves and jungle. [1:32]
- Seven and Wraith both have a hard early game and scale much better late. [1:32]
- Off carries are strong early and weaker late; if they make you feed early they can ride the soul lead to the end before you hit your power spike. [1:32]
- Early game play passive and take picks only when you have the advantage. [1:32]
- Mid game farm waves, jungle and boxes. [2:03]
- Always take the wave, preferably solo soak it, since it is the main source of souls. [2:03]
- After clearing a wave you have about 30 seconds to do jungle or boxes before going back to the next wave. [2:03]
- Farm priority: wave first, then the center sacrifice, then jungle, then boxes. [2:03]
- Low rank teams often take pointless fights in random pockets of the map instead of fighting at the Rift or for an objective. [2:03]
- When teammates take a pointless fight, choose: help if you are close to your power spike, keep farming to reach it, or push the enemy walker alone. [2:34]
- His biggest piece of advice: know your positioning and your weaknesses. [2:34]
- As a squishy carry, stay back with the supports in a team fight, not in the middle where everyone can shoot you. [3:04]
- Later in the game, with more sustain, push up and play nearer the front, but never directly on the front line, since you can still be CC'd and combo'd. [3:04]
- Let front liners like Viscous, Mo and Krill, Abrams and Billy soak the enemy's damage and utility while you deal damage from the back. [3:04]
- As the main DPS, dying early in a team fight gives the enemy a huge advantage. [3:35]
- If you are bad early or keep getting picked off while farming, you will most likely lose; play patiently and make good decisions. [3:35]
- If several teammates are far negative (like 0 and 14), the game is unwinnable; accept it and move on. [4:06]
- Solo queue helps you see your own mistakes and is a good place to learn a new hero without dragging down friends' ranks. [4:37]
- Losses show what you need to work on; small gains in movement and aim add up over time. [5:07]
