# You Need to Limit Test | Coaching a Phantom Drifter
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-07-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfNZIk19ngA

## Covers
Midknight coaches a Phantom-ranked Drifter player who is still learning the hero (patch of late July 2026).
Themes: limit testing a new hero instead of playing scared, fighting on your spikes, itemization (Close Quarters vs Point Blank, Fortitude, Cultist timing, Unstoppable vs Indomitable against Dynamo, Metal Skin vs Haze), dead time, instant cast, and invading as an assassin.
He also mentions a published spirit Drifter build ("Difta", by an E6 player) as the best published one, though only about 80% efficient.

## Claims
Build and general Drifter notes:
- The published "Difta" spirit (Rend) Drifter build is the best published one he found, but he rates it about 80% efficient with some non-ideal items and an AP order he dislikes. [0:00]
- He prefers Obeycan's spirit Drifter build, which is not published. [0:30]
- He does not like Monster Rounds as the start item; Melee Lifesteal feels better for infinite healing, while Monster Rounds gives priority but makes it hard to trade. [1:01]
- Drifter has 3.5 base regen, but Melee Lifesteal still enables much better trades. [1:31]
- Rend is honestly easy to hit; it just takes a second to learn the trajectory. [2:31]
- He does not like the item captioned "A dash" on Drifter; his Drifter builds skip it. [5:33]
- Hybrid Drifter is slept on; people know it is good but do not play it. [11:17]
- Rend's spirit scaling is 1.8, so missing the spirit spike costs a lot of damage. [23:43]

Player (Drifter, Phantom):
- Way too scared to take a free kill: three lights, a light into a heavy, or a heavy into a light would have killed, or just chase. [1:01]
- Fix for playing scared on a new hero: limit test. You will not learn the hero's limits playing scared; play aggressive and accept dying a lot. [1:31]
- With full priority you wasted it by not harassing someone on their tower or boxing out; you can also drift your Mark to the buffs to steal them. [3:01]
- Buffs block line of sight for line-of-sight abilities: if a Haze ults you near a buff, stand behind it. [3:31]
- If you are saving for Stalker you should already be shopping; staying in lane was too greedy. [4:01]
- Buying Healing Rite on top of Extra Regen was a wasted buy: not enough spirit power for value, and it killed your kill potential in lane. Buy Stalker, or have had Melee Lifesteal at the start. [4:31]
- Healing Rite is not the best item on a gun hero, but if you must buy it, use it to stick center farms. [5:02]
- Died from burning too much stamina chasing, then a parry finished you; in a hard lane you still have to play more aggressive and bursty. [6:12]
- Stalker is flat damage with no scaling, so it gets worse as the game goes on; it is coming out far too late if you buy it at 10 minutes. [8:13]
- Point Blank is a slot-locked item; if you are not slot locked, Close Quarters gives about 40% of Point Blank's value for 25% of the price, with Point Blank as the later upgrade. [8:43]
- If you are a hyper farmer, always gank when you hit a spike; do not wait while farming for the next buy. [9:13]
- Go for a green spike; you should be itemizing defensively. [9:43]
- Common mistake: the Rift is not spawning for more than 20 seconds, so the whole team does not need to sit in that lane. Go to the secret shop for a Healbane, which is huge tempo for the Rift fight. [9:43]
- Instead you fought over aggressively and lost half your HP for nothing. [10:14]
- The 22% dash slow on the item captioned "slowing bolts" is overpowered: once you catch someone they cannot get away, even on a hybrid build. [11:17]
- Fortitude puts in more value the more ahead you are: you slip away at lower HP and it gives more map presence. He would like Fortitude next and prefers it over Stalker. [12:52]
- Drifter struggles to play for denies because of gun range. [12:52]
- When you are weak and the enemy is sitting on a spike, back off the fight; do not force anything. [13:22]
- Silence Wave is great against Dynamo: it is obvious when a Dynamo wants to ult, so silence him, especially before his Rejuvenator. [13:53]
- Dead time: too much downtime over-searching for a kill while the enemy sits on a spike and your wave crashes; go to the secret shop instead. [14:23]
- When there are two assassins in the game (Calico, Drifter, Haze), hard focus each other: every assassin one-shots the other, so it comes down to who sees who first. Going on the Haze was right. [14:54]
- Do not sell items like Extra Regen and Healing Rite without spending the souls. [15:54]
- Cultist scales your souls and falls off, so it must come out early; if you can afford it at 12 minutes, you want it online around 8. [15:54]
- Do not use confirm cast; use instant cast, especially with Cultist, or you will under-utilize it. Your Cultist was under-utilized (about a 5 minute gap). [15:54]
- With instant cast a small icon shows where an ability will land, so you do not miss. [21:07]
- He defines an assassin as the hero who goes into your jungle to one-shot you (Calico, Drifter, Haze, arguably Mina); roles are fluid. [17:25]
- Hunter's Aura is good but came online a little late. [17:55]
- A Haze ult could do around 800 to you; you put yourself in the line of fire for one Meganess. [17:55]
- He does not like Reactive Barrier here; they lack CC, so Counterspell is better. [18:25]
- Against a fed Haze, Metal Skin is great: her sleep's wake-up duration scales with spirit power, so she can one-shot you while you cannot act. [18:25]
- Metal Skin buys three seconds not to die, which is even better in high elo because teammates arrive in time; it only gets worse there if Haze re-applies Sleep Dagger at exactly the wake-up time. [20:35]
- The only way to learn Counterspell is to buy it and use it. [19:30]
- When chased, back off for a bit first and then ult, instead of burning the ult just to run away. [20:00]
- Against Dynamo, Indomitable is wrong; buy Unstoppable, though not this early. [21:38]
- Down dash off a ledge to kite Haze into a bad trade; peeking from the right side gives a right-angle advantage because of how the camera works, and trading while falling back from corners could win the fight. [21:38]
- Overextended for a kill for no reason. [22:39]
- Slowing Hex is not needed in that spot. [22:39]
- Indomitable is generally a good situational item (for example against Bebop hooks); if it saves you from dying at 30 to 40 minutes it has paid for itself. It is also anti-curse. [22:39]
- Unstoppable would have been better than Indomitable in that fight. [23:43]
- Items like Superior Duration, Spirit Snatch or Slowing Hex would have fit the spirit build better. [24:15]
- Indomitable kept breaking on Paradox's wall and knockdowns. [24:45]
- If the enemy Dynamo has Unstoppable, your team needs at least one or two Unstoppables or they fully control mid boss and mid. [25:16]

Summary:
- Too passive in lane while learning the hero; even in hard lanes, play aggressive and limit test. [25:58]
- Play around spikes more: you were not fighting on your spikes, not itemizing for spikes, and missed the spirit spike. [25:58]
- Point Blank came out too early, Fortitude too late, Indomitable should have been Unstoppable, and Metal Skin was bought when Haze was not a problem. [25:58]
- Dead time: running around looking for kills instead of stealing jungle. [26:28]
- Invade more: look to kill enemies on their centers, which is what assassins excel at. [26:28]
- Use instant cast. [26:28]
