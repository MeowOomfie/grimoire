# Deadlock Biggest Update Ever.
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-09-29
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JzHadjEd_Lw

## Covers
Deathy reads and reacts to the "City Never Sleeps" major update notes of late September 2026: six new heroes, a reworked New York map and districts, haunts replacing neutrals, new map mechanics (bell tower, sunken plaza, tough crates, steam vents, healing snacks), the Broker's corrupted items, HUD, shop, ping, sandbox and reporting changes. No balance changes in this patch. For players who want a quick tour of what is new and where the new soul sources are.

## Claims
- The update adds six new heroes and changes essentially the whole map; the neutrals behave differently and look different, so the whole jungle is reworked. [0:00]
- Chinatown has a bell tower: climbing to the top gives a highly concentrated soul hot spot, but the bell rings loudly across the map while you collect, alerting enemies that you are there. [0:30]
- The sunken plaza's smoke drains your stamina while you are inside, but it separates you from the outside world so you are not revealed (sound fades and it seems to block vision too); if enemies do know you are there, you can't run away with no stamina. [0:30]
- Mid lane is rebuilt as an uptown route (Times Square); Deathy calls it the quickest route to the action and says mid lane no longer looks horrible. [2:03]
- Neutrals are replaced by "haunts", creatures with new behaviors, including new haunt types like barrel mimics. [2:03]
- New and reworked districts named in the notes: Greenwich, Central Park (a big tree, possibly awkward geometry to move around), Brownstones, an industrial yellow side with the New York Stock Exchange, the seaport dock (partly kept from the old map), Old Fairfax factories, the Ford district back alleys, the theater district, Chinatown, a haunted lot (basketball court) and the plots district. [2:34]
- Golden statues look new in the theater district. [3:36]
- Tough crates require a heavy melee to break open (you can't slide through them) and give extra souls. [4:38]
- Buff containers: the revamped golden statue permanent buffs now also include spirit resist, bullet resist, ability range and move speed, with a full visual redesign. [5:08]
- Healing snacks are hidden around the map so keen-eyed players can heal in a pinch. [5:08]
- Steam vents give invisibility in a small area while you stand on them; you can wait on one to gank. Deathy expects Drifter, Calico and Haze players to love them, like bushes in League. [5:40]
- The Broker is a limited time mysterious merchant who sells corrupted items: upgraded versions of items with stronger stats plus a random negative stat, for example a corrupted item with ricochet and 40% fire rate but minus 17% ability cooldown reduction, or a Rapid Recharge with three charges, 50% faster time and 25 spirit but minus one stamina and minus 18% stamina regen. [5:40]
- Corrupted items get random negative attributes from a predefined set with a little variability, but they are the same for all players in a given game. [6:41]
- You exchange your high tier items with the Broker for corrupted versions; his location changes, his first shipment arrives around 30 minutes, and more corrupted items come on average every 15 minutes after that, so it is a late game mechanic. [7:11]
- The Broker is only around for a few days; Deathy is a little worried about its balance but doesn't mind since it is temporary and fun. [7:11]
- New heroes: Nurse Harrow, Rat King (a fan concept made real), Solomon (a chess themed strategist), Dead Man Danny (looks explosive, Deathy hopes for rocket jumping), Baba (a hexing witch) and Violet. [7:41]
- As with the previous three-hero vote, players vote on the release order; two heroes release each week on Tuesdays and Fridays at 2 p.m. PST. [8:42]
- Voting now happens in the new pregame lobby, and the winning team's votes count double; hero priority tokens, first win of the day and campaign posters return. [8:42]
- New health bars show player names, an ultimate ready indicator, a Rejuvenator indicator (when a Rejuvenator use is available), critical health status and stamina tracking. [9:13]
- Stamina tracking will be really strong in fights, letting you know how much stamina your enemy has, and will change fights a decent amount. [9:13]
- Haunt camps now show their soul value and tier (for example a tier three Gutter Ghoul vs a tier two camp). [9:43]
- Option for cumulative damage totals on damage numbers. [9:43]
- Stats screen now shows when you reach max investment in a category, and the shop shows the stat impact of purchases in real time. [10:13]
- New kill feed shows souls gained and which item or ability landed the killing blow; soul bags are now visible. [10:13]
- The shop shows popular items for your hero from live player data, tooltips show how often an item is bought and at what phase, and there are mix and match stat filters; Deathy says this helps people who don't know what is bought. [10:43]
- Shop number keys navigate the shop tabs by default; Mercurial Magnum appears to have new art. [11:14]
- Pings: cursor hints show what is pingable, ability ping information, item pings so you can tell a teammate to buy an item (for example a knockdown item), and more pingable targets. [11:44]
- New dialogue player; pre-match hero conversations doubled from over 700 to 1,500, and 10,000 non-ping voice lines. [12:14]
- Minimap location labels name each area, like Counter-Strike, so players will call places by the same names. [12:45]
- Parry cooldown now shows near your reticle. [12:45]
- In-world respawn timers show when haunt camps or Mid-Boss respawn when you are near; Deathy calls it huge for newer players who don't track timers. [12:45]
- A Mid-Boss timer on the top bar; before, you often couldn't see it. [12:45]
- A postgame graph tracks the permanent buffs each player collected, and picking up a permanent buff shows its type above your hero. [13:16]
- Custom lobbies support eight spectators, big for tournament and event production. [13:16]
- Accessibility: enemy UI color, voice subtitles, auto move (double tap forward), automantle onto ledges, and toggle fire. [13:47]
- Sandbox tools: bot recording and sharing recordings, a combat log against bots (damage events, buffs, debuffs), a speed graph, a DPS meter with total damage, build comparison, and weapon falloff guides showing where your damage starts to fall off. [14:17]
- Reporting: you are notified when action is taken on a player you reported; a player reported and found abusing comms is muted for the rest of the match and gets a 30-day comms ban, and Deathy believes you can't play ranked with a comms ban. [14:47]
- Settings: a keybind map, shareable reticle and keybind codes, spectator hotkeys and camera, and options for health numbers on health bars. [15:17]
- Not in this patch: no hero remodels, no new game mode, no balance changes. [16:17]
- Deathy's verdict: arguably the biggest update ever; the jungle and map changes should make the game feel really fresh, and he wants to see what the new neutral behaviors are like to fight. [16:48]
