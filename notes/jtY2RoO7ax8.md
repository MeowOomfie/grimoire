# Every Deadlock Character's REAL Role (5 Role System Explained)
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2026-02-09  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jtY2RoO7ax8

## Covers
Role system (carry, initiator/catch, tank, off carry, support, hybrid), Deadlock economy and farm allocation, what each role does with its time, when to fight, all heroes assigned to a role with per-hero reasoning

## Claims
### The role system and economy
- There are no selectable roles yet; he theorizes dedicated role queues are coming because roles heavily influence game outcomes, especially in high rank. Too many carries on one team in high rank hurts the team. [0:00] [0:30]
- Five categories: carries, initiators/catch characters, tanks, off carries, supports, plus a bonus hybrid category of heroes you can play multiple ways. [0:30] [1:00]
- Unlike Dota or League, in Deadlock you earn about as much as your lane partner and teams get roughly the same money; last hits do not matter as long as you are in lane with your partner. Kills give a bit more; leaving lane to farm jungle or boxes gives your partner less. [1:00] [1:30]
- Allocating money to the right people raises your win chance a lot: a support that farms too much hurts the team, and so does an off carry that farms too little. [1:30] [2:00]
- Too many carries lose because there is not enough jungle farm, and if everyone AFK farms the enemy pushes in, takes map control and kills your Walkers. Proactive play, looking for kills and objectives, is how you win. [19:54]
- Do not perma fight on carries or perma farm on supports. If you want money and items, pick a hero that needs it; do not pick Mo & Krill and play all-gun Mo & Krill, stealing farm from a Wraith or Seven. [22:30] [23:01]

### Carry
- Carries: Lady Geist, Mirage, Seven, Venator, Victor, Warden, Wraith. [2:00]
- Carries scale well into the late game through stats, items or both; they are usually bad in lane, early game or mid game without money, but with a lot of farm become the strongest heroes and win the fights; the enemy worries about them most. [2:00] [2:32] [3:02]
- Carry is not the most important role overall, it is the most important role later in the game. [3:02]
- Give carries the side lane: after laning it is the most efficient farm, you get 100% of the creeps. Not mid lane, because fights gravitate to the middle of the map (more objectives, more jungle, easier to reach, higher player density since the map is a circle). [3:02] [3:32]
- Avoid early team fights on most carries unless you had a really good lane; farm safely on the side lane. [3:32] [4:02]
- Avoid dying, especially with unsecured souls. A well played carry is the highest net worth, has the longest respawn (respawn grows with money), and when it dies the team loses more than one player's worth of power. [4:02] [4:32]
- Lady Geist: strong even early, but gets better with money and scales really hard; very fast jungle farmer; very safe in 1v1 and even 2v1 on the side lane because Soul Exchange (life swap) can turn a gank; basically unkillable late with a lot of money; especially strong in the current meta. [4:32] [5:02]
- Mirage: one of the weakest heroes in the game and very weak at clearing jungle, but a great side lane pick for money because Djinn's Mark scales into late game with fire rate and spirit power. Classic late combo is Ricochet, then Inhibitor and Silencer (usually also Toxic Bullets) so every enemy gets marked, silenced and inhibited. Hard to get there; sits safely on the side lane. [5:33] [6:05] [6:36]
- Mirage: his ultimate teleport lets him split push a side lane and still be the fastest hero to join a fight on the other side of the map. [6:36]
- Seven: very weak early, especially in lane; Power Surge, ball, lightning, ultimate and the stun all need max level to become strong; with the right items he is a monster in team fights; one of the best, if not the best, jungle farmers in the game. [6:36] [7:07]
- Venator: pretty weak in lane, but with a lot of farm, especially weapon items with bonus weapon damage, his ultimate one taps people. [7:07] [7:37]
- Victor: the first full spirit carry; most damage from his aura; fine in lane but does no damage in mid game; once he gets Infuser then Ethereal Shift and many items he becomes really scary and basically unkillable. [7:37]
- Warden: classic gun carry, the only real gun carry besides Venator; good and safe on a side lane, but needs a lot of items to get online. [8:07]
- Wraith: needs items even more than the others; still the strongest late game carry; very weak in early team fights and does not want to be there unless she has to; at 30K plus she becomes a monster who can kill the whole enemy team. [8:07]

### Initiator / catch
- Initiators/catch: Haze, Holliday, Paradox. Bebop is debatable but he put Bebop in tanks. [8:37]
- Haze is not a hard carry, a common new player misconception (farming jungle 20 to 30 minutes). She has a decent laning stage, and once she has some money she is an insanely good pick and initiator character. [8:37] [9:07]
- Haze: go invisible, scout, initiate with Sleep Dagger on a pick target, then follow with a silence, Slowing Hex or Curse and her ultimate for the first pick. She is the best at this, more so than Holliday and Paradox, and that is how she is played at very high level. [9:07] [9:38]
- Holliday and Paradox both have ultimates that initiate easily: Holliday's lasso pulls an enemy toward your team, Paradox swap swaps an enemy into your team. [9:38]
- Initiators do not need as much money as carries; farm when there is nothing else to do (jungle camps on the way, a bit of lane), but their strength is their abilities. Items like Superior Stamina, or Rapid Recharge on Holliday for more barrels, help, but do not give them most of the farm; they get money mostly by finding kills. [9:38] [10:09] [10:40]
- Do not AFK farm on Haze: at 30 to 40K she is outclassed by carries like Wraith or Victor of equal skill, who do more damage and are harder to kill; she is very squishy. [10:40] [11:12]

### Tank
- Tanks: Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Shiv, Silver. They benefit somewhat from farm but are inherently strong at tanking: very high base HP and abilities that keep them alive and absorbing damage. [11:12] [11:43]
- Abrams: Siphon Life lets him sustain in the front line, and the passive Infernal Resilience lets him tank a lot. [11:43] [12:14]
- Bebop: Uppercut can regen over and over; very high base HP and very high HP scaling. [12:14]
- Billy: goes in and tanks all the damage, uses survival items, and his ultimate makes him really tanky; a frontline brawler. Billy is an exception who farms jungle well early, around 8 minutes, because of Blasted. [12:14] [12:44]
- Shiv: does everything; Bloodletting lets him absorb a lot of damage without many items, and with Rage fully built his abilities do ridiculous damage regardless of money. [12:14] [12:44]
- Tanks are generally not great at farming jungle; they get most of their money by being in team fights. A tank missing from a team fight, for example while the enemy forces an Urn fight, loses the game. [12:44] [13:15]
- Tanks are not good at split pushing; they do not kill a Walker fast like carries and off carries. Be in the fight and fight a lot. [13:15]
- Silver: some think she is a damage dealer because of gun items, but the main goal is to get into wolf form as fast as possible, then go in and go ham. [13:15] [13:45]

### Off carry
- Off carries sit between carry and support: played somewhat like a carry but need less money and start doing things much earlier. [13:45]
- Off carries: Apollo, Calico, Celeste, Grey Talon, Infernus, Lash, Mina, Pocket, Vindicta, Yamato. Yamato could also be classified as a tank. [14:15]
- They tend to have a decent laning stage, then deal a lot of damage or disrupt fights without having the most farm; they are not the greatest farmers. Find a fine balance between clearing jungle for your own money and helping the team fight. [14:15] [14:46]
- Mina: the strongest or a top three laner; most Minas win lane, then wrongly sit on a side lane farming like a carry. She is insane in lane and mid game (wins any 1v1, nukes people, joins fights) but has no super strong late game; for about the first 20 minutes she has as much or more money than carries and kills them easily, then a carry turns it around. [14:46] [15:17]
- Mina: go around getting kills; between fights take a jungle camp to regen with Rake and clear a few waves. [15:47]
- Grey Talon and Vindicta (the flyers): an exception, they want to sit safely in the back on the mid lane, since both can join team fights from long range (Vindicta's snipe, Grey Talon's bird sent anywhere). [15:47] [16:18]
- Calico: you cannot really farm much and want to go for kills; a very hybridish hero. [16:18]
- When ahead on an off carry, fight: these heroes are all crazy in the mid game and can win any fight when ahead. Yamato is also crazy in the late game. [16:49]
- Contrast: a carry with high money in mid game keeps farming because it is strongest late; an off carry wins by dominating the enemy in the mid game. [16:49]

### Support
- Supports want to avoid farming jungle, especially their own; they can still steal enemy Sinners. They do well with almost no money and have high impact even as the poorest player because their abilities, especially ultimates, are impactful at any soul count. [16:49] [17:20]
- Supports: The Doorman, Dynamo, Kelvin, Mo & Krill, Paige, Rem, Sinclair. [17:20]
- The Doorman can imprison people; Dynamo has the best ultimate in the game, Black Hole, which is the same with no souls or all the souls. [17:20] [17:50]
- On support: fight, fight, fight and help your team non-stop; accept you will not be the richest or get the credit. It is an unthankful job; he is a carry player. [17:50] [18:22]
- Support needs a team that does things; if everyone AFK farms jungle, be the one in text or voice chat calling for fights, and run Urns. [18:22] [18:53]
- Supports pick up Urns because carrying one stops you from farming jungle or lanes efficiently, so it costs supports the least; more Urns delivered means more money for the team. [18:53]
- A support can still be MVP: with Warp Stone and maybe Superior Duration, one Black Hole can win a fight even in late game when the enemy is ahead in money. [19:24]

### Hybrid
- Hybrids: Graves, Drifter, Ivy, McGinnis, Viscous, Vyper. They can be played as hard carries or very supportive heroes depending on team composition; all do well with low and high net worth. [19:24] [19:54] [20:26]
- Graves: Tombstone lets her push and pressure waves even when poor, so she can play for the team with map control and fights; if the team has no carry or the carry is bad, AFK farm on the side lane and get incredibly powerful late. [20:26]
- Drifter: scales really well with a lot of money but cannot farm; you can roam non-stop for kills, and if it goes badly pivot to an ultimate build that takes away the enemy's vision in fights. He can be very strong late but does not need a lot of money. [20:57]
- Ivy: the most versatile hero in the game; currently playable as a full gun carry on the side lane if nobody else carries, or the opposite way (as Piggy plays her): run Urns, fly teammates around and get them in and out of fights. [21:27]
- Viscous: pretty bad right now; playable as a support, or as a hybrid nuke DPS with some gun and melee items using Puddle Punch and his ultimate, with a lot of money or none. [21:59]
- Vyper: her ultimate is very strong on base with few items, disables many enemies, and she does a lot of spirit nuke damage; with a lot of farm she can become a gun carry with many gun items. [21:59] [22:30]
