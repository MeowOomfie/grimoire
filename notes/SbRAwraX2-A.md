# Deadlock Is Losing Players But Not For The Reason You Think
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-03-26
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbRAwraX2-A

## Covers
A March 2026 opinion piece on matchmaking and ranks: why new players quit, his rank-up experiences on several accounts, and proposals (performance-based rating, placements, rank reset).
No gameplay advice; the claims are his observations about how ranks behave plus his proposals.

## Claims
- A viewer said they were quitting until the player base grows because matchmaking kept putting them against tryhards so they could not learn the game. [0:00]
- Yoshi is the most visible Deadlock developer and is active in the Discord. [0:30]
- Deadlock holds about 80,000 players every day. [2:01]
- His main account is Archon 6 after about 200 games; his alt accounts reached about Phantom 4 in about 30 games and Ascendant 1 in about 20 games. [2:32]
- In his experience Archon, Oracle and Phantom lobbies are hard to tell apart; below Archon is "the circus"; Ascendant is the first rank where he got decent teammates, and even then not consistently. [3:02]
- Eternus players report the same thing: the top two ranks still contain many bad players. [3:33]
- Matchmaking right now is mostly wins and rank boosts. [4:03]
- He knows players who had 11, 15 and 20 game loss streaks without deranking. [4:03]
- He won 14 in a row at Ritualist 5 and 11 in a row at Archon 4 without ranking up, even while MVPing and while winning Oracle lobbies with higher-ranked friends. [4:34]
- Queueing with higher-ranked friends put him in their lobbies (Oracle lobbies while he was Ritualist). [4:34] [5:04]
- His view is that the longer you sit in a rank, the more stuck in it you get; it took him 100 to 150 games to get good, and by then he could not climb. [4:34] [5:04]
- The game is still invite-only. [5:34]
- Proposal: if individual performance counts, carry heroes would farm every stat (lifesteal healing, objective damage, player damage) and be boosted, so supports need their own metrics such as Curse casts that saved a teammate, lane denies, lane player damage and deaths compared with the rest of the lobby. [6:04] [6:35] [7:06]
- Proposal: performance should be judged against the same lobby (outfarming everyone, holding or recovering a soul lead), not against global averages for the hero. [7:36]
- Placements currently feel like throwing darts; his proposal starts every new player at the middle rank and moves them up or down each game by result and performance over about 5 to 10 games. [8:08] [8:38] [9:10] [10:10]
- A rank reset is necessary, ideally at open beta, with a cosmetic or charm showing the rank you held before the reset. [10:10] [11:11]
- The top Haze streamer is far better than most of his lobbies in about 8 of 10 games, so even Eternus 6 does not filter players well. [10:40] [11:11]

## Disagrees with
