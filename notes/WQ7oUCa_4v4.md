## Covers
Yamato coaching review by Midknight of an Eternus Yamato player laning with a Drifter into a Mirage, video dated 2025-12-07 (the game was played on an early December 2025 patch, around the walker rework).
Themes: over-buying defense, box routing on the 4:30 wave and bridge buff, not giving up space to shop, Urn versus brute-force walker sieges, wave priority and reacting to the right side, respecting the enemy's intelligence at high rank, and counter items.

## Claims
Yamato (Eternus):
- Three max (Crimson Slash) Yamato into rough lanes gives a lot of sustain and healing, but in this lane the enemy laners can in theory perma space you. [0:30]
- Spirit Yamato is still meta even after the big spirit nerf; the hero is overtuned enough to do 50% less damage and still be fine. [1:00]
- Restorative Shot, an extra regen item and three max together is being over-specced defensively; triple defensive items is rough in a winnable lane. [2:02]
- Mystic Shot is good on Yamato; you can spam your right clicks for big damage. [3:34]
- When the enemy Mirage's key ability is down, look to be aggressive and walk up for a 1 on their bridge. [1:31]
- Be careful walking up aggressively when your Drifter is not close to you. [4:04]
- The enemy ult is predictable when she is in an enclosed space; pre-set up a mantle ult there, since the chance of her ulting into you is high and it works as zoning and damage. Mantle ults are rarely useful, but strong when they are. [4:35]
- Your build is diversifying too much and you have none of your spikes; you need one of your spikes, spirit or gun. [8:17]
- Instead of Healing Booster, buy Torment Pulse so you hit your spirit spike and life is grand. [9:52]
- Rush Torment Pulse early for a quick spirit spike instead of going heavy healing early. [32:25]
- Weave right clicks into every combo on Yamato; a lot of Yamato players don't. [13:58]
- You can attack while ulted and while carrying the Urn on Yamato. [16:01]
- In a high-rank lobby you cannot brute force melee someone; if the Mirage jumps you in front of his wave, assume he has backup and try to run. [25:46]
- Use your 2 onto the Abrams and cancel it to get out; a cancel 2 gives a much higher chance of escaping. [26:16]
- Routing out through the vent instead of running to a walker where you would die was a good escape. [26:16]
- Heavy melees inside your ult are greedy; when an enemy (Mo & Krill) stands on top of your target he can parry for her, so run out of the ult instead of staying in. [30:53]
- At this rank assume your enemy is confident and intelligent; at Phantom level he would not tell you this. [31:24]
- Counter items missed: the enemy team is almost all gun (Haze, Mirage, Mina), so Plated Armor would feel great this game. [30:20]

Laning and early game:
- Hold the wave instead of turning to help a Drifter who engages at a bad time; the wave is more important. [5:42]
- Do not go back to buy an 800 item, especially an offensive one, while the enemies are low and hitting your tower; keep applying pressure, kill the wave instantly and take damage off the tower. [6:12]
- Never box route on the 4:30 wave; kill it as fast as possible so you can go for bridge buff. [3:04]
- Taking the long route for green boxes before buff is a bad box route when you should have shoved the bridge. [2:33]
- You should never box route before buffs; at this level you need to be on the buff timer, and his drills playlist has a bridge rush timing drill. [32:55]
- When your laning partner is diving a full HP enemy instead of killing tower, just hit tower and let him die; you could have taken tower there. [8:17]
- Typing "NT" after a kill does mental damage that objectively raises your win chance a little, even if it is unhealthy for the game. [11:56]

Macro:
- Pushing into a walker with no siege potential is an overcommit; eat camps and the jungle or roam instead. [10:53]
- Applying pressure on the left guardian is fine; killing it lets you invade their jungle and double sentries. [11:26]
- When your team is fighting elsewhere and the enemy is weighted to one side, steal their sentries and farm instead of rotating into a bad fight; use teammates as bait. [14:28]
- Sieging walkers at 15 minutes on the new patch, before the walker rework, is rough. [14:28]
- Fix your waves first, then look for a pickoff with Holliday's lasso; control when you look for the pick, after you have wave prio. [16:32]
- Brute force sieging a walker at 15 minutes with no player advantage is far worse than using that pressure to run Urn, about 20 times better. [17:35]
- If a Holliday is running around low, let her die; she has a bounce pad to survive and you will die for her mistake instead of pushing the wave and backing off. [18:05]
- Pushing a side wave while the enemy is near Urn is a gamble on the enemy's intelligence; he dislikes that normally but accepts it when you are already into the Urn. [19:07]
- With pressure mid and your team fighting on one side, invade their double camps once the wave is pushed instead of only securing your own farm. [20:07]
- Factor in whether the enemy has Rejuvenator before rotating into a fight. [20:37]
- Respond to a crashing side wave instead of rotating into a five-man fight, or a splitting Mirage gets free rein on your walker. [21:08]
- After a fight you cannot push (three down), go back and secure your own jungle. [24:13]
- Rotating into their whole healthy team for a 2v3 is a rage fight; you should lose it. [28:19]
- If two waves are pushing into walkers, one with a big team fight and one with a single splitter, go to the side with just one person, the reaction side. [33:25]
- Prioritize waves and keep them away from walkers. [33:25]

Coaching advice:
- For a coaching VOD, submit a game where you did okay, not one where you did very well or very badly. [29:20]
