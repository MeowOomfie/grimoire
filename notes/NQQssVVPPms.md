## Covers
Midknight Vods reviews three ranked replays on a free coaching stream: a Ritualist Solomon, a Phantom Paradox and a Phantom Holliday. Old replays broke on this patch, so only recent games could be coached.
Before the reviews he gives his views on the new heroes (Harrow, called "nurse", Violet, called "artist", Solomon, Baba, Danny, Rat King) and the new rank system.
Recurring themes: full resets and zipline resets instead of teleports and the secret shop, pushing the neutral wave before rotating, not side laning on heroes that don't want it, using cooldowns and ults instead of holding them, creating plays as a pick hero, forcing mid versus baiting it, and fighting while sitting just short of a spike.
