## Covers
A Soul Urn guide (October 2025): why to run or hold the Urn, the comeback side rules and candle colors, spawn sides, running after mid boss, routes, cashing tech, who should carry it, and the advanced tapping and staggering tricks.
For any player who rarely runs the Urn.

## Claims
- The Urn is the only way, apart from the much more conditional mid boss, to force a fight. [0:00]
- The team that wants the fight should grab and run the Urn; the team that doesn't should grab it and hold it. [0:00]
- When both teams want the fight equally, the team running the Urn dictates the terms of the fight. [0:00]
- While holding the Urn, seek height, ideally the defensible roofs with ropes and zip access on each side. [0:30]
- If the enemy answers with four or five defending, hold it safely, back up if they push, and split push the other side of the map; if the fight is never favorable, drop it and reset, having wasted their time. [0:30]
- Holding the Urn frees you to send players to push other lanes; if they send two to shove a wave and you sent one, you have a 5v4 Urn fight and should take it. [1:00]
- Holding the Urn to pressure one side of the map is as underused as running it. [1:00]
- The first Urn at 10 minutes needs a 15% soul difference to sit on one team's side; later Urns (15, 20, 25, 30 minutes) need 10%. [1:31]
- Candles show the side: green candles mean the enemy has more souls and it is on your side, red means it isn't, no candles means neutral. [2:02]
- The losing team should run a comeback Urn on their side; it is by far the best and one of the only reliable ways to come back. [2:02]
- The winning team should pressure that side harder, grab the comeback Urn off spawn and hold it in their base or on their side to create pressure with no real intention of running it. [2:33]
- Only run an Urn on the enemy side with a significant kill advantage or a massive ult advantage. [2:33]
- Check whether the two enemy walkers next to the Urn are down; if not, the enemies can fall back onto their resists and the position is even easier for them to defend. [3:03]
- After the first one, the Urn spawns on green at every 5 minute interval and on yellow at every 10 minute interval, unless it has been staggered. [3:03]
- Run the Urn with a small advantage, like one kill or a big ult advantage (for example after they wasted a team fight Lash, Warden or Pocket ult), which isn't enough for mid boss but should be used. [3:35]
- Advanced: with heavy wave pressure crashing into their objectives, running the Urn across the map forces them to answer waves, which gives you a player advantage. [4:06]
- Stop forcing fights on the mid walker with a small lead (for example 16 minutes in); defenders draw back to the walker, have defensible height the walker covers, and get resists near it. Run the Urn instead. [4:06]
- Always run the Urn after mid boss: nine times out of ten they won't contest a neutral Urn, and if they do it is a great fight for you while up three Rejuvenators; then take their base or bank the souls. [5:07]
- After mid boss, while everyone buys and fixes waves, start pressuring the Urn; if it is on their side, run it with most of your team since they will probably contest, still better than diving their mid walker. [5:37]
- Fastest route across the map is through the vent and on to the next air vent, not through mid; mid forces you through a major choke point where you get ambushed by a melee. [5:37]
- Usually route safely on your side of the map, backing off if pushed; if they aren't aggressive mid you can route slightly more aggressively, or take it up to the rooftops if they push to fight. The goal is ending on one of the defensible roofs. [6:38]
- While cashing you can double jump to keep cashing, and even work in a wall bounce (let yourself fall a bit first, you are near the height ceiling); it dodges melees. [7:08]
- The counter to a double-jumping casher is to look up while meleeing, which still hits them. [7:08]
- If The Doorman has a door set up on the drop-off, walk up and double jump when he opens the door to cash and avoid the cart. [7:38]
- The Urn carrier should generally be your support: being out of the fight and silenced matters less for them, and if the enemy jumps the known carrier, better your support caught than your hard carry. [7:38]
- Whoever cashes the Urn gets about 25% individual bonus souls (the rest split six ways) plus one ability point; you need two Urns to fully max all abilities, so give it to a hero who wants key AP early. [7:38]
- When looking for a fight around the Urn, don't have a hero like Dynamo carry it since his ult is too valuable; shuffle it so Holliday can lasso to initiate. [8:09]
- The Urn still cashes while Dynamo's 2 lifts you, so a Dynamo with the carrier can 2 them and deposit it for free. [8:09]
- Tapping: pick up the Urn and drop it to lock in its current state; if you lead by 8% and are about to reach 10%, tap it to keep it neutral so it won't land on the enemy side. [8:39]
- Tapping's rare downside: if the enemy then takes a huge lead, the Urn stays neutral instead of flipping to your side. [9:10]
- Staggering: hold the Urn when the timer hits the swap time (25, 30 minutes) and drop it after, and it doesn't swap; combine with tapping to keep a locked neutral Urn from resetting to their side. [9:10]
- Or pick it up with about 20 seconds left and carry it across the map past the swap threshold; after you cash and gain enough lead, they must wait five more minutes for a comeback Urn. [9:41]
- Staggering can be chained; staggering twice in a game basically wins it. [10:12]
- When winning, stagger the enemy's Urns: run an Urn on their side at the last possible second (for example 24:30) so whoever gets it, they wait 5 more minutes for a comeback; if you take it while already up about 20%, you reach about 25% and the game is basically over. [10:43]
