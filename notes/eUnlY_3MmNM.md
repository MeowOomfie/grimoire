# Give Me 17 Minutes, You'll Outscale Everyone
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-10-04
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUnlY_3MmNM

## Covers
How to snowball a lead and keep it after the map update: playing around your hero's power spike, leaving a side lane to run boxes, farming the enemy side, walkers, staying with the team, and Mid Boss into first shrine.
For players who win early but lose the game late.

## Claims
- A common lane mistake is not playing around your hero's power spike; learn when your hero becomes strong and play passive until that point. [0:30]
- Vindicta can be a bit aggressive in lane with her 1 and 3, but does not commit to anything until she has her ultimate. [0:30]
- Put two points into Vindicta's ultimate immediately when you get it (extra charge, maybe Mystic Burst); it then does about 300 damage and takes someone from half health to dead almost instantly. [1:01]
- With Vindicta, spend the first half of lane getting the enemy to about half health and keeping them there, so the moment you get ult you blow up their health bar. [1:01]
- Mo & Krill is aggressive the whole lane and keeps hitting his 1, but does not commit hard until his ultimate, then ults with his duo and stacks his 1 and ult to melt the target. [1:31]
- Apollo is very strong for most of the first 15 minutes; play off that the whole time and get max points into his 3. [1:31]
- Boxes were increased and changed in the update; start running boxes at 3 minutes when they spawn. [2:32]
- In a side lane, if you trust your lane partner, tell them you will run boxes and be back around minute 5 and let them solo lane. [3:03]
- Do not leave a mid lane partner alone unless they are very good at solo laning; mid is an aggressive lane where the enemy can take bridge control and stomp right away. [2:32]
- Box route: run all the way back (walking, using the zipline for a small boost) to your base guardian door, wrap around up to mid lane, take all the mid lane boxes in the back, then run back to your lane. [3:03]
- Finishing the box route lands you at about 5 minutes; take the bridge buff, then return to lane. [3:34]
- With one partner solo laning and one running boxes, both end up with about the same souls, roughly double what you would get sharing the lane; the speaker expects this to become the meta. [3:34]
- In higher ranks solo laning is punished by instant guardian pressure, but most players do not know how to punish it, so you can get away with it easily. [3:34]
- Vindicta is an extremely good solo laner; played properly, divers cannot stop her. [4:04]
- After the update, parrying the guardian no longer gives a parry reset, so a diver who parries the guardian gets hit by a full charged heavy melee and slammed into the guardian, losing the tower dive. [4:04]
- Mid lane is an aimer's lane: it is very open, you take bridge control and shoot the enemy team, and even if they retreat toward the walker you can keep shooting them. [4:34]
- Vindicta is good in mid lane because ult shots have never been easier there. [5:04]
- If your hero can kill camps decently, buy Monster Rounds to farm much faster. [5:04]
- With the new jungle changes you can shoot one camp, walk to another, group them and kill both at once to speed farm. [5:04]
- By 10 to 12 minutes you should have a decent lead if you avoided deaths, because most players are not macroing or farming at this level. [5:35]
- Leaving lane to run boxes is mostly for heroes who are not strong laners. [5:35]
- Rat King is a good hero to side lane with and run boxes instead of fighting, because until he has a few points in his 1 or 3 many heroes outgun him; many players stack up, get his ultimate, then walk in for a guaranteed team or lane wipe. [5:35]
- The speaker runs boxes as Vindicta until he has his ultimate and does not fight before it. [6:05]
- A lane-dominant hero like Apollo should stay in lane and fight, and maybe be the solo laner. [6:05]
- If you face a lane-dominant enemy hero, leave the lane. [6:05]
- By this point you should have the enemy Guardian in almost every lane; then let two or three waves stack, kill them at once and walk to the enemy side to take their boxes, sentries and camps. [6:36]
- When farming the enemy jungle, play as if an enemy is about to arrive: save your stamina, your escape ability and your armor or barrier ability for when someone comes. [7:06]
- Wear headphones; you can hear enemies from a decent distance and start leaving immediately. [7:06]
- Do not go too deep into the enemy jungle. [7:36]
- Pushed waves pressure the enemy walker, so enemies rush to defend it instead of hunting you in their jungle; use the walker pressure as a distraction to take farm rather than throwing your life for the walker. [7:36]
- Negative (unrealized) souls: a dead player on a 60 second timer earns nothing, so killing an enemy or stealing their farm is worth double, the souls you gain plus the souls they lose. [8:06]
- Stealing enemy farm forces them to either fall behind or invade you back, which turns into free kills and ganks. [8:37]
- Dying for the first walker is fine; if it is around 25 minutes and the first walker is still up, wait for a Rift to start, tell your team to fight it, and go solo kill the walker. [8:37]
- Without walker kills your team gets slot locked and has to sell items it wants, no matter how far ahead you are. [9:07]
- Dying for a walker is usually worth it as long as you are not carrying a bag (Soul Urn): the solo kill souls are large, your whole team gets an extra item slot, and the enemy loses a safe space to fight. [9:07]
- When you get far ahead, do not 1v6 out of ego; any hero can die to the whole enemy team's CC, Curse or ultimates (the speaker died to a Celeste silence into a Dynamo ult). [12:40]
- When far ahead, a 1v2 while solo pushing (with the rest of the enemy team in the Rift) can be acceptable, but avoid anything bigger than a 1v2. [13:11]
- Be at every fight you can: you being ahead does not mean your team is ahead, and if your teammates get farmed the enemy counter-snowballs. [13:41]
- Do not fall into "I'm ahead, let me perma farm": while you farm, the enemy can gain double by killing your teammates, invading your jungle and taking objectives like Mid Boss. [14:12]
- Still farm when ahead, but never walk around aimlessly hunting kills; that leads to forced fights in positions that favor the enemy. [14:43]
- On every rotation grab a box or two, shoot some camps and take sentinels for the permanent buffs, and keep moving around the map. [15:13]
- Take Mid Boss when you have three or more kills, they are on timers over 30 seconds, and you have wave priority, ideally all at once. [15:13]
- Mid Boss is the biggest game changer and usually the win game button; it is basically the entire end game until more objectives exist. [15:43]
- After Mid Boss: if you do not have walkers, take walkers; if you have them, the number one priority is the first shrine. [15:43]
- The first shrine makes the enemy team get fewer souls from minion waves for the rest of the game, the ultimate snowball tool. [15:43]
- Killing a shrine makes your minions stronger in that lane, so the lane keeps pushing and you keep wave priority unless the enemy resets it, letting you steal jungle farm, camps and bridge buffs. [16:13]
- The first shrine is worth dying for if necessary, and more important than a few kills in their spawn or losing Mid Boss or the Rejuvenator. [16:13]
