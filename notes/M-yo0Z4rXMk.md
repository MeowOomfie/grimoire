# Stomp on Seven with the New Items | Detailed Eternus Seven Build Guide
channel: Natan  date: 2025-05-21  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M-yo0Z4rXMk

## Covers
Seven, game plan by phase (farm early, secondary carry mid, hyper carry late), skill order (Power Surge then Storm Cloud), lane wave shoving and Guardian, item update, core and situational items, Fleetfoot, Ethereal Shift, Infuser, Mercurial Magnum, items left out (Mystic Shot, Spirit Shredder Bullets, Cultist Sacrifice, Trophy Collector, Arcane Surge)

## Claims
- The build is a late game hyper carry: early game you play mostly to farm, mid game you are a secondary carry looking for picks with your team rather than plays alone, and your main purpose is spreading debuffs (Suppressor, Healbane, maybe Slowing Hex) because mid game damage is usually not impressive yet. [0:30]
- The build is versatile; if you are not having a great game you can go more utility focused. [1:00]
- Skill order: rush two points in Power Surge for lane, then a point in Lightning Ball to farm toward tier 3 Power Surge, which you rush as a very big spike; then one point in stun and max the ult. [1:00]
- In games where the ult is not good, max the stun and then the ult; ball is kind of useless, so put one point in it for the extra charge and ignore the rest. [1:31]
- In lane, hard push the wave every time: two points in Power Surge, Monster Rounds, and Compress Cooldown imbued on Power Surge gives Power Surge for every wave, so you push faster than anyone and have wave priority. [1:31]
- Once the wave is in, hitting their Guardian is the best choice; otherwise poke them so you can hit the Guardian next wave, or do a box run (boxes and golden statues spawn at 2 minutes). [2:02]
- With this strategy he takes the enemy Guardian in about four minutes in most lanes. [2:02]
- Spirit in lane is kind of useless because Power Surge has only 0.15 scaling then, so early items are gun items; at 8k souls with tier 3 Power Surge and a point in ball the scaling goes to 0.49 and spirit starts to matter. [2:32]
- The lane gun items give a 56 bullet magazine; with Fleetfoot you can extend it with slides for near infinite ammo. [3:02]
- Healing Rite is still amazing: 300 regen, two sprint speed for box runs and mid game rotations. [3:02]
- Extra Regen is good now because the item update removed regen from many items but not this one; take it when you have lane pressure and want to stay in lane instead of hiding or box running with Healing Rite. [3:32]
- Suppressor feels good in lane: 75 bonus health and a fire rate slow that is easy to proc with Power Surge up; you build it mid game anyway. [3:32]
- Buy the core roughly left to right, top row first; some games an early Healbane, Suppressor or Enchanter's Emblem. [4:02]
- Fleetfoot gives slide distance and a lot of bonus health, and removing the shooting movement speed penalty is amazing on Seven because his move speed scales with spirit power and the penalty is a percentage. [4:02]
- Fleetfoot's active (10 second cooldown, about 7 with Superior Cooldown, 3.5 move speed) makes you very mobile and helps clear camps because you can slide. [4:32]
- Extra Spirit and Improved Spirit are the spirit stats and build into Boundless Spirit. [5:02]
- Enchanter's Emblem gives spirit resist and out of combat regen, plus spirit power and cooldown reduction above 65% health, both good for Power Surge. [5:02]
- Suppressor and Healbane are bought because your damage is not great yet but you can be annoying by spamming Power Surge; Healbane also gives Seven spirit, and no hero applies Healbane and Suppressor better than Seven since his kit is all AoE spirit damage. [5:02]
- Surge of Power (he is not entirely sure about it yet) is extra damage: two move speed, fire rate and an imbued spirit power bonus to put on Power Surge; buy it early if you are snowballing with spare souls. [5:33]
- Boundless Spirit was changed to give 10% spirit power instead of the Mystic Regen passive, which was weak because Seven does not spam abilities like Lady Geist or Yamato; it is now a big spirit stat, and might be better bought a little later (after Superior Cooldown or Ethereal Shift) since the 10% grows with more spirit. [6:03]
- At Boundless Spirit you can sell Healing Rite, Extra Regen and Monster Rounds; at about 19k to 20k the build needs no flex slot. [7:04]
- Superior Cooldown now reduces all cooldowns including items: Fleetfoot to 7.3 seconds, Slowing Hex from 30 to 22 seconds. [7:34]
- Ethereal Shift is extremely underrated and amazing on Seven, who has no escape, is squishy and does not like stacking resists: a 16 second cooldown, usable multiple times a fight, dodging a Wraith ult or a Lash slam or ult. [7:34]
- After Ethereal Shift you get spirit power, spirit resist and move speed for 5 seconds; you can reload during it and cancel it early to dodge briefly and get the buff instantly. [8:05]
- Mid game situational items go in flex slots when you are ahead, which also helps reach the investment bonuses (one more item gives another 5% bonus HP, or 10 spirit power from Slowing Hex). [8:35]
- Spirit Shielding and Weapon Shielding both give a plain barrier that works against both damage types and benefits from resists; Spirit Shielding procs on 150 spirit damage in 3.5 seconds (good against Yamato, Lash, Pocket), Weapon Shielding on 200 weapon damage (good against Mirage, Haze, Wraith). [9:05]
- Reactive Barrier has a 22 second cooldown versus 40 for the shields and procs on being movement locked, stunned, chained, immobilized or slapped. [10:06]
- Debuff Reducer is no longer great or a must buy for Seven; buy it only to build Unstoppable or Debuff Remover. [10:06]
- Spirit Lifesteal is built when you are going for Infuser. [10:36]
- Slowing Hex is still really good: it stops Lash grappling away, Dynamo using Quantum Entanglement and Wraith using her card, for running people down and landing the stun. [10:36]
- Mystic Vulnerability is very disappointing mid game but gains value late (spirit shred, spirit resist) and builds into Escalating Exposure, which is pretty good now. [10:36]
- Quicksilver Reload is really bad unless you are building Mercurial Magnum. [11:06]
- Skill timing in this build: Power Surge maxed at 8k, Storm Cloud at 23k. [11:36]
- Infuser is "absolutely insane": 80% spirit lifesteal and 30 spirit power on a 22 second cooldown, also pretty good for Power Surge. [11:36]
- Unstoppable is for playing for the ult when they have several ways of cancelling it, guaranteeing at least 5 seconds of ult. [12:07]
- Stamina Mastery is "crazy"; air dash with it for a massive speed boost. [12:07]
- Bullet Resilience fills the bullet resist you lack (you already have spirit resist from Emblem and Ethereal Shift); Warp Stone also gives conditional bullet resist and is nice in an open active slot. [12:37]
- Debuff Remover is for playing for Power Surge rather than the ult, against Vindicta, Mo & Krill, Infernus and similar. [13:08]
- Spirit Resilience replaces Enchanter's Emblem very late; Spellbreaker is pricier and better against burst such as Lash, Yamato and Pocket. [13:08]
- Silence Wave can be built early instead of Surge of Power in a game where you are not the main carry; it silences almost 4 seconds, is easy to hit, and can be timed after the stun pops. [13:39]
- Decay is for a weaker game, spammed on HP building healers such as Abrams, Lady Geist and Shiv. [14:09]
- Alchemical Fire late game shreds 20% bullet resist, which helps bullet damage teammates more than you; it can also come mid game after Healbane. [14:09]
- Spirit Burn (500 spirit damage to proc, which Seven reaches relatively easily) is amazing if nobody else on your team builds it and the enemy heals a lot, reducing healing by 70%; sell Healbane when you get it. [14:40]
- Lightning Scroll is amazing if you play for your ult, stunning people almost a second, three seconds after the ult hits them. [15:10]
- Vortex Web was disappointing once, but can be good when Slowing Hex is doing a lot, combined with the stun or thrown with a Dynamo ult. [15:10]
- Curse is for shutting down one or two fed carries such as Pocket, Yamato, Wraith or Lady Geist. [15:10]
- Imbue Mercurial Magnum on the ball, not Power Surge, because you want an ability that can proc it off cooldown; it makes bullets deal spirit damage that scales with spirit, not weapon damage. [15:42]
- With Mercurial Magnum, throw a ball at the start of the engagement, shoot, then throw another ball when the magazine runs out to instantly reload; always pair it with Titanic Magazine or you run dry before it recharges. [16:12]
- Escalating Exposure is good in long team fights, but it amps only your own damage and only against the stacked target (plus 8% spirit shred). [16:43]
- Spiritual Overflow (procs after two full bursts on someone) is decent for full damage but not a priority. [17:15]
- Spirit Rend shreds spirit resist on bullet hits and more on headshots, but is currently bugged (20% on one headshot instead of 14%); he does not like exploiting it. [17:45]
- His own late build adds bullet resist and fills all active slots, maybe selling Slowing Hex for Silence Wave. [18:15]
- Mystic Shot is left out: early damage is lacking anyway and it is not worth the slot late. [18:45]
- Spirit Shredder Bullets is left out: around 10 minutes people have no more than 20% spirit resist and Power Surge already shreds 15%, and its damage and lifesteal are mediocre. [18:45]
- Cultist Sacrifice is not good for Seven: he does not need the fire rate or ability range, Fortitude gives bonus health, and it suits heroes like Calico who do not take tier 3 camps fast, unlike Seven. [19:15]
- Trophy Collector is overhyped and bad on Seven, who needs stats immediately since without spirit he does nothing; it suits roaming heroes with strong base kits like Mo & Krill or Paradox. [19:46]
- Arcane Surge is gimmicky and bugged on Power Surge (only duration applies, and range and spirit power only on the first proc), and you already have near 100% Power Surge uptime. [20:17]
