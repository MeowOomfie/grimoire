## Claims
- He calls his Drifter build pretty OP. [0:00]

- Push the side waves while fights happen elsewhere. [7:56]
- Toxic Bullets comes next after a bullet resist item. [12:48]
- Save Slowing Hex for the most mobile enemy (Apollo); his team ran three Slowing Hexes and it was still not enough. [15:24]
- He wanted Counterspell but could not afford it. [19:12]
- Leech versus Vampiric Burst is his late choice. [19:45]
- Sinclair is hard to play against on Drifter because of bunny (Rabbit Hex) and punches. [20:18]
- He cannot play Vindicta without Unstoppable. [21:21]
- Drifter likes hidey-holes. [24:06]
- Over Blood Tribute he takes an item to stop enemies healing so much; silence is also good because it shuts down Shiv. [24:06]
- Headshots on Drifter are very easy; you barely have to aim. [24:37]
- Watch out for double bunny from Sinclair when fighting. [25:39]
- When ahead, out-farm them first: take their farm, do not rush, stay in a pack, push all the waves and do not die. [26:40]
