## Covers
Pocket coaching by Midknight: he reviews an Ascendant Pocket's game (Pocket and Ivy laning into McGinnis and Graves), posted 2026-08-15.
Themes: lane starts and box routes, timers, using Flying Cloak for damage versus as an escape, playing for Rifts, finding side-lane kills with Affliction, hitting camps, using actives like Ethereal Shift, and shielding item choices.

## Claims
Lane and early game (Pocket into McGinnis and Graves):
- The player started Extra Regen; he would probably go Monster Rounds first for wave clear, though wave clear matters little before 3 minutes as long as you cloak the 1:30 wave to get your tier one. [0:30]
- Against McGinnis, the win-lane hero, buying defensive is good; he leans Restorative Shot over Extra Regen when you play an active lane, since many Pockets take it and its mid-fight healing is another outplay tool. [1:01]
- Monster Rounds no longer works on McGinnis turrets (if it ever did). [1:01]
- Do not cloak every wave; you may skip the one wave you actually needed to clear. [1:32]
- Before 3 minutes, priority does not matter much with no boxes on the map, unless you are against a hero like Calico who can gank, so a 25 second cooldown spent on it is not a big deal. [2:03]
- He likes hard shoving the 3 minute wave and then taking a longer walk side. [3:03]
- When you are on the side of your tier one, do a box route on the 2:30 wave; you reach the camp around 3:25 (more realistically 3:35) and it takes about 10 seconds to clear. [3:03]
- Into McGinnis you need priority to do box routes but cannot get it, so the lane turns passive, which is fine. [4:08]
- Someone told him meleeing turrets (like McGinnis turrets) is not efficient since turrets have melee resist, apart from saving ammo; he has not tested it. [4:08]
Timers:
- On the buff wave with priority, be set up on the buff; your Ivy left for buff while you were over-aggressive, and that is really bad. [4:39]
- Learning timers is not hard: focus on them for about 20 games at Ascendant and you will be significantly better. [5:09]
- Core pattern: in dead time between waves, glance at the timer; 2:00 camps spawn, 3:25 camps or tier ones respawn, 5:00 buffs, 8:00 sentinels, 10:00 the next boss. [5:40]
Pocket builds and abilities:
- This game used the old-gen Pocket build; new-gen Pocket maxes Barrage first, but that is play style, not better. [6:12]
- Barrage's amp has always been very strong and people are realizing it. [6:12]
- Ivy players should ult a teammate instead of only self-ulting; the outgoing damage bonus is about a 20% damage amp to anyone you want. [6:44]
- You wasted your cloak: without duration on it (not maxed) it does not last long, and the wave was at your Guardian, so you did not need to flick it that far back. Jump into the wave and use cloak for damage. [7:48]
- Aim the cloak toward an escape such as a veil or behind your Guardian, then use Enchanter's Satchel and get out. [8:18]
- One of Pocket's difficulties is knowing when you can greed your cloak for damage (cloak onto them and use it as the out) and when to cloak out preemptively; in lane you can generally greed it for damage. [8:48]
- You could have thrown one more light melee after your heavy melee. [9:50]
- Cultist Sacrifice made sense here; against a one-AP enemy it was a free 160 damage. [9:50]
- The standard old-gen build goes pure fire rate from Swift Striker, since Cultist Sacrifice gives the damage and Barrage gives the amp. [10:21]
- He really likes Expansion on Pocket; most Pockets who go Cultist Sacrifice skip it, but infinite ability range is very strong because Affliction hitting one extra person is worth more than extra damage on the people you would have hit anyway. [11:21]
- Neither old-gen nor new-gen Pocket is better; pick what you like. [11:51]
- If you are missing Affliction hits, do not lean on Expansion as a crutch; that is a fundamentals issue. [12:23]

- Without the kill, do not trade a Walker for the Rift, since the Walker is more valuable. [13:24]
- At Ascendant, Pocket should play for Rifts. [13:24]
- Classic Walker play: clear the wave, then cloak out; with cloak maxed, a wave and an objective is when you want to use it to escape. [13:55]
