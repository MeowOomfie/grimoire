## Covers
A 3-minute Pocket guide (February 2026): pros and cons, abilities, Majestic Leap and cloak combos, lane by partner type, mid game invading and team fight options, late game ult focus and builds.
For players learning Pocket.

## Claims
- Pocket has a ton of outplay potential and innate burst, and forces you to get good at the game. [0:01]
- The one con: any small mistake gets you punished or killed. [0:01]
- Barrage (1) shoots ghosts from the case; hits give you damage amplification stacks. [0:01]
- Flying Cloak (2) is thrown out, damages anyone it touches, and can be recast to teleport to it. [0:01]
- Enchanter's Satchel (3) makes you invulnerable for a short time, then you pop out dealing damage; cancel it early with another ability, an item or a recast. [0:31]
- Pocket likes Cold Front to pop out of the satchel and get burst off faster. [0:31]
- Entering the case during a reload skips the reload animation. [0:31]
- Affliction (ult) applies a DoT to everyone around you that can't kill but deals a lot of damage; Barrage damage amp gained after casting Affliction still applies to it. [0:31]
- Combo: cast Majestic Leap, then Barrage; you can recast the leap during Barrage without ending it, getting into enemy space with a lot of damage. [1:01]
- Combo: Majestic Leap, press Affliction, then recast Majestic Leap so you land on the enemies as the Affliction animation finishes, afflicting everyone below. [1:01]
- Most common combo: cloak, enter the case, recast the cloak while in the case, and the case damage lands where you teleport. [1:31]
- Always think of the cloak as an exit, not just damage; ideally throw it onto a roof or somewhere you can teleport out of the fray. [1:31]
- Early game, mirror your laner: with a slower or weaker laner like Seven, cloak every wave, play slow and box route. [1:31]
- With an aggressive laner like Yamato, cloak the wave for pressure and harass, or farm the wave and save the cloak for aggressive trades. [2:02]
- Once the ult is online, don't be afraid to Affliction the enemies just for damage if you have a couple of Barrage stacks. [2:02]
- Mid game: Pocket is so mobile that invading is easy; stealing enemy sinner's sacrifices or any camp you can get is a big part of the loop. [2:02]
- Mid game team fight option 1: jump a mispositioned squishy, burst them, cloak out. [2:02]
- Option 2: look for a big ult, usually with Majestic Leap, because Affliction can solo win team fights. [2:33]
- Later in the game you look almost solely for that ult; if fed you can still pick off squishies, but that falls off and the ult becomes primary. [2:33]
- Builds: Cosmetical's build, which he uses, or Adorean's; build IDs are in the description. [2:33]
