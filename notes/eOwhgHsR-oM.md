## Claims
Player 1 (Holliday, Archon):
- Holliday's first core items are pretty solved; you are never not building Extra Charge and Extra Spirit, the rest (like healing) is game dependent. [2:33]
- When boxes spawn is the perfect time to go break them. [0:30]
- Don't throw a barrel at just the enemy when the same barrel could also hit three minions on the wave; that is a lot more value. [0:30]
- Always be trying to get wave priority. [0:30]
- Peek with the wall, not from above, so you can kill minions without opening yourself up to a bunch of angles. [0:30]
- Holliday's gun is on her right side and her camera is rooted to the right, which matters for how you peek. [1:30]
- Shove the wave and then fight, instead of fighting on the wave. [1:00]
- Put respect on Viper's name; if you don't know whether she has Venom up, don't take the peek. [1:30]
- Getting wave priority only on the 1:30 wave is the biggest early issue; get hard priority, then go into a box route. [3:03]
- You don't need a double jump to get back onto a vent; you can just melee onto it. [4:03]
- Box routes should happen early; seeing more of them after 5 minutes is the opposite of how they should work. [4:33]
- In that spot you should have parried. [5:35]
- Never melee off your lasso: a lot of players parry expecting the barrel plus melee and get a free parry. [5:35]
- You should be hard shooting wave, always be shooting it. [5:35]
- Don't over-respect a lone Vindicta crashing the wave into his tower; fight him, back up and barrel him, he is dead to rights. [6:06]
- Pick up the orbs behind you; souls left on the ground are a lot of lost souls. [6:40]
- Viper and Vindicta are both mini-nuke heroes and work really well together: Vindicta takes your first 50% and Viper the second. [7:11]
- The lane felt bad because you did not respect Viper and Vindicta's damage; that is a huge reason. [7:11]
- You are forcing lassos too much: three times you missed the barrel and still tried to force the lasso. [7:41]
- He doesn't understand why low-level players like Tankbuster at minute 10 so much; probably because it does a lot of damage. [8:11]
- Getting caught with only one Bounce Pad charge up, again and again, is costly; you need charges up. [8:41]
- At 11 HP, gambling on the Bounce Pad to the vent was correct, since shooting him meant dying either way. [9:11]
- Bounce Pad into immediate dash tech: you are dashing a full second after the pad, which slows you down a lot; dash right away. [9:41]
- You need Bounce Pad discipline. [10:12]
- When your team won't defend a walker you cannot reach in time, the walker is dead; take space on the other side, take a fight and get kills. Rotating back just wastes your time. [11:14]
- You are slow to push. On that route you could have dashed right instead of doubling back. [12:19]
- Down dashing after a jump pad is no more useful than a normal down dash. [12:19]
- A missed lasso attempt is fine; the only way you will hit it is by doing it on repeat. [12:50]
- One of the biggest notes: your micro needs work, which mostly means more time in game. [12:50]
- After an ego play, reset; don't stay in a risky spot. If you are going to greed while sitting on a healing item, pop it. [14:22]
- Many new Holliday players try to finish kills with a melee; don't. A barrel or a headshot would have killed; trust your aim and the Crackshot. [15:23]
- Don't greed a second melee. [15:23]
- Rebind the grab zipline key away from Space bar; this play is exactly why. It is easy to change in settings. [16:24]
- Reading the enemy from a little trail and routing to cut him off was perfect. [16:56]
- Run Urn, and run it for pressure; no one ran the 10 minute or 20 minute Urn. [17:26]
- Don't use Alchemical Fire just to confirm a kill. It is really powerful around Mo & Krill burrowed and inside Kelvin's dome. [17:56]
- That fight was bad; you were playing too aggressively. [18:27]
- You should confirm the Urn orbs. Don't cash your Urn while your team isn't positioned around the cash; they should be helping. [19:28]
- If you really want to sweat, you could bait mid boss. [20:00]
- Metal Skin is 100% to play this game; it goes crazy into every hero on their comp. Close Quarters also wouldn't hurt, but they don't melee much. [21:01]
- In a 2v6 you cannot contest the mid boss; give it and push a walker. [21:31]
- Hitting their walker while they do mid boss is bad when they have six alive and you have four dead; with two of them dead it would be good. Going for it long after they already killed mid boss is very bad. [22:34]
- Saying "my bad" for every play is good for the team even when it is not objectively your fault. [23:05]
- Counterspell is much better than Dispel Magic this game: you can Counterspell Pocket's ult, Viper's Venom and Billy's ult. [23:36]
- Into a mainly hard-gun comp, go Metal Skin as the first defensive item. [23:36]
- Divine Barrier would be good this game because cleansing Pocket's Affliction is valuable; Dispel Magic can't do that. For a selfish buy take Counterspell; Divine Barrier is the good buy for the team. [24:39]
- When your team is there and you land a lasso, stand still and let your team shoot them in the head rather than jumping around. [24:39]
- You died with Alchemical Fire and Dispel Magic up: pop Dispel Magic for the heal, and Alchemical Fire would have done huge damage inside Kelvin's dome. [25:12]
- You over-extended and died. [25:43]

Summary notes:
- Bounce Pad usage: early you didn't have enough charges when caught, and by mid and late game you weren't using them when caught. [25:43]
- Always hit wave, get priority and use your barrels on wave. [25:43]
- Respect enemies when you are into two very strong laners. [25:43]
- Rebind your grab zipline key; it is super strong. [26:13]
- Run Urn more; Holliday can run Urn right off spawn. [26:13]
- Identify a hard-gun enemy comp and buy Metal Skin. [26:13]
- After a big lasso with your whole team there, grab them and dash backwards: you still get the damage but barely move them, so your team can farm them. [26:13]
