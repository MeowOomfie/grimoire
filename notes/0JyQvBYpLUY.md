## Covers
Bebop, aquatiq: a "green bomb" tank build (Sticky Bomb plus heavy vitality items) played in a hard solo lane against Mina and Vindicta.
Commentary over one full game, not a structured guide; September 2026 patch.

## Claims
- The build is still a bomb build but with lots of vitality (green) items: do a lot of damage with bombs while outliving everybody. [0:32]
- Mina and Vindicta both have very strong lanes and are awful to lane against. [0:32]
- Against Mina and Vindicta he bought Spirit Shielding first, since Mina's spirit damage would hurt more than Vindicta's gun. [1:34]
- When the enemy plays scared, go for bombs because you need Sticky Bomb stacks. [1:34]
- He says he should have rushed Veil Walker in this lane. [2:06]
- In a bad lane, sit back and wait for enemies to walk at you when the waves arrive, then bomb them. [2:38]
- If you can hook Vindicta out of the air she becomes an easy target; she is weak while flying if hooked down. [3:38]
- Plan in a losing lane: play slow, stay behind the minions, hold the wave and keep pushing lane up when possible. [5:10]
- Get boxes when the lane is stuck. [5:10]
- He had 13 bomb stacks by the middle of the lane phase and kept to the Veil Walker plan even while poor. [5:41]
- He listed Bladed armor, Knockdown and Disarming Hex as options against Vindicta and wanted Slowing Hex. [9:46]
- After lane: skip camps and roam to collect bomb stacks from kills. [12:24]
- His planned late tank items: Indomitable, Unstoppable, Plated Armor and Juggernaut. [15:01]
- When three enemies are dead and their waves are bad, go mid boss; his team skipped a free mid. [16:40]
- Veil Walker saved his life in a fight. [17:43]
- He timed mid boss: avoid dying in the minute before it spawns and group when it is up. [21:22]
- Do not fight a Billy when you are Slowing Hexed (teammate call). [21:52]
- Ran the Urn after mid boss because he needed money. [22:55]
- Buy Witchmail once you can; he felt the enemy could not win after it. [23:25]
- He used his ult (Hyper Beam) pretty much off cooldown. [26:31]
- Result: few kills but 16 assists, 14,000 healing and 10,000 barrier absorbed, with about +66% vitality investment by the end. [25:59]
