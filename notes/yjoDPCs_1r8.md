## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Drifter-only free coaching stream right after Drifter's release (August 2025 patch; this predates the 2026 map and item changes). He first spectates high-rank Drifter games, then coaches three Drifter players: an Arcanist, an Oracle playing in an Emissary lobby, and a second Arcanist.
Themes: Drifter as an M1 farmer and pub stomper, Kinetic Dash over Quicksilver Reload, the 20 to 24 m falloff window, Stalker's Mark (2) usage for backline dives, target priority (Paige and Mina first, Lady Geist last), ult value beyond the blind, overfighting, farming and urn, sell order, and Wander's build as the recommendation.
