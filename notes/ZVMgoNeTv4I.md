# The Best Box Routes in Deadlock (Updated)
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-02-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVMgoNeTv4I

## Covers
Box (breakable) farming routes for every lane, post January 2026 map (teams Hidden King and Archmother): left side lane, mid (blue) lane and right side lane routes, when to run each, gank and box-steal options, and the room that only opens at 10 minutes. For any player who wants more souls between waves. No exact soul values or route timings are given.

## Claims
- Routes are described by side (left lane, mid, right lane), not team color, because the color changes with Hidden King or Archmother but the map geometry does not. [3:33]
- Left lane route 1 (short and simple): after shoving the enemies into their guardian, walk back, go under the veil, jump into the ceiling to collect the soul buff from below, wrap around past the tier three camps and make your way back to your shop grabbing every box. [0:00]
- Left lane route 2: start on the bridge in the middle of the lane, grab the statue buff, wrap around to the boxes, loop toward the Sinner's Sacrifices, jump up by the vents into the tunnel system grabbing boxes, go up and around (or from below coming back up) and end right back in lane. There are a few variations; you are just grabbing the boxes you see. [0:00]
- Left lane route 3: start at your left lane guardian, zip back onto the roofs (reach them any way you like), farm the boxes hugging the edge, then go on top of your doubles camp (the camp with the two Sinner's Sacrifices). [0:30]
- Left lane route 3 option: with more time, go to the roof for extra boxes and possibly look for a mid gank, though you are already pressed for time; it works if your lane partner can anchor the wave well, like a Lady Geist. Otherwise take the lower boxes and walk all the way back to lane. [1:00]
- Left lane route 4 (longer, much more defensive, the last route you would want to do): from your guardian back up into the tunnel system, walk the tunnels collecting boxes, go up the stairs right behind your walker into the tight room full of boxes, pick up the statue buff there, then zip back to lane. [1:31]
- Mid lane route 1 (short, for when you need to buy): with the enemy shoved in, start on top of the bridge, go left back through the tunnel system next to the Sinner's Sacrifice, pick up the boxes and end at your shop. Do it when you get a little priority. [2:01]
- Mid lane route 2 (the classic subway route): from your mid guardian go left through the subway. Dive through the boxes by sliding and rolling into them instead of stopping to melee each one; it makes the route a little faster and is worth min-maxing. [2:01]
- Mid lane route 3: from your mid guardian walk behind it toward your walker, detour into the veil and grab every box in the room behind it, end at your walker and grab those boxes; two more sit on a ledge you can greed if you have time, though normally you won't. [2:32]
- Mid lane route 4: from your guardian go back and right between the two "hybrid" camps grabbing boxes, then choose: go right to finish the previous route's room and the walker, or go left for more boxes including a juke closet on the far right. [3:02]
- Urn buffs (caption "earn") have a big hitbox, so you can pick one up just by walking through the wall at more than a few places; the route ends back at your guardian. [3:33]
- Mid lane route 5: from your guardian go right, grab the boxes just to its right (grab these whenever you have time), head toward the right side lane, then choose the roof to the right or make your way back. [3:33]
- If you go back on mid lane route 5, grab the statue buff on the way and the couple of boxes nearby. [4:03]
- If you take the roof on mid lane route 5, grab its boxes, then make the jump onto the bridge with an air dash (better players can do it without a double jump, just a melee), grab the bridge boxes and look for a right lane gank; if none, head back. [4:03]
- To be more aggressive you can steal enemy boxes nearby; all of these routes can be run on the enemy side to deny them boxes, which is very valuable but risky. [4:33]
- Mid lane route 6: from your mid guardian grab the two boxes by the mid lane entrance rope, go back toward the Sinner's Sacrifices, go to the bottom of the bridge and walk up the rope. [4:33]
- On mid lane route 6, after the boxes up top, check for a gank; if none, keep farming the roof boxes, then decide how many enemy boxes to steal by running the mirrored route on their side, or head back quickly if your wave is being pushed in. [5:05]
- Mid lane route 7 (short, the one to do last when no other boxes are up): from your mid guardian go down under mid lane and grab all the boxes; you stay in range to get souls from minions dying above. Exit through the veil, take the zip and return toward the guardian. [5:05]
- Right lane route 1 (church route): from your guardian go into the church and grab the boxes by the camp. [5:36]
- Normally prioritize farming camps over boxes, but do most of these box routes before camps have spawned. [5:36]
- Right lane route 1 options: for a quick route go back to the guardian and grab that box; with more time go into the tunnel system and farm its boxes, optionally into the room to the left for even more, then normally hit the vent back to lane. [6:06]
- Right lane route 2: start like route 1 through the library and the same boxes, but go up and left instead of right and under the bridge, grabbing the boxes from the mid routes and all the soul buffs on the roof, then the two crates, then back to lane. [6:06]
- Right lane route 3: from your guardian go left into the library and farm its boxes, then into the room below the bridge for those boxes, then up; after the roof boxes look for a mid lane gank, otherwise keep farming. [6:36]
- There are a ton of boxes on the enemy roofs; this is the route if you want to deny many enemy boxes. You are on high ground so it is relatively safe, but have some priority or information on the enemy first. [7:07]
- Right lane route 4: from your guardian go all the way behind it, grab the boxes (including one easy to miss), then toward the hybrid camp and back to lane. [7:07]
- The two boxes in the back of right lane route 4 are best taken off the zip: every time you rotate and see them up, jump off and grab them, it is very quick for a lot of souls. [7:38]
- Bonus room: a room full of boxes and buffs that only spawns at 10 minutes, so it is only farmable after 10; it is worth grabbing, especially at lower elo where people don't know it exists. [7:38]
- These are the core routes you need to know; more exist, including tunnels left for a possible part two. [8:08]
