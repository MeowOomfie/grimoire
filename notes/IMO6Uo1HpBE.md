## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (Labor Day 2026, during the new ranked season before the next major patch). He reviews four games: an Oracle Sinclair laning with a Mirage, a low-rank Victor, a top-60 Eternus Calico, and an Oracle Victor on a Golden Goose Egg build.
Themes: wave priority in lane, Sinclair bolt build versus support/ult build, playing for Rifts, using picks for mid boss, overfighting and ego fights, farming share by source, walker timing, Calico target selection into spirit resist, cat amp, tunnel farming, Healbane, and taking shrines.
Side opinions: Mirage as the strongest laner, Victor quietly strong, Bebop slept on as a gun hero, Reactive Barrier on Calico into Abrams.
