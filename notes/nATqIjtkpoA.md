# Play like an Eternus - Deadlock Macro Guide
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-04-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nATqIjtkpoA

## Covers
Macro priorities by game stage (0-8, 8-10, 10-15, 15-20, 20-25, 25+ minutes), shown on a Lash game in a hard lane against Sinclair and Calico.
Covers lane goals, the 8 minute soul split change, Sinner's Sacrifice, buff and Urn timings and spawn sides, reading the game state, when Walkers and Mid Boss open up, and fighting around objectives.
For mid-rank players who take random fights and miss objective timers.

## Claims
- At a high level Lash is not that good, and in lane he gets blown up by a lot of heroes; Calico and Sinclair are among the best at it and among the strongest laners. [1:01]
- The laning stage is all about getting a soul lead, pressuring the opponents' tower and defending your own. [1:32]
- If you are winning the lane or the matchup favors you, pressure their tower and try to take it as fast as possible. [1:32]
- In an unfavorable matchup, defend your tower and minimize damage taken, while going for opportunities when the enemy overextends. [1:32]
- Behind in lane, keep the enemy zoned so they can't push your tower for free; trade damage when they step forward. Being down souls with zero tower damage taken is acceptable. [2:03]
- Even down over a thousand souls in lane, keep looking for plays; the best way back into a lane is a kill on an overextending enemy. Don't mindlessly force kills, but don't play super passive either. [2:33]
- Before 8 minutes, souls from troopers are duplicated 100% with your lane partner; after 8 minutes each of you gets 60% of the value, so you are incentivized to farm things other than the lane. [3:03]
- After 8 minutes, farm the lane but also the vaults (the Sinner's Sacrifice); punish enemies who go take theirs. [3:33]
- After buying, go to your vault unless something is more important; farm them as soon as they spawn, and stealing the opponents' is even better. It is one of the most important things before 10 minutes. [4:03]
- Be at the buffs and Urn right as they spawn so nobody can steal them; most ranks are late and don't get punished, but you should be early. [4:34]
- The in-game clock is wrong on replays. [4:34]
- Take the jump pad just before the buff spawns so you can punch it the moment it appears, then look for an early play. [4:34]
- Don't take fights with no reason; take a fight when you have an advantage or a particular reason. Having an ult, even net worth and a buff is not enough in the middle of nowhere. [5:04]
- With the buff, all you have to do is stay alive and play for the objective; the Urn is the most important objective at that time. [5:34]
- Ask why you are taking a fight: a specific advantage, an objective in mind. If you can't justify it, it is a bad fight. [6:04]
- Players he coaches don't prioritize the Urn enough; with any advantage from 10 to 15 minutes, the best play is almost always to run the Urn. [6:35]
- Check the wave states to find a free Urn: with the right wave shoving, left wave shoved and middle even, the right Urn is likely free. [6:35]
- From 10 to 15 minutes, if you are behind you can still run the Urn at the right timing, but ahead you should almost always run it, on any hero. [7:06]
- Even on Wraith, a quick Urn run gives about the same souls as farming, plus an advantage for the whole team and an extra AP that lets you hit AP power spikes earlier. [7:06]
- Whenever nothing is happening, check the game state, starting with souls at the top of the screen: are we ahead or behind? This should dictate every action. [7:37]
- Ahead: play on their side of the map and stop them farming back into the game. Behind: defend your objectives and punish overextensions for comeback souls. [8:08]
- 8,000 souls ahead at 13 minutes is a significant lead. [8:08]
- Next check the objective state: is the Urn up, and how many Guardians each team has. A team with zero Guardians taken is locked out of a flex slot, so defend your Guardians; keep at least two people on a side whose Guardian needs defending. [8:39]
- Then check for outliers: heroes with much higher net worth (for example Vindicta at 13k, Calico at 12k). Play with your strong ally; a fight without the enemy carry is good, and with her she is a high priority target. [9:10]
- When ahead, pressure enemies off the wave even without killing them, farm aggressively on their side, then return to defaulting. [9:40]
- In the mid game when ahead, keep the pressure, keep the waves shoving into them, and farm or look for ganks between waves; don't give them time to breathe. [9:40]
- From 10 to 15 minutes it is very hard to pressure Walkers: they have a lot of resistances, you don't do enough damage yet, and enemies fight well under them. Generally don't pressure Walkers too hard before 15 minutes; learn the rule before the exceptions. [10:10]
- Many leads are thrown by fighting under a Walker at 12 or 13 minutes. [10:40]
- Urn spawns are predictable: the 10 minute Urn always spawns on yellow lane (left lane from his side), the second on the opposite side on blue, and they alternate all game, so 20 minutes is yellow and 25 minutes is blue. [11:10]
- The only interruption to the Urn pattern is someone holding an Urn past the respawn point of the next one; 99% of the time they spawn on schedule. [11:40]
- At 15 minutes the Urn comeback mechanics start: if a team more than 10% behind picks up the Urn, the drop off is on their side and very hard to stop, so prevent them from picking it up in the first place. [11:40]
- Have most of your team on the Urn side ready to contest at 15 minutes; it should be priority number one. Against a lot of enemies you can't defend it, but against one, go for him off the Urn and then pick it up. [12:10]
- Holding an Urn whose drop off is on the enemy side is safer than forcing the turn in; a risky turn in that costs you a Walker is an even trade, which you don't want while ahead. [12:41]
- A classic blunder, even in high level games: fighting deep at the enemy Walker without a wave. Always ask what objective you are fighting around. [13:42]
- From 15 to 20 minutes you can start pressuring Walkers; if three teammates pressure one side, the enemy can't show up on the other side with many people. [13:42]
- It is hard to kill people at their Walker; pushing it alone and getting caught 2v1 is a risky play that gets punished. [14:13]
- When the game is fairly even (a 4k soul lead isn't enough to act on), the default is dealing with waves as aggressively as you can and looking for a numbers or ultimate advantage. [14:44]
- Push the middle wave before joining a fight; if you win the fight you have the option to pressure mid and more freedom on the map. [14:44]
- Ziplines are one of the biggest forms of movement; the further you push waves, the less zipline the enemy has, which is incredibly valuable. Always push one more wave; never stop at one. [15:14]
- Be at the buff spawn right on time, then play for the Urn. [15:14]
- Use the teleporter and zipline to get into position for an Urn fight. [15:44]
- After getting the Urn, reset onto defending your objectives; with a lead, don't give away any objective for free. [15:44]
- From 20 to 25 minutes, look to win a fight for Mid Boss or Walkers; at this stage Walkers go down very fast, and Mid Boss is possible after one or two kills depending on enemy abilities. [16:14]
- At 20 to 25 minutes, play aggressive on waves and look for anyone to overextend, then go in the moment you spot it. [16:44]
- After taking a Walker, push the wave all the way up; the goal is pushing the wave, not necessarily the farm. [17:15]
- Every time you win a fight you are on their side of the map. Always do the riskiest thing first when you have an advantage: order what you want to do by risk and do the riskiest first. [17:15]
- The in-game clock is about 30 seconds ahead on replays; time buff spawns from that. [17:45]
- After winning a fight around 20 to 25 minutes, go Mid Boss or a Walker; chasing a deep enemy into their base is an overextension and not worth it. [18:15]
- From 25 minutes on, it is late game and pretty much always about taking fights. [18:45]
- With an advantage and enemies down four, go pick up the Urn; letting the enemy grab it via a zipline on a pushed wave hands them comeback souls. [18:45]
- Summary: 0 to 8 minutes play the lane (deny tower damage if behind, get tower damage if ahead). [19:15]
- Summary: 8 to 10 minutes play for Sinner's Sacrifice the moment it spawns, steal theirs, farm yours as soon as you can. [19:46]
- Summary: from 10 minutes, buffs, then buffs and Urn every 5 minutes afterwards, the most important thing unless there is a huge team fight. [19:46]
- Summary: 10 to 15 minutes you can't really push Walkers, so look for Guardians and the Urn; with any advantage run the Urn quickly, people don't expect it. [19:46]
- Summary: 15 to 20 minutes after winning a fight you can go Walker, sometimes even Mid Boss; look for overextensions. [20:16]
- The 15 minute Urn is the first comeback Urn if either team is behind by 10%; the team behind should prioritize it over almost anything, and the team ahead must not let them get it. Same for 20 and 25 minutes. [20:16]
- General timers: Walkers from 15 minutes, Walkers and Mid Boss from 20 minutes; there are many exceptions. [20:46]
- Take fights when you have item advantage or map pressure advantage, or fight around objectives (Urn, Guardians, Walkers); stop fighting randomly in the middle of nowhere. [20:46]
- One kill from being slightly better than a guy doesn't give much beyond stopping his farm, and it is risky because you may be overextending and get caught. [21:17]
