# Stop Blaming Your Teammates (& Play With Them) | Coaching an Ascendant Celeste
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-09-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XRkguzFFL9Q

## Covers
Celeste coaching by Midknight: he reviews one game of an Ascendant Celeste laning with a Haze into Vindicta, in a lobby with Mirage, Viscous, Lash, Drifter, Ivy and Infernus.
Themes: playing with your lane partner, playing a scaling hero (side laning, not over-rotating), mental and muting, farming centers and asking for buffs, rift macro, item sell order.
Patch date: September 2026.

## Claims
Player 1 (Celeste, Ascendant):
- Not starting Golden Goose Egg is good. [0:30]
- Lane cycle on Celeste: Shining Wonder (1), harass, farm. [0:30]
- After the enemy sees you have no 1 up they run you down; give space instead of taking a bad trade. [0:30]
- Getting over aggressive while the tier one is spawning is a big mistake: they could hard collapse on you while your Haze secures it, or run at your Haze on the camp. [1:32]
- Use your 1 with or on the wave; otherwise you lose the multi-purpose value and they get wave priority on you. [2:35]
- You are trying to trade a lot in a lane that should be losing; don't fight the Vindicta, you are too over aggressive for your hero. [3:06]
- Against Vindicta Stake you lose at least 400 HP if she presses it, so you can't get away with those trades; it should be a massive trade in their favor. [3:37]
- You keep giving them good trades for no reason by not playing with your Haze. Play with your lane partner: even a weak lane hero is still a whole hero of damage, health and utility, and makes it a 2v2 not a 2v1. [4:07]
- He likes two points into the 1 first (learned from Shiro); it makes Celeste a lot easier to play. [5:11]
- You slowly got whittled down by consistently mispositioning, then wasted your last stamina; leave with your Haze instead of being forced to tuck. [6:13]
- Your Lash's ult should have been kited to the wave to make it valuable. [6:45]
- Mute teammates who tilt you instead of flaming back; giving a reaction encourages them, and the one you flamed then stole your farm. [7:15]
- A heavy fire rate comp can easily be countered with anti fire rate items, but at least you have a real M1. [8:16]
- Your lane was nearly unwinnable: Mirage is in his opinion the strongest laner in the game and scales well; Apollo is also one of the best and could contest Mirage. [8:46]
- Light melee would have farmed that camp more efficiently. [8:46]
- When you want double buffs, ask for them on mic instead of light meleeing the centers; don't farm the same centers as your Drifter, take the other one. Four buffs on Drifter beats one buff on you. [9:46]
- Fixing your mental lets you improve at the most efficient rate. [10:18]
- Great job mirroring the Viscous Battle Punch. [10:49]
- That time would have been better spent farming; arriving late to mid splits the mid wave between you and Lash when you should have kept holding your side lane. [10:49]
- Too many heroes on your team want to side lane (Infernus, Lash, Ivy). [11:19]
- Mental reset: never flame people to their face, find one thing you do when tilted and one thing you do before every game. [11:50]
- The enemy contesting a comeback rift instead of taking your mid walker was a huge throw by them. [12:25]
- Good vibes win games: telling a tilting team "it's still winnable" stops them from locking into the game-is-lost mindset. [13:03]
- When the enemy deathballs with all five, split push; countering a deathball with your own deathball is a lower-elo mistake. Lash could have taken the mid walker. [14:04]
- Pop Restorative Locket before the stun actually procs to greatly raise your chance to live; about 600 HP could let you survive the burst. [15:38]
- You waffled on your pathing and didn't pop Locket. [16:10]
- Your Haze going Golden Goose Egg was griefing, but pointing it out hurts rather than helps. [16:41]
- Ricochet rush Haze is okay only if she must be the M1; in a farm-hungry comp like Celeste, Lash and Ivy she should not. [17:15]
- In this part of the mid game (around 20 minutes) you should be AFK side laning until the next mid boss; your side laning came too late. [17:45]
- Over rotating to your team to fight like a Trophy Collector player is wrong for a hyper scaling hero: clear the wave and create pressure instead. [18:16]
- You could have made it to the rift before it expired; with three of theirs dead and them at only 5% comeback resist, don't give it up for no reason. [18:49]
- Main notes so far: mental, not playing to scale, playing too tempo heavy instead of side lane heavy. [19:19]
- You spend too much time around the mid lane. [21:20]
- That fight was forced: Infernus wasn't there, you weren't looking for a pick, you knew many of them were grouped, and Viscous's ult was up soon. [21:50]
- You died because you were too slow to reset, running in circles instead of going to your fountain. [23:27]
- In solo queue teammates will always bloodthirst for kills; accept it. [24:28]
- The map had a lot of farm and your team mostly farmed only doubles; farm more camps. [25:01]
- Giving a comeback rift is fine, but you must do something with it: fight before, fight after, look for split pressure or a walker. Be positioned to cut them off after rift. [26:01]
- Upgrading toward Opening Rounds for slot reasons is fine since you don't want to sell High Velocity Rounds; Express Shot is not much more expensive and worth considering if you are not immediately slot locked. [27:46]
- Sell order here: probably Spirit Shielding first, then Restorative Locket, then Suppressor, depending on the matchup. [28:47]
- Being dead one and looking for a fight or pick mid lane is far from ideal; if you run in, have all six alive, and ideally find the fight on a side lane where it isn't a 6v6. [30:23]
- Witchmail plus your shield kept you full HP through four Healbanes. [31:27]
- Tankbuster is basically free damage and does a lot in the late game. [31:57]
- Your ult came out late; ult early for its zoning potential rather than fearing they split from pit. [32:29]
- Never melee the Rejuvenator while it is contested; your play there was correct. [33:01]
- Escalating Exposure was very strong this game. [34:05]
- Light melee is not worth it at this point in the game because a parry is so valuable. [34:37]
- When you have the choice, grab the shrine on their doubles side (the right shrine here); more valuable, though minor. [35:37]
- Once scaled, Celeste can do whatever she wants; the mistakes that mattered were early, when you could be punished. [36:19]
- Enemies into Celeste need more than one Slowing Hex; she dispels it instantly. [36:19]
- Celeste's shield: 400 HP, 200 damage, 3 second silence, movement speed, AOE, and its cooldown starts on use, not on expiry. [36:49]
- No shrine early because the team fights were thrown, which made the game go late. [37:50]
- Your ult should not be your top damage at 24 minutes; do more work with your 1. [39:05]
- Lane summary: play with your Haze even if he is tilted and makes mistakes; he still did 60% of your damage, like a free item, plus an HP pool to tank. [39:35]
- Play to scale: in the mid game sit in a side lane and scale instead of over rotating to menial fights. [40:06]
- A partner not following you is different from a partner not following you 2v1 onto their tier one past your own pushing wave. [40:06]
- Fix the mental: everyone makes mistakes, shake it off and don't flame. [40:36]
- You only farmed about three centers; farm a lot more centers and buffs, which Celeste wants for scaling. [41:36]
- Rift macro: your team sold rifts for no reason; letting a team take rift and doing nothing is the easiest way to let them come back. [41:36]
