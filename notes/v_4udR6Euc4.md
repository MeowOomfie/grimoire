# You're Egoing Too Much | Oracle Paradox Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-01-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v_4udR6Euc4

## Covers
Midknight reviews an Archon Paradox (laning with Yamato into Dynamo and Calico).
Themes: playing around power spikes, not saving or fighting on a big bag, respecting enemies, prioritizing waves, ulting before walling, Kinetic Carbine pressure and the carbine dash boost, stealing enemy Sinner's Sacrifice, Slowing Hex and defensive items, and good versus bad fights into a Dynamo ult.
Coaching review, no patch named; video dated 2026-01-11.

## Claims
Laning:
- Paradox and Yamato into Dynamo and Calico is a positive lane; everyone but Dynamo is a strong laner, so as long as you press space bar when he comes near you, you win the lane. [0:31]
- In theory you should never parry off Calico's first two and Calico should never melee off her first two, but at Archon it is good to play it safe and parry the first one. [1:02]
- When learning the game, go into the sandbox and read every hero's abilities in full and shoot their gun. [1:02]
- Saving for Headhunter sacks your tempo: even up about 600 souls a piece, sitting on 2.4k unspent souls is a significant disadvantage. [2:03]
- Once you have the spike you are super strong and should look for a fight, especially with your ult up. [2:34]
- You are walling before you ult; ult first, then press wall. [3:05]
- Keep the pressure up: use Kinetic Carbine while you reload, since you can't shoot while reloading anyway. [3:35]
- Carbine pressure while walking up: either they don't peek to shoot the wave and your wave hits the tower for free, or they peek and eat a free carbine. [3:35]
- Be very scared when the enemies are about to get their ults off the current or next wave. [3:35]
- When your Yamato commits to a dive, you are playing too afraid. [4:05]
- Be careful diving a Dynamo about 400 souls off his ult when the heal creep is on the table: if minions die to tower he can kick the wave, kill the heal creep, get his ult and heal about half his HP. [4:05]
- For Bebop, Taz has a good build; nearly all the other top Bebop builds are troll, and no one at high elo plays hook Bebop. [4:36]
- Roaming to your losing lane is generally not great, because you are more likely to die and hand your lead to the enemy; here it was fine since your other lane was losing by more. [5:06]
- After you take the tower, back out instead of looking for another engage; your teammates were backing out. [5:36]

Items and power spikes:
- You are going too orange. Integrate more greens; you definitely shouldn't go Backstabber. [5:36]
- If you really want to, you can go Headhunter into Cultist before greens, though he doesn't think it is the play, but never Headhunter, Cultist and Backstabber before greens. [6:07]
- Normally for a spirit spike you go a cooldown item with BRS, or a cooldown item with Slowing Hex; this game he would go cooldown and Slowing Hex. [13:58]
- Slowing Hex is great this game: it locks down Calico completely, is good into Ivy, amazing into Viper and good into Dynamo. Buy it now, or even in laning. [13:58]
- Glass Cannon is only for when you are ahead, but being ahead should make you pivot to defensive itemization. [16:01]
- Metal Skin is good here, with a lot of value into Haze, Viper and Ivy. [17:02]
- With the Bebop on 51 stacks of Trophy Collector and his bombs, you will 100% need Counterspell this game. [11:56]

Macro and bags:
- After ganking mid while still fed but with an ally offline, use the advantage to invade the enemy jungle and steal their Sinner's Sacrifice instead of staying for a bad fight. [6:07]
- Their mid wave pushing back gives them pressure to contest you on the Sinner's Sacrifice. [6:38]
- Clear invading in solo queue is fine. [6:38]
- Don't gank with a 700 bag; you are 200 souls from the Cultist spike, so reset, buy, and come back on the spike to gank. [7:08]
- Don't solo fight as Paradox; defend the walker, and with the wave crashing into it, path into the walker and clear it. [7:40]
- When fed you can 1v1 someone, but you can never 1v2 or 1v3; respect the enemies more and prioritize waves. [8:42]
- In theory Paradox doesn't want to farm doubles, and doubles go to whoever grabs them, but in solo queue farming doubles to carry is fine. [9:48]
- You went in with a massive bag again and gave them a 1.2k bag. [9:48]
- Don't coin flip a walker with a big advantage; leverage it into running the urn, which in 10 seconds would also stagger them. [10:56]
- Don't re-engage to save a dying Yamato; leave her for dead instead of dying with her. [11:26]
- Medium elo pattern: you push deep into their walker, they ping pong the wave back and you all die, then it repeats; normally that leads into mid boss. [11:56]
- When the urn is big, grab it. [12:26]
- Run the urn; grabbing that box route was good. [13:28]
- Invading doubles: trying to 1v1 someone in their jungle is bad, but contesting someone on doubles is good, since they have probably taken HP from the camp. [15:30]
- That contest works because your Krill has priority, so whoever is on the left side must deal with Krill first; in theory call Krill over after he crashes the wave. He would not like the same play on the basement camp. [16:01]
- Your team should play around the urn; that is what the fight needs to be over. [17:02]
- As a carry Paradox, look for picks on a side lane with an aggressive carry: Yamato in the mid game, Wraith late once she has scaled, or Mo & Krill early. [17:02]
- Stealing their Sinner's Sacrifice is better than securing your own. [18:05]
- Into their Dynamo you don't want to look for a big team fight. [18:36]
- You showed way too early when anticipating their mid move. [19:06]
- To cancel the Dynamo ult without a curse, your Wraith has to ult the Dynamo or Kelvin has to dome you when Dynamo ults. [19:37]
- Don't take a fair fight in the pit, especially with no teammates nearby and down 16k souls; play around Rejuvenator, curse or Wraith ult the Dynamo, and then fight. [20:10]
- Generally curse the Dynamo out of a good ult opportunity or during his ult. [20:40]
- Paradoxical Swap should swap an enemy into danger, like into the pit; if forward is dangerous, backward is safe. A horizontal swap that leaves her where she started does nothing. [21:10]

Mechanics:
- Hold left click between melees to get free bullets on Paradox. [12:56]
- You could have thrown your Pulse Grenade under yourself. [12:56]
- Carbine dash boost (not patched): while in Kinetic Carbine, dash and right click halfway through the dash; ideally use a diagonal air dash, but a forward air dash is easier to practice. [10:23]

Summary:
- Wall after you ult. [21:40]
- Play more around your power spikes and don't go into fights with a massive bag. [21:40]
- Always prioritize waves, and don't look for fights in front of the wave when you are very fed. [21:40]
- Securing your own Sinner's Sacrifice is fine in solo queue, but invading and stealing theirs is essentially twice the value. [21:40]
- Late game, when the enemy has a Dynamo ult and you can't win a team fight, the onus is on you to look for a pick on a side lane with Yamato mid game or Wraith late game, with someone other than Kelvin. [22:10]
