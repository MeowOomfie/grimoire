# The Best Build To Hardcounter The Meta Right Now
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2025-11-30  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vfw8aqOH0tk

## Covers
Tank (green) Wraith build against a high burst death ball meta, Project Mind teleport maxed first, Card Trick lane start, Monster Rounds, Healing Rite, Weapon Shielding vs Spirit Shielding, Quicksilver Reload, 4.8k investment thresholds, Surge of Power, Fleetfoot, Tesla Bullets, Siphon Bullets, Toxic Bullets, Debuff Remover, Mercurial Magnum, Fortitude vs Veil Walker, Escalating Resilience, Capacitor, Leech, Spirit Resilience, Transcendent Cooldown, Spellslinger, farming waves, Sinner's Sacrifice and boxes over jungle, gold statue buffs, avoiding early Walker fights, principles for any hero

## Claims
- The current meta is dominated by high burst heroes (Mina, Pocket, Calico, Holliday, Paradox); his counter is a tank Wraith building all green items. [0:00]
- It is a death ball meta: one team fights the other in big 6v6, 5v5 or 4v4 fights for most of the game. [1:00]
- Jungle is much weaker: camps take longer to clear (especially for spirit heroes), deal more damage, have more spirit resist and a large HP increase, and pay less than lane creeps, which got a flat 10% per creep increase. [1:30]
- Fighting is encouraged because it is the best way to gain money; hard carries like Wraith are usually weak early, but every hero got stronger early, so itemizing to the 4.8k investment thresholds gives a lot more early damage. [2:02]
- Walkers barely die now, so you can let the enemy wave crash into your Walker, which is why games feel like everyone sits around the middle and fights. For carries, lane creeps pay more, boxes and gold statues are relatively better, and sinners got the 15% soul increase too; sinners hit harder, which he answers with lots of healing items. [2:32]
- Project Mind's cooldown was cut by about 10 seconds, so it is the ability to max first. [3:03]
- In lane start with cards (Card Trick) so you can do damage, max cards, put one point in Telekinesis, then max the teleport. [3:33]
- At a 14 second teleport this is the first meta where Project Mind is used to teleport in aggressively: it gives a barrier that scales well with spirit (500 to 600, about 800 late game), and it is usually off cooldown again when you want to disengage. [4:03]
- Maxing cards gives 50% faster charges and the slow, but that is less powerful than getting health twice, once going in and once going out. [4:33]
- Sinners deal about 80 per hit (four damaging hits, about 320 total, the last hit does no damage); teleporting in place before hitting them makes the shield take the damage instead of your health. [4:33]
- Laning build is standard Wraith: Monster Rounds to shove waves faster, then Healing Rite, which is crazy right now because health is harder to come by and fights last long. Healing Rite gives sprint speed, so you can proc it at full HP to get around the map faster, including on box runs. [5:33]
- Weapon Shielding over Spirit Shielding because it procs more easily in almost every lane: its threshold is 150 damage versus 225 for Spirit Shielding. [6:04]
- Only in rare cases where the enemy has two full spirit nukes and never shoots their gun should you still go Spirit Shielding. [6:34]
- Then the classic Quicksilver Reload; the first stat he takes to 4.8k is health, for the extra health and regen to farm sinners and stay alive. [6:34]
- Wraith is weakest in the early laning stage and the early mid game, so that is when she should avoid fighting and farm. [6:34]
- At 7.2k souls he is at about 1,600 health, which is high early. [7:06]
- Wraith's base gun damage is now quite okay and she is good with her gun early, so she is solid in lane. [8:09]
- At 8 minutes farm the first set of sinners; after lane, avoid the constant fighting until you have Surge of Power, Fleetfoot and Tesla Bullets. With all investments at 4.8k, cards deal about 200 damage each and weapon damage gets 49 extra. [8:40]
- Fleetfoot plus Healing Rite gives so much movement speed that you can rotate lane to lane and push waves deep into enemy territory, gaining map control. [9:11]
- Use the maxed teleport offensively to go lane to lane: shove a wave, teleport over, hit a sinner and boxes on the way, and shove the next wave. [9:41]
- Play very aggressive with this build: it lives, survives and pushes waves; knowing high ground teleport spots means you survive almost every situation. [10:11]
- Enemies have a hard time catching or bursting you because you are so fast, especially once Weapon Shielding procs and adds movement speed. [10:41]
- When three or four enemies chase you for long stretches, they either lose someone to your team, lose an objective elsewhere, or at worst kill you after wasting time they could have spent shoving waves. [10:41]
- It is still essential to shove creep waves in this meta, and this is the best build to shove with; you can shoot the wave while an enemy shoots you, then teleport out and regen. [11:11]
- Fortitude and Veil Walker are both strong for regen; he prefers Fortitude now for constant movement speed and regen. [11:42]
- Fortitude was bad last patch because he farmed jungle and camp damage interrupts it (13 seconds before healing resumes), but lane creeps and boxes do not interrupt it. [12:12]
- Farming priority: waves first, then sinners on both sides of the map (stealing enemy sinners is better), then boxes, and ignore jungle camps almost completely until about Mercurial Magnum (captioned "make real magnum"). [12:43]
- Wraith is strongest in the late mid game and late game; the longer the game goes, the better she gets, so even at even money she wins if she keeps up and farms. [13:14]
- Hitting many sinners and boxes stacks gold statue buffs, and Wraith benefits most from them because every stat helps a hybrid (gun damage, fire rate, cooldown, health, ammo, spirit power). [13:44]
- Wraith's spin speed scales with spirit power, so every spirit statue buff makes her faster on the map; she is the fastest hero to farm boxes, which used to be Calico before the cat form melee nerf. [14:16]
- The build gets really strong once Siphon Bullets and Toxic Bullets are added; teleporting in with them puts him on about 4,000 effective HP. [14:47]
- Siphon and Toxic both add up over time (Toxic does percentage damage over time, Siphon keeps draining while you shoot), so the longer you stay in a fight, the stronger they get; he has started fights at 3,000 HP and ended at 5,000. [15:18]
- Debuff Remover is a must in the current meta and core in this build, because tethers, silences and Slowing Hex stop your teleport; when the enemy buys slowing hexes or Focus Lens to catch you, debuff remove then teleport out. [15:49]
- After Debuff Remover he goes Mercurial, then starts ramping damage; until then in most Eternal 6 games he farms no creep camps, getting money from waves, sinners and gold statues. [16:51]
- You are not an AFK farmer leaving your team to fight 5v6: you push waves while they fight; until 20 minutes he avoids shooting Walkers because they are too tanky. Waves left at the enemy Walker cost the enemy that money if they do not clear them, so map control stays essential even though killing Walkers early is not. [17:21]
- With lots of bullet damage around, Escalating Resilience is a no-brainer, basically 30% bullet resist at all times. [17:51]
- Capacitor is a free upgrade from Tesla Bullets; not as good as before, but still a major shutdown tool because enemies without Debuff Remover cannot use their movement abilities. [18:22]
- After Leech you basically cannot die as long as you keep shooting. [18:22]
- Spirit Resilience is his number one situational item, bought when the enemy has spirit damage, usually before Leech; skip it in games that are mostly gun damage. [18:52]
- At Leech, around 40k, the game is usually over; in lower ranks where games run longer the build only gets stronger with luxury upgrades like Transcendent Cooldown and Spellslinger, eventually selling 3k items. [18:52]
- He rates Wraith the strongest late game carry right now, other than Warden. [19:23]
- Principle for any hero: be ready for team fights early by getting 4.8k in all three investment categories; he had 4.8k in each at 16k souls. [20:25]
- Avoid Walker fights at all costs right now until you are far ahead. If your team keeps dying pushing Walkers, get movement speed and tankiness and push out waves non-stop; solo waves pay a lot, then farm sinners and boxes. [20:55]
- Jungle holds you back even when it feels like money; even at 15k souls a tier three camp takes too long. [20:55]
- A full sinner buff is worth roughly 400 to 500 souls in stats (per his friend Ranger, a coach), lasts the whole game, takes no item slot and stacks infinitely. [21:55]
- Play aggressive and do not be scared of dying when you build tanky and have escapes, like Wraith teleport or Warden with Willpower maxed for the overshield, or Fleetfoot. [22:26]
- If the build is not working, you are fighting too early, not farming efficiently, or not teleporting to absorb damage from sinners and enemies and shoving waves into the enemy side. [22:56]
- Fight with your team if it is close; if your team is on the other side of the map, push waves first, then farm boxes and sinners, and ignore jungle unless you can clear it fast. [23:26]
- His hyper carry Wraith build is still good, but it lacks the early tankiness to survive Wraith's weak mid game; the tank build skips that weakness and goes straight to the late mid game. [23:56]
