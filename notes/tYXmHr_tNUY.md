# You'll Master The Laning Phase, Just Give Me 11 Minutes | Deadlock Laning Guide
channel: Shotty  date: 2026-03-23  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tYXmHr_tNUY

## Covers
Winning solo queue games in the first 10 minutes: early wave focus and few trades, kills vs souls, playing your spikes (Lady Geist and Mystic Regeneration, Apollo), roam early roam often, box routes while roaming, melee into parry mistakes, the 2, 5 and 8 minute timers (tier one camp, bridge buffs, tier three camps, Sinner's Sacrifice), freezing the wave for a dead teammate, carry takes wave while support runs boxes (Lady Geist, Kelvin), never die for the Guardian, assume a roam once a Guardian falls

## Claims
- Most solo queue games are lost in the first 10 minutes, even though Deadlock has many comeback mechanics and you can catch up by farming and playing your life. [0:00]
- In the early laning phase, especially the first 3 minutes, focus the wave as much as possible, minimize trades and try not to take too much damage. [1:31]
- A common failure is not buying healing and losing two trades, then hiding behind your tower praying they do not dive and waiting for the healing minion. [1:31]
- Early in lane kills do not actually matter as long as you are not falling behind in souls; dying in the first 3 minutes does not do a lot. [2:01]
- Early on, just get as many souls as possible, confirm, deny and wait, because every hero has different spikes. [2:01]
- On Lady Geist he would not fight until he had at least Mystic Regeneration, because nine times out of ten (especially before her self damage nerfs) he would do more damage to himself than to the enemy. [2:31]
- On Apollo, once you put points into your 3, you just dive people and they die. [3:02]
- Playing your spikes means learning when your hero gets strong, for example the moment an item gives enough spirit power to raise your 2's scaling, then going to fight. [3:02]
- You find your damage spikes through games: you suddenly take half a health bar and remember you just bought Berserker or something else. [3:33]
- When you hit your damage spikes, roam early and roam often. [3:33]
- Often every lane loses except yours, partly your fault if you sit at 10k farm in green lane while everyone else is at 6k doing nothing for them. [4:03]
- Instead cycle: at 5 minutes take the bridge buff, go help mid, maybe go to yellow, then boost back to spawn and reset in your lane; this keeps your farm up as long as you do not die rotating, and stops teammates losing their lanes. [4:03]
- Roaming this way, he got every Guardian or close to it in almost every game on the way to Ascendant. [4:33]
- Do not melee when the enemy has everything to gain from it: hitting a two health enemy with a light melee instead of one more bullet lets their desperate parry kill you. [5:03]
- In higher rank games everyone parries off the sound, often without seeing you, so even jumping out of a veil into a melee charge gets parried and you go from full health to dead. [5:34]
- Simple roam routes to or from mid lane pass boxes and statues worth 150, 300 or 400 souls just from walking over. [6:05]
- Every roam is a risk: you miss a wave (some of the best souls in the game), and your lane partner may lose the guardian or get dove while you are gone. [6:05]
- When roaming, take boxes on the way without wasting time and make it as quick as possible: get in on an overextended enemy at the mid tower, kill him, and jump back. [6:36]
- A roam should usually cost one wave, sometimes two, which is not the end of the world, especially since you can tax some of the wave in the lane you visit. [6:36]
- Know three times: 2, 5 and 8 minutes. [7:07]
- At 2 minutes the first tier one camp spawns: the small minions right next to your guardian in mid lane and yellow lane, and on the enemy side in green lane (he says he may have that backwards). [7:07]
- At 5 minutes the bridge buffs spawn (between mid lane and each side lane, shown by a map symbol), and respawn every 5 minutes after. [7:37]
- Bridge buffs are big damage spikes, especially early; getting a gun or spirit buff early can turn a losing lane into a favorable fight and a lane win. [7:37]
- At 8 minutes tier three camps and Sinner's Sacrifice spawn, so if you are getting Cultist Sacrifice or similar, have it by then. [8:08]
- Sinner's Sacrifices are the vending machines inside each lane's jungle; wave gives the most souls in the game and Sinner's Sacrifice the second most, so take it every time it comes up, which also gives permanent buffs. [8:08]
- If your lane partner dies, freeze the wave for him: let the enemy kill their wave and leave the wave you would kill pushed into their guardian to be killed by the tower, so you do not steal your partner's farm. [8:39]
- While waiting you should still confirm and deny; if they go for a dive on you, kill the wave, but otherwise wait a few seconds so your partner can zip back and take all the souls. [9:09]
- Sometimes taking the wave is fine, like a carry Lady Geist killing the wave for full souls while her friend Kelvin kills all the boxes on his way back to the guardian, but that needs coordination most teammates will not have. [9:09]
- Everybody gets this wrong: the Guardian does not matter, so let them have it and do not die for it, since if they kill you they get it anyway; if you cannot clear the wave, leave it. [10:09]
- Once any Guardian falls, assume a permanent roam, because the player who lost it usually sits back at the Walker and will not see who is missing. [10:09]
- When you lose your Guardian, call to your team that they may be roaming now. [10:39]
