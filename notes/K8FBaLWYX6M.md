# I HAD to Make a Video on This Patch
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-10-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K8FBaLWYX6M

## Covers
Coach's critical take on the 2025-10-25 balance patch: the new anti-vent-escape mechanic, Mystic Shot, Capacitor, Reactive Barrier, shielding items, Trophy Collector, Majestic Leap, Spirit Snatch and Silence Wave (resist shred), Ethereal Shift, and hero changes to Calico, The Doorman, Dynamo, Infernus, Kelvin, McGinnis, Paradox, Seven, Victor and Warden. For players judging what is still strong after the patch.

## Claims
- He hears from people under NDA that the next major patch (December) is worth the wait, but this balancing patch is rough. [0:00]
- A new mechanic appears meant to discourage using vents in a fight to escape; his initial reaction is negative, since juking around vents was fun, though he sees it let people be too slippery. [0:31]
- Mystic Shot nerfed; it needed it but it is not enough. Mathematically it does the most damage per soul of any item if you hit it, and he will still buy it first or second on Holliday. [1:02]
- Capacitor got +20 seconds cooldown on the active, a relatively big nerf; the active is insane, it can even cleanse bridge buffs. [1:33]
- He would prefer Capacitor be reworked to depend less on fire rate: it is a hard wall where you must build it to be a decent M1, which locks slower fire rate M1s out of the meta. [2:04]
- Capacitor cleanses Seven's Power Surge, which walls Seven completely out of the meta. [2:04]
- He hopes Capacitor becomes a niche item, like a gun Bebop capacitoring off a Viscous cube after a hook, not a meta defining one; this nerf is not enough to change that, and it still defines builds for heroes like Infernus. [2:35]
- Reactive Barrier got a 2 second cooldown increase, objectively not enough (it still has 2.1 spirit scaling), but it is almost solely keeping Abrams and Bebop in check, so a big nerf would unleash them; he doesn't want to lane against Abrams or a good hooking Bebop without it. [3:05]
- Reactive Barrier is one of the best defensive items for its price, and depending on the enemy comp the best defensive item in the game. [3:36]
- Spirit Shielding and Weapon Shielding: a little less base but better scaling; overall a nerf. There are 33 boons in total, so boon scaling matters most at max level. Nobody builds Weapon Shielding, so this is about Spirit Shielding, a fair nerf. [3:36]
- Trophy Collector souls per minute increased; there is no Cultist Sacrifice nerf in the patch. [4:06]
- He views Trophy Collector more favorably than most high level players: if your average game is over 40 minutes, it isn't a troll buy, which matters for lower elo players with long games. [4:06]
- Trophy Collector's problem is that it incentivizes fighting without giving any stats to fight with; he would add an offensive stat or scaling. [4:38]
- Trophy Collector Lash is now one of the top Lash builds, but he fears a Majestic Leap rush Lash much more, since a Trophy Collector Lash is sinking about 3k souls while saving for an early Boundless Spirit would be scarier. [5:09]
- Majestic Leap got +4 seconds cooldown, which he mocks as not enough: Pocket will keep rushing it and Lash keep buying it. The barrier is a major issue, and even +12 seconds cooldown with a gutted barrier might leave it overtuned. [5:40]
- Spirit Snatch and Silence Wave nerfs are a slap on the wrist; fights against Spirit Snatch rarely last more than 5 seconds, let alone its 14 second duration. [6:10]
- The core issue with Spirit Snatch and Silence Wave is resistance shred, which is fundamentally overpowered; the Silence Wave cooldown increase is good but doesn't address it. [6:41]
- He wants to believe Valve is stalling for a resist shred rework, but finds it hard to believe they understand how broken resist shred is; Spirit Snatch's 14 second duration was too long but the bonus damage was not the core issue. [7:12]
- Ethereal Shift got a long cooldown increase (25 seconds); he thinks it was a strong, overbought item that needed it, and it will probably push the item back into irrelevance until a later patch. [7:12]
- Calico: Gloom Bombs get a longer cooldown, less base damage and less spirit scaling; the multi-bomb damage ratio changed. He still thinks she will be absurd, at most going from high A to high B tier. [8:12]
- A main driver of Calico's one-shotting is Spirit Snatch and resist shred, not her kit; Ava no longer replenishing stamina is fine but not the major issue. [8:42]
- The Doorman: headshots now deal 25% extra damage instead of the normal 50%, and boon growth reduced; exactly the change he predicted and would have made, rather than shrinking the bullet hitbox. [9:12]
- The Doorman: Call Bell explosion damage increased; spirit Doorman may have needed a buff in solo queue, but his lane is already too strong. [9:42]
- The Doorman tier two Bell is the strongest lane spike in the game, +80 damage if he hits it, and his lane is among the strongest, like Yamato's; he wanted the tier two Bell cut by about 10 to 20 explosion damage. [9:42]
- Dynamo: his 2 buff (fire rate) won't do much, and Rejuvenating Aura cooldown -3 seconds; not enough for a hero that has been nearly unplayable for about four months. [10:42]
- Infernus: -0.04 bullet scaling per boon, a hefty gun nerf; Flame Dash tier two damage reduced again (it still does too much every patch); Afterburn spirit damage and DPS reduced. [11:13]
- Correction while editing: meta Infernus builds little weapon damage and mostly capitalizes on investment damage, so the base bullet nerf is only a few damage per bullet. [12:14]
- Infernus builds lots of fire rate to play around Capacitor being overpowered and builds Cultist Sacrifice, which also gives fire rate; he expects Infernus to stay the best M1, at worst high A tier. [12:45]
- Wraith is the next best M1, at best B tier, and he doubts she can test Infernus. [13:17]
- Kelvin: Frozen Shelter (the dome) got +20 seconds cooldown, but the real fix everyone has asked for is making the dome breakable. [13:17]
- Kelvin's dome is fundamentally unbalanced: fire rate slow, actual slow, insane healing, unbreakable, and it isolates whoever you want; besides Split Shot through the dome there is little way to beat it. His grenades may also be a little overtuned. [13:48]
- McGinnis: one extra charge on Medicinal Specter; he sees it as a token buff. [14:18]
- Paradox: base bullet damage and swap cooldown nerfed, but she is still Paradox. In pro play Paradox and Holliday kept each other in check; the major Holliday nerf plus a general gun buff effectively buffed Paradox. [14:48]
- Paradox needs drastic changes: +10 to 15 seconds on swap instead of 5, lower Kinetic Carbine scaling and bullet scaling; she does too much damage and utility, and her wall is absurd. She will still be picked first round in pro play. [15:18]
- Seven: Lightning Ball scaling increased, but Seven only returns to the meta if Capacitor leaves it, and this Capacitor nerf isn't enough. [16:18]
- Victor: Pain Battery tier one and tier two swapped; the new tier one gives a roughly 40% movement slow and the new tier two the extra 40 or 50 damage. Good, it hurts his early laning, and a 40% slow was a weird tier two spike. [16:48]
- Victor: his "Stinky" ability base and tier two damage nerfed, but that wasn't the issue; his build centers on his 1. Losing Ethereal Shift hurts him, but he may only drop from S+ to S tier, with absurd regen and the same mid and late game. [17:19]
- Warden got a small buff; Binding Word probably didn't need more range. [18:21]
