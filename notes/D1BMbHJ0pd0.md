# Deadlock Has a Serious Problem (And It’s Getting Worse)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-11-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D1BMbHJ0pd0

## Covers
Mostly an opinion piece on hero balance in late 2025: the coach argues balance is tuned to low-rank win rates, leaving a spirit and early-game nuke meta at high rank while gun carries get nerfed.
Useful facts inside: which heroes the coach rates strong at high rank (Pocket, Mina, Paradox, Kelvin, Shiv, Abrams, Calico), how long high-rank vs low-rank games last, what a carry is, team composition by game phase, and Spirit Resilience vs Bullet Resilience win rates. For mid to high rank players picking heroes.

## Claims
- A game's balance looks very different in low and middle ranks than at high rank, so win rates alone mislead. [0:30]
- Pocket sits at a 48% win rate with a 33% pick rate across all ranks, yet at high rank he has performed at the top of all heroes for about a year, with consistent play in pro play and Eternus. [4:35]
- Once a player learns Pocket's kit he is very oppressive: he can nuke you in a second and, played properly, almost never dies because of his many escape abilities. [5:05]
- Nukers are heroes that deal most of their damage in a very short period; the current high-rank meta, and the one since the patch with the new heroes, is a nuke meta of heroes that kill you in a second with little danger to themselves, such as Pocket, Mina and Paradox. [5:36]
- Paradox is currently considered one of the most broken heroes by pro players. [6:07]
- McGinnis shows as the strongest hero in Phantom to Eternus by win rate but arguably underperforms in almost every rank; her win rate comes from ease of use, since you throw a turret and the healing pad and get your value without much skill. [6:07]
- Easy heroes for new players: McGinnis and Haze (Haze suits players from shooters like Overwatch, Marvel Rivals or Counter-Strike), and Mo & Krill for players from Dota or League with weak aim. [6:39]
- Newer heroes such as The Doorman and Mina need a base understanding of the game; Mina without decent movement is very squishy and dies easily. [7:41]
- At high rank Mina usually has the highest net worth in the lobby: she can almost always win her lane against any matchup, is one of the strongest laners, performs very well through the early, middle and late mid game, and only falls off very late. [8:14]
- Most high-rank games end within 25 to 30 minutes, so heroes strong in the early game are better there because the late game often never arrives. [9:16]
- At high rank the late game effectively starts after about 20 minutes: respawn times are so long that a team wipe gives a free Mid Boss and the Patron, and a good team finishes the game in the next fight. [9:49]
- In low ranks up to Ascendant games almost always last 30 minutes or more because players do not know how to end games efficiently, and the longer a game goes the stronger carry heroes become. [10:19]
- A carry is a hero that benefits much more from getting a lot of souls, unrelated to spirit or gun damage: Wraith always has another item that adds a lot of damage, while Dynamo tops out after Warp Stone, Refresher (two Black Holes per fight) and some spirit items such as Mystic Reverb on his stomp, and will not outduel a late Wraith. [10:49]
- A good team composition has heroes strong early, some strong in the mid game and at least one late game carry, because with no late game power you simply lose once the game gets late against a team with lots of damage. [11:51]
- At high rank recently, if your team does not have five or six heroes strong in lane or the mid game you will lose, whereas in low rank two late game carries are welcome because games last so long. [12:21]
- Recent patches hard nerfed late game heroes such as Seven and Infernus; Infernus is still one of the only playable carries at high rank, and Ivy can maybe be played as a carry. [12:52]
- In the latest patch Paradox, Mina and Calico got only slight nerfs while the gun carries Infernus and Ivy were hard nerfed; Victor and Infernus were the only heroes notably changed. [13:54]
- Abrams is performing very well right now because he is tanky enough not to die to burst damage and is very easy to play. [14:56]
- At high rank the roster feels split: you pick an early game spirit or gun nuker like Paradox or you lose, and gun heroes are currently the unplayable side. [15:26]
- Spirit Resilience has an above 50% win rate on any hero at any rank while Bullet Resilience is below 50%, about a 7 to 8 point gap, which indicates spirit nuke damage is more prevalent than gun damage right now. [17:59]
- Gun damage mostly matters only in the super late game, which low-rank players see more because their games last long. [18:32]
- Many original gun heroes now play mostly spirit: Mirage's common build is full or heavy spirit with lots of damage from Djinn's Mark; Wraith's most viable style is about 80% plus spirit damage from her cards and Capacitor (a gun item that deals spirit damage); Billy, Infernus and Vyper have moved toward spirit, and Vyper now one-shots with her bola and poison. [19:32]
- Seven played with Power Surge is about hitting your gunshots, even though it is technically spirit damage. [21:05]
- In high-rank games with no draft, having too many of the lower rated heroes on your team means you almost always lose. [22:05]
- The top of the coach's tier list is heroes that one-shot you very fast no matter how many anti-resist items you buy, or that are very tanky like Kelvin and Shiv, or both (Shiv); Infernus is the one exception who dominates later in the game. [22:37]
- Viscous, Haze and Billy are possibly rated a bit low on the coach's tier list. [22:05]
- Vindicta at high rank builds her snipe with Rapid Recharge and kills you because the snipe does around 800 damage at minute 10. [25:11]
- Mina will feel overpowered once you learn to play her even with a 49% win rate; played badly she feeds. [26:12]
- If you play Seven, Drifter or Sinclair at a decent rank you would rather have Mina, Shiv, Kelvin or Paradox on your team than McGinnis or Vyper. [27:42]
- Kelvin has outperformed everyone for months because his dome saves you and your team, and enemies trapped in the dome with more than two of your teammates will die. [28:44]
- The coach plays Wraith at high rank by hiding from everyone and permanently farming, avoiding fights, then coming out of the jungle at 20 to 30 minutes to kill everyone. [30:47]
- If Pocket or Mina dominates for 20 to 30 minutes and then loses to a late Wraith, the loss came from failing to finish the game with the big lead, not from Wraith being overpowered. [32:18]
- "Just buy Spellbreaker" does not work at high rank: games do not last long enough to afford it, a good Mina wins lane and the mid game, takes Mid Boss and ends before you get it, and rushing Spellbreaker first leaves a damage hero doing no damage. [32:49]
