## Covers
Holliday coaching by Midknight: one Emissary Holliday player reviewed over a full game (lane partner Seven, enemy Pocket and Yamato in lane).
Themes: air strafing and bounce pad movement, playing behind the front line and throwing barrels, lasso-up hunting versus lasso-down farming, Rusted Barrel and Mystic Shot timing, Counterspell, Rift setups, side lane time and grouping six mid.
Patch date 2026-08-12.

## Claims
Player 1 (Holliday, Emissary):
- Extra Charge is a great buy when the enemy Pocket is dead to two barrels, since it immediately gives you two barrels to throw. [1:01]
- With boxes spawning at 3 minutes and comeback souls this strong, kills in the first 3 minutes barely matter: a 400-soul kill comes back to the enemy in one wave of comeback souls. [1:31]
- Before 3 minutes, hitting the wave and throwing barrels at the enemy laners is correct. [2:02]
- At 3 minutes, with pressure and the enemy Pocket at 1 HP, steal the enemy boxes. [2:02]
- When invading for boxes on Holliday, take the ones on the roof rather than indoors; indoors is scary, and in the open you can bounce pad away if anything happens. [2:02]
- Ground roll is not the most efficient movement; look for something like an edge boost instead. [2:33]
- Grab the golden statue; people always forget that one. [2:33]
- Do not shoot the enemy tier one respawn from long range while box routing; the damage is not worth giving away the information that you are still routing. [2:33]
- Do not trade with the Pocket once he has full amp stacks, or you get nuked in the trade; amp is OP. [4:04]
- Do not commit to a trade while your lane partner Seven is back farming; it is a 2v1. [4:04]
- Wave cutting is really good; top teams cut each other's waves before objectives like the Rift. [5:06]
- Do not wave cut during the laning stage, because you grief your lane partner, who splits souls with you; laning is a 2v2. [5:06]
- After the tower melees the creeps, pull them behind the tower, because the Pocket would otherwise just insta-clear at the tower. [6:39]
- The air strafing attempts are good but slow: you are not moving your camera fast enough. [6:39]
- Rush your purple items, because with max barrel and Rapid Recharge you kill people with one barrel. [7:09]
- Even unmaxed, one barrel (145 plus Mystic Burst plus the damage amp) does about 20 to 25% of the Pocket's HP; with max barrel it becomes a one shot with a double melee. [7:39]
- Holliday's bounce pad gives 100% air control, so stay in the air as long as you can. [8:42]
- Air dashing is not an efficient use of stamina; double jump and keep air strafing, unless you need to break line of sight or get momentum immediately. [8:42]
- Ideally wall bounce so you do not spend stamina, or wall bounce plus air jump. [8:42]
- You got poked down early by tanking the wave and letting the Yamato poke you. [10:16]
- Getting max barrel is a big spike. [10:46]
- Do not start a Rift at 500 HP unless teammates are there with you. [10:46]
- In fights your job is to play at the back, bounce pad around and hurl barrels like Donkey Kong. [10:46]
- If enemies run at you, you need to spring twice, so always keep a bounce pad charge up. [10:46]
- Put down two bounce pads behind the enemy and bounce between them while throwing barrels. [10:46]
- With lasso down it is fine to farm, but once lasso is up, play really actively. [11:17]
- Rusted Barrel came out really late; if you buy it, it must be one of your first few buys in lane. [11:51]
- With Rusted Barrel as your spirit spike and no Surge of Power in his build, Extra Spirit is not needed for the spike, but since you go Boundless Spirit eventually it is still a good buy; skipping it is also fine. [12:21]
- Melee boxes instead of sliding into them; sliding slows you down. [12:21]
- Holliday's loop is simple: lasso up, play aggressively, hunt in their jungle or catch someone on a side lane, get a kill. [12:51]
- Lasso down: play slow, hit camps, run boxes (roofs work), or scout to give your team information. [13:22]
- Mystic Shot came out very late; he moved it much earlier in his build, before the spirit spike. [13:22]
- Mystic Shot is about 500 damage a bullet and does big damage in lane too; he used to overrate gun spikes. [13:53]

- You should have used the vent there. [15:23]
