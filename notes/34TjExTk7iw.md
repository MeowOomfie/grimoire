# Deadlock's New Build System Is Actually Broken
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-11-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=34TjExTk7iw

## Covers
The late November patch's new investment system, where the big bonus moved from 9.6k to 4.8k per category, and how to build around it: hybrid builds that reach 4.8k in gun, vitality and spirit, example builds for Grey Talon, Lady Geist, Billy, Ivy, Warden and Wraith, the one-shot Focus Lens Infernus, and which items rose or fell (Curse, Cultus, shielding items, Capacitor, Headhunter, Opening Rounds).
For players adapting their builds to the new system before the strongest combos get nerfed.

## Claims
- The patch was only a 10% gun buff and a 10% nerf to upgraded spirit abilities; the real change is the investment tabs. [0:30]
- The big investment return used to come at 9.6k in one category; Valve cut it in half, so the best value is now at 4.8k and everything after is diminishing returns. [0:30]
- 99% of the roster wants 4.8k in every category very early; by 16k souls a working build should have 4.8k investment in all three. [1:03]
- Weapon: 20% weapon damage at 3.2k, and one more 1.6k tier two item more than doubles it to 49%. [1:36]
- Spirit: 19 spirit power at 3.2k, and another 1.6k item (for example Slowing Hex) adds up to 45 bonus spirit. [1:36]
- Vitality is even better, about 34% of base health extra. [2:06]
- Pick which category to max first by hero, but almost every hero should reach at least 4.8k in each. [2:06]
- Even Pocket, a spirit nuker, should get early gun investment to 4.8k; the most popular Pocket build reaches 4.8k in every category. [2:38]
- Cultus got better on Pocket because it now gives weapon damage instead of fire rate, a buff on heroes that shoot only a few shots between abilities. [2:38]
- Heroes that already benefit from every stat, like Wraith and Lady Geist, are even better; it is a hybrid meta. [4:11]
- Lady Geist is crazy this patch and so is Billy. [4:11]
- Grey Talon got a lot better: max the arrows almost instantly in lane and reach the 45 extra spirit very early with Improved Spirit and Rapid Recharge. [4:42]
- Talon lane example: Improved Spirit, Rapid Recharge, Monster Rounds to farm and some regen; at 6.4k souls he has 74 spirit power. [5:13]
- At 6,400 souls Talon's arrows do about 322 damage each and can one-shot any hero; early Rapid Recharge plus Improved Spirit on Grey Talon is the best power spike in the game right now. [5:43]
- Lady Geist: build last patch's build, with the lane regen item the captions call "radiant regen", and add a spirit item you would buy anyway, now Suppressor. [6:14]
- Suppressor is crazy good in and after lane because every hero does more gun damage, so reducing enemy gun damage is very valuable. [6:14]
- With Suppressor added Lady Geist reaches 62 spirit power at 6.4k, and with the build structured right reaches the other 4.8k investments very fast; she is one of the top three heroes now. [6:44]
- Billy: remove Backstabber from the early build and add a bullet resist shredder item; after lane he is at 4.8k vitality and spirit and the next two items reach the weapon investment. [7:46]
- Weapon investment is extra strong on Billy because melee damage scales with weapon investment stats. [8:17]
- Billy at 14k is the strongest hero in the game, wins any 1v1, and sits at about 2,500 max health without Blasted procs, with lots of resist; at 16k his slams do 250 to 300 damage. [8:47]
- Number one rule: build items from all three categories that make sense on your hero, for example melee damage plus survivability on Billy, Radiant regen for sustain and Kinetic Dash for fire rate on Lady Geist. [9:19]
- Ivy, played full gun at high level last patch (Lightbringers style), should keep the gun build but sprinkle in one or two early spirit items for the extra spike. [9:49]
- Experimental Ivy: Quicksilver Reload, then Surge of Power and Stone Form; Stone Form got a lower cooldown and better spirit scaling, does 250 damage every 18 seconds and lines up well with Surge of Power's cooldown, then build more gun later. [10:20]
- Spending 4,800 on items that suit your hero and give spirit power is a no-brainer even on gun builds. [10:50]
- Warden is the exception that delays spirit: Alchemical Flask only scales 0.7 with spirit, Willpower doesn't scale until leveled later, Binding Word can't be relied on to hit, and the ultimate gets stronger later. [11:21]
- On Warden rush gun investment first (80% extra weapon damage, up from about 58% before), then health investment, and only later two spirit items. [11:51]
- Warden's later spirit items: a bullet resist shredder and Superior Duration; at 20k with 68 spirit power Willpower gives a 500 barrier and the ultimate lasts very long. [12:22]
- Build crafting now: don't always take the single best item; take the best item while filling the 4.8k niche in spirit, gun and vitality. Gun first is fine if you like shooting early. [12:54]
- Green Wraith: same build with a super early Surge of Power and Quicksilver Reload; at 11k her cards do 200 damage each, very high for cards. [13:25]
- Focus Lens went from one of the worst items to probably the most broken item: it now upgrades from Spirit Sap and gets Spirit Sap's whole bonus effect, so the spirit resist shred is on top of the old Focus Lens. [13:57]
- Focus Lens is crazy on Haze, but Infernus is probably its best user. [14:28]
- One-shot Infernus (likely to be nerfed within days): play lane normally, get Superior Duration and Toxic Bullets (buffed to last longer), then Focus Lens. [14:58]
- Superior Duration extends Focus Lens to a 5.4 second silence and Toxic Bullets to 5 seconds. [15:28]
- Combo: walk up, Focus Lens, burn, throw Napalm, run through with Flame Dash; anyone around the same net worth dies in a free 1v1 kill, even a tanky Abrams with Spirit Snatch, Enchanter's Emblem, shielding and Fortitude. [15:59]
- Focus Lens silences for about 4.25 seconds on base and shreds spirit resist, so the target can't fight back; great on anyone, best on Infernus, also great on Haze. Abuse it before it is nerfed. [16:59]
- Curse got much better: it gets the old Capacitor-style effect, so besides silence and disarm it interrupts everything and removes all buffs, for example Metal Skin or Viscous's cube. [17:30]
- Curse is the "you" button against anyone annoying, a meta item, not as broken as Focus Lens because it does less damage, likely nerfed later; it also gives 50% weapon damage so it fits gun heroes. [18:01]
- Cultus is now weaker on fire rate heroes, so don't buy it on Ivy, Haze, Wraith or Infernus; buy it on Billy, Pocket and Mina, who don't need fire rate. It seems designed for heroes who don't farm much to keep up, not as a farming item. [18:32]
- Jungle does a lot more damage and is harder to farm, so jungle overall got worse. [19:02]
- Sinner's Sacrifice punches for about 80 damage now and was also nerfed, but takes the same time to farm, so it is the most efficient money after lane creeps and even more important. [19:35]
- Regen is even more important, so Healing Rite is stronger: activate it while farming sinners on any hero. [19:35]
- Shielding items are still great, mostly bought early in lane; buy Weapon Shielding first since everyone does more gun damage and it almost always procs when starting a fight. [20:06]
- Both shielding proc thresholds were increased (Weapon Shielding's probably still 225); the barrier amount and out of combat regen are the same. [20:36]
- Capacitor is still great on Wraith, Haze and Ivy even though its damage (and Tesla Bullets') was reduced: it now silences stamina use and slows for 5 seconds so they can't dash. For Infernus, Focus Lens is probably better. [20:36]
- Burst damage was nerfed overall: Headhunter is a bit worse because its flat bonus (75 headshot damage at level one, then scaling) matters less now that weapon investment is easier. [21:38]
- Items that add damage to every bullet, like Hollow Point, are better now; Mystic Shot got worse and was also nerfed. [22:08]
- Opening Rounds got better: a slight buff to 8 spirit power plus a flat damage increase on every bullet. [22:08]
- Gun heroes are strong: try Billy, Lady Geist, Warden and the Focus Lens Infernus before it is nerfed; Infernus is a bit weaker without it, and Warden is insane as a carry. [22:40]
- Lanes changed drastically and need a separate guide. [23:11]
