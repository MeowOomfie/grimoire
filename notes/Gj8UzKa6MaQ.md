## Covers
First-look quick start guide for Nurse Harrow, the new support and gun DPS hybrid who heals allies with her gun. Goes through each ability and its upgrades, the ability 2 into ult combo, two ways to play her (pocket healer or leech and ult max) and starting items. For players picking her up on release.

## Claims
- Nurse Harrow is a support and DPS hybrid: her gun deals damage and also gives big healing to the whole team. He rates her ult in the top four in the game. [0:00]
- Her gun is a fast-firing needle gun with headshot and body shot damage; gun damage seems all right at level 1. [0:00]
- Ability 1, Leech Therapy: throw leeches at the ground that attach to enemies and deal spirit damage over time; once full, a leech crawls toward you, with about an 8 m pickup range. [0:32]
- You can hold up to 10 leech stacks; each gives health regen and 5% fire rate, so 10 stacks is 50% fire rate. [1:02]
- Leeches also remove enemy buffs, for example Unstoppable or Surge of Power, damage amp and other buffs. [1:02]
- Leech Therapy upgrades add stamina drain and healing on pickup equal to the damage the enemy took, so more spirit means more healing. Leeches do not heal right away, only once you pick them up. [1:33]
- Max Leech Therapy has higher scaling and adds 8 more stacks, up to 18 total, which is about 90% fire rate plus a lot of regen. [2:05]
- On death you lose 25% of your max stacks, rounded down (2 of 10), so stay alive on this hero. [2:05]
- The level 2 Leech Therapy upgrade gives 4 spirit power per leech. [2:35]
- She starts with zero base regen, so get leech stacks early because you will not heal in lane otherwise; the suggested build includes Extra Regen. [3:05]
- Ability 2 (a stim): cast on an ally or yourself to speed up every action (faster shooting, jumping, dashing) and take zero damage during it. [3:05]
- When ability 2 ends, the crash hits: you take all the damage you took during it plus 30% extra, and you are slowed. [3:35]
- If caught, self-cast ability 2 to survive the burst, but you will die afterwards unless you have healing. [3:35]
- The crash cannot be cleansed or dispelled and goes through Ethereal Shift, so you cannot use Ethereal Shift to live through it. [4:07]
- During ability 2 you can take effectively infinite damage, like a cheat death, and a teammate under it also receives more healing. [4:38]
- Ability 3, Healing Needle: fills your magazine with healing needles to shoot allies for healing; it runs out after about two magazines. [4:38]
- Healing needle charges recharge by shooting enemies, like Wraith's cards, so you alternate between healing teammates and shooting enemies. [5:12]
- With Healing Needle active you cannot damage enemies; recast it to go back to normal shooting. [5:42]
- Healing Needle works with Ricochet, so after buying Ricochet later it heals your whole team; that is why it cannot heal and damage at once. [5:42]
- Healing Needle upgrades: more healing, level 2 recharges stamina, max level gives better spirit scaling and extends allies' non-ultimate buffs by 6% duration per shot. [6:12]
- Shooting an ally's buff with Healing Needle can keep it up; he thinks you can keep an ally's Unstoppable active at all times. [6:12]
- Ability 3 makes her the best single target healer and pocket tool in the game, especially combined with ability 2's healing boost on the target. [6:42]
- Pocket combo: when your ally is about to die, cast ability 2 on them so they take no damage, then out-heal the incoming damage with Healing Needle. [7:12]
- Ultimate, Lurking Terrors: she opens her bag and everyone in front of her takes spirit damage over time; enemies looking at her cannot use stamina or movement abilities. [7:44]
- At max terror stacks enemies are forced to run away and cannot do anything or turn around during it, so it works like a stun on the whole enemy team if they jump you. [8:14]
- Lurking Terrors covers a very large cone in front of you even without upgrades; enemies looking away still take half damage. [8:14]
- The last ult upgrade makes you immune while channeling, and the ult lasts 0.1 extra seconds per leech stack. [8:44]
- With max leech stacks plus Superior Duration it may be the longest lasting ultimate in the game, longer than Bebop's laser or McGinnis's turrets. [9:17]
- Combo in a 1v6: cast ability 2 on yourself, take any amount of damage, then ult before the crash; in testing 300,000 damage was wiped to zero by the immune ult. [9:47]
- The intended way to play her is pocketing your strongest teammate: they go in, give them ability 2 and heal them with ability 3 to keep them alive. [10:18]
- His alternative, more fun plan: max Leech Therapy, then max the ultimate, and roam around getting kills. [10:48]
- Build items he will definitely buy: Arcane Surge (captioned "K'Sante search"), which makes leeches last longer with bigger AoE and more spirit and also lengthens and strengthens the ult; later Superior Cooldown and Superior Duration. [10:48]
- The ult has a long cooldown, 2 minutes even at max, which is why Superior Cooldown is in the build. [11:19]
- The hard part of the leech plan is applying leeches and then getting into pickup range; leech crawl range scales with her level. [11:19]
- Ability order: get two points in Leech Therapy early to spam it; it has a 30 second cooldown at level 1. [11:50]
- Ability 2 is hit or miss early: you can only sustain through the crash if you have healing after, so self-casting without healing kills you; on an ally you can heal them after with ability 3. [11:50]
- Ability 2 at max level is a free dispel of non-ultimate debuffs, for example cleansing a stun from Seven. [12:21]
