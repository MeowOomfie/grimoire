# How to Build and Play Drifter | Oracle Drifter Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-08-28
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cAyhK4EWsyM

## Covers
Drifter coaching review by Midknight (former top 10 Mirage, free coach) of an Oracle-rank Drifter player in an Emissary lobby.
A 2025 video, recorded the day after Drifter's release (late August 2025 patch, Victor due that Friday).
Topics: Drifter gun falloff range, ability 2 (teleport) usage, target priority, spacing, the ult, item build and max order.

## Claims
- Drifter's bullets are deleted after 27 m, the only hero like that, but his weapon falloff only starts at 24 m. [1:02]
- Almost every hero's falloff starts at 20 m (Sinclair 25 m; Viper about 13 m, Grey Talon about 18 m), so taking trades from 20 to 24 m gives Drifter a big advantage. [1:33]
- Play around just over 20 m the way Sinclair abuses his 20 to 25 m range, unless you are against Sinclair. [2:33]
- Drifter's gun is forgiving: he gets a flat amount of damage as long as one pellet hits. [2:33]
- Dying in the sewers while routing is greedy; a Doorman door behind you makes that sewer route unpunishable. [2:33]
- Drifter's ability 1 counts as a light melee, so it procs Spirit Strike and melee lifesteal; melee lifesteal is decent lane sustain. [3:04]
- If you box route, expect your lane partner to shove unless you told them on mic not to, and decide whether pushing the wave is worth it on that basis. [3:34]
- Ability 2 can teleport you from one lane to another in about two seconds, and you can teleport to creeps from far away (cross-map lineups, including onto towers). [4:04]
- Do not take a fight in front of your own wave against a lane duo that is only about 500 souls down; their wave hits your back and you get punished. [5:35]
- Backstabber is a great item that fits Drifter's kit. [6:05]
- Battle Vest is weak on Drifter because his base gun is low; its 15% weapon damage is only about 2 damage a bullet, even though the fire rate and out-of-combat healing are nice. [7:06]
- Into Paige, Drifter can one-shot her every chance he gets and farm his passive stacks off her, since she is very squishy and immobile. [8:09]
- Kinetic Dash is much better than Quicksilver Reload or Active Reload on Drifter because his time to dump a mag (about 6.2 s, second highest) is long while his bullet count is low, so Kinetic Dash's flat 8 bullets is about 60 to 65% of his mag. [8:09]
- Kinetic Dash gives roughly another 4 seconds of holding left click plus fire rate. [9:12]
- Rushing melee lifesteal is not preferred; rushing Spirit Strike, or maxing it into Spirit Snatch, is better for the damage, and Spirit Snatch pairs well with the ult. [10:42]
- Put a point into the extra spear (ability 1) damage early. [10:42]
- When diving with 2 and 1, wait for the enemy stun to finish, then 2 and 1; using 1 too early loses the extra close-range damage (about 123). [11:43]
- Teleporting (2) on top of someone guarantees your 1 hits. [11:43]
- Cold Front is the best item for staying on top of people: good stats, decent damage, helps farming, and the movement slow suits heroes who stay in someone's face. [12:13]
- On Drifter, unlike other heroes, run toward an enemy who is already dead to rights, because getting the kill gives a stack worth 5% weapon damage. [13:44]
- Some assist-type credit (majority damage without the last hit) also gives stacks; the coach was unsure of the exact rule. [13:44]
- Drifter's long falloff lets him play just outside the ult's blind range and do heavy damage while enemies cannot see him. [14:45]
- Veil Walker is a great item on him, but buy Hunter's Aura before Veil Walker, since at that spike you are short on damage and Hunter's Aura gives much higher kill chance. [15:16]
- His weapon damage should come from his 3 (passive stacks) and fire rate, not from base weapon damage items. [15:16]
- Sometimes the "correct" play is not best on Drifter: try to steal last hits for stacks and ask a teammate to take the Urn instead of you. [16:47]
- Wall jump tech: hug one wall, then do an inner wall jump off the next for more distance, or an outer wall jump onto the roof, without spending air jump stamina. [17:17]
- Point Blank is the only weapon damage item that is justifiable on him, because you get so many stacks of your 3. [18:19]
- When your team is low and Walker is on cooldown, walking out of a 4v4 is the right call. [19:21]
- Siphon Bullets is not an every-game item, but it gives many stacks in a game where you are doing very well. [19:51]
- Target priority in the reviewed game: Paige first, then Mina, then Grey Talon, then Seven, Doorman, McGinnis. [20:24]
- Arctic Blast is very good on Drifter because Cold Front is everything you need early. [22:32]
- Do not use 2 to get onto the McGinnis when you are already next to her; save it as a gap closer onto the backline (Mina ulting in a corner, Grey Talon flying ahead). [22:32]
- The teleport is the most important part of 2, more than its bleed or fire rate. [23:03]
- Using 2 on minions to gap close is good, especially teleporting to a minion standing still fighting when ganking in lane. [23:33]
- Sell order for the build: Monster Rounds and Burst Fire (either order), then Mystic Expansion, for the earlier sells. [24:36]
- Late on, Hex is an optional item, and an item captioned "emblem" is worth building if you have spare slots. [25:06]
- You can siege the Patron from very far away with Drifter; shoot it while spacing. [25:06]
- Doorman wins any trade from his own fountain, so avoid his door and do not give him the angle. [25:06]
- Shadow Weave is good on Drifter, but with Veil Walker already you will not get much more catch value from it. [26:41]
- Burst Fire feels good on Drifter; the reviewed player had no artificial fire rate besides Kinetic Dash and Hunter's Aura, and needs more because the 3 gives so much base weapon damage. [27:12]
- A speed Drifter build exists but the coach does not think it is very good. [27:42]
- With two enemies dead, no Seven ult to hold you out and only one teammate low, you could have pushed to end; going back for the boss was the safer call, but the coach put the end at about an 80% chance. [28:42]
- Do not counter call: it confuses people in lower elo and causes ego fights in Oracle; it is fine around Ascendant. [29:13]
- AP awareness: sitting on 5 AP without the 1 max lost a game-winning fight; the 1 max basically makes you unkillable. [29:43]
- With lots of bullet lifesteal, after using 1 shoot the Patron for lifesteal and stall until 1 is back up. [30:14]
- Note 1: use 2 to get onto the backline; fight the front line only until the backline comes to support, then 2 onto them. [31:46]
- Most builds do not max 2, so with one charge be deliberate with it. [31:46]
- Note 2: space at 20 to 24 m; the dot by an enemy's name shows falloff (green = in falloff range, yellow = starting to fall off, red = fallen off). [32:16]
- Note 3: as an assassin Drifter, target the backline and avoid the McGinnis at all cost; do not play like Abrams, Bebop or Warden. [32:16]
- The ult blind is very strong; play right at the edge of the blind range to do damage they cannot see. [33:17]
- Recommended build: Wanderer's Drifter build (published by Midknight until Wanderer publishes his). [33:47]
- In that build Slowing Hex is optional and some late items were put in for testing. [33:47]
- If you buy Blood Tribute, mind its healing reduction when 1 is maxed; do not use it when you need to heal or are going on someone. [33:47]
- Most high-elo players do not max 1 first: they max 3 first, then usually the ult second (sometimes 1 second); the coach thinks a 3 max first is a little better than the build's 1 max first. [34:18]
