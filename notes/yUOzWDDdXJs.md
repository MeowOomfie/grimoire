# The Ultimate Guide To Infernus – Hardcarry 1v6 DPS
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-10-19
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yUOzWDDdXJs

## Covers
Full Infernus hero guide: how Afterburn, Napalm, Flame Dash and Concussive Combustion work, ability order, laning, the gun/burn build in order and situational items, Mid Boss and Walker tricks with the ult, and fighting by keeping the burn up.
For players who want to play Infernus as a farm-hungry carry; the coach says he is aim intensive.

## Claims
- Infernus is a hard carry duelist DPS, one of the strongest 1v1 heroes in the game, who can turn to AoE damage late with the right items; he benefits hugely from spirit items but needs quite a few gun items to get online, plus some vitality items. [0:00]
- At the time of the video Infernus is considered the best carry hero in the game (a hero who wants and benefits from lots of farm), arguably other than Victor. [0:30]
- Afterburn (third ability) is the ability that makes Infernus work; skill it first every game: every bullet builds up a burn, and when maxed the target burns for spirit damage over time, extended as long as you keep shooting. [1:00]
- Once the burn is active, any spirit damage from his other abilities also extends the burn. [1:31]
- Headshots build the burn almost twice as fast (a bit less after a recent nerf), so aim for the head if you have decent aim. [1:31]
- Infernus is quite aim intensive; if you are not comfortable aiming he is probably not the hero for you. [2:01]
- Afterburn level 1: the burn reduces the target's outgoing spirit damage by 25%. [2:01]
- Afterburn level 2: burn duration goes from 3 to 4 seconds, more damage and easier to keep the burn active. [2:01]
- A burning enemy should break your line of sight; many players fight back instead and usually die. [2:32]
- Afterburn level 5 (third upgrade) adds 28 burn damage per second: base burn goes from about 12 to about 40 per second, a huge spike. [2:32]
- Do not level any other ability before Afterburn is maxed. [3:02]
- Napalm (first ability) is an amplifier for all your damage: a cone that slows enemies hit and makes them take more damage for 8 seconds, and applying it to a burning target extends the burn. [3:02]
- Use Napalm every time you can hit it; delaying it is almost always wrong, even in tournaments, and a guaranteed Napalm is worth using even before you start the burn. [3:33]
- In a lane fight, start with Napalm so the enemy is slowed and easier to burn. [3:33]
- Napalm level 1 adds an extra charge; level 2 gives lifesteal on enemies it hits. [4:03]
- Napalm level 3 raises the damage amp from 20% to 40% and adds 33% healing reduction on the target, a huge spike; missing Napalm can decide whether you win or lose the fight. [4:03]
- Flame Dash: hold W to run faster in any direction, leaving a flame trail that damages enemies and reapplies the burn over and over; an enemy in the trail for more than a second or two is usually dead or very low. [4:33]
- Afterburn's range is farther than the animation suggests. [5:34]
- When farming (for example a tier 3 camp) you don't have to hold W during Flame Dash: burn the creeps, dash and strafe with A and D to keep the trail in the middle so the burn stays up and you farm faster. [5:34]
- Enemies will try to take high ground to escape Flame Dash; don't use it when the enemy is close to high ground, only when you are sure to hit them at least once. [6:06]
- You can't parry during Flame Dash; cancel it early (space bar for the coach) and parry immediately when you see an enemy charge a heavy melee. [6:36]
- At lower ranks people punch you less because they don't know you can't parry in the dash; once enemies know, they punch almost every game, so bait the melee, cancel and parry for a counter parry kill. [7:07]
- Until maxed, Flame Dash has a very long cooldown, about 38 seconds, and it is your only escape until you buy items, so learn to save it to get out. [7:38]
- Starting the fight with Flame Dash is one of the worst things you can do: the enemy isn't burning yet, so damage is far lower than burn first, then Napalm, then dash. Almost never open with Flame Dash. [7:38]
- You may Flame Dash to catch a very low enemy, but then you have no escape for a long time and are a sitting duck. [8:39]
- Always start the fight by burning; then weave in and out: dash in, reapply the burn, run away, and make them choose between retreating or running through the trail to catch you. [8:39]
- Dash Infernus (early Rapid Recharge and early max Flame Dash) still has about an 8 second dash cooldown, so you can still die if you overcommit. [9:09]
- Gun Infernus is by far stronger than Dash Infernus right now; most pro players play gun (Zergy is known for his Infernus), you can do everything Dash does better with gun, and early dash is out of the meta. [9:40]
- Rapid Recharge is a viable mid or late game upgrade for more dash charges; just don't go dash early. [9:40]
- Ability order: max Afterburn, one point in Flame Dash, max Napalm, max Flame Dash, then max the ultimate. [10:12]
- Flame Dash's level 2 damage upgrade is nice but delays the main Afterburn and Napalm power spike. [10:42]
- Concussive Combustion (ult) is very strong but its upgrades are weak: the 35 second cooldown cut is nice, the stun duration and radius rarely matter, and the level 5 heal is a super luxury almost never useful; don't level it until late. [10:42]
- The ult charges for 2 seconds then explodes, stunning and dealing spirit damage to everyone nearby, which resets the burn on burning targets to maximum. [11:13]
- To catch a fleeing enemy: burn them, then Flame Dash and ult at the same time, catch up, stun them and keep the burn on. [11:43]
- Enemies hear the loud ult across the map and see its big visual and will try to leave it, so usually use it together with Flame Dash to stay on them; have Flame Dash up when you ult. [12:13]
- If you are already in melee range you can ult and stick to them with dashes instead. [12:44]
- Use the ult to secure or steal Mid Boss: pop it about 2 seconds before the Rejuvenator touches the ground so anyone contesting, parrying or punching gets stunned. [12:44]
- The ult also stuns Walkers: chase a burning enemy back to their Walker with Flame Dash and ult, and if timed right you stun both the hero and the Walker, so you don't get Walker stomped. [13:46]
- In lane Infernus does decent early damage, not the best or worst laner; sit back and shove the wave. [13:46]
- When a wave spawns, put the burn on as many creeps as possible so they die fast and you shove quickly, while saving enough bullets to last hit and not get denied; this takes more micromanagement than most heroes. [14:17]
- Infernus needs fire rate; every build uses some fire rate item. [14:47]
- Build: Rapid Rounds first, so bullets fire faster and the burn applies faster. [15:18]
- Then Improved Spirit: out of combat regen for lane and spirit scaling for Afterburn, Flame Dash and Napalm (Flame Dash and Afterburn scale very well with spirit power). [15:18]
- Play passive in lane, but if an enemy hero is too far out of cover, burn them for harassment; every burn forces them to back out, and if they don't, Napalm or Flame Dash into them for big damage or kills. [15:50]
- In lane only level Afterburn; you can take two points in Flame Dash to be more aggressive, but it delays the Afterburn spike that lets you farm faster. [16:20]
- Next build items: Spirit Shielding and Sprint Boots, both for out of combat regen, so you heal fast in lane whenever you aren't shooting or being shot; Extra Health or Reactive Barrier are fine alternatives. [16:50]
- Extended Magazine is the only magazine item until much later; upgrade to Titanic Magazine if you're not comfortable sliding, or buy Quicksilver Reload earlier if you can't proc the burn consistently. 36 bullets should be enough to burn someone and extend it. [17:21]
- The burn has falloff: from far away it applies much slower, so stay at mid distance, around 40 m; sitting far back and shooting does almost nothing (a full magazine for 66 damage and no burn). [17:51]
- From range you can still extend a burn that is already on; chase only when they are very low, using Flame Dash and Napalm. [18:23]
- Next, Toxic Bullets; other builds take Tesla Bullets here, which is fine because it upgrades into Capacitor, one of the best items in the game on high fire rate heroes, which can remove buffs such as Viscous's cube. [18:23]
- Tesla/Capacitor is the more passive route: Tesla is mostly a farming item early, so you must farm more jungle for longer before you are online; the Toxic Bullets burn build gets online early so you can fight early, and it wins the coach games in Eternus 6. [19:24]
- Toxic Bullets is underrated on Infernus: it builds up per shot like the burn, so both apply at once, both reapply on every hit, and it is still good for farming creeps. [19:56]
- Next: Spirit Lifesteal, Enduring Speed and Burst Fire. [19:56]
- Burst Fire is the best fire rate item for applying the burn fast; with good aim the burn goes on almost instantly. A fire rate item the captions call "Cultist" is the farm-heavy alternative. [20:26]
- Then Duration Extender and Superior Duration: Afterburn goes to 4.88 seconds and then over 5, and Superior Duration also stretches Toxic Bullets from 3 to 3.8 seconds. [20:56]
- Toxic Bullets scales with spirit power; at 21k souls with Boundless Spirit it bleeds about 2.8% per second alongside huge Afterburn and Flame Dash damage. [20:56]
- Once the burn is on, Infernus has the highest DPS of any hero except spirit nukers like Pocket or Mina who insta cast everything. [21:26]
- Duration suits him because it extends the burn, Flame Dash, Napalm and the ult stun. [21:57]
- Then Quicksilver Reload, to shoot and live longer once your damage is online. [21:57]
- Then Leech, not Infuser: Infernus still does a lot of plain gun damage, especially early in bursts, so Leech heals far more over fights that last longer than Infuser's 6 seconds. Leech is a massive damage and survivability spike. [22:28]
- With Leech, burn plus Napalm then Flame Dash away or through them heals so much that you're very hard to kill even when low. [22:58]
- Spirit Lifesteal is bought early (it was just buffed) and also gives spirit power. [23:29]
- Even when getting ganked, get the burn off: it gets the enemy low without more shooting and it keeps you alive. [23:29]
- When jumped, don't always run immediately; if you can safely escape, Flame Dash out, but often bait them: burn, Napalm, then run, so they choose between chasing (extending the burn and your healing) or backing out. [23:59]
- If they stop chasing, cancel and turn on them while keeping the burn up; keep a mid distance they can't close but where your burn stays on. [24:30]
- As long as the burn is on the enemy you are winning the fight unless you are already very low. [25:02]
- What kills Infernus is tanks getting melee range (Mo & Krill grab, Abrams charge, Billy chain); even then a burn keeps you alive for a long time. He has low HP but lots of healing. [25:02]
- Spirit Lifesteal is a must; every build runs it. [25:02]
- Warp Stone is popular (the coach doesn't run it): burn, Flame Dash, then Warp Stone right on top of them so they can't avoid the fire, or Warp Stone onto high ground they jumped to; it also gives bullet resist. [25:33]
- Upgrade Extended Magazine into Escalating Resilience to save a slot; since you're always shooting you basically always have the extra 30 bullet resist. [26:04]
- Around 20k to 25k net worth, or once you have Boundless Spirit, Infernus becomes really scary in any good build. [26:34]
- Spirit Rend is an option for more debuff; Silence Wave is great into heroes with escapes like Mina or Pocket, silencing them and adding spirit shred. [27:06]
- After Leech the coach buys Crippling Headshot, insane on Infernus because it shreds both bullet and spirit resist and he deals both. [27:40]
- Imbue Quicksilver Reload and later Mercurial Magnum on the shortest cooldown ability: Napalm on Infernus (cards on Wraith, the third ability on Mirage). [28:11]
- Ethereal Shift is crazy right now: it gives spirit resist and spirit power on exit; Flame Dash past enemies then Ethereal Shift to dodge an ult, heal back while untargetable for 4 seconds, and come out with more speed, spirit power and burn damage. [28:41]
- You can cancel Ethereal Shift at any time, so exit early to reapply the burn before it runs out. [30:11]
- Ricochet works with Afterburn, burning several enemies at once; it suits a safer backline style when the enemy clumps, but it is team dependent. [30:11]
- Rule of thumb: close range heroes clump up, long range heroes (Vindicta, Wraith, Paige) don't; Ricochet is wasted money against teams that don't clump, versus Crippling Headshot, Mercurial Magnum or a survival item. [31:13]
- Almost always buy Leech first among late items; if enemies don't die fast enough add Mercurial Magnum or Crippling Headshot or both. [31:44]
- To dodge strong stuns such as Lash or Wraith's ult, buy Ethereal Shift, or Counterspell if you can't afford it. [32:16]
- Escalating Exposure is a late game luxury: it stacks over time and enemies often die before it is worth it. [32:46]
- Inhibitor and Ricochet work together (Inhibitor debuffs the whole enemy team's outgoing damage); buy Inhibitor alone against one specific high damage target. [32:46]
- Inhibitor is the ultimate anti-carry item: against a fed Wraith, one Inhibitor cut his damage 35% and made the 1v1 an easy win. All these items can come any time after Leech. [33:17]
- Other late options: Juggernaut as a luxury, Unstoppable against Black Hole, Spellbreaker into lots of spirit nukes, Plated Armor into gun damage or on-proc effects. [33:48]
- The build has no spirit armor, so you're squishy and rely on damage to heal; buy Spirit Resilience fairly early into heavy spirit damage. Bullet Resilience is bought less since there isn't much bullet damage in the game. [34:19]
- Ammo is low in this build until Quicksilver Reload, so slide shoot a lot; Burst Fire and Enduring Speed let you slide for a long time. [34:50]
- Buy Fleetfoot if you struggle with sliding or the enemy has lots of slows; it lets you slide without dashing. [35:21]
- The main skills to learn on Infernus are aim (apply the burn as fast as possible) and ammo management with slides. [35:52]
- Burn, Napalm and dash often kill even tanks because Toxic Bullets deals damage based on the target's HP; a tanky test Abrams (Lifestrike, Spirit Snatch, Spirit Resilience, Extra Health) barely survived burn, Napalm and one dash. [36:23]
- Burst Fire procs on the first bullet you shoot. [36:53]
- Realistic kill combo: burn, Napalm, then ult; after that the burn and Toxic Bullets last long enough that they die without more input. [37:23]
- Rapid Recharge after maxed Flame Dash gives a 10 second dash cooldown, effectively 7 seconds since the dash lasts about 3 seconds; buy it any time after Flame Dash is maxed. It also gives more Napalm charges (four). [37:23]
- Don't max Flame Dash first or second, since Afterburn plus Napalm do so much damage; the pure Flame Dash style doesn't work as well right now (Infernus may be nerfed next patch). [37:53]
- Most important: hit your bullets and apply the burn before anything else; the only time not to burn is when you are sure you can escape. Half health and fleeing without burning gets you killed; burning gives a good chance to turn the fight or live. [39:26]
- Even if you can't kill the enemy, apply the burn and Napalm and then run: you force them into bad spots and heal a lot, about 1,000 healing in under a second in the demo. [39:57]
- This works especially well when several enemies chase, since every enemy burned or in your Flame Dash trail heals you more. [40:27]
- Enemies see Infernus at half HP and think they can kill him, but his sustain lets you turn fights; the longer the fight lasts with them burning, the more likely you win. [40:57]
- As long as they're burning you're winning; as long as they're not burning you're losing. [40:57]
