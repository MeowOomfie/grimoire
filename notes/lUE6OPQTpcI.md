## Covers
Warden, aquatiq, commentary over one standard match (loss).
Why Warden is strong (Alchemical Flask, the silence, patch history), his item path and macro calls, on the 2026-09-08 patch.

## Claims
- Warden is very strong right now; he uses the "Lightbringer situation" build. [0:31]
- In lane, wait for Bebop's hook and stay well back so a Calico combo does not one-shot you. [1:31]
- Item start: Rapid Rounds first, then Swift Striker; he skips Opening Rounds since he is taking no damage. [2:32]
- For the full reasoning on this Warden, he points to Poshie Pop's video. [2:32]
- Nothing changed on the hero; the build was made and that made him strong. [3:02]
- The silence on his 3 is ridiculous and is one of the main reasons he is strong. [3:02]
- Alchemical Flask is one of the most broken abilities in the game, because it is the only ability or item that removes enemy stamina on hit; that is why Flask is maxed. [3:32]
- Skip Extra Regen if you are not taking damage and healing off lane; buy it once you start taking damage. [4:03]
- Extended Magazine should come first. [5:04]
- Flask's weapon damage reduction is bigger than any other hero's. [5:04]
- He expects Warden to be nerfed next patch. [5:35]
- With their stamina removed, enemies cannot move. [6:35]
- Patch history: his weapon damage per boon was cut a lot, while his 2 and his 4 were heavily buffed, and his bullet falloff range changed; the aim was to make him use his ultimate in fights instead of just shooting. [7:35]
- Enemies can cleanse Warden's chain. [10:43]
- He adds Veil Walker and Fleetfoot. [11:15]
- Pushing up onto an enemy walker is bad even when your team is stronger. [11:53]
- Take the survival buff, since it is good on Warden. [19:47]
- The enemy wasted their rejuvenators after the mid boss by coming in one at a time. [19:47]
- Never fight when your lanes are bad; push their walker instead, which pulls enemies away from the fight. [20:49]
- Against high HP heroes (Yamato 2.8k, Calico 3.2k, Abrams 3k) Siphon Bullets is good, and Frenzy is an option. [21:56]
- He buys Witchmail because most of the damage he takes is spirit. [21:56]
- Get rid of the Ivy before going for mid boss. [23:30]
- When the game is lost, stop fighting and stay in base. [25:04]
