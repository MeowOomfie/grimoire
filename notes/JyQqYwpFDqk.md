## Covers
Midknight free coaching VOD review of an Arcanist Kelvin in the solo lane against Grey Talon, then the mid and late game of the same match.
Themes: shoving the first wave, playing behind cover against Grey Talon poke, respecting minion damage, soul awareness and buying on time, box runs and medium camps, stamina use, parrying at 1 HP, deathballing versus splitting, fighting only over objectives, Urn, bridge buffs, denying Walker souls with Arctic Beam, and ending with Patron.
This is a late-2024 video (early November 2024 patch, around the Urn changes). The game has changed a lot since, so treat numbers, items and map details as dated.

## Claims
Laning against Grey Talon:
- Grey Talon is very hard to lane against on this patch. [0:00]
- Shove the wave as fast as you can, especially the first wave, unless you want to set up a freeze; in high Elo both players melee the first wave to push it fast. [0:30]
- Against Grey Talon, always be shooting, reloading or meleeing a minion to shove faster; the first wave was played too passively. [1:00]
- If you focus one creep in a wave, kill the heal creep first so you deny the enemy a heal or two. [1:00]
- Once you lose the wave and wave priority, back up and farm under Tower. [1:30]
- Minions do a lot of damage, most players sleep on them, and it is especially dangerous early. [1:30]
- Be aware of when the enemy is much closer to a minion than you, because killing a minion next to him offers him an easy deny. [2:00]
- On that side of the solo lane the barrels are very strong cover: play and crouch behind them and last-hit, and Grey Talon can barely poke you. [2:30]
- If you get pushed right, play around the sign so you can duck into cover from his Charged Shot, but once he levels his Spirit Snare the sign is a bad spot because he can trap you there. [2:30]
- Over-securing souls is better than under-securing. [3:00]
- Below Archon, just try to permanently shove the wave; the macro of box runs and camps comes once the wave is shoved. [4:00]
- Avoid tanking minions: back up toward the bridge and shoot them instead; even 50 damage you did not need to take matters. [5:32]
- When Grey Talon flies with Rain of Arrows, the better play is to run back and tuck behind the wall; going under the bridge to punish him when he lands is not awful, but don't stay in the open circling cover. [6:03]
- Against his flight with three stamina, roll, jump, slide behind cover and crouch until the flight ends; that can save around 150 damage, which matters a lot early. [6:33]
- When he starts poking you, roll to cover even if it costs a minion; keeping 150 to 200 HP is worth more than one last hit. [8:35]
- When enemy minions are at 1 HP and grouped and you are full HP, heavy melee them to secure both and deny Grey Talon the double deny; your regen does nothing while you are full HP. [11:40]
- Use your melee on minions more in lane. [12:10]
- Default to sitting under the bridge whenever you hear Grey Talon fly, moving a little so he can't shoot you. [12:42]
- Normally don't secure kills with melee because the enemy can parry; very high Elo players (high Ascendant, Eternus) parry off audio, but at this Elo a melee on a target with his back turned is good. [13:44]
- Don't use Frozen Shelter (the Dome) early in a skirmish when you have no kill potential and are not in danger; hold it for another wave of poke or a real dive. [14:15]
- When Grey Talon uses his ult (Guided Owl), go for a heavy melee instead of Arctic Beam, since he can't parry in that state. [14:45]
- Committing the Dome is good when you know Grey Talon already used his flight and has little escape. [16:18]
- Don't keep chasing after winning a trade. [16:18]

Soul awareness and items:
- After every wave check whether you can buy the item you want; sitting on 1K or more souls while taking aggressive fights is a mistake unless you are saving for a specific item. [7:03]
- Two tier one items are a big edge in lane: two gun items like Hollow Point Ward add roughly 60% damage at your HP, plus about 12% weapon damage for having two items. [7:34]
- Kelvin's skills scale at about 9% DPS per spirit point, so one Extra Spirit adds about 14 DPS to Arctic Beam; people really sleep on tier one items. [8:05]
- Against Grey Talon, Lady Geist or any heavy poke hero, buy Extra Regen (or extra health) for sustain before going double gun with Hollow Point Ward and High Velocity Mag. [10:38]
- Restorative Shot is good sustain; against Grey Talon he would pair Restorative Shot with Extra Regen instead of High Velocity Mag, especially with Hollow Point Ward since it wants you above 60% health. [11:09]
- Filling all your item slots early is good. [42:51]

Farming, boxes and movement:
- Solo lane box run after shoving: jump up to the box by the lane, break the next box, then the two boxes down below, plus the two well-known breakables on the bridge. [4:31]
- When resetting from lane, a decent alternative is walking to the shop, buying Healing Rite, pushing the wave and tucking to use it. [9:37]
- Never walk past Urns and boxes on the way back: melee the Urn, grab every box on the route, then take the rope back; that can be around 150 souls plus about 1.5% weapon damage from a buff. [9:37]
- Stair stutters: sliding gives infinite ammo, so repeatedly sliding on the edge of stairs extends your magazine and adds DPS to the tower. [15:16]
- After shoving the wave, take a camp, especially the medium camps once they spawn; few players camp in lane at this Elo and it gives a huge advantage. [15:47]
- Use stamina whenever it gets you somewhere faster and you are at three bars; don't sit on full stamina. [16:49]
- Always slide right after you roll (hold slide for about half a second to a second after the dash) for extra movement speed; you cannot slide when rolling up stairs. [17:20]
- At 1 HP against someone you can't beat, press parry and hope they melee; it is the only way you live, unless you have two points in Frost Grenade to heal. [18:51]
- The other side of the solo lane has a small camp to the right of your Guardian. [49:32]

Mid and late game macro:
- Looking for a roam and getting a bad one is better than never roaming, but if nothing is there, back off; staying in another lane without a kill or Guardian just splits souls with your two teammates. [20:23]
- Sieging a Guardian with three heroes for a long time wastes time; the enemy can send three players to split the other lanes and get every minion to themselves. [21:23]
- Once the enemy shows numbers and your team has died or bounced, run instead of trading; a half HP target is not worth it. [22:26]
- When four enemies brute force a Walker and Ice Path is on cooldown, you won't save it; pick another lane to push or split, or take jungle, instead of stalling five seconds and dying. [22:56]
- A fight after the enemy already won and took the Walker is unfavorable; split or take something else on the map instead. [25:00]
- To catch a fleeing enemy, move along the upper level, which is faster, and keep watching the minimap to drop on them. [25:30]
- Frozen Shelter turned a very negative fight into a neutral trade; it is great used reactively to keep teammates alive. [26:32]
- Avoid deathballing (all heroes in one lane); if a fight is not over anything, farm, catch waves or split instead of autopiloting to it. [28:03]
- Kelvin is not a split hero, but farming is still better than fighting for nothing while splitting one wave four ways. [28:33]
- Splitting the side lanes while the team fights, as the Haze did, wins games; learning when to split and when to fight will rank you up a lot. [29:03]
- A fight is good when all your waves are pushed and you have the numbers: win it and you get the Urn and crashing waves, lose it and they only get to push waves back. [29:33]
- Focus the back line in fights. [30:34]
- Bridge buffs spawn every 5 minutes after 10 minutes (10, 15, 20, 25); high Elo players stand on the vents waiting and skirmish over them, so grab them whenever you can. [31:04]
- After winning a fight, reset and ask what objectives are on the map (Urn, a low Walker, the next Walker, mid boss) and convert the kills; shoving an empty lane wastes the advantage. [32:05]
- Infernus and Dynamo are the two big threats for stopping mid boss on that enemy team. [32:37]
- When a 4v4 fight is net neutral and you don't know where two enemies are, skip it and take camps, or even force the Urn, which lower Elo teams often give up. [34:10]
- Trading Walkers is fine when three enemies are on yours, but with only two you can come back and defend it. [35:13]
- Walkers drop about 33% of their souls as orbs; shooting them denies those souls from the whole enemy team and gives them to your whole team. [36:13]
- As Kelvin, use Arctic Beam to deny Walker soul orbs; he is about the only hero who can deny a full Walker alone. [36:45]
- Breaking objective soul orbs (Walkers, towers, base Guardians, Shrines) gives the plus souls to everyone on your team. [37:15]
- Go to a fight only to get an objective, for example win it and immediately rush mid boss, not by autopilot. [37:46]
- If a teammate grabs the Urn while many enemies are on the map, splitting teammates should drop back so you are not down a player. [38:16]
- When a fight is unfavorable (5v4, a teammate dead, help far away), spam the retreat ping and run; not taking unfavorable fights is the key to winning MOBAs. [38:47]
- At this Elo it is fine to grab bridge buffs when you see or hear them spawn; tracking timers comes later. [39:47]
- Melee last hits are fine in chaotic team fights since nobody parries there, but avoid them in 1v1s where people are much more prone to parry. [40:17]
- If something happens in real life, pause the game, tell your team, and come back. [40:48]
- If you group and take a fight, leverage it into mid boss: a pick on Dynamo or Infernus, or on four of them, means mid boss. [41:19]
- When you have wave priority, group and look for kills and mid boss; when you lose it, everyone deals with their own lane. [41:50]
- With three enemies dead, knock the Patron and then run mid boss rather than over-staying, since two of them respawn by the time Patron is knocked. [43:52]
- Once Patron is knocked and you have a two player advantage, go into their base and end. [45:58]
- You can self-cast Rescue Beam to rescue yourself out of a tether. [46:29]
- Don't drop into the pit while Patron is condemning you before your minion wave reaches it, unless Patron is at 1 HP for a backdoor finish; wait the extra five seconds and you will be much healthier. [46:59]
- The next patch was expected to make the Urn less extreme after it started spawning in the enemy base, so run the Urn more; you can bind a "help me with the Urn" ping. [50:33]
