# Graves Still Does INFINITE Damage
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fIgR7goX13Q

## Covers
Graves, commentary over one game with abilities, items and macro reasoning.
aquatiq, lane with Shiv against Warden and Mo & Krill, Oracle lobby. No one is coached. Patch before the early March 2026 update.

## Claims
- Graves plays like Pocket: get in, do damage, get out. [0:38]
- He always uses the same build for Graves. [1:38]
- Ability 1 sends out skulls that damage enemies; enemies can break them. [2:08]
- Ability 3 steals weapon damage and resists from enemies. [2:08]
- Put as many upgrades into the 3 as possible early in lane. [2:08]
- Get Healing Rite in lane against Mo & Krill's Scorn damage. [2:08]
- Laning against two tanks (Warden and Mo & Krill) is hard; Mo & Krill has about a thousand HP in lane so you will not kill him. [2:39]
- When the lane is hard, focus on keeping your souls up and not dying early. [3:09]
- The lane is a battle of who overextends when they are not supposed to. [3:40]
- The grasp ability draws a line on the floor that stuns enemies who walk into it. [3:40]
- When you have more health than your lane partner, step in front of them so they stop taking damage. [4:41]
- Graves has auto aim on her abilities, so aiming is not demanding. [5:11]
- If the lane is lost and staying only loses money, leave it and go gank another lane. [5:11]
- Graves is not easy to bring online: she cannot 1v1 until she has a decent amount of souls, unlike heroes such as Paradox or Mirage who are good without money. [7:45]
- The ult summons ghouls (zombies). [7:14]
- Buy Tesla Bullets over Toxic Bullets when behind so you can farm, and keep them into late game; he later said Toxic might have been better but he needed farm. [8:16]
- Wait for Borrowed Decree before starting fights; without it she does little damage. [11:56]
- Place Borrowed Decree up high where enemies cannot easily reach it. [12:26]
- Borrowed Decree keeps pushing a lane for you; drop it to keep pressure on a lane. [11:24]
- Save Borrowed Decree for when it is needed rather than wasting it. [26:22]
- Check what you are taking damage from; against a mostly spirit team, buy spirit defense. [13:58]
- Buy Healbane, then Superior Duration, which is very helpful for the ult. [14:29]
- With a big unspent bag of souls, avoid fights and go spend it. [18:05]
- Pushing waves all the way to the enemy base to keep pressure on them is very important and often neglected. [19:37]
- Back out of bad fights and farm when ahead instead of forcing them. [21:42]
- Do not fight to save a walker that is not worth it; let it go and take a different lane. [22:44]
- Buy Counterspell against Apollo's ult; it replaced Intensifying Magazine. [23:16]
- Steal enemy jungle camps when you have map control. [24:49]
- After a won fight, clear the waves and do not let enemies catch people out. [25:22]
- If the enemy takes mid boss while your strongest players are alive, they are throwing. [25:52]
- Silencer replaced Healbane, since one teammate (Shiv) already had Healbane; one Healbane on the team is enough. [29:57]
- Later, against more spirit damage, he looked at Spellbreaker and then Leech. [32:33]
- To catch up from the lowest souls early, take more risks, look for damage on people and start farming camps as early as possible. [35:08]
- Leaving a lane that is going to fall is better than you and your partner both dying plus losing the lane. [35:08]
- Graves gets strong late game; he ended with the most souls and 54k player damage. [35:39]
