# How To Beat Every Hero In Deadlock (New Additions)
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2026-02-16  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UgVm8ujfMzs

## Covers
Counters to the six new heroes Celeste, Graves, Apollo, Silver, Venator, Rem; Dispel Magic, Spellbreaker, Spirit Resilience, Disarming Hex, Slowing Hex, Metal Skin, Counterspell, breaking skulls and gravestones with melee, AoE into zombies, baiting Apollo's riposte, kiting werewolf form, shooting Venator's traps, guarding sinners at 8 minutes

## Claims
- He has 20+ games on every new hero and starts with Celeste and Graves because those are the ones most people struggle with. [0:30]

### Celeste
- Even after her recent nerfs, Celeste is by far the strongest of the new heroes. [0:30]
- Her main damage is her one, Lighteater: while it is on you she gets huge spirit lifesteal (35% already at level two), and every auto attack or light melee she lands deals bonus damage and heals her. [1:00]
- Radiant Daggers (her three) is a big AoE but takes a long time to come down, so you can dodge it. [1:32]
- In lane Lighteater has short range at base and her base gun damage is very low, so play mid range and do not go close; once Lighteater hits you (it lasts 12 seconds) you cannot trade with her at all. [1:32]
- With Lighteater down she is quite weak, especially to gun damage, since she now has negative bullet resist at base; she is not tanky, so bursting or catching her first works, and if she is low it is worth bursting through her shield. [2:03]
- If she is not low (about a third or below), do not pop her shield: popping it silences you for a long time and damages you, which is what she wants. [2:34]
- Her ultimate bounces between enemies, including lane creeps and jungle creeps; when you have the aura on you, move away from allies and creeps, and isolated it does nothing and disappears after a few seconds. [3:04]
- Dispel Magic is an underrated buy: it gives spirit resist (her damage is 90%+ spirit even with gun items) and cleanses Lighteater, leaving her about 10 seconds where she does basically no damage to you. [4:06]
- The classic anti-spirit items also work (spirit resist, Spellbreaker); against a very farmed late game Celeste shooting fast with Spellslinger, consider Disarming Hex. [4:36]
- Against a Celeste building range on her three (Ballistic Enhancement or Greater Expansion on Radiant Daggers), do not clump up as a team. [5:07]

### Graves
- Graves does very well in low rank but has many weaknesses; she only trades efficiently when in her auto attack range, which is quite short, so stay outside it, where most heroes, even shotgun ones, out-trade her. [6:08]
- Skulls she throws at you break with one light melee; take the time to break them, especially against a spirit build, because once they are broken she is quite weak. [6:38]
- Gravestone is a strong area denial tool (after recent changes zombies spawn faster but it lasts less long); avoid fighting into it unless you can break it fast, kite back and wait it out, or break it right away with two heavy melees. [7:08]
- Billy and Viscous break the gravestone easily, since Puddle Punch and a maxed Billy one count as heavy melees. [7:39]
- AoE is good against her: use AoE spells to nuke the zombies in her ultimate, for example Seven's lightning balls or his three, or Celeste's three. [7:39]
- The longer she auto attacks you, the stronger she gets and the weaker you get; after four or more seconds on you, your gun damage to her drops a lot and she is tankier to spirit, so trade fast and burst her or go on her first, since she is not tanky at base. [8:10]
- Check her build: against gun items stay out of range, against spirit prioritize meleeing the skulls, and against a tanky build with lots of green items ignore her and kill everyone else first, because she does basically no damage. [8:42]

### Apollo
- People struggle less against Apollo because he is hard to play, but in high rank he is considered a top three hero; his strength comes from his two and three. [9:42]
- His gun damage is weak in lane and he is bad at farming creeps and jungle; his lane damage is his three, or his one if he gets close, where you can trade back. [10:14]
- After Flawless Advance's first lunge hits you, take cover so he struggles to land the other two; trading straight into the three, a good Apollo wins every time, but if you do not get hit three times in a row you are fine. [10:44]
- The three has a long cooldown, and without it he only has his riposte (captioned "repost") and his sigil, which is a bit of damage but nothing crazy. [11:15]
- Bait out the riposte by just shooting him; its stun is only 0.6 seconds, and once he uses it on an auto attack or light melee, hit him with your big stuns and damage abilities. [11:15]
- After he uses his three and two he is a sitting duck and cannot escape early, since Disengaging Sigil only hops him back a little and is usually not maxed early. [11:45]
- All his damage is spirit, including the ultimate's big bonus damage on enemies below 50% HP, so Spellbreaker and Spirit Resilience are excellent; he is probably the hero most countered by spirit resilience in the game, and late game his ultimate did almost nothing to resisted targets. [12:15]
- Slowing Hex destroys him: Flawless Advance counts as a movement ability with a short recast window, so hexing him after the first cast stops the rest, and it also stops his ultimate; any slow, even Billy's chain, hurts him. [13:15]
- His ultimate has a long cast time, so cancel it with stuns or burst him down during the cast. [13:46]

### Silver
- Silver was recently buffed and is very strong, but she is not strong in base form; her goal is werewolf form, where she is the strongest hero in the game and insanely tanky. [13:46]
- In lane she is tanky but does little damage and has a low fire rate, so do not respect her too much, trade with her, and get your last hits, which she struggles with. [14:18]
- To reach werewolf form she needs her combo (usually 3, 2, 1: go in, tangle you, kick you, then the one three times); burst her before she transforms, or step away once she has done decent damage unless she is really low. [14:49]
- In werewolf form kite her (walk away while shooting) and in team fights run from her and focus others; she has only one leap to catch you and her healing one and auto attacks are melee range, so stay out of melee range. [15:20]
- Slows are good against her because she cannot use her boot kick; in both forms play mid to long range or nuke her instantly. [15:51]
- Metal Skin and Disarming Hex are strong because most of her damage is gun based; Metal Skin reduces werewolf damage and cancels Slamfire damage, which also denies her ultimate charge so she transforms later. [16:22]
- If you give her werewolf form right at the start of a fight, you should run away. [16:52]

### Venator
- Venator is the true gun carry of this patch; recent changes made his ultimate a bit weaker and the rest of his kit stronger. [17:22]
- He is weak in lane: his one does little damage and needs close range, and his two is useless unless you stand next to a wall it can stun you into, so stay mid range, where his gun damage is low and he is squishy. [17:22]
- Avoid hallways and close quarters, where he lands traps, his one and his two; his purple traps on the floor are always visible and one bullet destroys them, so shoot them and fight him on open ground. [17:53]
- Against his ultimate take cover; it lasts only 15 seconds and the first shot probably will not kill you unless you were already low, so play cover, wait it out, then engage, since he is weak after it. [18:24]
- He is a great target to kill first: even with green items he is squishy for a carry and wants to play behind his team. [18:55]
- Metal Skin is his best counter, negating most of his damage including the ultimate, which does zero damage while Metal Skin is active; Counterspell can also block a predicted ultimate shot. [19:26]

### Rem
- Rem is strong in lane when he puts his little helpers on his creeps, so kill those creeps first; stay out of his range early and avoid trading until his helpers are gone. [19:57]
- Without helpers he is very squishy and Pillow Toss does little damage, so you can trade with him and focusing him first in fights is often good. [20:27]
- Slowing Hex shuts off Tag Along, his way out of team fights by jumping to teammates, since he cannot use it while slowed or silenced. [20:27]
- Counterspell counters Nap Time and the timing is forgiving. [20:57]
- At 8 minutes many Rems steal your sinners with helpers, so be at your sinners, especially doubles; since the latest patch the helper runs away when you hit the sinner, and an advanced play is letting Rem bring it low, then taking the last hit for most of the souls. [20:57]
- Watch the side lanes: Rems often put four helpers on a wave to push it, which can cost you towers. [21:58]
