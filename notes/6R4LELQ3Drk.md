# Egg Philosophy
channel: HERESY
date: 2026-03-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6R4LELQ3Drk

## Covers
A deep dive into the Golden Goose Egg: its soul generation, a soul value for each golden statue buff priced from shop items, when to buy it, and when to sell it (8 minutes vs just after 10), written partly as a polite rebuttal of Poshiepop's egg video.
For players deciding whether the egg is worth buying and which heroes can afford it.

## Claims
- The Golden Goose Egg is misunderstood by most players in all elos, including pro play. [0:00]
- The egg costs 800 souls, has a 10% damage penalty and some negligible regen stats. [1:03]
- The egg generates 90 souls per minute and grants a golden statue buff every 150 souls. [1:03]
- The golden statue buff tier depends on when you crack the egg, scaling at the 10 and 30 minute intervals, so the longer you wait the more the buffs are worth. [1:03]
- The egg starts with 400 souls that count toward the buffs, so cracking it immediately still grants two golden statue buffs on top of the 400 souls. [1:34]
- Pricing buffs from shop items: Extra Spirit is 800 souls for 10 spirit power, so 1 spirit power is worth 80 souls. [2:35]
- Extra Health is 800 souls for 210 health, so 1 health is worth about 3.8 souls. [2:35]
- The 3,200 item with 20% cooldown reduction on all abilities (plus 4 out of combat regen) values 1% cooldown reduction at about 160 souls. [3:06]
- An 800 soul item gives 9% fire rate and Swift Striker gives 20% for 1,600 (plus some sprint speed); generously, 1% fire rate is worth about 80 souls. [3:37]
- Weapon damage and magazine size have no flat stat item, so assuming the devs balanced the buffs, the average of the four known buffs (about 104 souls) is used, making weapon damage and max ammo each about 35 souls per percentage point. [4:09]
- By this method the egg is worth about 152 souls per minute if sold before 10 minutes and about 182 if sold after 10 minutes. [5:41]
- If you buy the egg, buy it immediately, right at the 60 second mark after the first wave; if you aren't buying it by then it likely isn't worth it. [6:12]
- The 90 souls per minute is fixed while your own income rises over the game (camps worth more, clearing two or three times faster, moving faster, running Urns, sieging Walkers), so the 90 is worth the most at minute one and less every minute after. [6:42]
- A synergistic 30,000 soul build beats 30,000 souls of random stats; fire rate multiplies with weapon damage but not with spirit power. [7:43]
- So a golden statue buff valued at 100 souls is worth less than 100 souls in your pocket, which you could spend on the exact item your hero and build want. [8:44]
- Many heroes don't use all the buffs: Paige doesn't care about magazine size, weapon damage or fire rate, and Rem doesn't use weapon damage, magazine size or fire rate either. [9:14]
- Wraith, a spirit and on-hit hero who uses her gun, uses all of the buffs effectively. [9:45]
- If a quarter of the buffs do nothing for your hero, the average buff is worth more like 75 souls than 100, and even that overestimates it. [9:45]
- Any raw estimated buff value is an upper ceiling; practical value is almost certainly lower. [10:15]
- Ideally buy the egg on a hero that can use all of the golden statue buffs. [10:45]
- Poshiepop's method values golden statue buffs at roughly double this video's estimate, which the coach thinks is self refuting: 147 souls per spirit power would make Extra Spirit worth about 1,470 souls, and 13.3 souls per HP would make Extra Health worth 2,793, almost the price of Fortitude. [12:18]
- The egg must be sold by about the 8 minute mark, even though buff value rises by about 50% at 10 minutes, because that is when the key mid game objectives spawn (captioned as "all of the urns", in the context of vaults). [13:21]
- Taking any vault (Sinner's Sacrifice) grants four golden statue buffs plus about 350 raw souls; by Poshiepop's buff values a vault would be worth about 1,150 souls. [13:53]
- Holding the egg cripples you during vault fights: the -10% damage penalty, plus playing down an 800 soul item that could give combat power to win lane, bully enemies out of their jungle to take their vault, or defend your own vaults. [14:23]
- Even if both teams go 50/50 on vaults while you hold the egg, without the egg you probably would have won a fight and taken one of their vaults, worth much more than holding the egg four more minutes. [15:25]
- The egg is worse than holding 800 unspent souls all game, because it actively debuffs you; a vault you could have stolen if stronger counts as lost soul generation. [16:27]
- Poshiepop advocates selling shortly after 10 minutes to get eight tier two buffs instead of seven tier one buffs, which by this video's method raises the egg's value from about 1,300 to about 2,000, roughly 700 extra for waiting 2 minutes past 8. [17:27]
- That 700 is a theoretical upper limit; many buffs are useless in practice, and the extra 180 souls from 90 per minute shouldn't count since that income is worth less over time. [17:58]
- Deciding between 8 and 10 minutes: if popping the egg at 8 for a power spike and losing the debuff would ever secure your own vault or steal an enemy one, sell at 8. [18:28]
- If you are very sure you won't take an enemy vault and will secure your own anyway, holding until just after 10 minutes is acceptable. [19:00]
- Fringe use seen in Eternus 6 lobbies: after 30 minutes, when buffs are maxed, buy and instantly resell the egg to farm golden statue buffs; by Poshiepop's level three values that prints about 1,200 souls. [19:32]
- By this video's method each golden statue buff after 30 minutes is worth about 200 souls at the upper end, so buying and reselling at 30 minutes returns about its own 800 cost, and probably less. [23:08]
- At 30 minutes targeted items beat random stats: a carry may want Unstoppable, Counterspell or Warp Stone to dodge key ults, whose value can be incalculable. [20:34]
- Late game gun builds don't want magazine size, they want slide distance from Fleetfoot, Burst Fire or Vampiric Burst for ammo; they don't want flat gun damage but fire rate, which is multiplicatively more effective with enough damage. [21:06]
- Late game you may not want raw HP since lifesteal gives far more effective HP, and against one damage type a 1,600 item like Battle Vest can give far more survivability than 1,600 souls of random HP. [21:37]
- Not all souls are equal: a 40K Wraith doesn't buy Heal Nova, Rescue Beam or Guardian Ward, since those are worth their cost for a support but not a carry. [22:38]
- Selling at 8 minutes is frequently optimal and gets only about 1,300 souls of value (practically less) for an 800 soul item. [23:39]
- Buying the egg probably loses you lane, and even an even lane means you would have won with a stronger early item; winning lane is worth a far bigger win rate increase than 300 to 400 extra souls. [24:09]
- In most cases don't buy the egg; it is only really purchasable on select late game hyper scaling carries that use the buffs and souls, like Wraith and perhaps Vindicta, and on supports less penalized by the early damage penalty. [24:40]
- Not most supports: The Doorman needs to win lane and is a very strong laner. Paige and Rem, who get little of their value from direct damage, can buy the egg without significant hindrance. [25:11]
- The egg in its current state is an underpowered item that will likely be buffed, similar to early Trophy Collector on release, which was bought on many heroes that shouldn't and faded as players learned. [25:41]
- The egg is more punishing than Trophy Collector because holding it too long is an enormous mistake. [26:11]
- When contesting enemy vaults properly in the 8 to 10 minute window, sell the egg at 8 minutes most of the time for a power spike; holding it for 15, 20 or 30 minutes loses far more than it gains. [26:41]
- Playing the whole game down 10% damage with 800 unspent souls makes you weaker at fighting for Urns, securing Walkers, winning team fights and securing the Mid Boss, and you farm your own camps slower. [27:12]
- On Wraith, 10% less damage can decide a kill at least every other game, worth thousands of souls; a mid game kill can turn into a Walker or Mid Boss. [27:12]
- A support holding the egg may be unable to afford core items like Heal Nova before an Urn fight, losing objectives worth thousands. [27:43]
- Summary: buy the egg only on very particular hyper carries and low damage non lane bully supports, sell it by 8 minutes in most cases to power spike and fight for vaults, otherwise shortly after 10 minutes; holding it longer griefs 99% of your games. [28:44]
