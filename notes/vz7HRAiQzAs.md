# The Best and Worst Items In Deadlock 2.0
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-05-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vz7HRAiQzAs

## Covers
Metro's winners and losers of the May 2025 item overhaul (Deadlock 2.0), recorded on stream the day after the patch. Why 800 items and full gun builds underperform, and verdicts with numbers on Cultist Sacrifice, Mercurial Magnum, Capacitor, Boundless Spirit, Healing Tempo, anti-heal, barriers, armor items and more. For players fixing builds in that patch; values will go stale.

## Claims
- 800 items give far less value than before: they cost exactly half a 1,600, whereas the old 500s let you buy almost three for one 1,600. [0:00]
- On most heroes don't spam 800 items in lane, and even spamming 1,600 items delays your build a lot. [0:30]
- The strong items start at 3.2k, and it is worth rushing some of them; a few 1,600s are really good, like Titanic Magazine, which is really strong now. [1:01]
- Run three 800 or 1,600 items maximum; more delays your build, and with fewer slots you run out in the mid game and lose a lot of souls selling items around seven minutes. [1:31]
- Cultist Sacrifice is broken and will probably get nerfed; he plans to rush it in lane on basically every hero. [2:01]
- Monster Rounds keeps you alive in lane: played passive, you shoot creeps down so fast the enemy can't push you; played aggressive, it lets you tower dive more. [2:01]
- Monster Rounds got a buff: 1.5 out of combat regen, so you can skip something like Extra Regen. Every hero also starts with more HP and gains more HP per level than last patch. [2:31]
- Order: Monster Rounds, then Cultist Sacrifice in lane, only 2,400 more since it builds from Monster Rounds. [2:31]
- Cultist Sacrifice gives 150% extra souls on the consumed creep; sacrifice a lane creep first, since it gives about 200 more than even a tier two creep, and tier three creeps shouldn't have spawned yet. [3:02]
- On the second and later cooldowns, sacrifice tier three creeps, which give a little more. [3:33]
- The buff took him from about 770 HP to about 1,100 instantly, a 33 to 40% health increase, plus 25% fire rate and ability range (the range is bugged and doesn't work). [3:33]
- The buff lasts 180 seconds, then there is about an 80 second downtime with no buff; avoid fighting during that downtime. As long as it's active it's really strong. [4:03]
- Cultist Sacrifice is amazing especially on farming heroes; some think it's better on supports, but he thinks it's good on almost anyone who benefits from fire rate. It also gives regen and resistance and damage against walkers, towers and creeps. [5:04]
- He rushes Cultist Sacrifice first unless he needs Extra Regen, then buys it third. His Wraith build runs only four 800 items, and 800 items are the biggest losers of the patch apart from a few exceptions. [5:36]
- Mystic Regeneration is a big winner: while you deal spirit damage it gives a high amount of HP regen, lasting 6 seconds. [6:06]
- Mystic Regeneration is amazing on Lady Geist and Mirage, who can apply spirit damage at all times (Malice and Mirage's 3 have no cooldown), and great on Infernus via Afterburn, Catalyst or running through enemies. [6:06]
- Mystic Regeneration stacks per hero hit, so hitting both enemies doubles the regen. [6:36]
- He doesn't run Mystic Regeneration on Wraith because you need to hit cards and don't throw them every few seconds; it heals about 4.5 HP per second only while active, versus Extra Regen's 3.5 always on, so it is only better if you permanently spam spirit damage. [6:36]
- Mystic Regeneration is probably good on Shiv if you hit your knives, though not as good as on Mirage or Lady Geist; it is one of the best 800 items. [7:06]
- Overall 800 and 1,600 items are losers, mostly low value. Intensifying Magazine is bait early because it is hard to keep up its max weapon damage bonus; Swift Striker is fine. [7:37]
- Opening Rounds is a big winner if you use your gun a lot: like a cheap Pristine Emblem, 40% weapon damage while the enemy is above 50% HP, think of it as about 20% at all times, plus spirit power. [8:09]
- The meta is a gun-spirit hybrid where spirit matters most; gun items look much weaker than spirit items and a full gun build isn't worth it. [8:39]
- Gun investment bonuses diminish past about 16k spent on gun items, so keep a mix of the three categories, with the least in vitality, since vitality scaling is the worst and green items got nerfed the hardest. [9:11]
- Mercurial Magnum is the most broken item in the game and he expects a nerf within days. It gives ammo, spirit power, fire rate, and builds from Quicksilver Reload. [9:41]
- Mercurial Magnum: imbue it on an easily spammed ability; after you cast, your whole clip deals a share of your gun damage as extra spirit damage based on your spirit power, plus a guaranteed 60 spirit damage. [9:41]
- On Wraith at about 11k he did more spirit damage than gun damage with his gun. On Infernus, put it on Napalm (his 1); you don't need much burn. [10:43]
- Every cast of the imbued ability gives a diminishing reload, so on Wraith throwing cards gives an effectively infinite magazine; at 23k net worth he did 6k spirit damage and 3k gun damage. [11:13]
- Quicksilver Reload is an amazing item in itself. [11:44]
- Tesla Bullets is stronger now because it upgrades into Capacitor (about 3k more), a skill shot that removes any active non-ultimate buff on the enemy, for example Viscous's cube. [12:46]
- Capacitor can't remove ultimate buffs (not Warden's ult) but removes Warden's spirit shield, Abrams's Infernal Resilience, Wraith's Full Auto, Vindicta's flight, Seven's Surge of Power and his 3, Lash's grapple (he thinks), Lady Geist's life drain, Kelvin's Arctic Beam and Ice Path, Infernus's Flame Dash, Grey Talon's flight, and Mo & Krill's burrow (with a bugged animation). [13:50]
- Capacitor also slows by 100% (maximum slow) and has about a 30 second cooldown, so maybe twice a fight; buy Tesla for 3k, then upgrade when you have the money and no extra slot. [14:51]
- Capacitor cannot remove Unstoppable. [15:55]
- Boundless Spirit is much stronger on many heroes: less spirit power and no 30% gun damage anymore, but every ability cast gives movement speed and healing, even if the ability misses. [15:55]
- Buy Boundless Spirit on Wraith (spam cards) or Lady Geist (spam Malice); add Healing Booster for 125 healing per cast. [16:28]
- About 30k spent is a realistic normal mid game; his Wraith build healed with zero lifesteal items, so you can skip all lifesteal items. [16:58]
- Bullet Lifesteal and Spirit Lifesteal got nerfed: they cost more and give only 16% until upgraded to Leech or Infuser. Skip them and buy Boundless Spirit, which also raises Full Auto, card and Mercurial Magnum damage. [17:59]
- Lady Geist is probably the most broken hero right now; with Boundless Spirit every cast, even Life Drain, heals her, on top of her spirit lifesteal. [18:29]
- Boundless Spirit is worse on heroes like Seven, who benefit from raw spirit power and don't spam abilities; it is strong on ability spammers. [19:00]
- Healing Tempo (6k, upgrades from Healing Booster) adds a bit more healing effectiveness and gives 40% fire rate and movement speed whenever you heal; healing means active items or healing from items, not lifesteal, but Boundless Spirit counts. [19:32]
- Healing Tempo lasts 7 seconds per heal with no cooldown, so with Boundless Spirit it is up permanently: 6k for 40% fire rate, movement speed, spirit resist, health and more healing. A crazy late game upgrade. [20:02]
- Anti-heal got nerfed across the board but is on more items: Healbane is now only 35%, Toxic Bullets about 30% (was about 40 to 45). [20:32]
- Decay is a 3k item that reduces healing by only 35%; healing reduction is now also on Crippling Headshot, Inhibitor and Spirit Burn. [21:04]
- Overall he counts it as an anti-heal nerf because until late game enemies will have less anti-heal on you, especially early and mid game. [21:34]
- Crippling Headshot gained healing reduction but is now on a cooldown and its resist shred dropped to 18% bullet and spirit, so it is debatable whether it's a buff. [21:34]
- Spirit Burn is really strong, the ultimate late game Toxic Bullets for spirit heroes: 80% healing reduction plus damage per second and an explosion when you deal 400 damage within 5 seconds. [22:06]
- Spirit Burn is great on Lash and Infernus (burns proc it within 5 seconds) and guaranteed anti-heal on Grey Talon, though raw spirit might be better there for now; it costs 6k. [22:36]
- Even against lots of anti-heal he still buys Boundless Spirit because it still gives about 40 spirit power for 6k plus the investment bonus. [23:06]
- He runs Quicksilver Reload and Surge of Power on Card Trick (card Wraith) only to keep Mercurial Magnum up. [23:36]
- Ricochet got a big buff: 25% fire rate and 65% ricochet damage applying all bullet procs, including Full Auto; every Infernus runs it now. [23:36]
- Unstoppable is almost a must on many carries because there are few other answers to CC. [24:06]
- Counterspell (3k) is low key the new Ethereal Shift: it can parry every ability, such as Paradox's swap, Holliday's lasso, Dynamo's Black Hole, Lash's Ground Strike (negating about 800 damage), Grey Talon's bird and his arrows if timed right. [24:06]
- Counterspell gives a bonus if you hit the parry and its cooldown isn't long, so it can save you from two spells a fight; a very high skill item. [24:39]
- Weapon Shielding and Spirit Shielding are bait and terrible, the worst items in the game: 1,600 for a worse Reactive Barrier. [25:10]
- Barrier now is basically bonus HP. These give 400 bonus HP only if the enemy deals 200 damage within 3.5 seconds, last 8 seconds and have a 40 second cooldown, while Reactive Barrier has half the cooldown and is much easier to proc with any slow or stun. [25:41]
- Enchanter's Emblem is insane: the old spirit armor with the old barrier effect, 18% spirit resist plus spirit power and cooldown reduction while above 65% HP, better than the old Enchanter's Barrier because you are above 65% far more often than a barrier stays up. [26:11]
- Enchanter's Emblem is not a lane item since early it only gives spirit resist; it is a high value mid game item. Battle Vest is the same, buy it mid game after laning. [26:42]
- Debuff Remover got a hard nerf: 30% debuff resist instead of about 45%, so it shortens things like Mo & Krill's ult or Holliday's lasso less, and it can't purge ultimates anymore, such as Pocket's Affliction. [27:13]
- Instead of Debuff Remover, buy Counterspell and learn to parry in time; Counterspell can parry Affliction. [27:44]
- Bullet Resilience and Spirit Resilience are like the old improved armors but worse: 30% resist, 60% below 30% health, when you are basically dead anyway; slightly cheaper but kind of bad. [28:15]
- For defense against bullets, buy Plated Armor: 30% of damage negated and 60% of on-hit effects prevented, amazing against Wraith, Seven or Haze since 60% of the time Full Auto, Power Surge and Fixation don't work. [28:46]
- Plated Armor is the new core anti-gun item, other than Metal Skin. [29:16]
- Spellbreaker (flat spirit resist, debuff resist, cuts one spirit burst by 75%, once every 14 seconds) might be bait because the 150 damage threshold is easy to hit, even a late game Wraith card procs it; he would go Plated Armor. [29:16]
- The new Infuser is kind of broken on Infernus: with Healing Booster it gives 100% spirit lifesteal, so proc it after burning someone; it builds from Spirit Lifesteal. [29:47]
- Infuser is also good on Seven, for his ult and during Power Surge. [30:17]
- A 6,400 vitality item with 20% spirit resist that cuts a random ability cooldown by 3 seconds per heavy spirit hit (the description matches Witchmail) is bad; for a tank, buy Plated Armor or Juggernaut instead. Maybe good on Shiv, not on Dynamo. [30:17]
