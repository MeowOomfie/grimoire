# How Farming In Deadlock Makes You Lose Games
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-03-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RjV40z_u5i4

## Covers
Positions (1 to 6) in Deadlock: which heroes need the most farm and which need the least, and why low-farm heroes should fight, gank and push while the carries get the jungle and the Sinner's Sacrifice golden statue buffs.
Gives a position for most of the roster and how to read your team composition in solo queue. For players who AFK farm the jungle on heroes that don't need it.

## Claims
- Unlike Dota, in Deadlock up until 8 minutes everyone around when a soul is secured in lane gets the same amount, but some heroes do far more with souls than others. [1:03]
- Kelvin is support focused and doesn't need much money; he is powerful early with healing and, even behind in souls mid game, his impact comes from Frozen Shelter and utility like his slowing beam. [1:35]
- Unless you play gun Kelvin, Kelvin is usually not the top damage dealer; he usually has the most healing. [2:06]
- Yamato is very strong in lane but needs a lot of farm after laning; for spirit Yamato the big power spike is usually Refresher, with many items needed before that. [2:06]
- Once Yamato hits her power spike she is like an unkillable carry that deals the most damage and tanks the most. [2:37]
- Mirage is a hybrid: he benefits from money but doesn't need much to come online; he is strong in lane and in the early mid game without AFK farming the jungle. [2:37]
- A patch guaranteed at least two frontliners per team, because a team with no one to absorb damage would auto-lose. [3:37]
- A comp of four raw damage dealers (for example Vindicta, Grey Talon, Wraith and Haze) is already bad whatever the last two are, lacking utility, stuns and tankiness. [4:07]
- On the new map there is more jungle and staying in lane after 8 minutes matters much less: only three Guardians and three Walkers to defend, so players have more free time. [4:37]
- Many players, with nothing attacking their towers, go AFK farm the jungle; a Pocket who won lane and farms his own jungle and Sinner's Sacrifice from 8 or 9 minutes can be why you lose at 25 minutes. [5:09]
- Pocket is a position 3 to 4 hero who doesn't need much money and has a power spike right after the laning stage with a few key items, doing a lot of burst with abilities alone. [5:40]
- Sinner's Sacrifice spawns at 8 minutes. [6:10]
- A hero like Pocket should go look for team fights and ganks into the enemy jungle instead, and give the farm to the position one or two heroes. [6:10]
- Example comp Seven, Ivy, Infernus, Mo & Krill, Abrams, Lady Geist: Infernus and Seven are positions one and two (need the most money, weaker early mid game), Lady Geist position three, Abrams position four, Mo & Krill and Ivy hard supports in five and six (interchangeable). [6:40]
- Lady Geist needs some farm to get strong but works without being the most farmed hero. [7:10]
- Abrams wants some items to become tanky but doesn't need much money to be useful. [7:41]
- Ivy is always strong without money: her ultimate (fly around, save teammates), Watcher's Covenant, Stone Form and Kudzu Bomb; as support Ivy all you really need is Healing Nova. [8:12]
- Kudzu Bomb doesn't scale well, a 0.6 spirit multiplier, so there is no reason to farm much on Ivy. [8:12]
- If Ivy farms the whole jungle and Sinner's Sacrifice while Infernus and Seven struggle in lane, there's a very good chance you lose; playing low-farm heroes and AFK farming is throwing. [8:42]
- Support players shouldn't starve themselves of money entirely. [8:42]
- Sinner's Sacrifice is insanely strong: a triple golden statue buff each time you finish them off at the right timing, on top of the souls. [9:13]
- Give Sinner's Sacrifice to your position one and two heroes, because they benefit most from the statue stats. [9:45]
- Golden statue buffs give spirit power, health, cooldown reduction, and three weapon upgrades: ammo, weapon damage and fire rate. [9:45]
- On a hero that doesn't scale with gun, going for the golden statue buffs is mostly wasted. [10:16]
- Still take a Sinner's Sacrifice if you are in the enemy jungle and the enemy is about to take it, or it's on your way. [10:16]
- Example: a Sinclair who was strong in lane left at 8 minutes to take all the team's Sinner's Sacrifice and the team lost hard; he should have ganked and pushed objectives and let the Infernus have them. [10:47]
- Farm that is on your way is always worth taking, for example as Ivy throw a Kudzu Bomb on a creep camp you pass while rotating. Efficiency is very important. [11:18]
- Pro games increasingly have one dedicated farmer, a hard carry the team funnels resources to. [11:51]
- Abrams: needs a base level of power and blows up if far behind, but holds his own on even or slightly lower money; spirit Abrams builds make him very hard to kill as the main tank. Position 3 to 4. [12:22]
- Abrams should not AFK farm the jungle after lane; in the mid game go for kills, push a tower and get an early flex slot from a Walker. [12:53]
- Bebop: position 3 to 4. Gun Bebop is insanely strong, but even poor he can get free kills by hooking someone into his team, or one-shot them in a 1v1 after a hook with Slowing Hex or Silence Glyph and a half decent gun build. [13:24]
- Calico: position 3 to 4. Very strong on the new map thanks to her cat mobility since there are fewer ziplines; strongest in lane and the mid game but scales well late. [13:54]
- On Calico, come out of lane with Torment Pulse or Hunter's Aura and gank, roam and force team fights over and over; she is not an AFK farm jungler, and the best way to get money on her is fights and kills. [14:25]
- Dynamo: support, position 5 to 6. His abilities work whatever his net worth: Singularity (black hole) does a lot if it hits multiple people, and the Kinetic Pulse knock-up works because its value is the disable, not the damage. [14:55]
- On a support you need some money, but buy Healing Nova and some increased healing items and you're fine. [15:56]
- Grey Talon: position three, more on the carry side, but not great at jungling, so he isn't one or two; he can push lanes, go for owl ganks or snipes. [15:56]
- Jungle clear speed matters for positions one and two: Wraith clears creep camps about twice as fast as Grey Talon, so even if they benefit equally from money it's not worth Grey Talon farming. [16:28]
- Haze: the first true position one to two carry; insanely efficient at farming the jungle and needs a lot of items. [16:58]
- At high level a Haze is not scary before Unstoppable or about a 6k damage item; spirit Haze is the meta and still needs many items before she can press her ult without dying instantly. [16:58]
- Position is a mix of who is fast at farming and who needs to farm. [17:29]
- Holliday: position 3 to 4. Spirit Holliday is played; she sets up fights by lassoing people into your team and chain stuns with Warp Stone and her Bounce Pad, without needing much farm. [17:29]
- Example: defending your Walker with Warp Stone, lasso someone, Warp Stone back, jump them into your Walker and they're dead. [18:01]
- Infernus: position one to two, needs a lot of money; there is always another item that makes him stronger. [18:32]
- A tanky Infernus variant (one max Catalyst, two max early, tanky items like Warp Stone) can be played as a position three or tank. [18:32]
- Ivy: clear support, position 5 to 6 in most cases; gun Ivy scales late but you should play Ivy to keep your team alive (Rescue Beam, ult to initiate and save people, Watcher's Covenant, Air Drop). [19:03]
- Kelvin is the hardest support in the game, the ultimate position six: no hero can be as poor and still do amazing; one good Frozen Shelter saving an ally and trapping an enemy gets all the value. [19:34]
- Kelvin's Arctic Beam does good damage in lane, but later it is used for the slow, to stop chasers or to stop enemies escaping. [20:04]
- If Kelvin goes into the jungle after laning and takes the Sinner's Sacrifice, he is throwing; he gains little from the ammo, fire rate and weapon damage buffs, which are worth far more on Wraith, Seven or Yamato. [20:04]
- Right now Sinner's Sacrifice is the most efficient farm. [20:34]
- Lady Geist: position three, needs a bit more money than Abrams; she clears jungle fast and can be run as a one or two, but is slow to rotate between camps. [21:05]
- Lash: position three or four, benefits from money but doesn't need the most. [21:37]
- McGinnis: worst hero in the game right now and a support, about position five; she needs some money to make her healing and turrets strong enough. [22:08]
- Mirage: a strong position three; scales well with money but is not great at farming the jungle, and wins most 1v1 duels even when not ahead. [22:08]
- Mo & Krill: position 5 to 6 and slow at farming; a 60k Yamato with a 30k Mo & Krill is better than the reverse. [22:39]
- Paradox: position 3 to 4. [23:09]
- Pocket: a strong position three whose peak is the mid game at 10 to 20 minutes before he falls off late, so don't spend time farming on him. [23:09]
- Seven needs the most money in the game to be strong, arguably along with Infernus: he needs Surge of Power and many items to start damaging, then Unstoppable for his ult. [23:09]
- Seven is probably the fastest farmer in the game: with high spirit and Surge of Power on his 3 he clears a creep camp in about one second, earning 5k in a third of the time other heroes need. [23:41]
- On Seven, be in the jungle at 8 minutes, especially farming Sinner's Sacrifice. [23:41]
- Shiv: position 3 to 4. [24:12]
- Sinclair: actually a support; the best laner in the game and a lane bully, strongest around 10 minutes and then weaker as the game goes on, so don't farm Sinner's Sacrifice at 8 minutes on him. [24:12]
- Fight when your hero is at its strongest. [24:43]
- Vindicta: position three or a strong two; needs a lot of money but is not great at farming the jungle. Spirit Vindicta with the Crow build and lots of spirit shred is the common build, and she wants to be in fights since her snipe gets money off enemy heroes. [24:43]
- Viscous: support, position 5 to 6. [25:14]
- Vyper: probably one to two, with a case for three or four since she is dangerous without much money, for example the spirit Petrifying Bola into poison combo. [25:14]
- Warden: position 3 to 4. Wraith: position 1 to 2. [25:14]
- Yamato is easily a position one; Seven and Yamato benefit the most from money of all heroes. [25:47]
- In solo queue, first understand what type of hero you're playing; if you play gun Ivy with a Seven on your team, give the Seven farm priority and focus on pushing waves and running Urns with your flight. [26:18]
- At load-in, look at your team composition and figure out where you stand; if you're one of several farm-heavy heroes, farm as efficiently as possible without letting objectives fall. [26:48]
- If you are the Kelvin and go into the jungle, you're throwing the game; supports must be okay with giving carries more money. [27:18]
- If you're the Calico in a comp with a slow-scaling Seven and Haze, perma fight, force fights and win or defend objectives to buy them time to farm. [27:18]
- Example comp: Wraith is undeniably position one and should get the most jungle and most of the Sinner's Sacrifice, then Warden two, then Calico and Holliday three, then Shiv, with Viscous the poorest. [28:18]
- If your position one isn't farming, you can take the jungle as position two, but encourage them to farm it; as Calico, meanwhile, invade for ganks and steal enemy Sinner's Sacrifice so your team doesn't suffer. [28:18]
- Don't pick a hard support like Kelvin and try to play a poor gun carry Kelvin; it lowers your team's chances. [29:20]
- Always ask: should I be the one farming the jungle or pushing objectives, and who should get the Sinner's Sacrifice to give the team the highest chance to win? [29:54]
