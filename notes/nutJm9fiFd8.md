# Stop throwing away your lead! How to END Deadlock games
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-09-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nutJm9fiFd8

## Covers
A 2024 macro video on closing out games from a lead: turning a small lead into a fight then Mid Boss, forcing Mid Boss with a big lead, pushing with the Rejuvenator, and taking late game fights once towers are gone. For players who get ahead but can't end. This is from September 2024; Mid Boss, base objectives and timings have changed since.

## Claims
- A slight lead (example: 14k souls ahead at 23 minutes) is not enough to just walk into the enemy base and end. [0:31]
- With a slight lead, look for an engage with some advantage, usually by playing around power spikes. [0:31]
- Example: Kelvin buys Curse, which makes some heroes much easier to kill; Yamato is exceptionally weak when cursed because she depends entirely on her ultimate. [0:31]
- Engage right after buying a power item like Curse, since the enemy most likely hasn't had time to check your items; curse the Yamato and kill her to start the fight with a numbers advantage. [1:01]
- Once you have a numbers advantage, keep fighting; when a second brawl breaks out elsewhere, join it to take a 5v4. [1:01]
- After winning a fight, do Mid Boss if it is up; if it is not up, push as many objectives as you can while their players are dead. [1:31]
- With about 25 seconds of enemy respawn time you can get the base Guardians and maybe one Shrine; once you see you can't get the Shrines in time, back up. [1:31]
- Mid Boss spawning soon makes it the next objective. [2:01]
- Some heroes are extremely strong at Mid Boss: Kelvin can force the Rejuvenator by using his bubble to protect it from being stolen, so a team with him can force Mid Boss whenever it wants when ahead. [2:01]
- The enemy knowing this has to contest Mid Boss before you even start it; a bad engage there is a high-risk high-reward play rather than a mistake, since if you get Mid Boss the game is probably over anyway. [2:31]
- With Mid Boss and a lead the game should pretty much be over: push objectives, and even losing or trading people doesn't matter because you have fast respawns, all lanes push, and the Mid Boss gives a health regen buff. [3:01]
- When you kill Mid Boss make sure your lanes are at least somewhat pushed; the Mid Boss creeps push lanes on their own so you don't need to shove them fully, but your waves should not be sitting at your own base. [3:01]
- Pressure multiple lanes, like pushing two lanes in Dota to take high ground; pushing only one lane can make it hard to go in. [3:31]
- With lanes pushed and the buff, go in and take a fight if you can; if you can't, shoot objectives. [3:31]
- If enemy heroes are in front of you, don't focus the objectives; if they play very passive, shoot the objectives. [4:02]
- After the first phase of the Patron, when it becomes weakened, you may want to back up depending on respawn timers: if theirs are still long stay in, if shorter leave. [4:02]
- With a huge early lead (example: about 27k souls ahead at 14 minutes, widening to 30k) you don't need a fight to force Mid Boss; go directly to it even if all enemies are alive. [4:34]
- Communicate when you want Mid Boss, by pinging it or voice chat; teammates won't always cooperate, but you can't blame them if you didn't ask. [5:04]
- When far ahead, forcing Mid Boss is usually correct because then you can end; skipping it may give enemy heroes time to come online and come back. [5:35]
- By late game most objectives are gone, so you must be able to take good fights on the map without a Walker or tower to play around (example: 36 minutes, only both Patrons left). [6:06]
- In late game look for a fight where you have either a good initiation or a numbers advantage. [6:38]
- Numbers advantages usually come from enemies splitting up to deal with waves, so constant wave pressure is still important in late game. [6:38]
- What counts as a good initiation depends on your team comp; know whether you need a specific ultimate or a combo to start a fight. [6:38]
- Example: losing your Infernus first is a bad start, but a good hook (Bebop) and a big Pocket ult can be enough initiation to win the fight. [7:09]
- Soul Rebirth is an extremely important late game item; at the 35 to 40 minute mark, highly consider buying it. [7:09]
- A pinch from two sides with an early pick (on their Wraith) is a good initiation even without a numbers advantage. [7:39]
- Before late fights ask: what does our comp need to start a fight (an ultimate, a hook, something else), did the fight start that way, and how can I make it more likely to start that way. [7:39]
- Summary point one: with a very strong early lead, force Mid Boss. [8:09]
- Summary point two: with a lead not strong enough to force Mid Boss, look for an advantageous fight somewhere, then do Mid Boss after that fight. [8:09]
- Summary point three: in late game, look for advantageous fights based on initiation and numbers. [8:39]
