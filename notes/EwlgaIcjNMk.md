# The Ultimate Guide To Wraith – Dominate Every Duel In Deadlock
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-10-06
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EwlgaIcjNMk

## Covers
Full Wraith hero guide from a 1,000 hour Wraith main: Card Trick aiming, Full Auto, Project Mind and Telekinesis, ability order, the spirit/card build in order and situational items, slide shooting and kiting, target priority, split pushing Walkers with escape routes, and the 8 minute Sinner's Sacrifice and jungle timings.
For players learning Wraith as a weak-laning, late game 1v1 hard carry.

## Claims
- Wraith is a hard carry duelist DPS with insane strength in isolated 1v1s: very bad in lane, a bit better mid game, a hard carry late. Most of her damage is spirit, with some gun and vitality items for damage and survival. [0:30]
- Most of Wraith's damage comes from Card Trick, not the gun; the cards are what you must learn to play her. [1:00]
- She is aim intensive: cards charge per bullet hit, and headshots charge them twice as fast as body shots. [1:00]
- Cards can be steered in flight; thrown close enough to an enemy they track to them, so keep your crosshair almost always on the enemy when throwing. [1:30]
- Enemies duck behind cover or dash at the last second to dodge cards; you can lead a card toward the corner they ducked behind, but out of line of sight it only tracks if very close, so time it to arrive close then curve it in. [2:00]
- Pre-throw a card at an enemy you know is behind a wall, then peek and track it onto them for a surprise hit. [3:01]
- Wraith is one of the weakest laners because level 1 cards do only 55 damage, and only 100 after the first upgrade. [3:01]
- In lane take Card Trick and get it to level 2 first. [3:31]
- Card Trick spirit scaling is 1.2, which is good (anything above 1 is good for most heroes), and most items raise card damage or spirit power. [3:31]
- Hold mouse one and spam the card key so you throw a card the moment one is ready, at all points of the game; higher fire rate means faster card charge and more damage. [4:03]
- Ability order: Card Trick first, then Full Auto maxed second; one early point in Full Auto and one early point in Project Mind; Telekinesis (ult) leveled last. [4:35]
- Full Auto was heavily nerfed: think of it as a 5 second damage boost (fire rate) at base that scales with spirit later, adding spirit damage per bullet, lifesteal at max and spirit scaling. [5:05]
- Use Full Auto only when committed to a fight where you can shoot the enemy for more than a few seconds, not when they aren't peeking. [5:36]
- Project Mind teleports you anywhere, horizontally or vertically onto rooftops; the base range is short, so take one early point to raise the cast range enough to escape most fights. [6:07]
- Use Project Mind vertically to escape ganks onto high ground, where most enemies struggle to chase. [6:38]
- Project Mind has a very long cooldown, almost 50 seconds, only reduced at max level, which comes late; use it to get out of fights, not into them, except to chase the last kill of a team fight when you're sure you won't die. [7:09]
- Project Mind level 2 gives a barrier that scales with spirit; take a point later. [7:40]
- All her abilities give decent value per level, so you neglect Project Mind and Telekinesis upgrades early; part of why she is so good late with money and levels. [8:10]
- Telekinesis throws an orb that silences and disarms the target (they can only punch); level 2 extends the duration and max level also hits another enemy near the target. [8:40]
- Telekinesis lets you win 1v1s or escape them safely: if solo ganked and too weak, throw it and they basically can't kill you. It can shut down an enemy hard carry, almost like a free Curse, and does a bit of damage. [9:10]
- Build: Monster Rounds first, essential because her early bullet damage is very low and weak heroes avoid losing lane by pushing the enemy creeps first. Headshot Booster is a more aggressive option but it is hard to win lane that way against good players. [9:41]
- Shove the enemy creeps first, then poke the enemy with cards and charge cards on them. [10:11]
- Then Quicksilver Reload, essential: it adds bonus card damage and reloads your gun on every card thrown, and since cards have basically no cooldown you proc it (and later Mercurial Magnum) constantly. [10:42]
- Then Tesla Bullets, essential: a chance per bullet to shock for spirit damage on a 0.25 second cooldown, worth it only on high fire rate heroes like Wraith; it clears waves and camps fast and adds lots of fight damage. Get it early. [11:12]
- The fire rate item the captions call "Cultist" is fine and "kind of broken right now", but the coach skips it, expecting a nerf, and goes Tesla then core items; Tesla gives more money early by clearing faster. [12:18]
- Then Spirit Shielding, almost always: Wraith's base HP and HP scaling are very low, she is very squishy early, and you want to avoid forced fights. [12:48]
- After lane (ideally even or won) sit on a side lane and farm; Wraith's sweet spot to start fighting is about 20k to 25k souls, around Mercurial Magnum and later Leech in the build. [13:18]
- Before that avoid most fights except 1v1s; she is very weak in early team fights. Keep your side lane pushed, keep your Walker safe and rotate side lane to jungle and back. [13:48]
- Wraith is great at split pushing due to single target damage; when an Urn fight breaks out across the map, pressure the enemy Walker instead of joining, to take it or pull defenders off the Urn fight. [14:18]
- Then Extended Magazine (upgraded later) for farming speed and ammo. [14:48]
- Then Surge of Power, imbued on Card Trick: it massively raises card spirit scaling and gives fire rate and movement speed. [14:48]
- Then Fleetfoot: it overlaps with Surge of Power on movement speed, but the coach tried Kinetic Dash and others, and more movement speed for kiting is better. [15:18]
- Kiting means staying in your effective range without the enemy closing in; against close range heroes like Abrams or Shiv stay mid range shooting and throwing cards so they can't reach melee range. [15:49]
- With Fleetfoot plus Surge of Power procs you reach about 14 m/s, enough to chase fleeing enemies quickly. [16:21]
- Perma slide shooting: proc Fleetfoot's active or throw a card (Surge of Power) for enough speed to keep sliding and shooting; by the time you need to reload you have another card and Quicksilver Reload, so you never reload, in camps and fights. [16:52]
- Technique: hold mouse one and press control to slide; when you feel too slow, stop sliding for a second to regain speed by walking, then slide again. [17:22]
- Then Capacitor (recently buffed with fire rate): an upgrade to Tesla Bullets with +10 damage per burst and higher proc chance. [17:53]
- Capacitor's active is a fast straight projectile that slows by 100% (the maximum slow, not a full stop) and removes all buffs on the target. [18:23]
- Capacitor removes buffs such as Seven's Power Surge, Viscous's cube (throw it at the cube and keep shooting), Warden's overshield, Billy's Blasted, and active items that counter Wraith like Metal Skin and Return Fire. [18:53]
- Upgrading Tesla to Capacitor that early gives a high weapon investment early, raising gun and Tesla damage. [19:25]
- Then Enchanter's Emblem for spirit power and survival, since a lot of meta damage is spirit; if the meta shifts to gun damage buy Battle Vest instead. [19:56]
- Then Mercurial Magnum, imbued on cards, an upgrade of Quicksilver Reload: bonus card damage and bonus spirit damage on all bullets after a card; Wraith is the best hero for it because she always has cards up. [19:56]
- From Mercurial Magnum on, build more spirit power and survivability; with Full Auto, Capacitor and cards you do insane single target damage, mostly spirit. [20:28]
- Wraith is a spirit damage hero right now; gun builds used to be good but spirit is the only way to make her work. [21:31]
- Then Escalating Resilience: on high fire rate heroes it hits the full 30% bullet resist in under half a second of shooting, lasts 24 seconds and stays active as long as you hit one bullet, so it is always maxed in fights. [21:31]
- Then Spirit Lifesteal upgraded into Leech, the best lifesteal and survivability option since she always does gun and spirit damage. Infuser lasts only 6 seconds while her carry fights last longer. [22:01]
- Around 30k, Leech plus Full Auto lifesteal heal you back very fast even against one target; once here you can win any 1v1 and most team fights. [23:04]
- The main threat late is hard stuns, since her HP stays low: buy Counterspell to negate a Lash ult or Abrams charge, and Debuff Remover against Disarming Hex or a Mo & Krill that stops you shooting. [23:34]
- Situational late items: Siphon Bullets when fights last long, as her fire rate steals every 0.8 seconds; Boundless Spirit if you need damage. [24:35]
- Boundless Spirit took spirit from about 106 to 175 in the demo: card damage 240 to 328, plus more Capacitor and Full Auto damage, and Mercurial Magnum's bonus from 67 to 99%. [24:35]
- To survive longer, Spellbreaker is the number one choice against spirit burst; Silencer is also great because her fire rate procs the silence fast and cuts enemy spirit damage. [25:36]
- As the hard carry against lots of hard stuns, buy Unstoppable to stop a Mo & Krill grab or Black Hole. [25:36]
- Late game luxury: Diviner's Kevlar, for more spirit, health and ability duration when you ult. [26:07]
- Start fights at medium distance, ideally from high ground; avoid melee range unless chasing. [26:37]
- Target priority: enemy backline damage dealers first, such as Vindicta and Grey Talon; come from the side, Telekinesis them and burst them. [27:08]
- Avoid shooting tanks unless there is no other target; pick off damage and snipers one by one. [27:08]
- If the enemy has a hard carry, you go on them as the harder 1v1 carry: Telekinesis anyone scary, then Capacitor, and with enough farm they die. [27:38]
- Wraith is about getting to the hard carry stage safely, which means using Project Mind smartly. [28:09]
- Always have an escape plan when pushing a Walker; if one enemy contests, Telekinesis disables them for 3.5 seconds, finish the Walker, then see if you can win the 1v1. [28:09]
- If more than one enemy shows, Project Mind to high ground; chasers must take the zipline unless they have movement abilities, and you can continue over rooftops or via jump pads toward middle. [28:39]
- At the mid Walker and the right side Walker, high ground and rooftops with jump pads give safe teleport escapes; with Project Mind up you really shouldn't die. [29:41]
- Project Mind can get stuck next to nearby objects and teleport you into a wall; take an extra moment to make sure the cast indicator is far from you, especially in a panic. [30:11]
- In close late fights, Project Mind right behind the enemy for the overshield (about 700 at that point) to distract them and buy HP, so it is also a fight winning tool, not only an escape. [31:13]
- At close range cards often fly over the enemy's head, so aim them at their feet; at medium range throw straight at them; at long range throw higher so they don't hit walls and curve them down near the target. [31:43]
- Wraith is deceptively easy to learn with a high skill ceiling; higher ranked enemies punish her hard before she is farmed. [32:43]
- Don't lose your lane; aim to have Tesla Bullets around 8 minutes. [32:43]
- At 8 minutes, the first thing to do is farm Sinner's Sacrifices, since she is still too weak for tier 3 camps; she benefits from their buffs more than most heroes because she uses weapon and spirit about equally. [33:14]
- After that sit on the side lane, always prioritize waves first: push the wave to the enemy Walker, then farm camps. [33:45]
- By about 10 minutes she is a good jungler: shove the green wave toward the enemy tower, then farm tier 3 camps with Full Auto and slide shooting. [34:15]
- In camps, shoot one creep and throw cards at the other two to split damage, since a card won't hit all three unless perfect; always use a card when up, otherwise shoot, and manage cards and Quicksilver Reload so you don't reload. [34:15]
- Prioritize lanes over everything else. [35:16]
