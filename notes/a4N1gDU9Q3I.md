channel: Midknight

## Covers
Coaching VOD review by Midknight of a Phantom-rank Dynamo on a kick (Kinetic Pulse) max build, April 2026 patch.
Themes: respecting minion damage, box routing (roof routes for rotation and information), over meleeing, using Arcane Surge, Slowing Hex into dash heroes, solo ults in team fights, Warp Stone and height for ults, ability points and cooldown.
Opens with a short side chat with a viewer about a gun Holliday build.

## Claims
Side chat (a viewer's gun Holliday):
- He doesn't believe in Vortex Web on Holliday, but Mystic Reverb and Boundless Spirit make sense. [1:39]
- His Holliday playstyle is about agency early: win or be useful in the first 12 minutes, then build pure support and hope your teammates are ahead enough to carry. [2:42]

Dynamo (Phantom) - lane:
- That heal was not worth it: you cost yourself more damage than you saved. Only panic heal when the health would actually save you. [0:31]
- Don't heal while you're scratching your tower; clear the wave first and then heal. [2:10]
- Box route more: 5 minutes in and not a single box route. [3:13]
- Once the tower is fragile you can just go siege it. [3:46]
- Don't heavy melee in their face. [3:46]
- You box routed well before shoving the wave in, which isn't ideal; shove first. [4:21]
- Movement is a little sloppy: several dashes without sliding for no reason. [4:53]
- With kicks in the chamber, kick the wave and kite back instead of resetting; you may not save the tower but you raise the enemy's time to kill on it. [5:23]
- Your 2 (Quantum Entanglement) makes you not the easiest hero to dive. [5:23]
- At your spirit spike you are very strong, so it's a good time to angle for a fight. [5:56]
- Heavy meleeing off your kicks is a really bad habit; the melees are too greedy. [5:56]
- You can now take a zipline while Dynamo is healing, which you could not do before. [6:26]
- When Rem ults you, run behind him: his camera sensitivity during the ult is so low he can't turn around, and if you break out even for a second you can press your 2. [6:58]
- Don't try to 1v1 a Mina, even with a greedy melee in her face. [6:58]
- Melee is not a reliable source of damage. [8:01]
- You full reset a lot for Dynamo; play slow and use your heal to get back to full instead, since resetting gives you a lot of downtime. [8:31]
- He would have bought Healing Booster before the orange spike. [9:02]

Dynamo - box routing and map:
- When you're healthy, take more aggressive roof box routes. [9:35]
- Reasons: you take boxes the enemy could otherwise steal; you rotate much faster to a fight mid than from a back route; and roofs give your team information, even just that an area is empty. [10:05]
- Before Warp Stone it is very obvious when Dynamo wants to ult you. [9:35]
- On the urn: you waited with five, then gave the urn while only down 2K. You should have swapped for it; one of you leaves to catch the mid wave. [11:11]
- If the call is to split the walker and give the urn, fine, but then the Seven should not rotate over; if the call is to contest, your team is down one because you're at the walker doing nothing. [11:41]
- You haven't been getting value from Arcane Surge: proc it before your 1 and before your ult about 90% of the time, and always before your heal. [12:44]
- He doesn't hate rushing Refresher (captioned "Reaver brush") since it guarantees a pick, though he personally plays Dynamo leaning more support. [13:14]
- Kick him and then ult him so the ult adds the bullet resist shred. [13:44]
- Don't look for picks on Shiv: he has an ability that makes him take 40% less damage, so his time to kill is too long. [13:44]
- With a hodgepodge of pick abilities (Abrams charge, Paige 3), look for invades off those. [14:46]
- As a support it's fine to box max; tell your team there are a lot of enemies on the left so they can take things on the right, eat farm if the enemy overstays, or secure safer camps and back off. [14:46]
- You should have warped before you kicked; Arcane Surge's extra range also makes the kick easier to hit. [15:49]

Dynamo - fights and items:
- Dynamo is really good into Viper because he can chain kick her. [15:49]
- Slowing Hex is great into Viper, Rem and Mina and good into Shiv; it is OP into dashes and people sleep on it. It would have been a very worthwhile buy this game. [16:20]
- A high-rank Mina has to buy Debuff Remover because she anticipates the Slowing Hex coming. [17:21]
- You have a consistent issue of only ulting one guy. Not being afraid to solo ult is good, but in a team fight only solo ult a carry, not a low-impact enemy. [16:50]
- An enemy forcing a fight with bad waves is a mistake on their part. [16:50]
- If you build ult items like duration and Expansion, put a couple points into the ult. [18:21]
- Don't fight outside your base when they have Rejuv and are pushing into you; fight with the height. [18:51]
- Killing him is worth losing some base guardians. [18:51]
- Someone should have pushed one more wave on that side. [19:22]
- Grab the comeback urn off a pick. [19:22]
- Choosing kick points instead of ult points is fine. [19:54]
- Old Dynamo order was a point in 1, max heal, max ult; now the 2 max is quite good. He would put a point in Quantum Entanglement earlier, and points into the ult and 2 rather than only kick. [19:54]
- The extra 6m range on the 2 makes it usable as a kite and movement ability. [20:26]
- A Slowing Hex would have done what that solo ult did. [20:26]
- Take mid boss off spawn. [20:56]
- Go for the walker and then deny the urn; if your team is winning the fight, still grab the urn to run it. [21:29]
- Meleeing walkers is good. [21:59]
- You have no cooldown item, which is bad: Dynamo loves cooldown, and even on a kick build the kicks have long cooldowns. [21:59]
- You didn't need the Warp Stone there; you were already going to make it. [22:43]
- Warp Stone lets you warp ult, and also lets you stand further back to see the fight. If you struggle to land high-value Dynamo ults, come into fights from heights for a bird's-eye view, then ult where enemies group. [23:15]
- Pick up soul bags; many players don't. [23:45]
- After a good ult in the pit, don't step out of it; you nearly died. [24:16]

Summary notes:
- In lane, take less minion damage, and box route more (none before 5 minutes). [24:47]
- Don't heavy melee in their face. [25:18]
- Use Arcane Surge before your 1 and ult, and always before your 3. [25:18]
- Slowing Hex would have felt really good this game. [25:18]
- Solo ulting is good, but in a team fight look for more targets or make the solo ult on the carry. [25:18]
