# The Deadlock Hero Tier List - Shiv Is Broken Edition
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-07-08
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DHRO3E4u-SU

## Covers
Full hero tier list for the patch current on 2025-07-08 (ultimate cooldowns nerfed 10%, spirit item investment bonus raised 25%, gun and melee damage nerfed, HP and Fortitude nerfed).
Argues the patch widened the gap between the best and worst heroes into a "tank or burst" deathball meta, and that win rate alone misleads.
For ranked players picking a hero; covers every hero's placement plus build tips for McGinnis, Holliday, Ivy, Warden, Bebop, Viscous, Shiv and Vyper counters.

## Claims
- The patch increased the win rate gap between the highest and lowest win rate heroes compared to before, from Ascendant to Eternus. [0:31]
- Win rate alone does not show how good a hero is: Shiv has historically had a low win rate and is one of the most broken heroes, if not the most broken. [0:31]
- The patch made strong heroes stronger and made many others unplayable, creating a two-class divide of very strong and unplayable heroes. [1:01]
- Calico is weak, the lowest he has ever rated her, partly because she used a lot of melee and melee and weapon damage were nerfed. [1:35]
- Calico's box-cutting rotations got weaker, she needs to fight constantly, there is slightly less fighting, lane is more important, and she is not great in lane. [2:06]
- Calico falls in the squishy category now because her HP and regen were nerfed multiple times. [4:07]
- The 10% ultimate cooldown nerf hit Dynamo hardest because he already has the longest ultimate cooldown in the game. [2:36]
- Nerfing ultimate cooldowns means you can force an enemy ultimate in one fight and take the next fight while they don't have it. [3:07]
- Dynamo got a bit better with some spirit investment because his knock-up does a lot of damage; he is a decent support. [3:07]
- Dynamo first goes to C because he is weak in lane and lane matters a lot; once Singularity (black hole) is on cooldown he is useless in constant 6v6 fights. [3:07]
- The deathball and snowball meta did not change: constant 6v6 team fighting the entire game. [3:37]
- Final call on Dynamo is B, since his ultimate makes C too low. [33:15]
- Grey Talon may be the worst hero in the game; the spirit investment buffs did not help him enough. [3:37]
- Grey Talon is very squishy and dies if caught; good late game damage, mid in lane, very weak mid game. [4:07]
- HP is very hard to come by now; low HP heroes are bad this patch. [4:38]
- Haze is rated low: the silence wave plus ultimate pickoff play style still works, but it cannot kill the tanky meta heroes. [5:09]
- Haze needs a lot of farm and is squishy; she has one strong mid-game window if she gets farm and the silence wave item early, but a 40k Haze is no scarier than a 30k one. [5:09]
- Holliday is B: high survivability from movement, Lasso is still insane, strong lane and lots of spirit burst. He will not rate Haze above Holliday. [5:40]
- Holliday tip: go back to the Rapid Recharge barrel play style, throw multiple barrels, build spirit damage for barrels plus survivability; people misplay her by playing for the ultimate only. [6:11]
- Infernus is still very solid despite big nerfs: high B, maybe low A. [7:11]
- Infernus's gun does less damage and the Ricochet and Inhibitor play style got much weaker, so build a bit more spirit on him; he can still extend burns. [7:41]
- McGinnis is the first big winner of the patch: buff after buff plus an HP-per-boon buff, and HP is the most valuable resource now that Fortitude is neutered. [8:11]
- Few items give a lot of HP that are worth the money anymore. [8:42]
- McGinnis's turrets and gun were buffed over recent patches, and turrets also got relatively better because Ricochet was nerfed. [8:42]
- McGinnis can be built carry, support, hybrid or spirit and all seem viable; best is a hybrid mix of gun and spirit. [9:14]
- On McGinnis, put down the healing for your team and pop Healing Tempo for a team-wide fire rate increase. [9:14]
- McGinnis is A at the lowest, almost S; she does more damage than any other left clicker, more than Mirage, Infernus, Wraith or Haze, "a better Wraith". [9:44]
- McGinnis's wall is insane; her ultimate is underwhelming unless you build for it; she is a tanky DPS, an actual ADC carry. The main takeaway of the video is that McGinnis is ridiculous. [10:15]
- Mirage got slightly weaker because he can no longer spam Djinn's Mark in lane from far away, but he is still one of the most annoying laners. [10:45]
- Mirage has some of the highest potential through his teleport ultimate: in a perma team fight meta he can split push, burst a walker and teleport into the fight. [11:15]
- The strongest gun in the game right now is McGinnis's; gun heroes notice the gun nerfs. He considers Mirage for A. [11:46]
- Pocket benefits most from the 25% spirit investment increase because he already builds mostly spirit and benefits from raw spirit. [11:46]
- Pocket's play: have an okay lane, farm the jungle for about two minutes, then go around the map killing everyone, and keep doing that with the money; he can now one-shot people late game. [12:16]
- Pocket is S tier, top five if played well, but not easy to learn; his only weakness is being a solid rather than elite laner. [13:18]
- Mo & Krill got better because high base HP and regen matter in this meta; he settles on B, solid but not meta defining. [13:18]
- The meta is a tank meta plus Pocket, because Pocket can teleport out after bursting no matter his HP. [13:48]
- Seven's ultimate was nerfed 10%; he is low S tier but not the best hero, contrary to an NA consensus. [14:19]
- Seven built shred, or the hybrid ultimate build, is the only hero that can deal damage to tanks late game; that says the tanks are too strong, not that Seven is OP. [14:50]
- No left clicker other than arguably McGinnis can kill tanks right now. [15:22]
- Shiv is the best hero in the game by far; he was already overpowered and got better because everyone else's HP was nerfed. [15:22]
- Fortitude was best value on low HP heroes (about 40% extra HP on a 1,000 HP Wraith) but only about 10% on Shiv. [15:52]
- Shiv buys the shielding items, which were not changed and are best on him because Bloodletting lets him take no damage, then Return Fire; he is nearly unkillable. [16:22]
- Sinclair's rabbit attacks were made less oppressive, but his lane is unchanged or better: still the best laner in the game. [16:54]
- Sinclair wins lane in 3 to 4 minutes and nukes you repeatedly; he falls off somewhat late game so not S, but has insane impact and can steal any ultimate in the game. [16:54]
- Shiv's win rate has always been below 50%, including at release when he was the most broken hero; a friend's line: when Shiv's win rate goes above 50% the game is dead. [17:25]
- Shiv and Pocket have a learning curve, which drags their win rates down. [18:26]
- By Eternus or Ascendant-plus win rate alone, Ivy would be top three and Vyper top five, which he does not agree with. [18:26]
- Vyper is a pub stomper and feared in Archon, but she is not OP. [18:56]
- Pick rate is a better indicator of hero strength than win rate, but popular heroes like Haze and Lash always have high pick rates; Statlocker's blended score of pick rate, win rate and impact tells a more accurate story. [18:56]
- Viscous is still ridiculous in pro play but B tier in ladder play. [19:56]
- Viscous's Splatter got better: buy green items like Warp Stone plus some purple items for big Splatter and ultimate damage. [19:56]
- The best Viscous play style in competitive is still the ultimate play style: wait for the ball and roll around. Puddle Punch is weak; do not run melee Viscous unless you are excellent at melee. [20:26]
- Abrams is the second best hero after Shiv, and may be better in lower ranks because he is easier to play. [20:56]
- Abrams has the best upgraded kit in the game, every ability upgrade is valuable (Infernal Resilience tier three is crazy), so you must choose where points go. [21:26]
- Abrams gets stronger the longer the game goes unless he falls behind in money, and he is doing well in every rank. [21:26]
- HP is king: this is a brawl deathball meta where you either instantly burst the enemy or are very tanky. [21:58]
- Bebop is middle of the pack: very tanky, but his lane is much weaker than a few patches ago, and after lane he is not scary. [22:28]
- Bomb Bebop is not back: his spirit scaling depends on stacks and is not worth it; run gun with Toxic Bullets, hook, uppercut and shoot. His ultimate is a bit better, so a hybrid with a few purple items can work. [22:58]
- Ivy is probably the best support in the game other than Kelvin, though her impact is hard to see: a fight swing often comes from her silencing five enemies, which only shows on replay. [23:29]
- Ivy is one of the best lane shovers: sit safely in the back and throw Kudzu Bomb to nuke the wave. [23:59]
- Build Ivy as a hybrid support with Heroic Aura (team-wide bullet resist) plus a mix of purple and green items, never as a carry; her downside is a weak lane. [24:30]
- In Eternus McGinnis has about a 66% win rate and Grey Talon about 33%, so picking McGinnis makes you about twice as likely to win. [24:30]
- Kelvin is broken: a tank who ignores the gun nerfs, his spirit is arguably stronger, and Frozen Shelter (dome) is one of the best ultimates. [25:01]
- The one Kelvin change: he can no longer drop the dome to get a free Urn, which is a good change. [25:32]
- Lady Geist got hit hard by nerfs: bomb disarm makes her bombs hard to hit consistently, she is weaker in lane, and her Malice was nerfed; she is now rated low after months at S plus. [25:32]
- Life Swap is still a great ability on Lady Geist, but she does far less overall. [26:04]
- Lash is B (C would be too low): the Flock changes hurt him and if you keep your distance in lane he does little. [26:35]
- Counterspell is one of the best items against Lash; with everyone buying it, his four-man ultimates often hit no one. Lash is middle of the pack. [27:06]
- Paradox is butchered: Kinetic Carbine damage was cut hard after a patch where she was already mid. [27:36]
- A good way to gauge the meta is to watch Michaels's stream and see what he plays (Shiv, Pocket, Yamato), except for gun heroes, which he does not play. [28:38]
- Vindicta is A: she can shred and is oppressive in lane, but she is squishy and needs people in front, so with a good comp she is A plus and with a bad one C. [29:09]
- Vyper got a bit better relative to other gun heroes because the rest of the gun heroes are bad now. [29:39]
- Against Vyper you need stuns; if your team has none, buy Knockdown or Return Fire, which kills her when she attacks; buy something that stops her slide or makes her damage hurt herself. [30:09]
- Warden is still very solid and good in lane; build hybrid leaning spirit since his ultimate scaling was buffed and purple items are valuable. [30:40]
- The best Warden build is probably the Majestic Leap ultimate build, but gun still works. [30:40]
- Wraith is the second worst hero in the game: the worst laner, and even 10 to 15k up she struggles to stay alive or kill. [31:10]
- Wraith is only dangerous when 15k ahead, where any hero would be; Telekinesis (lift) is a good 1v1 ability but the all-team-fighting meta does not suit her. [32:13]
- Yamato is very solid, tanky and strong in lane, but suffers in the Counterspell meta since countering her 1 is very effective; not quite S. [32:45]
- Summary: Lady Geist and Calico were finally brought down; Shiv, Kelvin, Pocket and now Abrams are the standouts. [33:15]
- Playing anything below B is close to throwing; B tier heroes just exist and hope the team carries. Do not pick C or D heroes. [33:46]
