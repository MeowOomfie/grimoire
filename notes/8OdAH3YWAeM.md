# Meta Report - July 14th - Seven reigns supreme...
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-07-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OdAH3YWAeM

## Covers
Short meta report on the patch live as of 2025-07-14 (after the spirit changes): the S tier heroes Seven, Shiv, Abrams, Mirage and Vindicta with how to build them, plus the outlier items Restorative Shot, Counterspell and Spirit Rend. Pub tier list that assumes the player is good at the hero.

## Claims
- The tier list is for pubs, not competitive play, but assumes the player is good at the hero; ordering within a tier doesn't mean much. [1:02] [0:31]
- Since the stream version he moved Vindicta into S tier, and moved Wraith and Sinclair up a tier. [0:00] [0:31]
- Sinclair is really bad at lower rank because he is hard to pilot, but he is still a good hero. [1:02] [1:32]
- Seven: S tier. He was already a menace in pubs (weaker in competitive only because laning matters more there), and the spirit changes buffed him significantly so he does much more damage. [1:32] [2:03]
- The meta shifted toward spirit, which raises Seven's value because one point in his third ability (Power Surge) gives 15% spirit shred. [2:03]
- Seven builds that all work right now: full ult build (max ult early, early Unstoppable, play around the ult), a Power Surge build, or maxing Lightning Ball first or second. [2:33]
- Shiv: one of the strongest heroes in the game and exceptionally strong now, even though his win rate doesn't show it as much, because people don't pilot or build him well. [3:04] [3:34]
- Community fix ideas for Shiv (remove Bloodletting, change rage, flat damage execute, non-refreshing execute) have some merit, but there are many ways to fix him. [3:34]
- Shiv builds: a weapon focused build, or a spirit focused build with Arctic Blast, Cold Front and Warp Stone played around his dash; both viable, slight edge to the weapon build. [4:06]
- Abrams: S tier, even though Abrams players like Lona call him A tier because of a weak laning stage; Lona agrees he is completely dominant in the mid game. [4:37]
- Most pub players can't punish Abrams in lane enough for that weakness to matter, so he stays S tier. [4:37] [5:08]
- Abrams build: take some ability points and max Siphon Life for a mid-game power spike, then fight people a lot; he feels unkillable once he gets going. [5:08] [5:38]
- Mirage: S tier. Like Shiv, meta changes barely affect him. [5:38]
- Mirage's only change was a very slight nerf: Djinn's Mark no longer applies beyond 45 m; right direction but nowhere near enough. [6:09]
- Answers to Mirage are Suppressor and other anti-weapon items, but he is still oppressive. [6:09]
- With Restorative Shot and Mystic Regeneration, Mirage is very hard to deal with in lane: if you can't burst him from full to zero you won't kill him and he whittles you down. His global ultimate is still very strong. [6:39]
- Vindicta: S tier, moved up from A. In organized play she gets counter-picked in draft (Lash, Haze, focusing her out of the sky), but in pubs you can't counter her well. [7:09]
- Vindicta's double Stake is incredibly oppressive, her laning stage is strong, and Crow Familiar is still ridiculous. She may not be quite as strong as the other S tier heroes. [7:09] [7:40]
- Vindicta build: go more spirit, around the debuffs, playing around the double tether; you do a lot of damage and give your team lots of utility. [7:40]
- Restorative Shot is incredibly powerful and overtuned: 50 healing from heroes; used off cooldown on heroes it is a total of 8.3 HP regen, on NPCs a bit over 3 HP regen, so off cooldown it beats Extra Regen. [8:11]
- Restorative Shot also gives 8% weapon damage plus the weapon investment bonus (an extra 7% if it is your first weapon item), about 15% weapon damage in total. He expects it to be nerfed again. [8:11] [8:43]
- Counterspell is now completely insane: 20 second cooldown, 8 spirit power, 100 health, and on a successful spell parry it heals you and gives spirit power and move speed for 6 seconds. [8:43] [9:13]
- Counterspell can block many ultimates, such as Lash's, Infernus's, Wraith's and Mo & Krill's, plus abilities that define a hero's kit. [9:13]
- Counterspell makes you fully immune to any spell used during its 1 second parry window, so a Pocket combo shorter than 1 second is blocked entirely; it can block hundreds to over a thousand damage. [9:43]
- Counterspell is a very strong Mid Boss item: stand on the crystal, Counterspell, and no stun lands, even Ivy's Stone Form. Buy it on almost every hero in a lot of games. [9:43] [10:14]
- Spirit Rend is a very valuable buy in this spirit meta because spirit resist is common; shred is worth more the more resist the target has. [10:14] [10:46]
- Don't buy Spirit Rend very early; buy it once many opponents have 20 to 30% spirit resist. [10:46]
- Two Spirit Rends on one team is fine, maybe three, more is overkill: headshots from different players ignore the 2.5 second cooldown and reach the max of four stacks faster, but the max stacks don't change (he hasn't tested this extensively). [10:46] [11:16]
- After two headshots on a target, Spirit Rend gives more shred than Crippling Headshot for spirit damage, plus a little lifesteal. [11:16]
