## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a relaxed Christmas-week free coaching stream (December 22, 2025, late in the patch before the January big patch) and reviews four games.
Player 1 is a Phantom gun Ivy, Player 2 an Ascendant 6 gun Doorman, Player 3 (Wolf) a high-rank player on his first Paige game, and Player 4 a South American player who laned against the region's number one Grey Talon (hero not clear from the captions).
Themes: why Ivy, Infernus and gun heroes are weak this patch, farming and box routing for weak laners, Cultist timing, audio parry drills, urn routing, comeback urn, shield order on Paige, ult usage, wave priority and fight macro.
