# The OFFICIAL Deadlock Hero Tier List - Calico Is Still Broken Edition
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-06-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=beZYWx0UF5w

## Covers
Hero tier list for ladder (ranked) play for the patch current on 2025-06-07, a few weeks after the item rework; not rated for pro play, so supports that need follow-up rank lower.
Places every hero, with build notes for Calico, Haze, Ivy, Kelvin, Paradox, Seven, Warden, Vindicta and how to punish Wraith.
For ranked players who want to know which heroes can carry solo queue.

## Claims
- The list rates ladder play, not pro play; in ladder you can't hard carry on a hard support like Dynamo because your team may not follow up. [0:00]
- Bebop is probably the weakest hero in the game but still playable: still strong in lane, but everyone has more HP since the item rework so hooks under tower get fewer kills, and he is much weaker in early mid game. [0:31]
- Holliday is low S: not prime Holliday with permanent barrels and staggers; she is used for a strong lane and her ultimate, the best initiation in the game other than Paradox. [1:02]
- Holliday is one of the best heroes at getting out of fights and relocating, with insane mobility; with a half-decent team, lasso in and survive, and you dictate game flow. [1:32]
- Lady Geist is S plus in ranked, top two or three: dominates lane and mid game, can hard carry, can be tank or DPS, with insane laning and sustain, so she can pick up the slack for a weak team. [2:03]
- Lash is solid and perfectly balanced: mid in lane, a great team fight ultimate, but a lot counters spirit damage now and he is weaker at bursting than Pocket. [2:35]
- McGinnis is not as strong as some say: an okay support, a really good split pusher, rarely seen as a carry in any rank; solid, better than before, and not as bad as Bebop. [3:05]
- Mo & Krill is A: he can't die if played properly, is insane in lane and has few weaknesses, though your team must follow up on his ultimate. [3:36]
- Paradox is meta defining S tier, rated even higher in pro play; she is not a hard carry in ranked. [4:06]
- Best Paradox play style is still the initiator: Silence Wall then swap enemies through the wall; Kinetic Carbine builds do a lot of damage and are fun but are not the best. [4:36]
- Pocket is 100% A, top two or three pick in pro play: highest spirit burst in the game, one-shots anything with decent farm, and if he leaves lane even or ahead he can dictate the mid game alone. [5:06]
- Pocket falls off later, which is why he is not S, but new items like Arctic Blast keep him viable longer; win a few big mid game team fights and the walkers fall. [5:36]
- Seven is solid but doesn't do anything great; Wraith is a stronger hard carry. Best Seven build is ultimate AoE plus shred; he is very scary late game and weak before then. [6:06]
- Shiv is at least A and he would personally put him S: the tankiest hero with insane damage, not a glass cannon; hard CC limits him in ranked. [6:36]
- Sinclair is meta defining broken but rarely played because he has one of the highest skill barriers: a good one one-shots you in lane, you can't win the lane, then he hexes everyone and punches them. [7:36]
- Sinclair's Vexing Bolt is much stronger after buffs and scales; he is close to S plus but rated below Lady Geist. [8:06]
- Vindicta is C, the worst she has felt: still played as a debuffer (Crow Familiar, Stake, Alchemical Fire), a good support and shred hero, but her hard carry days are over and she is team reliant. [8:37]
- Think of Vindicta as an AoE debuffer, and an AoE debuffer doesn't carry; she is playable in certain comps if your team is good. [9:07]
- Grey Talon is about the same tier as Vindicta: big nerfs to his early arrow damage made him far less oppressive in lane, and like Vindicta he needs people in front of him, though slightly less team dependent. [9:38]
- Vyper is A, he wanted S: cheesy and easy to play, insane burst damage, insanely survivable and forgiving; without enemy hard CC she just slides out and never gets caught. [10:39]
- Warden is easily S tier and ultimate Warden is the way to go: Lightning Scroll lets him wreck the enemy team in fights. [11:40]
- Gun Warden is still viable despite a small gun nerf: solid in lane, good mid game, scales into late game, and his ultimate is scarier than Seven's because it moves and can chase. [12:10]
- Warden can be built as the main raw gun damage dealer or more supportive, depending on what the team needs. [12:40]
- Wraith is high A: the best damage dealer mid to late game if she gets farm, but one of the weakest laners and she was nerfed a lot recently. [13:10]
- If a farmed Wraith comes out of your jungle at 25 minutes and wrecks you, the mistake was letting her get there; ladder players are bad at punishing farming, so gank her, win lane against her and shut her down early. [13:41]
- Wraith is still the best mouse-one hard carry if she gets farm, with Warden up there too. [14:11]
- Abrams is mid at best, maybe C: Shoulder Charge has about double the cooldown, roughly 40 seconds at level one, and needs two points to guarantee a heavy melee. [14:43]
- With points forced into charge, Abrams can't level his 1 for survivability or other abilities, e.g. to steal mid; he needs too many points to get online and is worse in lane. [14:43]
- Calico is the only hero more broken than Lady Geist in ranked, still overpowered after many nerfs, helped by certain items. [15:44]
- Calico builds: full melee with Crushing Fist, or better, full spirit with Arctic Blast: jump in, Arctic Blast, then Gloom Bombs all connect on frozen targets, then she goes "scourge" (as captioned) and runs around in it unkillable. [15:44]
- Boxes are still strong and Calico is the best box farmer: she does well in lane, goes cat form to farm the map's boxes, then comes out and kills you repeatedly. She can be built very tanky or for damage. [16:14]
- Dynamo is A, S tier if your team is good: the best ultimate in the game but teammates often don't shoot targets inside the black hole; very tanky, bad in lane, insane support. [17:15]
- Dynamo feels much tankier, he thinks because his HP per boon has been high since the three-item patch. [17:45]
- Haze is the best B tier hero (he considered low A): play her as a ganker, not an AFK farmer; build the silence wave item, press silence wave and ultimate, and whatever you hit dies. [18:15]
- If you want a hard carry, play Wraith, Seven or Infernus rather than Haze. [18:45]
- Infernus is A (low or high debatable): his early and mid game is much better than before and his carry potential is on par or better. [19:16]
- Ivy is B in ranked: a support hero, play her support and ultimate; Gun Ivy is inferior to Wraith, Haze or Infernus, but her ultimate is one of the best and she can save teammates. [19:46]
- Kelvin is S plus: farmed, he builds Arctic Blast, Suppressor, Mystic Slow and presses Infuser and outdamages any carry with long-range AoE you can't shoot back at. [20:16]
- Kelvin walks his ice path above you, has one of the best ultimates, AoE heals, can't die, can be built support or damage, and is dominant in lane; he wants Kelvin nerfed. [20:48]
- Viscous is S: Viscous players claim he is bad, then one-shot you; ball, cube and ultimate are insane, he is good in lane, and he takes some mechanical skill. [21:19]
- Viscous's ultimate counterargument: with Superior Cooldown you have the ball for the entire fight, and good players only fight when the ball is up. [22:21]
- Yamato is A; not much more to say than that she is really good. [22:52]
- Mirage is S: the best left clicker, does damage at every stage, Siphon Bullets, Inhibitor and Ricochet let him hit everyone at once, Tornado is an escape and stun, and his ultimate teleports him into or out of any fight. [23:22]
- Mirage is probably the best hero in pro play, first banned every game, and also insane on ladder; you can't trade against him in lane because he perma-applies Djinn's Mark stacks and scarabs heal him back. [23:54]
