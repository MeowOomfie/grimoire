# The Only Lane Guide You'll Ever Need | Deadlock Laning Guide
channel: Shotty  date: 2026-10-07  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SNmByf4pHHs

## Covers
Laning after the map update: health bar plus souls model, power spikes (Vindicta), 0-2 min poke and deny, lane power positions and cover, tier one camp, second item (Extra Health, Extra Regen, Healing Rite), 3 min boxes and box routes, unsecured souls and mid shop, bridge buff, solo lane against a freeze, rooftop escape, missing calls, guardians, Sinner's Sacrifice, supports giving buffs to carries, invading and roaming, mid lane guide (bridge top and bottom, mantle parries, quick melee mistakes, long range aim, 30 second box routes, side buildings, 5 min sewer boxes, the trap Sinner's room), Billy, Rat King, Shiv, Mina, Lady Geist, Kelvin, Mo & Krill, Paige

## Claims
- With the map changes from the new update, laning has completely changed. [0:00]
- Think of the lane phase as one health bar made of your health plus your lane partner's, against the enemy pair's combined health bar, plus a souls counter for each side. [0:31]
- Lane is more about the advantages you create or your hero's inherent advantages, because there is no time for many items and little team fighting; it depends mostly on your own skill, positioning and how you play fights. [0:31]
- You only have two ways to get ahead in lane: get more souls than the enemy, or have more health and win more trades; more souls means easier kills and lower health means easier deaths. [1:01]
- Some heroes are really strong early, and a newly released hero you face is probably stronger than you in lane, but much of that can be removed by playing properly or waiting until your hero gets strong. [1:31]
- On Vindicta he does not like to fight until he has unlocked his ultimate and put two points into it, because then one snipe shot does about 400 damage and almost no one can contest him, so he almost instantly wins lane from that point. [1:31]
- The common mistake is not reaching that point: playing too aggressive or too passive, or not knowing when to push up, and throwing the lead. [2:02]
- Look up a guide for your hero to learn when it gets strong (two points in an ability, or a certain item) and fight the most in lane from that point. [2:33]
- On the new map fights are more spaced out, so aim matters a lot more, especially in mid lane, which is now the hardest lane. [2:33]
- Troopers spawn every 30 seconds, so schedule your timings around waves (for example, leave right after killing a wave and plan to be back by about 4:30 or 4:45). [20:23]
- From 0 to 2 minutes the only goal is poke damage, kill minions, confirm, deny and repeat. [3:36]
- Be careful in the first 2 minutes because you have the fewest souls and least damage; play too far up and get shot by minions and you take far too much damage. [3:36]
- If neither side gets denied both sides get the same souls, so taking damage hands the enemy the advantage; try to win the HP trade instead. [4:07]
- Trading HP early is easier for gun heroes since they need not get close, and you can often take half the enemy's HP in the first few minutes because many players position badly. [4:07]
- Each lane has its own cover and power positions, for example a side area next to the tower in each lane (one has a gazebo you can stand on) and the two buildings you can stand inside in mid lane. [4:38]
- Controlling a power position lets you poke, pressure and shoot the enemy's minions for free unless they force you out, and the players who win lane usually dominate those spots early. [4:38]
- In mid lane, bridge control lets you shoot the enemy, poke and kill the wave without much repercussion. [4:38]
- At 2 minutes the tier one camps (three small minions) spawn; whether to fight for the enemy's or defend yours depends on your hero. [5:09]
- Some heroes one-shot the camp, like Mina and sort of Lady Geist; others need many abilities, more time or heavy punches to kill it. [5:40]
- If both of you are good at killing camps there is no reason not to fight for it; if you are weaker at camps and the enemy is stronger, use the camp as bait to make them position badly so you can damage them. [5:40]
- Do not contest the camp much against close quarters heroes like Billy, Rat King or Shiv, because you risk half your HP for only about 100 souls and lose the HP trade and the lane. [6:10]
- By this point you can buy two items; the first is usually the same every lane (Monster Rounds, High-Velocity Rounds, Mystic Burst or whatever your hero builds). [6:41]
- Decide the second item by how the lane is going: if you are losing trades or taking too much damage, buy Extra Health, Extra Regen or Healing Rite, since they make you win the HP trade even when tied on souls. [6:41]
- Boxes spawn at 3 minutes; with Extra Regen or Healing Rite you can leave for boxes while low, come back at full health with many souls, and gain a big lead if the enemy does not do the same. [7:12]
- 3 minutes is the point where everybody loses lane and the most important point of the game if you do not want to play from behind, because until then souls should be equal except the tier one camp and the only edge was damage or kills. [7:43]
- In lower ranks, if you have a duo, one of you should leave lane for at least 1:30 and run as many boxes as possible, especially the heavy boxes, which are not random and always have souls. [8:13]
- Box runner route: go all the way back to just before your base guardians (walking if needed), take the zip line breaking boxes on the way, walk from that base guardian to your mid base guardian, then work back to your lane's tower guardian. [8:44]
- Done quickly, the runner is back in time for the bridge buff or the lane, and if the lane partner plays the solo wave well both end up with similar souls despite the long route. [9:14]
- Spend your souls at the mid shop before going to the bridge buff, because souls from camps and boxes are haunted (unsecured) souls that you drop if you die. [9:44]
- A big mistake is coming back from a box or camp route and fighting right away, dropping 400 unsecured souls to the enemy and throwing the lane. [10:15]
- If you have no big bag, go take the bridge buff before everyone else; with decent aim you can shoot them off the zip line before they contest, for a free bridge buff. [10:15]
- Treat the bridge buff as a fake souls injection worth about a 1,600 soul item; whoever gets it can fight for a health or souls lead and pressure the guardian to win lane. [10:46]
- If the enemy gets the bridge buff it is not a big deal; play back and wait for it to expire. [11:17]
- If you solo lane while your partner runs boxes and the enemy freezes the wave on their side so you must walk up to get it, call your partner back or you will lose. [11:17]
- If they just kill the wave and let it come to you, sit at the guardian behind cover, shoot the heal minion first, then every other minion, and repeat. [11:47]
- Every lane except mid has a rooftop near it; if they dive you, climb up and circle the rooftop so they give up or take heavy guardian damage and you win the trade. [11:47]
- You must see the dive coming before it happens or they can stun and kill you fast; once someone walks up the staircase, get on the rooftop and shoot minions from there. [12:18]
- If you are the one in lane and want the bridge buff, have the whole wave killed by about 4:50, then take the bounce pad or walk and climb the ladder so you are set up when it spawns. [12:18]
- From 3 minutes, start calling the enemy laner missing (hold Tab, middle click their icon) in case they run boxes and gank another lane. [12:49]
- Overcall missing enemies; calling too often only makes teammates play a bit more passive, while not calling gets them killed. [13:19]
- Also call missing after you kill someone, since they may zip line straight to another lane. [13:49]
- 8 minutes is usually the end of lane; if you have not taken a guardian by then you probably will right after. [13:49]
- Guardians are more important than they used to be but still not super valuable, so never die attacking or defending one. [13:49]
- If you die for a guardian they usually take it anyway and the map opens up: after 8 minutes they take your Sinner's Sacrifice (the vending machines), camps and boxes too. [14:19]
- Sinner's Sacrifice gives very good souls and permanent buffs and is one of the biggest swing points for scaling into the late game, since more permanent buffs make every item stronger. [14:19]
- Playing for your life matters more than protecting a guardian. [14:49]
- Supports like Kelvin or Mo & Krill should usually give the permanent buffs to a decent carry instead of grabbing Sinner's Sacrifice themselves, since the buffs help others more. [14:49]
- In solo queue always take the Sinner's Sacrifice yourself and rely on your own scaling rather than a random teammate. [15:20]
- If you are winning lane and have the guardian before 8 minutes, invade the enemy's side of the map. [15:50]
- After winning lane you should also roam, spreading the advantage to help other lanes snowball. [15:50]
- If you do not roam, steal their Sinner's Sacrifice, but do not waste stamina on the way so you can escape when they contest and not drop your bag. [16:20]
- Mid lane is the hardest lane and needs more skill than before, because diving is much easier: there is no rooftop, and the only real escape is going underground in lane, which from your own side usually gets you chased and killed. [16:50]
- In mid lane for the first 2 minutes, put one player on top of the bridge and one below it. [16:50]
- Whoever has the better gun should be on top of the bridge, often the squishy M1 heroes like Vindicta or even Rat King, but only if you can lead Rat King's slow projectile shots. [17:20]
- Put the closer range, higher HP hero, for example Billy, under the bridge. [17:20]
- From the top you pressure the lane and anyone going for shop or troopers; the enemy must take damage or go under the bridge into your partner's range, so they have no safe position. [17:50]
- Do not quick melee someone who is mantling up to you; players now expect it and parry right away. [18:21]
- When you are the one mantling, try to parry, since many players quick melee the mantle. [18:21]
- Do not quick melee a low HP enemy who would die to two more bullets; they have nothing left but to parry and pray, and you give it to them. [18:51]
- Aim matters most in mid lane because most damage is long range, especially for heroes not strong up close. [18:51]
- Vindicta should never play under the bridge in mid, where stuns like Paige's and wall stuns make her blow up instantly; on top of the bridge she can do damage. [19:22]
- Before the map changes many gun fights were about 20 m; now many happen at 25 to 30 m and farther, so you have to hit your shots. [19:52]
- In mid lane do not do big box routes at 3 minutes; mid has a dense box population, so run at most a 30 second route. [20:23]
- Long box routes from mid are bad because mid is so easy to invade; a box runner who stays away long loses the guardian and mid lane. [20:53]
- As the enemy team, stand in mid's side buildings (with the grapples down to mid boss): you see their whole shop and escape easily if they dive, so you pressure them freely. [20:53]
- At 5 minutes sewer boxes spawn down the staircase beside that building; it is a short route worth a few hundred souls almost instantly, so be the first to run it. [21:23]
- A strong hero with pressure might invade the enemy's sewer boxes, but that mostly works in lower level play; in higher ranks you die as soon as they see you. [21:53]
- Running long box routes and leaving your teammate does not work in higher ranks, only in lower ranks, but about 95% of players do not capitalize on it, especially with a duo you trust to hold the tower alone. [21:53]
- Do not take the single Sinner's Sacrifice in the room next to mid lane (by the veil, with two creeps); it is a trap room. [22:24]
- That Sinner's Sacrifice does 80 damage per punch, about 320 total, plus the two creeps shooting you for maybe 300 more, and you will get dove and die in the time it takes. [22:55]
- At 8 minutes go for the double Sinner's Sacrifice in the jungle on each side instead, with your lane partner hovering to warn of invaders. [23:26]
- After 10 minutes the game depends on how well you farm, fight and decide. [23:26]
