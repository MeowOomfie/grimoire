# What is a Power Spike? And how can YOU abuse it
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-08-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AQxArnNQdKs

## Covers
What a power spike is (3.2k+ items, 5 AP upgrades), why strength is not linear in souls, and three principles for using spikes: plan the next one, get active when you hit it, spend souls right away so unsecured souls are not dropped.
Examples on Lash and Seven. For players who farm without intent or sit on unspent souls.

## Claims
- A power spike is a significant boost in your hero's power from an ability upgrade or an item purchase. [0:00]
- At a simple level, most items worth 3,200 souls or more are power spikes, and any 5 AP upgrade is a power spike. [0:00]
- A Seven with 8,000 souls and 4 AP unspent is much weaker than a Seven with 9,000 souls who just unlocked the last upgrade of Power Surge: only 1,000 souls apart but a huge difference in power. [0:31]
- Strength in Deadlock is not linear in souls: a 5 to 10% soul difference can mean being 30 or 40% stronger. [1:02]
- With a lot of unspent souls, or right before maxing an ability, you can be significantly weaker than your souls indicate. [1:02]
- With all souls spent on items and a fresh 5 AP upgrade (for example maxing Lash's Flog), you can be a bit stronger than your souls indicate; this should dictate a lot of your decisions. [1:33]
- Principle one, plan ahead: always know your next power spike, for example as Lash on 2,200 souls know what you will buy at 3,200. [2:03]
- Farm with intent: don't mindlessly hit troopers or neutrals; think about your next item and your next AP upgrade. [2:03]
- If you have 4 AP unspent and are close to maxing an ability, go get some farm so you can max it. [2:33]
- Lash example: on 2,100 souls, plan to buy Warp Stone, a 3,200 item, not a 1,600 item, and farm toward it. [2:33]
- The mid boxes are a great source of souls. [3:04]
- Always clear the wave first, even when you just reached the souls for your item; clear the wave in front of you, then go to the shop and buy right away. [3:04]
- Unsecured souls are spent last when you buy (the Warp Stone purchase took unsecured souls from 600 to 200). [3:35]
- Principle two, look for opportunities: the moment you hit a power spike you are the strongest you will be for the next 1 to 2 minutes, so get active, look for fights and be aggressive on the map. [3:35]
- Don't use a fresh power spike to just farm your own jungle; it is your best chance to fight and have impact on the map. [4:05]
- If you just spiked and the enemy is 500 souls short of a spike, or has had no shop nearby to buy, you may be significantly stronger than the scoreboard indicates; get active around power spike timings. [4:05]
- Lash example: on 6,300 souls, spend it all at the secret shop (Warp Stone and Debuffer Remover); having spent a large share of net worth is a huge spike. [4:36]
- After that spike, while doing the Sinner's Sacrifice, first back off a Warden so as not to get caged, then run at him to force the fight because you just got much stronger. [4:36]
- After a spike, position aggressively deep on the enemy side of the map even when your own jungle is up, to give them no breathing room. [5:06]
- Principle three, spend souls effectively: if you just farmed camps to reach your spike, spend all of it immediately so your unsecured souls get spent and you don't drop a big bag dying while getting active. [5:38]
- Purchases spend secured souls first, then unsecured souls. With 2,100 total and 800 unsecured, buying a 1,600 item spends the 1,300 secured plus 300 unsecured, leaving 500 unsecured. [5:38]
- With 1,600 total and 800 unsecured, buying a 1,600 item leaves exactly zero unsecured. [6:39]
- Keep farming after a big farm without spending and you build a huge bag; dying with a 1,500 soul bag can let someone 4,000 behind you draw even. [6:39]
- Spending right away reduces risk and lets you get active, because with few unsecured souls your death is not the end of the world. [7:11]
- Learn which items make your hero feel extremely strong versus meh, and plan around them. Example: if Yamato feels unbelievably strong with Colossus or Mystic Reverb, at 5,000 souls get 1,400 more, buy it, then get active on the map. [7:42]
- Don't force fights after a spike, but position where you can be aggressive and deny resources: at minimum be on the enemy side of the map, across the line, ready to take a fight. [8:12]
