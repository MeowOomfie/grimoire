## Covers
Violet, aquatiq.
Commentary over one of his games (no guide), with remarks on lane, Still Life stacks and item choices.
Patch as of 2026-10-31.

## Claims
- Violet does a lot of damage right from the start of the game; her wand (ability 1) hits very hard. [0:30]
- Against a Haze and Shiv lane he does not play passive; he plays aggressive. [1:03]
- He had only Extra Charge as an item and the enemy laner was still getting nailed. [1:36]
- You can keep drawing (painting) an enemy even while you are slept; the drawing does not disappear when you get slept. [1:36]
- Against enemies who do not know Violet, expect a three to four minute tower. [2:40]
- Enemies who run at you while you are painting them do not understand how Violet works. [3:11]
- Her ability 2 also blows people up. [3:11]
- When a lane gets boring he leaves to gank another lane. [4:48]
- He traded a tower for two kills and called it fine. [6:24]
- He uses Still Life on enemies to gain stacks and wants more stacks through the game. [8:15]
- In a Rift fight he maxes his ult. [8:15]
- When weak and behind he farms up instead of joining mid. [9:16]
- Against a Shiv he wants a remover item (debuff removal). [13:35]
- He skips Metal Skin and buys Plated Armor; in his experience Metal Skin has hindered him more than helped. [13:35]
- When your team-fight ult is up you should be with your team fighting. [15:08]
- Violet's ult is very strong in fights, even when defending your own base. [20:03]
- Rat King's rats are a hard counter to Haze. [20:33]
- Plated Armor is very good when the whole enemy team uses gun damage. [21:34]
- He ended with almost the most healing on his team, which he puts down to healing from his abilities. [22:40]
