# You'll Never Lose Lane Again, Just Give Me 20 Minutes | Deadlock Laning Guide
channel: Shotty  date: 2026-09-03  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_yU-0kb24g

## Covers
Health bar plus souls model of the lane, matchup nuance (Mina, Graves), sustain items (Extra Regen, Extra Health, Trophy Collector, Healing Rite), 0-2 min positive trades, trooper damage, tier one camp on strong and weak side, 30 second waves, 3 min boxes and circle box routes, support leaves lane for the carry, carries take souls better (Vyper vs Kelvin), Extra Regen as a 400 soul item, bridge buff timing and baiting parries, guardian defense with rooftops, tier two and tier three camps, Sinner's Sacrifice steals at 8 min, playing from behind, roam safety past the bridge, Urn to the carry, Vindicta, Apollo, Billy, Calico, Holiday

## Claims
- Think of the lane phase as one big health bar (your health plus your lane partner's, against the enemy pair's combined health) and a souls counter (your side's souls against theirs). [0:30]
- Lower health means a higher chance of dying and higher souls means a higher chance of killing someone; in lane the only things that matter are your health, your souls compared to your opponent, and the hero matchup. [0:30]
- The matchup adds nuance: as Mina being low health does not matter much because she can rake and heal up to full, and against a full health Graves it does not matter because Graves has such low HP that one rake and one Love Bite nearly kill. [1:00]
- Example: if your side averages about 70% HP and theirs about 85% with equal souls, it is a bad idea to take a trade or commit for a kill; whittle their HP down or farm more instead. [1:31]
- In the first 2 minutes both sides' souls should be exactly equal unless someone gets denied, so it comes down to how many and how good trades you take while still getting all the souls, which sets up your snowball. [2:01]
- Sustain items like Extra Regen, Extra Health, Trophy Collector, anything with out of combat regen, or a restorative item (captioned "resto shard") let you win the HP game even when equal or behind on souls. [2:31]
- For the first 2 minutes just take positive trades. [2:31]
- Some heroes are worse at trading: as Vindicta, with a slow shooting gun and 40 second ability cooldowns against an Apollo who can use his 3 and take more HP than you can stop, he would not take trades, just poke and play the long game until about 5 minutes. [2:31]
- As the Apollo in that matchup he would trade as hard as possible. [3:01]
- Troopers do a lot of damage in the first 2 minutes, so do not fight in front of troopers or while standing on top of them; their damage makes you lose the HP game. [3:31]
- Fighting in your troopers also risks some dying behind you, giving the enemy a free confirm or deny; the 10% HP you take off the enemy is not worth the souls and HP you lose. [3:31]
- At 2 minutes the tier one camp spawns; one side lane has the camp on its side and the other has none (mid lane is different), and the side with the camp should be camping it the second it spawns and take it as fast as possible. [4:02]
- If you are the side without the camp you almost always want to fight it, except when you would lose the HP game, for example against heroes strong at close range. [4:02]
- As Billy he would always fight the tier one camp, because his slam plus melee takes it easily and enemies trying to take it eat so much splash damage they nearly kill themselves. [4:32]
- About 150 souls from the camp is not worth losing 20% of your HP, so weigh the trade. [5:02]
- Waves spawn every 30 seconds, so a wave arrives soon after 2 minutes; if the enemy is slow on your camp, stall them without going in and let your minions kill their wave so you deny those souls, leaving them worse off. [5:02]
- 3 minutes is where most players start losing the game and the lane phase crumbles, because boxes spawn. [7:35]
- Until 3 minutes, souls should be equal (minus a tier one camp, which is not a lot), so HP was the only possible advantage; from boxes on, it becomes killing waves and boxes and rotating between them as fast and efficiently as possible, which most players mess up. [8:06]
- You can build a 1k to 2k soul lead in lane from boxes alone. [8:06]
- At minute three, first kill the incoming wave: by around 2:50 and then around 3:10 have the previous wave and the new wave killed, then leave lane as fast as possible. [8:37]
- You do not need box route videos to start (min-maxing routes is for later): leave lane, draw a circle on the map that ends back at the zip line, and zip back to lane. [8:37]
- A support and a carry should usually lane together; the support, especially if good at boxes, should leave lane (maybe fishing for a mid lane gank) and let the carry take one or two waves solo. [9:07]
- Efficient boxes can give the same or almost the same souls as a full wave, so your side gets double the souls of an enemy pair splitting waves, and unless they do the same they cannot win the souls game, only the HP game. [9:37]
- Do not miss more than two waves unless you are running a very long route through the opposite lane or found a successful gank. [10:07]
- Value how well each hero converts souls: some only need about 20k souls and the rest is cosmetic, while Vyper with tier fours becomes exponentially stronger; Kelvin with items is not comparable. [10:37]
- That is why a support is fine losing lane and giving it to a carry, since a carry always takes souls better than a support. [11:07]
- Exactly at 5 minutes the bridge buff spawns; treat it as a temporary souls injection. [11:07]
- Extra Regen is so powerful because after a box route you come back to lane full health, winning both the souls game and the HP game, so even if you are bad at trading you out-HP an enemy without it. [11:37]
- Healing Rite is another item like Extra Regen. [11:37]
- Extra Regen costs 800 but sells for 400, so it really costs 400 souls, which makes it a no-brainer for winning lane. [12:08]
- Go to the bridge buff around 4:50 (bounce pad or just walk, it is not far). [12:08]
- Getting there at 4:50 lets you bait and poke the enemy, who are often late: shoot them off the bounce pad as they rush it and take most of their HP. [12:39]
- Even if you then lose the buff, it is fine: they got a fake souls injection but lost so much HP they nearly lose it immediately by dying. [13:11]
- Be on the bridge by 4:50 and no later, or you become the one baited and killed for inching for the buff too hard. [13:11]
- After reaching the buff, do not show yourself; use the third person camera to watch where people come from and what they plan. [13:42]
- If people bounce pad over, stand dead center and shoot them down. [13:42]
- Do not over rush the buff; anyone who joins you on the bridge will instinctively heavy punch the buff even with you standing there, so stand there baiting for a parry. [13:42]
- Do not die for the bridge buff; it is like a free tier two item for a short time, not worth a 30 second death timer. [14:12]
- The bridge buff is a good comeback tool, because teams winning lane often do not prioritize it. [14:12]
- There are many boxes around the bridge buff (on the roof, on the nearby roof and at the bottom), so you can leave lane and come back with about 600 extra souls. [14:44]
- The 8 minute mark is the end of lane, especially what you do in its first 30 seconds. [15:14]
- Around 8 minutes enemies push harder for your guardian; let them have it. [15:14]
- Lane rooftops, routes around the guardian, rappels, the zip line and veils make it very hard to die at your guardian, so move around instead of standing still shooting. [15:14]
- Do not die for the guardian: most people who do would have lost it anyway, then sit on a 20 to 30 second timer missing souls. [15:44]
- If they kill you there they also steal your roof camp (captioned "roof cab"), a free tier two, and at or just after 8 minutes your Sinner's Sacrifice, setting you back about 3k souls and giving them 3k. [16:15]
- Tier two camps spawn at five minutes; take them if your hero can kill them, but usually only carries can without losing over half their HP, which is not worth about 95 souls. [16:46]
- Tier three camps spawn at 8 minutes; by then almost any hero can kill every tier two camp on the map. [16:46]
- The second 8 minutes hits, be standing at your Sinner's Sacrifice. [17:16]
- If you are winning the souls game and the HP game, clear your Sinner's Sacrifice area but head to the enemy's and steal it; if you are there on time they will not be, and they cannot challenge you without forfeiting their lives. [17:16]
- Then go back for yours, getting two Sinner's Sacrifices of permanent buffs, plus tier two camps and wave, enough souls for a new item. [17:47]
- If you are on the receiving end, low health and behind, play tower, get souls and boxes, and let them have it unless teammates gank for you; past a certain souls and health gap you just die fighting. [17:47]
- The goal is to leave lane phase with as many souls and as few deaths as possible. [18:17]
- Enemies will gank and roam, especially if yours is one of the last guardians standing; around 8 minutes, or around 5 minutes if they have a strong roamer like Calico or Holiday, be very cautious about walking up to or past the bridge. [18:48]
- On the side lanes and mid the bridge is a good stopping point for positioning; past it there are fewer walls to bounce back off, more minions shooting you and more distance from safety. [19:19]
- Waiting for a "Vindicta missing" call will get you killed, since in most ranks players forget, call too late or do not notice the roam; stay on your side of the bridge. [19:19]
- After that, grab the Urn and lane phase is over; if you are the carry take the Urn every time you can, because its souls go to whoever runs it as long as no one shoots or steals it. [19:50]
