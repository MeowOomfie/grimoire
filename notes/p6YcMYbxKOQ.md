## Covers
A Mystic-rank Paige player is reviewed by Midknight, who coaches for a coaching academy and a top three South American team.
Topics: wasted abilities and missed orbs, lane partner awareness, Paige builds (High Velocity, Opening Rounds, Express Shot, Extra Spirit), side laning and giving side waves, rotating off ziplines, cross-map ults and verticality.
Macro: playing comeback rifts and free mid boss, pointless 6v6 mid fights, making plays and IGLing on mic as a backline support, and a damage distribution benchmark at the end.
