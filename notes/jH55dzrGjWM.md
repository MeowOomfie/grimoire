## Covers
Coaching VOD review by Midknight of an Eternus-rank Doorman (lane with Abrams against Drifter and Seven), April 2026 patch.
Themes: respecting the enemy's boost, soul awareness, overstaying at walkers, playing for spikes (especially the green spike), who should run the urn, Counterspell practice, and Doorman itemization.

## Claims
Doorman and lane:
- Doorman is still a relatively strong lane hero, but not as good as he used to be; the bell's impact damage was brought back, which gives him surprising one-shots. [0:32]
- Focus the low Drifter who is still behind your tower; don't switch targets to the Seven. [1:32]
- After the Drifter dives your Abrams under tower, he will recast his teleport, so get on your Abrams; walking to him is a free kill. [2:04]
- You can read the recast with a parry. [2:36]
- Box farming tech: instead of meleeing boxes along a wall, diagonally air dash into the wall and slide along it to break them faster. [2:36]
- Buying Opening Rounds early is good. [3:06]
- Coming into a fight while low is fine when both enemies are on full cooldowns. [3:36]
- Stalker's Mark is on a 20 second cooldown; if the Drifter marks you, parry, reading that he will teleport back in. [3:36]
- If the enemy roams your lane, respond by diving. [5:08]
- The right enemy play was for the Seven to back off and hit the wave from far while they hit the tower; if someone overstays like that, full dive them, kill them, then break the tower. [5:08]
- Counter-roaming was good. [5:38]
- Instant cast is better on doors. [6:10]
- Use the cart (Luggage Cart) for its CC while the enemy is being moved; cart off the door even without the stun, for 156 free damage plus the CC. [6:41]
- You forced a dive without respecting the Yamato's boost, and died because you didn't close the door behind you instantly. [7:11]

Mid game and souls:
- Soul awareness: you were sitting on a ton of souls in that play. [8:41]
- Invading their double camps with a door behind you is Doorman bread and butter. [8:41]
- Preemptively placing a door in case someone pulls up and silences you is great. [8:41]
- Get a damage spike even if you're going spirit; Fortitude doesn't matter much if you have no damage in your kit. [9:13]
- Buy Fortitude as your green spike or after your first spike, not before a spike. [9:43]
- Golden statues give permanent buffs; three of them is worth a lot. [10:43]
- Push the wave; there's no reason to let it push itself. [10:43]
- Alchemical Fire is great against Mo and Krill's ult and good against Pocket's ult; it combos with Vindicta's stake, Lash's ult and Abrams' charge. It was early but he can't hate it. [11:14]
- Contest the urn; with that map positioning you have to catch the side wave and can't let the enemy get prio. [12:50]
- Running the urn at 12:30 is very late at this level. [13:51]
- Don't hit the walker while Vindicta stands under it: you can never kill her under her walker or kill the walker while she's there, so the expected value is nothing. Hit the wave or steal their sentries instead. [14:21]
- Overstaying at the walker gets you punished; a teammate should be called over and you die. [15:24]
- You're 800 souls off two spikes; focus on your spikes more. [15:24]
- He recommends Piggy's Doorman build and JohnJohn's, which is in his spreadsheet. [15:54]
- The support should run the urn. That lets your Apollo anchor the side instead of you being stuck alone there, and Apollo has better 1v1 potential against the Drifter. [16:24]
- Run the urn off spawn. [17:26]
- On 4K souls and 800 from two spikes: buy Extra Spirit to hit your spirit spike, or Extra Stamina, or sell Healing Rite and buy Guardian Ward. [17:26]
- The Drifter marks the soul bag and appears on you; you have to greed the bag and respond earlier. [18:29]
- Once you get up high, don't drop down; go for height (wall jump, double jump, mantle). [18:29]
- Tankbuster at minute 17 is too early. It deals current HP damage, so it is better the more max HP enemies have; only Lash (pure one-shot) and Mina (for farm) get away with it around 18 minutes. [19:32]
- Knockdown is OP on Doorman because it guarantees the cart. [20:36]
- Good map play on an urn run: your carry is the one on the weak side, and when the enemy Haze goes back to the wave you shuffle over and catch her. [22:42]
- Take the free urn instead of greeding a kill. [23:13]
- You need Divine Barrier this game against Drifter's mark and Pocket's Affliction; buy it next. [23:43]
- You should have held the urn. [24:17]
- Slowing Hex is not bad, but he prefers Divine Barrier as your next piece of utility. [24:48]
- Not having a green spike is very bad; you're missing about 17% HP, roughly 500 HP. [25:18]
- His next green would be Divine Barrier: buy Guardian Ward early to hit the spike, then upgrade it into Divine Barrier. [25:49]
- Counterspell is pretty good this game, especially if you can counter Pocket's ult, and against Drifter's ult. [26:20]
- Two ways to learn Counterspell: play normally but parry every ability you would otherwise counterspell, or simply buy it every game until you are comfortable. [27:22]
- Higher-level teams contest urns even when both sides are low. [27:52]
- Don't lead a contest when your team is far behind you. [28:25]
- Spending shots on a Yamato is a waste unless she is nearly dead. [28:25]
- You're playing too scared: at this level the enemy is at mid boss over 90% of the time, so shove your wave and go get the urn, holding it to deny. It is a comeback urn so it creates pressure. Put a door behind you as a panic button. [29:27]
- Not chasing the Yamato was good: you respected that she might be baiting. [31:38]
- You need a transcendent cooldown item and were in base without buying it; soul awareness issues. [31:38]
- Your team gave up height too easily. [32:40]
- Dying while trying to door your Abrams out is fine; it is support mentality. [34:49]

Summary notes:
- Respect the enemy boost and track the boost timer, which comes up at 6:05. [36:24]
- Soul awareness: several fights taken while sitting on a big item. [36:24]
- Play for your spikes, especially the green spike. [36:24]
- Overstaying at the walker: if one enemy is at their walker and you are alone, you cannot split it; you have zero kill potential on them and can't do meaningful walker damage. [36:56]
- The game was lost late because the team had no carry and gun out-scales flat. [36:24]
