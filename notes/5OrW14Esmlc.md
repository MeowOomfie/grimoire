# Shrines are Ridiculously Broken | Deadlock Guide
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-08-10
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OrW14Esmlc

## Covers
Why breaking one shrine is worth more than a walker: shrine HP, base guardian protection, bullet resist per nearby hero, and the super trooper effect.
Plus base guardian quirks, defending (give the first shrine, fight on the second), vents, veils, high ground spots and the shrine curse. For players closing out or defending a base.

## Claims
- A common mistake is splitting the final tier three walker, or any walker, instead of taking a shrine, or not pushing base guardians because players think the lane ends at the walker. [0:00]
- The first shrine is very squishy at only 5,000 HP; when it dies it gives 7,000 HP to the second shrine, which then has a total of about 12,000 HP (caption says 1,200), making it significantly stronger. [0:00]
- Shrines are shielded until you break a set of base guardians; any set counts, so if you broke the green side base guardians long ago and now push from yellow, jump past the base guardians and hit the shrine. [0:30]
- Without troopers nearby, shrines have 65% extra resist from base protection; troopers only need to be at the base guardians, not past them, to remove it. [1:01]
- A shrine has 60% base bullet resist, reduced by 20% for each additional hero next to it; with four people on the platform it has 0%, so focus the same shrine with as many people as you can. [1:01]
- Breaking a shrine makes every trooper that spawns in that lane and in mid lane a super trooper, with 60% more damage and 40% more health, which essentially guarantees lane priority. [1:31]
- Super troopers are also worth 15% less souls to the enemy. [2:02]
- Objectives never respawn, so one 5,000 HP shrine gives you guaranteed priority and passive pressure in a side lane and mid for the rest of the game, plus reduced enemy souls, which is why it is far more valuable than a walker. [2:32]
- With kills or a Rejuvenator and waves crashing into base guardians, call to push into base rather than taking a walker. [3:03]
- The downside of shrines is they are close to the enemy base and its full heal about 50 m behind; with several enemies alive, judge their ults and your strength, and if unsure you can get through, take the walker instead. [3:03]
- If you have the choice, take the shrine; when split pushing, killing one set of base guardians always opens the chance to backdoor a shrine. [3:34]
- After trading walkers, don't automatically reset or cross the map; if the enemies can't stop you, hit the base guardians. [4:04]
- Base guardians have the same 60% bullet resist that scales down per nearby attacking hero. [4:04]
- Base guardians can be damaged from any distance (a tier one patron may be similar), so you can hit them from very far away. [4:04]
- Isolate fights at the base guardians by taking the correct angle, because they deal a lot of damage; this matters especially for point-blank gun heroes like Calico. [4:37]
- When defending, if you can kill the attackers and stop them taking even one shrine, do it, because losing a shrine is a massive blow to your chance to win. [5:07]
- If the attackers have three Rejuvenator charges, a player advantage or all their ults and will clear your base, let the first shrine die and fight on the much tankier second shrine. [5:07]
- The first shrine often dies before it gets off one of its AoE curses. [5:38]
- On the second shrine, any knock-up or stall (Calico, Dynamo, Holliday's barrel) is huge, because attackers often greed the curse, hitting until the last frame before dashing back. [5:38]
- Attackers cluster in the small shrine pocket, so AoE is excellent there; Viscous fries on shrines. [6:09]
- When sieging, there is a spot above the shrine from which you can hit it from safety. [6:09]
- After killing the first shrine, rotate to the other through the vents, which is much quicker; good players will play to vent-stun you, but even in E6 lobbies it is rare, and he says you need to be top 200 to see it consistently. [6:09]
- When defending the second shrine on the Sapphire side, play the veil; unless they face check it you can wait for someone low and full combo or mag dump them. [7:12]
- On the Amber side, play the height behind the shrine; it is a very defensible straight-line retreat with few lines of sight from mid or the open base. [7:44]
- When sieging, you can jump on top of the shrine structures and melee from there for free damage (works on the Amber side too), but avoid it against heroes like Lash. [8:14]
- You now get cursed while standing on top of the shrine; you used to be safe there. [8:14]
- The shrine curse is over-respected: its hitbox is a slow rolling wave, not an instant area, so you can keep sieging longer than most people think before backing off. [8:46]
