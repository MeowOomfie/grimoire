# The Enemy Couldn't Stand This Mirage Build
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-06-15
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LtmOCVwvtsk

## Covers
Mirage with a Djinn's Mark focused build (Mark maxed first), by aquatiq.
Commentary over one full game against a Grey Talon lane, no coaching.
Patch of 2026-06-15.

## Claims
- Mirage was rated A tier in Eternus at the time. [0:01]
- A Grey Talon lane is all right for Mirage, long range against long range; Mirage is a long range hero who does not need to be near enemies to damage them. [0:32]
- Mirage's whole laning stage is built around his Fire Scarabs; if you miss both, do not fight until they are back. [1:02]
- The Scarabs are his lane sustain; without them he dies, because he has low health (the Grey Talon has more HP). [1:02]
- Aim Scarabs at Dynamo when he pokes out, his model is bigger and easier to hit. [1:32]
- Build: Djinn's Mark maxed first, not Tornado and not Scarabs, because Mark is still very strong. [2:33]
- Grey Talon was just nerfed but is still extremely strong. [3:35]
- Landing Scarabs on both laners can heal you back to about full HP and keep you from losing HP the whole lane. [5:07]
- Play oppressively in lane when the Scarabs are landing. [5:38]
- His teleport (Traveler) is useful in lane, but do not teleport away if it means losing the lane. [6:41]
- Hold Alt, put the mouse on an enemy hero and click to ping them as missing. [8:20]
- Calico disappears from the map in cat form, so a missing ping may not mean she left. [8:50]
- He teleported into a fight with five enemies and should have stayed where he was. [12:26]
- Against Dynamo and Viper the team needs curse items such as Silence Wave. [13:29]
- Holiday is pretty good now. [13:29]
- With Mark maxed, farm side waves and boxes to keep your net worth up, or you fall behind your team. [14:01]
- Item order: buy a debuff remover and Toxic Bullets, but Ricochet should come before Toxic Bullets. [18:19]
- Escalating Exposure is the big spike; once he has it the enemy cannot do much. [18:51]
- You can teleport into mid (the mid boss area). [20:02]
- When ahead, take the enemy's camps and vending machines. [23:09]
- Ricochet makes clearing camps much easier. [23:39]
- Mystic Reverb is a fun late pick he ran again. [26:42]

## Disagrees with

- mQ13JFHNHIQ (Midknight, 2026-05-31): Buy Toxic Bullets next on Mirage; rushing Ricochet makes you weak. [15:33]
