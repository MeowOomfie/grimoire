## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (patch of early July 2026), reviewing four viewer games: a Venator (Ascendant), a Pocket (Oracle), Zach on a 2-max Victor, and a crow-max Vindicta (Ascendant).
Themes: leaving mid lane between 8 and 12 minutes, Cultist Sacrifice timing, playing for Rifts (including comeback Rifts) and urns, holding ults and Affliction for Rift fights, not pushing walkers while sitting on souls, bag awareness, kiting to teammates, why early agency matters for a late-game player, and why to skip timer mods like Quality of Life Lock.
