- Afterburn Infernus is still extremely strong and there is nothing wrong with building it. [8:49]

- Restorative Locket is broken; people buy it mostly for the stamina, but the healing is good too. [2:06]

- When you have a lot of unspent souls, stay in lane and heal off the wave instead of backing and losing farm. [5:16]

- Infernus feels slow on this build; he wanted Enduring Speed. [13:04]
