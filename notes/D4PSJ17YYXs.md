# This New Patch BROKE Silver
channel: aquatiq

## Covers
Silver, aquatiq. Commentary over a full Silver game (a 50 minute win) into a heavy crowd-control enemy team with Ivy, The Doorman, Victor, McGinnis, Warden and Celeste.
Item choices as the game goes, the anti-CC and anti-spirit picks, and a tip on setting the Spirit Urn on your own side. Patch date 2026-05-24.

## Claims
- Silver shoots too slow to confirm souls well in lane; you have to wait for the minions to die before you can do it. [0:30]
- Buys extra regen early in lane and plans more of it once the lane gets rough. [1:00]
- Picked up Sprint Boots and Trophy Collector off the first shop trips. [3:00]
- Silver is not strong in this lane from range: you cannot hit an Ivy for much damage when you are too far away. [4:02]
- Get the lane ready to go for the buffs when the buff timer is close. [4:33]
- When a lane partner is getting targeted, let him take another wave alone so he can catch up on souls. [8:10]
- Rusted Barrel is wasted on Silver because her gun already fires slowly; the enemy should put it on a fast-firing hero like Seven instead. [8:44]
- Against Ivy's Stone Form, wait it out and unload your magazine on her after the stone form ends. [10:19]
- At first Silver feels like you have to do a lot to get anything, but you actually do very little for a lot: just shooting someone does a lot of damage. [13:25]
- Considered Inhibitor because a Doorman kept targeting his lane partner. [14:27]
- Got Express Shot as a mid game gun item. [14:27]
- If you run up on the Doorman you get sent into his hotel and come out to his whole team waiting, so do not chase him in. [21:22]
- With money but too few people alive, do not fight or push; defend the lane. [21:22]
- You can set the Spirit Urn on your own side of the map: pick it up and drop it again on your side, and it stays set there and will not swap to mid even when the soul lead would normally move it, unless it swaps to the other side. [20:52]
- Prepare for the buffs ahead of the timer and play to the map advantage you have. [22:26]
- He wanted Phantom Strike at this buy but picked a fighting item instead because he wanted to fight people more. [24:00]
- Itemizes heavily into spirit defense this game, including Spirit Resilience. [25:10]
- Bought Berserker in the mid game. [28:16]
- Against a team stacked with crowd control (Ivy's stone form, Victor's ult, Doorman's abilities and ult) you need Unstoppable; he considered it the final item of a maxed build. [29:27]
- Plans to sell Restorative Locket for Silencer: it gives a lot of spirit resist and your bullets reduce the target's outgoing spirit damage, which is good when your own team is heavy spirit damage like Seven and Grey Talon. [29:58]
- Silencer is a good item on Silver anyway. [29:58]
- When the enemy is poor and there is a lot of farm on the map, take your farm rather than forcing fights. [34:03]
- Applying Inhibitor on a target in a fight stops him healing. [36:05]
- Do not risk ending through an enemy team with every hard CC in the game; go mid boss instead. [37:39]
- A Viscous can go back to base and clear a wave there in one shot. [38:10]
- Verdict: Silver is a little broken after this patch. [40:49]
