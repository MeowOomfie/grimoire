# The New Deadlock Objective Changes EVERYTHING
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-06-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLnBCg527wk

## Covers
Live reaction and read-through of the last patch before the major update: the old King of the Hill objective becomes the Unstable Rift with Rift troopers and linear comeback scaling, Urn running becomes its own separate objective, kill and Guardian bounties drop, ult cooldowns rise, plus item and hero balance changes with the streamer's takes.
For players who want the patch rules and what they mean for macro.

## Claims
- The King of the Hill objective is renamed the Unstable Rift and no longer needs an Urn delivery to start. [0:30]
- The Rift has a variable start time of plus or minus 1 minute, a global announcement, and an in-world visual effect showing which lane it will spawn in. [0:30]
- Rift spawn interval increased from every 6 minutes to every 7 minutes; the first Rift can spawn as early as 11 minutes or as late as 13. [1:00]
- Rift comeback bonuses (area resists, extra comeback bounty, and the trailing or leading team claim timer) now scale linearly with how far behind on net worth a team is. [1:00]
- Before, the comeback bonus was all or nothing; now it ramps from no bonus to full bonus at 15% net worth behind, and the old 10% threshold is gone. [1:31]
- Example: 5% behind gives a bit of resists, making it a fair fight if both teams fight. [2:02]
- The team that secures the Rift gets a wave of five Rift troopers sent from the Rift location; a trailing team gets up to 12 on the same linear ramp. [2:02]
- This fixes the old problem where a far ahead team let the behind team finish the Urn and then killed them; now the claim gives power to do something right after. [2:33]
- Rift troopers spawn at 100% bonus health and damage, look larger with custom effects, and grow per event: 100, then 120, 140 and so on. [2:33]
- Any player inside the Unstable Rift is revealed on the minimap. [3:04]
- Rift bounty increased by 35% to make up for the lost Urn runner bounty; overall objective bounty is unchanged. [3:04]
- The Rift no longer grants a permanent buff to each player, and there are no longer plus three buffs for the Urn runner. [3:04]
- Urn running is now its own separate objective. [3:04]
- With an exactly even net worth the Rift spawns at 12 minutes. [3:35]
- Teleporting with the Urn works, which may be unbalanced on heroes like Holliday. [3:35]
- In testing, five Rift troopers are very strong and tanky; they look able to kill a Walker or tower alone, and five is the number for an even fight. [5:11]
- The Urn spawns on either side where the old neutral pickup was and is delivered to the opposite end. [7:27]
- The Urn runner is no longer revealed and is not disarmed or silenced, so they can use anything. [7:57]
- The Urn cannot be dropped manually; it drops when the runner is hit by a light or heavy melee, and it stays where dropped instead of walking back home. [7:57]
- The Urn runner can no longer parry. [7:57]
- Urn bounty starts decaying 45 seconds after pickup, drains over another 45 seconds, and the Urn is removed when the bounty is empty. [7:57]
- The Urn is deposited immediately at the drop-off point and is worth 250 plus 70 souls per minute, for the runner only; much less than before, but it keeps a comeback bounty component. [8:27]
- The Urn runner gets four permanent buffs on deposit. [8:59]
- The Urn is louder when it talks, so nearby enemies hear it more easily. [8:59]
- Three minutes after spawning, an untouched Urn starts walking very slowly toward its deposit location on its own. [8:59]
- The Urn runner's passive bonuses while holding it now scale with how far behind you are instead of on or off. [8:59]
- Souls required to unlock points moved up slightly, so you gain strength a bit slower and late game is a bit stronger. [9:30]
- Health per boon reduced by 5%, which is a big change. [9:30]
- All ultimate base and upgrade cooldowns nerfed by 15%, rounded to the nearest multiple of five; the streamer thinks this is good because there were too many ults too fast. [10:01]
- Rejuvenator buff duration reduced from four to three minutes. [10:01]
- Respawn ramp changed from 8 to 30 seconds over minutes 5 to 19, to 8 to 35 seconds over minutes 5 to 20, so respawns take longer. [10:31]
- Guardian bounty reduced from 1,500 to 1,250, nerfing early snowballing and early game heroes. [10:31]
- Walker bounty, first blood bounty and base kill bounty also reduced. [10:31]
- With kills worth less, jungle becomes relatively better; before this, only Sinner's Sacrifice was good jungle. [11:01]
- Trooper bounty increased; minimum souls allowed to drop reduced from 150 to 50. [11:31]
- Dash jump input window increased and dash jump distance increased, a buff to weaker movement players. [11:31]
- Jump pads on top of the double center building were removed. [12:01]
- Mystic Shot cooldown increased. [12:32]
- Toxic Bullets bleed damage increased; it was a bit too weak. [12:32]
- Scourge got a big nerf; tanks could buy it as their only purple item and do huge damage. [12:32]
- Cursed Relic damage penalty increased. [12:32]
