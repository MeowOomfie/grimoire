## Covers
Mina coaching by Midknight: a VOD review of an Ascendant Mina laning with Seven into Infernus and Yamato, recorded during the April 1 event patch.
Themes: playing and buying around the wave, ganking and overstaying, syncing double soul machines, itemization (Toxic Bullets, Healbane, Counterspell, Ethereal Shift), mosh pitting, dodging key abilities with the 2, running at enemies, mid boss macro and defending the Patron.

## Claims
Player 1 (Mina, Ascendant):
Lane:
- If you are going to buy, buy sooner: killing your wave, walking up to do 10 damage and then dropping back to buy an 800 item lets the enemies clear the wave while you are gone. [0:30]
- Fighting aggressively while your Seven clears the wave alone should get punished much more than it did. [0:30]
- Play and buy around the wave; don't give space while the waves are meeting, because giving wave priority dictates the pace of the lane. [1:00]
- Giving Infernus wave priority is especially bad because the hero needs it; if they used that priority to set up on bridge you would just lose lane. [1:30]
- Mina is a strong laner, but Infernus and Yamato should beat Mina and Seven in that lane. [1:00]
- Box routing early is good, but don't walk back to your shop and then box route. Start the route from the middle of lane, wrap around and end at your shop, buying at the end of the route to gain about five extra seconds. [2:30]
- Press 2 to dodge Yamato's slash; that is about 160 damage avoided with one button. [3:02]
- Wall bouncing off the lane wall is free and few people do it: walk up and wall bounce backwards to get out. [3:32]
- Setting up on the bridge buff is good, but if you set up preemptively you need to be watching them. [4:02]
- At five minutes with a fragile tower, the gank had decent expected value, but don't stay to steal farm; book it back. [4:02]
- Staying for that dive means your lane gets another wave into the tower, which should have cost you the tower. The issue is the initial overstay; once a free kill is there you can't not take it even if it costs a tower. [4:32]
- Stealing his camp and then going to shop was good; aside from the overstay the lane was played above your Elo. [6:33]

Double soul machines:
- It is better not to preemptively sync the two machines. Meleeing one and then the other only saves about one second, since you still wait a full rotation; meleeing the second while the first full cycles syncs them and saves about three seconds. [7:03]
- When you melee one and then finish the other, hold melee to finish the last one and they will be synced. [7:33]
- If you punch the first machine in roughly the last 30% of its window, they will not sync up. [10:35]

Mid game:
- At 7K, take some healing, buy a green item right away and look to be active. [8:03]
- With your side of the map dead, play to secure the enemy jungle; if you steal all that farm off spawn you basically win by minute 12. Instead you took a mosh pit mid. [8:33]
- Don't rush Toxic Bullets. When ahead buy defensive, when behind buy offensive; put a green item in the bag because your boons do enough damage. [9:03]
- You took that ultimate at a really bad angle. [9:03]
- Toxic Bullets is percent-of-HP damage, so like Tankbuster it gets better later in the game. At 10 minutes, with average max HP around 1.7K, it does not justify itself over a green item that would have saved you. [9:33]
- Mashing mid for fun let the enemies secure all the right side farm while you fought. [10:04]
- Into heavy healers, just buy Healbane: it is a green item, gives flat HP which makes it strong early, gives anti-heal and health on kill. Its stats are really good. [11:06]
- Choosing not to run Urn aggressively when it isn't safe is good. [11:36]
- Pick the Urn up and walk it toward your team; you died going back to pick it up. [11:36]
- Not pressing 2 to dodge Yamato's slash turned poke into a full death. [12:07]
- At 14 minutes you should not be walker diving. [12:38]
- Mechanically you look good; the issue is running at the enemies for fun. [12:38]
- Four M1s and two spirit nukers, with every M1 a spirit hybrid, is a terrible comp. [13:09]
- After a fight, check the map and look for bags; grab even a couple hundred souls, it is a good habit. [13:41]
- You are brawling too much and chasing a 2v2 on the other side of the map. The time spent in a coin flip fight could have been spent farming; with Urn on the map, play for Urn: more souls, more value, better team fight. [14:12]
- Full base resetting at full HP is not good; angle for a secret shop instead and farm a camp on the way. [15:16]
- Debuff Remover is good every game on Mina, but not ideal this game since there isn't much to remove (Yamato and a few slows). [15:46]
- Contest the Urn. If your walker is up you can collapse and trade the walker for the Urn; with no objective in the lane, one kill is not worth the Urn. [16:17]
- Killing Yamato and then beelining mid would work but is very sweaty, not expected below very high level. [16:48]
- Trading side walkers beats trading mid because of distance: enemies can easily rotate back to mid, sit between you and your escape zipline and get a kill or two, and one or two mobile players mid can reach the fight fast. Rotating from side is about 10 seconds slower even with the teleporter. [17:18]
- The souls you lost greeding Rake on Graves were worth more than the kill; melee the souls instantly and catch her again later. [18:21]
- Don't chase alone; if Grey Talon and Calico had helped they could have killed you. [18:51]
- Taking every double close to its spawn is good; your timers look good. [19:24]
- Buying in the enemy secret shop with no one around you is very bad; a teammate of Lash could just kill you. [19:55]
- A bad mid fight is excusable if your teammate called that Calico had a 2K bag, since Calico is worth about two kills; without that call it is a bad fight. [20:25]
- If you can't kill the wave quickly enough, they can collapse behind you. With more map awareness, rotate out a little sooner and not through the choke point. [20:56]
- A fight that started over Urn is excusable. [21:58]
- A mid boss bait is good, a mid boss commit is awful. [22:59]
- Your 2 was completely wasted; you need your silence on Lash to suppress his ult. [22:59]
- They stole every Rejuvenator; the expected value there was them stealing two and you getting one. [23:29]
- Calling a bad mid boss and learning why it was bad is much better than not calling mid boss at all. [23:29]
- Your ult is underused: four times in 29 minutes. Pressing 4 there on Grey Talon or Vindicta is the ideal ult and a free kill. [24:30]
- Stealing farm while ahead was good, but you stopped once behind. [25:31]
- Leave one creep alive on hybrid camps, especially camps with many creeps like basement: you steal a lot of farm and people walking by finish it off. On normal camps it is only a little value. [26:01]
- Late, don't commit to a fight where dying means staggered respawns and losing the game; be really careful. [26:32]
- You did not need to go to your fountain when Seven ulted; he is creating a ton of space for you. [27:04]
- Counterspell was insanely good this game: Yamato's slash, Sinclair's bolts, the Graves root, Lash's ult and Ground Strike, Calico's combo and bombs, Grey Talon's bird. [27:04]
- After running Urn and taking space, take the mid walker and then leave; don't overstay. [28:36]
- Ethereal Shift is a great buy now and a better late-game buy than Counterspell; it is really good on heroes like Mina, and its stats when you come out of it are insane. [28:36]
- When you can't contest the enemy mid boss, put pressure on the side lanes, with your mobile heroes like Infernus and Mina on side; if they do mid you get base value. Don't die doing it. [29:10]
- If you see the enemy six running your sewers and you know you shouldn't fight, steal farm instead. [29:40]
- Don't contest that mid when they have Yamato ult, Lash ult and Sinclair's Rabbit and you only have Infernus ult. [30:11]
- What crippled this game was the mid boss macro; from your own perspective you didn't initiate the bad fights. [30:42]
- Press 4 to secure the kill. Don't hold your 2 forever when you have a free recast. [31:44]
- Don't engage outside the pit when a teammate respawns in one second and they are pushing into the pit; fight in the pit. [31:44]
- As Mina, get handy at dodging things like decay with your 2. [32:14]
- Stand in the pit to make your Patron invulnerable; standing in pit would have won the game, but someone left it and they killed your Patron. [32:46]

Summary notes:
- In lane, play around the wave and don't buy when waves are crashing. [33:16]
- You overstayed the gank and cost your tower; get the last kill and back off. [33:16]
- Counterspell was valuable; getting Ethereal Shift later was good. [33:16]
- Mosh pitting too much in the mid game around the side lane jungle with no jungle up; invade instead. [33:47]
- Practice dodging Yamato's slash and things like decay with your 2, in a custom with a friend or by focusing on it in games; switch into heroes with telegraphed abilities to learn them. [33:47]
- Running at the enemies for fun: over-chasing and dying at their walker or past the map. [34:18]
- Mid boss macro: the first would have been a great bait but was a bad mid boss, and the last one should not have been contested. [34:18]
- Stand in the pit. [34:48]
