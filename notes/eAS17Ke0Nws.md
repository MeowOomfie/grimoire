# You're Brawling Too Much | Ascendant Haze Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-12-18
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eAS17Ke0Nws

## Covers
Midknight reviews one Ascendant Haze game (coaching VOD review, December 2025 patch, Haze, Paige, Abrams and Victor in the lobby).
Themes: movement as the Ascendant 5 skill check, secret shop and invading off a won lane, over meleeing, buying spirit for Haze's spirit scaling, never brute forcing walkers, when to join fights as a carry, urn versus mid boss baiting, and overfighting that threw a lead.

## Claims
Haze:
- Early, before Haze has spirit power, use slides to reload (slide to get the extra ammo). [1:34]
- Haze just wants to get permafed: after winning lane, anchor and farm. [5:13]
- Titanic Magazine is kind of bait on Haze because she has spirit scaling on her ammo; Extra Spirit or Improved Spirit would make the build look a lot better. [6:46]
- The only reason to take Titanic Magazine over Extra Spirit or Improved Spirit is if you need it for the gun spike. [7:18]
- Extra Spirit in lane gives about as much ammo as Extended Magazine and a lot more damage. [8:50]
- Being all gun with no spirit by this point is too invested; spirit investments would help a lot. [9:22]
- Active Reload is really strong right now and very numerically efficient: move speed, a lot of bullet lifesteal, 25% fire rate and the reload. [9:52]
- He is not a fan of Burst Fire: it gives fire rate, which over half the gun items already give, and it doesn't proc on camps, so it doesn't help farming; treat it as a luxury item. [11:22]
- With the build completely orange, Tesla Bullets (as captioned "test slow") would be a good option since she is fire rate specced; Toxic Bullets is fine. [16:35]
- If you slide and then melee on Haze it won't break your invisibility. [20:45]
- Haze's 2 has invulnerability: you could have used it to dodge the Lash ult. If you max it early, use the i-frames. [22:47]
- Recommended Haze builds: Renins's (Extra Spirit into Surge of Power for a spirit spike), Parzelion's, and Osiris's; none of them take Titanic Magazine, and Kinetic Dash comes early instead. [29:03]
- You are pressed for ammo early, so gun spike is still worth getting. [29:34]

Lane and early game:
- Don't brute force melee into a Paige you know parries off audio; that melee was needless danger. [1:34]
- When peeling for a teammate, after the kill stand on top of your Abrams so the Paige can't melee him and you win. [2:07]
- Use the gap when waves reset to take the box route and buy. [2:07]
- When dominant in lane, run at the enemy when they take bad angles, such as a Paige jumping down alone. [2:39]
- You didn't need to ult a 2.7k HP target when you both had almost 5k; save the ult. [4:42]
- Use tower pressure to invade the enemy jungle. [5:13]
- Try to be on buffs preemptively; the movement buff is the one you can ignore. [5:43]
- With a big net worth lead, push the wave into their walker and steal their jungle; stolen jungle is worth twice as much because you also deny it. [6:14]
- When you are sleeping someone as a mild nuisance in his jungle, it is better to let him walk out and steal the farm, since him knowing you are there costs more than the nuisance is worth. [7:18]
- Grab the center camps off spawn when invading. [7:48]
- For Sinner's Sacrifice timers, angle yourself on the right side so you can steal the one that sits in a double. [8:20]
- When not enough souls for an item while walking past it, go to your secret shop. [10:52]

Movement:
- His movement is rough: no wall bounces, no edge boosts, a lot of suboptimal slides. A week focused on movement or movement drills would do wonders. [3:39]
- Ascendant 5 right now is a movement check; the movement gap between Ascendant 3 and Ascendant 5 is huge. [4:12]
- Wall bounces now take stamina, so you can't wall jump as before. [16:35]

Macro and fights:
- You cannot siege the walker at 12 minutes because of scaling resist until 16 minutes; it is not worth it. [12:29]
- The game-flipping play was brute forcing mid walker for no reason and overstaying, and the whole team died for it. [13:01]
- One more green item (about 800 souls) and you might have lived that fight. [13:33]
- After a bad fight, don't look for a fight mid lane; stay toward your jungle. [14:03]
- The lead was slipping because of time spent shooting at enemies mid instead of hyper farming; hit creeps and jungle and path toward the side with more farm up. [14:33]
- As an M1 (carry) in solo queue, only join a fight when it is over an objective: urn, mid boss or defending a walker. Otherwise farm their jungle or your own. [16:04]
- Wraith wants to farm a little more than Haze, but both want to farm. [16:35]
- Never help push walkers as a carry unless you have an advantage, such as your team taking a fight elsewhere or the enemy showing five while you split. [17:09]
- Brute forcing walkers is a bad play unless you have a significant advantage (Rejuvenator, player or ult advantage); if you want to do something, run urn instead. [17:39]
- Fury Trance has been overpowered for a long time: 40% fire rate and 40% spirit resist active plus 22% bullet lifesteal passive; needing Fleetfoot doesn't matter since everyone buys it. [18:10]
- Only bait with mid boss when there is no urn and you have a small advantage that isn't enough to actually take mid boss. [19:10]
- That fight was pointless; staying in fights over nothing slowly loses your lead. [19:40]
- He tunnel visioned the Vyper a little too hard in that fight. [20:14]
- As a carry, don't defend a walker only when they have a significant player advantage (such as three sieging against you alone) and you are not an anti-siege hero like Ivy. Otherwise defend, unless you can trade a walker by counter splitting. [20:45]
- You don't have to join pointless fights as a fed player: use your team's pointless fight to split push or invade the enemy jungle, depending on how bad the fight is. [21:46]
- Mid fights where five players ram into each other are coin flips; get yourself fed while your team fights. [22:16]
- When you hear the enemy take mid boss, grab the urn and hold it in your base so they can't run it. [23:18]
- When your team has the wave pushed, don't defend the walker; farm or secure your own jungle. [25:23]
- With the urn next to your walker it is fightable, but fight it defensively when they have Rejuvenator. [25:23]
- It is fine to split a walker if your team doesn't commit to a fight defending yours. [26:53]
- Communicate splits: tell your team you are splitting the walker and either that they shouldn't commit to a defensive fight, or that you need to be there if they do. [30:34]
- Summary: work on movement, use the secret shop and keep pressure after winning lane, invest in spirit, stop brute forcing mid walker and taking bad fights, and farm instead. [28:33]

Other heroes:
- Victor isn't bad this patch, but Abrams and Billy are so overtuned that in pro play you'd pick them as front line instead. [1:03]
- Pocket can carry games and is probably the best spirit carry in solo queue. [0:00]
- Lash's ult needs to lock on faster because it is very counterplayable and counter itemable. [24:19]
