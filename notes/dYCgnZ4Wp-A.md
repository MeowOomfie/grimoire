## Covers
September 2025 A to Z pass over the in-game popular builds, picking one or two per hero (credited to top players) with what makes each good and how to play it, plus warnings about troll builds.
Context: the meta is spirit nuke and everyone buys Debuff Remover or Counterspell. For any rank.
Heroes: Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Calico, The Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash, McGinnis, Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Seven, Shiv, Sinclair, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous, Vyper, Warden, Wraith, Yamato.
