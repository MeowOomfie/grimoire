# NEW Eternus Seven Build - Ball Build Reborn
channel: Natan  date: 2025-07-07  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8RBqzO0I7VQ

## Covers
Seven, skill order change (Lightning Ball max second), Storm Cloud nerfs, Counterspell vs Static Charge, lane core (Monster Rounds, Compress Cooldown, Suppressor, Enchanter's Emblem, Extra Charge), spirit investment bonuses, core and situational items, Ethereal Shift, Spirit Shielding, Rapid Recharge, example full build

## Claims
- Power Surge is still maxed first because it is the most consistent source of damage and helps you farm. [0:00]
- Lightning Ball is maxed second again because the ult was nerfed significantly several times in a row. [0:30]
- Maxing the ult second is still viable: skip maxing ball second, skip Rapid Recharge, maybe skip Ethereal Shift, and go Unstoppable, Lightning Scroll, Infuser, Warp Stone. [0:30]
- Stun is not maxed second because Counterspell was buffed hard, and it feels bad to put points in it when people counter spell it anyway. [1:01]
- Buy the lane items roughly left to right; just buying them left to right every game works fine. [1:01]
- Monster Rounds helps clear waves and jungle camps early and gives out of combat regen; sell it early when you need slots. [1:01]
- Compress Cooldown imbued on Power Surge with two points in Power Surge gives a 26 second cooldown, so it is up every wave since waves spawn every 30 seconds. [1:32]
- In good matchups that lets you push harder and take the enemy Guardian; in neutral or bad matchups it lets you neutralize the wave, keep it out of your Guardian and stop them taking it. [1:32]
- Suppressor has great base stats (6 spirit power, 75 bonus health) and reduces fire rate for 4 seconds on spirit damage, which Seven reapplies to everyone all game with his AoE spirit kit. [1:32]
- Enchanter's Emblem is a great stat stick: spirit resist, out of combat regen, and spirit power and ability cooldown above 65% health. [2:03]
- Extra Charge with one point in ball gives three ball charges, nice for farming and fighting. [2:03]
- Healing Rite is generally better than Extra Regen because it carries into the mid game, helps you roam with two sprint speed and keeps you healthy while jungling; Extra Regen is good in a lane where you get chip poked constantly. [2:33]
- After lane, buy the core roughly left to right, top row first. [2:33]
- Fleetfoot gives bullet resist and slide distance and removes the shooting move speed penalty (9.1 drops to 6.4 without it); it matters more on Seven than any hero because his move speed scales with spirit, so the percentage penalty costs him more; the active gives move speed and slow resist. [2:33]
- Buy Extra Spirit but not Improved Spirit right after, because of the spirit investment bonuses (buffed this patch): Extra Spirit reaches the next bonus, Improved Spirit would only add 8 spirit power instead of 60, so keep buying vitality and later buy Superior Cooldown and Improved Spirit together to reach the next one. [3:33]
- Spirit Shielding is "absolutely crazy": an about 450 barrier against both weapon and spirit damage, plus move speed and out of combat regen, and every hero can proc it on you. [4:03]
- With slot issues at this point, sell Monster Rounds, Healing Rite or Extra Regen, or hold them and delay Healbane while upgrading first. [4:35]
- Healbane gives 7 spirit power and healing reduction on spirit damage, especially good because your teammates probably will not buy healing reduction. [4:35]
- Superior Cooldown gives 4 out of combat regen and reduces all ability cooldowns: one point stun to 17 seconds, ball from 26 to 20 seconds. [5:05]
- Rapid Recharge reaches the next spirit investment bonus, adds a charge and halves time between ball charges from 8 to 4 seconds. [5:05]
- Ethereal Shift is his favourite item and amazing on Seven, who has no defensive abilities or escapes: dodge anything and stall for 4 seconds, cooldown starts when pressed (about 10 seconds effective with Transcendent Cooldown), usable about twice a fight for 8 seconds of invulnerability, then 5 seconds of three move speed, 30% spirit resist and 20 spirit power; it also reaches the next investment bonus. [5:36]
- Boundless Spirit is a static spirit increase but does not reach the next investment bonus; for that you need another tier 2 spirit item such as Slowing Hex, Mystic Vulnerability or Quicksilver Reload. [6:37]
- Between core items you often pick up one or two mid game situational items. [6:37]
- Slowing Hex is great against movement reliant heroes: Lash cannot grapple, Abrams cannot shoulder charge, Infernus cannot flame dash, and Dynamo and Mirage cannot dodge your stun. [6:37]
- Return Fire is great against gun heroes such as Wraith, Haze and Infernus: bullet resist and 60% of bullet damage returned. [7:07]
- Reactive Barrier is great against heavy CC teams and can even be a lane buy, for example against Bebop (hook) and Vindicta (stake); it does not work against knockups such as Dynamo stomp, Lash slam and Holliday barrel. [7:07]
- Debuff Reducer is built only for Unstoppable or Debuff Remover; Spirit Lifesteal builds into Infuser and is better later, after Ethereal Shift or Boundless Spirit rather than right after Fleetfoot. [7:37]
- Counterspell was buffed: it now heals, has strong base stats (bonus health and spirit power) and a generous 1 second parry that blocks spells and gives the buffs. [8:07]
- After the core there is no left to right; pick survivability, damage or utility for your play style, your team and the enemy team. [8:37]
- Infuser gives lifesteal, spirit resist and bonus health, and its active gives a lot of spirit lifesteal and spirit power for 6 seconds. [8:37]
- If you still play for your ult, Unstoppable is a must buy, guaranteeing at least 5 seconds of ult. [8:37]
- Debuff Remover is great against many debuffs such as Vindicta, Infernus and Pocket. [9:07]
- Warp Stone (16 second cooldown, 11 with Transcendent Cooldown) gives 40% bullet resist after teleporting; with Ethereal Shift's spirit resist the pair covers both resists and makes you very slippery. [9:07]
- Stamina Mastery (two air dashes, triple jump, longer air dash) he has not been buying much recently but still thinks is good late. [9:38]
- Treat Spirit Resilience as a late Enchanter's Emblem replacement, but Escalating Exposure plus Transcendent Cooldown, with Ethereal Shift's spirit resist, maxes the spirit investment bonus and is generally better. [9:38]
- Mystic Vulnerability is for enemies stacking spirit resist (you already shred with the first Power Surge point) and reaches the next investment bonus; Crippling Headshot boosts weapon investment and adds shred and healing reduction; ignore Burst Fire, he is only testing it. [10:10]
- Quicksilver Reload and Mercurial Magnum are still solid for die hard Power Surge play but no longer amazing. [10:41]
- Silence Wave is still broken: AoE, easy to hit, reduces spirit resist and spirit power, silences almost 4 seconds, and is guaranteed to hit when chained with the stun. [10:41]
- Do not prioritize Transcendent Cooldown; over Superior Cooldown it only adds 5% ability and 30% item cooldown reduction, so it is worth it with many actives, though long games get it anyway. [11:11]
- Lightning Scroll is mainly for ult builds, but you only need to touch people with the ult briefly, so you can tap and cancel the ult and still stun. [11:11]
- Example full build: core plus Return Fire mid game, then Spirit Lifesteal toward Infuser, Debuff Remover against debuffs, selling Return Fire and Spirit Shielding for slots, Transcendent Cooldown with many actives, Crippling Headshot for resist and weapon investment, and Escalating Exposure to max the spirit investment bonus; Suppressor, Enchanter's Emblem and Healbane can be sold that late. [11:41]
