# The BEST Deadlock Build I Have Ever Used
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-05-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nUHH2wSoSBo

## Covers
A full gun Warden build for the May 2025 patch era (right after the big item update and a Wraith nerf), built around Cultist Sacrifice, Arcane Surge and Fury Trance. For Warden players: item order with the reason for each item, ability order, the Arcane Surge plus Cultist Sacrifice trick, the cage kill combo and when to pop Fury Trance and Vampiric Burst. Builds go stale with patches.

## Claims
- Despite patch notes that read like a nerf, Warden is busted this patch; this gun Warden build makes him broken if you understand how to play it. [0:30]
- The current meta is heavy spirit burst (Pocket, Dynamo) and high HP characters (Shiv, Abrams, Kelvin): you build really tanky or you die in one second, and this build counters that. [1:00]
- The three items that make the build work are Cultist Sacrifice, Arcane Surge and Fury Trance, with Fury Trance as the big power spike. [1:31]
- Start lane with Monster Rounds and ideally rush Cultist Sacrifice as the first real item. [2:01]
- Cultist Sacrifice now stays active if you die in lane, so you keep its 25% fire rate, bonus health and ability range, which makes it far more consistent. [2:01]
- Cultist Sacrifice is insane on Warden: ability range helps his cage and his ultimate, its NPC damage makes farming (an iffy point on Warden) easier, and its out of combat regen means you usually don't need Extra Regen in lane. [2:32]
- Rebuttal is in the build only for laning against Viscous, to counter his melees; usually skip it and go Cultist Sacrifice first. [2:32]
- Monster Rounds gives some regen, but Warden still has high base regen of 3, so you don't really need more. [3:03]
- Once you have Cultist Sacrifice, the first thing to sacrifice is a lane trooper, because it gives the most souls of any creep until tier three camps spawn, more than a tier two creep. [3:03]
- After the first sacrifice you are really strong in lane: the cage gets much more reach and the ultimate becomes much scarier. [3:33]
- Next buy Extended Magazine and Titanic Magazine; Titanic Magazine got a slight nerf but is an insanely solid item on Warden that enables the whole build, so do not skip it. [3:33]
- Quicksilver Reload comes much later because you usually won't run dry on enemies with this build and Titanic Magazine covers reload needs. [4:04]
- Then buy Extra Stamina and upgrade it right away into Arcane Surge, which is useful no matter how early you get it. [4:04]
- Arcane Surge: jump dash to get bonus range, bonus duration and bonus spirit power on the next ability or item you use. [4:04]
- Use Arcane Surge on the cage, on the ultimate, and on Cultist Sacrifice, since it works on items. [4:36]
- Once tier three camps are up, always sacrifice a tier three creep with Cultist Sacrifice, because it gives more souls than a lane trooper. [5:06]
- Jump dash to activate Arcane Surge before using Cultist Sacrifice: it extends the buff by about 26 seconds, so downtime drops to about 60 seconds instead of 80, a free extra 30 seconds that is easy to pull off. [5:06]
- With this trick Cultist Sacrifice is active for about 200 seconds; the only weak time is the last 60 seconds of its cooldown. [5:37]
- Ability order: two points in Alchemical Flask, then one in Willpower, one in Binding Word, then max the ultimate first. [6:08]
- With Cultist Sacrifice and Arcane Surge range the cage reaches insanely far, giving a lethal kill combo without needing Slowing Hex yet. [6:08]
- Buy Fleet Foot first after that for early movement speed, which helps box farming. [6:08]
- Boxes are way better since the big item update: about 60% of the time they spawn souls, so early on farming boxes is more efficient than farming creeps; gold statues also have a 60% drop. [6:38]
- Fleet Foot removes Warden's movement penalty while shooting, so he can chase and catch targets and stay in range during the ultimate. [6:38]
- Then buy Slowing Hex. [7:08]
- The ultimate is still really strong early; ideally proc Arcane Surge before ulting for extra duration, spirit power and reach. [7:08]
- Once you have Slowing Hex, do the classic combo: cage, Slowing Hex, then blast them; done correctly the enemy often gets trapped. [7:38]
- At this stage you can gank, farm boxes and start farming tier two camps, since Titanic Magazine makes jungle camps feasible. [7:38]
- Think of Warden as an early to early-mid game ganker; this is still not his strongest form. [8:09]
- Next buy Backstabber and Slowing Bullets. [8:09]
- Hitting investment thresholds for weapon, vitality and spirit is one of the biggest deals this patch and gives insane extra value; most builds ignore it. [8:09]
- The two 1600 items are there to reach 58% bonus weapon damage really early, an insane power spike on its own; each adds about 18% weapon damage on top of its own effect. [8:39]
- These two 1600 items are chosen over classics like Burst Fire or Berserker on Warden because Backstabber almost always procs: caged enemies walk away from you, not toward you. [9:09]
- Backstabber adds 100 spirit damage, reduces the enemy's bullet resist, gives you movement speed (almost 2 m/s) and bonus health, and makes enemies hear your footsteps 50% less. [9:09]
- The demo shows 50% quieter footsteps cuts the distance at which you are heard roughly in half, which makes ganks far easier. [10:12]
- Don't run Backstabber in lane: it is an early-mid game ganking item that stays valuable into late game; sell it much later if you run out of slots. [10:42]
- Slowing Bullets gives more fire rate (on Warden, the more fire rate the better) and slows enemies further, so you hit more cages and close distance even when you miss them. [11:12]
- You need to be close to the enemy because the big power spike, Fury Trance, is a close range item. [11:12]
- Next buy Battle Vest: about 10% extra health, bullet resist, plus weapon damage and fire rate, which stay active since Warden is usually the one engaging. [11:43]
- Then buy Bullet Lifesteal and upgrade it into Fury Trance. [11:43]
- With Fury Trance at roughly equal net worth to the enemy, you will kill anyone in the game 1v1. [12:15]
- Fury Trance is broken now because its active resist changed to 40% spirit resist instead of bullet resist; spirit resist was very hard to come by, and most enemy teams run spirit damage. [12:15]
- Against gun heroes like Haze or Wraith you still have Battle Vest and win the 1v1 on damage. [12:45]
- Fury Trance is the best item in the game on Warden, but people misuse it. [12:45]
- Fury Trance gives 40% fire rate and the resist but removes stamina usage and silences you, so no dashing, sliding or abilities; Warden doesn't care because he initiates and uses all his spells before activating it. [13:16]
- Fight setup: with ult, jump dash and ult; without, for a single target gank, jump dash, cage, Slowing Hex, grenade (Alchemical Flask), optionally Willpower, then activate Fury Trance as close to the enemy as possible. [13:46]
- After Fury Trance you slow down without dashes, but you still have Fleet Foot and likely the Backstabber movement speed, and the enemy is usually trapped, so keeping up is easy. [14:16]
- After a jump dash Arcane Surge is active for 7 seconds, and it only buffs the first spell used, items included, so cage first (unless it's a big team fight and you ult) because cage range matters most. [14:16]
- Full combo: jump dash, cage, Slowing Hex, grenade, Willpower, then activate Fury Trance; do it fast. [15:17]
- With 40% spirit resist during Fury Trance, enemy spells barely hurt (a Pocket trying to burst him did little); Haze is about the only worry, and you can leave her radius. [16:19]
- Fury Trance's damage and fire rate give ridiculous lifesteal for the whole fight, the most healing he has done this patch even though lifesteal got weaker. [16:19]
- Fleet Foot can still be used while silenced by Fury Trance; use it and slide to stretch your magazine. [16:49]
- Avoid having to reload during Fury Trance, because then you really can't do anything; it is easy not to run out until it ends. [16:49]
- Then buy Quicksilver Reload and right after it Mercurial Magnum, another damage spike; it got nerfed, but you throw only one flask at the start of a fight, so that one magazine gets the bonus (62% extra spirit damage here) plus more fire rate. [17:21]
- Spellslinger is not in the build because you use each spell once per fight rather than spamming them. [17:52]
- Max Willpower before Alchemical Flask for better scaling and way more health (about 600 overshield), and barriers now work with resistances. [17:52]
- Quicksilver Reload and Mercurial Magnum are bought for bonus fire rate and damage, not reload. [18:54]
- Next buy Escalating Resilience: 30% bullet resist for 24 seconds plus extra ammo, and Warden's fire rate stacks it to max almost instantly, giving 40% resist on both for cheap alongside 22% lifesteal. [19:24]
- Then buy Frenzy: it now also gives 40% spirit resist below 50% health, plus big fire rate and movement speed increases at half health; with this much regen and resist, low health makes you stronger. [19:54]
- In the late game he shows about 68% spirit resist and almost 45% bullet resist, Ivy-like fire rate and movement speed like Calico. [20:25]
- Then buy Siphon Bullets (Siphon Bullets, Vampiric Burst or Frenzy can be swapped in order, all three are good): it got a buff to steal every 0.7 seconds, about 12.5% more drain and 12.5% more healing. [20:57]
- By this point you usually need to sell: sell Backstabber, and Slowing Hex if needed, since late game caging matters less and you just run at the enemy and shoot. [21:58]
- Last, buy Vampiric Burst, a late game luxury: when scared of dying, activate it for 80% lifesteal and 40% extra fire rate. [22:29]
- Vampiric Burst also refills ammo, so when about to run out during Fury Trance while silenced, activate it as an instant reload. [22:59]
- The longer the fight goes the better for you, since Siphon Bullets keeps running. [23:29]
- It's easy to keep up in souls with this build because Cultist Sacrifice is always available and kills and creeps give lots of souls. [24:30]
- The build does insanely well in Eternus lobbies; with ult he can go in 1v3 and kill the whole enemy team. [24:30]
- Wraith got a major nerf this patch and is really weak now. [24:30]
- A spirit version (Metro Spirit Warden) goes heavily into the ultimate and is viable, but the gun build is better even for ult fans: it keeps enough spirit power for a strong ult and stays useful with the gun when the ult misses. [25:00]
- Utility as needed: Unstoppable if the enemy has lots of stuns, and practice Counter Spell, which counters many things; these two are the key defensive options. [26:03]
- Other situational options: Debuff Remover against Infernus, Blood Tribute against Mo & Krill (50% debuff resist), Plated Armor against heavy gun damage, Armor Piercing Rounds rarely. [26:03]
- Usually he adds Counter Spell or Unstoppable and follows the build left to right; it works against almost every comp. [26:34]
