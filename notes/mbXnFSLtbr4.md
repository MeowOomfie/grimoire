# The Consequences of Over Brawling | Ascendant Mina Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-12-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbXnFSLtbr4

## Covers
Mina, Midknight coaching review of one Ascendant Mina game (December 2025 patch), lane into Victor and Calico.
Themes: over brawling instead of farming and invading, who to focus (squishy targets over Victor), playing around the 2, positioning, denies, holding the spirit spike, Spellbreaker, and not clicking to level abilities.

## Claims
Lane (Mina):
- When the Victor overextends and the Calico leaves to fix her minion, that is a good window to punish; watch the map for it. [0:30]
- Kill the wave as fast as possible, proc Love Bites, then fight, instead of walking into a fight while minions are up. [0:30]
- Love Bites pretty much guarantees early wave prio, especially if they try to fight; use that prio to go kill them. [0:30]
- Do not click to level abilities: it slows you down, stops you clicking in fights and gives the enemy a level-up window. [1:02]
- Rushing the spirit spike with no greens is possible when ahead, but it is risky. [1:02]
- Do not dedicate your 2 for about three bullets of damage while Love Bites is not popped; it is only worth it while you are still stacking Love Bites or the target is close to the proc. [2:04]
- Once the lane is won, all you have to do is not dive. [2:04]
- Playing below the stairs puts you in an unfortunate spot. [2:34]
- If holding tab to level abilities stops your movement, try leveling with alt. [2:34]
- After you 2 into their back line, land on top of the bridge and jump back when they try to fight; Mina without her 2 in their back line is dead no matter what. [3:04]
- Aim the recast on top of the bridge or toward your own side, not to stay behind them. [3:04]
- Mina is unpunishable and overpowered with her 2 up and mortal without it; play around the 2. [3:35]
- Focus the squishier target: a third-HP Victor is harder to kill than a half-HP Calico, and Love Bites on Calico is damage she cannot regen as easily. [3:35]
- Focusing Victor is fine when Calico is behind the wall; shoot Calico when she is pixels away from a Love Bites proc. [4:06]
- Do not buy at that moment, it gives them free wave prio: farm the camp for the heal, kill the wave to create 5 to 10 seconds of neutral time, then buy; do one, not both. [4:39]
- Switch to Calico preemptively once Victor gets out of the way. [5:11]
- If you 2 away after getting hit by Calico's 2, throw your parry first and then 2 away: she cannot punish a parry, worst case you take one extra auto, best case a kill parry. If she casts her 1 first, just 2 away to dodge the bombs. [6:14]
- When your wave is dead and you cannot hit tower, start looking for harass. [6:45]
- Buy right away when the spirit spike is ready; normally do not give space for a tier 1 offensive item, but the spirit spike is massive. [6:45]
- Stalling the spirit spike by 800 souls is really rough: do not upgrade Extra Spirit to Improved Spirit until you already have Spirit Sap or your 4.8k spike. [7:16]
- When Calico has used her 1, walk up, but keep a finger on your 2 because her 1 does huge damage if it lands. [8:17]
- In lane it is better to hit tower than to keep focusing enemies who are not misplaying. [8:17]
- Rule: if you are winning lane 5 to 2 on kills and tower is still up at 10 minutes, you are misplaying the lane; the tower should be gone a minute or two earlier. [8:48]
- Even with bad aim it is better to prioritize the squishier target. [8:48]

Build (Mina):
- Rushing full cooldown is fine on Mina: green spike does little because she has no HP to work with and gun spike does little because she has no gun damage. [9:53]
- Mystic Shot is also fine with Cultist; Swift Striker is another gun spike option. [11:23]
- Tankbuster this early is for farming, not players. [14:29]
- Mina's HP pool is so low that she is the one hero who can hold her green spike late. [16:30]
- Mina is one of the only heroes who can delay the health spike and skip the gun spike because her gun does so little. [25:14]
- Spellbreaker was really good this game: great into Viscous, Lash and Calico, good into Victor, and not bad into Haze since many Hazes lead with Sleep Dagger. [22:42]
- He would buy Spellbreaker before Spiritual Overflow; after Spellbreaker you are nearly immortal. Spiritual Overflow's in-fight cooldown is good for Love Bites, but its gun investment is essentially useless on Mina. [27:18]
- Anti-heal for Victor: Crippling Headshot, Healbane, Toxic Bullets and Inhibitor; anything you can reapply after he cleanses. Not Decay or Spirit Burn, since his maxed 2 cleanses them for free. Crippling Headshot is generally better than Inhibitor against him. [33:02]
- Upgrade Spirit Shredder Bullets to Spirit Rend only if you already have Swift Striker, since then it completes a gun spike for 1600; do not go out of your way for the gun spike on Mina. [40:09]
- Trophy Collector sacks your tempo and makes you weak. [12:26]

Mid game and macro:
- When ahead with a soul lead early, run the urn; you do not need to tap it, and with full guardians up, trading a side guardian for it does not matter. [10:23]
- Prioritize the wave instead of splitting it between three people while looking for picks. [10:53]
- When winning, look at their jungle: shove the wave, eat their camps, shove the next wave, eat more camps. Do not spend downtime fishing for picks; if they come to farm their jungle you get the pick for free. [11:23]
- Sieging a walker too early gets you punished; eat their camps instead. [11:54]
- After the enemy takes a walker and the game is neutral, do not look for a fight; just eat the farm. [12:56]
- Camps next to the right lane are contested while that lane is shoving, but Mina is unlikely to get punished there. [13:28]
- Going into the enemy jungle on their strong side to steal camps turns into a coin-flip fight on their side, which favors them. [13:59]
- When trying to get out, 2 upward. [14:29]
- Mina does not want a permanent brawl; your farm directly reflects how much you brawl, so spend more time hitting jungle, especially invading. [15:29]
- Instead of splitting farm with your team near a guardian fight, be in your own jungle farming. [17:00]
- Do not base at low HP: walk to a wave or line up a tier 2 creep and heal around 600 HP with your 1. [17:00]
- If you are going to leave a walker push, leave before killing the wave under it; once you are at the walker, stay and hit it. With your 2 angled toward the roof and no enemy Slowing Hex, you are essentially immortal. [18:01]
- Use the pressure from a kill to invade. [19:06]
- Six people mid fighting over a guardian is not worth the farm lost; that guardian only gives the middle shop. [19:37]
- When you split a walker alone and someone comes through the zip, your mindset is "best case I die for this walker"; an ego split is acceptable in solo queue. [21:08]
- A coin-flip fight on your own side while defending is in your favor. Reacting to it is fine when you are close, not when you are in another lane. [22:10]
- Only go down mid when the team is calling mid boss, and then be in the pit hitting it. [22:42]
- As Mina, normally look to parry; someone on the team should parry, which is a coordination thing. [23:13]
- When ahead, do not melee into a likely parry, because getting parried can lose the fight; when behind it is fine to go for it. [23:43]
- After the urn runs, fix the wave and steal all the jungle during the Rejuvenator's 4 minutes; get an advantage with those minutes instead of throwing in the first one. [24:13]
- Bebop with McGinnis and Bebop with Mirage are two of the worst lanes to face, since a hook into a wall or tornado leaves no counterplay. [24:44]
- Reacting to a fight with Rejuvenator up is good. Focus Holliday when she walks by; a melee from her gives a guaranteed Love Bites proc, about half her HP bar. [26:14]
- With Superior Stamina, dash jump instead of forward dashing or dash sliding. [26:45]
- Collapsing on a Victor seen splitting alone was a fine decision. [28:19]
- Switch targets: going from Haze to Victor was good, but once Love Bites is procced on Victor and Lash walks by, switch again; Victor's job is to eat your focus and cooldowns. [29:22]
- The game was lost because the mid game was spent brawling instead of farming and invading; with Victor's farm the game would have been over. [29:57]
- Let a coin-flip fight die rather than join it. [31:28]
- Use Focus Lens on their back line and then jump them, not on their front line. [31:59]
- At 1 HP against a Viscous with Splatter up, back up, and hover Counterspell for the expected Splatter. [33:02]
- Do not be a front-line Mina and do not chase into them. [33:32]
- When Viscous lands one Puddle Punch on the urn runner, a second is always coming, so back off. [35:02]
- A Paige fixing the solo lane is not great; a hero who will use the farm should do it. Use that time to steal their jungle. [35:32]
- Walk up and grab the souls you leave behind. [36:04]
- You have to be able to leave your team for dead; do not run into the enemy while your whole team is dead. [37:04]

Summary notes:
- Two denies in a game is okay; aim for three. [37:36]
- When ahead, use the pressure to invade and steal jungle, not to run at their walker and die; this lane-won game should have been won by invading after the urn. [38:06]
- Even a full-HP Lash is easier to kill than a half-HP Victor; whenever there is an option besides Victor, hit them. [38:37]
- Late game, when your team is dead, back off, and in fights play a more back-line role instead of jumping into the fray. [40:39]
