# Applying Real Pressure | E6 Coaching Session Sample
channel: HERESY
date: 2026-01-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQa2deOmqmw

## Covers
VOD review of an Emissary 6 Mina (about 250 games played) against Infernus and Vindicta in lane, recorded before the late January 2026 patch.
Lane trading and early itemization, soul farming between waves, side laning and split pushing on Mina, not wasting time at useless fights, when to run the Urn, and Mid Boss as the win condition.

## Claims
- Mina's identity is doing very aggressive things on the map because she is so hard to punish; with good stamina use, Stamina Mastery and Sanguine Retreat she is almost impossible to kill. [1:00]
- On Mina do daring things: invade the enemy jungle and push Walkers even very early in the game. [1:00]
- At 250 games you do not yet understand Deadlock enough to fully leverage Mina, whatever your talent. [0:30]
- Mina is one of Deadlock's only true early to mid tempo heroes; she wants to get ahead early and snowball. [3:33]
- Mina vs Infernus is the same mini game: both proc your 3 (Love Bite vs his burn) with bullets, so you are evenly matched; if he lands burn plus Flame Dash he wins the trade, but your poke does more consistent damage tit for tat. [3:33]
- Do not face Infernus head on too often; when his burn starts to build, back off and let it tick away. [2:32]
- Vindicta's flight makes Love Bite harder to proc at range, but she is squishy with a low health pool, and Mina's quick burst exploits her when you get on top of her. [4:04]
- Since Mina is strong in this lane, use Love Bite to take aggressive trades; the more you fight while hitting Love Bites, the more you win. [4:35]
- Playing the veil early is correct when you are the stronger laner; it limits your opponents' options. [4:35]
- Shooting the minions before the Infernus to get the crash is good. [5:09]
- Lanes are duo lanes, so take cross angles from your teammate; when you crash the wave from the left side, wrap around toward the firehouse so the enemies cannot use that cover and must go further back. [5:39]
- Mistake: shoving without spending souls; unspent items meant the enemies got trades back and the shove gave no tactical advantage. Cash in items as soon as possible, and hit a box on the way back (there are about five on the way to shop) to reach the next item. [6:39]
- Mina scales with spirit because Love Bite does spirit damage off your gun, so early spirit items like Extra Spirit do a lot for her. [6:39]
- You can buy Extra Spirit after the first wave unless you were denied several times; with Love Bite procced about six times since wave one, Extra Spirit earlier would have left the Infernus at least 100 HP lower. [7:10]
- Extra Spirit takes priority over Mystic Burst early: Mystic Burst needs 80 ability damage on its tooltip (before resistances) to proc its 40 damage, and Mina's Love Bite tooltip was 74, so it did not proc. [8:41]
- Mystic Burst-style faster procs matter more against players who play cover well and give you short shooting windows, which is less common at this MMR; Extra Spirit simply makes Love Bite hit much harder. [8:10]
- Extra Spirit also raises Mina's heal per kill, letting her take ballsier Love Bite trades and heal from camps. [9:12]
- Take more aggressive Love Bite trades when the wave is shoved, and heal later, especially with a safe lane partner like Seven with Static Charge at full HP. [9:42]
- Once you can kill tier one camps fast, they heal Mina 500 to 600 instantly, so she rarely has to back to heal. [10:12]
- Check enemy items constantly; in Deadlock it matters even more than in other MOBAs. [11:14]
- If a low enemy has Healing Rite, he wants to hide, not fight; Mina can play past the wave to find him or fight his partner. If he has no healing item, he is probably backing and you can go look for him. [11:44]
- One bullet on a healing enemy cancels the regen, worth about 200 damage of denied healing. [12:14]
- After landing damage under tower with tower aggro on you, use Sanguine Retreat (2) backwards immediately; you could have died to a Vindicta snipe. [12:14]
- Knowing Vindicta has Stake, use Sanguine Retreat before she throws it when you are standing in an enclosed camp room, instead of trying to react to the Stake. [13:15]
- He avoids making mechanical points because mechanics improve naturally over time. [13:15]
- Optimally, run boxes or kill camps between basically every wave: once the wave is cleared and the enemies walk away, nothing happens until the next wave. [15:47]
- Mistake: lingering 10 to 15 seconds after each wave doing nothing until the next wave arrives. [15:47]
- Exception: when an enemy is very low you can stay and threaten tower dives instead of farming. [16:18]
- After clearing this wave, go into the jungle immediately. [17:20]
- Mistake: waiting about 10 seconds for the bridge buff; run boxes on the rooftops and the rooftop chain leading to the bridge buffs while you wait, worth around 200 souls at 10 minutes plus golden statue buffs. [17:50]
- Golden statues give only buffs, not souls; boxes give only souls. [19:23]
- Boxes have about a 50% chance to give souls, with a correction so that after dry boxes the next ones are more likely to give souls, evening out at 50%. [19:23]
- You should always be doing something; any seconds spent waiting in lane for the next wave could be spent on boxes. [20:24]
- Seconds are everything: a MOBA is a sandbox about getting as strong as possible in about 20 minutes and then taking the game winning fight. [20:55]
- Mina has a low health pool but high survivability through movement, offset by Rake, so she is best at constant skirmishing and looking for resources in the jungle. [21:25]
- Because Mina escapes so well, she should prioritize stealing enemy camps; avoid enemy vaults (Center Sacrifice) unless a camp is lined up next to it to Rake your health back, since a vault costs about 40% of her health. [21:56]
- Mid roams as a side laner are fine only if you can get back to your tower in time; here the Infernus was set to take her tower while she got distracted in the mid fight. [22:27]
- Mistake: roaming mid when not particularly strong; your presence was not going to swing the fight. In the minute after picking up the buff, Mina earned only about 500 souls. [22:57]
- Mistake: brawling for towers mid like it is Overwatch while the whole jungle on both sides is still alive; you have to go eat the jungle. [23:59]
- Even at half HP it is almost impossible to gank a Mina in her own jungle because of Sanguine Retreat. [23:59]
- After the rebalance a wave gives basically 100% souls however many players split it, about 8% more if two or three catch it, which is negligible. [24:29]
- If one player catches each wave, the other three running boxes or camps always earn more than two people splitting a wave for 8% extra. [25:01]
- If you see teammates mid, those souls are being harvested; only show up mid when you are specifically trying to kill someone. [25:01]
- Decide whether to join a fight from data: are you really strong right now, do the enemies have ultimates, do you have play-making ultimates. [25:31]
- Your default should be harvesting the jungle souls; Mina heals off the jungle in a way Bebop and Lash cannot. [25:31]
- Mistake: showing up to a tower that is already dead (one health with Paige and Shiv there), splitting the wave; you could have taken one camp and been killing the second. [26:31]
- A fight in your own jungle with your whole kit up, against a reasonably low Paradox, is a good fight to show up to; teammates respond faster in your jungle. [29:04]
- Mistake summary: three and a half minutes with zero camps killed and five waves split; you moved between events but did nothing useful. [27:01]
- Better: eat the jungle for a soul lead, hit a power spike by cashing out on a 3k item, then show up to one specific fight and make something happen while being 2,000 souls richer. [27:31]
- Judging a fight by whether you got a kill is too simplistic; the problem is joining fights without enough reason and sacrificing time you could spend getting something. [28:01]
- Tending toward farming camps means that even when you miss a fight, you always have something to show for it. [28:33]
- Team comms matter: if teammates call for help or say someone is low, that changes whether to join. [29:34]
- You kill people because they get in the way of you farming the map; you win by getting a soul lead and using it to kill them in their base. [30:05]
- The base is so defensible that you need an enormous soul lead to take it; Mid Boss exists so a roughly 10% soul lead can still win by fighting over a neutral objective. Mid Boss is the ticket to killing their base. [30:37]
- Everything in Deadlock is about getting strong enough to kill the Mid Boss, which wins the game. [30:37]
- Kill enemy players when they stand in the way of their jungle or the Walker; the Walker gives a lot of souls and opens the map. [31:07]
- If a fight is a 50/50 coin flip and there is jungle up right next to you, take the jungle; killing people does not win the game, being richer does. [31:07]
- Running the Urn is less preferable than running Mid Boss when both are options. [33:07]
- Do not call the Urn when your team is losing, except sometimes grabbing it to hide it from the opponents. [33:07]
- Run the Urn proactively when you have a discernible advantage you cannot convert into something more important like a Walker. [33:37]
- From 10 to 15 minutes Walkers are very hard and dangerous to push; going for one at 12 minutes with a lead most likely gets you killed and loses the lead. [33:37]
- The best time to run the Urn is generally when you are up almost 10% souls; the enemy team, up about 7k or 8% souls here, should have been thinking about running it. [33:37]
- Ultimate economy also decides the Urn: if your Dynamo has his ultimate and the enemy Kelvin does not, run the Urn. [34:09]
- The Urn is a dilemma for the opponents: let you run it and get richer, or fight you now; force that choice only when you are favored to win the fight. [34:39]
- Rule: if you think you can beat your opponents in a 4v4 right now, consider running the Urn. [35:09]
- Later in the game, take Mid Boss or push a Walker instead; in the 10 to 20 minute window, if you can win a 4v4, make it happen by picking up the Urn. [35:41]
- The Urn breaks late mid game standstills by forcing the enemies to come to you, but only do it when you have the advantage. [36:11]
- If you are the fed player (for example an Infernus on 19k), you become the team captain and should make the call: we are up 7k souls, they are missing this ultimate, Vindicta grab the Urn. [36:41]
- An Urn fight should be 5v5, with a teammate still protecting a Walker across the map; if all six show up to the Urn, something is probably going wrong. [36:41]
- When you are the strong player, calling the fight is how you leverage your combat power and snowball. [37:11]
- Mina should gravitate to the side lane because it lets her constantly threaten Walker pushes. [38:44]
- Side laners need good escapes (Mina, Infernus, sometimes Mirage), not raw tankiness like Abrams or Mo & Krill, because pushing past the center line invites several enemies to collapse on you. [38:44]
- Heroes without an escape like Bebop invite enemies to run them down, so they cannot push waves or even safely catch the mid-point wave alone. [39:44]
- Side lane is a great way to get fed because you get solo waves and farm jungle between them; that is why the enemy Infernus was fed after shoving the yellow lane and farming camps all game. [40:15]
- The enemy Infernus pushed a wave, caught the next one and took the Walker uncontested while everyone fought mid. [39:14]
- Heroes that want to play toward the middle are those that need less money and want lots of fights and skirmishing; Paige is good at permanently fighting around mid. [40:46]
- On Mina, constantly shove waves out: catch the wave on your side, then catch the next one, and crash your waves into their Walker; this takes space and gives teammates information about who shows up to defend. [41:17]
- Shove a wave, do jungle, shove a wave, do jungle; Mina is the hardest hero to kill in that situation, especially with Stamina Mastery. Bebop, Shiv and Paige cannot do this. [42:51]
- Pushing two waves to pull enemies to defend, then taking their jungle while they do, is exactly what the best Minas do. [42:20]
- Stamina Mastery is very good on Mina and a form of skill expression (wall jumps, jumping and dashing twice without touching the ground). [43:21]
- Roam to mid after handling the side lane, not instead of it; it is more productive that way. [43:21]
- Hovering mid forces someone else to catch the side lane; Seven has strong long range wave clear but cannot run up to the midpoint because he has no escape, and Shiv or Bebop alone there die if Mo & Krill shows up. [43:51]
- Every place you are is a sacrifice of being somewhere else: opportunity cost, much too high on a hero like Mina. [45:23]
- Do not just react to where everyone else is on the minimap; decide where you want to be based on your hero, while staying flexible since Deadlock moves fast. [45:53]
- Ido (a Mina player worth watching) does not perma fight in blue lane with his team; he rotates between a side lane Walker, small skirmishes (proc Love Bite on the Vyper) and jungle camps, and wastes the time of every two players who come to kill him. [46:23]
- Wasting two enemies' time gives your teammates an advantage. [46:54]
- Without your ultimate and not at full HP, fighting under the enemy Walker is pointless because enemies get resistance there; tell your team it is a bad fight and leave. [47:28]
- Make the decision to leave a bad fight 15 seconds earlier and you would already be clearing a camp, Raking back to full HP, or have killed the Center Sacrifice. [47:58]
- When using Sanguine Retreat, teleport to height: few heroes have vertical movement, and height gives you options the enemies do not have. [48:33]
- Down souls but with a big respawn timer on the enemy Vyper, calling the Urn is appropriate; you do not need to be up souls, being up a player is a discernible advantage. [49:34]
- Calling such an Urn fight must happen instantly; in lower elos teams waste time and the Vyper respawns. [50:04]
- In solo queue it is too risky to go for Mid Boss at a 6v5 like this; a coordinated league team (Deadlock Night Shift) might, when the most fed enemy has no ultimate. Solo queue wants a bigger advantage before calling Mid Boss. [50:04]
- Winning the forced Urn fight then opens Mid Boss: one advantage leads to the next, which is how you snowball. [50:36]
- Mistake: standing in Infernus's Flame Dash in a tight space; he also had Infuser and was healing off it. [51:36]
- When your team is down a player, tell them to play safe under your Walker so they fight with Walker resist instead of into the enemy's. [52:06]
- This was a great Debuff Remover game: Haze with Fixation and Capacitor, Infernus with a dash build proccing burns, Vindicta's Stake and Vyper's poison. [52:36]
- Side laning makes Debuff Remover's value obvious sooner because you take more 1v1s and 1v2s. [53:36]
- Siphon Bullets is the best 1v1 item in the game; with Siphon Bullets and Fixation Haze beats Mina in a 1v1 late, but in the mid game Mina could win a longer poke and reset engagement, especially with Debuff Remover. [54:07]
- In this phase of the game everything is about orchestrating a scenario to go for Mid Boss. [54:38]
- Use your team's hard CC (Bebop's hook, Seven's stun, Lash's ultimate) intentionally to start fights and get a pick. [54:38]
- Mistake: after winning a 6v4 your team defended base instead of taking Mid Boss; send Seven to clear the base alone and the rest go Mid Boss. [55:09]
- Seven's identity includes wave clear (ultimate and Power Surge), so he can be trusted to defend the base alone. [55:44]
- Even if the enemies take both shrines and drop your Patron, having Mid Boss at 31 minutes for your whole team still leaves you favored to win and at least down their Patron. [56:14]
- Defending instead of taking Mid Boss looked safe but just gave an even fight against their most fed players and put the game back to even. [56:45]
- Confident calls, especially when you are fed, get listened to more often than not; winning just 10% more games from Mid Boss calls is an enormous win rate increase and a new rank. [57:15]
- Helper syndrome: being too responsive to teammates' fights is not inherently bad and suits an Abrams or Paige, but it is not the best use of Mina's time. [58:15]
- Late in the game fight micro matters less because the team ahead in souls naturally chains kills. [58:15]
- At lower ranks, play for yourself: teammates often do the wrong thing even when fed, and Mina is a fairly selfish hero. [59:51]
- Playing for the team is more viable when everyone is the same skill, which is why support feels bad when you are better than your team; it is very hard to carry on support. [61:55]
- Assume your teammates and the enemies are fighting over something stupid; focus on the map's resources, the waves and available objectives and play aggressively for them. [62:25]
- Being stronger when you do join a fight makes that fight go positively. [62:55]
- Summary: do not drop waves; when playing anything remotely like a carry, catch waves, and never abandon a lane you are defending. [63:56]
- Summary: do not default to fighting; before every fight you should be able to name a reason (ultimates, souls, waves). If you cannot, you should have been in the jungle. [64:27]
