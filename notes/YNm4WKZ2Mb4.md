- Vindicta cannot fight in a hole or tight spot; stay out of them. [6:06]
- Look at other lanes for snipes. [6:36]
- Abrams heals by sucking your life with his 1; if you stand in it you give him HP, so do not stand next to him, he is a close range hero. [11:46]
- Kills with Assassinate give stacks. [13:20]
- On Vindicta Abrams is not much of a problem; just fly above him. [13:53]
- Sniping people is worth more than just farming. [14:24]
- Vindicta melts walkers. [15:27]
- Clear the jungle; if enemies walk into your jungle, they die. [18:42]
- He calls Paige probably the best support in the game right now. [19:46]
- When enemies dive him he needs Unstoppable. [20:17]
- Never let a Shiv get fed; his bloodletting and knives are ridiculous early. [20:51]
- Escalating Exposure lets him split push. [22:54]
- Blood Tribute and Vampiric Burst are later buys. [23:25]
- He may need Metal Skin when enemies dive him. [23:56]
