# How and when to FREEZE the Lane - Advanced Laning Tips
channel: Deathy  date: 2024-11-01  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErL1ZOb58eM

## Covers
Freezing the wave (2024 map): what it is, last hitting creeps only when they flash white, neutral/Amber/Sapphire lane states, freezing in a winning matchup (Mirage vs Infernus) and a losing one (Pocket vs Mirage), wave prio and what you give up, pressure under tower, cover and barrels, jungling and unsecured souls, bag knockdowns, when to push off a freeze, Djinn's Mark, Fire Scarabs, Tornado, Mystic Shot, Monster Rounds, Headshot Booster, Ammo Scavenger, Extra Regen, Infuser, Lash, Paradox

## Claims
- Freezing the wave means you do not attack the wave, keeping it in place; you only hit creeps when they are about to die, when they flash white just before dying, to get the last hit. [0:00]
- When freezing you do not hit creeps any further than the last hit unless they are already in your tower; it is maintaining the balance of the wave so it stays where it is. [0:30]
- Mirage into Infernus is a very advantageous matchup for Mirage; he used freezing to secure and dominate his lead and was up almost 4,000 souls at 9 minutes. [1:00]
- Pocket into Mirage is a very disadvantageous matchup; he used freezing there to avoid falling too far behind and still be able to play the game. [1:00]
- The Mirage lane is pretty much unplayable for Infernus if he pushes. [1:30]
- On wave one Mirage does not have much pressure, and Infernus playing it passive and only going for last hits is 100% correct; you cannot out-pressure Mirage, so you go for the last hits. [2:00]
- Infernus played the first waves right and lost the lane to one mistake: hard pushing the wave, which is where Deathy started freezing. [2:00]
- With the wave frozen near his own tower, Infernus cannot apply any pressure to him under tower and cannot get denies. [2:30]
- With the wave frozen, the enemy has to peek to get the creeps, which let Mirage put Fire Scarabs stacks on him. [2:30]
- Infernus, very low and having spent his money with only Extra Regen, could not show up to the lane. [3:00]
- Going box farming when the lane is unplayable is not a terrible play, since he cannot walk up to the lane and can come back the moment the wave is pushed. [3:00]
- At that point Infernus was only 300 souls behind and still doing fine. [3:30]
- Walking up the stairs to reach the frozen wave was a really big mistake that left Infernus at 100 health; he got some healing back from Infuser. [4:00]
- Against Mirage you have to not push the wave and keep it near your tower, because otherwise the lane is unplayable for almost every hero; as Pocket he kept the wave at his own tower the whole time. [4:00]
- Infernus going to jungle was kind of the correct play but it sucks: jungle is usually a get-further-ahead tool, but here he farms it just to compete, while Deathy gets the lane and can farm the jungle later. [4:30]
- Infernus can try to gank instead, but if he does not get a kill he is griefing his team, so he must be pretty confident of the kill. [5:00]
- He pushed off the freeze once he felt strong enough with Mystic Shot to pressure Infernus under tower, which he could not have done earlier. [5:00]
- Knocking Infernus down made him lose his bag, and at that point the lane was completely over. [5:31]
- There are three lane states: pushing Amber, pushing Sapphire, and neutral. [6:02]
- In a neutral lane with an extremely favourable matchup, if he gets the lane to sit near his side he can keep it there forever, and if the enemy walks up he takes a ton of damage or dies. [6:02]
- Infernus's mistake in the neutral lane was shooting the creeps, a habit most players have of pushing the wave without thinking about the lane; Deathy did not shoot the creeps, only the hero. [7:03]
- Four enemy creeps pushing on you is usually bad because the enemy can shoot you under tower, but with the new tower and people playing around cover it is pretty hard to pressure people under tower without a significant lead, which is not the case at 1:50. [7:03]
- Passively landing shots on a pushing enemy builds Djinn's Mark stacks for a huge damage burst. [7:34]
- While freezing, he only shoots creeps that are under his tower; creeps at his stairs he does not shoot until they are about to die. [8:04]
- Because the enemy fully pushed the creeps into his tower he could kill those; if they had not been fully pushed in he would not have. [8:04]
- Frozen like this, the lane keeps meeting in the same spot, and the enemy has to play around cover to avoid stacks. [8:34]
- Freezing makes it so the enemy cannot apply any pressure on you and you are the only person who can apply pressure. [9:04]
- The downside of freezing is giving up wave prio: the freedom, when your wave is pushing, to do the statues, do the boxes, or go gank the nearby lane. [9:04]
- Infernus does not use wave prio well; all he can really do is farm boxes and he is not good at ganking; against a hero like Lash who can gank, freezing might not be as advisable. [9:34]
- You are giving up map control by freezing, which is fine if you are hard winning the lane. [9:34]
- The freeze rule: once the creeps are under your tower you shoot them, when they are not under your tower you do not. [10:05]
- He does not watch the minimap for wave timings after about 1,000 hours, but if you are new, check the minimap and watch troopers on the zipline to time the wave. [10:05]
- Asked whether a losing player should boost to a duo lane against Paradox, he says it might not be a terrible idea but he has not seen it play out much; the downside is giving up a free tower, and it can be matched. [10:36]
- Being only 100 souls down is not disastrous, so leaving the lane is not worth it yet. [11:06]
