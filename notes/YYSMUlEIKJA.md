## Covers
Grey Talon, aquatiq, commentary over one of his own games (lane into a Billy, ult stacking, team fights).
Mostly banter; a few notes on the build source, ult use and taking less chip damage.
Patch date: early June 2026.

## Claims
- He always runs ABL's Grey Talon build. [1:02]
- Lesson learned: try not to take damage, from players but also from minions; minion damage adds up if you just sit there and take it. [2:03]
- Aim to land at least one arrow in lane. [3:05]
- Before ulting, check whether the target will actually die; he holds the bird when unsure it will kill the Billy. [6:15]
- Bird (ult) stacks matter a lot more than money; dying before he can get stacks costs him more than the gold he gains. [12:35]
- The ult is hard to get value from; using it just to stun people is fine, but he wants kills for stacks. [13:37]
- With a Rem on the enemy team, low HP enemies keep getting saved, so finishing 1 HP targets is unreliable. [14:10]
- Ult a target the enemy team depends on: his bird on Rem won the fight because without Rem the enemy team could not do anything. [19:23]
- Fix the wave first after a won fight; fights are much easier when your team has the wave. [19:23]
- He avoids ulting the wave because he does not want to waste a stack on it. [20:26]
- Ulting into a fight at an objective is worth it even without a kill, because the stuns still help the team. [21:29]
- A bird stun cancelled an enemy teleport. [26:14]
- Graves players he has seen are not performing that well anymore. [24:37]
- The gun damage scaling on his guided owl was buffed a lot. [29:24]
