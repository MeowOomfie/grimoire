# Why You’re Losing Lane | ETERNUS Deadlock Early Game Guide
channel: Sensu  date: 2026-04-26  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eb4u-TRmdqw

## Covers
Left vs right vs blue lane advantages, secret shop access, pushing and medic trooper first, solo heal, guardian damage resist, first item before the second wave, spawn timers (tier one, two and three camps, boxes, golden statues, bridge buff, 10 min room above mid boss, Sinner's Sacrifice), box run styles, cover and right shoulder peeking, stamina and ammo saving, parrying the guardian, ultimates at 3.6k souls, counter items, Healbane, high vs low cooldown heroes (Seven, Graves), roaming heroes (Drifter, Calico, Vindicta, Grey Talon), 4.8k soul break points, practice map while queuing

## Claims
- If you are in the left lane you have an inherent advantage over your lane opponents and in the right lane you are disadvantaged; blue lane is mirrored so neither side has a notable advantage. [0:00]
- Left lane advantages: easier tier one camps right next to your guardian, a one-way veil to clear waves and poke safely when enemies push up to your guardian, the guardian blocking shots at you while you shop, poor enemy line of sight when you zip back, better access to the vent for a faster bridge buff, and quick vent access to the double Sinner's Sacrifice room on your side. [0:00]
- The left lane's downside is that the secret shop is on the enemy side and riskier to reach, so in the left lane it is important not to lose your guardian early. [0:31]
- A fast way to the secret shop is the vent: air dash left once you hit it (from the blue lane, air dash right). [1:01]
- In the right lane, losing your guardian still leaves safe, quick access to the secret shop. [1:01]
- From the right lane it is hard to poke enemies at their shop because their guardian is in the way, so focus their guardian; once it is down they must use the secret shop on your side. [1:01]
- Enemies will always try for their tier one camp, so surprise them by contesting it and timing the respawn. [1:01]
- If you hear them taking their double Sinner's Sacrifice, contest them, since they will usually be low and you can steal their unsecured souls. [1:31]
- If you are being pushed in, play on top of the building instead of at the shop, for a better angle to clear the wave and so you are harder to poke. [1:31]
- When you first get in lane, the main goal is to push the lane by killing troopers as fast as you can; troopers do a lot of damage early, and having more alive than your opponents helps you win fights. [1:31]
- Kill the medic trooper first; many say kill it last for a slightly larger heal since the heal scales with missing health, but the medic pack also heals all allied troopers over 8 seconds, keeping yours alive to shove the lane. [1:31]
- The medic heal is split across heroes, so if you are low and your lane partner is not, ask for a solo heal: your partner stands 35 m away from the medic pack while you pick it up for the full heal. [2:01]
- Hitting the enemy guardian early after pushing is pointless: guardians start with 75% damage resist, decaying to minus 50% by 12 minutes; poke the enemies or deny their souls instead. [2:32]
- Buy an item before the second wave drops so you can keep the lane shoved and take better fights. [2:32]
- There are no objectives in the first 2 minutes, so do not stress if you need to play safe. [2:32]
- At 2 minutes the tier one camps spawn, giving around 40 souls each with a 1:25 respawn; they take under 5 seconds to kill and take priority after the wave is pushed. [2:32]
- The fastest way to take the tier one camp is to stand in a set spot and heavy melee while weaving gun attacks on the remaining minion. [3:02]
- Boxes and golden statues also spawn at 2 minutes; boxes have a 60% chance to drop unsecured souls, statues a 50% chance to give a random permanent buff, and each respawns after 3 minutes. [3:02]
- There are two ways to box run from lane: one player takes the solo wave while the other does a massive box run, which nets the lane the most souls but almost never happens in uncoordinated solo queue; usually both split off for boxes once the wave is pushed. [3:02]
- If your lane partner is not doing boxes, combine the routes for even more souls, and stealing the enemy's boxes is always worth it if you are in position. [7:10]
- At 10 minutes the room above mid boss spawns many boxes and golden statues, a free 500 plus souls. [7:10]
- Around 4:30 the last wave before the first bridge buff drops; push it as fast as you can so you reach the bridge buff a bit before 5 minutes without losing resources. [7:10]
- At 5 minutes the bridge buff spawns; check whether other lanes are also contesting it and call for help if needed. [7:10]
- At 5:30 most boxes from the first box run respawn; re-clear them if your lane is pushed. [7:41]
- At 6 minutes tier two camps spawn, around 70 souls each with a 4:50 respawn; they are quite fast to clear and take priority after pushing the wave, using heavy melees and abilities. [7:41]
- Sinner's Sacrifice spawns at 8 minutes and every 5 minutes after; it takes about 7 seconds and gives around 350 souls plus four permanent buffs if you heavy melee the last hit. [7:41]
- Charge your heavy melee when three lights remain on the Sinner's Sacrifice to get the buffs every time. [8:11]
- One Sinner's Sacrifice deals about 320 damage to you, so take it from a safe position. [8:11]
- The four permanent buffs can be worth up to 500 souls of stats; prioritize Sinner's Sacrifice over jungle camps if it is safe, and stealing the enemy's should be a priority if they cannot contest. [8:11]
- To take the double Sinner's Sacrifice fast, heavy melee one once, switch to the other and finish it, then switch back to the first, with no delay between heavy melees, so you never wait to time the last one. [8:11]
- Tier three camps also spawn at 8 minutes, with a 5:35 respawn and about 180 souls each, but can take a long time to kill at 8 minutes depending on the area; keep them low priority until you kill them efficiently, usually at least 15 minutes in. [8:42]
- Tier three camps take 20% more damage from melee, so a heavy melee hitting all three at once plus weaved auto attacks kills them faster. [8:42]
- In lane take as little damage as possible by using cover and not exposing yourself. [9:12]
- The third person camera is over your right shoulder, so jiggle peek over the right-hand side to deal damage without taking it. [9:12]
- Conserving stamina decides whether a fight or escape goes your way; do not waste it mindlessly, and look for wall jumps and heavy melees to reposition, since they cost no stamina. [9:42]
- Conserve ammo to push lane and take better fights: shooting while sliding uses no ammo, so look for downward slopes to slide down while poking or shooting troopers, and after dashing, crouch slide and shoot (minding stamina). [9:42]
- To dive or kill the guardian fast, parry it to disable it for 2.75 seconds: dash in, parry, heavy melee, then dash slide out while headshotting it. [10:13]
- No mechanics help if the enemy has an inherent advantage; ultimates unlock at 3.6k souls, so never fight if they get theirs before yours. [10:13]
- Make a habit of checking enemy items that counter you. [10:13]
- Counter items affordable in the laning phase should be bought, especially if you are struggling in lane (his on-screen cheat sheet lists them per hero). [10:44]
- Against heroes whose innate abilities regenerate their own health, do not pop cooldowns or waste ammo on them; focus the other lane opponent, and if you face two of them buy Healbane, the cheapest anti-heal item. [10:44]
- Heroes split into high cooldown and low cooldown; high cooldown heroes rely on those abilities for big damage, so after Seven's Power Surge or Graves's jar ability runs out they have basically no damage for a while, a good time to play aggressively. [11:14]
- Low cooldown heroes deal damage consistently, so only fight them when your big cooldowns are up and you can trade. [11:14]
- On roaming heroes, keep watching other lanes for gank chances; they need little solo farm and can pick people off, turning a losing lane into a winning one. [11:14]
- Some roaming heroes, such as Drifter, Calico, Vindicta and Grey Talon, gain stats or souls on kills. [11:44]
- On a roaming hero, swap to the blue lane to gank the side lanes efficiently. [11:44]
- If you do not know what to build, copy the pros, for example from Deathy's website tier list and pro builds, pasting the build ID in game. [11:44]
- Aim for the 4.8k soul break points for a big bonus on that stat; most builds hit 4.8k in gun or spirit first, then 4.8k in vitality as early as possible. [12:14]
- While waiting in queue, the console command map dl_midtown loads a Deadlock map and keeps your queue, so you can warm up or practice mechanics. [12:14]
