# Are The New Deadlock Heroes OP? | Meta Report
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-09-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ipV--I1l7DI

## Covers
Deathy's S tier meta report after the six new heroes were added and their first nerfs, as of the patch state on 2025-09-03 (no patch date named in the video).
Explains why Holliday, Victor, Vindicta, Warden, Drifter, Yamato and Paradox are S tier and which build he recommends on each.
For players picking a strong hero and build right now; he keeps a weekly updated spreadsheet of builds and tiers.

## Claims
- Holliday is at the top of the tier list; her numbers are overtuned. [0:31]
- Holliday's gun has some bloom but one of the highest DPS values in the game, especially at max level, which makes gun Holliday viable. [1:02]
- Holliday's barrels still do a ton of damage, her lasso is one of the best abilities in the game, and her jump pad mobility is insane. [1:02]
- Holliday is strong in lane, mid game and late game and has no real weakness. [1:02]
- Counterspell can stop Holliday's lasso and debuff reduction shortens it, but since the item shop rework it is much harder to slot a lot of debuff reduction because you have fewer slots. [1:32]
- Holliday can go gun or spirit focused; some mix is generally good. Deathy recommends the Saiia Holliday gun build, which is mostly spirit focused with gun items toward late game: Rapid Recharge, Cold Front, the vest and so on. [1:32]
- On Holliday, stay active around the map, push waves and look for ganks; her gank potential is one of the strongest in the game. [2:02]
- Victor, the latest released hero, was not S tier for Deathy after his nerfs, but he moved him to S tier after playing him with different builds; arguably a little overpowered. [2:33]
- Victor's biggest weakness is that he gives no team utility and does little if you run from him. [2:33]
- That weakness matters less because the fights that decide the game (mid boss, Walker fights, Urn fights) force enemies into specific areas where Victor stands and deals AoE damage. [2:33]
- Victor is too strong less because of his two lives than his numbers: his spirit scaling on every ability is insane and his gun has the sixth highest max level DPS in the game. [3:04]
- Victor needs maybe another batch of nerfs, nothing crazy, but he should be tuned down significantly. [3:04]
- Recommended Victor build: the "you can't kill me" Lightbringer build from the coach of the team Buffroyers (formerly VP), built around getting spirit resistance. [3:34]
- Do not use Victor's third ability (Aura of Suffering) until you have significant spirit resistance; it is really bad early. Pretend you do not have it until you have Spirit Resilience, except occasionally to farm camps. [4:04]
- Victor's build comes online with Infuser and Berserker; Berserker is always at max stacks because you hurt yourself so much. [4:04]
- With Frenzy, Victor becomes nearly unkillable and very fast, with high gun and aura damage plus debuff removal on charges from his second ability. Early he is not that strong, so take your time, scale and farm. [4:34]
- Vindicta is as overpowered as ever; at lower levels she may be less strong, but her combination of damage and utility is why she is so strong. [4:34]
- Vindicta's strength comes from Stake and Crow; Flight and Assassinate are good but not crazy good. [5:05]
- Maxed Stake has a massive AoE you basically cannot miss and it tethers people for a long time. [5:05]
- Maxed Crow gives -14% bullet and spirit resist shred, which is extremely valuable. [5:05]
- Crow deals 2.1% of max health per second with no spirit investment, so you do not need to build damage on Vindicta and can focus on Echo Shard. [5:35]
- Recommended Vindicta build: "I just want to grill the support" by Loving Snake, which is kept updated. The only flexible part is Mystic Shot early; swap to an item like Suppressor if you want more utility. [5:35]
- The Vindicta build revolves around double Stake: with Echo Shard she is nearly unplayable against, since even with a debuff remover you get staked again by the second Stake, and its cooldown is not long. [6:05]
- Warden is S tier and unbelievably powerful right now, mostly because of his ultimate. [6:35]
- Warden's gun is strong but has been stronger before; his laning is really strong with good base stats, including 3 health regen and 57 base DPS. [6:35]
- In team fights Warden goes in with Majestic Leap and ults; with a maxed ult plus Duration Extender and perhaps Arcane Surge, the ult easily lasts 10 to 12 seconds without much investment. [7:07]
- During Warden's ult he is very hard to fight unless you have a lot of anti-heal; Healbane alone usually is not enough, you need multiple sources such as Spirit Burn and Decay. [7:07]
- Games are decided by mid game team fights around mid boss, where Warden is unbelievably powerful, which is why he is S tier; he has no weak stage of the game. [7:38]
- Warden's biggest weakness is reliance on his ultimate; with it on cooldown he is significantly weaker, especially with the meta builds. [7:38]
- Recommended Warden builds: Hoot's build (in the spreadsheet, a little outdated but still correct) or Deathy's Hybrid Warden, similar but with annotations and more flexible options toward gun or other items. [7:38]
- Drifter is S tier even after his immediate nerfs because his kit is really strong. [8:39]
- Drifter is pretty much the best 1v1 hero in the game: his passive Bloodscent, maxed, gives 26% amplified damage against isolated targets, like a Pocket hitting every Barrage without max Barrage, permanently. [8:39]
- Because of that damage amp, Drifter can be very flexible with items and abuse whatever is meta. [9:09]
- Drifter's ultimate blinds two to three people in team fights, which is very valuable in matchmaking where communication is weak. [9:09]
- Drifter's damage output and laning stage are great. [9:09]
- Drifter's Stalker's Mark teleports him to close the gap on isolated targets and deals 3% HP per second with one point; a second point gives 25% fire rate after you teleport onto someone. [9:39]
- Drifter could drop to A tier after the nerfs, but good Drifter players are still destroying, so he stays S tier for now. [9:39]
- Recommended Drifter build: Parzilian Gun Drifter. [10:09]
- Yamato has been S tier for a long time; she and Paradox could be argued A tier, because the game's balance is pretty good and S tier heroes are not far ahead of the rest, with a few exceptions. [10:09]
- One point in Yamato's Crimson Slash slows the enemy's fire rate by 50% for 4 seconds on an 11 second cooldown, so in lane she can walk up, Crimson Slash and trade while the enemy cannot fight back. That plus her right click make her laning one of the strongest in the game. [10:41]
- Yamato's ultimate is a debuff remover giving 40% bullet and spirit resist (60% maxed), and it also removes ultimates, for example Pocket's Affliction. [11:11]
- Power Slash and Flying Strike are both very strong; Yamato has no weak abilities. Her weaknesses are cooldown reliance and getting melted without her ultimate. [11:41]
- Fewer people play Yamato now, probably not because she is much weaker but because her model and animations feel outdated next to the polished new heroes. [11:41]
- Recommended Yamato build: Soo's Yamato V2. [12:11]
- Paradox is S tier: her laning is very strong due to Kinetic Carbine and Pulse Grenade, which let her progress the lane and kill people. [12:42]
- At her ultimate power spike Paradox has a way to force the issue in lane, which many heroes lack when a lane becomes a stalemate. [12:42]
- Paradox's kit has always had utility: Time Wall is always great, Kinetic Carbine stopping time ruins many abilities, Paradoxical Swap is very useful against squishy targets like Mina or Vindicta, and the grenade is a solid area control tool. [13:12]
- Paradox has been S or A tier for a long time; recent nerfs to other heroes put her back on the map. [13:43]
- You see less Paradox in pro play because Holliday is basically a better version of her, but when Holliday is unavailable Paradox is one of the best heroes in the game. [13:43]
- Paradox builds are generally gun focused with some spirit, such as Tankbuster for burst. Recommended: the new Saiia build, a mix of mobility and damage. [13:43]
- Paradox is about putting yourself in position to use Wall, grenade and Swap, so items like Warp Stone and Stamina Mastery help a lot. [14:13]
