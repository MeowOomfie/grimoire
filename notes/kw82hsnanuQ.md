# NEW Deadlock Hotfix Update SHIFTS THE META
channel: Natan
date: 2026-03-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw82hsnanuQ

## Covers
Breakdown of a March 2026 hotfix: base Guardian health and shrine super trooper changes, the global health buff (start health, health per boon, vitality investment), how it shifts the item meta toward resist, shred, healing and anti-heal, a hidden Transcendent Cooldown buff, and the Calico, Victor, Wraith, Shiv and Celeste changes.
For players adjusting builds and hero picks to the new patch.

## Claims
- Games should be a little easier to close out for the winning team: base Guardian health was reduced. [0:00]
- The super troopers you get by taking shrines are worth fewer souls, so the losing team gets fewer souls defending its base against them. [0:00]
- The health changes probably change the game more than any hero nerf: everyone gets an extra 40 health at the start of the game. [0:30]
- Every hero's health per boon went up by a flat 4 health and 8%. [0:30]
- Mina, the lowest health scaling, went from about 27 or 28 health per boon to 34, about a 20% increase. [0:30]
- Mo & Krill, the highest health scaling, went from around 63 to 72 health per boon, about a 15% increase. [0:30]
- Vitality investment bonuses went up by 12%; since tanky heroes buy more vitality, the percentage increase roughly evens out. [1:00]
- Everyone is quite a bit tankier, so resistance items got stronger, because percentage damage reduction benefits you more the more health you have. [1:00]
- Shred items got stronger too, because shred is more valuable the more resist someone has. [1:30]
- Heroes with built-in healing and healing items got stronger, since you don't get one-shot before you can use healing abilities or lifesteal. [1:30]
- Healing reduction items got stronger in turn, and Toxic Bullets, Decay and Crippling Headshot disproportionately so; expect them bought more often. [1:30]
- Crippling Headshot is both healing reduction and shred on both resists; Decay deals percent current health damage; Toxic Bullets deals percent max health damage, so all scale with the extra health. [2:00]
- Scourge (40% spirit resist plus a percent max health damage aura) got indirectly buffed in two ways. [2:00]
- Glass Cannon got slightly nerfed because you lose more health when you buy it. [2:00]
- Colossus got buffed in two ways: its 15% base health increase is worth more on tanky heroes like Mo & Krill or Abrams with high health per boon, and vitality investment got buffed 12%; all vitality items are a bit stronger. [2:31]
- Tankbuster is now much more important for spirit nukers like Lash, Pocket and Mina, since flat nukes are weaker into tankier targets and Tankbuster deals percent current health damage that bypasses spirit resist. [2:31]
- Spirit nukers may want to prioritize cooldown earlier, since fights last longer and getting another ability rotation out matters when you can't one-shot people. [3:02]
- Hidden change not in the patch notes: Transcendent Cooldown now also reduces the cooldowns of Spirit Rend, Escalating Exposure and Siphon Bullets, which is significant if your hero synergizes with them. [3:02]
- Spirit Rend, Escalating Exposure and Siphon Bullets are also generally better because longer fights make them easier to stack, and Siphon Bullets steals percent max health. [3:33]
- Calico got hit hard: directly nerfed and indirectly nerfed by the vitality change, since killing slower means she snowballs less from her extra souls. [3:33]
- Victor and Wraith were nerfed directly, but the vitality changes partly counteract it: Victor is tanky with lots of healing and benefits from resist, and Wraith does constant DPS that handles tanks and survives spirit burst more easily early and mid game. [3:33]
- Shiv is likely back: his alt fire knockback was buffed, which is especially good in higher ranks where people use it for movement. [4:04]
- Shiv's knife cooldown was nerfed a little, but his ult got a much longer range and faster travel time. [4:34]
- Shiv was also indirectly buffed hard by the vitality changes because his ult is a percent max health execute that ignores the extra health, and healing being stronger helps him; expect Shiv and Kelvin again in the weekly Night Shift tournament. [4:34]
- Kelvin was also buffed in the previous week's patch. [4:34]
- Celeste finally got real nerfs: less damage from her Light Eater on-hits and her ult cooldown is no longer refunded on misses; the coach thinks these were very much needed. [5:04]
