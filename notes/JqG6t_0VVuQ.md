# How to Support in MM | Coaching a Mystic Viscous
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-09-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JqG6t_0VVuQ

## Covers
Viscous, Midknight, coaching review of a Viscous player at about old Oracle rank playing a punch build with Mystic Shot and Express Shot.
Themes: farming and soul efficiency, supportive play in matchmaking, using the ricochet of Splatter and Puddle Punch, Puddle Punch for movement and saving teammates, and the 30/40/30 mental rule. Opens with his view that the egg meta is not real. Patch period of mid September 2026.

## Claims
Egg meta and general state of the game:
- The meta is stale: people are tired of Carbine Paradox, Celeste, gun Lash, and Warden with Mercurial Magnum. [1:01]
- The egg strategy is not good; there is a reason it mostly happens in NA. If the enemies are all spamming Golden Goose Egg, punish them early: take their sinners, take as many buffs as they do, take their Guardians at minute five. [2:33]
- The reason egg is bad is that if you play aggressively and take pressure, you can take more farm and buffs than the egg gives them. [3:33]
- The counter to egg is simply to play aggressively. [5:05]
- He hopes egg is not hot-nerfed, because NA players need to learn to beat it by playing aggressively, or they keep a fundamental weakness to being slow played. [5:36]

Player 1 (Viscous, about old Oracle):
- You left soul orbs on the ground last wave, which is why you are down in souls. Pick up every orb. [0:31]
- The bounce on Splatter matters a lot: getting the double bounce on people does twice as much lane damage. [1:31]
- Your Cube was good there; your teammate just needed to cancel his parry. [2:02]
- Route to break line of sight; you could have tucked around the sign and not been hit by the Victor. [3:33]
- Getting Express Shot is a huge spike for you, and it is when the map opens up. [4:04]
- Farming priority is waves, then camps, then boxes. Box farming is the third-lowest priority and is only fine when nothing else is on the map. [4:34]
- When aggressive, highly stealable camps are unsecured and you have lost a Guardian, expect the enemy to look into that jungle. Secure those camps first. [4:34]
- Farm before you go box farming, because it is more soul efficient. [5:05]
- Your ult almost died for no reason; get out instead of trying to do damage. [5:36]
- Once two enemies show up after you kill the Seven, you need to leave. [7:11]
- You are not using Puddle Punch for movement. Punch along a wall to boost yourself out; if you use the punch for movement efficiently, you are essentially uncatchable on this hero. [7:11]
- Taking the side Guardians before the Rift is pretty important. [9:14]
- When you have Goo Ball and abilities up, height is very valuable, so take the roof. [9:45]
- Good Goo Ball movement with the down dash. [9:45]
- Aim Splatter so it rebounds off one wall and then the next, hitting the target twice and putting the next rebound into their path. [10:16]
- Teleport over to the Rift; the Rift is important and your team should be playing for it. [10:16]
- Punch build is good until people parry. It becomes progressively worse as you climb, because punch can be parried. [10:48]
- He does not like maxing Puddle Punch first; you should max Splatter over punch. Maxed Splatter does huge damage and cannot be parried and can ricochet. [11:18]
- You need to hit neutral camps no matter what hero you are. You were tied for lowest farm in the lobby because you did little to secure farm besides a few waves. [12:22]
- Rescue Beam is really good in lower elo, where people run in, overextend and die for no reason. [12:53]
- Right clicking the wave would have done a lot of damage with Mystic Shot and Express Shot. [13:26]
- Your biggest issue is efficiency in securing souls, with a lot of time spent base rotating. [13:56]
- Think about where you punch: against Victor's pathing, punch him off the wall instead of punching the ground, which mispositions him far more. [13:56]
- Think about the bounce of both your 1 and 3, and play from walls more. [14:27]
- In his opinion Mirage is the best laner in the game. [14:27]
- You are playing too reactively. Do not base rotate across the map to a lane with all its waves at your base when a closer teammate can respond; push out mid where you are, get some pressure and take space. [15:04]
- Puddle Punch your teammates out of danger, along a wall or off a sign. You can punch a teammate in your Cube, which basically guarantees he is out. Teammates will think you are the goat. [16:04]
- Mirage is broken but not a good solo carry for ranking up quickly. [16:04]
- This is when you pivot to Rescue Beam. Stay and grab the soul fountain souls. [16:37]
- Lead people with Puddle Punch: punch where they are going, not where they are. [17:07]
- You fold over to that hero because you were too poor and did not have your green spike from inefficient farming. [17:37]
- When you are this far behind, fighting for the Rift is pretty much your only chance back into the game. [17:37]
- Use Puddle Punch inside fights while looking for targets. [18:07]
- The build feels inefficient: you build everything for punch except Rapid Recharge. [18:37]
- Your whole team needs to hard focus and try to one-shot the Venator in that fight; if he dies, the fight is good. [18:37]
- You accidentally cast your ult. If you use hold-to-cast and it is too tight, turn it down a little and give it breathing room. [19:07]
- With Rescue Beam, once the teammate reaches you, sometimes cancel the beam so you are not dragging them along and they can start moving. [20:17]
- The 30/40/30 rule: about 30% of games are instant wins, 30% are instant losses, and the rest you control. Games like this where your whole team is negative happen. [20:49]
- You should not have re-engaged there. [20:49]
- Your biggest individual mistake was failing to obtain souls and hit camps. [20:49]
- In solo queue you need to play greedy no matter what hero you play; it is the only really efficient way to climb. [21:50]
- Decay for anti-heal is fine, but against a Victor with his 2 maxed it is not very valuable; Healbane or similar is better. [21:50]
- He would buy a curse for that target, or Disarming Hex since he had no dispel item. [21:50]
- Too many of your team played inside the pit. [22:20]
- Use your ult to stop the Seven ult when he does not have Unstoppable active. [22:20]
- Your neutral souls should be around 20% of your total, and your enemy-kill souls should not be higher than your neutral souls. [23:51]
- You maxed Puddle Punch first, yet Splatter outdamaged Puddle Punch by a significant margin at every point in the game. [24:23]
- Even in an unwinnable game, self-analyze what you could have done better. [24:53]
- His recommended Viscous builds feel good, do a lot of damage, have carry potential and still offer the supportive value of the Cube. [24:53]
