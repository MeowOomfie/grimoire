# How to Split Push EFFECTIVELY in Deadlock
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-11-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pFBFI9Wpq-I

## Covers
A 2024 macro guide (the game has changed a lot since) on split pushing: what it is, which heroes are good at it, when to do it (when behind, when something else is happening on the map), and why side Walkers are the better target. Uses clips from the Flicked Esports tournament. For players learning mid-game macro.

## Claims
- Split pushing means splitting from the core of your team (three or four grouped) to push objectives: Guardians, Walkers, base Guardians, Shrines; you can do it at any stage of the game. [0:30]
- A hero is good at split pushing if it can do a lot of objective damage and if it can escape, since split pushing is inherently risky. [1:00]
- Wraith and McGinnis both melt objectives very quickly. [1:00]
- Wraith has an escape with her teleport; McGinnis is worse at split pushing because her only escape is her wall, which there are many ways past. [1:30]
- Viscous can split push because his ultimate ball lets him get away and makes him very hard to kill. [2:00]
- Ivy can fly away when she sees danger, Warden is very quick, Haze escapes with her invisibility and Pocket with his cloak. [2:00]
- In this patch split pushing is very effective, whereas a week or two earlier it was not, because of the meta. [2:30]
- Split pushing is usually not what you do when ahead (say 10 to 20k souls up), because splitting up gives the enemy kills; split a little but do not hard commit to objectives. [3:31]
- When behind, split pushing is much more appealing: you care less if you die and should be willing to die for objectives, since it is a way back into the game. [4:01]
- Do not split push when nothing is happening on the map (for example everyone pushing a Walker at 9 minutes); it is most effective when the enemy must make hard decisions. [4:01] [4:32]
- If a fight is happening elsewhere, the enemy must choose between that fight and dealing with the split pusher; either they lose the numbers advantage in the fight or they lose the objective. [4:32] [5:03]
- If your entire team is passive, split pushing is very difficult. [5:03]
- Example: a 0/4 Wraith with 10k at 14 minutes in an even game (73k to 76k) pushed her wave to the Walker, drawing the Infernus to respond, which let her team look for plays elsewhere. [5:34] [6:06]
- Put yourself in a position to split push, then go to the Walker the moment something starts happening on the map. [7:07]
- When two Walkers are pressured at once (Mirage on one, Wraith on another) the defenders cannot respond to both, and even a lost fight in the middle still trades for a Walker. [6:37] [7:07]
- Walkers were recently buffed and the middle Walkers are particularly tanky at 8,000 health, so a team that won picks there still could not kill one in a fourth fight. [7:37]
- Side Walkers are better split push targets: they give the same souls as middle Walkers, and you need only two Walkers for your first flex slot. [7:37] [8:08]
- Side Walkers are also further from where the action usually is, since the side lanes are further apart from the middle lanes. [8:08]
- A hero who is far behind and not very useful in fights is in a good spot to split push. [8:38]
- After getting picks, knowing at least three enemies are on the other side of the map, a team of four split into two groups of two and pushed a middle and a side Walker at the same time. [9:39] [10:10]
- Mirage could join that fight right after respawning by using his teleport. [10:10]
- Even though that team then lost the fight, they came out one flex slot ahead by taking two Walkers, a decent mid-game advantage. [10:42]
- Do not all pressure the same objective: if everyone goes to one Walker the defenders send everyone there, especially with the recent Walker buffs; splitting to two Walkers makes them choose. [11:12]
- If you are too far to reach a big fight in time, push forward and split push the Walker instead of running back. [11:43] [12:14]
- A split pusher can pull two enemies away from the map: the Wraith traded one for one with a Mirage who teleported on her and forced the Warden back, while her team pressured another Walker. [12:14] [12:45]
- The mid game has an ebb and flow: you split push, force people to one side, and your team pressures the other side as they react. [12:45]
- A split push that pulls resources only pays off if the team takes advantage of it elsewhere. [12:45]
- When a teammate dies or gets ganked, ask how to get the most value from the space that death created instead of blaming them. [13:47]
- Farm the jungle less when your team is being active. [13:47]
- If your team is pushing lanes, look for other lanes to push; if the enemy responds to your team pushing a Walker, push a Walker yourself to force another response. [14:17]
