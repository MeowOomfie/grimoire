# 14 Deadlock Timers You Need To Know
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-07-02
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YHc-NMmPjHg

## Covers
Every game timer the coach could list: Guardian and Walker resistance curves, box and camp spawns and respawns, Sentinels, Urn, Rift, Mid Boss, Rejuvenator duration, bridge buffs, trooper spawn rates and scaling, golden statue buff tiers.
A reference sheet for any player who wants to plan objectives and farm around the clock.

## Claims
- Guardians spawn at 0:00 and their damage resistance depends on game time: 50% at 0:00, 25% at 3:00, 0% at 6:00, -25% (they take 25% more) at 9:00, -50% at 12:00. [0:31]
- The Guardian resistance scales gradually every second between those breakpoints; it is not on or off, so at 2:59 it is already below 50%. [1:02]
- He knows of no way to bypass the Guardian time-based damage resistance. [0:31]
- At 6:00 a gun doing 100 DPS does 100 DPS to the Guardian, and at 9:00 it does 125 DPS; 6:00 is when the game expects you to start killing Guardians. [1:02]
- Guardians can be killed earlier than 6:00 but it is significantly harder; past 12:00 they are trivial to kill. [1:32]
- Walkers follow a steeper curve: 65% resistance at 0:00 down to -65% at 18:00, with 0% at around 9:00. [1:32]
- From about 9:00 taking a Walker becomes realistic, past 12:00 it is very easy with a strong wave and enough damage, and by 18:00 the game wants you to take Walkers. [2:02]
- Walkers have 35% bullet resistance and no spirit resistance. [2:02]
- Gun heroes are still usually better at taking Walkers because they do more sustained damage, but sustained spirit damage heroes are also very good at it, for example Seven or Sinclair with his bolts. [2:02]
- Boxes around the map, including the golden satchels, spawn at 3:00 and respawn every 3 minutes. [2:32]
- Exception: the mid boxes spawn at 10:00, then respawn every 3 minutes. [2:32]
- Mid boxes give more souls on average than a Sinner's Sacrifice, so do them every 3 minutes when you are in the middle of the map with nothing else to do. [3:03]
- Tier one camps spawn at 2:00 and respawn every 1 minute 25 seconds; killed right at 2:00 they return at 3:25, in practice closer to 3:30. [3:03]
- Box and camp respawn timers only start once the boxes or camp are fully cleared; if even one creature remains the timer does not start. [3:33]
- Tier two camps spawn at 5:00 and respawn every 4 minutes 50 seconds. [3:33]
- Tier three camps spawn at 8:00 and respawn every 5 minutes 35 seconds. [4:04]
- A camp that mixes creature tiers uses the timer of its highest tier: a camp with tier two creatures plus one tier three spawns at 8:00 and respawns every 5 minutes 35 seconds. [4:04]
- Sentinels spawn at 8:00 and respawn every 5 minutes, only after the whole camp is cleared. [4:34]
- At the double Sentinels you must kill both Sentinels and the small neutral with them for the respawn timer to start. [4:34]
- If you clear your own Sentinels, kill that neutral too; if you clear the enemy's Sentinels, leave the neutral alive so their respawn timer does not start. [4:34]
- The Urn first spawns at 10:00 and then every 5 minutes (10, 15, 20, 25...), whether or not the previous Urn was delivered. [5:04]
- If someone is still holding the Urn when the next one is due, that spawn is skipped (for example no 15:00 Urn). [5:04]
- The first Urn always spawns on yellow and the second on green, then it alternates; easy rule: even ten-minute marks (10, 20, 30) are yellow, odd (15, 25) are green. [5:35]
- If an Urn spawn was skipped because someone held the Urn, the even/odd rule no longer works. [6:07]
- The Rift spawns at 12:00 plus or minus 1 minute, so between 11:00 and 13:00, and then respawns every 7 minutes. [6:07]
- He believes the Rift respawn has no variation (exactly 7 minutes after clearing) but has not confirmed it. [6:37]
- The first Rift's side is, he is fairly sure, random; he thinks later Rifts alternate sides but is not certain. [7:07]
- The Mid Boss spawns at 0:00 now (it did not used to). [7:07]
- Mid Boss respawn depends on how many have been killed: 7 minutes after the first kill, 6 minutes after the second, 5 minutes after the third and every later one, never faster than 5. [7:37]
- The Rejuvenator duration was recently cut to 3 minutes from 4. [7:37]
- Bridge buffs (power-ups) spawn at 5:00 and then every 5 minutes (5, 10, 15, 20) whether or not the previous buff was taken. [8:07]
- A bridge buff lasts 160 seconds (2 minutes 40 seconds), so taking every buff gives slightly more than 50% uptime. [8:07]
- The bridge buff may or may not last through the first Rift fight: a Rift at 11:30 keeps the 10:00 buff active, a Rift near 13:00 means it has run out. [8:37]
- Troopers spawn every 30 seconds from 0:00 to 20:00, every 25 seconds from 20:00 to 35:00, and every 20 seconds after 35:00. [9:07]
- Troopers gain spirit resistance over the game, scaling to 20% at 20:00 and 70% at 50:00. [9:37]
- At exactly 35:00 all troopers instantly get 50% extra HP and grow in size; this one is on or off, not scaling. [10:07]
- Golden statue buffs start at tier zero and upgrade at 10:00 and at 30:00; the HP buff is 15 HP at the start, 20 HP at 10:00 and 30 HP at 30:00. [10:07]
- That is why people pop the egg at 10:00 or 30:00; holding it to 10:00 is standard because popping it earlier is usually not worth it, holding to 30:00 is a bit of a troll. [10:38]
- Golden statue runs are worth more later in the game because the buffs are bigger. [11:09]
- He used the Deadlock Wiki and double-checked most of it; the wiki is almost always up to date. [11:09]
