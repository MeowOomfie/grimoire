# This is Why Your Leads Don't Win Games
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-04-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8dHrw9-0G3Y

## Covers
A VOD review of a Wraith who stomped lane and threw after 15 minutes: roles (support and roam vs carry), why carries should avoid low value fights, who is worth killing (Seven vs Dynamo), unsecured souls and bags, and how to recover after feeding a big bag to the enemy carry. For players who win lanes but lose games.

## Claims
- Many of the reviewed mistakes came from misunderstanding roles. Calico is a roam hero, Kelvin is support and also roams, Rem is in the support and roam category. [0:31]
- Support and roam heroes take roof boxes, sit on rooftops looking for ganks, and invade to steal farm: Rem stealing center sacrifices, Kelvin stealing the enemy's roof boxes, Calico on the roof or stealing tunnel boxes. [1:01]
- Support and roam heroes' job is not sitting in lane killing jungle camps; many of their kits struggle to kill camps. They can maybe reset the mid wave. [1:31]
- The carry sits in lane, farms the camps on its side and tries to get as strong as possible as fast as possible. [1:31]
- A roam hero's job is ganking, usually the lane with the enemy carry or an over pushed player, teaming up with the laner there to outnumber and shut the target out of farm. [1:31]
- As the carry, expect the enemy to hunt you to stop your farm for the next 60 seconds; play safe enough knowing a death costs two or three waves and the next three camps, and they may invade your camps. [2:02]
- Dying in the first 10 minutes matters less because Deadlock has many comeback mechanics, but a death past about 18 minutes can lose the game by giving the enemy the Mid Boss, two Walkers or a snowball. [2:33]
- As a carry in a side lane, farm and contest the enemy carry with help from a teammate; do not look for a solo fight with the enemy carry or your laner, since the risk of giving a solo kill is too high (Wraith is an exception because her ult can insta-kill). [3:03]
- First mistake: fighting too much. Fights give little farm, especially near teammates. [3:34]
- In the review, the carry left wave and camps and a tier three camp to join a team fight, killed a Dynamo for 500 souls and died; one tier three camp, or even one wave, would have been more souls. [3:34]
- Kill souls are split with nearby teammates who get assists, which can make a kill not worth it unless you do something off it. [4:04]
- A kill on a Seven has a lot of value; a kill on a Dynamo barely matters because his job is not farm, it is hitting his 4 (ultimate). [4:34]
- Dynamo can buy nothing but Refresher, "Greater Expansion" (as said) and Unstoppable and still win by landing his 4. [4:34]
- A Seven without farm is useless: he cannot buy Unstoppable, Escalating Exposure or most core items. Know who you are killing and why. [4:34]
- Kills that pad your stats do not matter if they are on the wrong target or pull you out of position or out of farm. [5:04]
- Second mistake: unsecured souls. Souls from jungle camps, boxes and Sinner's Sacrifice are unsecured and can drop when you die; you secure them by waiting, which takes forever, or by spending them. [5:04]
- The coach does not always rush to spend; he usually farms a little extra or plays extra safe, but against a Lash he spends right away so Lash cannot ult him out of his Walker. [5:34]
- Have a purpose for unsecured souls: either play passive and let waves come to you, or go back to spawn and buy a tier two or tier three item you normally would not. [5:34]
- Enemies will dive you under Walker, Guardian or even spawn for a big bag; a 2k bag will draw the whole enemy team, and your team will not punish it with a Walker, so you die for nothing. [6:05]
- With a bag, spend it or hide and farm a bit more for the item; almost never fight with a bag, and tell your team on mic that you need to spend first. [6:36]
- If you get dove while holding a big bag, you were probably out of position for the size of the bag. [7:07]
- The big mistake: the Wraith had been beating the lane Seven, then Wraith ulted the Seven past the bridge and took a 1k bag, which basically won the game; all he had to do was leave and spend. [7:07]
- Instead he farmed more camps, went to the enemy mid shop to spend, then went back to lane to fight; the Seven respawned, speed zipped back, killed him and took a 1.2k bag, and with a 50 second death timer the game was over despite being 3k to 4k ahead. [8:07]
- After giving up a big bag you cannot just farm lane, because the Seven will always out scale you; you need a big soul injection. [8:38]
- The fed Seven clears camps and waves as fast or faster and beats your team better, so equal farming leaves you behind; make a play. [9:08]
- Do not invade the fed carry's jungle, since he may be there; instead search for picks on the rest of the team, or split push for a solo Walker. [9:08]
- Mirror the fed carry: if the Seven is left lane, go right lane, play off your ult, get a pick and get out. [9:39]
- TLDR: do not let the enemy carry farm for free, contest them. If the enemy carry is overfed, either Curse him and ult jump him, or contest the rest of his team you out farm; killing two or three of his teammates gives you farm and leaves him in a 6v4. [9:39]
