## Covers
Calico coaching VOD review by Midknight of an Eternus Calico player (a streamer), laning with Bebop against Shiv.
Themes: holding lane space, Healing Rite timing, Sprint Boots versus Enduring Speed, anchoring mid, playing around item spikes, overfighting while behind, flashy movement, bag management after the bag-decay patch, Sinner's Sacrifice and making calls.
Reviewed around the start of January 2026, about two weeks after the patch that made bags take much longer to lose.

## Claims
Lane (Calico with Bebop vs Shiv):
- With a two minion advantage and nothing better to do, use Gloom Bombs but keep pushing up and harassing; only back off once you are down to one minion. Don't give space when you have pressure. [1:01]
- You were losing the 1v1 to the shop by saving souls for too long. [1:01]
- You committed too deep in that trade, and your dash went the wrong way; dash away from the Shiv, or a second knife would have rolled you. [1:32]
- When the only goal is clearing the wave as fast as possible, use your 1 to clean it up so you give no tower damage. [2:02]
- After a hard shove, run a box (breakables) route while you heal. [2:02]
- Don't waste time on flashy movement when you need to get to the wave fast. [2:02]
- Since you chose not to hit the wave, chase the Shiv and zone him completely off it: play around cover and keep him far away so he misses; if he walks up you can 100 to 0 him. [3:05]
- Shoving the wave does not make the Shiv miss more, so not hitting it was fine, but focus on confirms. [3:05]
- Gloom Bombs have very good spirit scaling, so extra spirit may be better than an early Cold Front. [4:05]
- Cold Front plus Torment Pulse for faster tier 2 camp clears is not needed when your team already has plenty of farmers (Ivy, Wraith and a Wanderer who eats a ton of farm). [5:12]
- When Bebop hooks the enemy, pop Healing Rite and hold the corner; the roughly 20% HP would have let you survive another bullet, which can be round saving. [6:43]
- Even in a fight at very low HP, popping Healing Rite for the couple of seconds it heals lets you tank one more bullet, and that bullet can decide whether you survive. [7:45]
- Making the teleport noise and tucking into the veil was a good idea, but she would have found you before you reached the veil if she chased. [7:14]
- When routing back, use your boost; jumping down and wall bouncing through the route is faster. [7:14]

Items:
- Keep Sprint Boots and don't upgrade to Enduring Speed on Calico; you box route a lot and the upgrade isn't worth 800 souls. [8:21]
- A big part of why Sprint Boots are viable early is the out of combat regen, which the upgrade doesn't improve; the slow resist does not matter enough to justify the souls. [8:51]
- Trophy Collector was never worth rushing in his opinion, and it 100% isn't now. [10:26]
- Not having your green spike is hindering you. [18:13]
- He doesn't think Spirit Rend is worth it on Calico, and doesn't think a gun spike is worth hitting on a spirit build, though he could be wrong. [20:58]
- He recommends Derma's Calico build as the most competent one right now. [21:31]

Mid and roaming:
- Your team abandoned mid. As Calico you don't really want to anchor, but you should anchor mid while Bebop roams, because Bebop has much better gank potential. [9:22]
- There is no context where roaming to pressure a tower is worth giving up a tower: their tower is unreliable, yours becomes guaranteed. [9:22]
- You can ask Ivy to take mid from you, but you can't rotate out until she actually takes it. [9:53]

Movement:
- You get caught up in flashy movement and run in circles; after coming back up you could simply have walked out. [10:56]
- Your movement is really good, but always track where the enemy is; the first part of the chase was great and the second part was overkill. [11:56]
- His pathing would have been jump off, press Ctrl twice for a down dash, and go toward the other side, so you are actually out of the fight. [16:39]
- Hiding in the veil after you were already out wastes about 30 seconds of farm; you could have box routed and left. [17:09]
- Again you ran in circles when you could have just zip lined out. [24:11]

Spikes and overfighting:
- Don't gank before you have Torment Pulse; hard farm for it, buy it, then look to gank. Don't fight while you are farming or saving for a spike. [11:26]
- Never gank the losing lane: you risk dying and making them even more fed. Gank the winning lane, where you have an advantage. [11:56]
- Your farm distribution shows overfighting: you and Bebop are the only ones who won lane, yet you are the lowest farm in the game. Spend more time farming and play selfishly. [12:58]
- Even after getting Torment Pulse, because you are so far behind you need your next spike before looking for a fight. [13:29]
- With the Torment Pulse build the idea is farming; you are Calico, so you should be box routing more. [13:29]
- They have stolen your farm, so their side of the map has a lot of farm; look at invading them or their boxes. [14:00]
- You are not going to beat someone 5k up on you; the fight may look close but he turns around, dashes and you both die. [14:30]
- The meta is more fight heavy, but calling it a deathball meta is misleading; you should still play a 1-1-1-3 style split and invade, not run five people at their jungle. [15:02]
- When behind, players tend to farm only defensive camps, but you have aggressive camps that are worth securing. [15:35]
- With how comeback souls work, invading is huge for getting back into a game; never be afraid to invade when you see the opportunity, especially when your team is farming your whole map. [15:35]
- Fighting on their strong side is bad. Trying to catch one at their shop is rarely right: best case you kill one and the others clean you up. [17:40]
- Don't fight a Mina only 2k up on you when you know nobody can back you up: you are down 2k (3k counting unspent souls), you have no green spike, and she has both her spikes. [19:20]
- Not having farmed a single Sinner's Sacrifice gives the enemy something like an invisible 500 soul buff over you. [19:50]
- That was a bad fight against a Shiv who is 24k to your 20k; you went back in when you should have left. [20:27]

Bags:
- You risked a 1k bag and put both your and Wraith's ult on cooldown when you should have walked out with the bag. [20:58]
- You are not respecting your bag. Joining a fight a teammate initiated is fine, but once you steal a big bag your only thought should be getting away as quickly as possible, since you are worth about three kills in souls. [22:34]
- Path carefully with a bag; a direct path lets one enemy turning around take another 800 bag. [24:43]
- Shop before the fight instead of going in on an 800 bag: upgrading to Arctic Blast would make you strong enough to look for that fight. [24:43]
- You dropped around 3,000 souls in bags this game. [25:13]

Urn and late game:
- Running the urn off a pick is good. [18:45]
- He doesn't like people trying to ninja cash the urn; in theory it should never be allowed. [18:45]
- Fighting to grab the comeback urn is fine even if you will probably lose the fight. [25:43]

Making calls and summary:
- Act on whatever you think, then analyze afterward whether it was good; you will make wrong calls often, that is how you learn, the same as force calling mid boss when new. Mute anyone who flames you. [26:14]
- When you make calls, review in the VOD what went wrong with them. [26:44]
- In lane, play around space: keep aggressive space up and don't give too much. [27:17]
- Don't be afraid to pop Healing Rite in a fight or while kiting at 1 HP if it lets you survive one more bullet. [27:17]
- Play around your item spikes: early that is Torment Pulse in this build, then your green spike or Spirit Snatch. Don't look for a fight while saving for something. [27:48]
- After winning lane, farm a bit, use that pressure to steal their jungle until you have Torment Pulse, buy it, then use the pressure to gank your winning lane. [28:19]
- Don't gank your losing lane; that is what set your team back. [28:19]
- In a Bebop and Calico lane, ask Ivy to hold mid so you both can rotate; if you don't call it, Bebop is the better roamer and you hold mid so they don't take it for free. [28:19]
- The biggest note is how much you were fighting: once behind you needed to farm or box route back into the game, not keep fighting. [28:50]
- In solo queue always play greedy and take as many Sinner's Sacrifices as possible, for both farm and pickups. [28:50]
- You need to play much more around your bag. [29:52]
