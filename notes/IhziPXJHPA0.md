## Covers
First-look breakdown of Rem, the support hero from the Old Gods New Blood update: Pillow Toss, Tag Along, Little Helpers and Nap Time with their upgrades, his gun, base HP and vent movement.
Also skill order, how to farm Sinner's Sacrifice and push lanes with helpers, and early item direction (support, duration, cooldown, range). For players picking up Rem.

## Claims
- Rem is a full support hero who uses the map's vents to weave in and out of fights, heals his team and attaches to allies to buff them. [0:00]
- Pillow Toss (1) has very long range; on hit it knocks enemies back, slows their movement speed and displaces them. [0:31]
- Landing Pillow Toss lowers your other cooldowns. [0:31]
- Pillow Toss upgrades: level 1 is 7 seconds cooldown reduction, level 2 applies a 40% fire rate slow (the highest fire rate slow of any ability, so gun heroes suffer most), level 3 adds 90 damage and more radius. [0:31]
- You probably will not max Pillow Toss first; take one point for the cooldown reduction, maybe two if you lane against gun heroes. [1:01]
- Tag Along (2) jumps onto an ally's back and naps alongside them, healing you and them. [1:31]
- During Tag Along you can jump between allies, even every ally in a 6v6 fight, healing all of them. [1:31]
- Tag Along makes Rem invulnerable while attached: enemies can only hit the ally he is on, even at level one. [1:31]
- Tag Along heals for 15% base health and roughly 28 per second at base (he was not sure of the second number). [2:02]
- Tag Along is one of the best escape tools in the game: jump on your tank so they must kill the tank first, then jump to the next ally; a good Rem should never die unless caught alone, so do not be alone. [2:33]
- Little Helpers (3) sends out helpers, one at base; you can send them to collect boxes, though there are better uses. [2:33]
- A helper sent to a Sinner's Sacrifice farms it automatically and perfectly while you go anywhere else on the map, and you still get the Sinner's Sacrifice buffs; the send range is very high even at base. [3:05]
- With one extra helper you can farm the double Sinner's Sacrifice at 8 minutes for free while doing anything else. [3:35]
- A helper can be sent to the shop to buy items for you when you are close but do not want to walk there; the ability's range works with range items. [3:35]
- A helper put on one of your lane troopers heals it to full and makes it do about three times as much damage; at base level one buffed trooper tanked the tower and several minions and got the tower to half. [4:06]
- Early tip: put the helper on your melee trooper to push lanes, since it has the most HP and damage of the four troopers and normally dies first on the front line. [4:37]
- You get the souls from the buffed trooper; unless players are around, the souls it collects are split across your whole team, so you can go lane to lane buffing troopers and push every lane yourself. [5:07]
- Little Helpers is probably the ability to level first, an early max, because of how much utility it has. [5:38]
- A helper put on an ally gives them movement speed and spirit resist; with all charges you can put helpers on four allies in a fight. [5:38]
- Greedy route with two helpers: farm the Sinner's Sacrifices, rotate mid and buff a trooper to push the wave, then do the same on the other side's Sinner's Sacrifices (he got eight buffs this way). [6:08]
- Helpers neither deal nor take damage from the Sinner's Sacrifice in the way you would, so you lose no health farming them; with helpers maxed you can walk around and take every Sinner's Sacrifice on the map. [6:38]
- Helpers can pick up a buff, so with their long range you can safely secure the bridge buffs at any point in the game when you are fairly close. [6:38]
- Nap Time (4) creates a field with a short channel; enemies hit are slowed, cannot dash out during the channel, then fall asleep, and take heavy spirit damage when they wake. [7:10]
- Nap Time upgrades: level 1 makes enemies wake with one less stamina and no stamina regen while asleep, level 2 makes Rem Unstoppable while casting so it goes off no matter what, level 3 reduces the cooldown. [7:40]
- Nap Time's base cooldown is very high at 172 seconds, and it has very large range. [7:40]
- Sleep lasts 4.25 seconds; think of Nap Time as a baby Black Hole: sleep the whole enemy team, then kill one of them while the rest cannot help. [8:17]
- Rem has 650 base HP, very solid for such a small hero. [8:47]
- His gun fires a very fast projectile with a little travel time that is almost instant, so he likely does not need High Velocity Mag. [8:47]
- His hitbox is quite forgiving and the gun has a solid ammo count at base; it feels like a lot of damage for a support, more base gun damage than Wraith in his test. [9:17]
- The gun has a headshot multiplier, so aim for the head: about 20 to 25 to the head and 16 to the body at base. [9:48]
- The gun has basically no falloff; at long range it still does almost max damage, very low falloff compared to other heroes. [10:19]
- Rem can crouch into vents around the map and gets a passive movement speed boost inside them, making rotations from vent to vent incredibly fast. [10:19]
- If harassed, run to a vent and slide in; no hero other than Calico can chase you once you are in. [10:49]
- Vent rotation is faster than using the jump pad in many cases, for example to reach Mid Boss or another lane. [11:50]
- Skill order: one point in Pillow Toss early for the cooldown reduction, which helps win lane; take the 40% fire rate point early only into an enemy gun hero. [11:50]
- One point in Tag Along in lane is reasonable for its large heal, though with only one teammate in lane you use it once and it has a long base cooldown. [12:21]
- Little Helpers will be the game winning tool; doubling the helpers is the highest value point, so take two points in it first in lane to buff two lane troopers or give a teammate spirit resist. [12:21]
- Little Helpers is the first ability to max; at max it gives up to 23 spirit resist and a total of four helpers. [12:52]
- Max the ult second, or max Pillow Toss and Tag Along second; he can see either working. [13:22]
- Pillow Toss has 1.6 spirit scaling, among the highest in the game; with 4.8k spirit investment, Surge of Power and Improved Spirit it goes from 75 to 203 damage at base level. [13:22]
- Pillow Toss is on a charge system with about a 6 second cooldown; Rapid Recharge can give three pillows in lane if you want to win lane with damage. [13:53]
- Expect Rem to be played as a support where helpers win the game, so anything that makes helpers stronger is great. [14:24]
- Spirit scaling is not very important on Rem, so expect many green (vitality) support items, as the default build suggests; invest in team healing and staying alive, though spirit items are not ruled out. [14:55]
- Playstyle: max helpers, rotate around the map, steal every Sinner's Sacrifice, push every wave by sending helpers to lane troopers, and when teammates need help send helpers or jump on their back to heal them. [15:25]
- Cooldown and duration items seem very strong; Superior Duration lengthens Nap Time, Tag Along and the Pillow Toss debuffs, and Superior Cooldown is also very strong. [15:56]
- Range (Expansion) still needs testing; at max range you can farm things from across the map while helping teammates, invading the enemy jungle and taking their Sinner's Sacrifices. [16:26]
