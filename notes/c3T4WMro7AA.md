## Claims
- The wave was pushing into him from minute zero, so he was losing from the start; be more aggressive early, and aggressive does not mean fighting the enemies. [1:45]
- Rat King is one of the worst heroes on the bridge: low velocity, a big hitbox and damage that is not crazy. [1:45]
- Instead of starting on the bridge, start down in lane, use the rats right away and heavy melee to try to get wave priority. [2:15]
- Rat King is an objective and area focused hero: very good at controlling areas, not good at skirmishing in random places. [2:15]
- Sewer boxes spawn at 5 minutes, a different time from other boxes. [2:45]
- Every box, sewer boxes included, respawns every 3 minutes. [2:45]
- You can win every game as Rat King without ever going into the sewers; sometimes it is optimal, but pick your battles and it is not his battle for now. [3:15]
- At 5 minutes always ask whether you are winning or losing lane and when you can fight these enemies; here the lane was even. [4:15]
- Default play is to push the wave, then read what is happening: if they defend their tower, go steal one of their camps; if they do not defend it, push their tower. [4:47]
- It is all about acting on the information you have. [4:47]
- When Haze shows up, press your 3; with two points in it it is almost 100% uptime, lasting 10 seconds on a 4 second cooldown, so spam it. [4:47]
- Very common at this rank: sitting in lane pushing the wave, neither getting a lead nor losing anything. [5:48]
- After pushing the wave, if you are not pushing their tower you should be taking a camp, ideally stealing theirs. [5:48]
- Rat King is really good at taking camps with just Scrap Grenade. [5:48]
- He ulted to protect his tower although the wave was already cleared, so the ult was not needed. [6:18]
- Using your ult defensively just to protect a tower is usually not great; you want kills. [6:18]
- You do not carry games by using your ult to protect areas without getting kills; use it to take objectives and get money. [6:18]
- Protecting your tower gives you no money, it only denies the enemy money; if three enemies take your tower, that is life, and when they rotate away use your ult to take their tower. [6:49]
- Stop jumping so much as Rat King (a habit he brought from Overwatch). [6:49]
- The number one carry mindset for the first 15 minutes: nothing matters except money, and kills matter only because they give money. [7:19]
- Having 6.4k souls at 10 minutes is poverty; do whatever gives you money, and kills can be one way. [7:49]
- Running around trying to fight people as Rat King is usually a waste of time; instead take an area, farm the centers, take a tower or play for the rift. [7:49]
- Those boxes all spawn at 5 minutes and the mid boxes spawn at 10 minutes. [8:19]
