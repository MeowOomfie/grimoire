# How to Build and Play Graves | Emissary Graves Coaching
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-01-31
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjnR5V2JKoM

## Covers
Graves guide plus coaching, Midknight reviewing an Emissary-rank Graves player's game (lane into Mina and Haze).
Covers the spirit build over gun, Echo Shard versus Rapid Recharge, Split Shot, AP order and leveling in fights, farming priority, placing the 2 so enemies cannot jump it, protecting Jar of the Dead skulls from light melee, and using the ult for side lane pressure, base defense and mid boss.
Ends with recommended builds: Lightbringer's spirit support Graves for advanced players, Wick Graves (with changes) below Ascendant, and his own copy. Patch date 2026-01-31.

## Claims
Build and items:
- Echo Shard is better than Rapid Recharge for this hero: you never use Rapid Recharge's bonus charges, and its only benefit is cutting the time between 1 charges from 13 to 6.5 seconds. [1:04]
- Echo Shard is on only a 30 second cooldown, so spam it just for 1 charges; in a fight it gives an instant second jar throw. [2:36]
- Echo Shard gets more value in fights under about 17 seconds; Rapid Recharge only outpaces it in long team fights. [3:06]
- With Echo Shard you can farm camps much faster because you throw many more jars on them. [4:08]
- Extra Regen is better than Restorative Shot on Graves: her gun range is only 18 m, and very few lanes let you walk that close for the heal without losing the net value (Haze, for example, just shoots you). [2:04]
- Rush spirit. Split Shot is a really strong lane item for Graves, but a gun spike does nothing for this hero. [4:08]
- Start Split Shot, then spirit spike, then Rapid Recharge and maybe another gun item, such as Active Reload. [5:10]
- Split Shot is good in lane partly because it gives far more farming. [11:14]
- Alchemical Fire is not bad but inefficient on her: she already has a lot of area denial and does not use the bullet resist much. He would never tell you to buy it. [5:41]
- Green items in lane are fine, but go spirit spike first; never gun spike first on Graves. Green spike first is forgivable since she needs health. [10:14]
- He hates Intensifying Magazine on her; her gun does nothing. Gun items should be utility like Split Shot or Rapid Recharge. [15:18]
- Her spirit is far better than her gun; do not build gun. [15:48]
- Duration is extremely strong on her: almost every ability gets much better with more duration. [16:19]
- The gun spike gives maybe 10 extra DPS even when stealing weapon damage, so you do not need it. [16:49]
- Active Reload is a good utility gun item if you want a tier 3, or Toxic Bullets, which feels very good once you have Split Shot. [16:49]
- You need a green spike; never getting one is rough. Enchanter's Emblem works if you forget what to buy, but it is inefficient. [19:22]
- Reactive Barrier is solid in that comp. Spirit Lifesteal is a good option because Leech feels very good on her. [19:53]
- Sharpshooter is not good; even on gun Graves you buy Point Blank and play close instead. [22:33]
- Transcendent Cooldown is not worth it: it only adds to Rapid Recharge and Split Shot. [29:38]
- Tankbuster is a poor fit; her 1 does not proc it. [32:41]
- Add tier 2 cooldown reduction rather than Tankbuster and Transcendent Cooldown. [31:40]
- Boundless Spirit is good on the hero. [32:41]
- Counterspell is something he buys more often than Healing Nova on her and likes a lot. [36:21]
- Rapid Recharge and Split Shot are core; choose either Active Reload or Toxic Bullets, not both. [37:52]
- Some games take Counterspell over Stamina Mastery. [38:27]

Ability order and leveling:
- Do not click to level abilities. Use the Ctrl plus ability key or Tab then the ability key (or Alt plus the key) so you can level and use it immediately in a fight. [1:34]
- Start the 1 in lane; it is better for fighting. [13:47]
- His general AP order: two points into the 3, then max the 1. Some players max the ult first. [12:15]
- Two points in the 3 is the best lane spike; the other lane spikes (the slow, the ult) are mediocre. [13:16]
- Do not max the 3: Split Shot does what the 3's max upgrade does. [12:46]
- Maxing the 3 is a major mistake; Split Shot gives you that for 1.6k souls, while the other maxes are huge. [21:32]
- Not having the ult maxed feels bad; even the tier 2 ult is very strong. [30:40]
- You were sitting on 5 unspent AP; watch your ability points. [11:44]
- Expected meta order: points in the 3, then max Jar of the Dead, then max the ult. Maxing the ult before the jar may be more meta at very high rank, but for general queue jar first is best. [38:58]
- His own build puts two points into the 3 early; he prefers a second point in the 3 over a second point in the 1 (its slow) for the lane spike, though he sees the argument since the slow is her only CC. [41:02]
- Firmly: max the 3 last and the 2 second to last. [41:33]

Jar of the Dead (1):
- Ideally use your abilities after you clear the wave; jars can help clear the wave faster, then throw them at enemies for harass. [0:32]
- Throwing jars as a random poke while enemies have nothing else to do lets them light melee the skulls and shatter them. [1:04]
- Use the 1 to farm camps. [3:38]
- Do not sit on max charges, but do not throw them so liberally that they do nothing. [14:17]
- Against Graves, light melee the skulls to clear them and she feels much weaker; if you do not, she is one of the strongest heroes in the game. [18:51]
- Advanced Graves players will throw their skulls then air dash parry into you, so when you go to light melee the skulls you get parried. Expect Rebuttal on her and parrying for your skulls to be a high skill ceiling play. [29:07]

The 2:
- Once enemies learn to double jump over the 2 in lane, it becomes more of a wave clear tool, mostly useful with an ally's CC so you can place it under them, or in tight spaces. [6:12]
- Place the 2 in a doorway or under a ceiling so enemies cannot double jump over it. [15:48]
- Around the veil, throw the 2 to block an entrance: they cannot leap over it and in theory cannot see it. [8:43]
- Stand on your 2 when enemies walk up to melee it instead of letting them clear it. [12:46]

Ult:
- The ult is better in enclosed spaces because of its area denial, and a ghoul spawning in a closed space just runs at the enemy. [14:17]
- When ganked, throw the ult behind tower; it helps clear the wave and stops the enemy taking your tower. [8:43]
- To hold space, block a veil entrance with the 2 and put the ult in there; very strong if teammates play around it. [8:43]
- The ult guarantees lane pressure: drop the gravestone behind you anywhere and the ghouls run up the whole lane. [23:33]
- Use the ult to push a side walker while your team pressures mid, so the enemy must send someone to answer. [23:33]
- Before resetting, toss the ult down; minions put pressure on the walker and someone from the mid fight has to rotate. [24:34]
- If you are splitting you need the ult: when chased, place it and run, or place it in a veil behind you and play around it to force them off. [31:10]
- Graves is great for mid boss; enemies who jump in the pit get destroyed by a maxed ult full of ghouls. [25:04]
- In base defense, throw the ult in your fountain, on high ground, or behind your base guardians. [33:14]
- If your Patron is knocked, toss the ult behind you, stand in the pit, keep the gravestone alive and spam jars at them. [35:49]
- You are underusing your ult; many high rank Graves players only spam it when jumped or use it in enclosed areas instead of using it for side lane pressure. [23:03]
- Dying with your ult up is a waste; place it in base, then play aggressively around it, kiting back to it or using your 3. [35:18]

Laning and positioning:
- Diving without a power spike after poking them down throws a winning lane. [1:34]
- If you are off a power spike you can dive. [2:04]
- Mina and Haze are your worst lane: Mina has no gun damage to steal and Haze's damage comes from her Fixation, not base gun damage. [7:12]
- Do not flank as Graves. [8:13]
- Do not try to defend a tower without your ult. [9:13]
- Do not go back in there; reset instead. [14:17]
- You are too aggressive farming waves; use Split Shot to clear and stop jumping in their face. [16:49]
- Play around your walker so you can fall back if they walk at you and keep pressure if they run; avoid open areas with nowhere to retreat. [17:20]
- Positioning killed you several times; always position with a route out. [38:58]

Farming and macro:
- Secure your more aggressive camps before your defensive ones, since the defensive camps are in no danger of being invaded. [13:16]
- Prioritize sinner camps when farming. [24:04]
- Take the aggressive doubles when up: more HP cost but much less time, you rotate sooner and get the buffs. [20:30]
- If you lost lane, do not push another lane: secure your own jungle so it cannot be invaded, and mirror enemy rotations rather than initiating one. Pick heroes like Paradox can roam for ganks. [14:48]
- A stalemate favors the losing team because of comeback souls. [18:51]
- Graves can side lane a bit, but should not be the weak side side laner unless actively splitting; a gun Viscous should be the one splitting. [20:30]
- Do not farm while the urn is being run; put pressure on the map, for example push a walker with your ult. [21:32]
- Catch the mid wave crashing into your walker instead of farming. [22:33]
- Rotating into a fight adds one player; pushing a side wave forces one enemy to respond, the same net gain. Tossing your ult on the side then rotating does both. [26:04]
- Mid boss rule of thumb: two picks on top of mid after 18 minutes, go mid; two picks anywhere after 22 to 23 minutes, go mid; one pick after 28 minutes, go mid. [27:36]
- With two teammates dead you cannot defend; let it go, and do not solo contest. [28:06]
- When enemies invade your strong side jungle, collapse on them; if it is your weak side, counter invade their jungle. [28:36]
- Against McGinnis turrets, spirit heroes should AoE clear them from distance or avoid them. [30:09]
- When the enemy has Rejuvenator they will always collapse on you, so do not push aggressively; grab the urn and hold it in base. [31:10]
- If they are knocking your Patron and your team responds in base, a fight there is good; coming out to take a staggered 3v6 loses the game. [34:14]
- Using attack pauses to get your team to agree to go mid can win games. [27:05]

Recommended builds:
- Advanced players: Lightbringer's spirit support Graves build, not his top gun build, which he admits is not good. [11:44]
- Lightbringer's spirit support build is very solid but has no left to right order; you buy the good items as you please. [36:21]
- Below Ascendant: Wick Graves, buying Split Shot in lane, skipping Ricochet, replacing Titanic Magazine with Rapid Recharge and Bullet Lifesteal with Spirit Lifesteal. [36:51]
- Do not use Wick Graves' AP order; it is very bad. [39:29]
- Midknight made his own copy of Wick Graves with a fixed AP order; the build id is in the video description. [40:01]
