# The OFFICIAL Deadlock Hero Tier List
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-12-06
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OT1WXp_Siic

## Covers
All-ranks hero tier list about three weeks into the big balance patch, as of 2025-12-06, after the follow-up adjustments (small jungle buff, Billy and other outliers adjusted).
A death ball, tank and hybrid meta: placement and reasoning for every hero the coach rates, from Abrams, Billy, Lady Geist and Shiv at the top to Drifter and Seven at the bottom. For players picking heroes in ranked.

## Claims
- The tier list is meant as an average across all ranks; some heroes are stronger or weaker at low rank. [0:31]
- The meta is still a death ball meta: stacking as four or five is really strong, jungle is still pretty weak, and split pushing Walkers got easier. [0:31]
- Infernus got his biggest nerf ever: hitting a bullet during Afterburn used to refresh the whole timer, now it only extends it by a percentage, so he must keep hitting bullets; his base gun damage was also nerfed significantly. [1:02]
- Against Infernus, his damage drops whenever he stops shooting to Flame Dash away, because the burn runs out. Placement: bottom of B tier, below average, the weakest he has been. [2:04]
- Abrams moves from S to S+: tanks are insane in the death ball, he kept his strength while others were nerfed, and he is the most solid front line apart from Billy; a top three, meta defining hero whose one slight weakness is an only okay lane. [2:34]
- Bebop: A tier, above average but not meta defining; still a very strong lane, tankiness is king, very good at brawling and in the death ball, and a better initiator now that Holliday and Pocket are weaker initiators. [3:35]
- Billy was nerfed (Blasted can no longer be used on Sinner's Sacrifice, and its scaling was nerfed) but is still a top three hero in the coach's view, only a tiny bit weaker. [4:06]
- Calico: average, middle of the pack; playable, okay in lane and okay at nuking. [4:36]
- The Doorman: S tier, meta defining, probably one of the strongest laners; Call Bell does more damage than any other early game nuke, he has the best utility in the game, and good players put you into a Walker in every situation. Not top three, but top five to ten. [4:36]
- S+ is reserved for the three best heroes in the game. [5:06]
- Drifter was gutted, his base bullet damage nerfed by over 10%; placement D tier, with Seven the only hero below him. [5:38]
- Against Drifter, keep one teammate with you and he becomes useless. [6:09]
- Drifter's one strength was laning from his high base regen, and with far less lane damage even that is weaker; he is bad in the death ball because he wants isolated targets and there are none in this meta. [6:41]
- Seven is the worst hero in the game: the only hero who can farm jungle efficiently in the mid game, but he does nothing else, relies entirely on his team, is a bottom three laner, and his ultimate does no damage. [7:11]
- Dynamo's Kinetic Pulse spirit scaling was nerfed slightly, which makes sense since he became scary in lane after the big patch; he loves the death ball and Singularity in big fights. Placement: S tier, the first time the coach has rated him that high. [8:12]
- Grey Talon: high B tier; good in lane and good damage but a glass cannon who needs his team in front of him. [9:13]
- Haze: high A tier; probably the most viable gun-only hero besides Warden, fills the open initiator role with Paradox and Holliday weaker, and is in the best laning shape she has ever been thanks to the bullet damage increase, though still squishy and not a hard carry. [9:44]
- Holliday: still above average despite many nerfs to her movement, escape, laning and mid game; Lasso and Bounce Pad are still crazy abilities and she is still a really good initiator. [10:47]
- Ivy: B tier; the coach's big mistake last list was expecting her to be insane, but she gains less than others from the new investment system, since early 4.8k spirit gives little when she plays either full gun carry or spirit support, and hybrid builds (the meta for everyone) do not work well on her. [11:17]
- Kelvin: C tier, below average support; his ultimate cooldown went up by 40 to 50 seconds, his gun shoots slower, his grenades are much weaker, and he is weaker in lane and mid game. The coach says the nerf is good after half a year of being insane. [11:48]
- Lady Geist (hot take): overpowered, a meta defining top three hero; most players do not get her full potential, but with good aim hybrid builds are crazy, she is one of the best laners again because other laners were nerfed, with insane farm and fight potential, and Soul Exchange (her health swap) will always be good. [12:48]
- Lash: still bad after his buffs; lower Flog cooldown and more Flog damage make his lane a bit better, but he is only viable in the left lane with high ground for slams, feels bad mid and right, Counterspell still makes him useless, and Death Slam is one of the worst ultimates now. Not anywhere above average. [13:18]
- McGinnis: S tier (a later aside while placing Wraith may move her to A); she benefited a lot from the changes, works well hybrid (gun first, then Mystic Slow or a few purple items so her turrets get scary), has a lot of healing, is very good at split pushing and strong at every rank; her only downside is a near useless ultimate. [14:20]
- Mina: low A tier; she dropped out of the meta, the nerfs hurt her especially in lane where she does less damage, but she is still slightly above average. [14:50]
- Mirage: A tier on average (S tier if rated for Eternus only); hybrid with Surge of Power, Mystic Burst and Extra Spirit lets one Djinn's Mark proc do almost 800 damage in lane; he underperforms at low rank because you must be very precise with positioning, and many find him boring. [15:20]
- Mo & Krill: roughly balanced; still awful to play against because a grab still means death, and pretty good in the death ball. [16:53]
- Paige: A tier; if played properly the best support in the game, especially for healing and giving armor; still strong in lane and can win lanes alone with the right combo, really good at stealing Mid Boss, and strong in team fights and the death ball with her ultimate. [16:53]
- Paradox is placed lower than Holliday: much weaker than before and weaker in lane than Holliday, who does similar things; she sits in B tier. [17:25]
- Pocket: S tier; the coach admits rating him D or C last time was his dumbest take, since despite the spirit nerfs he still presses his ultimate and kills you. [17:57]
- Shiv: top two hero, placed above Abrams (which almost made the coach move Lady Geist down to S); his rage build up is 8% slower but that does not matter; broken in lane and mid game, one-shots at any point and does not die, and is the best death baller because his dash hits many enemies at once. [18:28]
- The top heroes are all tanks that either do not die, one-shot you, or both. [18:28]
- Sinclair: B tier on average; S+ or S when the enemy has good ultimates to steal and C when they do not, so he is RNG until drafting arrives; with draft, picking him against strong ultimates like Warden's would make him S tier. [19:31]
- Victor: one of the worst heroes in the game, though not unplayable; do not stand in melee range and he does nothing; his gun scales poorly, he needs spirit and farms for ages, other hard carries do it better, anti-heal and shred punish him, and he needs a gun buff to be scary mid game. [20:01]
- Vindicta: S tier, top five, the best glass cannon (better than Grey Talon); run hybrid with some gun items, Assassinate and Rapid Recharge for 800 to 900 damage snipes; strong in lane, and hard to catch because the death ball tanks in front protect her if she positions carefully. [21:04]
- Viscous was passively nerfed because Express Shot was gutted, weakening the one-shot build (Express Shot, Glass Cannon, Tank Buster, then Splatter, Puddle Punch and right click at once); still really good in lane. Placement: high B, better than average if played well but low value at low rank. [21:35]
- Vyper: not A tier; S tier strength in lane, but the only carry route is to hard win lane then AFK farm, Metal Skin exists, and if she does not hard win lane she falls off hard. [22:37]
- Warden: still the best gun carry and the go-to hard gun carry who can also tank; rated S+ last list, nerfed since, still really good. [23:07]
- Wraith: rated high; she dominates low rank because low rank games run long and she is the best hard carry by about 50 minutes, but her laning was nerfed again and she is horrible in lane. [23:39]
- Yamato: A tier; her laning was nerfed, but she is still a solid tanky carry that nukes. [24:09]
- Early in the patch people thought gun was strong, but after a couple of weeks players learned to buy Metal Skin against gun heroes; it is a hybrid meta where everything is viable and spirit nukers are still thriving, though less broken than before. [24:40]
- Bigger HP pools across the board made tanks better because they excel in brawls and big fights: a squishy caught in a six-man fight dies instantly while Abrams can go 1v6 and live, creating space. The coach calls it a tank or goats meta. [25:11]
- Overall the patch is good and almost everything is viable; the gap between strong and weak heroes is smaller than before, and anything not in D can probably be played fine, with C and D questionable. [25:42]
- The average S tier hero is only a little stronger than the average B tier hero, so a B pick like Paradox is almost as strong as the S picks. [26:13]
