# The Deadlock Hero Tier List - Best Patch Ever?
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-08-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SKs6BH_1Ohw

## Covers
Stream tier list for ladder play (he later says European pubs) on the late-July 2025 patch, made about a week after it landed (video 2025-08-05). Calls it one of the best balanced metas ever, a burst meta of hyper carries and spirit nukes where Tank Buster, faster jungling and smaller skirmishes decide placements. For players picking heroes for pubs.

## Claims
- This is one of the better balanced metas ever: almost every hero is playable and most heroes are strong; there is no D tier hero and no truly bad hero this patch. [0:00] [37:16]
- Team composition matters a lot this meta: a comp with lots of spirit damage plus a spirit shred hero like Seven or Vindicta does insane damage and kills the enemy team very fast. [0:33]
- Seven and Vindicta are still the two best heroes to apply spirit shred; once shred is on the enemy, a Grey Talon shot kills them, which is why Grey Talon games feel random. [1:04]
- The tier list is based on ladder play, not on playing heroes to perfection; with perfect play Pocket would be the best hero in the game. [1:35] [18:54]
- B tier is the average rank. [4:07]
- The big winners this meta are heroes that are strong from the start or that farm very fast, because the jungle got better. [4:07]
- The meta is "kill everyone fast": you build to kill faster than the enemy, and the meta is either hyper carry or spirit burst. [32:00] [36:07]
- Grey Talon (first placed S, then moved to A): needs a team in front of him, but with one competent player in front he kills any single person in the game. [1:35] [27:21]
- Grey Talon: the Guided Owl cooldown buff gave him much more kill potential because he can get a kill from anywhere on the map; the long cooldown was what held the owl back. [2:06]
- Grey Talon also gained from walkers taking more damage from him, spirit resist nerfs (Spirit Resilience and other resists) and heroes being less tanky overall, so a one-shot hero now gets many more kills. [2:06]
- Grey Talon is very solid in lane and oppressive with a decent start, but he is not a solo carry who can 1v6. [2:36]
- Tank Buster, Grey Talon's main item, got buffed. He says Grey Talon needs nerfs. [3:06]
- Calico: high C tier, a bit below average. With Shiv nerfed she has more room as a tanky disruptor and front liner. [3:06] [3:37]
- Calico gets ahead by farming boxes, and boxes were nerfed again this patch, so box running on Calico is less valuable; she is mid in lane and not a fast farmer. [3:37] [4:07]
- Viscous: B tier, middle of the pack. A good ball Viscous can bully you and you can't do anything, and the best Viscous on the ladder can hard carry. [4:39] [5:09]
- Viscous: Tank Buster is really good on him, he is one of the few heroes whose light melee was not nerfed, and Puddle Punch got buffed, so he can still run melee builds. [4:39]
- Dynamo: low A tier, slightly above average, one of the better supports. Black Hole is weaker overall because there are fewer six-man fights until mid or late game, so he gets fewer ult kills. [5:40] [6:10]
- Dynamo: Tank Buster works really well with his stomp build. [5:40]
- Haze: A tier, not S. She benefited from this meta because she is insanely good at farming and at ganking for solo kills, and both got better: faster farming and more roaming finds enemies alone. [6:10] [6:40]
- Haze has been okay in lane for the last few patches and is now quite capable in lane; as a farmer she benefits from lots of money, which is easier to get this meta. [6:40] [7:10]
- Holliday: low S tier, "really OP again"; pure gun and barrel builds both work. [7:10] [8:11]
- Holliday has the best initiation in the game (Lasso is the best initiation pickoff tool) plus some of the best mobility and survivability through Bounce Pad; only heroes like Pocket come close to her escape. [7:40]
- Holliday is great in early fights and 2v2s; if she lands a Lasso it is almost always a guaranteed kill. Tank Buster works well on her. [7:40] [8:11]
- Infernus: low B tier at best. Since a few patches ago he is not a fast farmer until he gets Ricochet, he can lane but can't burst, and as a damage over time hero he does not benefit from the burst meta. [8:11] [8:41]
- Infernus has viable play styles; he planned to try Flame Dash builds. He later says he may have rated Infernus a bit too low. [8:41] [29:54]
- McGinnis: low A tier despite her health nerf. She is one of the best split pushers in the game, and since walkers die fast now you can split push and take a walker around 12 minutes. [9:11]
- McGinnis is probably still the tankiest of the damage-over-time heroes. [9:42]
- Mirage: B tier, average. He is still one of the best laners because of Djinn's Mark and Fire Scarabs, and his teleport lets him show up in any fight. [9:42] [10:15] [10:45]
- Mirage has the slowest jungle farming of any hero that becomes a carry (Haze and even Warden farm faster), and fast jungle clear is a big power spike this meta. [10:15]
- Mirage has the lowest gun DPS in the game, he thinks; winning lane got harder with the nerfs, and then he sits on side lane, can't clear jungle well and falls behind in money, so he is mid in pubs. [10:45] [34:35]
- Mo & Krill: S tier, maybe the best tank in the game. Resist nerfs (bullet resist removed or lowered, Berserker, Weighted Shots, Spirit Resilience nerfed) made inherently tanky heroes better, and he is the best of them. [11:16] [11:46]
- Mo & Krill does not die in Burrow, has insane lane sustain and doesn't need to build resistances. [11:46]
- Mo & Krill's ult is the best for ganks and early teamfight wins; it was always weaker in six-man fights (the enemy kills you before the execute) and better in 2v2s and 3v2s, and small skirmishes happen much more this patch. [12:16]
- Yamato: easily S tier. Play spirit Yamato; Tank Buster is insane on her. She gets 60% resist at max in her ultimate and is unkillable in it. [12:46] [13:16] [14:16]
- Yamato is the second best laner in the game after Sinclair, does not die in lane, benefits a lot from farming and gets better the longer the game goes; build Tank Buster, a few spirit items and a bit of resistance. [13:46]
- Pocket: S tier. Spirit burst is crazy this patch: even with Spellbreaker and Spirit Resilience bought against several spirit burst heroes, they still one-shot him. Tank Buster is core on Pocket. [14:16] [14:49]
- Pocket is the best hero to 1v1 with in the mid game, can 1v1 any hero and win, and is great at ganking and roaming; Affliction is less great in big team fights, but in a 2v2 you land a few Barrage stacks and everyone dies. [15:19]
- Seven: S tier, still the best hyper carry in the game and the best jungler; left alone in the jungle for 10 to 15 minutes he comes out ahead of your team in money and kills you. [15:50] [16:21]
- Seven got nerfed and is overall a tiny bit weaker; the best build now is the Power Surge build. His ult build got much worse, so low ranks, who always ran ult builds, will see less of him, and he has a higher skill ceiling now. [16:21] [16:52]
- At the end he suggests Seven and Yamato should maybe sit at the very top. [38:46]
- Shiv: C tier. He got nerfed incredibly hard and dies much faster with the Bloodletting nerf, but really good Shiv players can still make him work; he would be B tier if the average Shiv player played well. [16:52] [17:23] [18:24] [27:51]
- Shiv: the most common play style now is spirit Shiv, either a dash build or a knife build. With the new rage mechanic you start most fights without rage, so hit a few knives first, then engage and go for executes; you can't 1v6 tank everything anymore. [17:53]
- Shiv's melee build was hurt by the melee nerfs; he calls the Shiv nerf deserved. [18:24]
- Sinclair feels weak after many nerfs, though he is still insane in lane and probably the best laner in the game. [19:25]
- Sinclair: the bunny punch build is gone because the bunnied target runs very fast and escapes, so Rabbit Hex is a much weaker ability; the realistic build is a bolt build, which is still good. [19:25] [19:57]
- Sinclair is not tanky, so in a kill-fast meta Grey Talon, Pocket, Yamato or Seven can live through his damage and kill him first. He has faced Sinclair only about three times since the patch, so he may be wrong. [19:57] [20:29]
- Abrams: A tier, not S. He is okay in lane, but after lane he is the best tank and really scary if he did well in lane; if you need a front liner who never dies, Abrams is probably your guy if it is not Mo & Krill. [20:29] [21:04] [22:04]
- Abrams: punch build got weaker, but the rest of his kit got stronger; you no longer need a melee build and can run a spirit build with the shotgun and mostly spirit damage for the same result. [21:34] [22:04]
- Bebop: B tier, "kind of trash" or mid. In the tournament qualifiers he watched, the team with Bebop lost every time. He got nerfed hard but was so overloaded that he is still solid; he doesn't find him scary. [22:04] [22:35] [28:22]
- Ivy: middle of the pack; pretty good at clearing waves and at killing towers and walkers now. [30:55]
- Kelvin: A tier. By stats he is the second best hero in the game, but he feels weaker and no longer seems super strong. [31:26] [31:59]
- Lady Geist: C tier, "down for B" but never above average. She is good in longer engagements, which rarely happen; she is a bit better in lane again because the bomb is harder to dodge. [31:59] [32:30] [33:00]
- Wraith: low A tier, middle of the pack at best, nowhere near Seven's impact; she looks strong in NA only because players there don't end games. [33:30] [34:01]
- Lash: high A tier and feels really good, though on paper fewer big team fights should mean fewer big ults; he is worse than Pocket. [35:06] [35:37] [36:07]
- Lash with a debuffer like Vindicta or Seven can kill the whole enemy team with one Ground Strike. [35:37]
- Paradox is good but not as good as Holliday. [36:46]
- Vyper is very strong in ranked, almost S tier, "a pub stomper", the strongest she has felt since release; some heroes counter her hard, but without those heroes or counter items she runs the lobby. [37:16] [37:46] [38:16]
- Warden is still really good. [38:16]
- In NA the game drags on because players don't know when to push and end, which inflates heroes like Wraith; in Europe people know when to push. This is a list for European pubs. [29:23] [33:30]
