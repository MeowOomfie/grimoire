# The New Deadlock Update Is Better Than You Think
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-07-31
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9itR8G0BfKE

## Covers
Stream read-through of the late-July 2025 patch notes (video 2025-07-31), after about two games on the patch: split Mid Boss rejuvenators, melee nerfs, anti-deathball changes, jungle buffs, item changes and hero changes, with early winner and loser predictions. For players adjusting builds and macro to the patch.

## Claims
- Mid Boss now drops separate Rejuvenator buffs that can be stolen: up to three for the first Mid Boss, four for the second, and so on; each gives the old bonuses (max HP, fire rate, and he thinks spirit damage is new). [0:00]
- The Rejuvenator no longer buffs the whole team or your troopers, so there is no passive mega-creep push; you still have to manage lane control, and the team ahead can't run down one lane as six while other lanes push for free. [0:30] [1:02]
- Verdict on the Mid Boss change: great change and overall a Mid Boss nerf; the old all-or-nothing 50/50 contest had too much RNG, killing it still gives team souls, and a team that is behind has a good chance to steal at least one Rejuvenator. [1:02] [1:32] [2:32]
- Securing Mid Boss and the Rejuvenator is arguably worse for your team than before because it only affects three people and no creeps; giving it to your carry is strong, like a free Soul Rebirth with full HP. [2:02]
- The main issue is that the buff lasts too long; seven minutes is a long time, and cutting it to about 4 minutes would make Mid Boss less of a gamble. Values may need adjusting. [2:32] [3:02]
- Expect teams to plan Mid Boss: with three Rejuvenators, three people parry and three people punch. [2:32]
- You can no longer cancel someone mantling with a melee; good change because it favored the high-ground player too much. Meleeing a climber still slows them, so it still has value. [3:33] [4:04]
- Taking damage while climbing ropes now makes you climb slower, ending the up-down zipline cheese; really good change. [4:04] [4:37]
- Melee: you can no longer do 180 movements with melee, so melees are easier to parry. [4:37]
- Light melee damage reduced (he cites 20%) except for Viscous, Calico and Bebop, whose abilities do light melee damage; light melee was not necessarily the issue, and 20% may be too much, but overall good. [5:07]
- Heavy melee cooldown increased: perma heavy melee is slower and a miss is a big punishment, so fake-out play got nerfed a lot; really good. [5:07] [5:39]
- Parried stun duration is no longer affected by debuff resistance. [5:39]
- The parry bonus is no longer melee specific, so a hero bad at melee like Wraith can parry and then shoot for extra damage; good. [6:09]
- He would rather the melee changes be too much than stay in a melee meta. [5:39]
- Most controversial change: big or heavy heroes are now slower. It makes it harder for fat heroes like Bebop and McGinnis to chase; Kelvin (Ice Path), Shiv and Abrams have movement abilities to catch up. [6:40] [7:11]
- Verdict on the speed change: an interesting experiment and a nerf to tanks, who were too strong (the meta was tanks and Seven); not a big deal in his dash testing, and he wouldn't mind a revert. Warden is oddly not in the very tanky category. [7:11] [7:44] [8:15]
- Teleporters behind the walkers were removed: a great change that discourages deathballing, since you can no longer push a side lane as five or six and teleport back to a split-pushed lane instantly; teams must position smarter. [8:15] [8:46] [9:17]
- After 20 minutes trooper respawn time is reduced; he doesn't know how much it will matter. [9:17]
