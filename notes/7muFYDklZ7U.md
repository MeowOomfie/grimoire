# This Is Why Seven Is Still S Tier
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-04-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7muFYDklZ7U

## Covers
Seven on the ball (Lightning Ball) build, played and commented by aquatiq in an Oracle lobby.
Commentary over a game with item reasoning and a few matchup notes (Lash, Geist, Infernus, Mo and Krill, Bebop).
Patch around 2026-04-11.

## Claims
- He only plays the ball build on Seven; he finds the ult build boring even if it is overpowered. [9:19]
- The ball build is fun because the ball keeps growing; enemies who stand in it just get eaten. [20:31]
- Some Bebops uppercut their own creeps back toward their guardian to slow the wave down so it cannot be farmed. [2:03]
- Suppressor is fine to buy against Lash because Lash's gun is already slow. [3:03]
- A Geist should play far forward in lane to land her drain; a Geist playing back is weak. [3:33]
- Do not chase an enemy behind her guardian just because you have momentum. [4:37]
- He skipped boxes when going for them would have gotten him killed. [5:40]
- He bought anti-heal in lane. [6:10]
- Lash's ult is close to an auto win, and Seven's ult (Storm Cloud) is also a win button. [7:47]
- Leave the lane once your tower is dead and you would die there anyway. [8:47]
- Do not walk into a 2v1 when two enemies push one lane; fight the other lane instead. [9:52]
- Mo's only threat to him is the grab; the danger is Mo grabbing someone while Lash holds his ult to combo. [11:32]
- Seven scales well with money, so he goes into the camps. [12:33]
- Infernus kills him almost every time until he has Debuff Remover or Dispel Magic. [13:03]
- Items he bought into a Lash and Infernus focus: Toxic Bullets, and he considered Metal Skin and Spirit Resilience. [13:34]
- Check whether you are taking mostly gun or spirit damage before buying defense. [19:30]
- He says he should always get Leech on ball Seven; it lets him live forever in fights. [19:30]
- Buy Unstoppable even on the ball build so nothing interrupts your ult. [19:30]
- Mystic Expansion and Greater Expansion make the ball bigger. [23:48]
- Stay behind your guardians when defending base against a stronger team. [16:57]
- Geist's ult is not scary for mid boss. [19:00]
- Deaths do not matter that much in this game. [24:50]
