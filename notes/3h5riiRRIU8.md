# Mo & Krill CAN'T Die With This Build
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-08
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3h5riiRRIU8

## Covers
Mo & Krill on a tanky, ult-focused build, played right after a big patch.
aquatiq, commentary over one game (Mo & Krill and Vindicta lane against Dynamo and Apollo). No one is coached. Patch of early March 2026.

## Claims
- First item was Extra Health, because the lane against an aggressive Dynamo and Apollo duo needed the HP and he likes having HP on this hero. [2:08]
- Considered Monster Rounds early because he needed more damage on the troopers. [3:43]
- The build is a tanky Mo & Krill that builds around his ult and aims for assists more than kills. [4:45]
- Torment Pulse does a lot of damage on this build and is one of the biggest power spikes the hero can get; he saved up for it as the next big item once the lane went well. [4:45]
- Torment Pulse does a great job on troopers. [11:22]
- Apollo is hard to lane against because he has so much damage at the start. [6:15]
- Do not ult into a Dynamo you cannot kill: once your combo ends he can ult you right away. [6:45]
- Max the ult (4) on this build, even if the default build says not to. [8:18]
- He reached 2.2k HP, then went in and grabbed the Dynamo with the ult for a lane kill. [10:20]
- Golden Goose Egg gives souls and permanent buffs at a damage penalty, so it is really only good on heroes that do not deal gun damage, for example a spirit Ivy. [8:49]
- Dynamo cannot easily ult a Vindicta because she flies most of the time; a strong Dynamo could teleport onto her to do it. [9:19]
- Trophy Collector (3200 souls) gives passive soul generation, ability range and health; it is called a weak item, but on a hero with poor farm who gets a lot of assists it is worth it. [11:52]
- End result: Trophy Collector gave about 5,000 souls, more than enemy kills did, after buying it fairly early. [36:34]
- Buy Warp Stone before Trophy Collector so you can teleport to people when you ult. [14:27]
- As the laner, clear your own lane wave when you back rather than leaving it. [13:25]
- Mo & Krill is strong in every patch; Wraith is genuinely strong this patch. [14:58]
- Mo & Krill can now burrow into the new holes on the map, but a big Mo can get stuck in them. [20:41]
- Later he sold Monster Rounds; spirit resilience or any spirit armor is always good. [18:36]
- Scourge applies spirit resist and an aura that damages enemies, much like putting an Alchemical Flask on yourself; put it on yourself (or a front-line ally like Calico) before going into a fight. [22:12]
- Against an enemy team that has all gone spirit, take an early spirit defense item. [23:45]
- Ivy's thorns immobilize, and those tangles are very strong. [27:52]
- Against enemy knockdown your team wants a debuff remover. [28:52]
- Colossus can be used during the ult: pop Colossus and Scourge together, then ult someone. [31:24]
- Remember to use your active items during the ult. [31:55]
- His grab can get cancelled; he lost a combo when he got cancelled. [32:56]
- Juggernaut was useless in this game because only one enemy was buying gun items. [33:26]
- The downside of so much HP is spending a long time in spawn regenerating it. [33:26]
- Unstoppable would have been better than Colossus at the end, because the enemies cancelled his ult right away; with Unstoppable they would have won the fight. [35:33]
- Leave a fight when the team calls it; staying too long in a fight loses objectives elsewhere. [26:19]
