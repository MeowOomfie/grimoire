## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Paige-only free coaching stream right after Paige's release in August 2025 (she had been out about a day; Drifter and Victor were due later that week). It predates the 2026 changes, so builds, item names and numbers may be out of date.
He coaches three Paige players: an Archon/Emissary player laning with Billy into Mina, an Alchemist player laning with Doorman (addressed as Echo), and a Ritualist player laning with Viscous into Billy. Between VODs he reviews a viewer's Dynamo build.
Themes: barrier start over dragon start, lane partners with all-in, Paige's squishiness and backline positioning (fourth in line), ult value (three to four people), the 3 max, defensive item choice (Cultist, Warp Stone, Counterspell), Guardian Ward with the 2, Mystic Expansion but not Greater Expansion, Arcane Surge, and Deathy's build as the recommendation.
