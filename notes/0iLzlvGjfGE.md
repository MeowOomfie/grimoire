# The Only Beginner’s Guide You Need for Deadlock (Updated)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-10-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0iLzlvGjfGE

## Covers
Updated beginner guide: getting the game, picking heroes by background, using the build browser, item categories, game modes and ranked unlock, shooting, stamina, melee and parry, lane basics, jungle and Sinner's Sacrifice timing, risky farm spots, Urn, Rift, Walkers, Mid Boss, playing proactive, ziplines, and ending the game through shrines and the Patron.
For brand new and returning players.

## Claims
- Anyone can invite you through Steam; invites are also handed out daily in Discords, and the game is free to play. [0:30]
- Play what you enjoy; use bot matches to test heroes without penalty and learn at your own pace. [0:30]
- In standard you pick at least three heroes; right click a hero to raise its priority so you are more likely to get it, plus two backups. [1:00]
- Long run, learn at least three heroes, but at the start mostly stick to one hero. [1:30]
- From a shooter background, Haze is the easiest gun hero to learn (more aim, more damage); Infernus and Wraith are also gun-based picks. [1:30]
- From a MOBA caster background, try Graves (as captioned), who is auto aim and needs little aiming. [2:02]
- From a hero shooter tank background, Abrams is a straightforward tank, Reinhardt-like, with a passive so you do not juggle many actives. [2:02]
- There is no classic healer, but Dynamo and Paige are good support starts: they heal the team and turn fights while you play safe in the backline. [2:32]
- Press Tab to see a hero's abilities and what each level-up does. [3:04]
- Use the build browser: buy menu, build browser, public builds, pick one from a content creator, streamer, pro or someone you trust; avoid generated builds, which are often bad. [3:04]
- A selected build tells you item order and also shows suggested ability level-ups in game; just follow it. [3:34]
- In the quick buy menu enable auto queue selected build, and auto buy items when within range of the shop to buy automatically. [4:35]
- Three item categories: gun (raises left click auto attack damage, with investment bonuses as you spend in orange), vitality (health and resistances, investment raises your HP pool) and spirit. [4:35]
- Spirit power is a flat stat that rises as you buy spirit items (for example Improved Spirit gives +18 spirit) and scales ability damage via the multiplier on the spirit star. [5:37]
- Example: Seven's lightning ball goes from 134 to 148 damage after buying Improved Spirit. [6:07]
- Spellcasters want spirit items, tanks more vitality, gun carries gun items, though many heroes can be built as pure gun or pure spirit. [6:07]
- Making your own builds and counter buying comes later; at the start copy a build that works for high level players and focus on gameplay. [6:39]
- Souls are currency and also raise your level; each level is a boon and makes your abilities stronger over the game. [7:10]
- If the enemy has more souls they are stronger and you should play safer; with more souls fights are easier to win. [7:40]
- Standard has no ranking points and is the low stakes main mode; play it before ranked. [8:10]
- Ranked unlocks after 60 total wins in standard, and you can only pick heroes you have 15 wins on, so you need at least three heroes with 15 wins. [8:40]
- Street Brawl is a fast 4v4 one-lane mode with auto buy each round; good for warm-up but it will not teach how Deadlock is played, so play standard to improve fast. [9:10]
- Headshots do about 1.9 times damage, so go for heads when you can. [10:12]
- Build-up abilities proc faster on headshots, for example Infernus's Afterburn procs twice as fast on the head. [10:12]
- With shotgun heroes like Abrams, do not aim only for heads at medium range because of spread; with long range heroes like Vindicta go for headshots only, and her ultimate snipe gets bonus headshot damage. [10:42]
- Dash jump is a dash then a jump in a short window (the stamina bar blinks); you will miss it at first but it becomes natural after a few minutes or games of practice. [11:12]
- After a dash with enough speed, hold crouch to slide; shooting while sliding uses no ammo, and you can slide down any slope or stairs by holding crouch. [11:43]
- Movement is not the most important part of the game; the speaker is the highest rank with notoriously bad movement, and macro and decision making matter far more. [12:44]
- Dash costs 1 stamina, double jump 1, dash jump 2, and chained wall jumps after the first cost a little. [12:44]
- Manage stamina: if you spend it running around and then get into a fight, you cannot escape or chase. [13:16]
- Light melee is instant and does significant damage; heavy melee (hold) charges forward and in most cases does more damage than anyone's gun. [13:46]
- A successful parry stuns the attacker for 2 seconds and they take bonus damage from you. [14:16]
- Heavy melees are easy to parry by reacting to the animation; light melees are instant, so you must predict them. [14:46]
- Most heroes do not want to melee after lane; a few melee tanks do. [14:46]
- In low ranks everyone heavy melees and nobody parries; practice parry timing on the practice range bot and you can punish new players for free. [15:18]
- Laning stage is a 2v2 in an auto-assigned lane; the main goal is to kill the enemy Guardian, which protects the lane shop, shoots or melees divers and has a lot of HP. [15:49]
- Each wave starts as four creeps per side meeting in the middle. [16:19]
- A dying creep gives a ground orb you get automatically and a floating orb you must shoot after it lights up in a team color to get bonus souls; the enemy can steal it if you do not shoot it in time. [16:50]
- First focus in lane is killing enemy creeps, not shooting players: early creeps do a lot of damage and aggro whoever is closest. Stay behind your creeps. [17:20]
- Running in to shoot enemy players while their creeps shoot you is a major beginner mistake that gets you killed. [17:50]
- Secure your own souls first, deny enemy souls only after; the securing side has shooter's advantage and wins ties, so denying before securing is not worth it. [17:50]
- Once your creeps are at the enemy tower you can damage the tower or harass the enemy players; punish enemies who overstep out of cover. [18:53]
- Each lane wave has a medic creep (cross above its head) that drops a healing orb for anyone in range; it is your lane sustain. If you are full HP, kill it last so the heal is not wasted. [19:23]
- At 2 minutes boxes spawn (light melee or shoot for souls), and golden statues give permanent buffs for the whole game (movement speed, spirit power, resists, HP); pick them up. [19:53]
- Heavy locked boxes need a heavy melee but give double a normal box's souls, so they are worth taking. [19:53]
- Jungle camps: triangle is tier one (least souls), one line under is tier two, two lines is tier three; higher tiers are harder, and tier threes are scary and not killable early. [20:24]
- Sinner's Sacrifices (square with a circle) are the most valuable pickups on the map other than lane creeps. [20:54]
- Hit a Sinner's Sacrifice four times, then on the final hit time a heavy melee to the lights; on a normal one charge your heavy melee on the third to last light for full souls plus four golden statue buffs. [20:54]
- Speed sinners blink orange around the fourth hit, then run a faster light sequence; aim your heavy melee around the middle or the fourth light. They give no extra souls, just harder timing. [21:54]
- Jungle creeps have crit hitboxes that glow yellow and do double damage (a hand on some, the eyes on the sombrero creeps); red glow means body shots. [22:27]
- You can aggro creeps and parry their melees to stun them so they stop shooting; only worth it when they are stacked so you parry all three, since a big creep left out keeps shooting you. [22:57]
- The Chinatown tower is the only place with three Sinner's Sacrifices together plus gold statues and crates, so the souls are huge. [23:27]
- Doing anything on the tower plays a gong heard far across the map, Sinner's Sacrifices drain your HP, and you cannot jump off; the only escape is down through the hatch. [23:57]
- On the Archmother side, the purple pit stops you hearing anything outside, so enemies can gank you unheard, and drains your stamina with no regen, so you may be unable to wall jump out. [24:27]
- Both risky farm spots connect quickly to a secret shop (purple room exit to the forward shop, tower window to a secret shop); if ganked, get your buy in before dying so your unsecured souls are not stolen. [25:28]
- Some heroes want to roam and kill rather than farm; kills give a lot of souls, more the richer the enemy and the later the game, and respawn timers grow over the game. [25:58]
- Wiping the enemy team lets you push other objectives. [25:58]
- The Urn is a single person objective spawning every 5 minutes on the left or right (shown on the map before it spawns); melee to pick it up and deliver it on the other side. [26:29]
- While carrying the Urn you cannot use ziplines but can use abilities and shoot. [27:01]
- Delivering the Urn spawns soul orbs you get as they disappear (shoot them to secure if enemies are near), plus a soul boost and a few gold statue buffs. [27:01]
- The Rift is the team fight objective, spawning randomly after 11 minutes on one of two sides; king of the hill capture to 100% gives the whole team a lot of souls. [27:32]
- While both teams are in the Rift it sticks at 99% until one team leaves; after about a minute and a half it fades into a draw and its souls spawn in the middle to be shot. [28:03]
- Winning the Rift also spawns mega creeps, twice as strong and tanky, that help push that lane. [28:34]
- The team behind in souls gets more resistances inside the Rift circle, more the further behind and the later the game. [28:34]
- If you are behind, definitely take the Rift fight; if ahead, decide if you are strong enough, or let the enemy finish it and fight them elsewhere, outside the circle. [29:36]
- Standing near your Walker gives resistances; the Walker shoots and stuns enemies. Stay out of the stomp circle or you get stunned and take heavy damage; it also spews fire on the ground and fires a laser at a random player or creep. [30:06]
- Walkers are tankier than Guardians, and each Walker kill makes the remaining ones tankier; the last one is very tanky. [30:37]
- Mid Boss gives lots of souls and drops a Rejuvenator crystal; each heavy melee on it (up to three) gives an extra life, making a team fight basically 9v6. [31:07]
- After about 18 to 20 minutes, if you win a big team fight it is almost always correct to take Mid Boss. [31:37]
- Walking to Mid Boss with the whole enemy team up is usually bad: it screams at half HP for the whole map, takes time and damages you, so the enemy can contest and take the Rejuvenators. [32:07]
- Do Mid Boss after a big fight win with at least one or two enemies dead. [32:37]
- At the Rejuvenator, enemies can parry your heavy melee and stun you; some teammates should parry, some should melee, and AoE stuns that stop enemy melees are essential. [32:37]
- Play proactive instead of reactive; teams that sit around doing nothing lose. Looking for fights matters more than your KD. [33:38]
- The top bar shows both teams' souls; the higher number usually means winning, especially if your own number is higher and spent. [33:38]
- Comeback mechanics reward the team behind more for kills, so the further ahead you are the safer you should play and the more you keep your lead. [34:08]
- When behind, take more risks: take Rift fights and gank an enemy carry three on one to start fights up one. [34:39]
- When behind, trading one for one is usually good, and even losing four for three can be a net positive in net worth. [35:09]
- Do not worry about your KD: take fights you think you can win, and treat deaths as learning; playing proactive is the fastest way to improve. [35:40]
- Players who play safe and farm jungle for hundreds of hours never learn which fights to take. [35:40]
- Top Eternus players always play aggressive and stay in fights for value even when low; newer players leave to heal at half HP too often. [36:10]
- Each Walker kill unlocks another item (flex) slot for the team. [37:11]
- Dying for objectives is often good: a death for a Rift or a Mid Boss is worth it; do not value your life more than the game. [37:11]
- You win by getting ahead in souls, killing Walkers and pushing the enemy base. [37:42]
- Ziplines are faster than the ground; activate the zipline speed boost for much more speed. [37:42]
- In the first seconds on a zipline an enemy can shoot you off (hyper line), stunning you on the floor and likely killing you. [38:12]
- Escape trick: jump on the zipline, press Ctrl to get off right away, then dash for a big boost; you can also use the zipline to reach high ground. [38:43]
- If you are past your zipline in any lane you are probably too deep; your zipline is your lifeline, and an enemy with a zipline can chase you down the same way. [39:13]
- Zipline length follows where your creep wave is, which is why pushing waves and killing lane creeps is the most important thing in the game. [39:44]
- The enemy base has two base guardians (not super hard to kill once you siege) plus the Patron, many creeps, good enemy angles and their nearby spawn, so the deeper you go the harder fights get. [40:15]
- After the base guardians, kill the two shrines; you must be near a shrine to shoot it, and it charges up and sends out a wave that curses you. [40:46]
- Killing a shrine makes your creeps stronger in that side's lane and in the middle lane, so the first shrine is incredibly valuable; after both, your creeps almost permanently push the enemy side. [41:17]
- It is really hard to come back from losing shrines; with them you can play safe and steal the enemy jungle repeatedly while they are busy with creeps. [41:47]
- Patron phase one is not very dangerous. In phase two you must be in the center pit to shoot it, and it sends a big curse wave every few seconds that you do not want to be hit by. [42:17]
- With two or more enemies in the pit you cannot damage the Patron, and with one its damage taken is halved; kill the enemies in the pit first, avoid curses, then kill the Patron. [42:48]
