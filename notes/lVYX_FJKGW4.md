# The BEST Wraith Build In Deadlock - Hardcarry Build Update
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-12-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVYX_FJKGW4

## Covers
Metro's hard carry gun Wraith build after the Telekinesis and Full Auto nerfs: lane items, skill choices, Tesla Bullets and Quicksilver Reload farming, Fleetfoot over Slowing Bullets, Lucky Shot, and late game survival picks.
For Wraith players who want to carry pubs with the gun rather than play pickoffs. This is a late-2024 video (recorded on the 13 November patch, uploaded December 2024): the item shop, item stats and the map have changed a lot since. Metro's 8 December video (hiJQvuwXO3M) replaces this build after the next Wraith changes.

## Claims
- Metro thinks this is the weakest Wraith has ever been; many of her play styles no longer work, and Warden is "the better Wraith" right now. [0:00] [0:30]
- The build is for the 13 November patch and is listed in game under "Metro wraith"; he calls it his favorite and probably strongest way to carry on Wraith. [0:30] [1:01]
- Until the last patch he ran Shadow Weave with Burst Fire, but the nerfs changed that: Telekinesis now swings the target up and down while lifted, making headshots much harder and lowering gun damage. [1:01]
- Full Auto now needs to be maxed to get spirit scaling, so the pickoff Shadow Weave gun style does not work as well. [1:01] [1:32]
- Card Wraith is still really viable and some people prefer it, but he prefers gun Wraith, especially to carry in pubs. [1:32]
- Basic Magazine is the best 500 item for Wraith, along with Extra Charge; both are must picks. [1:32] [2:03]
- Monster Rounds is even more valuable this patch because Walkers got buffed; Wraith is still one of the best split pushers in the game with McGinnis, and Monster Rounds burns Guardians and Walkers fast and speeds up mid game farming a lot. [2:03]
- Buy Ammo Scavenger mostly in solo lanes; it was buffed to give +2 sprint speed at max stacks, nice for getting around after lane. [2:35]
- In duo lanes skip Ammo Scavenger or buy Extra Spirit instead, since you usually do not secure enough souls to make it worth it. [2:35]
- Then Infuser instead of the usual Mystic Burst: its cooldown dropped from 38 to 32 seconds, so it is no longer a waste to pop it for just one or two cards in lane plus some spirit lifesteal. [2:35] [3:06] [3:36]
- Mystic Burst still works if you prefer it; the difference for him is that Infuser gives 10% ammo, which he values over 6% weapon damage because this build skips Titanic Magazine, and Infuser stays until very late. [3:36] [4:06]
- Always start with at least two levels in Card Trick (one, then two), because without the extra 50 damage the cards do no damage. [4:36]
- Then skill by need: if the lane is scary and the enemy has a combo to catch you, put one point in Project Mind for range; otherwise put at least two points in Full Auto for more frequent use, faster mid game farm and 5 spirit damage per bullet. [4:36] [5:07]
- Put at least one, usually two, points in Telekinesis; the extra 0.5 second of lift can be the difference between a kill and an escape. [5:07] [5:38]
- Next choice: Full Auto level three now gives bullet lifesteal plus spirit scaling, adding a lot of single target damage. Level Full Auto first if you expect many 1v1s and small skirmishes; go max lift first for early big team fights or an early mid boss. [5:38] [6:10]
- Do not max Card Trick; cards are weak now and limited by a 0.65 second throw cooldown, so faster card charging does not help. [6:10] [6:40]
- Do not max Project Mind early anymore; people move around the map well now, so that is a late game luxury, and leveling Full Auto or Telekinesis first is worth more. [6:40] [7:10]
- Tesla Bullets is still the best item for Wraith: big farming speed, and its shock damage scales with whatever spirit power you have, adding a lot of team fight and duel damage. [7:10] [7:41]
- Then Quicksilver Reload imbued on Card Trick: great for farming and team fights; throw an early card in an engagement just for the extra fire rate. [7:41] [8:11]
- Engage option: lift, start Full Auto and shoot until the gun is almost empty, then throw a card to refresh the reload for sustained damage. [8:11]
- Wraith is still very good at quick jungle farming and fast split pushing; she burns towers down very fast. [8:41]
- Combat Barrier is an insanely solid item on gun Wraith: jungle creeps do not break its shield, so you keep the weapon damage and fire rate while farming. [8:41] [9:12]
- With a second point in Project Mind the teleport gives a bullet shield, so in a fight you can teleport away to regain the shield and reactivate Combat Barrier. [9:12] [9:43]
- Enchanter's Barrier is also a solid choice if you are more card focused; he prefers Combat Barrier for farming speed. [9:43]
- You should have at least one flex slot by now (about three Guardians down); if not, sell Ammo Scavenger, not a big loss. [9:43] [10:13]
- Fleetfoot is back in the build. Fights on Wraith are either chasing and shooting or kiting while they chase you. [10:13]
- He tested Slowing Bullets: shooting drops your speed from 9.1 to 6.4 m/s, a bigger slow than the 25% Slowing Bullets puts on the enemy, so a fleeing enemy still gains distance. [10:44] [11:16]
- Fleetfoot removes your own slow and, at a button press, gives more ammo or move speed, so it is better for chasing down kills and for kiting while you keep shooting. [11:16] [11:47]
- You can replace Fleetfoot with Kinetic Dash if you prefer it: a free dash plus more fire rate damage. [11:47]
- Then Escalating Resilience, building tanky while adding damage: 2% bullet resist per stack for 20 seconds, and Wraith shoots so fast it reaches the 40% max in about 10 to 15 bullets, about a second. [12:19] [12:50]
- Until Escalating Resilience you farm fast with decent mid game damage but are very squishy. [12:19]
- Then Lucky Shot or Spirit Armor; with the money he buys Lucky Shot, buffed to 110% bonus damage from 90%, plus more ammo. [12:50] [13:21]
- After Lucky Shot you have enough ammo that you can sell Basic Magazine if needed; Lucky Shot also gives more bullet shield, stacking with Combat Barrier and the Project Mind shield for about an 800 shield active nearly all the time. [13:21] [13:51]
- Then Spirit Armor to stack spirit resist on top of the bullet tankiness. [13:51]
- At this point sell Ammo Scavenger if still held, and sell Infuser too, since after Lucky Shot most damage comes from the gun; replace it with Duration Extender, which adds about 0.25 seconds to the ult and extends Full Auto. [13:51] [14:21]
- Then Siphon Bullets rather than Leech: Bullet Lifesteal and Leech do not stack efficiently (26 plus 32 gives about 50 overall). [14:21] [14:52]
- Every lifesteal item can be countered by Decay, Healbane or Toxic Bullets; anti-heal does not affect Siphon Bullets, which always steals 55 HP per stack every 0.8 seconds, and as a green item it adds base health, 20% bullet resist and weapon damage. [14:52] [15:23]
- If needed, upgrade to Improved Spirit Armor. [15:54]
- The build is flexible on flex slots: you can sell Basic Magazine and Extra Health, so it is strong even with one flex slot, though by now you should have two or three. [15:54]
- If you still need damage, buy Intensifying Magazine; buy it late because it gets stronger the longer you shoot and your ammo is low until Lucky Shot, so definitely after Lucky Shot. [15:54] [16:25]
- Intensifying Magazine adds more damage than Siphon Bullets, so buy it before Siphon Bullets if you need raw damage; otherwise Siphon Bullets, or skip it since you usually have enough damage. [16:55]
- For survival late: Vampiric Burst (80% lifesteal) is still great; Curse is the best item to shut down one specific enemy; Unstoppable if you are the hard carry being hard focused, for example against Mo & Krill's combo. [16:55] [17:26]
- Reactive Barrier is still very strong and the utility item he buys most, good against Bebop and Haze; with it a Haze Sleep Dart does not kill you. [17:26] [17:56]
- Many Haze players now roam early for Sleep Dart into ult, and early Wraith is often not tanky enough to survive without teleport up. [17:56]
- Ethereal Shift got buffed again with 20 spirit power after the shift and is back to a great survival option if you are being hard focused. [17:56]
- Slowing Hex is no longer core because you want to reach Lucky Shot and Spirit Armor or Siphon Bullets fast; this is a carry style, not a pickoff style that initiates with lift. [18:26]
- Telekinesis is very weak until maxed late, but rushing it leaves Full Auto doing less damage; it is slow to cast, easy to escape, and lifted targets shake around so they are hard to kill. [18:26] [18:56]
- If you want the early pickoff style, another hero is a better option. [18:56]
- Slowing Hex is a tool for early and mid game single pickoffs; he still buys it a lot against specific heroes and calls it almost a must against Infernus and Mirage. [19:27]
- Mirage can Tornado to make the ult disappear and turn it on you; a lifted Infernus can run through you the other way, avoid the lift, and burn you. [19:27]
- If you buy Slowing Hex you are probably better off on one of the spirit focused pickoff builds, like the "scumbag Wraith" build. [19:57]
- Glass Cannon is viable now: in the super late game it gives the bullet slow on enemies with full effect, a very slot-efficient item if you lack flex slots. [19:57] [20:27]
- Next video is a gun Warden build; Warden fits the 1v1 killing machine power fantasy well right now. [20:27] [20:58]
