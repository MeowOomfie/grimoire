# Lash is CRAZY! Gameplay analysis - How to win with Lash - Valve's Deadlock
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-08-29
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sO7WgNaPjeE

## Covers
A 2024 (August, early playtest era) commented replay of a high MMR Lash game Deathy went 10-0-30 in: skill order, lane trading with Flog, ganking and soul sharing, item choices (many 3,000 soul items over 6,300s, Silence Glyph), buffs, mid boss timing, Urn, staggered respawns and reading the post-game stats graphs.
For Lash players and anyone learning mid game map pressure. Item names, numbers and objectives are from the 2024 version of the game.

## Claims
- Deathy's in-game Lash build is published as "deathy brawling lash" in the public builds browser. [0:32]
- Leveling Flog first tends to be the play because it lets you take trades: Flog heals for 80% of the damage dealt, so hitting both heroes in a duo lane (about 65 x 2 = 130 damage) heals around 110 HP. [3:37]
- In lane, try to Flog both enemy heroes whenever you can; one is fine, but both is preferred. [4:08]
- Against Infernus (lots of harass) and Mo & Krill (lots of sustain) an early lane kill is unlikely, so focus on last hits. [4:08]
- Use Flog more often, ideally off cooldown, because it is sustain: if you are not full health you should be using it. [4:39]
- Lane items should help you in lane fights. Close Quarters gives 6% base weapon damage plus 25% within 15 m, which Lash usually is, and some bullet shield to take more trades. [4:39]
- Being too aggressive in lane is one of Deathy's biggest weaknesses; he took an unfavorable trade there. [5:10]
- When hurt, choose between buying Healing Rite and staying, or using speed boost to go back to base; going back lets you be greedier and buy items that help you kill instead of a defensive item. [5:40]
- Mystic Burst and Extra Charge are items Deathy likes to buy fairly early on Lash because they enable ganks and help the lane. [6:10]
- Use melee to secure creeps when you can afford to, because a melee last hit can't be denied and good players will deny you a lot. [6:42]
- Lash is very good at punishing people who step out of position; go on them right away. [6:42]
- If the wave is pushed to your tower, stay (buy healing) rather than go back, so you don't miss that whole wave; if it is not pushed, going back costs few creeps. You get souls as long as you are within range of them. [7:12]
- Healing Rite is never an item you want to buy, only one you sometimes have to buy; buy regen in lane only if you absolutely have to. [7:43]
- Shields are a good alternative to regen: Mystic Burst also gives 40 spirit shield health for a little extra sustain in lane. [8:14]
- As Lash in the laning stage, look for ganking opportunities; always keep an eye on the enemy lanes. [8:14]
- When an enemy (here Mo & Krill) rotates, counter-rotate to that lane; it got two kills and likely the tower. [8:44]
- After ganking you don't need to go straight back to your own lane. [8:44]
- Early soul sharing: a creep is worth about 80 souls; with two players in a lane each gets the full 80 (160 total); with three each gets roughly 40 (about 120 total). [9:14]
- So ganking the solo lane and staying to take creeps with that player is fine and generally what you want; staying in a duo lane after a gank effectively griefs their souls. [10:16]
- Guardians currently give AP when killed, so a 14-3 kill lead can be a much smaller lead than it looks if the enemy took objectives. [10:46]
- The game's stats say the laning stage ends at 9 minutes, but once towers start dying and people rotate, laning is over. [11:18]
- After laning, focus on getting your key items and look for opportunities on the map, for example enemies pushed up in a lane seen on the minimap. [11:18]
- You can punch Viscous's cube back; Deathy recommends it. [11:49]
- When you win your lane, roam the map applying pressure everywhere so the enemies can't breathe. [12:19]
- When on the enemy side with pressure, steal their jungle (it need not be alone; four players can farm every camp), but keep pushing lanes too and only take camps on the way. [12:50]
- Mystic Shot plus Cold Front give early burst: Mystic Shot about 84 damage on one shot and Cold Front a 120 damage burst, about 200 extra on top of Ground Strike and Flog. [12:50]
- With those items his full combo did about 700 to 800 damage, most of one enemy's health (Infernus about 1,400, Kelvin 1,100, Yamato 1,000). [13:21]
- Superior Stamina next, because it lets you move in a way that makes you very hard to kill; his escape there was mostly due to it. [13:21]
- When very strong, use zip speed to return to the map fast and stay as active as possible. [13:52]
- If you are ahead you must have impact: farm, but farm aggressively, shove the wave instantly and look for plays. [14:23]
- Misplay: pushing Lady Geist through the veil got him swapped; once Geist has used her swap she is much less scary. [14:23]
- You can often melee multiple creeps in one camp at once. [14:54]
- Lash is extremely mobile in the air, especially with Superior Stamina and later Rapid Recharge; watch your surroundings and don't tunnel vision. [15:24]
- Don't tunnel a target that takes a long time to kill (like Viscous in cube); look for easier targets and whether your backline needs help. [15:24]
- Don't rush the shop as soon as you have a lot of souls; first do things on the map that apply pressure, like pushing a wave, which gives souls, information and forces someone to answer. [15:54]
- With 6K souls unspent and an enemy showing on a nearby wave, back up but keep taking any farm on the way. [16:24]
- If you can't reach a fight on the map, go farm your jungle camp instead; in retrospect he could have taken the teleporter to join the big fight, a mistake since he was far ahead. [16:54]
- Lash doesn't benefit much from tier four (6,300 soul) items; he benefits from lots of 3,000 soul items like Rapid Recharge, Improved Burst and Superior Stamina. 6,300 items are not always better than 3,000 items on every hero. [17:54]
- With 8,900 souls he bought Rapid Recharge, Improved Burst, Silence Glyph and Improved Cooldown instead of a 6,300 item. [18:58]
- Buy Silence Glyph into heroes that rely on their abilities (here Viscous, Kelvin, Yamato, Lady Geist); it is not in his standard guide. [18:58]
- Baiting a wave: hide, let a lone enemy think it is safe to farm the wave, then go on them (here dealing damage then Silence Glyph on Yamato). It needs experience with your hero. [19:30]
- When a fight is not looking good, go push another lane so that even if teammates die you apply pressure elsewhere. [20:32]
- Always think about objectives; defend your walker and base guardians when they are pushed. [21:03]
- You get extra souls for killing someone richer, so as the richest player avoid risky plays (like diving to save a teammate) because your death is a comeback route for the enemy. [21:34]
- Despite a 31-17 kill lead his team fell 15 AP and 6K souls behind after bad mid game fights; kills are not the lead. [22:04]
- When the game is even, only take very advantageous fights: numbers or positioning advantage. [22:35]
- Point Blank (15% bullet resist) and Pristine Emblem (12% spirit resist) give a mix of damage and tankiness; Lash's left click damage is significant and often underestimated. [22:35]
- Don't hold your ultimate for multiple people; it is almost always better to use it on one person, get the guaranteed pick and move on. [23:05]
- Mid game objectives are the Urn, the mid boss and the buffs. [23:39]
- Buffs last about 2 minutes and give big stats (the Vitality buff gives around 400 max health plus health regen); if there is nothing else to do, take buffs, ideally as soon as they spawn. Higher MMR games take them faster. [24:10]
- Group around the Urn so the enemy can't just cap it. [24:40]
- If a fight doesn't look good, cut your losses and leave; don't try to salvage it. Go push lanes, objectives or farm jungle, even farming camps as you run away so the enemy can't take them. [25:10]
- Misplay: playing too close to Lady Geist got him swapped again; it should have been a kill. [26:12]
- Skill order in this game: Ground Strike maxed, Flog maxed, then ultimate maxed for the cooldown (60 seconds with his cooldown item), a greedy choice because he was far ahead. [26:12]
- In a Dynamo Singularity, once you have used both AOE abilities (Ground Strike and Flog), charge heavy melee instead of shooting; it usually does more damage, especially on two or three people. [26:44]
- Mid boss timing: after killing four enemies and being three up, they took a risky mid boss because the enemy Infernus could not be up in time to stun them with his ultimate. [27:14]
- Mid boss pushes all the lanes for you, turning an even game in your favor. [27:44]
- The gun buff gives extra ammo and extra fire rate and is very strong. [28:14]
- After mid boss the order is: first fix any lanes that need pushing, then pressure buildings and objectives. [28:14]
- With Rejuvenator the enemy must deal with their lanes, so turning in the Urn is safer; it gave him 1 AP and 800 souls personally. [28:46]
- Yamato kept dying to Silence Glyph because she skipped Debuff Remover for greedy items. [29:17]
- As by far the most farmed hero (12K net worth over anyone), rush to every team fight; the team wins most fights you are in, so just don't throw. [29:47]
- At 28 minutes walkers die extremely quickly, so go for the pick over the walker. [30:49]
- When pushing their base, always kill anyone poking out, because staggered respawns mean you keep fighting with a numbers advantage; killing them all at once lets them respawn together. [30:49]
- Strong 6,500 items on Lash right now: Diviner's Kevlar, Mystic Reverb, Unstoppable to get your ult off, and Soul Rebirth (a second life, good when far ahead). [31:20]
- On the weapon side Siphon Bullets is broken (likely nerfed soon); Spiritual Overflow is good for cooldown reduction and spirit lifesteal. [31:52]
- Far ahead, don't force a fight to end; first farm the 8 to 9 free jungle camps left on your side, since you already cleared the enemy jungle. [32:23]
- With Silence Glyph you can force out Yamato's ultimate; Ethereal Shift is a good item against Silence Glyph because you can shift and then use your ult afterward. [33:23]
- Don't be afraid to drop the Urn to take a fight; it resets, but that is not a big deal. [33:55]
- Don't use Ground Strike right after your ultimate: most people dash away when slammed, so wait to see their movement (dash, Warp Stone) and then use it. [33:55]
- Post-game: View graphs, Lane stats show souls, last hits, denies and player damage per lane at 9 minutes, showing which lane won or lost. [35:26]
- Damage breakdown shows damage to players vs troopers, telling you who pushed the most waves (he did 35,000 to players, 51,000 to troopers). [35:56]
- Damage dealt by type shows your main sources of damage; his were diverse: gun, Flog, Ground Strike, Improved Burst, gun crits. [36:28]
