# The 5 Levels Of Deadlock Players Explained
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-12-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9aFkofg3Lxc

## Covers
A tongue-in-cheek breakdown of five player levels by rank (Initiate, Seeker and Alchemist, Emissary to Oracle, Phantom and Ascendant, Eternus): how each plays, thinks and builds, and what holds each back.
December 2025, during the deathball meta. Useful as a self-check of habits.

## Claims
- Level 1, Initiate: maybe 50 games played, does not know what half the heroes do, and either one-tricks a single hero or swaps to a new one every match. [0:00] [0:31]
- Initiates are still discovering basics, such as sliding down stairs by holding crouch. [0:31]
- Center sacrifices give stat bonuses if you time them right, and those bonuses last the entire game; Metro calls them broken. [0:31]
- Initiates use default builds or a random Reddit build, have no idea what macro means, and their movement is dash and jump (dash jumping is hard for them). [0:31] [1:02]
- Level 2, Seeker or Alchemist: knows most abilities and dabbles in advanced movement: jump dashes, sliding up after dashes, the odd wall jump. [1:02] [1:33]
- Seekers discover Trophy Collector, which lets you die 10 times and still make money while dead in spawn. [1:33]
- Seekers kind of know how to farm and pretend to care about objectives, but will farm the tier two camp next to their own walker instead of clearing the wave. [1:33]
- Seekers use chat for strategy sometimes but mostly role-play their hero. [1:33]
- Seekers' builds come from a content creator or whatever got them a lucky win streak; they have found three main heroes or flex across 10, and they improve almost every match. [2:04]
- Level 3, Emissary, Archon and Oracle: hundreds of hours in, knows every hero, most matchups and which counter items to buy. [2:04]
- At level 3 you start to see Deadlock as a team game, or, if antisocial, as a 20-minute solo farm followed by one fight you either 1v6 or lose. [2:35]
- Level 3 movement is solid: sliding in fights to preserve ammo, landing jump dashes cleanly and consistently, occasional highlight escapes, and copying favorite creators' tech frame by frame. [2:35]
- Level 3 players either one-trick again or have a comfortable hero pool, and winning starts to matter. [2:35] [3:06]
- Level 4, Phantom and Ascendant: has played for a very long time, trusts the team a little, has a smaller ego and sharper decisions, or thinks Ascendant is basically Eternus and listens to no one. [3:06]
- Many current Ascendants were Oracle before the rank update. [3:06]
- By level 4 you should know Trophy Collector is back to being a pretty bad buy, even in the deathball meta, because it does not give enough value. [3:06] [3:37]
- Level 4 players mostly take favorable fights and understand macro: rotations, lane control and timing. [3:37]
- The level 4 trap: avoiding even or hard fights completely and playing too safe; that is what holds them back from Eternus. [3:37]
- Level 4 players still think their KD matters, which also holds them back, even though they are in the top two ranks. [3:37]
- Level 4 movement looks crisp, they know every escape route before the fight starts and survive fights on 10 HP. [3:37] [4:07]
- Level 4 builds are fully their own or adjusted from a favorite creator; winning is the only fun part and losses lead to raging at teammates or quitting for the day. [4:07]
- Level 5, Eternus: better than about 98.4% of the player base right now. [4:07]
- Eternus players are usually the highest ranked player in their lobby because few players are rated above them, so matchmaking feels bad. [4:07] [4:38]
- Eternus players take every 1v1 that looks even remotely winnable, and 9 times out of 10 they either escape or trade two enemies for their life. [4:38]
- Their impact can feel pointless when the team is still farming jungle at 25 minutes and letting objectives die, but playing the right way does win in the long run. [4:38]
- Eternus players are either permanently frustrated or have reached a zen level where they stop caring about matchmaking and play for themselves. [5:10]
- Eternus movement is art: they know every wall jump, vent bounce and escape route on the map. [5:10]
