Rem release testing and build:
- Rem's gun is not homing. [125:15]
- Pillow Toss (1): spirit damage and heavy knockback on charges, about 75 base damage, a fading slow of around 40% over 3 seconds, and a hit lowers your other abilities' cooldowns. It has big range and a huge AoE with Expansion. [125:46]
- Its first upgrade is not a good spike, so you probably rush the 2 in lane. [130:26]
- Tag Along (2): jump to an ally and nap with them; both get a burst heal based on missing health (about 16 to 18%) and then regen. You can hop to each ally once to refresh the duration, you can use items, and self-cast items go to the ally. [129:24]
- Points in the 2 add 20% barrier effectiveness (Divine Barrier went from 600 to 720) and bonus item range and duration while attached. [131:30]
- The 2's later upgrade gives bonus spirit power while napping and just after, about +35 and 15%. [132:00]
- The 3 sends helpers to do chores: collect boxes, do Sinner's Sacrifices, take the bridge buff and follow allies, at huge range and no HP cost. They can't take the Rejuvenator. [132:31]
- A helper on a hero gives about 23% spirit resist and 4 m/s fading move speed for 8 seconds, then naps 15 seconds. On a trooper it gives healing, damage and resist, so it wins the wave for you and you get the souls. Box or Sinner's farming has a 5-second cooldown with no nap. [134:05]
- Rem's ult casts a gaze forward through walls and floors: enemies are slowed, have reduced dash and can't use movement abilities, then sleep about 4 seconds and take heavy spirit damage on waking. Rem is unstoppable and takes reduced damage while channeling. [137:10]
- Damage past a threshold or a melee wakes sleeping targets. [138:40]
- Base gun DPS is about 116, below average, and only about 130 at max; a good gun scaling is about 160. [139:10]
- The ult can sleep everyone on mid boss from the floor above. Counterspell blocks it. [141:46]
- Superior Duration is a must on Rem because it also extends the 2. [142:46]
