## Claims
- Against Lash and Shiv in lane he played behind a wall the whole lane, because stepping forward gets him knifed. [0:30]
- Lane buys: Restorative Shot and extra health, then Extra Regen for more survivability in lane. [1:31]
- An enemy who took a camp and died for it actually lost money; going for the camp was not worth it. [2:41]
- Against Lash and Shiv, Slowing Hex is probably the item to get; Slow Bullets (captioned slowing bullets) is called a really bad item. [2:41]
- The boxes inside the new diner on blue lane were bugged at recording time. [3:11]
- Venator has no movement at all and is slow, so mobile heroes like Bebop can catch him. [12:07]
- If all your waves are pushed, the enemy cannot do Rift and cannot take your Walkers. [12:37]
- While on the Rift, keep the other waves pushing. [13:39]
- If the enemy goes for mid boss, play much more aggressively; taking a Walker meanwhile distracts them and wastes their time. [15:40]
- With 8K souls he bought Enduring Speed and Fortitude so he would not have to come back to base. [16:11]
- A solo kill gave a big soul swing. [18:12]
- He considered Silencer as a good buy against the enemy team comp; later said the team should get Silencer against it. [19:14]
- Debuff Remover answers Infernus's burn. [20:48]
- Hollow Point pairs with Fortitude: Hollow Point gives extra weapon damage above 65% health and Fortitude keeps him at full health almost all the time. [22:50]
- An enemy Bebop with Juggernaut and Witchmail had a lot of health. [23:52]
- Late game his gun damage was very high; he finished 9/2/17 with MVP. [24:22]
