## Covers
Warden, aquatiq, commentary over one ranked game on a barrier build, before a matchmaking update.
Includes his take on Seven and Graves builds from chat questions. Recorded 2026-07-28.

## Claims
- Going back is a good idea when the next wave has not arrived yet. [1:08]
- Seven should run the ultimate build; his ult is one of the best in the game and it is the only thing he is good for. [4:50]
- On Seven you want Unstoppable every game and to press 4. [6:29]
- Escalating Exposure is in the top Seven ult build; follow those items. [7:16]
- His Warden build stacks barrier; he has a double barrier going. [7:47]
- He commits to the barrier build and adds Reactive Barrier. [8:26]
- Reactive Barrier triggers when you are stunned, chained, immobilized or slept, so check whether the enemy has those first; here it is mainly against Celeste. [8:57]
- He is barrier maxing in this build. [9:32]
- Counterbuild whoever is doing the most damage to you, not just whoever is meant to be their carry. [10:02]
- Graves's wall is not a big problem because Warden can jump over it; the enemy shotgun damage is what he cannot avoid. [10:02]
- He buys Titanic Magazine mid game. [12:43]
- Warden's gun was nerfed hard and feels bad now. [12:43]
- In this build Binding Word (the cage) is levelled last. [13:44]
- He wants Spiritual Overflow for the regen from its lifesteal. [20:38]
- He buys Enduring Speed. [21:08]
- Graves should never go full gun; you want your ghouls and skulls doing the damage. [22:15]
- Graves's gun forces her into range, and in this meta nearly every hero flies; even Warden is too fast for her to keep up with because of Willpower. [22:15]
- Graves does not need more fire rate than her items give; she needs spirit damage. [22:15]
- Hybrid Graves is fine, just do not go all gun; Tesla Bullets lets her farm very well. [24:23]
- Do not put all your eggs in one basket on Graves. [24:23]
- He sells Reactive Barrier late; it is good, but he no longer needs it. [27:37]
