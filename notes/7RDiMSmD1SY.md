## Covers

aquatiq, lane against Billy and Seven. No one is coached. Recorded before the big early March 2026 patch.

## Claims
- Silver does a lot of damage, but her gun fires slowly. [1:02]
- First item: Monster Rounds instead of regen, going straight damage. [1:02]
- Against Billy and Seven the lane is hard because both have much faster guns. [1:33]
- Her shots two-shot troopers, but the slow gun makes it hard to confirm last hits and soul orbs; enemies with faster guns win the trooper race. [3:06]
- In lane get Lifestrike, then Extra Stamina. [5:43]
- Ability 1 reloads your gun and gives fire rate and bonus weapon damage. [6:44]
- Ability 2 is a movement ability; landing on an enemy kicks off them and marks them, and shooting the marked player does extra spirit damage. [6:44]
- The bowler (as captioned) does damage and slows; it also stops the target from using stamina, which is very strong against heroes that use a lot of stamina. [6:44]
- For the most part she deals melee damage, a different damage type. [7:14]
- The ult turns her into a werewolf with max health, movement speed, stacking fire rate and a new set of abilities. [7:45]
- Getting a kill during the ult refreshes it for the same duration again. [12:25]
- You do not press a button for the ult: it has about a 20 second cooldown and triggers on its own. [19:12]
- Parry incoming melee when you are low, or you are dead. [8:47]
- Clear the lane before leaving so you do not lose money. [8:47]
- After losing lane, go farm camps; plan to buy Hunter's Aura and Slowing Bullets. [10:52]
- Her farm is not good; she gets her souls from kills. [24:48]
- Toxic Bullets and Plated Armor were considered mid game; bullet armor was bought against gun damage. [15:39]
- Silencer is better than Toxic Bullets against a team like this one. [24:48]
- Phantom Strike is a strong pick so that when the ult pops you can jump onto someone and kill them. [25:49]
- Do not Phantom Strike into the whole enemy team and die. [30:26]
- Silver is very good at proccing Inhibitor and Toxic Bullets, so both are good items on her. [29:55]
- Deny enemy spirit heroes a spirit buff when you can, because a spirit buff on them is really bad for you. [27:22]
- The mid boss first punch gives full HP, so whoever is low should take it. [28:55]
- Take buffs and the Urn when ahead to snowball further. [22:45]
