## Covers
Ivy coaching review by Midknight of one Archon Ivy player, laning with Lady Geist into Abrams and Dynamo.
Themes: when to roam versus box route on a weak laner, what makes a gank successful, roam wave timings, Kudzu Bomb for poke and wave clear, Stone Form as a defensive tool, the Ivy ult combo, urn running and orb confirming, playing support while the team overfights, and item notes (Backstabber, Focus Lens, Metal Skin, Reactive Barrier).
Video date 2026-02-27; patch of that time.

## Claims
Player 1 (Ivy, Archon):
Lane and roaming:
- Ivy likes box routes because she is not a strong laner, but her Kudzu Bomb gives very good wave clear: shoot minions off the zipline, throw Kudzu, headshot the minions, and they stay in the Kudzu longer so it full clears the wave. [0:30]
- Lady Geist is a very good anchor because her bomb clears waves and her ult makes her very hard to dive, so this lane suits box routing. [0:30]
- In that lane, hard shove the wave with Kudzu, then go do a long box route. [1:02]
- Stealing enemy boxes is better than taking roof boxes. [1:02]
- Mistake: roaming when your lane partners had already shoved the enemy into tower, so there was no kill potential; that is a dead roam. Box route on the way instead. [1:32]
- Box route up to the bridge; after farming the boxes on top of bridge, check the map to assess if the lane is gankable. If the enemies are hard shoved into tower, go back, farm boxes and return to lane instead of forcing the gank. [2:03]
- Wanting to gank early at this level is a good instinct. [2:03]
- A gank does not have to net a kill: taking a big chunk of HP (about 30% of a Lash) and poking another enemy without losing anything is a success. [2:33]
- Leave after one wave; staying another wave is bad because you are feeding on your lane partner's souls and hurting them. [2:33]
- A roam only counts as successful if you box maxed first or on the way; with no boxes you gain nothing, though you still fed souls to your lane partner. [2:33]
- Not stealing waves from the lane you gank is good. [3:04]
- Backstabber is a good Ivy item, though optional; the coach is not a big fan of it in general. [3:35]
- After the first gank, push the wave, box route toward mid and take a gank only if you see one. [3:35]
- Do not roam on the 4:00 wave. Roam on the 4:30 wave: shove it and go, because bridge buffs are about to spawn and you can grab a bridge buff plus box routes. [4:05]
- Do not over burn your Kudzu; it is worth having it ready for the wave. [4:05]
- Gun Ivy is not great right now; spirit Ivy puts in work. [5:05]
- Box route with lane priority more often after the first gank. [5:35]
- Mistake: throwing Kudzu just for menial poke on the enemy. Throwing it on the wave is significantly better. [5:35]
- Shooting minions off the zipline makes them cluster together, which sets up a better Kudzu. [5:35]

Stone Form:
- Do not use Stone Form offensively on Ivy, especially while saving for a big tier 3 item. [7:07]
- With an Abrams in lane you must respect his charge: keep Stone Form to dodge it rather than using it offensively. Using it offensively got punished for about -600 HP. [11:52]
- Do not try to ult combo a Dynamo. [11:52]
- Kudzu Bomb should go on the camp, not Stone Form. [13:25]
- Stone Form is a key piece of utility; do not use it for fun or to dodge a minor ability like a Goon. [25:16]
- Stone Form in laning is a defensive tool, mostly to dodge Abrams' charge and later any key abilities, not for the menial stun or menial damage. [35:07]

Ult and combos:
- Ganking with the ult is extremely loud; the lane you gank should be calling it to their mid. [8:07]
- Ivy ult combo: drop your ult bomb, Vortex Web, Kudzu Bomb, Stone Form. Dump everything on top of each other and Stone Form them in the Kudzu; it guarantees about 1,000 to 2,000 damage depending on game time. The coach was unsure of the exact order and suggested watching a pro Ivy. [34:36]
- If you have Alchemical Fire, add it to the combo. [35:07]
- Use Kudzu late game together with Slowing Hex or Vortex Web, in the ult combo, or on minions. [34:05]
- Do not use Vortex Web when you have no Kudzu up. [31:25]
- As support Ivy your 1v1 is bad, so chasing a Celeste alone feels bad. [32:58]

Healing Nova and teamfights:
- You could have popped Healing Nova to save teammates; do not hold it waiting for tether to be up. [10:11]
- When an enemy like Lash groups your teammates for you, it is an amazing time to use Healing Nova. [11:21]
- No need to ult out a full HP ally who is being dived, especially a Shiv, since Bloodletting makes him very hard to kill. [10:11]
- When your team overfights or forces mid, spam the retreat ping every moment you are there. [23:45]
- Do not look for a pick on mid; look for picks on a side lane, because on mid the whole enemy team can rotate in. [23:13]
- Backing off from a split without map information is good; tell the team to back off together and do not re-engage. [16:58]
- Do not go back into a lost fight; ult and leave. [17:28]
- When low and the enemy has strong teamfight ults like Lash and Dynamo, knock and leave. [26:51]
- Greeding into the enemy fountain was the main late mistake; the team could have ended about five minutes earlier without it. [27:21]
- Heal to full before leaving; avoid a full reset when one is not needed and go mid instead. [19:05]

Urn and objectives:
- When carrying the urn past a wave, drop it, throw Kudzu on the wave and keep going. You can melee while holding the urn, but minions can melee it off you. [14:27]
- Run the urn from the roofs, not the ground; do not try to ninja cash it with the enemy team nearby. [14:57]
- Into a comp with Seven, Lash, Dynamo and Silver, objectives like the urn are hard; get a pick first (here with your Haze) before running it, because their teamfight is very strong. [15:27]
- Running a bad urn is far better than not running urn at all; at this level, call objectives, see why they fail, and learn. [15:58]
- The urn is good if you can run it before Dynamo's ult is up. [20:06]
- If you run urn consistently, learn the urn route (vent, corner boost off the bridge, dash into mid, slide through mid to the vent). [20:06]
- Confirm the urn soul orbs; a big share of the urn's value is in them and a denied orb counts double, so letting even one get denied costs a lot (he estimated about 200 souls). [20:36]
- Route in from the sky and down dash onto the urn point; it is very hard to stop. [25:16]
- Do not let the enemy cash the urn for free; the team flooding out of the entrance late is what let them. [28:22]
- You can tactical pause to call an early mid boss and wait for the team to agree. [20:36]
- Everyone except a pure gun carry should heavy melee mid boss until about 22 to 24 minutes, since it takes extra melee damage. [10:47]

Movement:
- On air vents, wall bounce off the bridge and slide more; double jump instead of a diagonal air dash unless you are dashing into a wall. [9:10]
- Routing back without boost, it is faster to jump off the zip, drop down and bounce off the wall back up than to fully reset to base. [31:57]
- On bridge buff, you do not need to parry until you see the melee. [22:39]

Items:
- Slowing Hex is good into Abrams, Dynamo and Lash. [17:58]
- Optional support items for this game: Healing Tempo (team likes fire rate), Heroic Aura, and Scourge into a hard spirit enemy team. [17:58]
- Lightning Scroll makes sense on Ivy; Vortex Web next. [21:38]
- Juggernaut was a bad buy because the enemy lacked the guns to justify it. [27:21]
- Against Abrams buy Reactive Barrier or Metal Skin; the rest of that comp is better countered by Spirit Resilience. [27:52]
- Metal Skin does not stop melee, but people like to melee into it, so you can land decent parries. [28:22]
- Metal Skin is the best gun counter right now because Armor Piercing Rounds goes through other gun counters; Plated Armor stopped being a good gun counter once Armor Piercing Rounds went through it, and was overhyped anyway. [28:52]

General advice:
- Focus Lens is very strong into Rem because Rem cannot really buy debuff removal. [9:40]
- On supports like Paige and Rem, do not heavy melee, but light melee a lot when you kit dump; forcing the enemy to parry is one of your biggest values. [7:37]
- If your lane is not broken it is fine to stay, but call on mic for teammates to come help you get out of the lane. [12:24]
- Shiv's biggest value is staying back and chopping off heads in a teamfight; players greeding damage on him deflate his win rate. [12:54]
- Teamfight structure: ideally get a pick with your pick hero, then your front line engages. [27:52]
- Current comp structure: double front line, true support, pick, M1 carry, off carry or flex. [28:52]
- Percent damage amp keeps being unhealthy for the game (Pocket's Barrage, Infernus's Catalyst, Celeste's 3). [32:58]
- The player's macro was good; the main gap is reading information efficiently, which comes with hours in the game. [26:18]
