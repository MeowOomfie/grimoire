## Covers
Haze (spirit build), aquatiq. Commentary over a stomp win (12 kills, 36,000 damage), with opinions on Bebop, Mina, Victor and Haze balance.
His first Haze game since her changes. Video dated 2026-05-21.

## Claims
- Spirit Haze build: Extra Spirit as the first item. [0:40]
- Spirit items scale the number of bullets in Haze's ultimate (Bullet Dance). [0:40]
- Swift Striker is one of his early buys. [3:15]
- He maxes Fixation this game. [10:55]
- He buys Boundless Spirit as the next item; with it he has a huge bullet count in the ultimate. [15:36]
- The build is spirit focused but still shoots: the gun does bullet damage while Fixation does spirit damage, so spirit items still help when shooting. [17:07]
- He buys Toxic Bullets so he can proc it on sleeping targets. [19:09]
- Vampiric Burst is a fun next item. [19:39]
- Take the healing minion when it drops. [15:36]
- With the ultimate up, take mid boss and then run at the enemy afterwards. [21:11]
- Haze's ultimate is very strong when the enemy team has nothing to take her out of it. [21:11]
- He thinks Haze is weak most games unless she is fed, around 20,000 souls by 20 minutes. [12:01]
- A Seven left alone in a solo lane for a long time gets a lot of souls without many kills. [8:52]
- Bebop in lane is unreasonable: the bomb does about 200 damage and is not a skill shot, so you cannot escape a Bebop walking at you; the hook is fair because it is a skill shot. [5:49]
- Bebop running at you, placing a bomb and then ulting has no counterplay in lane. [7:19]
- Victor is weak now, so some players run gun Victor. [7:19]
- Mina is a perma fighting hero and still very strong despite her ultimate nerf. [9:23]
- Warden is a sleeper hero and also very strong. [8:21]
- After this game he takes back his Bebop complaints and says Haze is the unreasonable one. [20:41]
- Haze often feels low impact because she roams for kills rather than joining everything, yet he ended with the most kills. [25:44]
