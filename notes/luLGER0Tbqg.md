# Is Seven the BEST HERO This Patch? - Eternus 6 Educational Commentary
channel: Natan  date: 2025-07-10  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=luLGER0Tbqg

## Covers
Seven, hard lane with Pocket vs Lady Geist and Mirage, 8.4K Power Surge spike, wave priority, Healing Rite timing, Suppressor, Spirit Shielding, solo farm and side waves, 19K at 15 minutes, waiting for buffs and Urn before Mid Boss, defending with wave clear, how to use Ethereal Shift, sliding, Kelvin, Paradox, Viscous

## Claims
- Seven is not great in lane, and in a hard lane your goal is just to survive, farm up and play for your power spikes; his Pocket did the heavy lifting. [0:30]
- Seven's most important power spike is 8.4K souls, the tier 3 Power Surge, which adds spirit scaling and base damage and massively increases farm speed and fight damage. [0:30]
- He pushes the wave and pings be right back before buying Monster Rounds, so his Pocket knows. [1:00]
- Against Mirage do not take long trades; he backs off right before the Mirage can proc the second Djinn's Mark on him. [1:35]
- Establishing wave priority is really good, because if the enemy gets it they can focus you down on your tower. [1:35]
- He clears the wave before popping Healing Rite, so the enemy cannot push them under tower; he died from walking up too far, and says the decision was fine, he should just have respected them more. [2:35]
- He cancelled the enemy Lady Geist's Healing Rite by hitting minions with Power Surge. [3:15]
- Staying to clear the wave while his Pocket fought was the correct decision. [3:15]
- He buys Suppressor early against this lane; it makes it really hard for the Lady Geist to win trades. [4:16]
- With the wave already under the enemy tower, enemy zipline boosts up and his Pocket low, he does not hit the tower; he grabs boxes and makes sure to be back in time. [4:16]
- Wait to clear the wave until your partner is back, and clear the wave first rather than running past the minions. [5:17]
- Spirit Shielding blocked all his damage on the Mirage; he calls it broken and says to abuse it on every hero. [5:48]
- When you are ahead and take their Guardian, push the wave and then take their resources. [6:19]
- With three teammates on one lane he covers another wave; he and his Pocket both want solo farm, so he will not W key into the fight and instead clears the wave and the nearby camps to maximise farm. [6:49]
- Already the most fed player in the lobby, he wants to accelerate by taking all the farm he can and keeping waves away from his Walker. [7:21]
- With almost 6K unspent souls he goes to the Guardian to buy; the secret shop is an option but risky without a flex. [7:51]
- He uses the zipline to get height and a good position for the ult. [8:22]
- He leaves a fight when there is no point chasing and grabs a wave instead. [9:12]
- He bought Spirit Lifesteal a bit too early. [9:12]
- He pushes the wave as far as he can into the enemy Walker, then takes the farm on his side. [9:42]
- Whenever you can take a solo wave somewhere, take it, because that is how you get fed. [10:14]
- He was at 19K souls at 15 minutes, which is really good. [10:45]
- Buffs and Urn spawn again at 15 minutes. [10:45]
- He pushes a wave to scare off the Mirage but does not chase, because he just saw the Kelvin and Viscous nearby and could be caught. [11:16]
- After taking a Walker deep and killing two, they were still 3v4, so he should have backed off; that was greedy, they already got what they came for. [12:16]
- When he realises a fight is bad he teleports out. [12:47]
- He does not chase the Lady Geist into a veil. [13:52]
- When the game is awkward, wait for buffs and Urn: with both buffs and a soul lead a fight is much better, the Urn forces the fight, and kills from the Urn fight open the Mid Boss. [13:52]
- Walking up while teammates were fixing waves got them caught and gave the enemy Mid Boss; a big throw, when all they had to do was wait. [14:23]
- He pushes out one wave and then teleports to help defend another lane. [14:53]
- Spirit Shielding blocked the entire Paradox combo. [15:24]
- To defend a Walker, clear the wave, because without a wave it is hard for them to take it; he shoots the wave, not the Viscous ball rolling behind him. [15:54]
- Even after the enemy took Mid Boss they lost no Walker, because they pushed out two waves and helped defend the third. [16:26]
- With the enemies seen on another lane, he looks for a pick on an overextended Kelvin. [16:58]
- He used Ethereal Shift twice in one fight, and you can cancel it early. [18:02]
- How to use Ethereal Shift: at the start of the game make a mental note of the high impact enemy abilities that lock you down or burst you, and dodge those; here Infernus's ult, Paradox swap, stalling inside a Kelvin dome or against Viscous ball, and maybe the damage of Lady Geist's ult (he is not sure). [18:32]
- As a squishy carry, use Ethereal Shift when many enemies are hitting you: it gives your team time to back you up and the enemies usually switch to another target instead of waiting four seconds. [19:02]
- In a random outnumbered fight he backs off and clears a wave, but stays near in case his team can do something. [19:32]
- He got overconfident running in with the new build and should have chilled and backed off. [20:05]
- He does not chase a Mirage on a side wave when he cannot see the enemy team and his team is not close enough to help. [20:35]
- When jumped, Ethereal Shift stalls four seconds for teammates to arrive; he shifts as soon as an enemy's Warp Stone pause ends. [21:05]
- After a pick with good wave states they go Mid Boss, since the enemy has to deal with the waves before they can contest. [21:35]
- On Seven slide constantly to extend your magazine; with his high movement speed (and Fleetfoot) he can slide on flat ground without dashing. [21:35]
- You can use Ethereal Shift to sit on the Rejuvenator and parry; he was a little late on the timing. [21:35]
