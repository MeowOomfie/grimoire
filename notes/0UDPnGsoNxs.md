## Covers
Warden, aquatiq commentary over one of his own games, with teammate voice chat.
Little structured advice: a few build, matchup and lane notes from the 2026-07 patch.

## Claims
- He calls Warden very busted and meta, and plays Renon's build, a hybrid flask and gun build. [0:32]
- When your lane is down souls you can no longer take 1v1s or 2v2s; sit back and wait until you catch up in money. [1:35]
- If your lane partner is not doing damage, leave to do boxes and hope your side lanes win. [2:37]
- Bebop is the matchup he hates on Warden: Warden is too slow, you cannot run away from Bebop, and going near him gets you hooked and killed. [5:44]
- A kill on the Bebop in lane is huge. [4:07]
- Take the small camp before the enemy steals it. [4:37]
- He maxes the cage (Binding Word) second. [8:53]
- Dynamo's Kinetic Pulse is hard to hit; he calls Dynamo a skillful, hard to use hero. [8:21]
- A teammate notes that Focus Lens (captioned "focus line") stops you from using your ult. [18:14]
- A teammate recommends Mystic Expansion as a good item to get. [18:47]
- He asks whether Warden players run Silencer and thinks most do; do not run in and die. [19:52]
- He needed Counterspell for the whole fight against the enemy disables but had no slots for it. [21:58]
- Against Infernus he needed a debuff remover. [21:28]
- Do not take fights deep in enemy territory when your team is far behind in souls (they were about 40k down). [20:55]
