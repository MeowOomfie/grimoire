# This is Why You Can't Carry | Phantom Seven Coaching
channel: Midknight  date: 2026-04-11  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0cTj49pB-oU

## Covers
Seven coaching review of a Phantom player (with a Lash lane partner): weak lane into Wraith and Mina, Extra Regen over Healing Rite, Sharpshooter, Cultist rush, Intensifying Magazine, Fleetfoot and Surge of Power, Colossus, Unstoppable, box routing, kill wave in a lost lane, playing for spikes, bag and soul awareness, comeback Urn, Rejuvenator hygiene, over fighting when fed, Sinner's Sacrifice, low elo farm-and-punish method, Patron pit fights

## Claims
- The review covers itemization, box routing, playing around your spikes, coordination, bag awareness, soul awareness, Rejuvenator hygiene, running the Urn, how to play when very fed, how not to throw your lead when carrying, and farming macro. [0:00]
- Against Wraith (not a strong laner, not a weak one) and a very strong laner, Seven is one of the weak laners. [0:30]
- The main reason Seven is bad is his big hitbox, especially his head. [0:30]
- The Phantom player should have bought Extra Regen in lane because he needs the defensiveness; skipping it forces you into Healing Rite, which is significantly worse, and he calls it a really bad misbuy. [1:00]
- Healing Rite is bad right now because its sprint speed is stopped by wall bouncing, so if you edge boost (which you should be doing) the sprint speed is useless. [1:31]
- Healing Rite only heals 4.2 health a second if used off cooldown for full value, while Extra Regen gives 3 flat regen plus 1 out of combat regen, so Extra Regen is better in pretty much every way; Healing Rite's only edges are the movement speed or catching the next wave from 1 HP. [2:03]
- In this lane any fair fight ends badly because the enemy laners are much stronger; the player was fighting too aggressively into her while she had bats. [3:34]
- He was expecting his stun to hit into Mina bats, and the enemy heroes are simply better than Seven in lane. [4:04]
- He assumes the lane fight was taken on the enemy's tier two spike, which is why she felt so strong. [4:40]
- Sharpshooter on Seven: not really; it is not that good right now, Seven is not consistently taking long range trades, and a lot of his damage comes from spirit (captioned "spear"). [4:40]
- You do not want to be boxing (breaking crates) anymore, especially as an M1 boxing out of a dead lane. [5:11]
- A lane you cannot win as an M1 should just be kill wave off, kill wave off; Power Surge lets Seven kill waves consistently and quickly. [5:43]
- Do not prioritize menial poke on the enemy over clearing the wave that is hitting your Tower. [6:14]
- He likes the Cultist rush, but suggests a gun item instead of Extra Spirit before it, such as Opening Rounds, for a similar amount of spirit power plus a gun spike off Cultist so you are really big after it. [6:45]
- He puts the Diamond equivalent in Deadlock at about Oracle. [7:17]
- If you choose to dive, coordinate with your lane partner; you cannot walk back into the Tower, and popping back into Tower means you are 100% dead. [7:17]
- The player was close to his Cultist soul spike off the kill; play for your spikes and coordinate with your lane partner. [7:47]
- When the Urn is comeback, 100% take it for souls. [8:47]
- Intensifying Magazine (captioned "intensifying") is Seven's core gun item. [8:47]
- A lot of solo queue players undervalue Cultist; it is good on Seven because he just wants to farm, his lane is slow so he tries to farm, and he likes the ability range and weapon damage. [9:17]
- Mindlessly farming is correct on Seven: if the review is boring enough that the coach goes on tangents, you are playing Seven correctly. [10:21]
- You should not try to fight and gank while sitting on a 1.3K bag of unspent souls (bag awareness, soul awareness). [10:21]
- Once you hit a spike, getting active and looking to fight is good. [11:22]
- Run the Urn when it is comeback; he is happy to say you should always run a 10 minute comeback Urn, with no situation where you should not. [11:22]
- Good pattern from the player: he got behind, mindlessly farmed for a while, then used his spike to be active. [11:53]
- After that the player overstayed his activeness: get the kills, then go farm (with lots of jungle up), reach about 20K, buy, and then get active again; greedy chases let the enemy catch up and throw the lead. [12:23]
- It is fine for Seven not to farm the Sinner's Sacrifice (captioned "centers") because they get farmed so quickly; he prioritizes hybrid camps. [13:25]
- Fighting while low on a 2K bag is not okay; with an 800 bag it would be risky but okay, with 2K you cannot do it. [14:56]
- The player has the climbing low elo method: mindlessly farm, kill everyone who overextends, mindlessly farm, repeat; played consistently it should give a decently high win rate. [15:26]
- Rotating mid for kills is fine; when the team is all low HP, just run around (farm) instead. [15:56]

- The fight took far too long to kill the Sinclair. [18:59]
- If you cannot slide while farming Mid Boss, use the stairs. [20:06]
