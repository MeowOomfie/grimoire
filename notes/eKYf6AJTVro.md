## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream from 6/1/26 (posted 2026-06-02), in the "burger box" urn meta after the urn change and a minor weekend patch that nerfed Silver.
Coaches four players: a Mina (Ascendant solo queue), an Ivy (Phantom, gun build), a Victor (Alchemist) and a Billy (Ascendant), then analyzes a Together Billy game that turns out to be a custom/scrim lobby.
Themes: tunnel vision and fights with no purpose, playing for urn and mid boss, slot locking and soul awareness, item opinions (Sharpshooter, Fortitude, Plated Armor, Witchmail, Radiant), light melee starts, walker versus urn value.
