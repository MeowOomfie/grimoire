channel: Midknight
date: 2025-10-27

## Covers
Shiv, Midknight. Coaching review of one Oracle Shiv one-trick (laning with Viscous into Paige, against Wraith, Infernus, Holliday and others).
Themes: soul awareness, lane healing items (why Healing Booster is bad, Restorative Shot and Enchanter's Emblem instead), green items and defensive itemization, tower parries, mantle sliding, Killing Blow and knife tech, walker dash, taking fights with bad waves, and how to call mid boss. Video dated 2025-10-27.

## Claims
Shiv itemization:
- Expect Shiv to start a healing item like Healing Rite or Restorative Shot; saving for a T2 this early is scary, though Battle Vest's out-of-combat regen would not be the worst. [0:30]
- Letting souls get this high this early without saving for a T2 is bad; starting with Mystic Regeneration or Restorative Shot would have made the lane easier. [1:31]
- Don't start Healing Booster: it gives no extra healing early. On a 1,600 item you heal maybe 8 extra per knife; even on Abrams or a melee lifesteal build you get at most about 200 healing, which Restorative Shot gives in about three shots. [2:02]
- By 9 minutes Mystic Regeneration had healed about 600 and Healing Booster added only 70 to 80 of that. [28:53]
- Healing Booster is only really good on a healing hero or one playing healing tempo; it is not worth a slot on Shiv even with lifesteal. [3:37]
- For healing in lane, go double vest; double vest is very strong. [5:12]
- Enchanter's Emblem was fantastic this game because nearly all enemy damage was spirit (about 80% spirit in lane); if you buy it, buy it in lane. [9:28]
- Mystic Regeneration is mostly not good; on most heroes it heals less than Restorative Shot. It fits Mirage, who can spam it, or a true DoT hero, or a knife Shiv build. [6:48]
- Items like Quicksilver Reload and Mystic Burst give much more healing than Mystic Regeneration because they apply more. [9:58]
- You badly need green vitality investments: Enchanter's Emblem, Spirit Shielding (good this game) or Counterspell. Reactive Barrier was not good this game and Veil Walker is not good. [12:45]
- Your next item should be green; orange is acceptable, but it just can't be purple. [13:47]
- Debuff Remover was very good this game: it cleanses Paige's root and burn, and debuff reduction is great into Holliday, Wraith, Paige, Infernus and good into Gist. [13:16]
- He prefers Debuff Remover over Counterspell here because it gives much more value into Infernus and Toxic Bullets; popping it after the enemy ult would have saved about 1,000 HP. [24:48]
- Torment Pulse is overpowered; not often seen on Shiv. [12:15]
- Arctic Blast gives more value than a regen item if you can afford it. [12:45]
- Juggernaut is strong, but against a mostly spirit-damage comp Spirit Resilience goes crazier. [23:46]
- Spirit Resilience then Transcendent Cooldown, which boosts Torment Pulse, Arctic Blast and the regen active, would be great. [23:46]
- Slowing Hex is great into Wraith, Infernus and Holliday, good into Paige; it catches very mobile heroes and stops Holliday escaping. It also affects Doorman's doors. [19:42]
- Spellbreaker is very good into a spirit Holliday but not good enough to buy this game. [24:16]

Shiv mechanics:
- Lead with your knife. [1:01]
- When an enemy teleports into your face, or roots you, insta dash out. [2:02]
- Sweaty kill tech: put a point in the knife for the extra charge and throw it in her back to stack knives; if the third knife hits she dies. A Shiv one-trick on a dash build should learn this, since the kill is worth the AP. [6:17]
- Shoot in between melees. [7:18]
- Dropping Killing Blow when the target is still 50 to 100 HP above the threshold is too early. [5:12]
- Throwing knives at the ground for a radiant speed boost is okay if you are full knives with CDR, but knives have a long cooldown. [11:01]
- Pop Bloodletting after the enemy swap; that is when you want it. [21:44]
- Dash in, hit the wave and parry; dash between two enemies so the dash and parry hit both. [24:48]

Lane and tower:
- Respect the enemy wave more; going in on a Paige with barrier on her wave won't get a good trade. [1:31]
- Stop parrying under the enemy tower; it nearly died three times to Paige roots and lasers. Parrying the tower after the wave is already dead is rough. [3:37]
- The time to walk up and parry the tower is when your whole team is there and they can't punish you. [10:29]
- She is very meleeable when she fumbles her parry. [1:01]

Soul awareness and shopping:
- Biggest note: soul awareness. First buy at 1,600 on two T1s, about 5k before the first T3, then 6k and 9k unspent. [27:53]
- Always know what item you want next and how close you are to it. [12:45]
- Know what you want before you reach the shop so you can auto buy as you walk by. Buy before farming a camp, since the items help you farm it faster. [8:21]
- When gold gets high, hard shove the lane and go buy; don't leave a wave to shop. [29:27]
- Farming while sitting on a ton of unspent souls loses a lot of efficiency. [8:52]

Movement:
- Use a diagonal air dash, not a forward air dash, to max movement. [8:52]
- Mantle slide on wave duty; it is free movement and missing it feels bad. [11:41]
- Walker tech: instead of walking out and dashing in, walk up and dash up to dodge walker stomp and do the damage. They jump, then dash at about a 60 to 80 degree angle; watch the DLNS Shiv games and practice it for about 10 minutes in Explore the Map. [15:30]
- Greedy farming is fine as long as you know your exit route if you get jumped, like wall bouncing up. Always have an exit plan. [16:00]

Macro and fights:
- After a tower kill, look left and right for Sinner's Sacrifice to steal; push toward it when you have made space. [11:01]
- Hard shove a wave and then steal the Sinner's Sacrifice when the enemy shows a lot on their strong side. [12:15]
- At higher levels, assume the side the urn spawned on is the enemy's strong side. [14:22]
- Pushing with nothing to pressure puts the ball in their court; go buy instead. [14:22]
- Trading a walker for a guardian that isn't their flex is good. [15:30]
- Bad fight: three waves crashing, fighting mid in front of the wave. Once the walker is lost, call mid boss immediately; it is the only way to make losing the walker worth it. [18:07]
- A good fight: up 20k, waves neutral, over urn; even better if you shove waves first. [21:13]
- After winning that fight, go mid boss instead of trading walkers. [21:44]
- How to call mid boss: on mic, or press Tab, put your cursor on mid and keep left clicking until all six teammates are there or agree. [22:46]
- Winning a fight while two teammates reset and your Mina is half HP and away is very risky; if they had flooded with six it would have been rough. [25:51]
- Up 50k with no Rejuv is still scary. [27:23]
- Don't take fights where your waves are negative, and don't fight in front of a wave. [29:58]
