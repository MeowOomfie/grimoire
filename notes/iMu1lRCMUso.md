## Covers
Eternus 6 Pocket game (0 deaths in 30 minutes) commented by the player, with Lash in lane vs Haze and Ivy.
Covers surviving a losing lane while blue wins, the Monster Rounds into Cultist Sacrifice by 8 minutes build, ability order (Barrage max), stealing enemy camps, pushing waves before joining fights, and when not to force walkers.
For Pocket players at any rank, July 2025 patch.

## Claims
- Pocket and Lash vs Haze and Ivy is not the best lane, because Ivy can out wave clear them easily. [0:30]
- Plan when your lane is weak but blue is strong (Grey Talon and Sinclair vs Haze and Kelvin): hang in there, survive and wait for blue to win and come help. [0:30]
- When Ivy doesn't have Stone Form, go in with Enchanter's Satchel for a good trade. [1:33]
- Build: start with Healing Rite first, pick up Monster Rounds, and build it into Cultist Sacrifice. [1:33]
- Goal of the build is Cultist Sacrifice at roughly 8 minutes, when tier three camps spawn, then eat a big creep for fire rate, range and health, all good stats for Pocket. [1:33]
- After Cultist Sacrifice, farm for the next spikes: Majestic Leap, Superior Cooldown. [2:04]
- His Lash trading into the entire wave was a big mistake; the right play was to clear the wave, or wait for the wave to crash into the tower before looking for Slam. [2:34]
- Being near full HP after Healing Rite does not mean you have to force something; just clear the wave. [2:34]
- Use Flying Cloak for extra wave clear. [3:04]
- Ping a teammate back when he goes for risky plays in a losing lane. [3:35]
- When an enemy has used all her cooldowns and is very low, it's a free kill. [3:35]
- Pop Healing Rite and run a quick box when you have a moment. [4:05]
- Ability order: two points in Flying Cloak, then two in Enchanter's Satchel, then choose ult max or Barrage max; he prefers Barrage max with one point in the ult for the cooldown. [4:05]
- Barrage max is better in the mid game: it gives amp and a bigger range that guarantees you get the amp, which amplifies your gun, cloak and satchel for burst; you still max the ult later for late game team fights when people group more. [4:05]
- Stack up Barrage amp (three stacks) and walk up looking for a play once all cooldowns are up. [5:07]
- Mistake: teleporting before popping Enchanter's Satchel cost him a solo kill. [5:07]
- You don't need to play perfectly to reach a high rank; most of the game is decision making, being in the right place at the right time and keeping up in farm, on top of solid mechanics. [5:38]
- When the enemy has her ult up, don't go in to finish her off. [6:09]
- Don't use your combo on Ivy if a teammate is also going in, because Stone Form can block both. [6:09]
- At 8 minutes center sacrifices and tier three camps spawn; with the enemy guardian down, steal their jungle and center sacrifices. [6:39]
- Get Cultist Sacrifice as fast as possible, then eat one of the enemy's tier three camps. [6:39]
- Mistake: a needless dash wasted his ult. [7:13]
- When ahead, go for the enemy camps rather than your own as much as possible, especially with teammates nearby. [7:44]
- Check enemy items: Ivy has Slowing Hex. [7:44]
- When teammates rotate away from blue, the empty lane is free for you to take. [7:44]
- Mistake: moving to the right side of the map on vision showed the enemy that Lash was alone on yellow with a numbers disadvantage; he should have moved to the left side instead of leaving Lash alone at the guardian. [8:15]
- Take the bridge buff (casting buff) when it's available. [8:45]
- When a teammate picks up the Urn and another goes to the Urn fight, holding yellow alone lets you solo farm quickly and get more fed. [8:45]
- Correction: since Lash had no ult and he did, the better play may have been Lash holding yellow and Pocket rotating to the Urn. [9:17]
- Steal enemy camps at every opportunity, but always be back in time to catch the wave. [9:17]
- Quickly push the wave and use Majestic Leap to rotate to camps. [9:51]
- Mistake: not noticing Holliday and Haze on yellow; with all cooldowns up he could have helped defend, and possibly killed them with Infernus. Keep an eye on the map while farming. [9:51]
- Use Affliction as you are already leaping downwards with Majestic Leap; using it too late makes it miss. [10:22]
- Track your Cultist Sacrifice buff: when it runs out and comes back up, use it again, preferably on an enemy tier three camp. [10:54]
- Don't chase a Holliday after a quick trade; there's no point and it does nothing for you. [10:54]
- When strong, join fights on the other side of the map, but push the mid wave first; get your wave states in order before joining any fight. [11:56]
- Pocket's abilities are all AoE, so pick the grouped targets (Holliday and Ivy) over a lone Shiv. [12:28]
- With the mid wave still neutral and not pushing your walker, you don't need to worry about it. [12:28]
- When the enemies are all respawning and you are only four, back off. [13:02]
- When the enemy has the Urn and Haze and Ivy push green while your Sinclair lacks wave clear to defend, go defend green. [13:33]
- Teleport to the fight once your team is holding well, and clear the wave quickly with Flying Cloak. [14:04]
- Ping lanes that are getting pushed in. [14:04]
- When enemy death timers are too short, don't push the walker; push the wave and back off. [14:04]
- Holliday corner peeking a walker can kill it from half HP without taking damage. [14:35]
- With max Barrage and Greater Expansion it is basically impossible to miss Barrage. [15:06]
- When you are very strong, it shouldn't be your job to defend a side walker; be at the fights. [15:06]
- Forcing a walker when the enemies are all up is pointless, especially with a weak yellow wave and someone needed on green. [15:36]
- When Kelvin's Frozen Shelter cuts you off, dip while still healthy, wait for cooldowns and teammates, then look for a re-engage. [15:36]
- Forcing down a walker, especially a mid walker, 5v5 or 6v6 is an easy way to throw games because it's so tanky. [16:07]
- With a numbers advantage, wait for more kills, which lead to walkers or mid boss, while not showing on the map and letting a teammate clear the wave. [17:13]
- Rotate to a lane with a good wave to help a teammate take the walker. [17:13]
- Don't beeline to a fight on the other side; push your wave first. [17:49]
- Up in numbers with the enemy low and without key cooldowns, the Urn is free. [18:20]
- Mistake: reactivating Flying Cloak too early loses its damage. [18:20]
- If anything about Pocket should be buffed, it's Flying Cloak travel speed. [18:51]
- Don't go mid boss when your only real DPS is Infernus and the enemy has Ivy, Holliday and Kelvin with lots of AoE; group up and push blue to take that walker, and communicate it by voice or spam pings. [19:21]
- Don't be afraid to use Affliction on one or two people; you don't have to wait for the perfect four or five person angle. [20:22]
- With two picks, mid boss is free. [20:22]
- Losing the second (green) walker is fine: it gives them some souls but not the flex slot. [20:22]
- Kelvin's ult should last 5.5 seconds; his seemed longer. [20:52]
- With a 30k to 40k lead the game should be over; keep eating tier three camps with Cultist Sacrifice. [21:22]
- Kelvin's Frozen Shelter can cut off Affliction. [21:58]
- Pushing a wave takes about 2 seconds and is so good; do it. [22:28]
- Mistake: engaging before Affliction was off cooldown lost a fight that would have been free with more than a one-person Affliction. [23:09]
- Without Rejuvenator, enemies get reduced death timers, so back off, take the jungle and wait for buffs and Urn. [24:41]
- Buy Diviner's Kevlar; with it you aren't afraid to jump in with your ult. [25:12]
- Farm amp stacks on an enemy before a fight so you already have four stacks, then jump in. [25:12]
- Ethereal Shift plus Enchanter's Satchel can stall and win a fight. [25:42]
- With no cooldowns and low health, don't stick around; push the wave instead. [25:42]
