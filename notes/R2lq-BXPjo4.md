## Covers
An Eternus 6 Yamato player reviews his own roughly 30-minute game (lane with Lash against Pocket and Mo & Krill), explaining mechanics, itemization, map movement and his mistakes.
Key topics: lane trades off enemy cooldowns, Extra Health in burst lanes, healing for the 4.8k vitality spike, defending centers, urn timing, Counterspell, Reactive Barrier, Mystic Reverb before mid boss, Rejuvenator pushes.
For Yamato players and mid-rank players learning decision making.

## Claims
- On Yamato, take the left high ground at the start of lane to get an angle for right-click poke. [0:00]
- When the enemy Pocket uses his cloak on the wave, you have an ability advantage, so trade. [0:30]
- He and his Lash stacked up too close and both got hit by Mo & Krill's Scorn, which is pretty bad. [0:30]
- After losing health early he should just buy Restorative Shot to get health back. [1:00]
- With push in the lane, take the left angle by the church for more poke. [1:36]
- Small mistake: using the right click on Mo & Krill as soul orbs pop meant he could not last hit them with left click. [1:36]
- At 2:00 boxes and the small camp spawn; one laner takes the small camp while the other pushes the wave. [1:36]
- Forcing the Mo to deal with the wave left Pocket out of position, which gave a 2v1 on the Pocket. [1:36]
- When Mo & Krill walks up too far and uses Scorn on the wave, punish with Power Slash; dash into him first so he runs to his guardian and cannot dodge it. [2:07]
- In lane he usually buys Extra Regen or Extra Health on Yamato; here Extra Health, because the lane could kill him with all-in burst (Mo & Krill ult plus a full Pocket combo). [2:41]
- Extra Health gives about 275 health plus about 75 from the 800 vitality investment, so roughly 350 HP; it saved him about three times this game. [2:41]
- When the waves have just spawned in base, clear the tunnel boxes. [3:11]
- When his Lash went in too early, he switched his Power Slash target from the tanky Mo to the squishy Pocket. [3:43]
- He should not have gone for a parry there, and he wrongly thought a heal creep was still up. [3:43]
- Getting the Pocket kill gave him his ultimate, then a parry gave a second kill; without Extra Health he would not have lived. In bursty lanes it is a very valuable item. [4:13]
- After a big soul gain he saved up for Torment Pulse rather than fighting: collect the wave, buy, then look for something again. [4:13]
- Trading with no purpose is pointless. [4:43]
- Big mistake: going in after missing Crimson Slash while Mo & Krill was about to have ult and Pocket already had it. After using cooldowns, chill for a bit; do not grapple in or go for a parry. [5:23]
- When Mo & Krill wastes Scorn on the wave you want to punish that cooldown, but he walked past the Pocket, got hit by the cloak and got ulted; he should have waited for his Lash to go first. [5:23]
- The idea behind the play was fine; the execution was bad. He ulted because he was not sure if Mo had used Scorn. [5:53]
- At 6:00 you have the zipline boost, so he went back to base to heal up quickly. [5:53]
- When the enemy blue lane wins, the enemy Paradox is free to roam. His teammates roamed to green instead of his lane, leaving a 2v3; he thinned the wave and left. [6:23]
- When the guardian cannot be saved, go take a camp instead. [6:57]

- Take the fastest path (the blue zipline) to your centers; when your team invades the enemy centers, your job is protecting yours. [7:27]
- He forced Abrams off, then waited around the corner for the Paradox for a free kill, then stole the enemy centers. [7:27]
