## Covers
Paradox coaching by Midknight: a replay review of one Oracle-rank Paradox player.
Topics: wave priority and bomb (Pulse Grenade) usage in lane, movement, roaming timing, not side laning as a pick hero, swap angles and using swap as displacement, playing height, pit fights at mid boss, playing around spikes and secret shop, respecting Silver, and Metal Skin.
Patch era of early March 2026 (Silver, Celeste and Abrams called the broken trio).

## Claims
Player (Paradox, Oracle):
- Lane: track your bomb (Pulse Grenade) usage; with bomb up you could clear the wave almost instantly, and without it they regain full wave priority. It is on a 26 second cooldown. [0:30]
- A forward air dash is inefficient movement. [0:30]
- Orbs last 18 seconds, so clear the minions first and retake the space on that wave instead of greeding orbs into a bad trade. [1:01]
- Do not give denies; hard shoving and leaving is fine, but you have to actually leave. [1:32]
- You could have lived with better movement or by parrying. [2:05]
- You are contesting into Silver; respect how strong Silver is in lane, especially once she has her ult, when she is nearly unkillable and can one shot you. [5:42]
- Do not fight that aggressively in a 2v2 lane against Silver; a 2v1 is fine. [8:14]
- Good that you throw your ult without hitting the Kinetic Carbine first, though that one missed. [6:42]
- Roaming: if you roam, do it early; roaming after about 5 minutes is hard. [9:46]
- Before leaving to gank, push the mid wave first. [10:17]
- Diving a Mina who is under her tower is a poor gank target if they play smart. [10:17]
- Box route on the way to a roam so you can see enemy positions before committing. [10:17]
- Roaming at 10 minutes with both towers in your lane up is a bad time; you essentially sack your tower, so the gank must take a tower to be worth it. Guardians are still worth more than kills, and the mid Guardian protects the shop. [12:49]
- Paradox should not try to take 1v1s; play to get kills off your swap with a teammate. [11:19]
- Confirm the urn orbs after a fight; not confirming them feels terrible. [11:49]
- As Paradox you should almost never die; deny orbs because they are worth two to three times your death. [12:19]
- Escaping: path out to the side, and use the Kinetic Carbine's roughly 30% movement speed while charging to get out, then only throw it when it is about to expire so you still get the stun. [12:19]
- Buy your green (vitality) spike at this point before pivoting to weapon items. [13:19]
- Do not side lane as Paradox; play around teammates, invade and steal enemy jungle, look for picks, or hover near your side laner instead. [13:50]
- Good that you run at them every time you hit a spike; fighting off spikes is right. [14:52]
- Looking for picks in mid lane is bad; cover a side lane or collapse on mid when you see them pushing in. [15:22]
- Paradox is middle of the pack in difficulty; she is very strong but you have to hit your shots. [15:53]
- Paradox ult is on about a 10 second cooldown, so over-using it is better than under-using it. [16:23]
- A pick hero sitting in a side lane should get collapsed on; enemies should never run at you 1v1, they should collapse with numbers. [17:23]
- Use the secret shop and TP back instead of a full reset when you are healthy. [17:54]
- Do not full reset at full HP just to buy; go secret shop. [21:35]
- Early urn jumps are fine but the team must be coordinated; ping the urn and call teammates to it. [22:06]
- Dying repeatedly in a side lane is the main recurring issue; they collapse because the Paradox is side laning. [24:08]
- Mid boss: kill it fast (the speaker notes 10 seconds); Hollow Point stops adding damage once the target drops below its health threshold. [25:40]
- After mid boss, put your wall (Time Wall) on the Rejuvenator in the pit rather than elsewhere unless someone contests from above. [26:12]
- Paradox with ult up should not play inside the pit; play outside it and ult (swap) an enemy out of the pit. [26:12]
- Throw your bomb into the pit for big damage and to set up the fight after the Rejuvenator. [26:42]
- Chasing at 2000 HP while Drifter is shooting you is greedy; kite backwards and respect Drifter more. [27:49]
- Swap from far away; ideal timing is just before the target goes behind a corner, the one point in their path they cannot vary, then swap them back into your team. [28:19]
- Do not jump in the enemy's face as Paradox; Kinetic Carbine from range and swap them in, otherwise you get little value and get focused. [29:52]
- After you cash an urn, back up instead of committing to the next fight. [31:46]
- Item: Metal Skin is strong here, especially with Superior Duration, since you were mostly dying to gun damage from Drifter and Silver; damage taken was about 50/50 gun and spirit, and Metal Skin fully stops the gun half while Spirit Resilience would only reduce the other. [32:16]
- Curse is valuable too; Mina is curse bait in general. [32:46]
- Do not fight aggressively in mid while the enemy has the Rejuvenator; play to defend from your base. [32:46]
- When defending, play height (base stairs and upper positions) instead of jumping into the fray; Paradox jumping in always gets focused. [33:48]
Summary notes:
- In lane, focus on wave priority and hitting minions; if waves keep crashing, Monster Rounds is a fine buy. [34:18]
- Movement was above par for Oracle but overused stamina by chaining it. [34:18]
- Ideal bomb usage in lane: hit a player for a tick or two and also hit two minions; throw it in pits. [34:48]
- Swap then wall: start the swap and place the Time Wall after, free damage that does not slow your combo. [35:19]
- Hovering a side lane and looking for picks there is fine; being the one farming the wave and showing on vision is not, since a pick hero seen on vision relieves pressure everywhere and only gets fought with a numbers advantage. [35:49]
- You cannot win a fair fight as a pick hero; take a 1v1 if handed to you but never seek one out. [36:19]
- Respect Silver much more; even before ult her 2-1 combo does half your HP bar. [36:50]
- Think of swap primarily as a displacement tool and swap enemies into your team. [37:21]
- Always try to play height; it is very strong, especially for Paradox with bomb. [37:51]
- Metal Skin hard counters Drifter and denies Silver her wolf form. [37:51]
General and patch opinions:
- Silver, Abrams and Celeste are the trio of brokenness right now; people build Silver randomly because she is strong enough to win anyway. [21:35]
- Celeste's innate movement gives her infinite survivability, her ult does too much damage and slows too much, and there is no counter to her grapple movement; he suggests the slowing hex should turn it off. [24:38]
