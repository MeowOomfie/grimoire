# The HALLOWEEN Patch | Pro Coach Patch Review
channel: HERESY
date: 2026-10-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Woroekp2lKo

## Covers
A pro coach reads the October 2026 Halloween patch notes line by line, the newest patch in this knowledge base: mid boss pit rework and split rejuv crystals, the healing item nerfs (Radiant Regen, Healing Booster, Extra Regen), jungle vs breakables, comeback souls, slows and air drag, and hero changes for Baba, Rat King, Solomon, Violet, Nurse Harrow, Celeste, Kelvin, Apollo, Warden and more.
For any player who wants to know what changed, who got stronger or weaker, and how to play and counter after the patch.

## Claims
- The mid boss pit terrain was significantly changed into a much more dynamic place to fight, aimed at the "AoE slop meta"; many AoE threats were also nerfed directly. [0:00]
- The mid boss now drops the rejuv as three separate crystals at semi-random positions; each gives a full health heal and its own soul bounty that scales over time. [0:30]
- The rejuv crystal drop time went from 6 to 7.5 seconds. [0:30]
- Verdict on the mid boss changes: fantastic, mid boss fights will be much more interesting. [0:30]
- Radiant Regen was nerfed in every aspect; it will still be bought on heroes that really use it (Baba, likely Victor and Celeste) but you won't see it on Wraith so much anymore. [1:01]
- Healing Booster no longer builds from Extra Regen and no longer gives regen; Extra Regen was also nerfed. [1:31]
- Strong lane heroes can no longer buy Extra Regen in lane and transition cheaply into Healing Booster to combo with Radiant Regen; late game Radiant Regen scalers like Spirit Victor and Celeste pay a cost now. Celeste and Baba are hit quite hard. [1:31]
- Jungle camps hit 10% harder, have 10% more health and are worth 10% more; breakables (boxes) are worth 10% less. This incentivizes jungling and buffs heroes efficient at it, such as Billy. [2:01]
- Baba's Thread Sap can now be dispelled with Dispel Magic, and cleansing it removes both the stolen spirit and the on-hit spirit damage buff she gets against all targets. [2:31]
- Against Baba in mid game 1v1s and small engagements, use a dispel on her Thread Sap: she loses over half her damage. [3:03]
- Rat King was nerfed hard: the gun damage side lane play style is brought down a lot, the grenade nerfed slightly and the ult nerfed significantly. [3:03]
- Expect Rat King to be built as a support tank: build for his rats debuff and his shield, cooldown, tank items, Heroic Aura, Juggernaut and items that buff teammates; Swift Striker, Titanic Magazine and Spellslinger are not optimal on him. [3:33]
- Solomon's lane phase is significantly nerfed and his gun damage cut; his Bishops are a better zone control tool; good balance overall. [3:33]
- Violet got mostly nerfs; most significantly her ult now ends early if Violet dies. [4:03]
- The way to counter Violet is to hunt her down and kill her: she is squishy, and items that kept her alive like Radiant Regen were nerfed. This does not work on Rat King, whose banner stays down when he dies. [4:03]
- Nurse Harrow is nerfed, especially early and early mid game: flat healing on Healing Noodles was reduced and moved into spirit scaling. A net nerf but a fairer way to tune her. [4:34]
- Nurse Harrow's ult has better targeting and is easier to react to: she must look at you more directly and can't be too far above or below you. [5:05]
- Even with a maxed ult Nurse Harrow is no longer immune to damage; she just takes less damage and can be killed. [5:05]
- Overall verdict: all the overpowered heroes everyone feared were genuinely nerfed, a good patch all around. [5:05]
- Spellbreaker is a much better item now; Healing Booster and Radiant Regen are much worse; anti-heal is buffed globally. [5:05]
- Heroes expected to be extra strong: Bebop (completely untouched, very good), melee Apollo (better than ever), Warden as a sleeper carry (slightly buffed while other carries were nerfed), and Kelvin. [5:35]
- Kelvin has been buffed for about the tenth patch in a row: slows matter more now because they increase air drag, and his ultimate got more buffs. He may show up in pro play. [5:35]
- New Halloween mechanic: heavy melee a bubbling cauldron to get candy, store it, and eat it later for a temporary Sugar Rush move speed buff. [6:36]
- Mid boss HP and HP scaling increased and resistance shred is much less effective on it, which hits Calico Gloom Bomb, melee and splatter mid boss strategies. [7:08]
- The mid boss roar now happens at 25% HP; the boss is much tankier, so taking it usually needs longer enemy respawn timers or maybe one more kill than before. [7:08]
- Because of the roar at 25% the defending team really sees it coming; no more killing two people and instantly going mid boss. Probably healthier. [7:38]
- The mid boss bounty was cut exactly in half; the coach agrees, since the boss's main value should come from winning the fight and using it to siege objectives or take jungle. [7:38]
- The three rejuv crystals are the anti AoE slop change and probably healthy, replacing everyone parrying and punching in one spot. [8:09]
- Each crystal gives a full heal; do not forget it, you can use it for big plays. [8:39]
- Each crystal gives an individual bounty to the player, so the bounty was really spread out rather than reduced; you can give the rejuv bounty to your Wraith. The coach is not sure he likes that. [8:39]
- Breakables growth values reduced by 10%: fantastic, they were too strong. [8:39]
- Unsecured souls are now secured slower, but faster if you are behind in net worth. [9:10]
- Ganking a player who power farms the jungle (for example an enemy Wraith farming too much) is now better for your team, and being far behind is much less punishing. [9:42]
- Comeback gold from non-hero sources (camps, boxes, objectives) increased from 26% to 30% when maxed, and the two poorest players get more souls. [9:42]
- Healing snacks (the floating health bats) went from 10% to 14% heal: enormous. They were already potentially overpowered but underplayed; hitting the double health bat spots heals you about 30% of your HP. [10:15]
- Steam vents now also increase stamina regeneration, so briefly using a vent still helps your escape even if you're found after a few seconds; a genuine strategy now. [10:46]
- The move slow to air drag ratio increased, basically a 50% increase to the effect; slows already nerfed the perma flyer meta and made Lifestrike meta, and this is a huge change that nerfs heroes like Celeste. [10:46]
- Speed in tunnels was reduced, all the more reason for Rat King not to go into the jungle. [11:17]
