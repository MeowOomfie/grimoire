# The BEST Seven Build In Deadlock Is Gun
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2025-07-11  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a4bUqmU1WD4

## Covers
Seven gun and Power Surge build (Metro God 7: Spirit Shielding, Cultist Sacrifice, Superior Duration, Burst Fire, Boundless Spirit, Leech, Siphon Bullets), investment thresholds (9.6k), fire rate and Power Surge procs, skill order, Burst Fire vs Surge of Power, Leech vs Infuser, ultimate builds, aim, Pocket, Lash, Vyper

## Claims
- Investment bonuses give weapon damage, health percent or raw spirit with diminishing returns; aim to reach at least 9.6k in weapon and spirit, which gives 44 spirit or 58% weapon damage, and do not worry about anything past that. [1:02]
- Seven benefits from all three investment stats; after the Wraith nerf he has one of the highest, if not the highest, weapon damage per boon in the game. [2:04]
- Power Surge procs on every burst you fire, so Power Surge damage scales roughly one to one with fire rate; with Burst Fire active it procs 45% more often. [2:34]
- The older Metro Shock 7 was a hybrid with Infuser for the ultimate; this build is 100% focused on Power Surge, not an ultimate build, and early on the ultimate is weaker than in ultimate builds. [3:36]
- Ultimate builds run early Suppressor, early Healbane and early Unstoppable, then go in, ultimate and debuff everyone (maybe with Mystic Slow and Lightning Scroll); with a good team that is probably the most solid way to play Seven on ladder. [4:07]
- For solo carrying, the ultimate build fails: if your team does not play around Storm Cloud, or it is on cooldown, you lose. [4:07]
- Seven is a top five hero right now, especially in low rank because people do not run out of Storm Cloud; expect a nerf to Storm Cloud damage or cooldown. [4:37]
- The speaker believes this is the hardest solo carry build of any hero right now. [4:37]
- Lane: Monster Rounds to shove waves faster and Extended Magazine for ammo; if low in lane, buy Restorative Shot (50 health per hero shot every 6 seconds, more consistent with good aim) or Healing Rite. [5:37]
- Often the regen from Monster Rounds and later Spirit Shielding is enough to stay alive in lane. [6:08]
- Health is king in this meta and hard to get after nerfs to Rescue Beam, Healing Nova and Fortitude, and Seven is squishy early. [6:38]
- Spirit Shielding is the first defensive item: 2.5 out of combat regen (so you can skip Extra Regen), 1.5 seconds of move speed on proc, and a barrier that scales with spirit; think of it as Fortitude for half the price, worth at least 400 extra HP in a fight. [7:09]
- Spirit Shielding is better than Weapon Shielding, which is inconsistent in lane because the enemy often does not deal enough gun damage in 3.5 seconds to proc it. [7:39]
- Cultist Sacrifice by itself is a weak item: 160 seconds up on a 250 second cooldown leaves 90 seconds without its effects. [8:10]
- Cultist Sacrifice is not a farming item; it is for stats Seven likes: health (300 plus late), fire rate (36% extra late) and ability range, which enlarges the ball, the stun AoE, the Power Surge radius and Storm Cloud, plus 30% damage against NPCs for shoving. [8:41]
- The speaker only runs Cultist Sacrifice because Superior Duration works on items, extending it to 205 seconds with a 45 second downtime; in those 45 seconds he avoids fights, and he makes sure it is active before any fight. [9:41]
- Transcendent Cooldown would also fix Cultist Sacrifice uptime but is too big an investment to justify. [10:11]
- Superior Duration over Superior Cooldown: Power Surge duration scales with spirit, so once you have Boundless Spirit (and spirit from gold statues) Power Surge is up nearly 100% of the time; before that there is a few seconds more downtime, and several items in the build gain from the duration. [10:42]
- Each item in the build lands on the next investment threshold, and the finished base build sits at exactly 9.6k in all three categories; the speaker won 1v3 and 1v4 fights while about 20k behind with this layout. [11:44]
- Skill order: one point in Static Charge for a low stun cooldown, max Power Surge, one point in the ball for two balls, then max the ultimate, then max Static Charge, then max the ball. [13:50]
- Rapid Rounds for fire rate, because by then Power Surge is close to maxed and fire rate raises its damage. [13:50]
- Enchanter's Emblem is still insane value: spirit resist, spirit power and cooldown reduction, basically free HP and regen; everyone buys it over Battle Vest because spirit has been king early and mid game. [14:20]
- Titanic Magazine for clearing camps fast without reloading; skip it if you want to slide more. [15:21]
- Fleetfoot is a staple and a top five item; not running it on Seven is throwing: slide, bullet resist, movement speed and slow resist. [15:21]
- Seven wants to chase targets down and kite back, staying at mid distance with Power Surge active, out of melee range of scary melee heroes but in range to deal damage, and never slowing down. [15:51]
- Improved Spirit is another power spike: more Power Surge damage, which stacks in synergy with fire rate. [16:22]
- Spirit Lifesteal comes earlyish: before Burst Fire this build does little damage except with the ultimate, and without lifesteal the enemy shoots you out of the sky and you die. [16:53]
- Burst Fire is the main damage spike of the Power Surge build and replaces Surge of Power because it does more damage: it lifts weapon investment from 40% to 58% and, with Superior Duration, is active 4.5 seconds out of every 7. [17:23]
- Surge of Power's bonus fire rate and movement speed only apply once at the start of Power Surge (about 7.7 seconds with Superior Duration), and with a 33 second Power Surge cooldown you use it once, at most twice, per fight. [18:24]
- Surge of Power's 25 imbued spirit adds nothing to the investment bonus once Boundless Spirit has reached 44, while Burst Fire at the same price adds 18% weapon damage. [18:54]
- In a damage meter test (caption: "hot damage meter") Power Surge with Burst Fire started around 700 DPS and settled near 600, while the same test with Surge of Power fell to about 470 DPS. [20:24]
- Boundless Spirit is the main spirit spike; with only two purple items (Boundless Spirit and Superior Duration) Seven reaches about 136 to 149 spirit, which is all the spirit the build needs. [21:58]
- After that the damage is usually high enough, so the next buys are for survivability, lifesteal, anti-heal or debuffs rather than raw damage; Mercurial Magnum imbued on the ball is a situational idea. [22:58]
- Leech over Infuser: Leech's 30% spirit and 30% bullet lifesteal lasts the whole fight while you keep shooting, which heals more than Infuser's 80% spirit lifesteal for about 7.5 to 8 seconds followed by only 16% on a 30 second cooldown. [24:29]
- The damage breakdown shows this build still deals a lot of gun damage, around 23% gun and 29% Power Surge in one game, and fights often last longer than 7.5 seconds. [24:29]
- Infuser stays situational: when many enemies are around and you are low, Infuser plus the ultimate still gives huge burst healing, so buy it when fights end fast, such as against burst heroes like Pocket and Lash. [26:33]
- Most meta heroes are tanky and do not one-shot you, so fights are about sustain and Leech fits better. [27:03]
- Once Leech is bought the core is done; with equal or higher net worth than the enemy (which Seven should have) you win any 1v1 and do massive team fight damage. [28:07]
- After the core, buy Spirit Resilience (usually first, since burst is what kills you), Bullet Resilience against Vyper or heavy gun teams, or both. [28:38]
- The build is not slot heavy; if short on slots sell Spirit Shielding first, then Enchanter's Emblem or Titanic Magazine. [29:09]
- Siphon Bullets works because the high fire rate keeps it permanently active like on auto-fire heroes (Ivy, Wraith, McGinnis); Superior Duration stretches its lifesteal to 26 seconds, often giving about 800 extra health. [29:40]
- Siphon Bullets pairs with the armors: late game the speaker reaches 4,000 to 5,000 HP with about 70% bullet and 66% spirit resist, so even when low he stops taking damage and heals back with Leech and Siphon Bullets. [30:42]
- Games usually end around 40k, but lower rank games run longer and the build scales: sell Enchanter's Emblem once resists are enough and buy Unstoppable against stuns, or Counterspell (about half the price) against something like a Lash ultimate. [32:15]
- For more damage and anti-heal late, buy Spirit Rend and Toxic Bullets (spirit shred, about 7 resist lost per headshot), or Crippling Headshot as a one-slot version that is a bit weaker but gives flat bullet and spirit resist reduction plus healing reduction. [32:45]
- Juggernaut is still great if you ultimate more and the enemy has a lot of gun damage, since it lowers the fire rate of everyone shooting you. [34:17]
- At around 50k, Lucky Shot is a luxury replacement for Titanic Magazine because it gives 40% extra ammo. [34:47]
- Spiritual Overflow is for raw damage when behind; the speaker likes it less on Seven because Power Surge is already always active and its ability duration only applies to the next cast, though the fire rate and spirit are good. [35:18]
- Weighted Shots instead of Spirit Rend or Toxic Bullets if you want more resists (16% debuff resist and 16% spirit resist) while staying aggressive. [35:49]
- Debuff Remover can be bought any time it is needed; Suppressor is rarely bought because it does not reach the next spirit threshold, only against many gun heroes or when you cannot afford Bullet Resilience. [36:19]
- Late items still land on the next investment threshold: Spirit Rend plus Toxic Bullets, two green armors, or Mercurial Magnum each reach the next step. [36:51]
- The build is aim intensive: in the old Shock build Power Surge procs if any one of the three burst bullets hits, but here you want every bullet to land, so if it feels weaker than the Shock build you are missing bullets. [37:54]
