# This Build Makes Abrams UNSTOPPABLE
channel: aquatiq

## Covers
Abrams, aquatiq, commentary over one game laning with Vindicta against Mo & Krill and a Venator (as captioned).
Ability rundown, item choices and some macro. Patch of February 2026.

## Claims
- He uses together's build, which he calls the only Abrams build you should use. [1:35]
- Get your 2 (Shoulder Charge) early so you can start playing; upgrade it so whenever your Vindicta stakes someone you can kill them. [2:06]
- Shoulder Charge appears to be parryable now. [2:06]
- Against a strong lane enemy who can throw you into a wall, play very safe early. [3:36]
- Battle Vest should have been his second item. [4:07]
- Abilities: 1 Siphon Life steals HP from the enemy, 2 Shoulder Charge, 3 a passive that regenerates you when you take damage (a bit like Shiv's Bloodletting), 4 Seismic Impact. [5:40]
- Use the ultimate as a movement ability to escape bad situations. [8:14]
- Shoulder Charge and Seismic Impact both count as movement abilities, and both stun. [8:14]
- Vindicta should stake a Mo & Krill whenever possible. [9:18]
- In lane he bought Melee Charge instead of Healing Booster because Abrams already has enough healing. [9:48]
- Melee Charge extends how far you can punch. [12:28]
- Healing minions saved him in lane, pick them up. [10:18]
- Mo & Krill always has around 2,000 HP. [11:58]
- Mina is ban-worthy: big damage from Love Bites and she can escape any situation unpunished. [12:58]
- Mina's ultimate throws bats and silences you, a problem in team fights. [22:41]
- Sinclair copies enemy ultimates; fine in normal games, not good competitively. [15:00]
- Taking the blue walker gives your team much more of the map than green or yellow would. [15:30]
- There are no set roles in Deadlock; you can read a hero's role from its abilities and model. [16:00]
- Be careful alone even on Abrams: the enemy can zipline to you and collapse with five. [18:06]
- Crushing Fists adds a lot of punch damage. [18:36]
- Clear the lane before fighting anything. [19:08]
- Boxes are free souls and nobody takes them. [20:08]
- Deliver the urn, then go straight to yellow. [20:41]
- Do not fight near the enemy's side, they rotate fast. [21:11]
- Buy Spirit Resilience when the enemy team has a lot of spirit damage. [21:11]
- Abrams works as parry bait so your team can kill the parrying enemy. [21:41]
- Hunter's Aura is a good item on Abrams in general; Phantom Strike lets you run people down. [22:11]
- Losing one walker to get the mid boss is probably fine. [23:12]
- Wait outside the mid boss pit ready to Shoulder Charge anyone who contests. [23:12]
- Do not dive in without your ultimate. [24:14]
- When you are up on souls, even if the enemy opens your base you win the defense. [25:46]
- Healing Tempo is really good: it heals and gives the target bonus fire rate and move speed. [26:16]
- Abrams's gun is surprisingly strong, so build a lot of gun plus survivability. [27:16]
