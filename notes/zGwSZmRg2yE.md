# How to "Win" Games With Incompetent Teammates | Phantom Warden Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2024-11-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zGwSZmRg2yE

## Covers
Midknight coaches a Phantom Warden aiming for Ascendant, laning with Viscous against Lash and Ivy, in a game lost to the team.
Themes: mirroring your lane partner's aggression, giving up wave priority against roamers, soul splitting with teammates under the then-new soul rules, bridge buffs and Urn, headshots, using Last Stand more, debuff items.
Late-2024 video (patch of mid November 2024); the game has changed a lot since, so soul-split numbers, Urn rules and items may no longer hold.

## Claims
Player (Warden, Phantom):
- In games with bad teammates, focus on your own mistakes and use the game to improve. [0:00]
- When your partner fights above you, join it by jumping up or backing up and shooting. [0:30]
- Playing a lane passively is fine if laning is your weakest part, but in solo queue mirror your lane partner: if he sits back, play passive; if he keeps going up and getting traded, push up and trade with him. [1:00]
- Hollow Point Ward with two regen items is a good defensive lane build. [1:31]
- Permanently giving up wave priority against a roaming lane like Lash and Ivy is dangerous: Ivy can hold the tower alone with Kudzu Bomb while Lash roams mid or the other lanes, and later Lash can ult you to another lane. [1:31] [2:01]
- With the wave stuck under your tower you get no chance to do the medium camp or boxes. [2:31]
- Punish an Ivy who goes too deep by walking in behind her. [2:31]
- When you get wave priority, do a box run and the medium camp on your lane. [3:01]
- Forcing the wave into Lash denies him from roaming. [3:33]
- Before 8 minutes souls are fully duplicated between duo laners (after 8 minutes, 70% each), so only last hit and give your partner time to reach lane; do not collect waves alone in a duo lane. [3:33]
- Slowing Hex is great against Lash, Pocket and Abrams, denying their dashes and Pocket's teleport. [4:05]
- Back off when you cannot hit the tower and Ivy can stall with one button while they rotate to you. [4:35]
- Grabbing a wave alone is fine when no teammate is near. [5:05]
- Head for the bridge buffs as soon as they spawn (the first one comes with the mid boss spawn noise); they are valuable. [5:05]
- When both heroes are on flat ground and the target is not jumping, aim for the head, especially on Warden, who does a lot of headshot damage; with verticality changes it is fine not to. [5:05] [5:35]
- Grab the Urn when behind to deny it; on the latest patch the first Urn spawns at the neutral spot, so leaving it lets them run it and force a favorable fight. [5:35] [6:06]
- Before taking a crashing wave, check the map and wait for a teammate who is coming; sharing gives extra souls (40% at the time). Retrain the old habit that splitting waves was bad. [6:06]
- Snap to the head once an enemy is in your cage. [6:36]
- Keep game audio up and watch the map around you while farming so you do not arrive 5 seconds late to fights. [7:06] [7:36]
- When you have one walker left, always push out that side's wave and ping or double-click the walker to call a defense. [7:36]
- A free Urn in your base can bring you back about 10K. [8:07]
- Do not walk into Pocket's suitcase; when he comes out he takes a tick of your ult, so back out and keep him at the edge of Last Stand. [8:37]
- Do not join a 6v5 fight when already down three players and 20K souls; let them die and play your own game. [9:08]
- Using Last Stand three times in a 25 to 30 minute game is too few; it does a lot of DPS. Ult in lane and early to get picks, use it like a normal ability, spamming it is better than underusing it. [9:38] [10:08]
- Buy Debuff Reducer or Debuff Remover against Pocket ult, Vindicta tether, Mo & Krill, Ivy stun, Lash fire rate slow and Abrams. [10:08] [10:39]
- Lash's fire rate slow hurts a fire-rate-based hero like Warden. [10:39]
- With Debuff Remover, you can usually parry Abrams before his melee after his dash if he has Duration Extender but not Superior Duration, unless his model is inside yours. [10:39] [11:09]
- Two enemies split from their team is the chance to jump them; Majestic Leap would allow the leap ult tech. [11:09]

Summary notes:
- Mirror your solo queue lane partner's aggression. [11:40]
- Do not play super passive against Lash; he loves to roam with lane priority and one-shot your other duo lane from mid. [11:40]
- Many Wardens buy Headhunter, which makes headshots even more valuable. [12:12]
- If you use your Warden ult only every 8 minutes or so, use it off cooldown to train the habit, or buy Majestic Leap for the leap ult tech. [12:42]
