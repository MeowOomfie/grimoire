## Covers
Lady Geist, aquatiq, commentary over one game with a hybrid bomb and gun build, against The Doorman, Bebop, Shiv and Billy.
The speaker is not a regular Geist player; covers the build idea, ability maxing, item choices against heals and high resist, and ultimate risk.
No coaching. Recorded 2026-08-08.

## Claims
- Build idea: a hybrid Geist that is a gun build at heart, with lots of Essence Bomb and Malice used to amp your gun damage. [2:11]
- Life Drain feels like a hard counter to a Doorman walking up to you, though the speaker is not sure. [2:11]
- Mystic Expansion as the third item makes you hit your bombs almost 100% of the time unless the enemy dodges. [3:12]
- The speaker calls the Mystic Expansion bomb "so stupid" for how well it hits. [6:23]
- Consider Healing Booster really early. [4:45]
- For the ultimate you want to be low HP, but being low is risky: a Doorman ult followed by a Bebop bomb kills you instantly. [6:53]
- The speaker hates Geist's gun but loves Life Drain. [6:53]
- Max Essence Bomb, because it lets you farm very well. [8:23]
- The speaker wonders if he should have maxed Life Drain first instead, but likes bomb max for farming. [10:36]
- Item plan: a cooldown item, then rush Spellslinger (as captioned) to see if it is worth it. [9:32]
- Buy cooldown items to spam Essence Bomb and Malice as much as possible, and unlock the extra charges upgrade. [11:41]
- After Spellslinger, consider Kinetic Dash and Spiritual Overflow; Toxic Bullets is a fair shout against this comp. [15:20]
- Buy Toxic Bullets against an annoying Shiv to burn him more; Berserker fits too. [20:39]
- Berserker pays off while you are taking damage. [22:58]
- Debuff Remover is a good buy against Shiv or Infernus; with it you will not die to Shiv. [22:58]
- Against a team with a lot of resist, Spirit Rend (as captioned) is necessary and a really good item; buy it before Spiritual Overflow. [25:10]
- When ahead, push up but play safe and farm; enemies walking up on you will get caught out eventually. [25:44]
- Seven teammate: press Unstoppable at the right time. [27:19]
