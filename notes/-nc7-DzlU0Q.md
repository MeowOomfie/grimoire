## Covers
Midknight and coach peercontent review an Eternus player's first Rem game. Rem's 3 (helpers on troopers) for lane pressure and macro, box and subway/tunnel farming routes, using the 1 (pillow) on waves, staying attached with the 2, front line to back line jump order.
Ability point order and itemization (Guardian Ward, Healing Booster, Reactive Barrier, Torment Pulse, Slowing Hex, Compressed Cooldown vs Extra Charge), plus a bad Mid Boss call. For mid to high rank Rem players.

## Claims
- Rem gets heavily punished for mispositioning when his 2 is off cooldown. [0:00]
- First use of the 3: if your lane is super oppressive, wait for minions to spawn and put the 3 on a trooper in a different lane; it doesn't cost that lane souls and gives them pressure. [0:30]
- Example: against a Yamato or Haze that keeps you under tower, putting the 3 on another lane's wave gives that lane pressure while you sack your own lane a bit and wait for the wave. [1:00]
- Second use of the 3: put it on your healing trooper so your wave is significantly stronger than theirs, and fight them while the healing trooper is going to win minion pressure. [1:00]
- How to put the 3 on another lane at the start: drop off the zipline instantly, wait in base for minion waves to spawn, toss it on the wave while they're on the zip, then take your zip to your assigned lane. [1:00]
- Most times you will lose pressure on wave one doing this, so don't be afraid of that. [1:00]
- In general Rem uses his 3 to get lane pressure and looks for boxes. [1:31]
- Don't put the 3 on your own lane if the lane beside you is a super strong lane (example Yamato and Mina); put it on their healing minion to make them even stronger, since under tower against that duo you aren't getting out anyway. [1:31]
- In a scrim setting Rem is played toward the stall lane: give wave priority with the 3 and permanently run boxes, with a partner like Ivy who can hold the wave. [2:01]
- Rem's 1 (pillow) refunds cooldown when it hits a minion wave, not only heroes (4 seconds reduced cooldown), but one pillow reduces at most 4 seconds no matter how many minions it hits. [2:31]
- You can hit the wave with the 1 to refund your 2 cooldown; it doesn't need to hit an enemy hero. [3:04]
- When you have wave priority, start looking toward boxes. [3:04]
- Parrying after an Abrams dash misses is a good habit. [3:04]
- After winning a fight in lane, take the enemy's basement or subway route. [3:34]
- Subway route: run the standard subway, but don't hit the three boxes at the back yourself; throw your 3 to farm those, then take the teleport, teleport again, grab the boxes in shop, hop in the vent (left side) and keep grabbing boxes. [3:34]
- The full extended route is a little slow; if pressed for time skip the extension, since it adds only a few boxes. [4:04]
- When the lane enemy dies early, go for boxes; towers barely take damage that early and he'll be back soon. [4:35]
- The very long subway route needs a lane partner who can solo hold a lane (like Yamato); with the extra 4 m/s movement speed in sewers it is faster than it feels and you can be back as your wave crashes. [5:06]
- Sacking full wave priority for the route doesn't matter much as long as it's before 5 minutes. [5:36]
- Guardian Ward rush is better than a spirit item rush (Midknight's opinion), and its movement speed helps box farming. [5:36]
- Throw the pillow on the wave to clear it; using it for harass poke is also fine. [6:06]
- Midknight doesn't really buy Nurse on Rem. [6:06]
- Midknight's three early green items for a health spike: Guardian Ward, Healing Booster and Enchanter's, with Reactive Barrier in reactive games. [6:06]
- Torment Pulse is good on Rem but not bought for the early spike. [7:07]
- Extra Charge is good because the second pillow refunds more of your 2 cooldown; some builds take Compressed Cooldown on the 1 instead for more casts. [7:07]
- Using the 3 on the minion you are sitting on is good. [7:07]
- You only need to stay on an ally for 3 seconds for maximum regen, since the regen duration is only 3 seconds; then hop off and hit tower. [7:07]
- A utility purple item would be much better than what he bought at that point. [7:37]
- Get duration early so you can stay on your front liner with your 2 longer. [7:37]
- Torment Pulse rush is good when you are attached to a front liner like Shiv. [8:07]
- When you see the enemies being aggressive or rotating, play defensive; when ganking, path aggressively unless you are mirroring an enemy rotation, so you come out behind them. [8:40]
- On Rem the gank is simple: tell the front liner to go forward and throw your 1 into your 2. [9:10]
- With Torment Pulse you want to be inside enemies, so stay attached to your front liner for the maximum duration possible. [9:10]
- If you are leaving a lane, toss your 3 on that wave's healing trooper first to give them the advantage in shoving. [9:41]
- Use vents to rotate rather than walking on the surface; there is one under the enemy tower and one in the secret shop. [9:41]
- Do not put one point in every ability; points go into your 3 (more helpers) instead of the ult. [10:13]
- Rem can buy from the enemy shop using helpers (likely to be patched), which is very nice when guardians are up. [10:44]
- Extra helpers are very strong in every fight: put your 3 on everyone, especially with the Torment Pulse build, to buff your whole team. [11:14]
- After you dash out, ult quickly to give yourself the peel, especially since the ult has damage reduction. [11:14]
- Restorative Locket is not that good right now and is very situational (against Warden or ability spammers); it is never a core item on Rem. [11:44]
- Replace Restorative Locket with Healing Booster: go Guardian Ward then Healing Booster, especially with a spirit build. [12:14]
- Ability point order (both coaches): 3 first, then 2, then 1, then another point in 3; then max 3, then max 2, then max ult, in that order. No ult points until 3 and 2 are maxed. [12:14]
- The ult points are only okay; if your ult keeps getting cancelled (for example by a Holliday) consider them, but mostly just position better. [12:44]
- Rem loves any game where he can buy Reactive Barrier; his spirit scales the Reactive shield, so Guardian Ward, Healing Booster and Reactive Barrier are perfect items into a team with lots of CC. [13:14]
- Being ult-stunned off a hero might proc Reactive Barrier (untested; later an ult knocked him off without stunning, so this may be wrong). [13:46]
- Use the 3 more: put it on the healing trooper to shove waves as you walk by, use it to steal enemy centers, box max, and throw it on almost every wave. [14:16]
- When you die or reset, always throw the 3 on your weak side's wave to give that side more pressure. [14:47]
- On Urn fights, throw the 3 on the side opposite the Urn so that side has pressure, then rotate to the Urn. [14:47]
- Think of Rem as giving a macro advantage every time he respawns or reaches a minion wave. [14:47]
- Their Mid Boss call while behind was bad and decided the game; they got annihilated. [15:17]
- Comeback souls are overtuned, which is why teams want to play from behind. [15:47]
- Rusted Barrel is extremely good on Rem. [15:47]
- Slowing Hex is the standard pick; in about 80% of games go Slowing Hex, and Duration Extender only if it isn't a Slowing Hex game. [16:17]
- Compressed Cooldown nets more cooldown than Extra Charge, and you build toward Transcendent Cooldown anyway; always have four actives. [16:47]
- Because the 1 gets its refund off minions, Compressed Cooldown is more valuable than saving charges for heroes. [16:47]
- Only buy Extra Charge as a tempo buy for immediate lane pressure, or to defend and clear a wave as fast as possible so they don't kill your tower. [16:47]
- Put the 3 on the healing trooper, not the melee trooper: it is ranged and has more stats, and it both heals and gets healed, so in an even lane none of your minions die. [17:17]
- Setting a lane: buff one minion so your wave pushes to their side; if they don't address it you can fight on the opposite side of the map or take an objective. [17:47]
- You can place the 3 on troopers through the zipline, so you can set waves from your base. [18:18]
- Maxing 2 before 3 feels bad. [18:18]
- Echo Shard or Scourge is probably better than Boundless Spirit on Rem, since he already has one built in; Scourge for front line Torment Pulse builds. [18:48]
- The two Boundless Spirit effects likely add as +30% spirit rather than multiplying (untested). [19:19]
- With no other raw spirit power, buying Boundless Spirit as your first tier four also spikes your other investments a lot. [19:49]
- Don't farm your own carry's farm run; box out instead, or you steal farm from teammates (he also lost a lot of HP to that camp). [20:51]
- If you have no time, just throw the 3 on the healer trooper: that wave will always win because of it. [21:21]
- Alchemical Fire is good on a double spirit build. [21:21]
- Echo Shard is good on the Torment Pulse build (Echo Shard your 2). [21:51]
- Not having Slowing Hex is a mistake. [21:51]
- Use your pillow for wave clear when there is no fight; skipping it let the wave hurt them. [21:51]
- Rem automatically gets the full buffs; rarely, when summoning multiple helpers, one of the buffs is missed. [22:21]
- In a fight, look to use your 2 on teammates who are making space for you. [22:51]
- Rem has 10% crit resist (the hat). [23:22]
- Rem should not be the one shoving side waves; with this build stick with your front liner (Shiv), and if a side wave needs shoving, toss a 3 on it and play base with your team. [23:53]
- Good default on Rem: start every fight jumping to the front line and end it jumping to the back line, so you finish in a safer position. [24:24]
- He positioned too aggressively, especially without his 2 up, front lining for his team and even on the opposite side of the fight from his Shiv. [25:26]
- Fix side waves with the 3 on troopers. [26:06]
- There is a graphic of all vents on the map pinned in Midknight's Discord. [27:07]

- Use helpers to buy instead of walking into the secret shop, where an Abrams hiding in the veil will kill you. [27:38]
- Biggest issue: he wasn't playing like a support but like a Yamato, permanently front lining with Torment Pulse. [28:08]
- Greater Expansion is extremely late core or luxury; don't buy it right after duration. Duration matters because it lets you stay on people longer. [28:39]
- On the Torment Pulse build your 2 should not come off before the fight ends. [28:39]
- Too many free item slots and zero tempo items. [29:40]
- He farmed his own side instead of the enemy's when he had pressure; with that lead he should have played on their side with his front liner. [30:14]
- Squishy builds that stay with the front line otherwise need Warp Stone, which feels bad since you want four better actives; start front, end back and abuse the jump. [30:44]
