# Reviewing my Game as the #1 Lash
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-04-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gg7_0yLOICs

## Covers
A rank one Lash reviews his own 32-minute Eternus 6 game (good, not dominant) to show real-time thinking and build decisions.
Hybrid gun Lash build (Headshot Booster, Quicksilver Reload, Recharging Rush, Siphon Bullets, Transcendent Cooldown, Divine Barrier), lane mentality, power spikes, ult advantage, ganking after pushing, stealing centers, the 15-minute comeback urn, mid boss and Rejuvenator use.
For Lash players and anyone who wants to convert a lead into a win.

## Claims
- Review games where your performance was normal, not ones where you went 15 and 0. [0:00]
- On Lash he does not go into lane super aggressive because Lash is not that strong early; the focus is clearing the wave, getting denies and finding an edge, using Flog whenever there is a decent position. [0:30]
- Track the enemy's ammo: if someone used most of their ammo clearing the wave, run at them for a very favorable trade while they reload. It is hard to exploit because they can slide to extend the mag. [1:00]
- He buys Headshot Booster as the first item because it lets Lash pressure the opponent the most. [1:31]
- Jumping then air dashing lets you parry at the same time, so you can parry while moving; it catches people who do not expect a parry. [2:01]
- After getting a lead (damage plus a Wraith kill) he goes straight to the enemy small camp, takes only the two troopers he can clear, then does boxes while the enemy pushes the wave in. [2:31]
- Even with a big soul advantage do not be aggressive all the time; Deadlock ebbs and flows, so play for your next power spike instead. [3:01]
- Lash's next early spike is Extra Spirit plus Mystic Burst and the second point in Flog. [3:31]
- Use your cooldowns every time an opportunity appears, and punish an enemy laner who is alone. [3:31]
- With a huge advantage you do not need to full commit; an enemy left on very low HP must go back to base or play weak, so keep pressuring the tower instead of chasing. [4:02]
- The aim is to keep the enemies hurt and the wave shoved so that something eventually cracks: they either go back to base or make a mistake. [4:32]
- Missing his Ground Strike cost a Lady Geist kill; that was his mistake. [4:32]
- After taking the tower he had 1,600 souls and bought Quicksilver Reload right away; with two points in Flog and Quicksilver Reload he felt strong enough to run at Lady Geist. [5:33]
- When you have ult advantage, use it: Geist (3.6k souls) had no ult while he (4.1k) and his Mo & Krill had theirs. The window is small, about one wave, before they get their ults back. [5:33]
- The fight failed because he and his Mo & Krill were not on the same target; Mo got parried alone. He still stands by the principle. [6:03]
- An advantage in souls is much smaller without an ult disparity; it matters what the souls give you, here the ultimates. [6:34]
- One ally death gave Geist assist souls that unlocked her ult immediately, which led to a double kill; small interactions have big effects at high level. [6:34]
- He went back to lane because the enemies were still very hurt and overextended, to secure trade kills and keep the lead. [7:04]
- A common mistake is winning lane and then doing nothing; usually you should be active on the map. [7:04]
- Push the wave before ganking: a failed gank then costs almost nothing because you just go back mid, defend the tower or turn it into a box route. The risk-to-reward is heavily in favor of ganking. [7:34]
- Pattern: push the wave, then gank the lane where enemies are half HP and their tower is being pushed. [8:06]
- When you are strong and your lane went well, your impact must not stay in your own lane. [8:36]
- Caveat: farming heroes like Wraith or Infernus should focus on scaling, farming efficiently and getting the centers; for any other hero ganking is possible, on more heroes than people think. [8:36]
- When centers spawn and you are strong, think about stealing the enemy's centers, not just securing your own; call it to your team when you hear enemies on them. [9:06]
- People farming centers are often hurt, so killing them while they farm is ideal; his play put Wraith 2,000 souls behind him. [9:06]
- Spend all your money after a pickup so you never hold a big bag; he bought Recharging Rush, a 1,600 item, to spend as much as possible (Mystic Shot was the alternative). [9:36]
- After pushing the mid wave he feels comfortable ganking in these situations all the time. [10:06]
- Max Flog is a strong window, so be as active as possible and join as many kills as you can then; ability points come more slowly later, so the next maxed ability takes a while. [10:36]
- He bought an early Healbane against Abrams, Lady Geist, Warden and Ivy, many heroes that heal; he called it pretty mandatory there. [10:36]
- With the team up 4,000 souls he took the urn, but he did not force it in past Abrams and Holliday who were watching him. [11:08]
- Reading two enemies mid and two left told him it was a 2v2 near the urn, so he waited on a rooftop for his team to come from mid for a numbers advantage rather than risk delivering the urn to the enemy. [11:39]
- When both of his towers were being pushed and he could not get there in time, he pushed the enemy guardian instead. [12:09]
- In retrospect he should have ulted the Warden (11,000 souls); it would likely have been a kill and a worthwhile ult. [12:40]
- When channeling Lash's ult, if the enemies have no stun or knockdown, always keep channeling unless the target is about to go around a corner, because you might catch someone else. At 300 to 400 games he did not do this and missed extra people. [13:11]
- The moment you have enough for your next item, buy it; he held souls basically zero times this game. [13:11]
- Using grapple forward rather than backward can help you dodge enemies when escaping. [13:41]
- His skirmishing happens only when the map shows an advantage (Mo & Krill ult up, numbers, team up 10,000 souls); look to start something but do not full commit. [14:11]
- Go aggressive on enemies who are farming so they get no free resources; his Wraith kill started from going at a farming Ivy. [14:41]
- With an Abrams kill and only two enemies showing elsewhere, he felt confident to push the right walker. [14:41]
- He was fine dying for the walker if he was the only one dying, but souls-wise dying for a walker at that point was not optimal; the walker gives map pressure, opens that side and a flex slot. [15:11]
- The urn is a comeback urn when the losing team is 10% of the winning team's net worth behind: at 90,000 souls the gap must be 9,000. [16:13]
- When the urn is close to becoming a comeback urn, pick it up as fast as possible; one kill a second later would have flipped this one. [16:43]
- If the urn becomes a comeback urn you either wait or deliver it on their side, which is their best chance to flip the game. [16:43]
- The 15-minute urn is one of the most important urns in the game, especially at high level, because it either snowballs your lead or lets the enemy come back. [17:14]
- Run at an Ivy who goes for denies so she cannot deny. [17:14]
- One of his ults in that fight was a mistake and should not have been used. [17:46]
- Always reset the high ground between combos on Lash; many Lash players do not. [17:46]
- Recharging Rush plus two points in grapple gives many grapples, so this build moves around far more than other Lash builds. [17:46]
- Siphon Bullets was his next item because the enemy had high HP-gain heroes (Abrams, Lady Geist, Warden); against Warden at 3k health it does around 75 damage per hit. [18:16]
- Fire rate items work at roughly half effectiveness on burst-fire guns like Lash's; it is not written anywhere but is real. To scale Lash's gun, scale weapon damage, shred, or buy Siphon Bullets, which is not affected. [18:47]
- Lash's gun does little damage by itself; much of it is carried by two points in his second ability. [19:18]
- Solo kills give a lot of souls. [19:18]
- Do not Ground Strike into a Warden with max cage, because he cages you and you cannot grapple away; harass and back up instead. [19:18]
- Going back to base to spend while hurt was arguable; he may have been better off using the side shop and staying active. [19:48]
- With a 15,000 soul lead at 18:42 the main thing is to find an angle to take mid boss, since the next urn will be a comeback urn for the enemy. [19:48]
- Any severely favorable fight is a chance for mid boss; even one or two of the right kills can be enough. [20:19]
- Siphon Bullets is very good against Warden, so he is comfortable fighting him. [20:49]
- Sometimes do not Ground Strike right away after the ult, because enemies use abilities that are problematic; against Ivy wait for Stone Form, then Ground Strike. Against Warden and Holliday you can Ground Strike right away. [21:20]
- Mid boss is the most important objective in the game; if you can get it you should, even with buffs and urn spawning in 8 seconds. Knowing when it is possible comes with experience. [21:50]
- Stagger your parry at mid boss: many players parry right away, so wait for the first round of parries, then parry to catch late melees. [22:21]
- After mid boss he cleared the wave before picking up the urn only because the enemies were mostly dead and his team had Rejuvenator; otherwise prioritize the urn, the enemy's best comeback path. [22:51]
- With an ally dead, do not rush the urn in, but keep it on that side for map pressure without being so forward that enemies can take it off you. [23:22]
- Transcendent Cooldown pairs with Siphon Bullets: cooldown is good on Lash and it makes Siphon about 25% stronger. [23:22]
- The long chase on Holliday was only possible because of Recharging Rush grapples; he does not recommend it. [23:53]
- With one Rejuvenator left, base guardians are the bare minimum; one shrine is good but do not force it. [24:23]
- He overextended deep, got Wraith ulted and caged, and died; he should have focused on the primary objective, the shrine. [24:54]
- He bought Divine Barrier as a greedy option to save himself or allies (Vindicta, Haze) and because it has a short cooldown with Transcendent Cooldown; the 100% safe buy is Unstoppable against Wraith ult, Ivy Stone Form, Phantom Strike or Abrams charge. [25:25]
- Up 20,000 to 25,000 souls, always think about the next objective and its timer. [25:56]
- Looking for kills is fine but think about the bigger picture: with buffs in 20 seconds and mid boss in 2:16, his team held the right buff, so he farmed a camp to afford his item. [26:26]
- The urn spawns on yellow at even times (10, 20, 30 minutes) and on green at odd times (15, 25, 35). [26:56]
- Up 23,000 souls you should normally win a fight, so do not force the urn but be willing to take a good fight; take safe pathing with the urn. [27:26]
- He played from the minimap, not dropping the urn until he was confident in the fight. [27:56]
- With mid boss spawning in 30 seconds the fight mattered more than the urn; losing it would have lost mid boss and the game despite the lead. [27:56]
- He told his team to fight outside because Lash cannot fight in that inside spot; do not force fights in bad places, wait and the opportunity often comes to you on Lash. Enemies chasing outside walked into his ult and the Haze ult. [28:27]
- Staggered parries at mid boss would also have stopped the enemy Holliday taking a Rejuvenator. [29:27]
- Rejuvenator lasts 4 minutes, so use all 4 minutes: it passively gives map control because enemies avoid fights against you and stay in base. [29:27]
- With no farm on the map, think one step ahead instead of pushing their base right away: with the urn in 40 seconds the plan is get the urn and buffs, spend all money, then push. [29:57]
- He held his ult channel the whole time because the enemies had no stun, and it got him an extra person. [31:30]
- Two of his four deaths came from running into the enemy spawn; do not do that, do what wins for your team, and with Rejuvenator the game should have been a guaranteed end. [31:30]
