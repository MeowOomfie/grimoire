# The BEST Seven Build In Deadlock - META Build Update
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2025-02-16  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7PMXTWcRLEc

## Covers
Seven hybrid Power Surge build (Surge of Power, Infuser, Cold Front, Echo Shard), skill order, lane combo, Storm Cloud knock-back trick, farming, Walkers, team fights, Haze, Mo & Krill

## Claims
- After the last patch Seven is top three in almost every rank and one of the hardest carries; the change is that you can cast the ultimate and then the Thunderbolt immediately, which gives huge burst. [0:00]
- Most players build Seven as a farmer: level the ball early, get Rapid Recharge, farm with the ball and come out of the jungle around 25 minutes; this build plays him as a hard carry that kicks in from the laning stage. [0:31]
- Level Lightning Ball first at level 1 because it deals good damage to the enemy in lane, pushes creep waves and secures last hits. [1:02]
- At level 2 take Static Charge for the stun and immediately put a second level in it for the lower cooldown, so you win the lane easily. [1:02]
- Seven is an insane laner and good in solo lane; at level 1 he regenerates about 8 health a second, which the speaker thinks is the highest base regen in the game. [1:33]
- One point in Lightning Ball gives a second ball charge; the extra Static Charge level basically halves the cooldown so the stun is ready whenever you need it. [2:03]
- Power Surge is then levelled all the way to level 5; in between the speaker usually puts one point in Storm Cloud so it is more useful early, which you can skip. [2:03]
- The idea is to make Power Surge insane early with Surge of Power for damage and farming speed, then level the ultimate, then Static Charge; it is a hybrid build, not an ultimate build, with most power from Power Surge early and mid game. [2:35]
- The longer the game goes and the more high level items you have, the more value Storm Cloud gets, giving a big late game power spike; the late levels also make the stun better. [3:06]
- Only throw the ball for farming, or in an engagement after you stun and chase someone down, until the very late game. [3:06]
- Rush orange items early in lane: Rapid Rounds first, because Seven's base fire rate is low and slow to farm with, so it makes him more responsive and raises damage a lot. [3:36]
- Then Hollow Point Ward and Basic Magazine; Basic Magazine is close to a must because the base magazine is small, and with it you can one-clip an enemy without reloading, since the build runs no reload items. [3:36]
- Buy Extra Regen when you need it; it lets you play more aggressively and tank everything the enemy throws at you in lane. [4:06]
- Infuser is the key to carrying the lane: it extends Power Surge duration (duration scales with spirit), raises Power Surge damage, and most importantly gives Storm Cloud burst and lifesteal through its 20% spirit lifesteal. [4:06]
- Lane combo once you have the ultimate: pop Infuser, pop your other cooldowns, then ultimate on the enemy before Infuser runs out; it is one of the highest burst combos of any hero early. [4:37]
- Up to about 3k souls, before the ultimate, Seven is not very lethal but still trades well because of his regen; once he has the ultimate, run in, stun, take two shots and finish the combo inside Infuser's 8 seconds. [5:38]
- Always pop Infuser before Power Surge to get the extra damage and duration. [5:38]
- Because of his regen Seven rarely loses a lane matchup and is one of the best laners in the game. [6:09]
- Extra Spirit is bought early and later upgraded to Improved Spirit and Boundless Spirit; Extra Stamina is the best 500 item in the game. [6:09]
- Ammo Scavenger is a good option because extra spirit is valuable on Seven, but this build skips it to reach the mid game core items faster. [6:42]
- The first mid game item, usually near the end of lane, is Cold Front; with Infuser and Power Surge popped it clears creep waves and tier 1 and tier 2 camps very fast; it is not needed for tier 3 camps. [6:42]
- In fights, stun first, then once the enemy is stunned and you are close, drop the ball and Cold Front to slow them more, then ultimate. [7:43]
- Advanced trick: cast the Storm Cloud second cast (the thunder strike) behind a fleeing enemy to knock them back into the ultimate, which raises your total ultimate damage. [7:43]
- Enchanter's Barrier works well because Seven wants early spirit and Power Surge gains a lot of damage and duration from it. [8:44]
- Soul Shredder Bullets add Power Surge damage when you shoot the enemy and give lifesteal against one target. [8:44]
- Surge of Power is the big power spike, and buying it at 3k lines up with the point where you can max Power Surge; at level 3 Power Surge damage and spirit scaling jump a lot. [9:15]
- From Surge of Power on, Seven is the fastest farmer in the game and can farm tier 3 camps faster than any other hero. [9:46]
- With Infuser and Enchanter's Barrier, Power Surge lasts around 20 seconds against a 33 second cooldown, so it is up almost all the time from early in the game. [10:17]
- From this point, as long as you have as much farm as the enemy or even a bit less, you win most fights including 1v1s: stun, use cooldowns for burst, and if you are scared of the enemy, pop the ultimate for damage and healing. [10:47]
- Fleetfoot adds movement speed; Seven's movement speed scales directly with spirit, and Fleetfoot lets him shoot without a movement penalty, so it is easy to get in and out of fights and chase targets to stun. [11:17]
- Do not actively look for fights; Seven is still a farmer, so clear all waves as fast as possible, push toward the enemy Walkers and clear tier 3 camps to snowball. [11:49]
- Avoid solo pushing Walkers: unlike Wraith, Seven has no escape and since buildings take 50% less spirit damage his Walker damage is low; only push when it is safe, the enemy is busy, or teammates are nearby. [12:19]
- Think of Seven as a team fighter who excels with someone in front of him; he can win 1v1s but should take them passively while farming. [12:51]
- If a Haze comes out of invisibility to ultimate you, stun, pop Fleetfoot and kite out of the ultimate radius, wait for them to be stunned, then go back in and ultimate. [13:21]
- Spirit Lifesteal gives consistent healing in the ultimate against every enemy when Infuser is on cooldown, while Soul Shredder Bullets only heal off the one target you shot. [13:53]
- If you have no flex slots yet, the first item to sell is Rapid Rounds or Hollow Point Ward. [14:23]
- Next Mystic Vulnerability to reduce enemy spirit resist, then Healbane, then Improved Spirit upgraded from Extra Spirit; sell Extra Regen only if you must. [14:23]
- The build is cheap early to mid game, but Seven needs a lot of money to be scary, so aim to be at or near the top net worth on your team. [14:53]
- Late game, Spiritual Overflow comes first: once procced it gives more spirit and fire rate for 18 seconds, enough to use all abilities and still ultimate with 50 extra spirit. [15:25]
- Then Mystic Slow (it can also come first): Power Surge AoE and the ultimate apply spirit damage to everyone nearby, so gun heroes trying to shoot you down in the ultimate lose 40% fire rate and you outheal them. [15:55]
- Skill order after maxing Power Surge: max the ultimate (more channel time, radius and damage, and you can move during it), then max Static Charge. [16:56]
- Around this point, if you lack a flex slot, sell Infuser if you shoot the gun a lot, or Rapid Rounds if you use the ultimate more. [16:56]
- Once Static Charge is near max, buy Echo Shard and use it on the stun: stun, wait a moment, stun again, chaining a 1.8 second stun into about 4 seconds on the ground for everyone hit. [17:26]
- Boundless Spirit is great because every Seven ability scales very well with spirit; the ultimate has a 0.7 spirit multiplier. [18:26]
- With Spiritual Overflow procced, Power Surge has only about a second of downtime, so Duration Extender is valuable but not necessary in this build. [18:57]
- Improved Reach on the stun gives it a big AoE that can chain stun the entire enemy team for about 4 seconds, like an ultimate in itself. [19:58]
- Unstoppable is situational against heroes with knockdowns or stuns that can pull you out of the ultimate, such as Mo & Krill, but never a wrong late game buy; Leech is good as the upgrade from Healbane. [20:28]
- Situational: Superior Cooldown cuts the ultimate cooldown from about 130 to 90 seconds; Diviner's Kevlar for more spirit and tankiness; Spellslinger Headshots as a weak luxury that builds from Soul Shredder Bullets; Superior Duration on the stun for a longer stun. [20:58]
- Late game, buy Improved Bullet Armor or Improved Spirit Armor depending on enemy damage; sell Enchanter's Barrier or Extra Stamina, which is not needed late because Seven already moves so fast. [22:03]
- Seven is one of the hardest carries in the game, especially in ranked solo queue. [22:34]
