# They Patched This Already?! | Deadlock Patch Notes Breakdown
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-09-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ywC2b6uFS6A

## Covers
Deathy reads the Deadlock patch released just before 2025-09-05 (the video names no exact date), with his verdict on each change.
Covers the self-deny respawn penalty, Rejuvenator, Patron, hero changes (Abrams to Warden, with big ones to Holliday, Seven, Shiv, Sinclair, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous) and a long list of item changes.
For players who want to know what changed in the meta and which builds or heroes move up or down.

## Claims
- Rejuvenator now only heals the player who hits the crystal; before it healed everybody, and Deathy calls it a great, overdue fix. [1:01]
- Dying to a non-player when there is no bounty or assist now adds 10 seconds to that death's respawn time. [1:01]
- Denying yourself is not dead: early assists are worth about 200 souls, so it is still worth it in specific situations. [1:31]
- With the change, a self-deny with no assist is not worth it; deny yourself faster than before, for example to the tower right after the enemy gets a kill, so you stay in assist range and your death is not longer. [1:31]
- Early game you usually cannot afford a death that is 10 seconds longer. [2:01]
- Patron first death time is now a constant 12 seconds, fixing the old case where a downed Patron right above the pit dropped much faster; a great change. [2:01]
- Abrams Infernal Resilience goes from 12 to 13 (the usual 1% change); a slight buff he did not think Abrams needed. [2:31]
- Bebop is getting buffed; he was pretty weak, so the buffs are acceptable. [2:31]
- Billy gets +10 base health and 3 seconds off Rising Ram; this helps his laning and his scaling off tanky items, though Deathy thinks Billy was already one of the stronger new heroes. [3:02]
- Calico Gloom Bombs tier three goes from +2 to +3 bombs, essentially a 50% buff and very significant; he wants to test a Gloom Bombs build. [3:02]
- Calico Leaping Slash healing and damage scaling increased. [3:32]
- Calico Ava tier three increased, so maxing Ava instead of going Fortitude might be viable, though he is not convinced. [3:32]
- Calico ultimate cooldown is 10 seconds lower, back to where it was before several nerfs. [3:32]
- The Doorman Call Bell damage reduced by 10 with spirit scaling up 0.1, so you need about 100 spirit to get back to the old Call Bell; overall an early game nerf. [4:02]
- The Doorman Hotel Guest now teleports the target back to the start of the hotel; previously you could drop into the pit and Counterspell it, now you cannot. [4:02]
- Drifter health gain per boon drops from 41 to 39 and his weapon falloff is reduced; both are nerfs. [4:33]
- Drifter weapon damage gain per boon is reduced, meaning worse weapon scaling, and his tier three damage amp is reduced. [4:33]
- Drifter Rend now makes you briefly float in the air during the cast time; neither clearly a nerf nor a buff, perhaps a buff. [4:33]
- Grey Talon bullet damage growth from spirit increased (read as 07 to 08). [5:04]
- Grey Talon Spirit Snare curse duration goes from 2 to 2.25 seconds. [5:04]
- Grey Talon Spirit Snare tier two changed from curse duration to minus bullet resistance, and the curse duration moved to tier three: +1 second curse duration and +1.5 m radius, making the curse pretty long. [5:04]
- There may be funny Grey Talon builds that go Ability Range and Echo Shard and lay down a lot of traps. [5:35]
- Holliday base bullet damage reduced by 4, about a 17% nerf and massive for her DPS; bullet growth increased to compensate, so it is mostly a laning nerf and she needs a lot of boons to get back to her old level. [5:35]
- Holliday Powder Keg displacement duration reduced significantly, base damage and tier two reduced, charges reduced; spirit scaling increased but you need about 100 spirit to reach the old damage; tier three buffed for displacement but still worse than before. [6:06]
- Deathy calls the Holliday nerfs well deserved and thinks she will be in a much better spot. [6:36]
- Infernus loses 3 DPS on Flame Dash and 2 DPS on its tier two; a slight, deserved nerf to the Flame Dash build, which was doing really well in lower ranks. Infernus is mostly balanced. [6:36]
- Kelvin Frost Grenade gets a 2 second change; essentially not much of a change. [7:07]
- Mina Rake heal spirit scaling increased from 1 to 1.5, significant for her sustain in lane and later. [7:07]
- Mina Love Bites buildup takes one less shot and its proc damage is +10, both significant; Love Bites spirit scaling increased and her ultimate cooldown decreased. [7:07]
- Mina's buffs are to damage, not tankiness or survivability, which keeps her identity; Deathy loves it. [7:37]
- Mirage tier three Scarabs buffed by 10; fine, since nobody maxed them, they were underwhelming, and they were not what made Mirage oppressive. [7:37]
- Mo & Krill Combo gets a very small cast range change; it feels bad to get grabbed from a distance. [7:37]
- Paige barrier spirit power scaling buffed; she is decent but not overwhelmingly strong. [8:09]
- Paradox bullet damage growth very slightly nerfed; it will not impact her DPS much, but she was slightly too strong (he had her in S tier). [8:09]
- Seven move speed reduced and his crit resistance (the helmet) drops from 35% to 25%. [8:09]
- Seven tier three spirit power scaling reduced, Storm Cloud DPS spirit scaling reduced, and Storm Cloud initial radius goes from 12 to 10 meters. [8:40]
- Seven was one of the strongest carries, if not the strongest; the nerfs should bring him in line with carries like Infernus and Wraith. [8:40]
- Shiv Serrated Knives tier three goes from 37 to 40 damage and from +6.5 to +7; significant, since the knives already do a lot of damage, so expect more knife Shiv. [8:40]
- Shiv Slice and Dice tier three max cooldown reduction gets another 2 seconds, and Bloodletting cooldown drops another 5 seconds, which incentivizes building cooldown; both buffs to spirit Shiv. [9:12]
- Shiv Bloodletting deferred damage increased by 5%, which helps all Shiv builds. [9:12]
- Expect a resurgence of spirit Shiv; the changes may move Shiv from his C tier to B or even A tier. [9:43]
- Sinclair Spectral Assistant base damage nerfed again but spirit scaling buffed, so with lots of spirit it does more damage than before. [9:43]
- Sinclair Spectral Assistant tier three fire rate goes from +45% to +60%, very significant. [10:13]
- Sinclair Rabbit Hex tier three damage amp goes from +5% to +7%, a small buff. [10:13]
- Sinclair ultimate cooldown and duration both buffed, though the cooldown was already short. [10:13]
- Sinclair was perhaps slightly underperforming but good in good hands; expect builds around the assistant with lots of spirit, maybe adding gun items like Long Range that benefit from the fire rate, since 60% fire rate from an ability is nearly unheard of outside Ivy's tether with huge spirit. [10:43]
- Victor gets a small health nerf. [10:43]
- Victor Jumpstart recharge timer goes from 3 to 8 seconds, very significant, making Rapid Recharge much more mandatory on him. [11:13]
- Victor aura radius reduced by 1 m, and its cooldown after toggling off goes from 1 to 2.5 seconds; this might bring him in line. [11:13]
- Vindicta Stake no longer pulls you in if you were outside the tether range; capture range and tether range are now the same, so it no longer feels like being pulled in from outside the ability. [11:44]
- Vindicta Stake duration reduced by 0.25 seconds and its slow increased from 30% to 40%; the slow compensates a little, but overall Stake is nerfed. [11:44]
- Vindicta Flight spirit damage, duration, and tier two and tier three upgrades buffed significantly, so a Flight build may be the way for a more carry Vindicta. [12:15]
- Vindicta Crow reworked: shorter debuff and nerfed spirit scaling but more bleed per second, so more damage in a smaller window but worse at applying every debuff; its tier two cooldown was also effectively nerfed (read as -6 to -12). [12:15]
- Viscous alt fire damage brought from 63 to 48, matching the earlier Yamato change; he should be much less annoying in lane. [12:45]
- Viscous alt fire damage growth buffed, but it takes time to get back to baseline; overall a nerf, especially to the Express Shot build, which was oppressive and cheesy. [12:45]
- Vyper Screw Jab and dagger buffs, cooldown decreases and more spirit scaling; nearly everything in her kit slightly buffed. [13:15]
- Vyper minimum falloff range increased by 2 meters, so she does more damage when targets are not right in her face. [13:15]
- Vyper Petrifying Bola cooldown reduced by 20 seconds, which could make spirit Vyper viable again: go Shadow Weave and roam. [13:15]
- Warden base sprint speed reduced by 0.5, Alchemical Flask tier two nerfed and Last Stand spirit scaling slightly reduced; he was strong but did not need massive nerfs, since the gap between S tier and A tier heroes is not huge. [13:46]
- Weakening Headshot changes from -12 to -3 and its debuff duration increased; both good. [13:46]
- Backstabber cooldown increased and damage reduced; a good change. [14:17]
- Long Range now gives falloff range, which matters because falloff range works with on-hit effects like Siphon Bullets and Toxic Bullets; expect more Long Range, though it was already quite strong. It makes sense since it builds into Sharpshooter, which also gives falloff range. [14:17]
- Sharpshooter gets a slight buff. [14:17]
- Blood Tribute gets a slight buff (10% spirit resistance, active move speed 2.25); Deathy calls the item unbelievably stacked. [14:48]
- Headhunter bonus damage boon scaling increased slightly; it was a bit underwhelming. [14:48]
- Shadow Weave sprint speed increased significantly, so you feel much faster using it; a great change. [14:48]
- Capacitor now grants 8% fire rate and faster projectile speed; it needed a buff and should see more play. [14:48]
- Crippling Headshot gets a slight buff. [15:19]
- Ricochet no longer targets zipline targets, so you do not randomly down people on the zipline. [15:19]
- Spellslinger gets a slight duration buff. [15:19]
- Rebuttal now gives 30% bonus damage on a successful parry instead of 50% melee damage; barely a buff early, when you can already two-shot with melee after a parry, but later a high damage, low melee hero like Wraith can melt the parried target. A crazy buff to an already strong item. [15:19]
- Weapon Shielding is easier to proc, needing a little less damage taken. [15:49]
- Return Fire gets a little more innate bullet resistance; it had fallen out of favor and is hard to balance. [15:49]
- Restorative Locket now scales with boons instead of spirit, which favors build diversity; Deathy expects more items to move to boon scaling and predicts Reactive Barrier will eventually follow. [16:19]
- Guardian Ward and Divine Barrier get a slight cast range buff. [16:50]
- Veil Walker heal per boon and duration increased; the duration matters for ganks, especially multiplied by Duration Extender. [16:50]
- Healing Nova gets a slight buff; it is one of the most underbought items. [16:50]
- Metal Skin duration goes from 4 to 5 seconds; with Duration Extender it lasts about 6.3 to 6.4 seconds, an incredibly powerful answer to gun heroes. A Haze cannot damage you during it, so this change might kill Haze. [17:20]
- Counterspell duration goes from 1 to 0.9 seconds, a very slight nerf that seems more in line. [17:51]
- Trophy Collector gets another buff, but it still needs stacks and gives no stats on purchase, so 2,400 souls is a gamble: insane with quick kills, worthless when the game goes badly. [17:51]
- Cheat Death gets a very small cooldown reduction; with Transcendent Cooldown its 90 second cooldown drops to about 65 seconds, so you have it nearly every fight late game and it could become pretty good. [18:21]
- Witchmail spirit power bonus goes from 10 to 14; a good but situational item that should feel more impactful. [18:51]
- Siphon Bullets max HP steal goes from 55 to 35 and now scales per boon; rushing it at 8 minutes or buying it first will not do much, and it takes about 20 to 25 boons (around 20k souls, out of about 40 boons total) to get back to its old strength. [18:51]
- Siphon Bullets duration also nerfed by 3 seconds. [19:21]

- Rusted Barrel gets another very slight buff; it is close to a good item, maybe -7 instead of -6 would get it there. [19:52]
- Bullet Resist Shredder no longer gives melee resistance and gives bullet resistance instead; with no other item gaining it, this is overall a buff to melee damage. [19:52]
- Cold Front damage reduced by 25; it was a very powerful flat damage item, and the change gives more incentive to upgrade to Arctic Blast, which was not nerfed. [20:22]

- Spirit Sap spirit power reduction goes from 18 to 24, which is huge. [20:54]
- Silence Wave duration reduced but width increased, and it gets the spirit power reduction buff, making it more of an anti-spellcaster item. [20:54]
- Torment Pulse cooldown slightly reduced, roughly a 5% DPS increase. [20:54]
- Decay gets a small buff and its spirit scaling goes from 0.3 to 0.55, a very significant buff. [21:26]
- Magic Carpet gets a buff. [21:26]
- Focus Lens now deals its damage whenever the buff is removed, not only on expiry; this fixes the problem of it doing nothing when debuff removed, so it is a real item now. [21:26]
- Escalating Exposure gets a nerf, surprising since it did not feel that powerful. [21:56]
- Spirit Burn ability range and proc damage nerfed; overall a nerf. [21:56]
- Overall most changes look good to Deathy, with only small issues here and there. [22:28]
