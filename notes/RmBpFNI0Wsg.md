## Covers
An uncommented 56 minute stack game on Seven (Storm Cloud) with a Dynamo teammate against a very mobile enemy lineup (Billy, Celeste, Mina, Vyper, Lash). Almost all of it is voice comms, so the advice is limited to a few in-game calls about Mid Boss, Rejuvenator, resets and splitting. Useful only as a picture of a long comeback game.

## Claims
- The enemy Billy ganked his lane at about a minute and a half. [1:44]
- An enemy Celeste carrying a large soul lead ("a huge bag") was called out as the target. [14:31]
- He weighed selling an item he had considered dropping but kept it because it builds into his 4.2k spike. [18:25]
- General tip: if everyone is calling Mid Boss and three enemies are dead, do not stop for buffs or to fix your wave; go straight and win the boss. [21:34]
- After a bad fight he told the team not to fight until the key ultimates were back up; his own ultimate was less important than the teammate's. [24:38]
- When the enemy is forcing a fight and has used everything, turn on them. [26:45]
- With Dynamo on the team, the plan for team fights is for Dynamo to land a big Black Hole; fight around the left shrine if you can. [27:18]
- After the Mid Boss fight, grab the Rejuvenator pickups. [30:24]
- With the enemy Billy respawning in seconds and the team low, kill and reset instead of pushing on. [30:54]
- Before the next push, fix the waves; the Mid Boss buff lasts about 2 minutes, so there is time. [31:57]
- With the Mid Boss up in about a minute and a half, push mid and the yellow lane. [32:57]
- Play together and do not split when the enemy has catch heroes like Lash on high ground. [33:57]
- When pushing for mid, make sure one player plays away to stop the enemy backdooring. [39:46]
- When the enemy Billy has invisibility (Shadow Wave was mentioned) and can run around, back off and wait for respawns rather than chasing. [40:16]
- In the late game push base as a group of six, with one player sent to quick lane (green) to split the defense. [41:54]
- The Seven ultimate in the final fight won the game. [42:54]
- His read on why the game went 56 minutes: the enemy had four of the most mobile heroes in the game, all able to kite Seven and Dynamo, take high ground and split, so no single player could pin them down. [43:57]
