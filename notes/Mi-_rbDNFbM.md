## Covers
Mirage coaching VOD review by Midknight of an Ascendant player who is normally a Kelvin one trick and is branching out. This is a 2025 video (June 2025 patch era), so items and abilities may have changed since.
Themes: abusing Mystic Regeneration with Djinn's Mark in lane, Tornado as Mirage's only movement and when not to use it, Monster Rounds in a pushed-in lane, Ricochet with a Fortitude rush, stamina items as crutches, and movement practice (diagonal air dash, air vents, corner boosts) shown in the practice tool at the end.

## Claims
Mirage lane and abilities:
- The immediate lane poke is mostly a Djinn's Mark play; leveling Fire Scarabs first lowers its value. The idea is to poke the enemy down so they pop their first healer minion tick early, before they actually need it, which is hard without Djinn's Mark. [1:02]
- Throw a Fire Scarab at an enemy as he runs away after a trade. [2:04]
- Buy Mystic Regeneration in lane so Mirage becomes unkillable: once you hit 800 souls, left click and press 3 (Djinn's Mark) and you heal about 40 HP. [2:04]
- Recast Djinn's Mark instantly instead of fishing for stacks when your HP is low: two x1 marks do the same damage as one x2 and refresh Mystic Regeneration twice. [3:05]
- He prefers three 1-stack ticks over one 4-stack tick unless at full HP, because it is so much free healing for the same damage. [4:14]
- You can combo the 1 into Djinn's Mark, but spam the mark as much as you can while your 1 is on cooldown. [4:14]
- Sliding to use your last bullet is good, but a light melee there is risky: it is 75 damage, and if it gets parried you lose more than that and put your teammate in danger. [3:38]
- Don't save the minion your laning partner can't reach in time; shoot the wave instead of taking tower damage. You can't set up a freeze against a Lash who can easily break it. [5:45]
- Save your wave when your partner is headed back and can actually get there. [5:45]
- Don't walk up while Lash's ult is online. [6:15]
- When an enemy overextends, punish him instead of staying focused on the other laner. [6:45]
- A light melee mantle cancel needs you to be in melee range; at that distance he was just barely out of range. [7:47]
- Don't spend Tornado on CC that dodges nothing in a Lash and Abrams lane; save it for denying the Lash combo. [8:49]
- You could predict that melee with a parry, but you need stamina to burn to throw a gamble parry. [8:49]
- Buy Monster Rounds when your wave is crashing into your tower: it clears the wave and denies a lot of tower damage. [9:20]
- Walking into that fight never had kill potential on the Lash; best case is an okay trade, worst case the Abrams eats you from behind. [9:50]
- Tornado him out of the Lash Ground Strike. [14:40]
- Djinn's Mark max order makes sense on a build where Mark and Toxic Bullets are your only real damage. [15:40]
- If you trade walkers, split that way, and make the decision earlier. [15:40]
- Tornado's eye frames and dodging bullets are its primary purpose; CC is secondary, though there are times it is right to use it for CC, like chasing a Dynamo. [17:15]
- Siphon Bullets gets progressively worse the more CC is in the game. [18:16]
- Don't use Fire Scarabs to farm a wave. [23:23]
- As a hero with one movement ability, never Tornado into their face in a negative situation: you dodge nothing and are left with no stamina, no Extra Stamina, no Stamina Mastery. [23:23]
- After using Tornado to get out, don't walk back in: alone with no Tornado walking toward them, you should die. [25:24]
- Mirage is a front liner when Tornado is online and a very squishy backliner when it is down; being cocky only hurts when Tornado is on cooldown. [26:28]
- If you rush Fortitude to live in the jungle, buy Ricochet sooner; Mirage needs Ricochet to farm well. [26:58]
- Spending your boost there was a waste: a fight is unlikely, and if one breaks out you can just press your ult. The ult is more valuable, but a boost is spent for sure while the ult is used reactively. [27:29]
- Down one with the enemy sieging, don't walk out of your base. [27:59]
- Late game, Djinn's Mark does less once you have pivoted to gun damage; recasting for Quicksilver Reload is fine. [27:59]
- When the enemy has no ults, space with Tornado from threats like Abrams' charge. [27:59]

Coaching summary (Mirage, Ascendant, Kelvin main):
- Biggest note: use Tornado better. Only Tornado in when you know the fight is won and they are on the back foot; in a neutral fight it leaves you like a Wraith in their face with no teleport. [31:36]
- Use Tornado to dodge things or to leave fights unless the fight is clearly won. [32:07]
- Your macro is strong, but Kelvin crutched your mechanics: you don't need good movement with Ice Path or great aim with a beam and grenade. Spend time in the practice tool on movement. [24:54]
- To fit into a high-rank fight, learn to jump in and immediately read the map; until then using Tornado as an information tool is acceptable. [20:18]
- Pull back the second two teammates die, even if the fight looked neutral when you arrived. [21:51]
- Don't run at enemies thinking you still have Kelvin's Ice Dome; you are a backliner. [36:12]

Crutches and stamina:
- Characters with get-out-of-jail cards build crutches: Kelvin Ice Path, Lash Flog, Wraith teleport and Infernus dash. Players switching off them are used to being less punishable. [13:27]
- Abrams mains switching heroes take too much minion damage in lane because they are used to his passive. [10:21]
- Extra Stamina is not a crutch, but people crutch on Superior Stamina and Stamina Mastery. [18:16]
- Kinetic Dash is great on heroes like Shiv or Dynamo who need to supplement their movement. [19:16]
- Stamina Mastery is a must on Lash and fine in some comps on a ground hero like Infernus, but many players autopilot it. [19:47]
- Always buy Extra Stamina on stamina-starved heroes like Warden. [21:21]
- Practice without stamina items to feel the cost of wasting stamina. [36:12]

Movement tech:
- To mantle you now have to hold W when you press space. [18:46]
- Practice by choosing specific jumps from clips and drilling them; big movement jumps build your fundamentals. [32:39]
- Blue lane rollout: jump off a couple of meters before the sign, then diagonal air dash and double jump. [33:40]
- How you hit an air vent matters and preserves momentum: hit it from the side you want to travel toward, or straight on to go straight. [34:10]
- After the vent, chain an outward wall jump, inward, outward. [34:40]
- Diagonal air dashing is the fastest way to gain speed: flick your camera to the side as you air dash. [35:11]
- Corner boost: melee and jump at the same time, hold W until you fall off, then double jump. [35:41]
- Do jumps quickly enough to get the stamina refund. [35:41]
- An escape from the same spot chains slide, jump, slide in one movement. [36:43]
