## Covers
Part 3 of a coach's four-part counter series (September 2025 patch), for any player struggling against Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Seven and Shiv. Each hero gets tech to play against them, counter items with special interactions, and the key ability to track in lane, timed against the 30 second wave clock.

## Claims
### General
- Track a key enemy ability in lane using the 30 second wave timer; for example Abrams's charge is a 37 second cooldown, so after he uses it he can't charge again until about that point in the next wave and can be punished. [0:30]
- Rebuttal (a buff on parry and lower parry cooldown) is good into players who like to melee. [1:01]
- Bullet Resilience, Spirit Resilience, Enchanter's Emblem and Battle Vest are good into their damage types, not into any specific hero; buy them by the enemy team's damage profile, the cheap ones for lane and the bigger ones for late game. [1:01]
- Bullet Resilience is slightly worse because Armor Piercing Rounds exists, but bullet resist also converts one to one to melee resist, so it helps against an Abrams or Drifter who melees you. [1:31]
- Curse counters every hero and is great to shut down anyone running the lobby. [1:31]
- "Healbane" or "anti-heal" here means any of Toxic Bullets, Decay, Spirit Burn, Inhibitor, Crippling Headshot or Healbane; which one depends on your hero. [2:02]
- Spirit Shielding is great into a double spirit lane and much better than Weapon Shielding, because spirit damage comes in bursts and it is a spirit meta. [2:02]
- Fire rate slows suppress gun heroes, chosen by your role: support buys Rusted Barrel and Disarming Hex, tank buys Juggernaut, spirit carry buys Suppressor; Suppressor and Juggernaut are amazing into a fed gun carry. [2:32]
- Hunter's Aura also slows fire rate but is mostly bought because it is a really strong item right now. [2:32]

### Mina
- Monitor her Love Bites: she is very strong early in lane because they do a lot of damage. [3:02]
- Her Rake also builds a good share of Love Bite stacks, so don't let her get close with Rake up when you are around 70% HP; expect burst. [3:02]
- Don't take extended gun trades with her; take short guerrilla poke trades, play around cover, and treat her like a faster-proc, scarier Infernus. Love Bites fade fairly quickly if you hide behind cover. [3:32]
- When a Mina uses her 2 to go into the air, she is about to ult; be ready for it. [3:32]
- Slowing Hex is by far the best item into her: it shuts down her 2, and without it she is basically a Paige, very squishy and jumpable. [4:03]
- Slowing Hex cast while she uses her 2 still hits her through the invulnerability frames. [4:03]
- Debuff Remover is good into her because it cleanses Love Bite stacks. [4:34]
- Knockdown is great into her because it forces out her 2, the same way you force out an Ivy Stone Form; if she doesn't dodge it, she is stunned and you kill her, and if she does, you catch her after. [4:34]
- Throw Knockdown on her before she looks for an ult or a team fight starts; a team fight without her 2 is much harder for her. [5:04]
- Spirit Shielding is really good into her in lane and especially against her ult; it still tanks 50 to 100 damage from a combo even outside a double spirit lane. [5:04]
- Her key ability is her 2: 27 second cooldown in lane (about one wave), 15 seconds when maxed. [6:05]
- When her 2 is down, treat her like a Paige: if she walks up, jump her and one-shot her; without the 2 or teammates to peel, anyone can one-shot her. [6:05]

### Mirage
- Play around his Tornado: don't use CC, key abilities, Bebop bombs, an Ivy Stone Form or similar while he has Tornado up, because it gives bullet evasion and dodges your abilities. [6:36]
- Once he is forced to use Tornado, dump your whole kit on him; this matters especially on heroes like Pocket in higher elo. [7:07]
- Mirage is less reliant on his 2 than Mina, since he can still hold his own without it. [7:07]
- Slowing Hex is good into him for the same reason as Mina: it suppresses his 2 so you can land things like a Bebop bomb. [7:37]
- Debuff Remover removes Djinn's Mark; pop it at about eight stacks against a maxed mark to dodge a big chunk of damage. It also cleanses the Fire Scarabs bullet resist reduction. [7:37]
- Capacitor removes his Tornado invulnerability state, and Armor Piercing Rounds pierces his evasion during Tornado; niche counters for a gun hero when he runs the lobby. [8:08]
- Restorative Locket is pretty good into him: every Scarab throw gives a stack and each 1 cast gives four stacks; a QSR or Mystic Regeneration Mirage spamming his mark gives more. [8:08]
- A Djinn's Mark that expires naturally gives no Restorative Locket stack; only a manual recast does. [8:39]
- His key ability is Tornado: 32 second cooldown, 18 with points; don't expect two points in lane. [8:39]
- Check enemy ability levels by the ring around each ability icon: no ring means unlevelled, a broken circle one point, small spikes two points, a full spiky circle maxed; useful when watching DLNS too. [9:09]

### Mo & Krill
- Use verticality when he burrows: jump onto a bridge and he can't reach you. [10:10]
- By mid game he usually has Warp Stone, which he uses from Burrow to reach height, so verticality works less then. [10:10]
- Verticality matters most if Krill takes two early points in Burrow for its surprisingly high early damage. [10:40]
- As a squishy hero, play around your front line and space; if you are repeatedly getting ulted by Mo (for example as Grey Talon), your positioning is wrong. [10:40]
- If you struggle, buy movement items to stay away; if he Phantom Strike ults you he should end up in the middle of your team, where someone should CC him since Mo rarely buys Unstoppable. [11:10]
- Mo & Krill has two headshot hitboxes; you can headshot the little Krill too, for example while he runs away. [11:40]
- Anti-heal is great into him: Scorn heals by percentage of damage dealt, so on-hit effects like Quicksilver Reload and Mystic Burst add healing, which is why Mo rushes QSR and Mystic Burst. Buy early Healbane. [12:10]
- Late game, with maxed Scorn and spirit lifesteal on his ult, Healbane feels really good. [12:41]
- As a front liner, Debuff Reducer is fantastic into him to shorten his ult; Debuff Remover also cleanses the damage-amp debuff from maxed Scorn. [12:41]
- As a backline, movement items (Warp Stone, Stamina Mastery) beat debuff reduction; you shouldn't be getting ulted, and as a squishy 70% vs 100% ult duration rarely saves you. [13:11]
- Tankbuster and Toxic Bullets are great into him because his ult gives infinitely scaling max HP and both deal max HP damage. [13:43]
- An Eternus 6 Mo & Krill player says early Knockdown (late early game, early mid game) is really good: it knocks him out of Burrow when he splits walkers and burrows away, and can cancel his ult to peel. [14:14]
- Burrow gives him 80% bullet resist; Armor Piercing Rounds pierces it. [14:44]
- Capacitor on him while burrowed removes the bullet resist (shown going from about 8 to about 40 damage per shot); generally Knockdown is the better counter. [14:44]
- Headshot Booster is really good into him in lane: he and Seven have two of the biggest heads in the game, and although both take reduced headshot damage, Headshot Booster's flat damage isn't reduced. [15:15]
- Headshot Booster in general is an overpowered early item; buy it if you have good aim or your hero hits headshots decently. [15:45]
- As a gun hero, monitor his Sand Blast: 42 second cooldown, 23 with two points (rare in lane); he likes to disarm you as you are about to kill a minion and steal the soul. [16:16]
- As a spirit hero, monitor Burrow: 37 second cooldown, a lot of damage; most Krills now go Scorn and QSR, but some still go Burrow. [16:46]
- Without Burrow up he lacks his escape (pressing 2 with high bullet resist), so if he overextends you can gank or catch him. [16:46]

### Paige
- Full focus her whenever she walks near you; she has one of the lowest health scalings in the game and evaporates. [17:17]
- Unlike Mina she has only two stamina and little mobility, so she plays around teammates. [17:17]
- If she mispositions or walks up to combo someone, turn on her like a bloodhound; Paiges in lower elo misposition a lot. [17:47]
- You can jump over her ult if you time it, more easily with a wall bounce; press space twice if you know it will hit and have no Counterspell. [18:18]
- Counterspell is great into her: Counterspell her ult and especially her 3, the root (Captivating Read), which is what makes her scary. [18:18]
- Reactive Barrier procs on both her ult and her root. [18:49]
- Spirit Shielding is also good because she hits sustained combos; Reactive Barrier is generally better, unless she is the only CC on her team. [18:49]
- Any item that lets you get on top of her helps: Warp Stone, Phantom Strike, Slowing Bullets, Slowing Hex, any slow. [19:19]
- Capacitor removes her shields: Defend and Fight (her 2) and items like Divine Barrier and Guardian Ward, on her or on allies. [19:49]
- Her key ability is mostly her barrier, 26 second cooldown and usually levelled first; a barrier can turn a fight, and without it she is much more vulnerable. [19:49]
- Experienced players should also track Captivating Read, about a 30 second cooldown; both are about one wave. [20:22]
- Manage stamina while her 3 is up: if you run out and she has Greater Expansion she can land the root and do a lot. When it is down, use stamina freely. [20:52]
- Keep a stamina in reserve against her, as against a Warden with cage up (keep two against Warden since his flask takes stamina). [20:52]

### Paradox
- Use your headphones: you can hear Kinetic Carbine, so when you hear it, tuck behind cover and let it end. [21:24]
- Kinetic Carbine is a 30 second cooldown (a full wave), usually levelled to 22. [21:54]
- Don't peek her with Carbine out; Carbine into headshot burst, grenade into Carbine, or Carbine into light melee all win the trade. [21:54]
- Warp Stone is great into her: after being hit by Carbine you can still Warp Stone away, which is the only way to dodge her Carbine into ult (swap) combo. [22:54]
- Even without her ult, warping away dodges the headshot burst from Headshot Booster and Headhunter. [23:24]
- Against a full Carbine glass cannon build (rarer since the Express Shot interaction nerf), Spellbreaker cuts her damage by 75% flat and obliterates the build. [23:24]
- Against a bomb (Pulse Grenade) build, Debuff Remover removes the extra-damage-taken debuff, and can remove the Time Wall slow or silence. [23:56]
- Reactive Barrier is so good into her if you can justify it; 2.1 spirit scaling on the shield holds up mid and late game. [24:27]
- In lane play around Carbine by hiding behind walls; post lane play around Time Wall, which with the silence has no counterplay besides waiting out its cooldown. [24:27]
- Time Wall with a few points does max health damage and denies a lot of damage, and she can kite on either side of it. [25:27]

### Pocket
- Late game, teams that group in six to siege base lose to a Pocket Majestic Leap into Affliction and ult; be very aware of your positioning, as against Dynamo. [25:27]
- In lane, if Pocket is on your side of the map without cloak or case up, focus them and they evaporate pretty quickly (tankier and more mobile than Mina). [25:59]
- In lane, don't stand next to your lane partner when Pocket has Barrage up; at full HP Pockets love to jump in, hit both of you and leave. [26:31]
- Slowing Hex is fantastic: it stops Pocket recasting Flying Cloak, so throw it when you see the cloak thrown and they are stuck. [26:31]
- Slowing Hex on a Pocket who Majestic Leaps in and tries to cloak out leaves them stuck in your team. [27:01]
- Divine Barrier still removes ult effects (Debuff Remover no longer cleanses ults after a nerf): it cleanses Drifter, Wraith and Pocket ults; buy it as a support into Pocket. [27:01]
- Counterspell into Pocket's ult takes practice but neuters much of their value since most of Pocket's damage comes off the ult; practice with a friend. [28:03]
- Any debuff reduction (Debuff Remover, Blood Tribute, Debuff Reducer) shortens their ult; Blood Tribute's 50% debuff reduction cuts it from 12 seconds to 6, or 16 to 8 when upgraded. [28:34]
- Their key ability is Enchanter's Satchel (case), a huge source of damage, invulnerability and survivability, often levelled early. [29:05]
- If Pocket aggressively uses case without cloaking away, and you have reactable CC (Bebop, Ivy Stone Form, Lash Ground Strike, Seven stun), both laners should dump everything on them while they can't dodge. [29:37]

### Seven
- When Seven ults before having points in it (he can't move during it), angle yourself so you can shoot him while the lightning beams don't hit you; he must cancel or take a lot of damage. [30:07]
- Knockdown is the best item into Seven, especially an ult build: it cancels his ult and forces him to buy Unstoppable, sinking 6k souls. [30:38]
- Even if he has Unstoppable, Knockdown cuts the Storm Cloud to about 8.5 seconds (time to proc Knockdown plus Unstoppable's duration). [30:38]
- He runs lower elo lobbies because people don't buy Unstoppable; without CC answers he just ults and wins. [31:09]
- Counterspell his stun (Static Charge) right before it procs to dodge the stun, which is long late game with duration items. [31:09]
- Debuff Remover also cleanses Seven's stun. [31:41]
- The meta Seven build is around Power Surge, so all fire rate slows feel great into him. [31:41]
- As a support, Divine Barrier cleanses his stun off teammates; for yourself get Debuff Remover or Counterspell. [32:12]
- Block his stun to cut his Lightning Ball damage, slow fire rate to cut his 3, buy Knockdown to cut his ult. [32:12]
- After the latest nerf he no longer runs high elo lobbies as much. [32:43]
- His key ability is Power Surge: 48 seconds by default, 33 with the usual point, about one wave. [32:43]
- Power Surge lets him clear waves very quickly with shock bounces and drastically raises his damage, more with Surge of Power; without it active he does roughly 60 to 70% of his damage. [33:13]
- If he wastes Power Surge or uses it on the wave, punish him and take a trade. [33:43]

### Shiv
- Space his dash (Slice and Dice): he needs it to get on you, so stand out of range or force him to dash in; without it he loses a ton of mobility. [33:43]
- Watch his rage: the bauble on his waist glows when he has rage. [34:14]
- Shiv rage now only procs on enemies; if he hasn't fought in a while he likely has rage, if he has been trading he always has it up. [34:44]
- At max rage he does about 40% more damage; without it about 60% of his damage. [35:14]
- Don't fight him with rage up; shove the wave, have both laners box or tuck so his rage dissipates, then fight him around 40% rage. [35:45]
- Slowing Hex is great into him: it stops his dash; when he runs at you to 2 then 4, throw it and kite away. [35:45]
- Counterspell his ult (easier to learn than Pocket's); if he has it maxed, countering it puts it on cooldown, gives you the heal and movement speed, and protects your team. [36:16]
- Anti-heal is good into him since Shivs love to build Leech and Infuser around Bloodletting for pseudo tankiness. [36:46]
- Against a knife build, Debuff Remover cleanses his Serrated Knives bleed and makes the build basically useless. [37:16]
- If he has dash maxed, don't chase him into a narrow corridor, especially as a group; he turns around or heals half his HP with Spirit Lifesteal, Leech or Infuser. [37:16]
- His key ability is the dash: 14 second cooldown (half a wave), 11 with a point. [37:46]
- Once Shiv uses his dash he is not mobile unless he knows flying Shiv tech or dash slides; after dashing in he is stuck backpedaling and vulnerable, especially early game. [38:17]
