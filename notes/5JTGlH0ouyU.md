## Covers
Paradox, aquatiq, commentary over one game on a gun Paradox build.
Item choices with short reasons (Restorative Shot, Headhunter, Hollow Point, Slowing Bullets, spirit resist), Time Wall charges, Pulse Grenade amp and swap tech.
Gameplay commentary, video dated 2026-07-03.

## Claims
- Set Time Wall to instant cast. [1:02]
- Restorative Shot is crazy on Paradox for lane sustain, but you have to actually hit your shots to proc it. [2:37]
- He considered maxing Time Wall first, since its upgrade gives two extra charges; it is a really good ability now. [3:40]
- Paradox is a bit of a gun character. [4:14]
- Pulse Grenade is still very strong: it increases all of Paradox's damage against the victim, including Kinetic Carbine, Time Wall and gun damage. [4:14]
- Her ult (Paradoxical Swap) is not that useful early, so losing it at the start is fine. [4:46]
- He took Kinetic Carbine early. [5:49]
- Restorative Shot is so good he called it the item he needed for heals in lane. [6:54]
- Headhunter fits because the enemy heads (Yamato especially) are massive; it gave him a lot of lane sustain. [6:54]
- He got Trophy Collector this game. [8:01]
- With cooldown items his swap was on a very low cooldown. [9:11]
- Paradox has mad tech, like midair swapping through the Time Wall. [9:43]
- Against Holliday buy Slowing Bullets: shoot her, slow her, then Slowing Hex and she cannot move. [12:23]
- When you are not needed on one side, shop and TP to where the enemy is pushing. [13:23]
- Against an enemy team that is all spirit damage, buy spirit resist right away: he got Spirit Shielding and Spirit Resilience so the Yamato would not shred him. [17:32]
- Hollow Point is a nice power spike and always a nice investment on Paradox. [20:06]
- He bought a weapon damage item that slows you, covered by Stamina Mastery, because he just wanted more gun damage. [23:15]
- Debuff resist was going to be really nice this game. [23:46]
- Use your swap sparingly. [24:17]
- He bought Crippling Headshot, with cooldown items after. [25:18]
- Spam Time Wall in fights; it had three charges late. [26:19]
- Note to self: do not go in to save your teammates. [27:21]
- Late he replaced an item with Spirit Resilience and considered dropping Spirit Shielding. [27:21]
- He needed lifesteal (Infuser) before he could ult into team fights. [27:53]
- He wanted Counterspell against much of the enemy team. [28:58]
- When dying would be really bad, do not push: stay back with the team and push side lanes so you can TP. [29:30]
- Paradox has some of the best farm in the game. [32:35]
- Paradoxical Swap can swap two, three or four people at once. [35:15]
