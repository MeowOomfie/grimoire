# You'll Master The Mid Game, Just Give Me 27 Minutes | Deadlock Mid Game Guide
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-03-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQ8ofLZiJr0

## Covers
A talk-over mid game guide in two parts: basics (rotations, judging ahead or behind, farming when behind, shutting down solo farmers, the map midline, spirit resist, slow pushing, stealing camps) and objectives (not forcing Walkers, ignoring Guardians, the Urn as a fight forcer, when and how to take or contest Mid Boss and Rejuvenator).
For low and mid rank players who lose games after lane or give up when behind.

## Claims
- One good fight is pretty much all you need to completely change the game; a team down 30 to 40K souls came back to about even by contesting and stealing Mid Boss and Rejuvenator. [0:00]
- Past laning phase the game turns into who takes and wins better fights; about three good fights and the game is over, so don't give up after losing one fight. [1:31]
- Hopeless games (lost by 20 minutes) are less than 10% of your matches. [2:02]
- Unless you are going across the map, most bounce pads are faster than taking the zipline back. [2:02]
- There is no point going all the way back to spawn: you have the mid shop, Guardian shops if you haven't lost them, healing troopers, and boxes on every rotation. [2:32]
- Rotating manually instead of ziplining lets you grab boxes, bridge buffs, help team fights and reset waves along the way; always grab boxes and Sinner's Sacrifices on every rotation. [2:32]
- At the start of the mid game decide whether you are ahead or behind, how the other lanes did, who the enemy's most fed player is, and how fed their team is on average. [3:03]
- If you are behind, ask to lane with someone or lane alone, preferably on a side lane, and farm: let the wave come to you, and you can hold the wave there by not killing it too fast so more troopers stack on your side. [3:33]
- When behind, kill the trooper waves yourself rather than letting them die on their own, so you get the souls. [3:33]
- Behind-player cycle for the next 10,000 souls: kill the wave, run to the jungle, grab boxes, kill camps, run back to the wave. [4:03]
- Figure out your damage spikes (for some heroes the last tier upgrade of an ability, or an item like Ricochet), play for that spike, and hide in the jungle until you get it. [4:03]
- If you are ahead, get to other lanes as fast as possible and shut down players who are farming. [4:35]
- In most games don't push past the midpoint of the map without vision (knowing where enemies are), or someone ganks you or comes out of the jungle and you die. [4:35]
- When ahead, look for enemies who push too far (for example a Seven trying to take a Guardian after losing lane), kill them and put them on a 30 second respawn timer. [5:05]
- Heroes like Victor, Seven and Wraith who disappear to farm can come back at 40K souls with only two kills because no one contested them. [5:05]
- Don't let enemies solo farm: gank them and put them on as many timers as possible, or scare them back into their Walker so half their wave dies before reaching them. [5:36]
- Being ahead doesn't mean you stop farming; keep your farm up. [6:08]
- When ahead, the coach usually avoids the mid lane because enemies can come from every side; on a side lane you only worry about one side, so they'd need to be three deep to fight you. [6:08]
- Always keep track of who is most fed on the enemy team; usually only one or two players are the problem. [6:39]
- With a coordinated team, group up and gank the fed enemy from all sides to deny their farm; Mo & Krill is designed for exactly this. [7:09]
- Without a coordinated team, make the fed enemy's job as hard as possible: counter rotate to the lane he goes to, or with a poke heavy hero poke him out of lane so he backs, loses farm and gets scared. [7:40]
- Play the matchup: against Bebop avoid long or exposed angles that invite a hook; against Paradox, run when she places her wall since she is probably looking for a swap. [8:11]
- In-game leading won the coach about 15 of his 20 climbing games to Ascendant: calling out, telling teammates what to do, rotating up to 20 times to help other lanes, taking all the Guardians and at least one Walker. [8:41]
- When behind and needing to farm, know who your carries are: a Seven or a Wraith should take the side lane and farm even if not behind, because they are carry heroes. [9:42]
- Play your spikes: as Wraith on a build rushing Ricochet (with Golden Goose Egg), once Ricochet was bought he went straight to team fights and never went back to lane. [10:12]
- Certain items (Ricochet, sometimes Surge of Power) or AP investments make you stronger than everyone else, so you don't need to farm the whole game once you have them. [10:43]
- As Wraith with Ricochet, stack up as many cards as you can, ult someone to instantly kill them, gun down others, and call teammates to keep finding 3v2 and 3v1 fights to snowball. [10:43]
- For an ult Seven build, the time to come out of the jungle may be maxing your ultimate or getting Unstoppable or Escalating Exposure. [11:14]
- Don't play farming simulator all mid game: every 2K souls you get, the enemy gets 4K because they farm lanes, jungle and your team while you're absent. [11:14]
- A perma-farming Haze with Ricochet, Inhibitor, Silencer, Siphon Bullets and a maxed ult already has plenty of power spikes and should come out and fight. [11:44]
- Picture an imaginary midline across the dead center of the map, roughly where Mid Boss is, and for the most part don't pass it, because you don't know where the enemies are or what they're doing. [12:14]
- Nine out of ten early deaths of the coach and his friends were on the enemy side of the map while trying to farm. [12:44]
- Default loop: push out the lane, kill everything, go to the jungle and kill camps or boxes, come back when the lane returns. [13:15]
- If you don't have vision on at least three enemies, don't walk past the midline. [13:15]
- Buy spirit resist every game on almost every hero (the chest plate, Dispel Magic, any kind); most enemies are spirit heroes and without it you take about 40% more damage and die to any shred. [13:15]
- Be okay with doing nothing when you have no pressure and no vision on the enemies; don't push out a lane just to do something. [13:47]
- The other side: when four enemies are on one player and nobody is pushing lanes, do something. [14:18]
- If you see more than three (preferably four) enemies on the map, or for example two dead and two visible, call to push every lane they're not in (if they're in blue lane, push yellow and green). [14:48]
- With four teammates fighting, the other two should push yellow and green as far as possible and attempt the Walker, but disengage rather than die for it. [15:18]
- If the unseen enemies come to stop your push, the main fight stays a 4v4 instead of becoming 4v6, and you either leave or get the Walker. [15:18]
- Winning a team fight does not always mean an objective; don't go Mid Boss if blue lane is pushed to your side or yellow and green aren't pushed out, since they can steal it or take your Walkers. [15:48]
- After a won fight you can instead steal every one of their camps. [16:18]
- Take camps backwards: on the enemy side, start with what is closest to you and move back toward your side; after winning a fight up yellow lane, take all the farm from their side of yellow back to yours. [16:18]
- Stealing all their farm after a won fight can deny the enemy team about 2,000 to 3,000 souls that you get instead. [16:49]
- Before leaving a lane to start a fight, kill only a few of the minions so your side outweighs theirs; the lane slow pushes into a bigger and bigger wave that can take Walkers and objectives without you. [17:19]
- Stop forcing objectives: people full send, die for a Walker, go on a 60 second timer and often don't even get it. [17:50]
- One person dying for a Walker is not the end of the world, especially if your team is slot locked and the enemy is outscaling with more slots; two is pushing it, more than two is not worth it. [17:50]
- At 20, 25 or 30 minutes one death costs about 70 seconds of farm, team fights and objectives, so don't take that risk lightly. [18:20]
- Guardians don't matter: stop dying for Guardians. By around 20 minutes you can walk into a Guardian and kill it without a wave. [18:50]
- If you lose lane and don't get their Guardian, don't push a wave to fight for it; let them win their lane, farm, catch up and kill the Guardian whenever you want later. [18:50]
- A gun carry like Wraith can walk into the first Walker and kill it without a wave by minute 30, sometimes 25 or 20 if scaled enough. [19:20]
- The second and third Walkers are much harder, because every objective you kill gives the other ones more health; the first Walker is extremely weak. [19:20]
- If you're really slot locked, sneak up on the first Walker while the enemies are pushed up, kill it and run away. [19:20]
- Almost always pick up the Urn, every time. [19:50]
- If it is your comeback Urn (your team is behind), run it pretty much 100% of the time; if they have Rejuvenator and are baiting you, run it halfway, and if they're waiting to kill you, bring it back to spawn and drop it so they can't have it. [19:50]
- For a neutral Urn, have your team push out waves and try to run it; by halfway you'll see whether the whole enemy team is waiting or only one or two. [20:20]
- Think of the Urn as a team fight forcer: use it to force a fight when you just hit your spike or have better ultimates. [20:20]
- Force fights with the Urn especially when ahead: if they don't fight you confirm it and get further ahead, if they do you probably win the fight and get the Urn plus kills. [20:50]
- If it is their comeback Urn, take it and run to deny it, even if it just resets and they get it 5 minutes later; keep them out of the game. [20:50]
- If the Urn run starts in green lane and ends in yellow, players not in that fight should push green and take the green Walker, which is likely free since the enemies must be in yellow; at worst it forces them to split resources and gives your team a favorable fight. [21:20]
- Mid Boss is one of the biggest game winners, arguably the biggest. [22:52]
- If you get three or more kills, or three or more enemies are on respawn timers, do Mid Boss almost 100% of the time. [22:52]
- Exception: if green and yellow lanes are pushed to your side, the living enemies can take those Walkers for free while you do Mid Boss. [23:24]
- If blue lane is pushed up without your pressure, the enemies can zip straight to Mid Boss and jump down to parry or contest (Dynamo, anyone). [23:24]
- After a team wipe Mid Boss isn't always the answer; it's okay to push up waves, steal their camps and call it a day. [23:54]
- Mid Boss makes a sound at half health; you can bait a fight by lowering it to just above half, setting up on the edges of the veil to see enemies coming, then having someone take it to half. [23:54]
- When taking Mid Boss with the whole team, keep at most three (maybe four) players in the pit and one or two outside waiting to ult anyone who jumps in. [24:24]
- In the pit, someone should be parrying and someone punching. [24:24]
- Whoever punches the Rejuvenator first gets healed to full health, so let your lowest health player punch first if you aren't worried about a steal; if you are, have someone sit on the crystal ready to parry while everyone else punches the second it spawns. [24:54]
- To contest an enemy Mid Boss, jump into the hole when the Rejuvenator crystal is about halfway down: sprint, dash and jump, and if you jump too high double tap crouch to drop straight down. [24:54]
- You should land dead center almost exactly when the crystal lands; spam parry to stun everyone who punches, or alternate parry and punch when alone. [25:25]
- If the enemy gets Rejuvenator and you couldn't contest, avoid fighting for its roughly 3 minute timer; poke them and get them low so they over fight and lose it, then fight. [25:56]
- An enemy with Rejuvenator who runs it down alone (for example a Celeste pushing all the way up yellow lane) should be jumped by all six of you and killed. [25:56]
- After killing a Rejuvenator holder, retreat to spawn and wait; waves crash into your base so you still farm, and the enemy often gives up on using Rejuvenator. [26:26]
