# This is Why Mirage Is S Tier
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Xk8vqy9cUg

## Covers
Mirage gameplay commentary by aquatiq: a full game on Mirage with a Holliday lane partner against Drifter and Rem, talking through item buys and decisions as he plays.
Not a structured guide; covers Monster Rounds into a Rem lane, the item path, using Traveler to buff allies, trading walkers and not being scared to make plays when enemies are dead.
Patch era of early March 2026.

## Claims
- Mirage against a Drifter and Rem lane: he thinks Mirage is stronger than the Drifter in lane, and Mirage with Holliday is a scary lane combo. [0:32]
- He bought Monster Rounds mainly because of Rem, whose 3 puts helpers on minions and makes them live almost forever; Monster Rounds is the best lane countermeasure to that. [2:04]
- Monster Rounds gives about 25% more damage to NPCs (he guesses 15 to 25%). [2:36]
- With Monster Rounds the Rem lane became much easier, and jungle camps and Sinner's Sacrifice are much nicer to kill. [6:42]
- Quicksilver Reload would make his lane much easier; he calls it very good on Mirage. [7:12]
- Holliday's opinion: she is not a great late game hero; late her barrels and ult are her best tools, jump pads are good for movement, and her 3 becomes redundant once people buy a lot of armor and you stop aiming for headshots. [3:37]
- Late game Holliday turns into something like a support. [4:07]
- Mirage might be one of the best laners in the game. [5:09]
- Mirage has good sustain on his own. [5:40]
- When the lane is dull and stalled he leaves to take Sinner's Sacrifice (the "vending machines"); he had 6k souls early from it, which is a good amount. [8:15]
- He teleported (Traveler) to save a walker and died, losing a guardian but not the walker, and calls that worth it. [10:52]
- An enemy soloing a walker at 13 minutes is bad; answer by pushing up and taking a walker back. [12:26]
- He bought Dispel Magic (as a debuff remover) once he was getting caught in heavy CC. [12:56]
- Do not take an objective when the enemy can just collapse on you; make space for it first, ideally when enemies are down. [14:29]
- Advice for lower ranks (Seeker, Alchemist): when enemies are dead you have a free window to take objectives; do not be too scared to make plays then. [15:00]
- Play with your team, but do not be scared to make plays or to commit when people are dead. [15:30]
- He wanted Ricochet next; with Ricochet he can farm the tier three camp. [18:03]
- Infernus's Counterspell cannot stop Djinn's Mark because he does not know when it will pop. [18:36]
- Giving up one walker to take two walkers is a fine trade; if six enemies are on one walker, let them have it as long as you do not die. [20:44]
- 35 more ammo from Titanic Magazine is a blessing. [21:46]
- Spirit Shielding is good when you take significant spirit damage, for example from Lash. [24:55]
- He considered Escalating Resilience for bullet resist and bought Crippling Headshot because he can hit headshots decently. [22:52]
- Mirage needs to be very rich; take free farm when the team says so. [29:06]
- Players on carry heroes are too scared to carry; take the risk and fail rather than sit in camps for 15 minutes doing nothing with your money. [29:37]
- When your team is ahead on souls (244k vs 200k), fight; being scared drags 20 to 30 minute games out to 45 minutes. [30:07]
- Keep the enemy away from mid boss when you have a big ult like a Dynamo black hole on your team. [31:07]
- Traveler: teleport to an ally (even one right next to you) just to give them the buffs; it gives immunity to silence and stun, so give it to your best performing player. [31:38]
- He felt he was dying because he lacked Leech; he likes Leech on Mirage and calls it a pretty broken item in general, buying it over Spirit Shielding. [32:40]
- Late game, push up and end rather than make the game longer than it has to be. [34:11]
