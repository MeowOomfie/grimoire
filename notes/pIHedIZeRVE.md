## Covers
Solo and duo ranked advice for carrying games yourself: using the pre-game hero and lane swap, lane goals by matchup, Mid Boss as the deciding objective, building when ahead or behind, the patron half-health respawn rule, parry and melee habits.
Then role tips for frontliners (Billy, Abrams, Lady Geist, Victor), roamers (Calico, Vyper, Vindicta, Apollo), supports (Dynamo, Paige) and carries (Seven, Wraith, Haze). For low to mid rank solo queue players.

## Claims
- In the new matchmaking you can see both team comps, change your hero and change your lane before the game; use it to fill what your team lacks. [0:00]
- Example: do not play Vyper into three stuns (Ivy, Abrams and similar); she would have to buy Unstoppable as a second item or not get to play, so swap hero instead. [0:31]
- In solo queue you are the common denominator; take as much into your own control as possible instead of saying what your team should do. [1:01]
- Into a carry lane, either do not feed them or stomp them so they do not scale and become an easy gank. [1:31]
- If a carry is underfed and your mid laner gets about 4k farm ahead of them, the mid laner can gank and shut down that carry's farming for the rest of the game. [1:31]
- Into a support heavy lane (for example tank plus support), avoid long trades, because they heal it all back with their healing and the healing trooper. [2:01]
- Into a spirit nuker lane, consider buying Spirit Resilience or Spirit Shielding, and take prolonged trades rather than short ones, since they must wait for cooldowns and are all or nothing. [2:33]
- Build around what you face so you make it to the 10-minute mark; do not give up after five or six lane deaths. [2:33]
- About 90% of games are won with Mid Boss; you cannot win a game without Mid Boss, especially in lower elo. [3:04]
- Mid Boss is the great equalizer even when you are behind. [3:34]
- Comps with Ivy, Lash, Dynamo, Wraith, Vyper, Abrams are very strong at fighting a Mid Boss; with them you can force Mid Boss even without pressure if you have real gun damage and can kill it fast. [3:34]
- Below about Phantom rank, play off Mid Boss to win, because most players do not know how to use a lead or end the game. [4:04]
- Guardian does not matter much: after lane phase it is so weak a decent gun hero (or a spirit nuker) can walk into tower and pop it. [4:35]
- Do not give the Guardian away for free, but do not die two or three times protecting it; let it go, then call on mic that they took it and are probably roaming. [5:38]
- Call everything on comms; in the new ranked people actually listen and you can strategize as a team. [6:09]
- Build according to how the game is going. If you are about 10k ahead of everyone, build almost all tanky items: Cheat Death, Frenzy, every Leech and Vampiric item, so you never die again. [6:40]
- With a big lead your job is to never die, because without your mistakes the enemy has no way to get comeback souls. [7:11]
- To secure a lead, play more with your team, deathball more and build tanky; buying Spellbreaker alone can be a huge game changer. [7:41]
- When behind, defense usually will not save you; build damage so you can kill the fed enemy and take their bag, and hope your support buys Curse. [8:11]
- When behind, try to put the fed enemy on a 2-minute respawn timer by killing them. [8:11]
- If an enemy genuinely one-shots you, buy something to counter it, but do not overbuild defense thinking it will save you. [8:41]
- Vyper has about 2,000 health at max build, so defense items do little; her big separator is whether she gets Unstoppable, plus flat damage reduction like Spellbreaker. [8:41]
- In general, if behind on a damage-dealing hero, buy more damage items; if behind on a support, buy counter items like Slowing Hex, Curse, or Rescue Beam. [9:11]
- Example game: down about 20k, the team contested Mid Boss while the coach went to the Rift alone; they stole Mid Boss, pushed Rift troopers to the enemy base and won in one push. [9:42]
- When the patron is brought to half health, the defenders' respawn timers are cut, so kills before the half count less. [10:42]
- When pushing their base, ignore defenders who come out of spawn one by one and pick them off, then down the patron to half as fast as possible; kill them after the half so they do not get the faster respawn. [11:14]
- If you are defending and the enemy will get the patron half no matter what, do not go fight; wait and only fight as a full team, or you lose a 5v6 and the game. [11:45]
- One kill in lane from a parry can decide the lane if you farm at least as well as your opponent. [12:16]
- Make every parry a dash parry: dash before or while parrying, so if they do not melee you are far enough away to avoid their heavy melee follow-up. [12:46]
- The downside of a dash parry is you cannot guarantee two melees, but one melee and a few hits is enough. [13:16]
- Most people parry right after coming out of being parried, so melee them again for a free hit. [13:16]
- Against enemies who melee minions from far away or quick melee a lot, dash in front of the melee to parry it and take a free kill. [13:16]
- Do not quick melee a one-shot enemy; there is a 0% chance they do not desperation parry. Shoot them, use an ability, or let them go. [13:46]
- Frontliners (Billy, Lady Geist, Abrams and similar): your main goal is never die; a dead frontliner has no use. [15:48]
- A frontliner should be a pest that cannot be killed and cannot be ignored, always in front of the fight drawing attention. [16:19]
- Victor is a strong carry because he is also a frontliner: he dives the backline, and if not focused he never dies, then ults and his team arrives. [16:19]
- Tank heroes should lane with a support and take the strongest enemy lane; worst case they leave lane neutral instead of a random feeding that lane. [16:49]
- A Billy and Rem lane should not die in lane; if the enemy counterbuilds (Healbane) you can just counterbuild their counterbuild. [17:20]
- Frontliner build: high health and healing plus only a few damage items so you are not useless. [17:51]
- In solo queue every hero and role, even support, should build some damage; do not rely fully on your team for damage. [17:51]
- Be at every fight if possible, take pressure and engage off pressure. [18:21]
- For almost every role, buy Trophy Collector unless you are already at the rank you think you deserve; if you outplay your lobby it should be max stacks by 25 minutes. [18:21]
- Buy Trophy Collector on most heroes if you are not a carry (not side laning); three to four players per lobby should have it. [19:21]
- The common frontliner mistake is running it down alone without pressure from the team; a frontline is powerful only with damage or distractions behind it. [19:21]
- Example: Billy with a Vindicta behind him forces the enemy to choose; ignore Billy and he keeps poking, focus Billy and they blow up to Vindicta. [19:53]
- Frontliners should play a sustained damage style, constantly staying in the fight as an opportunity maker. [20:24]
- Balance pressuring the enemy frontline with diving squishies like Vindicta (Billy can walk up and slam a Vindicta without her ult up), but always diving makes you a free kill. [20:24]
- Roamers include Calico, Vyper, Vindicta and Apollo: anyone who can go in, get a quick pick and leave. [20:55]
- Vyper with Trophy Collector perma-roaming (with Stamina Mastery, captioned "stand mastery") is amazing; the coach would not take a solo lane as Vyper. [20:55]
- Roamers should buy Trophy Collector and roam early, roam often. [21:25]
- A roamer laned with a carry like Seven should leave so Seven solo lanes; the roamer takes boxes and both end at similar souls, a better wealth distribution. [21:25]
- In higher ranks supports or roamers let the carry solo lane, then return with a kill or boxes; both are ahead, kill the enemy lane, go for Guardian, then perma-roam. [21:55]
- If you do not roam you are at the mercy of your teammates and cannot help a losing lane. [22:25]
- After lane phase a roamer should pressure the enemy carry and enemy jungle: reset the mid wave so it pushes into their side, then go into either jungle. [22:25]
- Look for an enemy on doubles, someone low farming a Sinner's Sacrifice, or a solo farming carry, and gank lanes based on those openings. [22:56]
- Example: Seven laning against Wraith; gank the Wraith with Seven, split the souls, Seven gets lane priority and can steal their jungle, and you gain a Trophy Collector stack. [22:56]
- Roamers (and supports) should not take too many camps; take boxes and the enemy's farm, and look for kills more than camps, because camps are for side laners like Seven. [23:26]
- Every bit of farm you take from your own side is farm your carry loses. [23:57]
- Roamer fight style: quick in and out damage, burst someone on a Sinner's Sacrifice, reset, repeat; reset mid wave, look for an opportunity, repeat, farming in between. [23:57]
- Supports should mostly build counter items like Slowing Hex, Rescue Beam and Curse. [24:27]
- If one enemy gets out of hand, Curse them and call "I cursed him, shoot"; one counter buy, even Phantom Strike, can win a game. [24:58]
- Supports should lurk near either the strongest or the dumbest player on the team. [24:58]
- Near the strongest player, you keep them alive so they scale, can limit test, play more aggressively and make more picks. [25:29]
- Near the dumbest player, you stop them throwing: a stupid play alone is a stupid play, with a group it is a plan; keep distance and have Rescue Beam ready. [25:29]
- Supports should still build damage when they can for poke, alongside utility. [26:00]
- Dynamo's job is hitting his ult (4): build Duration Extender, Refresher, Superior Cooldown (captioned "greater cool down") and anything that makes the ult better. [26:00]
- A utility support like Paige can get Greater Expansion to land stuns, or a spirit-hit debuff item; Escalating Exposure may work on her dragons but is expensive unless you are stomping. [26:30]
- A support laning with the carry should leave to gank or run boxes so the carry gets more farm, but tell them and do not abandon them to die. [27:00]
- If you are left solo in lane, play around tower: hide in veils, the sides and on the roof, kill minions from the roof, and if they dive and parry, jump off, loop around and climb back. [27:30]
- Solo laning is not hard because enemies in your games are not coordinated enough with CC to catch you. [28:00]
- Carry is a farming role: you play for scale, do not look for fights or damage in the first 20 minutes, disappear and come back with about 40k farm. [28:30]
- A carry must know their spike: for Seven, once ult is maxed and Escalating Exposure is bought, stop farming and hit 4 aggressively, then 1 and 3, occasionally 2. [29:00]
- Until your spike, whether the team is winning or losing, farm boxes, camps and Walkers, even stealing from the enemy side. [29:00]
- Do not die with a bag; spend all your souls before doing anything risky, limit testing with a 2,000 bag is not fine. [29:30]
- In lane do not push past the mid line (the bridge on your side); past it you are in victim territory and get ganked with no vision. [30:01]
- Best case pushing their side gives a few extra minions; worst case you die and lose four minion waves, so play on your side for scale. [30:31]
- Once a carry hits their spike, fish for picks; after two or three picks, take the team to Mid Boss and let them come fight you. [30:31]
- Victor can call his team to Mid Boss while he runs at the enemy base with ult (maybe Refresher) and a 10k lead; if they respond they lose Mid Boss, if not they lose base Guardians, shrines and the patron half. [31:02]
- With six stacks gone from ranked, teams are mostly uncoordinated, so that split play almost guarantees a Mid Boss. [32:04]
- A carry's only lane job is not getting farmed; ending lane neutral or slightly up means most gankers lose the 1v1 and often the 2v1. [32:04]
- Diving a Wraith loses the 1v1 nearly every time even if ahead on souls, because she ults and spams her 1. [32:35]
- Once fed, a carry should focus on picks, play behind the frontline and mind positioning, since they are the enemy's target number one. [32:35]
- Cheat Death is good on many carries, for example Lady Geist, though the coach only half recommends it outside of stomps. [33:06]
- Gun (M1) carries are very strong and arguably overtuned, partly because most people only buy Spirit Resilience and there is no Spellbreaker for gun damage. [33:36]
- Metal Skin counters gun damage only briefly; a decent player fully disengages during it, and there are counters like Mercurial Magnum (captioned "Merc Mag"). [33:36]
- Hybrid heroes like Wraith and Vyper deal equal spirit and gun damage, so they cannot be countered by one resistance. [34:07]
