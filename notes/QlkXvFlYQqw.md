# How to Read Your Enemies | Obscurus Kelvin Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2024-11-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QlkXvFlYQqw

## Covers
Midknight coaching review of an Obscurus-rank Kelvin in the solo lane against Lash.
Themes: where to stand to hit minions, denying souls and not missing creeps, box routes, respecting Lash's ult, camps versus a neutral wave, aggressive item choices when winning lane, reading where enemies are after an Urn or Walker, weak side and strong side, spending souls, filling slots, anti-heal checks and valuing Frozen Shelter.
Late-2024 video (early November 2024 patch, a couple of weeks after the Guardian changes); the game has changed a lot since, so treat numbers and item names as dated.

## Claims
Kelvin laning (Obscurus, solo lane vs Lash):
- In high ranks about 70% of the time both laners go to their player left and hit the wave from the side; fight minions from a side arch, not the middle arch, where you are exposed to poke from Lash on the stairs. [0:31]
- Hitting the wave from the middle arch is fine after the first wave. [3:34]
- With a normal wave you should have 225 souls instead of 150; do not miss minions. [2:33]
- Shoot the soul orb to deny it when the enemy is not securing it. [2:33]
- Do not siege the wave from the bridge: he took 150 damage for nothing, and a soul between you is a coin flip that favors Lash's closer position and faster bullets. [3:03]
- Play further back at the arch or use the gargoyles; play more passive when the enemy has two abilities on you. [3:34]
- You can walk about 80% of the way up the enemy stairs without the tower shooting you, further with minions there, to land more Arctic Beam poke. [4:04]
- Secure souls right away instead of reacting after the enemy almost denies them. [4:04]
- New players underestimate denying; deny as much as you can. [4:34]
- He missed about 30 to 40% of the creeps he could have had; two missed minions is 150 souls. [5:04]
- With the Guardian changes the enemy can play safe under tower and you cannot deny or poke him much. [5:34]
- When walking back, path through the vases and boxes; a vase can drop about 1.5% gun damage and about 120 souls, and boxes respawn (he thinks) every 3 minutes. [5:34]
- A box run through the church area is great. [6:05]
- Using Ice Path to grab vases is good Kelvin tech. [6:35]
- When you use Ice Path, you can go aggressive on the enemy instead of only running. [6:35]
- Once Lash hits 3K souls (his ult), do not walk past the gargoyles or maybe the bridge; he ults you into his Guardian for a free kill. [7:06]
- If Lash ults you into a Guardian, spam parry: the moment you land you parry the Guardian's hit. You still take a lot of damage, so respect it. [7:37]
- Hyper zooming on every minion is a habit to break because it cuts your vision; use it for last hits or soul orbs. [8:09]
- Wasting Ice Path for 10 seconds with no purpose is bad; it is very strong for escaping a gank, chasing, and getting above Lash's ground pounds. [8:40]
- Do not sit on 1.5K souls; buy. [8:40]
- Healing Nova plus Sprint Boots is normally a great buy, but Sprint Boots only make sense if you mean to roam. [9:41]
- When winning lane clearly, buy aggressive items to snowball: Fleetfoot to keep killing him, or Mystic Vulnerability, which builds into Escalating Exposure, a Kelvin favorite. [10:11]
- Going to a camp while the wave is neutral: assume both waves lose about 3.5 creeps (about 300 souls), roughly the camp's worth, so it is net neutral, leaves an unsecured bag at risk, and removes the camp from your team. [10:43]
- When winning, push the wave into the enemy tower: it tethers Lash to the lane so he cannot roam, and the wave naturally pushes back to you so you miss little while you box run. [11:45]
- Route boxes on your own side of the map; deep routes risk getting caught (Haze sleep is CC and he believes it makes you drop the Urn). [12:46]

Reading the enemy and mid game:
- After delivering the Urn uncontested, expect at least three enemies on that side of the map, since the objective pulled them there; do not push a tower expecting no collapse. [13:48]
- Panic-hitting a wave is bad, but if you know you are dead (five on you, no kill potential), kill the wave before dying: like a League of Legends cannon minion, you get about 250 souls and deny them minions to siege your Guardian. [14:50]
- If you play Mirage and see one enemy split on your Walker, teleport to them and deny it; it is worth the teleport. [16:52]
- Patch edit: after 8 minutes souls are now duplicated between you and one teammate, so splitting a wave with a teammate is now good; before that patch it was wrong. [17:23]
- Do not chase past the bridge and gargoyles with no information; once you lose kill potential on the target, back off. [17:53]
- After your team brute forces an objective, most enemies are probably on that side and will collapse; do not chase there. [18:54]
- Weak side is the side of the map with few teammates; strong side has most of them. Fight on strong side, but only when teammates can actually back you up. [19:25]
- In a 2v3 after over-chasing, walk away with your Paradox and let her support your exit instead of going back in. [20:27]
- Keep minimap awareness: three into three after getting jumped is still a negative position. [20:57]
- To bait an over-chase, take less poke and drop back toward your team so they collapse on the chasers. [21:57]
- Do not read a fight won only because the enemy missed melees as an advantage. [22:27]
- On weak side with your whole team far away, do not look for fights since you do not know where enemies are. [22:58]
- At 18 minutes the Guardian is not intimidating; with three teammates dead, back off after the wave so they cannot stagger your respawns and take a free mid boss. [24:29]
- When an ally is getting meleed repeatedly by Abrams, jump in front and parry. [25:00]
- Do not waste Frozen Shelter on a 1v3 against one Abrams who is not ahead. [25:30]
- Grab the bridge buff when you are next to it; understand how valuable it is. [25:30]
- When running away, check the map and run toward your teammates (here the Yamato). [26:00]
- Do not take a fight sitting on half your net worth; base and spend, since dying loses a 1K bag. [26:32]
- He died with Healing Nova on cooldown, a misplay. [27:03]

Items and slots:
- Fill your slots with tier 1 items: a tier 1 weapon item gives +6% weapon damage versus +14% for tier 3, so returns diminish; by this point have all slots filled, or at least only one empty flex slot. [27:33]
- By 20 minutes all your slots should be filled. [38:47]
- Value Buffs (bridge buffs); he took more than most at his rank. [28:04]
- With a big player advantage you would normally call mid boss, but not against several ult heroes (Infernus, Abrams, Lash) that can steal it. [28:35]
- When an enemy picks up a 2K bag, try to kill him at all costs. [29:36]
- As Kelvin, constantly Tab to check who on the enemy team has anti-heal; with Healing Nova, healing boost and grenade, Kelvin is a raid boss against a team with none, and you can fight those without it for free. [30:06]
- Do not Dome when you are not in danger; bait them into your Guardian instead. [31:06]
- Try to get your Dynamo inside your ult; he gets a free Singularity. [31:36]
- Check your own team for anti-heal too: Paradox and Yamato had Healbane; Mirage should build Toxic Bullets. [32:07]
- When Rejuvenator is about to be taken, go to mid boss yourself and be the one meleeing or parrying, rather than trusting three teammates. [32:39]
- Frozen Shelter is on a 68 second cooldown and is very strong around mid boss; do not use it just to heal one teammate. [33:42]
- With a player advantage, group and push; do not reset just because one teammate (Yamato) is low. Take your shrine flex slot and consider mid boss when it spawns, respecting Lash. [34:43]
- Do not Dome to secure one target who is going to die anyway. [35:13]
- Knock Patron first, then hit the side base Guardians in the 15 seconds before Patron falls into its weakened state; hitting side Guardians first lowers your win chance. [36:13]

Notes summary:
- Deny souls whenever you can. [36:43]
- Do not miss minions; aim to get every minion in each wave. [37:14]
- Track where enemies are or will be: objectives you take pull them to your side, so do not fight there after. [37:14]
- Soul awareness: he sat on 12,000 souls (about four tier 3 items); buy as soon as you can afford the next item. [38:16]
- Value your ult: do not Dome a target who is already dead or just to heal one teammate farming; with a Dynamo, stick to him and hit a good Dome on their team. [38:47]
