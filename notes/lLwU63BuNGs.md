## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream on 6/24/26, VOD reviews with no pro analysis segment. Four students: a Warden in an Arcanist lobby, a Silver (laning with Lash into Apollo and Seven), a Mina (with Ivy into Rem and Haze, Phantom lobby) and a Phantom Viscous (with Bebop into Apollo and Rem).
Themes: farming the tier one off spawn, soul awareness and playing for spikes, M1 heroes in side lanes, movement (slides, dash jumps, wall bounces), taking bad fights, playing for urns and the comeback urn, on-hit items on Silver, Spirit Shielding versus Enchanter's Emblem, Tank Buster timing, instant cast over confirm cast.
Side opinions on the current patch: movement slows got stronger, Holliday is broken, Call Bell Doorman, Dispel Magic and Silencer are strong.
