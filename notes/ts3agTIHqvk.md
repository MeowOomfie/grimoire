# Climbing In Deadlock Is Easy Once You Master This.
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-04-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ts3agTIHqvk

## Covers
How the coach climbed from Initiate to Ascendant 1 in two months: play a gun carry, take the hardest lane, farm and split push instead of over-rotating, mind unsecured souls, the best two out of three queue rule, and in-game leading.
For solo or duo queue players who want to rank up rather than just have fun.

## Claims
- The coach climbed from Initiate to Ascendant 1 in two months with a 90% win rate. [0:00]
- Most players in the game are bad and don't play Deadlock, they play "team fight the game" or "farm the game"; much of the weight in solo or duo queue is on you. [0:00]
- If you are better than the rank you are playing in, you will rank up for the most part, despite some unavoidable losses. [0:30]
- Rank alone (Ascendant, Phantom, Oracle) doesn't mean a player is good; plenty with 1,000+ games were boosted. [1:00]
- Duo queuing is by far the best way to climb, because the pair can cover different roles. [1:00]
- The coach's duo plays a spirit nuker or roamer who roams and gets picks in other lanes, while the coach as a gun carry hard farms, takes objectives, and doesn't get countered by one enemy resistance buy. [1:32]
- Even in solo queue, play a gun carry; not having one is like playing League without an ADC: nobody melts Walkers, kills objectives or melts Mid Boss. [2:03]
- Most of the roster and most players are spirit heroes; enemies buy Spellbreaker or Spirit Resilience and a spirit hero is useless, or they buy a dispel. [2:03]
- As a gun carry your team will likely be all spirit heroes who handle the early game while you scale and farm, take every objective, and melt people who didn't buy resistance against you. [2:33]
- Gun carry examples: Venator, Haze, Wraith (good because she also does spirit), Vyper (good early in lane, the coach doesn't like her much), and Warden (also does a lot of spirit). [2:33]
- Carrying starts in lane: try to take the worst lane, for example against an Apollo and Graves lane, unless your hero gets destroyed there with zero chance of impact. [3:04]
- Don't give the strongest lane or the hardest scalers to a random, because that puts how the game scales in a random's hands; if they leave lane with 10 deaths, you could have stopped it. [3:35]
- If you get stomped in lane, don't keep pushing out of Guardian expecting a kill; sit under Walker or Guardian, let them take Guardian, back to Walker, let waves crash on you, and end lane maybe 0 and 3 but farmed and alive so they aren't unbeatable past 10 minutes. [3:35]
- Some games are lost before the 10 minute mark. [4:05]
- After lane phase it is "farm simulator"; the biggest mistake of would-be carries is over-rotating for their team. [4:05]
- If your team wants to over fight or death ball, let them; don't give up your jungle or two farmable lanes to join a fight you can't impact because you are 2K farm under and the enemy is ahead. [4:35]
- Farming is by far the easiest way to win a game; you can walk out of jungle 10K farm ahead of everybody and there is nothing they can do unless they catch you and take your big unsecured souls bag. [5:05]
- Learn your hero's spikes: on Wraith, after Ricochet the coach farms for his next tier four, then perma fights the rest of the game, resetting waves if needed and always fighting or taking the enemy's jungle. [5:36]
- Example spike: as Seven, once you buy Unstoppable and max your ultimate, look for team fights and call your team in even if they are at around 23K farm to your 40K. [6:06]
- Past a certain farm level the enemy must spend Curse, Mo & Krill's ult, Dynamo's ult, Refresher and more just to remove you; even if you die, that spending puts them behind on items. [6:06]
- If someone buys Curse to answer you, that is a tier four they couldn't get, and it doesn't guarantee they kill you. [6:37]
- Mid game plan: jungle, split push, get objectives; you should be side laning. [6:37]
- When three enemies are in the lane opposite you, push your wave all the way, shoot the Walker and watch for anyone zipping over; if they come, back off to jungle while the wave returns, kill two or three camps and maybe a Sinner's Sacrifice, then clear the lane again, and repeat to pressure one side of the map. [7:08]
- Don't hold on to soul bags. [7:38]
- Unless you are Wraith (who can teleport across the map with her 2), don't push past the bridge on each side lane, and usually don't walk down into the pit past where the Guardian stands; kill the wave, push it past there and leave, because of gank risk. [7:38]
- Track your unsecured souls while jungling and spend them first; delivering one 2K bag to the enemy usually loses the game because you are 2K down and out for 60 seconds, leaving the game in your teammates' hands. [8:08]
- Two out of three rule: break your sessions into best two out of three; lose the first two and get off, win the first two and decide whether to continue, otherwise play three games at a time, to avoid tilt queuing. [8:08]
- Focus on IGLing (in-game leading): tell teammates what to do, for example after three picks with every lane pushed call Mid Boss, or call a split push for a Walker. [9:08]
- Many players don't know what to do and just wander, farm, or chase an enemy, or avoid a Walker for fear of dying without realizing six enemies are dead; a small push from you helps. [9:39]
- Most games people lose are lost in the first 15 minutes. [10:09]
