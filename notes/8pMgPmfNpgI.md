## Covers
A 2024 replay review of a high-rank Wraith game (1v1 lane vs Haze) where the team falls behind: laning into Haze, the patch change to Wraith's cards, when to farm vs team fight vs gank, beating an early five-man group by pushing other lanes, tower denies, bridge buffs at 10 minutes, ability points vs money, and grouping up late. For Wraith players and mid-game decision making. This is a 2024 video: the game, heroes and map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Haze is actually a weak laner; she is very strong up to very high level, where her squishiness gets punished easily. [0:30]
- The only thing to watch in lane against Haze is her Sleep Dagger: getting hit by it, especially out of position, lets her follow up with a melee and kill you. [1:01]
- Otherwise Haze does little damage in lane because she needs many bullets on you to stack Fixation. [1:32]
- Laning summary: push up to their tower, keep up the aggression, focus on last hitting enemy creeps and denying your own. [1:32]
- Kills matter little in the early game: he died twice in 3 minutes and was still ahead in money from creeps. [2:04]
- The more money you have, the more the enemy gets for killing you, so late game you don't want to die; but at level one a kill gives almost no money. [2:04]
- At level one two creep denies are worth more than a kill on the enemy; around level four it is about four denies. Focus on last hits in lane. [2:35]
- He considers his Wraith build (linked from his main channel) the best build for Wraith, even more so after the current patch changes. [2:35]
- Patch change: Wraith's cards now spawn overhead instead of to the side, making them easier to land, but they do less damage at level one (80 base instead of 100). [2:35]
- The cards now scale better with spirit power, so early spirit items or building more into cards can be valuable. [3:05]
- He still thinks Wraith is a hard carry who should build around gun damage, not cards; a top Wraith player popularized a card build for about a week, then returned to gun because the gun is better. [3:05]
- Killing more creeps let him reach the enemy tower at 5 minutes with far more money than the opponent despite being down two kills. [3:35]
- His two main early damage items are Mystic Burst (increases the damage of every card) and Extra Charge (an extra card). [4:05]
- Ability order: Card Trick to level two early, then stop; Card Trick level three is the last thing he levels in the game, because the build is around gun damage. [4:05]
- Wraith's gun damage is very weak in the early game, so in lane the cards carry you. [4:05]
- Burst is Wraith's lane strength: low on HP, he threw three cards and killed a full HP Haze who thought she could kill him; nobody expects that much burst. [4:35]
- After killing the tower, he pushed the wave into the tier two but would not farm there because he could get ganked easily. [5:40]
- Instead he farmed the enemy tier one creep camp to take money away from the enemy. [6:10]
- With ult up he considered a gank, but both teammates in that lane had pushed toward the enemy tower, so a gank risked dying or no kill; he went back to his lane instead. [6:42]
- He bought Bullet Lifesteal early so he could sustain in lane longer and bully the Haze out. [6:42]
- Staying in lane is right when the enemy laner is not last hitting and can't sustain. [7:14]
- When going for a kill and a ganker appears behind you (here Pocket), teleport out and play it safe. [7:44]
- The number one thing on his mind is the 10 minute mark, when he wants to be at one of the two bridges. [7:44]
- At 10 minutes the mid boss spawns, the tier three creep camps spawn, and the slot machines you punch for money spawn, a big money boost. [7:44]
- The two bridge buffs spawn at 10 minutes and last 3 minutes: movement, health regen, casting (reduces casting time) and gun, which is the strongest one to pick up early. [8:15]
- Farming the jungle while the enemy Seven was nearby was risky; he got cross map sniped by Vindicta and barely lived. [8:46]
- His number one rule for Wraith: don't look for too many ganks early, and especially avoid early team fights. Wraith is weak at team fighting early, good in 1v1 scenarios, and excels at farming. [8:46]
- Usually he buys Tesla Bullets before Bullet Lifesteal and Fleetfoot, but having won lane early and needing to walk around more, he took Fleetfoot and Bullet Lifesteal first. [9:17]
- Close to 10 minutes, with money in front of you, buy an item, then rotate to the bridge buff. [9:47]
- Deny your own guardian when it dies: the souls from a guardian are shared across the whole team, so denying it denies six times the money. Look for tower denies, especially early. [9:47]
- A movement buff is not as good as the weapon buff but still helps; the buff lasts about 3 minutes or until you die. [10:17]
- Following a fight that turned into a big team fight was a poor choice: he went very deep and broke rule number one, avoid big team fights early. [10:48]
- Afterwards, go to the lane with the most creeps pushing to your tower and farm those; with Fleetfoot your farming is fast and efficient. [11:19]
- To beat a team that groups as four or five early to push a tower, push out every other lane: grouping costs them time and money, other waves push into them, and you put counter pressure on their towers. [11:51]
- As long as he can farm safely while the enemy groups and fights, he can get ahead and carry the late game. [12:53]
- The enemy's double camp room is the room with the most money in the game; he took theirs for free. [12:53]
- When creep waves push your tower and no teammate is there, take them before the slot machine or jungle camp: the waves give as much or more money, nobody else on your team would get them, and the camp can wait. [13:24]
- Any time a lane is not occupied by a teammate, your team is missing money; mid game is about team efficiency: farm every lane and your jungle, and if you can get greedy, their jungle, all while staying alive. [13:55]
- Pushing enemy towers together as a team won't necessarily win the game, because you sacrifice a lot of income for it. [14:25]
- He starts looking for kills once he has his second tier three item, Intensifying Magazine; before it he is only good at 1v1s and efficient farming, after it he can contest anyone on the map. [14:55]
- If his team called a fight together he would help them. [15:26]
- Knowing exactly where an enemy is matters before a risky play: Bebop's laser has a very slow turning rate, so while he lasers a creep camp you can get behind him, lift him fast and burst him down. [16:57]
- Always keep an eye on the map and try to play safe. [17:28]
- He died because a Vindicta stun stopped him teleporting out while in Haze's ult; against an unreliable team it is better to play safer and push out other lanes. [17:59]
- With a team that is all over the place and mostly negative, split push and look for safe objectives and money instead of forcing team fights and falling further behind. [18:29]
- Project Mind (teleport) at level three is a big power spike: it teleports every 15 seconds and gives a bullet shield every 15 seconds, so you can initiate a fight and still teleport out if you play smart. Before level three he plays safer. [18:59]
- When two teammates in a fight have died, back out; staying is very risky. [20:05]
- He relied on Telekinesis too much: the Vindicta baited his ult with Ethereal Shift, so he could not shoot her, and the Warden came in and killed him. [20:37]
- Going to fight an enemy in your territory without enough info gets you punished; when you hear an enemy ability nearby, teleport out right away instead of waiting. [21:07]
- When behind with all lanes pushed to your side, play safe, get money and go for the next best item. [22:07]
- He built Improved Bullet Armor because the enemy had Haze, a bullet-building Vindicta and a Warden with a lot of bullet damage; in most games he goes Improved Spirit instead. [22:07]
- When calling a team fight at your Walker, he walked in thinking only the Vindicta was overstepping and got blown up by the whole team; it was his fault for not seeing they were all pushing him. [22:38]
- Toxic Bullets is almost always worth it on Wraith against heroes building lifesteal and tankiness like Warden and Bebop, since it stops healing; he buys it against Infernus nearly every game. [24:11]
- Farming and knowing where to be keeps you the richest player even when the team is losing fights. [24:11]
- When the team finally grouped up around middle as five, they caught the Warden off for a free kill; just having teammates nearby protects you and saves you from the back. [24:41]
- Haze does not do enough damage, even in her ult, to kill him when he is grouped with his team. [25:44]
- Look out for your teammates: a Paradox with all cooldowns up (Warp Stone, a shield, Time Wall) could have saved him but left instead. [27:14]
- Ability points (AP) are more important than money; killing towers early and delivering the Urn give money and also AP. [27:44]
- If a game feels even on money but you are getting stomped, the enemy probably has a lot more AP (more levels), so they can use their abilities better; this matters most early when you are only 2 to 3k behind. [28:17]
- In the late game, group up and fight as six; walk toward your team so you are with them if the enemy engages. [28:49]
- Keep picking up the bridge buffs; they are always worth it. [29:19]
- Stay on the side where your team is and from the backline look for a Telekinesis lift on someone farming; check downstairs because people are sometimes at the shop. [29:19]
- One pick is all you need to snowball a fight; down a player, the enemy probably won't win if they contest. [29:49]
- After a won fight, call for mid when everyone is there. A teammate pushing a Walker instead of grouping is okay as long as he comes back fast enough; it is always worth it and forces enemy rotations. [30:19]
- When the enemy farms a creep camp unaware of you, wait until you see the engage, then commit. [30:50]
- What mattered most was making enough good calls compared to the enemy to get far ahead; enough smart choices can lift a game where neither team is great. [31:52]
