## Covers
Midknight Vods runs an Apollo-only free coaching stream right after Apollo's release and a balance patch (February 2026). He first analyzes Adorian's third Apollo game, then coaches four Apollo players: his friend Rey (Eternus), Kai (Ascendant 6), and two unnamed raffle winners (one laning with a Yamato into Seven and Paige, one into a Celeste).
Themes: Apollo build (Opening Rounds, Headhunter, Headshot Booster, Cold Front, Torment Pulse rush), AP order (point in 1 in lane, ult max second in solo queue), parry and 2 tech, ulting below half HP and ulting out rather than in, soul awareness, dead time and farming, urn handling, slot locking, Metal Skin versus Bullet Resilience, and reading Lightbringer-format builds.
Side notes on the patch and meta: Celeste and Silver still strong, the ult "curse" text, new-hero win rates, and a tier list.
