## Covers
Drifter, aquatiq. Commentary over one pub game on a hybrid (mostly gun) Drifter build he calls solved, with item swaps against a fed Mirage and team-play complaints.
No one is coached. Recorded around the 2026-06-25 patch.

## Claims
- The build is a hybrid that is still mostly gun; the build ID is shown on screen and he thinks it solves Drifter. [0:03]
- He buys Toxic Bullets because the featured item in the build procs off Toxic Bullets. [0:34]
- In lane he lets the minions come back toward him so he can hide behind cover; pushed up too far there is nothing to hide behind. [4:08]
- He saves his blind for when the enemies are pushed further up. [6:10]
- Push the lane out, then go camp farming. [6:10]
- Buy Extra Regen early for some green (vitality) items. [7:41]
- Take a powerup yourself to deny it to a dangerous enemy carry like Wraith. [9:16]
- Holliday's explosive barrels do about 400 damage each, so two of them kill him. [13:45]
- Item path: Spirit Resilience, then Fortitude, and Toxic Bullets is a must. [13:45]
- Against Mirage's Djinn's Mark stacks, buy Debuff Remover next; stacked marks took him to 500 to 600 HP from full. [15:15]
- Debuff Remover does not help if a fed Mirage just keeps shooting you; get a disarm like Disarming Hex, or Curse to focus him down. [27:13]
- Mid game he sells Opening Rounds to make room for Escalating Exposure. [24:34]
- When your team has rejuvenator, fight together and push one lane at a time together instead of splitting up. [25:06]
- He lost a walker because the team did not follow him to yellow when they had rejuv. [21:29]
- When only three enemies are alive or they are coming to your base, take all their jungle. [28:47]
- With Escalating Exposure and Leech he stops dying; Blood Tribute and Mercurial Magnum (as captioned) come next. [29:18]
- With a big minion advantage, go for shrines. [31:53]
- He thinks many "auto parry" accusations are really players light meleeing too much. [9:47]
