# This Deadlock Character is Too Strong
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-02-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ucNZtuWJRYU

## Covers
Drifter gameplay commentary by aquatiq (Oracle rank, 1,300 hours) through a lane into Apollo and Sinclair, explaining his abilities and item calls.
Main points: the default build he trusts, Slowing Hex against Apollo, Fortitude on a roaming hero, and blinding teams with the ult.
Patch era February 2026.

## Claims
Drifter kit:
- His 1 is Rend, a swipe in front of him with a very small radius. [1:07]
- His 2 is a mark he can teleport to. [2:09]
- His 3 is basically stacking amp damage. [3:44]
- His ult blinds enemies, cutting their vision radius by about 15 m, which is a lot. [4:15]
- He uses the "devilish" build, which he says is pretty much the only good build on Drifter with consistent results; spirit Drifter is griefing. [2:43]
- He maxed his 3 going into mid game for more amplified damage and more kills. [11:32]
- Duration Extender on the ult extends the blindness, very helpful in team fights: blind the whole enemy team, then fight. [21:45]
- His ult is close to useless very late in the game. [25:21]
- Drifter has decent farm but cannot farm tier 3 camps well. [9:26]
- Drifter is made to travel the map killing people who are on their own. [20:44]

Drifter items:
- Lifestrike is really strong right now. [4:45]
- He bought Backstabber early because he likes the item. [7:52]
- Kinetic Dash gives fire rate and ammo. [8:25]
- Toxic Bullets then Point Blank let him start fighting people. [18:12]
- Fortitude is not a very good item in general, but it is on Drifter, because he travels between fights and regains full HP each time he is out of combat. [17:41]
- Fortitude's regen is now stopped by all damage taken, not just some. [17:11]
- He skipped Crippling Headshot because the enemies had almost no spirit resilience. [23:16]
- Against two gun heroes he considered Hunter's Aura to cut their fire rate, or Vampiric Burst so he never dies. [23:46]
- Against a mostly spirit team, buy Spirit Resilience; two gun heroes alone do not justify Bullet Resilience. [23:46]

Matchups:
- Apollo and Sinclair is an awful lane; Sinclair's laning is insane because of Vexing Bolt and his assistant. [3:13]
- Counter Apollo with Slowing Hex, or Slowing Bullets at minimum. [4:45]
- Slowing Hex is so strong against Apollo; consider it against Apollo and Celeste since their heroes get free movement. [9:26]
- Sinclair's rabbit ability (turns you into a bunny) is insane, especially for taking mid boss. [5:16]
- Graves's ult places a ghoul spawner anywhere on the map, even high up, and the ghouls explode on impact. [14:32]

Lane and macro:
- In lane, take your camps when there is no wave so the enemy cannot steal them. [5:47]
- Killing an enemy and taking their vending machine is a good play in lane. [8:25]
- Geist, Abrams, Haze and probably Wraith have the best farm in the game, more than gun carries. [9:26]
- Mid boss is probably the most important part of the game. [5:16]
- Do not fight three enemies with one teammate; leave. [10:32]
- When the enemy Warden ults, leave and let him waste it. [11:02]
- After lane, farm or side lane for souls. [16:10]
