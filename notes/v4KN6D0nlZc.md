# The BEST Calico Build In Deadlock - NOT CLICKBAIT
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-01-22

## Covers
Metro's spirit Calico build right after the January 2025 Calico buffs: how her kit works, the Leaping Slash into Gloom Bombs combo, ability order (max Return to Shadows first), full item order with reasons, items he rejects, sell order, Ava tricks and late game options. For players learning Calico as a front line initiator; items and numbers may be stale.

## Claims
- Recorded after the patch that buffed Calico; she was already strong before, and despite the consensus that she is weak she is one of the hardest carries in the game. [0:00]
- Calico is a front line disruptor and initiator: tanky, wants to be in the fight the whole time, starts fights and dictates their pace; Ava (cat form) is one of the best ganking tools in the game. [0:31]
- She needs little aim but is mechanically hard, with many tricks. [1:03]
- Buy the build left to right in lane, mid game and late game, with utility and situational items as needed. [1:33]
- Leaping Slash is her defining ability: a dash ending in a light melee hit that heals you, scaling with spirit; usually the first ability you use to initiate, often coming in from the side as a cat. [2:03]
- Leaping Slash's hit counts as light melee but cannot be parried. [2:33]
- Gloom Bombs throws skulls that attach and explode after a second; thrown into the air they explode midair, so you can still harass when they do not attach. [3:03]
- Gloom Bombs quirk: enemies hit by multiple bombs take 50% damage per bomb, so one or two bombs do the same 80; more than two does much more, a full burst of four with the upgrade is 200. [3:33]
- Bombs spread a lot and are hard to stick at range; the best way to attach several consistently is to be in melee range. [4:03]
- Core combo: press Leaping Slash then hold Gloom Bombs during the dash so the bombs pre-cast and attach before the dash animation ends, then a light melee right after; that is the highest burst. [4:34]
- Leaping Slash with a held heavy melee has a shorter animation but does less damage than the Gloom Bombs combo; Gloom Bombs should always be up given its low cooldown. [5:04]
- Basic gameplay loop: Leaping Slash, Gloom Bombs, light melee, then shoot. [5:34]
- Gloom Bombs is a strong lane harass tool with a 10 second base cooldown; level it first, farm the first wave and hit it on the enemy whenever they come close. [6:05]
- Then level Leaping Slash and take its first upgrade, raising the heal per dash to 80; you have this after about the first or second wave. [6:36]
- Ammo Scavenger early: Calico's magazine is small (about 12 bullets, 14 with Ammo Scavenger) and each secured last hit refunds two ammo. [7:07]
- Calico is a spirit scaling hero, not a gun hero, excelling at spirit damage especially with Torment Pulse; 20 Ammo Scavenger stacks raise Gloom Bombs damage and Leaping Slash healing. [7:39]
- Leaping Slash healing scales with spirit at a 1.6 multiplier; Gloom Bombs at 0.8. [8:09]
- After Ammo Scavenger, buy Spirit Strike and Melee Lifesteal; since Leaping Slash counts as light melee it procs both, and one level one hit heals about 200 total and deals about 126 melee plus 60 spirit damage. [8:09]
- Then Mystic Reach to make Leaping Slash more consistent and longer range; hitting it is essential: most fights he wins he hit his Leaping Slash, and missing it usually loses the fight. [9:09]
- Then Hollow Point Ward for gun damage and Headshot Booster: her big spread shotgun procs headshots very easily without precise aim. [10:13]
- Close Quarters is great value for cheap since Calico is always close to enemies. [10:43]
- Extra Stamina next to get around, farm faster and initiate better. [10:43]
- Restorative Shot if you need extra regen or are harassed a lot in lane; it is easy to hit. Usually Leaping Slash plus Melee Lifesteal is enough healing. [10:43]
- Lane points: one in Leaping Slash, one in Gloom Bombs, then a second in Leaping Slash; that reaches nearly 250 healing per dash. [11:13]
- Misconception: do not run melee Calico (Lifestrike, Melee Charge); the only melee you do is the one after the dash, and good players parry other melees. Scale spirit and a bit of gun instead. [12:15]
- After lane go straight to Cold Front: it scales with spirit, greatly speeds farming, and can be cast in cat form to farm or push without leaving it. [12:46]
- Cold Front combines with the combo (come out of cat form, Leaping Slash, Gloom Bombs, Cold Front) for a huge early nuke. [13:17]
- After two points in Leaping Slash and one in Gloom Bombs, max Return to Shadows from zero: its upgrade gives a minus 30 second cooldown down to 50 seconds, one of the lowest ult cooldowns, so you have it every fight. [13:48]
- Return to Shadows with +80 damage hits about 246 spirit damage, twice (start and end), so 500 plus, about 600 with 20 Ammo Scavenger stacks. [13:48]
- Full sequence: Leaping Slash, Gloom Bombs, light melee, empty the magazine, Cold Front, then ult and keep reloading during it. [14:19]
- Orange slot choices: Active Reload is a waste because you never reload except going into Return to Shadows, which is a free reload. [14:49]
- Kinetic Dash is low value since you engage from cat form with Leaping Slash, not jump dash; Long Range is bad on a shotgun hero. [15:19]
- Mystic Shot is inconsistent: it only applies to the one bottom bullet of the spread. [15:49]
- Slowing Bullets is solid but less valuable than Berserker; Soul Shredder Bullets is skipped because you have not shot before the first combo burst. [15:49]
- Swift Striker is pointless on her low bullet count; Fleetfoot is okay but Berserker is best. [16:21]
- Before Berserker, buy Enchanter's Barrier: it scales spirit, and since you initiate out of cat form with its bonus spirit shield (475 total with lane items), the first combo gets its extra 20 spirit. [16:53]
- Torment Pulse is the most important Calico item, no debate: always active because you stay close, during the pounce, while shooting and during the ult; it is top three damage in most of his games. Never skip it. [17:24]
- Then Berserker: you take lots of damage in the front line and heal it back, and its extra ammo (four bullets) equals Active Reload's. [16:53]
- Then Mystic Vulnerability: reduces enemy spirit resist and Torment Pulse procs it instantly; every ability procs it (Leaping Slash via Spirit Strike, Gloom Bombs, Cold Front, the ult). [18:27]
- Quicksilver Reload is an alternative if you like shooting more, but the combo then has to use Gloom Bombs for the reload and feels worse for burst; skipped since you empty the magazine and ult anyway. [18:58]
- At around 10,000 souls, right after lane, enemies already die very fast to this build. [19:29]
- Headhunter next: easiest hero to hit headshots with, procs about every 6 seconds even in chaotic fights, giving healing, movement speed and damage. [19:59]
- Then Spirit Lifesteal: in multi-enemy mid game fights her healing is huge, and Torment Pulse and Cold Front also feed it; even from 1 HP the combo plus ult heals around 1,000. [20:29]
- Max Return to Shadows before Leaping Slash or Gloom Bombs: maxed Leaping Slash halves its cooldown on hit (about 11 to 5 seconds), but the maxed ult resets all cooldowns when it ends and gives a 30% damage amp for 5 seconds. [21:31]
- After the ult, level Leaping Slash for more cooldown reduction, or max Gloom Bombs first for two more bombs; both are fine. [22:35]
- Be basically melee range to cast Gloom Bombs; you will miss many early on, keep practicing. You can also cast Gloom Bombs while dashing into the enemy to stick a few. [22:35]
- Gloom Bombs is probably her weakest ability and usually not the main damage; the abilities look weak on paper but synergize well with Torment Pulse. [23:36]
- Then Enduring Speed: enemies usually have movement slows, it helps chase and disengage, and raises your movement speed during the ult so enemies cannot escape before the second burst. [23:36]
- You want enemies inside the ult when it starts and when it ends; Headhunter's speed and Enduring Speed let you chase for the second burst. [24:08]
- Then Spirit Snatch, even though this is not a melee build: hitting Leaping Slash on multiple enemies steals spirit and spirit resist from each, about 50 spirit and 24 spirit resist from two (78 to 130 spirit from three). [24:39]
- Spirit Snatch applies every 6 seconds, matching Leaping Slash's short cooldown on hit, so you keep re-engaging and farming everyone's spirit. [25:40]
- At around 140 spirit the ult does about 450 twice (a 1k nuke) and Gloom Bombs about 800 if they all hit. [26:11]
- Diviner's Kevlar is the best 6k item for Calico: you take lots of damage in the fray and it gives about 1,400 shield every ult, plus spirit, and its cooldown roughly matches the ult's 50 seconds. [26:41]
- Many builds run gun items that look good on paper; Hunter's Aura is bait because you only empty your gun once and the rest of your damage is spirit. [28:13]
- The full build is about 27k net worth, achievable in a normal game. [28:45]
- Sell order: Ammo Scavenger first, then Extra Stamina; keep Mystic Reach (bigger Leaping Slash, Cold Front and ult radius), and never sell Cold Front, Torment Pulse or Mystic Vulnerability. Very late you can consider selling Enchanter's Barrier. [28:45]
- With an open flex slot, Ethereal Shift lets you stall: Torment Pulse keeps ticking lifesteal during it, then ult right after and you are back to full HP. [29:16]
- Ava is weak until late game but enemies rarely kill you in cat form; being hit by an enemy hero puts it on a 5 second cooldown, so you cannot use it to flee mid fight. [30:18]
- Use Ava to initiate: cast it, rotate to another lane hidden from the minimap (still visible physically), and pounce. [30:49]
- Escape trick: the ult lasts 4 seconds, so if you took no damage for a second before ulting you can cast Ava the moment the ult ends and leave. [31:20]
- Late game without a slot, Frenzy against heavy gun damage (it saves a slot since you already have Berserker); or Colossus, very strong for a brawler and it boosts melee and weapon damage. [31:50]
- Magic Carpet, a meme on most heroes, is great on Calico: in cat form it gives huge movement speed, a spirit and bullet shield to initiate, and you are hidden from the minimap, so you can fly over roofs and cross the map to initiate or escape. [32:22]
- Lifestrike sits at the very end: since Leaping Slash is a light melee it only raises the melee lifesteal heal from 150 to 300 for 3k; bought mainly for its movement slow. [33:53]
- Mystic Slow, if you have a flex slot and the enemy runs gun damage: everything you do applies spirit damage, so Torment Pulse spreads its slow and fire rate slow to everyone around you. [34:23]
- He always buys Diviner's Kevlar, then picks: Frenzy or Mystic Slow against gun damage, Colossus to brawl more, Lifestrike for slows, Magic Carpet to initiate across the map. [34:54]
- Warp Stone works if you initiate and keep dying: blow cooldowns, ult, Warp Stone out and cat; also chase low enemies with it. [35:25]
- Improved Spirit and Boundless Spirit are luxury: Diviner's Kevlar at 6k gives 40 spirit every ult plus 1,400 shield, while Boundless gives 65 for 9,500. [35:55]
- Leech is overkill if you really do not want to die late; Curse to disable someone specific. [36:25]
- Surge of Power can go on Gloom Bombs or the ult to raise their damage, speed and fire rate. [36:55]
- Escalating Exposure works with everything here, including Torment Pulse and Cold Front, good for long fights; Mystic Reverb can go on the ultimate for good damage. [36:55]
- He rarely buys Spirit Armor or Bullet Armor since lifesteal and the ult keep you alive; Debuff Reducer or Debuff Remover against Bebop or Metal Skin focus. [37:25]
- What kills Calico is missing Leaping Slash or a team with five knockdowns focusing you; then buy Ethereal Shift and Diviner's Kevlar, dodge the first with Ethereal Shift and the second with your ult. [37:56]
- Leaping Slash tech: double jump high to come down closer with the dash, and you can dash into the air if you jump. [38:56]
- Flyers are her biggest enemies (Grey Talon, Lash) because her air hitbox is weak; wait until they land, then nuke them. [39:27]
