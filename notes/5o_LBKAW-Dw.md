# Victor is Back | Deadlock Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-04-11

## Covers
Patch reaction by Midknight to the April 2026 balance patch (2026-04-11): movement, item and hero changes with a verdict on each.
Heroes covered: Bebop, Celeste, Doorman, Grey Talon, Haze, Kelvin, McGinnis, Mirage, Paradox, Rem, Silver, Vex (as captioned), Victor, Vyper. No one coached.

## Claims
- Parrying is now allowed out of ground dashes; verdict bad: it lowers the skill ceiling versus the old dash or IED into parry. [0:00]
- Medic troopers got a small nerf, long overdue. [0:31]
- Subsequent CC reduction was increased again; he thinks needing that mechanic shows the game has too much CC while anti crowd control items keep getting nerfed, and the buff is hasty since the interaction was only fixed about a month ago. [0:31]
- Recharging Rush was nerfed; as long as it outpaces Mirage's scarab cooldown it will still dictate how he builds. [1:02]
- Titanic Magazine got buffs, but in a sliding, infinite ammo meta (slides were also buffed this patch) people will not buy it over an infinite mag plus sliding. [1:02]
- Express Shot was buffed; he likes it but wanted Silver nerfed too, since the item is core to her kit and she is already a problem. [1:02]
- Toxic Bullets got a big nerf: 0.3% less health in the bleed and a slower build up, with slightly more anti heal to compensate. [1:33]
- Verdict on Toxic Bullets: good, it pushes the item back toward a counter or gun anti heal item instead of core on every gun hero, though it will still feel strong on Drifter. [1:33]
- Cult of Sacrifice was buffed; still not as good as before the creep changes, but he likes it and thinks players who love soul inflation items like the egg and Trophy Collector should be buying it. [2:03]
- Return Fire got a small buff. [2:03]
- Spirit Shielding and Weapon Shielding were buffed; they were mostly forgotten, and Spirit Shielding goes unbought because every tier two green item is absurdly good. [2:03]
- Golden Goose Egg: holding it longer than eight minutes is now better, but the late game tech of spam buying eggs for permanent buffs no longer works. [2:34]
- The cake got a small nerf that should stop it being abusable, like on Rem. [2:34]
- Silence Wave got big nerfs; good, since it was dictating the spirit hero meta and bought constantly in high Eternus and pro play. [2:34]
- Silence Wave now needs 142 spirit power to reach the threshold to pop Spell Breaker; he hopes it becomes a utility (silence) item again. [3:04]
- Echo Shard and Refresher should be removed from the game; they only accentuate broken abilities like Ivy's stone form. [3:04]
- Bebop's sticky bomb can be self cast again but deals 20% of the bomb damage to himself; expect bomb Bebop to show up a lot again, especially since cleansed sticky bombs still stay on the ground. [3:34]
- Bebop's grapple arm is now light melee; verdict bad, abilities that proc melee items without risk of being parried are too strong, though at least it is his 3, not his 1. [3:34]
- Celeste changes make her weaker early but scale better; with a health nerf and a small fire rate nerf it is overall a nerf. [4:04]
- Celeste's 2 barrier was reduced, which is good, but he thinks the ability should not silence and should be a defensive damage and utility tool. [4:04]
- Doorman's card can now be removed with Dispel Magic. [4:35]
- Grey Talon got spirit power per boon and other buffs; verdict bad, people skip him because he is boring, not because he lacks spirit nuke damage. [4:35]
- Haze changes are mostly reversions: sleep dagger wake up damage now scales with spirit and the dagger got an overall buff it did not need. [5:05]
- Haze's ultimate is no longer an AOE; it targets one player in front of her, with small buffs to compensate. [5:05]
- Haze is hard countered by Metal Skin; these changes push her back toward being played as a pick hero, especially with the pick role mediocre right now. [5:35]
- Kelvin got small grenade healing changes, not enough; he stays unbalanced as long as his dome cannot be broken. [5:35]
- McGinnis got another gun damage buff; her gun is not the issue, a turret, wall or ult buff would make more sense (the wall did get a buff). [5:35]
- Mirage nerfs were smaller than expected: minus 1 DPS on scarabs and 0.2 less spirit scaling; scarabs still do a lot of HP damage, and the real issue is the extra spirit scaling on the max upgrade. [6:05]
- Paradox got a small gun buff and a slightly bigger wall. [6:36]
- Rem got a massive nerf: the bug or tech where you use his 3 then melee a box was fixed, a big hit to his farming; his 2 was buffed and Rem is still broken. [6:36]
- Vex (as captioned) got trap nerfs moving power from base into spirit scaling; good, but the hero is very strong and should lose more, at least power from the 2. [6:36]
- Victor is back to viable with big buffs: more regen on his 2 and the Aura of Suffering changes walked back, so it does more damage but takes a little longer to ramp. [7:07]
- Victor's Aura of Suffering slow now affects dashes, a huge buff, since dash slows are very strong while normal slows get beaten by good movement. [7:07]
- Vyper got buffs he disagrees with: she terrorizes solo queue, and in pro play she will not be viable as an M1 hero until she gets survivability, since she explodes when focused. [7:37]

## Disagrees with
