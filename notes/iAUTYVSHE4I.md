## Covers
Vindicta gameplay with commentary by aquatiq, trying a borrowed roaming build, laning into a Bebop, April 2026 patch.
Mostly commentary over a game; the useful parts are build notes, Debuff Remover against Bebop and a few patch observations about Vindicta.

## Claims
- The build he tried is a roaming build: lots of damage early, very annoying early, hard to hit because of all the flight, and likely able to one-shot people from half health late game. [0:32]
- He upgrades the crow (Crow Familiar) second. [1:03]
- Against Bebop in lane, pushing up at all gives Bebop a free bomb stack and a lot of damage. [1:35]
- He bought Sprint Boots early. [2:39]
- An assassinate kill no longer gives souls; it now gives weapon damage. [6:51]
- He doesn't see a purpose for Mystic Shot on Vindicta; it is good on heroes like Paradox. [6:51]
- Vindicta's crow is now AoE and hits multiple enemies. [7:23]
- He bought Debuff Remover early against Bebop, plus spirit resist; with it, Bebop's hook no longer worked on him. [9:01, 12:45]
- Some debuffs can no longer be removed by Debuff Remover. [20:01]
- Vindicta is not good at fighting in small enclosed spaces. [19:00]
- You can now melee mid-flight. [16:50]
- After mid boss: get a pick, then take jungle camps (citing Metro). [17:25]
- Trophy Collector is not a good item anymore, even with 16 assists. [22:34]

## Disagrees with
- BiVXd_hmQbo (aquatiq, 2026-04-21): He wished he had bought Trophy Collector earlier on Vindicta. [27:40]
- eM-iY3IvHO8 (Metro Deadlock, 2026-03-12): The Vindicta build buys Trophy Collector right after Rapid Recharge to keep perma fighting in the perma fight meta. [34:47]
