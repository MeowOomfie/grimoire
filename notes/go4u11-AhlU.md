## Covers
Pocket, reviewed by Midknight as "unsolicited coaching": a Pocket on the enemy team of a game he coached on stream.
Short coaching breakdown of why a lower-level spirit carry kept dying the same way: no Dispel Magic into a Warden rushing a cage max.
Patch date not stated (uploaded 2026-07-12).

## Claims
Pocket (lower-level spirit carry, the player reviewed):
- First death: Pocket missed the Barrage stacks and still engaged because Calico ulted, not respecting that the Victor was backing off at 1 HP; even a Calico kill would have ended in a 1v2. [0:00]
- If you miss all your Barrage stacks, back off; with the stacks Pocket probably beats the Calico. [0:31]
- The enemy Warden rushed a cage max because the enemy comp had a Pocket, an Apollo and a Lash; a good play by the Warden. [0:31]
- Still trying to fight the Warden after getting hit by the cage is very bad. [1:02]
- Better movement could have kept Pocket out of the cage while that Warden had no slowing items. [1:02]
- If you keep dying like this as Pocket, pick up Dispel Magic. After Majestic Leap this Pocket bought raw flat filler items, which are good and bring the spirit spike online, but they should have angled for Dispel Magic next knowing the Warden had the silence cage. [1:02]
- Once the Warden gets items like Slowing Bullets or Slowing Hex, good movement will no longer consistently get you out of the cage. [1:32]
- Pocket did not respect the Warden silence because the Warden was behind in the lobby; dying to the silence again. [2:02]
- Hugging the wrong side of the wall kept Pocket inside the cage. [2:02]
- Boosting into the rift and leading with Affliction off Majestic Leap, then Barrage while holding the recast, was a good sequence. [2:33]
- Being caught by Rem's sleep and then woken by an Ivy is not really a mistake by the Ivy, but a Dispel Magic would have given a window to cleanse before the cage. [2:33]
- Counterspell would not be the worst buy either: it is very strong against the Rem ult, Abrams' charge and Ivy's Stone Form, which Dispel Magic does not stop. [3:03]
- Not being able to cleanse the Warden silence, even just 2.8 seconds of silence, is very significant for a carry this squishy who needs abilities. [3:03]
- Pocket was doing well early (3-1), but repeated deaths the same way stopped them from getting a lead. [3:34]
- Following up when the Victor acted as bait, with a big Affliction off the last shell, was good. [3:34]
- Three of Pocket's five deaths were to the Warden cage, which Dispel Magic (formerly Debuff Remover) would have prevented. [4:04]
- The other two deaths were from disrespecting the Calico, and Dispel Magic also gives spirit resist, so it might have saved those too. [4:04]
