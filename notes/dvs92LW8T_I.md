# The Ultimate Guide To Deadlock Laning - 2026
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2026-04-28  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dvs92LW8T_I

## Covers
Laning: wave prio, creep aggro, soul securing and testing denies, cover and high ground, guardian priority, buying early, winning/even/losing lanes, lane synergy (Haze with Abrams or Warden), healing creep and solo heals, guardian parry dives and melee cooldown, when to gank and calling missing, soul splitting with boxes and Sinner's Sacrifice, trading with abilities (Apollo, Warden), ultimates, wave state for trades, HP as a resource (Vindicta), cutting losses, soul and item checks (Rebuttal), Monster Rounds, Headshot Booster, Extra Regen vs Healing Rite

## Claims
- The laning stage is the most misunderstood part of the game and the most important stage for winning. [0:00]
- Wave prio means you always have to shove the wave first; creeps deal very high damage early, and new players who fight the enemy first lose fights because they are not prioritizing the wave. [0:30]
- In every lane, shove the creep wave first and then look for damage on enemy players, not the other way around. [1:01]
- If you walk past your creep wave to shoot an enemy, the enemy minions prioritize whoever is closest, so they target you over your creeps and you take a lot of damage. [1:01]
- You do not have to shoot a soul to get its money; it pops automatically after 2 seconds, but if the enemy shoots it first they get that share, roughly 33% of the overall soul income, so secure souls when the enemy is looking. [1:32]
- If you and the enemy shoot your own (green) soul at the same time, you always get it, so denying souls is usually not worth it. [2:04]
- High-level players test denies in the first or second wave; if the enemy last hits well and you get few denies, prioritize everything else, since denies cost time in which you cannot damage the enemy or shoot creeps for wave prio; if the enemy is bad at securing and you get lots of denies, keep going. [2:35]
- Always play cover; in the open you are an easy target for every hero and ability, and cover, high ground or veils let you trade efficiently. [3:38]
- Auto-fire heroes should peek around corners to give a small hitbox; taking high ground on the bridges and shooting down makes trading hard for the enemy. [3:38]
- Melee-centered tanks like Abrams or Billy can play around the veils and close cover to get melees on creeps or damage on enemies. [4:08]
- The number one priority in lane is killing the enemy guardian, but not by dying three to five times for it, which gives the enemy a gold lead and they take your guardian soon after. [4:08]
- The guardian takes very little damage early and gets easier to kill as the game goes, so play your life first, then the guardian. [4:39]
- Always prioritize buying items early; as soon as you have the first 800 after the first wave, go back and buy your first 800 item, and go back to shop every time you have the money for the next item. [4:39]
- There are three kinds of lanes, winning, even and losing, and which one you are in changes how you play; decide out of spawn by looking at the heroes on both sides. [5:09]
- Synergy decides lanes: Haze and Abrams are not amazing laners alone but strong together, since a Haze sleep sets up a free Abrams charge; with Haze and Warden, the sleep sets up a free cage. [6:10]
- In a winning lane, shove waves and then damage the enemy players or tower; a big mistake is killing the wave and going back to farm boxes when you could keep pressure on. [6:40]
- In a winning lane keep enemies low and force mistakes, take trades you come out on top in and pressure the guardian to win early, which opens the map for ganks and roams. [7:10]
- In an even lane play like a winning lane: shove first, trade when you can, and if you end up on the enemy tower a lot, dive and commit, or steal enemy boxes and the tier one camps that spawn at two minutes. [7:40]
- If an even lane turns out to put a lot of pressure on you, play more passive and farm your own boxes and creep camps, but still play for the win; proactive play forces the enemy to react and make the first mistake. [8:11]
- In a losing lane you can still come out on top in money: play safe, use longer-range cover and make sure the enemy creeps die first. [9:12]
- As long as you kill the enemy wave, the enemy cannot tower dive you or trade well; with more creeps they cannot dive, with even creeps you still get decent trades. [9:43]
- If the enemy keeps shoving to your tower, they cannot do much tower damage in the first few waves; secure your own souls, then farm boxes or creep camps after the wave. [9:43]
- Killing the enemy healing creep drops a healing orb that heals everyone in a radius, split between them, so with an ally the heal is 50/50; it is usually not good to kill the healing creep first unless you are already low. [10:44]
- Especially in the first few waves when you are full HP, kill the healing creep last; killing it first wastes the heal while an enemy who kills theirs last gets healed. [11:15]
- Later, if you are low and scared of dying, it is okay to kill the healing creep first on the next wave to get regen back. [11:46]
- Solo heal: if you are low and your ally is healthy, ask for a solo heal, kill the healing creep with your ally standing further away, and you get the full heal; high ranks do this a lot. [11:46]
- Guardians focus the closest target and do very high damage early, so when you shove into a guardian always keep creeps in front of you. [12:16]
- To dive a low enemy hiding behind the guardian, dash or walk into melee range and parry the guardian's melee; it will not attack for a second, enough to finish the enemy. [12:46]
- A guardian dive only works if you are healthy and the enemy is really low; diving a half-HP enemy in the first four or five minutes almost certainly gets you killed after the parry window. [13:17]
- When diving, parry the guardian first and then damage the enemy, almost always. [13:17]
- A guardian parry sometimes fails because the guardian's melee has a cooldown and it melees creeps too; make sure it has not meleed in the last second or two before you try to parry. [13:47]
- Gank scenario one: you have lots of wave prio and lane control; shove into the enemy guardian, then leave, so the enemy cannot tell whether you are buying, farming boxes or rotating. [14:48]
- Gank scenario two: the map shows multiple low enemies in another lane; even mid-fight you can rotate and get a free kill 3v2. [15:18]
- Gank scenario three: enemies in other lanes are deep under your allies' towers, exposed with a long walk back to their guardian. [15:18]
- Jump pads are usually the fastest way between lanes; the worst case of a failed gank is losing maybe one wave. [16:18]
- Call missing when your laner has shoved your wave to your tower and disappears from the map, by voice or command, so teammates in other lanes play safer; he often dies because teammates did not call missing. [16:48]
- Soul splitting: two laners split souls 50/50, so when nothing is happening one player stays and takes every last hit safely while the other runs boxes, farms tier one camps or ganks. [17:48]
- Soul splitting is especially good in Dispatch with one player good at clearing creeps fast; you get double the enemy's lane souls while your partner still earns from boxes or ganks. [18:19]
- If the lane drags past eight minutes, keep one player in lane while the other rotates to secure your Sinner's Sacrifices, or steal the enemy's, for more than twice the enemy's souls. [18:50]
- Avoid slugfests of heroes shooting each other instead of the waves; secure souls, play cover and use abilities whenever you see an opening. [19:50]
- Abilities are a resource that drains enemy health; every second an ability sits off cooldown unused is lost, for example Apollo's first ability with about an 8 second cooldown can damage an enemy or shove the wave. [19:50]
- Save long combo cooldowns like Warden's cage for a key moment, such as after your teammate's stun or your flask lands. [20:22]
- Track enemy ultimates; many early ultimates are fight winning, so if the enemy is one or two hundred souls ahead and gets their ultimate first they can get a free kill. [20:52]
- If three or more enemy creeps are alive and none of yours, you will never have a favorable trade even if you out-aim the enemy, because the creeps' damage adds up. [21:23]
- Engage head on with at most two enemy creeps left, ideally when your creeps are alive and theirs are dead; wave state matters more than any ability or how strong your hero is. [21:54]
- Common mistake at every rank: securing souls when no one is around; souls secure automatically, so each one you shoot uncontested wastes a second you could spend pushing the tower or backing. [22:24]
- If after a few waves the enemy never tries to deny or steal your souls, stop worrying about them and just kill the creeps. [22:55]
- Your health is a resource like cooldowns and money: spending 200 HP to deal 300 to 400 is always good, and an even 200-for-200 trade is good if you have more regen from a tank's base regen or Extra Regen. [23:25]
- If your ally is low and you are full HP, step in front and trade your own HP, even on a hero like Vindicta; being too scared to lose health loses games. [23:56]
- Even slightly bad trades are fine when you are high HP and the enemy is half, since every wave has a healing creep; players who hide under tower only last hitting lose their tower early and miss kills and guardian damage. [24:26]
- The goal of the lane is to kill the enemy guardian; more map control wins more games. [24:56]
- If you are getting rolled and always hiding under tower, it is okay at 5 to 6 minutes to let your guardian die, cut your losses and farm safely under your walker; some lanes are just designed that way. [24:56]
- Always check the enemy soul count and items; early items like Rebuttal can turn a fight. [25:27]
- If the enemy is 500 to 1K souls ahead at any point in laning they are probably significantly stronger, so play safer; a 1K lead of your own at 5 to 7 minutes means you can play more aggressive. [25:27]
- Monster Rounds lets you kill the enemy wave 25% faster on most heroes if you shoot your gun, which is why it is bought so widely; wave prio is number one. [25:58]
- Headshot Booster is the top aggressive lane item, a flat chunk of damage every few seconds, for heroes that want to trade and poke. [26:28]
- The two most common defensive lane items are Extra Regen and Healing Rite; buy Healing Rite after a big trade that left you very low, pop it and play safe until it runs out. [26:58]
- Healing Rite heals more than Extra Regen over the long run but cancels if you get hit; if you are getting low but can still trade and play the lane, Extra Regen is better since it always heals, in or out of a fight. [26:58]
- Other early items depend on the hero: some go early spirit items for spirit nuking, some tanks build early greens, others build gun items. [27:29]
- Summary: read the matchup, always shove the wave first, trade a lot if you can win, otherwise shove and then run boxes and jungle camps on your side; at 6 to 8 minutes when medium camps or Sinner's Sacrifices spawn, split up with one in lane and one farming jungle or ganking. [28:00]
