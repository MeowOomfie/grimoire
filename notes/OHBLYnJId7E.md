# They Added Bans to Deadlock!!!! | Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-01-31
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OHBLYnJId7E

## Covers
Patch reaction to the 2026-01-31 Deadlock patch by Midknight, a Pocket main, reading the notes line by line with a verdict on each.
Covers the new hero bans, global and item changes (Curse, Fury Trance, Debuff Remover rework into Dispel Magic, Rapid Recharge), hero changes (Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Lash, Mina, Shiv, Viscous, Vyper, Vindicta and more) and Street Brawl.
Overall verdict: bans are huge, but the balance side is "not enough" for the strongest heroes after a long wait.

## Claims
- Hero bans added in Standard and Street Brawl: you select one hero in the roster screen to tell the matchmaker you prefer to avoid it; two total bans per game for now, and you are told which heroes are banned. [0:01]
- New heroes in their initial release window are not affected by bans. [0:01]
- He expects bans to be increased slowly until there is a real draft, and calls this a good way of doing bans before draft. [0:01]
- Parties of three or more now need an additional hero in their roster; you now need five people to do a lockout. [0:31]
- Fire rate increases and reductions from most abilities and items were reduced globally by a small amount. [0:31]
- Upgrades with bullet resist reduction now apply increased damage on subsequent hits rather than the initial hit, so Hollow Point works more like an amp. [0:31]
- Dash speed was added to the vitality stat screen, so you can see what speed bucket a hero is in without testing. [1:01]
- Kinetic Dash bonus ammo no longer scales with percent ammo increases (for example a +2% ammo statue). [1:01]
- Intensifying Magazine time to max damage reduced and it now gives weapon damage instead of fire rate; he questions nerfing an already solid item but sees the vision, since the game has too much fire rate and not enough weapon damage. [1:01]
- Hollow Point flat nerf of 3%; he likes it. [1:31]
- Alchemical Fire spirit scaling reduced by a lot; it hurts but he thinks it is still viable because it was so overpowered. [1:31]
- Glass Cannon penalty increased (less max HP); he thinks this is the wrong fix and it should give far less flat weapon damage (he suggests 40% base plus about 10% per stack) so the stacks matter, instead of 80% flat weapon damage. [1:31]
- Lucky Shot was nerfed and he thanks them for it. [2:02]
- Capacitor duration reduced and cooldown reduced; he thinks the cooldown makes up for it. [2:02]
- Melee Lifesteal light melee cooldown multiplier reduced from 2x to 1.5x, a buff to melee heroes. [2:32]
- Extra Regen got more out of combat regen, roughly a 25% buff; it does not matter in fights, but he might start buying it over "resto". [2:32]
- Restorative Locket cooldown reduced and spirit resist buffed, but it is now self cast only and restores three stamina based on its stacks relative to max; he does not think people will buy it now (it was mostly bought by supports and ability spammers). [3:02]
- Fury Trance nerf is far too small: it still gives more bullet lifesteal than the item called Bullet Lifesteal, plus 40% resist and 40% fire rate, and still counts as green (vitality) investment after being broken for 6 months. [3:32]
- He suggests Fury Trance should lose about 10% spirit resist or be moved to orange so it stops granting green investment, since it is not really a defensive item. [4:04]
- Stamina Mastery nerf makes sense. [4:04]
- Metal Skin cooldown increased; it is still the best bullet counter and he will still buy it every game. [4:04]
- Bullet Resilience and Spirit Resilience low HP thresholds increased, which he calls buffing tanks in a tank meta. [4:04]
- Debuff Remover is renamed Dispel Magic, no longer builds from Debuff Reducer, no longer grants 30% debuff resistance or HP, now grants spirit resist, and its move speed is reduced. [4:34]
- Debuff Reducer debuff resistance raised from 20% to 25% and it no longer grants +50 health; it becomes a tier 2 debuff counter that he says he will never buy. [4:34]
- Unstoppable gets bonus health; Spellbreaker now builds out of Debuff Reducer, which he concedes means people will actually buy Debuff Reducer. [5:05]
- Soulmaker (as captioned) spirit resist reduced, making it more about its active, which it already was. [5:35]
- Inhibitor was buffed; he disagrees, since a low buy rate does not mean it is not still a strong item. [5:35]
- An item giving +40 health could now be decent; health matters in the current health meta because healing creeps heal missing health; he might start buying it again over Extra Regen. [6:05]
- Coldfront is a buff even though spread duration went up (0.5 to about 6 as captioned): the aura moves with your hitbox, so if you pop it and then dash it travels around you and still hits people; expanding slower is better. Same for Arctic Blast. [6:05]
- Quicksilver Reload bonus spirit damage now scales with spirit; at around 170 spirit power that is only about 28 extra damage, but not insignificant. [7:07]
- Rapid Recharge time between charged abilities reduced from +50 to +30, which he loves (no more instantly dumping charges like arrows or chained Dynamo kicks), but it now also reduces charged ability cooldowns by 14%, which he calls a terrible change giving charge heroes more CDR. [7:37]
