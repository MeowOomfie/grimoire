# Every Deadlock Character's REAL Role (Updated 5 Role Guide)
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2026-10-21  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JouUab99B4Y

## Covers
Role system (carry, off carry, support, initiator/catch, frontliner/tank, hybrid), what each role does with its time, farm priority, when to fight, all heroes assigned to a role

## Claims
### The role system
- Roles in Deadlock are an open system; most heroes can be played several ways. [0:00]
- Ranked matchmaking gives a team at least some tanks, at least one support and at least one carry; make sure you have at least one of each, because without a tank it is hard to take fights and without healing or support it is hard to navigate the map since everyone needs farm. [0:30]
- Three main categories: carries, off carries and supports. Share the soul farm in that order: carries first, off carries second, supports last. Initiator, tank and hybrid are sub roles. [26:05]
- Golden rule: the more you are ahead (the higher your money), the more you value your life and the safer you play; the more you are behind, the riskier you play. [5:33]
- The team that catches the enemy first in mid and late game fights usually wins the fight, from Initiate up to Eternus. [17:20] [17:52] [18:23]
- The system is not set in stone: an off carry or support close to a big item (Refresher on Dynamo, Boundless Spirit on Apollo) can ask the carry for a side lane for 500 to 1,000 souls, then fight. Communicate and plan ahead. [16:18]
- Games are lost both to supports that AFK farm the whole game and to carries that force fights and never farm enough. Know your role; pick a hybrid if you want to be versatile. [26:37]
- This list could change with a patch; heroes still play similarly to his list from almost a year ago. He expects Valve to add a draft mode that assigns heroes to roles. [27:07]

### Carry
- What a carry is: needs more farm than other heroes to reach full potential; scales through items, stat growth (boon per level: HP, weapon damage, spirit power, on some heroes movement or sprint speed) or high value at max ability levels. [1:00] [1:31]
- Play carries selfishly: the extra money turns them into the team's main damage threat in the mid or late game; before that they are not scary and need many purchases to be useful in a team fight. [2:01] [2:31]
- Not every carry has a weak lane, but on average carries are weaker in lane than mid game off carries. [2:31]
- Being the carry makes you the most important player in the late game only, not the whole game. [2:31]
- For everyone, the highest priority is to kill the enemy carry and to protect and give farm to your own carry; taking a carry's farm keeps them useless longer. [3:01]
- A good carry farms a lot, efficiently and safely, and knows when to show up in team fights; it is not AFK farming everything. [3:01]
- Rough rule: once you get your first big item that raises your power and damage a lot, start looking for team fights; before that play selfish. [3:31]
- When a big fight happens at a Rift, a carry can split push; many carries are left-click heroes that kill objectives well, and solo killing a Walker gives a lot of money. [3:31] [4:02]
- Farm on a side lane: it is the safest place, you get the lane farm solo and can clear many creep camps; most fights and deaths happen near mid lane and the center of the map because the map is a circle. Most carries are strong at clearing jungle and good at staying alive in 1v1s. [4:02] [4:32]
- Dying as a carry is very bad: it delays your power and carries tend to hold many unsecured souls, so you feed the enemy a lot of money. [5:02]
- Avoid joining a fight first or getting caught first; join after your tank or initiators engage, even when you feel strong or are the most farmed. [5:33]
- Mirror the map: be on the opposite side of the fight and split push the other side; when every enemy is missing, play far back, that is the scariest moment for a carry. [6:03] [6:34]
- Exception: carries with strong team fight ultimates, like Seven or Rat King, should join a nearby Rift fight when the ultimate is up. Join about 10-20% of fights when they are good (for example you are up numbers). [6:34] [7:06]
- Most carries come online around the first 6K item, roughly 20 to 25K net worth; at 30K plus you most definitely want to fight. [7:06] [7:36]
- A big carry mistake is to keep farming after the power spike; the team made space for you, so flip a switch in mid game and start joining fights. After a won fight you can take mid boss and still farm. [7:36] [8:07]
- Carries ending at 40 to 50K net worth with one fight joined and 5K to 10K damage are doing it wrong. [8:07]

### Carry heroes
- Carries: Infernus, Lady Geist, Mirage, Rat King, Seven, Venator, Victor, Warden, Wraith. [0:30] [1:00]
- Seven: every ability upgrade at max level adds a lot of damage; the ball's last level adds DPS and makes it much bigger, the stun lasts more than twice as long at max, Power Surge basically does not exist until maxed, Storm Cloud gets higher DPS. [1:31] [2:01]
- Wraith: needs many items to get online; okay in lane but low damage; her first few 6K items turn her from a lane creep into very strong. [2:01]
- Wraith at 15K net worth loses every 1v1 and team fight to Apollo; at 40K Wraith does ridiculous team fight damage and solo kills the Apollo. [9:41]
- Wraith: sit on a side lane and split push when a fight happens elsewhere and you are strong enough. [3:31] [4:02]
- Seven and Rat King: they need farm, but with the ultimate up (Rat King's flag, Seven's ult) join Rift fights near you. [6:34]

### Off carry
- Off carries come online earlier than carries, some during or right after laning, most around 15K net worth, which is when they are strongest; they are the strongest heroes in the mid game. [8:40]
- Off carries do not want to farm the whole game like a carry; if they spend their timing farming, the game goes late, they fall off and the carries do more and more. [9:10] [9:41]
- Farm when there is nothing else to do, but after your power spike farming is not the number one priority. [10:11]
- Being the highest net worth on the team as an off carry is good if you got there by hitting your spike and killing the enemy over and over (the on-fire meter), bad if you got there by farming. Sitting in jungle on Mina non-stop while your team dies is not right. [10:41]
- All off carries on the list are very strong in lane (Celeste a slight exception, she is kind of weak). Win your lane, then pressure other lanes, create kills, take objectives, join team fights, and farm waves and jungle only when there is nothing else. [11:12]
- Take unoccupied waves and push out the wave when you are alone on a side; steal enemy Sinners and enemy farm. [11:12] [11:43]
- Be in fights, most definitely Rift fights; get your lead by controlling the map and shutting down the enemy carries. [11:43]
- Fighting does not mean feeding: take smart fights, like a won fight followed by a Walker push. [12:14]
- Main off carry mistakes: farming away your strongest timing, or fighting too much. [12:14]
- Some heroes are arguably as strong as carries with a lot of money but cannot farm efficiently or a lot, so they are not carries; Drifter is one. [5:02]

### Off carry heroes
- Off carries: Apollo, Celeste, Calico, Mina, Pocket, Yamato, Grey Talon, Vindicta, Lash, Vyper, Violet. [8:40]
- Apollo: strongest he will ever be at around 12 to 15K net worth, when his core and main combo are ready; that is when he wants to fight. [9:10]
- Apollo near Boundless Spirit can ask for a side lane to finish it. [16:18]
- Celeste: slight exception to the strong-lane rule, kind of weak. [11:12]

### Support
- Supports have powerful abilities at all points in the game without needing much money: heals, disruptions, abilities that get teammates out, or big ultimates that need no net worth (like Black Hole). They can have the highest impact while being the poorest player. [12:44] [13:14]
- They work without an expensive damage build; buying damage items with spare money is fine, but never sit and farm in the mid game. [13:14] [13:46]
- Support priorities: first push lanes (number one priority on everyone), then help win team fights, save teammates or look for fights. [13:46]
- Number one support mistake: picking a support and farming the entire game. Only farm if you had a really bad lane and are far behind after lane; around or a bit below team net worth, fighting is the priority. Carries and off carries do more with farm. [13:46] [14:16]
- Support is an unthankful job; he does not play it himself. [14:16] [14:46]
- Never take farm off your carry: do not share a side lane or clear the jungle next to them. Go help the carry if they are under pressure. [14:46] [15:17]
- A good support is about positioning, map awareness and using cooldowns correctly. [15:17]
- If your team AFK farms, do not AFK farm too; communicate, call the next objective or play, and collect what nobody picks up. If your team overfights, keep side lanes pushed and do not force a fight alone. [15:17] [15:48]
- Most supports will not win a 1v1; they help win easy team fights or 2v1s. Have a teammate with you at all times. [15:48]

### Support heroes
- Supports: The Doorman, Dynamo, Kelvin, Mo & Krill, Nurse Harrow, Paige, Rem, Sinclair, Solomon. [12:14] [12:44]
- Dynamo: no matter how poor, a big Black Hole wins the fight; in mid or late game buy Refresher and Unstoppable or Indomitable to get it off safely, and little else is needed. [13:14]
- Dynamo near Refresher can ask for side lane souls to finish it. [16:18]

### Initiator / catch
- Initiators/catch heroes: Haze, Holliday, Paradox, Bebop. Haze could fit carry or off carry and Bebop could fit tank, but these four have a job no other hero really does: start the fight and catch the enemy. [16:49] [17:20]
- Their catch tools: hook (Bebop), lasso (Holliday), Paradox swap, sleep dart (Haze), used to start a fight and get a pick. [17:20]
- You do not need an initiator at all times, but in high rank, pro and Eternus meta there is almost always one catch hero; in recent Night Shift games one team played Holliday and the other Paradox almost every time. [17:20] [17:52]
- Job: find an isolated or vulnerable enemy and start the fight on your terms. [17:52]
- Haze is not a hard carry: she does well with gun damage if farmed, but catching is her strongest part; she is very squishy, low HP and has no good way out of a fight, so even at 60K net worth, highest in the lobby, she is easy to kill. Get money, then roam for pick-offs. [18:23] [18:54]
- These heroes have late game damage builds (gun Holliday, carbine Paradox, gun Haze and gun Bebop), but the main value is always the catch. [19:25]
- Rotate with purpose and stay hidden so the enemy must play safe, or you get a free kill every time your ultimate comes off cooldown. [19:25]
- Main mistake: disappearing into the jungle and AFK farming like everyone else, even on Haze. [19:25] [19:55]

### Frontliner / tank
- Frontliners/tanks: Abrams, Baba, Billy, Shiv, Silver. Yamato could be added because she is also very tanky. [19:55] [20:27]
- Tanks have high durability, sustain and defensive abilities; their job is to go to the front and absorb the enemy's main damage (usually the carry or off carry), or disrupt those heroes. [19:55] [20:27]
- Tanks are not weak in this game: on Billy you can go in, chain the enemy carry and get a kill. [20:27]
- Tanks almost always want to be in the team fight; as the only tank, do not play scared, go in first and take the hits like Overwatch tanks, or your carries and off carries die instantly. It requires an aggressive play style. Maybe you die first, but if you absorb and create the opening your team wins. [20:58] [21:29]
- Tanks need some farm: quite a few green items, and on Abrams also some gun or purple items to stay alive. [21:59]
- Rotate to Rift fights instead of split pushing; tanks do not deal damage well, so an Abrams split pushing a Walker during a Rift fight usually loses the fight and does not get the Walker. [21:59] [22:29]
- Be the first in the mid boss pit for any mid boss contest; tanks have stuns and displacement to stop the enemy (Abrams ultimate and charge, Billy chain). [22:29]
- Baba does ridiculous damage and is an anti-spirit hero, but is played as a frontliner and must be built very tanky in the current meta; she goes in and out like a disruptor and in Eternus games usually absorbs some of the most damage in team fights. [22:59]

### Hybrid
- Hybrids: Graves, Drifter, Ivy, McGinnis, Viscous. They adapt to the team composition and can contribute with low or high income. [22:59] [23:30]
- Ivy: with no support on the team, be the poorest player and buy only support items; with no carry, play full gun carry and farm a lot. She can also go full spirit, tanky or damage heavy. [23:30] [24:00]
- Graves: go spirit heavy and permanently split push, or go supporty with Suppressor and debuff items and show up to team fights. [24:30]
- McGinnis: full spirit so your lane cannot get pushed, full gun build to play carry when the team lacks damage, or support heavy. [24:30]
- The build must match the play style: support heavy means farm little; filling the carry or off carry gap means farm more. [24:30] [25:01]
- Main hybrid mistake: always building one way. Ivy going full gun and greedy AFK farming next to a Wraith and a Venator loses; so does support Ivy on a team with no damage. Make the call at the start of the game from your team comp. [25:01] [25:33] [26:05]
