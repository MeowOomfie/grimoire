# The Big Problem With Deadlock Right Now
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-03-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=29qdxbvdbBQ

## Covers
An opinion piece on the March 2025 patch: the coach argues jungle farm is now worth too little, so staying in lane as a duo and permanent teamfighting beat every other choice and farm-hungry carries lose their place.
Useful for players deciding between lane, jungle, ganks and invades after 8 minutes in that patch, and for the coach's account of the old trade-offs.

## Claims
- In this patch the skill gap between good and bad players is very small, so it is hard to abuse. [0:00]
- Feedback he collected from other players: deathballing is very strong, the item nerfs were good, but many jungle changes were bad, and having all objectives at 10 minutes means fighting forever. [0:30]
- With permanent fighting there is little reason to pick late game heroes; you can play Abrams, fight for 20 minutes and win. [1:00]
- He gets ahead in games not by AFK farming the jungle, which anyone can do, but by making macro choices and farming more efficiently than the average player. [1:30]
- His plan for this patch is to pick simple heroes like Calico and sit in lane. [2:00]
- Right now sitting in a lane as two at any point in the game loses you no souls: you earn as much as, or more than, a player trying to jungle. [2:30]
- Two players stacked in a lane are hard to gank: the enemy needs at least three people, and you have to play really poorly to die quickly in a 2v2. [3:00]
- Until this patch every play style was viable: hard carries, gankers like Mo & Krill, supports like Kelvin, and mid game carries like Pocket and Lash all had a place. [3:31]
- Now there is no point playing a hero that needs farm because you cannot get farm; pick a hero that is strong in teamfights at all times. [4:02]
- The issue is losing the choice: coming back from spawn on a farming hero there used to be a real choice between farming jungle and farming lane. [5:04]
- A jungle camp he cleared gave 65 souls per creep, 65 x 3 total, which he says is less than two lane creeps. [5:34]
- Another camp worth 163 plus 65 was less than three creeps in the lane. [6:05]
- He agrees with the balance changes to items: Cold Front needed a nerf and Holliday needed a nerf; he says Seven is still overpowered. [6:05]
- At 8 minutes, in a duo lane, creeps stop sharing souls fully, so each player gets only half (now 60%). [6:35]
- The old 8 minute choices: (1) stay in lane with your partner and push, (2) rotate and farm your jungle, (3) rotate to another lane, gank, get a kill and push that lane, (4) invade the enemy jungle to steal it or kill someone there. [7:07]
- Staying in lane: downside is fewer souls because creeps no longer share; upside is a safer tower and a higher chance to kill the enemy tower or even Walker, keeping pressure on. [7:37]
- Farming your jungle: downside is losing pressure on the enemy lane and risking your teammates' lanes being pushed; upside is more souls. This trade-off existed in every patch. [8:08]
- Ganking another lane: you lose souls while rotating, but a kill punishes them twice, with kill souls and by pushing that objective, and ganks are less predictable than sitting in lane. [8:38]
- Invading the enemy jungle is high risk high reward: denied souls are worth even more than your own jungle and a kill there gives a big head start, but you may get nothing, die, or be stopped and waste your time. [8:38]
- In this patch only one or two options remain, sitting in lane or ganking another lane, because the jungle is not worth enough and enemies are not there to kill when you invade. [9:38]
- Ganking is also weaker than before because of the three lane map: the main way to rotate is the zipline, walking takes longer. [9:38]
- Splitting a lane at 8 minutes gives both people the same souls as before, which is less, but still more than going to the jungle. [11:39]
- New tier two camps are worth about the same as the old tier one camps. [12:09]
- Jungling is still viable if you are winning at around 20 minutes because you can clear very fast; before that, do not jungle, fight permanently. [12:09]
- If you win your lane you probably win the game: the losing team cannot come back by invading your empty jungle or farming their own, so they keep fighting and the richer team wins. [12:09]
- Laning is the only matchup that matters now: pick a lane bully that is very strong early and mid game and you win. [12:39]
