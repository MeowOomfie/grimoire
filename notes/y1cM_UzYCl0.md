## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream right after the April Fools event ended, reviewing replays from just before the latest minor patch (the vitality tree change).
Four students: a Pocket (Oracle lobby) on an all-utility Idol build, an Apollo, a Haze (Ascendant), and a Viscous in a lane against Lash and Mirage with a roaming Grey Talon partner. Between sessions there is a Q&A on Ivy, Infernus, carry Haze, tempo buys and how well each role carries, then a battle royale event with no coaching.
Themes: box routes and box maxing, when to back to buy, parrying with purpose, fight only with an advantage or a purpose, playing the Urn and comeback Urn, mid boss trades, invading and stealing farm, utility-heavy builds, Tank Buster timing, and anchoring a lane.
