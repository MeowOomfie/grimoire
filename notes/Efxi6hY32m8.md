# The Major Patch is Finally Here | Deadlock Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-09-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Efxi6hY32m8

## Covers
Patch reaction by Midknight to the big 2026-09-30 update: he reads the patch page live, then walks the reworked map in a lobby.
Topics: map visual rework and new verticality, jungle rework with interactive camps, statues, heal snacks, smoke patches, the Broker and corrupted items, six new heroes and release voting, HUD and shop changes, parry and Sinner's Sacrifice changes, mid boss stairs, and his meta predictions.

## Claims
Patch page:
- The patch is a giant map overhaul with a massive visual update, six new heroes, quality of life and accessibility additions; the map overhaul is what took so long. [0:00]
- Mid lane is now square and there are many new movement spots to learn. [0:30]
- The jungle rework adds new creature types with different behavior that vary by district, and a lot of new camps. [0:30]
- The map adds a lot of verticality, which means Lash is going to be a problem. [2:01]
- Breakable boxes are now spread on the map; he hopes Calico can open them in cat form, or it is a big Calico nerf. [2:32]
- Golden statues now also roll spirit resist, bullet resist, ability range and raw move speed as permanent buffs; raw move speed is insane. [2:32]
- Healing snacks let a keen-eyed player heal up in a pinch. [3:02]
- Refreshing scenery (smoke) gives invisibility while you stand in it. [3:02]
- The Broker is a limited-time trader: from about 30 minutes, then roughly every 15 minutes, you can exchange high-tier items for corrupted versions with random negative attributes, the same for all players in a game. He hates adding raw RNG and calls it tilting. [3:32]
- Broker items are in ranked but only for a few days as a patch gimmick. [4:32]
- Six new heroes, including a new support and Rat King; two release per week on Tuesdays and Fridays at 2:00 pm, voted in the pre-game lobby, where the winning team's votes count double. [4:32]
- New health bars show rejuv, ult and stamina. Verdict: a mistake that makes the game baby mode and removes the skill of tracking someone's stamina. [5:32]
- Camps now show the souls they will drop. [6:02]
- The improved stats panel is really good for testing, though he won't look at it in a team fight. [6:32]
- The scoreboard now shows souls per kill, which he likes; it used to be guesswork. [7:02]
- The shop now shows popular item buys from live player data. Verdict: a mistake, fewer people will think about itemizing and will just buy left to right. [7:33]
- You can now ping when you are close to buying an item, and clustered minimap enemies are a good piece of information. [8:05]
- Your barrier cooldown is now shown near the reticle, which is fine. [9:07]
- Custom lobbies allow eight spectators, which makes community tournaments watchable. [9:38]
- A real report function was added. [11:10]
- Hidden but massive: parrying objectives no longer resets your parry cooldown. When people parry at your tower you can punish them with heavy melees, which makes diving healthy targets harder. [11:41]
- You can no longer sync up Sinner's Sacrifice camps; lots of muscle memory down the drain. [11:41]
- A Flying Strike bug fix means it now paths correctly instead of failing on walls. [12:11]
- Players can now see team minimaps in a live match. [12:11]

Walking the map:
- Neutral camps are no longer just damage checks: creatures move, avoid targeting and have attacks you can dodge, so you have to kite them. [12:41]
- Spirit resist from statues objectively makes spirit heroes worse, because spirit heroes don't have answers to spirit resist; bullet resist as a stat is garbage. [13:11]
- A veil was moved back on one lane, which makes that position much more defensible and hard to face check, while still useful for diving. [13:42]
- All buffs can now give spirit or weapon resist. [14:44]
- Camps come in tiers per creature type. Gun heroes still look by far the best at farming; spirit and melee heroes find the new camps a pain to farm. [15:14]
- Camps do not heal when they de-aggro. [15:14]
- Standing in a smoke patch gives invisibility and about 5 regen, like a bush; that is a strong spot for assassins. [15:44]
- Some Broker corruptions are huge, like minus 3 meters move speed or a big item cooldown change; the values vary. [16:44]
- In the fog zone you can't regenerate stamina, so it will be a kill zone where you can kite people who have no stamina. [17:46]
- Heal snacks respawn at guaranteed spots and gave about 80 HP early; the heal scales as the game goes on. [18:53]
- The new raw stat scaling goes crazy on gun heroes; Wraith is going to have a field day. [19:24]
- Mid boss kept the same model and attack, and tunnels feel dead now. [19:54]
- The mid boss stairs moved: the choke is wider and the stairs less steep. Before, an M1 could farm the stairs and kite back while enemies died; now there is room to get run at, which changes mid boss contests completely. [20:24]
- Valve plans mid boss, Guardians and walkers for upcoming updates. [20:55]
- Common mistake after a patch: assuming the meta resets. Heroes that benefit from choke points lose value since the map has fewer chokes, and a broken hero like Mina can be overshadowed by new heroes and come back unnerfed as the meta hero in two months, or just stay broken. [21:26]
- The map is much more open, and open maps benefit heroes like Lash. [22:27]
- A Lash meta tends to hurt a Vindicta meta; Grey Talon's birds might be a little stronger. [23:30]
- A good box route plus heal snack route could stop you from having to reset. [24:30]
