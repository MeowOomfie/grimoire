## Covers
Deathy's second session with SirActionSlacks, an Abrams player who climbed from Oracle 5 to Phantom 4 since the first session.
They review his latest Abrams game, then Deathy gives three focus points and watches him play a fresh game.
Topics: fighting for wave priority, the 4:30 wave and bridge buff, the Urn vs split pushing, Warp Stone vs rushing Phantom Strike, side lane ganks and Abrams' role as a tank initiator.
