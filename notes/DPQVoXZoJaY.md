## Covers
Midknight Vods, a matchmaking coach, reviews four replays: the last part of an Emissary Celeste review left over from the previous stream, a Calico (rank given as Sentinel in his thumbnail setup), a Phantom Violet laning with a Paige, and a Mystic Violet laning against a Lash.
Between reviews he answers chat about Violet and Calico builds, Heroic Aura, Silence Wave, Sinclair after his nerf, and tests Nurse Harrow's fear in the sandbox.
Recurring themes: dead time and box farming between waves, playing for item spikes (green spike, gun spike), using the ult much more, overextending, splitting waves with three players, setting the mid wave before Rifts, mid boss after kills, confirming orbs, and sunk cost decisions.
