## Covers
Breakdown of the 2026-01-31 balance update: hero bans in both Standard and Street Brawl, the big item nerfs (Curse, Debuff Remover becoming Dispel Magic, late game gun items) and hero winners and losers.
Heroes Abrams, Billy, Bebop, Drifter, Grey Talon, Ivy, Kelvin, Lash, Mina, Viscous, Paige, Vyper, Vindicta, Warden, Graves; items Curse, Dispel Magic, Spellbreaker, Unstoppable, Mystic Reverb, Rapid Recharge, Spirit Resilience.
For ranked players deciding what to ban and what to stop auto-buying.

## Claims
- Hero bans are now in both modes, Street Brawl and Standard: each player picks one hero to ban at match start, and the two most popular bans across both teams are banned for both sides. [0:00]
- There is no separate pick and ban phase, so bans add no time before the game. [0:30]
- Mina will be a fan favorite ban, at least at high rank; the coach will ban Mina basically every game. [0:30]
- Party stacking change: you now need one more player in your party to guarantee a hero. [1:01]
- Late game gun items got the biggest nerfs; Glass Cannon and Lucky Shot were both hit quite hard. [1:01]
- Debuff Remover is renamed Dispel Magic, no longer builds out of Debuff Reducer and no longer grants 30% debuff resistance; it still dispels negative effects like Slowing Hex and Bebop's bombs. [1:31]
- Verdict on Dispel Magic: a much needed nerf, since Debuff Remover was a must buy on most heroes; still good, but no longer a brain dead buy. [1:31]
- Instead of Dispel Magic you can now buy Unstoppable or Spellbreaker, which both got big buffs; Unstoppable gives more debuff resistance. [2:02]
- Spellbreaker now builds out of Debuff Reducer; it reduces debuffs by 25% and gives a big amount of spirit resist (spirit resist numbers nerfed a bit), and is better overall because you can get Debuff Reducer early and reach Spellbreaker without saving up 6k. [2:02]
- Curse had been a must buy on every hero because it stops the enemy from playing; it no longer gives weapon damage and now gives a total negative 8% damage penalty on everything (spells, gun, melee) while held. [2:33]
- Do not buy Curse on carries anymore (it used to go on Haze, Viscous and anyone with money); the 8% total penalty is a bigger deal than a 15% weapon damage cut. [3:05]
- Buy Curse on supports that do not aim to deal damage, such as Dynamo, Paige and Rem, and maybe hybrids; spirit burst off carries like Paradox and Holliday should think about when or whether to buy it. Verdict: massive, well needed nerf, still good on some heroes. [3:35]
- Biggest item buff: Mystic Reverb, which was basically never bought, now gives the imbued ability 30% spirit lifesteal; overall spirit lifesteal was nerfed, but on a big easy-to-hit AoE ability (example: Lady Geist's bomb) it will be bought a lot more. [4:39]
- Other item changes are mostly light gun nerfs aimed at the standout items like Hollow Point and the capacitor item, plus buffs to unused items; Restorative Locket got really big changes. [5:12]
- Gun Abrams was really strong; now he has much more damage falloff and worse gun scaling, and Siphon Life was nerfed, with some compensating buffs; overall nerfs, which is good. [5:12]
- Billy got slight nerfs and decent adjustments. [5:43]
- Biggest winner of the patch is probably Bebop: Uppercut no longer gives 100% armor but gives back 20% of your missing HP, so a low Bebop that uppercuts you is much harder to kill. [5:43]
- Bebop's Sticky Bomb is overbuffed in the coach's view: if you remove it with Dispel Magic (the main counter, even against Echo Shard double bombs), the bombs now drop to the ground and keep ticking, so you must still move out of the way. [6:13]
- Verdict: Bebop is the king of the patch. [10:53]
- Drifter got a big buff: the Bloodscent isolation range dropped from 25 to 20 m, so enemies can stand closer together and still count as isolated, and his passive will be active far more often. [6:45]
- A smart Drifter can find somewhat isolated players in team fights; he was bad before and this helps, though he will not be overpowered. [7:16]
- Against Drifter, stay close to your team even in team fights and do not fight him alone. [7:16]
- Grey Talon got small buffs and needed them, since he was really bad; Ivy and Kelvin got small quality of life buffs. [7:47]
- Lash got hit hard: you can only rotate 90 degrees when initiating a slam, so slams (where you sometimes turn 180 to hit) will be much less consistent, and Flog's spirit damage scaling was nerfed. [7:47]
- Mina only got a slap on the wrist; she is still at least the second strongest hero in the game, behind the new hero Graves. [8:18]
- Gun Viscous is completely gutted: a 40% nerf to his gun damage scaling, and The Cube no longer removes all debuffs, so it cannot answer Affliction; he got some spirit scaling buffs but Puddle Punch was nerfed. [8:49]
- Prediction: Viscous will be one of the worst heroes in the game, since gun was his one strength; the coach thinks he was over-nerfed and needed more compensating buffs. [8:49]
- Paige got slight nerfs. [9:20]
- Vyper got slight adjustments that shift some damage around; she is still a spirit nuke in lane and you will not notice much. [9:20]
- Vindicta was played with Rapid Recharge going for snipes that pay bonus souls; that bonus soul reward is removed and replaced with weapon damage, which is a nerf because the souls from snipes were what made her broken. [9:20]
- Rapid Recharge itself was nerfed: the time between charges is longer, with some cooldown reduction and spirit nerfs in compensation; the coach calls it a pretty weak item now. [9:51]
- Warden got a slight slap on the wrist and is still a great gun hero. [10:22]
- Spirit Resilience was buffed to give more spirit resist, 35% instead of 30%, so there are more options against spirit damage. [10:53]
- Overall verdict: a really good patch that addresses Curse, the capacitor item and big gun issues, but will not change the meta drastically; big winner Bebop, big losers Viscous and Lash, Mina a slap on the wrist. [10:53]
- Street Brawl now has its own separate balancing, including adjustments to ultimates that used to be really strong there, so some heroes will feel different between modes. [11:25]
- Graves was not adjusted and is absolutely busted (the coach is about 14 and 1 on him); new heroes are not affected by bans yet. [11:57]
