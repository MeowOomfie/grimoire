# You'll Never Play Deadlock The Same, Once You Learn This
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-10-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRbPjoBApWg

## Covers
What the best players do in each phase: lane phase (wave clear, healing minion, confirms and denies, timers, missing enemies, cooldown tracking, trading), between fights (jungle comfort, ganks, box routes, pushing two waves, spending souls, counting enemies, rotations), fights (purpose, kill bounties, spikes, cover, staying in at low HP, target priority, Mid Boss positioning) and accountability. For new to mid-rank players.

## Claims
- In lane, better players kill the minion wave as fast as possible and kill the healing minion early (first, second or third), because its pickup heals them and all their minions, making your wave harder to kill. [0:30]
- Killing the healing minion first and clearing fast denies the enemy lane priority and opens the lane, so you can roam, run boxes or take your tier one camp. [1:01]
- With your wave cleared and souls confirmed, you can fight the enemies while they clear theirs and deny their souls without risking your own denies. [1:32]
- Better players go for every confirm and deny, because early in lane the only souls are the wave and your tier one camp, with boxes only after 3 minutes. [1:32]
- Timers: 2 minutes tier one camp, 3 minutes boxes, 5 minutes bridge buff, 8 minutes Sinner's Sacrifices. [2:34]
- Around Oracle is where players start taking bridge buffs and boxes at 3 minutes consistently; below that, rooftop and mid boxes are often left untouched. [3:04]
- The top of your screen shows health bars next to enemy portraits; a missing health bar means that enemy has not been seen recently and could be ganking, so count who is missing without waiting for a teammate's call. [3:35]
- Track enemy cooldowns: Vindicta's abilities sit on long cooldowns until upgraded, so get aggressive once she has used them. [4:06]
- Heroes like Viscous can spam an ability repeatedly, leaving few windows; play aggressive when he uses his cube instead. [4:36]
- You do not always need to win the fight; trading is valuable without a kill. Laning is a souls counter mixed with a health tracker: a health lead can matter more than a soul lead and vice versa. [5:06]
- Measure net damage (dealt versus taken, and how much sticks): throwing half your HP for damage the enemy heals off one healing minion pickup was for nothing. [5:37]
- Get comfortable in the jungle: use healing snacks, vents and rooftop boxes, because if you skip them someone else takes them and you lose. Nothing is optional, especially as you climb. [6:08]
- In Ascendant and Eternus lobbies expect ganks early, for example a Holliday in your lane at 3 minutes; in Oracle and below most players stay in lane for 10 minutes and rarely gank or rotate. [7:09]
- Gank when the enemy is far extended (for example on your teammate's Guardian) or really low health; otherwise grab boxes, maybe steal the enemy's boxes, and return to lane. [8:09]
- Minions spawn every 30 seconds; pushing your wave into the enemy fast lets you run boxes longer and come back without missing a wave. Always come back for the wave. [8:09]
- Advanced: leave lane at 3 minutes, tell your teammate he is solo laning, and run a big box route for 1:30 or more on your side and maybe mid; you come back with similar souls to your partner and crush an enemy lane that did not do the same. [8:40]
- The solo-lane box route works in lower ranks (kill the wave, hide, repeat) but not in higher ranks, where enemies push hard and go for kills the moment they see one player at the tower. [9:11]
- If the enemy freezes the wave at the bridge while you solo lane, call your teammate back; they are baiting you to walk up for the souls so they can kill you. [9:11]
- Better players are never idle: always farming, pushing, looking for a pick or an objective. [10:12]
- Before leaving lane, push out two waves: wait for the next wave and kill both at about the same time, so the enemy must respond and you do not miss souls or ping pong back for your Walker. [10:43]
- Always spend your souls; a 5k bag means a hero you think you out-farm (10k vs your 15k) can still beat you. [11:14]
- If saving for a tier 4 item, do not fight; finishing an extra 1k takes only a few camps or waves. This applies to secured souls too, not only Haunted Souls. [11:45]
- Count enemies before you commit; a lone enemy on your side may be bait, like a Dynamo luring a Lash before teleporting three allies to him with his ult. [12:45]
- If three or more enemies are not on the map, do not commit: no Walkers, no kills on their side, nothing crazy. [13:46]
- Most of taking Mid Boss is waiting for the enemy to make a mistake so you get free picks; running in and dying gives them the picks instead. [14:16]
- When no objective is up or coming (no Rift, no Mid Boss), a risky death on a 2 minute timer for a Walker or a pick is acceptable; when objectives are up or coming soon, do not take that risk. [14:16]
- Rotate without ziplines: Zip Boost is the only thing faster than walking, and with jump pads and vents you can cross the map in about 10 seconds. [14:46]
- Use the zipline only to heal, to buy (buying in lane or at the mid shop is better anyway), or to cross the whole map with Zip Boost; zip if you lack mid priority and enemies may wait mid to kill you. [15:16]
- Fight with a purpose: an objective (Mid Boss, Rift), shutting down an over-farming player, or putting enemies on a long timer to steal farm or a Walker. [15:46]
- Hunt for kills only on heroes that scale from kills, like Vindicta or Grey Talon; otherwise the risk is not worth it versus farming. [16:17]
- Kill bounties split among the killers: a solo kill gives a lot of souls, but six players killing one each get about 200, roughly one tier three creep. [16:47]
- In lower ranks, if your teammates are permanently fighting, join them; they will fight anyway, so you may as well get souls and help. [17:18]
- Fight around your spikes: ask the team for a minute to finish an item like Lucky Shot; at 5,000 souls only 1,400 off a tier 4, finish it before fighting. [18:49]
- Use cover; everywhere except mid lane has lots of walls and boxes. [19:20]
- Do not run away the moment you drop under half HP; better players stay in fights at around 400 HP by zoning and playing the matchup at a safe distance and make huge impact. [19:51]
- Exception: against Apollo do not stay at low health, because if he parries and your teammates shoot him he teleports across and stuns and kills you; the same caution applies to any hero with big gap closers. [19:51]
- Target priority: 1 uncontested (the hero damaging your team for free, like a flying Vindicta or a farmed Wraith walking you down), 2 squishies, 3 tanks. [20:52]
- Not every hero should chase the uncontested target; Wraith, Abrams with Phantom Strike, or Lash should take a flying Vindicta, not someone who cannot hit a flier. [21:23]
- Ignore a low-damage tank like Billy and kill his teammates first; shooting him plays into his strengths. Exception: a Billy fed 20k to 30k ahead must die first. [21:53]
- At Mid Boss, designate roles and position to guarantee the Rejuvenator: he plays outside the pit behind a wall so a Doorman with Vortex Web cannot knock his whole team away. [22:54]
- Take the initiative at objectives instead of assuming someone else will secure it. [23:24]
- Be self-critical and assess why each death happened; be a little hard on yourself, within reason, but act on it rather than making excuses. [23:54]
- Some games are unwinnable, but unwinnable does not mean you could not have done anything better. [24:25]
- Ranked now has rank shields, so with well-timed losses you can rank up at 50% win rate or lower; he says rank is mostly playtime and individual performance barely matters. [24:55]
