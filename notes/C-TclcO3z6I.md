## Covers
Silver, aquatiq. Commentary over a full Silver game (a win) against Bebop, Mina, Dynamo, Haze and Yamato, with a Close Quarters opening build.
Lane against Bebop's hook and Mina, item choices including Silencer, Slowing Hex and Silence Wave, Trophy Collector, and when to farm or take the Spirit Urn. Patch date 2026-05-23.

## Claims
- The build tried this game starts with Close Quarters. [0:36]
- Against a Mina in lane, consider early Spirit Shielding. [1:36]
- Bebop is a very forgiving hero: missing a hook costs him little, and he can walk up and bomb you for free damage. [2:06]
- Mina can get out of almost any situation with her bats: getting hooked, getting ulted by Vindicta or a predictable Dynamo ult. [3:07]
- Mina was statistically the worst hero by win rate at the time, though he does not think she is actually the worst. [3:38]
- Skipped melee lifesteal and went straight damage first. [4:39]
- When Bebop is going to hook you under his tower no matter what, parry the tower so it does not keep hitting you. [5:39]
- Against a hooking hero, make your movements unpredictable or he will just hook you. [8:14]
- Silver's ult heals; getting into it at very low HP healed him about half and saved a death. [10:47]
- Trading one death for a walker is fine; four enemies collapsing on one player while the walker dies is incorrect play from them. [11:28]
- Land your ability procs in fights to charge Silver's ult faster. [13:01]
- Restorative Locket is a good item on Silver. [14:02]
- Getting Express Shot off was the gun spike he wanted. [15:13]
- Silver's ult is up very often and is very strong. [15:43]
- He considered Toxic Bullets. [15:43]
- When the enemy runs four players down on one ally, that ally should back off so the team can respawn and fight together instead of dying alone. [18:27]
- With a team full of heroes that Silencer and Inhibitor work well with, both are good buys. [19:00]
- With a lot of gun items already, start building into spirit. [20:32]
- Haze can turn invisible, so expect her to show up close. [21:03]
- When the enemy is ahead on the far side, take the Spirit Urn when it is on your side instead of going mid. [22:35]
- Spirit Resilience is the buy when the enemy is spirit heavy. [24:06]
- He wanted Slowing Hex specifically for Mina; Silence Wave was the other option. [24:06]
- Silencer is bought exactly for heroes like Dynamo, so his ult can be shut down. [25:07]
- After getting picks, take the walker. [26:48]
- When the enemy has no real carries, take your farm and starve them of money rather than forcing fights. [30:50]
- Against a strong enemy comp you want more than one slow or silence item on the team, so ask teammates for a second Slowing Hex. [31:21]
- Trophy Collector reached 16 to 17 stacks this game; he calls it cracked and never a waste. [32:21]
- Warp Stone would be good here, but with Phantom Strike already bought he skipped it. [33:55]
- He considered Spellbreaker instead of Spirit Resilience. [34:27]
- Hold Silence Wave for an enemy who is about to ult. [35:28]
