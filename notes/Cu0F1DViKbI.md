# They Just Buffed The Worst Items In Deadlock (New Item Added)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-05-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cu0F1DViKbI

## Covers
A patch rundown: the new tier four item Indomitable, boon and health changes, nerfs to gun damage, item reworks (Shadow Weave, Frenzy, Trophy Collector, Opening Rounds, Spiritual Overflow, Reactive Barrier) and hero adjustments, with the coach's read on who wins and loses.
For players catching up on this patch; numbers are as of 2026-05-01 and many will have changed since.

## Claims
- New tier four item Indomitable: the next stun, chain, immobilize, sleep or silence is automatically cleansed, for every stun in the game. [0:00]
- Base health per boon was reduced by 7 while vitality investment bonus was buffed slightly, so overall HP is lower but heavy green buyers stay as tanky. [0:31]
- Lower HP makes late game carries less tanky, an indirect buff to tanks; it should mean faster kills and make spirit a bit better, especially late. [0:31]
- Weapon investment bonus was nerfed: the 49 bonus is back to 46, and late game you get 115 instead of 135. [1:02]
- Bullet damage growth was reduced by 10% for Bebop, Pocket and Vyper, pretty massive nerfs to their left click. [1:32]
- Mo & Krill and Apollo got gun buffs; it barely matters except for melee Apollo, since melee damage scales with damage per bullet. [1:32]
- Guardians and shrines now have 10% bullet resist, and walker bullet resist rose from 15 to 25; this might make gun very weak, and spirit players are happy. [2:02]
- Medic Trooper missing-health healing was reduced and its flat regen growth per minute went from 3 to 2, so staying alive in lane and buying greens in lane matter more. [2:32]
- Getting knocked off a bounce pad now sends you downward quickly instead of continuing to the destination; if you catch someone on a bounce pad they will not get away and you will probably get a kill. [2:32]
- Souls required for boons after 13K, 15K and 17K changed so you reach max boons by 40K souls. [3:33]
- Faster max boons favors heroes with strong maxed abilities like Wraith and Seven, moves strength toward the early game and nerfs late game carry potential of hard scalers. [3:33]
- Fixed ability scaling not working for Yamato and Viscous, minor buffs to two already good heroes. [4:04]
- Indomitable builds from Reactive Barrier, so build Reactive Barrier early and upgrade late; it has passive 8% bullet and spirit resist and the same barrier size as Reactive Barrier. [4:35]
- Indomitable has a long 55 second cooldown, so it is not a free Unstoppable; it is for shutting down the one big stun that can kill you, for example frequent Mo & Krill grabs on a hard carry. [4:35]
- Indomitable will likely be bought a lot on hard carries who do not want to be hard CC'd, unless they buy Unstoppable. [5:05]
- When Indomitable activates, all your ability cooldowns are instantly reduced by 20%. [5:35]
- Indomitable's 55 second cooldown means at most once a fight, and 6K is a big investment; very situational but very good. [5:35]
- Active Reload's fire rate went from 20 to 25; it is close to being bought over Quicksilver Reload much more often. [6:05]
- Opening Rounds' weapon damage dropped from 45 to 30 (a 33% nerf), but it now upgrades from High Velocity Rounds including its stats. [6:05]
- Opening Rounds is now very strong on heroes that benefit from bullet velocity, like Warden and Wraith (people already ran High Velocity Rounds on Wraith for consistent long range shots), and weaker on heroes that do not. [7:06]
- Split Shot: max stacks from 6 to 5, less weapon damage per stack and a longer cooldown; mainly a nerf to Vyper, for whom it was core and still probably is. [7:36]
- Shadow Weave was reworked from tier four to tier three (3.2k): out-of-combat regen 10 to 5, duration 18 to 10, ambush duration 8 to 5, longer cooldown, bigger spot radius, much less ambush spirit power, less ambush fire rate and melee. [8:06]
- Since Shadow Weave is now half the price and not half as strong, it is a massive buff; Wraith benefits, and melee assassins like Abrams can buy it for a free Shoulder Charge plus ambush melee, fire rate and spirit power. It might need a nerf. [8:37]
- Shadow Weave's shorter duration and bigger detection radius do not matter much because you get the stats once you get it off. [9:08]
- Alchemical Fire spirit power went from 8 to 10, a slight buff to an already good item. [9:38]
- Heroic Aura got a cooldown reduction and a bigger bullet resist aura, another nerf to bullet damage. [9:38]
- Blood Tribute now has an innate 8% debuff resist and slightly less active debuff resist, roughly 42% when active and 8% when off; a small buff. [9:38]
- Spiritual Overflow base now gives 15% ability duration instead of 15% spirit lifesteal, and the active gives 20% spirit lifesteal instead of 20% ability duration. [10:10]
- This is a buff: before, abilities cast before Spiritual Overflow procced did not get the duration extension, but now it always works; it will be bought more by duration lovers like Wraith and Infernus. [10:41]
- Frenzy was reworked: all spirit resist removed (base and below 50% health), now gives debuff resist and 50% fire rate on base plus 40% below 50% health. [11:12]
- Frenzy should move back into the meta on hard carries; it was one of the worst items, and debuff resist is a really good stat because stuns are king. [11:42]
- Restorative Locket was nerfed: max stacks 20 to 25, max stamina restore now at 25 stacks, and less healing per stack. [12:13]
- Return Fire was buffed: more base bullet resist and 5% more returned bullet damage, another nerf to bullet damage, which might make a crazy spirit meta. [12:43]
- Reactive Barrier no longer restores one stamina or grants 6% base spirit resist, but now triggers off silence; stat-wise a nerf, but since everything silences you it procs far more often, and it now has an upgrade so you never have to sell it. [13:13]
- Trophy Collector moved from tier three to tier two: fewer souls and less out-of-combat regen and ability range per stack, no health per stack, but at half the price it is a buff and might show up at high level. [13:45]
- Trophy Collector pays for itself fast and you want it early; a 1,600 buy is much easier to justify than a first 3.2K; he will run it on Apollo for early ganks and kills. [14:15]
- Dispel Magic, the most bought item at the Night Shift pro tournament, got a nerf to its spirit resist. [15:17]
- Veil Walker now upgrades from Sprint Boots including its stats with slightly less bonus health; overall a buff. [15:17]
- Bullet Resilience and Spirit Resilience now grant 3 out-of-combat regen, buffs to items that were barely bought. [15:17]
- Counterspell's cooldown went from 20 to 23 seconds and bonus health from 75 to 50. [15:48]
- Rescue Beam got a longer cast range, a buff to an already really good item. [15:48]
- Colossus no longer gives 300 bonus health but more base health percentage, better on high HP tanks like Abrams or Shiv late and weaker on everyone else. [15:48]
- Leech got more bonus health, an unexpected buff to a solid item. [16:21]
- Inhibitor base weapon damage went from 25 to 10, a good nerf. [16:21]
- Suppressor no longer grants 50 bonus health and now grants 8% bullet resist. [16:53]
- Cold Front and Arctic Blast got increased damage height; Spirit Snatch steals more spirit power; Scourge trades cooldown reduction for debuff resist; Spirit Burn DPS scaling increased. Together spirit gets better and gun weaker. [16:53]
- Lifesteal was nerfed across items: Active Reload lifesteal and Bullet Lifesteal reduced, and the item he calls "Infuser" from 80 to 70. [17:23]
- Lady Geist's Life Drain heal went from 80 to 75 and Siphon Life healing from 80/40 to 70/35. [17:53]
- Weaker lifesteal also makes anti-heal weaker, since you need less of it. [17:53]
- Abrams: fixed the speed when cancelling Shoulder Charge on a hero, a nerf to the charge-through tech. [18:23]
- Apollo's Disengaging Sigil was nerfed, quite a bit in lane where he was very strong. [18:23]
- Billy's Rising Ram T3 now also reduces cooldowns by 10%. [18:54]
- Celeste got faster bullet cycle time and Shining Wonder radius plus damage from 120 to 135. [18:54]
- The Doorman: fixed the bug that let you carry the urn through a doorway. [18:54]
- Drifter: his tier 3 (caption "Venti 3") now also increases melee damage scaling and Blood Sentry T3 amplified damage went from 10% to 12%. [19:24]
- Dynamo's Kinetic Pull spirit scaling was reduced and Quantum Entanglement cooldown rose by 1 second; still a very strong hero. [19:24]
- Haze can slide during Bullet Dance, Bullet Dance range increased and base Bullet Dance has 30% evasion; T2 and T3 were reshuffled; overall slight buffs to Haze. [19:24]
- Holliday: fixed Rising Round interrupting lasso, Spirit Lasso T1 damage increased and T3 changed; she needs to be the initiator who catches people and there is more reason to max Spirit Lasso. [20:25]
- Infernus: Flame Dash spirit scaling reduced from 1 to 0.7 and Flame Dash DPS does half damage to objectives, ending the free objective damage from running through; a significant nerf. [20:25]
- Mechanist mini turret bounty changed from 80 plus 2 per minute to 40 plus 4, turrets locked on invulnerable objectives fixed, Medicinal Spray duration increased; slight buffs. [20:56]

- Shiv: alt fire knockback reduced and Serrated Knives spirit scaling (including T3) reduced; nerfs to knives and his free escape movement tech. [22:28]

- Overall verdict: good item refreshes; gun should feel weaker, Vyper weaker and Infernus addressed. [24:30]
