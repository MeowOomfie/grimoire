# The Most Misunderstood Items In Deadlock
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-11-13
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wu9ryF3mWM

## Covers
Item mechanics many players get wrong, tested in the sandbox: out of combat regen and why chip poke can cost you health, Healbane and stacking anti-heal, resist shred as a flat damage amp, which shred items work on NPCs and structures and which stack, Escalating Exposure, Mercurial Magnum, bullet resist vs melee, and Inhibitor stacking with other spirit damage reductions.
For players who want to itemize more precisely; useful at any rank.

## Claims
- Out of combat regen items include Sprint Boots, Monster Rounds and Battle Vest; search "out of combat" in the shop to find them. [0:31]
- Shooting a neutral (troopers, jungle creeps) drops out of combat regen only for about 0.5 seconds, while shooting or being shot by a hero drops it for 4 seconds. [1:02]
- Dealing damage to a hero also puts you in combat for 4 seconds, even if they aren't shooting you. [1:32]
- With out of combat regen items, don't poke for tiny damage in lane: with 8 regen (Monster Rounds plus Battle Vest plus Sprint Boots), 4 seconds in combat costs about 24 health, so poke dealing less than that is an inefficient trade. [1:32]
- More players now abuse out of combat regen early in lane; Sprint Boots gives sprint speed plus 2 out of combat regen, close to Extra Regen's value, and builds into Trophy Collector. [2:02]
- Trophy Collector is not the best item in the game, be careful buying it, but it got a buff last patch and is a lot better. [2:33]
- Healbane is worth buying even with no Abrams, Mo & Krill or Victor on the enemy team, because of its healing on kill and because healing reduction also works on health regen. [4:34]
- Test: 16 health regen dropped to 10.4 under Healbane applied through Infernus's Afterburn. [4:34]
- Healbane also cuts other healing sources: the healing trooper heals 27 instead of 40. [5:04]
- Healbane in lane is good against many heroes, especially ones stacking regen of any kind. [5:04]
- Healbane heals 275 on kill, at the moment you need it most, in fights around objectives like mid boss or the Urn. [5:34]
- Radiant Regeneration gives far more healing per game, but at less useful times while moving around the map; it is not a bad item. [6:06]
- Healbane also gives 7 spirit power, is a green item and reduces enemy healing for 8 seconds. [6:06]
- You should probably have two Healbanes on your team every game. [6:06]
- Healing reduction stacks diminishingly: Healbane at -35% plus Toxic Bullets at -30% gave -55%; multiple anti-heal sources are still good. [6:36]
- Resist shred items include Backstabber, Hollow Point Ward and Bullet Resist Shredder. [7:06]
- Shred is a damage amp: against 0 resist, Bullet Resist Shredder raised a 30 damage bullet to 33, about 13% more. [7:37]
- The extra damage from shred is a fixed amount regardless of target resist: against Bullet Resilience a headshot went 21 to 24, still +3, a much bigger percentage increase. [8:07]
- With Glass Cannon at max level: 81 to 97 (+16) with Bullet Resilience on the target, and 116 to 131 without it; same amount added, bigger percentage gain against resist. [9:07]
- The more resist the enemy has, the better resist reduction is. [9:39]
- Enemy shred doesn't mean you should skip resistances, but against heavy resist reduction, Spirit Shielding, Reactive Barrier or Weapon Shielding can give more effective health than pure resist items. [9:39]
- Almost every damage build needs some resist reduction at some point because it is a damage amp; heroes like Pocket rely fully on damage amps. [10:09]
- Some reduction items work against heroes only (Silence Wave, Rusty Barrel must target a hero); others work against everything including structures, NPCs and the jungle. [10:40]
- To farm faster or hit structures harder, pick the reductions that work against everything; for hero damage, pick the hero-only ones. [11:10]
- Hunter's Aura reduces neutrals' resist by only 8%; its "tripled if only one enemy hero nearby" applies only to the hero (24%), not to troopers. [11:42]
- Hollow Point Ward and Bullet Resist Shredder are so common partly because they also work on buildings, raising Walker damage and farm speed. [11:42]
- Weakening Headshot only works on heroes and doesn't help farm, even on jungle headshots (tested). [12:13]
- Two of the same reduction item (two Hollow Point Wards, two Bullet Resist Shredders) do not stack, so duplicate items on a team can be bad. [12:13]
- Exception: Crippling Headshot stacks at about 50% each: -18, then -9, then -4.5. [12:44]
- Rusty Barrel stacks diminishingly between players (two gave -15% resist in the test). [12:44]
- Spirit Rend stacks in that another player can accelerate the headshot debuff stacks; the base spirit resist reduction from hitting probably doesn't stack. [13:46]
- Spirit Shredder Bullets' part works against neutrals, but the headshot part doesn't. [13:46]
- Two Silence Waves don't stack and two Spirit Snatches don't stack; Hunter's Aura doesn't stack. [14:18]
- Two players with Crushing Fist can build its stacks faster. [14:18]
- Different resist reduction sources stack with each other diminishingly; if your team already has two Bullet Resist Shredders, probably don't buy a third. [14:18]
- Escalating Exposure only amps your own spirit damage to its target, not your team's; only its Mystic Vulnerability component part (8% spirit resist reduction) amps the whole team's spirit damage. [14:48]
- Mercurial Magnum's bonus lasts until your next reload, so not reloading or a large magazine lets you keep the bonus, even while farming. [15:20]
- Bullet damage and bullet resist both affect melee: bullet resist helps against melee, and reducing bullet resist (for example with Hunter's Aura) increases your melee damage. [15:50]
- Inhibitor stacks additively with other spirit damage reductions: with Infernus's burn (-20% spirit damage) plus Inhibitor you do -55% spirit damage. [16:22]
- Silencer's 25% spirit damage reduction stacks additively on top as well; a max level Afterburn with Boundless Spirit on a max level Infernus did only 287 total under these reductions. [17:22]
- These damage reduction items are situational and hard to fit into builds, but worth using at times. [17:53]
