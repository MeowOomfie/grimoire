## Covers
Midknight Vods reviews four short replays on a free coaching stream: a Doorman, an Oracle Solomon learning the new hero, a Victor (rank not clearly said), and a Danny (submitted as Phantom Danny).
Between reviews he gives meta opinions: new heroes are overtuned, M1 carries have too much agency, which carries are good, and which builds to trust.
Recurring themes: negative trades in lane, green (vitality) spikes, playing for the Urn, staggering, not dying for dead teammates, ego fighting, wasting Rejuvenator without waves, splitting waves, positioning in fights, and soul-efficient itemization.
