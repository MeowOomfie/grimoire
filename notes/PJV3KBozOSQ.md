## Covers
The 2025-10-11 rank distribution change announced by Valve in the Discord: where to see your new rank, why it is a display change and not a matchmaking change, and what the Statlocker match distribution showed before and after.
Explicitly the coach's speculation, since the developers gave no detail. For players worried that their new, higher badge is undeserved.

## Claims
- To check your new rank: press Escape in the hideout, go to the top right, click Skill Rating. [0:00]
- Everything in the video is speculation based on Discord, Twitch chat and his own and others' games; the developers have not explained the change in detail. [0:30]
- The rank change is not a matchmaking change: Valve's wording is percentile, so your percentile should have stayed the same, and there is no indication matchmaking was touched. [1:01]
- Most people in his chat and Discord said their games felt the same, so he believes matchmaking was not affected. [1:31]
- The change is mainly visual and about feel: many players were stuck in Initiate 1 and their visible rank did not move even after winning eight or nine games in a row. [1:31]
- Your badge now properly displays your rank relative to everyone else; with this many ranks, Archon or Emissary should be roughly the middle. [2:32]
- Statlocker's rank distribution is really match distribution, not exact rank distribution, but it is the best available without Valve's data. [2:32]
- Last week's match distribution: Alchemist 5 was the 50th percentile (an average player). [3:03]
- An abnormal 14% of the player base was in Initiate 1 (Initiate 2 starts at 86%). [3:03]
- Before the change Emissary 3 or 4 was a top 15% player, when you would expect it to be about average. [3:35]
- Players who were Emissary 3 or 4 have likely moved to Phantom or Ascendant, with the same percentile. [4:05]
- People did not all move the same amount; he believes the move is set by a hidden system, probably some form of MMR or Elo. [4:05]
- Early data from the last hour shows Phantom 1 now at top 15%, but the sample is tiny; give it a couple of days to a week. The distribution should look like a standard bell curve. [4:36]
- Your badge now accurately represents the MMR the system already had for you, so your actual matches should not change. [5:06]
- If you are now Phantom or Ascendant, you deserve to be there and should not be afraid to queue; your MMR has not changed. [5:06]
- Example: Ascendant 1 is now about top 9.1% (if the data holds), while before it was top 1.5%. [5:38]
- Players who were Ascendant 1 before are probably Eternus 3 or 4 now, because the percentages were adjusted. [6:08]
