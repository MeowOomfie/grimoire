# Laning Is Losing You Games. Let's Fix That
channel: Deathy  date: 2025-11-27  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e5WJbcIYgqc

## Covers
Laning on the new patch through three lanes (won, lost, even): healing trooper killed last, wave priority, freezing to deny walk-up souls, trading past the troopers, off angles and the church angle, diving, ult advantage, not throwing a won lane, towers vs souls, after-lane pressure, Lash, Abrams, Shiv, Sinclair, Warden, Mirage, Vindicta, Viscous, Bebop, effective range and base DPS, cover and geometry for shotgun heroes, Extra Regen vs Restorative Shot, Healbane, Headshot Booster, Healing Rite, Fleetfoot, Enduring Speed, Sprint Boots, 4.8k investment categories, resetting, roaming out of a lost lane, stalemate lanes and ganking

## Claims
- What changed on this patch for laning: new healing trooper mechanics, new soul mechanics (captioned "solar"), tankier guardians and a completely overhauled shop investment system. [0:00]
- You now need to kill the healing trooper last instead of first. [0:00]
- The new healing trooper heals you for a percentage of your missing health, so you are usually better off killing it last because you want to be more hurt when it dies; it is best to trade before it dies. [0:30]
- Towers got tankier, especially early, so you do not do a significant amount of tower damage early. [0:30]
- Wave priority means having more troopers pushing into them than they have into you (you have three, they have two). [0:30]

- Ability or cooldown advantage means you have your abilities and they do not, or yours are better or shorter. [1:01]
- HP advantage (more HP than them at a given moment) is an important concept and you need to take advantage of it. [1:01]
- Going into lane, think about base stats: your gun DPS versus theirs, your gun's effective range versus theirs, and your HP regen versus theirs, so you know what you can abuse. [1:01]
- From coaching hundreds of players, the number one thing holding people back in lane is that they are not trading enough with the enemy players. [2:02]
- His play style is to engage a lot, shoot the enemies and use his abilities, and the current laning system encourages that to an extent. [2:32]
- Trading matters because taking trades is how you prove you are the better player and win the lane. [2:32]
