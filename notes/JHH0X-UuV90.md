For players new to Holliday or the pick role.

## Claims
- Pros: very mobile and hard to punish, generally strong early with lots of early impact, and flashy. [0:00]
- Cons: her spirit falls off hard late, so you usually pivot into support or gun; as a pick hero, especially with her ult, she relies on teammates nearby to get kills. [0:00]
- Her 1 (Powder Keg) throws a barrel you shoot to explode, or jump and light melee it so it explodes on contact with terrain. [0:00]
- Her 2 (Bounce Pad) launches anyone who steps on it; the first use by her or allies gives 100% air control until landing, and she deals area damage when she lands. [0:00]
- Her 3 (Crack Shot) gives bonus damage and a small slow on headshots; at max level further headshots reduce its cooldown. [0:30]
- Her ult (lasso) stuns a hit enemy and keeps them tethered to her for a short duration. [0:30]
- Hitting a Bounce Pad while lassoing someone extends the lasso by 2 seconds. [0:30]
- Air dash after hitting the Bounce Pad to cancel the upward momentum, extending the lasso without dragging the target too far from your team. [1:01]
- You can use items during the lasso, so curse or silence an enemy right before it expires. [1:01]
- The Bounce Pad's 100% air control lets you air strafe: in the air, don't press W, press A or D and turn your camera that way. [1:01]
- During the lasso she can't shoot or melee but can use abilities: throw a barrel straight up so it lands, then drag the lassoed enemy into it. [1:31]
- Lane: she is a relatively strong laner; use that to win lane outright or get enough pressure to roam and gank other lanes. [1:31]
- Use barrels mainly for poke, ideally hitting both the wave and the enemy, until the barrel is tier two, a massive spike that lets you commit to trades. [1:31]
- Mid game: as a pick hero, when your ult is up call a strong mid game hero to invade enemy centers with you, or other important camps if centers are down; steal the farm, or lasso an enemy farming it for a kill. [1:31]
- Late game: use the lasso to stop a key channeled ultimate like Dynamo's or Seven's, or to lasso a very fed player (a 60k Wraith) so teammates kill them while stunned. [2:02]
- Build: his own build; for a more complicated one, Goober's, but you must know what you are buying, don't buy it left to right. [2:02]
- Aim your lasso: find your target first, then start casting and track them, instead of throwing it and then looking for the target. [2:33]
- Vision matters in the pick role: showing on one side of the map relieves pressure on the other side, the way you feel when you see a Haze or Mo & Krill on the far side. [2:33]
