# They Added a New Objective! | Deadlock Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-07-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agSqMqjoDtU

## Covers
Midknight reads through and reacts to a major Deadlock patch (video dated 2026-07-01): the King of the Hill objective becomes the Unstable Rift, an Urn rework, comeback-bonus changes, a boon rework, global health and ultimate cooldown nerfs, soul changes, item changes and a long list of hero changes.
Patch reaction with his verdict on each change and how it shifts objective play and team strategy.

## Claims
Objectives:
- The old King of the Hill objective is now the Unstable Rift. It spawns every 7 minutes, give or take 1 minute. [0:00]
- Go to the Rift's spawn area a minute early and you can see it is about to spawn; 25 seconds before, there is a global announcement. [0:00]
- Comeback bonuses are now percentage based on how far a team trails. He hates it: a team down 5 or 6% gets about 10% resist, which will swing a fight. [0:30]
- Securing the Rift spawns Rift troopers with roughly doubled stats. A trooper buffed by a minion-buffing support (captioned "rem minion") is insanely strong, so watch for that in solo queue. [0:30]
- The Rift no longer grants the permanent buffs, which were significant. [1:00]
- The Urn goes back to spawning every 5 minutes, always at its neutral locations. [1:00]
- The Urn runner is no longer revealed on the map and is not disarmed or silenced. He calls not being revealed huge and a blunder he hopes is reverted. [1:00]
- The runner can no longer drop the Urn manually; it only comes off if someone knocks it off you, you get stunned, or you die. [1:30]
- Since the enemy runner is hidden, your team often has to just sit near the cache. [2:02]
- Grabbing the Urn to bait the whole enemy team is still very good: if your team trades a Walker, or the enemies respond to your team, you just run the Urn. It is harder to execute now. [2:02]
- Which team picks up the Urn matters a lot, especially now the runner is not disarmed. [2:02]
- The Urn bounty now starts decaying 45 seconds after pickup instead of damaging the holder, gradually losing soul value until it disappears. So you can bait it for Walkers without trading your life, and hold it while you farm. [2:33]
- Urns deposit instantly at the drop-off again. He thinks a 1 to 2 second deposit was the best version and instant deposit feels bad for the defending team. [2:33]
- He never liked the forced Urn but prefers it to AoE slop; the changes are a step in the right direction. [3:04]
- Urn bounty on deposit is 250 plus 70 per minute, for the runner only, still with a comeback component. The old Urn was about 750 plus 165 per minute (his numbers may be slightly off), so a big decrease. [3:04]
- On deposit all souls are released as orbs in the air; if an ally secures them they share it. Denying these orbs is super important. [3:35]
- Since souls now go to the runner, an uncontested Urn is a good way to funnel souls into your carry, or have your position 2 run it while your carry splits a side lane. [4:07]
- As a support you can still run it while your position 1 secures the orbs, but if the enemies can secure orbs (at a high level they will), post up on roofs near the drop-off and deny as many orbs as you can. [4:38]
- The counter to the runner being invisible is that you can now hear the Urn. [4:38]
- After 3 minutes the Urn starts slowly running itself, getting about 60% of the way before it switches sides, to encourage low-level players to run it. [5:09]
- The Urn runner's passive holding bonus now scales with how far behind you are, the same linear scaling as the Rift. Fine for souls, he dislikes it for resist. The comeback resists on the runner appear to be cut, which is good. [5:09]

Boons, health, ultimates and souls:
- Boon rework: ability unlocks come earlier, an extra 600 souls on top of the starting 600, so more AP earlier before the 20k soul mark, then it slows and equalizes by the later AP. [5:40]
- He thinks this is meant to stop snowballing: a carry fed past 25k to 30k has a little less AP. Tier 5 AP spikes are very strong, so a fed enemy carry having one less will feel nice. He likes it. [6:12]
- Health per boon reduced by 5%: hurts tanks, which he likes, but lowers time to kill for assassins and M1 heroes, which he does not. He wanted overtuned gun damage nerfed alongside it. [6:44]
- Ultimate base and upgrade cooldowns nerfed by 15%. Amazing change, everyone was holding ults too much. [7:14]
- If an Urn and a Rift timer overlap you will have to choose where to use your ult; you should probably save it for the Rift, but it depends, and you might wipe the team on the Urn if it comes first. [7:45]
- Rejuvenator buff duration cut to 3 minutes. He likes it at a high level; most low lobbies use it within the first minute anyway. [7:45]
- Respawn timers are slightly longer before 20 minutes, which he likes: around the 6 minute mark the enemy could get back to a lane fight too quickly. [8:15]
- First blood souls reduced; he is glad. Walker and Guardian souls reduced, which makes sense since the second objective adds souls to the map and rewards neutral objectives over mindless split pushing. [8:15]
- Base kill bounty reduced from 250 to 200. He hates it: comeback souls in lane need the nerf instead, since you can kill someone four times in lane and they kill you once and are even. [8:46]
- Lower early souls make it harder to get your ult online early, which hurts roamers such as Lash. [9:46]
- Minimum unsecured souls allowed to drop reduced from 150 to 50 (he does not know what this means). [9:46]

Movement and map:
- Dash jump buffed, making it a little more comparable to edge boosting, which is still going to be OP. He prefers buffing base movement over nerfing movement tech, though he would rather the plain dash roll got love. [10:16]
- One stamina has to be paired with an edge boost or an IAD to get real value. [10:47]
- A map exit that let heroes invade the enemy doubles and escape was removed. Great change: invading center camps is now much harder and more punishable. [10:47]

Items:
- Mystic Shot nerfed; he loves it. [11:50]
- Toxic Bullets buffed; he wants it to be a gun anti-heal item more than a gun damage item, since heroes that core it are very strong. [11:50]
- Scourge max health reduced; it has been broken for a long time. [12:21]
- Curse (captioned "Kurr") gets a damage penalty nerf; he wants it nerfed further, it is also broken. [12:21]
- Not touched but should be: Opening Rounds is objectively broken, and Slowing Bullets is incredibly good. [12:21]

Heroes:
- Abrams: hurt by the 5% health nerf, plus 1 meter radius on his 1 and 2 seconds on his charge. He wanted Abrams to actually take damage. [12:51]
- Apollo: Riposte buffed after the minor rework, deserved. The heal scaling on his 3 buffed, which he accepts since it got outscaled. His late ult problem is that everything cancels it; hard CC should, soft CC should not. [13:22]
- Billy: damage per boon reduced by 10%, amazing. Bashdown melee scaling reduced; he would rather the spirit scaling were cut more. Rising Ram tier 3 at 8% max HP is not enough, it needs to be 5% or less, since you get at least two procs a fight on about a 10 second cooldown. Tier 3 max HP spirit damage scaling was increased. [13:53]
- Celeste: daggers and ult buffed; he does not think she needed a buff and thinks she needs a minor rework instead. [14:54]
- Doorman: a cooldown increased, but Call Bell does way too much damage and should have been hit; the Call Bell build is the real build, and the Kidnap Doorman build he rates about 6 out of 10. [15:24]
- Drifter: a relatively big buff he does not think was needed, since he terrorizes solo queue. He thinks Drifter could be the winner of the patch and S tier. [15:54]
- Grey Talon: spirit power gained from his bird finally reduced after about a year and a half of buffs. Rain of Arrows goes from 13 to 15 seconds with two points in lane, which he says will not fix how oppressive he is at tier 2. [15:54]
- Holliday: nerfs smaller than he wanted, 2 spirit scaling and 20 damage on the keg. Her gun is slept on; he predicts she stays good, spirit Holliday gets nerfed further, players then discover her gun and a gun Holliday meta follows. [16:54]
- Haze: Sleep Dagger buffed; he thinks it needs a rework, as it does too much as a pick tool, for example into Lash. [17:24]
- Ivy: an ability buff he does not understand, plus Air Drop cooldown reduction when used on allies. He thinks Stone Form is the most broken ability in the game and it was not touched. [17:55]
- Lash: Ground Strike losing 3 seconds of cooldown is not insignificant. Some Lashes put a point in their 1 before two points in their 3; now they may not need to, giving the same AP spike as other heroes. A decent lane buff, though he still will not be a good laner. [18:25]
- Mina: ult buffed, which he does not understand; he thinks her 2 scaling with spear range is already OP and her ult is already very good. [18:55]
- Mo & Krill: notably more health on his ults, offsetting the health per boon nerf; mostly a solo queue hero. [19:26]
- Paige: her 1 goes much further at max level and tier 1 gives 12 seconds off its cooldown, very significant for wave clear. More health per boon; if she gets tanky enough to survive an assassin's burst with her barriers she could go crazy. A 100% tier 3 change (barrier, as captioned) he calls the most insane change he has seen. Her ult gets charges and less cooldown when it hits nothing, which he says rewards bad play. [19:56]
- A hero's carbine ability (as captioned, hero not named) gets move speed spirit scaling on tier 2, about 6 move speed at 100 spirit power, which will make her very hard to catch. Tier 3 now affects min and max damage scaling, so early carbine tosses do more damage. [21:27]
- Pocket: gun nerfed and Flying Cloak tier 3 reduced from minus 12 to minus 11 seconds; a good step, he wanted more. [21:59]
- A hero with Tag Along and Little Helper (name not said): Tag Along heal per second and tier 3 missing health reduced; Little Helper spirit resist reduced. Tier 2 now gives one helper and 15 spirit resist instead of more trooper buff; 15 spirit resist on teammates is very impactful. He is glad the ceiling was cut. [22:29]
- Seven: Storm Cloud takes longer to expand; he thinks Seven will still terrorize solo queue. [23:00]
- Shiv: alt fire knockback movement now disabled by slows, an amazing change. Alt fire ammo per shot goes from 3 to 5, which hurts it in combat but not much for movement. [23:31]
- Shiv: knives cooldown reduced with no damage nerf, and Slice and Dice now deals 25 plus 25 light melee damage instead of 60 spirit damage and gives amp; he thinks giving heroes amp is always a mistake and this makes Shiv oppressive. Shiv's low win rate comes from people learning him; one tricks are disgustingly good. [24:01]
- Shiv: Bloodletting nerfed with 15% more incoming damage deferred removed, which was basically minus 15% damage taken; the spike comes later. Executing a hero with Killing Blow now instantly fills the rage bar, which is scary. Tier 3 recast within 20 seconds of a hero kill is now base. Killing Blow range reduced, so less use as an escape. [24:31]
- Vindicta: Stake and Crow Familiar buffed. He says she was already slept on and this patch is emblematic of power creep. [26:01]
- Vyper: dagger tier 3 now also reduces charge delay; she did not need a buff. [26:31]
- Warden: bullet damage reduced again. Willpower tier 2 increased and tier 3 gets more spirit scaling, pushing him back to spirit. He dislikes unstoppable on Warden's ult, which removed the counterplay of running away or catching him with CC or a Curse, for example by predicting a Majestic Leap. [27:02]
- He complains that balance changes follow win rate alone. [27:02]
