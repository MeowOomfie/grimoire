# The Importance of Urn | Emissary Yamato Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-12-31
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K75UNhTwDm0

## Covers
Yamato, a Midknight coaching review of one Emissary-rank Yamato player's game (December 2025 patch, holiday event).
Themes: hitting waves versus poking in lane, using wave priority and space, securing every soul orb, running the Urn, what to do with a rejuv, overchasing and "at best" fights in fog of war, base defense from height, weaving Yamato's right click into combos and buffering inputs during her ult.

## Claims
Yamato specific:
- Do not pre-queue your whole build in the shop: it makes you buy on autopilot without thinking about what you need. [0:30]
- When right click spamming on Yamato, try to be sliding while you do it, for example right click while sliding down stairs. [1:01]
- Do not be afraid to end your 1 (Power Slash) early, for example cancel it if the target is going behind walls. [1:01]
- Aim the 1 where the enemy is routing (for example the corner of shop) and catch them with the AoE, instead of aiming at their head. [2:02]
- Be careful right clicking near the enemy: if a right click gets you killed you were on your alt-fire timer. [2:32]
- Healing Booster this early is not worth it until the 3 is maxed, because the 3 is on such a high cooldown; if you are constantly full HP and not being harassed, keep itemizing offensively to keep pressure on the lane. [3:33]
- The 3 is the better max right now, mostly for the healing, because her 1 got heavily nerfed. [4:35]
- Beeline the spirit spike freely; only buy a healing item if they start harassing you a lot and you actually need it. [4:35]
- Using the 1 during the ult animation is good. [5:37]
- Saving for and buying Torment Pulse is a good call. [6:37]
- How you use right click is a big marker of high-level versus mid-level Yamato. [6:37]
- After your ult you should lead with the 2. [14:21]
- During the ult you can buffer your 1, a mantle, your grapple, your 3 and a melee, which helps you stay on top of a target. [14:21]
- Right clicks belong between abilities: right click, 1, right click, 2, right click, 3. Missing the right clicks between abilities is lost damage. [14:52]
- Once your combo is done and no ability is coming off cooldown, switch to left clicking, because then you are just taking a gun trade. [14:52]
- Right clicks are only good for early lane harass (targets far away and moving) or in between abilities, not as a general poking tool. [14:52]
- Against Mina as Yamato you basically have to buy Slowing Hex. [15:24]
- Use bounce pads to escape fights. [15:55]
- Slowing Hex is in a league of its own now that Silence Wave lost its resistance shred. [15:55]
- Mystic Shot would be solid next; Boundless Spirit is also good. [18:29]
- With around 100 gun DPS, left clicking does more than right click spamming in face-to-face fights. [19:29]
- How the buffer works: when the bar at the bottom of the ult finishes but the animation is still playing, you can already start abilities, a melee or a mantle. [22:37]
- He recommends Fred the Finch's Yamato build; the items are all good, the exact order is a matter of detail. [23:09]
- Against an all-spirit comp, Yamato badly needs an anti-spirit item: Spirit Shielding or Spirit Resilience. [23:39]
- Integrate right clicks into the combo, for example right click, 3, right click, 1; it is a lot of free damage. [23:39]
- Mystic Vulnerability should have come out sooner because it gives a lot of value with Torment Pulse and spirit burn; you can upgrade into Escalating Exposure as soon as you have Torment Pulse. [29:32]
- Against a Sinclair Bunny comp, Counterspell is very good; Debuff Remover helps by reducing the duration. [34:42]
- Demonstration: start ulting, start a melee, and the melee finishes as soon as the bar ends, while she is still crossing her arms. [43:27]

Lane:
- Between waves, right click spam the enemy to harass; once waves arrive, focus the wave instead of poking. [1:01]
- When you have wave priority and they have no minions, walk up and harass hard; when the next wave arrives you do not want to tank a full wave, so drop back to your own. [1:31]
- While hitting the wave, play cover against Sinclair; if he throws bolts, wall jump and double jump to juke. [3:03]
- If the enemy keeps crashing waves into you and your partner is not hitting minions, buy Monster Rounds to keep your tower alive. [3:33]
- With 800 souls, buy before you take an engage. [5:07]
- If you back off with no wave to hit, farm the tier one camp or steal the enemy boxes to keep your souls up. [6:07]
- When you are up a lot of souls, keep the pressure on and whittle down the tower; just do not get caught under the tower and respect the enemy's dive. [7:40]
- With a soul lead, weak enemy wave priority and ult advantage (Sinclair used his ult), punish the enemy's bad engage. [8:10]
- Do not over-respect and give the bridge: holding the bridge would have forced the enemy to fight on top of it for a big Lash ult on both. [8:10]
- Melee an enemy who is channelling an ult on your teammate just outside tower range: if he cancels and parries, it still frees your teammate. [10:11]
- Winning lane 6 to 0 but only taking tower at 12 minutes shows you did not press the advantage to hit tower enough. [10:42]
- Shoot at every soul orb you see, even ones you may not get; just try to secure or contest every orb. [41:23]

Items in general:
- Tankbuster was nerfed but is still good later; as a rough number it used to be good after 22 minutes. [9:11]
- Tankbuster also procs on minions, so Mina could buy it around 16 to 18 minutes to invade; Viper now wants it significantly later; Lash can justify it a little earlier for his combo. It is character dependent, just do not buy it too early. [9:41]
- Buying Tankbuster at 15 minutes to farm was not worth it in his opinion. [10:11]
- Tankbuster damage is applied after bolts (a chat point he says makes sense but was new to him). [11:13]
- Tankbuster at 22 minutes is probably fine. [20:00]

Fights and macro:
- Do not chase with no information on where the enemies are: someone farming a nearby camp or by their walker can easily reinforce. [11:46]
- An isolated 1v1 with your ult up can be won, but the chance is low when you have no information; with a full wave crashing into your walker, scare the enemy off and grab the wave or your camps instead. [12:16]
- Do not tunnel vision one target while the enemy is running the Urn. [12:47]
- When focusing the center camps (instead of the doubles), you are trading more HP for faster clear speed, which can be fine; just do not take fights while doing it. [13:50]
- Do not run at the enemy walker with no information or vision. [16:59]
- With a parry up and no kill potential, use the parry to kite out instead of trying to melee. [17:29]
- Do not take a side lane 1v1 with no information of what is around you. [18:59]
- Contest the comeback Urn, even down one player, when you have a Dynamo; do not give it for free. [20:34]
- If an enemy is solo cashing the Urn with teammates far away, walk in and melee it off them. [21:36]
- On the enemy team, never coin flip the mid boss against a Dynamo. [21:36]
- At mid boss, your ult needs to come out a second sooner, and the Dynamo ult as well. [22:07]
- Respect Mo & Krill: the second your ult ends he can combo you and you die. [25:44]
- At mid boss, compare your gun DPS against heavy melee: mid boss takes extra damage from heavy melees, so here (about 130 DPS) heavy melee is better. [27:26]
- Someone needs to parry at the rejuv: melee, then parry the enemy. When the enemy is in front of you, step forward a little before parrying instead of parrying immediately. [27:59]
- Counterspell would have prevented getting hit by the Dynamo ult. [27:59]
- Rejuv flowchart: first fix all your waves, then grab and run the Urn, then use the remaining space (about 2:30 of rejuv left) to take their centers, invade their camps and jungle until about 1:00 to 1:30 of rejuv is left, then all run at their walker. Try to use the whole rejuv window, and invading their jungle is the most important part. [28:30]
- Instead of taking a fight with the rejuv, you should be running the Urn. [29:32]
- Trading three regens for two picks wastes your rejuv; back off once your team is low. [30:03]
- After a pick you can still run the Urn, but the window is short. [30:34]
- If you are rotating to mid, do not go to your base: rotate across and grab the centers on the way. [30:34]
- Dynamo's ult should be your way to start a fight; a full-team mid coin flip that does not need to happen is bad. [32:05]
- After a pick, run the Urn instead of mindlessly running at their walker; Urn is very valuable right now. [34:10]
- Do not tunnel vision Mo & Krill: he has a lot of HP and many resists when burrowed. [34:42]
- If you are unhealthy while staging the enemy base, knock the Patron and leave instead of staying to clear base guardians, especially when enemies respawn in seconds and they have Sinclair Bunny plus Mo & Krill's ult. [36:43]
- On the way out of the enemy base, prioritize the center camps and leave. [37:14]
- Posturing aggressively at low HP gets you killed. [37:45]
- Base defense: do not hug the wall or play on the ground. With two teammates dead, give the space, poke them down, let them knock the Patron and leave; dying there gives them staggered respawns to kill the Patron. [38:47]
- When you choose to defend base, hold and fight from height; do not give height for free. [39:17]

Final notes:
- Focus waves in lane, poke between waves, push up with your wave and harass under their tower. [41:23]
- Run the Urn: whenever you have a rejuv, a small advantage, or just a Dynamo ult, run it. [41:54]
- Do not overchase: avoid 1v1s where you have no backup and chases into their jungle with no information. [42:26]
- An "at best" fight is one you cannot reinforce while the enemy might (like a 2v2 in fog of war); it is at best neutral, so do not take it. Climbing in MOBAs is about taking the most statistically advantaged fights. [42:26]
- Weave right clicks between every ability (on Abrams too, it is right click, 2, right click, 3, right click, 1), then switch to left clicking until something comes up. [42:56]
