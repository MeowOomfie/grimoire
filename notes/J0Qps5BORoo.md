## Covers
Coaching VOD review of an Ascendant gun Warden by Midknight, patch of May 2026.
Themes: dead time between waves, punishing a Lash in lane, box routing and invading when ahead, farming greedily as an M1 carry, tower and shrine timings, using and wasting Rejuvenator, ulting before you engage, and itemization (Armor Piercing Rounds, Silencer, Knockdown, Unstoppable).

## Claims
Player 1 (Warden, Ascendant):
- There is a roughly 10 second window to go harass while the enemies still have minions to hit, then back up to buy and push back with your wave; buy before the waves meet. [0:30]
- No reason to try to freeze in this lane; shove the wave in as fast as possible to guarantee prio, then go harass. [1:00]

- Taking the tier 2 camp off spawn is good, but you had a window to buy Opening Rounds before farming it, and you should have, because Opening Rounds is a big spike. [1:30]
- Biggest note early: about 10 seconds of dead time between waves. [2:01]
- Instead of the small box route, buy, get hard prio and do a bigger box route to the right of the lane, stealing the enemy's boxes, especially when winning this hard. [2:01]
- Invading boxes when you are winning the lane is very valuable, especially when you are the much stronger laners. [2:32]
- Extra Health gives a lot of value in lane; heal changes keep nerfing healing but missing-HP damage is still there, so higher max HP is very valuable. These days mostly heroes who upgrade it later buy it. [3:32]
- You are over-respecting the Lash: if he Ground Strikes in, he is dead. Going for the buff is good. [3:32]
- The wave before this, he Ground Struck and you backed off; punish Lash after Ground Strike and steal boxes when ahead. [4:02]
- Farm camps off spawn and know your timers: on the wave before 6 minutes, rather than boxing, get hard prio and be on the tier 2 camp right before it spawns. [4:33]
- Weighted Shots is a broken meta staple and it is crazy it did not get nerfed last patch. [4:33]
- Pre 6 minutes some roaming in your lane would have been great, mostly from your Silver while you anchor; you could have asked for it. [5:33]
- You are babysitting your lane partner, which is not good; do a long box route instead. If she chooses to fight alone and dies, that is on her. [5:33]
- Inflate your souls by farming camps as you move around the map. [6:04]
