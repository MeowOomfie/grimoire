# Deadlock Finally Fixed The Meta? (Balance Update)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-04-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ij0aCOP9l5g

## Covers
Patch breakdown read from the notes: parry changes, healing trooper heal, chain CC reduction, item changes (Recharging Rush, Titanic Magazine, Toxic Bullets, Plated Armor, Golden Goose Egg, Silence Wave and more) and hero changes (big Celeste and Haze nerfs, Bebop, Billy, Doorman, Grey Talon, Lady Geist, Mirage, Paradox, Rem, Venator, Victor and others) with the coach's verdict on each. For players wanting to know who won and lost the patch.

## Claims
- You can now parry at any time during a ground dash, without finishing or cancelling the dash first; a parry buff. [0:00]
- Parry anti-mash protection used to last 0.25 seconds and now triggers right away, so there is less delay before a back-to-back parry; the coach reads both changes as answering over-performing melees. [0:30]
- Verdict on the parry changes: not massive, but the dash parry helps a lot in lane and makes it scarier to melee creeps with an enemy close because they can counter parry more easily. [0:30]
- Medic (healing) trooper missing health heal reduced from 16% to 14%, so lane regen items matter more, strong laners do even better and laning matters more again; you cannot sustain on low HP as much. [1:00]
- Verdict on the trooper change: small but good; it should make lane more dynamic, stop nonstop box running and push people to go for more kills. [1:00]
- Subsequent CC reduction increased (captioned as 10/30 to 15/35), so chained stuns (Moon Glaive grab then more stuns) last less and less; a good change most of the community will appreciate. [1:32]
- Recharging Rush was over performing on many heroes because charged abilities are very strong right now, and it got a nerf. [2:02]
- Titanic Magazine now gives 100% extra ammo and weapon damage went from 12% to 14%; the coach expects it to stay niche because players preserve ammo by sliding, but it will be better on heroes that already buy it like Ivy. [2:02]
- Express Shot secondary fire weapon damage went from 35 plus 1 per boon to 40 plus 1.3 per boon; a slight buff that helps heroes like Silver, already scary with it. [2:32]
- Toxic Bullets build up is 10% slower, a big deal because heroes like Drifter and Celeste procced it in one or two hits and now need one more bullet. [2:32]
- Toxic Bullets bleed reduced from 2 to 1.7 and anti-heal increased from 30% to 35%, so buy it for the anti-heal now, not the damage. [3:03]
- Sharpshooter movement penalty is a bit weaker; not a big deal. [3:03]
- Cultist Sacrifice got a buff of about 20%; the coach has called it really bad for several patches, and this may push it over the edge on farming heroes that like its ability range and other stats. [3:34]
- Armor Piercing Rounds chance increased; still a niche item bought by few heroes. [3:34]
- Return Fire bullet damage return and cooldown improved; another gun counter buff because Metal Skin alone was not good enough. [4:04]
- Spirit Shielding and Weapon Shielding got move speed and barrier duration buffs, and Weapon Shielding's proc time window got longer, since in lane and early mid game there was not enough bullet damage to proc it. They are a tiny bit better but still niche because the barrier health values did not change. [4:04]
- Plated Armor got buffed even though it was already the best hard counter to on-hit effects like Infernus burn; it is now a must buy in late game against many on-hit effects (Mirage, Infernus). [4:34]
- Golden Goose Egg souls per permanent buff reduced from 150 to 100, and it now counts only souls actually acquired, not the initial 400 soul sell-back, so it no longer sells instantly for permanent buffs and the 30 minute rebuy and resell loop is gone. A slight early game buff, still niche. [5:04]
- Decay spirit scaling reduced; the coach did not think it was very strong. [5:35]
- Silence Wave got hit hard: less damage, less spirit scaling and cooldown increased from 30 to 42 seconds; very much needed because high rank teams ran five or six Silence Waves. [5:35]
- Echo Shard cooldown increased. [6:05]
- Lightning Scroll fix: its stun delay was being shortened by debuff reducer, making the stun go off faster. [6:05]
- Bebop got a significant weapon buff: wind up time reduced from 0.5 to 0.3 seconds and fast wind up from 0.1 to 0.05, nearly half the wind up, so he shoots you much faster and his DPS rises, especially noticeable in lane. [6:05]
- Bebop's Sticky Bomb can self-cast again but deals 20% of its damage to yourself; high risk, high reward, which the coach likes. [6:36]
- Bebop's grapple now deals light melee damage, presumably to synergize with melee items, though the coach does not think you will buy melee items on Bebop. [7:06]
- Billy's Bashdown T3 charge time improved by 1 more second (2 to 3 seconds reduction) and Rising Ram T3 damage went from 8 to 10; Billy was already really good, and the coach as a Billy main will play him more. [7:06]
- Celeste base health reduced by 90 (about 12% of her base health), less health per boon, and no more negative base bullet resist, so she is weaker to spirit damage and about the same to gun; a nerf overall. [7:36]
- Celeste bullet damage rescaled from 22 to 18 plus more scaling, and bullet cycle time increased (an 8% fire rate and DPS cut), which also cuts Light Eater damage since it procs on every shot; a massive early DPS nerf and less oppressive in lane. [8:07]
- Celeste Light Eater initial impact and on-hit damage were rescaled lower early and a bit better late; Light Eater debuff lasts 8 seconds instead of 12 and now scales with spirit. [8:38]
- Celeste Dazzling Trick barrier reduced; Radiant Daggers fire rate went from 7 to 9 but that hardly matters with her lower base fire rate; her ultimate damage increased a tiny bit. [9:08]
- Verdict on Celeste: gutted, definitely not S tier anymore, likely to fall off the meta or at least be much more balanced; good changes. [9:38]
- The Doorman's doorways now reveal on the enemy minimap when within line of sight, so if you pay attention you know when he has a setup and can play around it. [9:38]
- The Doorman's Luggage Cart collision size reduced, a necessary nerf since every Doorman builds Vortex Web. [10:09]
- Luggage Cart no longer stuns, it immobilizes and movement controls, so Dispel Magic can now get you off it in time and you will not be sent through his door into the enemy base. A big deal. [10:09]
- The coach finds The Doorman's send-the-enemy-to-your-Walker play style lame and wants him more interactive; these changes are a good step. [10:40]
- Dynamo got tiny ability buffs. [10:40]
- Grey Talon got slight buffs: spirit power per boon increased, a move speed increase on one ability, Guided Owl minimum speed reduced, maximum speed and duration increased, and Charged Shot move speed increased so it is easier to land. Valve is careful because small damage buffs can make him broken; he was weak before. [10:40]
- Haze Sleep Dagger and Smoke Bomb got buffs (wake up delay and spirit scaling, Sleep Dagger T2 cooldown reduction from 15 to 17 seconds, longer sleep, more Smoke Bomb lifesteal) and Fixation weapon damage increased. [11:41]
- Haze Bullet Dance now targets a cone in the direction you aim instead of a full radius, so people behind you are not hit and you do much less damage into groups. [12:12]
- Bullet Dance no longer grants evasion, which is why Haze survived so long in her ult; it is now more of a suicide ability that is a bit better on a single target, with higher fire rate and a slightly lower cooldown. [12:42]
- Bullet Dance T3 changed from one additional target to giving back the evasion and move speed, so until you max the ult it does much less damage and even then hits one target. Verdict: good nerfs, hopefully not as oppressive in ult. [13:12]
- Holliday got unspecified behavior and feel improvements. [13:42]
- Kelvin Frost Grenade healing scaling and T3 heal reduced; slight Kelvin nerfs. [13:42]
- Lady Geist bullet damage rescaled from 21.9 to 20.7 plus more scaling, and Soul Exchange cooldown increased by 5 seconds. [13:42]
- Verdict on Lady Geist: this does nothing, gun damage was not her issue until late, her drain is; she is still absolutely S tier, a slap on the wrist compared with gutting Celeste. [14:15]
- McGinnis bullet damage increased, Spectral Wall duration increased and its segment count went from 7 to 8; slight quality of life buffs she needed. [14:46]
- Mirage Fire Scarabs health steal per second reduced from 9 to 8 with lower spirit scaling, and Djinn's Mark got a bit weaker. [14:46]
- Verdict on Mirage: lifesteal was not the issue, the scarab damage reduction is, since you cannot hurt him once scarabs are on you; not enough to take him out of the meta, he stays oppressive until very late. [15:17]
- Paradox bullet damage increased, Time Wall wider and much taller, Kinetic Carbine hitbox slightly bigger; she is already the best pick hero and the significant bullet buff may push her into hot meta. [15:50]
- Rem fix: a months old bug let high level players hit the same box with his helpers and himself at once for double souls. [16:20]
- Rem's Tag Along can no longer be cancelled during the cast delay, and Nap Time sleep duration increased. [16:20]
- The coach thinks Rem's win rate is low because most Rems sit in their own jungle stealing their team's farm; Rems should steal the enemy Sinner's Sacrifices instead. [16:50]
- Shiv fix: rage now builds against barriers. [16:50]
- Venator trap immobilize duration reduced, trap cooldown increased, and trap T1 changed from 18 to 11 seconds cooldown reduction, encouraging you to level the trap back to the old values; the coach says the trap was not the problem, his stun, melees and huge damage are. [17:20]
- Victor bullet damage increased and Jumpstart T2 went from 50 to 70 total HP regen. [17:50]
- Victor Aura of Suffering now builds up over 8 seconds instead of 6 (a nerf), its T1 slow now also affects dashes, the slow is reduced, and minimum and maximum DPS increased (max by 20); overall a buff to Victor. [17:50]
- Victor's Shocking Reanimation now applies a heavy diminishing slow as soon as he dies, slowing enemies around him so it should be easier to keep them in stun range. [18:21]
- Vyper's dagger charge time reduced, and sliding uphill now allows lateral movement with more slither distance. [18:52]
- Yamato bug fix: Crimson Slash now hits through veils, which makes a really good hero stronger. [18:52]
- Overall verdict: good Haze and Celeste nerfs, good lane changes, but Venator and Lady Geist are not addressed; the meta will not change much beyond seeing less Celeste and Haze, and what is really needed is a matchmaking patch. [19:23]
