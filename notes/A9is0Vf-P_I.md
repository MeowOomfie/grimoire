## Covers
A high-rank Wraith game review from September 2024: a won solo lane against McGinnis thrown by a Kelvin gank and tilt, then playing from behind as a passive farming carry (Tesla Bullets at 3k, lane choice, splitting against a grouped enemy, tower rules) into a late tanky build that wins the fights.
For Wraith and other farm-carry players, and anyone learning safe farming and playing from behind. This is a 2024 video: Wraith's kit, the items, soul sharing and map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Early lane key: play pressure, play for creep souls, and only go for kills when you want to. [0:30]
- The best way to play Wraith is very defensive for most of the game, only going for kills when you have to. [1:00]
- Many players run around with Telekinesis lifting and killing someone in a lane over and over, but that is not Wraith's strength; she becomes insanely strong once really farmed, like a League AD carry or a Dota carry (Anti-Mage, Terrorblade). [1:30]
- After the laning stage it is very beneficial to play passively, secure more farm and save Telekinesis defensively for when an enemy ganks you. [2:00]
- You can tell a game is high rank from the in-game spectator mode: games on the first and second page have a lot of spectators. [2:00]
- Wraith is solid in lane against almost everything but doesn't win many lanes by much; he focused only on last hitting. [2:30]
- McGinnis is scary to play against in lane: if she charges up she does a lot of damage, and peeking her without respecting cover is horrible. [3:01]
- Track the enemy's reload; he survived a bad peek because he knew McGinnis had to reload. [3:01]
- McGinnis probably wins the lane against Wraith, but it is a pretty even matchup. [3:33]
- Never become complacent when far ahead. [3:33]
- Sit back while Healing Rite finishes to get back to full HP, then go back in aggressively. [3:33]
- Dodge McGinnis's wall; getting caught in it with lots of creeps and the enemy can kill you, and the wall does damage. [4:03]
- When you are winning lane, worry about ganks: a good enemy helps out the lanes that are losing. [4:03]
- Mistake: at double her souls (3.5k to 1.7k at 5 minutes) he stayed super far pushed to her tower and didn't watch the minimap, so a Kelvin gank killed him. [4:33]
- Kelvin is the most broken hero in the game right now. [5:35]
- One gank kill took the Kelvin from 3.3k to 4.1k instantly while he stayed at 3.5k; that is how fast you lose an advantage. [5:35]
- He wants to sit in lane on Wraith until Tesla Bullets: it is the main farm tool that scales Wraith into the late game and almost a must for gun Wraith. [6:37]
- Mistake: after getting McGinnis low and she pops her ult, run away; walking back in with Project Mind up was a tilt move. [7:10]
- McGinnis's ult is one of the worst ults in the game; really the only thing it does is splash damage behind walls. [7:10]
- Two missteps took the lane from completely winning to even. [7:40]
- When behind and without Tesla Bullets, look for a lane or any souls you can farm safely until 3k. [8:41]
- As soon as he hits 3k he backs out, plays safe and rotates to the shop; his play changes drastically after Tesla Bullets. [9:13]
- Mistake: staying greedy in lane after not noticing on the minimap that two enemies were missing; back out in the first place. [9:43]
- Respawn lane choice: pick a lane where the enemy creeps are pushed up to your tower, since you can farm safely under your Walker. [11:13]
- Avoid a lane where your creeps are pushed up to their Walker with nobody else around; you can very easily get ganked there. [11:13]
- Without Bullet Lifesteal he can't yet farm creep camps one after another; Bullet Lifesteal is a big part of what lets Wraith sustain and farm the jungle. [11:43]
- If four enemies are pushing one of your towers, don't go protect it; it is a waste of time. [11:43]
- Wraith number one rule: play for your souls and play safe, then help when you can; don't be an AFK farmer like many Infernus players who vanish into the jungle for 20 to 30 minutes. [12:14]
- If three of your team are already on a lane, there's no reason to go there. [12:14]
- If your team is fighting close to you, help: even one damage on a kill gives you assist souls, which is 100% worth it. [13:14]
- With Project Mind at level one (short range, 45 second cooldown) don't teleport in aggressively; save it defensively. [13:44]
- Stay on the side of your Seven while he ults, and lift an enemy who escapes toward you for a free card kill. [13:44]
- When Kelvin hits you with his laser you can't shoot him. [14:15]
- Back out as soon as you see enemies rotating in; this is how easily you die early on Wraith. [14:45]
- Playing aggressively for fights early is not worth it on Wraith. [15:17]
- Rule of thumb: with lift, Wraith wins a 1v1 against almost every hero most of the time, even one with more farm, but early team fights are not great for her since she needs damage items and defense items to stay alive. [15:17]
- After a fight, back out and regen, then go to a lane whose creeps are pushing into your Walker. [15:48]
- The most important part of getting good is knowing where to go for the safest and fastest farm; high rank players will punish overstepping. [16:19]
- If you know the whole enemy team is fighting your team, you can push further on a lane than usual. [16:49]
- Rule of thumb: once your tier 1 tower is gone, the furthest you should go is where your tier 1 tower used to be; further without info on the enemies and good players will kill you. [16:49]
- Don't join a fight when you are far away and don't have your ult; take the enemy tier 2 instead while they are all pushing your tower elsewhere. [17:21]
- Trading a lost team fight for an enemy Walker is worth it, and killing the first Walker unlocks a flex slot. [17:53]
- Pulling three or four enemies onto you while you take a tower, and getting out in time, is a really good play. [18:25]
- If you're still fairly full HP you don't need to go back to base; you can buy at the forward shop. [18:25]
- The Ancients is the big creep camp that gives the most souls. [18:25]
- Decision exercise: go to the lane where the enemy wave will push into your Walker (a defensive position) and where there are creep camps. [18:56]
- Skip a lane with no creep camps up and a teammate already farming it. [19:28]
- Past the 10 minute mark creep souls are shared when you kill creeps, so farming next to a teammate splits 50/50, but that beats no souls. [19:58]
- When the enemy team is aggressively fighting your team elsewhere, it is safe for a while to farm camps on the other side. [20:28]
- When behind, taking a fight is risky unless you're up in souls. [21:29]
- An enemy stacking five or six together can push towers early, but they lose a lot of souls because they farm one lane and share its souls between all of them. [21:29]
- Counter a grouped enemy by splitting: one or two players defend very passively (worst case the tower or Walker goes down, that's okay), while others push out other lanes and farm the jungle, even the enemy's. [22:00]
- Taking all the map's resources while the enemy groups up let his team catch up on souls despite losing towers. [23:01]
- Push out your lane before farming your jungle: if the enemy wave is pushed all the way up, enemies can rotate in unseen; a pushed lane gives you warning. [23:33]
- The vault room with the golden statues you punch is one of the best rooms to farm for value, but it is unsafe because it takes a long time and costs HP. [24:05]
- Mistake: he was out of the fight but turned back to save a caught Vindicta and died too; following his own rules he would have lived. [24:36]
- Defending your base when the enemy has pushed you in is actually very doable. [25:37]
- Project Mind at level 3 has a 15 second cooldown and gives a bullet shield on each teleport, so you can play more aggressive and drop in behind the enemy to harass them out. [25:37]
- One or two won team fights against an enemy who got cocky from going uncontested is all it takes to turn things. [26:40]
- Trading kill for kill while behind is definitely worth it. [27:10]
- Wraith scales incredibly well into the late game; she gets scarier the longer the game goes, as long as she is close to their souls, because lift and her damage are insane. [28:12]
- Taking mid boss is a very unsafe strategy at high rank; people will contest it unless they are down a lot of players. [29:15]
- Kelvin alive can drop into your team with his ice dome (Frozen Shelter) and win a mid boss fight, so it is scary to take mid unless you're up a lot. [29:46]
- The game is more about who makes more mistakes than who plays better. [30:16]
- Getting the forward towers is insanely valuable: killing enemy towers speeds up your zipline in that lane so you move around the map faster, and with no towers the creep waves keep pushing and pressure their base. [30:47]
- Burst down towers and use Full Auto to lifesteal back from them. [31:17]
- Vindicta gets more souls from kills with her ult, so it is normal for her to be far ahead. [31:47]
- With your team's Rejuvenator active, trading one for one is worth it since you come back faster and can end the game. [32:17]
- His main healing late is Full Auto's 35% extra lifesteal, which is on cooldown; Bullet Lifesteal is the rest. [32:17]
- When the Rejuvenator is gone and teammates are dead (30 seconds), play passive; read lane positions to know where the enemy is. [33:17]
- Once you have Tesla Bullets and Lucky Shot or Crippling Headshot (what he goes for), you usually have enough damage to kill anyone, so build very tanky. [34:20]
- He almost always buys Improved Spirit Armor because spirit damage is the main meta right now; here he also bought Improved Bullet Armor. [34:20]
- Reactive Barrier gives extra bullet and spirit overshield when you are stunned or slowed; with Healing Booster you get more health back when focused. [34:20]
- Built tanky, he can play forward: the enemy must spend a lot of resources to kill him. [34:51]
- He lifted a diving Kelvin, forced the Frozen Shelter, survived inside the bubble while two or three shot him, and the enemy wasted two ults so his team won. [35:22]
- In that comp the Vindicta is the kill securer and Wraith is the damage over time. [35:52]
