# The BEST Way to Learn New Heroes
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-06-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C7m-UdUIcQA

## Covers
Deathy's method for learning a new hero without a coach (June 2026): play a few games, find the weak phase, pick the top player of that hero from the Statlocker leaderboard, watch their replay against your own habits, pause and predict, write a short list of notes, then play a game applying only those notes.
His example hero is Abrams (lane plan, Golden Goose Egg, spirit item priority, Siphon Life and Shoulder Charge use, stalling for cooldowns), played in an Eternus 6 lobby.

## Claims
- Don't make alt accounts to learn new heroes; just do it on your main, treat it as a play test, and don't be too hard on yourself. [0:30]
- Step one: get a couple of games on the hero so you know what you are struggling with (for him, the Abrams laning stage game plan). [0:30]
- VOD reviewing only yourself is limited because you don't know what you don't know, so mistakes are hard to spot. [1:00]
- Use Statlocker (or any site with a leaderboard): Statlocker leaderboards, filter by hero, open the top player's profile, find a game on that hero, ideally a long one, and watch it in game with the match ID. [1:00]
- He picked the number one Abrams (caption "Overseas"), a 30 minute game where that player went 7-4-23. [1:30]
- While watching, take notes of what the top player does differently from you. [2:00]
- Check their stats too: the top Abrams also had very low lane damage, so low lane damage on Abrams may not be a problem; his player damage skyrocketed from 10 to 20 minutes (almost 16,000). [2:00]
- He had a lot of neutral damage in the first 10 minutes (likely Sinner's Sacrifices) and only about 3,000 from 10 to 20, hitting camps between rotations. [2:30]
- Damage dealt by type showed the gun was about 10% of his damage at 20 minutes; most was Siphon Life and melee. These stats tell you how a hero outputs damage and what to focus on. [2:30]
- Positioning around the veil is standard for Abrams in lane, since he is close range and people don't want to contest him there. [3:01]
- Holding a skill point is a more advanced option; the top player took Infernal Resilience as soon as he took poke. [3:01]
- The top player bought Golden Goose Egg in lane, and mostly didn't trade, focusing entirely on the wave and trading a little only once he had wave priority. [3:31]
- Compare what you did to what they did: Deathy traded far more, thinking Infernal Resilience regen made trades positive. [4:01]
- The top player leveled Shoulder Charge second where Deathy usually took Siphon Life; one replay can't tell you which is standard. [4:01]
- Shooting your own team's small camp for even one damage while a teammate kills it gets you its souls; you can't steal the enemy small camp that way. [4:32]
- When his lane partner (Lash) roamed, he just held the wave, then took the golden statue. [4:32]
- Boxes are up at 3 minutes; he started doing a few of them. [5:02]
- Takeaway: as Abrams you don't need to force early lane interactions because they won't favor you most of the time; unless hard winning, play safer. [5:02]
- His first aggressive interaction came once he had all three abilities (Shoulder Charge and Siphon Life); Abrams becomes a real threat then, so chill until you have all three and then look for openings. [5:33]
- With his partner roaming he held the fort, shooting enemies off the jump pad. [6:07]
- He went straight to the medium camp when it spawned at 5 minutes; many players don't adapt to it spawning then. Having Golden Goose Egg influenced this. [6:07]
- You don't need to know every play style of a hero when learning it, just what the possibilities are. [6:37]
- Use Siphon Life on the wave to clear it; it heals about 35%, half value, from NPCs, which is not bad. [7:07]
- Don't worry about small details when learning a new hero; focus on the general game plan. Nuances matter once you have around 100 games. [7:07]
- Identify which part of the game you actually struggle with. [7:37]
- Until you have both Shoulder Charge and Siphon Life, Abrams does little damage; his gun is bad, and good Siphon Life damage needs Shoulder Charge to get on top of the target and pull them out of position. [8:07]
- Pause the replay and guess what the player will do next, then compare. [8:37]
- With a big soul advantage near the enemy Sinner's Sacrifices, take them. [8:37]
- He cleared the wave before the sacrifices; Deathy would do the reverse, and doesn't think the order matters much. Then he went back for his own sacrifices. [9:37]
- He bought Spirit Snatch, prioritized the spirit (purple) spike hard, then Expansion, reaching an 8,000 soul spirit spike with nearly all souls on spirit. [9:37]
- Diving the walker and getting stunned by it was an obvious misplay. [9:37]
- Deathy's guess: Abrams is strong mid game, so pop the egg soon, buy a vitality item next, and start fighting aggressively. [10:08]
- The top player likely waited until 10 minutes to pop the egg for the upgraded golden statue buffs. [10:08]
- He popped the egg, cleared a wave to afford his item, then bought it (caption "kill bait"). [10:39]
- He pushed a side wave to the walker, hid around the wall, and when four enemies came he used Arcane Surge, got a kill on Warden and hurt Mina. It worked because he was one of the richest players and nobody expected him to stay after pushing. [11:09]
- At a high level, going against player expectations leads to good things; one kill plus four enemies reacting is huge value, letting his team farm the map. [11:39]
