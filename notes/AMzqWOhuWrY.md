## Claims
Ganking and laning:
- A good gank has three factors besides your lane winning: are the enemies pushed up with wave priority (a push on their tower is much harder to force), what are the health pools, and who has ults. [0:30]
- Even if the enemies are winning, if both are half HP and your laners are healthy it is a good angle to gank. [1:00]
- You can force ganks a lot more when your laners have ults like Mo & Krill, Warden or Kelvin, even under tower. [1:00]
- In a losing spot play more selfishly and farm, and only gank on an opportunity: the enemy overextends while low, or the enemies used their ults and your allies have theirs. [1:31]
- When you parry to bait something, wall jump or air dash first and then parry, so they can't punish a fumbled parry and you are making distance toward your escape. [1:31]
- With a weak laner (Haze) into an oppressive lane, you need to box max more. Ido, a top Calico, box maxes in pretty much every lane. [2:02]
- If you start the sprint boots item, use its sprint for box routing; its out-of-combat regen is overstated. [2:32]
- He would start Extra Spirit and then go to the boots; Ido starts spirit. [2:32]
- The first two or three waves dictate how the lane is played, so take a small early advantage, and once you have priority and tempo, start box routing. [2:32]
- Ido goes Healing Rite just for the sprint speed, and in a really hard lane it is not a bad option, more aggressively defensive. [3:02]
- When the enemies have used all their cooldowns, route through them: jump through the Yamato and parry. [3:02]
- Do the mid jump every time you come back down, it gets you back to lane quicker. [3:32]
- Exception: if the wave is crashing into your walker, come down, control off the zipline, ideally edge boost, and wall bounce off the wall to reach the walker. [4:03]
- When resetting, go through the doorway and edge boost off it, it is faster. [4:34]
- When your lane is down, kill the wave and box max; use your 1 on minions, and the second the wave dies go run boxes. [5:05]
- Tell your laner "let's push the wave as quickly as possible", kill your two minions and leave, and trust them to kill the other two in a similar time. [5:37]
- You should not be ganking that early; counter roaming the Paige was fine only because she was overextended and low, though it forced your 1. [6:07]
- You died because you did not track the enemy ult advantage; one Wraith card plus a Viscous combo evaporates you. Respect abilities that can kill you even without ults. [6:38]
- He generally doesn't like mirroring roams. He prefers hitting the tower, since a Yamato can't solo defend: back up, shoot the wave, use your 1 and play behind your tower. [7:09]
- Either respect an enemy a lot more or pay attention to whether she has her ult up. [8:10]
- If the Paige makes that roam around 9 minutes, just take the tower. [8:40]
- When you jump down to escape, hug the wall and get under it and out of line of sight as fast as possible; falling away from the wall got you killed. [9:10]

Playing from behind:
- When very behind, slow play, farm and box route. Comeback souls help when boxing your own jungle, but enemies can invade freely, so figure out where their strong side is. [10:10]
- Options from behind: shove a wave and pressure a tower, then when they defend it, steal a camp or box-route invade on the other side of the map. [10:10]
- Going into your own farm for five minutes is safer but very selfish, since you lose your teammates the opportunity. [10:40]
- When the enemies show four middle and right side, your team should create pressure on the left. [10:40]
- Ignoring the mid fight and slow farming was perfect. [11:11]
- Watch the map: when the enemy Sinner's Sacrifice disappears you know the Seven grabbed it, and he should head back to the neutral wave, so you should leave that point. [12:11]
- You shouldn't be invading there. [12:41]
- Your team should not contest that; look to kill the Seven or pressure the left side of the map. [14:13]
- The best way back into a game is the comeback urn. Second, play your pick characters (Mo & Krill, Haze) for picks on side lanes. [14:44]
- Your Mo & Krill should not sit mid all game. The Seven can keep pushing up because he sees Krill on the map; if Krill stops showing, he can't keep walking up to the walker. [15:14]
- You had the right idea to fight that, but you didn't heal first. [15:46]
- When this far behind, farm instead of walking toward a fight; with five enemies shown on the right side you have free roam of that jungle. [16:16]
- Running across the map to the enemy's strong side when you are this behind does not do much, because you won't do much in the fight. [16:47]
- Hybrid camps are very good for souls. Tier 1 and tier 2 creeps give fairly similar souls while tier 3 gives a lot more, so the basement camp with many small creeps is a huge amount of souls. [17:17]
- When you have pressure, invade their jungle; it is risky but you need to force a play when this behind. Stealing the Sinner's Sacrifice is relatively safe when you see so many mid. [18:19]
- Biggest issue: when behind you rotate into fights instead of netting jungle. Plus one Sinner's Sacrifice is a huge amount of farm for you and denies it to them. [18:49]
- When your team is down 20k, about 95% of mid fights are bad; mid is always a mosh pit, so look on a side lane. [19:20]
- It was good you and Krill didn't rotate in; they would have just taken mid boss. [19:51]
- Your Mo & Krill sitting mid when you are far behind is the main macro issue; he needs to be on a side lane. [19:51]
- Walk the comeback urn. [20:21]
- Leave your Haze to defend; she can solo the wave, and if they kill her they are trading a kill for the urn, which is very worth it for you. [20:51]
- After winning kills while still behind, use the pressure to steal their jungle, at least the single Sinner's Sacrifice camps, instead of looking for another fight mid. [22:29]
- You shouldn't die for the Wraith. [23:00]
- At 1 HP deep in their base you are in panic mode; path to the vent, it is your only option. [23:00]
- Vent tech: hit the vent while holding the opposite directional key, then double jump and wall bounce to make it up; if needed, dash and mantle. [23:30]
- If you are too low to survive the lightning tick, try a Hail Mary: hit the vent into their base, stun them when they follow, then dash out. [24:01]

Fighting:
- Instead of chasing three enemies at half HP, kill the mispositioned Paige 100 to 0; tunnel visioning on the rest of their team lets Paige support and leave easily. [25:03]
- Look for the Paige when you have the option, because they could have turned the fight. [25:34]
- When the target isn't looking at you, lead with your 1 (Gloom Bombs) and slow down for a second to get guaranteed bombs; you are not forced to full combo. [25:34]
- You can pretty consistently light melee after your 2. [26:05]
- Know your damage limits: count how many bombs stick, and if only one stuck, go for the light melee to finish. [26:05]
- The real problem is being down many Sinner's Sacrifice buffs while your team keeps fighting mid for no reason. [26:35]
- Dying for your teammates is really bad if you lose the bag. [27:05]
- You are fighting without your team; with two people on that wave and your mid wave about to crash, just fix your waves. [30:09]
- Your team keeps looking for picks in mid lane instead of side lane. [30:39]
- You ulted too early: when the Seven stuns you, hit the walker until the end of the stun, or roll into the walker, melee it and then ult, for more damage and a better timer. [31:09]
- High objective damage in solo queue is fine; in competitive it would point to an issue, but better that you split than no one. Calico has decent split potential, just not as good as a gun hero. [32:09]
- When your team takes a coin flip fight mid, use that pressure to hit their walker. [33:10]
- If a teammate keeps making the same mistake, counter call him instead of blindly following. [33:41]
- Looking for the pick on Paige was good, but cut toward the vent to get out. [34:12]

Items:
- Counterspell is really good but situational. This comp (Wraith, Seven, Viscous) is a great Counterspell comp, and it is amazing into Yamato's 1, denying about 800 damage, and against Abrams' charge. [28:37]
- He would not buy Counterspell in the green spike; buy it later in the game. [29:07]
- Counterspell is like Blood Tribute or Ethereal Shift: good, but learning the item takes time. [29:07]
- Counterspell's ideal targets are Yamato, Wraith and Shiv. [29:38]

Summary:
- Early game, box out more when you have a weak laner into two aggressive laners. [34:12]
- Do the mid jump every time. [34:12]
- When parrying, wall bounce first. [34:44]
- Don't be afraid of jungle camps, especially in their jungle: throw Gloom Bombs at a small or medium creep (melee the medium one too) and leave, using cooldowns for free farm. [34:44]
- Always invade Sinner's Sacrifice if you have the option. [34:44]
- Even in your own jungle, use your cooldowns on a camp as you walk through it to reset, leashing it for a teammate; skip this in cat form so you don't break it. [35:14]
- Biggest problem was perma fighting. [35:14]
