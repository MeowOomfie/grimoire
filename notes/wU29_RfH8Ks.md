# Tips to lane better against EVERY hero  - Valve's Deadlock
channel: Deathy  date: 2024-09-06  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wU29_RfH8Ks

## Covers
How to lane against each 2024 hero: Abrams, Bebop, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash, McGinnis, Mo & Krill, Paradox, Pocket, Seven, Shiv, Vindicta, Viscous, Warden, Wraith, Yamato; items Decay, Reactive Barrier, Healing Rite, Extra Health, Enduring Spirit, Extra Regen, Slowing Hex, Extra Stamina, Restorative Shot; cooldown windows, damage amp stacks, denies, wave pushing

## Claims
### General
- The video is not an exhaustive tips and tricks video, just what he thinks about when laning against specific heroes. [0:00]

### Abrams
- Abrams has a ton of regen, especially if you fight him in Siphon Life; do not try to trade with him while inside it. [0:32]
- Shoulder Charge will not necessarily kill you, but it makes you a lot easier to kill. [0:32]
- His passive gives a ton of health regen by default plus damage regenerated, which makes him very tanky. [0:32]
- Against Abrams, focus on last hits (captioned "lcid"); right now he is an extremely strong hero and hard to beat in lane. [0:32]
- To beat him in lane, get Decay very early, as Decay counters him pretty hard. [1:02]
- Reactive Barrier is also very good: if he charges you, you get 600 health worth of shields and are very hard to kill. [1:02]

### Bebop
- Bebop is a very specific matchup with two main ideas in lane; the first is to hook you. [1:02]
- Against hook, position so you are hard to hook: do not show yourself easily and do not make your movement predictable; the hook is not that easy to hit. [1:02]
- The second is his bomb, which he wants to apply as often as possible; many Bebops roll into enemies, apply the bomb and roll away, so position to make that harder. [1:32]
- Reactive Barrier works against hook and against uppercut, so buy it if you keep getting hooked and dying. [1:32]

### Dynamo
- The main thing to be careful of is his first ability, Kinetic Pulse; many Dynamos put two points into it right away and it does a surprising amount of damage. [2:02]
- If he hits you with Kinetic Pulse you will die, as his gun does a ton of damage; if you see him rolling at you aggressively to use it, back up to dodge it. [2:02]
- Otherwise the lane against Dynamo should generally be fairly easy, as he has a giant head hitbox and you can harass him a ton. [2:02]
- His other abilities are not very threatening in lane, but watch out for Quantum Entanglement if you have abilities he can dodge, for example Seven's stun. [2:32]
- His healing makes him a bit harder to bully out of lane, but he needs it because his head hitbox is so big; always shoot Dynamo as much as possible. [2:32]

### Grey Talon
- Charge Shot has a ton of burst damage and he can use it very often. [2:32]
- Rain of Arrows gives him a lot of damage while in the air and is extremely scary. [3:03]
- Grey Talon's entire game plan is to try to kill you; also watch out for his Immobilizing Trap. [3:03]
- The best thing you can do is buy health items early such as Healing Rite, Extra Health or Enduring Spirit to survive his burst. [3:03]
- If he cannot kill you in lane, you can usually buy items to deal with him later, so focus mostly on last hits against him. [3:03]

### Haze
- Haze is a very weak laning hero; push the wave in as much as possible, as she has no tools to deal with the wave and very little harass early. [3:34]
- Her passive is not very good early, Sleep Dagger is decent but not that scary, and her other ability offers her nothing in lane. [3:34]
- No specific item is needed against Haze; push the wave and be aggressive, because there is not a lot she can do. [3:34]

### Infernus
- Infernus is a very strong laner because he gets a lot of lifesteal really early. [4:04]
- His Catalyst gives 25% damage amp; if you get hit by Catalyst, run away. [4:04]
- His afterburn builds up: hitting you again refreshes the duration, so while you are burning do not peek him at all. [4:04]
- If he goes in with Flame Dash, run away as well; basically, if Infernus is using an ability you most likely cannot trade into him. [4:34]
- Decay is a good buy against him because he is very reliant on lifesteal. [4:34]
- Infernus is one of the strongest laners right now, but without Catalyst and Flame Dash he can be pretty weak to getting gone on by certain heroes. [4:34]

### Ivy
- Ivy is not an overall very strong laner. [4:34]
- Kudzu Bomb (captioned "kutub bomb") does a movement slow, so do not stand in it. [5:04]
- Watcher's Covenant gives bonuses to Ivy and her ally, so when the effect is active be a little more passive. [5:04]
- Stone Form is the one ability to watch out for and usually the one Ivy skills first; whenever Ivy uses it, use a stamina to roll back. [5:04]

### Kelvin
- Kelvin is an extremely oppressive laner: his beam slows your movement and attack speed, his grenade lets him nuke and push the wave, and Ice Path lets him gank other lanes very easily. [5:36]
- There are not a ton of items to buy specifically against Kelvin. [5:36]
- If he uses his Ice Beam, use your stamina to get as far away as possible. [5:36]
- Do not stand on top of creeps too much, so he has to choose rather than hitting you with Frost Grenade and pushing the wave at the same time. [5:36]
- When Kelvin does not have his Arctic Beam he can be pretty vulnerable to getting gone on. [6:07]

### Lady Geist
- All three of her lane abilities can be scary. [6:07]
- Essence Bomb does a lot of damage but is fairly easy to dodge if you pay attention; always be looking at Lady Geist and be ready for the bomb to be thrown. [6:07]
- Life Drain is only scary if you trade with her; if she uses it on you, you are usually better off hiding around a corner, as she needs line of sight. [6:07]
- Malice does damage amp per stack; with a lot of stacks you absolutely cannot trade with her, so play more passive with stacks on you and more aggressive without them. [6:38]
- Her main threat is nuking you down with Essence Bomb, so dodging her bomb is the key to winning lane against her. [6:38]

### Lash
- Lash has two main damage tools, Ground Strike and Flog: he usually uses Flog to trade with you and heal, and Ground Strike to try to kill you. [6:38]
- Position to make Ground Strike hard to hit: for example, if you are on top of the bridge and Lash grapples onto you, a double jump usually makes Ground Strike very hard to hit. [7:09]
- In a dual lane, do not stack on top of each other, so Lash cannot hit Flog on both of you at the same time. [7:09]

### McGinnis
- McGinnis is a decent laner, but if you focus her turrets she is not that scary; when she puts up a turret, make shooting it a priority, without missing creeps or taking a ton of damage for it. [7:40]
- Her wall is her main threat in the laning stage: if she cuts you off out of position with it, you might as well be dead. [7:40]
- She makes a little sound cue when deploying her wall; when you hear it, run away as fast as possible. [7:40]
- Her wall has a very long cooldown, so be aggressive on her while it is on cooldown. [8:10]

### Mo & Krill
- Mo & Krill can sometimes feel unbeatable in lane; the two main counters are dodging his Scorn and buying Decay. [8:10]
- Sand Blast lets him disarm you if you go on him, making him very hard to kill. [8:10]
- Scorn gives him a ton of healing on heroes, especially in a dual lane: hitting two heroes with no upgrades or items heals him for 240 health, so many Mo & Krill players dash into you and use Scorn. [8:10]
- Scorn has a vertical hitbox, so it still hits you even if you are on top of the bridge. [8:41]
- If you see him dashing forward, always dash backwards. [8:41]
- Rushing Decay very early, sometimes as your first item, lets you bully him out of lane fairly easily, as it does a lot of damage to him and significantly reduces his healing. [8:41]

### Paradox
- Paradox is a very oppressive laner; Kinetic Carbine has an audio cue that tells you when it is charging. [9:12]
- While she has Kinetic Carbine up, do not peek her at all and wait it out; if she consistently hits carbines on you, you are almost guaranteed to lose the lane. [9:12]
- Pulse Grenade does some damage and gives damage amp stacks; getting hit multiple times means taking 20% extra damage from Paradox, which is especially scary with Kinetic Carbine. [9:12]
- Reactive Barrier works against her Swap: if you expect an all-in as soon as she gets her ultimate, it gives you 600 effective health when she swaps you, often more health than before the swap. [9:42]
- Overall game plan: do not get hit by Kinetic Carbine, push the wave and harass her when you can, but recognise she is a very strong laner and it will be tough. [9:42]

### Pocket
- Pocket has -15% base spirit resist, making him very weak to spirit poke. [10:12]
- Barrage gives him 7% damage amp per stack up to four stacks (28% at four), applying to his abilities, gun and items. [10:12]
- Make sure you do not get hit by Barrage; you will usually get hit by at least one, but it is a fairly slow projectile, so dash in unpredictable, erratic patterns to only take one. [10:43]
- The amp lasts 15 seconds; with two stacks you need not be that scared, but with four do not fight him under any condition. [10:43]
- Flying Cloak and his suitcase make him fairly hard to kill; Slowing Hex stops him going into his cloak, but good Pockets cast the cloak, go into the suitcase and teleport to it once Slowing Hex is down. [11:14]
- With spirit nukes, focus Pocket all the time; in a dual lane against Pocket and another hero, for example as Grey Talon, use all your spirit nukes on Pocket. [11:14]

### Seven
- Seven's laning stage is mostly shoving the wave into you with Power Surge and his lightning ball, which both push the wave very fast. [11:44]
- His stun can be quite scary, as getting stunned out in the open usually means death, but there is a significant delay before it, so you usually have time to escape. [11:44]
- His biggest weakness is getting caught out of position: he has no escape and little survivability at first. [12:15]
- Seven has 8% base bullet resist, making him more resistant to bullet spam. [12:15]
- There are no specific items to buy against Seven in lane; mostly make sure your wave is not pushed in too hard and keep it even, or he will just harass you under Tower. [12:15]

### Shiv
- Shiv is a very strong laner, and how you play depends on his build: gun or knife. [12:46]
- You can check where he spends ability points by pressing Tab and hovering over his profile; if he maxes the knife you will be harassed a lot, so buy health regen such as Extra Regen. [12:46]
- If he goes the max Slice and Dice route instead, he still does a lot of spirit damage early either way. [13:16]
- Shiv relies on bursting you down and regening through Bloodletting: Decay counters Bloodletting, and Extra Health or barriers help you survive his burst. [13:16]
- A weak Shiv is fairly easy to deal with in lane, but a very good one will pressure you and you will need these items. [13:16]
- Shiv is a very strong shotgun character, so he can get a lot of denies on you; do not make them easy. [13:47]

### Vindicta
- Her lane abilities all offer different threats: Stake makes you vulnerable to ganks or other abilities and tethers you for 2 seconds, Flight gives her a lot of early damage, and Crow Familiar is decent damage over time plus percent health damage, making it particularly effective against tanks. [13:47]
- Be careful when she has Flight; most characters cannot really trade with her during it at the moment. [14:18]
- Flight has a 40 second cooldown, so if you avoid fighting her during its duration you can be aggressive for about 32 seconds at the start of the game. [14:18]
- Stake on its own is not that scary but can be very scary in dual lanes. [14:18]
- There is not much to itemize against Crow Familiar; it is a fairly slow projectile, so with good, unpredictable movement you should dodge it most of the time. [14:50]

### Viscous
- Viscous is a very strong laner: his punch does a lot of damage and counts as a melee attack, his nuke does a ton of damage, and his cube can save himself or an ally for 4 seconds and dispel them. [14:50]
- Focus on dodging the punches; they have a sound cue and are dodgeable but very hard, so make your movement as unpredictable as possible. [15:20]
- Do not stack on top of creeps, because a punch that hits you and the creeps pushes the wave, gets him the last hit and damages you. [15:20]
- Against Viscous you are generally not in a kill lane, so do not itemize to kill him unless you are doing very well; focus on farming. [15:20]

### Warden
- Warden's biggest lane threats are Binding Word (the cage) and his grenade. [15:50]
- All you need to get out of the cage early is two stamina, still fine if he has Mystic Reach: perform a dash jump (dash, then jump at a specific time), which you can practise in the hero sandbox. [15:50]
- Get Extra Stamina early, as one extra stamina makes it much easier to always have two ready for his cage. [15:50]
- Alchemical Flask is the other scary part: he spams it, it slows you and reduces your weapon damage, so if you get hit by it you pretty much cannot trade with him. [16:22]
- Do your best to dodge the flask; if he misses it, that is usually when you can be aggressive. [16:22]
- Warden is very slow and has a fairly large hitbox early, so be aggressive, especially on a gun damage hero like Infernus or Vindicta. [16:22]

### Wraith
- Wraith's two scary lane tools are her cards and Full Auto; most players put two points in cards early, making a card a 150 damage nuke with no items, with two charges, and they usually build extra charge and other items to amp it. [16:53]
- Full Auto is mostly a way to build cards faster and add a bit of damage; when she uses it, try not to show so she cannot build cards off you. [16:53]
- You can see how many cards Wraith has (one, two or three) on top of her hat. [17:23]
- Many Wraiths keep enough cards to burst you from nearly 100 to zero, building two or three and using them all at once; if you engage while she has two or three cards, your chances of winning are very small. [17:23]
- Her teleport is not particularly scary in lane but lets her escape some situations; it has a 45 second cooldown you can take advantage of. [17:53]
- With no cards she is a very weak hero and her gun does not do much damage, so be aggressive on her. [17:53]

### Yamato
- Yamato's main lane tool is Power Slash: it does a ton of damage and has a ridiculously large hitbox. [17:53]
- Focus on dodging power slashes; many Yamatos make it extremely obvious when they go for one, so hide around a corner or behind geometry. [18:24]
- If Yamato cannot hit power slashes on you, it will be very hard for her to win the lane. [18:24]
- She has the lowest base health in the game at 500, so she is extremely vulnerable to getting gone on early, especially before she has points in Power Slash, since by default she gets no bullet resist while channeling it. [18:24]
- In a dual lane, focusing her early can often get kills because she is so vulnerable. [18:55]
- Watch out for her right click explosive shot, which does a lot of damage if the Yamato is good at hitting it; there is not much you can do except expect a lot of harass, so Healing Rite, Restorative Shot or Extra Regen are good options. [18:55]
