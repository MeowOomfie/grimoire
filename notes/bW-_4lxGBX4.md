## Covers
Shiv, Midknight. Coaching review of an Oracle-rank Shiv (dash build into a knife build) laning with Lash into Paige and Seven, enemies later include Mina, McGinnis, Victor and Lady Geist, patch of October 2025.
Themes: running the Urn, egoing and over-aggression, sunk cost chases, playing around Paige's barrier, Reactive Barrier, healing items, slot management and target prioritization.

## Claims
Lane (Shiv with Lash into Paige and Seven):
- In this lane focus Paige. [0:31]
- Headshot Booster goes crazy into Seven: his big head lets you farm procs, and the item is already very good. [0:31]
- Seven takes reduced headshot damage, which balances him in lane, but headshot procs (Holliday's Crackshot, Infernus burn, Haze double Fixation, Headshot Booster) still go crazy on him. [1:33]
- He doesn't like a Monster Rounds start here; prefer classic regen like Restorative Shot. [1:03]
- On a dash Shiv, prefer Restorative Shot over Mystic Regeneration, because you are not putting points into knives so you don't get the duration and Mystic Regeneration won't heal much. [2:03]
- Holding the wave for your lane partner while he farms is great. [2:03]
- Against a Paige the whole lane revolves around her barrier: when she uses barrier, hide; when it is down and she misplaces by one step, put everything in your kit on her. [2:34]
- Don't go in while your Lash is behind or healing; don't use your dash to commit, try to roll in and dash out. [3:04]
- Track Paige's barrier; if she holds it until after you dash in and you lack kill potential, you probably die and she lives. [5:10]
- If you ever have the option to give them wave pressure when they aren't respecting Lash, take it. [4:39]
- Reactive Barrier is very OP; into Paige and Seven it is amazing: the second a stun procs it you get about +300 to 400 HP. [5:40]
- With Reactive Barrier it is fine to fight a Seven who has his stun up, since his 2 on you gives you about 400 HP. [7:10]
- Tanking Paige's root for fun procs Reactive Barrier, but in theory you take more damage than you get from it. [22:45]
- Stop egoing: stepping one more foot up while they had kit down could have caught you in the open. [6:40]
- When a lane partner is at 1 HP and doesn't buy Healing Rite, it is a bad sign; buying healing is right. [3:34]

Mechanics and tech:
- Shift dash into tower then parry, like Calico players do: you get the dash damage on the tower and a free parry without taking gap-closing damage. [8:42]
- Once the target gets to their tower and your ult doesn't kill, stop the sunk cost chase: throw a knife if you want and back off. [8:11]
- Be careful meleeing someone who is dead to rights; the only way they live is by parrying you. [8:42]
- On a tower, parry the tower and melee it; melee is the most efficient damage. Knives on tower barely increase farm speed and put a 45-second cooldown on them so you can't fight. [16:55]
- When escaping, seek verticality with your dash, for example dash up a ledge or wall jump. [23:47]

Itemization:
- He prefers a T3 over buying two T2s when your slots are already full and you are far from a flex slot; don't clog your slots. [12:50]
- Counterspell is decent but not crazy this game; you have to press it often. [13:21]
- Healbane is fine once you have a flex slot. [17:26]
- Radiant Regen should come before Spirit Lifesteal; a Radiant rush is not bad on Shiv. If you walk away from the shop with 2.4k souls, you could have bought it. [18:28]
- People sleep on Radiant Regen: the flat heal on cast is insane, plus the movement speed. [19:06]
- Sinclair buys Radiant Regen almost exclusively for the flat heal; with Veil Walker he is very hard to catch. [20:07]
- Remember to use Radiant Regen and Spirit Lifesteal in fights; your defense is your offense. [19:37]
- Spirit Resilience is very good into a spirit-heavy comp on a knife build; Spellbreaker is not crazy. [24:49]
- Escalating Exposure goes crazy this game on a knife build. [25:20]

Macro and the Urn:
- Put weight toward the lane where you still have a tower; break their tower so your team has space, push into stealing their centers. Worst case you back off and farm two centers. [9:12]
- A 2v2 Urn lane is important: the enemy should send someone, kill you, take the tower and run the Urn. [10:14]
- Don't abandon a wave when low; soak it from a safe spot so you don't lose a whole wave. [13:52]
- Don't path mid while a wave is heading for your Walker. [13:52]
- Run the Urn when it is on your side (green candles mean your side, red candles mean the enemy side); it is free souls and worth more than a double camp. [14:54]
- With two Walkers up the enemy can't contest the Urn, so run it. [14:54]
- 5-minute Urns are always on green lane and 10-minute Urns are always on yellow lane, unless the Urn is staggered. [22:45]
- Don't go for a fight with no backup when you don't know the map and they are only showing one. [21:43]
- Spirit McGinnis is countered by clearing turrets with AoE; Shiv dash clears them. If she ults, get in her face. [10:46]

Target priority:
- When Victor and Paige go on you, focus Paige, not Victor. [22:45]
- As a knife Shiv never focus Victor: your damage comes from stacking knives and Victor has one button that removes all of them. [25:20]
- Priority this game: one-shot Paige or Mina when they are there, Seven you can nearly one-shot, McGinnis is tankier, then Lady Geist second least and Victor least. [27:56]

Summary notes and general advice:
- If the Urn is on your side, run it; the Urn is OP. [27:26]
- Stop egoing 1v1s and 1v2s you can't win and tower dives that aren't the angle. [27:26]
- Avoid sunk cost: if they get out, let them go instead of diving and dying. [27:56]
- Before every fight look at your map and ask if you have an advantage; if not, don't take it. Winning MOBAs is about taking the most statistically advantaged fights. [28:27]
- Taking neutral fights while behind meant fighting for fun; he may have been a little light on dash usage overall. [28:57]
