# The New Heroes Will be Meta and You Will Love It | Deadlock Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-02-13
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4za_y_9IkW4

## Covers
Coach's verdict on the minor patch of 2026-02-13, which mostly tunes the six new heroes: Celeste, Graves, Rem, Silver and Venator nerfs and buffs, the new shrine trooper buff, and Quicksilver Reload and Mercurial Magnum procs. For players following the new-hero meta.

## Claims
- He wishes the patch had waited a few days to include Apollo balancing, since he thinks Apollo needs a couple of nerfs. [0:00]
- Breaking a shrine now buffs the troopers in that specific lane, and breaking both buffs them down the middle; shrine HP is also reduced. He likes it long run. [0:00]
- He sees an issue with the Patron drop respawn time reduction forcing two stages, because there are few situations where you get the shrines and don't knock the Patron. [0:00]
- Celeste: -6 bullet resist, giving her a negative starting resist like Pocket; he calls this a significant nerf. [0:30]
- Celeste: base gun damage reduced a little, but her gun damage wasn't the issue; her auto damage comes from her ability-based damage increase. [0:30]
- Celeste: Lighteater spirit lifesteal no longer grants lifesteal to allies dealing damage; a good nerf, but not why she dominated lobbies. Lighteater now respects line of sight. [0:30]
- Celeste: Dazzling Trick (her 2) explosion radius reduced slightly; he doesn't care. [1:00]
- Celeste: Radiant Dagger damage reduced by 5 and spirit scaling reduced; good direction, but her 3 still does absurd damage while also giving her 9% amp, and they should nerf it more. [1:00]
- Celeste: ult loses 10 damage and she can now glide while channeling it; this is nothing. [1:30]
- Celeste verdict: these small nerfs do not address her core issues (insane amp, lifesteal and damage) for a hero perma picked or banned in scrims and dominating solo queue; not enough. [1:30]
- Graves: minor base damage nerfs; max attack range increased by a meter, undoing half of last patch's 18 to 16 reduction. It will feel nice but doesn't solve her issues. [2:00]
- Graves: hand width on her 2 reduced; the real issue with her 2 is how far it pulls you in from and how long you are rooted, plus chain roots (for example, coming out of Pocket's suitcase and being re-rooted immediately). [2:00]
- Graves: jars last slightly less but her skulls do more damage, pushing her to use jars in fights; it helps her farm at low levels where skulls are cleared less, and at high levels where people insta-clear skulls it is a buff. He directionally likes it, though she is already strong. [2:31]
- Graves: ult lasts 20% less but still spits out the same number of goblins. [3:01]
- Graves verdict: changes are fine but basically nothing; players are not yet using her to full potential, and as they learn her she will become a problem. [3:01]
- Rem: he doesn't know why Rem got buffed; he likes the light melee damage increase, since encouraging supports to light melee is good. [3:32]
- Rem: Pillow Toss time between charges reduced; nobody maxes it because his other abilities are insane, not because it is weak. Since Pillow refunds his cooldowns, 2 seconds off it is like roughly 10% off his other cooldowns. [3:32]
- Rem: Pillow Toss radius moved to tier two, which he likes; you don't need another charge or the damage, because Rem isn't there for damage. He doesn't think the ability needed a buff. [4:02]
- Rem: Little Helper souls gained on troopers with no allies nearby increased from 50% to 100%; he disagrees, Rem can already set waves and the downside was good. [4:33]
- Rem: little helpers on troopers now give +20% bounty to the killer, like Mekanism turrets, so spamming helpers (especially on the heal creep) gives enemies a small soul advantage; a good change that makes Rems think about their 3. [4:33]
- Rem: helper heal reduced with higher scaling and trooper resist reduced; good, since his 3 is insane in lane and a helpered trooper took about five heavy melees to kill. [5:04]
- Rem: tier three on the 3 gives more spirit resist; in solo queue the meta is always max 2, but max 3 is really good in scrims, so it didn't need a buff. [5:34]
- Rem: enemies attacking a Sinner's Sacrifice near his helpers now scares them back to Rem. This is a bait change: the enemy is better off shooting the orb that comes up when the minion farms the Sinner's Sacrifice, denying it at no HP cost, so it may effectively be a Rem buff. Not bad for low elo. [5:34]
- Rem: Naptime tier three takes 10 more seconds off his ult; strong disagree, he didn't need it. [6:05]
- Silver: base health increased to fix her weak lane; he asks why every new hero needs a strong lane, a hero can just have a weak lane. [6:05]
- Silver: more gun range, which he likes since her gun felt short; maybe people will stop buying Point Blank on her. Ammo increased. [6:36]
- Silver: he questions upgrading her human mode when it already does work and her lightning mode is a guaranteed win. [6:36]
- Silver: Boot Kick spirit scaling increased a lot, but she has few spirit-scaling abilities so you won't build spirit on her; bullet scaling also increased. Valve seems to be trying to get people to invest in spirit on her. [6:36]
- Silver: Lightning Curse build-up rate improved "a bit"; she didn't need it, and he wants Valve to publish numbers instead of vague words. [7:07]
- He complains that only new heroes changed, with no Abrams, Shiv or Billy nerfs. [7:37]
- Venator is probably the weakest of the new heroes: his ult feels insane but the rest of the kit is so-so. [7:37]
- Venator: health increased, grenade tier two now gives duration, gunshot cooldown reduced. [8:07]
- Venator: his 2 buffed a lot: it scales correctly, has a bit more distance and does extra damage on wall hits, because people used it for spacing rather than the stun. He thinks people should use it for the stun and would nerf the fling distance instead. [8:07]
- Venator: his 2 tier two now gives stun duration; previously you could only guarantee a heavy melee after the stun from inside or next to the target, now from further away. Good for confidence, but it takes some skill out of the hero. [8:38]
- Venator: spike traps get more spirit scaling; he likes a pure gun hero having big spirit scaling on one odd ability (as with a hero the caption calls "Jin") because it allows fun off builds like trap Venator. Small cooldown reduction too, making the trap better in lane where it is already oppressive. [9:08]
- You can shoot Venator's traps to clear them; people walk into them because they are still learning. Good Venators place them behind veils, good players predict that, so the traps have a good skill ceiling thanks to breakability. [9:38]
- General issue: the new heroes have ridiculous ability maxes (Celeste has some of the best in the game), and Valve is buffing weak maxes instead of nerfing strong ones, which will not end well. [10:08]
- On Venator you will still max 1 and then ult, because charges on the 1 plus items like "ballistic" (caption) and Rapid Recharge are very valuable; the 3 is not a reliable damage source. [10:38]
- Venator: ult has a little less weapon damage scaling, which is good since it has been one-shotting people; he thinks the blast damage should scale less with boons. Good direction, moving power from the ult into the kit. [11:08]
- Venator: ult now shows a laser when he is aiming at you, like Vindicta's ult; he doesn't think it will matter much. His 2 tier two damage reduced a bit more, which is good. [11:38]
- Quicksilver Reload and Mercurial Magnum now proc on non-spirit abilities, so true damage abilities like Mirage's scarabs, or abilities that technically do weapon damage like Venator's 1 or bomb, can now proc them; good versatility. [12:09]
