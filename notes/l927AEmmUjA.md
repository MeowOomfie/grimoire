## Covers
Mirage, played by aquatiq with commentary over one full game (Djinn's Mark "devastation" build, laning with Wraith into Dynamo and Seven).
Commentary over a game, not a structured guide; item choices and a few lane decisions with reasons.
Patch date: March 2026 (video 2026-03-21).

## Claims
- The build is a Djinn's Mark "devastation" build: technically a spirit build that starts on gun and slowly builds into spirit. [0:30]
- He took Djinn's Mark first, which he calls unconventional; usually you take your 1 (Fire Scarabs) first for the healing and sustain in lane. [0:30]
- He went Djinn's Mark first to attack with his gun into a big-bodied Dynamo, since the lane damage you can do that way is huge. [1:03]
- He bought Healing Rite in lane because the enemy support's damage was very high and he did not expect to survive the lane without it. [1:03]
- He held off on Healing Rite on his lane partner while that partner was being attacked. [2:35]
- He considered Headshot Booster because he was landing headshots all lane against a Seven, whose head is easy to hit. [3:06]
- Wraith is now genuinely strong in lane; she used to be one of the worst laning heroes in the game. [3:36]
- Quicksilver Reload was the item he wanted all lane. [6:45]
- He says Mirage is overpowered and easy to win on even though people rate him D tier. [14:39]
- Against an enemy team with a lot of gun (Battle Vest seen), he skipped Spirit Resilience for now to buy damage instead. [15:43]
- Items he planned next: Ricochet and Titanic Magazine. [16:13]
- Mirage's teleport (ult) is only good in very specific situations, so he holds it. [16:13]
- Build path shown: Toxic Bullets, Titanic Magazine, then Escalating Exposure. [19:20]
- He bought Transcendent Cooldown so he can proc Escalating Exposure more often. [22:30]
- He needed healing from items and bought Leech; Leech works as automatic lifesteal from shooting people, not an active ability. [24:03]
- With Leech he would likely have survived a fight he died in. [26:36]
- He has heard Siphon Bullets is not as good as it used to be; he finds it in no builds now, though it was strong about six months earlier. [26:36]
- A fire rate (gun) buff makes Toxic Bullets proc faster. [27:40]
- Having two Dust Devil (tornado) casts won him a fight. [28:44]
- Silencer is a great item; Spiritual Overflow is the item he calls hard to go wrong with and bought first at that point. [29:16]
- When the enemy turtles in base, take their shrines. [30:24]
- You can hit the Archmother from a distance, but the Hidden King cannot be damaged from as far away from its pit. [30:54]
