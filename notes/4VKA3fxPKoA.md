# The Best Counter For Every Hero In Deadlock
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2025-10-01  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4VKA3fxPKoA

## Covers
One main counter per hero for Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Calico, The Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash, McGinnis, Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Seven, Shiv, Sinclair, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous, Vyper, Warden, Wraith, Yamato; Counterspell, Debuff Remover, Slowing Hex, Capacitor, Silence Wave, Unstoppable, Weighted Shots, parry timing, line of sight, stacking debuffs, focus squishy targets first

## Claims
- The video gives one counter per hero that works no matter what hero you play and no matter how many souls you have. [0:00]
- Counterspell is an amazing item against almost every character in the game. [11:11]

### Abrams
- Abrams is a tank front liner brawler whose whole kit revolves around Shoulder Charge; once it is level two, the wall duration extension guarantees he lands a heavy melee you cannot parry. [0:30]
- He is really strong right now and likes to hide and come in from the side, so in lane and later team fights always know where he is, because he can shut you down whatever hero you play. [1:01]
- Jump or dash out of the way as soon as he jumps in to dodge Shoulder Charge; without it he has nothing but Siphon Life. [1:01]
- Keep your distance, playing close range into Abrams is almost always deadly; when Siphon Life runs out you can fight him close, but watch for melees. [1:32]
- Any debuff reduction (a debuff reducer, Debuff Remover, or Weighted Shots) shortens the Shoulder Charge stun enough that you can still parry; parry right away after the charge hits. Abrams can answer this with Duration Extension or Superior Duration. [2:03]

### Bebop
- Bebop is really good right now, especially in lane; know where he is so you can dodge his hook, because if he misses the hook he most likely will not kill you in the current meta. [2:33]
- Play behind cover and stay out of hook range, or be ready to dodge it. [2:33]
- Like Abrams, you need to know where he is on the battlefield when the fight starts. [3:04]
- A Bebop running double bomb with Sticky Bomb and no gun is "kind of throwing": buy Counterspell or Debuff Remover to remove all his bomb stacks and he is basically useless in the fight. [3:04]

### Billy
- Billy is a melee brawler who wants to melee you, but that does not mean you should parry right away; the biggest mistake is parrying the moment he charges in with the ram. [3:04]
- His usual combo is charge in, slam, press Blasted, slam again, then shoot and start meleeing you. [3:34]
- Most players, especially low rank, parry far too early; take a few melees first and then land a sure parry, because without a parry he melees you over and over and gets a lot of health back. [4:04]
- Like Abrams he is a close range brawler: stay out of his range and be patient with parry timings. [4:04]

### Calico
- Calico is an assassin spirit nuke who comes in and presses all her buttons (slash, Gloom Bombs, ultimate) to nuke you. [4:34]
- After she uses her shadow form she is very vulnerable and cannot sustain, so put damage into her early to force out Return to Shadow early; while she still has it she can go ham. [4:34]
- Most Calicos light melee right after a slash; when you see the slash animation start, parry immediately and you will almost always stun her. You can even bait the slash knowing the melee follows. [5:04]

### The Doorman
- His ultimate puts you in the shadow realm (the hotel); at close range you cannot avoid it once he starts the animation, so stay out of melee range. [5:36]
- Inside the hotel, if you cannot reach the end in time you can dodge the burst: a well timed Mina teleport or Mirage tornado at the very end of the timer negates all the damage and returns you with the HP you left with. [6:06]
- Without a hero counter, buy Counterspell and time it at the very end of the duration; the bug where people jumped off on purpose is fixed. [6:37]
- He hits like a truck with his gun right now. [7:07]
- If he hits you with Call Bell you get minus 100 accuracy and miss most bullets, so play accordingly while debuffed. [7:07]

### Drifter
- Drifter is a ganker and the best 1v1 character in the game; the main mistake is taking any 1v1 against him. [7:38]
- If a teammate is fighting him alone, get close to that fight; you do not need to focus him, just be inside the 25 m isolation radius so his passive turns off. Multiple people looking at him makes him much weaker. [7:38]
- Never fight him alone unless you are very far ahead; even behind in souls he will most likely win the 1v1. [8:08]
- When he marks you he can teleport to you at any time; back out to your Walker or Tower and get ready for the teleport. [8:08]
- Drifters often melee as they teleport in, so if you predict the teleport you can parry and he stuns himself. [8:39]

### Dynamo
- Dynamo is a team fight hero whose kit revolves around Black Hole, one of the best ultimates; that is the one thing to respect. [8:39]
- You can jump Kinetic Pulse (his one); jump high enough and it will not hit you. [9:10]
- Before a team fight or Mid-Boss, check whether the enemy Dynamo has Black Hole; if he does, focus him first or position split from your team, and do not run in chasing one person, or he will Warp Stone in and Black Hole your whole team. [9:10]
- Black Hole is the longest ultimate cooldown in the game: right after he uses it (and Refresher, if he has it) force fights even if you are behind, because you get about two minutes where a Dynamo without Black Hole is not scary. [9:40]

### Grey Talon
- Grey Talon is a spirit nuke who does insane damage with charged shots and has a guided ultimate that reaches across the map. [10:10]
- Call out in voice or text when he uses his bird; in lane he will usually send it to another lane. [10:10]
- Prioritize him first in fights: he is a squishy glass cannon who needs his team in front of him, so find where he is before the fight and focus him. [10:40]
- Counterspell works on both his charged shot and his guided ultimate. [11:11]

### Haze
- Haze builds her damage with fixation stacks: she does little at the start, but with many stacks on you she does massive damage up close even with no items, so always watch how many stacks you have. [11:11]
- She is a ganker who goes invisible, Sleep Darts you, and often runs Silence Wave. [12:11]
- If you are alone inside her ultimate, get out; in most cases you cannot trade against it and will die. [12:11]
- If two or more of you are inside her ultimate, the damage and fixation stacks are split, so do not panic: stay close and shoot her and you will outdamage the ultimate, because she is very squishy. [12:42]

### Holliday
- Holliday is a mobile pickoff initiator who starts fights with the lasso, and her whole kit revolves around using the bounce pad to get in and out very quickly. [13:12]
- Be aware of when she is about to jump in; if she does not land the barrel on you, she will have a tough time landing the lasso. [13:12]
- The lasso has a cast time and a sound cue: listen for it and dodge left or right at the last moment with an air dash or jump. [13:43]
- You can jump over barrels she throws on the floor. [14:13]
- Stay either far from her or very close; without her ultimate she struggles very close, since the bounce pad and powder kick combos are hard to land there. [14:13]

### Infernus
- If you are burning, get out of his line of sight; the number one mistake is staying in it, since a maxed burn deals continuous spirit damage that his gun can extend indefinitely. [14:13]
- Napalm looks harmless but amplifies the damage you take by 40% and reduces your healing, so stay away or break line of sight when hit, especially while burning. [14:45]
- Debuff Remover is your best friend against him because it removes the burn fully. [15:16]
- Standing in Flame Dash extends the burn like his bullets do, so get out instantly: predict and jump it, wall jump, or take high ground, since he cannot climb while dashing. [15:16]

### Ivy
- Right now Ivy is played as a hard carry gun hero; he expects the next patch to nerf her gun damage and send her back to support. [16:16]
- Many Ivys buy Tesla Bullets and Siphon Bullets, so as long as they keep shooting you they will not die and you take a lot of damage. [16:16]
- Watcher's Covenant has a long cooldown and short duration; if it is on and she is more farmed than you, wait it out rather than take the fight. [16:46]
- Force out Stone Form (with an ability or just by shooting her) before trying to kill her; it has a very long cooldown, dodges a lot of spells, and she is not very tanky at base without it. [16:46]
- Do not sit in Kudzu, it slows you. [17:17]
- When she tries to fly away with her ultimate you can cancel it during the cast with a tornado or hard stuns like knockdown, Wraith's stun, or a Billy chain to pull her back down. [17:17]

### Kelvin
- He calls Kelvin kind of broken right now. [18:20]
- Stay out of Arctic Beam's radius; when he uses it he almost always gets a favorable trade. [18:20]
- Respect Frozen Shelter: if you are trying to live in a 1v1, stay far enough away that he cannot dome himself in with you. [18:20]
- If you are chasing or ganking him, stay as close as possible; his last escape is Ice Path, and if he gets far enough away he can dome, full heal, and turn the fight. [18:50]

### Lady Geist
- People struggle against her in lane because they do not respect Life Drain; while she has line of sight she trades favorably, healing herself while damaging you. [19:20]
- The only counter to Life Drain is getting out of line of sight immediately; out of sight it does not heal, but it stays active and resumes if she gets sight of you again. [19:51]
- Watch for Soul Exchange: when she is low she hides behind a corner, then comes back and swaps, leaving her full and you low enough to die instantly. [20:21]
- Play medium range against her. [20:21]
- Advanced tech: Soul Exchange has a small cast time, so a fast player with Counterspell can counter it. [20:51]

### Lash
- Lash is very easy to counter even though he is very strong: take fights inside buildings, not in the open, with cover above you, or he will land Death Slam on your team. [20:51]
- In 1v1s he needs to get high to make Ground Strike, his main damage, hit hard; close quarters are your friends and open spaces are scary. [21:22]
- When you must fight around Walkers and he has his ultimate, play under arches or other overhead cover (for example the yellow Walker on the left side) to block the Death Slam. [21:54]
- Counterspell is an insane counter; timed right, Lash does nothing. [22:25]

### McGinnis
- McGinnis is a split pusher currently played mostly gun focused, and her turrets hurt when she has several up. [22:25]
- Do not fight straight into multiple turrets she set up before the fight; catch her off guard without a setup. [22:57]
- Like Kelvin, when chasing her stay right on her, or she will put her wall behind her once she has distance and escape. [22:57]
- Her ultimate is not super scary; just watch the turrets. [23:29]

### Mina
- Mina is OP right now as a spirit nuker, and her only weakness is that she dies very easily. [23:29]
- Put damage into her immediately and never ignore her in a fight; depending on your hero you can sometimes shoot back and kill her even during her ultimate. [24:00]
- Slowing Hex (1,600 souls) shuts down her escape by stopping her casting her two for 3.5 seconds, forcing her to buy Debuff Remover; with two Slowing Hexes on your team you can hex, wait for the cleanse, and hex again. [24:31]
- When she looks at you, look back and fight her rather than running unless you are low. [24:31]
- In lane play safe and avoid line of sight so she does not get many Love Bites procs, which do insane lane damage. [25:01]
- Force her teleport out early; after that she cannot stay safe and dies to almost anything. [25:01]

### Mirage
- Mirage is pretty one-dimensional right now: he shoots for decent damage and sustains with his scarabs. [25:31]
- Tornado is his strongest ability: it gives him bullet evasion, stuns you, and dodges abilities, so force it out early before going for the kill. Slowing Hex stops him using it. [25:31]
- You can trade with him in lane since his gun damage is low, but do not let Djinn's Mark go past two stacks; at two, back off until they expire, because at four or eight the burst is insane. [26:01]
- In a straight 1v1 he cannot trade efficiently unless he is much more farmed; his strength is damage over time, like Infernus and Mina. [26:32]

### Mo & Krill
- Mo & Krill can shut down anyone; if he wants to ultimate you and lands the grab, there is nothing you can do, so the goal is to avoid being grabbed. [26:32]
- Know where he is, stay out of his radius, and check whether he has Warp Stone or Phantom Strike to close the distance. [27:03]
- Do not stay in melee range, where Scorn heals him; unless you are a melee brawler, poke him from mid range. [27:03]
- Burrow gives him 80% bullet resist but only 30% spirit resist, so spirit damage still hurts and chasing him with spirit abilities is fine; shooting the ground where he is also damages him. [27:34]
- A carry getting grabbed too much can buy Unstoppable once very farmed; the cheaper Debuff Remover removes Sand Blast and shortens the Combo, so you are more likely to survive the grab. [28:04]

### Paige
- Paige is a pretty mediocre hero right now. [28:35]
- In lane do not fight into her overshield (200 at base, 250 after one level); it lasts only 5 seconds, so wait it out, then trade. [28:35]
- Dodge her three; it immobilizes you and is the one thing that really punishes you. [29:05]
- Do not chase her in a straight line, since she can put down fire that slows and burns you; you should not struggle against her much otherwise. [29:05]

### Paradox
- Her Kinetic Carbine charge-up ticking is one of the loudest sounds on the map by design; when you hear it, avoid peeking her, since it is on a significant cooldown. [29:36]
- Her combo is carbine to slow you, swap you into the Time Wall, then Pulse Grenade. [30:06]
- Pulse Grenade stacks damage amplification; sitting in it for more than two or three ticks makes you take a lot of damage from everything, so stay out of it. [30:06]
- She is likely the enemy initiator, so take cover if you hear the ticking before the fight; once the carbine is used, fight her, she is much weaker. [30:37]

### Pocket
- Like Mina, Pocket is survivable with his abilities but very squishy; he comes in from the side, lands Barrage stacks, then a big Affliction. [31:07]
- Avoid Barrage stacks: up to two hits you can still fight, but past two stay away, because at four stacks his damage amp makes all his abilities, especially Affliction, nuke you instantly. [31:07]
- Dodge Affliction with your abilities if fast enough, or react with Counterspell. [32:07]
- If you are afflicted, play safe: it keeps ticking, so 75% health becomes under 50% in a few seconds and he will peek back out to finish you. [32:07]
- Barrage is a slow projectile, especially at long range from above, so jump and dash it; if he lands two or fewer, turn on him, silence him or Slowing Hex him so he cannot escape, and kill him. [32:38]

### Seven
- Seven's kit revolves around Power Surge; against ball builds, do not stand in the ball and get out of it fast. [33:10]
- In lane, when Power Surge is on (you hear the lightning sound), do not trade straight up; that is when he trades efficiently. [33:10]
- If his stun is a problem, Debuff Remover or Counterspell removes it. [33:41]
- Late game, get out of Storm Cloud and do not try to kill him inside it; he takes much less bullet damage there. Take cover, wait it out, then engage. Not moving out of Storm Cloud is why he is still strong in low rank. [33:41]
- Capacitor is a hard counter: used while Power Surge is on, it removes Power Surge and leaves him doing no damage. [34:12]

### Shiv
- Shiv's kit revolves around rage: any damage he does fills it, and at max rage (he glows red) all his abilities hit much harder, with Slice and Dice doing double damage via an aftershock. Stay away from him while in rage, he is very dangerous close range. [34:42]
- Knives are meta and stack up to four times; at two, avoid a third, because at three or four you bleed out fast and he finishes you with Killing Blow. [35:42]
- Counterspell works against his execute if you react fast enough. [35:42]
- Stay medium range and respect close quarters: dashing through several of you gives him big Slice and Dice cooldown resets, so do not over chase him. [36:13]

### Sinclair
- Sinclair does insane nuke damage, doubled while his assistant is out, which copies every spell and adds bonus auto attacks. [36:43]
- The assistant is on a very long cooldown; while it is out do not trade with him, especially in lane, and stay out of his line of sight. [37:14]
- His vexing balls track you but can be dodged with last-second dashes and jumps; the longer they travel, the more damage they do. [37:14]

### Victor
- Victor is the simplest hero to counter: stay out of his aura radius at all times; it damages him too but hurts a lot, and it is his only real damage and what makes him a late game carry. [37:44]
- Do not fight him in melee range; teams that walk into his aura of suffering lose, since he can heal all his health back. [38:14]
- Do not let the game go late; a very farmed late game Victor is fast and it becomes hard to stay out of the aura. Use movement abilities to keep your distance. [38:44]

### Vindicta
- Vindicta is simple to counter, even more than Grey Talon: while she is in flight and far away, do not trade with her unless she is out of position and three or four of you gank her. [38:44]
- Flight makes her strong and lets her escape, so stay out of her radius while it is active. [39:15]
- Focus her first like Grey Talon: she is an incredibly squishy glass cannon damage dealer and debuffer (stake, crow, assassinate), so catching her first from the side often wins the fight on the spot. [39:15]
- Either stay out of her line of sight so she cannot snipe you, or go on her first for an easy first kill. [39:45]

### Viscous
- His ball is hard to control and works like a bowling ball: when it rolls at you, wall jump or double jump over it, or dash to the side, since he struggles to change direction. [39:45]
- You can parry Puddle Punch; the timing is tighter than a normal melee and hard, but a parried Puddle Punch does nothing. [40:48]
- If your hero benefits from Capacitor, using it on his cube removes the cube instantly. [40:48]
- The ball is very tanky, so in team fights stay away and focus other targets unless he is alone, then focus him once the ball runs out. [41:20]

### Vyper
- Vyper's kit revolves around insane resists while sliding, and she can only shoot nonstop while sliding because her base magazine is very low. [41:20]
- Anything that knocks her out of the slide works: knockdown, stuns, Dynamo's knockup, Mirage's tornado, Billy's knockup, Abrams' charge. [41:50]
- She is only dangerous while sliding; stun her, lock her movement (for example Vindicta's stake), and focus her as a team, because out of the slide she dies very easily. [42:21]

### Warden
- Warden is an ultimate bot right now; his strength comes from Last Stand. [42:21]
- When you hear his ultimate voice line, run away unless he is low and you think you can trade; he heals a lot back, especially when several people are in it. Get out of the radius rather than fight it head on. [42:52]
- The ultimate is a cone: have one person in front of him and one behind, or hit him from different angles, so he cannot hit everyone at once. [42:52]
- After his ultimate he becomes pretty weak. [43:22]

### Wraith
- As with Drifter, avoid 1v1s: Telekinesis is the best 1v1 ultimate in the game, a single target shutdown of about 3 to 4 seconds. [43:22]
- She is very squishy, almost Vindicta and Grey Talon level, a medium to long range damage dealer, so focusing her first in team fights is usually correct. [43:52]
- Her teleport has a very long cooldown and Wraiths usually max it last; once she uses it you have about 50 seconds to find and kill her while she has no escape. Get your tanks on her and stun her. [43:52]

### Yamato
- Her strength is her ultimate, which resets all her abilities and makes her very tanky; without it she is very weak and you can almost always fight her unless she is mega farmed. [44:23]
- Bait out the ultimate, back off, and fight her after it is on cooldown. [44:54]
- Or kill her fast before she ults: stun her at the right time; Silence Wave is a great item to shut her down. [45:25]
- Power Slash does a lot at full charge but has one of the longest cast times of any normal ability, so get behind cover when she charges it. [45:25]
- Keep medium range so she cannot land a free Crimson Slash to heal, even though she can grapple to you. [45:56]
