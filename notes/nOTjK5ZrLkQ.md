# Analyzing and Breaking Down Today's Patch
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-12-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nOTjK5ZrLkQ

## Covers
Coach's breakdown of the 2025-12-16 patch: movement speed nerfs and diminishing move speed stacking (with his own test), Majestic Leap, flat vitality health, spirit spike and Tankbuster changes, shrines as inhibitors, soul and neutral changes, jump pad damage, Armor Piercing Rounds, and hero nerfs to Billy, Dynamo, Lady Geist, Mirage, Shiv and Wraith. For players adjusting builds and expectations after the patch.

## Claims
- General move speed nerf: instead of 10% of general movement speed, the effect is now 20% of base movement speed, which should be more overall. [0:00]
- Move speed bonuses now stack diminishingly (multiplicatively) instead of adding up; before, Enduring Speed at 2 m/s plus Surge of Power at 2.25 m/s simply summed. [0:00]
- His test with Enduring Speed and Frenzy (the only two items that give base movement speed): normally they total 10.75 m/s, now 10.6 m/s, a little over an 11% decrease, so the cap appears to be treated as 10 m/s, like resist stacking (50% plus 50% resist gives 75%). [0:31]
- The more move speed you have, especially from many small instances, the more you lose; past about three items the returns diminish very strongly. [1:01]
- Base move speed reduced, and heroes with move speed scaling on spirit have those values reduced by 40%; he loves this change. Those heroes are Sinclair, Vyper, Seven and Grey Talon. [1:31]
- Spirit move speed scaling is a big reason Seven can stay present on the map and farm permanently, and why Sinclair is so slippery; good that it is nerfed significantly. [1:31]
- Wraith's spirit scaling is on sprint speed, which is nerfed separately; every hero's base sprint speed is also reduced. [2:02]
- Slow resist is nerfed; he disagrees, since buying Enduring Speed to outplay slows like Kelvin's beam or dome is healthy. [2:02]
- Majestic Leap: +10 seconds cooldown and reduced barrier boon scaling; he is a big fan but thinks it needs one more small hit (boon scaling about 7, cooldown about 50 seconds), or it could lose the barrier at 45 seconds and stay viable. [2:33]
- He dislikes the big movement nerfs because base move and sprint speed aren't the issue; the issue is the many heroes with insane movement abilities (Wraith teleporting across the map, Mina's 2 with spirit scaling on distance). The nerfs make those abilities even harder to answer since nobody can chase normally. [3:04]
- He asks why edge boosting, the cornerstone of movement outplays, isn't touched, though he doesn't want it nerfed. [3:04]
- As a Holliday player he says it is unfair she can overextend and press her 2 to escape for free, and the move speed nerfs make that worse. [3:35]
- Superior Stamina stock skyrocketed: he might buy it on Holliday instead of Kinetic Dash (which he preferred for spirit Holliday because it gives investment), since everyone else will be slow; he also thinks Superior Stamina needs a nerf. [4:06]
- He objects that Paradox's right-click movement tech and carbine speed were not nerfed. [4:36]
- Bonus health on items reduced, and the vitality investment tree now gives flat health instead of percent of base health. A green spike is no longer as crazy on Krill (Mo & Krill), but on low base HP heroes like Mina an extra 525 HP almost doubles her base, so they will really want it. [4:36]
- The spirit 4.8k spike is nerfed, which he welcomes; he dislikes 4.8k spikes because they railroad builds, and Mina or Dynamo hitting the spirit spike in lane did comical damage. Building was more fun when spikes were softer around 7.8k. [5:37]
- Rapid Recharge and another spirit item got spirit or ability power nerfs, which he agrees with, but gun items were left largely untouched. [6:09]
- Spirit Snatch nerfed again; he is glad. [6:09]
- Tankbuster now deals 7.5% current health instead of 5% max health, still piercing resistances. Leading with it clears jungle faster, so it fits Lash and Mina (the only heroes who bought it before 20 to 22 minutes) even better, and hyper farmers like Pocket may start buying it. [6:39]
- Tankbuster otherwise fits more as a counter-tank or very late slot, and seems strong on heroes that lead with a lot of spirit burst to one-shot. [7:10]
- Trooper souls reduced by 12; part of a general reduction of souls in the game, which he likes. [7:10]
- Shrine health increased from 3,000 to 7,000, a significant change for sieging; killing both enemy shrines makes your troopers 30% stronger, like inhibitors in other MOBAs. He loves it. [7:40]
- The base is almost good; the down Patron still needs about triple the HP or an attack. [7:40]
- Walker fireballs got smaller; they shred now that they reduce your resistances on hit, so stay away from them. [8:12]
- Medic trooper does a little more damage, is tankier and heals allies more; it used to lead the wave so it was hard not to kill first, and now it is faster to kill the backline troopers, and a tankier front trooper suits the wave structure. Not major. [8:12]
- Comeback bounties increased; he thinks they are too strong early (kill someone four times in lane, they kill you once and catch up, and a 10k lead can evaporate in one fight around the first urns) and too weak late (a 30k lead takes several fights to turn). He would reduce them early and increase them late; this reduces how much laning matters. [8:44]
- Getting hit on a jump pad now deals 15% max HP damage. It is good risk-reward and lets melee heroes or rooters like Paige punish jump pad engages at bridges, on top of the 200 to 300 HP you could already guarantee, but a stun plus big damage is a heavy punish and he thinks the window is too long. [9:45]
- Neutral health increased, which slows hyper farmers; he is glad they didn't raise neutral damage again, since hyper farmers with built-in lifesteal or Bullet Lifesteal ignore damage and it only hurt everyone else. [10:16]
- Sinner's Sacrifice bounty reduced; good, since it is always the most prioritized camp, though the buffs are still very strong. [10:47]
- Cultist Sacrifice got a 10 second cooldown nerf, which doesn't matter; the item is insane for soul inflation and its stock goes up as camps get tankier and Sinner's Sacrifices are reduced. He thinks it has been overtuned for a long time. [11:18]
- Unsecured souls changed: as he reads it, small bags clear quicker and big bags slower, a nerf to hyper farmers; if you see their strong side is left, invade the right side jungle where their hyper farmer sits on bigger and bigger camps. [11:48]
- Armor Piercing Rounds got a big buff against Plated Armor: from about 13% extra damage to roughly 25 to 26%. Good for M1 carries like Haze, who no longer become cosmetic when the enemy itemizes Plated Armor, though it still stops her Fixation from stacking. [11:48]
- Billy: health regen -0.5, Blasted non-player effectiveness -10%, Blasted light melee effectiveness -10%, and his chain now breaks slightly faster out of line of sight. [12:50]
- Billy verdict: not even a slap on the wrist for a hero that has dominated the meta for multiple patches; his 1 still does about 500 damage and heals 500, he can tank and one-shot you, and his chain is an insane grouping tool with very little counterplay besides Counterspell. [12:50]
- Line-of-sight breaking is finicky because of the camera angle and badly coded walls; the camera issue (crosshair on a target but your model can't shoot it) should be fixed, and is the same reason Billy's chain can pull you around corners. [13:52]
- Dynamo kick spirit scaling on range reduced from 0.2 to 0.1; a good change, but not enough, he is still a solo queue terrorist whose kicks do huge damage. [14:22]
- Against a Dynamo who is rolling you, double jump; if you just press space twice, his kicks feel cosmetic. He would increase the kick's vertical hitbox and cut its damage by about a third to make it more reliable but less damaging. [14:52]
- Lady Geist: moved to dash bucket three (she dashes slower), more self damage scaling on her bomb and on Malice. Cool, but it doesn't address her hyper farming and one-shotting, and her 2 is untouched, still healing her to full in lane and draining about 300 damage. [15:22]
- Against Lady Geist he wants an item like a ping or scan that reveals around you so she can't hide around corners. This nerf is more than Billy's but still not much. [15:53]
- Mirage: Djinn's Mark cooldown +0.1 seconds and a significant bullet boon reduction; he thinks the nerf is decent and strong, and the base spirit spike nerf indirectly lowers his Mark damage, but Mark lasts too long and does too much damage, so it may not be enough. [16:24]
- Shiv: Killing Blow cast range reduced by 2 meters, which only matters against Lady Geist (caption "guys"), because at max range he could ult without getting swapped by her; 2 meters slightly matters. Rage buildup reduced. [16:54]
- He wants Shiv's rage shown as a bar over his head instead of on his hip, and to decay faster. [17:24]
- Shiv: damage deferral delayed; he thinks Bloodletting should be removed, because taking about 45% less damage is not fair and percent amp and damage reduction are very hard to balance. His knives and dash do too much damage; he calls it an embarrassment of a nerf. [17:24]
- Pro play perma bans have been Billy and Shiv; if first pick doesn't ban one, second pick won't ban the other, and he expects it to stay Billy and Shiv. [17:55]
- Wraith: Project Mind spirit scaling reduced; the real issue is its distance, which grows relatively with every move speed nerf. [18:25]
- Wraith: bullet damage nerf, about 11% less gun damage at max level; he thinks it is good and should let other M1s compete, though her cards are also an issue. It should slow her farming significantly together with the sprint speed scaling cut. [18:55]
- The movement speed decreases in general will shake up the meta significantly for a lot of heroes. [19:26]
