## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a free coaching stream (planned as Rat King only, switched to open raffles) during the new-hero patch, the week of Baba's release.
Before coaching he talks Calico after the map changes, his own Rat King ability order and a chat question on rushing damage. He then coaches five players, none named, with ranks only partly given: Rat King, a Baba build review, Grey Talon into Haze, Billy (Emissary) into Drifter, and Viscous (Oracle) into Baba.
Themes: catching waves between camps instead of AFK farming, flag (ult) placement around Rifts, dead time, soul awareness, movement and routing out, light versus heavy melees, Express Shot right clicks, not giving Rifts for free, disengaging, and many item calls (Ballistic Enchantment, Rebuttal, Life Strike, Reactive Barrier, Spirit Resilience, Rapid Recharge).
