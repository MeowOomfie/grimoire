## Covers
Vindicta, commentary over one game by aquatiq with a teammate in voice, using Cory's build.
Covers item picks, a rough lane against Drifter and Pocket, and late game snipe stacking.
Patch of late August 2026.

## Claims
- He uses Cory's Vindicta build and thinks there is no better build right now. [1:06]
- Her crows apply minus 15% resist; enemies who keep trading into them die eventually. [1:36]
- Drifter is a horrible lane matchup for Vindicta; flying up against him gets you killed. [5:14]
- He went Suppressor early, but it felt useless against a Calico who still had very high fire rate. [6:51]
- Drifter will teleport onto you if you are low and not behind your tower. [8:22]
- He bought Healing Rite when low in the early game. [9:22]
- When the enemy lets you farm their jungle, go buy Fortitude. [13:30]
- He took Enduring Speed next. [14:00]
- He wanted Boundless Spirit so his snipes do more damage. [15:30]
- Counterspell cannot cancel Ivy's Stone Form anymore; he tried twice. [18:36]
- Titanic Magazine is a buy he wanted mid game. [19:37]
- A bait that works: push the wave up to their walker, show on the map, then back out; three enemies rotated to him. [19:37]
- Stay low (out of sight) when setting up for the Rift. [16:00]
- Trading objectives is fine: losing blue while taking green is a fair trade. [16:34]
- A teammate says to ignore the first 3,000 souls of difference when reading the soul lead, and that 10% is comeback range. [23:45]
- He saved for Crippling Headshot after the next walker. [25:52]
- Remember that Blood Tribute costs you HP each time it is on. [27:55]
- He had 110 snipe stacks late. [27:55]
