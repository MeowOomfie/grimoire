# Deadlock Just Changed Forever (Massive Balance Patch)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-03-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIqA1wT_kXQ

## Covers
First-impressions breakdown of the 2026-03-07 balance patch, called the biggest balance update yet: biggest winners and losers, about 20 hero reworks and the new item changes.
Heroes Wraith, Victor, Pocket, Mirage, The Doorman, Paradox, Ivy, Drifter, Yamato, Calico, Infernus plus short verdicts on Abrams, Apollo, Billy, Celeste, Dynamo, Sylva, Venator; items Golden Goose Egg, Stalker, Blood Tribute, Weighted Shots, Siphon Bullets, Spiritual Overflow.
For players who want the short version of the patch and a first guess at the new meta.

## Claims
- Wraith's Card Trick is reworked into four random suits: spades add damage, clubs slow, one suit shreds 8% of both spirit and bullet resistance, and one heals you. [0:31]
- Wraith also gets a bonus Joker card that does all four effects at once; the chance of a Joker is under 10% at base and rises with upgrades. [1:01]
- At max level every card suit is improved (more slow, more shred, more heal) and Jokers are more likely; you can see which card is next before you throw. [1:01]
- Verdict on the Wraith cards: absolutely crazy buffs. [1:31]
- Wraith's Full Auto now gives infinite ammo at max level and lasts longer, is buffed for yourself, but no longer works on allies. [2:03]
- Wraith's Telekinesis now casts instantly so the enemy cannot get away, and works like a budget Lash: you lift them, deal the damage and then throw them where you aim. [2:03]
- Wraith was a bit weak before and is now very strong, but most other heroes were also buffed so she will not be as overpowered as she looks. [2:33]
- The newer heroes got small adjustments only: Apollo slight nerfs, Celeste slight nerfs, Sylva quite a few nerfs, Venator no changes, so they will feel much the same. [2:33]
- Victor: all abilities stronger; Pain Barrier does more damage with longer range; Jumpstart is a bit weaker but has more charges or lower cooldown. [3:03]
- Victor's ultimate now gives fire rate and spirit damage per bullet from level one, so one point in it gives those bonuses after you die and revive. [3:03]
- Victor's base bullet damage is much higher so he relies less on Aura of Suffering, which is the only ability in his kit that got slightly nerfed; verdict: Victor will be very strong. [3:34]
- Pocket is one of the two biggest winners: you can now use Flying Cloak and Enchanter's Satchel during Barrage and keep the Barrage cast going, and Barrage does more damage. [4:04]
- Pocket's Flying Cloak does more weapon damage on hits; Enchanter's Satchel does more damage and slows enemy fire rate and movement speed at max level. [4:04]
- Pocket's Affliction prevents all healing at max level; it is worse at base levels, but hitting the whole enemy team with it at max basically guarantees a late game team fight win. [4:04]
- Prediction: Pocket will be absolute S tier this patch. [4:35]
- Mirage is the other absolute S tier pick; Fire Scarabs now work on charges, so you do not have to cast all four in one time span. [4:35]
- Mirage's Fire Scarabs health steal now triggers instantly and works once per hero (no stacking four on one target), so hitting several heroes steals a lot of max health; it also amplifies the damage the target takes. Verdict: went from very weak to insanely strong. [5:06]
- Mirage's Tornado is renamed Dust Devil and can be recast at max level without Echo Shard; with Echo Shard you get four Dust Devils. [5:36]
- Mirage's Djinn's Mark does more damage and stuns enemies at max stacks. [6:06]
- Mirage's Traveler ultimate is no longer channeled, so you can walk and act while it counts down, and it can target any point on the map instead of only an enemy or ally. [6:06]
- Traveler now gives a barrier on arrival instead of fire rate, making Mirage tankier entering fights; use it actively to get into fights. [6:36]
- Nerf: any hit during the Traveler cast cancels it, so it is worse for escaping; the coach says the double Dust Devil makes up for that escape loss. [6:36]
- The Doorman is back and probably stronger than ever: Call Bell does more damage at every level, especially in lane; at max level it can be held up and slows enemies who walk in, but in practice you will mostly detonate it right away. [7:36]
- The Doorman's Luggage Cart has a bigger hitbox, so it is easier to hit. [7:36]
- The Doorman's Hotel Guest ultimate now stuns enemies who fail the checkout and slows them on exit even at base level. [8:07]
- If the enemy fails the checkout, Hotel Guest's cooldown is set to 10 seconds (not reduced by 10) at max level, against a 95 second base cooldown. [8:07]
- The Doorman is unstoppable for the whole time someone is inside Hotel Guest, so nobody can stun him and he can fight or escape freely; the coach calls this the biggest deal of the change. [8:37]
- Being put in Hotel Guest now also costs the victim one stamina. [9:07]
- Against The Doorman it is absolutely essential to escape the checkout: fail it and he gets the ultimate back instantly and you will die. [9:37]
- Paradox's Paradoxical Swap gets a new max level upgrade that swaps all enemies within 7 m and takes 10% of current health; the radius is forgiving and multi-swaps will happen often, an insane team fight win condition. [10:09]
- Paradox at Mid Boss: swap the enemy team out of the pit while they hover around the Rejuvenator and steal it. [10:39]
- Paradox's Time Wall is back to charges (two walls), bigger and taller, with very low base cooldown; Rapid Recharge could give about four walls, which may be overkill; at max level you also do more weapon damage through the walls. [10:39]
- Ivy's Kudzu Bomb is renamed Entangling Thorns; at max level enemies who stay in it longer than 2 seconds get rooted, which becomes relevant once you build duration. [11:11]
- Ivy's tether (caption calls it Kudzu Connection) stays active at all times at max level with zero cooldown, like a passive, giving fire rate, bullet lifesteal and replicated healing to you or a linked teammate. [11:40]
- Max Ivy's tether early on gun carry Ivy, or on support Ivy with a bullet damage carry, and sit on that teammate. [12:11]
- Drifter's Bloodscent now grants its permanent weapon damage on kills or assists, so a stolen kill still counts; the stack is now 3% instead of 4%. [12:44]
- Drifter's Eternal Night makes every enemy it hits count as isolated even next to their allies, so Bloodscent bonus damage applies to all of them. [12:44]
- Drifter's Eternal Night level one upgrade gives 10 m/s sprint speed when cast, so you can cross the map to the enemy in about a second; a strong setup and initiation tool. [13:14]
- Drifter's Rend is weaker: its bonus damage now comes on melee, so melee items like Lifestrike or Spirit Snatch need melee range, a nerf in lane early. [13:45]
- Drifter's Rend at max level loses the bullet lifesteal but a close range hit now silences the target for 2.3 seconds; the coach calls it arguably a buff and very scary. [13:45]
- Drifter's Stalker's Mark loses charges but adds healing reduction and more bleed, probably better overall. Verdict: Drifter, already very strong, might be absolutely top tier now. [14:15]
- Yamato is arguably S plus tier: Power Slash does more damage and its max upgrade gives an insanely increased cast range, a sniper ability across the map even without items. [14:47]
- Yamato can now parry at any time while charging Power Slash, instantly releasing the slash and parrying; use it in lane when an enemy tries to melee you during the charge. [14:47]
- Yamato's Flying Strike has multiple charges at max level and can grapple to allies: grapple in, then within about 3 seconds grapple out to an ally for huge mobility in fights. [15:19]
- Flying Strike now deals melee damage on arrival, so melee items like Lifestrike and Spirit Snatch work with it without you having to melee. [15:49]
- Yamato's Crimson Slash now increases your melee damage on hit for the next 4 seconds, so Yamato should weave in more light melees in fights, at the risk of more parries. [15:49]
- Yamato's ultimate was changed but not massively buffed or nerfed; verdict: Yamato, already crazy in lane, is even better. [16:20]
- Calico gets bonus gold for killing an enemy within 4 seconds of hitting Leaping Slash, so you can farm bonus gold off players. [16:50]
- Calico's ultimate no longer gives damage amp; at max level it heals you when you come out, so cast it when low in a fight. [16:50]
- Calico's Gloom Bombs is the first ability in the game that does bonus damage to barriers, shifting it from raw damage to more barrier damage. [17:20]
- Abrams got nerfs and, with most others buffed, will be a lot weaker; the coach calls that a good change. [17:20]
- Apollo got slight nerfs and will still be solid; Billy got slight changes or nerfs. [17:20]
- Celeste got slight nerfs to movement and to her ultimate but will still be a crazy hero. [17:20]
- Dynamo got many small changes and does not stand out from what the coach has seen. [17:50]
- Infernus got crazy buffs, saved for last as the best: Afterburn does more damage early, including at level one. [18:20]
- Infernus's max level Afterburn lets the burn duration be extended to an extremely long time (shown with Superior Duration), and all abilities still extend the burn, so it is much easier to keep up; hitting many bullets first makes the enemy take far more damage. [18:20]
- Infernus's Flame Dash is faster to cast at level one with low cooldown; maxed with Rapid Recharge the time between charges is low, about 5 to 6 seconds to the next dash. [18:51]
- Infernus's ultimate got slight changes; Infernus will be stronger, especially in lane. [19:21]
- Most ultimate cooldowns were increased because ultimate spam was a big problem; the coach thinks Valve agreed. [19:21]
- New item Golden Goose Egg: a farming item that lowers all your damage by 10% while held but gains 75 souls per minute in your inventory. [19:51]
- Golden Goose Egg can be consumed at any time; you get the stored souls plus one permanent Golden Statue buff for every 200 souls it gave, similar to Sinner's Sacrifice. [20:21]
- Bought around minute one and cashed around minute 10, Golden Goose Egg gives about 750 souls and four Golden Statue buffs, paying for itself after about 10 minutes; held to 15 to 20 minutes it returns about double plus the buffs. [20:21]
- Verdict on Golden Goose Egg: on paper a bit weak and likely to be buffed later; better on supports that focus on their stats, since the 10% damage penalty is big. [20:53]
- Gun items got many buffs overall. [21:23]
- Backstabber is renamed Stalker and now procs at close range from any side (front or back), about 8 m, doing what Backstabber used to; better on melee chasers like Billy. [21:54]
- Spirit Shredder Bullets got buffed, giving 4% more spirit lifesteal at base; Spirit Rend got the same buff. [22:24]
- Blood Tribute's healing penalty is removed completely; it only drains 50 HP per second, which life steal covers, so it has no real downside. The coach thinks it may be the most broken item and nerfed within days. [22:24]
- Use Blood Tribute on heroes like Wraith: buy your normal items and Leech first, then Blood Tribute later and keep it running through the fight. [22:55]
- Weighted Shots now builds out of Slowing Bullets, gives the Slowing Bullets effect plus more weapon damage, a smaller stamina recovery penalty and no bullet velocity penalty, but no longer gives spirit resist. Verdict: absolutely crazy, a no-brainer item. [23:56]
- If you need spirit resist after Weighted Shots, buy the reworked Spirit Resilience: it now procs below 50% health instead of 40%, gives only 15% extra spirit resist, and is better on squishy heroes. [24:26]
- Weighted Shots and Spirit Resilience are worse only on tanks. [24:26]
- Spiritual Overflow now gives 20% duration instead of 12% cooldown reduction and 40 spirit power instead of 30; verdict: massive buffs, an insane item (mentioned with Infernus). [24:26]
- Siphon Bullets now steals a percentage of the enemy's health (2.5%) instead of a flat amount, making it an anti-tank item; against a fully built late game Abrams it stole about 120 per tick. [24:58]
- The patch will shake up the meta and builds drastically over the next few days. [26:30]
