# Eternus Vod Review - Emissary Seven Coaching
channel: Natan  date: 2025-05-08  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=scgjparadeA

## Covers
Seven VOD review of an Emissary game, lane vs Mirage and Lady Geist, hard pushing, quick cast stun, Healing Rite vs going back, grenade spacing, Mystic Shot use, tier 3 Power Surge, side laning, Urn decisions, Mid Boss calls, pinging and chat wheel, ulting without Unstoppable, Debuff Reducer, Debuff Remover, Ethereal Shift, flex slots

## Claims
- Seven into Mirage and Lady Geist is a pretty rough lane but manageable if you minimise the poke you take. [0:31]
- In lanes like this hard push, because their plan is to push and poke you under tower while you hit minions; pushing does that to them and keeps them busy hitting troopers. [0:31]
- Turn on instant cast or quick cast for the stun; confirm cast locks you in an animation you have to cancel. [1:02]
- The player looked lost in lane; make a choice, either hit the enemy while they hit your creeps or hit your creeps so you can push. [1:32]
- Do not walk up to hit minions in full view of both enemies while tanking the creeps. [2:34]
- When your lane partner backs off to heal, do not go in 1v2; play together. [3:14]
- Basically never go back to base unless you really have to, and then only after 4:50 when the zipline boost unlocks; before that buy Healing Rite and sit back while it heals, so you do not miss the wave. [3:45]
- Sitting behind cover close to the wave invites a Lady Geist bomb; with two or more Mirage marks on you, back off and wait them out. [4:15]
- Against grenade heroes do not stack up next to your teammate, since it gives them two targets for their AoE. [4:48]
- When Lady Geist pops her drain, take cover and wait it out; you will not win that trade, and its roughly 30 second cooldown can be abused afterwards. [5:49]
- Mystic Shot is a really good item on Seven, but 1.5k Mystic Shot damage in a 45 minute game means you are not landing it, in which case avoid buying it. [6:20]
- Use Healing Rite earlier; troopers and jungle camps do not cancel it, but Mirage's Djinn's Mark does. [6:51]
- Standing in the open in the middle of the lane is a free lasso for Holliday. [7:55]
- Tier 3 Power Surge is your biggest power spike; before it you do not do much damage unless you have ult, so avoid fights and take camps, preferably the smaller ones, until you hit it. [8:26]
- When lanes drag on (no Guardian down at 12 minutes), instant push the wave every time, walk away to take a camp, and get back for the next wave so you do not lose your Guardian. [8:57]
- Going to the side lane while the team groups mid for no reason is an easy way to get far ahead in lower ranked games: push it as far as you can and take the camps around it. [9:28]
- Use the ball to push faster, since standing up there is risky. [9:28]
- This game he would buy Debuff Reducer instead of Healing Rite at this point, plus Healbane. [9:58]
- Leaving the Infernus on one lane and taking over another is good; every solo wave is worth so much at this point. [9:58]
- Clear the camps that have been up since minute 8; Seven is really good at clearing them, and the faster they are cleared the faster they respawn. [11:29]
- Taking the Urn with no Guardian down in that lane, no wave pushed mid and teammates elsewhere just donates it to the enemy. [11:29]
- Running in with two players to the most dangerous part of the map while the enemy team is unseen is "completely illegal" and should have been game losing; the enemy could have taken mid. [12:00]
- Debuff Remover would have been a safe pick this game; Debuff Reducer would have helped too. [13:02]
- Do not chase a fleeing enemy your teammate can finish; once you are out of position, take the L and go back quickly with the zipline boost. [13:33]
- Instead of walking across the map to fight (a teleporter would have been faster), take the Walker with your teammate. [14:35]
- After your team loses a fight, the Urn is off; drop it mid, clear the wave and pick it up again, or hold it to give teammates room to split push, and hold it longer to see how many enemies are waiting. [15:06]
- Communicate: ping on the map, draw, and set up the chat wheel; the most important lines are going in, retreat, be right back and help; always ping where you are going and where you want people to go, and click an enemy Walker to tell teammates to push it. [15:36]
- He walked a risky path while Paradox swap and Holliday lasso were up and no teammates were near; take a safer path and ping it. [16:37]
- After killing two enemies with Abrams having no ult, call Mid Boss for your team, or help push a Walker. [17:39]
- Flank with your team; Slowing Hex with a two second stun sets up an easy kill even on a Mo & Krill. [18:10]
- In a bad fight he stood with his back to a veil where he could be swapped or lassoed at any moment, then went blindly through the veil and ulted without Unstoppable. [18:40]
- You cannot assume the enemy will not use their cooldowns; that Holliday just never used her free lasso. [19:42]
- Without Unstoppable, only ult after the key cooldowns that can stop it are used: Abrams's ult or charge (with Warp Stone to reach you), Holliday's barrels and lasso, Paradox swap, and Mo & Krill's ult. [19:42]
- With three teammates dead, do not go in with just Seven and Infernus. [20:13]
- When the play is over, push the wave quickly, then defend a Walker or help elsewhere. [20:45]
- Fighting 2v4 is not good, though it made space for his teammate to take a Walker. [21:23]
- He is not a fan of Spiritual Overflow there; buy Unstoppable or Ethereal Shift, since you already have the damage and need to survive. [21:23]
- With these items you can perma slide and never reload during Mid Boss on Seven. [21:54]
- An Urn run with Rejuvenator is free; the enemy should never fight you there. [22:56]
- He was overdoing the Urn pickups and should ignore the Urn a bit more. [22:56]
- With an Urn spawning, prioritise taking the enemy base, because more kills, which are common at this rank, let you go for the full end. [23:27]
- With their Patron dropped and a quarter of Rejuvenator left, look for picks; at 6v5 one more pick basically guarantees the end, and if not, keep waves shoved and take all the jungle while waiting for the next Mid Boss. [23:59]
- Against a team buying spirit resist, sell Infuser for Mystic Vulnerability (heard as "mystic foam"); Escalating Exposure is fine at that point. [25:02]
- Prioritise defending a Walker over the Urn, because denying the flex slot is still so big. [26:49]
- Ping an overextending teammate back. [27:20]
- Summary: good micro on pushing waves, keeping them pushed and moving to the right lanes; Urn calls were iffy; lane positioning needs work; buy Debuff Remover, and prioritise Unstoppable over Spiritual Overflow when looking to ult in fights. [28:21]
