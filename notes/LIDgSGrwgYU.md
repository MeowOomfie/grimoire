## Covers
Gun Bebop, aquatiq, in a duo lane with a friend on voice against McGinnis and Rem. Commentary over a winning game, not a structured guide.
Mostly lane combos and item picks for a gun Bebop. Video dated 2026-04-30.

## Claims
- Lane combo: hook, then follow up with shotgun damage. [1:34]
- Hook enemies and pin them against a wall for kills in lane. [1:34]
- The enemy Rem heals her lane partner back to full health, which makes the lane hard to finish. [6:13]
- Level two uppercut helps a lot in lane. [4:41]
- The beam on middle mouse (Hyper Beam) does massive damage to an enemy that stays in it. [5:12]
- Bebop's ultimate is very uninteractive for the enemy; they cannot do anything about it. [8:17]
- Bebop in lane may be the most broken thing he has seen. [9:49]
- Using the ultimate to clear a lane near 15 minutes gives a lot of souls. [12:25]
- He buys Hunter's Aura after checking the enemy team is mixed gun and spirit. [12:25]
- He has Slowing Bullets but still avoids a bad fight. [12:55]
- Next item options: Rapid Rounds or Swift Striker; he picks Swift Striker. [13:56]
- He buys Spellslinger once he can afford a few things. [19:06]
- Fortitude lets him heal back before mid boss, but taking mid boss damage stops the Fortitude regen. [21:40]
- Headhunter heals him on a headshot so he can keep fighting. [24:14]
