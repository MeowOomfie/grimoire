# ULTIMATE IVY GUIDE FT. CATGIRL
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-09-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=83_xwq7I3bI

## Covers
A 2024 Ivy hero guide: Deathy reads the abilities and upgrades, then interviews Catgirl (about 700 Ivy games) on strengths, weaknesses, play style, the one skill order, and her DPS and support item builds built around sliding with move speed. For Ivy players. This is from September 2024; Ivy's kit and the items have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Kudzu Bomb summons a patch of choking vines that damages and slows enemies in its radius; upgrades are +1 charge, +2 seconds duration, +60 DPS. [0:31]
- Watcher's Covenant gives Ivy bonuses and automatically connects her to a nearby ally to share them; healing is replicated among everyone connected. [0:31]
- The Watcher's Covenant connection needs line of sight; press 2 near an ally to lock onto them; upgrades are +10% fire rate, +2 m/s movement bonus, +1 tether count. [1:01]
- Stone Form turns Ivy to stone and smashes the ground, stunning and damaging nearby enemies and healing her; you have air control before falling. [1:01]
- Stone Form upgrades: -20 second cooldown, +80 damage and +0.5 seconds stun, +20% max heal. [1:32]
- Air Drop: take flight with an ally or a bomb and drop it to cause a large explosion that slows movement; Ivy and the ally gain a bullet shield when flying ends. [1:32]
- While lifted, the ally gains bullet resist but cannot attack and deals -50% damage; Air Drop casts faster on an ally and can pick up any ally. [2:04]
- Ivy is a speed demon: two points in her 2 give +2 movement speed to her and the tethered ally, so once she has skills she can run people down. [2:35]
- Her strengths: great split pusher, great at running away, and a nice interrupting stun that is very good for Mid Boss. [3:08]
- Ivy gets free Fleetfoot by default from minute one (no movement penalty while shooting) and has four stamina. [3:08]
- Catgirl thinks Ivy is the worst laner in the entire game. [3:39]
- Ivy survives very well: early Stone Form gives about an extra 60 HP every time on a 20 second cooldown and makes her invincible, especially with Healing Nova for allies. [3:39]
- Her early kill power is non-existent; she only gets early kills if a teammate hands them over or she lands a parry. [4:10]
- After lane Ivy's weakness is no poke: Kelvin can throw a 500 damage ice bomb and Viscous a 400 damage goo ball, while Ivy is close range and her best poke is Air Drop on an 80 to 100 second cooldown. [4:40]
- Catgirl rates Ivy A or S tier because she is very versatile: a tank build that soaks and heals damage and frontlines when the team needs it, and a strong DPS build for climbing with randoms you don't trust. [5:11]
- Catgirl's two public in-game builds are a DPS build (about 70,000 likes) and a support build (about 20,000 likes), numbers one and two on public browse. [6:13]
- Early, before items, Ivy is a melee frontline stun bot: no poke or damage abilities, so she soaks damage for the team, gets people out with Air Drop and drops bombs with Air Drop. [7:14]
- Early on Catgirl split pushes a lot and avoids team fights because Ivy is very weak early; she joins ganks when she can sneak behind enemies and land Stone Form. [7:45]
- Once items are online Ivy becomes very fast for herself and an ally, like a Warden with Veil Walker; then she ganks fast, takes Mid Boss and kills objectives. [7:45]
- Throwing allies with the ult is limited: most allies hate being thrown (a Grey Talon will flame you); only pick up frontliners like Abrams or Shiv who are happy to go in. [8:47]
- Usual gank pattern: push a Walker, gank the two enemies there, push the Walker and leave, avoiding a drawn-out fight because Ivy has no poke; if they start throwing Lady Geist bombs from the Walker, retreat. [8:47]
- Carrying on Ivy revolves completely around sliding, enabled by free Fleetfoot and four stamina. [9:17]
- Last patch Ivy rushed Siphon Bullets and was the best carry in the game, but Haze got buffed and Ivy was made slower, so the build now puts a large emphasis on move speed. [9:48]
- With enough move speed you don't need to dash to slide, and sliding gives infinite ammo. [9:48]
- Ivy has the fastest shooting gun in the game: with the right items her 50 round magazine can put out 300 to 400 bullets at a very fast fire rate while moving fast. [10:19]
- Sliding makes Ivy hard to hit while she outputs huge damage and heals her team; if enemies dive her, use Stone Form. [10:50]
- If you think you are sliding enough as Ivy, you are not; slide more. [10:50]
- Once you have Heroic Aura, Enduring Speed and level two tether, you can slide whenever tether is up, about 15 seconds of basically infinite ammo. [11:20]
- Vampiric Burst gives 2 move speed as a passive stat, and with it you can slide whenever you want without tether or any actives. [11:51]
- After that build survivability like Soul Rebirth, Unstoppable or Metal Skin, then Curse; the DPS core is Heroic Aura, Enduring Speed, then Vampiric Burst. [11:51]
- Catgirl tried caster Ivy, punch Ivy and more, and says there is one clearly best skill order. [12:53]
- Skill order step one: max Stone Form first (3, 3, 3). [12:53]
- Stone Form heals 10% by default and maxed adds 20%, so every 20 seconds you get a 30% heal without hitting anyone; you never have to go back to the fountain (except when Pocket afflicts you). [13:24]
- Step two: the move speed upgrade on tether, the second most important point: it makes you better at attacking, running and catching people, gives speed to allies, and helps split pushing and sliding. [13:24]
- Step three: max Air Drop for its 4.5 second silence; she has considered Refresher for a 9 second silence, 12 with a duration item. [13:55]
- Step four: the last tether point, +1 tether count, nice when no teammates are near but not mandatory. [14:25]
- Step five: Kudzu Bomb last; Catgirl thinks it is an awful ability and only maxes it because nothing else is left. [14:25]
- The order would only change if Kudzu Bomb slowed dashes and stamina like a Slowing Hex effect, or if Stone Form got significant nerfs. [14:56]
- Early move speed isn't needed because Ivy does no damage without items; Air Drop is an unreliable 80 second cooldown often spent escaping; Kudzu Bomb is unreliable, with four or five charges doing about 50 damage to a charging Abrams. [15:26]
- DPS build lane items, bought left to right: Basic Magazine, Close Quarters, Extra Health, Sprint Boots, Active Reload, Ammo Scavenger. [16:57]
- Lane exceptions: against Bebop or Vindicta buy Reactive Barrier. [16:57]
- Against someone who wants to shove the lane then headshot you, like Warden using his grenade on creeps or Seven pressing 3 to shove, buy Monster Rounds. [16:57]
- Healing Nova is always a nice pickup on Ivy: with a tethered ally, Healing Nova basically heals you to full early no matter what. [17:28]
- Buy Sprint Boots earlier if you are ganking a lane or double soaking a lot. [17:28]
- Basic Magazine and Close Quarters come first because Ivy's gun is bad; they are the best 500 soul damage items for her, about 50% more damage. [17:28]
- Extra Health early gives about 200 to 300 HP, making Ivy much harder to kill alongside Stone Form. [17:59]
- Sprint Boots are for moving around; Active Reload because her magazine is small. [17:59]
- Ammo Scavenger is a good value item even ignoring its passive (kills give ammo and health), and the build has many optional purple slots. [18:29]
- In lane Catgirl often buys Basic Magazine, sometimes goes Healing Nova, then finishes the row left to right. [18:29]
- Optional items are situational actives, not core: Slowing Hex to gank a Wraith while split pushing, Debuff Remover against Pocket. [18:59]
- Decay is an optional pick for Mo & Krill and Abrams: it does about 30% of max health. [19:29]
- Active items are really strong right now. [19:29]
- Mid to late game order: rush Heroic Aura, then Enduring Speed, then Intensifying Magazine, then the rest. [19:29]
- Heroic Aura comes first because it gives bullet lifesteal and move speed, which Ivy needs anyway, and its damage is surprisingly good, especially with teammates; it is the best 3k item in the game, overtuned, and strong on many heroes. [19:59]
- Enduring Speed completes the move speed combo: with tether, Heroic Aura and Enduring Speed you slide from tether whenever you want, no dashes needed. [20:29]
- Intensifying Magazine is not 100% necessary but is the best 3k damage item right now, especially while sliding, turning a 50 magazine into 200 to 300 bullets; it was just buffed, and Active Reload helps maintain the stacks. [20:29]
- Next come three survivability items, because at high MMR you can't get away with only weapon items: Healing Booster, Bullet Armor and Spirit Armor. [20:59]
- Healing Booster replaced Bullet Lifesteal because Heroic Aura already gives bullet lifesteal; it also buffs Stone Form's heal and negates anti-heal. [21:30]
- Armor is bought instead of Veil Walker. [21:30]
- Then Vampiric Burst: decent damage though not the best 6k item, but mainly the move speed that lets you slide without tether anywhere. [21:30]
- Then finish upgrading the armors, since Ivy is a squishy carry. [22:01]
- In long 40 to 50 minute games, become the raid boss who cannot die and still does damage: the first endgame item is Unstoppable, Soul Rebirth or Curse (Inhibitor also suggested). [22:31]
- After becoming unkillable add damage: Crippling Headshot or Lucky Shot, Point Blank, Titanic Magazine. [23:01]
- Prioritize not being greedy: with this much lifesteal, move speed and resist you should be unkillable, and enemies having to send three people at one Ivy is the goal. [23:32]
- The support build gets actives faster so Ivy can frontline with a group of five pushing a Walker instead of being dead weight. [24:04]
- Support build starts Restorative Shot, Sprint Boots and Spirit Strike for tankiness, stronger punches and sustain, and always buys Healing Nova. [24:35]
- Which build is better depends on team comp: with no frontline (no Shiv, no Abrams) and squishies like Vindicta and Grey Talon who will get run at, go support Ivy. [25:06]
- If your team already has a frontline like Abrams or Dynamo, max your gun with the DPS build and shred people. [25:37]
- Against hyper late game enemies like Seven, Haze or Wraith, lean support Ivy, run at people early and win in 15 to 20 minutes. [26:08]
- New Ivy players should start with the DPS build to learn sliding, which is pivotal to her kit; Catgirl did not slide for her first 200 hours. [26:38]
- Practice sliding first on the Guardian (slide down the stairs), then on Mid Boss and objectives thanks to free Fleetfoot, then work it into shooting people. [27:09]
- Force yourself to slide all the time; it feels worse at first, but a week later you won't even think about it. [27:39]
- Done right, you slide at someone, they die, and your magazine is still full. [28:09]
- In lane, slide against creeps too (dash-slide or slide on stairs), though without tether move speed it is limited; mag dumping a creep at level one leaves Ivy about two shots. [28:09]
- Ivy's early game damage is so low that an enemy without even Extra Regen can stand on the bridge at full HP while you keep shooting them. [28:41]
