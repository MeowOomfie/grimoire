# What Are The Most Slept On Items | Alchemist Kelvin Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-06-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5_dUXU0fCaU

## Covers
Coaching review by Midknight (former top-10 Mirage) of an Alchemist-rank Kelvin player's game, a 2025 video on the latest patch as of June 2025.
Mostly building: Kelvin items, then generally slept-on items (Spirit Sap, Restorative Locket, Arcane Surge, Counterspell), plus Lash ult counterplay, the dome escape, movement practice and "low elo syndrome" after Urn runs.

## Claims
Kelvin lane:
- On this patch, if you lose the first-wave deny you do not get your ability, so play hyper passive until the second wave; if you get the deny, push that advantage. [1:00]
- Infernus can take a gun trade early because of his afterburn. [1:00]
- Buy between waves when you are next to the shop; there is no reason not to. [1:30]
- When you back off, at least finish the minion you were on. [1:30]
- Heavy melee there instead of light melee. [2:00]
- Last hit the creep the enemy is shooting. [2:30]
- Over-meleeing minions is fine if the enemy does not punish it, but a better team will punish aggressive melees. [2:30]
- The hybrid build with one point in Ice Beam and two in Frost Grenade does a huge amount of DPS. [3:00]
- Early aggression on tower is good. [3:31]
- Proxy the wave (farm it behind your tower) instead of giving the enemy an extra ~80 souls. [4:01]
- Better safe than sorry with the dome, especially at this elo. [5:01]
- When Lash ults you directly onto a tower, press parry and you will always parry the guardian; if he throws you next to the tower, roll into it to parry. [5:31]
- Into a Kelvin lane he usually rushes Extra Stamina, because Kelvin feels oppressive if you misposition against his beam. [7:04]

Kelvin items:
- Early Mystic Vulnerability is not the buy; look at sustain, Mystic Regen or Arcane Surge. [6:03]
- Arcane Surge is very strong right now on Kelvin: more damage and heal on Frost Grenade, longer beam, and an extra stamina. [6:34]
- Monster Rounds is good because it keeps the wave away from your tower. [7:34]
- Buy Monster Rounds against heroes that shove the wave or hit towers hard (like Wraith), not every lane, or it becomes a crutch; farm heroes like Wraith buy it every game. [8:34]
- Instead of Soul Shredder Bullets, Spirit Sap is better for DPS, since your gun will not do much even with investment. [9:34]
- Spirit Sap is slept on: it reduces enemy spirit resist and takes away 12 spirit power on a button, almost half of a Seven's 29; press it when Seven ults and the ult will tickle. [10:06]
- Mystic Slow is not bad if enemies are not building debuff resist. [10:36]
- Spirit Sap is fine even when the game is just okay because it gives defensive and offensive value; when behind there are better defensive tools. [11:06]
- Spirit Sap is very good into Seven and Lash, and use it on Warden whenever he ults. [27:34]
- Restorative Locket is very slept on since the item rework: two stamina on a point and click is unrivaled into heroes like Warden and Haze. [11:37]
- Do not rush Healing Tempo this early with a utility support Ivy on the team; build more beam damage. [14:39]
- Spirit Burn should come out before Escalating Exposure: it gives base damage, more damage scaling with Escalating Exposure, more burst, and anti-heal that is strong into Shiv, Lash and Warden. [30:37]
- Range, cooldown reduction and duration are three powerful stats for Kelvin. [19:19]
- Counterspell is one of the only ways for Kelvin to block a Shiv ult, and easily blocks Seven's stun and Warden's cage; Yamato's 1 is not hard to parry either. [23:59]
- Ethereal Shift costs about twice as much as Counterspell and is worth it on heroes that stall for something, like Calico cat form or Pocket cloak; Kelvin has nothing to stall for. [25:32]
- Lightning Scroll is pretty bad on Grey Talon. [31:08]
- Summary: building was very strong and versatile for the elo; do not rush Healing Tempo, and do not rush Escalating Exposure before Spirit Burn. [32:39]

Kelvin abilities and fights:
- Your instinct the second you get ulted should be an instant dome. [11:06]
- Drop the dome so you can chase. [13:38]
- Dome or ult off seeing Lash's rope above you, which means you are in range, not off the audio cue. [21:22]
- Rescue Beam can pull someone out of Lash's ult, but do it as they are being pulled up or after they are thrown, not as he slams them down. [21:54]
- Use the dome around the Lash himself to stop him pulling anyone to him, not only to hug teammates. [23:29]
- The second you get caged by Warden, move away from the epicenter; even Ice Path out. [25:00]
- Using the dome to claim the Urn is good; there are few contexts where you would not dome for an Urn cash. [29:04]
- React faster with Ice Path, for example the second Haze ults. [29:36]
- Dome escape at low HP: dome, Ice Path, hold shift and circle up, then cancel the dome at the top; you have height and are harder to shoot. Wait until the dash is fully initiated before holding shift. [31:38]
- Kelvin is less stamina dependent because you play around Ice Path. [17:46]

Movement and macro:
- The best tool for improving movement is the Explore the Map mode: spend 5 minutes in it every day before you play, moving around efficiently. [4:31]
- Practice one thing at a time; when new, something simple like wall jumping and heavy melee. [7:34]
- At this elo, focus on movement fundamentals first before quirky tech like sideways air dashing. [12:38]
- Go to the secret shop when you have the souls, or farm a camp during downtime. [13:08]
- Sitting on 5k souls is a big capacity loss. [14:08]
- In a bad fight, spam retreat or fall back and send a teammate to catch the wave; but if the team commits to the fight, fight with them. [16:13]
- Routing: take the correct zip and jump off instead of going through base to reach a walker sooner. [16:44]
- Stop at the secret shop on the way if you can afford a full item. [18:18]
- Go for bridge buffs when you see them spawn; learn the bridge buff timings. [19:51]
- "Low elo syndrome": after an uncontested objective like the Urn, the enemy shows up 10-15 seconds later with five or six players, so do not push aggressively after running Urn; have the whole team leave. [18:49]
- This mostly happens before Emissary rank. [33:39]
