## Covers

Themes: hitting the wave and orbs, denies, punishing roams by pushing tower, timers and Urn side, comeback Rift, Wraith card stacking, Ivy ult targets and Echo Shard into Stone Form, side laning, shrines over kills, and anti-CC items.
Side notes: Kidnap Dorman and Dispel Magic, Curse versus Haze's Fixation, Sharpshooter users, Magic Carpet.
