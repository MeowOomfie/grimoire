# The Biggest Winners and Losers In Deadlock 2.0
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-02-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QHtQOP6M4x8

## Covers
First read of the three lane map update (Deadlock 2.0) after eight hours of play: why farmers like Seven win, gankers like Mo & Krill and long range heroes like Vindicta and Grey Talon lose, the new no-last-hit laning with 50% denies, the Sprint Speed buff, Sinner's Sacrifice triple buffs, jungle invades and verticality for Lash.
For players adjusting to the new map and lane rules.

## Claims
- Few balance changes besides Holliday nerfs, but the game plays drastically differently and many heroes and items got stronger or weaker. [0:00]
- Big winner: Seven, and farmers and jungle heroes in general; jungling got a lot stronger. [0:31]
- There are many more creep camps and more Sinner's Sacrifices; camps have a bit less HP and give a bit less souls, but an efficient farmer clears the jungle very fast and gets rich quickly. [0:31]
- Infernus is really strong too because he gets around fast and clears camps very quickly. [1:02]
- The map is now three lanes in a 2-2-2 setup; with only three lanes for six players there is more free time, so it is strong to have one or two dedicated players roaming and farming camps while three protect Guardians and push lanes. [1:32]
- Wraith used to be the best split pusher and solo Walker killer; that happens less now because the enemy has only three objectives, side Walkers have more HP, and more enemies tend to show up to punish split pushing. [2:03]
- Wraith still farms camps very fast and gets around the map quickly. [2:34]
- Heroes that roam and need ganks, like Mo & Krill, got weaker: lanes are much farther apart, rotations take longer even with good routes, so ganks carry more risk and give the enemy more time to react. [3:05]
- Before, a duo mid lane could reach a side lane fast and almost always get a kill; now the lane you gank usually has two enemies, and a kill in a 3v2 is harder to secure than in a 2v1. [4:05]
- Bebop is probably about the same power level: better laning, weaker roaming. [4:35]
- New lane rule: you no longer need to last hit troopers to make souls appear, but denies are 50% of a creep's souls, so denying and harassing matter much more than last hitting. [4:35]
- Bebop is very strong in lane because his instant hitscan laser gets denies and he harasses well. [5:07]
- Laning is faster paced; you spend most of your time harassing the enemy, and if you push them back behind their tower they cannot go for denies while you wait for troopers to trade and take all the denies and souls. [5:07]
- Lane snowballing is more drastic: a first wave is usually 300 souls per team for the four creeps, but one team can end up with 450 and the other 150. [6:08]
- Lane bullies that push the enemy out of lane are much stronger. [6:08]
- Heroes that benefit from creep healing, like Mo & Krill, are weaker because you do not want to push the wave; keep the wave near your tower while forcing the enemy out. [6:38]
- Only attack and push creeps when you want to push a tower; before that there is little value in last hitting. [6:38]
- Killing creeps with melee still has value because it stops the soul from popping up, denying the enemy the chance to deny your soul. [7:09]
- Item winner: Sprint Speed. Before, any damage taken or dealt put sprint on a 5 second delay, so moving camp to camp it never kicked in. [7:40]
- Now after taking or dealing damage to creeps, sprint kicks in after half a second, almost instantly. [8:11]
- Against enemy heroes, sprint now kicks in 4 seconds after taking or dealing damage instead of 5. [8:42]
- Every item that gives Sprint Speed got better; he is back to buying Sprint Boots over Extra Stamina on every hero, buying them very early, though stamina is still useful for map traversal. [9:13]
- Sprint Boots help in lane: shoot, let sprint kick in, chase; if the enemy is not hitting you, you can shoot and chase right after. [9:43]
- Rapid Rounds, already a great item, gets better: its 1 meter extra sprint now adds up and makes you very fast early. [9:43]
- Sinner's Sacrifice is the most broken thing in the game: there are many on the map and they already gave more souls for the time invested than any tier three camp, even for a fast farmer like Seven. [10:43]
- They now give a triple gold statue buff that is easy to get: punch it four times, then start the heavy melee animation when the third to last lamp lights up so the heavy hits on the last blinking light. [11:14]
- Gold statue buffs scale with game time, getting stronger the longer the game goes. [12:16]
- His plan: at 8 minutes leave the lane and farm every Sinner's Sacrifice on your side, and on the enemy side when safe. [12:16]
- Doing that for a few minutes can put you 5-10k souls ahead with hidden stats (e.g. +20% weapon damage, +20% ammo, 20-30 extra spirit) that enemies cannot see, only your souls, so you feel unkillable in fights. [12:47]
- He expects a nerf: the triple statue buff is too much and there may be too many sacrifices on the map. [12:47]
- Junglers and heroes with an escape that can enter the enemy jungle safely are very strong: the jungle is isolated and far from the lanes, so enemies rarely scout you, and you can take a camp or two unnoticed. [13:18]
- Example: the old double Sinner's Sacrifice room used to sit next to a Guardian and lane where an enemy would hear you; now it is isolated. [13:48]
- Map awareness and game knowledge matter even more; know where the enemy could be before invading. [14:50]
- Your number one jungle priority should always be Sinner's Sacrifices, since they give more than anything else on the map, except the very big T3 side camp, which still gives a lot. [14:50]
- Verticality: the biggest winner is probably Lash, with lots of new high ground to abuse, especially in mid lane, where he can easily get up and set up engagements. [15:23]
- The more mobile the hero the better, because getting around the map faster matters. [15:55]
- Losers: long range heroes that deal damage from safe distance, like Vindicta and Grey Talon, because there are far fewer open sightlines. [15:55]
- The mid lane Walker funnels attackers into one alley, making it hard for Vindicta to find a position to deal damage without being hit. [16:27]
- The yellow lane Walker is now one hallway with a wall in your face, so you must tank the tower and be exposed to shoot it; a buff to the defending side, and flying Vindicta can no longer see the Walker from the sky. [16:57]
- Map geometry adds big buildings that block a flyer's line of sight, so Vindicta and Grey Talon get less value. [17:59]
- Rotations to other lanes take much longer, though people will find faster routes (there is a wall jump in one spot). [18:30]
- Mirage is insane because he can be farming anywhere in the jungle and reach a teamfight instantly. [19:02]
- Summary advice: play farmers and aggressive gankers that are fast and mobile; avoid stationary heroes and heroes that need 1v2 solo pickoffs like Mo & Krill. [19:33]
- Side lane 1v2 fights will almost never happen now since there are two per lane, so fights are 2v3 or 2v4. [19:33]
- Wraith can still use that: if your team pushes with two or three and the enemy shows up with five, you can split push another Walker. [20:06]
- He thinks lanes are more skillful, not simpler: you choose between meleeing a creep and harassing, and between pushing the enemy out or focusing on denies. [20:06]
- Final verdict: big winners Lash and Seven, big losers Mo & Krill and snipers. [21:08]
