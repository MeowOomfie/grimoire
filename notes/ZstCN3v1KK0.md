# Positioning is simple, actually
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-05-10
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZstCN3v1KK0

## Covers
The seven most common positioning mistakes: sightlines and angles that suit your weapon and abilities, stamina to reach cover, using cover to recharge cooldowns, respecting enemy catch items, high ground and third-person camera, not overcompensating for your team, and preloading your role before a fight, with a Victor Urn fight breakdown. For players of any rank.

## Claims
- The number one positioning mistake is not playing around sightlines and angles that benefit your hero. [0:00]
- Paige's projectile travels slowly and pierces, so play from the veil to pierce multiple targets, which is especially good for pushing the wave in lane. [0:00]
- Paige is also strong playing around corners because the corner timing matches the gap between her projectiles while holding M1. [0:00]
- Vindicta does not want to be in the veil; she wants a long open sightline because her weapon falloff range is larger than most heroes. [0:00]
- Check your hero's falloff range to pick sightlines: Vindicta is 20 m to 64 m (above average), Vyper is terrible at 15 m to 33 m. [0:30]
- Drifter is very strong shooting around corners because if any of his bullets hit, it counts as full damage (20 per shot even clipping the side of an Infernus). [1:00]
- The Doorman and Paige pierce and Celeste bounces; unique gun properties should change how you position. [1:00]
- Apollo's abilities move him around and benefit from open spaces, but they are short range, so he is a mid-range hero who wants to play on top of enemies in open areas. [1:00]
- Apollo's weapon falloff range is 25 m; past 25 m he literally cannot do damage. [1:30]
- Second most common mistake: not adjusting positioning to stamina. Rule of thumb, you want to be able to reach cover with one to two stamina. [4:00]
- An open spot like the bridge as Wraith is only comfortable with a significant advantage or plenty of stamina; with three stamina you can escape if someone goes on you. [4:30]
- Do not stand in large open areas with no nearby cover while at zero stamina; always think about your escape path to the next cover. [5:00]
- Third mistake: not using cover to recharge cooldowns. After using Wraith's teleport to escape, stay evasive behind a wall, juke, and by then the teleport is back up. [5:00]
- Use cover to reset and regain cooldowns and items, especially with an item like Warp Stone or a low cooldown movement ability that lets you stall. [5:31]
- Veil juke: when running through a veil, stop right after passing it, let the chaser follow through, then go back the other way using stamina. [6:02]
- Juking around pillars and cover to regain cooldowns lets you escape, stall for your team to arrive, or wait out your cooldowns and turn the fight. [6:32]
- Fourth mistake: not accounting for enemy items and abilities. Wraith may push a Walker or tower freely thinking she can teleport away, but must consider who can catch her. [6:32]
- Silver's bola works like Slowing Hex and stops Wraith from teleporting; Slowing Hex, Silence Wave (said as "silence wave"), Curse and similar items also stop the teleport. [7:03]
- Look at enemy items and position accordingly: before aggressive pushes either know where the catching hero (for example Silver with Slowing Hex) is on the map, or buy a counter item first. [7:03]
- In team fights keep a mental map of every tool the enemy has to catch you and wait until they use them on other people; most players do not have item discipline. [7:34]
- Do not go in first and dump everything; understand your role and the threats, itemize against them if you can, otherwise play more passive until those threats are used. [8:04]
- Fifth mistake: not using high ground enough. It is very good on Lash and beneficial to most heroes. [8:04]
- High ground gives options because there is no fall damage, so you can always come down; it gives great sightlines to see threats from far away and read how a fight is going. [8:35]
- From high ground you can use the third-person camera to spot people below (for example crouching above a Lash under the bridge) without being spotted yourself; people often have no idea you are there. [9:06]
- If a stalemate or a fight is about to happen, consider taking high ground to see what is coming. [9:36]
- Abrams does not like high ground as much: unless he has Phantom Strike, dropping onto someone to catch them with his charge takes too long. [9:36]
- Sixth mistake: doing too much outside your hero's job. Kelvin is a backline hero who does not initiate unless he catches someone with his dome alongside a teammate. [10:07]
- Do not run far ahead to pick a Lash because your team will not; you feeding out of frustration is worse than your team making the wrong play. [10:37]
- On Kelvin, wait for a good dome opportunity, or sit on high ground with Ice Path for a view and drop down to dome. [10:37]
- As Wraith without ultimate, diving an overextended Lash often puts you out of position unless you can melt him instantly; play more passive and let the right teammate initiate. [11:08]
- Do not try to make up your team's missing DPS by playing out of role; this is less of a mistake in Deadlock since every hero can do a lot of damage. Accept how your team plays and position accordingly. [11:39]
- Seventh mistake, and a huge one: know your role in the fight before it starts. Fights are too busy to decide targets on the spot, so preload the plan. [11:39]
- Sinclair against Dynamo: your role is to steal Dynamo's ultimate and go for big Singularities; play wide, look for the Dynamo, steal the ult. [12:10]
- Victor example build against gun: Suppressor (slows enemy fire rate), Return Fire (returns 65% of damage), Metal Skin and Warp Stone (bullet resist). [13:11]
- Return Fire's returned damage is calculated before mitigation, so even with 38% bullet resist you return about as much as they deal; with Metal Skin popped, enemies shooting you only damage themselves. [13:11]
- Check net worth to choose the target: the enemy Lady Geist was richest at 39,000 souls and had Rejuvenator, so she had to be shut down. [13:41]
- Victor's role while Mo & Krill turns in the Urn: create space for Mo & Krill and shut down the enemy gun carries (Lady Geist first as the richest, also Warden or Venator). [14:13]
- On seeing Lady Geist, go right on top of her with Return Fire and Metal Skin; your own life matters less because the goal is space for the Urn. [14:13]
- With Refresher-type resets and three lives (Victor), the front liner can be very aggressive to create space. [15:13]
- As front liner, stand between the enemy damage dealers and your team as the first line of contact, far forward so enemies use abilities on you, while still playing corners, tight angles for a close-range hero, and staying near cover when low on stamina. [15:43]
- Losing the trade against Lady Geist is fine if it keeps her out of the fight; that is the job. [16:13]
- When your team moves forward, Warp Stone forward, tank as much as possible and stay in the enemies' faces, focusing the targets that matter. [16:43]
- Decision between shooting Lady Geist and the Warden: following the plan (space plus shut down gun carries), he chased and zoned the Warden since he could not kill Lady Geist before she swapped, isolating the enemy so his team cleaned up. [17:14]
