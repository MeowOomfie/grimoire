# A Successful Call Isn't Always The Correct One | Arcanist Dynamo Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2024-11-21

## Covers
Midknight reviews one Arcanist-rank Dynamo game, a duo lane with a Yamato against a Kelvin and Wraith, that the player's team won by a lot.
Free coaching review over a replay. Recorded November 2024, on a late-2024 patch; the game has changed a lot since, so check items, numbers and rank names against the current patch.
Themes: plays that worked but were still wrong, cover and tower respect in lane, wave and health awareness, overextending with a lead, dash-slide movement, Refresher, spending ability points, and getting value from Singularity.

## Claims
Player 1 (Dynamo, Arcanist):
- Being aggressive early is good, but hug the boxes for cover if you want to hit the wave that early. [0:30]
- While your Yamato is all over the enemy, shoot the enemy hero (Kelvin) instead of only the wave. [0:30]
- If you go for an early tower dive (not suggested, very ego), after you kick him with Kinetic Pulse walk out the side instead of back through the tower; walking back into the tower is very dangerous. [1:01]
- Playing aggressive at low HP against a Wraith with two cards is very dangerous; if she cards you, you are dead. [1:01]
- At 1k souls, go buy. [1:32]
- When low, go to base and buy Healing Rite; normally Extra Regen or Restorative Shot also work, but Dynamo doesn't like building those items. [1:32]
- At 200 HP, being that deep is too risky even with the wave taking tower. [1:32]
- Buying three gun items with a good lane advantage was fine. [2:02]
- Diving early in the game was bad because it was riskier; once you have a big lead you can roll into the tower, parry, and try to kill the enemy there. [2:32]
- Playing like that with a lead risks throwing it: one bad trade or going too deep and they can make a full recovery off two kills. [2:32]
- Be aware of where your wave is so you don't take tower damage. [3:04]
- Roaming to another lane (on the Infernus) was good. [3:04]
- If you melee a tower, parry it first or be ready to parry after you melee. [3:04]
- Tower dive tech: dash into the tower, slide right before you reach it to cancel the dash, parry it, then melee it about twice and back off. [3:04]
- After your lane is won, it is better to roam than to autopilot down your own lane sieging. [3:35]
- A Singularity on a hero who is very behind is pointless: he drops only about 300 souls at that point, and your Black Hole is worth more than 300 souls. [3:35]
- Grab the bridge buff when nearby. [4:06]
- Pushing deep at 300 HP is very risky; a good team will send four people to that lane, kill you, then clean up the next lane and come back. [4:06]
- If you are at a buff (movement buff), walk into it and grab it; you get to your target faster. [4:37]
- Sieging a walker is fine when three enemies show on the map and two are dead. [4:37]
- Pushing alone into their backline with no team around is way too deep. [4:37]
- Always slide after you dash; you get more distance and it is a much more efficient way to move. Also use dash jumps. [4:37]
- The second you get shot on a zipline, drop off it. [5:08]
- Overextending deep into their base with no teammates gives the enemy an easy comeback and is the easiest way to throw a lead. [5:08]
- Three open flex slots at this point is fine; normally you would have at most one, so you don't need them filled yet, but you do need to buy. [5:38]
- Kick (Kinetic Pulse) first before the Singularity. [5:38]
- Waiting for the Wraith to arrive would have let you Black Hole both. [5:38]
- When your whole team is far ahead, you only want team Black Holes; solo Black Hole only an enemy who is really fed. [6:09]
- Baiting the Grey Talon trap when you know he is not around is good. [6:09]
- At 6k souls, don't buy two 3k items; Refresher is one of the key items in Dynamo's kit, in every Dynamo build, and a very big power spike, so buy it. [6:40]
- A Black Hole on enemies inside a Kelvin dome over a Rejuvenator is bad; if you want to ult them off the Rejuvenator, ult once it drops. [6:40]
- You were sitting on five unspent ability points; spend them. Max Rejuvenating Aurora next. [7:10]
- Learn from the enemy's mistakes: they kept ego dueling while down 20k. [7:10]
- Low rank games are one-sided stomps because new players don't know how to play from behind and keep fighting; at high MMR comebacks are strong and common. [7:40]
- Summary, lane: use cover; you face-tanked minions and enemies while killing minions. [8:11]
- Summary, lane: respect tower; you tanked it four times and died once. [8:11]
- Summary: wave awareness. Always play behind your wave, whether hitting the enemy wave or sieging towers. [8:11]
- Summary: stop overextending deep into their base with no or one teammate; a better team would have made the comeback. [8:42]
- Summary: health awareness; don't wander their side of the map at 300 HP; go to base or buy Healing Rite. [8:42]
- Summary: find more value with your ult. Ulting one person is fine, but not one who is very behind or already going to die; waiting about 5 seconds for the Wraith would have caught both. [9:12]
