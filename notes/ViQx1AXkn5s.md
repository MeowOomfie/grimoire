## Covers
Role guide for the spirit carry (spirit nuker, off carry): a high burst spirit hero who bullies lane, converts that into stolen jungle, and bursts squishies in team fights.
Which heroes fit, lane poke and diving, mid game Sinner's Sacrifices, urns and invades, timers, spikes, late game picks and team fight targeting.

## Claims
- A spirit carry is a very high burst spirit hero who bullies enemies in lane, converts that into stealing jungle, then bursts down squishy enemies in team fights. [0:00]
- Spirit carries: Apollo, Calico, Mina, Grey Talon, Sinclair on the general matchmaking build, and Yamato (who also fits the front line role); a hero captioned "Hauketh" is on the line. [0:00]
- The Doorman is also a spirit carry, though his best build right now is a different one (captioned "Bell") and he can also be played support. [0:31]
- Viscous is a spirit nuker or nuker/support rather than a true spirit carry: he invades less, plays more defensively to support his team while throwing Splatters, so the advice only half applies. [0:31]
- Spirit carries tend to be very strong in lane; with heroes like Apollo or Mina, poke the enemies and try to get them low. [1:01]
- If you struggle to poke, get wave priority first and then fight with your minion advantage; never fight in the enemy wave, since tanking minion damage makes trades very hard to win. [1:01]
- With single target poke like Mina, focus poke on enemies when they step out of cover; with AoE poke like Apollo, hit enemies while they hit your wave so you damage them and their minions together, gaining priority. [1:31]
- Because you want to play very aggressive, many of these heroes buy an early Extra Regen or Restorative Shot if the lane will be hyper active. [1:31]
- With regen items, leverage the passive HP advantage by trading; avoid overflow regen, so if you bought early Extra Regen at full HP, spend that HP on an aggressive trade next wave, since even a neutral trade comes back faster for you. [2:01]
- Once the enemies are low and playing safe under tower, diving is good; parry the tower when you dive. [2:01]
- Don't force dives: the enemies must be low enough, and don't dive into key defensive abilities like Ivy's stone form or Viscous's cube; forcing a bad dive and dying is the best way to throw a winning lane. [2:31]
- Leaving the enemies at 1 HP is also very valuable: they can never pressure you, so you keep wave priority, steal their boxes and camps, roam (after about 5 minutes) or hit their tower early. [3:01]
- On Calico he rolls into the tower, parries it, and rolls back if no dive appears; you lose only 50 to 100 HP and sometimes scare them into using a cooldown. [3:01]
- Against an undivable comp (Lady Geist with swap, Victor with ult, Ivy with stone form), convert the lead into stealing every camp and box on their side, building a soul lead that later lets you dive or win lane without fighting. [3:31]
- A soul lead before ults lets you get your ult first; fighting and diving with your ult before theirs is the best way to leverage a lead. [4:01]
- If you are two pure spirit nukers, the goal is to kill them before ults so you get one or two more free kills when ults come up. [4:32]
- With priority you can roam, especially on a roaming hero like Calico; if your partner anchors and clears well, ask them to buy Monster Rounds, shove immediately, roam for value in another lane, then come back. [4:32]
- The mid game starts at 8 minutes when Sinner's Sacrifices spawn, even though in average solo queue the first tower falls around 10. [5:03]
- In the mid game, steal the Sinner's Sacrifices next to your lane using lane pressure; if you took the tower before 8 minutes that Sinner's Sacrifice should be free. [5:03]
- Killing a tower opens that side of the jungle; if you are on the doubles side you can coordinate a doubles invade. [5:03]
- Alternatively, after breaking your tower, go straight to another lane and pressure that guardian; with their mid and right side guardians down, all the right side farm is open to steal. [5:34]
- One of the best ways to carry solo queue on a hero that doesn't scale as well as a gun carry is to win all of your lanes early, especially in a tempo meta. [5:34]
- Decide by timers: if no Sinner's Sacrifices are up on the side you opened, pressure the enemy guardian, leaving one person in your lane (a solo-farm hero like Venator can stay). [6:06]
- Example: as Yamato with an Apollo, ask a Wraith to swap and take green lane, then pressure mid while the Apollo roams or anchors. [6:06]
- After the first Sinner's Sacrifices, play for urns off spawn: the first urn spawns at 10 minutes and always on yellow side (10 has a zero, yellow has an O); urns spawn every 5 minutes, on green side when the minute ends in 5. [6:36]
- Stand on the buff on the urn's side and run the urn immediately; make sure you confirm the urn orbs. [7:06]
- If the enemies get the urn, play for denies: post up on a roof by the delivery point and shoot the orbs when they cash it, a ton of soul value, especially with a shotgun or piercing hero like Paige. [7:06]
- Sinner's Sacrifices have a 5 minute respawn, so if taken off spawn the next ones return around 13:30; at Oracle or above you will see many popping back around 13:30 to 14:00, which is the time to get active. [7:06]
- Whenever invading or pressuring jungle, get wave priority first and use the crashing waves to steal farm; almost never push ahead of a wave to steal jungle, unless you are hunting an unexpected pick on a hurt enemy at a Sinner's Sacrifice. [7:37]
- When invading you will often find an enemy at half HP farming; kill him, take the bag and leave. [8:07]
- Harassing enemies while they farm creates passive pressure whenever you are off vision: a hunted gun carry on the weak side has to reset early instead of greeding low. [8:38]
- If four enemies show on the far side and you would arrive late, spam retreat, call don't fight, and go very aggressively to the opposite side with a teammate, on their wave (especially their gun carry) or in their jungle. [8:38]
- Punish the enemy whenever they show three or four on the map; look for plays, especially suffocating the enemy gun carry, and show up as a surprise when off vision. [9:08]
- As a spirit carry you generally gravitate to mid lane: shove and set the mid wave, then look for a play; catching mid waves also keeps your farm up. [9:38]
- Pocket loves farm from stealing enemy Sinner's Sacrifices thanks to mobility, but fundamentally needs wave farm; Pocket can also side lane if the comp needs it. [9:38]
- Calico can side lane only without Trophy Collector and it feels bad, since she is strong in the mid game and wants to pressure their jungle; most spirit carries can side lane if needed. [10:09]
- With a front line in one side lane and a gun carry in the other (for example Mirage and Wraith in side lanes), you take farm from mid and the enemy jungle. [10:09]
- Securing your own Sinner's Sacrifices and jungle is fine, but never farm your own jungle when anything is happening on the map: pressure the opposite side during a fight, push waves or prepare for the orb fight when the urn is running. [10:40]
- Go to every rift fight unless it is a comeback rift, in which case trade a walker or something else on the other side of the map. [10:40]
- A big solo queue mistake is calling off a comeback rift and then doing nothing; since you know where the enemy team is stacking, take value on the map, even if that is just fighting them after the rift. [11:10]
- Rifts spawn first at 12 minutes (give or take a minute), then every 7 minutes (give or take a minute), and urns every 5 minutes; be doing something on those timers and fight, invade or farm for a spike between them. [11:10]
- Have a spike online before a rift fight, for example your first tier four item: Arctic Blast on Calico or Spirit Burn on Pocket, items good in AoE fights. [11:40]
- Spirit carry builds generally rush 4.8k soul spikes so they are stronger than gun carries and single-tree heroes who spike later, and they leverage that for kills. [11:40]
- In the late game, when respawn timers reach 40 to 60 seconds, picks matter most: with a pick hero, wait on side waves for whoever responds and nuke them, or hunt in the jungle. [12:10]
- Look for picks on side lanes, not mid, because everyone can rotate to the center quickly; use the pick before a key objective like mid boss respawning or a rift. [12:41]
- In team fights, jump and nuke an enemy squishy who mispositions, which in solo queue they 100% will. [13:11]
- Pocket wants a big Affliction; Calico wants to assassinate one person who missteps or come from the side, using her ult to kill and reposition; Mina with her 2 up can play more aggressively with Love Bites and spread her damage more. [13:11]
- Avoid spending cooldowns on, or being near, front liners; a front liner's job is to bait your cooldowns, so if your team is focusing an Abrams he is doing his job. [13:43]
- Peel for your gun carry or backline only if a front liner is badly mispositioned or diving them; otherwise find another angle to hit a squishy. [14:13]
- Call focus targets on mic ("focus Paige" when she is at 30%); in rift fights people split focus badly, so focusing the same player is very powerful. [14:13]
