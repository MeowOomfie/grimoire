## Covers
Vyper, Midknight. Coaching review of one Archon Vyper player's game (a long game against a Seven, Haze, Mirage and Drifter).
Themes: early resets and Healing Rite, buying greens and investment items instead of rushing oranges, Extra Spirit on Vyper, Bola usage, Fury Trance with Fleetfoot, Sinner's Sacrifice, calling mid boss (with his flowchart), wasting advantages, bad fights, farm priority, Rejuv habits, and a side answer on Holliday's gun pivot. Video dated 2025-10-29.

## Claims
Build and abilities:
- Starting Venom is fine, but he would go Mystic Burst first: put a point in Venom early, buy Mystic Burst and spam it for a lot of damage. [0:30]
- Mystic Burst is weaker into long-range laners who can dodge Venom; there Extra Spirit for Dagger play is fine. Mystic Burst shines into front-line comps, where you spam Venom off cooldown for poke. [0:30]
- Current Vyper meta seen in pro play: no points in Slide until last. [1:01]
- In lane, do not save for a T2 on Vyper; the only T2 he would accept saving for is Battle Vest. [1:01]
- He doesn't like rushing Opening Rounds: you want some spirit first so Venom can actually execute. [2:34]
- Battle Vest fits a lane against hybrid gun damage, and its out-of-combat regen is nice against a poke hero like Mirage. [2:34]
- Extra Spirit feels great early because Vyper's spirit scalings are comical: max Venom has 2.8 spirit scaling, about 250 to 300 more execute with Extra Spirit, and Bola scales 2.0. [3:35]
- Extra Spirit is +10 spirit power plus +7 from the spirit investment, so +17, worth about 1.4k souls. [16:55]
- Venom max first is fine, but only if you buy some spirit: even an 800 or 1.6k spirit item like Extra Spirit, Improved Spirit or Mystic Burst. [17:26]
- There is no clearly good Vyper build right now; both Venom max first and Slide max first are played at a higher level. [17:56]
- Fury Trance is broken right now and Vyper mains say she buys it: 40% spirit resist and 40% fire rate on a green item with health investment. [10:19]
- Before Fury Trance you want more slide distance: Burst Fire, Fleetfoot or another sliding item. Always have Fleetfoot on Vyper; it works well with her kit. [10:50]
- Fleetfoot removes the downsides of Fury Trance and gives active slow resist; hold Fleetfoot and pop it together with Fury Trance every time. [18:26]
- Fury Trance silences you, so dump your kit first: ult to cancel Seven or Haze, Venom someone, then Fury Trance, Fleetfoot, Split Shot and go in with your gun. [38:11]
- Repeatedly popping Fury Trance into fights left her silenced when Bola could have canceled Seven or Haze. [38:11]
- Escalating Resilience is probably the best T3 bullet defense item and was good this game. [21:07]
- Into a bullet-heavy enemy, Vyper doesn't mind Hunter's Aura; you could buy it, sit in a side lane, get fed and win every 1v1. [21:07]
- An AP Bola-max Vyper is funny but never meta, and is easily countered by Spellbreaker, debuff remover or Counterspell. [20:36]
- Against Spellbreaker, Dagger twice before you Venom or Bola to pop it. Cheaper items give much more debuff resist than Spellbreaker. [29:25]
- Classic mistake: buying Spellbreaker against Yamato. Most Yamato lead with 2 or 3, and a good Yamato who sees it just pops it first. [29:25]
- Daggers do not go through Bola (pre-rework testing): they may apply stacks and on-hit but no damage. Only Venom, or Armor Piercing Rounds procs, hit through Bola. [14:24]
- If you're ever in Seven's ball, get out; don't walk into the stun for fun. [4:07]
- With Opening Rounds, don't lead with Bola. Shoot first, then Bola at 40 to 50% HP and Venom off the back of it; with Extra Spirit, Bola, Venom, Dagger executes from about 45%. [8:16]
- Bola is fine as a pick tool, like a dormant ult, to catch someone out. [16:55]
- Save Bola for channeling ults: Bola the Seven when he presses his ult, and Bola Haze when she jumps you. Bola is S tier into those two. [26:22]
- Using Bola to cancel a beam Kelvin's ult is fine but a waste on an Opening Rounds build. [14:54]
- Greeding back in for a Venom on-hit on Haze is not worth it with 37 Fixation stacks on you, especially while her trance gives 40% spirit resist. [21:38]
- Throw Venom on someone before you die. [22:39]
- Vyper is meant to be farmed; her farm should match the carries, not the supports. At 33k she was level with the support heroes, which is why she felt useless in fights. [33:36]

Laning:
- Don't full reset from lane just for HP; buying Healing Rite instead costs only about 150 souls effectively (sells for 400) and saves a 250 to 300 soul wave. Missing six minions meant Healing Rite would have paid for itself. [5:10]
- Healing Rite also gives the green investment, +8% max HP, which a squishy build desperately needs. [5:10]
- If your next item isn't green after 4.6k souls of no green, you are griefing; Active Reload there is really bad. [6:10]
- Only reset from lane if you have boost (shove, boost back, boost there), or you are mid lane, where the jump makes cheeky resets possible, or you absolutely must. [6:40]
- Boost is a 30 second buff: boost back, get off the zip, heal, grab the zip and boost there. Sitting on it cost a walker. [19:30]
- If you lose your tower before 8 minutes, secure your Sinner's Sacrifice; the enemy will steal it as soon as they kill you or get wave pressure. [8:16]
- After you take their tower, invade and steal their Sinner's Sacrifice; it is worth far more than stealing a camp. [9:17]
- Buy at the secret shop when you are on top of it; bad routing wasted time and missed a crashing wave. [9:17]
- Never prioritize farming over a crashing wave: it bleeds damage onto your walker and costs minions. [13:53]

Movement:
- When climbing a wall, wall jump then mantle slide; wall bounce there instead of double jumping. Don't waste stamina dashing for nothing. [11:50]

Macro and fights:
- Before rotating to a fight, ask your team if they are fighting. If not, pressure your lane or steal a Sinner's Sacrifice while they show many players elsewhere. [12:52]
- Don't two-push into three when you know you have no backup. [14:54]
- When your team shows mid, make your way to the movement buff; the bridge buff is very strong and great on Vyper. [15:24]
- Two picks, 24 minutes in, healthy team: that is a mid boss angle. Reset, go mid, run urn, push base. Not calling mid boss there is where this game was lost. [22:39]
- Stagger the urn: once it is neutral, if it resets the enemy gets a comeback urn. [23:11]
- Mid boss flowchart: two picks with your team on top of mid after 18 minutes, go mid; two picks anywhere after 22 minutes, go mid; one pick anywhere after 28 minutes, go mid. Exceptions apply, like Dynamo. [46:26]
- Chasing a Drifter while the enemy shows only two is bad; go back to your team. [28:23]
- Fighting down two players with a wave crashing is a terrible fight. [29:56]
- When the next fight decides the game, don't six-man mid into a coin flip. Option one: shove waves and send four to look for a pick with Mo & Krill, then use it for mid boss. Option two, better: run the urn and dictate the fight on your terms. [36:08]
- Four on a side lane looking for a pick is fundamentally good, but you need wave priority first. [42:21]
- With three enemy Rejuvs, don't fight outside your base; hold the high ground on top of your base. [39:16]
- When your patrons are exposed, contest mid; a desperate fight with a 10% chance can beat a fight in base where they hit the patron. [43:23]
- Spend more time in side lanes and split objectives while your team fights; her teammates led on troopers, neutrals and objective souls. [47:28]
- To improve jungling routes, watch someone good at farming, like Zergy. [47:59]

Rejuv and melee:
- Tech or bug: if you look down while meleeing Rejuv, an enemy parry won't stop you getting it. Looking up to melee an urn carrier off is the same mechanic. [25:18]
- In a contested Rejuv, a fed carry should not melee the Rejuv: if they get parried the fight is lost. A team lost a pro game this way when their 60k Ivy got parried. [26:52]
- A Paige shouldn't jump in the pit to melee in a contested Rejuv; stay outside and use bolts and barriers. [27:23]
- One player sitting under the Rejuv is fine if they parry, since it signals the team they will parry; if they won't parry, walk outside. [27:53]
- Getting parried is inevitable when you melee; learn from it but don't stop meleeing. Gamble light melees at high level; heavy melee gambles work at lower ranks. [30:56]

Holliday side answer:
- If you are stuck farming a side lane on Holliday, first ask on mic for someone to swap; if not, farm it and do the gun pivot at around 27k on his build, then go back to spirit with something like Boundless Spirit, or a headshot item if they are tanky. If you can't aim, hard commit to offensive spirit items. [40:18]
