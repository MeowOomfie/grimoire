## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (patch of 9-16-26), four replay reviews: a Silver game, a Celeste game (Emissary, played just before the patch), a Vindicta game (Oracle) and a Pocket game.
Themes: Melee Lifesteal over Restorative Shot, hunting the enemy M1s and their bags, the new comeback rift, fighting with your team, movement practice, picks before mid boss, Vindicta playing carry with stacks and staying in the air, Pocket using Barrage and not crutching on Flying Cloak.
