# SHIELDS VS ARMORS  -  Deadlock Itemization Lesson
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-09-08
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eCf9wB3acjg

## Covers
An itemization lesson on when to buy shields (Combat Barrier, Enchanter's Barrier, Veil Walker) versus resistances (Bullet Armor, Spirit Armor and the many items with resist on them). September 2024 patch era, a much older game with a different shop. For players unsure which defensive layer fits their hero and the meta.

## Claims
- Only a few items exist mainly to give shields: Combat Barrier, Enchanter's Barrier and Veil Walker; many others give shields as a secondary stat, for example Spirit Strike (100 spirit shield), Headhunter (150 bullet shield) and Shadow Weave (300 spirit shield). [0:30]
- Type "shield" in the shop to list every item that gives shields, and "resist" to list every item that gives resistances; there are far more resist sources than shield sources. [1:00]
- Armors (resistances) get more effective the more health you have, so they are always better later in the game. [1:31]
- Example: 20% bullet reduction on 1,000 health is about 200 extra effective HP, but on 2,500 health it is about 500 effective HP. Effective HP is how much damage you must take before you die. [1:31]
- Armors synergize well with lifesteal and health regen items: with Leech you would rather be tanky through armors than shields, because you can leech health back but not shields. [2:03]
- The main downside of armors is that they are weaker early, so you hit a power spike later; high level players mostly skip armors until about 20 to 25 minutes, though it is situational. [2:35]
- Barriers and shields have a bigger impact early because they are not affected by resistances and give a lot of raw health. [3:05]
- Enchanter's Barrier (1,250 souls) gives 14% base health like any 1,250 vitality item plus 300 spirit shield; to get similar non-shield health you would need Fortitude, a 3,000 soul item. [3:05]
- Combat Barrier gives 28% weapon damage and 8% fire rate while shielded (buffed in the most recent patch). [3:36]
- Enchanter's Barrier gives 20 spirit power and 8% cooldown reduction while shielded. [3:36]
- So the barrier items give a lot of health plus roughly a 1,250 soul weapon item (Combat Barrier) or spirit item (Enchanter's Barrier) worth of value. [4:08]
- When your barrier is fully depleted it takes 60 seconds to come back; if it only goes down partly it regenerates much faster, around 15 to 20 seconds without taking damage. [4:08]
- Resistances do not apply to shields: 50 bullet resist does nothing for a 1,000 bullet shield. [4:38]
- Negative resistances, such as from Pocket or Vindicta, do not apply to shields either. [4:38]
- Because resist and shields don't stack, investing in both is very often inefficient; pick one. [5:09]
- Because of how barriers regenerate, you need Veil Walker for them to be a consistent defensive layer; Veil Walker refreshes your bullet and spirit shields every 17 seconds when you pass through a veil, over a couple of seconds. [5:09]
- Resistances are on many more items, so you build them easily over the game; Bullet Resist Shredder, Berserker, Escalating Resilience, Point Blank and Pristine Emblem all give resist while also being offensive options. [5:39]
- Even without buying armors you usually have around 15 to 20% spirit resist, bullet resist or both. [6:11]
- Mystic Reach gives 5% spirit resist and Restorative Shot gives 7% bullet resist, so two cheap items already give some resistance. [6:11]
- Many 500 soul lane items give small shields and are bought anyway, so you almost always have a small amount of shield too. [6:42]
- Shields vs armors is play style dependent, but in the current meta shields are usually worse: the meta revolves around tanky heroes and sustain through leech, and shields can't be regenerated through leech. [7:12]
- Items you buy anyway scale your resistances, which makes shields worse since they don't benefit from those resistances. [7:12]
- Shield items are not bad: Combat Barrier and Enchanter's Barrier feel like two 1,250 soul items in one, but only while a shield is up, so you ideally need to be shielded all the time, which means buying Veil Walker. [7:42]
- Once a fight breaks out you will most likely lose your shields within 10 seconds unless you are a backliner or the fight is easy. [8:13]
- Warden likes shield builds; Deathy likes the Veil Walker double shield build on him because his 2 (Willpower) already gives a spirit shield. [8:13]
- Most heroes right now are better off with armor builds. [8:44]
- Go shield builds only to hit an early power spike, or on a squishy hero who wants defensive layers without buying lots of health, such as Grey Talon or Vindicta, who often buy one or two shield items. [8:44]
- Backliners take little poke damage; their main danger is getting all-inned, for example Yamato jumping on Vindicta to one-shot her, and there Enchanter's Barrier or Combat Barrier give the most health for the money. [8:44]
- Reactive Barrier, Divine Barrier, Magic Carpet and Diviner's Kevlar give shields situationally, tied to an ability or item, so you don't need to be invested in shields to buy them. [9:15]
- Reactive Barrier and Divine Barrier are good if enemies keep committing heavily on you with all their abilities. [9:46]
- Magic Carpet is one of the best escape tools in the game right now. [9:46]
- Diviner's Kevlar is really good on heroes who use their ultimate to go in, for example Pocket, giving 1,400 health for a period plus spirit power. [9:46]
- If you go shields, Veil Walker is almost mandatory. [10:17]
- Veil Walker is extremely strong and underrated: high mobility and ganking potential from its invisibility, which also makes your footsteps silent for 5 seconds, and sound matters in this game. [10:17]
- If the meta changes he expects barrier items to be bought more often. [10:47]
