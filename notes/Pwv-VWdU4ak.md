## Covers
Paradox, aquatiq commentary over one of his own games on a one-shot Kinetic Carbine build, patch of 2026-07.
Build order notes, why it works in this meta, its late game weakness and a Rift note.

## Claims
- The build goes almost only into Kinetic Carbine damage; the build ID is shown on screen at the start. [1:01]
- Early on most of the carbine damage comes from Mystic Shot, not the carbine itself. [1:31]
- Charge the carbine before engaging and look for low enemies to finish. [2:39]
- The build is strong because enemies rarely buy spirit resist in lane; players do not counter build early. [3:40]
- Buying spirit defense in lane is usually a dumb idea; players go Enchanter's Emblem, which is not enough against Kinetic Carbine plus Mystic Burst plus Mystic Shot. [4:10]
- He buys Extra Regen in lane. [6:15]
- He buys early Sprint Boots for extra HP healing. [6:15]
- Tank Buster comes next after the lane items. [11:58]
- Do not walk into an area covered by many McGinnis turrets. [15:04]
- Rift is good for money, but you mostly take it for the minions. [16:07]
- Rift now acts like the Urn used to, giving money to the whole team, because the Urn no longer gives money to the whole team. [16:37]
- A hero stuck in the tunnels cannot use unstuck, because the game thinks you are still in the map. [17:38]
- The build maxes the wall (Time Wall) around 18 minutes. [18:09]
- Mystic Reverb is the big buy; he calls it insane. [21:46]
- Rift minions do little for a team that has no enemy walker to push into. [23:20]
- Glass Cannon follows Mystic Reverb. [23:20]
- He skips Spirit Burn. [24:21]
- Fortitude is a good item: it lets him go straight back out without sitting in base. [24:21]
- Enemies dispel Mystic Reverb when they see it on them. [26:27]
- The build is strong until late game, when everyone buys spirit resilience and you stop doing damage. [28:03]
