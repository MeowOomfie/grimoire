## Covers
Wraith played with an AI-generated item build, aquatiq, commentary over one low-rank game.
Rules: buy the items as given, upgrades allowed, greens chosen freely. Result 14-1. Patch of May 2026.

## Claims
- The build tested on Wraith: Monster Rounds, Extra Health, Headshot Booster, Enduring Speed, Tesla Bullets, Burst Fire, Quicksilver Reload, Spirit Armor, Ricochet, Inhibitor (captioned "Unstable Inhibitor"), Mystic Vulnerability; it contained no green items. [1:01]
- Upgrading a lane item counts as following the build, for example Extra Health into Fortitude. [0:31]
- Monster Rounds as the first item on Wraith is insanely good. [2:34]
- The lane items in this build (Monster Rounds, Extra Health, Headshot Booster, Enduring Speed) are generally considered good laning items for Wraith. [7:47]
- Enduring Speed is not needed on Wraith: she is not slow, has three stamina bars and a teleport. [4:41]
- He does not like Burst Fire on Wraith. [10:54]
- Early ability plan: max the cards (1) first, then Full Auto (3) for its unlimited ammo, which is really strong. [8:20]
- Maxing the 1 gives you a lot more Jokers, a very strong card. [11:25]
- Later in this game he switched to maxing Full Auto first, then putting points in the ultimate. [16:03]
- Taking a Walker early is not very useful, but walkers gain HP as the game goes on, so getting it early is still good. [11:58]
- Ricochet is a good item on Wraith, though early here; it is really good at procing Toxic Bullets. [13:30]
- Spirit Armor this early is questionable. [13:30]
- Against Shiv, Inhibitor was bought before the silence option. [21:45]
- Items he wished the build had: Surge of Power, Titanic Magazine, Mercurial Magnum. [16:33]
- With no purple items the cards do no damage; the build only does gun damage. [19:08]
- Wraith felt slow without Fleetfoot even with Enduring Speed. [26:54]
- Fortitude lets you heal without going back to base. [24:51]
- Keep making money; leaving lane lets the enemy push the wave straight away. [10:21]
- Do not fight when your strongest player is not there. [21:45]
- Before going into the enemy base, take time to farm money first, there is time. [22:46]
- Back off when not everyone on your team is there and the enemy has more people up. [25:52]
- Wraith's ultimate (lift) is a bit buggy. [16:33]
- Verdict: the build went 14-1 in a low-rank lobby and he called it good, but told viewers not to use AI builds. [29:32]
