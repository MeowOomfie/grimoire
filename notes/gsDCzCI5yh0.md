# The OFFICIAL Deadlock Hero Tier List (With All New Heroes)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-01-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gsDCzCI5yh0

## Covers
A patch read-through plus high-rank ladder tier list made about three days into the mid-January 2025 patch that cut every hero's gun damage by 10% and nerfed Mirage, Vyper, Sinclair and Wraith's cards, with the four new heroes (Calico, Holliday, Sinclair, Vyper) included. Covers each hero change, the item changes (Extra Stamina, Alchemical Fire, Hunter's Aura, Tesla Bullets, Sharpshooter) and why spirit heroes rise.
For ranked players (around Ascendant+). Early 2025; the game has changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Bebop's Hook tier 1 went from 30% to 25% more damage, which doesn't make him less lethal: hook plus uppercut still one-shots you most of the time if he is in position. [0:00]
- Exploding Uppercut tier 2 went from 60% to 50% weapon damage, which alone barely matters, though with the gun nerfs it might; Sticky Bomb damage was also reduced a bit. Not big nerfs, Bebop is still very playable. [0:30]
- Calico's bullet damage went up 10% (2 to 2.2), Leaping Slash radius increased so its second hit is easier to land, and its tier 2 damage went from +50 to +75, so level Leaping Slash to tier 2 early. [1:01]
- Calico's Ava now destroys breakables while you run, so you can farm boxes in cat form, and its movement speed went up to 35%; he still levels Ava last. [1:31]
- Holliday's changes are overall buffs: Crackshot now triggers on units at half cooldown, scales better with spirit and works on creeps so you can farm with it, a buff to gun Holliday; Crackshot's Slowing Hex anti-teleport debuff was nerfed; lasso duration increased; and a Bounce Pad height bug that stopped damage on lassoed heroes was fixed. [2:03]
- Holliday is a high skill ceiling hero who was already very good and will be insane once people learn her. [3:04]
- Kelvin got 50 free health; he was already good and will be insane now because spirit will be better. [3:04]
- Mirage got a huge nerf: Scarab lifesteal reduced by 25 points (about 33%); Fire Scarabs' max health spirit scaling went up, helping hybrid or spirit builds, a change meant to push people off gun. [3:04]
- Shiv's bullet damage and Slice and Dice base and tier 2 damage were nerfed, but Slice and Dice spirit scaling increased, so with enough spirit items it does more overall; he is just less dangerous early in lane. Bloodletting now deals its full damage over 12 seconds instead of 30 and its cooldown went from 50 to 60. He thinks Shiv may be even better than before, possibly the best hero in the game outside the new ones. [3:38]
- Sinclair got hard nerfs but is still OP: rabbit-hexed targets move faster, Rabbit Hex no longer applies through Unstoppable, it triggers Reactive Barrier (buy it to counter the hex a bit), Debuff Remover now works on it, and teleporting now drops the urn (reportedly still bugged). [4:40]
- Sinclair's Vexing Bolt base damage went from 120 to 110 (still insane damage) and Spectral Assistant's base cooldown went from 30 to 40 seconds with a shorter duration. [5:41]
- Sinclair's one-shot combo still works: walk into the enemy, hex them, place Spectral Assistant and nuke them with Vexing Bolt. His ult copies any enemy ult, technically the best ult in the game, and with gun nerfed he is probably the most broken hero in the game. [6:11]
- Vindicta was nerfed through the gun damage nerf (she relies on her gun), plus slower movement speed and bullet velocity. [6:42]
- Vyper's base damage went from 9 to 7 (6.3 in game), about a 33% nerf plus the 10% global gun nerf, along with movement speed, Lethal Venom damage and the tier 3 slide spirit and bullet resist. He thinks she is C tier at best. [6:42]
- Wraith was a solid high rank hero running gun with Full Auto; the card nerfs (lower scaling, higher cooldown, slower card summoning from non-heroes) make her completely horrible in lane and leave less cards for farming and fights. He thinks these were the wrong nerfs: cards were not one-shotting in lane, her strength was gun damage and the ult combo. [8:15] [10:50]
- Wraith's Telekinesis was buffed: about 100 ms faster cast, silence half a second longer and the lift lasts 0.25 seconds longer as a full stun, so you can steal mid boss more easily with it. [9:16]
- Wraith is mid tier now: she needed to come out of lane at least even to snowball, and she will most likely lose most lanes. [10:19]
- Yamato's bullet damage was reduced. [11:21]
- The big change: every hero's gun damage was cut another 10%, so heroes that use their gun less are better. Pocket wins (no changes but his damage is spirit burst), Kelvin's strength is spirit and tankiness, and heroes that shoot nonstop are a lot weaker. [11:51]
- Extra Stamina was nerfed but is still the best 500 soul item in the game; he will still buy it on all his heroes. [12:52]
- Alchemical Fire was nerfed: -50% effectiveness against non-heroes (was -40%), weaker against objectives and creeps, and its weapon damage in the radius was reduced. Still great for spirit builds, but no longer something everyone in the lobby should buy. [12:52]
- Hunter's Aura bullet resist reduction dropped by about 3 points (27 to 24 when alone) but its fire rate reduction went up, so fights just last a bit longer; it is not really a nerf and is now arguably the best gun item, since raw gun damage items are weaker. [13:23]
- Tesla Bullets and anything that does spirit damage are better now. [14:24]
- Sharpshooter's 10% nerf is big, so don't run it on every hero; it is still great on long range heroes like Grey Talon, Vindicta, Mirage and probably Holliday. [14:24]
- Holliday's gun has basically no falloff, about the same as Vindicta's, so she plays like a sniper. [14:54]
- A 10% base gun damage cut also shrinks every gun damage item, like every hero starting with a negative Basic Magazine; the meta will move drastically toward spirit. [16:25]
- The list is for ladder around high rank (roughly Ascendant+), not pro play, but the concepts apply to any rank. [18:30]
- Abrams is low A: his spirit scaling was buffed over recent patches, he is the best hero to steal mid boss (his ult guarantees a punch or parry on the Rejuvenator), and he runs melee items that the gun nerf doesn't touch. [17:26]
- Bebop is A tier for ladder: he was very strong in pro play and a hook still gives a free kill; hook plus uppercut still kills instantly and his spirit barely got nerfed. [19:00]
- Dynamo is high B: heroes that mostly cast are better now, his Singularity ult is still the best ultimate in the game, and gun Dynamo is a troll build. [20:03]
- Grey Talon is very solid now, nearly A, the best sniper for a spirit meta: spirit Grey Talon is far better than spirit Vindicta and the biggest safe-distance spirit nuke in the game. He was later moved to B to keep the list balanced. [20:34] [42:04]
- Haze is C: she does more spirit damage now and Fixation (spirit damage driven by fire rate, not base gun damage) holds up, but she is still first a gun hero; she was D tier before and is only a bit better. [21:04]
- Infernus is low S: most of his damage comes from Afterburn and his ult, so the gun nerf doesn't matter, and few heroes can do the damage-and-heal carry job better. [21:34]
- Ivy is S: the best support besides Kelvin, benefits from spirit, and her strength is her ult saving teammates and initiating; her late game is a bit weaker, her early game a lot stronger. [22:35]
- Kelvin is insane: 50 HP is almost a 10% HP buff, making him very tanky in lane on top of his base spirit shield, and he scales well with spirit as the best healer and support. If you like playing support, play Kelvin and you'll win. [23:05]
- Lady Geist will be meta: even with Frenzy gun builds she was always a spirit spam hero, so the gun nerf hurts her less than Wraith or Warden; not quite as strong as Infernus. [23:36]
- Lash is A tier: his strength is his ult, ground slam and initiation (throwing enemies into Walkers), not his gun. Gun damage was one of his biggest counters because gunners could shoot him in the air, and few spirit abilities can reach him there. [24:06]
- Sinclair will be the best hero in the game; people play him wrong. Learn to land Vexing Bolt, and you can't punish him: smart players leave Spectral Assistant far back to bait cooldowns and teleport out, and you can't tell whether a copy is out because it has infinite range. [25:06] [26:41]
- Rabbit Hex is about a 3 second disable at max level on a normal ability, and his ult copies whatever you need (Warden's ult for life, Dynamo's ult to Singularity the enemy Dynamo). He needs a lot of nerfs. [27:11]
- Vyper is one of the weakest heroes now, kept just below Haze: her damage was cut by over 33%, her burst is gone and her slide gives less tankiness. Her gun has the highest damage in the game but is one of the least accurate and reloads a lot, and her spirit damage is weak since her 2 only does damage on low enemies. Weak, not unplayable. [28:12]
- McGinnis depends on the player: gun McGinnis is dead, but turrets are better with the gun nerf; a solid support that is great at split pushing, not S tier. [29:45]
- Lady Geist goes to A tier and Mo & Krill to S: Mo & Krill got buffed, is already strong in pro play getting a kill about every minute off his cooldown, and cares about disabling, not gun damage. [30:15] [30:47]
- Paradox is S: she already did a lot of spirit damage and her strength is swapping someone for a free kill; she was already OP. High skill ceiling. [30:47]
- Pocket is better than before but high B, not meta again; before this patch he would have been C. [31:17]
- Tournament meta: Kelvin, Infernus, Mo & Krill and Ivy are almost always picked, Paradox always banned, Lady Geist often picked. [32:19]
- Seven is C+, lower middle of the pack: before this patch he was the worst hero in the game, but with guns weaker he takes less damage in lane (he has the biggest head hitbox in the game and gets perma headshot) and he is built around spirit. [32:49]
- Vindicta drops from hard meta to high middle of the pack: slower movement, lower bullet velocity so less accurate at long range, and gun builds nerfed. Spirit Vindicta is good but not as good as spirit Grey Talon; top Vindicta players ran gun items like Sharpshooter and Long Range. [33:50]
- Warden is B, one of the hardest hit: gun Warden had already fallen out of the meta because gun Shiv did his job better, and with the gun nerf gun Warden is weak. The way to play him now is spirit with Majestic Leap and Arcane Surge, playing around his ult; not OP. [35:24]
- Wraith is bad: a terrible laner after more card nerfs, nobody in pro or high ranked play used cards after lane, and now she must run gun in a spirit meta, similar to Haze. [36:54]
- On Wraith, diversify damage with spirit on your gun, not cards: Surge of Power used on Telekinesis (her 3), and Tesla Bullets, which scale with spirit and get even better. [38:26]
- Yamato is easy A and might become S: she was always a strong spirit hero (refresher, permanent slash and ult cooldown builds) but her broken gun meant nobody ran spirit; spirit Yamato wasn't nerfed. She will be a bit weaker in lane from the base gun nerf. [38:56]
- Mirage is a lot weaker, the hardest hit hero: Scarabs, nerfed by 33%, were what made him strong in lane and mid game, and his gun carry potential dropped. Spirit burst builds exist but people avoid taking eight or twelve Djinn's Mark stacks. He drops from S to middle of the pack at best (B). [40:00] [45:41]
- Shiv is S: knife bleed builds were already almost as good as the unkillable gun builds, and the Slice and Dice dash does more in spirit builds, making spirit Shiv better than before and harder to play. [40:31]
- Viscous is top of A: his strength is his ult and Puddle Punch, not his gun, so he should benefit a bit. [42:35]
- Holliday is S, maybe the best laner in the game now, ahead of Mirage: one headshot does 300 damage, Powder Keg barrels can perma-stun enemies in the air, she has insane movement and a stun on her 2. She is the best 1v1 laner with good aim and will grow stronger as people learn her. [43:05]
- Calico is low mid even after buffs, by far the worst of the new heroes: a frontline brawler, tank and disruptor who initiates, survives a long time (especially with Ethereal Shift) and gets value baiting cooldowns with her ult, but does little damage alone. If you think she is OP you are probably a noob. [44:08]
- Pro tip against Calico: run into melee range and she cannot hit you with Leaping Slash, her 2 is still too inconsistent. [45:41]
- The two biggest changes this patch: Mirage from best hero to middle of the pack at best, and Vyper from among the best to the worst. [46:11]
- Expect more spirit builds on heroes that ran gun before: Shiv, Lady Geist, Yamato, Mirage, Vindicta, Grey Talon and maybe a spirit sustain Abrams. [47:12]
- The new heroes still need a lot of balancing: Sinclair and Holliday are broken, Calico needs buffs and Vyper is hard to balance because she relies on her gun. [47:42]
- In low ranks, gun heroes like Haze and Seven might be a bit better because enemies move worse and are easier to hit. [48:43]
