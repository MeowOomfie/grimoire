# Deadlock's Items are ready for release - Initial Patch Breakdown
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-05-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NNevOpe1lF0

## Covers
Deathy's first-day big picture take on the May 2025 item overhaul (patch of 2025-05-08): universal slots, the soul-investment bonus system, stripped-down item stats, hidden healing, gun and health changes, and what they mean for time to kill. For any player rethinking builds in that patch; no builds given on purpose, numbers will go stale.

## Claims
- This is arguably the biggest update in Deadlock's history, changing more fundamentals than the switch from four lanes to three; many changes aren't in the patch notes. [0:00]
- The item system was completely reworked with new art, animations and sounds for nearly every item. [1:00]
- Slots are no longer dedicated to a category; every slot is universal. [1:00]
- Four slots were removed: you start with eight slots and can unlock up to four extra slots (no longer called flex slots), 12 total versus 16 before. [1:30]
- With universal slots you can build fully into one category, for example a full damage Lash with eight orange items, full spirit, or full tank, or any combination. [1:30]
- Before, a spirit build still had four orange slots to fill, so players bought items that didn't fit the build's purpose just because filling slots was efficient. [2:02]
- Category bonuses now come from souls spent in that category, not from item count or tier; his orange items showed +88% weapon damage. [2:33]
- Spending 800 on weapon items gives 6% weapon damage and 1,600 gives 8%, so two 800 items now give 8% total where two old tier ones gave 12%. [3:03]
- This is a large nerf to filling your slots with cheap items; filling slots got much less efficient. [3:33]
- The only advantage left to cheap items is that they often have more value per soul, and even that isn't always true: Rapid Rounds gives 10% fire rate and Swift Striker, at twice the cost, gives 20%, so there's no value reason not to upgrade. [3:33]
- The whole building philosophy changed; expect many builds to be completely overhauled. [4:04]
- There are roughly 30 to 40 new items, about 10 per category; new weapon items include Backstabber, Opening Rounds, Split Shot, Blood Tribute, Cultist Sacrifice, Express Shot, and Spirit Rend. [4:04]
- Nearly every item lost a lot of stats: Alchemical Fire used to give about 225 bullet shield, 14% weapon damage, 8 spirit power and 1 m/s sprint speed, and now gives only 8 spirit power. [5:07]
- Most items now give only one stat (some, like Escalating Resilience, give several), which makes items more intuitive; old oddities like Extra Charge giving weapon damage are gone. [5:37]
- Every hero's power is reduced: fewer slots, fewer stats per item, and the first items are 800s that are worse than the old 500s. [6:07]
- Every hero with a healing ability had its healing nerfed, often outside the patch notes: Lash's Flog heals 50% versus heroes (he thinks about 65% before). [6:38]
- Abrams's Infernal Resilience is now 10%; Ivy's Stone Form is 6% max health (about 8% before) and 19% maxed (about 25% before). [7:10]
- Kelvin's grenade healing was almost certainly nerfed and his dome possibly; Mirage's base Scarabs heal about 40 versus about 60 before. [7:10]
- Most heroes' guns were nerfed; Bebop's gun was nerfed by something like 25 to 30%. [7:41]
- Weaker heroes mean a higher average time to kill. [8:13]
- Heroes have more health, another unlisted change: health per boon roughly doubled for most heroes. Lash gains 81 per boon (probably about 40 before), Calico 69, Abrams 81, Vindicta 47, which would have been the highest boon scaling last patch. [8:45]
- More health, weaker heroes and fewer slots combine into a higher average time to kill, which he thinks is great because the previous patch's time to kill was extremely short. [9:16]
- You can still die fast because people can build concentrated glass cannon damage of one type earlier, since they don't need flex slots to buy six weapon items. [9:46]
- He gives no builds on purpose: nobody, not even top players, knows what is optimal yet, so use the weekend to test your own theories. [10:16]
- Pick a direction for your build: support, spirit nuker, tank (prioritize green items), or full glass cannon (orange items). [10:48]
- Don't try too many new items in one game or you'll be overwhelmed; build each game around one new item, for example Cultist Sacrifice one game and Vortex Web (which he calls really sick) the next. [11:18]
- There were also map, power-up and combat mechanic changes; he will cover their meta effects in a later video. [11:48]
