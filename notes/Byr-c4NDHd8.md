## Covers
Midknight Vods free solo-queue coaching stream from January 7, 2026, recorded before the next patch. Four students: a Shiv (Emissary) laning into Mina, a Mina (Ritualist) laning with a Seven duo, a Billy (Eternus, E6 lobby) and a Paradox (Archon) laning with Lady Geist into Sinclair and Haze.
Between games he reviews a Paige build and a spirit Calico build from chat and names recommended Shiv, Mina and Calico build creators.
Themes: prioritizing waves in lane, weaving gun shots into combos, light melee and parry conditioning, overchasing and coin-flip fights, running the Urn, invading with the right teammate, Cultist timing and green spikes.
