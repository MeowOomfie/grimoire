## Covers
Lady Geist coaching review by Midknight of an Ascendant Lady Geist laning with Pocket into Warden and Viscous, in a coordinated (team) environment, video dated 2025-12-02 (late November 2025 patch with the new item investment spikes and walker flat damage resist before 15 minutes).
Themes: taking space and bridge control, shooting the wave, healing items, Headhunter and spirit spikes, side laning, soul awareness (not fighting on a big bag), Urn calls, and Warden cage, and countering Viper and Paige.

## Claims
Lady Geist (Ascendant):
- Focus the wave, not the Warden, early in lane. [0:30]
- When both enemy laners fold back to buy, take the space on top of the bridge and harass. [0:30]
- You are playing below the bridge too much; Lady Geist has a lot of harass from the top of the bridge. [1:00]
- A lot of Lady Geist players ego and don't buy healing; you 100% need to buy healing when you are low and have no boost to reset. [1:30]
- At 180 HP keep shooting the wave instead of giving the enemy prio to walk up on your tower, where they get damage and denies. [2:00]
- Viscous likes to cube liberally, so space out your bomb and hold it until the cube is used. [2:00]
- In his opinion you should buy Mystic Burst every game unless your other laner has mega wave clear. [2:32]
- Lady Geist only has early Mystic Regen for sustain and no Restorative Shot, which she loves, so this build is pressed for regen. [4:32]
- Wave clear matters because bridge control is super important in this lane. [4:32]
- Headhunter feels great on Lady Geist: it is an A tier item that gives healing and damage. Malice does not amp Headhunter's headshot damage. [5:02]
- If 50% of your shots are headshots, buy Headhunter. [26:22]
- Against this lineup, Lady Geist likes Healbane, Counterspell, and any of the resilience items. [3:32]
- You're a little greedy with your light melees; fine while you are not getting parried, and it conditions them. [3:32]
- Never sac the tower, especially mid. [4:02]
- Kinetic Dash is good; buying it gets you your spikes. [8:07]
- Go Healbane at tier two spirit because the spirit spike is decent; BRS is strong if you go gun. [13:14]
- The meta is to beeline every investment spike; you could modify a build to beeline spikes sooner. [15:31]
- With spirit spikes the early BRS would feel great: about +16 spirit power and a multiplicative 7% damage, an item you should slot in. [35:41]
- Healing plus Spirit Resilience is fine for the spirit spike. [18:03]
- Learning air strafing and edge boosting makes life a lot easier. [6:34]
- Running down that ravine, diagonally air dash into the wall, slide against the wall with that momentum, then edge boost off the corner; it is required there. [28:24]
- Whenever you are in a Warden cage, spam crouch so Seven cannot farm headshots on you. [30:24]
- Against Viper, buy regenerative items and stay above 50% HP; her Venom and bola are allowed to be overtuned because she does little else. [32:02]
- Against Paige there is no real counter besides hard focusing her when she mispositions and playing around her barrier cooldown. [32:02]

Waves, space and souls:
- These enemy box routes give a ton of space; you need to be on top of the bridge. [1:00]
- Joining a fight with low kill potential costs your tower; one pick is not worth a guardian. [3:02]
- Even after the nerf to 8% bonus for two players in a lane, 8% of two waves is not insignificant; when two farm-hungry heroes are near one lane, still split two into it. [7:07]
- Don't take fights while you are weak and missing your investment spikes; get to shop. [8:07]
- If the enemy puts four in one lane to trade a walker at 11 minutes, don't reset to it; shove your wave and rotate to take a second guardian. [9:09]
- Pre-15 minutes, with flat damage resist on walkers, hitting walker is not worth giving the Urn. Post-15 walkers are siegeable again. [10:09]
- Hit the wave before farming a camp while waves are crashing; the camp is not going anywhere and the wave is. [11:11]
- When you have created pressure and only one enemy is on that side of the map, steal their Sinner's Sacrifice. [11:42]
- Farming pattern: shove the wave, grab the sinners, fall back with the wave as it pushes back and farm your own, shove again, then angle toward a shop. [12:12]
- Two souls off both spikes means you don't actually win the 1v1; get to shop first. [12:43]
- In a coordinated environment, grab the Urn off spawn, especially with Dynamo ult and a small advantage. [13:51]
- Side lanes later: you on one side, your other farmer on the other; he prefers you weak side and Infernus on strong side. [14:52]
- Don't chase into their base with no info and three negative waves; with all the farm on the left and them running Urn, assume left is their strong side. [16:01]
- Overfighting and overchasing on the left side directly cost 80% of your walker HP. [17:32]
- Crashing waves and then eating your own jungle is perfect. [18:35]
- Buy instead of aggressively chasing; take the sinners and go straight to the secret shop. [19:08]
- Do not fight on a big bag: you cannot be sitting on 6k souls and a tier four item for an Urn fight. Beeline a camp, go to base and buy. [19:38]
- When your goal is to buy, path toward the side with the secret shop. [21:12]
- Against Paradox's ult, taking a more passive route instead of the rope is better, because on the rope you would just get ulted down. [23:17]
- When you are very fed, you should be at the Urn fight and let a less fed teammate farm the weak side. [24:19]
- Pressuring with the team and taking the left walker when the enemies show right is a good call. [24:50]
- When you are down a fight, hold in base; he would grab the Urn right then because the enemy has a really good window to run it. [25:20]
- Mid is the right call, but with a 40 second window you can take the mid walker in 15 to 20 seconds first; in solo queue just call mid, in coordinated play squeeze the walker out. [26:22]
- He would have parried there; you were way too far from the pit. [26:53]
- Someone should peel back and run the Urn when it is uncontested and you have the HP. [29:55]
- Running Urn takes the comeback Urn off the table, which is huge value; they got a comeback Urn off wiping you. [31:28]
- Prioritize front line camps over backline camps. [31:28]
- Never try to get picks on mid lane for a team fight; get picks on side lanes. [33:37]
- Rejuvenator starts ticking down off the first melee on it, so in theory you can start meleeing and wait. [34:39]
- Late, hit the patron instead of padding stats on enemies. [35:09]

Summary notes:
- Play around space and bridge control; when the enemy backs up and offers space, take it. Bridge control is valuable on Lady Geist and valuable to deny from Viscous. [35:41]
- Always prioritize shooting the wave over shooting enemies or shooting nothing. [35:41]
- You should be the one side laning, not your Kelvin. [36:11]
- Work on soul awareness: do not engage on a big bag, play to buy. [36:41]
- When you are winning, put one person on the Urn early instead of all getting wiped in their base. [36:41]
