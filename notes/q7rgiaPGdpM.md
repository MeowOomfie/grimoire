# Lash is still OP - Updated Lash Build showcase
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-10-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q7rgiaPGdpM

## Covers
Deathy walks through his "brawling Lash" build on a scrim replay: Flog-max sustain build, lane plan, item order, mid game roaming, late game frontlining, and the Warp Stone answer to Paradox swaps. October 2024 patch era, a much older game (old items like Bullet Resist Shredder, Healing Rite, the 6200 tier). For Lash players who want a sustain fighter build instead of Majestic Leap.

## Claims
- The build is published on Mobalytics as "Deathy Brawling Lash". [0:00]
- Lash is usually better in duo lanes; Deathy laned solo here as a personal preference. [0:31]
- The most important thing in Lash's lane is using Flog as often as you can to out-sustain your opponent; trade a decent amount, not too much. [1:01]
- In lane prioritize buying gun items on Lash, because his gun is very good early and a couple of gun items get you early kills. [1:01]
- First item was Restorative Shot because he was at half health and wanted sustain; with a better start he would buy Headshot Booster first. [1:31]
- Use Flog as soon as it comes off cooldown, but don't force it. [2:02]
- Headshot Booster is the standard next item: a decent burst of damage and harass; shoot the enemy at every chance, the 130 damage pokes add up to a kill. [2:02]
- When the enemy uses Healing Rite, dive them to cancel it; cancelling it is a really big deal. [2:33]
- If you are winning a lane hard, expect ganks, because you are always playing on the enemy side. [3:03]
- After the first three items you have options: Extra Charge for two grapple charges, easiest when you want to be active or get a lane kill, or Mystic Burst for extra kill damage. He went Extra Charge then Mystic Burst. [3:35]
- When an enemy goes aggressive on Lash on your side of the bridge, you can almost always kill them with your ultimate (Death Slam); catching someone there with the ult means they are dead 99% of the time. [4:06]
- After winning lane, pressure the tower; once the tower is dead you are free to roam the map for kills. [4:38]
- Once you take the tower, leave; staying greedy for more was a mistake when the enemies rotated. [5:08]
- Once your lane is basically done, buy Sprint Boots to move around the map. [5:08]
- Getting active is good, but don't forget your own lane entirely; leaving it cost them their tower. [5:39]
- After a good engagement, go back to your side of the map rather than forcing a tower with four people in one lane, which wastes souls when you are not far ahead. [6:09]
- Next item is usually Slowing Hex; he does not buy the green (vitality) items early. [6:09]
- Lash's first items are ones that let him get active and kill: Slowing Hex, Sprint Boots and Mystic Shot. Slowing Hex gives 1 m/s sprint speed and Sprint Boots gives 2 m/s. [6:40]
- The play to look for as Lash is getting on a rooftop behind enemies to gank them. [6:40]
- When you see a 4v2 at a tower, leave and give up the tower instead of being slow about it. [7:11]
- After the early kill items, the next items are mostly green: Healing Booster, Spirit Armor, then Bullet Armor and other tankiness items. [8:11]
- The Majestic Leap Lash build is good for one-shotting people with Ground Strike but is not consistent and is not good at prolonged fights; this build revolves around staying in fights a long time sustaining through Flog. [8:11]
- Ability order: max Flog first, no points in anything else before Flog is maxed, because Flog gives so much sustain and healing early. [8:41]
- With Healing Booster, Flog heals for 100% of its damage dealt to heroes; it does about 200 damage, so hitting two or three heroes heals 400 to 600 health instantly early on, about a full charged Restorative Locket on a 12 second cooldown. [8:41]
- Quicksilver Reload adds 65 damage to Flog (220 becomes 285 when it procs), and the bonus also applies to the healing. [9:13]
- Lash's power spike in this build is when you have your armors and Improved Burst. [9:44]
- The build is spirit focused more than gun focused, but gun items still go in because Lash's gun is very good; Headhunter is great value and fits since you already buy Headshot Booster. [10:15]
- With a flex slot open he bought Bullet Resist Shredder: some bullet resist, some health, and Lash always applies spirit damage; since armors are so prevalent, shred is important, and it is good on any hero that can apply it consistently. [10:15]
- Lash can farm two or three jungle camps but it is painful; he farms far slower than Wraith, Infernus, McGinnis or even Ivy. [10:45]
- Don't risk dropping a 500 soul bag to finish one more creep when an enemy is walking forward. [11:15]
- In the mid game Lash is really good at moving around the map: he is one of the few heroes with base sprint speed, so with just Sprint Boots and Slowing Hex he is very fast. [11:15]
- With Quicksilver Reload and max Flog, Flog pretty much one-shots creep waves. [11:46]
- If an enemy is nearby, go for the denies. [11:46]
- Lash has really good solo kill potential with this build because Flog damage is so high; use Slowing Hex on the target (he missed doing so). [12:17]
- Don't grab a small soul bag when coordinated enemies may be nearby; it is not worth the risk. [12:47]
- Next item: Headhunter, or Improved Burst instead; both are good in different situations. Headhunter gives nice healing, nice stats and a huge burst of damage. [12:47]
- Metal Skin on an enemy (here Warden) makes him very hard for Lash to finish; that is good itemization against Lash. [13:18]
- Even though Lash is active, don't show up to every fight; keep getting your own farm and pressuring your own waves. [13:48]
- When ahead on Lash it is fine to fight for solo kills (he was 20k vs a 13k Mirage), which creates space for your team. [13:48]
- Fighting is not always about showing up where your team is; create your own favorable fights, for example hide on rooftops, wait for enemies to go for a wave, then jump on them with all abilities. The enemy team responding to that kill creates space for your team. [14:18]
- After the other core items buy Improved Burst (for the damage) and Warp Stone (damage, resist, spirit power and a really good active); you are never unhappy having Warp Stone on Lash. [14:48]
- Paradox counters Lash because of the swap; it is really hard to get an ultimate off against her. [15:18]
- Wave management is like personal finances: if your creep waves are not dealt with or pushed, you can't go make plays. [15:49]
- Paradox's pick combo is Kinetic Carbine (stops you in time) into Paradoxical Swap. Counter it by using Warp Stone as soon as you are hit by Carbine; most Paradoxes swap right away at the same crosshair position, so they miss. [15:49]
- If you keep getting swapped by Paradox, buy Warp Stone and save it for the moment you get Carbined; items still work while you are stopped in time, and Ethereal Shift works the same way. [16:20]
- First 6200 item depends on the game: Diviner's Kevlar if you want to rely on your ultimate more, Soul Rebirth for a second life (for example if you die to curses or swaps), Mystic Reverb for more damage. [16:50]
- He chose Colossus and loves it in a resistance heavy meta: 600 bonus health plus 20% base health (about 700 to 800 health in practice), and a 7 second active with 40% spirit resist, 40% bullet resist and a 35% movement slow on nearby enemies, plus weapon and melee damage. [17:21]
- Colossus fits this Lash because you are in the fight tanking a lot while healing with Flog; cutting the damage taken makes you feel almost immortal. [17:51]
- Pick up the bridge buffs: the survival buff gave an extra 400 health (3,200 total with Colossus) and percentage health regen. [18:22]
- When you are ahead (20k before anyone died) and got the Guardians you came for, don't overstay in the enemy base; leave, farm the whole map, extend the lead and fight if they come out. [20:25]
- Late game, Lash is good at punishing out-of-position players and getting in and out of fights. Against a Paradox charging her shot, hide until she commits, then jump on her. [20:56]
- Don't fully commit right away; play slightly off to the side, regen, and go in and out of the fight sustaining with Flog. [21:26]
- Healing from Headhunter is amplified by Healing Booster, healing you for 10% of max health per headshot, which makes Colossus even more value. [21:26]
- Next 6200 item: Soul Rebirth. Lash is really good with it because he respawns at 50% health and hitting a couple of heroes with Flog usually gets him back to full. [21:56]
- A big strength of this build is buying time: being very hard to kill and coming back with Soul Rebirth stops the enemy from taking Mid-Boss. [22:59]
- With this build you are a frontliner: incredibly hard to kill with good damage from Flog; Majestic Leap builds don't help in these situations. [23:29]
- Result: 58,000 damage with mostly green items; Flog did 10,000, gun about 20,000, Improved Burst 7,000, Ground Strike 6,000, roughly 50% spirit and 50% gun. [24:38]
