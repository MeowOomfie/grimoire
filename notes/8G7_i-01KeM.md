## Covers
Midknight Vods does one quick coaching session, then a Deadlock Night Shift (EU split) watch party with Pure Content and Jaxi, the week the new heroes came out (patch of February 2026, before Apollo).
Coached: an Ascendant Lash in a Lash and Paige lane into Silver and Haze, whose main problems are map awareness, overbrawling while far behind, and missing the game-deciding Urn and mid boss fights.
The watch party is mostly downtime and banter, but it has draft talk (roles, Silver and Celeste bans, Mo & Krill into Silver), build reviews (Vyper, Yamato, Lash, Calico, Grey Talon, Celeste, Infernus, Vindicta), roaming and Urn notes, and item verdicts (Counterspell, Slowing Bullets, bullet resist items, Heroic Aura).

- A roamer's income comes from boxes; learning box routes leads to roaming. [63:59]
