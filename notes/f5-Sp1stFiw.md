## Covers
Midknight reviews one Emissary-rank Paradox game, solo lane against a Mo & Krill, for a player new to MOBAs.
Free coaching review over a replay. Recorded December 2024, on a late-2024 patch; the game has changed a lot since, so check items, numbers, soul-sharing rules and rank names against the current patch.
Themes: punishing ability cooldowns in lane, treating the Paradox ult as a normal ability, anti-heal (Decay), filling item slots, leaving your lane after laning stage, picks with Paradoxical Swap, and trading objectives.

## Claims
Player 1 (Paradox, Emissary):
- Against Mo & Krill, be ready to parry; after a double melee he will probably look for a third. [1:02]
- Keep shooting while you drop back; there is no downside to holding left click as you run. [1:02]
- Missing minions on the first waves puts you very behind. [1:02]
- Against a hyper-aggressive laner who misses a lot of minions, play passive and make him dive you under tower; he will kill himself out of impatience. [1:32]
- Take the small camp early; it is good for your rank. [1:32]
- Shoot in between melees on camps; holding left click while meleeing gets a gun burst between each melee and kills the camp faster. [1:32]
- At 1,000 souls and near shop, take three seconds to buy a couple of tier one items. [2:02]
- Don't place Time Wall pointlessly; save it for when he goes in on you or you go in on him. [2:33]
- Mystic Shot and Hollow Point Ward were a good buy, but don't sit on 1,800 souls by the second shop. [2:33]
- Mo & Krill is very vulnerable after he throws Sand Blast: about a 40 second base cooldown. Scorn is about 15 seconds and Burrow about 30. Chase him after he wastes Sand Blast. [3:04]
- Do a box run when the wave allows. [3:04]
- Don't tank the wave; back up a few meters behind your tower instead of taking damage and wasting Time Wall. [3:35]
- Paradox's ult has a low cooldown compared to Mo & Krill's, so play under your tower and look to ult him into the tower when he overextends. [3:35]
- High Velocity Mag and Extra Health as two tier ones at 1.2k was a good buy. [4:05]
- Against a hyper-aggressive laner who does nothing with lane pressure (no roams, no camps), let him push the wave and hold it under your Guardian, then ult him under tower when he steps up. Freezing the wave is possible but more complex than expected at Emissary. [4:05]
- When the enemy has used his whole kit including ult, you don't need to be scared even at half HP; trade back and punish with your ult. [4:36]
- Your ult cooldown (about 35 seconds) is lower than his Sand Blast cooldown, so think of it as a normal ability, not a precious ult. [4:36]
- You don't have to hit Kinetic Carbine before using the ult; it helps, but if Carbine is on cooldown and you have ult, just throw it. [5:07]
- Against a lot of healing, buy anti-heal; at 1.25k you could buy Healbane, especially against Krill. [5:07]
- Slowing Hex is fine on Paradox, but anti-heal was the better buy here. [5:39]
- A Time Wall that forces the enemy to take a long route is good. [5:39]
- Don't overchase after winning a trade, and don't tank the wave afterward. [6:10]
- An over-aggressive player will keep pushing in bad situations; note it and keep killing him for it. [6:10]
- Hold the ult a moment when an enemy dives your team: the second she starts backing off, ult her to keep her in your team longer. [6:41]
- After your Guardian falls, grab the medium camps nearby and wait for the aggressive laner to push the wave back to you. [7:11]
- Take an extra half second to line up a shot rather than forcing a quick one. [7:41]
- Deny souls sooner. [7:41]
- Sitting on lots of souls is fine only when saving for a 3k item. [7:41]
- At 13 to 14 minutes with your Guardian dead, stop gravitating to your lane; have more impact on the map. Paradox is great at getting picks, so be with your team looking for picks. [7:41]
- Don't take a fight sitting on 4k unspent souls; you are weak until you shop. [8:12]
- Laning stage ends around 8 minutes. [8:42]
- When you are out of a fight, don't come back into it; walk to the bridge buff or reset to shop. [9:13]
- At around 16 minutes souls are no longer split, beyond about 20% extra for two people in a lane, so don't join a teammate in their lane; farm camps and grab bridge buffs instead. [9:44]
- After respawning, look at the minimap for bridge buffs and take a zipline to grab them. [10:15]
- Against heavy healing (Krill, Dynamo, Seven, Yamato, Viscus, Shiv), anti-heal is very strong. Viscus's Cube cleanses debuffs, but that is still early. [10:15]
- Correction: buy Decay, not Healbane, on Paradox; Decay goes crazy on her and puts in a lot of value against heavy-healing comps. [10:45]
- Grouping on a Guardian that is not a flex slot objective is not a big deal. [10:45]
- Mirage's ult teleport turns a 4v3 urn fight into a 5v3 because he can join it; call for the Mirage ult in game. [11:15]
- With an ult, it is better to wait until the target starts to run away and then swap him back in, rather than ulting him in place. [11:46]
- Use a two-kill advantage to take a walker. [12:17]
- Trading a Guardian for a walker is fine, and trading flex objectives evenly is good. [12:17]
- Someone needs to catch the split wave, or the enemy will come and take it. [12:47]
- If five of you show on one side, expect the enemy to push a side lane and try to take a walker. [13:17]
- Melee the bridge buff while you fight. [13:17]
- Self-cast Divine Barrier, especially for the movement speed. [13:47]
- After killing three of them, call mid boss. [13:47]
- With a player advantage, keep splitting a low walker; it is very back-doorable. [14:19]
- Don't waste your boost to rotate to a walker you can't save; only come back to catch the wave. [14:19]
- Grab the urn and run it in rather than chasing a Viscus. [14:49]
- When you are down a player, the fight is negative; run away. [15:19]
- Getting shot off a zipline is a risk when fleeing on one. [15:19]
- In a 5v6 while they tank mid boss, with Mirage's ult and his stun available, fight for it or at least try to steal. [15:51]
- If you are going to give mid boss, take something else on the map in return; waffling and catching one wave is not enough. [16:22]
- Emissary players and new MOBA players struggle to use actives; you used them well. [16:22]
- Pinging "fall back" because they have Rejuvenator is good if it means play around your walker, bad if it means refuse to interact with them at all. [16:53]
- In high rank, if four of you show on one side lane for 5 seconds with that wave pushed in, the enemy will instantly send two to take a walker. Showing that many on the map risks a side-objective trade. [17:23]
- When the enemy runs urn, expect all six of them to be there. [17:56]
- With a three-player advantage, push it: kill them and roll into their last walker. [18:27]
- Divine Barrier gives movement speed, so self-cast it to chase. [18:27]
- After you hit Kinetic Carbine, swap; Paradox is really good at catching people because of that. [18:27]
- Jump a lone enemy from the side with ult and let your McGinnis turret him to death. [19:59]
- Three open flex slots at 30 minutes is too many; buy. [19:59]
- Using Paradox to get picks with a teammate nearby is exactly how she should be played; do more of it. [20:30]
- When the enemy sends players deep into your base while two of theirs are dead, rotate with a teammate, kill them, and stagger their respawns. Punish their first mistake, not just their second. [20:30]
- Don't pass shop with 4k souls; buy a tier three and keep slots filled. [21:00]
- With an advantage, group and death ball, force a fight, roll it into mid boss and then end; splitting up risks them retaking a flex slot. [21:31]
- As long as the wave at your last walker is pushed out, you can keep looking for fights; once it starts pushing in, stop. [22:02]
- Base Guardians are the most useless objective in the game compared to walkers, Shrines or the Patron; don't leave the fight with your team just to stop one enemy taking them. [22:32]
- Chasing far gives the enemy time to respawn and contest your mid boss. [23:02]
- Summary, lane: use your ult far more; Mo & Krill's Sand Blast cooldown is longer than your ult, so throw it like a normal ability and try to ult him into your tower. [23:33]
- Summary, lane: punish enemies when their abilities are on cooldown; for about 40 seconds after Sand Blast, poke and trade more, then back off when it is back up. [24:03]
- Fill your item slots: tier one items give extra bonuses (weapon items about 7% weapon damage each) and the bonus per item falls off at higher tiers (about 18% for a 6k tier four), so tier ones are the most efficient. Fill open slots with tier ones or twos. [24:03]
- By 20 to 22 minutes all your slots should be filled, and you should not have open flex slots. [24:33]
- Don't autopilot to your lane after laning stage (around 8 minutes or when your first Guardian falls); go to other lanes and look for picks. [24:33]
- Paradox loves getting picks in the midgame with her ult, especially at lower ranks. [25:03]
- Move around the map with Divine Barrier or Kinetic Carbine, play around one or two teammates, swap a misplaced enemy into your team, kill them quickly, and press the advantage into an urn run, stealing their jungle, or a walker. [25:33]
- Do camps out of laning stage between waves; as Paradox, toss a Pulse Grenade on a camp and it basically kills it on its own. Route between camps like connect the dots. [26:03]
