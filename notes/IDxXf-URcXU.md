# Just Give Me 33 Minutes, You'll Out Farm Everyone | Deadlock Farming Guide
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-09-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IDxXf-URcXU

## Covers
A general farming guide for players who feel behind or unsure whether to farm or fight: simple box routes, the soul hierarchy (lane, Sinner's Sacrifice, camps, boxes), negative souls, solo kills and solo Walkers, when not to fight, the Mid Boss line rule, and backing less.
Also a timeline of the game (2:00 tier one camp, 3:00 boxes, 5:00 tier two camps and bridge buff, 8:00 Sinner's Sacrifice, Urn) and how much each objective matters (Guardian, Rift, Walker, base Guardians, Shrines, late game).

## Claims
- Keep box routes simple: grab boxes on the way whenever you run from place to place, and take routes you run into them on. [0:31]
- Think of box routes as big circles that route back to whatever lane you're in or wherever you want to be; don't try to memorize "optimal" routes from videos. [0:31]
- When you leave lane, run boxes and plan to be back at the zipline before the 30 second mark when the next wave spawns, then repeat. [1:33]
- The soul hierarchy: lane is number one, because minions give more souls than every other source. [1:33]
- Always try to get every wave, and when you go for waves push out more than one. [2:04]
- Second in the hierarchy is Sinner's Sacrifice, for the souls and the permanent golden statue buffs, which scale you in damage, health and cooldowns for the whole game; not getting them can lose you games on its own. [2:04]
- Third are camps (tier ones, tier twos, tier threes). [2:36]
- Never run boxes or camps while a lane wave is up; camps, boxes and Sinner's Sacrifice are a side note for the gaps between waves. [3:08]
- Exception: the boxes and statues right above the Mid Boss are on average better than a Sinner's Sacrifice. [3:08]
- The boxes above the Mid Boss don't spawn until 10 minutes, instead of the normal 3 minutes for boxes. [3:38]
- Always try to get the Mid Boss boxes when you can, but it is a big fight spot, so be careful of getting contested and dying there. [3:38]
- Solo kills, solo objectives and solo everything are the main soul injections; group or team kills don't give much. This is why a player with only one to three kills and objectives can have a 10k soul lead. [4:08]
- Killing someone also gives you extra souls because the dead player can't farm and you can steal their farm, boxes and Sinner's Sacrifice. [4:38]
- A solo Walker gives around 3,000 souls, like a free tier three item; don't leave your lane open (bad Rift timing, backing for an item) for a Wraith to take it alone. [5:09]
- As the farmer, look for as many solo kills and solo Walkers as possible. [5:39]
- "Negative souls": every time you die, or you're not fighting or doing something useful, you are losing souls. [6:40]
- Example: pushing two waves to the enemy Walker with the enemy team missing from the map, then walking all the way back in fear, wastes the time you could have spent on boxes, Sinner's Sacrifice or camps. [7:10]
- Dying costs 2x to 3x the souls when the enemy uses your 30 second death timer to clear a wave, push a wave into your Walker so its souls are lost, and take your camps and boxes: a 500 soul death becomes about 1,000. [7:40]
- A common VOD pattern: farming well until 12 minutes, then doing nothing from 12 to 16 minutes while looking for opportunities. [8:42]
- The core loop: kill two waves, get a box, camp or Sinner's Sacrifice, get back to the wave before or as it hits your Walker, repeat. [9:13]
- Avoid fights when you have a bag of unspent souls or an objective is coming up soon; many fights are avoidable. [9:44]
- If Mid Boss is in 30 seconds, try not to fight at all unless you know you can win. [9:44]
- With a bag of 500 to 2K souls, don't fight; spend it first and play very safe. [10:15]
- Imagine a line going left and right out of the Mid Boss symbol on the map; never cross it when you have a bag and no enemies on vision. [10:15]
- Enemy health bars showing at the top of the screen mean those enemies were seen recently; a missing health bar means they haven't been on vision recently, so play careful. [10:45]
- If more than three enemies are off vision, always stay on your side of the map, because they can all come out of the jungle and jump you. [10:45]
- Don't walk up to the enemy Walker with a 2K bag when nobody on the enemy team has been seen. [11:16]
- Don't push for an objective when your team has no pressure and no objectives on the map. [11:47]
- If a team fight is across the map and you don't have zip boost (for example you are green lane and it's in yellow), don't go; by the time you arrive it's over. Farm or take an objective instead. [11:47]
- Pushing out your wave during a fight elsewhere forces one enemy to leave the fight to defend their Walker, or else you get a free objective; it helps the fight without being there. [12:17]
- Team fights don't net many souls because souls are split among the players around; six people killing one Paige might give about 200 souls, the same as one camp. [12:47]
- Think of kills as objective openers and space creators, not souls: after a pick, push mid for an objective, or push a wave into the Walker and steal their camps while they handle minions. [13:18]
- Use the zipline (jump on and instantly off for momentum), jump pads and wall jumps to move faster while farming; don't walk everywhere. [13:48]
- When behind, don't push past the mid line; focus on farming your own side of the map. [14:19]
- When ahead, look for opening opportunities and deny souls: push waves into objectives or create pressure so someone must respond, then take other objectives. [14:19]
- Example: push a lane into a Walker and take camps; if they answer your Sinner's Sacrifice, your pushed wave is already damaging the Walker. [14:50]
- Think of play as conditioning the enemy, like fighting games; for example an Abrams who always quick melees can bait your parry and then heavy melee you. [15:21]
- When the next wave is coming, leave the camp unfinished and take the lane; it won't disappear, and 50 souls from a tier one or tier two camp isn't worth missing a wave or tower damage. [16:21]
- In lane phase, use cover on tier one and tier three camps: throw an ability, shoot around a corner or jiggle peek instead of losing 600 HP and then losing lane. [16:52]
- Stop backing to spawn so much; backing is negative souls (missed boxes and minions), and the heal creep heals a lot. [17:23]
- With lane priority and known enemy positions, use the mid shops: buy quickly and leave, grabbing boxes and maybe a camp on the way in and out; you come out about 600 souls ahead of backing to spawn. [18:24]
- Don't go to the mid shop without lane priority when enemies may be waiting there. [18:24]
- First 2 minutes: nothing but lane; grab the waves and move on. [20:56]
- At 2:00 the tier one camp spawns. Don't split its souls; give it to one person, ideally a hero good at getting kills, so they get ahead, get early picks and your lane snowballs. [21:27]
- 3:00 is where 90% of players lose lane phase: until then both sides have the same souls from waves and denies (and camps in mid lane), and boxes are what separates them. [21:58]
- Kill the wave first, always, then get boxes, and repeat; boxes must not cut into your wave. [22:28]
- Get comfortable with the wave timer instead of rushing back: a wave arrives every 30 seconds, and you have about an extra 10 seconds because the minions take time to die, especially if an enemy is in lane. [22:58]
- In a carry plus support lane, the support should leave lane to run a box route or two for one or two waves, funneling the lane souls into the carry. [22:58]
- Don't leave for more than two waves unless you're very good with box routes or see a near-certain gank; past that you fall too low on health and souls and get killed at your Guardian. [23:29]
- With some luck on boxes the support gets about as many souls as the laner, and the carry gets a much better start for the 20 to 30 minute game; supports gain less from items than carries or Vyper. [23:29]
- At 5:00 tier two camps and the bridge buff spawn. [24:30]
- Don't take tier two camps at 5:00 unless you are a carry like Seven or Wraith who can kill them; for most players they cost so much health that you miss waves or back and throw lane, especially without Healing Rite or Extra Regen. [25:00]
- At 8:00 the Sinner's Sacrifice spawns; always get yours as fast as possible, and steal the enemy's when you're ahead. [25:31]
- If you go for Sinner's Sacrifice without Healing Rite and you're not very fed, always get a teammate to hover near you and protect you, for single ones and especially for the double. [25:31]
- The Urn now gives souls only to the player who carries it, unless someone shoots them off. [26:32]
- If you're a carry, run every Urn off cooldown at every opportunity. [27:03]
- Guardian: it doesn't matter much, let them have it rather than dying for it and giving a free kill that wrecks your tempo; retreat to the Walker and farm. [27:03]
- Losing a Guardian at about 5 minutes is bad, but if you hold it past around 8 minutes you're fine to let it go; its main benefit is the safe shop. [27:34]
- The Rift is a force-a-fight tool to speed the game up. [27:34]
- If your team is behind on souls, always fight the Rift, because its resists are the only way besides souls to get back into the game. [28:05]
- At the Rift, don't over-prioritize winning the Rift itself or its souls and minions; prioritize getting kills, since dead enemies can't farm and it opens your Walker, Mid Boss and Guardian opportunities. [28:05]
- Walkers: get the solo kill every chance you get. [28:35]
- Dying for a Walker is only worth it if your team is hard soul locked, or you're a carry and your team is hard soul locked. [28:35]
- Don't stand in front of the Walker where it fireballs you and your minions; bait out the slam, then play under, behind, around and above it using cover, angles and rooftops. [29:05]
- Example: as Vyper in mid, keep sliding down the little staircase by the veil while shooting the Walker, then leave through the veil in the back. [29:36]
- Base Guardians don't really matter; you need them down to hit the Shrines and Patron, but almost never solo push them unless your team goes together. [30:07]
- Shrines are the one objective worth dying for: kill one or two whenever you can, it is positive souls. [30:37]
- Killing a Shrine makes that lane's enemy minions give less souls for the rest of the game; killing both makes all three lanes give them less, cutting their biggest soul supply. [30:37]
- After a Shrine falls your minions are stronger and always push into the enemy, giving you lane priority for the rest of the game unless they respond. [31:08]
- Late game there should be no objectives on the map: insta-kill waves, kill every camp and box as it comes up, and cycle waves while waiting for the Rift, the Urn or usually the Mid Boss. [31:39]
- Late game, don't donate bags: wait to buy your spike before fighting, for example finishing Ricochet after Split Shot on Vyper, then look for a fight. [32:09]
- A good lane start is extremely important, because all of this farming gets much harder if you lose lane phase. [32:41]
