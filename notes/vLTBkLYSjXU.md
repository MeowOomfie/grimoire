## Covers
Midknight Vods, a matchmaking coach who used to coach pro teams, reviews four replays on a free coaching stream: an Eternus 6 player learning Haze, an Archon Silver, an Archon Shiv, and a Holliday in a top-level EU lobby near Eternus 6.
The stream also has a live read of a small balance patch with his views on each change.
Recurring themes: respect vision, dead time and full resets, catching waves, bad fights with no advantage or objective, playing Rifts and comeback souls when behind, mid boss after won fights, parry and rebuttal tricks, and item choices.
