## Covers
Vindicta gameplay with commentary by aquatiq on an updated gun-leaning Vindicta build, in a comeback win.
Commentary over one game, no coaching. Recorded 2026-05-05, just after a patch that nerfed Infernus's Flame Dash and Viper.
Topics: skill order, gun item choices, denying, solo lane farm, and Vindicta's objective damage.

## Claims
Vindicta build and play:
- He uses an updated Vindicta build that changed with the recent patch. [1:02]
- High Velocity Rounds make denying easier because the shots land faster. [2:36]
- Double level the snipe (Assassinate) early; once it was double leveled it did good damage. [3:37]
- He took a Survival buff from a powerup and calls it really good. [4:41]
- Recharging Rush on Vindicta is okay, and he also buys Swift Striker. [7:13]
- He decides not to max the ultimate first because he is going gun this game, and maxes his 1 and then his 2 instead. [14:27]
- He cannot fight a Warden on this build because Warden's gun is too strong. [14:59]
- Counterspell is so broken you can counter almost anything with it. [16:30]
- Push the waves; stop giving the enemy free control of the map. [17:00]
- Soloing a side lane gave him free money; solo laning is strong because you get all the souls. [19:36]
- He skips more snipe damage because his snipe damage is already very high, and goes Fortitude instead. [20:10]
- He wants 6k items over cheaper fillers once he has the money. [22:18]
- He considers Inhibitor if no teammate has it. [22:48]
- Against a team that is all tanks with a lot of spirit resist, he wants Tank Buster (as he named it). [29:01]
- He buys Sharpshooter for more weapon damage. [31:42]
- He sells Opening Rounds late in the game. [32:44]
- He skipped armor items for damage and later admits he needs armor really badly. [34:49]
- He bought Alchemical Fire largely to clear creeps. [35:22]
- Vindicta is better at taking walkers than Haze because she can fly above people; he had 13,000 objective damage mid game. [25:52]
- He ended with 20,000 objective damage, about double his Haze's, and 60,000 player damage. [37:55]

Patch:
