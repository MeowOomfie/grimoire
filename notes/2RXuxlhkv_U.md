# NEW ITEM! and Trophy Collector Is Good? | Patch Breakdown
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-05-01

## Covers
Line by line breakdown of the May 1 2026 patch: health and investment rescaling, boon changes, the new item Indomitable, Trophy Collector moved to tier two, many item changes and every hero change, with the coach's verdicts.
Heroes include Abrams, Apollo, Billy, Celeste, Drifter, Dynamo, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, McGinnis, Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Rem, Shiv, Silver, Venator, Victor, Viscous, Vyper.
For players who want to know what changed and what it means.

## Claims
- Base health per boon was reduced by 7, aimed at overall tankiness after complaints that fights took too long. [0:31]
- Vitality investment bonuses were increased across the board by about 1 to 6% (top end 56% to 62%), widening the gap between heroes with many green items and few; he thinks you should be rewarded for going green. [1:02]
- Weapon investment was rescaled: the first three tiers give more (numbers quoted as 9 and 12, and 15 to 13), and from the fourth investment on it is worse, so the top end of gun is less crazy. [1:33]
- Bullet damage growth was reduced 10% for Bebop, Pocket and Vyper; Pocket and Vyper had the two highest max boon gun DPS, base DPS is unaffected, and Vyper still has the highest DPS in the game. [2:03]
- Victor got a 5% bullet damage growth reduction. [2:35]
- Mo & Krill and Apollo got 10% more gun damage growth; Apollo's gun already wasn't terrible, so the gun investment may be worth looking at on him. [2:35]
- Base Guardians and Shrines now have bullet resistance and Walkers got 10% more bullet resistance, an anti weapon change that hurts split pushing gun heroes like Lady Geist, Vyper and maybe Warden; Wraith cares less because she does a lot of spirit damage. [3:06]
- Medic trooper was nerfed again: less regen growth and a heal of 12% missing health instead of 14%, so it is harder to stay on the map and killing two or three medics no longer tops you up as much. [3:37]
- Getting knocked off a bounce pad now sends you downward quickly instead of continuing toward the destination, so you can no longer tank damage to contest a buff; take a bounce pad only when you know you won't get shot. [4:07]
- Boons now cost 1,500 souls each after 14,500 souls (was 2,000 each from 17,000), so max boons take 40,000 souls instead of 49,000. [4:38]
- This evens out the very late game: once everyone has 40,000 souls, a soul lead no longer adds boons, so comebacks are a bit easier, and the gap between super farmed and moderately farmed heroes is smaller. [5:08]
- The alt fire scaling fix: he wasn't aware it wasn't working. [6:08]
- New item Indomitable upgrades from Reactive Barrier and automatically removes the next stun, chain, immobilize, sleep or silence. [6:08]
- In testing Indomitable blocked Mo & Krill's ult, cleansing it and giving a barrier; it may be really good if you keep getting Mo ulted. [6:39]
- When Indomitable pops it gives the barrier, cleanses, and reduces all your cooldowns by 20%, which he calls insane; he is scared of it on Shiv. [7:09]
- Indomitable is close to Dota's Linken's Sphere and answers the many CC complaints. [7:40]
- Indomitable even cleanses Curse in testing, a new counter to Curse besides Unstoppable. [8:10]
- Indomitable will not help pick heroes, who are already quite bad. [8:10]
- Active Reload got a slight buff. [8:40]
- Opening Rounds now builds from High Velocity Rounds, gives less weapon damage from its conditional but about 8% base weapon damage from the High Velocity Rounds stats, and slightly less spirit power; it does a lot of everything and he expects to see much more of it. [8:40]
- Split Shot was nerfed again (less damage per stack, fewer max stacks, longer cooldown), hurting Vyper most, which he likes. [9:11]
- Shadow Weave moved from tier four to tier three; with Veil Walker that makes two tier three invisibility items, which helps roaming heroes and could be a problem since invisibility is traditionally very strong in MOBAs. [9:11]
- Shadow Weave is weaker than before: shorter duration, longer cooldown, shorter ambush duration, and less spirit power, fire rate and melee. [9:41]
- Alchemical Fire got a very slight buff; it was a little slept on and is even better now. [10:12]
- Heroic Aura cooldown reduced by 3 seconds (a bit more than 10%) and its bullet resistance raised by 2%; it was already decent. [10:12]
- Blood Tribute now has 8% innate debuff resistance and its active debuff resistance was nerfed by 5%, so more total debuff resistance and a more consistent, better item. [10:42]
- Spiritual Overflow swapped: the base stats now give ability duration and the proc buff gives spirit lifesteal, since most heroes wanted duration before activating the buff (Infernus was one of the few that benefited after). [10:42]
- Frenzy was reworked because it was literally never bought: no base spirit resist or base move speed or 20% low HP spirit resist; it now gives health and more fire rate. [11:13]
- Frenzy low HP bonus: about +4 move speed, fire rate up from 25% to 40%, and 30% debuff resistance that also reduces existing debuffs' duration, which should help against things like Pocket's Affliction. [11:44]
- Frenzy's low HP bonus now triggers below 50%, lasts 10 seconds and has a 16 second cooldown, like an enrage item. [12:15]
- Frenzy should be insane on Silver, who easily drops below half HP, turns into wolf form and heals, keeping the buff for 10 seconds; good on any hero that recovers quickly from low HP. [12:45]
- Restorative Locket nerf: max stacks went from 20 to 25 but max stamina restore now needs 25 stacks and each stack heals 16 instead of 20; same at max stacks, each stack worse, maybe enough that it isn't in every game. [12:45]
- Return Fire was buffed to 10% resist and 65% bullet damage returned, making it insanely good against Haze, Vyper and even Venator; bad news for gun heroes. [13:45]
- Reactive Barrier was nerfed across the board (no stats anymore, much longer cooldown, same cooldown as Indomitable); its only buff is that it now triggers off silences. [13:45]
- Trophy Collector moved from tier three to tier two: 1,600 souls, builds from Sprint Boots, no health anymore and less souls per minute, but super cheap and easy to get very early. [14:16]
- Getting Trophy Collector stacks early matters because it snowballs and pays for itself sooner; at max stacks he estimates about 288 souls per minute. [14:47]
- Losing the health is a big deal, but Trophy Collector still gives ability range and suits active heroes: supports like Kelvin and Paige, and roamers like Vindicta and Lash. [14:47]
- Dispel Magic was slightly nerfed: spirit resistance 16% to 10%; overall spirit is looking a bit better and gun a bit worse. [15:18]
- Veil Walker now upgrades from Sprint Boots with slightly less bonus health but includes Sprint Boots' stats; expect invisibility items back, and you can speed stack (Veil Walker with Enduring Speed or Trophy Collector) for a lot of sprint speed. [15:48]
- Bullet Resilience and Spirit Resilience got a very slight buff of 3 out of combat regen, to make staying on the map easier after the medic trooper nerf. [15:48]
- Counterspell was nerfed slightly again; it is conceptually such an insane item. [16:18]
- Rescue Beam got about a 10% range buff, not too important. [16:48]
- Colossus no longer gives 300 flat health; its base health increase is now 25% instead of 15%, so it is only better if your base health is above 3,000; it is now mainly for high health gain heroes and significantly worse on heroes like Wraith. [16:48]
- Leech was buffed because its components were buffed. [17:18]
- Inhibitor's passive weapon damage was cut from 25% to 10%; still good, but less good to build on a hero captioned "Vex". [17:18]
- Suppressor now gives bullet resistance instead of bonus health. [17:48]
- Cold Front and Arctic Blast damage height was increased, making them more consistent against airborne heroes like Lash, Celeste or Calico. [17:48]
- Spirit Snatch gives a little more spirit on melee. [17:48]
- Scourge no longer gives cooldown reduction but gives debuff resistance instead, arguably significantly better; it was already good and will be really strong. [17:48]
- Spirit Burn got a very slight scaling upgrade; he was already melting people with it on Kelvin. [18:18]
- Every source of lifesteal was nerfed, plus life drain and siphon life healing; Lady Geist's and Abrams' healing abilities were nerfed, but Lash's Flog was not. [18:18]
- Abrams: canceling Shoulder Charge after hitting a hero into a slide no longer launches you forward; that likely bug was a huge part of why Abrams was so strong at high level; a jump still gives a little, but it is very hard. [18:48]
- Apollo: an ability captioned "Disengaging Soldier" lost 15 base damage, making his lane a bit worse. [19:49]
- Billy: Rising Ram tier three now also reduces its cooldown by 10 seconds; with cooldown reduction it is a 16 second cooldown dealing 10% max health spirit damage, and with Tankbuster (and Boundless Spirit late) it did about 1,000 damage even on a max level Mo & Krill. [19:49]
- Billy could get many Rising Rams in with something like Witchmail; the 10 second cooldown matters a lot. [20:50]
- Celeste: bullet cycle time reduced from 0.65 to 0.58 (faster fire rate) and Shining Wonder radius and damage increased; she was strong at high rank and struggling at lower ranks, so she will be strong at high rank and better in mid ranks. [20:50]
- Drifter: Rend tier three also increases melee scaling and Blood Scent tier three goes from +10% to +12%, raising his damage ceiling; he will feel better but probably won't be seen much more. [21:20]
- Dynamo: Kinetic Pulse speed scaling nerfed by about 8 to 10% and Quantum Entanglement slightly nerfed; Valve nerfs in small increments, and after a long time unplayed it is fine for him to have his time. [21:50]
- Haze can slide again during Bullet Dance, which he thought was too much to remove last patch; the ult also gets evasion in the base ability and more range. [22:20]
- Haze ult tier two now gives 3 m move speed instead of cooldown, and tier three gives -75 seconds cooldown, so you'll have it a lot more. [22:50]
- Holliday: Spirit Lasso tier three is -50 seconds instead of -30 and does a little more damage; he doesn't think it is enough to make her relevant. [22:50]
- Infernus: Flame Dash spirit scaling cut from 1.0 to 0.7 and it does half damage to objectives; walkers died in 2 seconds if left alone, so this is a step in the right direction. [23:21]
- McGinnis: turrets give a little less money when killed early and Medicinal Specter lasts longer, very slight buffs. [23:51]
- Mina: Nox Nostra DPS reduced by 6%; it was melting people, so good. [23:51]
- Mirage: slightly less base health regen and shorter scarab duration; he thinks Mirage is now fully balanced, if not a little weak, but still annoying. [23:51]
- Mo & Krill: faster move speed in burrow; won't change priority, but Trophy Collector Mo with Veil Walker or Shadow Weave collecting stacks could be interesting. [24:21]
- Paige now has three stamina instead of two (most female heroes had two, except Celeste with four), but her dash speed moved to the slow bucket like Abrams, so Stamina Mastery is not as good on her. [24:21]
- Paige: cooldown reduction on Plot Armor and a shorter Bookworm recharge timer, so more dragon spam, especially with Rapid Recharge; slight buffs. [24:53]
- Paradox: Kinetic Carbine cooldown 2 seconds shorter, Time Wall tier two weapon damage 30% to 35%, and a charged Kinetic Carbine now gives 20% air dash distance. [25:23]
- Paradox is already in the fastest dash bucket, so with Stamina Mastery and a charged carbine instant air dashes go very far. [25:53]
- Paradox is probably in a better spot than Holliday and slightly underestimated: with two points the wall gives 65% weapon damage to anyone shooting through it, which people forgot to play around. [25:53]
- Pocket: an ability captioned "Shadow Sash" spirit scaling 0.9 to 1.1 and Affliction cooldown reduced by 10 seconds, slight and probably needed buffs. [26:24]
- Rem: a Naptime bug was fixed and Pillow Toss recharge time was reduced a little; it slightly improves every build since most builds buy Extra Charge, and Rem isn't bad, so a small change is fine. [26:24]
- Shiv: alt fire knockback reduced 10%, so his right click movement is far less crazy; combined with Killing Blow and Slice and Dice it was insane, so the nerf is reasonable. [26:55]
- Shiv: Serrated Knives' damage was reduced and tier three scaling reduced; knives do a bit less but close to before. [26:55]
- Silver: fixed Tail Whip applying bullet resist from the disarm duration, which may explain why Silver felt so powerful. [27:25]
- Silver: Slamfire current health damage reduced from 4% to 3%, about a 25% nerf to that part; he still expects Silver to be one of the best heroes next patch. [27:55]
- Venator only got very small gun nerfs, but the gun nerfs elsewhere (Return Fire buff, more bullet resistance items, worse late gun investment) may mean he is not as good as before. [27:55]
- Victor: Shocking Reanimation tier three is now -130 seconds instead of -100; with max ult and Superior Cooldown the ult is 80 seconds, 75 with Transcendent Cooldown, and maybe about a minute with more cooldown sources. [28:25]
- Victor may develop a playstyle of maxing his ult early and pushing walkers with two lives, using objective damage items like Monster Rounds, Close Quarters, and Heroic Aura or Alchemical Fire, or both. [29:27]
- Victor: Aura of Suffering max DPS increased by 6. [29:59]
- Viscous: a lockout period was reduced from 1.35 to 1.2 seconds and a debuff resistance bug on it was fixed; this should make the goo ball build better. [29:59]
- Vyper: Lethal Venom cooldown 24 to 28 seconds, with tier two compensating so it is the same if you invest; its cast range was reduced by 5 m but scales back with boons (about 25 boons), so it is weaker in lane. [30:30]
- Vyper: Armor Piercing Rounds doing damage through her bola was fixed. [30:30]
- Street Brawl: Unstable Concoction reduces player respawn time by 50%. [31:01]
- Street Brawl: these abilities have -15% radius: Paige's Bookworm, Infernus' Flame Dash, Lady Geist's Essence Bomb, Ivy's Tangling Thorns, Seven's Lightning Ball, Paradox's Pulse Grenade and Kelvin's Frost Grenade. [31:01]
- Street Brawl: many expansion effects and Enhanced Escalating Exposure were nerfed, and Enhanced Weighted Shots had a bug fix. [31:01]
