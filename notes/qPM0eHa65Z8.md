channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-11-01

## Covers
An Eternus 6 Wraith and Billy player streams ranked games while asking two chatbots for live advice, mostly as comedy.
The useful parts are his corrections: Wraith's kit and teleport cooldown, Billy's Blasted vs Rebuttal, Rejuvenator, Pocket's ult, counter items.
For viewers who want the game facts he states while rejecting wrong advice; most of the video is banter.

## Claims
- Billy's Blasted is a melee ability, so it cannot be used to poke from a distance. [3:03]
- Vindicta's Stake can hit you behind walls, and she has a crow ability that ricochets, so "dodge with mobility" is not a real answer against her. [4:03]
- With your wave pushing a Walker and the half HP Warden hiding behind the Walker where you can barely hit him, shoot the Walker; focusing the Warden as suggested would have got him killed. [10:37]
- The Sinner's Sacrifices are up at 8 minutes. [19:19]
- An enemy Vyper he thought he had killed survived because of Spirit Shielding. [22:51]
- The Rejuvenator is not just a burst of health: it gives the team three extra lives, stats and bonus damage. [25:24]
- Wraith is a late game carry with four abilities and no hook: cards, a teleport, Full Auto (more gun damage and faster fire) and Telekinesis, which lifts the enemy. [30:32]
- Wraith's cards are not hitscan: they track toward your crosshair but have a projectile speed. [31:03]
- Wraith's teleport has a 50 second cooldown, so if you teleport in you will die before it is back to teleport out. [34:06]
- Wraith's teleport is an escape ability most of the time; teleporting in puts you in the enemy's face where melee heroes kill you (a Shiv almost killed him and only the teleport out saved him). [34:36]
- Wraith needs a lot of souls to get online; a poor Wraith can be killed for free. [35:37]
- Wraith's Full Auto does not ramp damage while firing; it is a flat percentage fire rate increase. [39:10]
- Teleporting in to initiate is risky on Wraith because the teleport goes on a 50 second cooldown and Wraith has no other mobility, so anyone else who joins kills you. [41:12]
- Infernus does not root and Lash does not paralyze; advice that relies on those is wrong. [41:42]
- Pocket's ult, Affliction, is a big AoE that also hits you when you are behind him. [44:27]
- The counter items against abilities are Spellbreaker, Counterspell and Debuff Remover; there is no "Spell Blocker". [44:57]
- Rebuttal is an item that lets the enemy parry more often (an enemy in his Billy lane had it). [49:08]
- Debuff Remover does not stop Blasted getting parried. [50:18]
- Against Rebuttal, Billy gets parried and stunned more often, and the stunned Billy takes extra damage, so he dies. [51:18]
- Debuff Remover cannot be used while you are stunned. [51:18]
- On Billy in the mid game, Lifestrike first and then a melee charge item was the first good advice he got. [52:19]
- Going to mid boss up two players while the enemies are about to respawn looked to him like a horrible idea. [54:52]
- Metal Skin against Haze's ult is a good idea, though it costs a lot of souls. [57:35]
- Deaths are fine if they are valuable. [59:07]
