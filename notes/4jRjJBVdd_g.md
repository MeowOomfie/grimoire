# My NEW Tierlist & Builds for this Deadlock Patch
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-12-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4jRjJBVdd_g

## Covers
Deathy's early December matchmaking tier list (kept on his interactive tier list website with recommended builds per hero) and why the top heroes are strong: the S+ tier of Billy, Infernus and Vindicta, plus patch risers Abrams, Wraith, Sinclair, Warden and Drifter.
For players picking heroes to climb on this patch; it is a high rank (Eternus 6) perspective.

## Claims
- The tier list is an interactive website, updated roughly every one to two weeks; clicking a hero shows recommended builds. [0:30]
- Build votes on the site reflect creator popularity, not build quality; any build he lists is a good build. [0:30]
- No hero in Deadlock is unplayable right now; there is always an optimal way to play each hero that is at least decent. [1:30]
- Bebop's reported ~42% win rate is skewed by players, especially in lower ranks, playing bomb Bebop, which is easily countered by Debuff Remover, Counterspell and similar items. [2:00]
- S+ tier: Billy, Infernus and Vindicta. [2:30]
- Billy benefited from the system changes: strong weapon damage, very tanky, and he uses all three 4.8k investment categories (some weapon, some vitality, some spirit with spirit snatching items). [2:30]
- Billy can farm Sinner's Sacrifice almost for free: with Blasted active he is one of the only heroes who can farm them without losing health, while they now cost other heroes hundreds of health. [3:00]
- Billy farms at an incredible pace, has great team fight, a good ultimate and good abilities, and still got a buff he did not need. [3:30]
- Infernus was first placed S tier and moved up to S+; he is absurdly strong at all stages, and even Debuff Remover, hiding around corners or double jumping out of Flame Dash doesn't do much against him. [3:30]
- The most broken part of Infernus's kit is Afterburn and its scaling, refreshed by a single bullet; he could be balanced by having one bullet only extend the duration a little. [4:00]
- Infernus spikes hard early: at 4.8k spirit Afterburn chunks you, and with Duration Extender it lasts so long you must hide for a very long time. [4:30]
- Vindicta benefited from the 4.8k spirit spike almost more than any hero: Assassinate does 400+ damage at 4.8k souls, enough to basically one-shot players below 50% health very early. [4:30]
- Vindicta adds utility with Crow Familiar and Stake, generates a lot of momentum, wins lanes and is hard to deal with once she wins lane and rotates. [5:00]
- Vindicta can be shut down in pro play, so she is probably more S tier there, but the list is based on matchmaking from an Eternus 6 perspective. [5:00]
- Abrams was always solid and benefits a lot from this patch: he deals a lot of weapon damage and benefits heavily from the 4.8k vitality investment. [5:30]
- The patch is a bit of a bruiser meta: tanky heroes rate highly because they get the most out of the vitality investment and become very tanky very quickly. [6:00]
- Abrams is not S+ because of the same weaknesses: his ultimate is still meh and Shoulder Charge has a long cooldown until maxed. [6:00]
- With vitality and spirit investment Abrams's Siphon Life does a lot of damage, a couple of weapon items add significant shotgun damage, and he is a strong pick in virtually any composition. [6:00]
- Wraith is one of the biggest winners of the patch and is picked fairly consistently in pro play. [6:31]
- Wraith is hard to itemize against because she does hybrid gun and spirit damage, like Infernus; Bullet Resilience and Metal Skin don't do much. [6:31]
- Plated Armor is not great against Wraith because Full Auto doesn't count as on-hit and ignores it (the deflect probably still works). [7:01]
- Options against Wraith are a curse-style hex item or Warp Stone to dodge her ultimate, or Counterspell. [7:01]
- At 20 to 25 minutes with her full build Wraith's sustained damage is almost unrivaled, and she is now good even at a high level of play. [7:01]
- Sinclair gained a lot: he now has two teleports, and the bolt build has an insane spike with max Vexing Bolt, 4.8k spirit investment with Rapid Recharge and Improved Spirit, plus green items like Veil Walker and Enchanter's Emblem. [7:31]
- Sinclair reaches over 100 spirit at about 7 or 8 minutes with max bolt and one-shots people. [8:02]
- Two teleports let Sinclair swap back and forth with his clone ability, making the clone viable in fights rather than just an escape. [8:02]
- Warden is seen much more; his gun buffs were too much at first and were quickly rolled back. [8:02]
- Warden sits at A or S tier (currently S) because he gets very tanky, around 4 to 5k health late, while still dealing a significant amount of damage. [8:32]
- Warden has a good lane with decent regen and a very spammable Alchemical Flask; try him this patch. [8:32]
- Drifter was one of the worst heroes and went up a lot in popularity and success after the gun changes and slight buffs; he deals stellar damage, has a good lane and great team fight with his ultimate. [9:03]
- Drifter's passive is still meh and his stacks aren't what carries him; he mostly had a numbers issue. [9:03]
- Tier lists are subjective and shaped by what you play against in your region; make your own conclusions at your rank. [10:04]
