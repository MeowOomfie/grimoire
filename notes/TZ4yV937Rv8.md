# NEW ITEMS BREAKDOWN: Part 1 - Vitality
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-05-10
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TZ4yV937Rv8

## Covers
A walkthrough of every new or reworked vitality item in the big May 2025 item update (early May 2025 patch era, numbers and balance will change), then first impressions on balance after about 16 to 20 games. For anyone learning the new item shop: what each item does, the numbers, which heroes want it, and which items look overrated (Trophy Collector, Fury Trance) or underbought (Inhibitor, Plated Armor).

## Claims
- Rebuttal (tier 1, 800): reduces your parry cooldown; a successful parry heals you for the damage that would have been dealt, returns that damage to the attacker, and gives you increased melee damage after. [0:30]
- With Rebuttal, parrying someone in lane pretty much kills them instantly: they take their own melee damage back, are parried (taking extra damage), and you get bonus melee damage on them. [1:00]
- Rebuttal is very good against Viscous and hard counters any melee build that gets parried; quite strong situationally. [1:00]
- Rebuttal's biggest drawback is that it has no upgrades, so with the new limited slots you will eventually want to sell it. [1:31]
- Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem combine the old armors and barriers: Battle Vest gives bullet resist plus weapon damage and fire rate while you are above 65% health; Enchanter's Emblem is the same for spirit, giving spirit and cooldown reduction above 65% health. [1:31]
- Guardian Ward is essentially the old Divine Barrier (which moved to tier 4): it shields someone, and its cooldown is halved when cast on someone else, so it is marketed toward supports. [2:01]
- Spirit Shielding and Weapon Shielding (tier 2) replace the old barriers: Spirit Shielding triggers on 150 spirit damage within 3.5 seconds, Weapon Shielding on 200 weapon damage within 3.5 seconds, both easy to trigger, each giving a 400 barrier with 1.2 spirit scaling. [2:31]
- Bullet Resilience (tier 3) is an improved Bullet Armor that gives extra bullet resist when you are below 30% health, similar to Frenzy's effect; he hasn't noticed it much. [3:02]
- Counter Spell is the coolest item ever added to Deadlock; people call it OP, he does not think it is. [3:32]
- Counter Spell can parry almost any ability: he hasn't found one it can't; reportedly even Black Hole (giving time to move out), plus Guy Swap, Lash's ult, Infernus, Yamato's Power Slash and Grey Talon's arrow. [4:02]
- Counter Spell has a fairly long cooldown, and a successful parry gives spirit power and move speed for 6 seconds. [4:02]
- Fury Trance upgrades from Bullet Lifesteal: it gives fire rate and bullet resist but silences you and disables stamina usage and regeneration, so you can only aim, shoot and move; it is Dota's Mask of Madness. [4:32]
- Many of the new items are heavily inspired by Dota. [4:32]
- Spirit Resilience is the spirit version of Bullet Resilience and works exactly the same. [4:32]
- Trophy Collector: each kill or assist gives a stack, up to 14; each stack gives 25 health, 20 souls per minute, 1% ability range and 0.2 m sprint speed. [5:03]
- At max stacks Trophy Collector is insane: 350 health, 280 souls per minute, 14% ability range and 2.8 m sprint speed, on top of the 2 m sprint speed in its base because it builds out of Sprint Boots. [5:03]
- Veil Walker moved back from tier 4 to tier 3; it no longer gives shields, still makes you invisible, heals 150 as you pass through, and gives health and spirit power. [5:33]
- Veil Walker is very strong, especially on gankers like Holliday or Paradox. [6:03]
- Warp Stone is now a green item and otherwise unchanged except it gives zero stats; many items don't give many stats anymore. [6:03]
- Cheat Death (tier 4, new) makes you death immune temporarily, while you deal less damage, heal less and move slower; Shiv's execute seems to go through it. [6:33]
- Cheat Death is good on heroes who need to get an ability off before dying, like Lady Geist: if you die before swapping, it gives you 4 seconds to get the swap off and avoid the death. [6:33]
- Divine Barrier is now tier 4 and fully changed: 600 barrier, 15% ability range, and it removes all non-stun debuffs from the target, so Affliction gets completely removed. [7:03]
- Divine Barrier's cooldown is halved to 18 seconds when used on teammates, and with cooldown reduction he has had it as low as 12 or 13 seconds. [7:03]
- Kevlar is now green, giving spirit power and barrier, slightly changed. [7:34]
- Barriers are now universal: they block all damage types and are affected by resistances, so Kevlar is a lot better on Yamato, who benefits from the resistances. [7:34]
- Healing Tempo (new, upgrades from Healing Booster): healing yourself or an ally gives the target bonus fire rate and move speed. [7:34]
- Don't buy Healing Tempo on carry heroes most of the time; it feels really strong on Kelvin and the more traditional supports, and is pretty good on heroes like Lady Geist as a late, non-mandatory upgrade. [8:04]
- Infuser: active gives 80% spirit lifesteal and 35 spirit power for 6 seconds; base stats are 16 spirit lifesteal, 10 spirit resist and 100 health; it went from a 500 cost item to 6400. [8:34]
- Inhibitor is essentially the same item but now gives healing reduction. [8:34]
- Juggernaut (new): anyone shooting you gets -40% fire rate, and you get 50% slow resist, 2 m/s move speed, 25% melee resist and 8 health regen; for heroes who want to get in and create chaos. [9:05]
- Leech and Phantom Strike are essentially unchanged. [9:05]
- Plated Armor (new): a chance to deflect incoming bullets (preventing all weapon damage) or to prevent all on-hit effects from bullets. [9:05]
- Plated Armor is very good against heroes who need to proc things, like Infernus, who struggles much more to apply afterburn; it also counters Armor Piercing Rounds. [9:35]
- Spellbreaker: the next instance of high spirit damage (above 150) is reduced by 75%, on a 14 second cooldown; a 1,000 damage Lash slam deals only 250. [9:35]
- Spellbreaker is very strong against burst heavy heroes like Lash, Yamato or Grey Talon, and its base stats give 25% spirit resist and 25% debuff resist. [10:06]
- Unstoppable is not new but now builds out of Debuff Reducer. [10:06]
- Vampiric Burst is now a green item giving bullet lifesteal and fire rate, the weapon equivalent of Infuser with similar strength. [10:36]
- Witch Mail: taking spirit hits above 75 damage (not really heavy, many abilities exceed it) reduces a random ability's cooldown by 3 seconds; it also gives 20 spirit resist and 10 spirit power. [10:36]
- Green items in general are quite strong. [11:06]
- Rebuttal is extremely strong against specific heroes but feels bad when you have to sell it. [11:06]
- Not sure about the shielding items yet; they could be strong but don't seem super OP. [11:06]
- Reactive Barrier seems better: its barrier has 2.2 spirit scaling versus 1.2 on the shielding items, so at some point it gives the bigger barrier, and it triggers on movement lock effects. [11:06]
- The barriers trigger very easily and are a lot of EHP for the money, especially against burst: if Lash slams you with Spirit Shielding, you suddenly have an extra 400 HP. [11:36]
- Healing Nova stands out among the 1600s: buy Healing Rite in lane, then pay only 800 more for Healing Nova; its healing scales with spirit at 1.0. [12:06]
- The new Healing Nova tooltip no longer mentions healing more per nearby ally, unlike the old one; unclear if that is true. [12:06]
- Restorative Locket now scales with spirit; many healing items feel better later in the game, not earlier. [12:36]
- Trophy Collector is probably the most overrated item of the patch, despite Reddit and stream chat calling it OP (based on his 16 to 20 games). [13:06]
- Trophy Collector is a dead item when first bought: you want it early for stacks, but at zero stacks its 3200 souls give only 2 m sprint speed and 2 out of combat regen, useless in a fight, gimping your early and mid game power spikes. [13:36]
- On Lash, he would rather have early Stamina Mastery, Majestic Leap to set up ults, or Tankbuster than Trophy Collector. [14:06]
- Trophy Collector is good if you are really far ahead and can afford it, or on heroes that don't need items to function: Mo & Krill is probably the best user (his ult lets teammates kill the target), and roamers like Holliday (lasso) and Paradox (swap) also like it. [14:36]
- Trophy Collector is not a must buy and is bad on many heroes; teams with four or five Trophy Collectors that aren't getting kills tend to lose. [15:06]
- Feel free to test Trophy Collector yourself; explore and discover. [15:36]
- Counter Spell is not OP because you usually must parry something high value for it to be great, and parrying effectively stuns yourself. [16:06]
- Counter Spell is the new counter to Lash's ult: if you're getting Lash ulted, don't buy Ethereal Shift, buy Counter Spell and parry it; you can also parry Infernus. [16:06]
- Given its cooldown and 3200 cost, Counter Spell is probably balanced, maybe a little strong. [16:36]
- Fury Trance mostly feels underwhelming: the heroes who want its fire rate (best case Haze during her ult) don't care about bullet resist. [16:36]
- Fury Trance's 40% bullet resist lasts only 6 seconds, and Warp Stone gives the same resist; lifesteal only goes from 16 to 22, so its main advantage is fire rate. [17:07]
- Warp Stone and Stamina Mastery are still really strong. [17:07]
- Cheat Death is good situationally but not really that good. [17:07]
- Many tier 4 vitality items have spirit resist; stacking Spirit Resilience with one of them (for example Spellbreaker) gets you to 50%+ spirit resist. [17:37]
- Colossus is still quite strong. [17:37]
- Colossus and Fortitude are effectively nerfed: every hero's base health and health per boon went up while their flat health did not, and green items now give percent health based on base health. [18:07]
- Divine Barrier and Healing Tempo are very strong. [18:38]
- Infuser might be a little overtuned; Infernus and Pocket really love it. [18:38]
- Inhibitor is one of the most underbought items: healing is very strong but there are many anti-heal options (Inhibitor, Toxic Bullets, Crippling Headshot, Decay, Spirit Burn), and different ones stack with diminishing returns, so spread them across your team. [18:38]
- Juggernaut is very strong but a late game item; rushed, its stats don't feel good, but Shiv or Lady Geist with it feel really strong. [19:10]
- Plated Armor is probably criminally underbought; on Viscous or Yamato against Armor Piercing Rounds you kind of need it. [19:10]
- Spellbreaker seems really strong against burst like Yamato or Lash. [19:41]
- Unstoppable is not a must buy, but carries should consider it because there is a lot of CC this patch, especially from the new spirit items. [19:41]
- Vampiric Burst is just all right. [20:11]
- Witch Mail feels underwhelming but has potential: probably insane on Yamato and felt really good on Lash; 3 seconds off a random ability every time you take 75+ spirit damage adds up. [20:11]
