## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (April 2026 patch) with replay reviews of four viewers: a Victor (Ascendant), a Mo & Krill (Eternus 5 in an Eternus 6 lobby), a Rem (Emissary) and a Graves into a Silver lane. A Dynamo replay was broken and not reviewed; a viewer's Holliday build is reviewed after the Rem session.
Themes: Golden Goose Egg rules, heal creeps and dead time, shrines, urn tricks, movement efficiency (wall jumps, diagonal air dashes), playing like a support, green spike timing, over-fighting and fighting with your team.
