## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream on 2/2/26, the day Silver released. He does a quick Grey Talon build review, then coaches two Graves games: Phantom (Oracle 6, on one of his first Graves games) and a second Graves game into a Mina lane.
Themes: Graves pickles, root and ult placement, Restorative Shot being bad, split pushing instead of mid mosh pits, the three Urn game states, overextending without ult, farming camps, tempo items and Split Shot.
After the coaching he tests Silver in the sandbox on release: her kit, AP upgrades, interactions and first build thoughts. Side notes on Slowing Bullets, Active Reload and his top-five heroes.

## Claims

Silver first look (sandbox, release day):
- Her 1 (Slam Fire) instantly reloads and gives 300% fire rate and 4% current health damage for a few shots, with reduced accuracy. [135:56]
- Her 2 is a dash kick that marks; shooting the marked target deals spirit damage. It is a standard mobility ability you can use to escape. [136:58]
- Her 3 throws a bola that deals spirit damage, slows and prevents movement abilities, which makes her a counter hero. [138:29]
- Passive: dealing damage builds bloodlust, faster at low health; at max it automatically casts Lykan Curse, her transform. Camps also build it, and you lose stacks out of combat. [139:01]
- You can press the ult early once the curse prompt appears instead of waiting for the auto cast. [165:26]
- The transform gives max health, move speed, stacking fire rate on enemy heroes, healing for missing HP and a lot of resistances, and swaps her abilities and gun. [139:01]
- Wolf 1 is a melee chomp: 75 damage plus 8% missing health, with 40% life steal. [139:31]
- Wolf 2 leaps and mauls the first enemy hit, reducing bullet resist and adding a bleed; it ends early on hit. [140:32]
- Wolf 3 (tail whack) is AoE spirit damage with about a 2.5 second disarm in a huge radius; get into the middle of them. [141:02]
- Combo: dump your mag while chasing, land the bola, then go in. Use your 2 to disengage, your wolf 2 to re-engage, then chomp. [141:32]
- Use your 1 before the kick, not after it. [159:41]
- You will build her with some melee items; melee life steal looks strong. [142:02]
- AP: the 1's tier two is +30% weapon damage, a real lane spike; its max deals 10% current health if all shots hit. Put two points into the 1 early. [142:33]
- The wolf leap's tier two is minus 9 seconds off a 16 second base, so it is on a very low cooldown. [143:36]
- Ult upgrades: +50% bullet and spirit resist, and on hero kill you refresh abilities, duration and the transformation heal. [144:37]
- Kinetic Dash is a 100% buy on her. [147:11]
- The default build's point order is awful; rush the gun spike and two points in the 1. [147:42]
- In lane, rush two points into the 1; the 1 is current HP damage, so a 1 max makes sense. [175:20]
- Superior Duration looks good: it extends the wolf form, the bleed, the bola slow and the disarm. [150:48]
- Her spirit scaling is low; you will still buy your spirit spike but he doesn't expect a spirit build to be playable. [152:21]
- Lightning Scroll does not work with her. [152:51]
- Her base gun is weak, about 72 DPS, and her wave clear is bad, so you probably go Monster Rounds. [156:33]
- She seems weak to anti-heal. [156:00]
- Toxic Bullets is a must: the 1 builds it up instantly. Still pop the 1 only after you mag dump. [173:00]
- Intensifying Magazine has an angle, since the 1 reloads you once you have full stacks. [162:15]
- Glass Cannon's minus 15% max HP is a nothing downside for how much damage it gives; Lucky Shot also fits. [163:49]
- Silence does not stop the transform, and Curse does not dispel the Lykan Curse. [165:26]
- Headhunter fits: it stacks her rage fast and headshots build Toxic Bullets much faster. [174:50]
- Torment Pulse is a strong pick if you want it. [177:55]
- The bola ricochets about 15 m and searches again from each new target. [182:02]
- Spirit Snatch might be core, perhaps with Superior Duration. [184:10]
- She is a frontline carry like Shiv or Abrams, not an M1 farmer. [190:38]
- Slide while using the 1 recast to conserve ammo. [186:21]

General:

- Active Reload is the most overstated item in the game and escaped a nerf. [100:22]
