## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Deadman Danny-only free coaching stream the day after a big Danny buff. He first analyzes a Danny game by Lustig, a top-10 player, to see how the hero plays post-patch, then coaches two Danny players: Obscurus (EU, a long game played on Lightbringer's build) and Scout.
Themes: playing for the 1 after the buff, skill order, spirit spike versus gun spike, Express Shot and on-hit items, rocket jumping and overheating costing HP, playing in the air, ulting only below half HP, fight positioning and kiting to exits, CC and curse as Danny's counters, dead time and farming, slot management, and why Spirit Resilience and Indomitable fit.
Side notes on the patch: stuns now cancel parries, Holliday's melee, Radiant, and a movement-speed test on the jetpack.
