## Covers
Drifter, aquatiq commentary over one of his own games using a build ("Wonder Drifter") that buys Long Range first.
Lane against Calico, items bought against Abrams, Graves and Haze, team fight and Mid-Boss calls, March 2026 patch.

## Claims
- Build used: "Wonder Drifter", which goes Long Range first even though Drifter's claws do not reach far. [1:03]
- Verdict on Long Range: it did him good and might actually be decent. [6:23]
- In a lane against Calico, Extra Regen is enough because she has a lot of burst. [2:03]
- Push the wave back first; without a pushed wave he has to play passive. [3:07]
- Put his mark on an enemy so that if they try to escape he can teleport to them. [3:07]
- Even a little tower damage is good. [3:07]
- In one trade he should have used light punches instead of a heavy punch. [5:53]
- He considers Backstabber (Stalker) as an option. [8:00]
- Drifter does not like being slowed; against Slowing Hex he wants Debuff Remover. [11:41]
- Mid game buys: Hollow Point and Enduring Speed. [12:42]
- He buys Toxic Bullets against Abrams, who is very hard to deal with without it. [12:42]
- He maxed his 4 (the darkness ult that blinds enemies). [14:21]
- He considers Slowing Bullets for chasing. [15:22]
- Check what you are dying to most; he was dying to spirit, so he buys spirit resist next. [16:55]
- Do not leave a wave to die. [17:25]
- When the enemy freely took your walker in one lane, the best thing to do can be running up the other lane to clear the wave. [18:28]
- This was an Inhibitor game. [20:41]
- He buys Disarming Hex, mostly for Haze, so they stop shooting him. [21:41]
- Drifter has one of the biggest hitboxes, so small corridors with Doorman do not work well for him. [23:12]
- Killing enemies who hold the Rejuvenator removes it; he blinds to open space so his team can kill everyone. [23:44]
- Use the ult so it blinds as many enemies as possible when they come at you; he hit three. [27:25]
- Ricochet is in the build and he bought it. [29:27]
- Doorman's door gives an ally about 900 shield when they walk through. [29:57]
- He sells Long Range late. [29:57]
- Get a pick before touching the Mid-Boss. [30:28]
- Always do Mid-Boss; games where they skipped it they lost. [31:29]
- Colossus makes the enemy grow larger and gain bullet, spirit and melee resist; he answers with Juggernaut. [32:00]
- After taking Mid-Boss the game was basically over, even though they were behind most of the game. [33:35]
