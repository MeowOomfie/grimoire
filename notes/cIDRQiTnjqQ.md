# Losing Early Doesn't Matter If You Know This (Eternus 6 POV)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-05-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cIDRQiTnjqQ

## Covers
Commented Eternus 6 Wraith game: losing lane with a feeding Infernus, falling behind, and winning through macro.
Pre-game checks, playing a lost lane, wave priority, Sinner's Sacrifice vs camps, Urn and Mid Boss decisions, dead maps, shrines and mega creeps, spirit counter-items.
For players who focus on mechanics and want the hard carry decision-making after a bad lane.

## Claims
- The main thing to learn is the early mid game: what to do after you lost the lane or know it is lost. [1:01]
- At the start of every game check two things: first your lane matchup (here Wraith and Infernus vs Warden and Yamato, a tough lane since Yamato is very strong, Warden solid and Wraith not strong in lane; Infernus can counter Warden because he is easy to set on fire). [1:01]
- Second, check the team comps and especially the enemy's damage type: Pocket, Celeste, Paradox, Mina and Yamato mostly can't build gun, so expect a lot of spirit burst; plan mid game counter buys around it. [1:31]
- Also check what lockdown the enemy has; here the only hard stun was Paradox's Swap. [2:01]
- In lane, first gauge how scary and aggressive the enemies are. Metro planned to play this lane passively and always kills the creep first. [2:01]
- If your lane mate is over-aggressive and fighting out of reach, don't throw yourself after them; play safe. [2:31]
- Trades are survivable when the lane is pushed and you have pressure; with high HP you can trade your HP for damage on the enemy. [3:02]
- When you have a lane advantage, always consider your lane mate's HP too; if they are low, don't play over-aggressive. [3:32]
- At 2 minutes boxes come up: when far ahead in lane, steal the enemy tier one camp; when the lane is even, go back and farm your own boxes (on this lane the closest ones are left of the forward shop), then push the wave again. [3:32]
- Diving past the wave when your creeps are at the enemy tower is a horrible push: you lose creeps or take creep damage during the fight. [4:32]
- Mistake: harassing a low enemy from too far forward and committing for the healing creep got Metro killed; he should have backed behind the tower and killed creeps safely. [5:02]
- When your lane is clearly lost (enemy at double your net worth), accept the tower falling first; the goal becomes shoving the wave as well as you can without dying again. [5:32]
- A lane mate who gives up and proxies the enemy wave in their spawn is making a bad play: it ruins your farm and the enemy can cut him off. [6:03]
- Extra Regen is very strong in the current meta: always active, works when trading (unlike Healing Rite style items), and regens you while shoving waves and rotating to boxes. [6:33]
- Number one tip for new players: always look at the minimap and take a head count. [7:03]
- With four teammates mid and the lane lost, Metro let his tower die instead of risking a defense against two enemy ults (Yamato and Warden), trading it for the mid tower and kills. [7:33]
- An enemy Yamato diving deep at your Walker can be lifted (Telekinesis) into the Walker, forcing her ult and making her back out. [7:33]
- At 6 minutes, behind in money and map control, farm safely under the Walker and don't push the wave out to the enemy tower without map info; clear the creep wave first. [8:04]
- When the enemy jungle-steals you and you are behind, stay safe; avoid the fight once the creep wave is gone. Make the enemy make the mistakes. [8:34]
- Around 8 minutes Sinner's Sacrifices come up and Metro would usually take them, but skips them when teammates already control that side. [9:34]
- Sinner's Sacrifices are worth more than creep camps, especially tier two camps; until you can farm tier three camps efficiently, prioritize Sinner's Sacrifices and waves over jungle camps. [10:04]
- With three teammates around you have map control, so walk up into the enemy side to try to steal a Sinner's Sacrifice; that got a free kill on the Yamato farming hers. [10:35]
- Always keep track of enemy numbers; it is the most important part of deciding when you can move forward. [11:05]
- With waves pushed deep into enemy territory and enemies located, it is safe to take an enemy jungle camp; when you lose map control on that side, go back to your own jungle. [11:05]
- On a carry, sitting alone on a side lane and farming it is the most efficient income, but adapt to teammates; if both side lanes are occupied and they won't swap, farm a jungle camp rather than share a lane's creeps as two. [11:37]
- Go to a lane where the enemy is pushing your teammate's Walker hard, to force them out and hopefully take the lane solo or push with two or three and get Urn control. [12:08]
- With two teammates along it was safe to push and kill a tower while enemies fought over the Urn; losing early Urn control is not a big deal since you can still contest at the drop-off spot. [12:40]
- While backing to spend souls, watch the map; when there's a mid skirmish the team's number one priority is the Urn. [13:11]
- If a fight breaks out over the Urn, show up for it even as a hard carry; if the Urn goes in free, keep side lane control instead. [13:42]
- Against a 2v1 (Pocket and Warden), back to your Walker as soon as you hear Warden's flask so you don't get caged; reengage aggressively when a teammate arrives (Rem landing a sleep), but don't go too deep if you might get afflicted. [13:42]
- The wave is always the number one priority; Metro had farmed only about three creep camps by this point. [14:42]
- Keep a head count mid: with four enemies seen mid, at most two can contest a side Walker. [14:42]
- Trading one life for a Walker in the mid game, especially early at 13 to 14 minutes, is excellent macro. [15:13]
- Mistake: Metro teleported without stamina before reaching the high ground; walk a little farther before teleporting. [15:13]
- Every time you respawn, look at the wave state on each lane to decide where to go, for example defend the lane pushing into your Walker. [15:43]
- When you don't need to worry about objectives or team fights, go straight to the enemy side to steal a Sinner's Sacrifice, pick up the bridge buff on the way, then clear your respawned jungle; hard carries always want more money. [16:13]
- Always prioritize the wave first; if a teammate takes the Sinner's Sacrifice it's fine as long as the team splits the money efficiently, but taking it yourself and giving up map control is really bad. [16:44]
- Common mistake: arriving late to a wave on your side, killing it, then immediately farming; there is usually a second wave right behind it. Clear both and push the wave into the enemy side before farming or rotating. [17:14]
- Two teammates taking Mid Boss as two while not up in numbers and while the team fights over the Urn pickup is a horrible play. [18:17]
- A forward Urn picked up by the enemy gives them a lot of comeback money, so the team should focus on stopping or punishing the pickup; win that fight with one or two extra kills, then do Mid Boss safely. [18:48]
- That mis-macro let the enemy win the fight up in numbers, take the Mid Boss with two Rejuvenators to one, and pick up the Urn, which is how they got back in net worth. [19:49]
- When the enemy has the Urn and will deposit it on their side, most of them focus around the drop-off spot, so you have more map control on the other side; farm that side's jungle aggressively. [20:20]
- Mistake: after losing track of enemies, Metro stayed to secure a tier three camp when Pocket engaged him aggressively; when an enemy engages confidently, assume a teammate is with them. Also, Pocket was actually 3k ahead of him. [21:24]
- On respawn, check health bars and numbers: if your team is healthy and up in numbers, skip the fight and fix the middle wave to protect your Walker instead; showing up late adds maybe one assist while costing Walker HP and waves. [21:54]
- A dead map is one with little value to fight over (jungle empty on both sides, maybe one Sinner's Sacrifice, few camps, no objectives); on a dead map both teams look for pick-offs, so be very careful. [22:56]
- On a dead map, back out once a target retreats too far, because enemies will show up to a fight or gank fast; committing there risks a 5v3 in enemy territory. [23:26]
- Down a player with no map control, shove waves and wait for the teammate to return, or for the jungle to respawn, before looking for a kill. [24:28]
- Use the zipline speed boost to reach a teammates' 2v2 fight in time. [24:58]
- Mistake: Metro used his cards before Pocket used Satchel; save cards until after Satchel. [24:58]
- On Wraith, before teleport (Project Mind) is maxed its cooldown is very long, so be careful about teleporting in too early. [25:28]
- A fight goes your way when your team rotates more people than the enemy. [25:28]
- Mid Boss respawned 1 minute 20 seconds after this fight, not enough time to do it, so the team ran the secured Urn instead. [25:59]
- With enemies down multiple players and good map control, it is safe to farm Sinner's Sacrifices and take the rest of the map's value; then push the wave out first to keep map control. [25:59]
- On a dead map before Mid Boss, tell your team you are on the other side and not to get picked: the team that gets the next pick usually wins the next fight, gets the next Mid Boss and probably wins the game. At even net worth this is the most important stage. [26:30]
- Before Mid Boss, push waves near your teammates so you can help get the pick first. [27:32]
- A split-pushing teammate deep in enemy spawn can create space by baiting enemies into chasing him, letting the team take Mid Boss uncontested. [27:32]
- Killing an enemy shrine is very high value: that lane's and the middle wave get stronger mega creeps on your side, the enemy earns less from killing creep waves for the rest of the game, and you get automatic map control. [28:33]
- Metro cites a Statlocker statistic that when a team kills one shrine, the enemy's chance to win drops to about 20%, from less money and from map control. [29:04]
- Around the 25 minute mark Metro starts speed farming tier three camps when jungle is up, unless a big team fight is happening. [30:05]
- After roughly 25 minutes respawns are so long that every team fight usually means a big objective, so showing up to team fights is important even on a hard carry. [30:05]
- Late game, farm in a position where you can help your team and rotate to the fight as soon as they engage, without hard committing; push out the wave first. [30:35]
- Usually after 25 minutes a lost team fight means a free objective for the enemy, but mega creeps pushing your lanes keep the waves in the enemy base, so the enemy can't get the value. [31:35]
- When down two players, tell your team not to fight; push the wave out first when it pushes into you. [32:35]
- Mistake: Metro wasted his speed going back to base for a fight that turned out to be one Pocket overcommitting. [33:05]
- Before the next Mid Boss spawn, getting a kill is very important; grouped as six, the team caught Celeste first (60 second respawn, Pocket 40), then took a free Mid Boss as it spawned. [33:05]
- Even behind in net worth, more shrines, more mega creeps and the third Rejuvenator (three extra lives in the late game) make you likely to win. [33:35]
- Mid Boss is always the most important objective, and late game Mid Bosses especially can win you the game. [34:05]
- Very few heroes can hold their own against a farmed Wraith late game; the threats here were Pocket's Affliction, which stops healing altogether, and Warden's strong single target damage. [34:05]
- With map control, take the enemy side jungle and secure the next Urn to get even or ahead in net worth. [34:35]
- Late game Wraith items: choose between Inhibitor, Silencer, maybe Siphon (Spirit Siphon), maybe Boundless Spirit for extra damage. [34:35]
- Silencer is insane value against spirit heroes: an early silence on Pocket, Celeste or Mina gets a kill, and on Yamato it stops her ulting in time. [35:06]
- Against a heavy spirit damage team Metro bought an early Spirit Resilience, which he usually doesn't on Wraith since she is not very tanky. [35:06]
- With Rejuvenators two to zero, a forced fight should be good as long as you don't hard feed; engage carefully and wait for a teammate's big ult (Lash). [35:36]
- At the Patron, kill the enemy players first: if the enemy has two people in the pit for a few seconds the Patron becomes invulnerable, and with one person in the pit it takes half damage. [36:06]
- The game was won by getting money and soul lead through safe play and being in the right spot at the right time; focus fire and ult targets come second. [37:06]
- Macro is 80% plus of the game, not flashy movement or aim. [37:36]
