# The Best and Worst Heroes in Deadlock Street Brawl
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-01-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bGY-2U8A5FI

## Covers
Metro's tier list for the Street Brawl mode about a day after it launched (January 2026): how the 4v4 mode differs from normal Deadlock and a placement and reason for each hero.
Big AoE ultimates and stuns win; single-target pickoff heroes and heroes that need specific item rolls lose.

## Claims
- Street Brawl has no farming and everyone has the same net worth; it is always a 4v4 in the lane, so teamfight heroes with AoE, especially AoE ultimates, beat single-target pickoff heroes like Haze. [0:00] [0:30]
- You have your ultimate at least once every round, often twice, because it comes back so fast. [0:30]
- Before each round you pick from random item rolls, so heroes that need specific items can be left without a build. [2:02]
- Your creeps must reach the enemy tower to damage it, so wave pushing matters. [6:36]
- Stuns are everything in the mode; many players instantly buy Curse, Focus Lens and Silence Wave. [3:03] [17:51]
- Abrams: S tier. Without a tank it is very hard to initiate and the team with a tank tends to win; Abrams can go in 1v4, stun, get a free kill and leave with his ultimate. [1:00]
- Bebop: S+, top three in the mode. Hyperbeam from behind your tower nukes the whole wave and anyone in line of sight, even in the veil; take Lightning Scroll. He is weak in normal mode right now. [1:32]
- Billy: A tier (Metro considered B). Decent tank with a strong AoE ultimate and Bashdown that gets value on multiple targets, but hard to build because he wants melee and close-range items and long-range rolls leave him with nothing. [2:02] [2:33]
- Calico: C tier. She is a pickoff assassin who puts Gloom Bombs on one person, does little AoE damage and suffers against the stuns everyone buys. [2:33] [3:03]
- The Doorman: D tier. His ultimate is single target and cannot set up a kill because the other three enemies are always nearby, so it only stalls; his normal-mode tricks (doors on your walker, relocating one enemy) do not apply. [3:03] [3:33] [5:34]
- Drifter: same problem as The Doorman; he is good in isolated fights, and with four enemies in the lane his isolation spell is active 0% of the time. [4:04]
- Dynamo: S tier, almost S+. Black Hole once a round is scary (twice with Refresher), but he does not scale much and is not meta-defining like Bebop. [4:04] [4:34]
- Grey Talon: C tier. Single-target damage does poorly; he can sometimes land an AoE owl from the back and finish kills, and needs very specific items (lots of spirit or long-range abilities). [4:34] [5:04]
- Haze: B tier. Fixation takes too long to build and the fixated target just hides in a 4v4, but she scales well and becomes very scary around rounds 4 or 5 with a full build, her ultimate and Unstoppable. [5:04] [5:34]
- Holliday: solid, like Billy (A). She can go in immediately and lasso someone to turn the fight into a 3v4. [5:34] [6:05]
- Infernus: A tier, better than in normal mode. Run range on Flame Dash or an ultimate build, since enemies are usually clumped; he has playmaking potential but dies fast. [6:05]
- Ivy: S tier. One of the best lane pushers, since Kudzu Bomb nukes the entire lane; her ultimate maxed early gives a four-man silence that wins fights alone; Stone Form cancels big ultimates like Seven's and Bebop's. [6:36] [7:06]
- Seven: S+. There are few places to hide in a 4v4 lane, his ultimate covers the map, and Lightning Ball with extra radius nukes the wave; multi-target heroes beat single-target carries in this mode. [7:06] [7:36]
- Wraith: just okay, like Haze. Single-target damage, needs a specific item set, dies easily, and does nothing in rounds 1 and 2. [7:36] [8:06]
- Kelvin: S tier, a bit below Dynamo. A dome on two enemies with three teammates inside wins the fight; Metro rates the Kelvin dome the second best ultimate in the game after Dynamo's. [8:06] [8:36]
- Lady Geist: A tier, solid with no real weaknesses. Essence Bombs are very strong and in a 4v4 you will land her life swap if you play properly; in normal Deadlock he would rate her S+. [8:36]
- Mo & Krill: S tier, the best single-target lockdown. He has his ultimate twice a fight, and the enemy team needs something to cancel it or gives up a free kill. [9:08]
- Lash: S tier, close to S+. Insane AoE ultimate that catches multiple people, and he can go in, initiate and get out. [9:40]
- McGinnis: upgraded to A tier. Her wall helps the team in and out, the AoE heal helps multiple teammates, and turrets are annoying; her ultimate is still weak. [9:40] [10:10]
- Mina: low B tier. She is the best 1v1 assassin, but in this mode there are always four enemies in front of you, she dies easily, and she wants isolated 1v1s and 1v2s the mode does not allow. [10:40] [11:11]
- Pocket: S+, the third S+ hero. Affliction hits all four in the lane; with Mystic Vulnerability one Affliction wins the fight, sometimes twice a fight with the right build; he goes in, nukes wave and players, Afflicts and gets out. [11:11] [11:42]
- Mirage: very low. He needs an AoE debuff build that only works if he rolls Ricochet and then Silencer or Inhibitor (and wants Toxic Bullets); his ultimate is almost always useless because the lane is always fighting and there is no split pushing. [11:42] [12:13]
- Paige: weak, Metro nearly placed her in C. She is good at pocketing one player, but shields on one person do little when everyone takes AoE damage, and she needs specific rolls (Divine Barrier, Guardian Ward early, Mystic Expansion, Knockdown); Boundless Spirit only adds some ability damage. Her ultimate is very good but usually once per round without Refresher. [12:13] [12:45] [13:15]
- Paradox: fine, a bit below Holliday. Holliday can jump in and lasso an enemy to your team, while Paradox has to swap herself in among four enemies and can blow up; she is good once her wall is maxed. [13:15] [13:47]
- Shiv: very strong in normal Deadlock right now, but not amazing here. Stuns and disables shut him down, gun Shiv is not great, and he wants spirit items plus specific purples like Decay. [13:47] [14:17] [14:47]
- Sinclair: unrated, none seen in 20+ games. Metro guesses he is a good counter pick, since the meta is big ultimates and stealing Seven's or Bebop's does a lot. [14:47] [15:17]
- Victor: very bad. He wants to frontline but dies, needs Infuser every time and is useless without it; he has the most specialized kit in the game and 99% of games you will not roll the right items. [15:17] [15:47]
- Vindicta: okay, a single-target pickoff hero like Grey Talon who needs specific items. [15:47]
- Viscous: very solid. The cube saves teammates in these fights, Goo Ball is good and Puddle Punch hits multiple people; the normal meta is gun Viscous, but spirit builds (Mystic Reach on the ball, or a Splatter build) do a lot of damage here. [15:47] [16:18]
- Vyper: low C at best, almost D. She is a single-target nuker who uses all her spells on one target and is useless if it survives, even with the items she wants. [16:18] [16:50]
- Warden: S tier. You can build around Last Stand because you have it every fight in one big teamfight; with Mystic Reach, duration and Lightning Scroll it wins fights; top five ultimate, and he can also carry with gun. [16:50] [17:21]
- Yamato: a good frontline tank but not nearly as good as Mo & Krill or Abrams, because she has no stun and stuns are everything in this mode. [17:21] [17:51]
- Joke prediction: the upcoming heroes Silver, Apollo, Celeste, Graves, Rem and Venator will all be S tier, because in the last hero patch every new hero was broken except Paige. [17:51] [18:22]
- Summary: get big AoEs and roll the enemy. [18:22]
