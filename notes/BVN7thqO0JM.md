## Covers
Paradox, coached by Midknight: a VOD review of an Oracle-rank Paradox player (one student), posted 2026-02-25.
Themes: box routing in lane, respecting your HP and minions, heal crates versus full resets, playing around the ult, how a pick hero looks for picks (where, when, on whom, how many), swap distance, Mystic Shot, and defensive items against a spirit-heavy comp. Side notes on melee builds and a movement patch that lets you jump out of a ground slide.

## Claims
Paradox, lane:
- Your damage to towers early is menial, so look for poke on the enemies instead. [0:30]
- That would be a great box-routing timing, but at 1:30 boxes have not spawned yet, so there is nothing to route. [0:30]
- Once boxes have spawned, get hard prio and then go box routing. [1:00]
- You are not respecting minions; you took about 200 damage from them that you did not need to take. [1:00]
- With hard prio, box route to the bridge, then get on top of the bridge and think about ganking, though as Paradox ganking is not the most needed thing. [1:30]
- Use your bomb (Pulse Grenade) on the early waves; you could have used it on this wave again to guarantee the wave. [2:01]
- Staying was fine result-wise because you kept constant pressure, but it only worked because they did not respect you; they should have played safer. [3:02]
- When the enemies overextend while you are fed but you have not bought, you are not at a significant advantage in the fight; you need to buy. [3:34]
- If you are saving for Headhunter, play safer and with more respect until you have it, and buy it once you have that many souls saved. [4:05]
- When you win lane this early, you want to be on their side around 8 minutes to see and steal their Sinner's Sacrifices, and the 2 minutes before that are a good window to gank; as a Paradox with swap, really look for a gank. [4:35]
- Denying their farm is fine in theory, but very bad when the whole wave is crashing into your tower. [4:35]
- Mystic Shot is still fine on Paradox without much spirit: it gives more one-shot on the whole Kinetic Carbine combo, and you time it with the carbine. [5:35]
- People like Mystic Shot on Paradox because it has no damage falloff, which suits her long carbine poke shots. [6:06]
- Focus the heal crate when you are near 1 HP defending the wave, because you need the heal as quickly as possible. [6:38]
- Go toward right buff if your team already has left buff secured. [7:08]

Paradox, picks and fights:
- Grouping for mid when you have a Guardian up and they do not gives no real reason to be there. [8:41]
- After looking for a catch on Victor and getting his ult out, don't stay. [9:12]
- As a pick hero, hearing someone on their basement camp and calling teammates over to jump them is good vision. [9:42]
- You cannot really jump frontliners like Victor, Shiv or Abrams, because their time to kill is too high. [9:42]
- Make sure to confirm the orbs. [11:13]
- After a lucky kill, book it out instead of staying. [11:44]
- With the enemy sitting on 3K and the urn already cashed, there is no reason to fish for a fight; you are not healthy, so reset and buy. [11:44]
- Instead of a full reset you could have grabbed the heal crate and then gone to the secret shop. [12:14]
- The issue with playing this aggressively is that your ult is not up. A fresh item spike is the time to look for a pick or a fight, but wait until your ult is up and then use it to look for a pick in their jungle. [13:15]
- You saw four enemies on the map, so put respect on them. [13:46]
- It is more efficient to climb on a hero you enjoy but are bad at than on one you are good at but dislike, because you will play and think about the game more. [14:19]
- If you saw three enemies on one side 30 seconds ago, that is their strong side. Do not look for a pick there as Paradox; teleport to the other side of the map and look for a pick there. [14:49]
- You keep going back in at 1 HP or 20% HP; you are not respecting your HP. [15:50]
- Fortitude is not that good right now. It was good before the heal crate rework, but now one heal crate heals you to full, so just play around heal crates when you are low. [16:22]
- It is bad that you, a pick hero, are the one anchoring your right side lane: showing on vision there relieves pressure on the left side of the map, and the enemies will never run at you unless they have a numbers advantage. [16:22]
- Better: let your Graves hit the lane, and when they chase, you as the pick hero jump down and swap for the kill; they also put much more respect on you. [16:53]
- Playing for their Sinner's Sacrifices with a lot of prio on the right side is fine even without full map info. [17:23]
- When you go in, scream on your mic for teammates to come over. [18:23]
- Hold your swap for when your teammates arrive and collapse, rather than using it early. [18:23]
- Against Sinclair, leaving one person to anchor her Spectral Assistant in case she teleports back is a good way to play. [18:23]
- Drifter and Yamato are both feast or famine, but Yamato can still farm Sinner's Sacrifices and do fine. [18:53]
- Never look for a pick near mid, because enemies there can easily collapse on you. [19:26]
- Victor is not who you want to pick off; you have no kill potential on him. [19:26]
- Fighting mid on their walker is worse than a coin flip. [20:57]
- Drifter has to keep brawling; if you give the enemies a second to breathe you lose stacks, and any time you hit Sinner's Sacrifices you deny yourself stacks, so you really need to know a good fight from a bad one. [20:57]
- For side-lane picks, five people is overkill; you need three, maybe four. [26:15]
- Look for picks on a camp rather than in the lane, since a camp is a better place to find someone alone. [26:15]
- Looking for picks while you have two very bad waves makes it not good. [26:15]
- Check waves while the Rejuvenator is falling (about 8.5 seconds) instead of just waiting on it. [27:16]
- As Paradox with ult up, don't play inside pits; play outside, ready to swap out anyone who jumps in. [27:16]
- Rejuvenator hygiene: once you get the Rejuvenator, run the urn first. [27:16]

Paradox, items:
- Against this comp, Spellbreaker instead of Spirit Lens; definitely don't buy both. [22:02]
- With an Infernus on Toxic Bullets and Escalating Exposure, this is a debuff remover game. [22:02]
- The debuff remover lost its debuff reduction, which is a big nerf, but it now gives spirit resist, so it is worth it against a relatively hard spirit comp; Dispel Magic is very good this game. [22:34]
- Spellbreaker is great into Grey Talon and Sinclair, and that is about it; his route here is Dispel Magic, then Spellbreaker or Spirit Resilience. [23:05]

Summary notes:
- Pre-5 minutes, when you have good wave prio but no kill potential on them under tower, box route; siege when you do have kill potential. [28:18]
- Respect the enemies and your own HP; don't go back in when low. [28:50]
- At half or 60% HP, don't full reset: one heal crate heals you to full, and you can work in the secret shop if you need to buy. [28:50]
- Only look for picks and ganks when your ult is online. [29:20]
- Don't look for ganks on their strong side, and don't look for picks around mid or you get fully collapsed on. [29:20]
- Play around how you show on vision, so you are not poking out on one side and scaring the enemies off. [29:51]
- Short "penny swaps" are not bad, but the further you swap an enemy the more you displace them, so aim for a carbine into a long-range swap into your team, then wall them after the swap and max the time. [29:51]

General and patch notes:
- Almost every melee build is a meme; melee Bebop is not good. [2:01]
- Melee Vindicta is not bad in isolation but is worse than gun Vindicta and plays the hero at an inefficient ratio. [2:32]
- Grey Talon traps are overpowered right now; he suggests letting players shoot them before they arm. [3:02]
- The movement patch lets you jump out of a ground slide; he dislikes the change because the crouch-cancel skill ceiling was cool and helped Ivy. [24:08]
