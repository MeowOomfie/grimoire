# They FIXED this - My thoughts on it
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-09-23
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YGo8YvaW8tQ

## Covers
A short 2024 reaction to Valve's fix for double soaking: each match now assigns dedicated solo and dual lanes, and extra players in a lane split souls at a loss. Explains how the rule works and what it does to ganking. For players confused by the change. This is a 2024 video: lane rules and soul sharing have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- The fix: every match now has dedicated solo lanes and dedicated dual lanes, decided arbitrarily at the start of the match (in his example yellow and blue were dual lanes, green and purple solo lanes). [0:00]
- If more players than intended are in a lane (more than one in a solo lane, more than two in a dual lane), the souls are split in a disadvantageous way, so everyone gets less value. [0:31]
- If a second player comes into a solo lane, the solo player gets half value for his souls. [0:31]
- If a third player joins a dual lane and kills a creep worth 77 at that point, the two laners get 25 each, 50 total instead of 77, so the visitor ruins their soul gain. [1:02]
- He is glad the developers listened and acted quickly once double soaking became widely known, and he prefers the fix to the previous state of double soaking, even though it is not perfect. [1:33]
- The change hurts ganking a lot; before, the lanes were so close together that ganking felt dynamic. [2:03]
- Now a failed gank actively griefs your teammates: if you take creeps in their lane while ganking without getting the kill, you took souls away from them. If you gank, you absolutely need the kill. [2:34]
- Before the change a failed gank was not that bad; now not getting the kill is disastrous. [3:04]
- The fix doesn't feel like a Valve-like decision, because Deadlock usually gives freedom (punch, gun or spirit Abrams builds all work), while this forces players into set lanes. [3:34]
- You cannot choose which lanes are solo or dual, even in tournaments; the game picks them at random every match, which feels scuffed. [4:05]
- His suggestion: if players must be forced, make the side lanes always solo and the middle lanes always dual, for consistency. [4:36]
- Verdict: overall the change is better than before, but it does not feel like a very good solution. [5:06]
