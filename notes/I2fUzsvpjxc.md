## Covers
Shiv coaching VOD review by Midknight of a Ritualist-rank Shiv player, from a 2025 patch (August 2025 video).
Themes: itemization and skipping item colors (never skip green), Shiv greens and Debuff Remover, map and teamfight awareness, overextending in lane, when to give a tower, who to dive as Shiv, and playing as an early-game powerhouse when your micro outpaces your macro.

## Claims
Shiv tech and ability order:
- Correction from Midknight: he had said he prefers maxing dash or knife before the ult, but after more testing he admits maxing the ult feels very nice. [0:00]
- Use Shiv's right click cooldown to chain other abilities: right click into dash, right click into knife, right click into ult. Many newer Shivs never pick this up. [1:02]

Player (Shiv, Ritualist):
- Lane opening read: enemy went Rapid Rounds and Extra Regen, so expect a slower lane with defensive itemization; your Warden hadn't bought yet, so on paper you were worse to fight, though Abrams without dash is not in a great spot to fight either. [2:04]
- Mistake: blood-hounding the low-HP enemy and pushing forward. Both sides did it; whichever player turned on the other with their own low-HP teammate would have won the trade 2v1 instead of chasing a sub-5% kill on the Abrams. [2:04]
- Good dive in lane. [5:13]
- Your soul lead is not big for how you are playing, so be careful: this is a "whoever rotates first wins" spot, and if you overcommit past the line they rotate someone from their two winning lanes and you get jumped. [7:16]
- The game hinges on who carries: your team relies on you or your Warden, theirs on Seven, Ivy or Lash, and whichever carry kills the other first decides a lot. [8:18]
- You have a bad habit of being very jumpable; your micro and Shiv time are clearly strong, but you overextend and throw your lead. [9:20]
- Mistake: with only about a 1k soul lead you cannot kill both Abrams and Haze sieging your tower. The ideal is calling a teammate over to kill the two most-behind enemies brute-force sieging with no Guardian behind them; if nobody comes, give the tower. The worst outcome is what happened: they get the tower and you die. [9:50]
- Do not look at a Walker again when the map only shows three enemies, especially once your Mirage shows on the map, because they can rotate two players onto you and keep the player advantage. [10:53]
- When your lane is pushed and you have map control, grab their center camps or invade their jungle. [11:23]
- Do not seek a fight with the Abrams when you have no backup and do not know if he has backup; pushing that wave was fine but do not push further. [13:58]
- Shiv should take the solo lane wave here since he is kind of the carry; at Phantom or higher he would give it to the struggling Infernus, but at this elo he would just take it. [14:29]
- Still no green items, which is why the Holliday deletes you; it is really bad to have no tankiness while you are the one carrying. [15:29]
- Your resists are near nothing, so she does a crazy amount of damage to you. [16:01]
- Mistake: missing two major team fights entirely due to map awareness; the biggest note of the review. [18:04]
- A mid boss call around 20 minutes would have been fine; at higher elo he would expect it, but at this elo half the team shows up 10 seconds late and the window is small. [18:34]
- When two enemies drop off a zip toward you that is a terrible play by them and your team should collapse on them; since your team is very passive, you sometimes have to force fights yourself, even if you go in late. [20:41]
- Unfortunate death from pushing up too much; play more around your own Walker. [22:17]
- Mistake: farming with Haze nearby and Seven close; Haze can hear you and call Seven over. Lack of map awareness. [23:18]
- If your build and playstyle skip greens, do not buy the one defensive orange item when you buy defensively; buy Extra Regen or Extra Health as the early defensive item. [24:19]
- In the teamfight you were the center of the fight, which is fine, but as an assassin-built Shiv you should be on their backline. Do not go on the Abrams, and especially do not spend your dash on him. [25:53]
- Your dash, especially rage dash, is hyper valuable offensive utility, doing around 600 damage with rage in a hybrid build. Spend it on the Haze; if you had got on her or thinned her before she ulted, the fight would have been far less one-sided. [26:24]
- Summary: as a micro-heavy player you will usually be ahead, so being in big team fights matters even more since you are your team's scary player. [28:26]
- Summary: with Oracle or Phantom micro but Ritualist macro, play as an early-game powerhouse: build a big lead in lane, roam to other lanes to force your lead, and play for any early urn fight. [28:56]
- Summary: you missed the first urn fight, which was game swinging, and got only one T2 camp from that time. [29:26]
- Summary: you were late to another fight later; watch for signals like the enemy sieging or running urn that a big fight is about to happen. [29:56]

Itemization:
- Getting all your colors to some capacity is generally good because investments are valuable. [12:26]
- Higher players skip a color fairly often, but they never skip green: builds are blue and green or orange and green. You can skip one damage type, but skipping health makes you very squishy. [12:58]
- If you skip a damage type, lean hard into the other and be aware you are stacking it for meaningful returns. [13:28]
- Greens Shiv likes: Extra Health and Extra Regen never hurt, Return Fire is good. [13:28]
- Return Fire feels good on Shiv because it almost instantly stacks rage; it is great into Haze and good into Ivy. [13:58]
- Healbane is a great item here; realistically any green would help. [16:32]
- Debuff Remover fits into a comp with Lash ult stun, Ivy Stone Form, Holliday lasso, Seven stun, and Haze sleep, Fixation and stun. [16:32]
- Greens are generally optional on Shiv individually, but you need a decent number of them. [24:49]
- Debuff Remover is technically optional on Shiv, but you buy it in 95% or more of games; skip it only if nothing on the enemy team is removable by it. [24:49]
- This game he wanted 100% Debuff Remover, and Healbane would not hurt. [29:56]
- If you build defensively, go defensive green (Extra Regen, Extra Health), not defensive orange. Defensive orange is only for when you know you will not buy many weapon items but still want defense, so the investment goes to weapon damage instead of health. [30:26]
