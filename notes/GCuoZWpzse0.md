# The OFFICIAL Deadlock Hero Tier List (NEW PATCH)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-04-23
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCuoZWpzse0

## Covers
A high-rank ladder (Eternus+) tier list made three to four days after the 2025-04-17 patch that nerfed lane bullies, removed heavy melee cancel and moved Ethereal Shift to 6k. Every hero gets a placement and a reason, plus a note on the new shove-the-lane meta.
For ranked players, not pro play. Early 2025; the game has changed a lot since.

## Claims
- The tier list is for high-level ladder play (Eternus+ games), not pro play. [0:00]
- The main change this patch is that lane bullies and heroes that were very strong in lane got nerfed quite a bit. [0:31]
- Bebop stays in low A tier: the stamina, healing and spirit resist nerfs make him easier to kill in lane and mid game, but he is still very lethal in lane and a hit hook still wins the lane; chasing for free bomb stacks is just less free. [0:31]
- Hook should be reworked into Bebop's ultimate; as long as it is a normal ability he will be oppressive. [1:34]
- Holliday got nerfed (less left click damage in lane, lasso half a second shorter) but is still the most oppressive initiator in the game, a better Paradox: very mobile, escapes and initiates with jump pads, strong in lane, mid game and as a hard carry. She still needs nerfs. [1:34]
- Lady Geist went up to A tier (maybe low A): almost every other hero got nerfed and she didn't, and she doesn't build many barriers because she wants to take damage for Soul Exchange. She is very tanky, does big damage and a good swap can turn fights. [2:05]
- Lady Geist and Bebop are on about the same level. [2:36]
- Lash was technically nerfed (you must lock on a bit longer to grab people in the ult), but Ethereal Shift, the best counter to Lash that everyone ran last patch, got gutted, so on average he gets more people with his ult. Middle of the pack, not C tier; placed around high B. [2:36] [22:42]
- McGinnis is still the weakest hero in the game: her turrets last 18 seconds longer, which sounds good for defending mid boss or base, but she is still bad at pushing and her split-push strength is bad in the current meta. [3:39]
- Mo & Krill is still S tier: only slight nerfs, and he benefits from the new patch because ganking early punishes your lane partner less (you lose the money but your teammate gets more). He is insane in lane, insane for early ganks and the best anti-carry in the game. [4:42]
- Mo & Krill is rumored to be reworked; there are files in the game with different abilities. [5:13]
- Paradox's nerf was minuscule; she is almost S tier: big early damage, A tier in lane, one of the best ults in the game (about 40 second cooldown), very survivable and tanky. [5:13]
- Pocket is a bit weaker and goes top of B tier: the lane nuke got nerfed slightly, he is still the biggest nuker early mid game with a good start, but he falls off if the game goes long. [5:43]
- Seven is still A tier and probably still the best carry (a hero that wants to farm a lot early): the lightning strike was nerfed a little but its radius increased. [6:14]
- Shiv is low A tier or top of B: Decay was nerfed, but Shiv is very tanky and strong against high HP targets, and the better gun heroes get, the better Shiv gets, because Bloodletting is strong against many small damage-over-time bullets and gun heroes can't burst him, especially with Return Fire or Lead Return Fire. [6:44]
- Sinclair got nerfed a lot (less Vexing Bolt damage, Rabbit Hex nerfed, rabbits move faster, less damage amp); he drops from S to A if played well. He is still strong in lane but no longer an unkillable lane bully that instantly nukes you. He is a very high skill ceiling hero. [8:17]
- Vindicta stays high, maybe even S tier. [8:49]
- Grey Talon was not nerfed enough: his lane damage dropped slightly but arrow scaling at max level is the same, he is still the highest DPS hero and the best nuker, with an ult that can hit anyone on the map. [8:49] [22:07]
- Grey Talon is the better of the two flyers right now; Vindicta got more significant nerfs (less resist shred). Both are only a tiny bit worse than last patch. [9:19]
- Vyper was slept on and got better because everything else that nukes got nerfed; she is one buff or one good build away from being insane. Heavy CC and stuns shut her down, but without CC she lives and kills you. Most underrated hero in the game. [9:50]
- Warden is a noob stomper that works better in low ranks because they don't manage their stamina; with good stamina management he is good in lane and has more sustain so he dies less. Otherwise an okay, perfectly balanced, middle of the pack hero (maybe a bit higher), where before he was really weak. [10:21] [23:14]
- Wraith is still very weak with no buffs: her main damage is Full Auto, and per-bullet barriers absorb it, so against a Combat Barrier she does zero spirit damage, and Metal Skin also blocks it. Green (spirit) items were nerfed so barriers are a bit rarer, but Divine Barrier is still hard meta. [10:52]
- Wraith's main issue is she is so weak in lane that it is very hard to come out at least even, which she needs to carry later. [11:52]
- Abrams is very weak now, the most controversial pick: every good Abrams in Eternus used heavy melee cancel to go in or get out with Shoulder Charge, and that tech is gone. Spirit Abrams is the meta build (nobody plays gun Abrams), and he is just a weaker tank than Mo & Krill, Bebop, Shiv or even Warden. [12:22]
- Calico is still solid and above average: she is more in line with other tanks but still scary in lane and can still nuke; her late game falls off. She is a tank, stall and disruptor hero who can go in, ult to disrupt longer and buy Metal Skin (Ethereal Shift is now 6k). She no longer one-shots you, especially late with Lucky Shot. [13:22] [22:42]
- Melee damage scales with gun damage in this game. [13:54]
- Dynamo goes to lower B: Singularity is a good ult on paper, and his past weakness was lane, but the new lane meta of shoving suits him since he can melee creeps and Kinetic Pulse the enemy. He is very bad in a stall lane where everyone waits in the middle. [14:55]
- New lane meta: shoving the lane over and over seems best against most comps, because as long as your healing creep stays alive you can sustain. Try shoving lanes. [15:25]
- Haze is C tier (below average but playable) and better than Wraith: she got quality of life buffs; played as an assassin, level two Sleep Dagger matters, and she got a 50 DPS ult buff. [15:55]
- Infernus is better than Wraith and Haze as a hard carry but weaker than Seven: low B, then moved a bit lower. Flame Dash needs a lower cooldown or the burn more damage. [16:26] [19:32]
- Infernus's old loop of burning people and stalling with heals no longer works because everyone runs anti-heal, so Infernus players build gun or the "exploder" early nuke build. [16:57]
- Left click (gun) heroes are still kind of weak right now. [19:32]
- Ivy got buffs and might be really strong: her head hitbox is about half the size, so she takes less damage in lane and later. Her urn running bug (full speed flying) was fixed. She is a very solid support, not at Mo & Krill's level. [17:27]
- Kelvin goes to B, the pick he is least sure about: he lost his free base spirit resist and dies a lot faster in fights while spirit is still king. Frozen Shelter (dome) is still insane in coordinated play, but in ranked he can't solo carry; like all supports he depends on his team. [18:30]
- The tier list is more balanced than last patch, closer to a normal distribution, where before half the roster was S and half was C. [19:32]
- Viscous is high A: no nerfs, the best support for healing; Mo & Krill is better for pick-off potential. [20:02]
- Yamato is high B: still a top three laner, but she falls off after lane and needs a lot of farm; Seven is a bit weaker in lane but gets online faster. Yamato is a tank hard carry late, Seven a glass cannon DPS. [20:35]
- Mirage goes to high A: he got nerfed a fair amount (50 less HP, scarabs nerfed, Djinn's Mark slow nerfed), is weak in lane, and is probably no longer the best hero in the game. [21:05]
- Standouts this patch: Paradox and Holliday (the initiators running the game), Mo & Krill (can't die) and Grey Talon (best nuker). [22:07]
- Calico was in an S+ tier of her own last tier list; she is a lot weaker now but by no means average. [22:42]
- This is an early tier list and the meta will likely change. [23:14]
