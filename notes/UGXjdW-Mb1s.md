## Covers
Natan (Eternus 6) reviews his Calico game laning with Lady Geist against Venator and Ivy, a game his team fell behind in and won back with the comeback urn.
Topics: Calico build with no points in Leaping Slash, roaming as Ava, tunnel box runs, urn sides and turn-ins, defending the mid walker, contesting mid boss, flanking, and items (Melee Lifesteal, Transcendent Cooldown, Arctic Blast, Point Blank vs Hunter's Aura).
For mid to high rank Calico players. Game from the patch before mid April 2026.

## Claims
- Against Venator, do not put yourself between Venator and a wall or you instantly lose half your HP. [0:00]
- Calico and Lady Geist are on the stronger side of laners, Venator is decent, and Ivy is weaker but good at neutralizing with her wave clear and stone form. [0:30]
- After the first wave you can buy a tier one item; buy Melee Lifesteal on Calico. [0:30]
- Leaping Slash counts as a light melee, so it procs Melee Lifesteal healing every time you hit someone with it. [0:30]
- His build puts no points in Leaping Slash: it felt unreliable, enemies took too long to die after the slash so he missed the souls, and it gives no extra damage to kill with. [1:01]
- Instead, prioritize the first two points in Ava and the ultimate. [1:01]
- Two points in Ava give 100% uptime on the cat, so you can roam the map permanently as Ava, plus a lot of movement speed. [1:32]
- The ult is your playmaking tool, so get it off cooldown as fast as possible. [1:32]
- The second ult point gives 120 extra damage if you hit both casts and more movement speed during it, so chasing down people is more reliable. [1:32]
- In lane you almost always want to push the wave as fast as you can, so while enemies clear it under their tower you can do something else, like grab their boxes. [2:02]
- Clear the wave even if your lane partner walks away, because pushing is what frees you to act. [2:02]
- With the enemies stuck under tower he took their boxes and looked to roam to a fight in the yellow lane while his Lady Geist cleared the mid wave solo. [2:33]
- Mistake: opening with Leaping Slash on Infernus. The slash is also your dash, so start by shooting and attaching Gloom Bombs, then use the slash to close in when he runs past you to his tower. [3:04]
- Using abilities instantly to burst is a natural bad habit that is tricky to overcome. [3:04]
- He fought Ivy instead of roaming green because green was not the most gankable lane at that moment. [3:04]
- Use Melee Lifesteal's cooldown on enemy heroes; on minions it only gives about 30 health. [3:35]

- Roam with purpose, not randomly through a different lane, and let your teammates know. [4:05]
