## Covers
Warden gameplay commentary by aquatiq, May 2026 patch.
A played game with item reasoning: Opening Rounds rush for bullet velocity, Battle Vest, Slowing Bullets, max Binding Word for the silence, Mercurial Magnum, Surge of Power, Colossus and Crippling Headshot.

## Claims
- Warden is very strong right now, though he finds the hero boring. [1:31]
- Opening Rounds is really strong right now: it gives weapon damage, spirit power and bullet velocity; he buys it as soon as possible. [2:01]
- The bullet velocity from Opening Rounds is very strong on Warden, especially in lane. [3:32]
- Throwing Alchemical Flask between two enemies hoping one walks into it missed both; throw it in one direction instead. [3:02]
- Heroes with burst like Paradox, Seven and Lash are easy to headshot. [4:35]
- When the enemy lane is gunning you down, Battle Vest is a good next item. [7:13]
- Rebuttal is not bad when both lane opponents melee. [8:46]
- When his team is all on one side of the map and the enemy on the other, he farms the side lane alone. [11:21]
- Slowing Bullets is the way on Warden. [15:03]
- He maxes Binding Word (cage) next so it gets its silence. [15:34]
- Warden is not a favorable 1v1 for most characters. [18:16]
- He greeds Mercurial Magnum mid game. [18:16]
- Build after his 4.8k spike: Enchanter's Emblem, then Surge of Power, then Colossus. [20:48]
- Because he chains enemies and hits them in the head constantly, he buys Crippling Headshot. [24:22]
- With max Binding Word the enemy cannot move or act; their only answer is a counter spell. [24:54]
- He can take mid boss alone if needed while the enemy is down. [26:58]
- After Colossus he walks into fights like a tank; he considers the game over once he has it. [28:00]
