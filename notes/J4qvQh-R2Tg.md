## Covers
Lady Geist bomb build (Essence Bomb focus), from aquatiq.
Commentary over a ranked game against Viper and Shiv, with lane items and a few macro calls.
Recorded 2026-09-28.

## Claims
- On bomb Lady Geist he buys Rusted Barrel first in this lane. [0:30]
- He finds normal Lady Geist boring but finds bomb Lady Geist fun. [1:01]
- In a hard lane he follows up with Extra Regen or Mystic Regeneration for sustain. [2:37]
- He took Life Drain early in lane. [2:37]
- He plays the bomb build partly because he does not feel like aiming. [4:11]
- Her kit spends HP to do things, so being low HP is fine when the ultimate is up. [5:45]
- Do not sit at low HP when the ultimate is down, or you will just die. [6:46]
- Next items: a green spirit lifesteal item, then Tank Buster. [8:26]
- Maxing the bomb is the spike; once the bomb was maxed he called the hero broken. [8:59]
- With a maxed bomb he could defend a lane against two enemies. [10:32]
- The bomb's lingering pool goes through walls but the explosion does not. [14:41]
- On a spirit build, pick up the casting (spirit) buff when it is in front of you. [18:57]
- Life Drain can be used on the mid boss. [20:40]
- With no green (vitality) items he explodes as soon as he takes damage. [22:47]
- He pops Infuser while using Life Drain. [28:36]
- He used Disarming Hex only on Shiv, who was on the right click build. [32:21]
- After taking shrines, drop the Patron and leave because the waves are bad; if they chase you can turn on them. [28:36]
- Pushing too deep with no wave loses the fight. [29:09]
- Do not let enemy waves walk up on your base; someone has to clear them or you lose the game. [30:10]
- Even when you could end, keep the minion waves as a safety net before going in. [31:18]
- Think about the minion waves before committing to the base. [31:49]
