## Covers
Three minute Venator guide (July 2026): why to play him, each ability, combos, early, mid and late game plans, recommended builds and one stun trick. For players new to Venator.

## Claims
- Pros: very high damage, scales extremely well, a threat in lane, and easy and one-dimensional. [0:01]
- Con: as gun carries go he has no get-out-of-jail card like Wraith's teleport, so he is generally very immobile. [0:01]
- His 1 (Concentrating Grenade in the captions) explodes and lights anyone hit on fire briefly, applying a damage over time and briefly reducing their healing. [0:01]
- His 2, Gut Shot, knocks back anyone in front of him and briefly stuns them if they hit a wall. [0:01]
- With two points in Gut Shot you can heavy melee someone stunned by it without them being able to parry; at max level it enhances your next heavy melee. [0:32]
- His 3, Hexline Spike Trap, kicks a trap forward that arms after a short delay and roots and reveals anyone who steps in it. [0:32]
- Enemies can shoot the traps to break them, so throw them against cover or somewhere unexpected. [0:32]
- His ultimate pulls out a crossbow with three bolts; the final bolt deals extra true damage with a minor execute, and at max level all bolts are enhanced. [0:32]
- Combo: with two points in Gut Shot, Gut Shot into a heavy melee. [1:03]
- Combo: with an item like Superior Duration extending the stun, follow Gut Shot with a trap to root them after the stun. [1:03]
- Combo: Gut Shot then immediately ultimate to guarantee a free headshot. [1:03]
- Combo without duration: predict where the enemy is going, throw the trap there, then Gut Shot them so they are stunned on top of it. [1:03]
- All his abilities except the traps scale with raw gun damage, so flat weapon damage is very valuable on him. [1:03]
- Early game: min-max your farm. Fight in lane only if you face weak laners you think you can beat. [1:33]
- Farm every camp on spawn: tier one at 2 minutes and every 1 minute 25 seconds after, tier twos at 6 minutes, sentinels at 8 minutes. [1:33]
- If you fight in lane, most of your damage comes from Gut Shot, so trade around it and its cooldown. [1:33]
- Mid game: go to a side lane, farm and shove the wave without missing one, then take camps. [1:33]
- The only exception is Urn: have a teammate take it, go to the Urn's side, be in that fight and have your ult online, since it gives a ton of damage there. That is why Venators currently max their ult first. [2:03]
- Late game: circle the fight, stay alive because you are squishy, play around supports who can peel for you, deal as much gun damage as you can and use your own traps for self peel. [2:03]
- Recommended build: Osis's Venator build, by far the best right now; Ronin's Venator build is a very different alternative. [2:35]
- Bonus tip: indoors, if an enemy is too far from a wall to stun, look up at the ceiling and use Gut Shot; if they would have connected with the ceiling off the ricochet, they travel a short distance and are stunned. [2:35]
