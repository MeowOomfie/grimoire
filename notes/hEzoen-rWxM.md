# More Deadlock Shadow Changes | Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-02-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEzoen-rWxM

## Covers
Midknight reacts to a small shadow patch released 2026-02-05.
Main focus is Silver (fire rate, the 1 tier swap, tier three changes, Lykan form cooldown) and Graves (gun range, Jar of Dead changes, new max order), plus Bebop, Dispel Magic, resilience items, Mystic Reverb and Street Brawl changes.
Patch reaction with a verdict on each change.

## Claims
Bebop:
- Uppercut tier three healing was slightly nerfed; tank Bebop has been doing work, so good. [0:00]
- Spirit Bebop scaling got a lot of nerfs; it has been terrorizing low elo, with one Bebop reaching about 190 stacks. [0:00]
- Against Bebop buy Dispel Magic (formerly Debuff Remover) or Counterspell to dodge his bombs. [0:30]

Graves:
- Close Quarters and Point Blank now work with her gun; build those on Graves. [0:30]
- Don't overinvest in her gun: rely on her ult and her 1 for spirit damage and buy gun items for utility, like Split Shot, Active Reload, Toxic Bullets and Recharging Rush. [0:30]
- Fire rate was fixed, but he doesn't recommend fire rate on her; with Toxic Bullets you can, but it doesn't feel good. [1:01]
- Gun range cut by 2 meters is a major nerf. [1:01]
- Never buy Restorative Shot on Graves: she is a two-stamina hero, and at 16 m only about two heroes can be approached without taking more than the 40 HP it heals (four heroes at 18 m). [1:01]
- Restorative Shot on Graves only works as a pushing-the-lead item to perma harass when ahead, never as a defensive regen item; a good player will run you down when you walk up to hit it. [1:31]
- The gun nerf will hurt most in lane; the movement speed buff helps later, so this buffs her late game. [2:01]
- Starting with two Jar of Dead charges makes waves one to three feel better and gives utility if they run at you. [2:01]
- Jar of Dead ghouls now attack staggered instead of all at once, so they can no longer be cleared with one light melee; he calls it great for the hero's long-run health. [2:31]
- Pickup range almost doubled, a lane quality of life buff so you don't get punished walking up. [3:01]
- Shooting ghouls now slows them, which helps survival; Jar cooldown went up a bit. [3:01]
- Since her ult changed from max HP to current HP damage, the Graves meta shifts from maxing ult first to maxing Jar (the 1) first. [3:01]
- Suggested order: maybe two points into the 3 in lane, then max the 1, then the ult, then the 2. [3:31]
- Current HP damage gets more valuable later because people have bigger max HP pools. [4:04]
- You still don't max the 3 early; maybe before the 2, but Split Shot does the same thing and is 5 AP cheaper. [4:04]

Silver:
- Base fire rate up, about 11% DPS, which doesn't matter for damage; it matters because she can now kill minions and shoot the orb in lane. [4:04]
- He liked her old unique lane where she couldn't kill minions with her gun without getting orbs denied, and is sad it is gone, though it is good for lower ranks. [4:35]
- She got one extra ammo; Express Shot still only takes one extra ammo (he tested it), so it still feels good. [5:05]
- Base regen buff helps a weak laner who is a monster mid to late game. [5:05]
- Her 1 got more cooldown, and the tiers swapped: tier one is now +15% weapon damage, tier two is -7 s cooldown. [5:36]
- He dislikes the swap: it mirrors Abrams' tier one dash (+2.2 damage per bullet), the strongest 1 AP spike in the game, which makes Abrams a monster in lane once he has 900 souls. [6:06]
- Expect Silvers to put one point in the 1, then a point in the 2 for cooldown, making her very oppressive in lane at 2 AP. [6:36]
- Respect Silver between 900 and about 2,800 souls (1 to 3 AP); at 3,600 souls she has her ult and is oppressive again, so the punish window is only 2.8k to 3.6k souls. [6:36]
- The -7 s cooldown tier two is still significant and probably still her first tier two. [7:37]
- Tier three on the 1 current-health damage cut from 10% to 8% is a great change; he expects it to keep dropping, to 6% or lower, with 7% the highest it stays. [7:37]
- Percent current health damage is unfun because it ignores what items either side buys; missing health damage feels better because it is more counterable and takes more skill to time. [8:38]
- Tier three kick went to -35% damage (from -25%) plus 80 spirit damage, and the bola tier three changed from ricocheting to two others to hitting three people for 5 s; he dislikes both buffs. [9:08]
- Her tier three spikes were already ridiculous; people skip maxing her 2 and 3 because the 1 max and ult max are so strong, not because those maxes are weak. [10:09]
- Still max the 1 and then the ult: the ult max gives a refund and the 1 max gets you into fury form with more healing and damage. [10:09]
- With these buffs her late game will feel monstrous. [10:41]
- Attack range up is quality of life; helmet nerfed by 15%, which he likes because she already feels unkillable in Lykan form. [10:41]
- She has Fleet Foot built into Lykan mode, so he doesn't like buying Fleet Foot on her. [10:41]
- Her ult now gets extra damage from Point Blank and Close Quarters, but still prioritize shred first; go Hollow Point for teamfight play, or Hunter first for individual play hunting their jungle and side lanes. [11:12]
- Maybe buy Point Blank later for gun damage after your shred item, but at high level you will still prioritize more shred or utility like Inhibitor, since she applies it quickly with her 1; never buy these before your shred item. [11:43]
- Transform cooldown went from 22 to 35 seconds, so once Lykan form expires she is very vulnerable; turn on her and chase her when it is about to expire. [12:13]
- One of the best counters right now is Metal Skin against her 1 so she can't get into Lykan mode. [12:43]
- Lykan form bonus health reduced and movement speed slightly buffed; Go for the Throat (her 1 in fury form) and its tier three lose some damage, which also lowers its flat 65% life steal. [13:14]
- Still max her 1 first because it is so strong. [13:14]
- Her 1 cooldown is 1.5 s longer, meaning less healing, damage and missing health damage; his favorite nerf. [13:44]
- If teammates keep feeding her Lykan mode so Metal Skin can't stop her, Warp Stone is a good option to kite her out. [13:44]

Items:
- Dispel Magic (formerly Debuff Remover) got 2% more debuff resist; he has mixed feelings. [13:44]
- Frontlines' best defensive options are percentage reduction because of their big HP pool; backlines want Counterspell, Warp Stone or Dispel Magic, items that make the ability not affect you. [14:45]
- It is hard for Dispel Magic to outperform Counterspell; the one case he would buy it was Shiv knives, and those were nerfed, so he can't think of many worlds where he buys it over Counterspell. [15:15]
- He is glad an item he has called OP for 6 months (40% spirit resist) got nerfed but thinks it isn't enough: drop it to about 30%, stop Fleet Foot removing its downside, or make it orange. [15:45]
- Mystic Reverb lost some life steal; it felt oppressive on heroes like Yamato imbuing her 1. [16:16]
- Resilience items now trigger 5% HP higher but give 8% less resistance, pushing them toward frontlines. [16:16]
- He likes the vision of making tanks buy tank items, but frontlines are the strongest role and build pure damage (like Abrams one-shotting people); buffing tank items without nerfing the damage items they buy leads to an awful meta. [16:46]

Street Brawl:
- Base healing is twice as fast; queuing random now gives an extra reroll on round three as well as rounds one and two. [17:18]
- He thinks that incentive is too small; he suggests a reroll every round, better odds of enhanced items, a guaranteed legendary on round five, or an extra item round. [17:48]
- McGinnis ult duration nerfed, good since she has been oppressive in the mode. [18:49]
- Graves Jar cooldown up 3 seconds doesn't fix her core issue of infinite jars; he suggests her jar should need about 120% of the normal-mode souls to charge. [18:49]
