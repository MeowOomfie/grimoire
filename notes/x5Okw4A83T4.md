# The Ultimate Guide To Seven - Shockingly Strong Carry
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2025-09-28  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x5Okw4A83T4

## Covers
Seven full hero guide: Lightning Ball, Static Charge, Power Surge, Storm Cloud (post-nerf), skill order, passive laning, lowest base regen and big head hitbox, jungle farming, kiting and slide-shooting, Power Surge plus stun build around Superior Duration, Cultist, Surge of Power, Boundless Spirit, Silence Wave, Infuser, situational items and sell order, counters (Counterspell, Debuff Remover), Victor, Infernus, Haze, Mina, Ivy, Dynamo, Pocket, Abrams

## Claims
- Seven is a damage dealer that can hit the whole enemy team at once; he has a below average laning stage, gets better in the mid game and turns into a hard carry in the late game. [0:00]
- He benefits mostly from spirit items, has decent gun damage scaling, and benefits from vitality items later on. [0:31]
- All four abilities are active and all are AoE, so Seven is amazing at team fighting; built correctly, the abilities get more radius the longer the game goes. [0:31]
- Think of Seven as a massive AoE damage dealer that can turn into an unkillable carry monster. [1:02]
- Lightning Ball slows down and damages anything in its radius that can take damage (creeps, players, jungle creeps), but after a while passes through and keeps going. [1:02]
- To use Lightning Ball well on a player, throw it on the ground right below you or the enemy; with a downward trajectory it keeps ticking on that spot. [1:32]
- If the enemy is running, predict their path and throw the ball where they will run; in close quarters throw it below you. [2:03]
- Lightning Ball upgrades give extra charges, a movement slow, and finally more DPS and radius, but they are not very important for this build. [2:03]
- Some builds make Lightning Ball the main damage ability, but that is much less consistent and most pro and high level players do not do it; treat the ball as a tool to speed up farming and deal close range damage. [2:33]
- Static Charge puts a debuff on an enemy that triggers after a few seconds and stuns them and enemies around them in a radius shown on the ground. [2:33]
- Static Charge upgrades lower its cooldown and increase the radius and stun duration; at max level it is a really big AoE field that easily hits multiple people. [3:04]
- Static Charge is the second ability to level and max in this build, and it is really strong right now. [3:04]
- Power Surge is the main thing that makes Seven work: while buffed, every bullet or burst you hit also deals spirit damage, which jumps to enemies around the target. [3:34]
- Leveled up, Power Surge jumps to six targets, so shooting one player can hit the entire enemy team if they are close. [4:04]
- Each Seven burst has three bullets; hitting just one of them procs full Power Surge damage, so you must hit at least one bullet per burst. [4:04]
- Power Surge is the main tool in every stage: clearing and shoving waves in lane, farming afterwards, and the main damage source later. [4:35]
- Storm Cloud lifts Seven into the air and creates a storm around him that damages enemies over time; while active you can cast a Thunder Strike that does extra damage and boops enemies away. [4:35]
- Leveling Storm Cloud makes you more resistant while in it, especially to bullet damage, increases radius and DPS, and at max level allows movement control. [5:07]
- Until the latest patch Seven was mainly played by maxing the ultimate and winning fights by sitting in it; Storm Cloud got nerfed quite hard and is no longer the main way to deal damage or stay alive. [5:07]
- Use Storm Cloud when there are multiple enemy targets around you; it is not great against fewer targets. [5:39]
- With spirit lifesteal and Leech or Infuser, the more enemies in Storm Cloud's radius the more you heal, so think of it as a surviving tool, not the killing tool: cast it when multiple enemies start focusing you. [6:10]
- Seven has the lowest base regen in the game, 1 HP a second, so he struggles to recover from creep, Tower or player damage. [6:41]
- Because of the low regen you do not want to take much damage, and you cannot trade well with enemy players early until you get certain items; play the laning stage passively. [7:12]
- Seven has one of the biggest, if not the biggest, head hitboxes in the game, so heroes whose effects proc more on headshots (Infernus burn, Haze Fixation at twice the stacks, the Mina 3) proc them easily on him. [7:12]
- Seven is volatile in lane; be careful. [7:44]
- Skill order in lane: usually Power Surge first (you can also take Static Charge first), then Static Charge. [7:44]
- In early lane, play cover, activate Power Surge and push the creep wave; it easily damages multiple creeps and nearby enemy players. [8:14]
- You win the lane on Seven by killing the enemy wave faster than they kill yours; unlike heroes who want to trade a lot, Seven shoves first and then damages the enemy player. [8:44]
- First items: Monster Rounds to kill creeps faster, then Improved Spirit for spirit damage. [8:44]
- Seven is one of the characters that benefits most from spirit, because the shock damage applies to every shot you hit, so the effective scaling is insane even if the numbers do not look it. [8:44]
- Spirit raises Power Surge damage massively and also Lightning Ball and Static Charge, but buy spirit or anything that makes the 3 stronger, since that also speeds up farming of waves and camps. [9:15]
- If you did a good job in lane, Seven is the fastest jungle farmer in the game for the entire game once out of laning stage. [9:45]
- Usually one point in the Power Surge upgrade, then one in the stun; with both at level two, Power Surge comes off cooldown for every creep wave. [9:45]
- Power Surge can be used aggressively after shoving to shoot players, but early it does not do much damage. [10:16]
- The stun can secure lane kills but mostly keeps the enemy away: if they walk up close, stun them; if they do not back out, they are stunned and you can follow with Lightning Ball and kill them. [10:16]
- The low stun cooldown means you have it almost every wave, so enemies have to respect it. [10:47]
- "Winning" lane on Seven really means not losing it: a bad laning stage is the one thing that holds him back, and going even with your money up is enough. [10:47]
- The later the game goes the better for Seven; he is the best carry in the game other than Victor, so worry if the enemy has a Victor. [11:17]
- If you are confident in your skill, Seven wins the longer the game goes as long as you do not fall too far behind in money; do not rush things. [11:17]
- For early team fighting there are better characters; Seven is the 1v6 hyper carry of the late game, though you can always join an Urn fight or a big team fight and use Storm Cloud. [11:47]
- Play the laning stage passively and get out with an okay amount of money so you can start farming jungle camps. [11:47]
- Power Surge goes to level 2 then level 5; the level 5 upgrade more than doubles Power Surge damage and increases its jump radius. [11:47]
- In every build, always max Power Surge first. [12:17]
- He usually buys Spirit Shielding in lane for extra HP and a barrier in fights. [12:47]
- Next is Cultist, which he calls insane on Seven right now and may get nerfed; he ran it for about three months before others caught on. [12:47]
- Cultist gives health and ability range; ability range helps every Seven ability (bigger ball, bigger stun radius, bigger Power Surge jump radius, bigger ult). [13:18]
- Fire rate on burst heroes gives diminishing returns compared with full auto heroes like Wraith, so 25 to 30% fire rate on Seven is not the same as on Wraith. [13:18]
- Fire rate is still solid on Seven because Power Surge procs on every burst, so fire rate also raises Power Surge damage; he says this from damage testing over about a thousand games. [14:21]
- Cultist speeds up creep farming and accelerates the snowball; Seven is a snowball character who comes out of lane and outfarms everyone in the jungle. [14:51]
- Out of the jungle you can be 5K to 10K ahead of everyone, and even if not, his late game power per level is better than most characters. [15:22]
- Coming out of the laning stage you should be around 7,000 to 8,000 souls and can start farming. [15:22]
- Then Enchanter's Emblem: some spirit resist in case you must fight, but mainly spirit power and ability cooldown to speed up farming. [15:22]
- Farm pattern: sit on a side lane, clear the wave fast with Power Surge, go right back into the jungle, and vice versa; any creep camp dies in seconds. [15:53]
- Then Surge of Power, on Power Surge: it raises its spirit scaling (another 20 to 30% damage) and gives fire rate, movement speed and no slow while shooting when you use Power Surge. [16:23]
- Seven is the only hero whose movement speed scales with spirit power; this is movement speed, not sprint speed, so it stays in combat. [16:23]
- Sprint speed stops as soon as you take damage or start shooting; Seven's spirit movement speed stays always, so he gets faster the longer the game goes. [16:54]
- Kiting is important on Seven: stay in your effective range while moving forward or back and stay outside the enemy's effective range, for example backing off while shooting an Abrams and following if he runs. [17:25]
- Kite at medium to long range, where Seven still does maximum damage, without being in the enemy's effective range; play him as a runner and gunner, because the gun is what matters even though most damage is spirit. [17:55]
- Then Duration Extender and Superior Duration rather than Superior Cooldown: the goal is Power Surge active 100% of the time, lasting until it is off cooldown again. [18:26]
- Cooldown raises Power Surge uptime a bit early, but Superior Duration works on items while Superior Cooldown (same 3.2K) does not; cooldown on items now needs Transcendent Cooldown at 6K. [18:58]
- Duration raises Cultist uptime: the Cultist buff normally lasts 160 seconds on a 250 second cooldown, a 90 second dead window; with duration the downtime drops to about 45 seconds. [19:30]
- Duration also lengthens Power Surge and, most importantly for this build, the stun. [20:32]
- After laning and some farm, put one value point in Lightning Ball (two charges) and one in Storm Cloud (more bullet resist for defensive use), then max the stun. [20:32]
- Max Static Charge stuns for 1.8 seconds, about 2.3 seconds with Superior Duration, so enemies are often dead before they get up. [21:02]
- With Cultist active the max stun radius is ridiculously big; one good stun on the right target hits multiple enemies and often wins the team fight by itself. [21:34]
- He calls Static Charge the best stun in the game outside ultimates like Black Hole or Lash's ult, on a 20 second cooldown. [22:04]
- At this point you are still not strong enough to really fight; if the enemy pushes your Walker, sit on it and shove the creeps back with Power Surge, but mostly keep farming. [22:04]
- With these items Power Surge (30 second cooldown) lasts long enough to clear several tier 3 camps in one activation, with about a 7 second downtime. [22:35]
- Next Spirit Sap, later upgraded into Silence Wave; optional, but it lowers enemy spirit resist so your spirit damage hits harder. [23:05]
- After it you are at 44 extra spirit power, which he calls the sweet spot of spirit investment; it is essential to reach that early on Seven. [23:36]
- Then a flex slot item: Fleetfoot (more aggressive, faster farming and chasing) or Spirit Lifesteal (more survivable). [23:36]
- If you only have one flex slot, pick one of those and spend on the next three upgrades, which need no slot; by about 28K you should have two flex slots. [24:06]
- Boundless Spirit is the major power spike; you are way past 100 spirit and Power Surge does about 100 spirit damage per shot, more with the Sap debuff. [24:36]
- After the core, max Storm Cloud for survivability, then max Lightning Ball in the late game. [25:07]
- Silence Wave also benefits from Superior Duration: about a 4 second silence that can hit multiple people in a line so they cannot fight back with abilities. [25:07]
- After that the worry is surviving or anti-healing: he gets Escalating Resilience and Toxic Bullets; Toxic Bullets scales with spirit and benefits from duration. [25:37]
- Superior Duration improves Cultist, Surge of Power, Fleetfoot, Silence Wave and Toxic Bullets, all for 3.2K; the build is designed around it. [26:07]
- Next Infuser; Leech is an alternative if you are not comfortable using four actives. [26:07]
- Many players avoid actives because they are hard; the only fix is to buy them and practice, since active items are some of the best in the game and win games when used well. [26:37]
- Infuser with duration lasts about 8 seconds; when low in a fight, pop Infuser and Storm Cloud plus Thunder Strike and you heal back to near full almost instantly (about 2.8K in a test). [27:07]
- With Infuser active you regen even while the enemy focuses you; Infuser into ult into Thunder Strike is the get-out button. [27:38]
- That core is all you need to kill anyone and win team fights; everything after counters specific threats. [28:09]
- In fights always keep damage on the enemy: keep shooting with Power Surge even without ult; pop Infuser when low or for damage, since the lightning bounces heal you a lot. [28:40]
- Everything heals you through spirit lifesteal, including Toxic Bullets procs and Lightning Ball, so the key is never stop shooting. [29:10]
- Play passive the whole game: farm the jungle, keep waves pushed, and only show up for a big Urn fight or a big fight in the middle, where Seven is always strong. [29:42]
- Avoid fighting otherwise, especially small skirmishes, unless you have far more money than the enemy, until your core and Boundless Spirit are ready. [29:42]
- With Boundless Spirit and Silence Wave ready you can start hunting enemies; Silence Wave plus the stun is deadly to most heroes. [30:13]
- Some heroes can dodge Static Charge: Ivy (stone form), Dynamo (teleport), Pocket (satchel), Abrams (charging out of range). [30:13]
- Silence Wave then stun works because the silence lasts 4 seconds and the stun goes off after 3.5, so they cannot escape it without an item. [30:43]
- Counterspell and Debuff Remover are the hardest counters to Seven; check enemy items and stun the players who do not have them. [30:43]
- A stunned enemy without those items is silenced before the stun lands and then on the floor for another two and a half seconds. [31:14]
- If the stunned enemy dies before the stun goes off, it goes off immediately, so you can put the stun on a low enemy and kill them to stun everyone around them at once. [31:14]
- Players around the stunned target usually do not see the stun coming and cannot debuff remove it because it is on someone else, so a big AoE stun often catches several. [32:16]
- To get value from the ball, use it a lot, throw it on walls behind enemies or on the floor at a downward angle so it stops where you want; avoid throwing it straight, since it passes through and is wasted. [32:46]
- Press Power Surge whenever it is up. [33:18]
- With Infuser and Boundless Spirit, Power Surge duration also scales with spirit, so it is up the whole time. [33:48]
- Ult tech: casting Storm Cloud from higher ground or right after dashing off a zipline puts you much higher; at max level you can then fly higher and move. [33:48]
- High in the air enemies do less gun damage due to damage falloff, while Storm Cloud still does full damage anywhere in its area. [34:50]
- To secure a fleeing kill, ult and immediately press Thunder Strike for burst. [34:50]
- Thunder Strike boops: cast right behind an enemy to boop them back into you, or in front of their feet to push them away. [35:20]
- Because Storm Cloud was nerfed, its overall damage is lower than just shooting with Power Surge, even against three or four clumped enemies. [35:50]
- Use the ult defensively to get back to full with Infuser, live longer with its bullet resist, and as area denial. [36:22]
- Storm Cloud is strong at Mid Boss (it hits everyone in the arena) and when attacking or defending a Patron, forcing enemies to choose between cover, the Seven and his team. [36:52]
- The ult is better than Power Surge when enemies are split apart, since it hits everyone in line of sight and its lifesteal is huge. [37:52]
- This is a Power Surge and stun build first and an ult build second; full ult is not great anymore, almost nobody plays it, and it is less skillful. [37:52]
- Toxic Bullets and Escalating Resilience both proc from your bullets. [38:23]
- With Surge of Power and Fleetfoot active, Seven's movement speed lets him slide while shooting almost indefinitely: hold crouch on each burst to sync it and you barely lose ammo. [38:53]
- For Mid Boss you can do one long slide and slide occasionally instead. [40:00]
- After Infuser you have enough damage to win the game; late items are about staying alive in a spirit meta. [40:30]
- Spellbreaker is the anti-burst spirit item: against Grey Talon, Mina, Pocket and Lash it cuts burst ability damage and gives debuff and spirit resist. [41:00]
- Metal Skin against a scary gun carry; it lasts 5 seconds at base, about 6.4 with duration. [41:00]
- Infernus is a great counter to Seven because burning Seven is easy; buy Debuff Remover when needed. [41:31]
- Buy Counterspell against hard stuns that can kill you, such as Wraith ult, Infernus ult and Lash. [42:04]

- Siphon Bullets against a mix of bullet and spirit damage: more bullet resist and another layer of healing per shot. [43:05]
- Unstoppable when one Counterspell cannot handle the enemy's hard stuns, such as Mo & Krill or Dynamo. [43:35]
- An item captioned "dragon" is anti-gun: it gives a 40% fire rate reduction to everyone shooting you, including while in ult. [43:35]
- Sell order: Spirit Shielding first, then Enchanter's Emblem when you get Spellbreaker, then Cultist (or Silence Wave if you need Unstoppable), then Fleetfoot; Juggernaut is a luxury upgrade that keeps you fast. [44:05]
