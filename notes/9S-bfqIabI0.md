# Best Heroes For Anchoring | Deadlock Tier List
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-04-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9S-bfqIabI0

## Covers
Part two of Midknight's laning series: a tier list of every hero's ability to anchor (hold a lane alone while the partner box farms or roams). Rates wave clear and anti-dive for each hero.
Tiers used: great anchor, good anchor, okay, can anchor, do not leave alone. For players setting up roam and anchor lane pairs.

## Claims
- Anchoring is being left alone in a lane during laning while your partner box farms or roams to gank another lane. [0:00]
- The anchor's goal is to thin the waves so the enemies can't crash a wave or two into you and then dive you; heroes with good anti-dive ults excel at it. [0:00]
- If you're struggling to anchor, buy Monster Rounds to clear waves faster, and respect the enemies. [0:00]
- Abrams: can anchor; doesn't love it, but his 1 gives good wave clear (charge the wave, then melee it) and he's tanky enough not to get dove often. [0:30]
- Apollo: okay (bottom of can anchor); lots of damage and not easy to dive, but wave clear isn't ideal and he doesn't scale well. [0:30]
- Bebop: half-decent anchor; hook and bomb give a lot of AoE and clear waves quickly. [0:30]
- Billy: overrated as an anchor; his 1 anchors one wave very well, but on cooldown he has no other AoE or wave clear; very hard to dive though. [1:00]
- Calico: okay; a worse Apollo, a bit of AoE, quite diveable apart from ult survivability, and doesn't want to be left alone. [1:00]
- Celeste: okay, same as Calico; a little survivability in her 2 but very little AoE. [1:30]
- The Doorman: okay; AoE in his Bell, not the best wave clear, but his gun does decent creep damage with Monster Rounds; his only anti-dive is his cart. [1:30]
- Drifter: below okay (he adds a tier between okay and do not leave alone); some 1v1 potential, very little AoE, bad at clearing waves. [1:30]
- Dynamo: decent AoE and his kicks do decent wave damage, but he does badly alone and is very easy to dive without ult. [2:01]
- Graves: not a bad anchor; her ult gives anti-dive and split push potential, her root does too and has slept-on wave clear when tossed in lane, and her skulls give decent middle wave clear; she can still get chipped down by waves being run into her. [2:01]
- Grey Talon: do not leave alone; he clears from very far with his 1 but gets dove easily and his tower falls quickly. [2:31]
- Haze: not a great anchor; some self-peel, very little wave clear. [2:31]
- Holliday: okay; good wave clear from her barrels, but she wants to be the one roaming. [2:31]
- Infernus: good anchor who will anchor relatively often, not quite want-to-anchor tier because his wave clear isn't the best. [2:31]
- M1 (gun) heroes tend to land in the want-to-anchor (great anchor) tier. [3:01]
- Ivy: great anchor; she doesn't really want to anchor, but Kudzu Bomb and stone form make her insanely good at it. [3:01]
- Kelvin: don't leave him alone; decent wave clear but his gun does little, he relies on abilities and will probably be dove on the second wave. [3:01]
- Lady Geist: great anchor and the best anchor in the game (his opinion); a ton of AoE from her bomb and her ult makes her basically impossible to dive. [3:31]
- Lash: not a good anchor; some AoE and some survivability when ult is up, but don't leave him under tower. [3:31]
- McGinnis: really good anchor; great wave clear, hard to dive with her wall and turrets, and left alone with your tower she'll make it explode. [3:31]
- Mina: bottom of good anchor; doesn't want to anchor but can; her 2 gives survivability and her 1 cleans up creeps. [4:02]
- Mirage: good anchor, becoming great later; early wave clear isn't the best, so although he likes to farm and is a strong laner, waves can be shoved into him fairly easily. [4:02]
- Mo & Krill: good anchor; good wave clear from Scorn, good self-survivability, and he can use his sand in a pinch to clear a wave without taking damage. [4:02]
- Paige: deceptively good anchor; good wave clear with her 3 and dragon, and her 3, ult and 2 give anti-dive. Sometimes played mid with a roamer so they swap who roams and who anchors. [4:32]
- Paradox: okay; her bomb gives lots of wave clear, but like Holliday she'd rather gank once ult is online. [4:32]
- Pocket: pretty good anchor; good AoE, and cloaking every wave essentially gives full wave priority; lots of innate self-survivability, just respect the enemies. [5:02]
- Rem: do not leave alone; some AoE from his pillow and his 3 keeps waves alive, but if enemies run at him or shove a wave in, he gets dove on the first or second wave and dies. [5:02]
- Seven: pretty good anchor; very diveable before ult, but his 3 gives a lot of wave clear and he likes to scale. [5:34]
- Shiv: okay; Bloodletting makes him very hard to dive, but weak wave clear lets enemies shove into him and chip his tower while he can only dash the wave. [5:34]
- Silver: top of okay, almost good; her low cooldown, high damage 1 gives decent wave clear and her ults make her hard to dive, but waves still get shoved into her consistently. [5:34]
- Sinclair: bad anchor; his 3 keeps him alive but wave clear isn't great, good players will crash waves into him, and without his 2 he's easily dove. [6:04]
- Venator: pretty good anchor; his 2 gives a ton of survivability, his ult punishes one enemy mistake with lots of damage, traps keep him alive, and he loves solo farm because he scales very well. [6:04]
- Victor: okay; very hard to dive and can clear with his 1, but hates using it for wave clear; enemies whittle him down and he must sacrifice his ult to keep the tower alive past two waves. [6:35]
- Vindicta: do not leave alone, same as Grey Talon; she can hide far behind tower and clear, but enemies take the tower after a few waves. [7:05]
- Viscous: okay; some wave clear and survivability from his cube, but he relies on one long-cooldown ability to survive a dive. [7:05]
- Vyper: not a good anchor; with ult up she can take anyone from 70% to zero with ult into 2, but weak wave clear and she's easily dove without her bola. [7:05]
- Warden: good anchor; his flask (especially maxed) gives good wave clear, his cage gives anti-dive, he has a barrier and ult if needed, and he scales well with farm. [7:35]
- Wraith: top of good, bottom of great; she likes to anchor, scales well and her cards give AoE, but she can be run at. [7:35]
- Yamato: top of okay; often played mid with a roamer like Bebop, roaming with ult up and anchoring safely with it down; her ult makes her basically undiveable, her 1 on the wave gives good clear and her 3 keeps her up. [8:06]
