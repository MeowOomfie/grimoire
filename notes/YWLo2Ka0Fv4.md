## Covers
Shiv coaching review by Midknight (a former top 10 Mirage turned free coach) of an Arcanist-rank Shiv player on a knife build, starting at the mid game because the lane had nothing useful.
Themes: "ankle weight training" (playing games focused on one weakness), rage management with knives, melee and camps, practice drills for right-click and Rejuvenator melee timing, movement cleanup, Arctic Blast combos, and macro around Urn, Walkers and mid boss.
A 2025 video (June 2025 patch, shortly after the Rescue Beam nerf).

## Claims
Shiv:
- Ankle weight training means hyperfixating on one aspect of a hero until you play worse and probably lose games, because it improves the aspect you struggle with most. [0:00]
- Meleeing is the most efficient way to build rage on Shiv, even if you are not on a melee build: melee camps, and even melee the wave when the enemy is there. [1:02]
- Don't run from fight after fight without building rage first. [1:32]
- When you are not in a good spot, throw a knife at a camp and reset instead of staring at a fight you will die in and losing the rage you built. [2:02]
- To reset without losing rage: walk back, throw a knife into a creep, reset, then on the way back hop off the zipline, throw a knife at the big creep and keep going. [2:32]
- To get comfortable with Shiv's right-click movement, play about five games where you only right click and never left click; you will lose most of them but come out much more confident. [3:02]
- For right-click-only games, probably play a gun build, or a spirit build and right click for the gun damage. [31:08]
- Use mantle slides. [3:33]
- Slide after your rolls, and don't cut slides short. [4:36]
- If your movement is sloppy, spend 5 to 10 minutes a day in the practice tool just jumping around the map before you play, or focus on movement in game. [5:06]
- Shiv's low win rate is mostly because players still don't know how to manage rage after the rage changes; Shiv one-tricks keep it up when resetting and always knife a camp. [5:37]
- When your rage starts to drop, take a pot shot at something instead of watching it bleed out. [6:08]
- Not knifing a camp on a reset is forgivable; walking past a camp while your rage is actively bleeding and not even shooting it is the real mistake. [8:42]
- If you max the knife first you want as many knives as possible to stack bleed, so get Rapid Recharge even when you already have Compress Cooldown on the knife. [7:10]
- Salty's knife build buys Rapid Recharge a little later, and he trusts that over his own take since he mostly plays dash Shiv. [10:46]
- Don't waste the ult on someone who is dead anyway. [8:42]
- Before the ult is maxed it is a valuable tool to save; once maxed you can spam it. [8:42]
- Practice Rejuvenator meleeing in the practice tool by spamming melees on the Rejuvenator until the timing is consistent. [10:14]
- Once a Kelvin dome goes up, leave; you have no chance of winning that fight. [10:46]
- At full rage the dash does a lot of damage. [12:58]
- The main reason to buy Decay is to keep rage up: press it before a fight and your rage goes up. [14:29]
- Rushing Decay is good into a heal-heavy lane such as Kelvin or Mo & Krill. [14:29]
- When about to die with no escape, dash into them, press Cold Front and do as much damage as you can, hoping to force one of them to reset. [17:02]
- A knife Shiv with no knives left and no dash max has no second tool to rely on, so be careful. [19:36]
- Running Urn on Shiv loses a lot of rage; melee camps and breakables like the vending machines as you walk past to stop it decaying. [20:38]
- Throwing a knife at an enemy who is about to reach spawn is worth it for the rage it gives, not for the damage; think "get to full rage before a fight", not "chip him". [22:20]
- Late game with no camps on the map is when keeping rage up is hardest; he has not found a perfect answer. [24:57]
- Arctic Blast frozen targets take extra damage. With no rage the combo is Arctic Blast then dash. With rage the combo is dash, Arctic Blast, then the second dash lands on the frozen target for guaranteed extra damage. [27:32]
- Your combo should change depending on whether you have rage. [28:34]
- One melee attempt is fine at this rank, but don't melee twice: the first melee reminds them parry exists. [28:34]
- Be safe with the ult: hit the target a couple more times instead of ulting too early or out of range. [29:06]
- Shoot the wave while waiting so your rage doesn't decay. [29:06]
- Without ult online never walk back into a fight looking for an assassin play, especially with a fed Krill alive; at this rank just play safe. [29:38]
- Being at max rage also helps your farm, so aim to be at max rage as much as possible. [30:08]
- Flying Shiv tech: right click into a wall then wall jump; he lands it about 70% of the time. [31:08]

Drills he prescribed:
- Play Shiv games where your only goal is keeping rage as high as possible. [30:08]
- Practice the melee timing on the Rejuvenator in the hero tool. [30:38]
- Play games where you only right click. [30:38]
- Practice flying Shiv tech. [31:08]

Items and macro:
- Rescue Beam was nerfed hard and he hasn't seen it built, but pulling an inting teammate out is still huge value, especially in solo queue. [1:32]
- Rescue Beam's cooldown went from 30 seconds to 1 minute; with Superior Cooldown from 24 to about 52 seconds (he had not tested it). [6:38]
- The biggest team fight ults are Lash, Dynamo and Pocket; with Pocket and Lash ults up it is good to look for a team fight. [12:58]
- Once you lose sight of the enemy you were chasing, go straight to the wave; that time is worth more on the wave, especially against a hard-to-catch Holliday. [13:59]
- Rotating too many players to your strong side shows a lot on the map and is risky. [15:01]
- With two picks go Urn, and even one pick can be enough. [15:31]
- Be careful sieging a Walker with two players while your team shows no one: the enemy sees only you two and sends four to clean you up, so don't overstay. [16:01]
- To escape onto a roof, wall jump, dash straight up, double jump and mantle; it gives a lot more space and you are harder to chase. [16:31]
- Be objective oriented: when your team runs Urn, pull back and play around the Urn, not the Walker. [17:34]
- With three enemies dead and Dynamo ult up, push a lane and brute force the Walker instead of going mid boss, unless you have easy point-and-click CC like Holliday; Stone Form is unreliable. [18:04]
- He believes macro is easier to learn than micro: in Valorant he was hard stuck silver for two years working on aim, then hit Ascendant in under two months after learning macro. [18:35]
- If you are overextended alone in a side lane while your team shows four mid, higher rank enemies will send three to collapse on you. [21:19]
- After clearing, hit the Patron immediately. [21:50]
- Routing trap: instead of walking up the stairs there, side mantle and then mantle again; it saves about 2 seconds and catches or escapes many chasers. [23:56]
- Don't force mid boss: forcing an objective when the enemy has time to get there puts you in the negative, the way League coaches warn. [25:58]
- Especially don't force mid boss into Dynamo, the best mid boss steal ult, when your comp has no easy hard CC like Holliday or a curse. [26:29]
- Curse cancels Dynamo's ult while it is active, so it is a good counter build; on Shiv you only buy it if Dynamo is losing you the game and nobody else will. It is also good against a fed Wraith or Haze. [27:00]
- A turbo-fed Krill on a hard tank build should be ignored in fights; focus everyone else first. [27:00]
