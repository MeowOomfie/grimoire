## Covers
A Phantom-rank Paradox player is reviewed by Midknight, who coaches a top three South American team and works as a coach for a coaching academy.
Topics: trooper damage and over-meleeing minions in lane, buff timers, heal snack routes, carbine vs spirit Paradox, Kinetic Carbine and Pulse Grenade combos, swap (ult) usage and spacing, movement and stamina.
Macro: being active as a pick hero, why Paradox should not side lane, passive pressure off vision, secret shop vs full resets, rifts, not staggering, and Paradox items (Siphon Bullets, Transcendent Cooldown, Plated Armor).
