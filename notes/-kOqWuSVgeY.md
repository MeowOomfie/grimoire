# Why Is Deadlock So Frustrating? | My thoughts on matchmaking, draft & more
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-09-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-kOqWuSVgeY

## Covers
An unedited opinion talk on why Deadlock feels frustrating in September 2025: matchmaking, rank decay, slow climbing and the lack of a draft.
The practical part is a mindset fix for solo queue players of any rank: set session goals other than winning (soul benchmarks, learning a hero in steps, new builds).
The matchmaking and draft sections are mostly opinion and wishes for Valve; only the stated game facts are kept below.

## Claims
- Matchmaking has not been good for a long time; low rank games are very random right now because of smurfs, new players, and higher skill players who decayed all the way to low ranks. [1:02]
- Ranks are not properly displayed, and some players win a decent amount without ranking up at all. [1:02]
- Wanting to win games makes you lose more and have less fun, because chasing short term results in solo queue sets you up for disappointment roughly half the time. [1:33]
- If you are competitive and need an objective, replace "win" with another objective for the session. [3:05]
- Set a goal for the night or week, for example a farming goal of at least 20,000 souls at 18 minutes or 23,000 souls at 20 minutes; hitting it gives satisfaction even in a loss. [3:36]
- Small objectives make a huge difference in coaching students; if you hit the objective, switch your mind to caring about it rather than the result. [4:08]
- When learning a new hero, break it into small per-game goals. For Pocket: one game focus on hitting good ultimates, another on always using your cloak to escape. [4:39]
- If the current matchmaking isn't challenging you, learn a new hero outside your comfort zone, or try wild build ideas on your hero; you will likely have more fun than bashing your head trying to win. [5:10]
- Still play to win, but don't make winning your primary goal in a session, since so many factors (random teammates, random team composition) are out of your control. [6:11]
- Many players decayed to Initiate 1, including former Ascendant or Phantom players who stopped playing for a long time; as far as he knows there is no limit to how far you can decay. [7:11]
- Rank climb after decay is slow: he has seen profiles win a couple of games, rank up one sub rank, then lose one game and rank down again. [8:43]
- Elo hell may be real in Deadlock right now, though he believes an Eternus 6 player could still climb out of a decayed Initiate 1 account. [9:14]
- Above average players may find low games too random to carry: on the lucky side you get the heavily decayed teammate (the Mina who goes 25 and 1), on the unlucky side you face them. [9:44]
- There are only 32 heroes, so a draft is not strictly needed yet; Valve could still gather useful data from picks and bans (a hero banned every game is either overpowered or disliked). [10:44]
- Without draft you fairly often get a team of four carries, for example Haze, Infernus, Wraith and Seven, which puts the odds against you. [11:45]
- His view is that a draft would not change much because many players would still pick selfishly; it would mostly shift frustration from Valve to teammates, while making bad drafts somewhat less common. [12:16]
- Skipped as pure opinion with nothing a player can act on: wishes that decay be capped at about two full ranks and that decayed accounts rank up faster, the view that the ranked system was a mistake, and speculation about what draft Valve will add. [7:42] [10:14] [13:17]
