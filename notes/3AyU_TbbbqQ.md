# This Mirage Build Will Get You Out Of Low Rank
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-07-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3AyU_TbbbqQ

## Covers
Mirage, aquatiq, commentary over one game testing a community Mirage build that "builds like Tank Buster" (patch of late July 2026).
Covers ability max order, the items he bought or changed from the build, and a few matchup notes (Rem, Victor, a spirit-heavy enemy team).

## Claims
- He tried a Mirage build from a build ID that builds like Tank Buster, after testing it in the testing chambers. [0:31]
- After a rough start he bought Monster Rounds and Restorative Shot for sustain; Restorative Shot seemed fine. [1:01]
- Headshot Booster is worth it on Mirage when the enemy heads are easy to hit (a big Rem head). [1:33]
- He maxes Djinn's Mark first. [1:33]
- Usually at that point you get Mystic Regen for your Scarabs; it works with your 3 as well. [2:06]
- Vindicta can snipe Rem off someone. [4:38]
- Ability max order: 3, then 1, then 2, then 4; he always goes that order. [7:17]
- Try to be alive, with your ult up, for the vending machines. [7:17]
- He wants Mystic Vulnerability next, then Arcane Surge; the build does not include Mystic Vulnerability, but he would put it in decently early because it is crazy on Mirage. [9:24]
- Against heavy sustain, Healbane is important. [9:55]
- He picked Dispel Magic over Fortitude as his green. [9:55]
- Mystic Shot is a great item. [6:10]
- He wanted Titanic Magazine and later sold Headshot Booster to fit it. [12:00]
- He bought Toxic Bullets as the anti-tank option. [13:38]
- Push another lane instead of fighting four enemies; get the waves pushed. [14:43]
- Kill Victor before taking mid boss, or he becomes a problem later. [17:16]
- He realized late he had no greens and needed Dispel Magic. [18:19]
- Against a spirit-heavy team, the next green is Spirit Resilience. [18:51]
- Siphon Bullets lets him live in fights; with it, enemies must 4v1 him to kill him. [19:21]
- Seven should take a side lane and farm hard. [20:28]
- With no greens he was dying too quickly and getting caught; he bought another green (Spirit Resilience). [22:33]
- Mystic Shot is strong but not needed by the late game. [24:38]
- Ricochet is an option for farming. [26:13]
- His verdict: the build is good, but he started with no greens and later went full green plus purples once he had his important spikes. [30:26]
- Late game he would just buy cooldown items. [29:56]

## Disagrees with
- l927AEmmUjA (aquatiq, 2026-03-21): Taking Djinn's Mark first is unconventional; the usual first pick is Fire Scarabs for lane sustain. [0:30]
