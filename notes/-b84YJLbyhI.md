# Biggest Deadlock Balance Patch YET
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-11-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-b84YJLbyhI

## Covers
First impressions of the big late-November 2025 balance patch, out on the day of this 2025-11-22 video: new healing trooper and soul orbs, bounty split, neutrals, spirit nerfs and gun buffs, 4.8k investment breakpoints, movement, walkers, flex slots, Rejuvenator, items (Capacitor, Curse, Focus Lens, Silence Wave, barriers, Cheat Death).
For any player who wants the big picture of the patch without reading every line.

## Claims
- This is possibly one of the biggest balance updates in Deadlock's history. [0:00]
- There is a new laning trooper: instead of the old healing trooper healing you every 6 seconds, the enemy healing trooper drops a medkit when it dies that heals for a flat amount plus a percentage of your missing health. [0:31]
- Because the heal scales with missing health, killing the enemy healing trooper last is now more effective; before you killed it first to stop it healing the enemy. [1:02]
- To secure a last hit you must now move close to the trooper to pick up the orb on the ground; pickup range is forgiving, but from the opposite side of the lane you won't get it. [1:02]
- Denies are worth 40% instead of 50%. [1:33]
- Together the lane changes make lane more brawly. [1:33]
- Trooper bounty split for two players went from 65% each (130% total) to 108% total, so sharing waves as two is heavily nerfed. [2:05]
- Sharing a wave with more than two players is significantly buffed: three players on a wave give your team about the same souls, which allows positions other than a default 2-2-2 split and in theory Dota-style trilanes. [2:35]
- Ganking another lane is punished less, so ganking is a little better. [3:06]
- Neutrals are stronger and harder to farm but give more souls; they are especially harder for spirit damage heroes because camp spirit resist went up. [3:06]
- Sinner's Sacrifice now deals 80 damage per punch instead of 60; with five punches that is an extra 100 damage per sacrifice, so double sacrifices leave you very low. [3:36]
- This incentivizes invading enemy Sinner's Sacrifices and killing players while they are doing them. [3:36]
- Overall spirit is significantly nerfed and weapon is significantly buffed this patch. [4:07]
- All ability damage upgrades were reduced by 10%, meaning any upgrade that gives flat damage to an ability, for example Infernus's max Afterburn upgrade, Ivy's Kudzu Bomb max upgrade, Warden's ult damage upgrade and Lash's Flog. [4:07]
- Base gun damage and gun damage growth increased by 10% for all heroes except Paige, making the meta much more gun forward. [4:38]
- New investment bonuses put a big chunk of the bonus at 4.8k souls: spirit investment gives 19 spirit at 3.2k and 45 at 4.8k, more than double. [5:10]
- Before, 9.6k was the big build threshold; now hitting 4.8k in each category is heavily incentivized, which encourages hybrid builds, for example a spirit hero getting 4.8k in gun items or a gun hero getting some spirit. [5:10]
- The 4.8k bonus gives strong early power spikes to some heroes and makes builds creative again after they had been mostly solved. [5:40]
- The initial wall jump now costs 25 stamina and stamina regen rate is reduced by 5%, continuing the movement nerfs so that positioning and map position matter more. [5:40]
- The stamina regen reduction is felt on some heroes, particularly Lady Geist; the 25 stamina wall jump is felt most on two-stamina heroes. [6:12]
- Walkers are about 40% tankier. [6:42]
- Walkers now show two circles: the inner one is the stomp range, which has a much shorter cooldown so you can't easily go under the walker; the outer one is the range you must be inside to attack it at all. [6:42]
- Walker fireballs do a lot of damage; walkers are much harder to siege and much easier to defend, which will probably make games longer, especially at lower ranks. [7:13]
- Guardians and walkers start with damage resist that decays to -50% over 10 minutes for guardians and 20 minutes for walkers. [7:44]
- You now start with nine item slots and there are three flex slots for a total of 12; each walker killed unlocks one flex slot. [8:14]
- Starting with more slots means it feels less bad when you are behind and your team can't take guardians. [8:44]
- The Rejuvenator buff now only gives the extra lives, no longer the max HP, fire rate and spirit damage amp; Deathy calls this good since three extra lives was already strong. [8:44]
- The team that kills the mid boss gets +2 permanent buffs per player; these are golden statue buffs (ammo, cooldown or health), not more Rejuvenator. [9:15]
- Delivering the Soul Urn now gives the person who drops it four permanent golden statue buffs. [9:15]
- Dropping the Urn now puts parry on cooldown, so the cheesy instant parry after dropping it is gone. [9:15]
- Jump pad stun has a longer window, so you can be stunned for longer after leaving a jump pad. [9:45]
- If you melee an enemy while they mantle, they can no longer mantle slide right after; this makes chasing feel much better. [9:45]
- Slide got a buff to CC reduction that only applies to crowd control that stuns you or procs Reactive Barrier (Slowing Hex does not count). [9:45]
- Successive CC is reduced: hit by a Mo & Krill ult alone, no reduction; Mo & Krill ult then a Seven stun, 8%; a third stun such as Paige's root has 24% less duration. [10:15]
- The bridge buffs were rebalanced and should be stronger overall. [10:15]
- Every source of cooldown reduction was nerfed significantly, so high cooldown reduction is much harder to get; this is part of why spirit is worse and gun better. [10:45]
- Cultist Sacrifice now gives weapon damage instead of fire rate, making it significantly worse on some heroes, for example Wraith. [10:45]
- Split Shot has double the cooldown, making it significantly worse. [11:15]
- Tesla Bullets and Capacitor got big nerfs: Capacitor no longer dispels buffs (moved to Curse) and its shock damage and spirit scaling were nerfed, so you need more spirit investment for damage. [11:15]
- Capacitor now prevents stamina usage and silences movement abilities: no stamina, no wall jumps, plus a Slowing Hex effect, so it is still powerful, just differently. [11:15]
- Spellslinger's fire rate per stack was buffed; Deathy expects it to be insane on a few heroes and finds it really strong on Lady Geist. [11:45]
- All barriers were nerfed: Reactive Barrier, Spirit Shielding and Weapon Shielding got longer cooldowns and shorter durations. [11:45]
- Spirit Shielding now needs 225 damage taken to activate, which is part nerf and part tweak. [11:45]
- Cheat Death might be viable now: on proc it removes all non-standard debuffs (such as Shiv knives or Afterburn), so you can survive if you escape. [12:16]
- Curse now dispels buffs, doing what Capacitor did before, a significant buff; expect it to be bought a lot more. [12:16]
- Silence Wave no longer builds from Spirit Sap and no longer shreds; it now does damage instead of reducing resist. [12:47]
- Spirit Sap now builds into Focus Lens, so Focus Lens is the item for spirit resist shred; to reduce resist you must invest all the way into Focus Lens, another spirit nerf. Deathy found it pretty good on Lash. [12:47]
- Early biggest winners: Lady Geist, Warden, and Mo & Krill, who feels really powerful. [13:17]
- Early biggest losers: Pocket, Mina and, to an extent, Holliday. [13:17]
- On a new patch nobody knows what is optimal, so experiment: make your own builds, try hitting the 4.8k investment thresholds and building more gun; gun Dynamo is probably a lot better now. [14:17]
- The next patch is aimed for December, without a guarantee. [14:49]
