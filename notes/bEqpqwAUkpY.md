## Covers
Part 2 of a four part counter guide (September 2025): Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash and McGinnis (Mina is held for the next part). Each hero gets counter tech, counter items, and the key lane ability whose cooldown to track in waves. For any player who wants to know how to play against these heroes.

## Claims
### General rules
- Use the 30 second wave timer to track key cooldowns; Abrams' charge at 37 seconds is a one wave cooldown. [0:30]
- Rebuttal is good into anyone who melees a lot: it heals you and gives bonus damage when you parry, returns their melee damage, and most importantly cuts your parry cooldown. [0:30]
- Bullet Resilience, Spirit Resilience, Enchanter's Emblem and Battle Vest are good into their damage types; buy them if that type of damage bullies you in lane or the enemy comp favours it. [1:01]
- Bullet resilience is slightly worse because Armor Piercing Rounds roughly halves it, but it translates one to one into melee resist, so it is full value when you are meleed a lot. [1:01]
- Curse is good into every hero; watch for i-frames (Dynamo's 2, Pocket's case, Ivy's Stone Form). It also stops channeled ults such as Haze's and Dynamo's. [1:31]
- Hover an enemy to check an ability's cooldown and whether they put a point in it. [13:43]
- The filled eye under a hero's portrait shows they have their ult. [18:49]

### Haze
- Play around corners, especially when she has ult or is picking people off in the jungle, so you can hop around a corner when she jumps you. [2:01]
- Track her location: if she is on one lane, the other lane is safer to push and catch waves. [2:31]
- In lane let Fixation reset; good Hazes keep tossing bullets into you to keep stacks up, then catch you off guard in a big trade (two points adds more duration). [3:02]
- Plated Armor is by far the best item into her: 30% less damage from her and half the Fixation stacks. [3:02]
- Warp Stone is great unless she builds Slowing Hex or Silence Wave: escape her 1 when caught out, get out of her ult, and its 40% bullet resist is very good. [3:33]
- Return Fire popped when she ults reflects a lot of damage; Metal Skin works the same way and is cheaper. [4:04]
- Disarming Hex and Rusted Barrel are good against any gun carry, including her. [4:04]
- Her ult damage is split between targets; before Ricochet it hits one person at a time, so with four people on her you can usually just burst her down. [4:35]
- Key ability: Sleep Dagger, 26 second cooldown, about one wave; very few Hazes put two points in early. [5:06]
- If she sleeps a teammate, roll on top of them and parry her heavy melee for them. [5:06]
- Without Sleep Dagger she is fairly immobile and jumpable in lane. [5:37]

### Holliday
- Her barrels only explode instantly if she melees them out of the air, so in her face you can parry her predictably, especially when she barrels right after a lasso. Unparried, the light melee still hits. [5:37]
- Warp Stone: high level Hollidays barrel first to set up the lasso; warp out of the knock-up to dodge the lasso, and it also dodges barrels. [6:37]
- Counterspell blocks her lasso if you read the barrel setup or hear the wind up, and also blocks barrels. [7:08]
- Debuff removal and debuff reduction shorten her lasso and how far she drags you, and cut the Crack Shot slow. [7:38]
- Slowing Hex stops her 2, giving your team 3.5 seconds to kill her when she jumps in to lasso someone. [7:38]
- Key lane ability: Crack Shot, 20 second cooldown, about two thirds of a wave; most Hollidays start it. Her gun glows when it is up; do not take gun trades while it is up. [8:08]

### Infernus
- Do not let him slowly stack burn: tuck and let it reset, hug cover, avoid extended trades, peek and roll back behind corners. Never peek him while you are burning. [8:39]
- Check your burn stacks by the igniting icon, which also shows the duration. [9:10]
- His dash cannot go up unless he buys Warp Stone, which 99% do not, so stand on a bridge against it in lane. [9:40]
- His dash stops your afterburn from decaying, so never tank a dash while already burning. [9:40]
- Debuff Remover cleanses his burn and Napalm, and debuff reduction shortens his ult stun and his Napalm slow. [10:11]
- Plated Armor cuts his bullet damage and his on-hits, so he needs twice as long to stack afterburn. [10:11]
- Slowing Hex stops his dash, good against a Rapid Recharge build or when he overextends on a walker expecting to dash out. [10:41]
- Key ability: Flame Dash, 37 second cooldown, about one wave; with it he escapes easily and wins most trades, without it he is immobile and jumpable. [10:41]

### Ivy
- Use verticality against her Kudzu Bomb: if she throws it on top of something, play under it; if below, jump on top, usually using the bridge in lane. [11:41]
- CC her during her ult: Mirage's tornado used right after the ult starts cancels it (during the channel it does not); other CC like Abrams' charge works during the channel. [11:41]
- Knockdown baits out Stone Form, and on her ult knocks her down or forces her to cancel into Stone Form, stopping her split pushing or ulting away; it also works during the channel. [12:12]
- Slowing Hex stops her ulting; not worth more than Knockdown, but good value if you already buy it for other enemies. [12:42]
- Warp Stone into a gun Ivy: warp away with 40% bullet resist, and warp up to dodge her bomb drop from the ult. [12:42]
- Metal Skin and Disarming Hex into a gun Ivy; Silence Wave into a spirit Ivy to cut spirit power and stop Stone Form. [13:13]
- Key ability: Stone Form, 42 second cooldown, 23 seconds with a point (most put one early); it dodges nearly anything, so time Lash combos, Bebop bombs and Mirage marks around it. Without it she is very punishable. [13:43]
- Capacitor is good into a gun Ivy: it removes her 2 and tethers and cleanses Vampiric Burst and Heroic Aura on movement-speed builds. [14:14]

### Kelvin
- Especially in mid to low elo Kelvins love to melee inside their dome, so keep your finger on parry when domed. [14:44]
- Avoid trading while he beams you; if forced, in low elo just heavy melee him, and higher up use melees and fake melees to make him parry, because parrying cancels his beam. [14:44]
- Enduring Speed is very good for slow resist (Fleetfoot's active also gives slow resist, but Enduring Speed is more universal); almost all his abilities except Ice Path slow. [15:15]
- Debuff Remover at max beam stacks lets you escape. [15:45]
- Knockdown's stun cancels his beam; higher level Kelvins buy Counterspell into it, but it still takes the beam offline. [16:15]
- Anti-heal: Healbane early so he cannot heal his team to full in the dome; later AoE anti-heal like Spirit Burn against dome and grenade heals. [16:15]
- Spirit Shielding is normally only worth it into a double spirit lane (with one spirit enemy it tends to pop after their burst), but Kelvin is an exception because he sustains trades while his escalating beam slow weakens you. [16:45]
- Key lane ability: Ice Beam, 25 second cooldown, 18 with a point (most start it unless going a grenade max support build), half a wave or a full wave. Never peek or trade while he has it; without it you normally win trades. [17:48]

### Lady Geist
- Always assume she has ult unless you have checked; stay at arm's length and out of melee range. [18:49]
- Call her ult status to your team ("Geist has ult", "Geist no ult"); at 1 HP hiding, call "Geist one HP with ult" so low elo teammates do not chase. [19:20]
- Silence Wave: at about 30% HP silence her so she cannot ult. [19:50]
- Debuff Remover removes Malice damage amp stacks and cancels her life drain. [19:50]
- Counterspell blocks her bombs, and if timed when she predictably jumps or turns to ult, blocks the ult. [20:20]
- Spellbreaker blocks most bomb damage against a bomb Geist, and its debuff resist shortens life drain and Malice stacks. [20:50]
- Anti-heal cuts her healing from her 2, but does not affect how much health her ult swaps. [21:20]
- Her bomb is about half a wave of cooldown (10 seconds with a point); she loves bombing. [21:50]
- Key ability: Life Drain, 30 second cooldown, one wave; it wins most trades. Putting a wall between you cancels its damage and her healing. [21:50]

### Lash
- Play around aerial cover and the bridge; when he jumps for a Ground Strike, hop on top of something, or shuffle under or in front if he aims to land on the structure. [22:21]
- A wall bounce gets more height than a double jump to dodge Ground Strike; treat the floor as lava until you reach cover or your team. [23:22]
- Knockdown right before his ult cancels it: he lifts people but falls before recasting. Its stun scales with fall height. Great in the pit when ending the game. [23:52]
- Slowing Hex fully suppresses his grapple, so he cannot jump in and out; call it for your team to focus him. [24:23]
- Silence Wave as he sets up his ult drops him and stops the ult (but not once he is already ulting). [24:54]
- Counterspell is phenomenal: time it as he left clicks the tether pull (the speaker thinks about .8 seconds, it has changed); also press it when the blue Ground Strike cone appears at your feet. [25:24]
- If his ult has no stun, Counterspell after you hit the ground to deny Ground Strike, Flog and follow-up burst. [26:26]
- If he ults you into the guardian, parry the moment you hit the ground to parry the tower; good Lashes drop you just outside melee range instead. [26:26]
- Spellbreaker cuts Ground Strike damage by 75%; better Lashes lead with Flog against it. [27:26]
- Flog: 30 second cooldown, about one wave, heals 50%; a two point Flog build with Quicksilver Reload and Mystic Burst drops it to 12 seconds (now off meta). Do not stand next to your lane partner while he has it. [27:56]
- If he whiffs Flog, you have the next wave to punish him while his healing is down. [28:58]
- Revised key ability: his 2, the grapple, 42 second cooldown, one wave and change; without it he cannot escape and is much more punishable than without Flog. [28:58]

### McGinnis
- Position carefully, mirror your laner's aggression and hit the wave from a little distance; her wall punishes mispositioning. [29:58]
- Against spirit McGinnis clear her turrets whenever you can, especially with AoE like Doorman's bell or Lash's Ground Strike. [30:29]
- Against gun McGinnis take short guerrilla trades of about 2 seconds; her gun needs time to ramp up and is weak in its first seconds. [30:59]
- Warp Stone lets you double jump over her wall; you cannot wall jump off her wall, but wall jumping off another wall then double jumping also clears it. [31:30]
- Enduring Speed (slow resist) and other debuff resistance counter the slows from her turrets and wall. [32:01]
- Knockdown cancels a spirit McGinnis ult channel, which she relies on for her wall plus ult combo. [32:01]
- Return Fire (6 seconds) or Metal Skin (3 seconds) makes a gun McGinnis stop shooting and lose her ramp, and buys time for your team to get over the wall. [32:32]
- Key ability: her wall, 50 second cooldown, about two waves; she rarely puts two points in early. Without it she cannot cut you off, and with only two stamina she struggles to escape, so siege the wave. [33:02]
