## Covers
Deathy coaches Eskay, an Emissary rank player new to MOBAs, through a replay of her Mina game (lane vs Vindicta and Lash, then the 10 to 25 minute mid game).
Stat benchmarks (souls by source, player and trooper damage), lane trading, healing trooper, power spikes, Urn, wave filling, fight checklist, escaping, and playing to a hero's strengths.
For new and low rank players, October 2025.

## Claims
- The coaching focus that helps most is the 10 to 20-25 minute window; before that the game is straightforward and after 25 minutes it is mostly teamfighting, where macro is mostly "get a pick and go mid boss". [1:31]
- Stats never tell the whole story but can point you somewhere; check souls by source at 10 and 20 minutes to see if you are jungling too much, not killing enough or not getting enough troopers. [2:32]
- In the damage breakdown at 20 minutes, compare player damage from 10 to 20 minutes against the first 10 minutes; you should do more between 10 and 20 because you have more items. Similar damage in both windows means you did not take many engagements. [3:03]
- A general, easy to hit benchmark is roughly 20k trooper damage at 20 minutes; 15k is on the low end and suggests farming too much and being too passive. [3:33]
- Being passive is not justified when you are one of the richer players in the lobby. [4:03]
- In lane, think about the effective range of the enemies' weapons and your own; Mina is a mid to close range hero who does nothing at long range because she cannot apply Love Bites, so stay mid range and only get close to use Rake. [5:03]
- Vindicta is a long range hero who plays out of Mina's range a lot but can still be poked; Lash plays in a similar range to Mina, so Mina into Vindicta and Lash is a decent matchup. [5:33]
- Ask who has the stronger early levels. Mina's base DPS is low (28) but Love Bites roughly doubles it, so whenever you can proc Love Bites you will usually out trade Lash early and out trade Vindicta outside her burst. [5:33]
- Against a shotgun hero like Shiv, stay just outside his range at about 10 to 15 meters, be annoying enough that he has to walk up to you, and kite him. [6:04]
- An off angle on the high ground is good lane positioning because it lets you poke. [6:34]
- Lane is about taking good trades and having wave priority (your troopers pushing into theirs): your healing trooper heals you more and you have pressure, which makes good trades easier. [7:04]
- A pushed wave gives you freedom to do other things, including boxes. [7:04]
- A big mistake: never taking the heal from your healing trooper. It heals you 40 each time on a 6 second cooldown; taking it every wave would have put her near 200 HP instead of 90. [7:04]
- When you are not dominating the lane, starting Extra Spirit instead of Headshot Booster heals you more from minions and camps, because the heal per kill has a 1.5 spirit scaling. [7:36]
- Health advantage should not be forgotten: after coming back from base with more HP, trade instead of running boxes. Example: Vindicta at 400 HP and Lash at 500; trading 200 for 200 leaves you at 480 of 680 but leaves Vindicta at 200, one Rake from death. [8:07]
- Players at this rank are not disciplined and will give you many chances to get back into the lane, so don't give them up to run boxes. [8:37]
- Always ask what advantage you have: did I just hit an item timing, do I have more health, do I have cooldowns they don't? [8:37]
- Boxes are not a priority; treat them as something to do when there is nothing better to do. [9:08]
- Mina gets really strong with two points in Love Bites because it nearly doubles Love Bite damage. [9:38]
- Lash's lane power spike is two points in Flog plus Quicksilver Reload and Mystic Burst; a Lash not building toward it is free, and you know your spike is stronger. [9:38]
- Vindicta has no real power spike before her ultimate. [9:38]
- In this lane Mina had a timing at about 2.6k souls had she not been behind. [10:09]
- Nearly every hero is built the same way: most have a power spike when they put two points in an ability. [10:09]
- Against Lash, use your 2 (Nox Nostra) preemptively so you cannot be hit by the slam rather than relying on reaction time: go into the air with the first teleport, trade while in the air where he cannot slam you, then use the second teleport to go forward or back depending on damage dealt. [10:39]
- Don't over-preserve that ability; its cooldown is only 27 seconds. [11:09]
- When you are that low as Mina against a Vindicta, go back to base; it is better than dying, especially with the speed boost zip, because the death is basically inevitable. [11:09]
- Right after returning from base with HP and cooldown advantage, go full blast at the enemies instead of dropping off the zipline normally and engaging from far. [11:39]
- Running at enemies when a teammate is respawning forces them into a choice: if they run away you kill them, if they turn on you your teammate arrives off the zip to help. [12:09]
- Sinner's Sacrifice (vaults) spawning is important to play around. [12:39]
- A good gank should get your team the mid tower and then you are pretty much free to steal the enemy's center vaults. [12:39]
- Listen for the heartbeat sound that signals Drifter is nearby. [12:39]
- Whenever you are super low against Vindicta, respect her; even a bad Vindicta needs only one shot. [13:39]
- Avoidable deaths in lane are the main reason for being behind in souls. [14:09]
- The whole game of a MOBA is power spikes: fight people you are stronger than, don't fight people stronger than you. Playing around your own spikes is the number one way to get an average advantage in fights. [14:40]
- Play to the strength of your item: Stamina Mastery makes you very hard to catch, so it is a good item for split pushing and getting aggressive in waves. [15:41]
- Cultist Sacrifice gives good stats and can be a good spike; generally fight right after you buy your items. [15:41]
- Urn comeback mechanic: the first Urn gives 15%, every Urn after that 10%; an Urn with no candles is a neutral Urn. [16:41]
- When your team is ahead, the easiest way to progress the game is to run the Urn, even if you personally don't. [16:41]
- When you feel weak and need money, don't look for many fights; scale and focus on not missing any waves. [17:12]
- Mistake: going to a wave your teammate (Paige) is already at when nobody is catching the mid wave. Taking the open wave is better for your souls (no splitting) and for team pressure; always look for the holes on the map you can fix. [17:12]
- A very bad habit is overstaying after objectives; once your team got what it wanted (the Urn), leave, especially at 400 HP and not having bought. [17:42]
- Build systems in your brain: whenever you are dead or just farming, look at the team souls at the top and the clock, and ask what is coming up next. [18:43]
- Example: at 14:30 with a slight lead, push the side lane for pressure, plan to take the bridge buffs that come up in 30 seconds, and buy the next item you are close to. [18:43]
- Not all deaths are bad; a death that gets a kill and rotates several enemies over is fine. Fear dying for no reason. [19:14]
- The worst deaths are dying in your jungle or their jungle while holding a big bag of unsecured souls. [19:44]
- Ask who you can 1v1 to judge the game state. [19:44]
- With a big bag (1,600 unsecured souls), go buy; going one more wave first is fine, the main advantage of buying right away is securing the bag. [20:14]
- The number one thing that makes you likely to win a fight is numbers advantage; more souls is a big factor but numbers come first. [21:16]
- Mistake: jumping into a 1v3 to save a teammate when you know three enemies are there and your Abrams and Paige are far away. Without numbers advantage leave and push mid instead. [21:46]
- The number one mid game improvement: after clearing a wave, don't stroll around hitting a box or two and leisurely shopping. Clearing a wave buys time; use it efficiently to steal their jungle, gank, buy or farm your own jungle. [22:16]
- After clearing a wave, try to create advantageous engagements, for example set yourself up on another lane to take a 2v1. [22:46]
- Think about what your hero is good at. Mina bursts people, farms objectives fast and has a very good escape, especially with Superior Stamina, so she can push waves riskily without taking much risk. [23:46]
- A walker left alone for even 10 seconds falls to Mina, so be more aggressive on side objectives. [24:16]
- Mina should prefer the side lanes over mid: fewer people to split souls with, enemies found more isolated, and objectives melted when left alone; she has strong split push potential. [24:16]
- Buying right away when you have the souls is a key habit. [24:46]
- Use Cultist Sacrifice when you need it rather than saving it only for tier 3 minions when none are nearby; on Mina it is about 250 health, roughly 15% more health. [24:46]
- When the enemy runs the Urn, your waves are bad and your team is mostly dead, give up the Urn rather than fight for it. [25:16]
- Fight checklist: numbers advantage (the big one), soul lead, ultimate advantage; if the answer is no to all, don't take the fight. [25:46]
- Escaping on Mina: use the first teleport to dodge (for example a stomp), let time run between the two teleports instead of using both right away, then take the high ground, where enemies need very good wall jumps to catch you. [26:17]
- When you can't contest mid, going to hit a side walker is a good play; holding a walker briefly costs little. [27:18]
- When far behind and the enemy has extra lives (Rejuvenator), your best chance is catching someone out, since people get cocky and split up with Rejuvenator; a Bebop looking for a hook is a good play. [27:50]
- If you catch someone and kill them twice, the fight still starts 6v5 even with their extra lives. [27:50]
- Good fighting as Mina: go in and out, stay in their face while confusing them, and bait out their abilities without dying, similar to Pocket in being hard to catch. Mina draws attention easily. [29:21]
- When the enemy drops your base objective (patron), your best comeback chance is to run at them, because many of them are low and it reduces your teammates' respawn timers. [30:22]
- Chase them so they can't use the zipline out, letting your respawning teammates join with fresh health bars; start keeping them there even before the patron goes down. [30:52]
- At that stage respawn timers are about 70 seconds, so one or two kills give 70 seconds of map presence, often a stagger into more kills, then mid boss and a second mid boss with four Rejuvenators, and you are back in the game. [30:52]
- By default the enemy leaves as soon as they drop the patron unless they killed several of your team; staying without Rejuvenator is risky for them because comeback mechanics are very strong and a wipe puts you back in the game. [31:22]
- Drifter is pretty good against Mina. [32:22]
- Whiteboard focus 1: learn which waves are going to open up on the map and fill those gaps instead of going to waves teammates already cover; doubling up reduces both map pressure and your souls. [32:52]
- Whiteboard focus 2: play around your power spikes, in lane and in the game, and apply them in good fights. [33:23]
- Fight flow chart at a basic level: do we have numbers advantage, are the waves good, did I just hit a power spike, are we ahead in souls? Cooldowns, ultimates and items matter too, but numbers is the biggest; a 4v3 is good if the rest isn't a disaster. [33:53]
- Play with purpose: think about what your hero wants to do and play to those strengths. [33:53]
- Paradox vs Mina: Mina escapes easier and does objectives, so side lane more; Paradox gets picks, so join more fights, look for openings, consider items like Veil Walker, and play around the strong people on your team because you need someone with you to kill. [34:25]
- Mina, like Wraith, wants to play away from the group, clearing waves instantly and melting walkers the moment they open. [35:26]
- Getting as much money as possible and showing up to fights is a good base, but put more thought into which fights you show up to. [35:56]
- Limit test more; the rule "always go one more wave" applies, push one more wave than you think. [35:56]
- Improve one thing at a time: pick one focus from the notes each session. [36:29]
