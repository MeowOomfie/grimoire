## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream, recorded 10/13/26 on the patch that added Baba, Rat King and Solomon. Two VOD reviews: a Venator player laning against Bebop, then a Baba player (a friend of the streamer) into a Haze lane.
Ends with a short Solomon build recommendation segment and a first look at the newly revealed Deadman Danny in the sandbox, with early build and counter guesses.

Deadman Danny first look (sandbox):
- His 1 is a salvo of spirit missiles that lodge in the ground along a line and mark targets; a charge shot on a marked enemy consumes the mark for bonus spirit damage. Hold the 1 to pick an anchor, then aim the direction, so you can send it around a corner. [93:45]
- His 2 is a jetpack dive: hitting an enemy hero means no self-stun, and heat vents much faster while grounded. [95:19]
- His 3 is a flamethrower dealing weapon damage over time, shredding bullet resist and building a back blast that knocks you backward. [98:25]
- His ult carries an enemy into the air and slams them; you also take damage, damage is higher on enemies below 50% HP and grows per meter fallen, so ult them off a roof. It works like a suicide execute. [99:27]
- His charged shot counts as a primary fire with two bullets and skips the alt-fire damage reduction, so Express Shot gives a static 100% extra; you probably rush Express Shot early. [103:46]
- Start the 1, then the 2, then the 3. [106:55]
- You may rush the tier 2 on the jetpack (2) because its early lane heal seems strong if you hit it reliably; the area is massive and easy to hit. [107:25]
- His base gun DPS is only about 48, so you probably build a lot of spirit. [107:55]
- On-hits proc fast with his charged shot, so Toxic Bullets could work. [108:56]
- Lightning Scroll procs on yourself. [110:33]
- He can't headshot and can't buy Ricochet. [111:04]
- He probably buys Superior Stamina rather than another Magic Carpet. [114:42]
- Jetpack onto isolated targets rather than into a full team fight, and buy an escape item like Warp Stone for after you go in. [116:47]
- He explodes on death. [117:18]
- Reactive Barrier fits him because it procs on his self-stun, covering a missed dive. [119:56]
- He is hard countered by curse because he has to play close range, and he seems one of the more balanced heroes of the patch. [124:22]
