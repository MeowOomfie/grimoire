# The Biggest Update In Deadlock History
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-08-19
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6VkyUbuoNI

## Covers
A live first look at the 2025-08-19 update: new hero models and icons, the new heroes (Mina tested in the practice range, Paige, Doorman, Drifter, Billy and a zombie-like hero shown in the menu), the community vote on hero unlocks and the reworked map.
Mostly reaction footage. The concrete content is Mina's ability numbers and a few map changes. For players who want a quick look at what the update added.

## Claims
- Some ability icons were updated, not all; Wraith's Full Auto and teleport icons are new, while her cards and Telekinesis icons look the same. [1:02]
- The new melee sound is a useful audio cue: you can now hear someone trying to melee you from behind. [1:02]
- Vindicta's new model is much better than the old one and looks human now. [2:05]
- Mina's Rake (umbrella slash) deals spirit damage that increases with missing health, and if it kills a target Mina is healed. [2:35]
- Rake's damage scales x1 with spirit power, which is very solid scaling, so Mina can build spirit. [3:05]
- Rake's heal on kill is 20 and works on troopers ("targets"), so you can use it to heal in lane. [3:36]
- Rake has a 10 second cooldown, an upgrade adds damage per slash, and both its damage and its heal per kill scale with spirit power. [3:36]
- Rake's upgrade adds 6% missing health as damage, which makes it an execute on low health targets. [4:08]
- Sanguine Retreat makes Mina disperse, become untargetable and fly to a target location; it can be recast once within a 3 second window, with a 10 m teleport radius. [4:08]
- Sanguine Retreat upgrades: on cast gain 30% fire rate and reload 8 bullets, plus a cooldown reduction. It works like a double teleport and the 3 second recast window is forgiving. [4:38]
- Mina floats while moving because of her umbrella; it is a passive, not part of Sanguine Retreat. [5:09]
- Love Bites: Mina's bullets and abilities apply spirit damage that builds up to a burst of bonus spirit damage, with a unique cooldown per target (8 seconds per target). Its scaling is weak apart from the bonus damage. [5:09]
- Love Bites upgrades: on proc gain 4 m/s movement speed for 3 seconds, +55 bonus damage, and a 3 second cut to the per-target cooldown. It is quite easy to build up. [5:40]
- Nox Nostra (ultimate) unleashes a cloud of bats that seek out targets, each dealing spirit damage and applying silence. [5:40]
- Triggering Love Bites against heroes permanently increases the number of bats Nox Nostra releases, so apply Love Bites and then use the ultimate. [5:40]
- Nox Nostra upgrades: +2 damage per bat, a 40 second cooldown reduction, and on hit 1% current health as damage. [6:12]
- Nox Nostra works like an AoE and does huge damage; in the practice range it still shredded a Shiv bot given bullet and spirit resilience plus Colossus, Fortitude and Spellbreaker. [6:43]
- Paige is a nerdy hero who fights holding a book. [7:45]
- Drifter's model looks great, but the coach expects to hate playing against him. [7:45]
- Billy's new look is a punk goat; the new heroes' visuals and voice lines are very well done, especially Mina's. [8:16]
- Ivy's redesign keeps her identity while making her more presentable. [8:47]
- The map got a big visual overhaul but is the same layout; the T3 camps can still be farmed. [9:52]
- One area of the map is now much easier to defend. [10:22]
- Creep camps changed slightly: a normal tier two camp, a tier three camp and one of the smaller camps sit in one area. On one side the farming route is slower and more awkward than before and the camps are not mirrored; the side where you used to farm two camps together is weaker. [10:52]
- There is a secret room on the map, and the community votes on which new hero is unlocked next; Billy was the most wanted. [11:22]
- With this update the final visuals are done for Wraith, Lady Geist, Ivy, Vindicta, Pocket and Lash; Shiv's is not finished yet. [12:23]
- You can no-clip under the map and see a hidden Doorman area. The coach's guess is that Doorman places two doors, and going through one drops you into a space where you must jump across to reach the other teleport, so the trip takes a few seconds and missing the jump may kill you. [12:55]
