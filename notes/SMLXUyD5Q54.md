# Don't Play Another Match of Deadlock Until You Learn This
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-09-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SMLXUyD5Q54

## Covers
Tips for the late September 2026 map update: running boxes in circles, leaving lane at 3:00 for a box route, heavy boxes, triple Sinner's Sacrifices (the mist pool and the Chinatown bell tower), jungling as a role, kiting camps, corrupted items, slower rotations, walker and mid lane positioning, and guardian parry changes.
For all ranks adjusting to the new map, no single hero.

## Claims
- You don't need box route videos for the new map; learn boxes by walking circles: leave a lane, walk away from it, loop around killing any camps or boxes you see, and end at a zipline to return to lane. Repeat over and over. [0:31]
- This works even better on the new map because almost all camps and boxes are in open spots, with only a few in corners or up grapples. [0:31]
- Around 3 minutes, if your lane partner can survive alone (good sustain, a competent teammate or duo) and the enemies are weak early heroes playing back that you can't kill, leave lane and run a big box route. [1:31]
- Come back in time for the 4:30 wave to get its souls, then push out waves so you can take the bridge buff afterward. [2:02]
- Waves spawn every 30 seconds. [2:02]
- If you see a heavy box, get it: it gives double the souls and is probably guaranteed souls. [2:32]
- Boxes are the most busted thing in this update; one or two good box routes set you up for the game because there are many more souls on the map. [2:32]
- There are two spots with triple Sinner's Sacrifices. One is in the pool under a purple mist: inside you can't use stamina and make no footsteps or sound, so it is a hidden safe area, but deadly if you get jumped; it also has camps. [2:32]
- The other is at the top of the Chinatown bell tower with three Sinner's Sacrifices and loot, but taking those souls rings the bell and alerts the whole map. [3:02]
- The concentrated souls set up invades: coordinate to take the enemy triples and camps. [3:32]
- The 8 minute spike is now much bigger than before because you spend less time moving between sacrifices and boxes, so you can finish farming and get back to playing actively. [3:32]
- There is a new red Sinner's Sacrifice that fills faster: for regular ones start charging heavy melee around the third from last light; for the red one start charging around the fifth light. [4:02]
- The map is more spaced out, so mid and side lanes favor long range M1 heroes like Vindicta; every gunfight and ability now needs more precision, so solid aim fundamentals matter more than ever. [4:33]
- Jungling is now a real role: vents, healing soul bats, more boxes and more, harder camps give reasons to stay and rotate around the jungle. [5:33]
- Vents heal you now, and the flying bats around the map heal you too. [6:03]
- Kite troopers when farming: shooting them makes them chase and group up, so they are easier to kill. [6:03]
- This is an indirect buff to AOE heroes like Seven and Lady Geist, who can group two to three camps and nuke them at once, farming faster than everyone else. [6:33]
- On tier one camps, shoot one at the far end and start heavy meleeing; they group up and one heavy melee hits all of them. [7:04]
- Early game, depending on hero, buy Extra Regen and Monster Rounds so you can loop push wave, kill jungle, repeat. [7:04]
- The new camps are harder, some with special or heavy attacks (exploding barrels), and he nearly lost one to camps. [7:04]
- Corrupted items (limited time): two shops appear on the map, one on your side and one on the enemy side, marked by a symbol at 30 minutes, then respawning every 15 minutes (30, 45, 60 and so on). [8:35]
- Corrupting an item buffs it with one drawback; his Spellslinger got more fire rate, duration and stats but lost ability range, which didn't matter on Vindicta. [8:35]
- Corrupted items give late game scaling where long games used to stall with no more power spikes; remember to take them every time they respawn. [9:05]
- Rotations are slower than ever: the map is more spaced out and no movement tech for it is known yet. [9:36]
- Walkers and guardians are closer together, especially mid, where the walker is right past the guardian. [10:06]
- In side lanes the walker is farther, so split pushing deep is riskier and harder to escape because your zipline sits far back. Ziplines are much farther from lane now. [10:06]
- When split pushing, kill at least two waves every time you leave lane; losing lane priority is much costlier now and walkers fall for free. [10:37]
- Walkers now have lots of space around them: when taking or fighting under a walker (for example with Rejuvenator), keep moving underneath and around it and its pillars to dodge fireballs and slams. [11:07]
- As Vyper he slid around an entire walker, got hit maybe once and took it, by positioning and not tanking every hit. [11:37]
- Mid is a death zone if you overcommit: the lane past the walker is longer than the stretch between guardian and walker, and it is open. [12:08]
- Vindicta is amazing this patch because of the open space; watch for Abrams closing with Phantom Strike, and dispel, Counterspell or Enhanced Dispel save you. [12:08]
- There is almost no lane cover between lane, guardian and walker now; cover is mostly in the jungle, so commit to positioning carefully because getting caught out of the jungle usually means death. [12:38]
- Protect your guardian more than ever because of how much map presence you lose without it. [13:08]
- Parrying the guardian no longer resets your parry, so if an enemy dives you under your guardian, punch them: they have no parry. Watch for Rebuttal. [13:08]
- Farming is more important than ever with these changes. [13:39]
