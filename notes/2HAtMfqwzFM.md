## Claims
Vyper:
- Vyper technically has two passives: her 2 turns into a passive when maxed, and her 3 is a passive. [17:01]
- Her 1 is very easy to hit because its hitbox is pretty large. [17:01]
- Her 4 was just buffed with a movement buff. [17:31]
- Her stamina is a limit; wait for your stamina before going in. [4:05]
- He considers her one of the most broken heroes right now; people playing only Vyper reach Ascendant. [12:26]
- If you can aim even mildly okay, she is unbelievably strong. [20:37]
- Every Vyper player buys Split Shot; he suggests he should get Ricochet and drop Split Shot. [10:21] [20:37]
- When dying to gun damage he buys shielding (a bullet barrier). [6:38]
- He fills all slots with barriers and Extra Health when he shops with the Guardian down. [13:26]
- He buys spirit resist when he sees he is dying to spirit damage. [20:04]
- Unstoppable is what let him run at fights; with it the enemy can never get the fight, and you cannot lose mid boss. [28:22] [30:24]
- He heals out of combat from his build, so he does not need extra healing. [15:29]

Lane and game:
- Clear the wave before hitting the enemy heroes. [3:34]
- An enemy can put up a wall and then parry you when you punch; it works on some people. [3:34]
- Before pushing up alone, check if the enemy Drifter has Veil Walker; if he did, he would not push there because Drifter could one-shot him. [14:27]
- A trade where you kill an enemy so he loses money and damage their walker, while they get nothing, is a good trade. [14:27]
- A vending machine can be more important than the mid boss at that moment. [17:31]
- The Rift would have been the losing team's chance to come back, but don't die for it. [10:53]
- Shoot the enemy turrets in fights; clear them before they do damage. [19:34] [27:20]

Patch notes (minor patch):
- Turrets (McGinnis's, from context): decay is a lot lower but their HP is higher; generally a nerf but more complex than that. They are no longer impossible-to-kill mini cannons, though still strong. [4:35] [5:06]
- The enemy hero he faced first was nerfed the day before, which he approves of. [1:00]
- A teammate says the minor patch also changed Repose and, he thinks, disengaging. [1:30]

General advice:
- When your team is globally up money (he says 20k), stop being scared to fight; being scared to fight is how you lose games, and the money is for fighting. [27:52] [30:24]
- If you monopolize the game and make the most money, you win team fights, and with Unstoppable you cannot lose mid boss. [30:24]
- You can take more risks when you are ahead, and you get further ahead by killing people. [30:24]
- When he ran up on three enemies, his team should have pushed forward and gone for whoever he was going for. [29:53]
