## Covers
Viscous, coached by Midknight over a replay of an Oracle-rank player's game.
A coaching session: Puddle Punch for movement, routing, melee hygiene, Rifts, and team fighting.
Patch of early October 2026 (video dated 2026-10-11).

## Claims
- To solo contest a jungle camp against an enemy, wait until they start to Splatter it so you can steal the last hit; you will not get it on your own. [1:07]
- Old tier 1 camps could be one-shot by Viscous if you angled Splatter correctly; on new tier 1 it looked like it did not quite one-shot, and Mystic Burst would add a lot of damage. [1:07]
- Route out of a bad spot instead of routing in circles. [1:43]
- In Baba lanes the coach pretty much always buys Rebel (as captioned), same as on Viscous: a free 20% damage amp whenever you fight her, and it damages her on the fairies. [1:43]
- A heavy melee near the enemy tower was too risky: if parried there you probably die because you are in tower aggro range, and getting a parry is pretty much the only way Baba wins the lane or fight. [2:15]
- Use Puddle Punch for movement; it is really important. [2:47]
- You can Puddle Punch a teammate out of danger, which would have done wonders in that spot. [2:47]
- Routing in circles slows your time to get back to your tower. [2:47]
- A tempo hero is one that fights all game; Baba cannot farm camps well but is ridiculously strong in a 1v1. [2:47]
- Bunny hopping is good, but do not waste stamina on a double jump that loops you into an inefficient route. [3:18]
- When caught, look for a Puddle Punch to get out and live. [3:48]
- Once Express Shot (as captioned) is online on Viscous, right click for its large extra damage rather than left clicking. [3:48]
- Against Baba, roll out of her 1 when she casts it on you if you have stamina; that makes her feel much weaker. [4:19]
- Route through Baba: with three stamina you can dash past her; when she melees, parry and leave. [4:19]
- You died because you routed in a circle when they ran at you; just run to your Guardian and you are fine. [4:19]
- Common mistake: with good movement you keep trying to break line of sight instead of pathing out to the objective. [4:49]
- You held on to your ultimate too hard; use it as a tool or escape (press it and leave) without cutting it that close. [5:27]
- Spirit Shielding was a good item for this comp. [5:57]
- Viscous always goes his gun spike first, rushing Express Shot (as captioned) because his alt fire is strong. [5:57]
- Even on a spirit hero like Lash, go your gun spike first into Baba lanes because she takes away all your spirit power right now. [5:57]
- Players also tend to build more melee early against Baba because you do not have spirit to do anything with. [6:27]
- Stay in the shop to full buy; leaving with a big unspent bag means you die with 500 souls instead of 100. [6:27]
- If you are probably going to die anyway, roll back to the shop and buy rather than die with a huge bag. [6:58]
- When Baba engages, dash left or right instead of backwards so you juke her 1. [6:58]
- Baba relies on hitting her 1 and the spirit damage on her autos; if you kite out her 1, she does not do much. [7:28]
- Viscous wants to be rotating into fights and brawling; an active game is good. [7:28]
- Do not side lane on Viscous, but catching a couple of waves because your team's map positioning requires it is fine. [7:58]
- Buying while carrying the Urn is fine when you are in no danger, but good players stack Urn side to catch or kill you at that shop. [8:28]
- Against Mirage's Djinn's Mark, cube when the marks start stacking; break line of sight for about 50 to 65% of the mark and cube at about half duration to avoid the mark hit. [8:58]
- Do not try to 1v1 a Mirage with max mark: his Fire Scarabs cut your damage by about 20% and he stacks mark on you and you die. [9:29]
- Cube does stop the mark stacking but does not cleanse it; time it after Mirage's ultimate (cube after the first bird hits) so you dodge the ult instead of taking full damage plus the bird. [10:07]
- Practice the cube timing: cube right after you get hit by the first bird. [10:37]
- Chunking the mid Walker without finishing it was a wasted advantage. [10:37]
- Giving a comeback Rift is fine only if your team does something with the time; giving it and doing nothing hands the enemy a path back into the game. [11:07]
- With a Lash on your team, do not fight in an enclosed area; kite the enemy out of it. [11:39]
- Nobody cleaning up the Rift wave feels really bad. [11:39]
- When a fight is lost, disengage; any more dying guarantees the enemy takes mid boss. [12:09]
- Try to path out instead of continuing to trade when you should leave. [12:09]
- Always push the neutral wave out before you rotate; otherwise it pushes weak and slow and your team misses the souls. [12:39]
- Hit shrines together, not split, since up to four people on a shrine reduces its bullet resist. [12:39]
- The second shrine is much harder to kill; once your team disengages after the first shrine, fully disengage too. [13:40]
- If the enemy overextends after their Rift and pushes the Walker with six, your team should end up with mid boss. [14:44]
- After taking an Abrams kill with his ult offline, keep your waves up and you can do mid boss off his respawn timer. [14:44]
- Letting the enemy Abrams respawn before acting wasted the window. [15:17]
- Having almost no Counterspell and no stun stoppers on the team felt bad. [15:47]
- Play outside the Infernus ultimate radius; positioning too close got you clipped. [15:47]
- Get into the fight sooner and melee the crystal. [15:47]
- You could have baited mid boss because the enemy had Infernus and Abrams ults. [16:50]
- Your Mina must silence the Abrams before he holds his ultimate, and the Counterspell holder must be in position. [16:50]
- You played the fight too defensively; do range poke from on top of the bridge where you have line of sight. [17:21]
- Set up side waves and wait for them to create pressure so they pull people off mid, then look for the fight mid or a pick in the side lanes. [17:53]
- Do not stagger into a fight; after a teammate dies, sit in spawn, because staggering can cost the game. [18:24]
- If mid boss is not coming up, do not look for an early fight; defend on the shrine instead of actively hunting. [18:55]
- If mid boss is coming up, chasing kills is good, because unkilled enemies get a free mid boss. [19:25]
- You can cube teammates who are hit by Mirage's birds. [19:58]
- With three picks and nothing for the enemy to contest, go mid boss off the respawn. [19:58]
- You cannot dodge the Infernus ult if you stand in the pit; play outside the pit, and when the ult goes off, Phantom Strike back in and parry. [20:29]
- Do not 1v1 the Ivy individually; fight with your team. [21:00]
- At low health, walk out and do not chase into a fight. [21:32]
- Focus the Mirage, not the Baba; Baba had 53% spirit resist from her passive and would not die. [21:32]
- Team fix for split focus: call targets, or have one player call targets. [22:02]
- Individual fix: fight with your team, do not go on the Ivy alone, and disengage at 1 HP. [22:02]
- Looking for a fight mid while the enemy is busy at Rift is good, since someone is pulled off. [23:02]
- A free Rift is about 8k souls plus Rift minions that put heavy pressure on teams that do not clear their side waves. [23:33]
- Dying right before mid boss is very bad. [24:03]
- Fight inside your base when defending, not chasing outside it. [24:36]
- Viscous got melted for lack of defensive items; against a gun and fire rate heavy team, buy Juggernaut or Plated Armor, and Plated is the pick against Mirage. [25:06]
- Summary: use Puddle Punch (3) for movement more early. [25:06]
- Summary: route out to the objective, not in circles and not to break line of sight. [25:36]
- Summary: stop over-meleeing; light melees are good, heavy melees are weak, do not rip heavies for no reason. [25:36]
- Summary: do not give Rifts for free; three free Rifts ultimately cost this game. [26:07]
- Summary: fight with your team and do not re-engage at 1 HP. [26:07]
