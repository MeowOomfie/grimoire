# This is Why Drifter is STILL Broken
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-04-17
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JaTcFLnlFSE

## Covers
Drifter gameplay with commentary by aquatiq, lane with Viscous against Abrams and Pocket, April 2026 patch.
Covers his item path (Long Range first, Unstoppable into a CC-heavy enemy comp, Inhibitor and Silencer), ability max order, breaking boxes and statues, dying for objectives, and pushing waves and stealing farm when ahead.

## Claims
Drifter:
- He buys Long Range first, as usual, and was glad to have it in lane. [3:02]
- With a Viscous lane partner he plays more aggressive and risky, because Viscous will cube him if he does something crazy. [4:07]
- Pocket does a lot of damage without needing many items, and Abrams can half your health bar with a punch, so he is normally scared of both in lane. [4:07]
- Abrams is too strong early and in his view needs a nerf. [2:02]
- He bought Extra Regen in lane because he had extra health but no sustain. [6:11]
- He went Toxic Bullets after that. [9:52]
- He usually maxes his 1, then his ult, then Stalker's Mark (captioned "Jin Mark"); this game he maxed the ult, which he found really strong. [16:37]
- Into Mo and Krill, Dynamo, Abrams' charge and Mirage's tornado, he goes Vampiric Burst and Unstoppable, because the enemy has everything that can stop him from running around. [18:09]
- He bought Fortitude for now; once he has Vampiric Burst he no longer needs as much healing. [21:13]
- Only press Unstoppable when Abrams or Dynamo actually tries to ult you; he keeps it on a separate key so he does not fat finger it. [25:15, 35:13]
- He wanted to sell Fortitude for Spirit Resilience: he would rather not take the damage in the first place. [27:19]
- Inhibitor and Silencer were what he needed into their team; Inhibitor definitely helped in the fights that followed. [27:51, 35:13]
- When afflicted by Pocket, leave; you can't do much while afflicted. [29:59]
- He sold Fleetfoot for Juggernaut late. [35:13, 36:47]
- With unstoppable up, an enemy hero's CC does not matter going into mid boss. [33:38]
- His ult is weak late game. [28:55]

General:
- Break boxes on the way when you move to another lane to gank, especially late game; it takes a couple of seconds and gives a lot of souls. [9:20]
- Break golden statues too; they give permanent buffs, such as +3% weapon damage and spirit. [9:20]
- Dying for a good reason (taking the urn, a walker, or any advantage) is fine; KDA is not everything if you have big objective damage. [15:06]
- When you are behind, take more risks than you normally would; he dove into the urn knowing no enemy was near and his team was there, died, and the urn put his team up in souls. [15:36]
- Push all your waves deep: two layers of troopers at their base force them to go back and defend, so they stop coming for you. Keep the pressure up. [31:03]
- When several enemies are dead, steal all their farm, including T3 camps, and take the urn: starve them of resources even if you don't need the souls. [37:53]
- Play with McGinnis' turrets; they are useful most of the time. [28:24]
