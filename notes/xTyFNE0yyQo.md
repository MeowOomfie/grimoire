## Covers
Grey Talon, aquatiq, commentary over one of his own games playing a spirit "bird build" that maxes Guided Owl, after an earlier gun build video.

## Claims
- This is a bird (Guided Owl) build, a spirit build instead of his usual gun build. [1:03]
- Grey Talon is a little weak right now, especially in a meta where everybody buys resilience and he does little damage late game. [2:05]
- He wanted extra regen or healing rate early in a rough lane. [2:05]
- Haze needs a side lane, so swapping lanes with her was a good idea. [2:35]
- His early damage is insane; later it is less consistent but can be good. [5:08]
- Do not use the bird when you do not know where the target is. [5:39]
- Maxing the bird does a lot of damage and the cooldown is short; he could use it about every 70 seconds. [7:41]
- Use the bird as soon as it comes up. [8:44]
- Watch enemy health bars a lot so you do not waste birds; do not bird an Ivy who will just Stone Form it. [13:21]
- He bought Sharpshooter over Mystic Shot. [10:15]
- He considered Veil Walker. [11:16]
- He rarely dies on Grey Talon because you play from the back, enemies cannot reach you, a single arrow deletes much of their HP bar, and they know a maxed bird can one-shot them. [12:20]
- Bird a whole fight when you have it; if it gets someone, good, and if not, it is fine. [13:21]
- He bought Tank Buster and Mystic Reverb, and put Mystic Reverb on the bird. Later he wished it was on his 1 instead. [13:52]
- He does little to Drifter alone, so he does not fight him solo. [18:36]
- He skipped Healbane because his team already had three spirit heroes, and went for Lightning Scroll, then Diviner's Kevlar for the barrier. [20:07]
- Bird Talon might be the best way to play the hero: execute people with the bird to get stacks, then shoot people with Charged Shot, which does a lot of damage from the extra spirit power. [15:22]
- The stacks give a lot of spirit power; do not underestimate it. [15:22]
- With a Mo and Krill under him he needed Counterspell. [16:24]

- He needed Indomitable that game because Mo kept ulting him. [23:41]
- He considered Cheat Death later. [23:41]
- A bag is not worth it if it costs you mid boss. [23:09]
- He wanted Diviner's Kevlar over Expansion for the extra protection when he birds. [25:44]
- Pushing every wave lets you go for shrines. [26:44]
- Downing the Patron when you get the chance keeps the game close even after losing a fight. [29:52]
- With Lightning Scroll and Mystic Reverb, birding a grouped enemy team stuns them all. [28:46]
