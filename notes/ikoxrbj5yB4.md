## Covers
Quick-start guide for Rat King, the new tank gun hybrid hero: each ability and its upgrades, ability level order, a default item build and how to counter him.
For players picking up Rat King on release or wanting to know what he does.

## Claims
- Rat King is a tank gun hybrid that uses very little spirit and is very tanky. [0:00]
- Ability 1, Scrap Grenade, works much like Paradox's grenade: long throw range, explodes multiple times and tracks toward enemies who try to run away. [0:31]
- Scrap Grenade is your first harassment tool in lane; it does a decent amount of damage and slows. [0:31]
- Scrap Grenade upgrades: lower cooldown, more damage per detonation, and a final upgrade that adds a big final detonation that does a lot of damage. [0:31]
- Scrap Grenade damage scales with gun; only its movement slow scales with spirit, so build gun items for it. [1:02]
- Ability 2, Rat Swarm, is close to Vindicta's crows: rats attach to an enemy, each bite does damage and reduces the enemy's bullet resist. [1:02]
- Rat Swarm base is one charge and 1 bullet resist shred per bite; maxed it does more damage, has a bigger swarm and shreds 2. [1:33]
- Rat Swarm is very forgiving to aim: it hits basically anything on your screen, though not enemies behind you. [1:33]
- Rat Swarm is probably the ability to level first in lane, since the extra charge, longer debuff and bullet resist reduction feed his main damage source, his gun. [2:05]
- His gun fires fast, with all right velocity and a big magazine. [2:05]
- Rat Swarm scales with spirit, but you do not want to build a lot of spirit on him. [2:36]
- Ability 3 (Royal Pestiments as captioned) gives a barrier that scales with max health and has a chance to return enemy bullets when they shoot you; it is what makes him tanky. [2:36]
- At level one the barrier lasts 6 seconds on a 22 second cooldown; after the first two upgrades it lasts 10 seconds on a 14 second cooldown, so it is up basically the whole game. [2:36]
- Use the barrier before hitting a sacrifice (vault) or shooting jungle camps to save HP while farming. [3:07]
- The first two points in the barrier are very high value to take early. [3:07]
- The barrier's final upgrade caps damage from any single ability hit at 250 and adds another 10% max health as barrier. [3:38]
- Against the 250 cap, big single hits like Yamato's or a full-gun ultimate shot only do 250, so do not use big one-hit ults on him. [3:38]
- Damage over time effects like Toxic Bullets or Pocket's Affliction should be good against him. [4:08]
- The ultimate is the most broken part of his kit: he carries a banner forward (cannot shoot while carrying it) and plants it anywhere by pressing 1. [4:39]
- The banner gives your whole team slow immunity, movement speed and reduced damage taken from enemies. [4:39]
- The banner's range is huge even at base with no items, covering roughly an entire Rift fight area. [5:09]
- While the banner is up, rats spawn at intervals and apply the Rat Swarm debuff to enemies who come close. [5:09]
- Unlike Graves's ult (as captioned), the banner cannot be broken and lasts its full duration. [5:39]
- Counter to the banner: do not fight in it; if he drops it, get out of there or you will probably lose. [5:39]
- In lane your harass tools are Scrap Grenade and Rat Swarm, then the shield; the shield is weak early because of its long cooldown and 6 second duration. [5:39]
- Scrap Grenade's final explosion is easy to escape, so its max upgrade is a late game upgrade; the ult's bonus damage and damage reduction are not needed super early either. [6:09]
- Shown level order start: one point in Scrap Grenade, then a second in Scrap Grenade, then one in Rat Swarm, then definitely the shield to level two (cheap, not a five-point upgrade). [6:39]
- The shield at level two is your highest value over time once you leave lane to farm or roam, because it is always active. [6:39]
- If you want to join team fights more, level the ult; its early levels (cooldown, charge duration) are not strong, but the final level raises its damage reduction from 20 to 35. [7:09]
- If you want to farm, be tankier and take single target fights, go Rat Swarm; its final level doubles the bullet resist shred. [7:09]
- The speaker's order: Rat Swarm, then max the ult for mid game team fights, delay the shield's third upgrade (early abilities rarely hit for more than 250), and max Scrap Grenade last. [7:39]
- Fleetfoot is a must have and worth buying earlier, because he has no movement abilities and struggles with enemies kiting him or running away. [8:09]
- The default build shown is solid; Close Quarters seems reasonable early. [8:09]
- Skip Mystic Expansion from the default build. [8:39]
- Fortitude is questionable but fits, since the shield scales with max health. [8:39]
- Extra health items are very strong on him; do not sleep on green (vitality) items, especially anything with more health. [8:39]
- Big buy: Colossus, because it gives the most base health in the game; at 4.9k HP his shield gives more than 1k extra health on every use. [9:09]
- Witchmail is another strong buy because spirit damage is still common in this meta and it lets you spam your shield permanently. [9:09]
- He is one of the first heroes with his own bullet shred based purely on bullet damage, so he may not need many orange (weapon) items to be scary. [9:40]
- Press crouch to have him squeeze into the sewers (bugged at the time of recording). [9:40]
- He should show up in mid game team fights; his ult is described as the first in the game that buffs your entire team. [10:12]
