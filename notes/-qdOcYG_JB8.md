## Covers
First-look sandbox analysis of Celeste, a new disruptor and spirit nuker hero from the Old Gods New Blood update, on release.
Every ability with numbers and upgrades, the ultimate's bounce range and how to counter it, item picks (High Velocity Rounds, Arcane Surge, Kinetic Dash, Rapid Recharge, Boundless Spirit, Superior Cooldown) and skill order.
For players picking up or playing against Celeste in February 2026.

## Claims
- Celeste is a disruptor who weaves in and out of fights dealing massive damage; he calls her the most broken release so far, a spirit nuker that seems easy to get into and likely to be nerfed very soon. [0:00]
- Her weapon is a staff firing a fairly slow projectile that bounces and can headshot; shots that hit the floor near an enemy ricochet to them for the same damage, so you can hit people behind corners. [0:32]
- The ricochet makes her left click an insane lane harass tool; because the projectile is slow, High Velocity Rounds feels like a requirement. [1:04]
- Ability 1, Light Eater: a large cone in front of you that is hard to miss; hit enemies take spirit damage when you hit them with bullets and give you spirit lifesteal. It is a lane harass debuff. [1:04]
- Light Eater's spirit lifesteal means your other abilities also heal you on debuffed enemies. [1:34]
- Light Eater upgrades: 10-second cooldown, 3 m cast range, more spirit lifesteal; max level gives 25 damage and increased spirit scaling. [2:04]
- Put only one point in Light Eater early; its spirit scaling barely matters in lane, but it scales very well into late game. [2:04]
- Ability 2, Dazzling Trick: a barrier with bonus movement speed; if enemies break it while active, it silences everyone around you in a big radius and deals 50% of the barrier as damage (a 128 barrier did about 60). [2:35]
- Dazzling Trick upgrades: level one movement speed; level two +100 barrier and scaling from 1.1 to 1.8; level three longer silence, cooldown cut in half, and it combos with the ult. [3:06]
- Ability 3, Radiant Daggers: calls a beam of light on an area (similar to Paige's 3); enemies hit take impact damage and amplified spirit damage from you. [3:06]
- Radiant Daggers upgrades: it uses charges and the first upgrade gives three charges in lane; then cooldown cut almost in half and more impact damage; max level more spirit amp on the enemy and more fire rate. [3:37]
- Keep shooting enemies with auto attacks because Light Eater works with them. [4:07]
- Ultimate: an orb of light that bounces between enemies, slowing movement and reducing dash distance; base level bounces up to eight times. [4:37]
- Ult upgrades: more movement slow and dash reduction, more damage, and at level three 10 more max bounces plus lower cooldown. [4:37]
- Counter to the ult: move out of range of your teammates; if there is only one enemy, the orb finds no bounce target and disappears after a while. [5:10]
- With Dazzling Trick maxed the ult can bounce off Celeste herself, so even in a 1v1 you can bounce it back to yourself; later it feels like there is no counter. [5:41]
- The ult's bounce distance on base level with no items is very large, larger than he expected in testing. [6:17]
- The kit has insane synergy: everything amps spirit damage, more spirit damage gives life back, and at max level the shield is on a 17-second cooldown and scales with spirit. [6:47]
- Raw spirit will be crazy on her; Arcane Surge and Kinetic Dash will also be crazy. [6:47]
- Arcane Surge works on every ability; Light Eater already lasts 12 seconds so use Surge elsewhere. On the shield it extends duration, enlarges the area and adds spirit power, meaning more health and more damage. [7:18]
- The shield lasts 4 seconds; breaking it silences you and deals a lot of damage, so the counter to the shield is not breaking it. [7:18]
- Arcane Surge also works very well on Radiant Daggers by increasing its radius. [7:49]
- Radiant Daggers charges have about a 2-second delay between casts, so Rapid Recharge works insanely well, giving about five charges you can cast almost instantly. [8:21]
- At 8k souls without many good items each Radiant Dagger did 172 damage; the cooldown is low at 15 seconds per charge, and it can probably farm creep camps well. [8:52]
- She is a hybrid hero who wants to shoot while casting because of Light Eater; buy some gun items, the best being Kinetic Dash for extra stamina and fire rate. [9:22]
- Get the 4k gun investment spike because her gun works really well, but overall buy mostly spirit: Boundless Spirit is probably key, plus Superior Cooldown and Rapid Recharge. [9:52]
- Likely ult tech: when the orb stops bouncing, use yourself (with the maxed shield) to keep it bouncing and keep chaining it while chasing. [10:22]
- Skill order: start 1, 2, 3, 4 in lane, then a point in Light Eater for its 8-second cooldown so you can use it every time you engage. [10:22]
- At least two points in Radiant Daggers early for three charges, lower cooldown and more damage. [10:52]
- Then max Dazzling Trick: level two makes the shield last longer and bigger, and max level cuts the cooldown by more than half, enables the ult combo and lengthens the silence. Maxing Radiant Daggers for more amp and fire rate per stack may also turn out overpowered. [10:52]
- Verdict: broken, better and cooler than he expected; he jokes that as a spirit hero she will not get nerfed, so enjoy her while you can. [11:22]
