# The Biggest Economy Change EVER in Deadlock - How does it affect you?
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-11-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PRjWVA5CmbQ

## Covers
The November 2024 soul sharing change (sharing kept past the laning stage, then nerfed to 75% each for two players a few days later): measured soul gain, what it devalued (Urn, guardians, jungle), how to adapt builds and play, and the removal of Soul Rebirth.
Patch-specific; old economy.

## Claims
- In the Thursday patch, soul sharing (both players in a dual lane getting full value for a soul) was extended from the laning stage to the whole game. [0:01] [0:32]
- Before the patch, soul sharing stopped at 8 minutes. [2:38]
- Measured effect: a fairly even game before the change had about 120k vs 125k team souls at 20 minutes; a game after had 135k vs 144k, roughly 20,000 more souls at 20 minutes, about 1,000 souls per minute per team or 160 to 170 per player. [1:04] [1:34]
- The common benchmark of 1,000 souls per minute is now closer to 1,200 because of the change. [1:34]
- Before the change, after 8 minutes you wanted one person per lane; more than one in a lane should be doing something worth it (pushing a guardian, getting kills), because two players just farming was inefficient. [2:38] [3:09]
- That old rule made jungle farming after 8 minutes valuable and made fast jungle farmers like Seven valuable; the meta was one or two players in the jungle while the rest played lanes for the highest soul efficiency. [3:09] [3:39]
- The formula was reworked again on Sunday after community criticism, nerfing the sharing to stop the deathball meta. [2:05] [5:12]
- Current split: a solo player gets 100% of a soul; two players each get 75%, 150% in total (he first said 70%, then corrected it). [3:39] [7:15]
- More players in a lane give more total souls up to a point, with little difference between two and three, and six players total about 1.32 times the value. [4:41]
- The deathball meta happened because the jungle was cut by 5% while lanes paid a lot more for groups, which devalued the rest of the map. [5:43]
- Now split pushing alone (for example Wraith, Haze, Mirage) has a purpose again, since a solo player gets the most souls to one person, while two in a lane is still the most souls in total for the team. [6:14] [6:44] [7:15]
- Valve's goal is faster games; Deathy has had many more 20-minute games since the change, since more souls means hitting timings and mid boss sooner. [8:16]
- The Urn is worth less now: its soul reward was not buffed while lane farm pays more, so spending a lot of resources to take it safely costs more lane farm. [8:47] [9:17]
- Guardians (towers) are also devalued because their soul bounty was not adjusted. [9:17]
- Neutrals (the jungle) are double devalued: their bounty did not rise, their sharing was not reworked, and they took a 5% cut. [9:48]
- The Urn still gives 1 AP and guardians still give flex slots, so they are not worthless. [10:18]
- Faster games mean tempo: avoid super greedy builds that rely on a lot of 6,200 items, such as Haze rushing Ricochet to come online at 25 minutes as a raid boss. [10:48] [11:18]
- With more money overall, heroes that rely on one key item get it roughly 15 to 20% faster. [11:49]
- Dynamo got stronger: he needs Unstoppable (and Refresher) to use his ultimate safely, and early Unstoppable is easier to reach now. [12:19]
- Lash, a tempo hero who tends to fall off late, feels very strong because the late game arrives faster and is less extreme. [12:50]
- Soul Rebirth was removed; it had become mandatory late. In Ascendant lobbies and tournaments after 30 minutes the game became a race to buy the most Soul Rebirths, and a team with six would force a fight against a team with two. [13:21] [13:52]
- Takeaways: objectives and the Urn are worth a bit less and fights worth a bit more, so do not throw your life away for objectives as easily; avoid greedy builds; be active on the map early. [14:22] [14:52]
- Prioritize lanes and objectives over the jungle; still farm the jungle when you can, but it is not a high priority, and its long respawn timers suggest the game is not built around it. [14:52]
