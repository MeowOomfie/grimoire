# Deadlock Has A Big Problem Right Now
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-06-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4zwLo1g2b4Q

## Covers
Opinion piece on Deadlock balance as of late June 2025: items nerfed instead of buffed, slow patch turnaround, item nerfs aimed at one hero, balancing for pick rate and low rank, and the loss of early-weak/late-strong hero design. Mostly opinion, but it records the meta of that patch (lane bully, snowball, teamfight) and a few concrete game facts and tips.
For players curious about the June 2025 meta and how hero power curves shape play.

## Claims
- Recent patches since the item rework have been nearly all item nerfs and few buffs, so standout builds are hard to make; healing items in particular are weakened. [1:04]
- With most healing nerfed, players lean on Fortitude to stay alive in lane instead of going back to base. [1:34]
- If healing keeps being cut, the only viable style becomes poke heroes that force enemies back to base, giving a slow, boring meta. [2:04]
- The patch planned about four weeks earlier hard nerfed Wraith's lane health and hero damage after the meta had already moved on to Infernus and Mirage, leaving Wraith arguably bottom three while Infernus got no nerf. [3:06]
- After that patch the game is back to a lane bully meta. [4:39]
- Radiant Regeneration is weak on most heroes but worked very well on Lady Geist, and to a lesser extent Mirage; same for Mystic Regeneration. After its hard nerf it is pretty weak even on Lady Geist and unplayable on anyone else. [5:09]
- Mobas have heroes like Dota's Anti-Mage that are weak early but dominate late once farmed; the team's job is to hold off until that hero is farmed. [7:40]
- Before the nerf, Wraith was weak in lane but came out of the jungle farmed at 20 to 25 minutes and dominated the server. [8:11]
- In this meta most meta heroes are strong early, mid and late; Infernus is strong early, stronger mid and even stronger late, which is why he is so strong right now. [9:42]
- Pocket is pretty good in lane, extremely strong mid game (can one-shot everyone) and falls off late game. [10:45]
- Spirit heroes feeling weak is mainly a late game thing; a late game spirit hero would need to be weaker early to be balanced. [11:15]
- Different play styles need different skills: Viscous needs spatial awareness and timing to cube teammates; Lash needs great movement and positioning; most gun heroes need a lot of aim. [13:16]
- In this meta snowballing and teamfighting are very strong: if you are ahead after laning you most likely win, and the team that shows up with more players on average wins. [14:50]
- Lash and Haze historically have high pick rates because people enjoy them, not because they are strong; Haze appeals to shooter players who don't want to think about abilities, Lash to players who like flying around. [15:51]
- Bebop is not fun to play against when he is meta because he dominates everyone. [16:52]
- Haze has the highest win rate in low ranks (up to Emissary and Archon) and drops off more and more through Oracle, Phantom, Ascendant and Eternus; she is good but not overpowered. [18:23]
- Haze wins low rank games because low rank players are bad at movement and macro positioning, so she can come in, go invisible, Sleep Dagger, ult, and players fail to get out of the ult in time. [18:53]
- Mirage and Infernus are too strong, but many spirit heroes are also strong; Lady Geist builds a lot of gun items yet is strong from her abilities, Soul Exchange, Life Drain and Malice. [19:24]
- If the enemy hard carry (like Wraith) AFK farms the jungle, punish them early: take towers more effectively, use the extra map space to gank, and invade the enemy jungle to kill the Wraith. Letting them farm until 30 minutes is misplaying. [19:55]
- After the patch Wraith is the worst laner in the game and no longer gets strong late. [20:26]
- Meta heroes now are strong in lane, strong mid and don't fall off late, so the meta is lane bully: pick Yamato or Mirage, win lane, stay strong mid. [21:56]
- Late game Mirage with Inhibitor and Ricochet kills everyone. [21:56]
- Shiv is a hybrid tank, insane in lane and arguably the strongest hero in early mid game fights, and doesn't really fall off. [22:27]
- Kelvin is the biggest lane bully in the game, still very good mid game, and doesn't fall off late because the dome and Arctic Beam AoE always do something. [22:27]
- Calico, after multiple nerfs, is still good in lane and mid game and very strong late. [23:28]
- Haze is pretty weak in lane but very good mid and late game. [23:28]
- Pocket is a good design like Dota's Puck: solid in lane, strong in mid game ganks, weak late, so you know when you are useful. [23:58]
- Lash is one of the best heroes in mid game and later mid game fights and his ground slam does insane burst damage, but if you let the game run to 40 minutes the gun heroes will kill you; end the game in your window. [24:28]
