# Valve Just Changed Mirage and now he's BROKEN
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-10

## Covers
Mirage after the March 2026 patch, played by aquatiq with commentary over one full game (a "Wick Mirage" build).
Patch reaction over gameplay: what changed on Fire Scarabs, Dust Devil (tornado) and the teleport ult, plus item choices and notes on Wraith, Ivy and the new Golden Goose Egg.
Patch date: March 2026 (video 2026-03-10).

## Claims
- Mirage was already strong and did not need buffs, but he got buffed. [0:30]
- Fire Scarabs no longer stack damage over time; they deal their damage and give the heal immediately, which is better sustain. [1:31]
- Fire Scarabs still have charges and a short cooldown; they steal HP and the enemy takes increased damage from you. [2:02]
- Downside of the change: you cannot spam Fire Scarabs as freely as before. [2:33]
- He considered Extra Charge to use Fire Scarabs more. [3:04]
- The tornado (renamed Dust Devil) now has charges, so you can cast it twice in a short time; it was already a great escape and is now about twice as good. [2:33]
- The build uses Echo Shard on the tornado, giving three tornado casts in the late game. [4:05]
- On max tornado it can be recast within about 5 seconds. [4:35]
- He wanted Quicksilver Reload next. [6:08]
- He skipped Healing Rite in a lane that did not need it and rushed Toxic Bullets over Improved Spirit. [6:39]
- Max the 3 (Djinn's Mark), as usual. [7:09]
- Call it when enemies are missing from your lane. [7:41]
- 1v1s are hard to lose on Mirage because Djinn's Mark does so much damage and the tornado gets you away, though his win rate is not that good. [9:47]
- The teleport ult can now go to any location on the minimap after a short wait, and you can move while channeling it. [11:49]
- Teleport upgrades give barriers, and the tier three upgrade cuts its cooldown (minus 80 as captioned). [12:52]
- Now any damage taken during the teleport cancels it completely; before, damage did not matter. [13:22]
- Toxic Bullets is very strong, especially on Mirage; it killed a Bebop who had no healing. [13:54]
- Golden Goose Egg is a new item: it sits in a slot, you hatch it for souls and permanent buffs, and it lowers your damage while held; it can be good when behind. [14:25]
- He would not buy Golden Goose Egg even on supports; it does too little. [21:01]
- He bought Ricochet. [14:55]
- Dispel Magic against Bebop removes his bombs but drops them on the ground rather than deleting them. [15:25]
- Fire Scarabs are much bigger now, so they hit people quickly and easily. [16:26]
- He skipped a gun buff because staying alive mattered more. [18:29]
- Buy Inhibitor against heavy healing; here Mo & Krill and Mina. [19:00]
- He considered Burst Fire after Ricochet. [20:00]
- Ability order in this build: max tornado and max the 3, leave the 1 untouched; max tornado is so good now it makes sense to max it. [21:31]
- Silencer is a strong next buy: Mina cannot fly away and Wraith cannot use her cards. [22:03]
- Planned path: Inhibitor, then Silencer, then Boundless Spirit. [23:33]
- Wraith is OP after the patch. [25:35]
- Take the fire rate buff as a gun hero. [25:35]
- Two Dust Devil charges make him nearly uncatchable; Wraith could do nothing to him. [26:37]
- Remember to use Counterspell. [27:07]
- Ivy's 1 is devastating now; it actually traps you in the web. [30:10]
- With Echo Shard he cast the tornado three times in one fight. [33:51]
- He went Bullet Resilience instead of Metal Skin, which he dislikes. [33:51]
- An enemy team down two players should not take mid boss. [35:25]
