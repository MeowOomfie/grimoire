# How To Win More Deadlock Games | The 3 Pillars of Macro
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-09-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pwr2WGHEKMA

## Covers
Post-lane macro built on three pillars: efficiency (souls per minute, kill participation, movement), pressure (waves, contesting, stealing) and objectives (Walkers, Mid Boss, Urn and buffs).
Includes jungle priority, power-spike timing, jungling backwards, the 45-second wave rule, Walker aura value, Mid Boss calls and securing, and replay examples on Lash.
For players who feel lost after lane, especially Archon to Ascendant players who farm well but apply little pressure.

## Claims
- Troopers are always the number one source of souls in the souls-by-source graph, so never miss a wave. [1:01]
- Avoid splitting waves with more than one other person; avoid four to six people grouped in one area unless doing a major objective like the Urn or a Walker. [1:01]
- Few things are worth missing a wave for; prioritize waves over other things and jungle backwards. [1:32]
- Jungle in between waves; with a lead, always jungle on the enemy side. [1:32]
- Sinner's Sacrifice is always the most important camp: around 350 souls at 8 minutes, scaling through the game, and every hero kills it equally fast (Ivy, Lash, Infernus, Abrams alike). [2:03]
- Hitting the jackpot on a Sinner's Sacrifice gives four golden statue buffs, which are extremely important. [2:34]
- Tier 3 camps are the second best source, sometimes the best if you farm them fast; Bebop (with his ultimate), Infernus, Seven and Wraith farm them well, Abrams and Lash do not. [2:34]
- After that come tier 2 camps, tier 1 camps and boxes; by then the difference between tier 2 and tier 1 does not matter, take whatever is nearest on your path and always try to steal the enemy jungle. [3:04]
- Boxes scale up late: around 30 minutes they give a lot of souls, as good as tier 2 camps or better. [3:04]
- Kills give souls and become worth exponentially more around 20 to 25 minutes; in the first 15 minutes they are worth little, which is why a farming Wraith can out-earn a laner with five or six kills. [3:34]
- Assists also give souls. [4:04]
- Keep kill participation as high as possible without sacrificing other things. [4:04]
- Play around your power spikes and always be active after buying. [4:36]
- Spend souls as soon as you have them; farm with purpose by knowing the next item and its cost (for example Toxic Bullets, or Majestic Leap on Pocket). [4:36]
- Do not hold 4,000 to 5,000 souls in the early game. [5:07]
- After hitting a spike, be active for about 60 seconds; after that others have bought too and you are close to your next spike, so dial it back, save up, and repeat. [5:07]
- Do not play ARAM (all random all mid, 5v5 in one lane); it splits souls and creates the low-elo mid staredown where nothing happens. [5:39]
- Grouping five in mid is only for later in the game when it leads to something like Mid Boss; early on make plays in small groups with one or two other heroes. [6:11]
- Look to the side lanes and invade the enemy jungle there with one or two allies, calling targets (for example a Wraith about to farm a camp or catch a wave); this creates far more kills. [6:43]
- With an advantage, avoid playing passively: do not farm your own jungle, play on the enemy side of the map. [7:13]
- Deadlock is about having more souls than the enemy, so a less efficient action that steals their souls is better because it widens the differential. [7:43]
- Use jump pads whenever rotating; many players with 600 to 700 games under-use them. [8:14]
- Use ziplines whenever you can, and get off with crouch instead of jump to keep more momentum. [8:14]
- Wall jumps save stamina and let you cross the map much faster. [8:44]
- While shooting a wave or camp, plan your next move and the path (jump pad, zipline, walk, teleporter); teleporters are niche but important. [9:14]
- Replay: out of lane about 1,000 souls up, with Warden farming the Sinner's Sacrifices, he takes the wave alone rather than fight a 1v2. [9:45]
- Taking risk to clear a wave is fine; even dying after clearing it was worth it because the enemies were out of position with the wave at their Walker. [10:16]
- Recognize what teammates are doing: if your partner is on the Sinner's Sacrifices, you take the wave so both jobs get done. [10:46]
- The moment you clear a wave you buy time to do anything else: clear the wave, do something, go back to the wave. [11:17]
- Buy Cultist Sacrifice and use it right away on a tier 3 camp. [11:17]
- Watch the clock while farming and head to the buffs before they spawn. [11:17]
- As Lash, with no Sinner's Sacrifices left on your side and no fast tier 3 clear, farm tier 2 camps and waves; catching waves alone made him the richest player in the lobby. [11:47]
- Buy as soon as you have the souls (Majestic Leap plus Extra Stamina), then use the 60-second window, for example punishing a Paradox heard farming a camp. [12:18]
- Players with 27,000 to 28,000 souls at 20 minutes but low damage need the pressure pillar; Archon to Ascendant players mostly fail to apply enough pressure when ahead. [13:18]
- Pressure has three parts: waves, contesting enemies (who, when, how and where to fight), and stealing (map position, jungle, unsecured souls, buffs, Urn). [13:48]
- Push waves until you start dying; not all deaths are bad, and if you clear a wave and die they usually cannot push your objective for 30 to 45 seconds. [14:19]
- Early death timers are short because the game rewards trying to make plays. [14:50]
- If you die consistently while pushing waves you are too aggressive, so dial it back. [14:50]
- You have to die to learn your limit (limit testing); how far you can push depends on your rank, opponents and heroes, and only repetition teaches it. [15:20]
- Jungle backwards: do the riskiest thing first, push the wave as far as you can, go "one more wave", then jungle back toward your side. [15:51]
- Clear the wave right before rotating; rotating without clearing gives up pressure on that side, while clearing first stops counter pressure and pins an enemy there, making a numbers advantage elsewhere likely. [16:22]
- Clearing a wave buys roughly 45 seconds on the map. [16:52]
- Do not leave your lane for long unless going for objectives or someone else takes it; ask a teammate to take it over if you must leave. [16:52]
- Contesting comes down to matchup, items and rotations. [16:52]
- Lash loses fair 1v1s against Wraith, Drifter or Lady Geist; he can win by slamming every wave with Majestic Leap into Ground Strike and backing up, but must avoid extended trades. [17:22]
- In a losing side-lane matchup, ask to swap lanes with a teammate who has a better matchup or who wants to farm while you want to be active. [17:52]
- If you are winning the matchup, put pressure on: Yamato against Wraith at even or higher net worth should go aggressive. [18:23]
- Yamato against Wraith wants Counterspell, because Wraith's ultimate silences you and stops Yamato's ultimate; with Counterspell you can block it and have the edge. [18:23]
- Against Mirage, Slowing Hex (3.5 second slow) stops him using Tornado to dodge your abilities. [18:55]
- To ultimate a Mirage as Lash you need Silence Wave or Slowing Hex, otherwise he dodges with Tornado; go on him right after buying the counter item when he will not expect it. [18:55]
- Rotate to match the heroes you can pressure, and the ones you are far richer than. [19:25]
- If you are the richest in the lobby and an enemy like Wraith is getting rich, match her to shut her down. [19:25]
- Stealing an enemy Sinner's Sacrifice worth 350 is a 700 soul swing (plus 350 for you, minus 350 for them); taking your own is only 350. Do what gives you the most souls and them the least. [19:55]
- With an advantage play on their side of the map as much as you can so they feel suffocated, cannot get position or information, and get punished for leaving their Walkers. [20:55]
- Predict the enemy: ask what they will do or farm next, go there, and contest or steal it. [21:26]
- Replay: after clearing the left lane, rotate mid to take a numbers advantage fight (Grey Talon kill), using the 45 seconds the clear bought. [21:26]
- Enemies chasing your ally on a lane whose wave they have not cleared create little real pressure. [21:57]
- Take fights when you have wave advantage and numbers advantage; a roaming hero like Lash should move between lanes while a partner holds the side lane 1v1. [22:27]
- Only go back to your own side when ahead if something needs doing there, such as when all their objectives on their side are taken. [22:58]
- Replay: ask teammates to take your lanes (Pocket left, Ivy mid), then teleport across the map to hit the right Walker with a fresh cooldown spike while richest in the lobby (16k), aiming for an extra flex slot. [22:58]
- If a play fails, use Cultist Sacrifice right away and get back to mid; always stay active. [24:29]
- Objectives are arguably the most important pillar; the key misunderstood ones are Walkers, Mid Boss and the Urn and buffs. [25:01]
- Walkers are worth dying for before 20 minutes, as long as you are not feeding repeated free kills; do not run away from a nearly dead Walker. [25:01]
- Before 15 minutes Walkers are hard to take; in lower ranks do not go for them too much before 15 minutes and steal jungle instead, unless you have a big lead. EU and higher ranks take them earlier. [25:32]
- Walker resistances decrease over the game. [26:02]
- Being near your own Walker gives an aura of 15% bullet and 15% spirit resistance, worth roughly 1,600 souls each (like Battle Vest plus Enchanter's Emblem), so defenders have about 2,000 to 3,200 bonus souls of value; an even fight at their Walker favors them. [26:33]
- Mid Boss is the win condition and the best way to finish games. [27:03]
- If your lead is over 10,000 souls, go Mid Boss after the next fight you win. [27:03]
- Mid Boss before 15 minutes needs a lot of coordination and damage and is unrealistic in matchmaking; pros sometimes take it at 12 or 13 minutes. [27:33]
- Tell your team before the fight: "next time we win a fight, let's go Mid Boss", so everyone goes the moment the fight is won or you get one or two kills. [27:33]
- Early on it is worth contesting Mid Boss even to steal one Rejuvenator if you have a decent chance to win the fight; trading three kills for four or five deaths at 15 to 20 minutes is fine because respawns are short and it removes their Rejuvenator buffs. [28:03]
- Mid Boss is still worth contesting after its changes; do not force the first one, but the second or third usually must be contested because losing it is likely game over. [28:33]
- When going Mid Boss, check which enemy ultimates can steal it and which of yours can secure it: Dynamo's Singularity on the pit can secure; Lash can pull Dynamo away and cancel it; Yamato's ultimate is Unstoppable against Lash's ultimate; Haze's Sleep Dagger can stop a Lash. [29:03]
- Pay attention to the Urn and buff timers; at lower ranks being on time makes them a free objective. [29:34]
- At 14:30, with buffs and Urn spawning in 30 seconds, push every wave aggressively to pressure the whole map, then go to the buffs the moment they spawn. Farming your own jungle then gives them up. [29:34]
- Even high-level players are often late to these objectives; Eternus 6 lobbies are usually on time, below that often not. [30:36]
- The Urn and buffs are forced-fight opportunities: if ahead, either you get them free or the enemy must fight you for them. [30:36]
- If behind, predict which side the enemy stacks for the Urn and play the other side of the map looking for a pick. [31:06]
- Urn souls can be denied for up to 40% of the Urn's value; shoot the souls from a nearby rooftop if you cannot contest it. [31:06]
- If you cannot contest the Urn, do not farm your own jungle when they pick it up; push a Walker on the other side, pressure, or steal their jungle in exchange. Teams lose by going AFK while the enemy delivers the Urn. [32:08]
- Cutting the wave: kill the wave as it arrives in a lane to push that lane; with mid and left pushed, the team took the left buff for free. [32:38]
- Replay: with a 3v2 and the wave pushing, push the Walker and be willing to die for it rather than leave when it is at 1 HP. [33:08]
- After taking an objective, do not overstay: back up and reset. [33:38]
- Up 14k, keep pressure up by going for a second Walker; you do not get anything without risking something. [33:38]
- Good counterplay seen from the enemy: instead of defending their Walker at all costs, they took the mid Walker and used the comeback Urn in exchange. [34:08]
- At 17 minutes after three kills, call Mid Boss after checking enemy steal ultimates (their Grey Talon dead, Viscous without ultimate) and your own; with a numbers advantage they gave it up free. [34:38]
- After getting Mid Boss you do not need to force the issue right away. [35:08]
- Exception to playing on their side: if their side has no farm left and yours has plenty, the whole team clears your own jungle quickly while the Rejuvenators last. [35:39]
- Punish an enemy out of position in his jungle (a Billy) by calling the team over without overcommitting. [36:10]
- If the enemy cannot contest the buff, go one more wave rather than standing on the buff the minute it spawns. [36:10]
- Bait a fight you cannot 1v1 (Billy) so a teammate (Paige) can collapse; but check spirit resistance, the Billy had too much to kill quickly. [36:41]
- Up 20k with Urn control, keep going for the Urn even without Rejuvenators; when close to a spike (Spirit Burn) you can take a fight but do not overcommit. [37:12]
- As Lash you often need just one good kill with the ultimate, not a huge multi-hero Lash ultimate. [38:14]
- After winning a fight near Mid Boss, ignore chasing a low-value Haze and take Mid Boss; the second Mid Boss is almost always GG. [38:14]
- During Mid Boss, sometimes fight the enemies outside the pit (Grey Talon arrows, Viscous) and delay your parry so they cannot steal a Rejuvenator. [38:44]
- After Mid Boss you do not need to walk into their base right away; take the Urn and Walker and keep pushing side waves (one hero left, one right) to keep pressure on the whole map, then end. [39:14]
