## Covers
Viscous coaching review by Midknight of an Oracle Viscous laning with a Yamato into Infernus and Paige, video dated 2025-11-16 (mid November 2025 patch).
Themes: playing around Paige's barrier and bridge control, right click usage and the punch, right click, Splatter combo, going in on low HP and resetting, stealing Sinner's Sacrifice after an early guardian, Reactive Barrier and counter items, Trophy Collector, running down mid, Urn and mid boss rules.

## Claims
Viscous (Oracle):
- Lanes against Paige revolve around her barrier. [0:00]
- With Yamato and Viscous, hold bridge control and poke the Paige the whole lane; ignore the Infernus. When her barrier is on cooldown, your Yamato should commit to a fight and you follow up. [0:31]
- Cube a teammate when things get rough, and whenever Infernus burns someone, cube them. [1:01]
- The Viscous build is pretty solved: the Goober Leap Viscous build is the best one right now; L main's Viscous build is the OG one and not bad. [1:01]
- He names Goober Leap build ID 333302 as the best Viscous build in the game. [32:18]
- You could have right clicked with that Goo Ball; your muscle memory should always be to right click with it. [1:31]
- To keep bridge control, get wave prio: hit every new wave with Splatter and a right click to chunk the minions, which keeps aggressive prio and makes it hard for the enemy to take the bridge. [1:31]
- Train yourself to right click with your last shot before the enemy or you get behind cover; the AOE hits people behind cover, and it does more damage per bullet than your gun, so trading one bullet for one right click is always worth it. [2:33]
- You don't need healing in this lane; if you want a defensive item, buy Restorative Shot or an extra regen, since Enchanter's Emblem comes later if you go Express Shot. The green investment's 8% max HP is nice. [3:33]
- You can tell an Infernus player's skill level by whether he maxes dash or maxes gun. [4:36]
- Paige's barrier animation is a little hand flick; once she uses barrier you have 20 seconds of free rein, so dive. [4:36]
- Whenever you dive, parry the tower every single time; it would have saved about 200 damage. [5:06]
- If Cube is not online, counter ulting Infernus for the resistances is great, but when Cube is online, cube instead. [5:37]
- A second heavy melee is greedy and can get parried; one melee is fine, but don't be greedier than that. [5:37]
- Once you have Express Shot, just spam right clicks; this power spike is what dictates how you play Viscous. [6:10]
- If you take the guardian before 8 minutes, use the pressure to steal the enemy's sentries and Sinner's Sacrifice; sinners are the highest priority. [6:10]
- Reset when you have boost online, so you are back for prio on the sinners spawn wave and can eat theirs. [7:12]
- Sinners spawn at 8 minutes, the most important timing, and respawn around 13 to 14 minutes. [9:42]
- Reactive Barrier is very worth buying when the enemy has three or more basic abilities that can proc it; he buys it in lane into Paige, Abrams, Bebop or Haze, or anyone with basic-ability CC, and late game the whole enemy team decides it. [8:12]
- Staging on low HP (below 400 for three minutes) cost you pressure, stolen sinners and your own sinners. [9:12]
- If you won't reset, pop Guardian Ward on yourself while farming the sinners; it eats 200 of the 240 damage. [10:13]
- When the Infernus stuns your Mo & Krill and you cannot save him, take the rope out instead of dying; the fight is only okay if your Grey Talon mirrors for a 3v3. [11:44]
- Stop dying for your teammates. To train it, he used to say "let the idiot die" out loud when teammates ran in. [13:20]
- Chasing a caught-out Paige is fine, but the second she gets under the walker you have no kill potential and must stop. [13:20]
- Instead of chasing Paige, hit her and then go to the Urn fight; if you trade mid walker for the Urn, tell your teammates. [13:50]
- Rushing Trophy Collector is not meta on anyone; it teaches you to overfight and take bad fights and deaths, which stunts your growth. [14:22]
- For your first 10 games on a hero, overfight to improve micro; after that be hyper aggressive in the laning stage. [16:24]
- Use your abilities on the wave, not the players, when they are sieging; clearing it removes their siege. [16:54]
- Going in on 900 HP and then 600 HP with no cube is not respecting your own HP; don't be afraid to reset. [17:25]
- If you see Escalating Resilience in the core of a build, don't use that build; the item is situational, like Haze buying it into a hard spirit comp. [17:56]
- Mantle slide there to make life easier. [18:59]
- Your fundamentals are good but your map awareness is lacking. [19:30]
- Cooldown items early are not the solved build; you need Enchanter's Emblem, which is very strong. [20:04]
- Winning by 10k (about 8%), run the Urn. [20:40]
- Off spawn you are running down mid looking for a fight with no thought; every wave you spend in mid with two teammates loses about 30% souls net, and with four about 20%. [21:10]
- Fights should happen over an Urn run: the team that is ahead grabs the Urn and fights around whoever runs it. [21:40]
- Whenever you see Paige in a fight, turn on her and one-shot her with your full combo instead of chasing a Holliday far out of your backline. [21:40]
- He prefers the solved Goober Leap build over a supportive Healing Nova Viscous build. [23:12]
- Don't run at the enemies to stop one guy with no teammates around, especially once a second and third show up. [23:12]
- Canceling your ult into Cube is a good idea; you died with Nova up, so pop it. [23:42]
- Divine Barrier is overpowered on support Viscous, and late Tankbuster is good into a tanky comp with Billy and Abrams. [28:47]
- The punch, right click, Splatter combo (he calls it the Skinner combo) is Viscous' most efficient damage. Splatter eats into your right click cooldown, and a bullet resist shredder applies from the right click before the punch hits. [26:17]
- He corrects the order in the end notes: punch, then right click, then Splatter, all at once, and Express Shot adds more damage. [33:18]
- Sweatier: do the combo on top of the enemies and add a punch midair; enemies are less likely to parry in the air. Sweatiest: finish with the ult melee cancel in the air for a chain stun. [33:48]
- Look up Viscous dribbling tech. [32:48]
- Don't go push their walker alone at 800 HP sitting on a full tier four item. [26:47]
- With an enemy Dynamo ult up, Grey Talon can play out of the pit with a trap and cancel the ult if Dynamo jumps in, so mid boss is still the angle. [27:47]
- Don't take a 1v1 on their walker when their Paige duo could stand on top of him and kill you. [30:17]
- Against a hard spirit, heavy CC team, buy Spirit Resilience (best option), Spellbreaker, Counterspell, Debuff Remover or Reactive Barrier; Enchanter's Emblem also gives spirit resist. [30:48]
- Your biggest mistake was going in below 25% HP and not resetting, especially with boost. [31:48]
- You gave a lot of denies when you had no ammo in the mag; watch your ammo count. [34:48]
- As Viscous you want to hover mid and play around mid, but never just run down mid looking for fights. [35:18]
- Tapping the Urn: pick it up and drop it when next to it to cement it as a neutral Urn. [35:49]
- Avoid at-best side lane fights like 1v1s or diving into Mo & Krill. [35:49]

Solo queue flowchart:
- Don't buy Tankbuster before 22 minutes. [28:47]
- Post 22 minutes with two or more picks, go mid boss. Post 18 minutes, if they are on top of mid, go mid. Post 28 minutes with one pick, go mid. An enemy Dynamo ult is the exception that complicates this. [28:47]
- Always know your next buy before you shop, so after mid boss you can check your souls and know whether to go to shop. [29:17]
- After mid boss, whoever doesn't need to buy grabs and runs the Urn while the team eats the aggressive farm. [29:47]
- If you had called mid boss, the game would have ended about five minutes sooner. [34:18]
