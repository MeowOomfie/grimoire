## Covers
Wraith, aquatiq: a Golden Goose Egg rush into Ricochet build, with notes on when to sell the egg and early ability priority.
Commentary over one full game, not a structured guide; July 2026 patch.

## Claims
- The build rushes Golden Goose Egg early and sells it at roughly 12 minutes. [0:00]
- Buying the egg at about 4 minutes meant waiting until about 16 minutes to sell it, which he says is not a good idea; if you go egg, get it early. [0:31]
- Hitting two enemies with one card is very strong. [2:34]
- There are two routes for this build, with and without the egg. [3:06]
- On the egg route you skip Tesla Bullets because you are rushing Ricochet; Tesla Bullets does a smaller version of what Ricochet does, bouncing bullets off other targets. [3:06]
- Do not stay in a lane where teammates are farming and take their souls; leave to farm elsewhere. [6:09]
- With a teammate who heals in lane, you may not need to buy healing early. [5:09]
- Plan: pop the egg, then buy Ricochet a few minutes later. [7:11]
- Max cards (Card Toss) first. [7:11]
- He wanted Rapid Recharge. [8:18]
- He sold the egg at about 3k because his damage was tanking while holding it, then sat on a wave until he had 6k for Ricochet. [10:54]
- After Ricochet, buy vitality items: Spirit Resilience or Fortitude next, and he chose Fortitude. [11:54]
- If you die and get something from it, it is fine; dying for nothing is bad, so back off when a fight is not worth it. [12:24]
- With max cards, a stacked Card Toss hand makes him strong in fights. [13:27]
- Rushing Ricochet worked: the enemy team died fast in fights. [13:58]
- Against Infernus he wanted a dispel item to avoid being burned. [15:35]
- He prioritized clearing lanes over joining every team fight. [18:43]
- He also bought Burst Fire. [21:46]
- He needed Fortitude to stop taking damage and called his next big item a giant power spike. [22:47]
