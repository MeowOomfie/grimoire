# A Pretty Good Patch | 9/16/26
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-09-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tStHVQcEVeE

## Covers
Midknight reads and reacts to the 9/16/26 Deadlock patch: comeback resist and soul changes, parry and reload changes, tunnel box timers, global slow nerfs, item changes (Veil Walker, Shadow Weave, Spiritual Overflow, Tank Buster, Golden Goose Egg) and hero changes (Celeste, Lash, Paige, Paradox, Silver, Viscous, Vyper, Warden, Wraith).
Verdict: a good patch, still missing a Pocket and a Doorman nerf. For players who want a quick opinionated read of the patch.

## Claims
- Comeback resist now scales over the game: previously max 35, now 10 plus 1 per minute; good, it makes comeback resist far less impactful at the first Rift. [0:00]
- Removed a fixed amount of extra boon souls you got for being behind by even a slight net worth amount; comeback bounties reduced (he calls it a fix). [0:00]
- A portion of comeback souls earned by players above the enemy team's average net worth is instead distributed via the gold tick to the lowest two players; more souls go to the lowest net worth players, which he says makes a lot of sense. [0:30]
- Guardian and walker bounty increased; he questions the walker bounty increase. [0:30]
- Objective bounty share to nearby heroes reduced from 30% to 25%, so less reward for splitting ("thank god"). [1:00]
- A timer at 20 minutes was increased, aimed at making games a little shorter. [1:00]
- Parrying while dodging: doing a regular dash now cancels the parry, so go back to air dashing for dash parries. [1:31]
- Getting stunned now pauses your reload rather than restarting it; he calls it amazing and something he has asked for a long time. [1:31]
- You will now actually get punished for parrying; wall jump parries are the meta now. [2:02]
- Breakable underground tunnel boxes: initial spawn moved from 3 to 5 minutes and respawn increased to 5 minutes; feels bad, and it will hurt tunnel heroes like Saku a lot right before more tunnel heroes come in the next major patch. [2:02]
- All movement slow values decreased by 20% globally and dash slow reduced by 10; he is happy with this. [2:33]
- Slows now also affect air drag. [2:33]
- Dash and light melee no longer pause your gun cycle time. [2:33]
- The bleeding headshot effect was nerfed; he thinks it needs to drop to about 10%, or at least 11. [2:33]
- Hollow Point and Toxic Bullets buffed. [2:33]
- Shadow Weave now builds from Sprint Boots with a sprint speed increase; he warns not to repeat the Veil Walker mistake. [2:33]
- Mystic enchantment duration change: no one buys this item. [3:04]
- Spiritual Overflow: spirit power build up on proc reduced from 40 to 30 and fire rate to 25; a decent nerf, but it is still one of the few items giving four flat stats, and he thinks the Warden build stays viable. [3:04]
- A small stack range nerf to Restorative Locket (he hates the item and wanted it gutted); probably a luxury nerf. [3:34]
- Veil Walker no longer builds from Sprint Boots and no longer grants +2 sprint and +2 out-of-combat regen; its movement speed bonus is now removed when the invis is removed; spirit mod decreased. He calls these really good changes, since it gave 5.5 movement speed. [4:04]
- Veil Walker should still feel good on Holliday. [4:34]
- Fortitude max health change: no one buys this item. [4:34]
- Lifestrike heal on hit increased (20 extra healing with 0.25 scaling); not a big deal, though Lifestrike is a dangerous item. [4:34]
- Leech buffed; it is not a bad item, but in a burst meta you need to nerf burst heroes and items rather than buff lifesteal. [5:06]
- On-hit bug fix: some on-hit damage was not blocked properly (he had been buying the item as a counter to Vindicta); fixes also hit Mercurial Magnum and Tesla Bullets. [5:06]
- Diviner's Kevlar buffed again; he disagrees, it is already a very good item. [5:38]
- Golden Goose Egg nerfed (souls per minute reduced); it will stay a troll solo queue item that teammates int-buy. [5:38]
- Phoenix cooldown increased and Radiant Orb reduced a bit; fine. [6:09]
- Tank Buster: current health bonus reduced (good) and DPS reduced 25%, but duration increased 20%; the duration buff was unnecessary since it already gives a lot of anti-heal for a long time, but overall still a significant nerf. [6:09]
- Ogre's Lens damage buffed; the item is slept on and buffs to it need care. [7:12]
- Abrams is insane and still didn't get a nerf. [7:12]
- Celeste: stamina cooldown increased from 5 to 5.3 (he thinks she shouldn't have four stamina), spirit scaling at levels two and three changed, Dazzling Trick cooldown increased, dagger damage decreased, Shining Wonder changed; not a big deal, Celeste will still be Celeste, though her Restorative Locket and Radiant Orb were also nerfed. [7:12]
- The air drag slow change will hurt Celeste a lot (a chat point he agrees with). [7:43]
- A hero he plays all day got an alpha boon increase and Crack Shot tier two changes; he calls it a terrible change that makes him even more of an aim hero who one-shots you unless you have Metal Skin. [8:13]
- An ability got its radius decreased; it needs a real cooldown instead. [9:14]
- Kelvin's shelter is not that good right now, per players. [9:14]
- Lady Geist Essence Bomb tier two and three change: no one maxes bomb. [9:14]
- Lash: Ground Strike spirit buffed while his gun is nerfed. [9:44]
- Lash's real issue is that one wrong Ground Strike gets him instantly killed; he feels like a terrorist in low elo because people don't punish. A better change would give a less punishing slam, like a 0.25 second minor silence on max Ground Strike. [9:44]
- QSR and on-hit should not give additional healing; that should be nerfed. [10:45]
- Paige: an angle decrease and a tier two and three reduction; it will bait Paiges into heavy meleeing for free kills. [10:45]
- Paradox Carbine minimum damage reduced from 25 to 10; fine if they fix the Carbine glitches. [11:16]
- Paradox pierces Spellbreaker too much because of how she builds (possibly via Express Shot). [11:46]
- Pocket untouched: they never nerf Pocket when he is broken and always nerf him when he is weak. [12:16]
- Shiv: base gun damage increased (unneeded); knives at full rage now deal 3.5 crit on impact instead of ricocheting; run speed changed. [12:16]
- Silver: Weighted Bola now increases gravity and interrupts flying abilities, so Silver may have a role as an anti-flier hero. [12:47]
- Ricochet is good on Silver as a late game item; Split Shot won't be bought on her; it is a buff but more for a meme build than a broken one. [13:19]
- Opportunity cost: no broken gun tier fours were nerfed except Spiritual Overflow; Inhibitor and Silencer were left alone. [13:49]
- Opal heart, splatter and detonator changes he likes. [14:19]
- Viscous: Cube cast range changed and Puddle Punch cooldown increased; good. [14:19]
- Minor Vyper nerfs (bullet damage reduced, fire rate scaling); he wanted a little more. [14:49]
- Warden: Willpower tier three spirit scaling reduced and tier two and three debuff resist reduced, but the real issue (Alchemical Flask's 40 to 50% weapon damage reduction) was not touched. [14:49]
- Wraith: a big heal scaling decrease, a big Wraith nerf. [15:20]
- Crimson Slash now also increases the heal spirit scaling by 0.4; he doesn't know why it was buffed but it seems fine. [15:54]
- Overall he likes the patch and wanted it more heavy-handed; the only things missing are a Pocket nerf and a Doorman nerf (Bell does too much damage). [15:54]
