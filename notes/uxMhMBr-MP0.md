# You Are Playing Your Character Wrong - The Ultimate Guide To Roles In Deadlock
channel: Metro Deadlock

## Covers
What carry and support mean in Deadlock, then every hero placed on a support-to-carry scale with why: which abilities work without souls, gun and spirit scaling, jungle farming speed, and how each wants to spend the game (fight, farm, side lane, split push).
For players unsure whether their hero should farm or fight, and for teams where everyone thinks they are the carry.

## Claims
- A carry needs a lot of farm to get online and is weak without souls; a support gets most of its value from abilities even with few souls. [0:31]
- A carry usually plays the early game passively and skips many team fights; a support should be active early and fight the entire game. [0:31]
- Approaching your hero's role the wrong way hinders your team. [1:01]
- Every hero gets stronger with souls, but some benefit far more from a big soul amount; supports still need some money and carries do not need all of it. [1:01]
- Treat the scale as flexible: a middle hero can farm more when the team lacks carries, or less when it has them. [1:31]
- If several teammates want farm and you only somewhat do, step back and let hard carries like Seven and Wraith farm. [2:03]
