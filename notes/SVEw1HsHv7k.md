# How To Change Your Cursor In Deadlock
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-03-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVEw1HsHv7k

## Covers
A short how-to for replacing the in-game Deadlock cursor with any custom image, using a free pixel art site and a PNG-to-BMP converter. For anyone wanting a custom cursor; no gameplay advice.

## Claims
- A PNG with its background already cut out makes the job easier, but the background can be removed in the editor. [0:00]
- Use the free pixel art site from the description (Photoshop works too); create a new 40 x 40 pixel image with background turned off and title it "cursor". [0:31]
- Drag your image in with "add to current", not "create new", resize it to fit, then click off it so it pixelates. [0:31]
- To remove a background, use the wand select tool with tolerance raised from about 30 to about 70, click the white areas and press backspace; use the lasso tool for pixels the wand misses. [1:01]
- Save as PNG with transparency toggled on, named cursor.png. [2:02]
- Convert the PNG to BMP with the converter from the second description link and download it. [2:02]
- In Steam, right click Deadlock, Properties, Installed Files, Browse, then open game, citadel, resource, cursors to find cursor.bmp. [2:33]
- Back up the original cursor.bmp somewhere you know; you can also restore it with Verify integrity of game files. [2:33]
- Drop the new file in and make sure it is named exactly cursor.bmp (lowercase to be safe) or it will not work, then launch Deadlock. [3:05]
- 40 x 40 is the normal size; a bigger square such as 100 x 100 gives a bigger cursor in game. [3:36]
