## Covers
Yamato, aquatiq, commentary over one ranked game on a hero he rarely plays.

## Claims
- Yamato's right click (alt fire) does much more damage and is her long range tool, since her left click does not have much range. [1:02]
- Yamato needs a lot of survivability; most builds he looked at are a bunch of spirit plus survivability items. [1:02]
- The enemy lane had a lot of poke priority on them in this matchup. [1:33]
- Graves only has two stamina, so a stamina buff is valuable to her. [5:20]
- Her gun has a big spread like Drifter's claws; use the alt fire for range. [5:52]
- He should have hit Power Slash (ability 1) earlier; one hit would have killed the target. [6:33]
- When the enemy has only one healthy hero at a tower, run at the tower and do damage. [6:33]
- Grit (as captioned) means he takes less damage from minions while clearing the wave and fighting players. [7:04]
- He activates Grit (as captioned) so he does not get one shot by Paradox. [8:07]
- He used his ult early just in case Paradox jumped on him. [9:09]
- Item plan: Healbane, then Weapon Shielding and straight into Spirit Snatch; if he takes too much spirit damage he gets both shields. [11:11]
- Tip for new players: do not play overly aggressive, especially after losing lane; Shiv can get away with it, Seven cannot. [14:52]
- He stacks as much HP as possible. [14:52]
- Veil Walker is worth trying on Yamato. [15:22]
- Fully charge Power Slash: fully charged it does about 500 damage, so it is very worth charging. [15:52]
- A free Rift is one of the best things you can get right now: it gives money and minions, annoys the enemy and pushes the wave for you. [17:54]
- Veil Walker saved him from an Apollo. [20:36]
- Late items: Hunter's Aura, a resilience item and Extra Health, taking him to about 3,300 HP. [21:06]
- Veil Walker is very useful on her. [22:08]
- As Infernus, time your ult: do not go in too early, and do not die. [23:09]
- He buys Debuff Remover when the enemy keeps locking him down. [25:15]
- After winning a fight, drop their shrines and then take their farm. [26:15]
- When the enemy all have ults back up, wait for a pick before going in. [26:45]
- When your whole team has ults up there is no reason to be scared to go in. [27:16]
- He adds Torment Pulse late. [27:16]
- Use your team's sleep ultimate to set up the fight. [27:16]
- Clear the minions first before taking the next mid boss. [29:21]
- Against Paradox's Kinetic Carbine cannon build, which can hit for about 500, buy Spellbreaker. [30:23]
- As Graves, place your ult where the enemy cannot punch it out quickly. [30:23]
