## Covers
Edited VOD review of a Billy player (a low-farm support Billy laning with The Doorman vs Seven and Lash) who plays too passively with Blasted and struggles with Chain Gang. Lane trades and the Blasted, Bashdown, Rising Ram combo, wave clear for box time, the 4:30 buff wave, vaults, Counterspell timing, 4.8k investment spikes, urn play (comeback urn, spending while holding it), where a team-fight hero should be in the mid game, split push value and how to land Chain Gang.
For Billy players and any team-fight hero unsure where to be after lane.

## Claims
- Loading into lane, make a battle plan: know what your character is good and bad at and what you plan to do in the lane phase. [0:00]
- In matchmaking, err on the side of aggression; Deathy advocates very aggressive play to learn faster, and playing like you have the advantage puts people on the back foot. [0:30]
- Even if the enemies have the advantage, aggression forces them to assert it, and very often they won't. [1:01]
- If you are not using Blasted, you are playing down an ability. [1:01]
- When you play one side of the lane, your Doorman should gravitate to the other side; taking angles your teammate is not means one of you can always shoot an enemy behind cover. [1:01]
- Circling and flanking during the lane phase automatically puts pressure on the enemies. [1:33]
- Once you accept a melee range fight with Seven, you have to pop Blasted. [2:05]
- Walking down a Lash with Seven nearby while your Doorman clears the wave is a fight that is about 30% to work out, a 1v2 taken just to make something happen. [3:06]
- Your Doorman shoving the wave instead of joining is what he should be doing. [3:06]
- Crashing four minions into the enemy tower gives you complete freedom; unless you are a very strong poke character, use it to secure a camp and even steal their boxes. [3:36]
- Pop Blasted before Bashdown so the Bashdown procs Blasted: about 7 seconds of 10% more bullet damage, a reload of your gun and a bit of HP. [4:06]
- Once you have used Bashdown and procced your melee lifesteal you have won the trade; back up, because a second fight right after is suicide. [4:06]
- You and your lane partner never took a fight together; you should be fighting as a pair, not tagging in and out. [4:37]
- Secure soul orbs on the ground quickly because they disappear. [5:08]
- With all three stamina, Rising Ram up and a team health advantage, move to the cover closer to the minions to do more damage, grab the orb, and punch the minions to proc melee lifesteal and shove faster. [5:08]
- Clearing waves faster gives you the timer to leave lane and hit boxes; players lack box souls in lane not because they forget boxes but because they never clear waves fast enough to have the opportunity. [5:38]
- It is always wrong to fight the opponents when the wave is neutral. [6:08]
- Secure the 5-minute buff by winning the 4:30 buff wave fight: clear the camp as the wave drops, walk with the wave, shove it instantly, then go straight to the buff. [6:39]
- When alone and about to be stunned, dash away, wall bounce and Rising Ram in the air to put distance; if they chase they overextend, and you can Chain Gang them on your side of the lane. [6:39]
- Standing in Seven's Lightning Ball cost over 100 unnecessary damage. [7:10]
- A competent Mirage will try to kill you under your tower; when he appears there, your only option to survive is to run forward and kill the healing minion. [8:11]
- When the vault spawns, the whole jungle is online and the lane phase is ending; lane interactions are no longer just about attacking the tower. [8:42]
- Chain Gang in a 1v1 slows the target so you hit more shots, pulls him to you so you can walk him down, and while he is pulled you hit Bashdown, Blasted, Bashdown to reload, deal damage and proc your passive to amp your gun. [9:15]
- With your gun items, that Chain Gang combo puts the target at critical HP, so your Doorman can finish him or you can tower dive him. [9:46]
- Reducing an enemy to almost no HP gives you pressure on that side of the map to take his guardian or the vaults. [10:16]
- Vaults are worth so many souls that they are the most important jungle camp until the urn spawns; people habitually leave them up for minutes. [10:16]
- Until the urn spawns, contesting the center sacrifices is the most valuable thing on the map. [10:47]
- Invading the jungle means picking a fight and assuming someone responds; do it when you think you can win: "fight me on your camp and I'll beat you up, or let me have it". [10:47]
- Counterspell is good as you enter the stage where team fights and deaths matter more; bought early it blocks maybe a 200 damage ability and you stay alive without the damage to be useful. [11:18]
- Instead of an early Counterspell, a red spike would give you about 60 to 70% more damage (the item plus the 4.8k spike), or greens like Battle Vest plus Enduring Speed give more map presence and effective survivability. [11:49]
- Counterspell shines in four and five person fights where you can counter a Paradox swap, an Infernus nuke or a Lash ult; in 1v1s and 2v2s the most you block is something like Paradox's Carbine or Seven's stun. [12:19]
- Most of the lobby had not reached a 4.8k spike; a Mirage with 4k spirit invested loses something like 30% of his Djinn's Mark and Fire Scarabs damage for want of an 800 soul item. [13:23]
- Each 4.8k investment spike gives over 1,000 souls of value on its own, so thousands of souls of combat power sit unused across a lobby. [13:23]
- The Doorman at 4k in spirit would make his Call Bell hit much harder by spending 800 more on another spirit item. [13:54]
- Track your own investment spikes to make calculated decisions about when to apply pressure, win fights, then bully people off towers and vaults to snowball the mid game. [13:54]
- At 13 minutes you should probably be playing for the urn rather than mashing your lane. [14:24]
- Some characters want to farm and side lane alone; Billy, with inherent tankiness and Chain Gang CC, specializes in early and mid game team fighting and wants to be where the fights happen. [14:54]
- Fights should happen around the most important objectives at that time; at the 13-minute mark that is always the urns, any guardians left, then vaults and the center sacrifice. [15:25]
- Decide where to go by asking where the value on the map is and what is most valuable to control right now; go there on a character like Billy. [15:25]
- Your team should not random team fight; it should control certain parts of the map on purpose for particular reasons, and that is where you use your ult. [16:27]
- No one in that game picked up the urn; Billy could have left lane sooner for an almost guaranteed urn pickup, chain ganging anyone who contested. [16:57]
- Everyone coached thinks they are the one who plays the urn and calls mid boss more than everyone else. [17:59]
- The comeback urn shows a green arrow; when your team is about 10% up on souls you want to control the comeback urn and make it really hard for the enemies to get it, zoning them off. [18:59]
- In pro play the best thing is to pick up the urn and hide it in your base, but that is usually not practical in matchmaking. [18:59]
- With all walkers alive the urn bonus is only 10%, and your team will not beat them in a 50/50 fight on their base, so if they pick up the comeback urn they will get it. [19:29]
- When an enemy (the Kelvin) goes for the comeback urn, kill your wave like a robot and go straight to help your Venator fight him off; if he gets out, their team gets about 5k free souls and you are barely winning. [19:59]
- Hitting boxes while the enemy was on the urn shows you were not thinking about the most important objective on the map. [19:59]
- Proper side laner play: fall back, let the enemy shove, take a camp while the wave crashes, leave the camp alive to catch the wave before it hits the walker, then go back to the camp. [21:30]
- When the urn is hurting you, run it back to your base and drop it, and spend your souls; carrying 5.5k unspent into a fight leaves you severely underpowered. [22:33]
- 5.5k unspent is an entire 4.8k spirit spike. [23:34]
- You can spend your money while holding the urn by running through a secret shop. [23:34]
- Urn fight decisions depend on who is ahead in souls, power spikes and important ultimates; your team should talk about it and you should facilitate that. [24:04]
- Do not walk into an urn fight in silence; ask simple questions like "can we play for the depot here, can we win the fight?" so you know whether you have time for the secret shop. [24:36]
- You are habitually fighting with most of your souls sitting in your pocket. [25:06]
- Standard Billy mechanics: after Spirit Snatch procs, Bashdown the chaser in a doorway to hit him with Spirit Snatch again. [25:36]
- Bashdown immediately after Rising Ram is guaranteed to hit; a light melee can be parried, so use it only when you need to proc Blasted or your passive, or reload. [26:06]
- With two Bashdowns available, Bashdown instantly, pop Blasted, then Bashdown again for maximum damage plus a little healing; anything else is very risky. [26:36]
- Invading the enemy center sacrifices is great on Billy when you can, but not with unspent souls and low health. [27:37]
- When up 20k and favored, play with your team to take resources; the worst thing is farming your safest camps, which puts you far from your team, does not inflict your lead and wastes your ult charging. [28:11]
- When you are in the lead, take enemy stuff before your own safe stuff. [28:42]
- Play near the urn pickup area (more jungle camps too) so the enemies do not get it; the urn switches sides about 45 seconds later. [29:13]
- Either intentionally reinforce your team to pick up or deny the urn, or confidently walk to the next buff to fight for the next urn; always move toward the action and support your team. [29:43]
- A split push on base guardians does negligible damage, because it is easy for enemies to respond to threats in their own base with little travel time. [30:14]
- Split pushes work while walkers are up because teams control more of the map; on blue lane base guardians there is about a 2% chance you get more than 5 seconds before someone shows up, maybe several to kill you, then they run comeback urn or mid boss. [30:45]
- That push had little to gain and everything to lose. [31:16]
- The time to fight aggressively over camps was roughly the 8 to 20 minute mark; after that, winning team fights turns into urns and mid boss. [32:18]
- Farming a 700 soul camp as the least farmed support Billy while your team marches on a walker invites a collapse that gives the enemies their comeback. [32:50]
- There was a real way to partly carry the game by being your Venator's bodyguard. [33:21]
- If you do not have your ult and you are lowest souls, farm the camp; you are not very useful in fights without your CC. [33:52]
- Chain Gang is a CC ult whose value does not depend on souls; when far behind, your number one way to help is big Chain Gangs defending your team. [34:23]
- If you have your ult and your team is posturing for a team fight, that is all you need to know: go join it. [34:53]
- If you lack your ult or have all your souls unspent, that can be a reason to farm and not take the fight. [35:23]
- Low kill participation came from never landing an effective Chain Gang all game. [35:54]
- Late game Chain Gang is like Dynamo's ult: holding people in place and pulling them into your team's AoE is a death sentence. [36:24]
- Early game, pulling one enemy a few feet will not get him killed; Chain Gang is a 1v1 or 2v2 tool to force a fight or stop someone escaping after you popped Blasted and hit him once. [36:54]
- With high team DPS Chain Gang becomes a pick tool like Paradox's swap, which pulls someone out of place for your team to burst. [37:24]
- The major Chain Gang mistake is popping it before you are on top of the enemies. [37:54]
- Chain Gang needs forward momentum: from a Warp Stone, a dash off the zipline, a dash jump they did not see coming, or Rising Ram; pop out of the corner, Rising Ram instantly, and pop Chain Gang while still charging. [38:24]
- The chains hold as long as targets do not break line of sight, so what matters is connecting the chains at the start, not being on top of them at the end. [38:54]
- Connected chains slow targets and stop movement skills; popping Chain Gang and then running at them lets them Warp Stone, bat away or tornado away (Mirage) for no value. [39:24]
- Fixing Chain Gang is decision making, not harder mechanics. [39:24]
- After dropping the enemy base, taking the timing to go for mid boss is the proper call; many games are lost by not making it. [40:56]
- Catching many waves gives your team tempo, but you had basically no influence on any team fight. [41:26]
