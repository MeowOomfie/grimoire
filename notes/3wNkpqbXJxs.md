## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream the day after a minor patch (May 2026), reviewing four solo games, mostly played on the previous patch: a Phantom Warden, an Oracle Holliday, an Oracle Mina and a Wraith.
Themes: giving or taking wave priority, box routing versus harassing, double meleeing camps, Slowing Hex, dives and ult timing, fighting after objectives, weak side play, farming between waves, sinners and invading, urns and shrines, Mina ult-max builds, and when to buy tempo items.
