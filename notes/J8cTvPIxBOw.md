# The BIG Problem with Deadlock Macro
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-11-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8cTvPIxBOw

## Covers
A 2024 stream rant (the game has changed a lot since) about a patch that made two players sharing a lane earn as much as a solo player, which he says killed the solo farm vs group trade-off, made jungle and split pushing pointless and hurt Wraith. Includes the hotfix that cut duo sharing to 75%. Mostly opinion, but it has concrete soul numbers and how that meta was played at high rank.

## Claims
- After that patch, even past 8 minutes, two players in one lane each got the same souls as a solo player, and jungle creeps were nerfed, so you always want to be in a lane with one teammate. [0:00]
- Your teammates should wait until you are at the wave so you can share the souls. [0:00] [0:31]
- The change makes split pushers and 1v1 heroes much worse; Wraith, the best split pusher and 1v1 hero, now has two people in her face when she split pushes or looks for a pick, so he thinks Wraith is really bad in this patch. [1:01]
- Before the patch the macro trade-off was stacking with teammates for a kill, teamfight or objective versus going solo for more money; now there is no downside to being with a teammate. [1:31]
- The high-rank meta is everyone waiting on lanes until a big triple creep wave pushes in, then two players stacking on it to farm. [2:03]
- If your teammates keep pushing out lanes alone while the enemy farms them in pairs, the enemy pulls ahead no matter what you do. [2:33]
- One creep is worth roughly 95 to 100 souls after 10 minutes, so a 10-creep wave is about 1,000 souls; anyone who pushes it out alone instead of together loses the team about a thousand souls. [3:35]
- With four lanes and six players you can always have two players on one lane together, and lanes give far more souls than jungle creeps, so jungling is only useful when your team is playing the lanes incorrectly. [4:36] [5:07]
- Even in Ascendant and Eternus games, teammates push out lanes alone instead of waiting two seconds for you to soak the wave. [5:07]
- Hero kill bounty was increased in the same patch, on top of comeback souls (killing a hero with more souls than you gives much more). [5:37] [7:08]
- The more souls you have, the safer you have to play, since dying gives the enemy comeback souls. [7:38]
- At high level, a team 20 to 30k behind can dive and focus the enemy's richest carry, lose two players, and still gain more souls from that fight than the enemy. [7:38] [8:08]
- A follow-up hotfix cut soul sharing so a duo gets 75% each instead of the full 100% a solo player gets; jungle and solo pushing may be more valuable again, though jungle creeps are still nerfed. [9:10] [9:41]
- The hotfix does not fix the late-game issue of heroes giving too much souls on kill. [10:12]
- The same hotfix reduced Shadow Transformation: bullet and spirit resist 60 to 50, heal 30 to 25, duration 6 to 5.5 seconds; Paradoxical Swap time was increased to help with the camera. [10:12]
