# The OFFICIAL Deadlock Hero Tier List
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-03-23
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmLuOrj6NWI

## Covers
A ranked ladder tier list recorded 2025-03-23, a few weeks into the three-lane map from the 2025-02-25 update (no specific patch is named). Every hero gets a placement, with notes on which heroes are better in coordinated or pro play than in solo ranked.
For ranked players. Early 2025; the game has changed a lot since.

## Claims
- This is a ladder tier list; some heroes are obviously better in coordinated pro play. [0:00]
- Calico gets an S+ tier of her own because she is just so good. [0:00]
- Abrams is S tier, a top three hero in ranked: he doesn't die in lane, and in mid game team fights with the spirit build he is the best hero in the game, running into everyone and not dying. [0:30] [9:09]
- Dynamo is good but near the bottom. [1:00]
- Grey Talon is an easy A tier. [1:00]
- Haze is not completely unplayable, with certain lane comps where she is okay, but Metal Skin makes her kind of useless; bottom of the barrel. [1:30]
- Holliday is still very solid but not S tier. [1:30]
- Mirage is insane in coordinated play and a bit weaker on the ladder: S, not S+. The more coordinated your team, the more you can abuse his teleport and split push. He is insane in lane but not an insane hard carry in many ranked games. [2:00]
- Infernus is very solid, low A tier. [2:32]
- Ivy is very solid, as always. [2:32]
- Kelvin would be S in coordinated play, but in ranked he needs his team to do something; with bad teammates you must do more yourself and Kelvin can't. Mo & Krill is the best ranked support. [3:06]
- Lady Geist is an easy A tier, a very solid tank carry; some would rate her higher. [3:36]
- Lash felt really OP when the new map came out because people forced team fights under Walkers, but now people position smarter and there are many urn fights where a well-positioned player can dodge his ult; he goes to high B (maybe A in ranked). [3:36] [14:19]
- Paradox is insane in coordinated play; in ranked she is A. [4:06]
- Mo & Krill is broken: every time anyone in pro play picks him in the last four weeks he is the most impactful hero, he can't lose lane and can't die, and he should be first pick. [4:36]
- Pocket is B in ranked: mid in lane, really good early mid game, but he can't carry alone late. [5:06]
- Seven is S tier, the best hard carry right now (a hero that needs some money). The lower your rank the better he gets, because low rank players don't punish farming; the higher you go the more other heroes outclass him. [5:36]
- Lady Geist and Paradox are high A, not S; he would rather face them in ranked than a Seven, Bebop, Mo & Krill or Abrams. [6:06]
- Shiv is low B, not as bad as Dynamo, Haze and McGinnis; he has power spikes and can carry to an extent in pubs. His win rate has been below 50% since release in every meta, even when he was broken, so win rate is no argument. [6:36]
- Shiv is more playable than Warden, Dynamo, Haze and McGinnis; that is not a debate. [13:48]
- Sinclair is S for ranked and on paper almost S+, but very skillful: weak if you are not good on him, oppressive if you are. Nobody plays him in comp. He is less oppressive in lane now, no longer a free lane win, but still a top four laner. With the best Sinclair in the game he would be S+. [7:37]
- Vindicta is S in ranked. [8:39]
- Bebop is one of the best heroes for ranked; he refuses to drop him below S. [8:39]
- The supports (Paradox, Ivy, Kelvin) can go lower for ranked, since a support's impact relies more on the team; in comp they are better, and one is almost always picked. [9:09] [14:19]
- Viscous is a very solid support but needs an actual carry; he won't hard carry by himself. [9:39]
- Wraith is really bad and loses lane, and if you lose lane in this game you are in trouble unless your second laner is very good. He still puts her in B tier because she is better in pubs. [10:09] [12:46]
- In the last Deadlock Fight Night, Wraith had 0% pick and ban rate; the only heroes never picked or banned were Wraith, Dynamo, Haze and McGinnis. [10:09]
- Vyper is low on the list but good in pubs and undervalued even in pro play: one of the best mid boss steal mechanics and really good damage. [11:10]
- If you want a hard carry there are four better options than Wraith right now, including Seven and Infernus (and Vindicta, though she fills a different niche); Wraith needs longer than Seven to get online. [11:43]
- If the lane goes even and Wraith gets to farm for a while she is still scary, but she is garbage in lane and in early mid game fights. Seven gets online, lanes and fights back far more easily. Wraith only fights better in a pure 1v1, such as someone walking into her jungle while she has cooldowns. [11:43]
- Warden is kind of bad: he is played a bit in comp, but he has no purpose that other heroes don't do better, even in pubs. [12:46]
- Yamato is S tier, a top three laner with no debate: she wins the lane unless the enemy also has broken laners, then needs time to become useful. Not S+, but given enough farm she becomes unkillable and can walk into a 1v6. [13:18]
- Ivy can be insane with a good team, for example hard carrying by flying the urn. [14:49]
