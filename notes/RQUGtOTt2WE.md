## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream from the July 2026 patch, no pro analysis this time. He coaches four players: a Seven in a Phantom lobby laning into Haze and gun Bebop, Beer Lord on Graves in an E6 lobby, an Ascendant Calico, and a Doorman.
Themes: respecting Haze's Fixation, playing every Rift with your ult, Graves split pushing and root placement, Calico's Arctic Blast into Gloom Bombs, Dispel Magic versus Counterspell, urn orbs, roaming, invading Sinner's Sacrifice, baiting mid, and being proactive instead of reactive.
Side opinions on Sleep Dagger, Curse, comeback souls, Victor builds and the mid boss rope routes.
