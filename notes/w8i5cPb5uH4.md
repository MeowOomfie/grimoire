# The Best Build for Every Hero in Deadlock - 2026 Update
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-12-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8i5cPb5uH4

## Covers
A late December 2025 pick of the best build for every hero, Abrams to Yamato, mostly credited to named high level players (Piggy for the supports), with a short note on how to play each. Running themes: reach 4.8k investment in all three categories, Toxic Bullets is the best gun item, Siphon Bullets is dead, Colossus is rising on tanks, small solo and duo kills pay best this meta. For anyone picking a build for any hero.

## Claims
General
- The in-game build browser boosts builds to the top that can actively lower your chances of winning. [0:00]
- Every build he picked reaches 4.8k investment in every investment category; even on a mostly gun hero it's worth eventually getting the free extra spirit (about 38 to 40) from the 4.8k spirit investment. [2:31] [3:02]

- Toxic Bullets is the best gun item in the game on almost anyone who shoots reasonably fast, partly because the other 3k gun items are weaker. [5:03] [5:34]

Abrams (Listic's build)
- Abrams gains less from the green investment because he is inherently tanky, so rush 4.8k orange: Siphon Life and his other abilities scale poorly early, and he needs melee and gun damage to get kills in lane and early mid game. [1:00] [1:30]
- Get lots of regen early, then Cultist, ideally by 8 minutes, take the tier three camp, then roam permanently looking for kills, including in the enemy jungle. [2:00] [2:31]
- Later get the 4.8k spirit investment with Superior Duration, which makes Siphon Life and the stuns on Shoulder Charge and Seismic Impact last much longer. [3:02]
- Follow early game then mid game items left to right; with no slots left buy Boundless Spirit, which pro players run and which has a very high late game win rate on Abrams because the durations scale. [3:32]
