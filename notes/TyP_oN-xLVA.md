# Why I Switched To Deadlock, And Why You Should Too!
channel: Bogur Two
date: 2026-10-26
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyP_oN-xLVA

## Covers
General pitch for new players coming from hero shooters like Overwatch and Marvel Rivals, from Bogur Two.
Commentary, not a guide: how the map, shops, souls and timed objectives work, and how to get builds without studying items.
No one coached. Recorded around the 2026-10-26 patch.

## Claims
- You do not have to be good at the game to enjoy it; you improve from playing it and having fun. [0:00]
- Ziplines get you to lane fast at the start and let you rotate around the map without walking or recalling. [2:34]
- The first 10 minutes play like a hero shooter: you are fighting all the time, shooting creeps, landing abilities and fighting for space. [5:05]
- For items, open the build browser, search the hero, pick a build and buy it left to right. [6:06]
- There is a lot of bait in the build browser; find creators who know what they are doing and stick to their builds. [6:06]
- Souls are both money and XP; when you die you lose some of your money but your XP stays. [7:06]
- Around 2 minutes, after one or two waves, side camps spawn to kill. [7:37]
- Around 3 minutes breakable boxes appear that give souls and sometimes a buff. [7:37]
- Around 5 minutes buffs and more creep camps become available. [8:07]
- Sinners (the gambling boxes) are a mini game; one spawns from the start, others around 8 minutes. [8:07]
- Around 12 minutes the Rift opens; it is the objective that forces you to play as a team. [8:38]
- Watch streams of good players to learn how the game is supposed to be played. [9:08]
- Air strafing off the mid zipline gets you to a side lane quickly for a gank, and this works from side lanes to mid too. [12:43]
- If your movement is bad, there is a teleporter to the other lane. [13:45]
- You can shop in each lane while your tier one guardian stands, plus two more shops on the map, so you do not have to go back to base. [14:17]
- Warden's ultimate no longer gets him feared by Nidus Harrow's (as captioned) fear ultimate. [17:23]
- Parrying exists and enemies can parry you; there is risk and reward to it. [19:25]
- The game rewards aim; landing headshots on gun heroes is strong. [19:55]
- Street Brawl, a 4v4 mode, is a low pressure way to mess around, buy items and learn the game. [20:55]
