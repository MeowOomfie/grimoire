## Covers
Midknight Vods' first free coaching stream back from a week and a half off, during the ranked season with the new rank names (stream of 8/31/26). He reviews four replays: a Mirage (old rank Oracle) laning with The Doorman into Holliday and Grey Talon, a Bebop (Ritualist) he has coached three times before, a Wraith (Oracle) laning with Yamato into Abrams and Infernus, and Zach's Victor (Oracle) laning with Paige into Dynamo and Apollo.
Themes: syncing with your lane partner, farming outside lane and going side lane as an M1, buy timers, utility items after the damage spike, gun utility items, Rift and walker trades, Wraith card stacking, parry drills, overfighting and mental.
