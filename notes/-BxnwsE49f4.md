# Spirit Is Too Strong Now? - Deadlock Meta Analysis
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-07-13
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-BxnwsE49f4

## Covers
Meta opinion piece on the July 2025 patch after the gun versus spirit debate: argues balance is character specific, not gun vs spirit.
Covers weak items after the item rework, passive investment stats, high base HP heroes (Shiv, Warden, McGinnis), one build per hero, and the perma team fight map.
For anyone following balance and build crafting, not tied to one rank.

## Claims
- Verdict: gun was never dead and never too strong; balance is a character-specific issue, not gun damage vs spirit damage. [1:00]
- Deadlock has always centered on early game spirit damage: almost every lane kill is mostly spirit damage. [1:00]
- Exceptions: Haze does mostly gun; Vyper's lane kill comes from getting the target low with gun (and teammate damage) and then finishing with a spirit ability. [1:30]
- Even Mirage's lane pressure is amplified by spirit damage: his spirit keeps applying Djinn's Mark, which hurts a lot when stacked; without Djinn's Mark he wouldn't be oppressive in lane. [2:02]
- When a gun hero like Mirage dominates, the right conclusion is "Mirage is OP", not "gun is OP". [2:32]
- Per deadlock-api and Statlocker win rates, this patch statistically made the gap between best and worst heroes larger: more heroes are very strong and more are very weak. [3:04]
- Since the item rework, items are individually weaker but you get passive investment stats. [4:04]
- Spirit Resilience is much weaker than old Spirit Armor, a bit cheaper but doing less, yet it is still really good right now. [4:04]
- The weaker items get, the more hero base stats matter; meta heroes either do huge burst damage or, mostly, are very tanky on base. [4:34]
- Shiv at max level with zero items has 2,200 HP, effectively more with Bloodletting. [5:04]
- Warden at max level with no items has 2,500 HP; squishier heroes like Haze are way lower. [5:04]
- Shiv does huge damage without orange or purple items, so he can spam green items and not be punished. [6:06]
- Buying a single 3.2k spirit item gives about 19 spirit for free from investment; on Shiv something like Superior Cooldown, or Torment Pulse, adds another 19. [6:36]
- Passive investment gives so many stats and items are so weak that there is very little skill expression in build crafting; every build feels similar and you can buy a bunch of greens and be fine. [7:08]
- Return Fire is still a standout item; otherwise people stack barriers for HP, resists, then Boundless Spirit for spirit power or Superior Cooldown into Transcendent Cooldown. [7:08]
- Transcendent Cooldown looked weak at double the price of Superior Cooldown but is actually really strong; a 6k item still gives about 25 to 32 spirit power from investment. [7:39]
- With Transcendent Cooldown and Torment Pulse you have 44 spirit from investment without a spirit item, and about 80 spirit at max level with nothing invested in spirit power directly. [7:39]
- Mystic Vulnerability is trash: a tiny bit of spirit resist and a tiny spirit resist reduction on the enemy. [8:10]
- Proposed fix: buff items to make them unique and stand out, and nerf passive investment in all three categories (weapon, vitality, spirit). [8:41]
- Boon upgrades are badly imbalanced for some heroes, and HP is the main thing skewing the meta toward a death ball meta. [8:41]
- Meta heroes are mostly really tanky or have insane nukes like Seven or Pocket. [9:12]
- Most items feel like stat sticks unless active; exceptions that feel good are Warp Stone, Phantom Strike and Heroic Aura. [9:12]
- Green items give too many stats. [9:42]
- On Haze, Swift Striker, Slowing Bullets, Intensifying Magazine, Kinetic Dash or Burst Fire all feel about the same; none changes the play style or makes or breaks a game. [9:42]
- A gun hero like Haze should not get 10 to 11 spirit for free from one cheap item, or 25 to 30 spirit after an upgrade, without buying a spirit item. [11:14]
- The meta is about hitting stat thresholds, not which items you use: on a gun hero about 58% extra weapon damage, on a spirit hero about 44 spirit, mostly reached with greens and resists. [11:45]
- Build crafting used to support many play styles; now it is condensed into one build per hero (Haze used to be viable as spirit or gun). [11:45]
- Pocket is a spirit nuker and always has been. [12:17]
- McGinnis is the good example: carry gun, support, hybrid and turret (ult) builds all feel viable depending on the situation. [12:17]
- The Glock build is bad despite hype from a pro player in Archon and an Eternus game; for the same gun investment Warden does more. [12:47]
- Seven is an ult bot (unless on Metro's build), Grey Talon is only a spirit nuker, Dynamo is an ult bot, Warden is kind of an ult bot. [13:19]
- Wraith used to have two distinct viable builds (full card or full gun); now neither works and she buys a bit of everything, and is weak no matter what. [13:19]
- Viscous used to have melee punch, ult and Splatter builds; now there is one viable build per hero. [13:49]
- Ivy's gun was nerfed so she is only a support; spirit nuke Ivy (ult and Kudzu Bomb damage), full heal support and gun carry Ivy should all be viable. [13:49]
- The only things to nerf are the weapon, vitality and spirit investment bonuses. [14:19]
- The current meta encourages early and mid game team fighting, the jungle is basically worthless, and the higher the rank the more people group as six and perma team fight. [14:50]
- Heroes good in lane that scale well, and tanky heroes, win this meta. [14:50]
- Green items were nerfed so much that HP is hard to build, so carries that are not inherently tanky are weaker; the tanky exceptions are Abrams, Bebop, Warden, Shiv and Kelvin. [15:20]
- McGinnis is making a comeback because her HP scaling is insane: 2.5k HP at max level base. [15:51]
- He doesn't want to nerf every hero's base stats, only the heroes with too much base HP. [15:51]
- The problem with Shiv and Abrams is that they can build full tank and still one-shot you; either reduce their HP or their damage. [16:21]
- Kills give more souls than anything else; if suiciding to trade one for one is always smarter than clearing jungle, split pushing or stealing enemy jungle, the game is bad. [16:51]
- Stealing the enemy jungle used to feel valuable; now it doesn't matter, and an enemy who steals your jungle just carries unsecured souls into the team fight that you get if you kill them. [17:21]
- He prefers the three lane map over four lanes: four lanes were unbalanced because the duo lane ganks the solo lane after, which felt bad in high rank. [17:51]
- Gun is not dead, spirit is not dead; characters are the issue. [18:52]
