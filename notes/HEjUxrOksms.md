## Covers
Midknight free coaching VOD review of an Ascendant-level Dynamo (unranked game) duo laning with Mirage against Mo & Krill and Paradox, a short game that ends in a loss.
Themes: Kinetic Pulse kick timing, leveling abilities with hotkeys, skill order (Rejuvenating Aura over Quantum Entanglement early), buying Healing Rite for a teammate, overeager reloads, baiting parries with fake-out melees, box runs, secret shop versus resetting, respecting a soul deficit, bridge buffs, and playing verticality for Singularity when behind.
This is a late-2024 video (early November 2024 patch). The game has changed a lot since, so treat numbers, items and map details as dated.

## Claims
Dynamo laning:
- Dynamo is one of the heroes who can win the game for the whole team with one ult. [0:30]
- Mirage is one of the strongest laners right now, and Dynamo's knock-up with Mirage's Tornado can get a lot of kills, especially before Paradox gets her ult. [0:30]
- Going for melees while Kinetic Pulse (the kick) is on cooldown is very aggressive; an enemy who knows it is down can punish you hard. [1:31]
- When you hear Paradox charging Kinetic Carbine, tuck behind cover and circle it; dash in and kick her only if you know the shot is about to expire. [2:01]
- Level abilities with the hotkey (hold the level-up modifier and press the ability key) instead of opening Tab and clicking, so you don't stop moving and can use a fresh point instantly in a fight. [2:31]
- Don't touch the enemy Tower unless you know you have the kill or your laning partner is with you; that dive cost about 200 damage and put the lane in a negative spot. [4:03]
- Stop reloading for one or a few bullets; it loses a lot of damage, especially while chasing. [4:34]
- Level Rejuvenating Aura second, not Quantum Entanglement; early Entanglement dodges little against Mo & Krill and Paradox, and Aura can heal your laning partner. [5:05]
- Entanglement becomes useful once Paradox has her ult: you can activate it while stunned and it still goes off, dodging her swap. Against Bebop it can dodge his bombs. [5:05]
- Buy Healing Rite at least, or Extra Regen or Restorative Shot when you are high HP. [5:36]
- As a supportive Dynamo, a strong play is buying Healing Rite and using it on your laning partner when he hasn't bought one, then topping both of you off with Rejuvenating Aura. [6:07]
- At 3 minutes boxes start spawning, and the small camp should be farmed. [7:07]
- After the first parry, don't keep heavy meleeing that player: fake out a heavy melee (turn away at the last second) to see whether they react with a parry; if they do, you get a huge punish. [8:40]
- The first melee in a game can be a normal one; after that use fake-out melees, or stop meleeing unless the enemy is facing away. [31:16]
- Relying on heavy melee is a crutch that gets punished more as Elo rises because people parry a lot more. [9:10]
- When Paradox wastes her Time Wall, that is your window to go aggressive for a kick. [10:10]
- At Ascendant you need box runs after shoving: one route drops down and melees the boxes and comes back; the other crosses to several boxes and can turn into a gank. [10:40]
- The longer gank route usually costs minions unless both waves are dead and shoved under the enemy Tower; it suits Dynamo because he is a good ganker once he has his ult. [11:10]
- A 500 soul gap in lane is significant; when Paradox has her ult and you are behind, play really passive. [11:40]
- Not roaming pre-ult on Dynamo is fine, but once you have Singularity be more roamy. [12:11]
- When Paradox places her Time Wall, expect her to Kinetic Carbine or ult you through it; tuck until the wall drops, then punish her. [12:11]
- His build: Hollow Point Ward, High Velocity Mag, then Extra Charge. Since he loves kicking, he should buy Extra Charge earlier so Kinetic Pulse is available more often. [12:41]
- After pushing a wave in, do a box run or the medium camp instead of sieging the Tower for a little poke, especially when the enemy is ahead. [13:42]
- If you don't use voice, ping a lot, including enemy missing pings. [14:13]
- Always keep track of where your laning partner is; when he teleported away you got jumped alone. [15:16]
- If a teammate keeps playing too aggressively, don't follow him: let him die and play passive yourself. [15:47]
- Respect a much stronger enemy laner: Mo & Krill with spirit items has a ton of burst and can turn on you and ult you even without Paradox. [16:17]
- Don't reset to base when you are near full HP and going straight back to lane; do the nearby camps and buy at the secret shop instead. Resetting only makes sense if you then go gank. [17:18]
- Mystic Shot and Enchanter's Barrier fit this game. [17:18]
- Pushing a wave aggressively with no teammates near when you are far behind will never get the Tower and wastes time you could spend farming or taking neutrals. [18:51]
- Rotating from the duo lane across the map to a fight you won't reach in time wastes time; split or farm instead. [19:52]
- With the Urn in your base, boost back to contest it, especially as Dynamo, because one good Singularity wins that fight. [20:24]

Mid and late game:
- When you can't see any enemies, play a two-on-one more slowly and gather information before deciding to take it. [21:31]
- When you are 6K souls behind Paradox and she misses her wall, take the blessing and leave; you won that duel only because she misplayed. [22:03]
- The main way to win a MOBA is to not take unfavorable fights. [22:33]
- An ego helps players around Archon take fights, but by Ascendant it gives diminishing returns; when you are down, farm and play around your teammates when your ult is up. [23:33]
- Midknight heal-maxes Dynamo as a hyper support, because it trains awareness of where everyone is in a team fight, especially with Rescue Beam. [24:03]
- Respect a Viscous who can always roll away in his ball if you go in on him. [24:03]
- Don't sit on 4K souls unless you are saving for something like Refresher. [24:34]
- His mid-game hyper-kick Dynamo with Rapid Recharge and barriers is a good build, but barriers are mid-game items that get a lot worse after about 30 minutes. [24:34]
- In a fight where enemies may be hiding below, stay up high and look down for them; you can react to a jump-out with Quantum Entanglement. [26:06]
- Look for fights when Singularity is up. [26:36]
- Bridge buffs are worth about a 3K item each, so both are 6K, and denying them from the enemy can be a 12K swing; in real Ascendant they get taken within 30 to 40 seconds of spawning. [27:06]
- Don't posture up with no teammates nearby when you are squishy and far behind; three enemies will hard burst you. [28:40]
- When you are down 40K, play in base with your team, let them siege the Walker or walk past the Guardians, and then jump out for the Singularity. [29:11]
- When behind and squishy, the only way to get a good Dynamo ult is verticality: wait on top of the bridge or mid and drop into choke points like funnels for the ult. [29:43]
- Use the ult more: even solo-ulting to secure a kill in laning is not bad, and he saw only two ults this game from the most important part of the kit. [32:19]
- When far behind in souls, spend more time in the jungle, roaming or ganking to get souls back instead of taking ego duels. [31:48]
- Positioning: stop walking up to enemies with no teammates around, especially when behind; Shiv, Paradox and Wraith all jumped you at the end. [33:21]
