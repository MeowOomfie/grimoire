# The Deadlock Hero Tier List For Every Rank
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-06-18
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S9Z3eaPVapQ

## Covers
Tier list for the mid June 2026 patch (Urn king of the hill era, before the 2026-06-30 Unstable Rift patch), built from watching games at every rank but rated for the average ladder player.
Every hero gets a placement and the reason, with notes where pro play, Eternus or low ranks differ (Holliday, Seven, Shiv, McGinnis, Viscous). Theme: Urn fights decide games, so big AoE ultimates are king and pick-off heroes and tanks suffer.

## Claims
- The Urn is still king this patch: the first few Urn fights dictate the game, so mass AoE abilities are very strong. [0:31]
- Warden got a massive ultimate buff last patch plus more Willpower spirit scaling, so spirit or hybrid Warden is back; with a few early ult points he feels very powerful. [0:31]
- Warden: buy duration and max the ultimate and you will win Urn fights; max Willpower counters almost everything and his debuff resist means he basically cannot be stunned. S tier, possibly S plus later, a meta-defining character. [1:01]
- Holliday: Powder Keg was made stronger in every way (easier to hit, more damage); max it first and it is crazy, and at Eternus 6 and in pro games her damage is crazy. [3:33]
- Holliday is hard to play and get value from, especially for newer or middle rank players, and her win rate up to Eternus barely changed. [3:33]
- Holliday is a pick-off and initiation hero; the lasso is less valuable in big Urn, mid boss and walker fights, the meta does not favor pick heroes, and your teammates often do not follow up, so she lacks carry potential: B tier in ladder play. [4:03]
- Silver got massive nerfs (ult cooldown roughly doubled from 40 to 70 seconds, no healing at max ult) but is still S tier: play the same way, tanky in lane, go in with ult and dominate, then wait between ults. [4:33]
- Silver is still great at catching with her bola and her kick and can roam for kills, as long as you track her ult cooldown and are confident hitting her spells. [5:03]
- Pocket is probably still the strongest hero despite a bullet damage decrease and cooldown increases, because Affliction is one of the best ultimates: max it, hit two or three people and the fight is basically won. [5:34]
- Pocket has insane pick-off potential as a mid game solo carry: win lane, then roam and one shot people. First pick, first ban in pro play and a menace at every rank. [6:04]
- Statlocker's new mastery stat (win rate over games played) puts Pocket on top: Pocket players gain the most win rate with experience, with a big jump from 100 to 250 games, so if Pocket feels weak you probably lack games on him. [6:04]
- Pocket stays the best until he is hotfixed or his ult no longer fully stops healing. [7:36]
- Venator is misunderstood and underrated at most ranks: he got much of his HP back over recent patches and is a tanky carry again. [7:36]
- Venator is scary at every point of the game, especially in 1v1s, unlike carries like Wraith who do little until late, and he scales very well. [8:07]
- Venator is the only hero with pure damage on multiple abilities: his 1, his ultimate and his blessed melees ignore bullet resist and Metal Skin, so a fed Venator is very hard to itemize against. One of the strongest heroes for carrying a team 1v6, but he takes time to learn. [8:07]
- Seven has had the highest win rate for two years because he is easy to get value from (press 4), but once you learn to counter him he is not insane. [9:10]
- Seven's latest nerfs: bullet damage growth reduced 40% and Power Surge cooldown increased, so no permanent Power Surge even with Superior Duration. [9:10]
- Seven: almost S tier on ladder because his ult in big Urn team fights makes up for the nerfs; still squishy, burstable first, and slow to come online. Up to about Ascendant he will carry you if you play him properly. [9:40]
- Abrams: few recent changes, a very solid tank who frontlines and controls fights well, but tanks are no longer must picks. [10:10]
- Abrams is not rated higher because, like initiators such as Holliday, if the team does not follow up you lose; he gets maybe one pick and is spent. [10:41]
- Players at every rank have become more passive: fight the Urn every 6 minutes, then everyone AFK farms. [11:11]
- The meta favors AoE heroes with big ults, not tanks. [11:11]
- Apollo: Disengaging Sigil is now a real movement ability that moves you further away to disengage, but Slowing Hex hard counters him by disabling all his movement. [11:11]
- Apollo used to roll lane and mid game then fall off; after his lane nerfs he often does not hard win lane, so even the mid game is hard. Niche (C) tier, struggling but not unplayable. [11:41]
- Bebop is being pushed toward tank: Sticky Bomb now gives resistances instead of gun damage, Uppercut got a 1% HP increase, and his spirit and gun damage have been nerfed several times. [12:12]
- Bebop is very solid as a tank and initiator: if you hit a hook and the team does not follow up, you still do not die, which makes him safer than Holliday. [12:43]
- Billy got damage nerfs but is the last true tank with a very solid HP pool, the strongest tank if played well. [13:14]
- Billy can transition into a carry with spirit or gun if the team does not pick up the slack; top NA player Wander carries Eternus 6 lobbies with gun Billy. S tier, rated above Bebop and Abrams, probably better than Silver. [13:45]
- Calico: Gloom Bombs now apply a melee resist debuff on trigger so Leaping Slash and melees follow up harder, and her ultimate heals more and deals more damage; she can one shot again and is picked more in pro play. [13:45]
- Calico: low A tier, because her solo carry potential is lacking; win lane and snowball the mid game or she falls off. [14:46]
- Celeste: no changes this patch, a nuker who can also be tanky and disruptive, never weak; S tier and no lower. [14:46]
- Celeste has more movement options than any other hero and can carry start to finish; late game, move well to get a pick, survive, and bait the enemy team into chasing too far until your team shows up. [15:17]
- The Doorman: Call Bell does about five less damage, the cheesy door play style is still how you get value, and he is S tier and no lower. [15:49]
- Drifter is in a tough spot: he wants solo kills but the meta is team fights and people stack more than usual, he is not good in lane, and there are better carries and better mid game roamers; niche tier. His 1 is stronger with the silence and his bleed hits hard. [15:49]
- Dynamo is niche: everything but Black Hole is weak, and with the new Urn you only need to be in the radius, so people stack less and big Black Holes are harder. [16:50]
- Graves got hit hard again (skull damage and charge rate reduced); she is a bad hero but a noob stomper who punishes bad aim and positioning with little effort. B tier: she gets value easily but will not carry. [17:20]
- Grey Talon: minor Rain of Arrows nerfs mean less flying around, but his damage is ridiculous; S plus with a good team, S tier because he is a team reliant glass cannon who dies if not protected. [17:50]
- Grey Talon is the better glass cannon than Vindicta in every regard: the owl crosses the whole map and his gun and spirit damage are far higher (a pro scrim Grey Talon did about 200k damage vs 80k for the next player). Vindicta got a slight buff but is worse. [18:20]
- Haze is rated the worst hero in the game: her play style is max ult, Sleep Dagger, Slowing Hex or Curse, then ult; her gun is not crazy, she is not great in team fights, and solo pick-offs are hard to find this patch, even more so for her than Drifter. [19:21]
- Infernus: burn is more consistent after fixes; high A tier, no lower than A. Play him as a tank, disruptor or front liner: the best Infernus players build all green and purple items in a Flame Dash build; more orange gun items do worse. [19:53]
- Infernus' dash build sets the map on fire and does huge team fight damage without dying and is hard to punish, though the coach thinks Infernus should be a gun hero. [20:24]
- Ivy is mediocre, a jack of all trades support who usually buys support items (Rescue Beam, maybe Tesla Bullets, Vortex Web, Mystic Expansion, Echo Shard with Stone Form), and is inferior to Rem. [20:54]
- Rem is S plus, by far the best support: the most healing, survivability and utility, a crazy ult, Little Helpers that push lanes for free, the ability to invade the enemy jungle and steal sinners, and huge map mobility with the vents. [20:54]
- Rem with Echo Shard on Tag Along can heal a teammate from 1 HP to full in about 2 seconds; he needs nerfs, though like any support he needs a team. [21:25]
- Kelvin and Paige are mediocre: only support builds work (gun Kelvin is not great), and Rem outshines them at every point. Kelvin is slightly more valuable because his dome matters more than Paige's ult in most situations. [21:57]
- Lady Geist: solid A tier, a carry who is good at every point and tankier and better at frontlining than Venator, but does less damage than him, especially late. [22:28]
- Lash: solid, a bit above average; the new Urn drop-off no longer needs people on the bridge, so big Lash ults are harder to land, he is only okay in lane and needs time to come online. [22:58]
- McGinnis: turrets made much weaker and ult nerfed again; an okay hero who will still see play in lower ranks, where people sit in her ult like they do against Seven, and spirit turrets placed correctly get value by themselves, similar to Graves. [23:28]
- Mina is making a comeback without changes while others get nerfed; her ult is still strong, she has an insane lane, and she can roam and snowball the mid game like Pocket after winning lane. A tier to high A. [23:58]
- Mina falls off very late (a Mina up 20k could not carry the late game), and without good movement she drops lower. [24:28]
- Mirage: S tier, cannot be A. He can split push and still join the 6v6 Urn fight, which the team with six usually wins and snowballs; he cannot really lose 1v1s with Scarabs and has a lot of burst. [24:59]
- Mirage is more of a spirit hero than a gun hero right now and keeps scaling through utility, for example Echo Shard Tornado for four tornadoes. [25:30]
- Mo & Krill: niche; great in small skirmishes, which happen less often, and in team fights a big Abrams ult or Billy chain beats grabbing one person. [25:30]
- Paradox is still really strong, rated above Holliday: Echo Shard with a double Pulse Grenade is the meta and does huge team fight damage in this circle meta. S tier. [26:02]
- Shiv was nerfed but stays meta in pro and coordinated play for absorbing damage and executing the right targets; on ladder with randoms he is mediocre and cannot carry alone. [26:32]
- Shiv's win rate rises significantly after about 250 games on him, so unless you are a Shiv one trick he is not the hero to pick up now to carry. [27:03]
- Sinclair is a bit better with no draft but at the mercy of RNG: games with good ults to copy go great, games without go badly. His rabbits are also great in team fights. Low B tier, and he needs a lot of practice. [27:03]
- Victor got small nerfs; build him hybrid, his ultimate does a lot and he survives a long time in long Urn fights. High A tier. [27:34]
- Viscous: Puddle Punch got a massive buff to 60% lifesteal at level two, so Puddle Punch builds will be back. [28:04]
- Viscous is the one hybrid support who can contest Rem: Goo Ball disrupts, Cube saves allies and yourself, and Puddle Punch sustains and causes havoc. High A tier, S for a Viscous master, so now is the time for Viscous mains. [28:34]
- Vyper: squishy, very high damage, probably the second highest burst in the game, with a bola for team fights; like a Grey Talon who goes in and commits everything, the fight ends fast either way. A tier glass cannon carry. [28:34]
- Wraith: mediocre, does a lot in team fights but is weak early and mid; a late game Wraith can still be scary, but Venator, Warden with his ult, or Mirage in 1v1s do her job better. Solid B tier. [29:04]
- Yamato: Power Slash damage was reverted back down; still a good tank and easily a top three laner, present early, but lacks the AoE this patch needs. Considered low S or high A, placed in A tier. [29:34]
- Overall balance is close: outside the S and S plus heroes the roster is tight, arguably except Haze, and no hero is unplayable. The list is for the average player and some placements may change for Eternus. [30:04]
