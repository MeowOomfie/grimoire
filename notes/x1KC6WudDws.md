## Covers
Grey Talon, aquatiq, commentary over one of his own pub games (solo lane, with a Lash and Dynamo on his team).

Patch around 2026-06-23.

## Claims
- Counterspell is hard to use because if you forget you have it, it is useless; if you remember it, it is one of the top five items in the game for him. [1:03]
- Counterspell counters Lash, and anything that counters Lash is great because Lash takes control of every game. [1:03]
- Against this lane (Mina) he wanted Spirit Shielding. [1:33]
- With Guided Owl maxed you can be anywhere on the map: when another lane gets someone low, bird them. [4:56]
- With max bird it does not matter much where you are, which is why he likes to solo lane as Grey Talon and just go kill people. [8:42]
- At 26 bird stacks (+10 on the kill) he thought about buying cooldown. [9:12]
- Grey Talon after the nerf still does huge damage; he questions whether it was a real nerf. [12:17]
- He expected a Grey Talon draft with Lash and Dynamo on his team to be strong, and it was. [22:10]
