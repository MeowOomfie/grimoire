# Every Hero in Deadlock Explained

## Covers
All 38 heroes, abilities and team role of each: Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Calico, The Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash, McGinnis, Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Seven, Shiv, Sinclair, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous, Vyper, Warden, Wraith, Yamato, and the Old Gods: New Blood six (Apollo, Celeste, Graves, Rem, Silver, Venator). Lore segments skipped. No lane, farming, spike or build advice in this video.

## Claims

### General
- Deadlock has 38 playable heroes; most of the information comes from the Deadlock wiki [0:06]
- The six newest heroes, from the Old Gods: New Blood major update, are Apollo, Celeste, Graves, Rem, Silver and Venator [42:14]

### Abrams
- One of the toughest frontline heroes, for players who like charging straight into the enemy team instead of sitting in the back [0:06]
- High health and self-healing make him very hard to take down, so he can stay in the middle of a fight longer than most [0:37]
- Siphon Life damages enemies in front of him while healing himself [0:37]
- Shoulder Charge rushes forward and slams enemies into walls, stunning them if it connects [0:37]
- Passive Infernal Resilience recovers a portion of the damage he takes over time [0:37]
- Seismic Impact launches him into the air and crashes down, dealing heavy damage and stunning anyone caught in the blast [0:37]
- A great hero to learn if you enjoy being first into every fight, protecting your team and soaking up most of the damage [1:07]

### Bebop
- All about grabbing enemies and throwing them out of position; extremely annoying to play against [1:37]
- Described as a disruptor: not there to soak up damage but to put the enemy team at a disadvantage [1:37]
- Exploding Uppercut sends an enemy flying backwards [1:37]
- Sticky Bomb attaches a bomb to the target that explodes after a very short delay [1:37]
- Hook, the ability he is most known for, launches his arm out to grab an enemy and pull them in [1:37]
- Hyper Beam charges up and fires a massive energy beam that deals continuous damage to anyone in sight [2:07]

### Billy
- Built around getting right in someone's face: a strong frontline hero with high health, close-range damage and plenty of disruption [2:37]
- Bashdown slams his bat into the ground, pulling nearby enemies down and creating a shock wave that damages and knocks them into the air [2:37]
- Rising Ram charges head first into an enemy, launching both Billy and the target into the air [3:08]
- Blasted rewards getting into melee range; landing melee attacks can temporarily increase his maximum health [3:08]
- Chain Gang chains nearby enemies to Billy and pulls everybody towards him, a great way to disrupt a fight [3:08]

### Calico
- Highly mobile assassin or flanker, good at taking down individual enemies before the enemy team can retaliate [3:39]
- Unlike Abrams or Billy she does not stand in front of the line; she is best weaving in and out of fights looking for vulnerable enemies [3:39]
- Gloom Bombs throws a cluster of bombs that explode after a short delay for spirit damage [3:39]
- Leaping Slash dashes forward and slashes everyone around her [4:09]
- Ava, her most unique ability, possesses her cat for increased movement speed and hides her from the enemy minimap [4:09]
- Return to Shadows makes her temporarily untargetable and deals damage when she enters and leaves that form [4:09]
- A good pick if you like being fast, sneaking around the map and picking off isolated enemies [4:40]

### The Doorman
- One of the strangest heroes, built around map control, mobility and confusing the other team; not a damage dealer, he creates opportunities for his team and messes with enemy positioning [4:40]
- Call Bell throws an exploding hotel bell that damages the enemy [5:10]
- Doorway, the ability he is most known for, connects two doors that players and projectiles can travel through [5:10]
- Luggage Cart sends out a cart that damages enemies and drags them along; it can also hit your teammates [5:10]
- Hotel Guest sends an enemy to the Baroness Hotel; they have limited time to reach the exit elevator, and if they fail they take extra damage before being sent back [5:10]

### Drifter
- Assassin who hunts isolated enemies rather than staying grouped all game; the kit rewards tracking down someone alone and killing them before teammates can rescue them [6:11]
- Rend swipes at enemies in front of him for spirit damage [6:11]
- Stalker's Mark makes an enemy bleed over time; reactivating it while they bleed teleports him directly behind them [6:11]
- Passive Bloodscent detects isolated enemies, deals increased damage to them, and when one dies nearby he permanently gains weapon damage [6:42]
- Eternal Night surrounds enemy heroes in darkness, limiting their vision [6:42]

### Dynamo
- Support and crowd-control hero, great at controlling team fights; he heals his team and repositions himself and allies [7:13]
- Kinetic Pulse sends out a blast that damages enemies and launches them into the air [7:13]
- Quantum Entanglement lets him briefly disappear into the void and reappear a short distance away [7:13]
- Rejuvenating Aura channels an aura that continuously heals him and nearby teammates [7:43]
- Singularity creates a massive black hole that pulls in nearby enemies and holds them in place while dealing damage [7:43]
- Worth trying if you like setting teammates up and potentially winning a whole team fight with one well-placed ultimate [8:13]

### Grey Talon
- Long-range marksman who uses his bow and traps to pick off enemies while staying out of danger; rewards good positioning and consistently landing shots from far enough away that enemies struggle to reach him [8:44]
- Charged Shot charges a powerful arrow that deals heavy damage and can pierce multiple enemies [8:44]
- Rain of Arrows launches him into the air to glide over the battlefield, and his weapon gets stronger while airborne [8:44]
- Spirit Snare is a trap that triggers when an enemy gets close, cursing and slowing them so they cannot use abilities, weapons or items for a time [9:14]
- Guided Owl launches a massive spirit owl that he controls and flies across the map [9:14]

### Haze
- Stealthy assassin who sneaks up, puts enemies to sleep and unloads on them; focused on gun damage rather than spirit damage [10:16]
- Especially dangerous against isolated enemies: the longer she keeps shooting one target, the more damage she deals [10:47]
- Sleep Dagger damages and sleeps an enemy, giving her the chance to close distance, line up shots, or escape if the fight goes badly [10:47]
- Smoke Bomb turns her invisible and increases sprint speed; attacking breaks it, so it is for sneaking around and getting the jump on someone [10:47]
- Fixation builds stacks with every bullet on the same target, increasing her bullet damage to that enemy [11:17]
- Bullet Dance unleashes a barrage at enemies in front of her with increased fire rate and evasion; if she catches you in a bad position she can turn a fight very quickly [11:17]
- Suits players who enjoy stealth and setting up ambushes [11:47]

### Holliday
- Gunslinger who relies on accurate shots, mobility and explosives; works best as a long-range damage dealer and disruptor [11:47]
- Instead of standing still and shooting she launches herself into the air, knocks enemies out of position and punishes anyone caught in the open with her revolver [12:18]
- Powder Keg throws an explosive barrel onto the battlefield [12:18]
- Bounce Pad launches Holliday or anyone else who steps on it into the air [12:18]
- Crackshot rewards headshots: when it activates the shot deals extra damage and slows the enemy [12:18]
- Spirit Lasso captures the first enemy it hits and drags them behind her, pulling someone away from their team towards her allies [12:48]

### Infernus
- Fast-moving damage dealer about setting people on fire; he constantly applies burn so damage keeps going after he stops shooting [13:50]
- Napalm sprays an incendiary mixture that damages and slows enemies and makes them more vulnerable to his attacks [13:50]
- Flame Dash rushes forward at high speed leaving a fire trail; good for chasing, escaping or setting an area on fire [14:20]
- Passive Afterburn is one of the most important parts of his kit: shooting builds afterburn until the enemy ignites and takes continuous spirit damage [14:20]
- Concussive Combustion turns him into a living bomb that explodes after a short delay, damaging and stunning every enemy around him; once activated the explosion cannot be interrupted [14:20]

### Ivy
- One of the more team-focused heroes: she can fight alone, but much of her kit gets stronger working directly with a teammate [14:50]
- Entangling Thorns creates a patch of vines that continuously damages and slows enemies inside it [15:22]
- Kudzu Connection links her to a nearby teammate, giving them bonuses like fire rate and bullet lifesteal and sharing healing between them [15:22]
- Stone Form turns her into an invulnerable stone statue that crashes into the ground, damaging and stunning nearby enemies and healing herself [15:22]
- Air Drop grabs a teammate, flies them across the battlefield and drops them at a chosen location [15:53]

### Kelvin
- Specializes in slowing enemies and healing teammates; keeps his team alive while making it very hard for enemies to move or escape [16:24]
- Frost Grenade explodes in an area, damaging and slowing enemies and healing teammates caught in it [16:24]
- Ice Path creates a floating trail of ice that he and his teammates can travel across [16:24]
- Arctic Beam fires a continuous freezing beam that damages and slows both movement and fire rate [16:24]
- Frozen Shelter creates an impenetrable dome around Kelvin or another hero; allies inside regenerate health rapidly and enemies are slowed; useful for saving a teammate or turning a fight [16:54]

### Lady Geist
- Duelist and damage dealer whose damage mostly requires sacrificing her own health, then stealing health back from enemies to stay alive [17:24]
- Essence Bomb costs health and throws a bomb that deals heavy spirit damage [17:54]
- Life Drain tethers her to an enemy, continuously stealing their health [17:54]
- Malice costs more health to fire blood shards that slow and stack, so hit enemies take increasingly more damage from her [17:54]
- Soul Exchange swaps health with an enemy: a nearly dead Geist who gets close to a high-health enemy puts herself back in the fight and leaves them low [18:24]

### Lash
- Highly mobile brawler who flies into fights for big damage: jump in, kill or disrupt, then escape before it gets too dangerous [19:26]
- Ground Strike slams the ground in front of him; used while airborne he dives down, dealing more damage the higher he was [19:26]
- Grapple pulls him through the air to a target and resets his air jumps and dashes, which is where his mobility comes from [19:26]
- Flog whips enemies in front of him and heals him for part of the damage [19:57]
- Death Slam connects his whips to nearby enemies, lifts them into the air and slams them down wherever he chooses [19:57]

### McGinnis
- Engineer, support and area-control hero; good at defending positions, pushing lanes and making areas the enemy does not want to walk into [20:27]
- Mini Turret deploys an automated turret that shoots nearby enemies for a limited time [20:27]
- Medicinal Specter summons a spirit that continuously heals her and nearby teammates [20:27]
- Spectral Wall sends out a massive wall that slows and damages enemies when it goes up [20:58]
- Heavy Barrage unleashes a huge volley of rockets at a targeted area [20:58]
- Worth a try if you enjoy building defenses and turning the objective into your own fortress [21:28]

### Mina
- Mobile marksman and harasser who harasses with spirit damage and bats, jumping in and out of danger while building extra damage on the enemies she fights [21:28]
- Rake slashes with her umbrella for more damage the more health the target is missing, and heals her for enemies she kills [21:28]
- Sanguine Retreat briefly disperses her, untargetable, flying to a chosen location [21:58]
- Love Bites makes her bullets deal extra spirit damage [21:58]
- Nox Nostra releases a swarm of bats that seek nearby enemies, dealing spirit damage and silencing them [21:58]

### Mirage
- Mobile damage dealer who chases enemies and teleports across the map to help his team; weakens enemies, controls their movement and travels fast when a fight breaks out [22:29]
- Fire Scarabs steal some of the enemy's health and make Mirage deal increased damage to them [22:59]
- Dust Devil turns him into a whirlwind that travels forward, damaging and launching enemies into the air [22:59]
- Djinn's Mark builds marks with his shots that can be consumed for extra spirit damage, rewarding repeated shots on one target [22:59]
- Traveler lets him pick a minimap location and teleport there after a short delay [22:59]
- A good pick if you enjoy chasing, moving around fights and teleporting wherever your team needs you [23:29]

### Mo & Krill
- Tank and initiators: very good at getting into the middle of fights, controlling enemies and staying alive while doing it [24:00]
- Scorn damages enemies around them and heals them based on the damage dealt [24:00]
- Burrow sends them underground with increased movement speed and damage resistance; coming back up damages, slows and launches nearby enemies [24:00]
- Sand Blast throws sand in front of them, damaging and disarming enemies [24:30]
- Combo has Mo grab an enemy and hold them in place while beating them, stunning and damaging throughout [24:30]

### Paige
- Support and control hero: protects teammates, locks down enemies and summons creatures to control a fight [25:00]
- Conjure Dragon throws a book that summons a dragon, damaging enemies that get too close [25:00]
- Plot Armor gives an ally a barrier and makes them stronger in combat, to keep an important teammate alive [25:00]
- Captivating Story traps enemies within an area, good for holding someone in place and setting them up for her or her team [25:30]
- Rallying Charge summons a cavalry charge that rushes across the battlefield [25:30]

### Paradox
- Great control and utility damage: controls fights, sets up teammates and swaps places with enemies [26:00]
- A little harder to learn than some heroes, but in the right hands she manipulates enemy positioning for very strong plays [26:00]
- Pulse Grenade creates a damaging area that repeatedly pulses spirit damage [26:31]
- Time Wall creates a massive wall that slows enemy projectiles and bullets passing through it [26:31]
- Kinetic Carbine charges a powerful shot that deals extra damage and briefly freezes the enemy in time [26:31]
- Paradoxical Swap fires a projectile at an enemy hero; if it connects, Paradox and the enemy swap positions [26:31]

### Pocket
- Mobile assassin and disruptor who gets into the enemy backline, deals burst damage and returns to his team before they can kill him [27:32]
- Can be pretty difficult to learn, but the abilities give many ways to move around and avoid damage [27:32]
- Barrage launches projectiles that damage and slow; every hit on an enemy hero increases Pocket's damage, so land as many as possible [27:32]
- Flying Cloak sends a cloak forward that damages enemies it passes; he can teleport to it to dive in or escape [28:02]
- Enchanter's Satchel hides him inside his suitcase, disappearing from the fight, then he bursts out damaging nearby enemies [28:02]
- Affliction applies a strong damage-over-time effect to nearby enemies; it cannot kill, but drains them very low for Pocket or teammates to finish [28:02]

### Seven
- Spirit damage dealer and area-control hero; very beginner friendly, clears lanes quickly and becomes extremely dangerous in large team fights [29:04]
- Lightning Ball fires a slow ball of electricity that continuously damages enemies around it, good for clearing lanes or forcing enemies out of an area [29:34]
- Static Charge places a charge on an enemy that, after a short delay, damages and stuns them and nearby enemies [29:34]
- Power Surge electrifies his weapon so shots deal extra spirit damage that chains between nearby enemies [29:34]
- Storm Cloud channels a growing electrical storm around him that can cover an enormous area; especially strong in team fights or when the enemy team is grouped [29:34]

### Shiv
- Close-range brawler and assassin who bleeds enemies and gets more dangerous the longer he stays in a fight; dealing damage builds rage, which empowers his abilities [30:34]
- Serrated Knives throws knives that damage and make enemies bleed over time [30:34]
- Slice and Dice dashes forward, damaging anyone in his path [30:34]
- Bloodletting postpones some of the damage he takes, giving him a chance to clear it and survive fights that would normally kill him [31:04]
- Killing Blow charges at an enemy for heavy damage and executes them below a certain health, so he is extremely dangerous to anyone trying to escape a fight [31:04]

### Sinclair
- Damage and utility hero built on misdirection: confuses enemies, is hard to predict, and can steal enemy abilities [31:34]
- Vexing Bolt fires a projectile that grows stronger as it travels and can be redirected mid-flight to curve towards enemies [32:04]
- Spectral Assistant summons an assistant who attacks alongside Sinclair, who can swap places with her while she is active [32:04]
- Rabbit Hex turns an enemy into a rabbit, temporarily preventing most abilities and attacks [32:04]
- Audience Participation copies another hero's ultimate for Sinclair to use [32:04]

### Victor
- Frontline brawler or tank who benefits from taking damage, making him very dangerous in long drawn-out fights; he can come back from the dead [33:05]
- Pain Battery charges whenever he takes damage; when full he releases it as electrical bolts that damage nearby enemies [33:05]
- Jumpstart shocks himself, costing health for increased movement speed and massive health regeneration [33:35]
- Aura of Suffering damages both Victor and nearby enemies continuously, getting stronger the longer it stays active [33:35]
- Shocking Reanimation triggers on lethal damage: instead of dying he reassembles and comes back to life, damaging and stunning nearby enemies [33:35]

### Vindicta
- Long-range marksman who picks off enemies from across the battlefield; especially dangerous when she stays out of reach and attacks from above [34:05]
- Stake tethers nearby enemies to a spot, limiting their movement and making them easier to shoot [34:36]
- Flight takes her into the air for a better view and attacks from above [34:36]
- Crow Familiar sends a crow that damages and bleeds enemies, weakening targets before she finishes them [34:36]
- Assassinate turns her rifle into a long-range sniper that gets stronger the longer she scopes; good for finishing enemies who think they escaped [35:07]

### Viscous
- Support and disruptor who controls enemy positioning and has one of the strongest abilities for saving teammates from danger [35:37]
- Splatter throws a bouncing goo ball that damages enemies and leaves puddles that slow enemies and speed up Viscous and his teammates [36:07]
- The Cube encases Viscous or a teammate in goo, fully protected from damage while rapidly regenerating health [36:07]
- Puddle Punch creates a goo puddle and damages anyone near it [36:07]
- Goo Ball turns him into a giant bouncing ball that damages and stuns enemies he crashes into [36:07]

### Vyper
- Damage dealer or assassin who unloads bullets and poison before enemies can escape; her play revolves around movement, especially sliding, which makes her hard to hit [36:37]
- Screwjab Dagger damages and slows; hitting the same target with multiple daggers makes the damage stronger [37:08]
- Lethal Venom poisons an enemy and explodes after a short delay, dealing more damage the lower the enemy's health [37:08]
- Passive Slither improves sliding: farther, easier turning, even uphill; firing while sliding does not consume ammo [37:08]
- Petrifying Bola damages and slows; a direct hit petrifies the target briefly, so they cannot do anything [37:08]

### Warden
- Initiator and frontline brawler who locks enemies in place and survives in the middle of a fight; weakens, traps and has enough survivability to stay aggressive [38:41]
- Alchemical Flask damages enemies and reduces their movement speed and weapon damage [38:41]
- Willpower gives him a barrier and movement speed, to chase enemies or survive when it gets dangerous [38:41]
- Binding Word curses an enemy who has a short time to get far enough away or be trapped in place [38:41]
- Last Stand, after a brief charge, releases pulses that steal health from enemy heroes and heal him, so he gets harder to deal with the more enemies surround him [39:11]
- Suits players who enjoy chasing enemies, trapping them and being a durable frontliner [39:42]

### Wraith
- Duelist, fast-firing, good at singling out individual targets and overwhelming them quickly; can teleport around the map [39:42]
- Card Trick generates playing cards as she deals weapon damage; thrown cards can add damage, slow, reduce defense or heal her [39:42]
- Project Mind teleports her to a targeted location, to chase someone or escape [40:13]
- Full Auto temporarily increases her fire rate and adds bonus spirit damage to her bullets [40:13]
- Telekinesis lifts an enemy and throws them towards a chosen location, damaging, slowing and briefly restricting their movement abilities [40:13]

### Yamato
- Close-range assassin who closes distance fast, deals big sword burst damage and keeps herself alive once committed [41:14]
- Power Slash gets stronger the longer she charges it [41:14]
- Flying Slash throws a grappling hook that pulls her to an enemy, then strikes and slows them, for closing distance [41:14]
- Crimson Slash damages enemies in front of her and reduces their fire rate; hitting an enemy hero heals her [41:14]
- Shadow Transformation refreshes and greatly speeds up her abilities and gives increased resistance and immunity to negative status effects [41:44]

### Apollo
- Duelist or assassin whose kit rewards timing; he gets much stronger when abilities are used at the exact right moment [42:14]
- Disengaging Sigil creates an explosive sigil, then he flips backward, damaging and slowing nearby enemies [42:45]
- Riposte prepares a counterattack: if an enemy hits him in the right window he blocks the damage and retaliates, stunning them for a follow-up [42:45]
- Flawless Advance lunges forward with his sword; timing it correctly deals significantly more damage [42:45]
- His ultimate (captioned "itani loan") charges a massive long-range slash; enemies caught are trapped in slow motion before taking big delayed damage [43:16]

### Celeste
- Damage dealer and disruptor: long-range spirit damage and control, weakening groups of enemies while staying safe behind her team [43:46]
- Light Eater blasts enemies in front of her; hit enemies take extra spirit damage from her attacks and she gains spirit lifesteal [44:16]
- Dazzling Trick surrounds her in a protective prism; if enemies break it, it explodes, damaging and silencing nearby enemies [44:16]
- Radiant Daggers calls down a beam of light that explodes after a short delay [44:16]
- Shining Wonder launches an orb that damages and slows nearby enemies, then bounces between targets, possibly spreading through the whole group [44:16]

### Graves
- Necromancer about summoning the dead, controlling areas and overwhelming enemies with an undead army; much of the kit makes parts of the map hard for enemies to move through [44:46]
- Jar of the Dead collects energy whenever something dies nearby; thrown, it summons heads that chase and repeatedly attack nearby enemies [45:17]
- Grasping Hands tears open a line of undead hands; enemies who cross it are damaged and briefly immobilized [45:17]
- Essence Theft lets her weapon gradually steal weapon damage and spirit resistance from enemies [45:17]
- Borrowed Decree creates a gravestone that keeps summoning ghouls that chase enemies and objectives and then explode [45:47]

### Rem
- Support and utility hero built around helping and keeping teammates alive and putting groups of enemies to sleep [46:17]
- Pillow Toss deals spirit damage and knocks enemies back [46:17]
- Tagalong jumps onto a teammate's shoulder, healing both of them; he can hop between teammates to heal several [46:17]
- Little Helpers sends his tiny friends around the map, where they can also support teammates and troopers [46:17]
- Nap Time channels at enemies in front of him to put them to sleep, slowing them and preventing movement abilities [46:47]

### Silver
- Close-range brawler and damage dealer who hunts down enemies; unique because she can transform into a werewolf [47:17]
- Slamfire instantly reloads her weapon and gives fast, powerful shots [47:17]
- Boot Kick dashes forward and kicks an enemy away from her [47:47]
- Entangling Bola damages and slows an enemy and prevents most movement abilities [47:47]
- Lycan Curse: dealing damage builds blood lust; when full she becomes a werewolf with more health, movement speed and fire rate [47:47]

### Venator
- Supernatural hunter who locks down enemies and deals big damage, especially good at picking one target, trapping it and killing it before it escapes [48:17]
- Consecrating Grenade explodes, setting enemies on fire and reducing their healing [48:17]
- Gutshot fires a shotgun blast that knocks enemies back; knocked into a wall they take extra damage and are stunned [48:47]
- Snap Trap immobilizes and reveals an enemy who touches it for him to follow up [48:47]
- His ultimate (captioned "Erodominee") loads his crossbow with three very strong stakes; the last deals extra damage and executes low-health enemies [48:47]
