## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream from 6/18/26 (June 2026 patch, Holliday after her barrel velocity change). Four replays are coached: a Bebop (Ascendant), a Celeste duo-queued with a Viscous into Lash and Apollo, a support Kelvin (Oracle lobby) and a melee-build Drifter (Ascendant).
