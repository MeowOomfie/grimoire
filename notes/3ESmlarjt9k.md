## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Venator-only free coaching stream the day after Venator's release, with notes on how to counter him first. He coaches three Venator players: a Phantom player who overfights, an Ascendant player on Lightbringer's build, and a Seeker player, plus an analysis of an Eternus 6 scrim Venator game.
Themes: building pure weapon damage over fire rate, ability order (grenade first), using the 2 to stun into walls, traps, farming as an M1 that still fights mid game, Rejuvenator use, urn and strong side reads, and how to read Lightbringer's build. Side notes on other heroes' builds, Trophy Collector and Street Brawl. Patch: Venator release, February 2026.
