# The BIGGEST Deadlock Update Of All Time
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-02-26
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S3szHWgcc7s

## Covers
A live reaction to the 2025-02-25 major update that replaced the four-lane map with three lanes: soul orb and deny changes, sprint, Sinner's Sacrifice, flex slots, jungle respawns, Walker and mid boss HP, the new underground mid boss and Rejuvenator timer, plus a first walk around the new map.
For any player wanting the history of the three-lane map. Early 2025; the game has changed a lot since.

## Claims
- The map was completely reworked to three lanes and the old mid boss location is gone. [0:00]
- Soul orbs no longer need to be last hit by a player to generate souls, as long as you are nearby, and troopers no longer flash when near death. This makes laning a bit easier. [0:30]
- Soul orb lifetime in the air was reduced significantly, and the deny split changed from 65/35 to 50/50: orbs pop no matter what, but if you don't deny them the enemy gets 50% of the creep. Last hitting and denying matter more, more like Dota, which he likes. [1:02]
- Soul orb radius increased by 10%, horizontal movement of trooper soul orbs reduced, and souls from kills now visibly fly towards you. [1:32]
- Default sprint speed increased from 0.5 to 1.0; sprint is disabled for 4 seconds (was 5) after dealing or taking hero damage and only 0.5 seconds (was 5) after dealing or taking non-hero damage. Sprint was useless before for active players hitting creeps; this makes sprint items like Sprint Boots more valuable, where people used to buy Extra Stamina over and over. [1:32]
- Sinner's Sacrifice no longer grants a random gold statue buff; it now has a looping time window with audio and visual cues where a heavy melee kill gives three gold statue buffs and a light melee kill only one. It already had the highest souls per minute in the game, so a slight nerf makes sense, and the mini-game beats AFK shooting it. [2:34]
- Post-lane trooper bounty was increased to match the previous four-lane global economy. [3:34]
- Flex slots: the first now needs two Guardians (was three, since there are only three in total), the second one Walker (was two); the third still needs all Walkers (now three) and the last a Shrine. [3:34]
- Small neutral camps respawn in 3 minutes (was 4), medium camps in 5 minutes (was 6), neutral soul values were cut 10%, and overall camp count and frequency went up. With six players and fewer lanes, jungling becomes more important. [4:05]
- Side Walker HP went from 5,170 to 7,000 and mid Walker HP from 8,000 to 9,000; side Walkers were too easy to kill and fewer Walkers need to be tankier. [4:36]
- Mid boss HP and HP growth increased by 30%, a good change since it was too easy to kill at any time; he wishes mid boss also hit harder, since with lifesteal it barely does anything. [4:36]
- Outer zipline speed increased 15%, presumably because the lanes are longer. [5:07]
- Breakable soul values reduced by 5%. [5:07]
- All juke spots now have veils, adding a new level of outplays in fights. [5:07]
- The gun power-up's min/max fire rate went from 10/30% to 15/35%, and a bug where it didn't scale ammo to 60% was fixed: a gun buff buff. [5:07]
- A new mid boss Rejuvenator symbol shows three visual states and warns 1 minute before spawn; pro players already typed out the timer, so this makes it easier to get into. [5:38]
- A new large cluster of breakables in the mid boss area first spawns at the same time as mid boss. [6:08]
- Holliday nerfs: Bounce Pad damage from 120 to 90, shorter default Bounce Pad stun, lower Spirit Lasso damage. [6:08]
- Superior Stamina no longer gives 7% fire rate; it was as broken as Extra Stamina. [6:38]
- With less split pushing, heroes like Wraith and McGinnis get inherently weaker; with more jungle camps, strong jungle farmers like Infernus may rise; with three lanes there will be more team fights with more people per lane. [6:38]
- He is glad there are no big balance changes, since balance was pretty good and the new map needs to be played out first; he predicts some heroes will jump in popularity and others get much weaker within a week or two. [7:08]
- The death damage report now shows damage reduced by resistances and shields. [7:40]
- Quick buy: right click a build category header to queue all its items; the default goes back to showing purchase prompts when the item is actually bought. [8:11]
- Ziplines are now curved. [8:11]
- Abandoning before the 5 minute mark now shows a message that the game will not be scored; after 5 minutes the match can be abandoned without penalty but leaving is a loss. [8:41]
- Disjoints now break hook tracking, and Uppercut's movement lockout was changed, balance changes not listed in the balance section. [8:41]
- New map first look: the new middle lane is insanely wide open with lots of space and ziplines on both sides. [9:41]
- The mid boss is now underground with veils on the sides and multiple underground pathways to it; you can slide in and dunk on people, which will make for great rollouts. [10:12]
- Medium camps sit very close to mid lane, and there is a path down to the Rejuvenator right next to your lane, which makes juke spots. [11:13]
- The walk from mid to the side lanes is very long with no good zipline rollout, so ganking costs a lot more time. [12:13]
- The side lanes were redesigned with more cover, which he had been asking for; the side tier three camps are unchanged and the side lanes still have high ground. [12:43]
- A new open area near the side lanes looks like the new bridge buff spawn, which would take more time to reach; the map feels bigger with only three lanes. [13:43]
- There are more tier three camps, including middle tier three camps moved to new places, and more camps and boxes overall; junglers will love it. [15:14]
- You can no longer cheese the Guardian from the cover he wanted to use. [15:44]
- First impression: a good step in the right direction that streamlines the game, makes it less complicated and should bring in new players. [16:14]
