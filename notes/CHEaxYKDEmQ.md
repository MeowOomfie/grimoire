# One side of the map has MORE souls?! - The Farming Bible
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-09-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CHEaxYKDEmQ

## Covers
A farming guide: every soul source and its value (troopers, neutral camps, Sinner's Sacrifice, boxes, buildings), jungle souls per side of the map, efficient farming, map pressure, and souls-per-minute benchmarks.
For any player who wants more souls per minute, especially farming heroes like Seven, Infernus, Haze, Wraith and Lash.
It is a 2024 video: the map, camps and soul values have changed a lot since, so treat the numbers as history.

## Claims
- Soul sources are lane creeps (troopers), jungle creeps, buildings (Guardians, Walkers, Shrines) and boxes. [1:00]
- Troopers, jungle creeps and boxes all scale with game time by a formula; buildings are the only static source and give the same souls whenever you kill them. [1:31]
- At 6 minutes a trooper is worth 82 souls. [1:00]
- Troopers start at 76 base souls and scale by 1 per minute for the first 10 minutes. [2:02]
- At 10 minutes troopers jump to 104 base souls and then scale by 2 per extra minute, so a trooper is worth 104 at 10 minutes and 124 at 20 minutes. [2:02]
- Boxes spawn at 3 minutes, give 45 souls at 3 minutes, and scale by about 2.66 souls per minute (from the speaker's own testing). [2:02]
- Small neutral creeps spawn at 2 minutes, respawn every 4 minutes, start at 44 souls and scale by 0.5 per minute, so 49 souls each at 10 minutes. [2:32]
- Medium neutral creeps spawn at 7 minutes, take 6 minutes to respawn, give 88 base souls plus 1 per minute, so 99 souls each at 10 minutes. [2:32]
- Large neutral creeps give 220 base souls plus 2.64 per minute rounded down, so 246 each at 10 minutes. [3:02]
- The Sinner's Sacrifice spawns at 10 minutes, takes 5 minutes to respawn, and gives 300 souls plus 3 per minute, so 330 souls at 10 minutes. [3:32]
- Denied souls are not shared across the enemy team. [3:32]
- When your team kills a building, the souls (base and deniable) are always shared to the whole team, so everyone gets the same amount. [4:02]
- Guardians give 125 base souls and 150 deniable souls, 275 per person, so a Guardian with nothing denied is worth 1,375 souls to your team. [4:02]
- Walkers give 250 base souls and 500 deniable souls, 750 per person if you get every soul, or 3,750 for the whole team. [4:02]
- Shrines give 500 souls and are not deniable. [4:35]
- Base Guardians give no souls when taken. [4:35]
- The jungle is mirrored, and one side has much more jungle farm than the other: at 10 minutes the side with the basement jungle camp has 5,210 souls and the side without it has 3,978, a gap of about 1,200 souls. [5:06]
- The side-to-side percentages stay the same as the game goes on even though the totals scale with game time. [5:06]
- One idea for the soul gap is to be more aggressive and steal more of the enemy jungle on the side that has more souls early. [6:08]
- Know what you need before you farm the jungle: for Wraith that may be Tesla Bullets, for Haze the same, or a Ricochet rush may be the point you start jungling. [6:38]
- There is no definite answer for when or whether to go to the jungle; some heroes like Lash just want to farm jungle camps as they move around the map. [7:09]
- Always farm things on your way: when moving between lanes, shove a lane and then farm the nearby jungle camps. [7:09]
- If there are no jungle camps and no lanes to farm, farm boxes; there are many on the rooftops and a lot underneath near the shop area in the tunnel. [7:41]
- There are no official jungle paths; the most efficient path is the one that takes you to the most lanes and other objectives. [7:41]
- Usually prioritize farming lanes first because waves give more souls on average than jungle camps. [8:11]
- Some heroes clear the jungle so fast they are better off jungling than farming waves, for example Seven, who can clear two to three jungle camps in the time it takes one wave to spawn. [8:11]
- Waves are temporary: if you do not kill them your own troopers will and you get no value, so someone should always be farming the creeps when possible. [8:41]
- Kill jungle camps as fast as possible to get the respawn timer started. [8:41]
- Map pressure means having creeps or farming on their side of the map, or generally better map control; the standard way to get it is pushing waves. [9:11]
- Pushing waves toward their Walkers forces their heroes to show up on those waves or lose the Walkers, which tells you where they are so you can play elsewhere or take fights with a numbers advantage. [9:42]
- If they have to deal with waves you do not have to show on waves, so you can farm the jungle without them knowing where you are. [9:42]
- It is more efficient to farm their jungle than your own when you can afford to, since your jungle will always be there and theirs is only reachable when you have an advantage. [10:14]
- After you win a fight, a great play is often to steal their jungle and objectives: take their towers and especially their highest-value camps, the large (tier three) camps and the Sinner's Sacrifices. [10:14]
- Do not just AFK jungle on your side; one hero can sometimes, but if everybody does it you will probably lose. [10:45]
- You get ahead by stealing souls from their side and their jungle and doing map pressure in general; if you are behind, farming your own jungle may be wise depending on the situation. [11:15]
- Getting better at farming is a map awareness issue, not a mechanical one: after clearing a wave, always be thinking where to go next and what you can farm right now. [11:45]
- Players on farming heroes like Seven, Infernus or Haze should know their timings and exactly when they can start farming large creep camps. [11:45]
- Reviewing your games with a critical eye is the best tool to get better at farming. [12:15]
- Under 1,000 souls per minute in a game means you are definitely underfarmed. [12:15]
- The longer the game, the more souls per minute you should have: in games over 40 minutes a farming hero should have over 1,500 souls per minute. [12:15]
- In a shorter game, 1,000 souls per minute is a decent benchmark, and you want to aim higher. [12:46]
- In the first 10 minutes, 800 to 1,000 souls per minute is doing fantastic. [12:46]
- From 10 to 20 minutes, around 1,500 souls per minute is doing really well. [12:46]
- After 20 minutes, aim for 1,500 to 2,000 souls per minute depending on game state; these are numbers to strive for, not passing numbers. [12:46]
- Always deal with a nearby creep wave, since its souls disappear if you do not kill it then, it usually gives more than one jungle camp, and it is much easier to kill. [13:17]
- For heroes that cannot jungle effectively, creep waves are always the main source of souls. [13:48]
- On a farming hero, consider starting your jungle on the side of the map that has more souls. [13:48]
- You need map pressure to farm safely: push waves, get information, and press your advantage by farming their jungle when ahead. [13:48]
- Always be doing something: after clearing a wave, check the minimap for the next thing to clear, a jungle camp, boxes or an objective. [14:18]
- Do not underestimate boxes; many players do not break them, and quite a few boxes add up to a good amount of souls. [14:48]
