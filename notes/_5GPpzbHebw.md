## Covers

Patch of November 2024.

## Claims
- Reactive Barrier is missing from almost every community build, so guide followers may never buy it. [0:30]
- Reactive Barrier: tier 2, 1,250 souls, automatically gives a big bullet and spirit shield when you are stunned, chained, slept, immobilized or movement locked. [0:30]
- Vindicta has the only movement lock in the game. [1:02]
- When it triggers it gives about 600 health worth of shields for 9 seconds on a 30 second cooldown. [1:02]
- Getting that much shield from Combat Barrier plus Enchanter's Barrier would cost about 2,500 souls and two green slots. [1:02]
- 600 shield lets you survive things you otherwise would not and turn around fights you were too low to take. [1:32]
- Passive stats: 20% ammo, 75 health and 2 regen; it also restores stamina when it triggers, great for backing out. [1:32]
- It is cheap enough to buy early in lane to counter one specific hero, but weigh the rest of the enemy team. [2:04]
- How to read his graphic: buy it against tier 1 heroes, situationally or when several tier 2 heroes are on the enemy team, and not just for tier 3 heroes who rarely trigger it. [2:34]
- Against Bebop it is especially punishing: both Hook and Uppercut trigger it, so his combo struggles to deal damage. [2:34]
- Against Haze: only Sleep Dagger triggers it, but landing a dagger only to face 600 extra HP feels useless for a pick hero. [3:05]
- Against Mo & Krill, buy it early: it blocks the ult damage and much of the teammates' combo damage during the stun. [3:36]
- Against Abrams it triggers on both Shoulder Charge and Seismic Impact stuns, giving you a longer window to parry. [3:36]
- Against a lineup with no natural trigger it does nothing; the enemy might buy Knockdown or Curse, which trigger it, but your souls are better spent elsewhere. [4:07]
