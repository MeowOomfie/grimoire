# How to Build & Play Venator | Ascendant Venator Coaching
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-02-07

## Covers
Venator coaching from Midknight: one Ascendant Venator player (around A2 to A4) is reviewed over a full game.
Mostly itemization for an M1 hero with three weapon damage scalers, plus how much to farm, AP order, map awareness, strong side reads, Urn running and over chasing.
Ends with the builds he recommends: Lightbringer's build for Ascendant, Osis's build for Phantom and below. Patch of early February 2026.

## Claims
Venator builds and items:
- Lightbringer's Venator build is the one he recommends most, for Ascendant players. [0:30]
- For Phantom and below he prefers left-to-right builds, because a Lightbringer build only works if you understand the hero and the items on a complex level; he recommends Osis's build. [0:30]
- Lightbringer builds: buy the listed items and aim for 4.8k spent in every category, tier twos on top and tier threes on the bottom; you can greed a little extra (you will probably also get Ballistic Enchantment), then go into late game. [34:18]
- On Osis's build, skip Rapid Recharge and buy Superior Duration instead. [34:48]
- On Osis's build you 100% need Lucky Shot; buy it either before or after Vampiric Burst. [34:48]
- Monster Rounds gives a lot of value in lane but you don't need it on this hero. [0:30]
- Venator wants a ton of weapon damage because he has three weapon damage scalers in his abilities. [2:01]
- One, maybe two fire rate items in lane is fine, like Active Reload and maybe Kinetic Dash; late game fire rate should come from Glass Cannon stacks. [2:01]
- Fire rate gives no value to his ult, nade or 2, so going full fire rate (Kinetic Dash, Active Reload, Swift Striker, Rapid Rounds) is wrong on this hero. [7:39]
- Instead of more fire rate he prefers Slowing Bullets and other weapon damage items. [8:10]
- Burst Fire was the first major mistake: it gives no value to his ult, 2 or 1, so a damage item is much better. [17:58]
- Recharging Rush is crazy on this hero. [10:46]
- Echo Shard, Battle Vest, Bullet Lifesteal and Hollow Point are all fine buys; Hollow Point is good. [12:47]
- Warp Stone or Counterspell is fine, but don't buy both; the player was a little too green. [22:39]
- Lucky Shot is great on Venator because it procs on the nade and on the ult, but buy Glass Cannon first, then Lucky Shot and Vampiric Burst. [22:39]
- You need Glass Cannon on this hero, so having Lucky Shot with no Glass Cannon feels really bad. [29:42]
- When behind in lane and staying, buy defensive items like Restorative Shot or Extra Regen instead of walking to shop while healthy. [4:04]

Venator AP order:
- He prefers maxing the 1 (nade) into ult over maxing ult first, with a point in the 3 (trap) at some point down the line. [9:14]
- Ult max first is okay but the ult cooldown is about 125 seconds, so you get two minutes of downtime where you just farm and only use ult around objectives. [9:14]
- If you go ult max and something happens while the ult is down, you are kind of cosmetic in the fight. [33:15]
- A 25 damage upgrade on a 26 second cooldown is about 1 DPS and barely matters; 12 seconds off trap is a much more valuable early impact point. [9:45]
- Two points into the 2 is not bad, but not before nade max, because nade max lets you buy Recharging Rush and then items like Ballistic Enchantment. [20:36]
- His order: two points in nade, maybe a point in trap, max nade, a point in trap if not taken yet, max ult, two points in the 2, max trap, then max the 2. [32:14]
- The nade spike (explosion and burn) is strong; no flat damage, but hitting one more person is huge. [32:45]

Venator play and tech:
- Venator has a stronger mid game, so play him more like Infernus than Wraith, but he is still an M1 who farms, then shoots and kills. [10:46]
- Shiv right-click tech works on Venator: jump, press 2, then instantly wall bounce to fly backwards; it could have dodged Lash's ult. [6:38]
- Using your 2 for basic movement is good muscle memory when escaping something like Lash's ult. [6:38]
- When Lash dives you, walk into him so that after his Ground Strike you can immediately get him into your trap and do a ton of damage. [4:04]
- When you see Lash running at you, double jump and wall jump to get in the air and dodge his Ground Strike; that is your only way to live. [3:03]
- When cutting back while someone chases you, you can likely stun him and then ult to guarantee a full ult shot (maybe not a headshot, since he can move before the animation finishes). [15:18]
- Kicking a trap into a veil or door as you walk through it would give a lot of value. [23:09]
- Placing a trap on a veil is a great idea; enemies can shoot the traps but breaking one makes a sound, so you still know someone is coming that way. [24:10]
- Kiting back to your trap was good. [23:09]
- If your ult aim feels bad, take about 0.3 seconds to line up the shot instead of rushing every shot. [23:39]
- As an M1 you need to spend time farming, especially while your ult is down and especially on an ult max build. [16:22]
- As an M1 you don't fish for fights with teammates who want to brawl; your fights are over objectives, otherwise you farm or box farm. [21:06]
- The only time an M1 can force a fight like that is when the enemy has a lot of camps up and your team has none. [22:09]
- Limit testing is a great philosophy, but limit test around objectives and farm otherwise; testing at 70% of the hero's capacity is an inefficient feedback loop. [31:44]

Lane and early game corrections:
- With boxes not up yet, hard shove the wave. [1:01]
- Get on top of bridge or near the stairs to immediately shoot minions as they come; the height is for hitting the wave, not for poking. [2:32]
- Shoving gives you a ton of prio when boxes spawn, so go steal the enemy boxes, do a minor box route, then walk back toward your side and buy. [1:31]
- If there is kill potential on your laner you can greed for it. [1:31]
- Prioritize hitting the wave before looking for fights. [3:34]
- Don't take an aggressive trade when you are close to a tier two item spike and the enemy is about 1,600 spent souls ahead; even a neutral trade is bad. [3:34]
- 24 damage on a hero is worth less than half a minion's HP in the same time; focus the wave. [5:36]
- When far behind, slow play: give them prio, box route, farm for your ult and hold tower with traps; don't walk past bridge while Lash ult is up because you have no siege potential. [5:06]
- Use the mid skip: drop off the zipline, diagonal air dash, double jump and land on top; it saves about 4 to 5 seconds and is easy to learn. [4:36]

Reading enemies:
- If an enemy plays greedy, assume she is close to her ult and doesn't want the charge to drain; respect her more. [6:08]
- An enemy Silver near ult is diving to fill her meter, so assume she is one hit from going into fury form. [7:09]
- When enemies go under your tower they will always parry, not melee. [6:38]
- A Haze engaging on you can be assumed alone, because she likes to look for picks. [14:18]
- Don't one-v-one a Lash who is 5k souls ahead; play behind your wave so you have information on how many enemies are there. [19:00]
- Fighting in front of your wave means you have no information on enemy numbers. [19:00]
- Don't over chase; he died chasing a Haze. [33:47]

Urn, strong side and farming:
- Urn route: from up high, jump off diagonally or dash, wall jump, double jump if needed and drop onto it; down dash at the end onto the cash and they can't stop you cashing. [11:17]
- Strong side is the side of the map where you or they have most players. [12:47]
- If you run Urn and the enemy isn't taking space on the left side, assume their strong side is the same side as the Urn, otherwise they would push your left walker. [12:17]
- 40% of the Urn souls come out as denies and are stealable, so watch the map and fish for those denies. [16:22]
- Pay attention to the Urn so you don't get ninja cashed, and take fights around Urn and objectives rather than general ones. [33:15]
- Funneling farm into an anchoring teammate is fine, but funneling it into you as the M1 feels better. [21:38]
- If no camps are up and you need farm, box max. [21:38]
- When you have rejuv, farming your own camps is the wrong time; use the first half to fix waves, run Urn and farm their camps, the second half to siege. [26:11]
- Steal the enemy Sinner's Sacrifice that just came up instead of giving it to them. [26:11]
- Enemy camps are worth double because you deny them; with rejuv they won't contest you stealing them. [27:42]
- Carrying a big bag from your own deep camp into a fight costs extra: if you die they steal it, about 1k value lost. [26:41]
- Waves always give more souls than camps, so shoving an incoming wave beats farming your own tier three camp. [28:42]
- Invade the enemy jungle in a pack of three on the strong side, not solo, and call it with a pick hero or a fed teammate even if nobody comes, to build the habit. [28:12]
- Healing Tempo procs off McGinnis's healing. [28:12]

Map awareness and defensives:
- There is no cheat for map awareness; you play games focusing on it. His drill video says "map" every 5 seconds, in the Drills and Exercises playlist on his channel. [16:54]
- He could have used Counterspell on the enemy Focus Lens; using Warp Stone to get out of her ult was good. [30:43]
