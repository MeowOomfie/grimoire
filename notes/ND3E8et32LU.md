# Nurse Harrow Is Actually Broken
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-10-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ND3E8et32LU

## Covers
Nurse Harrow, the new full-support healer, aquatiq channel.
Commentary over his first ranked game on her (with a lot of off-topic talk): kit, a gun-heal build, item swaps, how to play her in fights.
Video dated 2026-10-22.

## Claims
- Nurse Harrow is a full support healer; there is nothing in between. [3:04]
- She can switch to a syringe that loads healing needles; shoot them at allies (his Graves) to give them health. [3:04]
- The healing needles seem to last as long as you keep hitting your ally. [5:09]
- Her 2 injects yourself or a teammate with speed. [4:05]
- Leeches latch onto someone, and you then pick them up for a stacking bonus of healing, fire rate and regen (as captioned "healing fire and regen"). [4:36]
- Leech pickups also give you health. [5:39]
- You have to go pick the leeches up; you cannot stick them in yourself. [5:09]
- Her gun is weak early ("this gun does no damage"). [1:00]
- Against heavy spirit damage, consider Spirit Shielding over Extra Regen. [2:32]
- His build is gun heal, which builds into her 3; he went all green (vitality) items early. [4:05]
- Avoid taking minion damage on her. [4:36]
- Don't stand up front with your frontliner; stay back and inject from behind. [6:09]
- As a healer, stay with your team rather than off alone. [7:13]
- She can fear enemies who come close, and enemies won't want to fight into it. [7:50]
- He used his ult to fear and zone enemies off the Rift so his team could take it for free. [12:57]
- She is bad at farming: no clear ability like Infernus's flame dash, so go for boxes or sit mid lane. [18:04]
- At 6k souls: a cooldown item imbued on the ult, Toxic Bullets instead of Opening Rounds, then duration. [21:37]
- Toxic Bullets is genuinely good on her. [22:40]
- Stay back and heal your teammates; that is her job. [22:40]
- She builds up many needle charges, so Rapid Recharge is worth considering. [23:11]
- Buy Spirit Resilience when taking spirit damage. [25:50]
- He called her ult very strong after it feared enemies who then died. [26:20]
- Late game her gun felt strong. [27:20]
- "Gun Harrow is the way": some gun plus Greater Expansion on the ult is very strong. [30:25]
- Her ult transforms her. [30:25]
- Result of his first game on her: about 18,000 to 20,000 healing. [30:25]
