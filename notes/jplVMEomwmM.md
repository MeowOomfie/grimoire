# I Tried YOUR Deadlock Builds for a Week – Here’s What Happened
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-10-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jplVMEomwmM

## Covers
Metro plays viewer-submitted off-meta builds (melee knife Haze, melee Seven, long range kite Drifter, "ethical" gun Infernus) and rates each for fun, cohesion and viability.
Mostly gameplay reaction; the useful parts are why each build fails and what the standard build does instead. For players tempted by gimmick builds.

## Claims
- Melee knife Haze build: Metro would run Melee Charge instead of Swift Striker in it. [0:33]
- Melee knife Haze build: it takes early points in the knife ability without maxing Fixation; a melee build should max Fixation or the knife, and putting a point into the ult seems random. [1:35]
- Melee knife Haze build: to make punching work you need earlier punch (melee) items, and Swift Striker barely gets used. [2:41]
- Melee knife Haze rating: fun 5/5, cohesion 3/5, viability 2/5. [3:47]
- Melee knife Haze fix: drop Cultist, get a very early Surge of Power and Enduring Speed later; the build is about the knife, not Spirit Snatch, so Spirit Snatch can come later. [3:47]
- Melee knife Haze fix: skip Mystic Reach or Mystic Burst until later (when you get Tankbuster) to reach Surge of Power faster, which is more of a damage increase. [4:17]
- Melee knife Haze leaves you very exposed: if enemies get close with your cooldowns down you die. That is why Silence Wave, or at least Slowing Hex, is meta on Haze, so melee heroes like Abrams can't catch you. [4:17]
- The melee Haze build is a one trick: knife, Echo Shard, knife again, and either they are dead or you are. [4:47]
- Normal Haze is safer: Surge of Power is a super solid item, and you Sleep Dart, Silence Wave, then ult from longer distance instead of having to melee. [4:47]
- Melee Seven ("online at 40k"): there is a reason you don't see melee Seven; only Initiate players or trolls play it. [5:47]
- Melee Seven has no farming items, so you have no way to get money. [6:55]
- Melee Seven does a bit more early game damage than Metro's usual Seven. [6:55]
- Melee Seven rating: fun 1/5, cohesion 4/5 (it is what you would run if you wanted melee Seven), viability 0/5, because it is the worst hero with the worst build. [7:59]
- Max kite long range Drifter: leveling Bloodscent first is useless in lane because there are two enemies there and it gives no extra damage. [8:30]
- Long range Drifter makes no sense: Stalk puts you in melee range, and Drifter wants to be close, yet the items only make him strong at long range. [9:01]
- Against a long range Drifter the enemy can just hide behind corners and he can't hit them. [10:02]
- Drifter has two abilities, one that needs close range and one that teleports you into close range, and his gun stops dealing full damage after about 20 meters, so you must fight in a small range. [11:02]
- Long range kite Drifter rating: fun 3/5, coherence 2/5 (starting Bloodscent at level 1 is useless in lane), viability 0/5, the worst build of the day for viability. [11:02]
- "Ethical" gun Infernus: the most normal build of the day, with very early Rapid Recharge, maxing the dash first then the 1. [12:04]
- "Ethical" gun Infernus: Long Range only gives 4% falloff; if you want long range, run Sharpshooter. [12:34]
- "Ethical" gun Infernus: the build has no purple items, so it does little burn damage; if you run Rapid Recharge early you need more spirit investment. [12:34]
- On Infernus you can use the dash to farm. [13:41]
- One of Infernus's main strengths is that he can fight anyone at any point; this build only works once you have enough farm, and Metro started fighting way too early for it. [14:12]
- Maxing the ult before the 3 on Infernus is not acceptable. [14:42]
- The ethical Infernus build is a raw gun build that would suit Ivy; Infernus's gun damage is just not great anymore. [15:13]
