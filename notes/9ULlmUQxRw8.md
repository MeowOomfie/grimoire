# This Deadlock Patch Makes No Sense
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-03-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9ULlmUQxRw8

## Covers
Critical breakdown of the balance patch released around 2026-03-21: vitality back to percentage HP, ability range nerfs, item tweaks (Enchanter's Emblem, Metal Skin, Golden Goose Egg) and hero changes, with winners and losers.
For players who want a blunt tier read: Abrams, Apollo, Celeste, Shiv and Silver buffed, Victor gutted, still a spirit meta and no matchmaking changes.

## Claims
- There are no matchmaking changes, which the coach thinks is the biggest problem with the game right now. [0:00]
- Vitality investment no longer gives base HP but percentage HP again, so the tankier a hero is at base the more they gain from green investment; this already buffs tanks. [0:00]
- Spirit investment scaling changed slightly: nothing changes at the 4.8k investment, a bit less for two levels after, then more; the coach thinks spirit strength is unchanged. [0:30]
- Small (tier 1 lane) neutrals are more resistant, which mostly matters for Mina and Apollo, who can no longer one-shot tier 1 camps; it will not matter much. [1:01]
- Ability range is nerfed across the board on range items; Greater Expansion only lost 5% and is still crazy because it gives 10% base spirit resist. Expect less spirit spam that hits the whole screen, so aim abilities more precisely. [1:32]
- Burst Fire tiny buff (still a terrible item), Weighted Shots tiny buff. [2:02]
- Enchanter's Emblem now gives 18% spirit resist, the most significant item buff, on par with Battle Vest for buying against spirit. [2:02]
- Vestal Locket, already a crazy item, got even better. [2:32]
- Metal Skin no longer gives 15% base bullet resist but its invulnerability is the same; not significant. Counterspell tiny nerf, still Thanos tier. [2:32]
- Golden Goose Egg now takes 2 seconds to hatch, killing the 30-minute strategy of buying and selling eggs in base when out of flex slots; good change. Boundless Spirit nerfed by 5. [2:32]
- Abrams is back to "double buff Thanos tier": more melee damage, more Siphon Life range, more Infernal Resilience tankiness and a better ult, on top of the vitality change; probably back to must pick or must ban. [3:33]
- Apollo got buffed though he did not need it: more bullet damage, more resist and stun duration on his parry ability. [4:04]
- Calico nerfed, the first good hero change: the bonus souls now need a kill within 3 seconds instead of 4 and give only 200; Leaping Slash and ult nerfs, slower and higher cooldown. [4:35]
- Celeste buffed though she did not need it: three small nerfs (more bullet damage taken, two fewer bounces on max Shining Wonder, 1 m less range), while bullet damage gets +4 base and better scaling and Light Eater, Dazzling Trick and Radiant Daggers are buffed (Radiant Daggers quite insane). [5:07]
- Doorman: Call Bell better in several ways, Doorway range reduced so he cannot pull the Walker on you as much; deadlier in fights, a small adjustment he probably needed. [5:37]
- Dynamo small buffs: more Singularity damage at tier 3, more Kinetic Pulse range, more ammo. [6:07]
- Graves big buff: max Grasping Hands now spawns two ghouls without the ult, a crazy damage increase; Jar of Dead lasts longer, more spirit scaling and health per boon; ult better at base and slightly worse maxed; easier to pull multiple targets at tier 3. Graves might be pretty good now. [6:38]
- Infernus: the ult build gets even more lifesteal, Flame Dash scales above max move speed, faster reload; good buffs. [7:09]
- Ivy overall nerf: scaling reduced on Stone Form and Air Drop weaker; still a great hero. [7:39]
- Kelvin buffed: Frost Grenade scaling down 0.1 but bigger radius and easier to hit, Ice Path buffed with more spirit power maxed, ult buffed; overall stronger late game. [8:09]
- McGinnis hard nerf to Heavy Barrage, which was too strong, compensated with buffs to her gun and other abilities; overall a heavy nerf. [8:40]
- Mina buffed: longer umbrella float that can be toggled by zooming for the full four seconds; bug fixes make it slightly harder to kill creep camps for healing; Nox Nostra hit fixes with DPS reduced; about the same strength. [9:11]
- Mirage buffed: Fire Scarabs become a passive damage-reduction debuff (20%, 35% at tier 3) without the damage amp; Dust Devil can be recast longer; Djinn's Mark slight nerfs; Traveler teleports after two seconds, is not cancelled by non-hero or damage-over-time sources, has lower cooldown and can target units. He is now a show up and kill you hero. [9:42]
- Mo & Krill: Combo nerfed in 1v1s with less damage but on a lower cooldown; still a guaranteed kill when you combo with teammates. [10:44]
- Shiv buffed: stamina from 2 to 3 (massive), Bloodletting no longer interrupted by sliding, and Killing Blow now travels through enemies until it reaches one it can execute, so it can hit all six enemies to reach a low target behind them. [11:15]
- Silver buffed though she is must pick or must ban in pro play: Lycan's Curse quality of life, more HP, resist, move speed, a kill credit buffer, more Boot Kick movement, and range items now work on Go for the Throat, Boot Kick and Mauling Leap. [12:16]
- Victor got the hardest nerf of the patch: built-in anti-heal against him, so any lifesteal he buys is reduced 30%, while his kit relies on Leech, Infuser and early Spirit Lifesteal; the coach calls him unplayable. Shocking Reanimation has a bigger stun radius, so a suicide ult Victor may be a thing. [13:17]
- Viscous: Puddle Punch and Goo Ball less deadly; no more chain stuns from Goo Ball, less Puddle Punch air time, worse scaling and 50 ms longer windup so it is easier to parry; may make him bad again. [14:18]
- Wraith: Card Trick heals less on multiple targets; teleport longer base cooldown but more reduction maxed (consider maxing it earlier); Telekinesis has a much longer cast time, so it can be Counterspelled and dodged, a shorter debuff by 0.5 s, much less range and more cooldown. Really big nerfs. [15:19]
- Venator, Warden and Lady Geist were untouched though Venator and Lady Geist were already the two best gun and hybrid carries; with tanks buffed by vitality, Venator, Geist and Warden are all better. [16:21]
- Verdict: a really bad patch; Apollo, Celeste, Abrams and Shiv got undeserved buffs, and after weeks of Night Shift with no gun items bought, gun was barely touched, so it stays a spirit meta. [16:51]
