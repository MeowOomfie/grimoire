# Best Heroes For Roaming | Deadlock Tier List
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-04-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hA-3BxoVzv8

## Covers
Part one of Midknight's laning series: how to judge a good roam during the laning phase (wave position, HP, ults, when to leave) and a tier list of every hero's roaming and ganking potential.
Tiers used: needs to roam, good roam, if needed, roam with abilities, doesn't like to but can. For players who don't roam enough in lane.

## Claims
- One of the biggest mistakes he sees is people not roaming enough during the laning phase. [0:00]
- There is no "can't roam" tier: roaming is very strong, and even the worst roamer should gank when the opportunity presents itself. [0:00]
- A good roam: get wave priority, then do a box route that ends on top of the bridges between mid, then look at the lane next to you. [0:00]
- Roam check 1, wave position: if your team has shoved the wave into the enemies it is much harder to gank them; if the enemies are shoved into your team it is much easier. [0:30]
- Roam check 2, HP: even if your team is shoved in, if your teammates are both 1 HP it's very hard to roam; if your teammates are shoved in and the enemies are very low, you can gank and dive. [0:30]
- Roam check 3, later in lane: who has ultimates up; forcing a gank is much easier when your teammates have ultimates, and heroes like Paradox roam off their ult cooldown. [0:30]
- Don't stay past two waves without asking whoever is anchoring your lane if they're okay; staying longer often gets your ally dove on the third wave. [1:00]
- After using the surprise of the gank, leave unless the enemies are 1 HP under tower or you can really force kills; staying and splitting the wave with your teammates is griefing them. [1:00]
- If your lane partner dies a lot or asks you not to roam, ask them to buy Monster Rounds; it makes anchoring significantly easier. [1:00]
- Look for roamers with a CC tool that can set up a kill. [1:30]
- Abrams: good roam; a charge from behind the enemies can easily net a kill. [1:30]
- Apollo: not a bad roam, worse than Abrams since his 2 (siren) is much harder to hit, but his high damage forces kills without CC. [1:30]
- Bebop: good roam; his hook sets up kills, and it is also very good in lane, so he can force plays even when the wave is shoved into the enemies. [1:30]
- Billy: if needed; his ult gives some roaming potential, but he's more often in the anchor position. [2:01]
- Calico: solid roam, below Apollo for raw early damage; mobile on rotation with decent ganking damage. [2:01]
- Celeste: if needed; very mobile, but no tools to set up allies and less burst than Calico. [2:32]
- The Doorman: first put in good roam for his door, which lets him gank on a much tighter timer, then dropped to if needed. [2:32]
- Drifter: if needed; likes hitting stacks with his 3 and his 2 gives mobility, but no tool to set up teammates. [2:32]
- Dynamo: if needed; ganks much better with ult, but before that he isn't very mobile and his kicks are easily dodged; the ult's long cooldown is worth spending if it guarantees a kill or two. [2:32]
- Graves: doesn't like to roam; a better anchor with her ult; good players mostly get hit by her root only while actively fighting her, and a ganked enemy will just back off. [3:04]
- Grey Talon: own tier (roam with abilities); he doesn't like to roam on foot but loves ganking with his bird, especially from mid lane to finish kills. [3:04]
- Haze: very good roam; sleep dagger and invis catch enemies off guard, but if ahead she can also harass under tower and take it early. [3:34]
- Holliday: needs to roam; bounce pads make her mobile, she loves ganking with lasso, and even before that it feels very good to roam once she has tier two barrel. [3:34]
- Infernus: doesn't like to roam; no CC to offer and he does better with farm than true roamers. [4:04]
- Ivy: solid roam, but only off her ult; don't roam on her before ult. [4:04]
- Kelvin: if needed; a support with a decent gank, and his dome can force kills. [4:04]
- Lady Geist: doesn't like to roam; arguably the best anchor in the game, she prefers to sit in the side lane and farm. [4:35]
- Lash: if needed; more solid with ult and coming off height, but his early burst isn't high. [4:35]
- McGinnis: doesn't like to roam; too immobile, though her wall is useful for ganks. [4:35]
- Mina: if needed, like Apollo and Calico; mobile with burst but without Apollo's kill potential right now. [4:35]
- Mirage: roam with abilities; doesn't like to roam but his ult is valuable for roaming. [5:05]
- Mo & Krill: not a good roamer until ult, then strong; bumped to needs to roam once ult is online. [5:05]
- Paige: likes to gank but is middling; she won't guarantee her root until she has knock down; deceptively a pretty good anchor. [5:05]
- Paradox: same as Mo & Krill; needs to roam once ult is online. Holliday can roam earlier than both. [5:35]
- Pocket: bottom of if needed; good gank impact once Affliction is online. [5:35]
- Rem: 100% needs to roam; learn tunnel box routes that end in ganks, have your lane partner buy Monster Rounds, and you will win every game. [5:35]
- Seven: doesn't like to roam; relatively immobile, his stun is okay for ganks, but he'd rather farm his lane. [5:35]
- Shiv: if needed, but really doesn't like to. [6:05]
- Silver: good roam (top of if needed); her bola gives gank potential and with ult she has extremely good dive potential. [6:05]
- Sinclair: if needed before ult; a good roam once ult is online, especially into lanes with good ganking ults, like a Dynamo lane after 3.6k souls. [6:05]
- Venator: doesn't like to but can; his 2 is decent for roaming and his ult can take someone from 100 to zero, but he'd prefer to farm and scale. [6:38]
- Victor: no CC and not mobile enough to roam; if needed he can force a dive with ult. [6:38]
- Vindicta: roam with abilities; good Vindictas who know snipe angles and look for cross-map snipes are very valuable. [6:38]
- Viscous: if needed; decent damage and some mobility, nothing crazy. [6:38]
- Vyper: really good roam, arguably needs to roam if built for it (Superior Stamina early); with the average solo queue build she is a good roam. [7:09]
- Warden: doesn't like to; good for roaming and forcing towers down with ult once it's online, but he wants to protect his own tower first. [7:09]
- The tier list assumes roaming before your own tower dies; once your tower is dead it is much easier to move around the map. [7:39]
- Wraith: doesn't like to but can; every M1 hero lands here because they prefer farming to roaming. [7:39]
- Pair an M1 who wants farm with a hero who loves to roam: the M1 scales and the roamer gets early impact. [7:39]
- Yamato: good roam; lots of kill potential like Apollo, and her 2 is much better for roaming as a cross-map teleport grab (and it gives 40 spirit power). [7:39]
