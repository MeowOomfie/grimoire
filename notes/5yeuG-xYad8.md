## Covers
A beginner glossary walked through the map from base to lane: Shrine, Patron, base guardians, fast lane, zipline boost, boxes and golden statues, Walkers, haunted souls, camp tiers, bridge buffs, Sinner's Sacrifice, Guardian, shops, Rift, troopers, wave priority, vents, veils, ganks, teleporters, Mid Boss, Rejuvenator, Urn, split pushing and the farm spots (doubles, purple pool, Chinatown bell tower).
For brand new players who don't know what the common terms mean.

## Claims
- Souls: the currency you collect from many sources across the map. [0:00]
- Shrine: very important objective, especially the first one, because destroying it buffs your troopers and gives you more souls for the rest of the game. [0:00]
- Patron: the main objective; lose it and you lose the game. It has two phases and two health bars. [0:30]
- When the enemy damages your Patron into its next phase, your respawn timer is cut by about 20 to 30 seconds, giving you another chance to defend. [0:30]
- Patron second phase: it changes form and you can stand in the pit below it; if enough people stand in it, the Patron can't take damage, letting you fight back. [0:30]
- Base guardians: not a great objective, few souls; taking one makes that lane a fast lane, where you move faster than normal on its zipline. [1:00]
- Zipline boost: a key (he thinks the parry or quick melee key) that speeds you up on the zipline; first unlocks at 5:10, then goes on a 6 minute timer after each use, so use it sparingly, ideally for fast rotations. [1:00]
- Golden statues: give permanent buffs that stack through the game and help you scale; kill them by shooting, meleeing or sliding into them. [1:31]
- Regular boxes: all over the map, have a chance to give souls. [1:31]
- Light melee is a tap of the melee button, heavy melee is holding it. [2:01]
- Heavy boxes: must be broken with a heavy melee, give double the souls of a regular box and are guaranteed to give souls. [2:01]
- Walker: you start every game with three item slots locked, unlocked by killing the three enemy Walkers, so kill all three to open every slot. [2:32]
- Each Walker gets harder after you kill one; the first is the easiest. [3:03]
- Standing near your own Walker gives you 15% all resists, so fight under it when defending. [3:03]
- Tier 2 camp: level two enemies in many variations that give souls, including haunted souls. [3:03]
- Haunted souls: if you die while holding them, you drop them (shown as a bag on your hip) and enemies can steal them; they become unhaunted only by waiting or by spending them. [3:33]
- Purchases use haunted souls last: with 900 souls buying an 800 item, you still hold 100 unsecured haunted souls. [4:03]
- Camp tiers on the map: one triangle is a tier 1 camp, spawning at 2 minutes; triangle with one bar is tier 2, spawning at 5 minutes; triangle with two bars is tier 3, the most souls and hardest, spawning at 8 minutes. [4:03]
- Bridge buffs: buffs that spawn every 5 minutes and make you stronger for a while. [4:34]
- Sinner's Sacrifice (boxes with a dot on the map): heavy melee it until all its lights go out, then time a final heavy melee as the last light fills. [4:34]
- A Sinner's Sacrifice deals 80 damage to you on every heavy melee except the last one. [4:34]
- There are red and orange Sinner's Sacrifices with different timings; on the red ones start charging the final heavy melee early (shown on screen). [5:04]
- Red (golden) Sinner's Sacrifices give permanent buffs such as 4% weapon damage, 5% max ammo and ability range; they spawn at 8 minutes, so kill them as soon as possible through the game. [5:04]
- On regular Sinner's Sacrifices, start charging your heavy melee at the third light for the permanent buffs and souls. [5:35]
- Guardian: the least important objective, but while it is alive you can shop in lane; once it is lost you must use the base shop or the mid shop. [5:35]
- Mid shop: one on each side, yours in green lane and the enemy's in yellow lane. [6:06]
- Rift: a king of the hill objective that spawns at different times; stand in it, and whoever is in the area last wins once they reach 100% capture. [6:06]
- Standing in the Rift blocks the other team from capturing until someone leaves; if it goes on too long it ends automatically and shoots the souls into the air. [6:06]
- Winning the Rift gives Rift soldiers, much stronger minions that push lanes for priority and can kill a guardian on their own. [6:36]
- Regular trooper wave: four troopers, a melee one, ranged ones and a healing one. [6:36]
- You can parry troopers to stun them, but you lose your parry. [6:36]
- A killed trooper drops a soul you must shoot to secure; if you are too far away it drops a soul orb on the ground. [7:06]
- The healing trooper drops a green orb that heals you over time when you walk up to it. [7:06]
- Wave priority: killing a wave or two keeps your wave pushing into the enemy; if they don't stop it you snowball and can roam, switch lanes or farm without running back. [7:06]
- Every wave spawns every 30 seconds. [7:37]
- If you feel you must run back to lane every two seconds or lose a guardian or Walker, it is because you don't have wave priority. [8:07]
- Bounce vents (jump pads) in both lanes knock you up toward mid shop and the bridge buffs; heavy melee the bridge buff to take it. [8:07]
- Shooting someone on the zipline knocks them off and stuns them briefly, so arrive first and shoot enemies down to defend the bridge buff. [8:37]
- Vents (the bushes near mid shop): standing in them makes you completely invisible and heals you a bit faster; enemies must walk in or shoot you to see you, so they are easy spots to ambush. [8:37]
- Veils: inside one no one can see you but you can see them; use them to escape, juke, or bait enemies in for a free kill, for example by setting up your abilities inside. [9:07]
- Gank: leaving your lane to sneak into another and jump the enemies there for a kill and an objective. [9:38]
- Teleporters in both shops: stand in one and after a short time it moves you exactly across the map. [9:38]
- Mid Boss: found down the stairs in mid; at half health it makes a sound across the map that tells everyone. [10:09]
- Killing the Mid Boss drops a crystal; heavy punch it for a Rejuvenator, which you can hit up to three times for three buffs. [10:41]
- Rejuvenator buff: if you die once you are revived in the exact spot you died; a gold bar under your name means you have it, and when it is gone you actually die. [10:41]
- Urn: quick melee it to pick it up on your back; it makes you a bit faster and gives more stamina; run it across the map to the drop-off marked on the map. [11:11]
- You don't have to rush the Urn, but an enemy quick melee or any stun knocks it off you, so avoid getting stunned. [11:11]
- Delivering the Urn gives a soul injection based on how many souls it held; enemies can shoot the dropped souls and steal them, so collect them fast. [11:42]
- Split pushing: push one lane while teammates push another, to distract enemies, split their attention and fight them spread out. [11:42]
- Doubles (in Theater): two Sinner's Sacrifices and a minion; the enemy's doubles is directly diagonal across the map. [12:13]
- Purple pool: has minions, Sinner's Sacrifices, heavy boxes and more, an extremely good soul spot, but you lose stamina inside so you can be caught and killed easily. [12:43]
- Chinatown bell tower (grapple up, diagonal across from purple pool): three Sinner's Sacrifices, the most in the game, but hitting anything rings a bell that tells the whole map you are there, so be careful of ganks. [13:14]
