## Covers
Warden gameplay commentary by aquatiq, a Warden one-trick, May 2026 patch.
Mostly a played game with item reasoning along the way: Battle Vest, Slowing Bullets over Slowing Hex, Titanic Magazine, Mystic Shot, Mercurial Magnum and Fleetfoot, plus thoughts on Mina and Paradox.

## Claims
- He has been one-tricking Warden because the hero is really strong right now. [1:34]
- Mina is great for getting kills and objective damage but falls flat late game and in team fights except for her ult; his win rate on her is low. [1:34]
- Warden can do a lot of damage late with his gun, pick people out with his cage and use his ult in fights. [2:05]
- He usually buys Crippling Headshot late on Warden to reduce enemy resist (he says spirit resist). [2:05]
- Paradox is weaker than people think but not weak enough to ignore. [3:07]
- If a Paradox cannot reach you in lane, keep shooting her in the head; she just eats it. [3:38]
- Against a lane dealing gun damage he picks up Battle Vest. [5:41]
- Against this gun-heavy lane Battle Vest alone is not enough; he adds Titanic Magazine and Slowing Bullets. [8:19]
- Slowing Bullets or Slowing Hex is needed on Warden regardless; he prefers Slowing Bullets because it keeps applying to multiple people while shooting, while Slowing Hex only hits the one target you click. [9:00]
- Mystic Shot did a lot of damage to him, so he suspects Mystic Shot may be the way on Warden. [11:37]
- He goes Titanic Magazine and then Bullet Lifesteal for sustain. [13:09]
- When the enemy is far from your side of the map, take advantage and farm the camps deep on their side. [14:11]
- Warden always feels slow, even with Enduring Speed. [14:41]
- When the enemy sends everyone to one lane, push a different lane on your own. [16:13]
- Carrying the Urn to base is fine, but do not die with it, because the enemy has Rejuvenators. [19:16]
- He buys Fleetfoot to fix Warden's lack of speed. [19:46]
- Get Mercurial Magnum before buying luxury items like Blood Tribute; he also considers Cultist. [23:36]
- With two Rejuvenators and two minutes left, fight, and back off once you start losing people. [25:08]
- When your team is dying in a fight, go steal the enemy's farm instead, but avoid getting trapped where you cannot get out easily. [25:39]
- Mercurial Magnum makes your bullets deal bonus spirit damage that charges up over time; he thinks Spiritual Overflow may be good on Warden too. [28:10]
- A Seven with Plated Armor was barely damaged by him. [28:10]
