channel: aquatiq

## Covers
Vindicta, aquatiq, commentary over one game (joking that it is Team Fortress 2) using a build from a top Vindicta player, with Paige, Billy, Lash and Haze against Bebop, Grey Talon, Viscous and Abrams.
Covers lane survival against Bebop, item timings (Veil Walker, Counterspell, Burst Fire, Blood Tribute, Spiritual Overflow) and when to stop sniping and start shooting.
No coaching. Recorded 2026-08-08.

## Claims
- Remember Bebop can hook you and instantly kill you in lane. [3:34]
- Get your heal before the trade in lane. [3:34]
- Lane with Bebop and Grey Talon is a hard poke lane; they can easily overrun you. [4:06]
- Healing Rite is a must, since one hook does about 400 damage and kills you. [4:39]
- Veil Walker as the second item. [7:12]
- With three enemies greeding a spot, leave and take the vending machines instead. [8:14]
- The build used is from a player the speaker calls the number one Vindicta in the game. [9:18]
- Buy Counterspell and remember you have it, to dodge things like a Viscous ult, Wraith or a Bebop hook if you react in time. [11:53]
- After Blood Tribute, stop sniping between fights and just shoot people to death; only finish them off with Assassinate. [15:02]
- Late, Vindicta does most of her damage with her gun. [15:33]
- Max Assassinate. [18:13]
- Burst Fire is a big spike: once he has it the speaker says the fight is over for them. [19:19]
- Spirit Shielding saved him under heavy fire. [19:19]
- Vindicta is always poor; Spiritual Overflow is needed early. [21:21]
- Against a team with a lot of spirit damage, get Spirit Lifesteel and probably Spirit Resilience. [21:21]
- At 4.8k souls, get another gun item and healing; Blood Tribute is good healing, and after it you just shoot people. [23:29]
- Vampiric Burst lets you do a huge amount of damage. [25:01]
- An enemy team without waves pushing has a hard time contesting your objective. [25:01]
- Vindicta can shoot enemies in their base and kill them. [26:33]
