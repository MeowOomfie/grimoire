# Why You're NOT GETTING SOULS in Deadlock | Eternus 6 Coaching
channel: Natan
date: 2026-02-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X_6oZZK-RJg

## Covers
An Eternus 6 viewer VOD review of an Abrams (laning with Seven into Haze and Dynamo) who fought and died on repeat early. For players who fight too much and fall behind on souls.
Trooper angles, first item, Sinner's Sacrifice over Guardians, the Urn when behind, secret shops over base, Abrams build and skill order notes, and closing with the Mid Boss instead of the Patron.

## Claims
- Abrams and Seven into Haze and Dynamo is a pretty even lane, maybe a small disadvantage: Abrams is an easy target for Haze's Fixation stacks and Dynamo can dodge Seven's stun. [0:00]
- When taking out a trooper, find an angle where you can see the trooper but the enemy laners cannot see you, to avoid unnecessary damage. [0:32]
- Laning rule: whenever both enemy laners are looking at and shooting you, dip instantly and find cover. [0:32]
- If you didn't buy after the first wave, push the wave and go buy right after. [1:05]
- For a first item on Abrams, buy Close Quarters (Point Blank) first to shove the wave and get more poke; at full HP, Extra Regen isn't necessary. [1:05]
- The first ability point in Abrams's 3 is bait; there's no real reason to ever go that. [1:36]
- Back off as soon as the enemy pops their heal; you won't kill them anymore. [2:07]
- Don't take extra damage from the Guardian for no reason. [2:37]
- If your lane partner won't get the wave, push it quickly instead of chasing a kill you won't get, and take the heal creep when low. [2:37]
- If a teammate comes into a lane where you can't help them, call them back. [2:37]
- With bridge buffs in 30 seconds, clear the wave and then go for one. [3:38]
- Don't walk up too far as Abrams without Shoulder Charge available. [3:38]
- Losing 1,300 health instead of at most about 500 was an angles issue: shoot troopers from where you can see them but they and the enemy can't hit you. [4:10]
- As Abrams, don't Shoulder Charge instantly from range; dash jump in first to close the distance on the Haze, then charge her into your team. [4:44]
- At 6 minutes you can go for a gank elsewhere if your other lane is winning hard; going mid is fine if there's no other angle. [4:44]
- When players group up in mid or one lane and it becomes a 4v4 or 3v4 trying to force a Guardian, be the one who pings pull back and goes somewhere else; it loses the team souls. [5:18]
- At 8 minutes Sinner's Sacrifice spawns: get a wave pushed, then rotate to the enemy Sinner's Sacrifice. [5:48]
- When two of you are on a half HP Haze, commit; stun her into the wall and kill her instead of backing off. [6:19]
- Dying six times in the first 10 minutes and sitting under 7k souls at 10 minutes is the result of running in, fighting and dying on repeat. [7:39]
- Taking all the Sinner's Sacrifice on the map would have been far more valuable than the Guardian; they give stats and souls. [7:39]
- Pushing a wave and then taking the enemy Sinner's Sacrifice is more valuable than a Guardian, which will die at some point anyway; if you do take the Guardian, leave right after and farm. [8:10]
- On Abrams, the item the captions call "cultist" isn't the play anymore; go Point Blank or Hollow Point. [8:10]
- Keep track of soul counts and numbers; if you're at a numbers disadvantage and behind, don't go in. [8:41]
- 1k souls in 2 minutes (500 per minute) is too little; when you're already behind you need souls, and flipping more fights won't get them. [8:41]
- Abrams is an active hero who likes fighting, but you can overdo it; the biggest fix was to stop fighting as much. [9:11]
- The Urn now goes to whoever picks it up about 90% of the time; be the first to pick it up and run to the point to deliver it instantly, since it's very hard to knock off you. [9:41]
- When the Urn is on your side, your team is behind (here 15%), so definitely prioritize the Urn. [10:12]
- At full HP, don't go back to base to shop; go to a secret shop instead, for example one on the way to the Urn. Backing to base wasted about 30 seconds. [10:43]
- An active brawler hero like Abrams should be prioritizing Sinner's Sacrifice. [10:43]
- On Abrams, go Healing Booster too. [11:13]
- Abrams skill order: max Siphon Life before Shoulder Charge; without melee items there's no point putting two or three points in Shoulder Charge, and two points in Infernal Resilience doesn't seem worth it. [11:13]
- When a new Urn spawns on your side, prioritize it as a team; the Urn shows on the map. [11:44]
- When teammates show on the map elsewhere and the enemies have a 5v4 while you're behind, it's a bad fight; back off. [13:49]
- After picking up the Urn at a numbers disadvantage, back off, deliver the Urn and take the respawned Sinner's Sacrifice. [13:49]
- Don't use your zipline boost when nothing is happening on the map. [14:19]
- Slowing Hex is a good Abrams purchase. [14:50]
- After pushing a wave while your whole team is near your base, don't go for a tier two camp up the map; you're overextended. Steal a nearby Sinner's Sacrifice or go back to yours, with the Urn as first priority. [15:51]
- Don't chase a fleeing enemy when the Urn is right there; grab the Urn. [16:53]
- As Abrams, don't keep going for melees on a gun build with no melee items, especially short-range ones; just shoot and don't get baited by melees. [19:29]
- After winning a fight and getting three kills, call Mid Boss. [19:29]
- When souls are even and no Walkers are left, you generally can't take the base instantly; you need a bigger soul lead. Keep your waves shoved and take all the jungle that's up to build a proper lead. [19:59]
- After taking the first phase Patron, back off: take the Urn, the buffs and all the farm on the map, and end when the next Mid Boss spawns. [21:31]
- When you know you have a numbers advantage, it's a good fight, but stay aware of your base. [22:32]
- Abrams mistake: input buffer your melee when you Shoulder Charge someone; hold the melee button during the charge so you punch instantly and don't get parried. [23:32]
- When the Mid Boss is about to spawn, take Mid Boss rather than the first phase Patron; hitting the Patron shortens their respawn timers so you can't do Mid Boss afterwards. [24:03]
- Trying to force the end instead of taking Mid Boss together is the only way to lose a game in that position. [24:36]
- When Dynamo is ulting and very low, shoot the Dynamo instead of chasing someone else; a few shots kill him. [24:36]
- Keep track of the Mid Boss respawn timer. [25:07]
- When your base is under threat, the most important thing is to back off, wait for teammates to respawn and defend; a lost Walker doesn't matter as long as the base holds. [26:09]
- Abrams pretty much needs Warp Stone or Phantom Strike: Shoulder Charge is a gap closer that's easy to react to, and these make it hard to see coming. [26:40]
- Warp Stone and Phantom Strike are both good against Haze: Warp Stone gives bullet resist and Phantom Strike disarms. [26:40]
- Lower rank games are all winnable because people don't know how to use the Mid Boss to close out; play a stable game, not a volatile one. [27:40]
- Stable game: take all the Sinner's Sacrifice on the map, play for objectives including the Urn, and understand when you can actually end. [28:10]
- Communicate with your team before fights, for example "I'm not ready yet, I'm still buying". [28:42]
- A late even team fight flipping the game doesn't mean you lacked impact; you set yourself up for the late game much earlier. [29:14]
