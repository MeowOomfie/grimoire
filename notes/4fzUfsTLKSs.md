# You're Over Committing | Eternus Holiday Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-01-06
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4fzUfsTLKSs

## Covers
Midknight reviews an Eternus Holliday player's game on stream (coaching), in early January 2026 just before the big patch everyone expected.
Themes: respecting towers and minions on an aggressive laner, not committing to trades before the tier 2 Powder Keg spike, the sunk cost fallacy after a missed Spirit Lasso, waiting out a teleporter before lassoing, playing for side-lane picks instead of coin-flip walker fights, his Holliday build (gun spike, Kinetic Dash versus stamina, Veil Walker) and Crackshot versus Bounce Pad skill order.

## Claims
Holliday build, skills and lane:
- As Holliday you don't want to commit to trades into any hero before your tier 2 Powder Keg upgrade; before it you just poke and whittle the enemy down. [0:30]
- Once you hit the tier 2 barrel spike and get your extra charge, you go all in. [0:30]
- During your Bounce Pad it is better to double jump than to dash. [2:00]
- Always melee or jump before you throw the barrel. [3:00]
- Don't barrel in people's faces; it also gives them the chance to parry. [4:01]
- Throwing the barrel from the ground came up three times in the game and is worth fixing. [13:17]
- Midknight's own build goes gun spike first with Mystic Shot; if you are using his build here, go Mystic Shot rather than Kinetic Dash. [11:44]
- If you pivot into stamina (Extra Stamina) instead, you still need to buy Veil Walker. Buying both is inefficient. [11:44]
- In his build Kinetic Dash is not bought for the active; it is bought because it is an upgrade of Extra Stamina that fills the gun spike criteria. Otherwise Mystic Shot gives more damage. [12:16]
- Veil Walker plus Bounce Pad gives you the movement you need. Veil Walker gives slightly less movement than stamina but much more playability around your lasso. [12:46]
- The stamina option is viable but inefficient; it is subjective, so build it if you like it. [13:47]
- Veil Walker is OP mid to late game: you secure kills off the invis and catch people who would have run if they saw you. [16:24]
- The two items the player had just bought give Holliday a lot of damage. He would go a tier 1 cooldown item into Veil Walker, though you don't strictly need it when you have a fed teammate to play around. [17:01]
- If you can aim, put two points into Crackshot before maxing Bounce Pad. Midknight maxes Bounce Pad first only because he can't aim. [43:43]
- Crackshot's fading slow is great for chasing, but only if you can hit it. [44:14]
- Bounce Pad damage at 3% of the total is too low. Maxed Bounce Pad adds a lot of damage in a combo and people don't think about it. [43:13]
- Melee damage was higher than it should be, but you never got parried, so it cost nothing. [42:43]
- Your movement looked a lot better: more air strafing and better wall bouncing. [44:44]

Mistakes and fixes in this game:
- When backing out of tower range, hold S (walk backwards) rather than tanking it. [1:30]
- You are not respecting tower much. On Holliday you don't need to fear tower, but don't tank it for fun. [3:00]
- Don't stand close to your Shiv so Mo & Krill's Scorn hits two of you. Once he has used Scorn, you can go in his face. [3:00]
- Going in front of the enemy to create space for your Shiv is good, but once Lady Geist lands her 2 on you after your Shiv goes in, you need to leave the fight. [4:01]
- Don't force Spirit Lasso on Lady Geist: she can swap out of it. Only go for her if you have kill potential before the lasso ends. [5:34]
- Respect Mo & Krill coming online if he kills your Shiv. [6:04]
- With no stamina and no Bounce Pad you should still run: drop down, wall bounce off an edge and get behind cover. 300 to 400 HP is enough to get away. [6:04]
- Lady Geist and Billy are the best 1v1 heroes right now. [7:06]
- Let a teammate's bad fight go: it is better for him to die than for you to take 60% of your HP bar for free. [7:37]
- If you are invading that camp, it is better to just farm the Sinner's Sacrifice and leave. [8:07]
- Kill those two leftover minions, because they slow down your next wave pushes. [8:37]
- You shouldn't commit to the Seven under his tower. You were only 200 souls up on him and won only because he was sitting on half his net worth; leave sooner. [9:11]
- Never path toward the ground against a Lash, because he can ground strike you; path toward a vent. [9:41]
- Getting shot used to slow you by 20% but that was removed. If you drop to the ground, a chasing Lash at the roof edge can ground strike you for far more than one burst in your back. [10:42]
- The catch was fine but the chase was bad: they were at their walker, you had no backup and no idea whether they had backup. With your lasso up you could go for it; without it, don't. [14:49]
- Forcing the walker after the kills is acceptable because your Shiv called it and following a call is better than ignoring it, though counter-calling would have been better there. [15:20]
- Without lasso you need to leave; re-engaging is only excusable with lasso up. [15:53]
- The risk/reward of looking for that barrel was not worth it. [16:24]
- Your team should catch and hold mid. Your Drifter, not you, should be catching the wave; the fed player should not be the one rotating out, someone behind should. [17:32]
- When you are slightly ahead, your goal is to run the neutral urn. [19:39]
- When an enemy is channeling a teleporter, don't lead with your lasso. Wait until about one second is left on the teleport, then lasso him. [20:09]
- Standing in his face while he channels has no downside: he can't go anywhere and your teammates catch up. [21:10]
- Waiting also lets you line up the lasso so it doesn't catch on the edge of the teleporter. [20:39]
- If you hook early and drag him to your Wraith, the lasso expires around when you arrive and Mo & Krill can Sand Blast, Burrow away and Scorn to heal. [24:45]
- Waiting removes his chance to live, keeps your Wraith from burning her cooldowns, lines up the lasso and stalls him longer for your team. There are four upsides and no downside. [26:46]
- Without lasso, just hit the walker instead of chasing. [21:43]
- With no kill advantage and bad prio in both side lanes, don't burger-flip the walker. Reset, fix your waves, look for a side-lane pick with your lasso (the side where they have the walker), then go mid. [22:13]
- Three seconds on the walker can be 15% of its HP, which beats the best case of finding one guy and throwing one barrel. [22:43]
- Your team got punished for the full walker collapse. After it you should go mid. [27:48]
- Shuffle over to catch the wave sooner and leave the Sinner's Sacrifice to your teammates. [29:52]
- Watch for proxy waves: the Mina was proxy pushing and you should have seen it sooner, though in a fight that is forgivable. [31:22]
- Run the urn off a pick. [31:22]
- Trading the walker for the urn is the correct play. [32:24]
- Into Lash and Seven you have little siege potential. After the lasso you tunnel-vision chased; you should let her get out. [33:26]
- With no objective to farm, dying there was not a big deal. [33:56]
- When stealing, ask a teammate to walk up with you so you can lasso-kill anyone who pulls up. [34:30]
- That fight without your Drifter was worse than a coin flip. [35:02]
- After mid boss, splitting between going mid and knocking Patron is a great split. [36:03]
- When you see Victor in base, run the urn. Against Seven, Lash ult and Refresher defending, don't brute force: push waves, run urn, then look for a pick with lasso. [36:34]
- You were pushed too far up with two bad waves. Fix waves and run urn first, or they get wave pressure, pressure your base, knock Patron and run urn. [37:35]
- Brute forcing there risked around a 35% chance of losing the fight, while the waves-and-urn plan gave them nothing. [38:06]
- Posturing up when looking for a pick is good, but don't front line. [39:09]
- Respect the Lash: his ult and Refresher are the only way they win, so back off when the ult is about to come up. [40:12]
- The sunk cost fallacy is huge in this game: using your ult does not mean you have to commit to the fight. Appeal to authority (trusting the 20 and 0 teammate's calls) is common too. [40:42]
- Sometimes you have to let an over-aggressive teammate be over-aggressive. [41:12]
- Your top source of damage taken was creeps and towers, which shows overextending. [42:12]
- After you aggro a tower it stays aggroed for about 3 to 4 extra meters, so backpedal or put cover between you and it. [42:12]
- Summary: respect towers more, avoid the sunk cost chase after a missed lasso, stop posturing too aggressively, stop coin-flipping walkers, and play for side-lane picks with lasso before a fight. [45:14]

Hero notes from the stream:
- Shiv's Bloodletting is about 42% damage deferral plus a cleanse, which is not balanceable. [2:00]
- Healbane is good into Shiv because he builds a lot of healing, though he believes it does not affect Bloodletting itself (untested). [4:32]
- Against Shiv, avoid letting him build rage. [5:03]
- If you are full HP and a teammate is low, an ability with i-frames (like Dynamo's 2) lets you dodge a heal pickup so they get it. [2:30]
- Wraith's lane is too strong for her scaling; she should have a point in the game where she is clearly bad. [19:07]
