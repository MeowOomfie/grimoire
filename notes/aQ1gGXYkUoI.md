# I Don't Think The Warden Nerfs Did Anything
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-09-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQ1gGXYkUoI

## Covers
Warden in a ranked game on aquatiq, commentary over gameplay with a patch reaction.
Gives a verdict on the Warden, Celeste and Veil Walker nerfs and notes Abrams and Silver buffs, plus a few item and macro calls.
No one is coached. Patch released the day of recording, around 2026-09-20.

## Claims
- The patch nerfed Warden's items, Mercurial Magnum and Spiritual Overflow. [1:34]
- The -1 stamina on Warden's Alchemical Flask was not nerfed and is still busted, and his cage still silences, so he is still insane. [2:06]
- Expect people to build Warden's ultimate more again after this patch. [2:06]
- Celeste was hit hard and will be much weaker, no longer triple S tier; the earlier Celeste nerfs did nothing to her kit. [2:36]
- Celeste was a problem because she had everything: movement, damage and sustain. [3:06]
- With Celeste weaker, expect more Mina; as Warden, Mina is easy to kill: silence her with the cage, throw a Flask, she cannot move and dies. [2:36]
- Silver was buffed enough that it should actually matter. [3:36]
- Abrams's Infernal Resilience was buffed slightly; one of its tiers gets nerfed or buffed nearly every patch. [6:41]
- He is running the Lightbringer build with a change: Veil Walker is out, replaced by any of the 3k items. [8:45]
- Fortitude is a replacement for Veil Walker because Fortitude is just better now. [9:15]
- Veil Walker nerfs: it no longer builds from Sprint Boost, the movement speed bonus is removed when the invisibility ends, spirit power went from 10 to 6, and the +2 sprint and +2 out of combat regen are gone. [10:51]
- Verdict on Veil Walker: the nerfs are significant but not enough to never use it; it is still a situational buy depending on the game. [11:56]
- Warden nerfs: Willpower nerfed, the Flask nerfed, and his bullet damage nerfed again. [12:27]
- Verdict on Warden: bullet damage is not what makes him busted, tier one of the Flask is, so he is not as nerfed as people will think. [12:27]
- His bullet damage per boon was nerfed, but he still does a lot of damage. [15:02]
- Spiritual Overflow as the next item after Mercurial Magnum, and go back to base when you can afford Fortitude. [16:37]
- Siphon Bullets is getting bought this game. [19:48]
- Against an all spirit enemy team he weighs Witchmail and buys Inhibitor next. [24:28]
- Refresher on Celeste is good in late game team fights if you can afford it. [23:27]
- When the enemy will get the Rift regardless, skip it, take a walker instead and push both waves to make them bad. [21:21]
- He took two walkers back to back while the enemy was at the Rift. [21:52]
- For mid boss, sacrificing a lane is worth it: one player handles the side and the rest go mid to contest. [27:37]
- On the base push, shoot the left shrine together. [29:09]
- Letting two walkers die to one player within about 2 minutes is where the enemy lost the game. [31:15]
