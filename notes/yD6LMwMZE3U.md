# Rushing Juggernaut In Lane Against Eternus Players
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2025-10-16  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD6LMwMZE3U

## Covers
Billy rushing Juggernaut as the first item in lane, why 6K item base stats are weak early, Sprint Boots and Enduring Speed into Juggernaut, skipping Enchanter's, Life Strike, health thresholds, Plated Armor, fire rate reduction against gun heroes and towers, lane against Mo & Krill and Paradox

## Claims
- Early game items are designed to be useful early, but 6K items have weak base stats for early and mid game; their power is the special effect (Ricochet hitting several targets), and their stats can be matched by cheap items (a 40% stat by Extended Magazine, 30% fire rate by Swift Striker). [0:00]
- Juggernaut is the exception: a 6K upgrade of Enduring Speed with slow resist, 8 health regen (a stat not very useful late but wanted early), and a 40% fire rate reduction on enemies that shoot you, so rushed in lane, enemies should not be able to kill you early. [1:02]
- Build: his normal Billy build with Juggernaut moved to the first item, starting with Sprint Boots and Enduring Speed since both build into it; the expected cost is having no damage early. [1:32]
- He planned to skip the melee item Life Strike and drop Enchanter's, since everything else is delayed and he needs damage after spending 6K on an item that only heals. [2:02]
- He builds around health thresholds: at 22% extra health he added Life Strike back for 32% bonus health early, and because it gives damage after Spirit Snatch. [2:33]
- First game result: he did no damage but had huge regen; his team was fed, so he did not count it as a good test, called it "not good" and requeued wanting a good lane start. [3:40]
- Second game, laning into Mo & Krill: while saving for Juggernaut he was technically behind in money, holding 2K unspent. [5:19]
- Juggernaut's fire rate reduction also works on towers. [6:22]
- He died close to a kill to normal bullets, but healed from the sinners and ended with more HP than he started (plus 200 HP). [6:22]
- Facing many gun heroes made the fire rate reduction strong; gun heroes shooting him could not kill him because their fire rate was too low. [6:52]
- Next he wanted Escalating before Plated Armor, because Plated Armor is diminishing and he wanted base resist first; after Plated Armor he called himself the tankiest Billy. [8:36]
- With Plated Armor he took fights the enemy could not finish and said he literally could not die. [10:44]
- His verdict was "Juggernaut first" is OP, though he joked that the video cuts there to pretend it is good and only Twitch knows how it really went. [11:52]
