# Everything You Need To Know About Laning In Deadlock
channel: Midknight
date: 2024-11-29
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cz3vBILDWMI

## Covers
General laning guide: wave priority, trooper damage and positioning, the ping-pong wave cycle for boxes, camps and roams, the 5 and 8 minute timings, double soaking, when to fight, and freezing (how, why, risks and how to break it).
Midknight, fast-paced guide drawn from coaching, no one coached on screen. Patch of late November 2024.

## Claims
- Push your first wave as hard as you can to get wave priority as soon as possible. [0:00]
- With wave priority your minions shoot the enemy laner too, a big advantage in fights; if the enemy backs off, look for denies. [0:00]
- This is why in high level games both players go to one side of the wave and push as hard as they can. [0:30]
- If the opponent has priority and is killing your minions, do not always go for melee secures; a good player with priority will shoot you, and that damage is worth more than the soul orb. [0:30]
- Before 2 minutes laning is simple: get priority, poke the enemy, deny every soul you can. [0:30]
- At 2 minutes the first small camp spawns on one side; optimal play is to shove, get priority, then farm the small camp while the opponent pushes the wave back. [1:00]
- If you do not time it at exactly 2 minutes, shove the wave and grab the small camp when you notice it has spawned. [1:00]
- The biggest laning mistake is positioning: shoot minions from behind cover to avoid enemy poke, and stand behind your own wave to avoid trooper damage. [1:00]
- If you are below Oracle, check a replay at 8 minutes and look at trooper damage taken in lane; you will be surprised how much it is. People do not respect troopers enough. [1:30]
- Laning is ping-pong: once your wave is pushed onto their side it naturally pushes back to you, unless they freeze. [2:01]
- If you take a camp while the waves meet in the middle, you miss four minions or the whole wave; if you shove first, the wave stacks and pushes back and you miss only one or two minions. [2:01]
- The time after shoving the wave in is your timer for boxes, camps or roaming. [2:32]
- Side objectives: box routes, the small camp next to you, and the medium camp from 6 minutes. [2:32]
- Killing a wave gives the most souls, so never do a box route or camp if it costs you a wave; losing a wave for a small or medium camp loses souls and deprives your team of farm. [3:02]
- In a duo lane, tell your partner your plan (boxes, camp, roam); if your duo comes back to reshove while the wave pushes in, stop the camp and join them so you do not miss souls. [3:34]
- Make roams count, since you usually will not get back in time for your wave and must ask someone to cover the lane or take tower damage. [4:05]
- Best time to roam: when enemies in the next lane have priority and are pushed deep, so you can get there fast, try to kill, and come back. [4:05]
- If your team has shoved both lanes next to you, there is no point roaming; the enemies will hug their tower. [4:05]
- Know when to call a roam dead: if the enemy immediately drops back, do not overstay, and do not stay hitting a duo lane's minions after a failed roam; that steals your team's souls while your own laner sieges your tower. [4:36]
- Look at the top of the screen for low enemy health; low, pushed-in enemies in a nearby lane are a great time to roam. [5:07]
- At 5 minutes boosts come up: enemies get back to lane faster after dying and can boost to another lane to roam. [5:07]
- After a kill, ping them missing (in base with boost up) so your team knows. [5:07]
- After 5 minutes, do not shove the wave too hard after a kill, because the enemy can boost back and trade you, nullifying your lead. [5:38]
- Roaming to help a solo lane that is safe (for example an Ivy throwing Kudzu), helping shove an extra wave before 8 minutes duplicates souls rather than leeching. [5:38]
- Double soaking (one player shoving waves in two lanes back and forth) works now, but Valve has pushed back against it before, so do not expect it to stay strong. [6:08]
- After 8 minutes souls are no longer split between two people in a duo lane (only about 20% extra instead of 100%), so start farming camps, roaming and doing other things. [6:08]
- Fight when your wave has more minions, and do not fight when the opponent has more minions and their wave is pushing you, because troopers do a lot of damage. [6:39]
- Freezing: when the wave is on your side, only shoot minions when they are flashing (last hits only), unless you have more minions; the wave stays where it is. [7:09]
- A freeze is easier to hold if the opponent's wave has one more trooper than yours; if it grows to two more, kill one and keep the one trooper advantage. [7:09]
- Freezing is very valuable against Seven or other hyper carries: if Seven shoves and goes to ult the basement or farm camps, freezing means he loses the wave instead of getting the camps and the wave. [7:39]
- Freeze after killing the enemy laner early when respawn timers are too short to push the wave in; last hit only so your troopers take damage and some die before they return. [8:10]
- The first few times you freeze you will miss minions; watch all four minions' health and shoot right before they die, and it becomes powerful with practice. [8:40]
- If you have died a couple of times and keep getting killed, set up a freeze to stay near your Guardian and still get your troopers. [9:10]
- In high level play you can freeze preemptively in a bad matchup, for example Abrams against a good Mirage who tornadoes your dash. [9:10]
- Freezing gives the enemy priority by default, so heroes who roam well like Lash can roam to the duo lanes. [9:40]
- Freezing in duo lanes is very hard in solo queue because you would have to explain it; the speaker keeps it to solo lanes. [9:40]
- If you freeze against Lash, ping as soon as he leaves vision; a successful roam or two can hurt your team. Freezing against Seven, who cannot really roam, is great. [10:11]
- Breaking a freeze is hard in Deadlock: you need the enemy to mess up, or shove so hard that two waves go into their Guardian, or ask a teammate to roam and help shove. [10:41]
- Laning ends at 8 minutes or when you lose your first Guardian; once your Guardian falls, leave, ask a teammate to cover the lane, and gank or jungle instead of staying until 15 or 16 minutes. [11:12]
