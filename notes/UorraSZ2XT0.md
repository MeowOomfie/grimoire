# 1 Lane Tip Against Every Hero
channel: Deathy  date: 2025-10-10  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UorraSZ2XT0

## Covers
One laning tip against each hero: Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Calico, The Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash, McGinnis, Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Seven, Shiv, Sinclair, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous, Viper, Warden, Wraith, Yamato; items Reactive Barrier, Rebuttal, Close Quarters, Extra Regen, Restorative Shot, Healing Rite, Mystic Shot, Headshot Booster, Cold Front, Backstabber, Extra Stamina, Spirit Shielding, Quicksilver Reload; cooldown windows, cover, veils, stamina dodges

## Claims
### Abrams
- Charge is both his greatest strength and greatest weakness in lane: it has an insanely long 37 second cooldown, so when Abrams does not have it you can be aggressive. [0:00]
- Try to bait out his charge and position in ways that make it hard for him to reach you, such as on the bridge. [0:00]

### Bebop
- If Bebop cannot hook you, he cannot win the lane. [0:00]
- Stand behind your troopers: hook does not go through troopers, so it will not reach you. [0:00]
- If you do not feel confident dodging his hook, consider buying Reactive Barrier. [0:30]

### Billy
- Billy is a bit like Abrams: he likes to get in your face for an all-in combo. [0:30]
- Rebuttal is an incredible item against Billy, as most Billys spam light melees between their abilities and do not expect the low parry cooldown from Rebuttal. [0:30]

### Calico
- Spacing is your best ally: if she does not hit you with Leaping Slash, her entire lane kit is very underwhelming. [0:30]
- Always keep a good amount of distance from her, especially on a hero with a decent long range weapon, and avoid close range areas like the veil. [0:30]
- Sometimes, instead of running away, it is best to stay very close so she cannot use Leaping Slash. [1:01]

### The Doorman
- His doorbells are the main issue in lane, but it is very hard for him to judge distance with them. [1:01]
- Play either very close or very far from him, as that makes it a lot harder for him to hit them. [1:01]
- If you stand right in front of the doorbell, or the doorbell has been thrown behind a trooper, he might not be able to hit it. [1:01]

### Drifter
- Avoid getting very close to Drifter, as the bonus damage from his "Rand's" (as captioned) will hurt you a lot. [1:01]
- Stay just outside his gun's effective range: he is the only hero other than Bebop with a limited range on his gun instead of falloff. [1:31]
- If your hero has to get close, consider buying Close Quarters to reduce the melee damage he deals to you for cheap. [1:31]

### Dynamo
- Dynamo loves short trades with his first ability; if you get hit by the shockwave, take cover for a few seconds so he does not benefit from the fire rate slow and increased bullet damage he gains. [1:31]
- Outside of that, Dynamo is generally very weak and has a massive hitbox, so abuse that by constantly poking him. [1:31]

### Grey Talon
- Playing in cover or in the veil is your best friend: it lets you dodge his arrows and minimizes the poke you take while he is in the air. [2:01]
- Grey Talon is squishy, so if he walks up in lane you can dive past the wave to kill him if your hero allows that playstyle. [2:01]

### Haze
- Haze deals the majority of her damage through Fixation. [2:01]
- Avoid prolonged trades with her when you have a lot of Fixation stacks on you; go for short trades and wait the stacks out when you see them stacking up. [2:01]

### Holliday
- Holliday wants to use her barrels and headshot you. [2:01]
- Her barrels are quite difficult to do anything against, but make your movement unpredictable and crouch to make her headshots hard to hit. [2:31]
- She wants to burst you down when she unlocks her ultimate, so consider a defensive barrier such as Reactive Barrier, Spirit Shielding or Bullet Shielding to survive her lasso. [2:31]

### Infernus
- Avoid standing out in the open and do not make it easy for him to set you on fire with his afterburn. [2:31]
- When he uses Flame Dash, abuse height and wall jump stalling to avoid as much of the damage as possible; wall jump stalling was nerfed recently but is still possible with enough stamina. [2:31]

### Ivy
- Ivy is generally a weak laner that can be hard to kill because of Stone Form, but it has a very long 42 second cooldown. [3:01]
- Force her to use Stone Form, then be aggressive for the next 40 seconds. [3:01]
- Her head has a large hitbox, so she is much easier to hit headshots on. [3:01]

### Kelvin
- Kelvin's hitbox is quite big, so he easily takes poke from weapons; Restorative Shot, Mystic Shot or Headshot Booster allow easy poke. [3:01]
- Always stay one dash away from cover so he cannot keep Arctic Beam on you for long, because it has a build-up mechanic and is more effective after a second or two. [3:01]

### Lady Geist
- Avoid trading into Lady Geist while she uses Life Drain on you; hide behind cover immediately, which basically makes the ability useless. [3:32]
- You dodge her bomb best by looking at her throw it rather than at where it lands, which gives you more time to react. [3:32]
- Malice works like Fixation or other amps: with Malice stacks on you, hide and avoid taking another, as a new one refreshes the duration of the previous ones. [3:32]

### Lash
- Your best bet is to position so you cannot get hit by Ground Strike: stand on the opposite side of the high ground, where you can keep clear vision of Lash. [4:03]

### McGinnis
- Her biggest threat in lane is her wall; do not overextend significantly past the wave without a planned escape route if she walls you. [4:03]
- If she does wall you, know how you are going to retreat. [4:33]

### Mina
- Mina bursts you down very easily when you are below half HP, so always buy defensive healing items (Extra Regen, Restorative Shot or Healing Rite) to stay above that threshold. [4:33]
- Flat damage items such as Cold Front, Mystic Shot, Backstabber or Headshot Booster are great against her because she has very low base HP. [4:33]

### Mirage
- Mirage is often considered a lane dominator but is actually quite weak early: his gun DPS is one of the worst in the game and he depends on his scarabs and Djinn's Mark (captioned "Jin's mark") for damage. [4:33]
- Be aggressive when his scarabs are on cooldown and before he hits 2.6k souls, when he becomes quite strong. [5:03]

### Mo & Krill
- Mo & Krill relies on hitting multiple targets with Scorn for sustain, so do not stand in the middle of your troopers. [5:03]
- Poke him from off angles to force him to choose between you and your troopers. [5:03]

### Paige
- Focus on denies against Paige: her bullet velocity is the worst in the game and she struggles to secure souls despite the recent buff. [5:03]
- When she uses her barrier on herself or her ally, shoot the other hero, run away, or both; you will not win that trade. [5:03]

### Paradox
- When Paradox charges her carbine, do not hide behind obvious cover: that is what she wants, and she will use her bomb to get you out of it and then shoot you. [5:34]
- Use veils, or do not let her know your position, so she cannot carbine you in the first place. [5:34]

### Pocket
- Pocket starts with -15% spirit resistance, so focus any spirit damage you have on him during the laning phase. [5:34]
- Mystic Shot, Cold Front and Backstabber are quite effective against him. [5:34]

### Seven
- Seven has a giant head: aim for headshots and buy items like Headshot Booster. [5:34]
- His hat gives him 25% reduction against headshots, but headshots are still a lot of damage. [6:06]
- Do not stand right beside your trooper when his Power Surge is active. [6:06]

### Shiv
- Shiv's knives refresh the previous knife's duration when chained, one of his main ways to poke you in lane. [6:06]
- When hit by a knife, hide until the knife damage has fully stopped to avoid giving him multiple stacks. [6:06]

### Sinclair
- Sinclair's bolt can do hundreds of damage in record time, but you can use your stamina to dodge it at the last second. [6:06]
- Wait until you see or hear Sinclair redirect the bolt, then use your stamina to dodge it. [6:06]

### Victor
- Victor does not do much in lane unless you focus him; focus your damage on his laning partner instead. [6:36]
- Victor wants you to shoot him so he can charge his pain battery. [6:36]

### Vindicta
- Be most careful of the Stake and Flight combo: when your wave starts flying, do not get caught out in the open. [6:36]
- Play around corners to make it hard for her to deal lots of damage with Flight. [6:36]
- When Flight and Stake are on cooldown, play aggressively; you can also abuse veils to hide from her. [6:36]

### Viscous
- If you get good at parrying Puddle Punch, Rebuttal completely shuts down that ability in lane: it reflects damage onto Viscous and also heals you. [6:36]

### Viper
- Like Mina, being at half health against Viper is very problematic. [7:06]
- Take trades with her at range because her weapon falloff and spread are really bad, and buy regen items so you do not fall below half HP. [7:06]
- At half health, play very defensively, as Lethal Venom will just kill you instantly. [7:06]

### Warden
- Always keep two stamina in lane against Warden; an easy way is to buy Extra Stamina, which is really important on heroes that only start with two stamina like Bebop. [7:06]
- If you keep two stamina, you can usually get out of his cage without issue. [7:37]

### Wraith
- Do not stand out in the open too much against Wraith, as she wants to charge her cards on you. [7:37]
- She charges her cards much faster on heroes than on troopers, and they do a surprising amount of damage once she has 2.6k souls and an item like Quicksilver Reload. [7:37]

### Yamato
- Avoid close range trades against Yamato, as she will always win them with Crimson Slash; poke her at range instead. [7:37]
- Use your abilities on her while she is charging Power Slash; she is very easy to hit during that time. [7:37]

### General
- The video is not a comprehensive laning guide, just one tip per hero that may or may not be obvious, aimed at new and slightly more experienced players. [8:07]
