## Covers
Every hero's abilities and playstyle, grouped as brawlers, spirit nukers, pick heroes, utility/support and M1 carries. The video says it shows builds, a difficulty rating and a tier rating per hero, but those are not spoken in the transcript (on screen only), so they are not recorded here except where he says them aloud.

## Claims
- The speaker (Natan, Eternus 6) gives each hero an ability and playstyle overview; builds, difficulty and tier are meant as a rough guide for the average player. [0:01]
- Brawlers are frontliners that like to get in and duke it out. [0:01] [0:31]
- Spirit nukers tend to be squishy with a hit-and-run spirit burst playstyle. [4:05]

### Abrams (brawler)
- Often described as a melee hero; two points in Shoulder Charge guarantees a heavy melee, but his days of holding the melee button all game are mostly over. [0:31]
- Sustain from Infernal Resilience (heals back a portion of damage taken over time) and Siphon Life (life drain aura); the more things around you and the more damage you take, the more you heal. [0:31]
- Seismic Impact lets him disengage, re-engage or chase, and makes him unstoppable for a few seconds when maxed. [0:31] [1:01]

### Billy (brawler)
- His melee is faster than other heroes and restores ammo, so use it a lot. [1:01]
- With Blasted active his gun damage is amped and each melee hit adds max health and extends Blasted; get in, shoot, parry, then beat them up. [1:01]
- Rising Ram closes the gap and lifts himself and nearby enemies; Bashdown slams them down and knocks them up again. [1:01]
- Chain Gang disables a fleeing enemy's movement abilities and reels them back in after a short delay. [1:01]

### Mo & Krill (brawler)
- A frontliner, a pick hero and a true anti-carry. [1:32]
- Get active, run with a teammate, find carries farming jungle camps and stun them for way too long with the ultimate combo. [1:32]
- Then Sand Blast to disarm, or Burrow for resists, movement speed and damage, knocking people up on unburrow; Scorn is a small AoE that heals based on damage done, bigger with more enemy heroes around. [1:32]

### Shiv (brawler)
- Mobile, tanky and does a bunch of damage. [2:03]
- Build up Rage, his ultimate's passive; fully stacked it raises his damage and empowers his other abilities (Serrated Knives ricochet, Slice and Dice trail echoes). [2:03]
- Bloodletting delays part of the damage taken; press it when the orange bar is big to clear part of it. [2:03]
- Killing Blow executes enemies below a health threshold and resets on kill when maxed. [2:03] [2:33]

### Silver (brawler)
- A close-range brawler who becomes a true frontliner after dealing enough damage to enter wolf form (Lycan Curse). [2:33]
- Combo: Boot Kick to get behind and knock the target away, Entangling Bola to stop stamina and movement abilities, then three quick Slamfire shots for percent current health damage. [2:33]
- In wolf form she gains movement speed, health, resists and stacking fire rate; her abilities become Mauling Leap, Tail Whack (disarm) and Go for the Throat (heal and missing health damage). [2:33] [3:03]

### Yamato (brawler)
- A lane bully and dive hero who harasses backline carries: Flying Strike to close in, Crimson Slash to slow their fire rate, Power Slash to burst. [3:03]
- Unlike most spirit nukers she does not mind long fights thanks to short cooldowns, Crimson Slash sustain and Shadow Transformation (resets her abilities, more cast speed, resists and unstoppable). [3:03]
- Her right-click bombs are great lane poke; weave them between abilities to maximize damage. [3:03] [3:34]

### Victor (brawler)
- Deadlock's true juggernaut: immobile and no crowd control, but up close he does a lot of damage and survives long thanks to four ways to sustain. [3:34]
- Sustain: health regen scaling with spirit, Jumpstart (heal, speed, cleanse at tier three), Pain Battery after taking enough damage (heals at max level), and Shocking Reanimation to revive in place. Aura of Suffering damages enemies around him and himself. [3:34] [4:05]

### Apollo (spirit nuker)
- His ultimate charges a long-range dash through walls that hits all enemies in range and curses them, then deals big damage, more on enemies below 50% health. [4:05]
- Riposte parries any attack then dashes forward to stun and lower bullet resist, setting up Flawless Advance (three short dashes into a charged stab, more damage when timed right); leap out with Disengaging Sigil. [4:05] [4:36]

### Calico (spirit nuker)
- A roaming spirit nuker assassin who can also be played as more of a brawler. [4:36]
- Roams in Ava form using the small map tunnels looking for kills. Gloom Bombs go wherever they want but hurt a lot when several hit; Leaping Slash damages and heals. [4:36]
- After the Leaping Slash and Gloom Bomb combo, Return to Shadows makes her untargetable and deals damage on cast and end; at max level it adds damage amp and refreshes all cooldowns. [5:06]

### Celeste (spirit nuker)
- Does not quite fit any category; almost a battle mage or maybe an M1 carry. [5:06]
- Slow ricochet projectiles with soft aim lock; extremely mobile with low gravity and air control, so she sticks close to use Light Eater (spirit damage on hit and spirit lifesteal). [5:06] [5:37]
- Dazzling Trick barrier silences and damages when broken; Radiant Daggers add stacking spirit damage amp; Shining Wonder bounces between targets, damaging and slowing. [5:37]

### Grey Talon (spirit nuker)
- One charged shot takes half your HP when you peek; with Rapid Recharge he has four. [5:37]
- Rain of Arrows lets him glide and turns his bow multi-shot; Spirit Snare curses enemies out of cover. [5:37] [6:07]
- Guided Owl is a flyable owl that stuns, executes below 20% health when maxed, and grants 10 permanent spirit power on kills. [6:07]

### Lash (spirit nuker)
- Gets active on the map roaming and ganking for kills. [6:07]
- Despite one of the highest health-per-boon scalings and low cooldown sustain from Flog, he is built and played as a spirit nuker, not a frontliner or brawler. [6:07]
- Ground slam damage scales with height, so run on rooftops, slam down, Grapple back to high ground to wait or disengage; his ultimate is great for turning team fights or finding picks in open areas. [6:37]

### Mina (spirit nuker)
- Love Bites makes shots and abilities build up a big spirit burst; each proc adds bats to Nox Nostra, extending the ultimate. [6:37] [7:08]
- Sanguine Retreat is two untargetable dashes to reposition or dodge; Rake deals missing health damage and heals her per target that dies to it. [7:08]

### Pocket (spirit nuker)
- A hit-and-run spirit nuker, tricky to play because two abilities are both damage and escapes. [7:08]
- Barrage cast in the air floats you and stacks up to 40% damage amp when maxed and all four projectiles hit; Affliction deals current health damage plus ticks that cannot kill. [7:08] [7:38]
- Enchanter's Satchel makes Pocket untargetable and deals damage around; Flying Cloak damages what it passes and can be recast to teleport to it. [7:38]

### Bebop (pick)
- Hooks you from across the map, puts a Sticky Bomb on you that grows with each hero hit, then sends you flying with Exploding Uppercut; maxed Uppercut resets the hook cooldown. [7:38] [8:08]
- Hyper Beam is a massive laser, great for zone control when pushing or defending an objective. [8:08]

### Haze (pick)
- A hybrid between a pick hero and an M1 hero; Fixation adds bullet damage on a target per stack. [8:08]
- Plays like an assassin: Smoke Bomb invisibility to roam the enemy jungle for lone enemies, Sleep Dagger to put them to sleep and cripple stamina, then Bullet Dance (a steroid aimbot zone around her). [8:38]

### Holliday (pick)
- A hyper mobile spirit nuker pick hero; bounce pads give 100% air control and a small landing explosion. [8:38]
- Powder Kegs can be quick meleed in the air, knocking enemies up for an easy Crackshot headshot or a Spirit Lasso; take the lassoed enemy back over a jump pad to extend the stun and let teammates finish them. [9:08]

### Paradox (pick)
- Active all game looking for kills with Paradoxical Swap, which swaps her with the enemy hero hit. [9:08]
- Pulse Grenade forces people out of cover to hit Kinetic Carbine, a charged shot that speeds her up and time-slows the target, setting up an easy swap through Time Wall (slow, percent max health damage, silence when maxed, delays enemy projectiles for one-sided firefights). [9:08] [9:38]

### Vindicta (pick)
- Flies around the map sniping with Assassinate, bonus damage below 50% health and bonus weapon damage per snipe kill. [9:38]
- Crow Familiar shreds resists and bleeds percent current health; Stake tethers enemies so teammates can follow and snipes are easier; Flight keeps her up a long time and adds spirit damage on hit. [10:09]

### The Doorman (utility/support)
- A utility and pick hero. Doorbell is poke that can be shot to detonate for damage and slow. [10:09]
- Doorways allow coordinated plays or escapes, and can kidnap an enemy to your side with Luggage Cart; the cart is tricky to hit, but ulting someone into the hotel (Hotel Guest) guarantees an easy cart hit on their return if you listen for the exit audio cue. [10:39]

### Dynamo (utility/support)
- A support who does a surprising amount of damage. [10:39]
- Kinetic Pulse is his only damaging basic ability, range scaling with spirit power; devastating on hit (knock up, slow, lower fire rate, more bullet damage taken). [11:09]
- Quantum Entanglement is a short untargetable dash that can take nearby teammates; Rejuvenating Aurora heals him and allies; Singularity is an absolutely game-changing ultimate when it hits several people. [11:09]

### Graves (utility/support)
- Hard to fit in a category: not quite an M1 hero nor a spirit nuker, more a hybrid bringing utility. [11:09] [11:39]
- Grasping Hands immobilizes enemies passing through; her ultimate summons a gravestone that spawns ghouls running at the enemy base, exploding on heroes or structures. [11:39]
- Collects death essence when things die nearby to summon death heads on her weapon target; her passive Essence Theft steals weapon damage and spirit resist over time and can target two enemies when maxed. [11:39]

### Ivy (utility/support)
- The support for players who like to shoot. [11:39]
- Great map presence: Kudzu Bombs push and clear a lane's troopers, then Air Drop flies her to another lane (silence bomb when maxed) or carries an ally anywhere with a barrier on release. [12:09]
- Watcher's Covenant links her to an ally for fire rate, bullet lifesteal and shared healing, including Stone Form's healing; Stone Form makes her briefly invulnerable and stuns on landing. [12:09]

### Kelvin (utility/support)
- A brawly frontliner support with utility and heals and lots of slows. [12:40]
- Frost Grenades damage, slow and cut stamina regen, and heal allies with the tier two upgrade; Ice Path for fast rotations and new grenade angles; Arctic Beam slows movement and fire rate and ricochets when maxed. [12:40]
- Frozen Shelter is a dome that heals allies a lot and slows enemies inside. [13:10]

### McGinnis (utility/support)
- Some kind of M1 carry utility support focused on area control and denial; her gun ramps fire rate over time. [13:10]
- Mini turrets attack enemies; Medicinal Specter heals, raises stamina regen and buffs fire rate inside. She is immobile and wants to fight inside her Specter near her turrets. [13:10]
- Spectral Wall delays enemy escapes and sets up Heavy Barrage, AoE rockets that deal spirit damage and slow. [13:10] [13:41]

### Paige (utility/support)
- A classic enchanter with shields, heals and utility; her barrier grants weapon damage and bounces to two more targets when maxed. [13:41]
- Captivating Wreath slows then immobilizes, setting up Conjure Dragon (burning trail). [13:41]
- Her global ultimate Rallying Charge turns fights anywhere on the map; horses knock up and damage enemies and heal allies, more with travel distance. [13:41]

### Rem (utility/support)
- Uses small map tunnels like Calico, moving faster inside for quick rotations and boxes. [13:41] [14:13]
- Little helpers grab boxes or slot machines, empower an ally with spirit resist and movement speed, or create super troopers; Tag Along dashes to an ally, untargetable, healing them and giving spirit power when maxed. [14:13]
- Pillow Toss knocks enemies away and cuts his other cooldowns by 5 seconds, including Naptime, which slows, disables movement abilities, then puts enemies to sleep with damage on wake up. [14:13] [14:43]

### Sinclair (utility/support)
- A lane bully, spirit nuker and utility hero; Audience Participation steals enemy ultimates with reduced cooldown. [14:43]
- Rabbit Hex turns enemies into bunnies that move faster with smaller hitboxes but take more damage and cannot use abilities. [14:43]
- Spectral Assistant adds spirit damage on hit, can swap positions twice, and copies Vexing Bolt, whose damage grows with travel distance and can be redirected once. [14:43] [15:14]

### Viscous (utility/support)
- Arguably has two of the best abilities in the game: The Cube (makes an ally untargetable and heals them) and Puddle Punch (knocks everyone in the area away with melee damage), useful to push enemies out of position, push allies to safety or reposition. [15:14]
- Goo Ball damages and stuns on impact with lots of resists; when maxed you can use all items and abilities in ball form, including Splatter (damage and a slowing area). [15:14] [15:44]

### Drifter (M1 carry)
- Wanders the map hunting isolated targets: Blood Scent gives bonus damage against targets with no nearby allies and permanent stacking weapon damage per isolated kill. [15:44]
- Stalker's Mark is a skill shot bleed that can be recast to teleport behind the target, setting up Rend (melee and spirit damage, bullet lifesteal when maxed). [15:44] [16:15]
- Eternal Night is a utility ultimate that limits vision for two or three enemies and gives Drifter spirit damage on hit. [16:15]

### Infernus (M1 carry)
- All about proccing Afterburn by shooting or hitting with abilities to make targets burn. [16:15]
- Napalm slows, raises his damage against them and cuts healing when maxed; Flame Dash to escape or engage and close the gap for Concussive Combustion, a delayed AoE stun. [16:45]

### Lady Geist (M1 carry)
- A beefy frontlining M1 carry with self-damage and healing. [16:45]
- Essence Bomb and Malice damage her on cast; Life Drain and Soul Exchange heal her. When low, find a full health enemy, hide around a corner and swap with Soul Exchange. [16:45] [17:16]

### Mirage (M1 carry)
- Great at side laning and split pushing because of strong dueling and Traveler, which teleports him to any hero or friendly structure after a channel. [17:16]
- Djinn's Mark stacks a multiplier that pops for damage; Fire Scarabs steal health and lower bullet resist; Tornado is an untargetable dash that lifts enemies and grants bullet evasion. [17:16] [17:47]

### Seven (M1 carry)
- A scaling carry focused on AoE spirit damage, debuffs and late game team fighting. [17:47]
- Lightning Ball slows and stops on surfaces; Static Charge is a delayed AoE stun; Power Surge adds bouncing spirit damage that lowers spirit resist; Storm Cloud is a channel ultimate dealing tick damage in range. [17:47] [18:17]

### Venator (M1 carry)
- Almost like a turret: not mobile at all but dishes out a ton of damage. [18:17]
- Grenades set enemies on fire, reduce healing and deal pure damage; Gut Shot knocks away and stuns against walls, setting up a snap trap that immobilizes, then a free hit with his ultimate (three stake shots, the last executing below a health threshold). [18:17] [18:48]

### Vyper (M1 carry)
- All about sliding with her passive Slither; infinite ammo while sliding and she can cast without breaking the slide. [18:48] [19:18]
- Daggers slow and stack damage; Lethal Venom deals big missing health spirit damage and is the only damage that goes through the damage block of targets petrified by Petrifying Bola (all enemies in the area when maxed). [19:18] [19:48]

### Warden (M1 carry)
- Punishes bad stamina management: Alchemical Flask slows, reduces weapon damage and takes one stamina with one point, setting up Binding Word to immobilize. [19:48]
- Willpower gives a barrier and movement speed; Last Stand is a team fight ultimate for counter engages when enemies overcommit, healing him from damage dealt. [19:48] [20:19]

### Wraith (M1 carry)
- A scaling carry great at side laning and split pushing thanks to Project Mind (escape teleport) and Full Auto (fire rate for her and nearby friendly units, spirit on hit at second upgrade). [20:19]
- Card Trick cards follow your crosshair and slow on impact; Telekinesis lifts, slams, then silences and disarms the target. [20:19] [20:49]
