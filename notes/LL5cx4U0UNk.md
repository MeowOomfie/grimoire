## Covers
Solomon, the new chess-themed hero, first look by aquatiq in the sandbox.
Reads each ability, tests it, and notes first combos and item ideas.
Patch of early October 2026 (video dated 2026-10-09).

## Claims
- Ability 1 creates a Bishop that remains in place indefinitely and can be broken by pressing 1. [0:00]
- You can move the Bishop around; it slows and deals spirit damage to enemies it passes through, and after a delay it activates diagonal squares that deal more spirit damage when stepped on. [0:30]
- Pieces can only be placed on certain squares, like real chess. [1:02]
- The Knight ability promotes an ally to a Knight, granting bonus stamina and barrier for a duration; you can then move that ally. [1:02]
- You can promote and move yourself too. [1:32]
- Rook Battery releases a pair of rooks that travel forward, dealing spirit damage and immobilizing; if they hit a wall they reflect back the opposite way. [1:32]
- The rooks drag enemies only a little (a small knock), immobilize for 1 second, and reflect once; leveling likely makes them reflect twice. [2:04]
- The Knight promote can target two allies. [2:04]
- Ultimate: summon a Queen that crashes down for area spirit damage; after a delay it releases squares in a straight line that deal spirit damage again and stun. [2:35]
- Move the Queen with right mouse; it leaps to the target location and repeats the effect. [3:05]
- His gun is really slow. [2:35]
- He reads as part support, part spirit nuker. [3:05]
- Combo: knock enemies into a Bishop with the rooks, then move the Bishop over them, trapping them among pawns and bishops. [3:05]
- The default build is spirit; he wants Magic Carpet. [3:36]
- He wondered about Hollow Point because the hero still did a lot of gun damage. [4:07]
- Upgraded rooks showed a 2 second immobilize; he calls Rook Battery one of his favorite abilities in the game. [4:38]
