# The REAL Way To Get Ahead In Deadlock (Macro Guide)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-02-18
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-PhKQRhABhg

## Covers
Part one of a macro guide on farming efficiency: soul sources ranked (lane creeps, Sinner's Sacrifice, boxes, jungle camps), side lane farming, box routes per lane during the laning stage, the double Sinner's Sacrifice trick, who takes which resources, melee tricks for tier three camps and fast mid game camp routes.
For players who fall behind on souls or want to become their team's carry farmer.

## Claims
- Macro is every non-fighting choice (split pushing, farming, where to farm, where to go next); making those choices more efficiently than the enemy is why some players end up with far more souls than you. [0:30]
- Souls also grant boons, passive power ups independent of items: more souls means more HP, spirit power and bullet damage no matter what you buy. [1:00]
- The most efficient soul source is lane creeps, and has always been; AFK farming the jungle all game will not get you ahead, combining lane and jungle gives the most souls. [1:00]
- At the start of the game one enemy wave of four creeps gives roughly 600 souls, more than any camp and comparable to a tier three camp, in far less time. [1:31]
- After lane creeps, the next best sources are the Sinner's Sacrifices (slightly fewer souls, bonus buffs, but they damage you), then boxes, then jungle camps. [2:02]
- Boxes are an efficient way to pick up souls while crossing from one lane to another. [2:02]
- Tier one camps spawn at 2 minutes and are easy to kill for low souls; tier twos are the medium camps; tier three camps spawn at 8 minutes and give the most souls of any jungle camp. [2:32]
- If you are behind after laning, want to catch up, or want to be the richest on your team, sit on a side lane, usually alone, and clear the waves. [2:32]
- Fighting revolves around mid because mid is easiest to reach from anywhere on the circular map, which is why side lane farmers and carries sit on the side lane. [3:03]
- From 8 minutes on, clearing side lane waves and securing last hits should be your number one priority to get ahead. [3:03]
- Pushing side lane creeps also opens the map and pressures the enemy, forcing them to defend objectives, which frees you to farm your jungle and invade theirs (stealing camps and Sinner's Sacrifices). [3:33]
- Classic farm carry pattern (shown on Warden): farm the side lane wave safely and each time the wave is pushed toward the enemy side, rotate into jungle camps. [3:33]
- During the laning stage, after fighting or shoving a wave, farm a box route instead of doing nothing in the downtime; it is free passive income. [4:03]
- Box routes can put you 1k souls up on the enemy in lane without a single fight or kill. [4:34]
- Run the routes efficiently and you return in time for the next wave without losing a single creep. [4:34]
- Left side lane route: after shoving the wave (boxes spawn at 2 minutes), take the two boxes, the next two, then the three in your shop; short, close together and you can shop on the way. [5:04]
- Left side lane, after the next shove: run the other side of the lane through its nearby boxes and be back right before the next wave. [5:04]
- Mid lane route: two boxes into the three creep camp, then back to lane after the next wave; after the following wave, take two boxes and that camp and return. [5:34]
- Mid lane route: slide down into your basement and run its three boxes; very strong early because the basement has many boxes close together. [6:05]
- If you are extra greedy or fast, take the extra three boxes on the way out of the basement; otherwise skip them and go straight back to lane. [6:35]
- Green side lane: go left and take the boxes there, or, more advanced, go up into your tunnels and on toward the Walker. [6:35]
- Whenever boxes are on your path, break them: a light melee does it, or slide through them to break them with almost no time lost. [7:06]
- Boxes are currently really good and worth picking up even as a side lane farmer or jungler. [7:36]
- The double Sinner's Sacrifice room in your jungle is the most farmed, and at 8 minutes it gives a lot of souls. [8:06]
- Sinner's Sacrifices cost HP, so dying during or right after farming them drops your unsecured souls. [8:06]
- Every income source except lane creeps is unsecured, a mechanic that punishes safe AFK jungling; know where the enemy could be before farming Sinner's Sacrifices at 8 minutes. [8:37]
- Farm a Sinner's Sacrifice with four heavy melees, charging the heavy so it lands at the end of the cycle; a well timed hit plays a sound and grants a bonus buff. [9:08]
- Sinner's Sacrifice buffs last the entire game (spirit power, health, weapon damage and more) and add up even though they don't show in the stats. [9:08]
- Double Sinner's Sacrifice trick: kill the creep, hit one Sacrifice once, then finish the other with continuous punches, getting the +4 buff as early as possible in the circle; then go back to the first, hold melee and it lines up to give the buff automatically. [9:38]
- Arriving at the double room at 8 minutes with about 800 health is risky: the clip went from 700 HP to 100 HP while stacking unsecured souls. [10:38]
- Enemies can hear Sinner's Sacrifice punching from far away, so an idle enemy aware of you can come and kill you easily; treat them as high risk, high reward. [10:38]
- Your own side Sinner's Sacrifices should go to carries; supports should not take them. [11:09]
- Rem players taking their own Sinner's Sacrifices is a mistake; Rem should steal the enemy side ones, sending his helpers so he takes no damage. [11:09]
- Carries should not take the enemy side Sinner's Sacrifices because they end up low health with high death risk; tanky supports like Mo & Krill or Rem can contest and fight there. [11:40]
- If nobody on your team is farming them and you pass by, take them; spread them evenly and let players with a bad laning stage take them, since everyone farms them at the same speed regardless of items. [12:10]
- Don't go out of your way to farm Sinner's Sacrifices; if an enemy wave is pushing your Walker, shove the wave first and then take them safely, or your Walker takes damage, you lose map control and lose money overall. [12:41]
- The longer the game goes, the more income you get from everything. [12:41]
- Jungle camps are a supplement to lane creeps and kills, not a sole source; add them right after you shove out waves. [13:12]
- Aim for roughly 1,000+ souls per minute; lower than that means you are not farming efficiently enough. [13:42]
- Meleeing is always faster on camps; sit on top of one creep and aim at another while holding heavy melee to hit both and farm at almost double speed, where the creeps are close enough. [14:14]
- Tier three trick one: on your safe camp in the lower right of the map, sit on the middle creep, shoot the creep in front of you and melee at the same time to split the damage and clear very fast. [14:44]
- Tier three trick two: the middle camp on the green side (the park side by the docks) has creeps too far apart to melee both, so sit on the middle creep, hold melee and shoot a side creep, then do the same on the other. [15:14]
- These tier three tricks work on most heroes with decent melee damage; Metro uses them for an early power spike on Billy. [15:44]
- Find your own hero's fast clears: a fast AoE hero like Pocket clears some camps very fast with one ability but tier three camps slowly. [15:44]
- Give tier three camps mostly to carries (Seven, Victor, maybe Haze or Wraith), who have the damage output to clear them. [16:14]
- If it feels like you are taking too long to clear a camp, you are. [16:44]
- Mid game hyper carry route one: the four close camps in the bottom right; start moving to the next camp while the current one is almost dead. [16:44]
- Seconds saved moving early on every camp, box route and rotation add up to multiple minutes over a 20 to 50 minute game and make you the soul leader. [17:15]
- Route two, if the Sinner's Sacrifices are up: clear a camp, farm the single Sinner's Sacrifice, go to the next camp, farm the double Sinner's Sacrifices, then clear the tier three. [18:15]
- Avoid the tier three camp in the basement unless you have a big AoE; you are trapped below if ganked, it is hard to escape and it takes too long. [18:15]
- Most used route, from the side lane after shoving: tier one camp if time allows, up to the tier two camp, boxes on the way, jump down to the next camp; by then the wave is back at your Walker, so shove it out, then jump off the zipline to the tier three and the last camp. [18:46]
- Knowing where to go next and planning ahead raises your souls per minute by a huge margin; efficiency in farming is king. [19:16]
