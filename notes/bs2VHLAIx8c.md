## Covers
Shiv, aquatiq commentary over one of his own games, patch of 2026-07.
Lane matchup against healing, item order (Healbane, Counterspell, spirit defense) and his view that Shiv is overpowered.

## Claims
- Counterspell looks very good against an enemy team with lots of disables. [1:05]
- Against an enemy lane that heals, you cannot win lane on Shiv; they heal through all the knives you put in. [2:19]
- Restoration Shot helps a lot in lane; watch when it is up so you can use it. [2:19]
- He gets Extra Regen early. [2:49]
- He does not like fighting Viscous: Viscous goes in his cube and you cannot press anything. [11:26]
- Get Healbane first before anything, then spirit resilience. [11:26]
- Lash is someone Shiv can fight. [11:26]
- He wants Torment Pulse and Counterspell, getting Counterspell first because it becomes important soon. [12:59]
- Buy spirit resilience very early or you explode every time you go into a fight. [13:29]
- Expect enemies to start buying debuff removers against Shiv. [16:47]
- He calls the new Shiv overpowered. [16:47]
- He binds his knives to 1 and his mouse. [17:50]
- He considers taking both Spirit Shielding and spirit resilience to survive. [19:55]
- He would never go gun on Shiv. [22:01]
- Reverb or Scourge is good when enemies keep walking up on you. [26:18]
