# How To Stop Throwing In Deadlock (Macro Guide)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-02-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bjPveO-_Pgs

## Covers
Part two of a macro guide (part one is farming): when to take fights, the three cases for pushing Walkers, split pushing and good split pushers, when to pick up and run the Urn, when to take or contest Mid Boss and Rejuvenator, and when and how to siege the redesigned base.
For players who throw leads by pushing Walkers or the base too early, or who never use the Urn as a comeback.

## Claims
- Take fights whenever you have an advantage. [0:31]
- Advantage one is a power spike; the main item power spike right now is the 4.8k investment, which gives a lot of power, especially in the early and mid game. [1:02]
- Maxing key abilities is also a power spike; almost every hero has some really good maxed abilities, and you need to know which ones on your hero. [1:02]
- Builds from pro players or streamers line up the item spike with the ability spike, since souls give both XP and money; for example maxing Flawless Advance (as captioned) at the same time as the full spirit spike and some green items. [1:33]
- If you win the laning stage with a lot of money, actively look for fights in the enemy jungle or gank other lanes, since you are higher level with more items. [2:03]
- If you are behind, don't look for fights; react to the enemy, who will often be aggressive on your side and overchase, especially into Walkers. [2:34]
- Advantage two: always fight under objectives. Guardians shoot the enemy and are powerful early. [2:34]
- Walkers get tankier the longer the game goes and the last Walker has the most HP; they absorb damage, give cover, and passively give around 15% bullet and spirit resist, like owning both Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem. [3:04]
- Even behind in money, you can take favorable fights under your Walker. [3:04]
- Advantage three: ultimates. Track enemy and team ults at the top of the screen; if your team has five ults and the enemy one, take a big fight. If the enemy has many and you have few, play more passively. [3:04]
- Advantage four: positioning. High ground is strong on most heroes: easier to hit enemies, harder to chase you, and you can shoulder and corner peek from above. High ground is the number one positioning tip. [3:35]
- Sitting behind a one-sided veil you can see through and the enemy can't gives an inherent fight advantage. [4:06]
- Ahead: proactively look for kills, steal the enemy jungle and deny their farm; that is how you keep a lead. Behind: secure your own jungle without dying. [4:36]
- Do not push into Walkers early; sending three or four people into a defended Walker while ahead often gets you killed and evens the game. [4:36]
- Walker scenario one: you are up at least two players (6v4 or 5v3) after kills, which in the mid game lets you attack one or multiple Walkers while the defense advantage is diminished. [5:07]
- Walker scenario two: all three lanes are pushing into the enemy side, ideally into every enemy Walker; then even 6v6, push multiple Walkers at once. [5:38]
- Defending three Walkers at once needs a perfect 2-2-2 split, which is very hard even in Eternus 6; usually the enemy overcommits to one side (that side's attackers die but two Walkers fall) or forgets one side and it dies for free. [6:09]
- That is why you always push creep waves first: without pushed waves the enemy gets more map control and more of those scenarios against you. [6:40]
- Walker scenario three: you are a split pusher and a bigger fight breaks out on the other side; with at least five enemies fighting there, pushing the far side Walker alone is usually very safe, since at most one enemy can defend. [7:11]
- The last enemy often doesn't even show up, because they are AFK farming, pushing mid or joining the big fight. [7:42]
- If you can kill the Walker fast enough, split pushing is always worth it over joining the team fight; it is worth dying for a Walker 99% of the time. [7:42]
- Good split pushers have high auto attack damage, because Walkers resist spirit burst damage more: Warden, McGinnis, Wraith, and the new heroes Graves and Celeste. [8:13]
- Celeste is strong at Walkers because her 1 (the Light Eater debuff) works on the Walker, so she can left click it down fast. [8:13]
- Split pushing works on almost every hero: if a Walker is undefended and the enemy is busy elsewhere, going for it is almost always worth it. [8:44]
- Otherwise never push Walkers; if the game is even, even a bit ahead without Rejuvenator, don't push them. [9:14]
- In the mid game it might not be worth pushing a Walker after a big team fight win, because you can take Mid Boss instead. [9:14]
- Pick up the Urn almost always if you are on the Urn side; it gives a lot of money whether you are ahead or behind. [9:45]
- Even means 10% or less net worth difference; in an even game the drop off point is in the middle on the other side of the map. [9:45]
- In an even game, running the Urn forces the enemy to react: chase you and waste time, ignore you and give free money, or take an Urn fight. [10:15]
- If many enemies show up near the drop off and your team doesn't, hold the Urn, run back to your Walker and sit there; the enemy is forced to stay in that lane, since you drop it the moment they leave. Having control of the Urn is almost always better. [10:15]
- If you are behind by more than 10%, the Urn matters even more: comeback gold gives more gold from every source the further behind you are, and the Urn is the main massive source. [10:46]
- When behind, the drop off is on your side of the map, usually near a Walker, so it is easy to reach and hard to contest; enemies must go deep into your territory, giving your team a favorable fight. [11:16]
- The Urn is the only real way high-rank games come back; you won't come back by farming or sitting in base. Even in high rank, enemies often fail or forget to contest, and one or two Urns bring the game back to even. [11:46]
- If you are ahead, still pick up the Urn, but not to deposit it, because the drop off is deep on the enemy side and very risky. [12:17]
- Ahead, especially as a support who doesn't need farm, pick up the Urn and run it to your base or keep it to deny the enemy comeback gold. Low ranks don't run the Urn enough. [12:17]
- Holding the Urn too long deals damage to you. [12:47]
- If you are really far ahead and get kills, you can walk into the enemy side and turn the Urn in, but it is very risky until you are super far ahead. [12:47]
- Urn summary: pick it up and run it or protect it; as long as it is in your possession it is good, since the enemy must invest resources to get it. [13:18]
- Mid Boss is the strongest objective in the game; it gives the killing team gold, and securing the Rejuvenator gives up to three extra lives for a fight. [13:18]
- Mid Boss is what lets you securely push Walkers in the mid game and the enemy base later; the only other tool is being very far ahead in gold. [13:49]
- Mid Boss scenario one: after about 20 minutes, enemy respawns are long enough that if you get at least two kills and walk into Mid Boss fast, you usually secure it for free, or the enemy is down players and you secure more of the Rejuvenator. [14:20]
- Mid Boss scenario two: in a 6v6, take Mid Boss when the enemy is out of position, for example all focused on running a comeback Urn; they get the Urn, you get three extra lives, which is worth their Urn money. [14:51]
- Mid Boss scenario three: you are very far ahead or have more fight-deciding ultimates (Lash, Dynamo, Ivy's stun, Abrams, Yamato) and are a bit ahead, especially if the enemy is down ultimates. [15:21]
- If the enemy is split, one farming on the left and one on the right, while your team is around middle, go into the Mid Boss pit and force a fight; you usually come out on top. [15:51]
- Contest the enemy Mid Boss almost always if you can get there in time, even down a player or two: they take damage from the boss, and you can stun them or parry on the Rejuvenator crystal. [15:51]
- Contesting while down people usually still fails, but giving them free Rejuvenators is worse: with three Rejuvenators against nothing they can take any fight, push your base, take the Urn and steal your jungle, which makes a comeback very hard. [16:22]
- Contest especially when you are up ultimates. [16:53]
- If you are too far from Mid Boss to get there in time, take the second best option: kill a Walker on the side of the map you are on, or run the Urn. Trading Mid Boss for one Walker is not horrible, for two Walkers it is a good trade. [16:53]
- Since the latest patch the base was redesigned and is very hard to crack. [17:54]
- Base condition one: you are up at least 35k to 40k; then, playing safe, you can siege the base without instantly dying, though it can still backfire. [18:24]
- Base condition two: you are up in money and have Rejuvenator, ideally both the 35k to 40k lead and Rejuvenator. [18:24]
- With three Rejuvenators but only up 10k to 20k, a properly defending enemy will probably win the fight and get a chance to come back. [18:55]
- With Rejuvenator but no 40k lead, take the rest of the map instead: kill every Walker, take the last Walker in a team fight, and take their entire jungle if they sit in base, until you have a 35k to 40k lead. [18:55]
- If the Rejuvenator runs out while you are up only about 20k, look for pickoffs, but usually farm a bit and wait for the next Mid Boss; that is why games last long even at high level. [19:25]
- To siege safely, go together as a team to one Shrine, then the other, then all focus the Patron; the enemy has two easy pathways to the Shrines and a good team sends six to one side to kill split attackers. [19:56]
- After downing Patron phase one there is a 15 second downtime before you can attack the final phase. [20:27]
- The final phase is the hardest: you must be in the pit, the Patron can curse you and deal damage, enemies can get in easily, and if more than one enemy is in the pit the Patron is invulnerable, so clean up enemies first. [20:27]
- After phase one, check your team's health, how many Rejuvenator lives you have and the enemy respawns; if six enemies are up and can defend, or you have no Rejuvenator, back out and try again after a pickoff or two. [20:57]
- Finishing the game is the one time to be careful and passive: a turned fight in the late game with 90 second respawns is a way back for the enemy, and greedy early finishes often flip games. [21:27]
- Otherwise aggression is king: put pressure on the enemy at every stage, from lane to mid game, and play proactively. [21:27]
- The team that has to react to every wave pushing its Walkers falls behind and makes more mistakes; the team with a plan that plays proactively, even risky, usually wins. [21:58]
- In later mid game fights with nothing else to do, especially with Mid Boss about to spawn, go as six and look for a kill first; it works every time in Eternus. [22:28]
- In the mid game keep the waves pushed; be aggressive, invade the enemy jungle and look for kills when you are strong, up in net worth, or just bought a big item. [22:28]
- Don't spend all day farming unless you are the hard carry, and even the hard carry eventually has enough farm and should start fighting. [22:58]
