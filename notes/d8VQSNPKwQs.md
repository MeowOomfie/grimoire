## Covers
Viscous coaching by Midknight: a replay review of one Eternus-rank Viscous player on a Puddle Punch build, in a high-level game against Apollo, Venator, Silver, Drifter and Dynamo.
Topics: dead time and box routes, over-committing to teammates' bad fights, respecting enemies, full resetting, Puddle Punch as displacement, Cultist Sacrifice uptime and instant cast, defaulting to mid, picks on side lanes, melee and parry tech, and resist items.
Patch era of early March 2026.

## Claims
Player (Viscous, Eternus):
- He dislikes the Puddle Punch start even on a punch build; people wrongly feel they must start the ability their build revolves around, but starting Splatter is better. [0:31]
- Good players parry Viscous's Puddle Punch easily if they are on under 70 ping, which makes a punch start categorically worse. [1:02]
- Go Monster Rounds this lane: Haze needs wave priority, Apollo can bully you under tower and invade your tier one, and Venator is a good laner. [1:02]
- Standing around after your tier one camp gets stolen is about 15 seconds of downtime; box route instead, there are many boxes on the map. [1:32]
- Apollo's 3 does a lot of damage and heals him, making him a scary lane bully, and he dives easily with his 2. [2:02]
- Do not contest a tier two camp against Venator and Apollo; Venator's 2 is very scary in enclosed spaces and Apollo does a lot of damage. [2:34]
- Once Venator has his ult he is terrifying when you are under tower. [3:04]
- Venator traps can be shot. [3:34]
- When Haze starts a bad engage, do not commit; play safe on top of the bridge instead of dropping off it. [4:34]
- After your green spike this is the pivot-or-buy-Lifestrike moment; Lifestrike is stupidly overpowered, so buying it is respectable. [5:06]
- Support Haze when she ults in, but from the bridge, without committing yourself, and punch her out if needed; she will sunk-cost into a bad fight. [5:36]
- Do not repeek an enemy who has his final snipe (ult) up; hold the wall and juke instead. [6:09]
- Do not trade into a burst hero without your Cube up; you cannot net HP in that trade, especially when 2k souls behind. [9:54]
- Use the momentum of Puddle Punch to get onto roofs and play the roofs to survive instead of committing. [10:25]
- He does not like you always Puddle Punching the ground. [10:56]
- Do not full reset when you are relatively healthy with Lifestrike; one heal creep gets you to full, so go to secret shop instead. Full resetting wastes so much time. [11:36]
- Sinner's Sacrifice takes 5 minutes to respawn; most get farmed at spawn at 8 minutes, so look for pressure and steal some when they respawn around 13 minutes. [12:07]
- Push the mid wave when you have map pressure. [12:37]
- Cultist Sacrifice at 13 minutes is late but not awful; you only miss about one use. [13:07]
- Do not rotate into a fight before there is really a fight; you and Rem could have taken mid priority and then invaded. [13:38]
- Puddle Punch's biggest value is displacement: punch behind someone so they fly out of position, unless they parry. [14:08]
- You had map pressure at 13:30 and did not use it, letting them take all their Sinner's Sacrifices. [14:39]
- With ult up Viscous should almost never die; ult and Puddle Punch away. Right now his ult is mainly useful as an escape tool, so using it offensively is not worth it. [15:09]
- Do not over-rotate: if all six go to one side, the enemy trades the walker on the other. Staying mid when the team rotated was the correct choice; ask on mic to not abandon the other side. [15:39]
- Drifter's ult is easy to Counterspell once you practice; you can also cleanse it with Divine Barrier. [16:08]
- At high level, assume an enemy running at you alone (Paradox with no ult on mid) has teammates nearby and back off. [17:09]
- When running away, look at the map; if your Haze engages, help her from range and hug a wall with punches ready, but do not commit. [17:39]
- If Dynamo ran away and then comes back to take space, assume he has teammates nearby; farming there while your team shows four elsewhere is way too greedy. [18:09]
- Do not sit in mid; everyone is there and you cannot do much. [20:12]
- If you buy Cultist Sacrifice, use it off cooldown; instant cast reminds you when it is up. [20:43]
- At an urn pickup (not a cash) against six visible enemies, back off, give them the pickup and let your teammate take the walker instead of a 5v6. [22:52]
- Do not melee at the same moment as your Puddle Punch; melee while they are in the air from the punch, when they are disoriented, or you get parried. [23:22]
- Disarming Hex is a fine support item, but Metal Skin is probably better for you. [25:24]
- You have huge downtime between waves, which is why your farm is among the lowest in the lobby; box route for 10 to 20 boxes instead of standing there. [25:56]
- Only siege the mid Walker with a huge advantage; pro games sometimes win without it, since map geometry favors the defenders. [26:27]
- When rotating into a teammate's bad fight, recognize it is bad, call it on mic, and be ready to leave the second it goes south. [27:30]
- Be ready to parry Apollo when he melees back in after you saw him parry. [28:00]
- On the Rejuvenator, melee on frame one and then instantly parry; one of the best ways to secure Rejuv and parry someone. [28:00]
