# The BEST Wraith Build In Deadlock Refined.
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-10-18
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2h5iBP9SbQ

## Covers
Metro's refined gun and hybrid Wraith build, item by item: why each item is there, when to buy it and when to sell it. Covers card-based laning, the Fleetfoot, Quicksilver Reload, Tesla Bullets farming core, Combat Barrier, the Intensifying Magazine or Hunter's Aura choice, tier four options, late game and situational picks, and why gun damage works while everyone stacks spirit resist.
For Wraith players at any rank. This is a late-2024 video: the item shop, hero kits and map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Spirit is still king in the meta, but this build works because everyone counters spirit damage and not enough people counter gun damage. [0:30]
- Start with Basic Magazine (just buffed, about 2% more ammo), a super solid item. [0:30]
- Second damage slot: Monster Rounds, though you could run Close Quarters or Headshot Booster. [0:30]
- Monster Rounds works in lane now because towers were buffed (tankier, more damage to you): it lets you pressure the tower early, win lane, pressure opponents, and survive a bit longer on a tower dive. [1:00]
- The one takeaway for Wraith laning: the only way to win your lane is cards. Whether you go spirit or gun afterwards, run cards in lane. [1:30]
- Extra Charge is the most important lane item: you keep it from start to finish, it is usually your first or second buy and the last 500 item you would ever sell, because it gives an extra card; a must have even in this gun or hybrid build. [1:30]
- Then Mystic Burst and Extra Spirit; with Extra Charge he rushes these three before anything else if ahead in lane, otherwise Extra Regen or Restorative Shot if he needs health. [2:01]
- These let you throw cards with Mystic Burst bonus damage, keep shooting to recharge cards, and harass the enemy over and over. [2:01]
- Another lane style uses Infuser, and you can add it, but he caps it at three spirit items because later he goes Quicksilver Reload and Bullet Resist Shredder and doesn't want to sell too many items early. [2:32]
- Two lane play styles: save cards for a big burst when the enemy overcommits (Infuser is better there: pop it for 6 seconds of insane damage and lifesteal), or throw cards at every opportunity for constant pressure, his style, where Extra Spirit is better. [3:02]
- Ammo Scavenger was just nerfed: the 20 extra spirit now needs 20 stacks, meaning 20 soul last hits, which is very hard in lane, and dying resets it. He calls it balanced, still viable. [3:32]
- Extra Stamina is "really broken", just buffed to 16% stamina recovery: for 500 it moves you around the map faster and gets you into and out of fights, often leaving you a jump or dash you would otherwise not have. [3:32]
- You don't need to sell Extra Stamina, since the build doesn't go for many vitality items later. [4:03]
- At the very end of lane, once you have those items, buy Sprint Boots for map movement; less important than Extra Stamina. [4:03]
- Extra Regen and Restorative Shot are situational: buy Extra Regen first if you take a lot of damage (he buys it about 50% of the time), and both if the enemy is really pressuring you. [4:34]
- He likes Healing Rite less now because the full duration takes 19 seconds; still a great option, but he prefers staying active in lane, sustaining and building up cards. [4:34]
- Mid game principle unchanged from his older build: after lane get Fleetfoot, in his opinion the best item in the game for Wraith. [5:04]
- Fleetfoot removes the movement penalty while shooting, so you fight and farm better while moving at full speed or faster, and you get temporary armor if needed. [5:04]
- Then Quicksilver Reload, on cards: in the farming cycle shoot the gun empty, then throw a card to reload; he rarely throws multiple cards early to kill a camp. [5:34]
- Not on the 3 (Full Auto): it has a very long cooldown and you sometimes pop it very early in fights, wasting Quicksilver Reload. Since most damage is gun, start fights shooting and throw cards 3 or 4 seconds in for an almost full reload. [6:04]
- Main item: Tesla Bullets, buffed to scale shock damage with spirit; not a big buff, and don't scale lots of spirit just for it. [6:35]
- Tesla Bullets is a must pick: it boosts farming speed by an insane amount, clears tier three camps really early, clears waves very fast, and makes tier two camps a joke. [6:35]
- Tesla Bullets also applies instant AOE spirit damage in fights; later it instantly applies Bullet Resist Shredder's minus bullet resist, bouncing to multiple enemies, without needing to open with cards or Full Auto. [7:05]
- With Mystic Slow later, Tesla bounces also apply Mystic Slow to every enemy they hit. [7:36]
- Then Combat Barrier, even better in this build: mostly for the gun damage, a green slot filler; while you have a bullet shield you get 22% weapon damage and 6% fire rate. [7:36]
- Latest change: creeps and neutral camps no longer remove the bullet shield, so outside fights it is always active from camp to camp to lane, and it saves you in fights with a big shield; he calls it the best overall defensive item on gun Wraith. [8:06]
- Then Bullet Lifesteal: bonus health and sustain, you never need to go back to base because you full heal off creeps, and it is very good in fights. [8:37]
- Up to this point you can follow the build left to right; in game he lists the purple lane items early because he buys them first. [8:37]
- Choice: Intensifying Magazine first or Hunter's Aura first. [9:08]
- Intensifying Magazine first if you are free to farm anything (even faster farming) or team fight a lot, since it ramps up damage massively unless you are the one being focused. [9:39]
- Hunter's Aura first if the game has more 1v1s, people ganking you or you ganking them: it basically guarantees nobody kills you in a 1v1. [9:39]
- Hunter's Aura triples its bullet resist reduction and fire rate slow in a 1v1, so if you are near the enemy's net worth and they 1v1 you, lift them and they die before hitting the ground every time. [10:11]
- Hunter's Aura is also great in team fights, applying fire rate slow and bullet resist reduction to everyone, plus 150 extra HP; it is the main new addition, and lets him scale harder than going straight into Lucky Shot, Inhibitor or Vampiric Burst. [10:42]
- Sell order: if you need a flex slot for Intensifying Magazine or Hunter's Aura, sell Monster Rounds first, since lane is over and Intensifying Magazine farms faster. [11:13]
- Bullet Resist Shredder next, usually the first item where you must sell: sell Extra Spirit, unless you have two flex slots open. [11:13]
- Never sell Extra Charge (extra cards); Mystic Burst usually adds more damage per card than Extra Spirit plus 6% weapon damage, so keep it over Extra Spirit. [11:43]
- After Bullet Resist Shredder you really should have a flex slot; without one you have probably already lost the game. [11:43]
- Enduring Speed is an instant upgrade, so it doesn't need a slot. [11:43]
- Then pick a tier four item. Lucky Shot is the easiest: flat damage and a slow, with the bonus bullet proc every third shot. [12:15]
- Inhibitor is the safer, more defensive option: it slows movement and cuts the damage enemies deal to you; probably better in most team fights where you sit in the back helping your team. [12:15]
- He often goes Vampiric Burst first instead, also buffed: 100% lifesteal and 40% fire rate when popped, plus move speed, health and damage. [12:46]
- Shadow Weave is more for a card build: its main buff after invis is 55% spirit power, and card Wraith was nerfed, now about as strong as gun Wraith. He doesn't see it beating Vampiric Burst often, but it is not bad if you are the initiator and like the invis. [13:17]
- Everyone buys Spirit Armor now; the only people building gun are Haze (always, because of how the hero works), some Vindictas and Wraiths. Seven and Infernus are spirit damage. [13:47]
- So in many games enemies have a lot of spirit resist and little gun resist. [14:18]
- Counters to gun are Return Fire and Metal Skin (no gun damage to them for 3.5 seconds); Vampiric Burst is good because when they proc Return Fire you can shoot through it and they still die. [14:18]
- Hunter's Aura plus Bullet Resist Shredder can push the enemy's bullet resist very low, sometimes negative, which works because few people buy bullet resist. [14:49]
- That is the main reason he runs Hunter's Aura: people don't expect it; he has run it for 3 days and wins far more with it. Skip it if you want and go straight to a tier four item if very farmed. [14:49]
- He expects the meta to split spirit and gun damage on teams: one or two farmed gun heroes force the whole enemy team into Improved Bullet Armor, Metal Skin or Return Fire, which wins in the long run. [15:20]
- If they proc Metal Skin properly, disengage and play safe until it is over; Return Fire doesn't bother you because of all the lifesteal (Bullet Lifesteal, Vampiric Burst). [15:51]
- Leech, which got buffed, is an easier option you can take instead, though he thinks Vampiric Burst is better in most cases. [15:51]
- By Vampiric Burst you should have two flex slots; if not, you can sell Basic Magazine, since your magazine should be big enough by then. [16:21]
- If you need a defensive item (Metal Skin, Return Fire or Spirit Armor, for example against a farmed enemy Haze) and have no flex slot, sell Extra Stamina: farming time is over and it is mid to late game team fight territory. [16:21]
- Unstoppable if the enemy has a lot of stun and shuts you down: proc it and they can't stop you; almost a must have against a Seven who keeps stunning you, or Dynamo; a hard carry item if procced at the right time. [16:51]
- Colossus as an alternative or addition if you need to front line, your team lacks a tank, or your team is less farmed than you: 40% bullet resist, 40% spirit resist and an aura slow that makes enemies run away. [17:23]
- Very deep in late game go Soul Rebirth. [17:54]
- He has been experimenting with Mystic Slow: it only gives its effect, but the effect is insane with Tesla Bullets, 30% movement slow and 40% fire rate slow on anyone hit by your gun damage including Tesla procs, like a budget Ricochet. [17:54]
- Buy Mystic Slow over Lucky Shot or Inhibitor, since they do similar things: Lucky Shot has the same movement slow and Inhibitor almost the same (25%). [18:24]
- Mystic Slow is the more team fight option: AOE through Tesla, a fire rate penalty instead of a damage penalty (similar to Inhibitor, but Inhibitor is not AOE), and cheaper, though with no health or damage. [18:56]
- Crippling Headshot if enemies stack resist or your team needs more damage: it applies bullet and spirit resist reduction for your whole team on enemies you hit, insane in team fights; he buys it almost as much as Lucky Shot. [18:56]
- Toxic Bullets is not a must; he runs it when at least two or three enemies have a lot of lifesteal. [19:26]
- Toxic Bullets is a must pick against Infernus, who probably has the highest lifesteal in the game. [19:56]
- Lifesteal was nerfed this patch to stack less, so Leech, Vampiric Burst and Bullet Lifesteal together heal less than before, though still a lot. [19:56]
- Otherwise he skips Toxic Bullets; Vampiric Burst's burst or Crippling Headshot usually edges it out. It is still the best Wraith item when you need healing reduction. [20:27]
- Late, with two flex slots, sell Combat Barrier: barriers do less super late; he usually swaps it for Improved Spirit Armor, his most used defensive item because spirit damage is king. [20:59]
- Then Unstoppable if needed, and Soul Rebirth or Colossus. [20:59]
- Glass Cannon is a really risky snowball pick: it makes you less tanky when HP is very good, and you need extra kills to make it worth it; very situational, only if you are super early ahead with lots of farm. [21:29]
- Surge of Power if you need more movement speed; put it on your cards for some extra damage. [22:00]
- Ethereal Shift is still good but was nerfed (can't cancel it early); pop it against stacked debuffs, for example getting Bebop bombed while stunned, to be safe for 3.5 seconds while your team catches up or before you teleport out. Not a must pick any more. [22:00]
- Game plan: Wraith is an insane laner, so win the lane or go even at worst; once you reach Tesla Bullets farm insanely fast and suck the map dry until Hunter's Aura, then you can kill anyone in the game. [22:31]
