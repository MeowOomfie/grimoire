# How the Patch affected LANING - Meta Update
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-10-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s0bCd_dobYI

## Covers
Deathy on how the October 2024 patch changed laning: tougher guardians, earlier medium camps, soul sharing ending at 8 minutes, earlier boxes and golden statues, which heroes it favors and how to spend time in a duo lane.
For players wondering how to lane and farm after that patch. This is a late-2024 video; timings, guardians and soul rules have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Guardians can no longer be hit from the stairs, troopers deal significantly less damage to them early, and guardians deal more damage to heroes, so diving and pressuring the guardian early is a bit harder. [0:00]
- Medium jungle camps now spawn at 5 minutes instead of 7; large camps still spawn at 7 minutes. [0:30]
- Lane souls are now only shared (duplicated) up to the 8 minute mark instead of 10; past 8 minutes, more than one person in a lane splits the souls, so in theory it is inefficient. [0:30]
- Boxes and golden statues now spawn at 2 minutes instead of 3, and golden statue buffs scale much better with time. [1:01]
- The laning meta now favors heroes that push the wave quickly even more: clear the wave and farm one camp between waves to get significantly ahead, since guardians live longer and the jungle spawns earlier. [1:31]
- Favored farming heroes: Seven (Lightning Ball, Power Surge), Lady Geist (Essence Bomb), Pocket (cloak, suitcase and Barrage, all AOE clears) and Ivy (her Thorns); any hero that clears a wave and a camp quickly. [2:02]
- This resembles an older Dota meta where every mid laner had to shove the wave to farm a nearby small camp. [2:02]
- Guardian damage and range changes make pressuring an opponent under tower harder (not drastically): creeps must get closer to the guardian, so the defender can secure them with melees more easily, and diving costs more guardian damage. [2:33]
- Lanes can still snowball fast with a big skill gap (seen in tournaments), but a slightly unfavorable matchup or slightly better opponent can no longer just snowball all over you. [3:04]
- Farming the jungle early, especially tier three camps, is more important now because souls are not duplicated past 8 minutes. [3:34]
- In a duo lane, the only reasons to keep two people in lane past 8 minutes are going for a kill (for example a gank) or taking the tower. [3:34]
- Taking towers is still very important for the flex slot, and harder now, so bring the manpower to take them. [3:34]
- If you can't get a kill or pressure the tower, one of the duo should jungle, or farm boxes or golden statues; that is far better than doing nothing. [4:04]
- Resources can now be funneled into carries more: jungle heroes get ahead faster with the 5 minute camps, and staying in lane past 8 minutes doing nothing can grief your carry by splitting souls. [4:35]
- Grab boxes and golden statues whenever you are not doing anything else. [5:05]
- Boxes and golden statues are important but never worth missing troopers for, at least early on; maybe past 8 minutes since then lane souls are not shared. [5:35]
- Golden statues seem better value than boxes because their drops are more valuable than the souls from boxes. [5:35]
- Scaling per minute: small camps 0.5 souls, medium camps 1 soul, boxes 3 souls; with a box drop rate of roughly 40%, boxes average about 1.2 souls per minute of scaling. [5:35]
- Later in the game boxes may be more efficient than small or medium camps, but doing both is ideal. [6:05]
- Golden statue drop rate is roughly 50% in their testing. [6:05]
- Overall the laning stage has slowed down, with more focus on farm efficiency; winning lane still gives a decent advantage. [6:05]
- If you take the enemy guardian early you can steal their jungle from 5 minutes, snowballing further. [6:36]
- Myth: don't take the guardian early because the enemy can freeze the wave and farm safely. Deathy says they can freeze, but since you can steal their jungle at 5 minutes you should always take the guardian as early as possible, and all guardians give a flex slot; never delay it. [6:36]
