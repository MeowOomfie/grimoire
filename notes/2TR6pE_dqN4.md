channel: Deathy

## Covers
Deathy coaches SirActionSlacks, a former Dota player in low rank on an all-green "Green Abrams" build who never hits waves, first on a replay and then in a live game.
Covers trooper damage benchmarks, playing from the enemy veil, why percentage items like Healing Booster are bad early, investment categories, rotation timing, Sinner's Sacrifices, Trophy Collector and the Abrams core (Phantom Strike, Warp Stone, Spirit Snatch).
For low-rank players, especially ones coming from Dota, who fight and jungle but are always the poorest on their team.

## Claims
- The student's first mistake: more damage to neutrals than to troopers. In this game pushing waves is everything no matter what; there is no real concept of roles like in Dota. [4:06]
- As Abrams your job is definitely not jungling. [4:36]
- Deathy's benchmark is 20,000 trooper damage at 20 minutes; the student had 3,800, and an Abrams in Deathy's own recent game had 22,000. [4:36]
- Fighting and going to the Sinner's Sacrifice is not a bad gameplay loop, but add a wave: wave, Sinner's Sacrifice, fight. [5:06]
- Splitting souls two ways is efficient: with two people in a wave, each gets 65%. [5:06]
- Letting a teammate solo a wave is fine only when you are doing something very important on the map. [5:37]
- Lane tip that "changes your life": instead of standing behind your wave, stand in the enemy veil the whole lane; if they approach you in the veil, charge them. [6:37]
- Do not buy Healing Booster first item: items that give percentage stats are bad early. [7:08]
- On Abrams, Healing Booster does not work on health regen, only on the damage regenerated by Infernal Resilience, for about 2.6% extra; even taking a generous 1,000 damage it heals 150 instead of 130, roughly 25 more HP. [7:40]
- Melee Lifesteal heals about 100 when you hit someone, which is insignificant later in the game but very strong early. [8:10]
- You are always better off buying flat damage early; Mystic Shot, Cold Front and similar flat damage items are meta early for that reason. [8:40]
- While you sit in the veil you are pushing the wave, so your lane partner is not just tanking damage. [8:40]
- A Healing Booster boost on one Healing Rite was about 65 HP, roughly one tick of the healing trooper. [9:11]
- Orange items add weapon investment and green items add vitality investment; returns diminish the more you invest in one category, very much so after 9,600 souls in one category. [9:41]
- Four green items leave you with very little pressure: Healing Rite and Sprint Boots give essentially none and Healing Booster does nearly nothing, so 2,400 souls went into items that don't let you kill. [9:41]
- Close Quarters gives 20% weapon damage plus 7% from weapon investment, about 27% weapon damage total. [10:12]
- Weapon damage also applies to your melees at half value. [10:12]
- For purple (spirit) items on this Abrams, buy Cold Front and Spirit Strike. [10:44]
- Don't invest everything in one category, especially all green, because you can't really kill anyone. [10:44]

- If you gank, it is generally better to help a lane that is winning: comeback souls are much more generous than in Dota, and ganking a lane that is being snowballed is risky because if you die both lanes get rolled. [12:44]
