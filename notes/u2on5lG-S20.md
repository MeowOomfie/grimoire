# Educational Eternus Seven Gameplay Commentary #2 - Ft. MikaelS & Lystic
channel: Natan  date: 2025-01-17  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u2on5lG-S20

## Covers
Seven, duo lane with Grey Talon vs Haze and Lash, Power Surge first vs ball, Cold Front camp timing, 8K and 11K spikes, snowballing a lead, refusing teammates' bad fights, Mid Boss calls, Ethereal Shift, Patron defence, Bebop, Yamato, Lady Geist

## Claims
- A duo lane of Seven and Grey Talon into Haze and Lash should be easy: Haze is a weak duo laner, Lash is strong only with a strong duo, and both Seven and Grey Talon are long range and can poke safely. [0:30]
- He maxes Power Surge (his 3) first for much more early power; he calls maxing ball "grief" because you cannot play the game before about 20 minutes, when the game is often already decided at this rank. [1:33]
- The camp spawns at 2 minutes and the boxes at 3 minutes; he likes picking them up. [2:04]
- He slides to extend his magazine. [2:42]
- He picks up Cold Front before 5 minutes because the tier 2 camps around the lane spawn at 5, so he can farm them faster. [3:42]
- At 5 minutes he clears the wave, takes the camp quickly with Cold Front and goes straight back to the wave. [4:24]
- Clearing a camp before the wave was not the correct play; clear the wave first. [4:56]
- He was hesitant to push up while the enemy Lash had ult; once he saw the Lash on another lane he knew he could push. [5:27]
- He should not have gone for the Bebop because he lacked the damage; Bebop has innate spirit resist and there is no killing him before 8K. [6:29]
- At 8K you unlock tier 3 Power Surge and can really start playing the game; the next goal is 11K for Surge of Power, which adds about 100 DPS to the 3. [6:59]
- At 8K and ahead, he makes a play to take Guardians even with the whole jungle up; with this build you do not have to AFK farm, and a lead should be used to snowball and end quickly. [6:59]
- When he gets low the play is over and he leaves. [8:00]
- The build is not amazing at tier 3 camps this early, but it works, just a little slowly. [8:00]
- The ult is kind of a useless ability with this build, so use it to clear camps. [8:32]
- After the camps he gets back to the wave instantly so the souls do not go to waste. [8:32]
- He ignored a teammate's call to push a Walker because he was half HP, carrying unsecured souls and playing Seven; he pushed his wave and cleared camps instead. [9:03]
- At this point he had 13K while the rest of the lobby was at 11K or lower. [9:36]
- He builds cooldown reduction because using his 3 on camps left him without it for a fight. [10:08]
- An enemy at 10K who was not paying attention was one shot by him at 15K. [10:40]
- Using his zipline boost to defend a Walker nobody was actually hitting was a mistake, because he no longer had it when he might need it. [11:11]
- At almost twice an enemy's souls, a pick is a free kill. [11:55]
- When a teammate goes to die for a Walker, the worst thing is to join him; two deaths give the enemy a free Mid Boss. [12:29]
- Walking into the enemy as they head for the Urn while half your team is on the other side of the map is very risky. [12:29]
- He refuses to get sucked into bad decisions, because if you die as well you are the one throwing. [12:59]
- Calling Mid Boss when the enemy lacked key ults was not a bad call; they lost it to bad coordination when the Grey Talon went back to base at full HP. [14:05]
- After losing three Walkers while still even, the game is winnable; you just chill and farm the camps that are up. [14:35]
- His team should not fight while the enemy still has the Rejuvenator up; they need to chill and not get caught. [15:05]
- He did not chase an overextended enemy when he could not see who was behind her. [15:05]
- He buys Ethereal Shift against a double stake Vindicta build with chemical fire. [16:39]
- While his team chain died, he made sure he was not the one dying because he has the wave clear to farm and hold the base. [17:41]
- He called Mid Boss right away because the enemy Yamato was respawning soon and they did not want her ult up; with a few kills he stops the team chasing more kills. [18:11]
- Buy Ethereal Shift almost every game, if not every game; with his cooldown items it was 21 seconds, so it can be used twice in a fight. [18:47]
- An overextended Lash is a free kill with Slowing Hex and stun. [19:17]
- After the Urn they push for shrines; he died trying to force out the enemy, but that relieved pressure and they got the flex slot, which is all they came for. [19:52]
- He will not hit camps alone with the enemy Haze nearby, because they could hear him and collapse while he might not hear them. [20:22]
- He should have stunned the Haze instead of the Lady Geist; then he could have killed the Haze. [20:52]
- Before pushing deep he checks enemy ult cooldowns (Yamato and Lady Geist had none); Ethereal Shift lets him push really deep when his team can follow. [21:23]
- After taking the Patron objective they back off and plan for the next Mid Boss. [21:54]
- Improved Spirit Armor and Improved Bullet Armor with decent lifesteal and Ethereal Shift every 20 seconds made him almost immortal. [21:54]
- The game is never over until your weakened Patron falls; giving the first Patron phase is fine as long as nobody dies, so all six are up to defend the second phase, and at even souls you can win that fight. [23:57]
- When behind he clears waves in base and waits for enemy mistakes; making sure you do not lose is a great way to win more games. [24:58]
- At 35 minutes with two enemies dead for 70 seconds they called the end; he pushed the wave himself because you need the wave to hit the Patron. [25:30]
- In the final push he jumps into the pit even if he dies, to get as much damage on the Patron as possible. [26:01]
