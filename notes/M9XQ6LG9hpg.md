# Calico Doesn't Fight Fair
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-08-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M9XQ6LG9hpg

## Covers
Calico, aquatiq, commentary over one ranked game on a spirit burst build (2026-08-30 patch).
Light reasoning while playing: item choices, tunnel boxes and cat form, Calico's weak early lane, and target priority into squishies.

## Claims
- He plays A Bell's spirit build, not gun Calico; gun Calico is not a bad build. [0:31]
- With squishies on the enemy team (Mina, Paige, Seven) the burst build can one-shot people, but Calico is very squishy herself. [0:31]
- Stalker is his next buy after the opening items. [1:03]
- Calico is useless early in lane: all she can do is poke with her gun until she gets Stalker. [1:33]
- Lash can carry, Haze cannot, Wraith can. [2:34]
- Calico can only very slowly burst people down early. [3:05]
- Once laning allows, he goes straight to the tunnel boxes; one tunnel leads behind the guardian. [3:35]
- Spirit Strike next, for the bursts on her 2. [3:35]
- He got boxes really early so his Wraith gets her ult early. [4:05]
- When playing Calico with a carry in your lane, go straight for boxes almost every time and let the carry take the lane. [5:08]
- Cold Front is his next buy. [5:40]
- He waited for the enemy's parry before committing in melee range. [7:43]
- Against burst damage he wants Spirit Shielding; he buys Enchanter's Emblem. [8:43]
- Use cat form to travel through the tunnels. [9:14]
- Inside the tunnels the enemy can't see you, so you can sit there safely during a fight. [10:15]
- Don't go for that objective when it is too resisted and many enemies are still there. [11:20]
- He takes Enduring Speed over Fleetfoot so he does not have to press a button. [11:50]
- Leave a Wraith who can fight on her own and go help another lane. [12:20]
- Calico can't be in fights nearly as much as other heroes and can't tank much HP; you can't play her like Wraith or Warden. [12:51]
- Stay in cat form as long as possible; he never wants to be out of it. [13:21]
- Priority targets are Mina and Seven. [14:22]
- The tunnels let you just leave a fight. [14:52]
- Once Calico has used her abilities, that's all she can do until they come back. [19:30]
- With enemies dead, take the free mid boss. [20:01]
- Try to execute low-HP targets with her 2. [20:31]
- Buy Arctic Blast for the active; he regretted using it on minions. [21:02]
- Wait for Arctic Blast to be up before engaging. [21:35]
- In the tunnels she doesn't show on the enemy's map, so they don't know she is there. [23:42]
- Spirit Burn and a cooldown item come next; with Spirit Burn he expects big damage. [24:42]
- His teammate's call: fight the enemy outside the Rift. [24:42]
- Don't take mid when the enemy's big ults are up and their Abrams has max ult. [26:46]
- Later in the game he needs greens to avoid being instantly killed. [28:55]
