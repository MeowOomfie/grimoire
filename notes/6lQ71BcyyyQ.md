# My initial thoughts on the new patch - July 31st
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-07-31
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQ71BcyyyQ

## Covers
Deathy's first impressions of the late July 2025 hotfix after 5 to 10 games: the end of the deathball meta (walker teleports removed, trooper soul split, faster troopers, neutral value), melee and parry nerfs, the new mid boss with Rejuvenators, Spirit vs buildings, Tankbuster and Cheat Death changes.
Ends with his heroes of the patch (Mirage, Pocket, Yamato, Vindicta). For any rank, patch dated July 31, 2025.

## Claims
- The previous patch was a deathball meta: groups of four to six fought big fights because the souls from kills paid off and little was given up on the map. This hotfix moves the game toward smaller groups, objectives and stealing jungle. [0:30]
- The walker teleports were removed, so you can no longer defend one walker with five people and instantly show up at the other; macro and wave management matter more and grouping up is riskier. [1:30]
- The trooper soul split for allies was reduced once three or more share a wave: about 2% less per person at three, more at four (he believes 5%), and more again at five. The more people share a wave, the more you are punished. [2:01]
- The trooper split change also punishes a team stuck holding its base, since they share wave souls five or six ways and get far fewer souls than before. [2:32]
- Comeback mechanics were reduced by an unknown amount, so winning a fight gives fewer comeback souls than before. [3:02]
- Troopers now sprint much faster, so a lane left unattended becomes a problem sooner. [3:02]
- Neutral camps are worth more souls each but spawn less often by about the same amount, so they are not worth more per minute; a stolen neutral camp gives about 15% more souls than before, which helps you hit early power spikes. [3:32]
- Verdict: play in small groups and steal enemy neutrals early, which the patch incentivizes even more than before. [4:03]
- He likes the changes because they reward macro and smaller fights across the map; the TP removal is unpopular with players who want constant teamfights. [4:34]
- Melee was hit very hard: turn speed nerfed and the time between heavy melees nerfed. [6:06]
- Parry stun duration is no longer reduced by debuff resistance, so a parried player is stunned for 3 seconds with nothing to shorten it. [6:06]
- The parry damage amp was 35% on melee hits only; it is now 30% on all damage, so a parried player takes 30% more damage from everything and usually dies unless playing a very tanky hero. [6:36]
- Full melee builds are now unlikely on heroes with no way to secure melees. Abrams is still fine because Shoulder Charge secures his melee and has a very low cooldown when maxed. [7:06]
- Yamato can still build melee because she is not stunned during her ultimate, so occasional parries are not the end of the world. [7:36]
- Melee Shiv is basically dead at a high level: Bloodletting's duration was nerfed and parries were buffed a lot; it may still be viable in average games. [7:36]
- Many items that give resistances were nerfed, including Melee Charge, Fleetfoot, Return Fire and Cold Front. Melee was strong partly because items like Crushing Fist (20% bullet resist) and Spirit Snatch gave lots of resist on top of damage. [8:07]
- Bebop, Shiv, Seven, Mirage, Warden, Yamato and Vindicta received mostly small nerfs; none are killed. Vindicta, Mirage and Seven should stay strong, especially Seven for the average player. [8:38]
- Warden is probably decent but has started to fall off. [9:09]
- Yamato's early right click damage in lane is more manageable but still very oppressive; Bebop is still fine. [9:09]
- The mid boss is completely different, neither better nor worse: it now drops three Rejuvenators that give a second life and last 7 minutes. [9:40]
- The old mid boss gave the whole team half respawn timers and mega creeps; those were removed, so a team that wins the fight but loses the mid boss still comes out on top. [10:10]
- Rejuvenator holders get bonuses such as bonus health and bonus spirit, so it is good for every hero type, including spirit carries. [11:12]
- Objectives no longer have spirit resistance, so spirit heroes can now take walkers quickly instead of relying on punching or gun heroes like Wraith or McGinnis. [11:43]
- Spirit vs buildings was buffed: Sinclair with a bolt build pretty much one-shots walkers right now, as at his original release; he expects that to be changed quickly. [12:14]
- Viscous, Wraith, Pocket, Paradox, Lady Geist, Grey Talon and Dynamo got slight buffs. [12:45]
- Viscous got cooldown changes; Wraith got slightly more early gun damage with the same late game damage; Pocket got a little more damage on Affliction. [13:15]
- Paradox got 10% more gun damage, which is significant and might bring him back. [13:15]
- Lady Geist got changes to her bomb (Essence Bomb), Grey Talon got small buffs, and Dynamo's 1 got a height buff so it is harder to jump over. [13:15]
- Cheat Death now works against executes; before, a Grey Talon or Shiv on the enemy team made it pointless because they would just instantly kill you. [13:45]
- Tankbuster now ignores resistances. Tested: its damage is amplified by damage amps such as Pocket's Barrage, but not by shred; it does the same damage to a target at -40% or +40% spirit resist. [14:16]
- That makes Tankbuster a real counter tank item for spirit heroes late game, when people sit at 40, 50 or even 60% spirit resist (for example with Spellbreaker). [14:47]
- Vyper and Wraith are probably underrated and quite strong right now depending on the game. [15:18]
- Hero picks for the patch (a best guess from 5 to 10 games, no tournaments yet): Mirage stays really strong because the TP removal makes his ultimate more valuable, though his early game is a bit worse. [16:19]
- Pocket benefits from the Affliction buff and the Tankbuster change; Affliction is still insanely strong and Divine Barrier is still its only counter, which is hard to obtain and justify. [16:49]
- Yamato's nerfs are mostly fake: her laning is worse but still incredibly oppressive and she wins lane against almost every hero. [16:49]
- The Vindicta nerfs are also mostly fake; she will stay really strong. Seven and others are contenders too. [17:19]
- Skipped as pure opinion: his wish for mega troopers to return on another objective or Shrines and that the game needs more objectives. [10:40]
