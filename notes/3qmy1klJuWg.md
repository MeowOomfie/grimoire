## Claims
- Baba wants a teammate's sleep on the target so she can land her 1 ("I need you to sleep him so I can hit my one"). [2:03]
- In lane, keep zoning the enemy duo off the wave from one side and trade when they walk up. [3:07]
- When a lane cannot be held, give the tower rather than die defending it. [6:41]
- When the enemy takes their double camps, secure your own doubles in the same window. [8:29]
- When the enemy is about to take an objective you cannot contest and you are up gold, take camps instead of forcing the fight. [12:45]
- Play the Urn pickup with the team. [15:19]
- After taking the double camps, come straight to mid to try for the Walker. [17:54]
- When Rift is up and you are not fighting, take the vaults. [19:56]
- When not strong enough to fight (waiting on a Plated item), play your own side of the map together. [20:30]
- Wait until a teammate finishes their Echo Shard before grouping all six; do not fight before that. [23:03]
- Fight ahead of the Rift spawn, but only with good waves; bad waves make it hard. [26:07]
- Read the enemy's options before a Rift: play Rift with their Rat King, cross map, vault, or fog. [26:37]
- At low team health, play together and do not leave anyone on the opposite side of the map. [28:39]
- Note when the enemy Rat King has no banner (ult) available and fight then. [28:39]
- You cannot six-man mid until the side lane waves are pushed. [31:16]
- Do not overextend and get lassoed; that was the only way they could lose, and a Debuff Remover answers the lasso. [33:17]
- Better play: take the Rift and then push with the Rift minions while the advantage lasts. [33:49]
- When the enemy has six ults up and must make a play, get off the map, fix your waves and wait. [34:19]
- After the enemy Rat King ult is used, push the waves out and look for the next play. [34:52]
- When your comp wins hard late, do not force; push waves and keep suffocating the enemy's farm. [35:22]
- With mid boss 40 seconds away, play mid while your side waves are good. [36:58]
- After picking up the green wave, walk into the base with the wave since they cannot kill you with the last wave. [37:30]
- Post game: the only real mistake in the whole game was not getting that Rift. [38:34]
