# How to Build and Play Paige | Alchemist Paige Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-08-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W0VbR1tTzc4

## Covers
Paige coaching review by Midknight (former top 10 Mirage, free coach) of an Alchemist-rank Paige player (Eka).
A 2025 video, recorded the day before Drifter's release (late August 2025 patch).
Topics: Paige ult usage, max order, core and defensive items, her squishiness, lane partners, farming tech and fighting with the team.

## Claims
- Do not kill minions from so far away that the orb is easily denied. [0:30]
- Prefer a barrier (ability 2) start over a dragon (ability 1) start: Paige has low bullet velocity, so she must go close to get orbs and melee, and the barrier lets her do that safely. [0:30]
- Lining up melees on two minions, barriering yourself, then meleeing two more saves denies and pushes the wave. [1:32]
- Skip Restorative Shot first: it is an orange (weapon) item, and on hyper squishy Paige with bad health scaling, Extra Regen's 8% max health is far better than 7% weapon damage. [1:32]
- Restorative Shot is also hard to land on Paige because her bullet velocity is so slow. [2:35]
- Do not buy boots first; if you want a movement item take Extra Stamina, which upgrades into Arcane Surge, a great item on her. [2:35]
- Preferred maxes: barrier max in lane; for full support, 3 max into barrier max, or barrier then 3 then ult, or 3 then ult then barrier; a full spirit build maxing 1 is acceptable in solo queue. [3:05]
- Only use 3 for aggressive poke when you know the enemy will step back into it. [3:37]
- Do not push forward without parry; Paige is hyper squishy with poor base HP and terrible HP scaling. [4:38]
- Enemies should always jump on Paige when they can, like a Yamato or a Mina without her 2, because she disintegrates. [4:38]
- Barriering yourself and then doing nothing is not worth it; barrier to go into minions and melee them. [5:08]
- The dragon is really good for taking towers. [5:40]
- The coach expects Paige to be at least A tier, likely S tier in pro play, picked or banned almost every game. [6:10]
- Sprint Boots become redundant; for a movement-based defensive item Warp Stone is better. [7:10]
- Paige should have at least one defensive item every game: Cultist, Counterspell or Warp Stone are what high elo builds. [7:10]
- Counterspell is rarer; buy it into heroes with counterspellable all-ins like Shiv, Seven or Pocket. [7:10]
- Cultist gives a lot of max HP, fire rate and ability range, all things Paige likes. [7:40]
- Arcane Surge is preferred over Extra Spirit; avoid buying too many tier 1 items. [7:40]
- Lane partner matters a lot: Paige wants a strong laner with good all-in potential who jumps in the enemy's face. [8:43]
- Good partners: Yamato (likes the shield for all-ins) and Warden; M1 heroes like Infernus are fine; Haze is less good since she does not go in. [9:14]
- Doorman is not a good Paige partner: he is a spirit laner who benefits less from her weapon buffs and does not jump in; Calico would be the better pick in the reviewed game. [9:44]
- Guardian Ward is a great support item: extra shield, upgrades into Divine Barrier, and used at the same time as her 2 it keeps the buff applied longer. [9:14]
- With a strong all-in partner, double shield them for a big buff and lots of damage. [9:44]
- Gun Paige with Split Shot will be like early Gun Ivy: fun for a few one-tricks, not seen in pro play. [10:46]
- Put abilities on instant cast, not confirmed cast; for 3, just place it slightly behind enemies and learn the AoE. [11:17]
- Slowing Hex is a great item into Mina, but do not look for a 1v1 on Mina; you have no chance of solo killing her. [11:49]
- Wall jump forward when you are being dove and slowed. [12:19]
- Never try to 1v1 on Paige, even against someone at 20% HP, especially on their side of the map. [12:50]
- Mid skip: go to the sign, diagonal jump, diagonal air jump, then double jump slide back onto the rope for a faster return to mid. [12:50]
- Slowing Hex is great into Infernus and Mina but rarely built because of its short cast range and because Paige does not want Greater Expansion. [13:25]
- Build Mystic Expansion for her 3 (better AoE), but not Greater Expansion: shield range is low value, trail and stun AoE are good, and range is useless on her ult. [13:25]
- If you go a gun build you must commit early; buying Split Shot late is too late. [13:55]
- High elo players nearly all max 3 instead of 1, because the AoE stun is great and the minus 18% spirit resist is huge. [14:25]
- When farming, line up your crosshair to hit two minions with each auto; she is a support who farms very well and has decent melee scaling. [14:25]
- Do not push a Walker when you are losing your player advantage; with an enemy respawning in base, someone will rotate and the squad will appear. [15:28]
- Vending machine tech: heavy melee one machine, farm the next normally, then hold melee without letting go and you instantly farm the next one, because machines share a server timer. [16:28]
- When farming a camp, stand at the edge of the first creep's hitbox to melee both creeps. [17:29]
- Great items on Paige: Mystic Expansion for 3, Guardian Ward for 2, Arcane Surge before 3 (range), before 1 (damage) or before the ult (damage and healing). [18:31]
- Improved Spirit is good for lane damage; Rescue Beam is a strong item; build Guardian Ward into Divine Barrier. [19:02]
- Vortex Web is commonly built: it keeps people in your 1 and pulls them into your 3 and your ult. [19:32]
- Refresher is seen in the very late game; an item captioned "Herald's" is good in mid fights where everyone is grouped. [20:02]
- Do not split push on Paige; she has no mobility and explodes when touched; play with your team as backline support. [21:09]
- Fortitude gives little value on Paige; she is rarely at medium to low HP, since if you lose 30% of your HP you usually lose all of it. [21:42]
- Fortitude is more justifiable on Mina because her 2 lets her escape at low HP; Paige has no such movement. [23:15]
- Taking a bag is fine at low elo in solo queue, though as a support you should be last to take it; full farm first. [23:46]
- Use your full combo on the creeps before going to the sentries. [23:46]
- When a Paradox uses a vertical movement ability, 3 immediately; she is locked in and cannot escape the radius. [23:46]
- A defensive item like that should come much earlier; with two defensive items already, buy offense, healing or support items (Divine Barrier, Rescue Beam). [24:16]
- Healing Nova is rarely built on Paige because she cannot position aggressively enough. [24:46]
- Spirit offense for 1: upgrade Extra Spirit into Improved Spirit or Boundless Spirit; Surge of Power fits a Split Shot build for the fire rate. [24:46]
- A Split Shot plus Cultist build has lots of fire rate but little base weapon damage, and Split Shot stacks are inconsistent. [25:16]
- An item captioned "Sam master" (likely Superior Stamina) is not a good pseudo-defensive item when you already have plenty of defense. [25:46]
- Get Transcendent Cooldown when you have many actives (Counterspell, Warp Stone, Cultist, Split Shot, Slowing Hex), especially when slots are tight. [26:17]
- Spirit Burn would give a lot of value into Bebop, Warden and Infernus and okay value into Paradox and Haze. [26:48]
- Ult usage: do not hold it for huge multi-hero hits; aim for four and settle for three. [27:53]
- Do not use the ult as a solo attempt on one target with no payoff. [27:53]
- Exceptions for small ults: a make or break kill or save with about 80% confidence, or a one-man stop on something like a Warden ult or a counter-ult on a Dynamo. [28:53]
- Alchemical Fire feels great on Paige: place it around your 1 for more damage, or before or after the ult and 3 to trap enemies in it; it shreds 20% bullet resist so the team can melt them. [31:04]
- Do not take a technically even 4v4 when two enemy waves are pushing into your base; the fight is not neutral. [32:08]
- Do not front line on Paige; sit about fourth in line as the fight rolls in. [33:11]
- When your team is down three, run; do not stay to end with three against three alive and two respawning in under 10 seconds. [35:22]
- If your team insists on contesting the Urn, ping retreat, but go anyway; the fight is far worse without you, and at minimum lob your ult toward them. [37:00]
- When the enemy has Rejuvenator, tuck instead of pushing up aggressively, and give the Urn. [37:00]
- Do not contest an Urn the enemy will clearly get; just give it. [38:48]
- Doorman's ult: you do not need to shoot until the key turns, about 1 second before the target comes back. [39:20]
- When up a kill, fix your waves, buy at the secret shop and push aggressively. [40:20]
- Do not buy Unstoppable or Juggernaut on Paige; Unstoppable mostly helps against Infernus ults, which Warp Stone should solve. [40:20]
- When a teammate dies and you are the only one showing on the map pushed up, you are the free kill; back off. [41:20]
- If your team postures for a fight you need to be there; ideally fight in your base, not outside it. [42:21]
- Notes for the player: tune up ult usage, fix the building, and improve map awareness so you are at key team fights instead of farming across the map. [43:23]
- Judging by a play style not built on barriers, max dragon (1) first, then the 3, then the ult. [43:54]
- Recommended build: Deathy's Paige build; Paige is one-dimensional, just support items, health items and more support items. [44:24]
- Core of that build: Alchemical Fire with 3, Arcane Surge before abilities, Divine Barrier (clears Pocket ults, curses, Wraith), Rescue Beam, Guardian Ward with 2 for the extra shield and buff. [44:55]
