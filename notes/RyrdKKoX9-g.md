# A TERRIFYING Balance Patch has hit Deadlock...
channel: Eido
date: 2026-10-31
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RyrdKKoX9-g

## Covers
Patch reaction by Eido to the 2026-10-31 balance patch: mid boss and Rejuvenator rework, Haunted Souls and comeback souls, item changes (Radiant Regeneration, Juggernaut, Spellbreaker, Shadow Weave) and hero-by-hero changes with his verdicts.
Not a guide or coaching; he reads the patch notes live and tries a few changes in game. Hero names are often misheard in the captions; unclear ones are marked.

## Claims
- The mid boss area is bigger and has more cover. [0:00]
- Mid boss health and health gain per minute increased; resistance reductions on it are now 50% as effective. [0:31]
- The Rejuvenator now drops as three crystals placed evenly around the mid boss pit instead of one in the center; each crystal breaks in one heavy melee. [0:31]
- Each Rejuvenator crystal gives a bounty to the player who breaks it, so the souls moved from the mid boss kill into the individual crystals; the kill is still worth some souls and stats. [0:31]
- Verdict: they fixed or are trying to fix mid boss so it is no longer a pile-up in the pit; there is now a vantage point and cover to hide from incoming damage. [1:02]
- Haunt base bounty and growth increased by 10%. [1:34]
- Breakables value reduced; he likes the change. [2:05]
- Haunted Souls secure 25% slower, but up to 50% faster than baseline based on how far behind your team is in net worth versus the enemy team average. [2:05]
- Comeback souls from non-hero sources raised from a max of 26% to 30%. [2:05]
- Healing snacks heal increased (about 50% as read) and seats now increase stamina generation. [2:35]
- Movement slow to air drag ratio increased from 0.35 to 0.5, so slowed heroes are slower in the air; verdict: healthy, since there has been movement creep and slows should be effective. [2:35]
- The six recent heroes are no longer marked new: they have normal unlock requirements, are playable in ranked and can be banned. [2:35]
- Shadow Weave now has +50% ambush weapon damage; ambush bonuses build up over 6 seconds of stealth before full power; abilities used to break stealth now get the ambush bonus on their own damage. [3:07]
- Anti-heal was buffed across the board; chat says Healbane now stacks. [3:07]
- Radiant Regeneration: regen on spirit damage reduced, heal on cast now only triggers in combat, healing and boon scaling reduced. Verdict: the item was killed; with anti-heal also buffed he does not see why you would buy it now. [3:07]
- Echo Shard buffed again; maybe bomb Paradox is back, but the 0.5 second delay felt horrible and he does not think the item feels viable. [4:09]
- Lightning Scroll (captioned "lightning spell") now damages with all skills scaled by spirit power; maybe it becomes viable. [4:09]
- Juggernaut health regen increased (from +8 +8 to +15 as read); verdict: tanks should rush Juggernaut as their third item, the stats are disgusting. [4:09]
- Spellbreaker: when it procs it now keeps reducing the follow-up spirit damage in the damage stack; verdict: huge buff. [4:09]
- Abrams base gun damage increased by 5%, Siphon Life DPS increased, and a fix so Shoulder Charge holds targets in place longer if you do not hit a wall; verdict: very good change. [4:39]
- Apollo Riposte pommel hit changed from a stun to an immobilize plus parry disable; disable duration increased from 0.8 to 1.5 seconds; tier two disable duration increased. [4:39]
- Apollo fixes: counterattack dash no longer misses its target or launches away, including when Apollo is on top of the target; being stunned or put to sleep during the ability refunds it and keeps the recast. Verdict: great quality of life changes. [4:39]
- With Riposte no longer a stun there is counterplay: if you dispel his Riposte you can parry when he heavy attacks. [5:10]
- Baba: spirit resist scaling reduced; Thread Sap spirit steal duration reduced by 6 seconds, and cleansing the Thread Sap debuff also removes the matching spirit gain. Verdict: massive nerf, it was broken. [5:10]
- A hero captioned as "the great long legs": light melee knockback distance reduced, heavy melee cooldown reduced, heavy melee stomp now only hits the closest Rejuvenator crystal in range so you cannot triple hit them, heavy melee slow duration reduced. [5:42]
- Baba's Brew no longer has an initial brew time and now gives a random effect, like a Twist of Fate. [6:12]
- Rising Ram tier three changed from +10% max health for 60 seconds to +15% for 20 seconds, plus a cooldown reduction. [6:12]
- Leaping Slash buffed by 30 on one hero (name unclear in captions); he says that hero's bigger problem is not being able to hit heavy boxes, which he calls the most broken thing in the game right now. [6:44]
- Danny: jetpack can no longer be used while cursed; items and abilities that pull to the ground work more reliably against him; slows reduce the jetpack's in-air impulse more, and even more if movement abilities are silenced. [6:44]
- Doorman Luggage Cart tier two raised from +75 to +90 damage and tier three gets a cooldown change; he does not think the cart was a problem. [7:14]
- Drifter gun increased, Bloodscent tier three increased by 1%, Eternal Night duration increased. [7:14]
- Graves: move speed reduced, bullet damage per boon reduced, Grasping Hands can now be double jumped over, deploy time increased from 0.6 to 0.8, the ult tier two no longer has its 0.3 second spawn time, tier three changed. Verdict: a real nerf to Graves. [7:44]
- Grey Talon Charged Shot: tier two charge delay change, tier three change, and the shot can now bounce once off a wall; Guided Owl targeting improved. He likes both. [8:15]

- Lash: Essence Bomb spirit scaling increased, Grapple tier three now increases jump velocity, targeting indicator changed from a cone to a cylinder to match the real range. Verdict: mostly a gun Lash buff. [9:16]
- McGinnis Mini Turrets nerfed. [9:46]
