## Covers
Guide to the Soul Urn: its value, comeback mechanic, courier rules, when to run it and when not to, and two high level tricks for controlling the drop-off location.
moe9times, educational guide, no one coached. Numbers as of the January 2025 patch.

## Claims
- The Urn spawns at 10 minutes and every 5 minutes after. [0:30]
- It gives a team 700 souls plus 230 souls per minute of game time, so the first 10 minute Urn is worth 3,000 souls and each later one adds about 1,150. [0:30]
- On delivery 60% of the value is shared immediately to the team and 40% floats out as orbs. [1:01]
- The deliverer gets a 25% soul bonus and an ability point; the whole team gets a random golden statue buff. [1:01]
- The Urn has a comeback mechanic that gives the losing team extra souls depending on how far behind they are. [1:01]
- After the first Urn, if there is a 10% soul difference the drop-off location favors the team that is behind. [1:32]
- The courier gains a little sprint speed (2 if ahead, 6 if behind) but cannot use teleporters or ziplines and is silenced and disarmed. [1:32]
- If the courier is spotted by minions or enemies they are revealed on the minimap, and after 25 seconds they are revealed regardless. [2:03]
- A heavy melee or any stun forces the courier to drop the Urn. [2:03]
- The risk of running the Urn can be greater than the reward; running it carelessly hands the enemy its souls. [2:33]
- Run it when you have a numbers advantage (after a good fight or a lane snowballing): you threaten a bad fight for the enemy and get a free Urn, a won fight or both. [3:05]
- Run it when you are stronger than the enemy team and want to force a fight; riskier depending on the strength gap. [3:35]
- When behind, if the enemy is not paying attention, grab it and run it to the favorable drop-off where your team can group and secure comeback souls. [3:35]
- Watch where the enemies are; if you see six of them away from the Urn and drop-off, try to sneak it. In desperate games, sneak it while they do mid boss. [3:35]
- Don't leave right after delivering: melee the floating souls quickly or an enemy Haze will take the 40%. [4:35]
- Don't run it whenever you feel like it; analyze the situation and communicate with your team. [4:35]
- Don't ignore the Urn either, or you cannot extend a lead or come back as easily. [4:35]
- If you are 10% behind and the enemy can barge into your side of the map, be extremely careful or skip the Urn. [5:05]
- If your team will not move to the drop-off despite comms and pings, do not run it; free enemy souls are not worth it. [5:05]
- Always assume the whole enemy team will come to stop you; if they are not on the map, assume they are guarding the drop-off. [5:36]
- You can drop the Urn whenever needed, for example to join a fight, but not where the enemy can easily reach it. [5:36]
- Drop-off math: take the behind team's souls and add 10% (150K becomes 165K); if the winning team has more than that, it spawns in the behind team's favorable spot. [5:36]
- Run the Urn after winning a fight when you cannot end the game; often one person can run it in the background while the rest take objectives like flex slots. [6:08]
- If you can take mid boss after a won fight, that is probably more important; running the Urn after Rejuvenator is easier because buffed minions push out the enemy lanes. [6:08]
- You can run the Urn to force a fight, which can let you close out games. [6:39]
- Heroes and items that run it faster: Ivy's ultimate, Kelvin's Ice Path, and a Haze ability (unclear in the captions). [6:39]
- Trick 1: when net worth is within 10% but you expect to start winning, pick up and immediately drop the Urn to lock the balanced drop-off location. [7:10]
- This denies the enemy the favorable drop-off if they pick it up, and they are revealed immediately on pickup, so you can track them the whole way. [7:41]
- Trick 2: if you are holding the Urn when the next one would spawn (for example 15 minutes), the next one does not spawn and the drop-off position stays unchanged for that bracket; this can be repeated. [7:41]
