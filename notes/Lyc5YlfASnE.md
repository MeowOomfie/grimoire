# Valve Refuses to Nerf This Character
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-13
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lyc5YlfASnE

## Covers
Warden gameplay commentary by aquatiq in a lane against Geist and Wraith, with item choices and ability leveling talked through live.
Main points: how strong Wraith is right now, the Binding Word cage now silencing at max level, and an off-meta Escalating Resilience buy.
Patch era March 2026 (new gun sounds, Drifter stacks change mentioned).

## Claims
Warden:
- A lane into Geist and Wraith is terrible for Warden; he expected to lose it and bought healing early. [1:01]
- Against early lane damage he considered Spirit Resilience or Spirit Shielding but skipped them because the enemies were hitting him with gun damage. [2:01]
- He bought High-Velocity Rounds early to help hit minions and heroes. [1:31]
- What Warden needs early is Monster Rounds; he has little use for a movement buff item. [4:04]
- Use Healing Rite on a teammate who is low in lane. [5:05]
- He bought Silencer, calling it useful against a lot of the enemy heroes. [7:37]
- He considered Glass Cannon and going straight gun. [8:09]
- He dropped Battle Vest once he was past it. [9:09]
- Binding Word (the cage) upgrade text he read: minus 18 second cooldown, and it now silences enemies until they escape; he calls the cage busted after the change. [9:09]
- He maxed the cage and found a caged enemy immobilized and silenced, which let him take a fight he otherwise would not. [12:13]
- He bought Mercurial Magnum mid game, an item he had not used in a while. [11:13]
- Later he maxed his 3 and his 1, getting minus 7 cooldown, plus 2 radius and extra fire rate, which he found really nice. [14:52]
- Warden's fire rate is a little hard to manage, especially without Titanic Magazine. [14:52]
- Duration Extender on the ward/cage is useless if enemies buy a debuff remover, so he put the duration upgrade on his strongest ability, the ult. [16:53]
- Fleetfoot is very fun on this hero. [17:57]
- He tried Escalating Resilience for the first time on Warden because the enemies were doing gun damage to him, noting it also gives ammo. [18:28]
- He felt short on HP mid game and wanted Bookworm. [20:02]
- He bought Leech later in the game. [24:08]
- Late he bought Refresher and wanted Spellbreaker; he finally got Silencer 34 minutes in. [27:47]
- Warden's money scales his damage a lot, so earning souls matters more than kills. [14:22]

Matchups:
- Wraith is overtuned right now; keep her poor, because with money she is very scary, though she is still strong early from her sustain. [6:35]
- The enemy Wraith had Mystic Expansion early, which is why her abilities hit from so far. [3:34]
- Shiv is scary because he can still kill you even when he is poor. [17:25]
- Paradox walls are big and scary to play against. [19:32]
- He banned Rem because Rem's minions end up with huge HP and he did not know how to play against it. [4:04]
- Check whether the enemy has Juggernaut before diving. [26:10]

Macro:
- Take your own camps before the enemy eats them. [7:05]
- When the enemy sends everyone to one lane, take the free walker elsewhere; they lost the blue walker that way. [21:33]
- Blue lane is really important to hold; your walker existing gives you a lot of map control. [22:03]
- Drifter having kills is probably better than you having them because he gets stacks; Drifter now gets stacks from dealing damage, not only from kills. [22:03]
- A poor enemy team will take more risks. [24:39]
- Coordinate ults: wait for an ally's ult and stack yours on top for a big double ult. [5:35]
