## Covers
Seven, aquatiq, commentary over one game laning with Bebop against Grey Talon and Rem.
Item choices, lane combos and hero tier opinions. Patch of April 2026.

## Claims
- Get Monster Rounds early on Seven. [1:02]
- Healbane as the second item is good, especially against Rem; it also heals you when you kill someone. [1:02]
- Suppressor is not great in this lane but still worth it: it gives spirit power and health, and everyone shoots in this game. [4:06]
- Grey Talon and Rem cannot confirm kills well and do little damage early; not a good lane combo. [1:33]
- Bebop and Seven is a very good combo: Bebop hooks an enemy to you, then you Static Charge them and put a Lightning Ball on them. [2:04]
- If your Bebop lands a hook, press 1 (Lightning Ball). [9:46]
- A gun Grey Talon is awful to lane against; unless you have Knockdown he kills you the whole game. [3:06]
- Battle Vest does not stop a gun Grey Talon, his gun and his 1 both do a lot of damage. [9:14]
- Denying souls matters much less than it did 6 to 9 months ago. [4:06]
- Take the movement buff when you can; extra stamina is always worth it. [5:06]
- Toxic Bullets is one of his favorite items on Seven, it does a lot of damage. [5:38]
- Mirage is one of the most broken heroes right now: some of the best lane sustain in the game, huge damage on Djinn's Mark and scarabs. [11:23]
- Mirage with a maxed ultimate and cooldown can teleport every 25 seconds. [11:54]
- Mirage can double tornado an enemy for two times the stun. [11:54]
- Any ability that stuns or sleeps is very strong because it stops people playing; that is why Static Charge is strong. [12:25]
- Mid-game buys here: duration (Duration Extender) and Rapid Rounds, scaling items properly instead of going too crazy. [12:55]
- Extra Charge is useful for the ball. [13:58]
- Easiest strong heroes for new players: Mo & Krill (just press 4), or Abrams if you do not want to play Mo. [12:55]
- Warden is probably the pick if you want a gun hero. [13:58]
- Ivy is one of the best heroes in the game and takes no skill. [10:17]
- Seven has a spike at 4.8k souls; farm for it. [18:44]
- Without Leech you cannot heal through fights. [17:10]
- Against a team that can stop you playing, plan Leech, then Boundless Spirit, then Unstoppable. [19:18]
- Metal Skin kept saving an enemy from his damage. [24:29]
- He soloed the mid boss while teammates kept the enemy busy, without Unstoppable. [22:26]
