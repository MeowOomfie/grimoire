## Covers
Shiv coaching review by Midknight of an Emissary-rank, melee-leaning Shiv laning against Calico and Viscous.
Themes: contextual building (Rebuttal, Plated Armor, Spirit Resilience, not following builds blindly), rage management with knives and camps, dash management as Shiv's only movement tool, map awareness and wave catching, using kill leads for mid boss or Walkers, and the flying Shiv wall-jump tech.
A 2025 video (June 2025 patch).

## Claims
Shiv:
- At full rage Shiv is about twice as effective, so keep rage topped off with knives and camps, especially when resetting. [0:00]
- Shiv has one movement tool, a dash usable aggressively or defensively; like Pocket, don't dash in and strand yourself in a team fight. [0:30]
- When a Calico jumps at you like that she is going to melee; go in her face and parry, since a parry is a free kill at level one. [1:01]
- From your backline a single dash jump gets you out of her melee range; double jump, double jump, dash is safer but slower. [1:01]
- The risk/reward of forcing a parry is high: if you fumble she has maybe a 30% chance to hit you for about 150, if she fumbles she dies. [1:31]
- Know which heroes melee a lot; Mo & Krill love to light melee, so be ready to parry when they jump. [2:02]
- Once she rushes Rebuttal, never melee her. [2:33]
- Buy Rebuttal into Viscous and Calico; parrying a Viscous puddle punch with Rebuttal reflects the damage back to him. [3:03]
- Rebuttal was the play before Close Quarters; it also gives health, while a little Close Quarters weapon damage is less useful at half HP taking poke. [3:33]
- Don't walk toward a 1 HP enemy on your side of the map; just walk back. She has melee lifesteal and could kill you over one HP. Too kill hungry. [4:33]
- You should have bought Healing Rite. [4:33]
- With no rage and the enemy holding the wave, walk far back; don't let yourself get dove. [5:34]
- Throw the extra knife at the Calico for a double bleed stack instead of at the Viscous for a second Mystic Regeneration stack. [6:36]
- Throw knives in tandem to stack the bleed, not just whenever they come off cooldown, or you lose damage. [7:06]
- When you are lower HP than the enemy, roll on top of them and dash back out, like Pocket going in and cloaking back; never dash in and end up in their face with no movement. [7:37]
- Look back while running away to throw a knife for double bleed and double Mystic Regeneration. [8:08]
- At full HP and full rage, look for a trade on the Calico: jump on her, knife, press your dash on her. If she dashes at you she gets hit by the second dash; if she ults you get her ult out. Just don't melee. [9:10]
- Enchanter's Emblem would feel good when getting poked: extra sustain and the cooldown reduction. [9:40]
- Ping when the enemy Calico is missing; she is probably going for the buffs. [10:10]
- Most Shiv builds max the knife or the 2 for damage, then the ult for cleaning up; maxing the ult first leaves you short on damage. A point in Bloodletting is understandable into a poke/burst lane, but you need a second point in the 2, which is better than the knives. [10:40]
- When backing, press your knife on a camp so your rage doesn't disintegrate. [12:11]
- Coming out of base or from mid, throw a knife at the camps you have line of sight to as you pass. [12:11]
- Plated Armor goes crazy this game; Spellbreaker is not good this game. [13:12]
- For a tanky build take Spirit Resilience and Plated Armor for double resistances instead of two 6k resist items; Bullet Resilience is a fake item. Plated Armor plus Enchanter's Emblem also works. [13:12]
- If you notice yourself mindlessly following a build, stop using builds; build guides are fine until around Emissary, and from about Archon you should start making your own. [13:43]
- The trial-by-fire option is using no build at all and just buying items, though he doesn't recommend it at this rank. [15:16]
- A single pot shot at a camp is enough to stop rage loss; you just lost 20% rage for no reason. [14:14]
- Melee minions for the extra rage. [15:16]
- Torment Pulse is broken; expect it to be at least 10% of your damage. [17:48]
- As a Shiv main play fight-happy, not passive; at Emissary micro still matters, so taking non-ideal fights to improve micro and confidence is not awful, and confidence is tied to mechanical skill. [19:18]
- If you dash into a fight it must be a positive fight you can win and clean up; otherwise position so your dash takes you out, because without it you are stranded among enemies. [22:24]
- Shiv doesn't have much movement unless you know the right click wall bounce tech. [21:54]
- Counterspell feels great into their comp of Infernus and Wraith. [24:26]
- With Ricochet on the enemy, don't stand near your Walker or you will explode. [24:26]
- Resistances are overdue, especially since Radiant Regeneration already gives healing. [27:00]
- Plated Armor would have felt amazing: Wraith, Haze and Infernus are the trio for Plated Armor, missing only a Vyper. [33:36]
- Healing Rite lets you reset less, which keeps your rage up. [34:06]
- Flying Shiv tech: at a wall, right click and then wall jump in very quick succession, repeating right click, wall jump; practice it in a custom game on roofs. He lands it about 60% of the time. [35:06]

Macro:
- With a pick and your team going blue, it is fine to go blue as long as you don't farm camps on the way. [16:18]
- Better still, siege the Guardian to pull one more enemy away from the Urn fight while they already show many on that side. [16:48]
- Don't come to the mid fight or farm there; take the wave, and at least stop the Urn from resetting. [16:48]
- At minimum catch your crashing mid wave. [17:48]
- Jump the Haze who was pushed on your side right after they ran Urn; the chance she has backup is tiny since most of her team is around the Urn. [18:18]
- The average rank has dropped from Oracle about 10 months earlier to Archon about 4 months earlier to Emissary now, so Emissary lobbies are still fairly skilled. [19:48]
- Pushing too deep while the enemy isn't showing on the map gets your team wiped. [21:23]
- When your Walker is attacked, use the jump route to come in behind the enemy and dash into them. [21:23]
- Don't go to the Guardian with six on one side of the map; go to the Walker your wave is about to push into. [21:54]
- When poked low defending a Walker, call a teammate over; an overextended Infernus is hard to punish without Slowing Hex but shouldn't siege unpunished. [24:26]
- You have no CC to take Calico out of her cat form. [25:27]
- Ping retreat instead of typing "bad fight"; typing won't bring people back if pings didn't. [25:58]
- Don't set up to hold the Urn without your team; walk with your Urn carriers and clear slightly ahead. [27:30]
- After a three-kill advantage take mid boss, or at minimum a Walker within 20 seconds, instead of farming. [28:00]
- When your team gets jumped in a bad fight, spam ping to hang back instead of running in. [29:01]
- When down one, keep your wave semi-shoved, farm the camps on your side and wait for teammates. [29:31]
- Stamina Mastery is fine this game, but don't burn it dashing at them right after buying. [30:31]
- Shoot the soul orbs to confirm them when the enemy could deny and you have nothing better to do. [31:04]
- Go to spawn and top off your health when you can; there is no reason not to. [31:04]
- With Seven ult on your team, don't fight in an enclosed space; wait in your base for them to push in the open and press ult. [32:05]
- That game was lost by not calling mid boss and not even taking Walkers off the kill lead. [32:05]
- Map awareness: watch for crashing waves and catch them, and notice when someone is mispositioned alone. [34:36]
