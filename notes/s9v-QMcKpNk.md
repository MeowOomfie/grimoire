## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream from 1/5/26, on the patch before the big early 2026 update. He coaches Noob (Abrams, Ascendant), a Holliday player in an Archon lobby and a gun Paradox player, then reviews a Gluten Snake team VOD (McGinnis, Phantom level, a six stack running an all-Ricochet strat in an Oracle lobby) and does a relaxed review of Shyro's Shiv in an Eternus lobby.
Themes: playing behind your wave and wave priority, gun spike versus green spike and the new item spike stars, Rejuvenator and urn macro, waiting on Spirit Lasso, Paradox as a pick hero instead of a side laner, ninja caches, Curse into Seven, Trophy Collector, Shiv right-click weaving, aim training and map awareness, plus a summary of the patch changes.
