## Covers
A January 2025 Eternus Calico game with live commentary against a Vyper in lane. Mostly banter; the few concrete points are Calico's lane strength, her Leaping Slash and Gloom Bombs combo, and some item choices. For Calico players; the hero and items have changed since.

## Claims
- Vyper is supposed to win the lane against Calico, but he plays it anyway. [0:09]
- Melee Lifesteal is not doing much for him this game. [2:20]
- If you hit the combo it does insane burst early; it can be parried, but it is so fast it is hard to parry. [2:20]
- He bought Extra Regen in lane. [2:52]
- The combo is 2, 1, then 2, 1, then melee (Leaping Slash, Gloom Bombs, Leaping Slash, Gloom Bombs, melee). [2:52]
- Calico is really strong in lane but falls off heavily afterwards, so you have to keep up the pace early. [5:12]
- In a gank he should have pressed 4 (Return to Shadows) sooner; he pressed it a little too late. [5:46]
- Early game the enemy has to respect Calico's melee; hitting her 2 early does insane burst. [7:22]
- He rushes Torment Pulse, and enemies were dying to it. [7:22] [9:34]
- Calico is not an aim-based hero. [9:34]
- His build is public, named "Metro Calico". [11:47]
- He goes Combat Barrier next; Spirit Lifesteal seems nice and he will get it later. [12:50]
- You do not need to rush Enduring Speed early, and he is unsure Burst Fire is worth running. [12:50] [13:20]
- After taking the enemy blue guardian, push out green and then either take the jungle farm or run in on the enemy. [16:45]
- Calico is very good for baiting; he survived with Ethereal Shift. [18:27]
