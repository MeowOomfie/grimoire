## Covers
A duo gameplay video on spirit Drifter, one of their first games on the new map, mostly banter with little teaching and no full build or ability order given.
For viewers curious about first impressions of the new map (health bar numbers, health packs, box ghouls, bell tower, vents); not a build guide.

## Claims
- The new health bars show a number underneath that is the health remaining, which is useful information, though it makes it harder to gauge how low enemies are at first. [1:38]
- In lane he pushes the wave and then does the camp. [1:38]
- Lane plan with burst up: play aggressive. [2:13]
- Box ghouls: hit the furthest one away, then melee. [3:15]
- The small health pickups heal based on missing health; one gave him 120 HP. [4:47]
- Item icons now sit further to the bottom left of the HUD. [4:47]
- Spirit Drifter feels very strong on him ("OP"). [6:51]
- There is a bell tower objective an enemy or ally can ring; the fast red version of its challenge is very hard. [8:27]
- Ringing the bell does not seem to change permanent stats; they were unsure whether it just gives weapon damage. [12:43]
- Mid boss was not changed with the new map. [13:24]
- Jungle trick: group up all the mobs and then heavy melee them; some mobs have different aggro mechanics and run. [13:24]
- Vent positions changed and he finds them much nicer. [13:56]
- With about 2,000 souls of power he found a mid-sized objective easier to kill than the T3. [13:56]
- At an upgrade pick he chose between a Spirit Snatch upgrade and Shadow Weave, taking Shadow Weave for the movement speed. [14:58]
