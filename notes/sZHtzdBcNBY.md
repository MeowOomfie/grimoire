## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (patch state of 2026-08-19) reviewing three ranked games: a Celeste player (with a Calico duo, Phantom-level lobby), a Wraith player (Oracle 6 lobby) and a Seven player (Phantom 7).
Themes: Celeste being overtuned, her 3 aim and wave clear, farming for spikes as a scaler, splitting walkers, walker fireball shred, Wraith card stacking, Seven ability maxing and builds, bag awareness, respecting spikes, using the ult, popping Indomitable through Unstoppable, and taking shrines over tier 3 walkers.

## Claims
General and meta notes:
- Celeste has been overtuned for three or four weeks, and Mina too; she has huge healing, movement, shield, CC, damage and amp. [10:12]
- Herald does too much damage because of amp, which he calls the issue with every overperforming hero. [11:16]
- Torment Pulse is basically only bought on amp heroes. [31:44]
- Calling Celeste a weak laner comes from people putting two points in their 1 first. Put two points in your 3 instead and you get huge wave clear: sit at your tower, one-shot the wave, stack amp, then kill everyone. [14:34]
- Tier 2 on Celeste's daggers is a better spike than tier 2 on her 1. [19:14]
- He would rush two points in Radiant rather than the 15% spirit lifesteal option. [19:44]
- If you send five to a tier 3 walker for no reason, just take the base guardians and look for a shrine or leave, or take the boosted lane. Forcing a tier 3 walker when you could take base guardians and a shrine is one of the biggest mistakes. [46:49]
- You do not need to play the same lane as your duo; it is great if you are a comp together, but not required. [48:50]
- When a teammate dies on a side lane to three ults, the enemy then gets mid boss for that, so it is not just unlucky. [54:18]
- An 18-minute mid boss is still rare even in high lobbies; it also needs the fight won handily, so taking a free walker is often more expected value. [34:18]
- Invading the enemy jungle as a scaler is good, depending on map state. [64:10]
- If an enemy has Indomitable and Unstoppable, CC them during Unstoppable to pop the Indomitable so you can kill them when Unstoppable ends (for example lassoing a Dynamo or Seven). Only do it when your comp has spare light CC, not your important CC. [139:26]
- When someone knocks the urn off you, or you knock it off someone, parry right away: they want it back quickly and it is often a free parry. [92:50]
- Before you hit a vent to grab a buff, always scout the area first. [138:24]
- Track when camps should respawn in a perfectly played game (for example the 13:30 Sinner's Sacrifice timer), but also check whether the enemy actually farmed them, or the timer is not real. [116:04]
- Prioritize farming the aggressive camps that can be stolen; once wave state makes a safe camp the closest one, go farm it, it is a lot of souls. [81:56]
- Calico makes a lot of farm from boxes but is very prone to dropping a huge bag, so killing a Calico is important. [88:08]

Player 1 (Celeste, Phantom-level lobby, with a Calico duo):
