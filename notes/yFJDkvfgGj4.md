# Grey Talon is King - Meta Report Ep. 4
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-10-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFJDkvfgGj4

## Covers
Deathy's meta report for the late October 2024 patch: the new Urn comeback mechanic, walkers shredding at 14 minutes, the top heroes (Grey Talon, Vindicta, Mirage, Wraith) with recommended builds, and standout items (Siphon Bullets, Spiritual Overflow, Phantom Strike, Cold Front).
For players wanting a meta overview. This is a late-2024 video; the Urn, walkers, heroes and items have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- The new Urn has a comeback mechanic: a team behind by 10% can turn it in on its own side of the map. [0:00]
- If you are 10% behind, prioritize the Urn; it is a very easy way to come back. If you are more than 10% ahead, grab the Urn to deny it, since it is the enemy's easiest comeback. [0:30]
- Walkers get shredded at 14 minutes because they have negative resistance to players; abuse it and solo push walkers at 14 minutes, or a bit earlier with decent gun damage. Wraith shreds walkers in seconds. [0:30]
- Grey Talon is at the top of the list: his buffs outweigh the usual sniper weaknesses (squishy, a target in the air for Yamato, Lash and others), and teams in Europe and North America first pick or first ban him. [1:00]
- Recommended Grey Talon build: Salty's Spirit Talon (Deathy's teammate, so he admits bias). [1:31]
- On Grey Talon, imbue items like Surge of Power or Quicksilver Reload onto Charged Shot. [1:31]
- Some items are optional (you can skip Restorative Locket if you are having a really good game), but don't skip too many defensives: Grey Talon is still vulnerable to being jumped. [2:01]
- Vindicta is back on the menu and strong even at very high rank, though less prioritized in competitive play. [2:01]
- Recommended Vindicta build: a spirit build (dated October 25, codes in the description) built around Crow Familiar and Assassinate, still the best way; spirit now scales her gun damage anyway, and Crow and snipe are ridiculously strong. [2:31]
- Mirage was already strong and didn't need a buff, but got buffs to his third skill and to Scarabs, plus better visibility when teleporting with Traveler; he feels very strong. [2:31]
- Several Mirage builds work: around Djinn's Mark, around Scarabs, or the Echo Shard Tornado style. Deathy recommends a more gun-focused build around Scarabs and Djinn's Mark ("Deathy gun Mirage"). [3:03]
- An alternative Mirage build from European player Shariq takes more utility items early, Kinetic Dash and Long Range; many people buy Long Range on Mirage and it seems strong. Experiment. [3:35]
- Mirage feels like one of the most broken heroes, behind Grey Talon and maybe Vindicta but stronger than Vindicta, in Deathy's view: Tornado evasion, Scarab -bullet resist and Djinn's Mark damage make him nearly impossible to 1v1, and his global teleport makes him very good at split pushing. [4:05]
- The meta is somewhat on-hit: on-hit items like Tesla Bullets and Siphon Bullets are strong, and Wraith uses them well. [4:05]
- Recommended Wraith build: tank Wraith by Reena VX, with decent farming, Tesla Bullets damage, lots of resistances, and damage from Quicksilver Reload, Swift Striker, Intensifying Magazine and Siphon Bullets. [4:36]
- Siphon Bullets is the biggest power spike in that build: lots of bullet resist and damage, about 55 damage every 0.5 seconds since it procs all the time, a non-negligible share of total damage if bought early, and it steals life, making you tankier. [5:06]
- Wraith adds an AOE ult that lifts people for 1.75 seconds, a teleport to escape, and Full Auto that destroys buildings in seconds, so she is very powerful; her laning was nerfed a little but is still incredibly strong. [5:37]
- Siphon Bullets is very strong, especially on high fire rate heroes but on almost everyone; it only got +5 max HP, which was all it needed. It was already strong but overshadowed by other items. [5:37]
- Start buying Siphon Bullets now to stay ahead of the curve; best on Haze, Wraith and Mirage, and Deathy also rates it on Warden. [6:08]
- Spiritual Overflow has been buffed about seven patches in a row and is now very strong: 35% fire rate when shooting, 50 spirit power, 225 bullet shield, cooldown reduction and spirit lifesteal. [6:38]
- Spiritual Overflow feels insane on heroes that benefit from fire rate and spirit power like Wraith, and on Warden, whose fire rate scales from spirit, so its activation gives an insane amount of fire rate. [7:08]
- Phantom Strike is back on the menu: it switched from bullet resist to spirit resist, which is a buff since spirit resist is harder to get than bullet resist; good on heroes like Abrams and Mo & Krill. [7:08]
- Cold Front got a big buff: double damage to non-hero units (creeps and neutrals), so it almost one-shots small creeps with no spirit; later you can one-shot creep waves. It also gives spirit resist and is making a comeback. [7:38]
