# 13 Minutes of Brutally Honest Deadlock Advice
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-04-19
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AsmoMLBNdms

## Covers
Improvement mindset for players stuck in a rank: stop blaming the team, practice one goal per day, alt accounts, rank anxiety, positive comms, fighting too much versus macro, playing every hero once, picking two or three mains and reviewing your own VODs. For low to mid rank players who play a lot but do not climb.

## Claims
- It is usually not your team: many games go from very winnable to lost on one mistake, so look for that mistake and the decisions you could have made instead of blaming teammates. [0:30]
- You are the only common factor in every game you play, so focus on what you can do, not what your teammates did. [1:00]
- Play time alone is not improvement: the coach climbed from Initiate to Ascendant 1 in under 150 hours, while many players with 500 to 1,000 hours sit at Phantom going win-loss. [1:30]
- It takes about six wins to rank up now. [1:30]
- Play every session with one goal in mind, and watch what friends and opponents do right and wrong. [2:01]
- First goal: find one decent build you can buy left to right every game and pick a hero you like; then spend a whole day learning only laning. [2:32]
- Give each day one focus: a movement day, a rotations day, a box route day, a macro day, a team fight day. With this the coach reached about Oracle in eight days (about 80 hours). [3:03]
- Trying to improve at everything at once improves nothing and keeps you at the level you started; set specific goals. [3:03]
- Make an alt account: the longer you stay in a rank the more stuck you get, and win streaks of 11 to 15 games only gave about two rank ups out of roughly 56 divisions. [3:34]
- Stuck on his main stomping Oracle and Archon players, the coach's improvement stopped for about two weeks; a new alt reached Ascendant 1 in 23 games. [4:34]
- Always look for difficulty: once games become too easy, or you either stomp or get stomped, the feeling of improvement disappears. [5:04]
- An alt costs about five dollars for a second Steam account that you invite yourself to; without one you improve more slowly because you stay at ranks you are better than for longer. [5:34]
- Do not have too much stake in your rank; fear of losing or de-ranking stops you focusing on improvement. [5:34]
- Rank means next to nothing right now: the system has no way of tracking whether you are good, so many players climb to Ascendant or Eternus on a 51% win rate over a thousand games and are still bad. [6:04]
- Just queue, even tilt queue, and use an alt account if it removes the rank anxiety. [6:36]
- There is almost zero benefit to saying anything bad about teammates in a game; it does not make them play better. [7:37]
- If you use your mic, say something positive or funny; most players are one bad comment away from sitting in spawn, and negative comments get your game thrown nine times out of ten. [8:08]
- Most players fight too much: a reviewed Ritualist player fought 60 to 70% of the game and got about six kills. [8:39]
- Do not take fights that do not matter, like fighting in front of the enemy Walker mid lane while the enemy runs the Urn; farm the map or do something else. [9:09]
- Most fights are meaningless, and many are a net negative compared to farming camps, farming lane or doing something macro. [9:09]
- Play every hero in the game at least a few games so you know their weak windows. [9:40]
- Once Vindicta has used her 2 and it is on cooldown she is out of her cooldowns, so everyone should jump her right then. [9:40]
- Bebop has nothing left in his kit after he hooks you in and bombs you, so instead of running away after a hook, run him down; if he does not have his uppercut, hunt him. This got many lane kills. [10:10]
- Apollo is an all-in then out hero, and Calico dumps her whole kit and is weak if you survive it. [10:40]
- After trying everyone, pick two or three mains. [10:40]
- Know the roles (gun carry, support, front line) and decide which you want to play. [11:10]
- Play mains that share a role, for example if you play Billy also play another front line hero, and play only those two or three for at least the first few ranks. [11:41]
- The real reason most players do not improve is not aim or game sense: they cannot see their mistakes in the moment and they never VOD review themselves. [11:41]
- In almost every one-on-one review, the player says they thought they played well until they watched it back. [12:11]
- People know what is correct but do not apply it in the moment, so they think they are better than they are; review your own gameplay even though it is boring. [12:42]
