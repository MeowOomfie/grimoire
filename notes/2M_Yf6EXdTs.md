# Explaining ALL MY THOUGHTS In an Eternus Deadlock Match
channel: Natan
date: 2026-10-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2M_Yf6EXdTs

## Covers
Educational commentary of a solo queue rank-up game to Eternus (2026-10-14) on Pocket with a burst "infinite damage" build, laning mid with Holliday against Shiv and Baba.
Lane mistakes, playing around Baba's thread pull, ult unlock timing, mid-lane opening, power-spike fight loop, urn, mid boss, target focus and Rejuvenator pushes. For players climbing toward high rank.

## Claims
- Baba is a bad matchup for two spirit reliant heroes. [0:00]
- Against Baba, avoid her thread pull: if you dodge it you keep your damage and spirit and she loses the on-hit gun damage, leaving her gun at about 30 DPS. [0:30]
- As soon as Baba misses her pull, go in aggressively, since you still have your ability and a better gun. [0:30]
- He bought Mystic Burst as the first tier one item but should have bought Restorative Shot, because these heroes have big hitboxes so it is easy to hit and gives a lot of sustain. [1:00]
- After trading with Flying Cloak he should have backed off right away; he disrespected that he had been hit by Baba's pull and Shiv jumped him for a 2v1 trade. [1:00]
- Trading into an enemy while your ability is on cooldown is the same mistake he punished Baba for a wave earlier. [1:30]
- The third wave unlocks your second ability: kill a trooper quickly to get it, then hard commit on the enemy with the ability advantage. [1:30]
- When you shove the wave and are very low, just reset; do not get baited into chasing a 1 HP enemy, since she healed off your wave and you ended at a health disadvantage. [2:01]
- An instant reset to base would have had him back in time for the next wave while Baba was still low. [2:01]
- After falling behind in lane you get poked under tower and lose your small camp. [2:31]
- With wave priority, clear the wave and run the enemy's boxes on their side of the map. [3:02]
- Always ping missing on whoever is missing from your lane. [3:34]
- Going for a camp after pushing out lost them wave priority, so they took heavy damage dealing with the whole enemy wave. [4:04]
- When bridge buffs spawn, pick the buff on the side where you have wave priority; without priority on green, going to the green buff risks running into the enemy rotating there, so he took the yellow side buff. [4:35]
- Getting hit by two Baba pulls in a row made her huge and lost the lane; the second one was avoidable. [5:08]
- He hesitated to commit on a low Baba because the guardian was about to fall and enemies unlock their ultimates at 3.8k souls; committing with no cooldowns, amp or gun items would leave him a sitting duck to her pigeon ult. [5:38]
- In hindsight he should have gone in on that Baba, but it was a hard call. [6:08]
- Losing the mid guardian costs a lot of map control, but being only slightly behind in souls it is not the end of the world. [6:44]
- Avoid playing mid lane in a bad matchup; he tried to swap lanes at the start. [6:44]
- Enemies running past the wave without killing you are not achieving anything on the map, so that is not bad for you. [7:15]
- A downside of losing your guardian is unspent souls, since you must go back to base or a secret shop to spend. [7:15]
- Do not contest a 2v2 mid lane when you are not stronger, for example with unspent souls; give up mid priority, let your laner take the solo wave, spend, and look for a gank elsewhere. [7:45]
- When you are 1v1 on a wave, clear it, then look for an opportunity on the winning lane. [8:15]
- Always gank your winning lanes. [8:48]
- Be more patient with Flying Cloak when chasing; he misread where the target would run and took guardian damage. [8:48]
- Ping enemies you hear so teammates know. [8:48]
- When the map shows three enemies in one lane and two in another, the remaining enemy is 1v1 against you, an opening for a solo kill. [9:50]
- On Pocket, weave in light melee between abilities. [9:50]
- When many enemies on a lane are low, that is an opening for a kill; he missed one while focused on resetting toward his Majestic Leap power spike. [9:50]
- Respect enemies who can run at you while you are resetting (Sinclair here), or you waste time. [10:20]
- At max Barrage plus his spirit and vitality spikes he was ready to fight, so he hovered an isolated teammate and rotated to the fight. [10:50]
- With this build, join fights already in progress and burst a distracted enemy; an 80% HP Lash got deleted. [11:20]
- Approach the Rift from the high ground. [11:20]
- Pushing a walker with the Rift troopers is very often bait: early walkers are tanky, get extra resist when many heroes show up, and your team usually needs to reset after winning fights. [11:51]
- Use the pressure Rift troopers create to reset, push elsewhere or take the enemy jungle. [12:23]
- Gameplay loop: fight right after getting your power spikes; after a won fight you are close to the next spike, so farm a bit, then fight again once you pick it up. [12:23]
- While shopping, check your team's CC and ults and group with whoever has an ultimate ready for a duo kill. [12:54]
- Target the same enemy as your teammates; almost no hero has enough damage to kill one target alone quickly, and a later fight was lost because of split targets. [13:24]
- Use zip lines even in fights to gap close and re-engage. [13:55]
- The urn spawns in yellow at 10 minutes, the next spawn is in green (caption says 50 minutes, likely 15), and it alternates between the two. [13:55]
- He opens in the mid lane with this build because you fight for priority on the most important wave: the mid wave gives the most map control and you can rotate anywhere from there. [14:26]
- As Pocket, land behind the target with Majestic Leap so Flying Cloak passes through them, dealing damage while also serving as your escape. [14:26]
- The urn is underrated: it is worth about the same as a solo kill. [15:29]
- A teammate's ult locking two enemies in place is the perfect moment to hit your entire combo. [15:29]
- Two picks right in front of mid boss with okay wave states means take the mid boss, even early. [15:29]
- Use Barrage to check who is where before committing. [16:01]
- The mid boss is worth 3K souls and each team member gets two permanent golden statue buffs. [16:01]
- After mid boss, reset and fix your waves. [16:32]
- Spend all your souls efficiently: with 2,400 he bought exactly Tankbuster so he was instantly ready to fight again. [16:32]
- Exceptions to opening mid: several teammates already there, a mid wave too dangerous to contest (Vindicta, Bebop or Drifter around mid), or no dedicated side laners so you must side lane. [16:32]
- A teammate going to push a walker should push one more wave on his own lane first, or ult there, or ult toward the walker; leaving a lane lets the enemy split push for a one-for-one walker trade. [17:35]
- A one-for-one walker trade favors the team that is behind, because they get comeback souls for it. [17:35]
- The Venator is the highest priority target. [18:07]
- After using all cooldowns in a fight your team is winning, be the one to fix the empty side wave; without him that walker would have fallen. [18:07]
- He feared a Shiv with sustain and Counterspell even though he should be stronger, because he did not have his anti-spirit item yet. [18:42]
- When on the winning team, pick up the urn yourself; leaving it gave the enemy a comeback urn. [19:46]
- If you are on the losing team, up in players on the map, and see an opening to pick up and deliver a comeback urn, always do it. [20:17]
- With a soul lead you can still be down souls on the map when a player is dead: a 13k lead with a dead 18k Holliday means you are behind on the map, so wait for them to respawn. [20:17]
- When a teammate walks up far while the team is not on the map, hover them in case enemies rotate. [20:48]
- After an initial burst, back off, wait out the enemy ult and reassess by checking health bars before helping a low teammate. [21:18]
- Walking up far when nobody can help can get you instantly killed and bait your team into a bad fight with bad wave states, which also cost a walker. [21:49]
- With a big lead and mid boss respawning in about 30 to 40 seconds, look for one or two kills and then take mid boss; that is how you win. [22:19]
- With your side waves pushed into you, a pick attempt is on a timer because the whole enemy team rotates in and you get outnumbered 4v6. [22:49]
- He could have mantled onto the bridge and waited for Flying Cloak to stall or survive. [22:49]
- In a pick: go in fast, take one person and disengage; an extended fight with bad wave states and split targets became a big throw. [23:19]
- Play with the teammate whose ultimate is ready; follow the Wraith with ult and nuke whoever he ults. [23:52]
- At the Rejuvenator fight, wait for someone to throw a punch so you can parry. [24:53]
- Spirit Burn is a big power spike for poke. [25:24]
- When pushing with the Rejuvenator, push several waves instead of running down one lane, to create more pressure when sieging. [25:24]
- The main purpose of this Pocket build is nuking people, not Affliction damage. [25:55]
- When a teammate is your win condition, your life is worth less than theirs: going in to draw enemies away from the committing Wraith is worth dying for. [26:25]
