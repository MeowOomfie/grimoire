# New Deadlock Patch has some CRAZY changes | Patch Breakdown
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-06-12

## Covers
Breakdown of the patch released around 2026-06-12: urn give-up time and bounty, comeback bounties, golden statue nerf, and hero changes for Apollo, Bebop, Calico, Doorman, Graves, Grey Talon, Holliday, McGinnis, Paige, Pocket, Seven, Shiv, Silver, Viscous, Victor, Venator and Warden.
For players who want each change with a verdict and how it shifts builds. Headline: Holliday barrel spam is back.

## Claims
- Urn give-up time cut from 75 to 60 seconds, so it throws its souls in the air sooner. [0:00]
- Urn bounty reduced 10% but unchanged for the trailing team, so the comeback urn is even stronger relative to the regular urn. [0:30]
- Trading Walkers for the urn is now slightly better than before; before it was almost always a losing trade. [0:30]
- Breakable (golden statue) permanent health bonus reduced from 15/25/35 to 15/20/30: heroes are less tanky, and since the leading team usually has more statue buffs, comebacks should be slightly easier. [1:00]
- Kill comeback bounty values increased by 8%, so kills while behind pay more. [1:30]
- In Street Brawl all ability and item ranges are reduced 10%. [1:30]
- Opening Rounds is more of a spirit item again: slightly less weapon damage but 7 spirit power again. [1:30]
- Arcane Surge gets more bug fixes; already a great item, fantastic once bugs are gone. [2:00]
- Apollo: Disengaging Sigil velocity increased and you steer it with A, D or S (W sends you nowhere); tier 1 now gives 25% fire rate instead of +30 damage, a slight lane nerf, so build one or two gun items early; tier 2 gives stamina and resets air jump and dash limits; tier 3 lets you recast; you can parry during Flawless Advance. [2:00]
- Hitting someone with the sigil resets air dashes, so combined with Stamina Mastery Apollo has crazy movement again and is a full movement hero; it is still shut down by Slowing Hex. Verdict reserved for a few days. [3:33]
- Bebop is pushed toward tank: Exploding Uppercut tier 3 missing-health gain +1%, a tier 3 duration bug fix, and Sticky Bomb tier 3 now gives move speed and debuff resistance instead of fire rate. [4:04]
- Bebop's debuff resistance applies retroactively, which matters against Infernus afterburn and Pocket's ultimate. [4:35]
- Calico: Gloom Bombs melee resist reduction now stacks additively, goes from -5% to -6% per bomb, and applies on impact instead of explosion, so Gloom Bombs straight into Leaping Slash does a lot of melee damage; Return to Shadows +10 base damage, +10 on tier 2 and +100 healing on tier 3. [4:35]
- Doorman: Call Bell explosion spirit scaling 1.3 to 1.2 and tier 3 scaling slightly down; utility is still really strong and he may keep getting nerfed. [5:05]
- Graves: Jar of Dead needs about 20% more kills to recharge, has one less base damage and worse scaling, and gives enemies more souls when killed; Grasping Hands tier 3 slightly nerfed. May be balanced for the average player but not very good at high ranks. [5:35]
- Grey Talon's gun nerfed about 20% (cycle time 4% worse, bullet damage growth -15%); Rain of Arrows gets 1 s more cooldown and 1 s less reduction from tier 2, so 7 s downtime with two points instead of 5. [6:05]
- Holliday: Powder Keg alt cast places the barrel at her feet, likely to avoid getting parried after a lasso; launch angles improved so barrels fly fast and almost straight and are easy to snipe with. [6:35]
- Powder Keg starts with two charges; base charge time 3.5 to 7 s, but maxed it is 2 s, and 1.4 s with Rapid Recharge. Tier 3 gives +100 damage and +0.5 spirit scaling (1.9 total). Holliday barrel spam is back and she may be top tier and need a nerf. [7:07]
- Bounce Pad landing radius nerfed, but maxing it now gives a stun again (weaker than the old one); with her air control it is an easy stun that counters problem heroes such as McGinnis during her ult. The coach expects Holliday to go from D tier to S tier. [8:38]
- Infernus and Ivy (Stone Form sometimes doing too much damage) get bug fixes only. [9:09]
- McGinnis turret base damage -6 (about 20%), with better scaling that breaks even only at 200 spirit power, so it is a nerf almost always; mini turrets tier 3 -5% fire rate; Heavy Barrage nerfed by 1.5 (about 7% DPS). Welcome changes. [9:39]
- Paige: Rallying Charge reaches max damage amp at 250 m instead of 350, so it is more realistic to hit; tier 2 gives 20 s more cooldown reduction; Plot Armor barrier spirit scaling +0.2 on base and +0.2 on tier 3, so a maxed shield has 2.0 spirit scaling. [10:09]
- Pocket: bullet damage growth 0.2 to 0.16 (about a 20% gun growth nerf), Flying Cloak tier 3 +1 s cooldown, Affliction does half damage to objectives. Significant, maybe not enough. [10:39]
- Seven: gun growth cut to 0.24, roughly 30%, one of the biggest ever; they want him to deal spirit damage, not hybrid, which is what made him strong at lower ranks. Power Surge tier 3 slight nerf so it is harder to keep it up permanently. [11:12]
- Shiv: Killing Blow has 30% extra cooldown when it does not execute a player; you can still use it for movement but pay with a longer cooldown. [12:14]
- Silver: Lycan form cooldown 40 to 70 seconds, giving her real downtime where you can punish her; Lycan's Curse tier 3 no longer heals (still refreshes abilities). A massive nerf; she felt insanely strong at higher ranks. [12:46]
- Viscous: Puddle Punch tier 2 lifesteal +40 to +60 and Goo Ball tier 2 gives more bullet and spirit resist, to make his other AP choices attractive. [13:47]
- Victor: Aura of Suffering base radius -1 m (back when maxed); ult base cooldown +10 s and tier 3 reduction 10 s smaller, so a maxed ult is 20 s longer; Shocking Reanimation tier 3 +15 to 25 damage as small compensation. Clear nerfs. [13:47]
- Venator: more spirit scaling on the max damage bonus of Assassinate, only stronger on targets below 50% HP. [14:47]
- Warden: bullet falloff significantly worse, so he does much less damage at range, a straight nerf fitting a close to mid range carry with lots of health; more heroes' falloff ranges may be adjusted. [14:47]
- Warden: Alchemical Flask spirit scaling 0.73 to 0.63 (tier 2 less damage but 5% more enemy damage reduction), overall a nerf; Willpower spirit scaling +0.5 to +0.8; Binding Word tier 3 cooldown reduction from -18 to -14 s; Last Stand lifesteal buffed and tier 2 and tier 3 buffed (tier 3 +4 s duration). Expect some spirit Wardens. [15:48]
