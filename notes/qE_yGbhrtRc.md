## Covers
Celeste, commentary over one game by aquatiq on a "hyper carry 3000 DPS" build, in a lane with Ivy against Bebop and Drifter.
Covers her soul-securing problem early, item picks, and how she scales late.
Patch of late August 2026.

## Claims
- He uses a hyper carry 3000 DPS build that has given him good results. [0:37]
- Celeste's early game is terrible because of her soul-securing problem; her late game is one of the best in the game. [1:07]
- Opening Rounds is so broken right now that he buys it every game until it is nerfed. [5:26]
- He also bought Kinetic Dash early. [5:26]
- Celeste with Ivy is a lane of two bad early heroes; against Bebop and Drifter, who have amazing early games, the lane is rough. [5:56]
- Do not get hooked by Bebop as Celeste, or you just die. [6:58]
- Killing a fed Apollo is not worth losing a walker for. [10:07]
- Celeste farms very fast; when behind, go farm. [11:07]
- Do not keep fighting a hero who is far richer (15k) than you; it just feeds him. [12:39]
- Do not fight over the Urn when it costs you a walker. [13:39]
- He says Celeste scales about as well as Wraith. [13:39]
- She has huge healing late, so he felt he could not die in fights. [17:55]
- Get picks, then take Mid Boss when the enemy has nothing to stop you. [19:28]
- Toxic Bullets is very good for finishing people off, and it also helps poke Vindicta down. [20:31]
- With a 6k bag he skipped a Spiritual item and took Greater Expansion because he already had a lot of spirit power. [22:37]
- After a big win, contesting their shrines and base keeps their waves bad and forces them back. [26:22]
- Spamming her 3 on the Rift area made the enemies move off it. [27:56]
