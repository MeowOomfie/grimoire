## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a free raffle coaching stream (recorded 8/14/26) reviewing four ranked games: an Ascendant Sinclair, a Calico laning into Graves, a Shiv, and a gun Lash in a Phantom lobby.
Themes: wave priority and hard shoving in lane, punishing roams, fight positioning and height, tunnels and breakables, invading the weak side, needless deaths before mid boss, bag awareness and secret shops, calling mid boss, knife stacking, gun Lash versus spirit Lash, and calling stuns on comms.
Side notes: Golden Goose Egg, Slowing Hex into Calico, Spellbreaker, Radiant, Merc Mag versus Surge of Power on Vindicta, Spirit Resilience, damage amp on heroes and the hidden MMR rank reset.
