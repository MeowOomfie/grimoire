# You're Spacing Wrong | Oracle Vyper Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-06-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c4fjZOtLi14

## Covers
Vyper, VOD review coaching by Midknight of an Oracle-rank Vyper player (one student). Mid-2025 video, patch around June 2025.
Themes: first-wave denies and early shopping, box routes and camps in lane, sliding on Vyper, pushing a lead by farming, retreat routing, spacing around enemy abilities (Ivy Stone Form, Seven, Paradox), Spellbreaker, Cultist Sacrifice timing, overextending and taking fights in your base.

## Claims
Player 1 (Vyper, Oracle):
- Two denies on the first wave is rare but impactful: run to shop and buy after the first wave, so you have an ability and an item on the second wave and can look for aggressive trades or poke while stronger, plus the extra boon power. [0:30]
- Focus the deny on your side rather than the minion; when they kill a minion on your side of the bridge, watch the soul orb, because the bridge often blocks them from shooting it and it is free souls. Waiting two seconds to shoot the orb costs less than the deny gains. [1:02]
- Not buying by this point is really bad; the only excusable reason is saving for a tier 3 like Rescue Beam (Vyper would not even save for Tesla). Saving for Opening Rounds is respectable, a very strong item to push your advantage. [1:32]
- With two minions under tower and both enemies full HP you have no kill potential; it is much more impactful to do a box route, especially on fast Vyper. Calico is the only hero that outclasses you at boxing. [2:34]
- Shown box route options: a quick route of a few boxes plus a vase on the way back, or a more extended strong route that does not fit a short window. [3:05]
- Wrapping back into the enemy's face after their boxes is risky; in higher elo they should hear you and cut you off. Route out through their basement camp and come all the way around, slower but significantly safer. [4:08]
- As Vyper you should be sliding 99% of the time you are shooting, holding crouch, especially when shooting tower. [4:38]
- When Infernus presses his ult and his dash is not up, start sliding away immediately; he will not catch Vyper. Know his dash timer, and use the nearby staircase to kite. [5:39]
- When you are ahead, trading in lane is generally worse for you because the enemy gets comeback souls, unless your wave is hard shoved and they will miss a ton of minions. [6:40]
- Dying intentionally with good wave management (wave hard shoved) for faster resets is a very high-level concept seen more in other MOBAs. [7:12]
- Don't waste your ult when the target (Abrams) was not in the fight. Don't go in on the Seven; he should ult and shoot you while Infernus shoots from behind. [7:44]
- With Fortitude ticking, do not push up; treat it like you have healing up, because getting shot loses the regen. [8:17]
- Big flaw: winning lane hard on kills but leaving many boxes and camps unfarmed. With a lead where you always get wave priority but cannot get kills, abuse farm: hard shove wave, go farm, hard shove wave, go farm, to keep extending the lead. [9:19]
- Mantle slides are great movement, especially for Vyper: hold crouch whenever you mantle to slide. [9:51]
- Don't stack three heroes in one lane; you leave your laner defending against two and split the wave three ways. [11:57]
- In lane you hit lots of headshots, but later you aim at the feet too much; aim higher. [13:00]
- Decide your escape route before you turn the corner. Waffling, starting one way and looping back, wastes about a second and lets the chaser catch up. Always think about 2 seconds ahead for routing out. [13:32]
- Take the survival buff instead of greeding one more wave. [15:04]
- Spending three stamina bars at once is fine burst movement on a normal hero, but on Vyper it is three slides worth of movement; you keep escaping on two bars. If you are doing burst movement, be at or close to full stamina. [15:34]
- Clinging to Mystic Burst as your only spirit damage is odd; Vyper benefits a lot from a couple of small spirit items, even something like Disarming Hex for the investment bonus and value. [16:35]
- When the enemy has Rescue Beam, be cognizant of it; you can no longer count on finishing that target. [17:05]
- Against a chasing Ivy, put a wall between you and her as quickly as possible since she cannot really chase; cling to the right side. [17:36]
- Slide while shooting Guardians; it raises your DPS. [18:37]
- Many defensive ults are a consequence of mispositioning. [19:07]
- Buy Spellbreaker here: it completely shuts down Paradox and helps a lot against Lash. [19:40]
- With two allies dead, the textbook play is to grab the Urn and hold it in your base so the enemy cannot run it. [20:10]
- Without Lethal Venom maxed (four points), don't run at enemies like that; you will not win it. [20:40]
- Don't route through mid with the Urn, especially when the enemy is at your Walkers and likely nearby; route safely. [22:13]
- Cultist Sacrifice bought this late and used three times gives only about 600 to 700 extra souls. It now scales with boons and is better mid to late, but you still want to buy it earlier if buying it for the financial reward. [22:44]
- With only three enemies showing, the correct call was to turn around and boost back, or split and take base Guardians, not route the way you did. [24:44]
- Behind their backline, petrify one with Petrifying Bola so your team can catch up; petrify one and focus the other to split them into 1v1s, which the ult is best for. [25:15]
- Re-slide and re-dash instead of reloading: slide to your first circle, slide back for the second half circle. [26:16]
- Vyper needs Quicksilver Reload on Screwjab Dagger; it means you never have to reload. [26:16]
- If you run away from a fight, actually run away. [27:19]
- When you put Lethal Venom on Ivy she will always Stone Form; it is hyper predictable. Step away and space her Stone Form instead of standing in its stun range and giving her double value. [27:19]
- Melee Ivy is not really viable anymore since her base melee damage was nerfed. [28:19]
- When your wave is far and they show only three on the map, back off from the Walker siege; they are coming back. [28:49]
- When chased, route to the mid boss arena, the best spot for outplays and jukes: slide in the veil and go up the rope, or slide down and crouch in a corner (stop the slide early so they do not see the trail), or path up the stairs since Vyper can slide up stairs and they cannot. Routing backwards into open space guarantees death, especially into Lash's Ground Strike. [28:49]
- Sliding into a bad spot while down two players gets you killed; don't. [31:54]
- Biggest issue this game: not buying Spellbreaker. The strong Paradox build is allowed to exist because Spellbreaker makes her useless against you; it also neuters Lash and helps a little against Seven and Toxic Bullets through debuff and spirit resist. [31:54]
- Flat sliding too far is too aggressive; slide and then stop instead of going further. [33:27]
- Work on spacing: respect the enemy's burst and catch, especially while Paradox is online. [34:00]
- When buying Spellbreaker, sell Mystic Burst, not Opening Rounds; you can use the next flex slot on spirit, and you are not upgrading Tankbuster. [34:30]
- When the enemy pushes into your base, take the fight in your base, not outside: you get fountain heal, chokepoints and high ground, and here Seven's ult was answered by Unstoppable and Bola. [35:38]
- Flanking onto the backline is fine in a fully committed teamfight, not in a fight that is already negative. [36:39]
- Summary: early, lane and do more camps and boxes; without kill potential under tower, shove wave, camp, box route, repeat; on Vyper you are not really looking for ganks then. [37:45]
- Summary: mid and late game, work on spacing, stop sliding too deep, play around spawn, and stop overextending alone where Lash can appear and one-shot you. [38:46]
- Summary: fight taking, avoid fights while down a player or two and take fights in your base when your Patron is vulnerable. [40:18]

General:
- Laning is the most micro-intensive point in the game (avoiding minion poke, knowing when to trade with your wave and when to box), so macro-heavy players often find it their weakest phase. [2:03]
- Counterspell is very good against Lady Geist because players behave the same way: they run until below 20% HP and then run at you to ult, so pressing Counterspell when they are in range gives a free kill. Good Bebops hook and then immediately Counterspell because people ult when hooked. [10:21]
- Oracle is the last rank where you can be carried by micro; you cannot reach Phantom on micro alone. [21:42]
