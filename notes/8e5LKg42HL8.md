## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a free coaching stream (patch of late March 2026, right after a minor patch that changed Mirage's Scarabs to damage suppression) and reviews four viewer replays: a Pocket, an Archon Holliday, an Emissary Mirage and an Arcanist Paradox.
Themes: wave focus before fights, box routes and camp farming, Sinner's Sacrifice farming, urn running and confirming orbs, mid boss calls, hitting the patron, counter itemizing (Plated Armor, Metal Skin, Unstoppable, Slowing Hex), not over-meleeing, mantle sliding and diagonal air dashes.
Side notes on build reviews (Vyper, gun Dynamo, Victor, Venator), Siphon Bullets, Healing Booster timing, Lady Geist, auto-queue, and the HP vs resistance math.
