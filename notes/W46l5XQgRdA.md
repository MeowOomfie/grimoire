## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (February 2026 patch era). He opens with movement tech on Rem in the sandbox (short hop, dash hop, instant air dash versus ground dash), then reviews four players: a punch Viscous in an Eternus-level game, a Shiv laning into Silver, an Ascendant Celeste laning with Silver, and a Phantom Billy laning with Lady Geist into Mo & Krill and Pocket.
Themes: dead time and box routing, not committing to teammates' bad fights, assuming the enemy has backup, picks on side lanes instead of mid, urn and mid boss decisions, walker trades, soul awareness, stamina use, and Counterspell timing.
Side notes: urn soul split, soul priority at 10 minutes, Cultist Sacrifice value, a Curse on Haze test, Shiv right-click combos, Victor's mid game, and Rem versus Calico in the tunnels.

## Claims
Movement tech (shown on Rem):
- Short hop: slide or dash, crouch to cancel the dash momentum (most people bind crouch to scroll wheel), then jump out of it. [7:09]
- After a movement change you can now jump straight out of a ground dash without crouch cancelling; crouch only when you want to end the dash early. [7:39]
- Crouch cancelling helps reach a corner boost that is close to you, but if you need it you have used your stamina wrong; dash preemptively toward where you want to end up and wall bounce. [8:41]
- Dash hopping is technically better than instant air dashing: an instant air dash gets one extra slide, a dash jump gets two. [10:49]
- Ground dashes are faster than instant air dashes without Superior Stamina, but you cannot air strafe in a ground dash; if you are air strafing, instant air dash then strafe for much more distance per stamina. For short dashes or jump-dash cancels, ground dash is faster. [11:24]
