channel: Midknight

## Covers
Billy guide and coaching review by Midknight: opens with Billy ult and 1 tech, then reviews one Ritualist-rank gun-focused Billy player (unnamed) laning into Lady Geist and Mina, with some notes on spirit Billy.
Covers ability order and maxes, gun versus melee versus spirit builds, item choices, parry conditioning, Walker Stomp dodging, escapes and lane shop timing.
This is a 2025 video from Billy's release week (August 2025 patch), so builds and numbers may be out of date.

## Claims
Billy tech (intro):
- Ult: do not just stand in range and pull; get the target inside the circle, then run as far away as you can while keeping line of sight to pull and displace them as far as possible. [0:00]
- If the target breaks line of sight for too long, the chain breaks. [0:31]
- You have full control during the ult: shoot, use abilities, do combos and melee. Most Billys just stand still. [0:31]
- Tapping 1 just drops you a fair distance; hold 1 briefly and release for maximum distance. Holding it a long time freezes you in the air and then smashes down. [0:31]
- Holding 1 for distance matters when engaging off a zipline or a rooftop for a gank, or to close the gap on someone running away. [1:01]

Player (Billy, Ritualist):
- Do not start with the bat; start with your 3. It is very strong into Lady Geist and Mina because early trades rely on melee, and a light melee debuffs them by 15% fire rate. [2:34]
- The 3 also gives extra health from melees, so meleeing works as a panic button when losing a fight, and its 8% bullet damage amp is great on a committed engage. [3:35]
- Pro players in all three Deadlock Night Shift games leveled 3 first; the general level order is 3, 1, 2. One point in the 1 at tier one is fine, and a point in the 2 is not bad. [4:06]
- Once you melee-kill someone in front of another enemy, do not melee that enemy next; they saw it and will be ready to parry. [3:05]
- The first melee in a fight reminds the person hit and the person hitting that the parry key exists, at any elo. [3:35]
- He does not love Monster Rounds as a start item; rush it only on weak wave clear or when you are getting hard shoved. [4:36]
- High elo builds take Monster Rounds because Billy is about a C-tier laner who gets zoned off minions and shoved in, and it keeps your tower alive. [5:37]
- You can light melee troopers as an easier way to finish them. [5:07]
- Billy's gun still does significant damage at medium range and an absurd amount at melee range. [6:09]
- Playing this aggressively into a Mina who can kill you with Love Bite is ego. [6:41]
- Resetting at full HP with 2.6k: buy Melee Lifesteal. Billy's 1 procs it for free; it is a very strong item on him. [6:41]
- Only buy Extra Regen when resetting at about 60% HP or lower; here Monster Rounds already gave regen. [7:11]
- Extra Stamina is good, building into Kinetic Dash. [7:11]
- Do not buy regen items at full HP on respawn unless it is semi-offensive regen like Mystic Regeneration on Shiv. [7:41]
- Shiv and Mo & Krill can take intentionally negative trades because their healing turns them positive; Billy cannot do that as easily. [8:13]
- Bad angle: playing aggressive and low with no teammate near, where both enemies can go on you and Geist has lots of area denial, is what got you killed. [8:46]
- Popping 3 for the 1 refund is good. [9:16]
- Do not lead with 2 there: come off the zipline from above, use 1 for extra distance to close the gap, then use 2 for the knockup and gun damage. [9:46]
- He does not like Extra Spirit; spirit Billy feels like a poor man's Holliday. He expects the meta to be gun with some melee, or melee with some gun. [10:18]
- Once your 3 has its charge refund (two levels), do not lead with 3; lead with the bat, or 2 then bat, then 3. [10:48]
- Many players parry right after your 2 because bad Billys go 2, light melee, 2, light melee, 1. Use that: 2 them and shoot them in the head while they fall. [10:48]
- He prefers more gun: Rapid Rounds, infinite reloading off your melees and Intensifying Magazine mean you never have to reload. [11:50]
- Spirit melee: melee HP scales with spirit; the combo is 1-2-1 or 1-2-1-2-3-1, then stay in their face spamming 1, with Infuser popped for a big burst. [11:50]
- After your 2, shoot her head instead of meleeing, because she expects the melee. [12:21]
- Point Blank is an amazing item on Billy: 138 DPS point blank here, against about 105 to 117 on other gun carries. [12:51]
- When your team takes an aggressive fight mid, splitting is fine, but back off once you will not get the full walker; get the wave and leave. [13:53]
- Escaping a walker siege on a hero with a dash: wall jump and air dash up the side; poor man's version is to land on the ledge to refresh stamina and then go up. Shiv can clear it with a wall jump and two air jumps. [14:23]
- He prefers Kinetic Dash over Stamina Mastery, for fire rate and never reloading. [15:25]
- Pop 3 sooner to escape; pressing 1 would have got you Melee Lifesteal healing. [15:55]
- Learn to melee walkers on Billy; melee does a lot of objective damage, and Warp Stone gives easy guaranteed melees (unsure about Warp Stone on him). [16:25]
- Using the ult to run away instead of staying on top of enemies is good. [16:55]
- Do not put two points in the 2 (bash) until your 3 is maxed; 3 max gives more HP. [17:25]
- Walker Stomp with Warp Stone: jump in, melee, Warp Stone up when it is about to stomp, then melee or 2 and run. [18:57]
- Without Warp Stone: go in without starting a melee, dash up with 2 when it stomps, then hit two (maybe three) melees and back off. The 2 removes your hitbox a little slowly, so it is harder than on most heroes. [33:44]
- The 2 has a lot of vertical momentum; wall jump, air dash, then 2 out is a great outplay (he dodged an Infernus with it). [19:29]
- On enemy hybrid camps, leave one medium creep alive; stealing a camp with no backup is bad if anyone hears you. [19:29]
- Post 20 minutes at higher elo it is better to full farm the enemy hybrid camp. [26:38]
- Use the 2 to escape the way you would use Shiv's dash. [19:59]
- Never melee someone in a juke closet; they are hovering their parry key because they have no other out. [20:30]
- Maxing 1 first is wrong; 3 max is far more important. 1 max adds little damage (about 30 to 40); it gets good later because it stacks Crushing Fists. [21:00]
- He would prefer Extra Charge over Rapid Recharge. [21:31]
- With Crushing Fists, 1, 3, 1, melee stacks the stun in under 3 seconds, which cancels Haze's ult, Warden's ult and Mo & Krill's ult. [21:31]
- Max 3 first, then max 1 at about 4,000 to 5,000 souls. [22:01]
- Maxing 2 on a melee build is wrong. The 2's 6% max HP damage is good for spirit builds because the percentage scales with spirit (about 8 to 9% at full build, per chat). [24:07]
- 3 max gives 65 max HP per bat; with four bats in a fight that is 260 HP without even pressing melee. [24:07]
- Hitting multiple enemies with one melee gives the max HP for each of them (tested). [30:41]
- Two points in the ult give 15% more resistances while ulting, which matters since you have no other real resistances. [27:08]
- This player needed more resistances; the enemy damage was fairly balanced, probably more spirit. [27:38]
- If you main Shiv, Billy should be in your pool; he is like the gun counterpart of Shiv with a similar role. [28:08]
- Missing two vending machines in a game shows a problem; practice the timing in the range for five minutes. [28:38]
- Curb your expectations for your first few Billy games; as a close-range commit hero you have to know your limits in lane and are limit testing every fight. [28:38]
- When your team is ending the game, do not farm. [29:39]
- Recommended build: Wander's, the most competent Billy theorycrafting he saw. If you use it, put two points in Ram (as captioned) before Bottlenecks (as captioned), directly after or before maxing your 3. [31:12]
- Best melee (punch) build he has seen is Cray's (as captioned), though it needs more time. [32:42]
- Soul awareness: early game souls got too high before shopping. [34:47]
- Lane shop timing: go to shop after the second wave, then about every three waves, basically every time you hit 800 souls; check your soul count after every wave and how long until the next wave, to see if you can run boxes. [35:17]
- When your teammate is not there, you cannot play with any aggression; respect the enemies. [35:48]
- Berserker is a very good item on Billy. [36:18]
- Summary notes: shoot during your ult, use your 3 for reloads, do not max 2 before 3 on a melee build, and be less eager with melees. [36:18]
