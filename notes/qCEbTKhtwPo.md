## Covers
Midknight Vods reviews four replays on a free coaching stream: an Oracle Sinclair, an Ascendant Violet (laning into Wraith and Kelvin), a Ritualist Graves on a gun build, and a Seeker Deadman Danny with strong rocket jump movement.
He also gives verdicts on the new heroes (Solomon's ult, Deadman Danny, Nurse Harrow, Baba) and on items like Rusted Barrel, Trophy Collector, Enchanter's Emblem and Alchemical Fire.
Recurring themes: dead time, over-grouping and splitting souls with the team, over-rotating, map awareness before fights, souls-by-source breakdowns (jungle and Sinner's Sacrifice), Rift timing, Rejuvenator fights, and saving mid boss ults for the second shrine.
