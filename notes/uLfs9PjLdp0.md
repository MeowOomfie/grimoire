# The Walk Back Patch | Deadlock Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-03-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLfs9PjLdp0

## Covers
Midknight reacts to a sizable minor patch (March 2026) in which Valve walked back many of its previous changes.
Patch reaction with a verdict on each change: Base Guardian, Super Troopers, hero health, Golden Goose Egg, Apollo, Calico, Celeste, The Doorman, Ivy, Lash, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Pocket, Shiv, Silver, Victor, Viscous and Wraith.

## Claims
General:
- Base Guardian is a little less tanky; fine. [0:00]
- Super Troopers now give 15% less bounty, which he reads as a buff he doesn't understand; they are incredibly strong and once the enemy cracks two shrines it is almost impossible to come back because of their infinite wave priority. [0:00]
- He hopes Super Trooper damage gets nerfed or a shrine respawn mechanic is added, so a team with broken shrines has counterplay instead of one player permanently clearing waves. [0:30]
- Extra hero health across the board is a good change: last patch buffed every hero below S+ tier instead of nerfing the OP ones, so everyone was getting one-shot; extra health is a weird fix but achieves the goal. [0:30]
- Golden Goose Egg now pays souls every 150 instead of every 200; he still thinks it is bad because Deadlock is tempo and early-game based. [1:01]
- A support sacrificing early tempo, the role that wants to roam and have map impact, for about 800 extra souls and one more tier two item is hard to justify; a niche strat of Rem buying Golden Goose Eggs and executing in front of their carry could work, but he never sees it. [1:31]
- Apollo got his speed back on the lunge, now on the tier three. [1:31]

Calico:
- Calico got a nerf (move speed reduced) and he doesn't know if it is enough. [1:31]
- Bug fix: her Gloom Bomb tier two was secretly giving an extra 15 damage. [2:02]
- Her slash scaling and the souls she gets on the tier two slash were reduced; he doesn't think she needed souls on the tier two slash. [2:02]
- Her real issue is how much damage Gloom Bombs and her ult do, more than her 2. [2:02]
- She lost 3% gravity for 6% more air control, which is a general buff she didn't need. [2:02]

Celeste:
- Light Eater on-hit damage reduced by 5; good. [2:02]
- Her 2 barrier spirit scaling reduced; good, but it does not solve infinite barrier uptime, a very high barrier, and the barrier damaging and silencing you when you break it, which makes you not want to hit it. [2:34]
- Pearl no longer gives half cooldown on a miss; fine, but a good Celeste doesn't miss, or shoots a minion to start it. [2:34]
- Blink time also slightly reduced. The two biggest nerfs are not insignificant but are not significant for one of the strongest heroes in the game. [2:34]

The Doorman:
- His bell is back to having on-hit explosion damage plus active explosion damage; he preferred the flat bell. [3:05]
- His tier two is back to being the strongest tier two spike in the game, a net 70 damage if he hits his bell. [3:05]
- This should make him less oppressive in lane for now, but just nerfing the bell damage would have felt better; hitting someone with the bell feels clunky. [3:05]
- Tier three got a slight slow increase. [3:35]

Ivy:
- Bullet damage buffed a bit; it won't bring back gun Ivy after it was nuked, and Ivy is still insane. [3:35]
- Her 2 gives only half the buff if she isn't connected to an ally; not bad, but it doesn't fix her 2 constantly giving a ridiculous buff to her and an ally when maxed. [3:35]
- Air Drop spirit scaling, barrier spirit scaling and Air Drop slow reduced; he likes the Air Drop changes but asks where the changes to her ridiculously powerful Stone Form are. [4:05]

Lash and others:
- Lash ult scaling with spirit power is back, with reduced range on the upgrades; it is basically the old Lash ult again. [4:05]
- "Dizzy" and "Magenus" (as captioned) nerfs target that hero's ult, which has felt oppressive; you just explode if she has no build. He calls the hero the second most poorly designed in the game. [4:35]
- Mirage's Fire Scarabs cooldown buff is good because the cooldown was too long, but the hero is objectively not weak despite a really bad win rate, so he doesn't like an overall buff; no one puts points into his ult right now because his other maxes are so good. [4:35]

Mo & Krill:
- Big nerf: Scorn heals less against minions and camps, its cooldown is a little longer and its DPS is reduced. [5:06]
- He prefers the old Mo & Krill; Scorn makes sense as a CC tool, not a damage tool, because his whole role is being anti-carry for his teammates. [5:06]
- Tier two now gives cooldown instead of DPS and tier three gives the damage back; it doesn't solve the core issue, and taking 15 damage out of it overall won't fix him. [5:37]

Pocket:
- Affliction attacks a little faster with a small DPS nerf, so it expires a second sooner: its damage comes out quicker but its full anti-heal lasts less. [5:37]
- Small duration decrease on the tier two, a little more damage on the tier three. [6:07]

Shiv:
- His alt fire got its full knockdown back. [6:07]
- His knives got a cooldown increase, which makes sense because they were melting this patch. [6:07]
- Killing Blow got a little extra range, which he likes for executing, gap closing and moving. [6:07]
- Max range ulting a Lady Geist: with less than 15 m range on the ult she can't swap you before it connects because of its velocity; the travel speed buff probably changes that 15 m number. [6:07]

Silver:
- Small ult buffs: a little more resist and a little less cooldown. [6:37]
- Her human form does way too much: her 1 with just Toxic Bullets can take half your HP bar and apply Toxic. Take power out of her 1, maybe her bullet max, and put it back in her ult. [6:37]
- If Valve wants her ult to be a debuff in a new gameplay loop, the ult can't have a cooldown. He is currently more afraid of a human-mode Silver than an ult-mode one. [7:07]
- Once Silvers start buying Phantom Strike to make up for her lack of movement, she will be extremely scary again and her win rate will snowball. [7:07]

Victor:
- Pain Battery tier three healing reduced by 3%, Jumpstart scaling reduced, his 3 radius reduced, tier two max DPS scaling reduced, and a longer ult cooldown that comes back with the tier three ult. [7:07]
- He likes the changes; Victor needed nerfs and walked away from the patch a winner despite people saying he got much worse. [7:37]

Viscous:
- Puddle Punch was secretly getting an extra 60 melee damage on tier three, and its scaling was reduced; still not enough, it is just so strong. [7:37]
- Puddle Punch is a ping check: past roughly 70 to 77 ping you literally can't parry it, and many players are on high ping (he is on East Coast servers from the West Coast at 60+ ping). [8:08]
- Fix: really nerf its damage and displacement, then make it unparryable, a bit slower, or give ping forgiveness. [8:38]

Wraith:
- The Joker no longer bounces to another target; good. [8:38]
- Card cooldown slightly up and one AP into your cards no longer gives two cards instantly; this sounds small but is a real nerf (Extra Charge and Rapid Recharge are affected too, though no one buys them on her). [9:08]
- Alt fire now casts Wraith's cards in inverse order. He thinks this is a terrible change: it removes the punishment for farming and stacking cards without managing them, such as a Joker stuck in your third-to-last slot. [9:08]
- Spade damage nerfed a bit and Heart scaling reduced; he doubts it is the last Heart nerf. [9:39]
- More ult nerfs, not enough: make the ult slower so it can be Counterspelled again, and make it not "a Paradox ult" (as he puts it). [9:39]
