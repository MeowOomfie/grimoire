# Community Tier List - How good is every Deadlock hero, and why?
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-04-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1LZn2lXsNPo

## Covers
A stream segment where Deathy's Twitch chat votes every hero into S to D tier while Deathy and Vegas (a comp player) explain each hero's strengths and weaknesses, then Deathy adjusts the list to his own opinion. Early April 2025 balance (early-game-centric meta, strong early spirit stacking); heroes and items have changed a lot since.
