# Everything you NEED to know about Deadlock's Latest Patch - Patch Highlights
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-11-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qWgd3BbxRME

## Covers
A 2024 patch rundown (the game has changed a lot since) of the November 2024 update: ranked queue removed for per-hero skill rating, bridge buffs claimed by heavy melee, soul sharing nerfed, Walkers and Patron buffed, Urn nerfed, healing nerfed across heroes, three new items (Spellslinger Headshots, Arcane Surge, Spirit Snatch), Silencer and Metal Skin reworks, and hero changes to Warden, Yamato, Mirage, Viscous, Wraith, Vindicta, Shiv, McGinnis, Haze, Grey Talon and Ivy. For players adjusting to that patch.

## Claims
- The ranked queue was removed; there is just "Play Deadlock" and every game establishes a rating. [0:30]
- Skill rating is now shown per hero, and needs 50 games played on that hero in the past 30 days. [0:30] [1:02]
- Any stack size can queue, except that players at Ascendant 1 or above are limited to stacks of at most three Ascendant 1+ players. [1:02]
- The console command for solo-only matchmaking (captions "mm prefer solo only") makes you play against solo queue players too, possibly with longer queue times. [1:02] [1:33]
- Settings has a new skill rating section with "show notification when my rating changes" and "show average team rating after the match". [1:33]
- A browse builds feature is back in the main menu: pick a hero, click Builds and browse any build. [1:33] [2:04]
- Bridge buffs are no longer claimed by walking over them; you must heavy melee them, like the Rejuvenator, so expect melee and parry fights around them. [2:04] [2:34]
- Bridge buffs were changed to be weaker early in the game and stronger later. [2:34]
- Soul sharing was nerfed almost back to where it was two weeks earlier: two players in a lane get 60% each (120% total), each extra player adds only a little over 100% total, and five or six players in a lane get no more souls than one solo player. [3:04]
- The goal of the soul sharing change is to end the death ball meta of the previous weeks. [3:04]
- Middle Walkers went from roughly 5,000 to 8,000 health. [3:04] [3:35]
- Walkers now give resistances to nearby allied players and gain resistance based on the number of nearby enemies: a lone Wraith needs 8,000 damage, but with six attackers the Walker has 40% resist and needs roughly 11,000. [3:35]
- Taking middle Walkers is much harder and defending teams can take fights at them more easily, so you need a significant advantage to take that fight. [4:05]
- The Walker stomp now deals 10% max health damage and its laser 2% max health per second, making Walkers much scarier to dive. [4:05]
- The Patron laser now deals 3% max HP per second. [4:05]
- The last flex slot now needs one Shrine and two Guardians, likely because bases are harder to push. [4:05] [4:35]
- His prediction: the death ball meta should be gone and games will last about five more minutes on average, maybe 5 to 10. [4:35]
- When the Patron's first phase dies and it transforms, the defending team's dead players get respawn timers reduced by 20 seconds (not the attackers), so a second fight often happens as the Patron goes down. [4:35] [5:05]
- Urn: time to reveal went from 20 to 25 seconds, so it is easier to sneak in, especially on fast heroes or flying with Ivy. [5:05]
- Urn base bounty dropped to 3,000 from a higher figure the captions garble as "4,50" and its scaling was not improved; he thinks the Urn is a bit worse overall, nerfed more than lane soul sharing. [5:35]
- An alternate Urn mechanic was going to be tested that weekend. [5:35] [6:06]
- Valve rebalanced nearly every heal and anti-heal to make healing slightly worse; Abrams' Infernal Resilience went from 14% to 11% damage regenerated and its tier three was lowered a little. [6:06]
- Kelvin's grenade heal and ultimate heal were nerfed, McGinnis got similar healing nerfs, and many lifesteal sources were nerfed, to make heroes easier to finish off when a team lacks anti-heal. [6:37]
- New item Spellslinger Headshots: each headshot grants 3% spirit power, stacking up to 12 times for 36%. [7:07] [8:09]
- Spellslinger Headshots is percentage based, so it does almost nothing early (14 spirit to 15); do not buy it too early, it is a late game item for spirit builds. [7:07] [7:38] [8:09]
- With about 140 spirit, each headshot gave about 4 spirit, reaching almost 200; it also has spirit lifesteal and spirit amp and builds from Soul Shredder Bullets, like an upgraded Soul Shredder. [7:38] [8:09]
- New item Arcane Surge (4,200, upgrades from Kinetic Dash): your next dash jump costs no extra stamina and your next ability within up to 7 seconds gets 20% range, 20% duration and 20 spirit power, while keeping Kinetic Dash's fire rate and temporary ammo. [8:39] [9:09]
- Arcane Surge also gives +1 stamina and movement slow resist. [9:40]
- On Dynamo, Divine Barrier plus Improved Reach plus Arcane Surge plus the Singularity radius upgrade makes an enormous black hole; he thinks it is probably not okay on Dynamo. [9:09]
- Divine Barrier now gives range, not Healing Nova. [9:40]
- On Viscous, Improved Reach plus Divine Barrier plus Arcane Surge makes a massive ball with extra duration for little investment; Arcane Surge is good for ult-dependent heroes and may be too strong on some. [9:40]
- New item Spirit Snatch (upgrades from Spirit Strike): melee damage, bonus health, spirit resist and sprint speed; a light or heavy melee on a hero deals extra spirit damage and steals 12 spirit resist and spirit power. [10:11]
- Spirit Snatch gives about 26 spirit power and stacks across multiple targets; good on heroes who go in, he names Shiv, Yamato, Viscous (Puddle Punch) and Bebop, since any light melee applies it. [10:42]
- Debuff Reducer was nerfed by 2% and Reactive Barrier by 50 shield, among many small number changes. [11:13]
- Silencer was reworked into an anti spirit item: shooting a target passively silences them and for 6 seconds they deal -40% spirit damage; it also gives spirit resist and debuff resist. [11:13] [11:43]
- Silencer is a big nerf to spirit builds once you get it, and is the spirit-build counter alongside Metal Skin for gun builds. [11:43]
- Metal Skin now slows you while active, about 1.5 m/s. [12:13]
- Warden: spirit scaling on his fire rate was reduced, Alchemical Flask now respects line of sight (cannot hit through a wall), Last Stand cooldown went up 2 seconds, and resistance during the channel was increased. [12:43]
- Yamato no longer heals during her ult; she now heals 15% of max health on kill, the ult tier three got an extra second, and Power Slash no longer hits heroes behind cover. [13:13]
- Mirage: Tornado travels a shorter distance and can no longer be used while immobilized (no more escaping Warden's cage), and his ult cooldown went up 15 seconds. [13:43]
- Viscous's Puddle Punch can now be parried; the parry does not stun Viscous, it only stops the damage. [13:43] [14:14]
- Wraith: Full Auto was nerfed slightly, and her ult now moves targets up and down, so go for body shots on an ulted target rather than headshots. [14:14] [14:44]
- Vindicta's crow lost its tier three (+3% bleed and 40% anti-heal) and now gives -12% spirit resist and bullet resist (the tooltip shows only spirit; tested -18% on both); with the ricocheting bird and extra Stake duration, support Vindicta may return. [14:44] [15:15]
- Shiv was buffed all around: more damage from full rage early, an extra meter of range on his ult, higher Slice and Dice base damage and lower cooldown; he felt strong in scrims. [15:15] [15:46]
- McGinnis turrets now always take 3 seconds to kill, no longer scale with health, and have lower spirit resist; testing suggested the turret build is much better, possibly broken. [16:17]
- McGinnis got higher bullet velocity, a buff to her gun build. [16:48]
- Haze is the biggest winner: a new Smoke Bomb tier three gives charges and phases you out of the world for 0.5 seconds, dodging abilities and damage. [16:48] [17:19]
- With Rapid Recharge the Smoke Bomb charges come about every 4 seconds, with roughly 20 seconds between charge refills; maxing Smoke Bomb has a lot of potential as a high-skill dodge. [17:49] [18:20]
- Haze's Bullet Dance bonus damage is now spirit damage: 7.5 spirit damage by default plus another 7.7 with one point; with one ult point and only Rapid Recharge she did about 1,000 damage to a dummy with no Fixation stacks, very potent for solo kills. [18:20] [18:50]
- Grey Talon was buffed, but maybe not enough to be meta again. [18:50]
- Ivy's flight now scales with sprint speed while out of combat, so sprint items (for example Restorative Locket and Rapid Rounds) make her fly faster; this probably makes split pushing Ivy much better. [19:21]
