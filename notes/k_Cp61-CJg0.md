# How to Gank and Anchor | Deadlock Guide
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-04-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Cp61-CJg0

## Covers
An informal talk on roaming, ganking and anchoring in the lane phase: when to gank, the three questions to ask, when to stay or leave, what makes a hero a good ganker or a good anchor, and when to mirror a roam.
For players deciding who roams and who stays in lane.

## Claims
- Most roams happen between 2 and 6 minutes, because before 5 or 6 minutes Guardians are very tanky from their resistances, so enemies can hardly punish your absence by damaging the Guardian. [0:00]
- Enemies can still punish a roam by diving your lane partner, which is why a good anchor matters. [0:30]
- Roam route: come up to bridge, or take a box route under the bridge, farming the boxes on the roofs, then look at the adjacent lane. [0:30]
- Question 1 before ganking: where is the wave? If it is shoved into the enemies a gank is harder; if the enemies are shoved into your lane it is much easier. [0:30]
- Question 2: HP pools. If your teammates are 1 HP you can't force a gank; if the enemies are 1 HP you can force it and dive them even under tower. [1:00]
- Question 3, later in the window: do the enemies have ultimates? Ganks are easier when your teammates have ultimates and harder when the enemies do. [1:00]
- If there is no good gank, it is fine to keep box farming the nearby roofs and go back to lane. [1:00]
- If you are unsure whether a gank is good, gank, then review the VOD if it fails to see whether it was a missed key ability or a micro misplay; a misplay doesn't make the gank the wrong call. [1:30]
- The goal of a gank, especially an early one, isn't always to kill; doing a chunk of damage to one enemy and backing off is fine. [2:01]
- A gank is bad if you leave your lane partner alone for more than two waves, since they normally get dove, unless you ask them on the second wave whether you can stay a third. [2:01]
- Don't gank, deal a couple hundred damage, then stay and split one to three waves between three players; that minimizes your team's farm and is griefing. [2:31]
- Route aggressively so you come in behind the enemies. [2:31]
- Only stay in the ganked lane to hit the tower if you killed one or both enemies or got both low so they can't defend; take a big chunk of the tower, then leave when they return, and ask your lane partner if they are safe for you to stay longer. [2:31]
- The other reason to stay: both enemies are low HP and still trying to defend, so you can dive them. [3:02]
- Don't split a wave between three people unless it is for a greater purpose, like killing a Guardian or the enemies. [3:02]
- A good ganker has early game strength. [3:32]
- A good ganker has crowd control to set up ganks, for example Holliday's lasso, Paradox, or Abrams' Shoulder Charge. [3:32]
- Gap closers also help gankers, though less than CC; Yamato's 2 makes her a better ganker, and she has a lot of dive potential. [3:32]
- Wraith with cards or other gun carries are not good gankers: the roamer ends up with less farm than the anchor, so the gun hero or whoever scales should stay in lane. [4:02]
- Gun always outscales spirit, so gun heroes like Wraith or Venator want to stay in lane, where they get almost double the farm the roamer gets. [4:32]
- Play for your late game: let your carry keep scaling and start the snowball early for them. [4:32]
- Holliday likes roaming because she doesn't need much farm; she is usually played position 5, or 4 or 6 (6 if she is forced to be the support). [4:32]
- Positions are just who gets the most farm. [5:03]
- If Holliday shifts back to a gun meta and is played as a carry, she wants to roam less, but her lasso always makes her a hero that innately wants to roam. [5:03]
- A good anchor scales; heroes that do a lot of gun damage generally scale better. [5:03]
- Spirit is flat additive numbers, so it is strong early; gun is a multiplier of base damage, and base damage rises every level, so gun scaling can get out of control; there is an argument gun is still stronger after about 8 minutes in this meta. [5:33]
- A good anchor has wave clear; Ivy, Kelvin ("Katsu" in captions) and Paradox's swap ("Guy's Swap" in captions) boost anchoring. [5:33]
- If the enemies crash big waves into an anchor without wave clear, they can harass under tower, shoot the tower, or crash waves and mirror the roam. [6:03]
- Common mistake: when a roamer shows up in your lane (example Dynamo), don't leave the lane to mirror them; by the time you rotate they are gone, or they hover and counter-engage you. [6:03]
- When the enemy roamer leaves their lane, tell your team they are coming and to play safe, then threaten the enemy tower and dive; towers don't do much damage, so a 2v1 dive is very achievable. [6:34]
- Only mirror a roam if you see the roamer leave, so you can warn your team to slow play and can show up within about 10 seconds of them appearing in the lane; reacting after they show on the gank is too late. [7:04]
- A good anchor has anti-dive, for example Ivy's Stone Form or Paradox's swap; with the swap, divers who commit get swapped and both die. [7:35]
