# Massive Deadlock Balance Patch!!!!
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-11-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5yMTAJ3ZlKU

## Covers
Live read-through of the 2025-11-22 "minor" balance patch: ground soul orbs and denies, medic trooper packs, neutral camps, weapon and headshot changes, wall jump stamina, walker and guardian buffs, flex slots from walkers, Rejuvenator and mid boss, Urn, powerup buffs, a long list of item changes, and hero changes from Bebop to Yamato. For players relearning the game after the patch.

## Claims
- When soul orbs drop, the first half still uses the floating orb mechanic, but the orb then drops onto the ground and must be picked up; he thinks it is better, since it stops freezing to a degree and getting zoned off a minion was annoying. [0:00]
- Ground orbs last 18 seconds, plus 2 seconds per minute up to 40 seconds after 10 minutes. [0:00]
- Enemy orbs show as faint colorless orbs to your team and cannot be claimed; you can still deny the floating orb but not the ground orb. [0:32]
- Trooper range increased. [0:32]
- Medic troopers no longer have a passive healing pulse; instead they drop a medic pack when killed, with the same pickup range and duration as ground souls, healing everyone in a 30 m radius for 10% of missing health plus 75, plus 2 per game minute. [1:02]
- The player camera position was reworked in various situations. [1:02]
- Neutral bounties and neutral HP increased, so camps are tankier but give more souls; small camps deal more damage; neutral respawn timer decreased. [1:02]
- Medium camps deal more damage; hard camps get more spirit resist (harder to farm with spirit AoE) and more damage. Camps overall are stronger, which he likes. [1:32]
- Return damage raised from 60 to 80; minimum unsecured souls to drop raised from 100 to 150; the out of combat regen timer against neutrals increased. [1:32]
- All ability damage instances reduced by 10%, while base gun damage and gun damage growth increased by 10%, excluding Paige. [2:03]
- Headshot multiplier reduced; he calls it a big change. [2:03]
- Weapon investment bonuses now grant more, to encourage build diversity. [2:03]
- Initial wall jumps now cost 0.25 stamina; he strongly dislikes making wall jumps worse, nobody asked for it. [2:35]
- Stamina regen rate reduced. [2:35]
- After 35 minutes all troopers gain more HP, to end games quicker. [3:07]
- Guardian HP increased; Walker HP increased by 40%, making side lane Walkers the same HP as mid lane. [3:07]
- Walker engagement range reduced, so stomps are shorter and you must go further in to bait stomps. [3:07]
- Walker fireballs have a larger radius and now linger; stomp damage increased to 15% max HP; Walker fireballs now reduce bullet and spirit resist by 10%. He finds fireballs annoying. [3:37]
- Guardians will be harder to kill early. [3:37]
- Base item slots increased from 8 to 9 and flex slots reduced from 4 to 3; flex slots now come only from Walkers killed (one per Walker). Walkers are much more valuable, and he likes it since base guardians were a pointless flex slot. [3:37]
- The Rejuvenator no longer grants a buff; he loves that, the heal is already very strong. Mid boss health increased. [4:09]
- The team that kills the mid boss now gets two permanent buffs as compensation; still good, but not close to as strong as before. [4:40]
- Dropping or storing the Urn now puts parry on cooldown, so the carrier can't insta-parry when you go to melee the Urn off them. [4:40]
- The Urn no longer grants one ability point to the person dropping it off; it now gives four permanent buffs to that person, so you no longer need to run around to max your abilities. He calls it a W change. [4:40]
- Jump pad stun window increased; he dislikes it. [5:10]
- After meleeing an enemy off a mantle and applying the slow, they can no longer mantle-slide afterward, so meleeing off mantles is actually useful now. [5:10]
- Powerup buffs: the health buff now gives fixed regen instead of max HP (good, it was overpowered); the movement buff gives more stamina; spirit buff spirit power increased; gun buff now only increases ammo; casting buff spirit power increased and its cooldown reduction rescaled lower later. [5:41]

- Headshot Booster cooldown increased; it makes sense, the item was overpowered. [6:42]
- Split Shot cooldown increased massively. [6:42]
- Mystic Shot proc reduced again with updated spirit scaling. [6:42]
