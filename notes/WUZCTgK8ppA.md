## Covers
Haze coaching by Midknight; the student is an Alchemist-rank Haze laning with a Billy against a Drifter and Graves duo. Video dated 2026-08-24.
Themes: Haze item starts, desyncing with your lane partner, overextending and getting ganked by assassins, freezing waves, playing for comeback Rifts, aim micro (letting go of M1 between targets, sliding), Seven stun tech, and aim consistency as a mental issue.

## Claims
Haze build and items:
- Extended Magazine is not a good start; Haze's magazine has spirit scaling, so start Extra Spirit if you want ammo. [0:31]
- Extended Magazine on Haze only comes later, if at all. [1:02]
- You missed your window to buy on the first wave, which slowed the lane down. [1:02]
- Escalating Resilience is a slept-on item but not a core item, and he does not like an Extended Magazine start even when going for it. [5:31]
- Get wave priority first before backing to buy. [7:36]
- Grab the gun spike rather than going straight into Battle Vest. [8:45]
- He is not sure he likes Kinetic Dash on Haze, though he understands it for the fire rate. [9:16]
- Ending up selling Extended Magazine shows Extra Spirit was the better pickup. [17:38]
- Haze needs some CC or soft CC to finish kills; you keep letting people run because you have no movement slow, whether Slowing Hex or something like it. [19:16]
- Losing your spirit spike costs you a lot of ammo. [24:32]
- Being so far behind, your team needs damage more than utility; Indomitable was fine but not ideal. [26:36]
- Shred is broken at every point in the game, so a shred item like BRS should be low on your sell priority. [28:10]
- Use your 2 more for its active dispel. [28:42]
- You itemized yourself into a corner and got slot locked; you should have sold Battle Vest. [29:14]

Lane (Haze and Billy into Drifter and Graves):
- You and your Billy are desynced: you go in while he backs off and he goes in while you back off. [2:03]
- After winning a trade you never punished in a meaningful way, so you over traded. [2:03]
- Don't fight on the wave when your Billy is feeding the Drifter. [3:35]
- Respect how squishy you are and don't take angles that put you behind the enemies. [4:15]
- Don't overstay; ideally you would go for buffs then, but buff timers are not expected at this rank. [5:00]
- Don't space yourself out by dashing back right before you ult. [6:01]
- When your Billy engages, you have to engage with him even if fighting on the wave feels bad. [7:36]
- It is expected that a Drifter recasts his ability. [8:45]

Mid game, overextending and freezing:
- Don't push up aggressively when your teammates aren't nearby; you can easily get jumped. [9:51]
- Farm camps between waves; when you have wave priority, hit nearby camps (the church, the roof tier two) instead of walking at their tower. [10:21]
- When the gank comes, clear the wave from a safe distance and go to a camp rather than fight. [10:21]
- A Haze alone on an unbroken side lane should expect to get ganked. [10:52]
- If an enemy is running at you, her teammates are probably nearby, so back out; the Seven is duo-queued with her. [12:24]
- Freezing is not worth it with camps up next to the wave; after getting wave priority, go hit camps instead of walking up and asking to get ganked. [12:24]
- Hard freezing is only useful when there are no camps on the map, which almost never happens in a solo queue lobby; do it only if your nearby camps are cleared and you are far behind. [12:55]
- Soft freeze: hold the wave until the next enemy wave arrives, then clear both from a safe position so your wave pushes. Soft freezing is very useful in this game. [12:55]
- Slow push: let the wave push back naturally by hitting only the orbs, not the minions. [14:01]
- Soft freeze when enemies died or backed to box and they have more minions, so they miss a whole wave, then push it back and go box yourself. [14:32]
- At high level one player hard freezing while zoning the enemy happens, but most freezes last only a wave or two. [15:02]
- You keep getting ganked because you overextend against a duo. [15:02]
- Ult people in the open, not next to cover where they can drop down under it and escape. [16:05]
- Route on top of mid, not through it; it is not about efficiency but about not forcing yourself through a choke point. [17:38]
- Don't look aggressively with no ult up unless your aim can finish the overextended target. [17:38]
- After killing on their side, don't keep walking into their map; that is how the Drifter and his team kill you. Ask a teammate to come help kill the Seven instead. [18:45]
- Your team should be contesting the comeback Rift. [10:52]
- Without comeback Rifts the game is basically unwinnable; you will win many more games just calling people to fight comeback Rifts, because at this level enemies will always fight them and you get free kills, free comeback Rifts and mid boss. [22:24]
- After your team takes mid boss and is not positioned to brute force the walker, back off and buy. [24:02]
- Don't push the walker when the enemies are about to respawn; being up only one player is not enough to brute force mid walker. [25:34]
- Make a stand to stop them at your walker instead of running out. [26:05]

Seven matchup:
- You take way too much damage from the Seven. [25:03]
- Seven stun tech: dash before the stun procs, or keep momentum so you go flying while stunned. [25:34]
- The stun sets your vertical momentum to zero, so don't jump right before it goes off; jump, then dash right before it hits. [26:05]

Aim and farming micro:
- You lose a lot of DPS by missing a big portion of your bullets. [6:35]
- Let go of M1 between targets when flicking to orbs or minions; you are prefiring orbs too early. [6:35]
- Mantle slide while farming. [7:06]
- Slide to avoid having to reload while farming. [13:27]
- You hold left click while flicking and don't stop shooting between targets, which forces a lot of reloads. [22:54]
- When farming a group, shoot each minion or creep an equal amount of time to farm more efficiently. [27:10]

Summary:
- Play more with your lane partner. [29:45]
- In the mid game you were consistently overextending and getting ganked. [29:45]
- Against assassins like Drifter, Calico or Haze, be conscious of when they show on the map. [29:45]
- Assassins always target other assassins because they can one-shot each other. [30:16]
- Getting the enemy assassin behind is very valuable: if you are more fed, he can never enter your jungle because you keep killing him there. [30:16]
- Play for comeback Rifts. [30:16]
- Stop shooting between targets and stop sliding in a way that wastes ammo. [30:46]
- Mute teammates who flame; you were tilted. [30:46]
- Aim that swings between games is a mental and consistency issue; fix it with about 15 minutes of Kovaak's or Aim Lab a day. [31:17]
