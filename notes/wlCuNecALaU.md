## Covers
Midknight coaches an Ascendant Dynamo in a five-stack tournament game, critiquing both the player and the team's macro against a split-push comp with Mirage and Ivy.
Themes: punishing lane roams, calling when leaving lane, bridge buff fights, playing walkers and wave state, reading a split-heavy draft, Dynamo as first point of contact with Singularity, Refresher, Urn versus walker trades.
Late-2024 video (patch of mid November 2024); the game has changed a lot since, so check items and map details against the current patch.

## Claims
Player (Dynamo, Ascendant, tournament team game):
- When enemies position far apart in lane, punish it. [1:02]
- Do not tank a Mirage's damage; respect it, and punish him when he is separated from his partner. [1:32]
- When the enemy pushes the wave, grab a couple of boxes. [1:32]
- If you leave lane to take the middle Urns, tell your lane partner; he should then back off to the stairs instead of standing on bridge. [2:02]
- Do not walk back in against a Viscous and Mirage; even a Mirage Scarab could kill you. [3:02]
- Chasing into the enemy medium camp is too deep in a high-level game; expect them to boost down a lane to help. [3:32]
- Use idle time for boxes or the closest medium camp instead of doing nothing. [4:02]
- Kinetic Dash and Divine Barrier fit a gun support Dynamo, but still buy Extra Charge, because two kicks in a fight is influential. [4:32]
- Typing enemy timings in chat is good team play. [4:32]
- When the enemy shows three on the other side and one pushes deep alone, send two or three to kill him. [5:02]
- Know bridge buff timers; expect a fight on every bridge buff at this level, and expect a lone enemy coming for the buff. [5:32] [6:02]
- When behind, the win condition is a good Singularity: position so you are in every fight and use it. [6:34]
- React sooner to a walker getting pushed in; sending four 10 seconds late lost it to the split, while Seven could have stopped it in time. [6:34] [7:05]
- A Mirage splitting for the sake of splitting, without knowing where you are, is the one to punish. [7:35]
- With max level Rejuvenating Aura, use it as soon as a fight breaks out and someone like Lash is diving you, then walk out. [8:05] [8:36]
- At that soul count you should be saving for Refresher. [8:36]
- Do not take a fight for the 30 seconds Singularity is on cooldown; with Refresher, double ult. [9:06] [9:36]
- When farm gets sparse, expect enemies on the side of the map with the last camps, and expect them to start sieging walkers after farming. [9:36]
- The moment you see three enemies elsewhere, turn around to defend your walker. [10:09]
- Track the mobile heroes (Mirage, Ivy) as the likely split pushers; when they push a walker while you collapse elsewhere, come back fast. [10:41] [11:12]
- Mirage's teleport ult turns a fight into a 5v4 for them. [11:43]
- When jumping onto a rope under fire, spam Ctrl as you reach it so you jump off immediately and keep the momentum without getting shot off. [11:43]
- With one walker left, keep four people on that side for most of the game, keep that lane shoved, and kill their guardian there to push the wave further. [12:15]
- With map state like this, the side with your last walker is both teams' strong side. [12:45]
- Draft: your comp's split push is weak (only Pocket with Majestic Leap) against Mirage and Ivy, so play carefully around splits. [12:45]
- Your comp relies on chaining CC: Lash ult into Singularity, into Abrams ult, Seven ult or Pocket ult. [13:16]
- Do not have three people standing at an Urn drop-off just after they pick it up. [13:16]
- Read a split-heavy enemy and be ready to jump them, then leverage kills into the Urn, instead of just pushing them back. [13:46]
- Cancel Singularity sooner when it only holds one enemy who gets rescued (Ivy's beam); then use Refresher and Warp Stone into the group and ult again, or cancel and run. [14:47]
- Dynamo should be the first point of contact on a fight such as Rejuvenator, with Pocket tucking and waiting for the Dynamo ult rather than ulting first. [15:47] [16:17]
- When the enemy starts mid boss, go straight for the vent instead of farming a camp; pop out and immediately Warp Stone into Singularity so they cannot react or Stone Form. [16:47] [17:49]
- Coming from above and jumping down for the Dynamo ult is normally better than the vent. [16:47]
- Ivy's Stone Form knocks you out of your ult. [17:49]
- From mid game on the team did not position around the last walker; with Mirage free to split, the majority of players should be around it. [19:20]
- Giving the walker for the Urn can be right, but not with Abrams dead: it is a 5v5 plus Mirage teleport, a negative fight. Give the Urn, push out the walker wave, take their guardian, or split their middle walker. [19:51] [20:21]
- A side lane guardian is normally low value, but killing the one in your last walker's lane lets you push the wave deeper and slows their siege. [20:51]
- When the enemy sends all five to your side for the Urn, send someone boosting down the other lane to take their walker and keep flex slots equal. [21:21] [21:51]

Summary notes:
- In lane, punish when the enemy laner leaves lane; their Mirage hit you for half HP on the tunnel while their Viscous roamed and you did nothing. [22:53]
- Always call when you leave lane, even for 10 seconds of Urns or a camp. [23:24]
- Read the draft: against heavy split pushers with weak split of your own, punish the splitters or counter split; it is easier to punish a splitting Mirage than fight the team he teleports to. [23:54] [24:25]
- Watch walkers and wave state; with one or two walkers left keep the wave far from them, and when you show many players on one side expect a push on the other walker. [24:25]
- Have a player anticipate the opposite push and shove that lane out or clear the minions so they cannot take the walker. [24:55]
