# Haze NERFED, Item Changes and MORE in New Patch!
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-04-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ix9H5IIAaD0

## Covers
Line by line breakdown of the April 2026 balance patch with the coach's verdicts: parry, medic trooper, CC debuff resistance, items and heroes.
Big Haze ult nerf; items Recharging Rush, Titanic Magazine, Toxic Bullets, Return Fire, Golden Goose Egg, Silence Wave; heroes Bebop, Billy, Celeste, The Doorman, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Lady Geist, McGinnis, Mirage, Paradox, Rem, Shiv, Venator, Victor, Vyper.
For players who want to know what changed and what it means.

## Claims
- You can now parry during a grounded dash, not only from an instant air dash into parry; he thinks this good change brings ground dashes closer in power to instant air dashes. [0:00]
- Parry anti-mash protection was changed slightly, so you can parry again faster to parry several people in a row. [0:30]
- Medic trooper nerf: it now gives 14% of your missing health and has a little less health regen. [0:30]
- Repeated CC now gives more debuff resistance: the first instance gives 15% (was 10%) and the second 35% (was 30%), so chain stuns (for example Infernus ult then a Mo & Krill combo) are less bad. [1:01]
- Recharging Rush cooldown went from 20 to 24 seconds; it was really powerful on many heroes including Lash, mainly because of how often it refunded ability charges, so a slight nerf makes sense. [1:31]
- Titanic Magazine was buffed: +100% max ammo (was 90%) and slightly more weapon damage; it was already really good on paper depending on the hero. [2:02]
- Express Shot secondary fire bonus is now 40% instead of 35% and grows slightly more per boon; better on right click heroes like Yamato or Viscous, probably not enough to bring it back yet, though it might return on Yamato. [2:02]
- Toxic Bullets nerf: base damage 1.7% instead of 2% (more than a 10% DPS nerf), buildup 10% slower, anti-heal buffed from -30% to -35%; it is now more an anti-heal item and worse on heroes that already struggled to activate it, which he thinks makes sense. [2:33]
- Sharpshooter's move speed penalty was reduced from -1 to -0.7, a very slight buff. [3:04]
- Sinner's Sacrifice was buffed for the first time in a while: its active now gives 170% bonus souls, leaning into money generation after many stat nerfs; it was already good and is now better. [3:34]
- Armor Piercing Rounds now has a 55% proc chance instead of 50%; many call it one of the worst items in the game and he tends to agree, but it is slightly better now. [4:04]
- Return Fire now returns 60% of bullet damage (was 50%) and has a 2 second shorter cooldown; it is a more viable option against gun heroes that melt you, like Haze or Vyper, and if you can tank a little it will be crazy. [4:35]
- Weapon Shielding's trigger time window was extended slightly (not Spirit Shielding's, he believes); both shields got 1 second more barrier duration and slightly more move speed during it; they were a bit weak and people usually bought Reactive Barrier instead. [4:35]
- Plated Armor got +30 health; it was already really good against heroes that rely on on-hit damage and should now be incredible. [5:06]
- Golden Goose Egg: souls needed per permanent buff dropped from 150 to 100 (a buff), but permanent buffs now only count souls actually accrued, not the initial 400, so you can no longer buy and sell the egg repeatedly to print permanent buffs when slot locked. [5:36]
- Decay (captioned "The K") got an extremely small nerf to its spirit scaling. [5:36]
- Silence Wave was hit hard: damage -25, scaling 1.0 to 0.7, cooldown 30 to 42 seconds, now slightly longer than Dispel Magic's 40 seconds. [6:07]
- Echo Shard cooldown was increased by 5 seconds. [6:07]
- Lightning Scroll bug fixed: debuff resistance was making its stun go off faster. [6:07]
- Bebop: weapon windup time reduced from 0.5 to 0.3 seconds, extremely noticeable; the right click also fires almost instantly. [6:38]
- Bebop: Sticky Bomb can be self-cast again but deals 20% of its damage to you; its radius was nerfed by 2 m to compensate. [6:38]
- Bebop: the hook now does light melee damage, so it applies melee items, but not heavy melee. [7:09]
- Billy: max Bashdown charge time reduction is now -3 seconds instead of -2, and max Rising Ram deals 10% max health damage instead of 8%; quality of life buffs that make him slightly stronger, especially later. [7:40]
- Celeste: base health 700 instead of 790, health per boon 45 instead of 47, but she no longer has negative bullet resistance (was about -8%, now 0). [7:40]
- Celeste: base gun damage back to 18 from 22 with a little more gun growth, and her fire rate (base cycle time) was nerfed, so a gun Celeste build (early gun with Active Reload) is significantly worse; maxing your 1 with Spellslinger is still really good. [8:10]
- Celeste: Light Eater base damage is worse by 5 with better scaling; Dazzling Trick barrier went from 120 to 100 and its tier two from +100 to +80. [8:41]
- Celeste: only Radiant Dagger (max AP now 9% fire rate per stack) and her ult Shining Wonder (base damage 105 to 120, scaling 0.63 to 0.73) were buffed; he dislikes the ult buff because it showcases the least skillful and most frustrating way to play her, especially at lower ranks. [9:11]
- The Doorman: Luggage Cart no longer stuns, it only immobilizes and controls movement, so Dispel Magic should get you off the cart, meant as counterplay to being carted into the enemy spawn; in his sandbox test Dispel Magic didn't work, possibly a bug. [9:41]
- Dynamo: Quantum Entanglement base cooldown went from 16 to 19 seconds; a good change since with CDR it was nearly permanent, and with Superior Cooldown it is still about 10 seconds. [10:11]
- Grey Talon: spirit per boon up from 1.1 to 1.6; Charge Shot channel move speed up from 1.3 m to 1.5 m. [10:41]
- Grey Talon: Guided Owl is faster at max speed and slower at base speed, so it handles tight turns better, and lasts 2 seconds longer. [11:11]
- Haze: her ult's max AP no longer gives +1 target, which should end the ult build; even with Ricochet it won't hit as many targets, and teams can tank it, especially with the Return Fire buff. [11:42]
- Haze: you can now Sleep Dagger without leaving zoom; Sleep Dagger spirit healing went up a little, its tier two cooldown reduction went from -15 to -17 seconds, and its tier three slow duration was buffed. [11:42]
- Haze: Smoke Bomb spirit scaling buffed (easier to get more duration) and its tier three spirit lifesteal buffed. [12:12]
- Haze: base Fixation was buffed but tier three Fixation was nerfed. [12:12]
- Haze: you can no longer slide during her ult (the tech of ulting on stairs and sliding is gone), which he thinks may be more than necessary. [12:12]
- Haze: her ult now hits in a cone where you aim instead of all around you, so you must aim it in a general direction. [12:42]
- Overall Haze was nerfed so much she might not be very good anymore; no more Haze ulting entire teams soon. [13:13]
- Holliday: "various improvements to her gun", which he couldn't pin down beyond gun feel. [13:13]
- Kelvin: healing spirit scaling of his grenade nerfed by 0.1 on base and tier three, a slap on the wrist. [13:43]
- Lady Geist: Soul Exchange cooldown +5 seconds; base bullet damage 20.7 instead of 21.94 with slightly better scaling, so her gun is worse early. [13:43]
- McGinnis: bullet damage 6.2 to 6.4 with slightly better scaling; Spectral Wall lasts 6 seconds instead of 5.5 and has eight segments instead of seven, so it blocks bigger areas. [14:14]
- Mirage was incredibly powerful and got slight nerfs: Fire Scarabs health shield per second 9 to 8 and spirit scaling 0.12 to 0.1, Djinn's Mark spirit scaling 0.38 to 0.35; he is not sure it is enough, though small changes often are. [14:44]
- Paradox: gun base damage 6.4 to 6.8 plus some boon growth, about a 7% gun buff that should make it feel much better. [15:14]
- Paradox: her wall is slightly wider and taller and Kinetic Carbine has a slightly bigger collision radius, more forgiving to hit; she was slightly weak, so the buffs are welcome. [15:14]
- Rem: the soul bug was fixed (breaking a box while putting your helper on it gave duplicate souls). [15:44]
- Rem: you can no longer cancel Tag Along by accident while casting it to a teammate. [15:44]
- Rem: Naptime base radius is 19 m instead of 17, and two points now give 0.75 sleep duration per the notes (his game still showed 0.5); Rem was a little weaker than other supports but mostly fine. [16:14]
- Shiv: rage now builds against barriers; before, shooting someone with Divine Barrier, a spirit or reactive barrier, or a Paige shield built no rage. [16:44]
- Venator: base trap cooldown is now 28 seconds and base immobilize 1.25 seconds instead of 1.5, but two points in the trap give everything back, so at max AP he is the same hero. [17:16]
- Victor: bullet damage 12 to 13, already really good, so gun Victor might become a thing quickly. [17:16]
- Victor: Jumpstart tier two is +70 instead of +50. [17:48]
- Victor: Aura of Suffering builds up over 8 seconds instead of 6; its tier one slow now also affects dashes, a huge deal since most movement in Deadlock is dashes; its min and max DPS were increased by 10% and 20%. [17:48]
- Victor: Shocking Reanimation now applies a heavy diminishing slow when you die. [18:18]
- Vyper: she can now throw a dagger without leaving zoom; Screwjab Dagger charge time 4.5 to 4 seconds. [18:18]
- Vyper: sliding uphill allows lateral movement, which feels like more control; Slither slide distance bonus up from 10% to 15%. [18:18]
- Yamato: bug fix where Power Slash hit her own veil. [18:48]
