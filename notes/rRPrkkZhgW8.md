## Covers
Midknight coaches an Eternus 6 Viscous player (EU, mid lane) through a lost high E6 game against an Ivy, Sinclair and Wraith.
Themes: dash parry and wall jump parry, false-security shop angles, Splatter on camps and its ricochet, greedy buying with mid lane resets, always pushing the neutral wave, soul awareness, Puddle Punch usage, movement (edge boosts), Tank Buster timing, walker value, and playing safe in bad fights.
Patch around 2026-06-17.
