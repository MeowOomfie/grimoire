# We Need to Talk About Warden...
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-09-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZBhdWd-MbAg

## Covers
Warden, aquatiq, commentary over one standard match (win).
Lane against a Mina, the item path and a few macro calls, on the 2026-09-12 patch.

## Claims
- Warden is very strong right now, which is why he is being played. [0:00]
- He uses the "Lightbringer situation" Warden build. [1:02]
- In lane, clear your waves very fast, then keep poking the enemy laners after the waves are dead; shoot Mina as much as possible. [2:37]
- Early buys: Extra Regen, then Opening Rounds (he could afford both). [3:39]
- To do tower damage, push when the enemies are not in the wave. [5:11]
- Next he buys Enchanter's Emblem, then Swift Striker. [5:41]
- Mina's lane is very hard to play against and there is little you can do; keep pushing the wave, since wave priority is good for you. [6:47]
- Pulling several enemies onto himself (dying to four of them) let his team take two towers elsewhere, a trade he calls worth it. [8:23]
- Warden can simply walk at people and fight them. [10:00]
- After seeing a gun Grey Talon with Glass Cannon he plans Mercurial Magnum as his next item. [12:15]
- When the rest of his team is on one lane, he stays on the side wave. [12:45]
- When behind on souls, he would rather farm most of his next item (20k of 25k) before fighting again. [13:45]
- When the enemy team all has barriers and the fight is too tanky, it is not worth taking. [14:16]
- When your waves are bad, send one person to do the waves and the rest go mid. [19:28]
- Against a Mina holding a cleanse, silence her first to force her to use it. [20:01]
- After a won fight, push the walker with it. [20:31]
- With about 40 seconds before enemies respawn, take a shrine. [21:01]
- He buys Unstoppable because he keeps getting shoulder charged (Abrams). [22:33]
