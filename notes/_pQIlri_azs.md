# How To Play Wraith (Best Build) | In-Depth Game Analysis
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-12-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_pQIlri_azs

## Covers
Full replay review of a high-rated (Ascendant 4 average) Wraith game showing Metro's hybrid gun and spirit Wraith build in action: Hollow Point Ward start, Infuser, Tesla Bullets, Surge of Power with Full Auto maxed first, Slowing Hex, then Burst Fire, Siphon Bullets and Unstoppable. Covers lane aggression, farming the enemy jungle, side lane pressure and the reworked Telekinesis ult.
For Wraith players. This is a late-2024 video and the game, Wraith and these items have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Denying now gives only 30% of a soul's worth, so lane play has shifted to more aggression: focus less on denying and securing and more on putting damage on the enemy hero. [1:30]
- Still deny and secure when you have nothing better to do, but when it is a choice between denying and pressuring the enemy hero, pressure works out better in the current meta. [2:02]
- Buy Hollow Point Ward first if you are high HP, since it gives spirit power and weapon damage and adds damage to everything Wraith uses. [2:33]
- If you took a lot of damage in an early trade, go Extra Regen first (a healing item also works) so you can stay in lane and keep the pressure up. [3:03]
- The new Infuser lasts 8 seconds, which makes lane kills easy. [3:34]
- In a duo lane always be mindful of other lanes rotating over to gank you. [3:34]
- You can pressure the enemy by ganking other lanes or by pushing out your lane and stealing their jungle, which takes a lot of their economy. [4:04]
- The new Telekinesis ult is weaker in the laning stage because it no longer holds the enemy in place for two seconds, but it travels much faster and casts faster, so it still gets kills fairly easily. [4:04]
- After winning your lane, rotate to lanes where an enemy (here a Yamato) is too far forward to help a teammate who is losing or even. [5:05]
- Once the enemy towers on a side are down, you are still fairly safe on that side because you can fall back to your tower, while the enemy cannot move up far without being punished. [5:35]
- Going to a creep camp at 7 minutes while your creep wave was pushing back in was probably wrong; when there are creeps on the lane, killing the lane creeps is always worth more than jungle camps. [6:06]
- Four creeps in an early wave are worth 400 souls, while a tier two camp at about 75 per creep is worth about 225, takes longer, and costs map control because your wave stops pushing. [6:36]
- Only jungle when there are no creep waves pushing in anywhere. [7:06]
- At 8 minutes soul sharing is only 60%, so avoid sharing a lane: from 8 minutes on it is more efficient for four players to be on lanes and two to farm the jungle. [7:36]
- When ahead, farm the enemy jungle rather than your own; it is worth about double because you gain the souls and the enemy loses them, and they cannot get them back without map control on your side. [8:08]
- Skip the second enemy camp when it is unsafe and two of them could dive you. [8:39]
- By about 8 minutes the early game setup of Tesla Bullets and Bullet Lifesteal is ready. [8:39]
- With Infuser, Card Toss and a soul lead, Wraith can burst down a near full HP Infernus who does not expect it. [9:11]
- Put a little pressure on a walker, take the creep wave and back out when someone comes; it forces the enemy to rotate two people from farming, so they lose souls while you lose nothing. [9:42]
- Back out toward the side where you know a teammate is, and keep the whole side of the map pushed to their side so they constantly have to defend that walker. [9:42]
- As soon as someone is too far forward on the side you control, go for the kill. [10:12]
- Not looking for the kill there was probably a misplay; he held back because he had 3k unspent souls, so he was not truly far ahead of the Shiv in effective power. [11:14]
- Surge of Power is the best item for Wraith right now and the build's big power spike, with Full Auto (her 3) maxed first. [11:45]
- With Surge of Power and maxed Full Auto active you get high spirit and gun damage plus 40% lifesteal on both bullet and spirit damage, enough to kill anyone in a 1v1. [12:17]
- With Project Mind (teleport) up as an escape, chase, but do not commit if a second enemy is nearby. [12:17]
- As the richest player in the lobby a one-for-one trade is almost never worth it unless you also get something else, such as a walker. [12:48]
- Bait an enemy into a teammate who is coming, then push the walker. [12:48]
- The new Telekinesis has a very fast cast and travel time, so enemies who are not spot on with their reaction get caught; the target is silenced for 3 seconds, which secured the walker. [13:18]
- Full Auto lifesteal is like an early Vampiric Burst for 8 seconds; it heals back an enormous amount mid fight. [13:48]
- Dying after farming without cooldowns near enemies who commit hard was a misplay; he should have backed out further and farmed a tier three camp. [14:53]
- The one time this build is weak is when Full Auto is down; without it Wraith is very weak, especially in the early and mid game. [15:23]
- Correct 1v1 combo after teleporting aggressively: pop Infuser and Slowing Hex first, then the ult, then Full Auto. [15:53]
- Using the ult first without Slowing Hex let the target get far away before being slammed down; skipping Infuser was a second misplay because Infuser buffs Full Auto. [16:24]
- At level two the ult silences for about 3.5 seconds after the target is slammed, which stops a Paradox from using Paradoxical Swap and is usually enough time to kill her if played properly. [16:24]
- After dying, slow down and avoid feeding, especially as kill leader; go back to clearing tier three camps. [16:54]
- With Tesla Bullets plus Full Auto Wraith clears tier three camps almost instantly. [17:24]
- Even without the kill, forcing your ult onto an overextended target forces his team to rotate. [17:54]
- His flaw: when he feels ahead and powerful he plays overconfident and forces fights he should not. [17:54]
- Mid game priorities on Wraith: first take side walkers, which are very easy to kill; second punish players who are too far forward; third take the forward camps for souls. [17:54]
- With this build Wraith is again a hero that cannot lose a proper 1v1; his deaths came from overstepping into their base, misplays or forcing a 2v1. [18:56]
- Pushing out all the waves is the number one priority. [19:58]
- Burst Fire is the next buy in this stage of the game. [20:29]
- Lifesteal lets you chase and recover from half to almost full HP mid fight. [20:59]
- After losing both side walkers, keep the enemy away from your middle walkers. [20:59]
- The new ult is much better against Shiv: the old lift of 1.75 seconds was not enough to kill a tanky Shiv, but now wait until he commits his ult, then ult him; he is disarmed and silenced for about 3 seconds and can only run or melee. [22:01]
- In a fight, go for the target that is easiest to kill; Yamato is always committed, so she is a good target. [22:32]
- Do not force a 1v1 against a Paradox who still has Paradoxical Swap when your ult is down. [22:32]
- When all waves are pushing toward the enemy and teammates cover the lanes, split up and farm instead of sharing a single lane. [23:02]
- Go down side lanes that are pushed into the enemy; the enemy often pushes back from their spawn too far, and since the wave is near their spawn there is rarely a second enemy right behind. [23:02]
- If the enemy does not push further, push the wave back and farm that whole side of their jungle, including the tier three camp and side camps. [23:33]
- Slowing Hex, then lift, then everything else kills a Shiv who cannot do anything. [24:04]
- Old Wraith builds no longer work: you need slowing (Slowing Hex and Slowing Bullets) because the only way out of the new ult is running away after the lift; with slows they stay sitting ducks, so it acts as a longer disable. [24:35]
- With the new ult you must aim better because the target can move, but with slows it matters little. [25:06]
- After a kill, back out sooner; he could have teleported out earlier. [26:07]
- A free kill is a free kill: kill plus assist gold for a teammate is worth it even if the teammate would have won the 1v1 alone. [26:37]
- Any time you farm the enemy jungle safely it is a win for you and your team. [27:07]
- Push out a lane for one wave, then rotate to a heavy team fight to look for 1v1 pickoffs. [27:07]
- If your team can take mid boss alone and is up players, go stop an enemy who wants to trade for a walker instead of joining mid. [27:38]
- Teammates should not teleport away from mid for no reason; Mirage's Tornado is one of the best tools to stop a mid boss steal, so leaving cost them the mid boss. [28:41]
- To beat a Yamato parry, punch (melee) right away before it is active; his team punched too late and got triple parried. [29:12]
- When the enemy overcommits and uses everything, regen and then go back in for kills. [29:42]
- Dying in a trade that kills the enemy Yamato is fine and he would play it the same way; sometimes you have to die for your team. [30:13]
- After a fight, the first thing is to fix lanes. [30:43]
- Push out far when you can see several enemies in middle; seeing three or four of them on the map tells you it is safe to farm the side. [30:43]
- Teleport out at the last second to avoid a Mo & Krill grab. [31:14]
- At half HP with Full Auto and the ult up, and knowing the enemy ults were used, you are safe: anyone who touches you dies and you come out healthier. [31:44]
- Pop Infuser before Full Auto to make it last longer. [32:15]
- An enemy Infernus who thinks he is safe behind walkers can be killed instantly with Slowing Hex and burst. [32:15]
- Teleporting right into the melee range of walkers is not ideal. [32:47]
- Shoot walkers while waiting to regen and for Full Auto to come back off cooldown, then go back in. [32:47]
- The entire Wraith game loop revolves around Full Auto: back out when it is down, move in when it is about to be up. [33:17]
- After a lost fight, check the map state and see which lanes need fixing. [33:48]
- With 6k and enough damage to kill anyone, buy Siphon Bullets for tankiness. [34:18]
- Late in the game, when the next team to win a fight can run in and kill the Patron, the mid boss matters little, though you can use it as bait to make the enemy engage. [35:19]
- Take the Urn delivery when you know nobody is coming. [35:49]
- If the enemy cannot finish mid boss before your team can contest, there is no rush to stop it. [36:19]
- Wraith is not great at stealing mid anymore; as the most farmed player, play safe and guarantee kills instead of teleporting in to lift people. [36:49]
- The mass lift now lifts a maximum of two and is very short, so lifted enemies can still melee for the mid boss after. [36:49]
- Stay in a position where you can help your team without dying. [37:50]
- Metal Skin blocks your gun damage, so back out and wait for it to end. [37:50]
- When your team is mostly dead and the enemy is about to respawn, hold and defend. [38:53]
- Unstoppable counters a team with many disables (disarm, stun, knockdown); pop it and kill one person guaranteed. He could have bought it before Glass Cannon. [39:23]
- An enemy with Diviner's Kevlar can survive if he reacts, but the new ult is so fast he may not react in time. [41:26]
- Full Auto spirit damage over the game was almost as high as Card Toss damage, even though it only gets strong after Surge of Power; it beat crit, Tesla Bullets and the ult damage. [42:26]
- Siphon Bullets looks weak on the damage graph (about 3%) because it came late, but it is good for extra lifesteal and sustain. [42:26]
