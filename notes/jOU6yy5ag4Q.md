# How To CARRY BAD TEAMMATES in Deadlock Ranked | Educational Gameplay Commentary
channel: Natan
date: 2026-08-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jOU6yy5ag4Q

## Covers
Commented Pocket game (with Holliday in lane vs Calico and Infernus) where the team falls behind and Pocket carries a comeback.
Lane trading windows, ability unlock timing, box runs vs wave priority, playing for item spikes and timers, solo kills into Walker damage, freezing waves, Rejuvenator pushes, picks before Mid Boss and buffs, base defense after losing Mid Boss.
For ranked players stuck with teammates who fall behind.

## Claims
- Calico and Infernus are strong at level one (Calico's short cooldown slash heals and damages, Infernus has Afterburn), so on the first wave just neutralize: hide behind the Guardian, clear the wave safely and buy your item. [0:31]
- If the enemies stay long on the first wave they will be late to the second because they still need to buy; Pocket can then delete the second wave by throwing Flying Cloak through it. [1:01]
- Killing the first trooper of the third wave unlocks your second ability; if you kill yours before the enemies kill theirs, you have more abilities than them and a window to trade. [1:01]
- Natan neutralized waves one and two on purpose because he knew he would be strong on the third. [1:01]
- Camps spawn at 2 minutes; either be on the camp when it spawns to one-shot it, or wait until after the wave, because taking it late lets the enemies clear the wave and reset. [2:03]
- On Pocket, throw Flying Cloak back toward your Guardian before taking high ground so you can always teleport out. [2:03]
- When your lane partner keeps trading badly, clear the healing trooper to get them back to decent HP. [2:34]
- When both of you are half HP and an enemy is full, do a box run; be aware this hands the enemy lane priority and lets them shove or roam. [3:05]
- Watch behind you for a missing enemy coming back to lane; when the Calico showed mid at low HP, she was coming back. [3:35]
- If your lane partner is at 1 HP and the wave is cleared with enough time, they should reset. [4:36]
- When the enemy has no ultimates and you do, play very aggressively. [5:07]
- Natan's Pocket build maxes Barrage early and rushes a basic spirit spike, doing a lot of damage at any stage. [5:07]
- When the enemies are very low, the correct play is to dash in under their tower and fight them. [5:38]
- Natan disliked his Holliday roaming blue at about 6 minutes: their own enemy Guardian was very low and could be taken right away, the mid lane was losing, the target lane was 2v2 because Celeste was absent, and Holliday had no rope (only Paradox swap) to gank with. [6:40]
- Pushing your waves further creates more pressure and map control. [7:10]
- After winning lane, shove the wave and secure all Sinner's Sacrifice on your side of the map (start with your doubles) to accelerate your soul count. [8:17]
- Do not go for low probability plays (for example joining a lost fight on blue with a dead Celeste and low Paradox) when you are not confident you will kill people and save the Guardian. [8:17]
- Play for your power spikes: maxing Barrage is a huge spike, and Majestic Leap makes Pocket very strong, after which you look for fights. [8:47]
- Play around timers: shove the wave and take one more camp to buy your spike item before the bridge buff spawns, then be on the buff on time. [8:47]
- Bait enemies by letting them walk up to the wave; even a failed kill attempt creates pressure and pulls enemies over, relieving your teammates elsewhere. [9:49]
- After a kill, take the enemy jungle camps and all their resources. [10:19]
- Escort a teammate delivering the Urn to keep them safe instead of taking a tier two camp. [10:19]
- Check enemy ultimates and items before a fight; Wraith can teleport away, so Natan did not follow his Ivy into her. [10:19]
- When the Rift spawns and you are just short of your item spike, go back and buy rather than walking up to a Walker and risking being last hit or burned. [10:49]
- Spam ping a lane to warn about an enemy pushing it. [11:21]
- Fake backing off while waiting for your wave to walk up, then engage; with three Barrage stacks Pocket has huge amp. [11:21]
- On any side lane carry look for solo kills that lead straight into Walker damage; it is the infinite souls glitch. [11:52]
- With Flying Cloak maxed Pocket can stay at the Walker, throwing cloak for extra damage. [11:52]
- When a play is risky and you are low, dump damage and leave; if the carry dies they are dead a long time, lose souls and give the enemy laner space to take the Guardian and farm. [12:22]
- When you are low HP, Sinner's Sacrifice is respawning and you lack enemy positions, take the Sinner's Sacrifice before a low Walker. [12:54]
- On Pocket, throw your cloak backwards so you can show up to a wave and push it even when low. [13:30]
- Never throw Flying Cloak forwards to chase, since it is your escape; doing so got Natan collapsed on and killed. [13:58]
- Natan died at 15 minutes with a 40 second death timer while earning 1,200 to 1,600 souls per minute, so a minute dead is a lot of souls lost. [13:58]
- If you are able, always shove the mid wave and at least keep it out of your Walker. [14:30]
- When you see four or five enemies on one side of the map, the other side is safe to push and take a Guardian. [14:30]
- Freeze a wave (do not touch it) when enemy troopers outnumber yours and your team has no map pressure: their troopers kill yours and the enemy misses those souls. [15:01]
- If your team is pressuring another lane hard, push the wave instantly instead, to use that pressure. [15:01]
- Pretend to back off after pushing to give the enemy a false sense of security, then go for the Walker. [15:31]
- Do not push up far when the enemy cannot see a carry on the map; you can easily be collapsed on. [16:01]
- After buying items, force the enemy to show on the wave; with Wraith dead and Calico low, look for a fight. [16:01]
- When kills open a window, ping Mid Boss, but first back up and fix your waves rather than fighting again instantly. [17:02]
- With Rejuvenator, plan the goals: the next Rift, one of the remaining Walkers, and the Sinner's Sacrifice left on the map. [17:02]
- Do not rush a lane when a Rift is spawning and teammates cannot trade or help on that side. [17:02]
- Use voice comms to give information, for example enemies grouping or possibly teleporting toward the Rift. [17:33]
- With Rejuvenator, push all your waves and sync them so you pressure several places at once and spread the enemy out; Rift troopers in one lane plus pushes in the others make a Walker very likely. [18:03]
- If your team pushes too late and the enemy trades a side Walker, the enemy hero taking it is not defending the other Walkers, which is still an advantage. [18:34]
- The blue (mid) Walker is worth more than a side Walker and gives the most map control; left for last it is the hardest to take because it is central and easy to defend. [19:04]
- When Rejuvenator is running out, the enemies have ultimates up and the last Walker is very tanky, stop pushing and take all the farm on the map instead. [19:04]
- A five-man enemy push on a Walker after seeing only two of you is a high-risk 5v4 that would not happen in a full Eternus 6 lobby. [19:36]
- When most of your team's net worth is on you, your priority is not dying. [20:37]
- When far ahead you normally buy defensive items, since dying ahead gives a long death timer and no damage; Natan took Spirit Burn instead because his team would not deal damage. [20:37]
- About 1 minute before Mid Boss is when you look for a pick; a pick earlier is nearly useless because the enemy respawns in time for Mid Boss. [21:38]
- Do not spend key ultimates (for example Holliday's lasso) on a pick too early, since they will be on cooldown for Mid Boss. [21:38]
- Plan picks around the bridge buffs: enemies go for them, so ambush there, then do Mid Boss. [21:38]
- Commit a minute and a half in advance on what the team should do around Mid Boss. [22:39]
- When your waves are shoved in and you are too low to contest, give the enemy Mid Boss. [22:39]
- After the enemy gets Mid Boss, keep most of the waves out of your base: fewer waves in base means less enemy pressure and an easier defense. [23:09]
- During an enemy Rejuvenator, look for overextended isolated targets to kill. [23:09]
- If the enemies flock to a Rift on one side, the other side is safer; push out the blue and yellow waves, the safest waves, to delay them. [23:40]
- Natan bought Indomitable because the way they lose is the carry getting locked down by Mo & Krill's or Wraith's ultimate. [24:15]
- Pocket is very good at base defense: ziplines charge Barrage, you can throw your cloak into spawn and teleport back to safety. [24:48]
- When several enemies are dead and a wave is pushed far, call the team to take a Shrine. [25:18]
- Staggering enemy deaths (killing another enemy while their team is dead) keeps their whole team off the map longer. [25:48]
- With 2 or more minutes until Mid Boss, hard commit on the Patron: even if you all die you respawn for Mid Boss, as long as you drop it and take the first phase. [26:19]
- Plan your exit route before a base push; the lane with a zipline (green here) is the safest and fastest way out. [26:19]
- Before Mid Boss, shove out the waves and group to look for a pick with your pick heroes (Holliday, Paradox, spirit Ivy). [26:55]
- With Indomitable, bait out the Wraith ultimate instead of avoiding it. [27:28]
- Without their mobility ultimates the enemy cannot look for an effective pick, giving your team the edge. [27:28]
- With enemies dead for a long time after Mid Boss, press the advantage immediately and protect the incoming wave. [28:32]
- In fights, target the Wraith first because she is the scariest enemy. [29:03]
