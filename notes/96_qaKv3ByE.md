## Covers
Final part of a coach's four-part counter series (September 2025 patch), for any player struggling against Sinclair, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous, Vyper, Warden, Wraith and Yamato. Each hero gets tech to play against them, counter items with special interactions, and the key ability to track in lane, timed against the 30 second wave clock. A Google Sheet cheat sheet of counter items is linked in the description.

## Claims
### General
- Track a key enemy ability using the 30 second wave timer; Abrams's charge is a 37 second cooldown, so after he uses it he is punishable until about that point in the next wave. [0:30]
- Rebuttal counters players who like to melee; it gives a buff when you parry and reduces parry cooldown. [1:01]
- Bullet Resilience and Spirit Resilience (lane) and Enchanter's Emblem and Battle Vest (later) are good into their damage types, not specific heroes. [1:01]
- Bullet Resilience is slightly worse because enemies can buy Armor Piercing Rounds to pierce stacked bullet resist. [1:32]
- Curse is good into every hero. [1:32]
- Spirit Shielding feels great into two spirit heroes and is much better than Weapon Shielding. [1:32]
- Into oppressive gun damage, fire rate slows by role: support Rusted Barrel or Disarming Hex, tank Juggernaut, spirit Suppressor; one more fire rate slow item is bought mostly because it is overpowered right now. [2:03]

### Sinclair
- He relies on being on the bridge with wave priority firing bolts; keep him off height by focusing the wave, which is why better Sinclairs buy Monster Rounds early. [2:34]
- When he recasts his bolts, double jump if far, jump if close; wall bounce away from the wall so he can't bank it off the wall, or dash jump. Quirky movement makes bolts much harder to hit. [2:34]
- Call it out when he steals your ult (Rabbit Hex copy), especially big team fight ults like Lash's or Dynamo's; the copy lasts only 12 seconds, so back up and re-engage after. [3:05]
- Call out even random stolen ults like Infernus's, because stolen ults aren't visually distinct and teammates may think it's their own. [3:35]
- Any debuff reduction shortens time spent as a bunny (Rabbit Hex), relevant against the rarer melee Sinclair. [4:06]
- Counterspell is great into him: his bolts are predictable and his bunny has a long wind up like Lady Geist's bomb. [4:06]
- If he is super ahead and nuking you with bolts, Spellbreaker neuters their damage. [4:36]
- Silence Wave on him when he grabs or looks to grab a big ult suppresses him. [4:36]
- Reactive Barrier is strong in lane because it activates when you get bunnied, letting you survive bunny into double bolt burst. [4:36]
- Slowing Hex doesn't stop him placing Spectral Assistant but stops him recasting to teleport to it; use it when he places the assistant and rats your walker. Silence Wave works too but Slowing Hex is more targeted. [5:06]
- His key ability in lane is Spectral Assistant: 40 second cooldown, 30 with one point, about one wave. [5:06]
- The assistant adds significant weapon damage and doubles his bolt burst, and makes him slippery; when he uses it, tuck behind cover so you don't get bolted. [5:37]
- The assistant lasts 6 seconds (13 with points); once it expires, come back and trade since he is much weaker. [6:08]

### Victor
- Victor isn't too scary in lane; manage stamina so he can't get on you and gun you down, keep a couple of stamina in reserve and use wall bounces to escape. [6:08]
- Don't eat a lot of his 1s; if you know it's up, play near a corner, fake a wide swing and tuck to bait it out; it is easier to dodge than you think. [6:38]
- Post lane, always watch whether he has ult using the eye icons at the top. [6:38]
- Like Abrams, don't focus him: he wants to eat your cooldowns while his carries do the damage. [6:38]
- Warp Stone and any movement are very good into him; late game the main counter is staying out of his radius, with Warp Stone, Enduring Speed, Slowing Bullets or slows. [7:09]
- Slowing Hex isn't great since he can cleanse it with his 2, but it's not awful if bought for others. [7:09]
- Capacitor (or Curse) turns off his 3, resetting its ramping DPS from 75 back to 15. [7:39]
- Anti-heal is good into him; Healbane and Toxic Bullets are best since they are easy to reapply, while Spirit Burn he usually cleanses with his 2. Gun heroes are a hard counter to him. [7:39]
- Restorative Locket keeps stamina in reserve to get away from him. [8:09]
- Silence Wave him at about 30% HP while he sits on his 2 charges and kill him before he can spam them. [8:09]
- In lane monitor his 2: 22 second cooldown; with Extra Charge and points he has two charges worth 200 to 400 HP plus movement speed to escape. [8:39]
- Pain Battery charges by damage, not time; when he plays aggressive looking for it, play safe, and damage you deal while it is full charged is even more important. [9:10]

### Vindicta
- Reset when low; overstaying in a Vindicta lane feeds her souls and a death from her snipe. If you sit on 1 HP it's the same as against Grey Talon. [9:40]
- Debuff Remover is one of the best items: it cleanses her Stake; her damage comes when she stakes you in the open, then flies and crows. Late game it also stops her setting you up to be collapsed on by her team. [9:40]
- Healing Rite is fantastic in lane; the biggest thing you can do is stay healthy above her execute threshold. [10:11]
- Spellbreaker reduces her snipe damage by 75%, so a snipe can rarely execute you, even from a Vindicta who knows weird lineups. [10:11]
- Knockdown takes her out of the air; the longer a target falls, the longer they are stunned, so if she doesn't cancel dash to the ground she is stunned for a long time. [10:42]
- Reactive Barrier procs when she stakes you. [11:13]
- Monitor Crow Familiar (bullet and spirit resist shred plus a heavy bleed, huge poke, more damage taken while crowed) and Stake; cooldowns 40 and 30 seconds. [11:13]
- Her kit is inflated (Flight is her worst ability), but she is not good on this patch. [11:43]

### Viscous
- As with Sinclair, shove the wave and deny him the bridge; without height Splatter and Skinner combos are much harder to hit. [11:43]
- Double jump to dodge his ult; good Viscouses predict it by double jumping themselves, then slide under him or roll to the side. [12:14]
- Parry Puddle Punch before it hits to block the damage; it takes practice with a friend. [12:44]
- Most Viscouses lead with Splatter then Puddle Punch, so when you see Splatter, drop a parry to read the punch. [13:14]
- Viscous has few direct counter items; Spirit Shielding is good against him. [13:14]
- Capacitor removes The Cube from someone, a great counter to a support Viscous saving people; good with the month-old "low main" build. [13:44]
- Close Quarters gives melee resist that applies to Puddle Punch. [13:44]
- With Rebuttal, parrying his Puddle Punch damages him. [13:44]
- Armor Piercing Rounds is very good because his ult gives 60% spirit and bullet resistance. [14:14]
- His key ability in lane is The Cube: 42 second cooldown, invulnerability he can put on a teammate; without it he is much easier to all in. [14:14]

### Vyper
- Use verticality: she can't slide up things, so play around the bridge and jump up when she starts a slide, making her waste stamina. [14:44]
- Monitor her stamina; out of stamina she can't abuse slides and is vulnerable. [14:44]
- Slowing Hex reduces her dash distance so she can't slide as far; anything reducing her mobility is good. [15:14]
- Knockdown stuns her so your team can focus or chain CC her. [15:14]
- Plated Armor blocks about 30% of her bullets and reduces the on-hit she gets on a maxed Venom. [15:14]
- Return Fire and Metal Skin help survive her burst, as do fire rate slows. [15:44]
- Debuff Remover cleanses stacked Venom; Counterspell can block Venom when it ends, or her ult with fast reactions. [15:44]
- Her key ability in lane is Venom: 20 second cooldown, a lot of damage, especially with an early Mystic Burst. [16:15]
- Treat about 250 HP as her lane execute threshold for a Venom one-shot (assuming Mystic Burst). [16:15]
- Keep HP topped off (Healing Rite, resetting) since she does more damage the lower you are; don't let her land Venom while low. [16:45]
- If she throws Venom on a healthy teammate just for a Mystic Burst proc, all in on her since she lacks her execute. [16:45]

### Warden
- If you have instant CC or Curse, use it on him while he channels his ult; it puts it on its full 155 second cooldown and leaves him vulnerable. [17:16]
- Better Wardens Majestic Leap before ulting, but many ult next to a Viscous or Holliday with ult; take his ult out of the equation. [17:47]
- Always have an escape plan in lane and count one less stamina than you have, since Alchemical Flask with a point takes stamina away. [17:47]
- When he cages, wall bounce or double jump out as fast as possible; keep stamina topped off. [18:17]
- Restorative Locket in lane is great into him, mostly for the extra two stamina to escape the cage or his ult. [18:48]
- Curse shuts down his ult. [19:18]
- Debuff Remover removes the cage, useful against a cage build. [19:18]
- Counterspell while he ults guarantees surviving a couple ult ticks, and can block the cage when it lands. [19:18]
- Enduring Speed's slow resist (or Debuff Remover's cleanse) helps escape the cage and resist his Slowing Hex and Slowing Bullets; cage Wardens are more common in low elo. [19:49]
- Slowing Hex him when he Majestic Leaps in and ults so you and teammates can kite. [19:49]
- His key ability is kind of his 2, Willpower; if you are lower elo and getting hit by the cage (Binding Word) a lot, monitor the cage instead: 37 second cooldown, 18 with points. [20:20]
- Willpower's 125 barrier and movement speed make him slippery; once you are comfortable dodging cage, it is the scarier one to track, and without it he is basically minus 125 HP, so go for him. [20:20]

### Wraith
- Monitor her cards, shown above her health bar; with two or three cards (117 damage with points, three with Extra Charge) she has lots of burst, so play safe. [20:50]
- Make her spend damage while holding all card charges; never fight her when she has stacked cards, when she is strongest. [21:20]
- Disarming Hex and Return Fire shut down her damage if she teleports in; all fire rate slows are good into her. [21:20]
- Counterspell blocks her ult easily since it has a long wind up; Divine Barrier cleanses the ult from teammates or yourself. [21:51]
- Slowing Hex shuts down her teleport for a 3.5 second window when she splits or overextends; call it out. [21:51]
- In lane monitor her Full Auto: 48 second cooldown, 34 with a point; +20% fire rate plus later spirit damage per bullet, also buffing allies and minions. [22:23]
- She usually puts two points into cards before Full Auto; when she uses Full Auto, punish for the next 30 seconds. [22:53]

### Yamato
- Respect her in lane: one of the strongest laners in the game whose role is to dominate lane and snowball. [23:23]
- If she is doing well, play slow, buy Monster Rounds, kill minions and keep your tower alive; you don't need to force trades. [23:23]
- She buys Restorative Shot early to force trades; Power Slash (with slide or mantle tech) and her right clicks are oppressive in lane. [23:54]
- Build regen and sustain and don't die; Monster Rounds, Restorative Shot and Healing Rite are worth it, because a Yamato who doesn't snowball is much worse. [23:54]
- Yamato players who lose lane tend not to be good in the game; they only know how to play from very ahead. [24:25]
- Counterspell her 1 (Power Slash): it is predictable with a long channel; in ult her 1s come out faster than the Counterspell duration, so react to the channel start. [24:25]
- Armor Piercing Rounds is great since her ult gives 40% of each resistance, up to 60% maxed. [24:55]
- Silence Wave her at about 50% HP after she dumps Power Slash and her other cooldowns before ulting (her ult resets cooldowns); the 3.25 second silence is enough to kill her; call it out. [25:25]
- Warp Stone helps escape her, including when she flies at you with her 2, and can dodge Power Slash in ult if timed when it's about to release. [25:55]
- Rebuttal is good into her because she has very high base melee damage and likes to melee in lane. [25:55]
- Monitor her 3: hero heal on hit (6% max HP plus about 90 flat) can catch you off guard. [26:26]
- Her 3 and Power Slash are both about 10 second cooldowns, so in a fight trade after she uses them and backpedal by around 7 seconds before her burst returns. [26:57]
