## Covers
Mirage, aquatiq's first ranked game on the big new map update, with light commentary.

Patch of early October 2026 (video 2026-10-03).

## Claims
- He maxed Djinn's Mark this game. [1:10]
- Into Mo & Krill and a high HP lane, he planned Healbane, mostly for the Mo & Krill. [2:10]
- He considered Headshot Booster because he could hit their heads easily. [2:40]
- A Dynamo partner was useful in a hard lane. [8:32]
- When a lane is unwinnable (enemy Mo & Krill with huge HP), leave it and go to a side lane rather than sharing souls. [8:32]
- Slowing Hex on you shuts off Mirage, because you cannot use your Tornado to escape. [11:18]
- Check whether your team is winning the Rift; if so you are not needed there and can take a guardian instead. [10:43]
- Sit on the vents to regen instead of buying regen items. [13:01]
- When the mid boss is free, the team should heal up and go straight mid. [16:15]
- Double Tornado (two charges) makes him comfortable going into fights. [17:56]
- Late game he needed both Inhibitor and Silencer. [19:37]
- Against this comp he took Inhibitor and Siphon Bullets, saying it is 100% Siphon Bullets this game. [27:06]
- Giving up Rifts is fine when you trade them for pushing the enemy Walkers and structures. [23:15]
- Getting both shrines is good for the game. [27:06]
- Mirage is still very strong if you know how to play him. [30:22]
