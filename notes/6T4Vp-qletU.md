# How Strong is Each Hero in Lane?
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-02-28
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T4Vp-qletU

## Covers
A coach's lane-phase tier list (lane bully, good, mid, bad) for the whole roster as of late February 2026, before the 3/6/26 patch, with the reason for each rating and how to play into or out of the strongest laners.
For anyone deciding whether a lane is one to win, slow play or just survive.

## Claims
- Winning lane is not always about breaking the enemy tower sooner than they break yours; against stronger laners your goal is to slow play the wave, keep your tower alive and box route. [0:00]
- Against a super strong lane, your goal is to survive until an adjacent lane wins and can send help. [0:00]
- Abrams: good tier. One point in his charge (Shoulder Charge) is an extremely strong spike, probably the strongest use of one ability point in the game, and gives him a lot of oppression. [0:00]
- Abrams: if he is getting poked a lot, a point in his 3 (Infernal Resilience) gives a ton of regen and makes him very hard to kill. [0:30]
- Abrams: he is still a shotgun hero you can space, so he is not quite lane bully tier. [0:30]
- Apollo: lane bully. He feels extremely strong before 25 minutes and is "completely cosmetic" after 25 minutes. [0:30]
- Apollo: his 3 is oppressive in lane for both damage and healing. [0:30]
- Apollo: he is hard to punish because of his 2, Repose. [1:00]
- Bebop: good tier, not lane bully. His Hook has a very low cooldown and he can uppercut laners for a lot of damage. [1:00]
- Bebop: he lacks the dive potential of the lane bullies and has little innate healing in his kit. [1:00]
- Good laner against good laner mostly comes down to skill or specific matchup nuances. [1:00]
- Billy: good tier. His 1 provides a lot of damage and his 3 gives a lot of healing. [1:00]
- The melee lifesteal mechanic is strong right now; any hero with something in their kit that innately triggers melee lifesteal (like Billy's 1) ends up in the good or lane bully tier, because melee lifesteal is a large amount of flat healing. [1:32]
- Calico: good tier. Her 2 innately procs melee lifesteal. [1:32]
- Calico: her 1 (Gloom Bombs) does an absurd amount of damage but is not easy to hit. [1:32]
- Calico: her ult can bail her out of bad situations. [1:32]
- Celeste: good tier, "extremely overtuned right now"; arguably lane bully but lacks dive potential. [1:32]
- Celeste: much of her damage comes from the amp and poke of her 3, plus her 1, and her 2 gives survivability. [2:03]
- The Doorman: good tier. He is very oppressive if he hits the initial contact damage of his bell (Call Bell). [2:03]
- The Doorman: his door gives mobility and a good way to get off angles. [2:03]
- Drifter: lane bully (first rated mid, changed after talking to other coaches). His 1 rapidly applies melee lifesteal and melee life strike if he rushes it. [2:03]
- Drifter: innate 3.5 regen in his kit makes him hard to kill. [2:33]
- Dynamo: bad tier. He could almost be good if his kit had a vertical hitbox, but he is hard countered by jumping twice. [2:33]
- Against a Dynamo bully, stand on top of something and jump twice if he comes near; ideally wall bounce and then jump. [2:33]
- Graves: bad tier, one of the most objective picks on the list. [3:03]
- Graves: dodge her 2 and its root; with only a 17 m gun range, two stamina and one regen she is very punishable if she makes mistakes. [3:03]
- Graves: light melee to clear her 1; she does not do a crazy amount of damage in lane. [3:03]
- Grey Talon: good tier, almost lane bully, with a lot of damage. His trap is "stupidly overpowered". [3:03]
- Grey Talon: one point into his trap gives a massive cooldown reduction that people sleep on. [3:33]
- Grey Talon: he lacks dive potential; if he has you low, buy regen items and play a little safe, since he can only confirm the kill with his bird (Guided Owl), which you can move and space to stay out of its execute threshold. [3:33]
- Haze: good tier; normally mid to bad, but she becomes a lane bully with lane priority. [3:33]
- Haze: her Fixation lasts way too long, so with minions pushing into you she can shoot you under tower and feel absurdly strong. [4:03]
- Haze: lane priority is hard for her to get; stop her from getting it (buy Monster Rounds, etc.) and she sits in a lower tier. [4:03]
- Holliday: good tier, almost lane bully, but she only gets bully status around her tier two barrel spike and her spirit spike. [4:03]
- Holliday: with rapid recharge from Mystic Burst or extra spirit, around 5k souls her barrels feel like mini nukes, but it takes a lot of the laning stage for her to come online. [4:03]
- Infernus: mid tier, overhated. He needs a comp that plays around him and must be played aggressively. [4:33]
- Infernus: a player who hits consistent headshots can still get ignites quickly. [4:33]
- Ivy: mid tier. She is not inherently good in lane and has a big head, so she is easy to headshot. [4:33]
- Ivy: she can Kudzu the wave and headshot the minions held in the Kudzu, so she clears waves very easily and can then box route. [4:33]
- Kelvin: good tier; much of his strength comes from his tier two grenade, which adds healing to it. [5:05]
- Kelvin: he enables a partner like Shiv to be very oppressive. [5:05]
- Kelvin: his dome is overtuned once his ultimate is online, but the counterplay is spacing it. [5:05]
- Lady Geist: very good, especially defensively. Her 1 clears the wave quickly and she has some poke. [5:05]
- Lady Geist: she is a very good anchor while you roam or box route, because you cannot dive her with her ult online or she swaps you and nets the kills. [5:35]
- Lady Geist: if you dodge her bombs (hard to do) she is not that scary, and she lacks dive potential if you play around the cooldown of her 2. [5:35]
- Lash: okay now; very bad last year, better after the Flog buffs. Flog gives a lot of healing. [5:35]
- Lash: play pretty safe, play around height, Ground Strike the enemy or the wave when you cannot hit them, and use Flog to keep topped off. [5:35]
- Mina: lane bully, "by far the strongest lane bully". She gets to your tower and decimates it, and her gun numbers are way too strong. [6:06]
- Mina: her 3 does way too much damage; it is odd that her late game keeps being nerfed but not her laning stage. [6:06]
- Mirage: very strong laner with a lot of survivability from his innate; his 3 gives a lot of poke and damage. [6:06]
- Mirage: he lacks the nuke potential of Mina or Apollo and their dive potential. [6:36]
- Mo & Krill: very strong once he has his QSR (Quicksilver Reload), but before 1.6k souls without it he is very punishable. [6:36]
- Mo & Krill: get him below 50% HP before his QSR and his lane feels awful; most players fail to punish him then and let him get QSR for free. [6:36]
- Paige: good laner; like Kelvin she makes her partner stronger with her shields, and she has very high base gun damage. [6:36]
- Paige: her low bullet velocity forces her to play aggressively, and she often has to use her shield on herself just to hit minions. [7:06]
- Paige: punish her around her 2 cooldown and by denying orbs. [7:06]
- Paradox: good tier. Her carbine does a lot of damage, her grenade clears waves so she can box route, and her ult has a very low cooldown. [7:06]
- Paradox: oppressive but lacks crazy dive potential. [7:06]
- Pocket: good, though not that strong in lane. Affliction keeps you on the back foot. [7:06]
- Pocket: cloaking every wave keeps decent priority so they can box route and slow play; their damage only feels oppressive when you are already low and they burst to finish. [7:36]
- Rem: good laner almost solely because he presses his 3 on the healing creep, which is stronger than anyone else's healing in lane or the game; he expects that scaling to be changed. [7:36]
- Seven: bad laner, mostly because he has the biggest head hitbox in the game; buy Headshot Booster or chain headshot him and he is very vulnerable. [7:36]
- Seven: decent wave clear from his 3, and hard to kill once he has his ult, since he can panic ult with spirit lifesteal for decent survivability. [8:06]
- Shiv: depends on his partner; with Kelvin both heroes go to S tier, but with a random partner he is mid tier, and with someone like Lash he does not feel crazy. [8:06]
- Shiv: oppressive once his ult and rage are online; before that you can play around him, space his shotgun, or focus his partner if they are squishy. [8:06]
- Silver: lane bully. She was once one of the worst laners and was buffed repeatedly; pressing her 2 and 1 takes half the enemy HP bar with next to no counterplay. [8:37]
- Sinclair: lane bully (another coach rated him good). His bolts do a lot of damage and his assistant does a lot. [8:37]
- Sinclair: if you stop him from having wave priority he is okay to play against; with priority he chains bolts on you under tower, Rabbit Hexes you and melees you, and his 2 does a lot of gun damage when up. [9:08]
- Venator: good. He was buffed and now his 2 melee does a ton of damage; his traps still feel oppressive even though you can shoot them. [9:08]
- Venator: if you stumble into him he takes half your HP bar; his grenade and heal are good into most of the roster. [9:08]
- Victor: not crazy, not the worst; more about scaling. He is hard to kill but does not do much. [9:38]
- Victor: play around his 1, space it and try to get damage while it is up, and he does not feel strong. [9:38]
- Vindicta: from a kit perspective she is good tier, but numerically right now she does not feel strong to lane against or in general. [9:38]
- Viscous: good tier. His Splatter does a lot of damage, and if you cannot parry his Puddle Punches he feels oppressive, but not to lane bully level. [9:38]
- Vyper: some of the most ridiculous numbers in the game, even stronger than McGinnis; insanely numerically overtuned even though nobody wants to play her. [10:09]
- Warden: good tier, can feel oppressive. His ult is very good for forcing towers. [10:09]
- Warden: his flask (Alchemical Flask) feels bad in lane if you do not manage your stamina; flask into cage (Binding Word) is a combo, but he is not that crazy. [10:09]
- Wraith: mid tier, possibly good, and undervalued in lane; with two cards she can one shot anyone. Bad without cards, good with them, so mid on average. [10:39]
- Yamato: lane bully. Her 1 does a lot of damage and her 3 gives a lot of survivability. [10:39]
- A Dynamo or Graves into an Apollo or Drifter lane should just try to survive. [10:39]
- An Ivy or Lash into an Abrams and Calico lane should slow play, cut waves and get boxes. [10:39]
