# Deadlock's Solo Lane is GONE - My initial thoughts on the new patch
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-02-26
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BwS2AZBoM8s

## Covers
Deathy's first reaction to the February 2025 map patch that cut Deadlock from four lanes to three: no more solo lane, new mid lane with mid boss moved underground, no trooper last hits, slower rotations. For any player adjusting to the three-lane map; the map has changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Most of this patch is art passes, visual effect changes and quality of life; the big change is three lanes instead of four. [0:00] [0:30]
- Side lane geometry is mostly the same, with some extra cover since every lane is now a duo lane; one side lane has a giant bridge overhead. [1:01]
- The middle is completely different: instead of two central lanes there is one clear mid lane with a big tower-like structure, and mid boss sits underneath, reached through small ziplines or a ramp. [1:01] [1:32]
- Solo lane is gone. Solo queuers liked it because they controlled 100% of their own variables and felt more confident carrying than when laning with a random partner. [1:32] [2:03]
- The main complaint about solo lane was how easily other lanes affected it: zipline tech made rotation between lanes about 5 seconds, so a losing duo lane next to you undid your solo lane. [2:34]
- Time between lanes is now much longer (jump pads help), so ganking in the laning stage is punished much more. [2:34] [3:05]
- Soul sharing: with two players in a lane early, souls are duplicated so both get 100%; with more than two they are split. Before, a duo laner ganking the solo lane lost nothing; now any gank is into a duo lane, so you grief the souls of everyone there, yourself included. [3:05] [3:37]
- Verdict on ganking: the risk-to-reward now makes much more sense; it might end up too punishing but he thinks it is right, because ganking very early should be either later or risky. [4:08]
- Four lanes meant constant wave management, and in unorganized play teams forgot waves, lost two walkers and got snowballed because walkers give so many souls; three lanes make that cheese much harder. [4:39] [5:12]
- With only three lanes there is much more space on the map, which he expects to change the meta in both organized and unorganized play, for example more jungle invades. [5:12]
- Zipline tech was not removed (press Ctrl to get off a zipline and keep momentum); ziplines are the most broken movement in the game, especially with Superior Stamina, so removing one zipline lowers everyone's mobility. [5:43]
- Slower rotation buffs heroes with inherent mobility (for example Haze, whose Smoke Bomb gives lots of sprint speed) and nerfs heroes without it (for example Lady Geist). [6:14]
- You will be slower to respond to fights, so you need more predictive plays; before you could reach any fight in about 5 seconds. [6:14] [6:44]
- You no longer need the last hit on a trooper to drop its soul orb; you still must secure it or it can be denied for 50% of its value. [6:44]
- He thinks the last-hit change came from people missing troopers constantly, made worse by bad trooper AI; it makes the game more accessible without costing skill expression, and he calls it a great change. [7:14] [7:44]
- Mid boss is now in an underground arena with big sliding stairs; the stairs give a direct sightline into the pit, so for example a Bebop can ult or hook into the mid boss pit from far above. He submitted that as feedback. [8:44] [9:15]
- Otherwise the new mid boss is a step in the right direction: it is harder to reach from everywhere and travel time is longer, so winning a side lane fight early usually does not let you take mid boss, or it takes a while. [9:15]
- Taking mid boss is more punishing because it is easy to play for exit kills: the pit has only a few small, narrow exits (the ziplines and both stairs), so the enemy can ambush at the chokes. [9:45] [10:16]
- After about 16 games his impression is very positive; his games have been around 35 minutes, not longer, though he is a higher rank. [10:16] [10:46]
- The spectated example game had 66 kills in 16 minutes, and he expects the change to make the game better overall. [11:16]
