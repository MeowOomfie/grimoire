# How to Play Pick | Deadlock Role Guide
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-04-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4VsD-4QfolA

## Covers
A role guide for pick heroes (Holliday, Paradox, Mo & Krill, Haze, Bebop): lane AoE usage, box routes into ganks and the three gank checks, mid game invades for sinner's sacrifices, hovering a side lane without showing, Urn play and late game team fight jobs.
For players on pick heroes.

## Claims
- Pick heroes are Holliday, Paradox, Mo & Krill, Haze and Bebop: heroes with a piece of utility that locks down an enemy so the team can pick them off, like Holliday's lasso dragging an enemy into her team. [0:01]
- Before 2 minutes, try to get wave priority and poke the enemies low without dying. [0:01]
- Most pick heroes have AoE abilities; ideally hit both the wave and the enemies. [0:01]
- When an enemy plays behind cover and the wave walks down a staircase, that is a perfect barrel or Paradox bomb; staircases where the wave groups and enemies use cover are your bread and butter. [0:32]
- After 2 minutes boxes spawn, so switch to prioritizing wave priority by hitting the minions. [0:32]
- Between 2 and 5 minutes the tower is strong and hard to damage, so with full wave priority shove the wave in and do a box route. [1:03]
- End your box route on top of the mid bridge, then check the minimap for a potential gank on the enemy side lane. [1:03]
- Gank check 1, HP states: if your teammates are 30% HP and the enemies full, you can't force a gank. [1:03]
- Even if your teammates are low, enemies pushed into their bridge trying to dive or siege them under tower is a great time to roam. [1:34]
- If your team has a small HP advantage but is shoved into the wave, it is hard to force a dive. [1:34]
- In lower level solo queue, don't do forced roam dives even if both enemies are 20% HP; it is a quick way to throw a winning lane. [1:34]
- Gank check 2, wave state: see if you can get behind the enemies for a surprise gank. [1:34]
- Gank check 3, ults: if your teammates don't have ults it is very hard to force a gank; ults are one of the best factors for forcing dives. [1:34]
- Prefer ganking into an ult that supports you, like Dynamo's, over one that doesn't help much, like Mirage's. [2:05]
- After the gank, leave the lane; staying eats their waves. Staying is fine only if you got a kill or two or forced them back and you actively hit the enemy tower; otherwise you tax your teammates and deny your team farm. [2:05]
- A successful gank doesn't need a kill; a good HP trade or meaningful damage that gives your lane partners an advantage counts. [2:35]
- Loosely aim for about two ganks a game before 5 or 6 minutes, depending on wave priority, enemy aggression and how your lanes are doing. [2:35]
- Mid game: steal enemy sinner's sacrifices; when one is up in their jungle, call a strong mid game hero over, ideally invading with three (two is achievable), and let them take it or catch someone farming it with your pick tool (Holliday's lasso, Paradox's swap) to confirm the kill. [3:05]
- If your picks rely on your ult (Holliday, Paradox, Mo & Krill), don't invade without the ult up; play safer, farm, or play reactively to support a teammate. [3:35]
- When hovering a side lane or your carry, never show on vision: being seen, being hit by troopers, or shooting the wave puts you on the enemy minimap. [4:05]
- Showing on a side lane as the pick hero relieves pressure on the other side of the map, letting them invade your jungle, play more aggressively and farm more securely. [4:05]
- Invading is especially useful when you see most enemies on the other side of the map: invade their weak side, where their carry usually farms; the carry is your ideal pick target to deny farm and take their bag. [4:35]
- If enemies see you hovering your carry, they won't walk at you unless they have a big number advantage, and you lose the surprise kill. [4:35]
- While hovering, farm a camp next to your carry or run boxes on the nearby roofs, waiting for enemies to engage your carry or step up too far, then jump down from height with your pick tool. [4:35]
- A pick like this gains pressure on that side so your carry can keep pushing; if you are on the Urn side, it is a great time to grab and run the Urn. [5:06]
- Use picks to run or play around the Urn; if there is an Urn fight, be in it, ideally giving your team an advantage before running it. [5:06]
- Late game in general: if you see many enemies on one side, go to the other side with someone strong to look for a pick and set up a mid boss or mid boss bait. [5:36]
- Late game team fights: grab the enemy carry or anyone squishy, drag them out of position into your team; or if your carry is being dove, use Holliday's lasso or Paradox's swap to peel the attacker away. [5:36]
