# The BEST Wraith Build In Deadlock - META Build Update
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-08-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cqWxajdEJY8

## Covers
A full hyper carry Wraith build for the early August 2025 patch (jungle buff era), item by item with the reasoning behind each pick and order.
For Wraith players of any rank who want a farm-first spirit and gun build, shielding items for survivability, and the 9.6k investment thresholds.

## Claims
- Since the latest patch Wraith is probably the hardest carry in the game again; the jungle got buffed. [0:00]
- The build was tested over about 3 days and is claimed strong in every rank. [0:30]
- Start lane with Monster Rounds: it helps you shove the creep wave, which is the number one thing to do in lane; shoot the creeps first, clear the wave, then harass the enemy. [0:30]
- Wraith was buffed early and does a bit more damage, but she is still kind of weak in lane. [0:30]
- Second item is Quicksilver Reload; by then you should have cards (Card Trick) at level two, and cards start doing a lot of damage. [1:01]
- Wraith does not auto lose lane anymore but is still one of the weaker laners; she needs a lot of money to get online, is weak in the early mid game, then becomes the hardest carry in the game. [1:01]
- Third item is Extra Charge: Wraith can win lanes again this patch, and an extra card helps secure lane kills (go in and throw three cards for burst) and helps farm later with an extra card for Quicksilver Reload. [1:32]
- Extra Charge is skippable; sell it later in the game. [2:03]
- Next is Tesla Bullets, ideally bought before Spirit Shielding, because it is the big spike that adds a lot of damage and lets you start farming jungle; before Tesla you can't do much. [2:03]
- Get Tesla Bullets as early in lane as possible: tier two camps can be farmed from 6 minutes, so Tesla at 6 minutes gets you ahead fast; around 8 minutes punch a few Sinner's Sacrifices then farm tier two and even tier three camps. [2:34]
- Tesla Bullets is Wraith's main farming item and her highest damage item; a must have. [2:34]
- Then Spirit Shielding: shielding items were nerfed, but Wraith's main problem is survivability, with very low base HP and HP scaling (about 1,700 HP at max level at 45k souls). [2:34]
- The build runs shielding items because barriers now work with resists. [3:04]
- Fortitude gives about 400 extra HP, but if you don't use its passive regen, Spirit Shielding gives the same HP in a fight for half the price once you reach a certain level, since it scales with boons. [3:36]
- Treat a shielding item as a Fortitude that gives you 400 HP when you need it; it is as valuable as base HP because your resists apply to it, and you won't regen the damage back until late game with leech anyway. [4:06]
- Reactive Barrier is an alternative to Spirit Shielding: more HP, lower cooldown and lasts longer, but no out of combat regen and no movement speed when hit. Spirit Shielding's movement speed helps escape sticky situations. [4:36]
- Run Reactive Barrier instead in lanes against heroes that almost always proc it, like Bebop, since it gives more HP overall. [4:36]
- Shieldings are in the main build and Reactive Barrier is optional because shieldings always proc: Spirit Shielding procs early against almost everyone and later you take enough gun damage for Weapon Shielding to proc. [5:06]
- Even against comps with three CC heroes (Bebop, Abrams, a Haze with Sleep Dagger, Lash) Reactive Barrier often did not proc, because enemies don't always use their stuns on you, but they always use gun and spirit damage. Consistency is key. [5:36]
- Later, with both shieldings you effectively get a free 800 extra HP for the same price as Fortitude's 400 (or 375). [6:07]
- Then Extended Magazine, later upgraded into Escalating Resilience. [6:07]
- This build skips Titanic Magazine; Mercurial Magnum comes fairly early and gives ammo and big reload. [6:07]
- Max cards first in this build. [6:07]
- This is mostly a spirit build: Wraith's gun scaling is okay but not what makes her strong; her damage comes from Tesla, cards and later Full Auto, and Mercurial Magnum adds spirit damage to bullets. [6:39]
- Then Rapid Rounds and Fleetfoot; Fleetfoot comes early because at about 11k you clear jungle well, and with the Fleetfoot slide tech you can slide constantly so ammo is not an issue. [7:11]
- Early damage comes from the early weapon investment plus Tesla. [7:44]
- At about 11k you are still very weak in team fights; sit on a side lane and protect the tower by shooting pushers from afar, as Tesla and cards do full damage, but don't fully commit. [7:44]
- This is a greedy build: until Swift Striker, or even Leech, don't fight much, farm; the items keep you alive if you get caught or must join a fight. [8:15]
- Then Mercurial Magnum before Surge of Power: testing showed Mercurial Magnum adds more damage and is only 1,600 more; with Quicksilver already owned, spend 4,800 on Mercurial rather than 3.2k on Surge. Surge first is also fine; they come one after another. [8:15]
- After Mercurial Magnum creep camps die very fast, tier three camps faster than any hero, and farm speed is about on par with Seven. [8:45]
- Wraith's win rate rose this patch mainly because the jungle buff suits her as the best or second best jungle clearer, not because of Wraith changes. She can clear a whole side of the jungle quickly. [9:15]
- Wraith is still strong at split pushing: walkers no longer have spirit resist, so with this much spirit damage you can walk up to an unwatched walker and kill it fast. [9:15]
- Surge of Power is the second major power spike. [9:45]
- Fleetfoot and Surge of Power both remove the move penalty while shooting, but Surge lasts 8 seconds with about 6 seconds of downtime, and Fleetfoot also gives slide distance, bullet resist, movement speed and slow resist. [9:45]
- Fleetfoot is still the best item in the game for Wraith; its slide distance means less ammo worry in fights and farming, another reason to skip Titanic Magazine and go Escalating Resilience. [10:16]
- After Surge of Power your damage is set: you should kill anyone with the same or more money, but you still die easily if two gank you or you get shot first, so start buying resists. [10:47]
- Then Enchanter's Emblem, which also adds a little spirit damage. [11:17]
- Then Weapon Shielding, or Reactive Barrier here if you prefer. [11:17]
- At about 22k your effective HP is not 1,500 but more like 2,500 plus, with about 400 from each shielding and movement speed when they proc. [11:17]
- Then Escalating Resilience, an upgrade to Extended Magazine giving another 30% bullet resist; it is an insane item for Wraith. [11:48]
- Bullet resist works on melee, so Escalating Resilience is almost always worth it against any bullet or melee damage; it gives HP, weapon damage and resists. [12:19]
- Once procced you sit at about 34% bullet resist and 15% spirit resist. [12:19]
- Then Swift Striker as an upgrade, and sell Monster Rounds around now since you no longer need it; Swift Striker also gives great stat distribution. [12:19]
- Most builds ignore item order and over or underinvest; at this point you can also sell Extra Charge, so the build is not slot heavy. [12:49]
- Target 9.6k invested in both weapon and spirit: here weapon is at 58% bonus for 9.6k and spirit at 44 extra spirit for 9.6k. For most heroes that do both gun and spirit damage these stats are worth having. [13:19]
- Item order is built to cross a new investment threshold almost every purchase: Fleetfoot takes weapon from about 29 to 40, Mercurial Magnum spirit from 15 to 32, Surge of Power 32 to 44, Enchanter's Emblem and Weapon Shielding add vitality tiers, Escalating Resilience hits 58%. [14:21]
- Past 9.6k extra investment matters much less; the jump to 72% weapon is expensive, so don't worry about buying more gun items. [14:52]
- Until this point buy nothing else in utility, since these items give tankiness, health and damage. [15:22]
- After the mid game build, against heavy spirit burst buy Spirit Resilience, though many games don't need it. [15:22]
- Against heroes like Bebop or Infernus buy Debuff Remover instead; to counter something like Lash, buy Counterspell. [15:22]
- Toxic Bullets can be bought any time anti-heal is needed, but it is rarely bought: anti-heal is not that strong this meta, which is about killing fast while staying alive, not long fights. Buy at most one of these. [15:53]
- From here you can start fighting sustainably, especially with Leech, which also brings vitality investment to 9.6k. [16:23]
- Leech is insane in this build since you heal from gun, Tesla and cards, especially on multiple enemies and when Full Auto is procced. [16:23]
- If enemies die fast enough that you don't need lifesteal, go straight into Boundless Spirit; usually Leech first. [16:54]
- Boundless Spirit is another big spike (about 177 spirit): cards hit like a truck and full auto does huge damage. [16:54]
- Around 40k souls Wraith can start 1v6ing; items after Boundless Spirit are utility and tanking, and Boundless is even skippable since the build does insane damage without it. [17:24]
- Capacitor was buffed and is slot efficient as an upgrade from Tesla Bullets. [17:54]
- Capacitor removes buffs from the enemy and slows them by 100%, so you can catch up. Removable buffs include Return Fire, Seven's Power Surge, Warden's overshield and the buffs Bebop gets from his combo. [17:54]
- Capacitor is often the third or fourth highest damage source in the build. [18:55]
- Late game, pick Spellbreaker, Siphon Bullets, Diviner's Kevlar or Unstoppable as needed; Leech is the only late item always bought. [18:55]
- Spellbreaker is the best counter to spirit burst: it greatly reduces the first instance of damage (a Lash slam, Grey Talon, a Yamato slash for about 50 damage) and gives 25% debuff resist. [19:25]
- Siphon Bullets is better the longer fights last; this meta has short, bursty fights, so it is bought less than Spellbreaker. [19:56]
- Diviner's Kevlar is underrated, and since every other vitality item was nerfed it keeps getting relatively better: about another 1,000 HP barrier and 25 spirit power on ult, lasting 15 seconds. [19:56]
- Example: skipping Boundless Spirit and Capacitor at about 36k, you have about 2.5k HP plus 450 each from the shieldings for about 3.5k, and Diviner's Kevlar adds 1k on ult for about 4,500 HP; resists apply to that barrier too. [20:27]
- Against stuns used on you, like Mo & Krill or a black hole, buy Unstoppable; against Lash's ult buy Counterspell instead, which does the same for half the price. [21:28]
- The shielding idea came from a viewer suggesting Fortitude; shieldings give the HP of Fortitude for half the price. [21:58]
