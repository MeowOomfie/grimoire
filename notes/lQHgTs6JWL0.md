## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream from 8/5/26, early in the new ranked season with the reworked rank names. He coaches four players: a Wraith in a high-rank solo queue lobby, a Vindicta who climbed to Phantom and now meets former Eternus players, an Archon Rem playing support and an Oracle Dynamo.
Themes: Wraith card stacking and farm, respecting a deficit, the support pivot, pushing waves before Rift, Rebuttal, Curse timing, active slots, Rem and Dynamo i-frame tech, Knockdown and debuff removal into CC comps, routing to height, plus his own air strafing practice.
