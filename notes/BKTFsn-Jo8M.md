## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (May 2026, games on the May 4th patch). Opens with chat questions on Indomitable versus Unstoppable, Street Brawl and Divine Barrier, then coaches four players: a gun Warden (Archon), a spirit Bebop (Oracle), an Apollo (Emissary) and a carbine Paradox (Phantom) on an Average Jonas build.
In between he tears apart a TikTok Rem build (Healing Booster, Rescue Beam, Superior Duration, skill order) and answers questions on countering gun Victor, Stalker on Mo & Krill and Victor, Mo & Krill core items, and who takes the boxes.
Recurring themes: urn is the most overpowered mechanic, invading is OP, play for spikes, never skip green spike, side lane after 8 minutes, disengage from bad fights, don't take at-best fights.
