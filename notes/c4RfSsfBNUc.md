## Covers
First-impressions breakdown of Graves, the area control necromancer released 2026-01-29 in the Old Gods New Blood update.
Auto aim gun, every ability with upgrades, the tombstone ultimate and how to kill it, Split Shot / Ricochet / Tesla synergies, skill order and a tanky gun build. For anyone picking up or facing Graves on release.

## Claims
- Graves is an area denial hero whose abilities stop the enemy from pushing you; think of her as a stronger McGinnis. [0:33]
- She is the first hero with auto aim, like Symmetra in Overwatch: you do not need to point your crosshair at the enemy, so she suits players who cannot aim. [0:33]
- Her gun has an insanely fast fire rate on top of auto aim. [0:33]
- Jar of the Dead (1): a charge system; anything that dies near you (minions, heroes, even your own minions or teammates) gives you jars, with four charges at base. [1:04]
- Throwing a jar summons deadheads, little ghosts that deal spirit damage to nearby enemies, follow you when no enemy is in sight, and prioritize what you are targeting. [1:04]
- You can preload deadheads before a fight; they attack as soon as you start shooting, stay aggroed once aggroed, and one charge lasts a very long time. [1:34]
- Jar of the Dead level 1: 5 health per pickup when collecting the dead; with eight creeps dying every wave this is near infinite lane sustain, probably the first ability to level. [2:06]
- Jar of the Dead level 2 adds a 30% slow for one second, applied by the auto aiming deadheads. [2:06]
- Jar of the Dead max level summons two more deadheads and lets deadheads trigger Essence Theft. [2:06]
- Essence Theft (3, passive): your weapon steals enemy weapon damage and spirit resist; at most 26% weapon damage stolen, taking full effect after 4 seconds, plus 10% spirit resist, from up to three players. [2:38]
- Essence Theft level 1 steals more spirit resist (useful later), level 2 steals more weapon damage, and max level lets your gun hit multiple targets. [3:09]
- The multi-target hit is very forgiving: enemies only need to be close together, and it also steals from each target; at level 2 hitting two enemies gave about 60% extra weapon damage and 20% extra spirit armor. [3:39]
- With Jar of the Dead maxed, deadheads apply Essence Theft even while you are not attacking, so you can kite and run away while still gaining the buffs. [4:10]
- Grasping Hands (2): places a line on the ground for 7 seconds; any enemy crossing it is rooted for 1.5 seconds. [4:10]
- Grasping Hands upgrades: 2 seconds more duration, longer immobilize, and at max level a longer wall and lower cooldown. [4:42]
- Grasping Hands is great area denial: put it in front of enemies pushing your tower or attacking you; even a jump dash seemed unable to cross it. [5:12]
- Level Grasping Hands later; at base it already does its job of scaring enemies off, and 1 and 3 are much better early points. [5:12]
- Ultimate (caption "Burrow Decree"): like Undying's ultimate in Dota, it summons a tombstone that sends ghouls down the lane toward the enemy Patron, acting like extra creeps. [5:42]
- The tombstone spawns a ghoul every 5 seconds; ghouls do melee damage and on hitting an objective or enemy hero they deal area spirit damage and an 80% movement slow. [5:42]
- Ultimate upgrades: lower cooldown, more ghoul movement speed, and at max level the explosion deals up to 5% health damage. [6:13]
- The tombstone stays active for about 30 seconds; ghouls run across the map like minions and explode on objectives. [6:44]
- If creeps are defending, ghouls do not explode, so managed ghouls are not deadly; once the creeps are gone they do massive damage to towers, objectives and heroes. [6:44]
- The ultimate is insane lane pressure on any lane; cooldown is 110 seconds at base and 90 seconds with the upgrade. [7:15]
- The only way to destroy the tombstone is two heavy melees; Graves defending it can parry those, and enemies must also deal with ghouls attacking them. [7:15]
- Her fire rate is about the same as Haze's, so Tesla Bullets procs very consistently. [7:45]
- Split Shot is the best item for her: because her gun is auto aim, every pellet hits everyone in front of you; buy it every game even if you play spirit. [8:16]
- During the Split Shot demo she gained 200 weapon damage from Essence Theft while also stealing everyone's spirit resist. [8:51]
- Ricochet also works; with auto aim every bounce connects, so Ricochet plus Split Shot plus Tesla Bullets chains across all targets. [8:51]
- Toxic Bullets also works very well on her. [9:23]
- Jar of the Dead has weak spirit scaling (x0.3 damage) and also procs Essence Theft. [9:59]
- Grasping Hands scales well but is situational area denial, so do not build spirit for it. [10:29]
- Essence Theft's spirit scaling only raises the weapon damage steal. [10:29]
- The ultimate has very good spirit scaling, but what makes it strong is lane pressure, not spirit damage. [10:29]
- Get some spirit items for synergy, but buy Split Shot every game for hitting multiple targets; auto aim plus becoming very resistant is her strength. [10:59]
- Best items: Split Shot and Leech, since Jar of the Dead gives constant spirit damage and the gun constant weapon damage, and you want to stay alive. [11:29]
- Valve's default build runs Extra Charge, Rapid Recharge, Tesla Bullets, Capacitor and Ricochet; leaving out Split Shot is a mistake. [11:29]
- He does not see raw spirit items like Boundless Spirit making her strong; he will play a gun build. [12:00]
- Skill order: one point in Jar of the Dead early for the healing, avoid points in Grasping Hands (like Warden's cage, good to scare but rarely procs), then max Essence Theft early. [12:31]
- Do not max Jar of the Dead first; it procs Essence Theft, so Essence Theft needs a high level first to make Jar worth it. [13:02]
- Plan: one point in Jar, max Essence Theft early, tanky gun build with a lot of lifesteal, harass enemies out of lane. [13:02]
- Verdict: the craziest hero they have released; he expects nerfs very quickly. [13:02]
