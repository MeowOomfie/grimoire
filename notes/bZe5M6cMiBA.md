# Infernus is actually a problem
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-04-10

## Covers
Infernus played and commented by aquatiq, with item reasoning around Duration Extender, Toxic Bullets and counter items.
Commentary over a game, no coaching. Patch around 2026-04-10.

## Claims
- He wants Duration Extender very early on Infernus. [1:34]
- Duration Extender is good on Infernus because Afterburn lasts a long time, and it also extends Flame Dash's napalm effect and, he thinks, the stun; it helps his whole kit. [13:25]
- Building up Afterburn from far away is almost impossible; you need to be close. [1:34]
- Get Toxic Bullets as early as possible in this game. [6:38]
- Extra Spirit early for damage, and Spirit Lifesteal is a nice pickup. [1:02]
- The camp close to the yellow lane is very strong and the other side has no camp that close, so there is an advantage to being on yellow. [7:41]
- Midgame purchases he named: Enchanter's Emblem (as captioned "Enchantress"), Titanic Magazine, then Boundless Spirit. [10:51]
- Skip Warp Stone unless you are on a Flame Dash build. [12:54]
- An enemy McGinnis bought his hard counter item, so he bought Dispel Magic. [13:25]
- He calls Dispel Magic broken. [23:16]
- Counterspell does not save an enemy from Toxic Bullets plus Afterburn. [20:40]
- About 29% spirit resist was enough for him; he still considered more Spirit Resilience. [18:39]
- Before mid boss, check whether anyone on the team has Unstoppable; without it a fight into strong enemy ults (Viper here) can go badly. [17:03]
- He bought Leech and then Unstoppable as late items. [20:09]
- Pop Unstoppable as soon as the enemy ult drops, because half the team will not react in time. [23:47]
- Infernus has the movement to carry the Urn. [14:57]
- Collapse together on a lone enemy in a lane; if the team does not follow, he escapes. [17:34]
- He admits he barely touched objectives this game despite high player damage. [25:20]
