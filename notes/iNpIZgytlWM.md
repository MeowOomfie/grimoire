# I Played Deadlock's Biggest Update - Here's What Actually Changed
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-01-23
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iNpIZgytlWM

## Covers
First look at the 2026-01-23 update: six new heroes on a voting release schedule, the new Street Brawl mode, the base and map rework, UI and post-game changes, and two new gun items (Ballistic Enhancement, Recharging Rush).
No balance changes in this patch. Much of the video is reactions to hero models and Street Brawl gameplay; the useful parts are the mode rules and item descriptions. For players catching up on what is new.

## Claims
- Six new heroes release on a voting schedule: after every game you get a vote to cast in the hideout; the first releases Monday at 2:00 p.m. PST, then every Monday and Thursday, so all six are out in about 3 weeks. [0:32]
- The coach's pick for the vote is Silver, a cat girl and werewolf with a shotgun. [0:32]
- New game mode Street Brawl: 4 versus 4 in one lane, up to five rounds; the goal is to kill the enemy guardian before they kill yours, and after two guardians the final round is to kill the enemy Walker. [6:10]
- Street Brawl has no souls: before each round you choose from three item offers, three items per round, with one reroll per round; some offers are rare, higher tier items. [6:40]
- Street Brawl sometimes offers ultra unique items with effects not found elsewhere, such as phasing out of reality like E-shift and shooting lasers for several seconds. [7:11]
- Street Brawl is easy for new players who do not want to learn the full MOBA side, and good for warm-ups; build crafting still has depth because the offers push you into builds you would not normally try (example: Vortex Web Wraith pulling the enemy team together then throwing cards for big AoE damage). [7:41]
- Street Brawl is separate from ranked, launched from the hideout, has much shorter matches and does not affect your rank; it is the first unranked mode. [8:11]
- Street Brawl has little snowballing because everyone gets stronger by the same amount each round, and a team that falls behind seems to get rare items more often, so 3-0 stomps are rare. [8:41]
- The bases and much of the map are reworked visually: buildings fit the scenery, jungle creeps have new attack animations, and there are many new vents. [14:39]
- New troopers for each side: the Ember Heart troopers have candles and the Sapphire Flame troopers have ice crystals; learn the new creep colors. [15:09]
- The post-game screen is revamped with an MVP, two key players per team, and cleaner stats, items, damage and graphs. [15:09]
- UI changes: hero icons change when allies are low or have their ultimate ready or active, and every hero has custom text when they ult or use a key ability, with a countdown. [15:40]
- Settings are reworked with proper crosshair options, so you no longer need a crosshair overlay. [16:11]
- New gun item Ballistic Enhancement: imbue it on an ability, and every enemy hit by that ability gives you 18% weapon damage, stacking per enemy (up to six). [16:41]
- Ballistic Enhancement also increases the imbued ability's range; strong uses are Abrams's aura hitting several enemies for 60 to 80% more gun damage, Wraith's cards hitting every enemy, and Seven's ball. Expect many new builds around it. [17:11]
- New gun item Recharging Rush: 1,600 souls, gives ammo and weapon damage, and after dealing a certain amount of weapon damage to an enemy you get back one charge on all your charged abilities. [17:41]
- There are no balance changes in this patch; the coach expects the meta to shift drastically as the new heroes release anyway, with balance updates to follow once several are out. [18:12]
- In Street Brawl on Wraith the coach rerolled away from a melee lifesteal offer, skipped Trophy Collector, and judged Spirit Resilience and Metal Skin both valid picks, taking Spirit Resilience. [21:38]
- The game feels smoother and runs at higher frame rates after the update for the coach. [26:44]
