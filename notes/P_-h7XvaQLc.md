## Covers
Pocket coaching by Midknight: he reviews one game of an Oracle-rank Pocket student on a defensive build, into a Paradox, Infernus and Billy side.
Themes: securing souls and confirming orbs, itemization (Healing Rite, Extra Regen, Tankbuster vs Spirit Burn, Leap timing, slot pressure), Barrage as a damage amp before combos, Satchel and Flying Cloak usage, soul awareness and buy timings, invading on a spike, rift and walker macro.
Patch date: September 2026.

## Claims
Player 1 (Pocket, Oracle):
- Fighting a Paradox under her tower is bad risk-reward: miss and you lose about 550 HP, hit and she parries under tower, you do about 150 damage and take more from her tower than you deal. [0:32]
- When you are in a bad spot in lane, start Healing Rite. [0:32]
- Healing Rite is forgotten but still buyable; base regen items have outclassed it. If you buy it, angle to farm the doubles and centers and be a spirit hero who buys spirit items to scale it, which gives a lot of its souls and value back. [1:03]
- Too low to trade in a camp: toss a Cloak in to one-shot it and steal the farm. [1:33]
- You are giving a lot of denies by not shooting the orbs; shoot the orbs. [1:33]
- If you can avoid buying Healing Rite, avoid it; buy it when you are very low (about 200 HP) and can't contest the tier one, since ducking for 20 seconds forfeits nothing, especially when no boxes are up. [2:05]
- Splitting a wave's souls with your lane partner is more even, but in solo queue it is better to play selfishly. [3:05]
- With the 4:30 wave you need to be there to shove it, so do only a small box run; with no boxes left, look to steal their charge, and if it is not there, back up, charge the wave, then go for the buff. [3:35]
- You knew Infernus had dash, so you should have stayed at half height to deny the extra afterburn damage. [4:06]
- Check the bridge from the other side; it is dangerous if mid lane has set up preemptively. [4:06]
- New-gen Pocket maxes Barrage first and puts early AP into it; that build has more carry potential in solo queue (1v1 power, side lane pressure, farm), while the old bot build is significantly easier to pilot. Not confident on the hero: play the old build. [4:39]
- Track the enemy Paradox's Carbine: after you full burst and she has not used it, insta recast the Cloak, because she is very likely about to Carbine you in the back. Always use the information you have about what the enemy laner is doing. [5:41]
- When baiting the Carbine, commit your Cloak and then your Satchel in case both enemies run at you; Satchel with Carbine and Infernus on you is very dangerous. [6:12]
- Your Warden burned his Cage and you burned everything, so smarter enemies could trade you hard; be careful how you trade. [7:14]
- Cloak a little to the side of the lane so it stays an escape option, instead of cloaking straight at their tower. [7:44]
- Since you got your value out of Extra Regen and never needed Healing Rite, Extra Regen was the correct buy in the long run. [8:14]
- Biggest micro issue: Barrage usage. You use one Barrage and use it after your cooldowns instead of before; Barrage first could have set up an Affliction on both enemies. [8:14]
- TP out earlier when the Infernus is shooting you with afterburn on you, rather than greeding a farther Cloak. [9:18]
- His Calico trick: expect the Pocket to greed damage in Satchel and pre-fire a bomb, slowing hex or silence so it lands as the Satchel ends. If you greed in Satchel, know what the enemies have up. [9:50]
- Urn is not worth much on a slow runner, but pick it off rather than give it up. [10:20]
- When you are winning and the enemy holds the Urn, they get comeback resists (about 30% of each resist) in fights; melee the Urn off them. [10:51]
- When the enemy is down 10% on rift and their mid wave is crashing, splitting the mid walker is a lot more valuable than fighting the rift. [11:52]
- Kite back to your walker when chased. [12:22]
- Fighting 800 souls short of your gun spike the whole time feels really bad; know your next spike. [12:22]
- Barrage the rift minions: you get the individual amp on them and kill them while stunned, which makes rift minions far less consequential. [12:54]
- He likes the ability range item (expansion) plus Cultist on Pocket; double ability range is good. [13:55]
- Do not max Satchel second; that AP should go into your ult. Max the ult second. [13:55]
- Ability range is worth more than damage because hitting one extra person with your ult beats extra damage on the people already hit. [14:27]
- On bot Pocket, Barrage should be about your second highest damage source after the ult, and at least top three. [14:57]
