# This is how it feels to carry as Mirage
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-06-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1FkrUUcJ3s8

## Covers
Mirage, aquatiq, commentary over a won game where he gets MVP.
Covers his skill order, Suppressor in lane, Tornado use and items, plus team fight and Urn decisions.
Patch date: early June 2026.

## Claims
- He does not love starting with his 1 (Tornado) but follows the build's order. [0:00]
- Suppressor is a good item to buy in lane and comfy on Mirage; the lane was much easier with it. [1:01]
- Suppressor matters most against gun heroes; it does little against Kelvin, whose damage is from abilities. [3:34]
- Skill order he follows: two points into 1 (Tornado), then two points into 2 (Fire Scarabs). [1:32]
- Then two points into 3 (Djinn's Mark) to do a lot of damage. [7:11]
- Order recap: two points, two points, three points in Scarabs, two points in Djinn's Mark. [11:19]
- Do not miss your Scarabs in lane. [5:07]
- Keep hitting a low enemy so he does not come back to the lane. [1:32]
- He uses anti-heal when fighting at the enemy tower. [6:40]
- He does not go into a fight with 600 HP while Seven is ulting. [9:46]
- He wants the Kinetic item (as captioned) next; the items in this build are described as insane on Mirage. [9:46]
- He saves the teleport (ult) when the fight is close enough to walk to. [10:49]
- Put Duration Extender on your 1 (Tornado). [12:21]
- He considers Toxic Bullets in that game. [12:54]
- Max the 3 first, then put a point into the ult, because being able to teleport everywhere is more beneficial. [17:02]
- At that point he takes Superior Duration and Superior Cooldown but does not sell Suppressor, which he still rates very highly. [17:34]
- When the enemy team is all spirit damage, buy Counterspell or Spellbreaker. [19:06]
- You cannot ignore the Urn fight, but while you fight it the enemy can take walkers for free. [16:32]
- Do not feed off the Urn: if you die there they take blue for free and eat the walker. [22:12]
- Against a dash build Shiv, avoid fighting in choke points; do not walk in the room with him. [22:43]
- Do not go help a teammate who is dying to four enemies; if he died, there is more than one enemy there, and two deaths are worse than one. [23:44]
- When the enemy team is split, stay together; if they go mid boss as a group you can win it. [25:21]
- Tornado has a recast: hit it twice, then press E after. [26:54]
- You can tornado enemies against a wall. [30:34]
- With a large bank he put Mystic Reverb on Tornado, and he heals a lot off tornadoes. [32:06]
- You do not need to chase fleeing enemies; go to the Urn drop-off spot instead. [32:37]
- With cooldown items he still has a 12 second tornado. [32:37]
- Late game plan after a won fight: farm up, get Urn, take mid boss. [34:12]
