# Educational Eternus Seven Gameplay Commentary #3
channel: Natan  date: 2025-02-05  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qevTcyl8hkA

## Covers
Seven, losing a solo lane vs Mirage, Djinn's Mark, wave staggering and freezing, recovery farming to 8K, Surge of Power, stun then Slowing Hex timing vs Mirage tornado, side laning when ahead, Mid Boss after picks, pushing two lanes, Ethereal Shift, Yamato, Wraith

## Claims
- He is bad at solo lanes and really only plays duo lanes, which he thinks are better for Seven. [0:01]
- Yamato and Mirage are both bad solo lane matchups for Seven; he swapped to take the bad one so his Abrams got the matchup he wanted. [0:31]
- In a bad lane his goal is to let the Mirage push and secure as many last hits as he can. [1:02]
- He keeps the wave on his side and avoids as much poke as possible; against Mirage watch the Djinn's Mark multiplier and stay behind cover after each hit to let it expire. [1:33]
- He was 200 to 300 souls behind from missed last hits, but at least he was not dying. [2:04]
- Any time the Mirage tries to engage he pops the stun to make him back off, and when the Mirage has a trade window he dashes out as fast as he can. [2:04]
- He does not instantly kill the minions, so his wave staggers and stays on his side. [2:36]
- Pushing the wave to go for a box run was bad: the wave sits on the enemy side, the enemy can see him leave, and a smart laner just freezes it there, which the Mirage did. [3:07]
- Pay attention to Djinn's Mark stacks and do not dash into walls; taking another hit at four stacks cost him all his HP. [3:38]
- Buying Cold Front then was greedy, but his zipline boost came up at 4:50 so he could go back to base quickly. [4:09]
- A gank with little damage on a full HP lane was never going to work; he should have pinged the Lash back. [4:09]
- His ult was bad; he should have gone over the bridge first to cut off the Mirage's path. [4:41]
- His Lash should have been matching the enemy Wraith in lane instead of taking a jungle camp for about 200 souls. [5:11]
- In hindsight he could have gone back to the shared lane, since before 8 minutes the troopers are still shared. [5:42]
- After losing his Guardian he goes into full recovery mode and farms to 8K souls, the tier 3 Power Surge spike, when he can start to fight again. [5:42]
- He does not agree with his own Healing Rite purchase there; it is a good item but was not needed anymore. [6:13]
- He used his boost to defend a lane when he could have used the teleporter, wasting the boost. [6:45]
- With the 8K spike he can fight the Mirage; he slides to extend his magazine and uses the new instant bolt from the ult buff for the kill. [7:18]
- Once ahead he could 1v1 the Mirage, but with 3K souls in his inventory he goes back to spend first. [7:18]
- With Surge of Power he is insanely strong and does not have to fear the Mirage. [7:49]
- Against Mirage, cast the stun and then use Slowing Hex half a second to a second later, so he cannot use his tornado to dodge the stun. [8:21]
- When he does not see anyone on the map he backs off a Guardian after getting some damage on it. [8:21]
- Check enemy items; the Mirage had Withering Whip. [8:57]
- When his Haze arrived he could push the Guardian, using Cold Front to push the wave quickly, then dip again. [9:30]
- As the strongest player in the lobby he side lanes to force 1v1s, or force the enemy to send several people to him. [10:01]
- After picking up a bag and farming a tier 3 camp he does not push, because dying could lose or give the enemy 2K souls or more; he goes back and spends instead. [11:03]
- Far ahead after picks, take Mid Boss; that is how games end quickly in Eternus. [11:34]
- After Mid Boss his goal is to keep waves pushed and look for picks, because picks open up Walkers. [12:07]
- He waits rather than walking up alone against two, and does not solo push a Walker while he cannot see everyone on the map. [12:37]
- After the outer Walkers they push both lanes at the same time for the inner Walkers to overwhelm the enemy. [13:19]
- Spend your souls; he sold Healing Rite for Ethereal Shift and bought Spirit Lifesteal. [13:54]
- When a Walker elsewhere is already dead, going back to kill the Wraith there does nothing and is probably what she wants; keep pushing while you have Rejuvenator. [14:26]
- Slowing Hex stops an enemy from using her ult, and by now he has a two second stun. [14:58]
- When pulling back, grab the buffs and take the enemy jungle to starve them out. [15:29]
- He used Slowing Hex too early, which let the Mirage tornado out of the stun. [15:29]
- With three enemies dead and two waves close to their base, call to take the Patron. [15:59]
- With his tier 3 ult he ults over the pit and calls the end. [16:29]
- You do not have to be the main character every game; just be consistent and do your part. He died once. [16:59]

## Disagrees with
- Here he lets the Mirage push and keeps the wave on his own side in a hard lane; in scgjparadeA, against Mirage and Lady Geist, he recommends hard pushing to neutralise their push and poke plan.
- Puts the tier 3 Power Surge spike at 8K souls; luLGER0Tbqg (a later patch) puts it at 8.4K.
