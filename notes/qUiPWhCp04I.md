## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (no pro analysis this time), coaching four players: a Mina (Koala) laning with Billy into Venator and Celeste, an Eternus 6 Ivy, an Ascendant Shiv and an Oracle Victor.
Themes: playing for urns and trading walkers for urns, efficiency and dead time, not dying for teammates, melee hygiene and parry timing, soul awareness, box routes on the 3-minute wave, Ivy flight and Decay builds, Shiv right click movement, Victor positioning and side lane sharing, isolating 1v1s with cover.
Patch context: the June 2026 patch, which he calls the worst Deadlock has had, an AoE "burger box" meta dominated by tanky M1s (jungle carries).
