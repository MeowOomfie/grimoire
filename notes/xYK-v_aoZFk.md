# The New Item Meta in Deadlock Explained
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-12-19
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xYK-v_aoZFk

## Covers
The December holiday balance patch and its hotfix: which items got stronger or weaker, the case for an early 4.8k vitality investment built on Extra Health, stamina items, movement items, Toxic Bullets, the Patron stall rule and Cheat Death, and how carries, early fighters and supports should play the new map.
For players who want to climb on the patch that runs until the January update.

## Claims
- Extra Health is the strongest early 800 soul item right now even though it was not buffed; the investment system now gives flat health instead of a percentage. [1:00]
- All the orange and purple health items in the early and mid game were nerfed by 25, so there is less health around unless you invest in vitality. [1:00]
- Reaching 4.8k vitality investment early gives 525 flat health at base. [1:30]
- The early spirit power spike (rushing 4.8k spirit with Surge of Power on Mirage or Rapid Recharge on Grey Talon or Dynamo) was nerfed both in the items and in the spirit investment tree, so there is less reason to rush 4.8k spirit. [2:01]
- The healing trooper heals you on kill, and a big part of that heal is a percentage of your max health; at about 100 health solo you gain roughly 300 health, and 50% of that with a lane partner. [2:32]
- With Extra Health the same healing trooper kill gave about 100 more health solo, roughly 25 to 50 more with two in lane, every wave. [3:03]
- Extra Health works two ways: flat health for trades and a bigger max pool that makes trooper healing stronger, so it acts like a budget Extra Regen or restorative item for lane sustain. [3:35]
- Warden at 800 souls with Extra Health has about 1,000 to 1,100 health; Wraith or Haze go from about 700 base to almost 1,000 at level one, about 30% more, and it can be bought after the first wave. [4:05]
- Pair Extra Health with Healing Rite right after for very high lane sustain; when low, hide for a wave and let Healing Rite work. [4:35]
- After those two, buy Battle Vest, Enchanter's Emblem or another 1.6k item to reach the 4.8k investment. [5:05]
- On heroes that shoot a lot buy Battle Vest, since it gives resistance plus weapon damage and fire rate; on spirit heroes like Pocket go Enchanter's Emblem for spirit damage. [5:05]
- Then pick by hero: Enduring Speed for movement, an early Return Fire on a tanky hero against heavy gun damage, Guardian Ward on supports, Healbane on Seven for very early anti-heal; none of these are bad. [5:35]
- Warden at the 4.8k vitality investment sits at an effective 2,000 health, far higher than before. [6:06]
- At 4.8k vitality investment every hero in the game has more health than before, and it is stronger the lower the hero's base health, for example Haze and Mina. [6:06]
- Main takeaway: early 4.8k vitality investment is very strong as long as you add at least one vest. [7:09]
- Bullet and spirit resist are percentage based, so with more base health on everyone Enchanter's Emblem and Battle Vest got stronger without being touched in the patch notes. [7:09]
- Spirit Shielding and Weapon Shielding got a bit weaker because they give health but no resist on top. [7:39]
- With the vitality build you sit around 2,000 to 2,500 effective health on almost every hero depending on the damage taken. [7:39]
- The vitality build does slow your damage and farm compared to something like Tesla Bullets, but double the health beats 40 to 50% more damage in most trades. [8:09]
- Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem give active bonuses like weapon damage and fire rate that other early green items don't, so you can still trade and farm camps and Sinner's Sacrifice while sustaining. [8:40]
- Jungle camps have lower base health up to 15 minutes; from 6 minutes (first tier two camps) to 15 minutes, farming jungle is worth it on heroes that can. [9:12]
- Sinner's Sacrifice and lane creeps got nerfed, so the jungle is relatively more worth it. [9:12]
- Don't AFK farm on every hero; early fighters and roamers like Mo & Krill and Abrams looking for early kills are still viable, maybe better. [9:43]
- Death ball is weaker because kills give more souls in 1v1 and 2v1 situations, so invading the jungle with Mo & Krill plus one to kill the enemy carry is far more viable. [9:43]
- Smaller skirmishes are a good way to get ahead because the enemy probably won't stack as four or five. [10:13]
- Hotfix: wall jumps no longer cost stamina. [10:44]
- Hotfix: stamina regeneration is 25% faster, so items that use stamina got stronger: Kinetic Dash, Arcane Surge and Stamina Mastery. [10:44]
- Kinetic Dash and Arcane Surge reward jump dashes and you can use them more often with more stamina regen; Stamina Mastery adds distance to dashes, jumps and air dashes. [11:15]
- These three stamina items are now in many cases stronger than the base movement speed items Enduring Speed and Fleetfoot; expect more builds to include them. [11:45]
- Movement items are not dead, be selective: either ignore them and use Stamina Mastery, or double dip and buy both Fleetfoot and Enduring Speed. [12:15]
- With both Enduring Speed and Fleetfoot you are fast enough to close the gap on or escape from anyone, more so than before, because many players skip movement speed. [12:15]
- If you see the enemy not buying movement speed, buy it and you get in and out of situations for free. [12:45]
- Enduring Speed and Fleetfoot are arguably a bit better, because higher investment movement items like Burst Fire and Headshot Booster will be bought less. [12:45]
- Burst Fire is a really bad item now: with Enduring Speed and Burst Fire active you are not fast enough to start a slide from base movement, so the perma slide and extra slide distance rarely apply. [13:16]
- Burst Fire's slide only works if you dash in, and often you don't want to or have no stamina to dash mid fight. [13:46]
- Heroic Aura and other stacking movement speed items have diminishing stacking, so stacking them for perma movement speed is not worth it; take Enduring Speed, Fleetfoot or both instead. [14:17]
- The orange 3k items got over nerfed because their value was movement speed or extra health; Headhunter got double nerfed (less health and less movement speed), and Hollow Point is weaker too. [14:47]
- Toxic Bullets is the biggest winner of the patch and a top three, maybe the best, item in the game; it never relied on movement speed or health, only damage over time. [14:47]
- Toxic Bullets is probably the best 3k choice if you can proc it decently; the coach now runs it on Warden, where it was rarely seen. [15:18]
- Slower fights keep the Toxic Bullets effect procced more easily and more health overall makes it even better; try it on many gun heroes. [15:50]
- Hotfix: at the tier two Patron, with one defender in the pit it takes 50% less damage, and with two or more defenders in the pit it takes zero damage. [16:20]
- This creates a stall meta: defenders keep two people in the pit and rotate anyone low back to base to heal, like Overwatch 2CP. [16:50]
- Items that keep you alive in the pit got better; in a desperate final defense buy Cheat Death or Ethereal Shift, selling an item if needed, instead of the old last-hurrah Refresher. [17:21]
- Ethereal Shift gives 4 seconds of invulnerability in the pit and Cheat Death even longer; leave just before dying. [17:53]
- The Patron damages the attacking team and the attackers cannot heal, which matters now that stalls last longer. [17:53]
- Cheat Death is a patch winner but still niche and a big investment; Ethereal Shift is probably the better option on many heroes. [18:24]
- As attacker, don't enter the final pit while enemy ults are up; clean up first with ultimates or lots of damage, then end. Expect more comebacks. [18:54]
- In lane, getting to 4.8k green investment with these items will likely be the meta and on average stronger than 4.8k gun or spirit. [19:24]
- After lane the five-man death ball with one carry farming from 10 minutes is mostly over. [19:56]
- Carries (Wraith, Seven, Infernus) should still hold a side lane, shove waves and farm the jungle on that side. [20:26]
- Sinner's Sacrifice is still probably the best income source if you can reach it, but leaving your lane to cross the map for a few sinners is probably not worth it; jungle plus lane gives as much or more. [20:58]
- Split pushing and sitting on side lanes are more viable because there are more small skirmishes. [21:28]
- Early fighters like Abrams and Mo & Krill can still roam and look for team fights, and going into the enemy jungle with Mo & Krill plus one to kill the carry or steal camps is good. [21:28]
- Supports and fighters should grab a tier two camp on the way, for example Pocket nuking it, since camps die much faster. [21:58]
- Lane creeps give less for everyone, so action moves away from lanes and invading the enemy jungle is better. [21:58]
- Support playstyle is the same: show up where the team needs you; Pocket or Mina can gank early or farm up. [21:58]
- Unstoppable is making a comeback because stuns are the most viable way to kill in a slower meta; buy it as a carry with your core up when you are the target of enemy stuns. [22:30]
- Do not buy Siphon Bullets on any hero: it does 33% less damage and 33% less healing, almost the worst item in the game. [23:02]
- For healing buy Infuser, Vampiric Burst or Leech instead of Siphon Bullets; for damage there are better orange and spirit options. [23:33]
- The 4.8k vitality investment is best on squishy heroes and might not be the best on Abrams or Shiv, who already have high base health. [23:33]
- Vitality items are stronger for non-tanks because the health is flat; tanky heroes get relatively less, so they lean on Spirit Resilience, Bullet Resilience and late options like Spellbreaker. [24:03]
