# Initiate To Eternus Deadlock Hero Tier List
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-10-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CIsvCWMQOvg

## Covers
Two hero tier lists from the patch current on 2025-10-14 (just after the 2025-10-11 rank redistribution): one for Initiate and the lower half of the ladder, one for Eternus 6.
Every hero is placed in each list with the reason, plus the general rules for why heroes rise or fall between low and high rank.
For any player choosing a hero to climb with, especially below Ascendant.

## Claims
- The meta in Eternus and very high ranks is very different from other ranks; below Oracle the Initiate list is probably the more accurate one for you. [0:00]
- Low rank (Initiate): Abrams is S tier. Easy to get into: hit the charge for a free melee or free bullets, very survivable, does not need good aim (big hitbox when stunned on the wall, you melee half the time), and the ult is easy to use. Top three tank, maybe number one; the pick for new players who want a tank. [0:30]
- Low rank: Bebop is C tier. He needs skill; bomb Bebop is mostly useless now because Debuff Remover and Counterspell are bought in every rank (it can still get value when the enemy skips counter items), and gun Bebop needs really good aim and hook hits, which low rank players struggle with. [1:32]
- Low rank: Billy is B tier although he is S tier played well. Low rank Billys spam heavy melees and get parried; he needs good movement and aim, unlike Abrams where you press the button and get value. [2:33]
- Low rank: Calico is A tier. Her combos are not super hard and do a lot of damage, but low rank players struggle with the one shot combo (leap in, an item like a blast, hit all Gloom Bombs) and waste the ult too early, then die without it. [3:03]
- Low rank: Doorman is C tier (he first said F). People do nothing on him: the abilities are hard to hit and hard to get value from, especially the teleport out and the Call Bell, and he is not easy in lane for a new player. [4:06]
- Low rank: Drifter is high B tier. Low rank players are often out of position, so his passive gets a lot of value and he takes many 1v1s, where he is really strong. [5:07]
- Low rank games last a long time, so carries and heroes that scale into the late game are better; everyone is rich at the end and Drifter is very scary late. [5:37]
- Low rank: Dynamo is easy to get into, but having your team follow up a big Black Hole is not trivial, so he depends on your team; first rated middle of the pack, then moved to A after being told Dynamo is top three win rate in low elo. He notes win rates alone do not tell the whole story. [6:07]
- Low rank: Grey Talon is the biggest gap between the two lists. He is spirit heavy but aim and positioning intensive and dies very easily; at low rank he often dies first out of position or the team will not go in. He guesses his low rank win rate is really low. [6:37]
- One of the big symptoms of low rank is that people are too scared to fight. [7:08]
- Low rank: Haze is S tier. People do not walk out of her ult, so she gets full value, and she has always had a high low rank win rate in every meta; she scales well into long games and people ignore Fixation stacks. If you are new and like shooting, pick Haze. [7:38]
- Low rank: Holliday is F tier. Her win rate is really low there because she needs good aim, really good movement and learned combos, and the lasso needs team follow-up; she is hard for new players. [8:39]
- Low rank: Infernus is very strong this patch, probably the best left click carry, and gets stronger the longer the game goes. [9:10]
- Low rank: Ivy is low B tier; not a hero you pick up and do a lot with as a new or mid rank player. [9:41]
- Low rank: Kelvin is S tier for the same reasons as Abrams: very easy, hard to kill, abilities hard to miss, free escape and free kills with the dome. He scales well and gun builds are still viable at low rank, but he falls off a bit late compared to hard carries and mega tanks. [10:11]
- Low rank: Lady Geist is S tier. Not hard to get into and people do badly against her in lane; her 2 (Life Drain) is auto aim and low rank players do not break line of sight, so they fight her and she cannot die. People also give her free life swaps when she hides low behind a corner. [11:11]
- Low rank: Lash is mid; moved down to C after chat pointed at his low rank win rate (lower middle of the pack). Not a hard carry or very high impact. [11:41]
- Many heroes that would never be bad in Eternus are bad at low rank only because they are hard to get into. [12:47]
- Low rank: McGinnis is B tier; bad in high rank right now, but at low rank she does her job and scales pretty well. [12:47]
- Low rank: Mina is C tier although still S tier in Eternus. She is hard to learn, has the lowest HP in the game (squishier than Haze) and low rank players die even with her teleport. [13:17]
- Low rank: Mirage is F tier, probably the lowest low rank win rate. He cannot farm jungle, so he needs souls from lane or kills, and mid to low ranks are farm fests, so he never gets rich enough to be scary late. [14:20]
- Mirage looks simple but needs a precise playstyle and proper teleport use; he watched a Mirage teleport once in 26 minutes. Correct play: sit on a side lane and farm it, and if anyone comes, contest them there and teleport. [14:50]
- Low rank: Mo & Krill is A tier, not S because he is not a hard carry still scary at 40 to 50 minutes and alone he is not scary; but he is so easy: spam your 1 and your barrels and you cannot really die, and with your team the ult is a kill button. [15:22]
- Low rank: Paige first low B or C tier as the hardest support (played as a heal bot, you play blind to your team), then moved up because her win rate is high, possibly from a gun build. Up to about Phantom or Ascendant you want a hero that can almost 1v6 or not die, and Paige is not that. [15:52]
- Low rank: Paradox is middle of the pack: you need good aim, movement and ability usage just to get her base value, and she does not scale insanely into the late game like the carries. [16:54]
- Low rank: Pocket is the same story as Mina but easier to get into: hit Affliction in the fight and you do your job, but you will not solo carry, and one misclick and you die. [17:24]
- Low rank: Seven still has a decent win rate below Ascendant and is A tier there (chat said S): people do not move out of his ult and games last long, which are the only games where he does anything now. [17:54]
- Low rank: Shiv historically has a low win rate there because he is not easy to get into and people feed on him; he is actually broken and in Eternus needs nerfs more than buffs. [18:57]
- Low rank: Sinclair sits at the bottom; he has never seen a Sinclair do anything, even in Eternus. He is the hardest hero to learn and master, and without a draft you can get games with no good enemy ult to steal. [19:27]
- Low rank: Victor is S tier. Not the best hero anymore but still an insane late game character, top three late if farmed, and strong mid game; if you have souls on Victor you win. [19:59]
- Low rank: Vindicta (caption says "Viktor") is B tier, similar to Grey Talon but easier: she needs a team in front of her, but a random gun build gets value and can take 1v1s. Too squishy for higher. [20:29]
- In low rank the two things that matter are whether the hero is good late game and whether the hero is tanky. [21:00]
- Low rank: Viscous is C tier (chat claimed F): one of the hardest heroes to learn and master with lots of tools most players do not use, but crazy once mastered. [21:30]
- Low rank: Vyper is C tier. The new Vyper is better overall than the old one but trickier: in the mid game you now must hit your 1 to get value instead of going full gun, and she is weaker in super late game because her old slide resists were better than the ult shield she gets now. [22:01]
- Low rank: Warden is high B tier. People do not move out of his ult; you avoid it by hearing it, seeing it and getting out, but teams tank it at his 6 and die. Once he has his full build he is scary, he is just not good before. [23:02]
- Low rank: Wraith is S tier, the highest damage late game hero, even more than Infernus; she takes longer to get there, which does not matter when every game is 30 plus minutes. [23:33]
- Low rank: Yamato is B tier, solid. [24:04]
- Summary: carry heroes are a lot better in low rank. Below Ascendant (including Archon and Oracle after the rank reset) the Initiate list is probably the accurate one; the shift to the high rank list happens around Phantom, Ascendant and Eternus. [24:04]
- High rank (Eternus 6): Abrams stays S tier, he does not die and is the tankiest hero; Eternus players know what they can get away with and on Abrams that is almost always go in and kill. He is very hard to punish right now. [25:06]
- High rank: Bebop is a lot stronger; picked in pro play, especially EU, with some of the highest pick and ban rates. High rank Bebops play gun with really strong aim. [25:38]
- High rank: Billy is A tier (S on his past lists, not weaker, others got stronger). He can dictate the game at any point, is super strong mid game and still scales well into late game. [26:39]
- High rank: Calico is S tier and broken: Eternus players hit the combo and you die, her ult lets her get in and out for free, Gloom Bombs are some of the highest nuke damage, and with Ethereal Shift she ults, shifts, and has the ult back. [27:10]
- High rank: Doorman is A tier; people hit his abilities and gun Doorman is very popular, but he is not a solo carry, needs his team and does not have the highest impact at all points, though very scary once mastered. [27:41]
- High rank: Drifter is terrible. Higher up, people show up and stack for team fights, which turns off his isolation passive most of the game, and when they see Drifter in a 1v1 someone comes to help. [28:12]
- High rank: Seven is the worst hero in the game: 0% pick or ban rate in the last Night Shift, a terrible lane, a terrible mid game, and late game you might as well pick Infernus, Wraith or Victor. Once one of the most popular heroes, now not picked in any rank. [29:14]
- High rank: Dynamo is weak, not quite F tier; his one redeeming quality is that when behind, a big Black Hole can always turn the game around. His problem is he is bad in lane. [29:44]
- In Eternus the laning stage is by far the most important stage of the game (probably in Ascendant too), because Eternus players carry an advantage forward and do not die pointlessly; at lower ranks so many mistakes happen that a lane lead often does not hold. [30:14]
- High rank: Grey Talon gets a massive upgrade at Eternus 6 (he still struggles a bit in lower Eternus): with a team playing around him he is the highest spirit nuker, safely nuking the enemy team. [30:46]
- Grey Talon's Guided Owl ult is one of the strongest in high rank: the extra bit of damage that wins a fight across the map, used nonstop by good Talons, and it gives huge scouting. Always be aware of the bird. [31:17]
- High rank: Haze is rated really high. Her builds got better last patch, Blood Tribute is a new item she benefits from in particular, she is really strong in lane in Eternus, and she kills everyone before late game even if she is not a hard late carry like Wraith. [31:47]
- High rank: Holliday gets a massive upgrade but is a weaker Paradox, with a bit more movement and better escapes with her jump pads. [32:48]
- High rank: Paradox is top three, probably second best. With one green item she has 2,000 to 2,500 HP at 10 minutes, more than some tanks. [33:19]
- Paradox is played with the one shot carbine combo: Express Shot plus a spirit burn item or Tankbuster on Kinetic Carbine. She is the best initiator (swap) and her wall is great defensively; a carry that does ridiculous damage starting in lane. [33:19]
- High rank: Kelvin is the best hero in the game by far: first banned all seven of the last Night Shift pro games and never played. The dome protects from an ult, wins fights, can catch someone for a free kill, and heals; ice beam is insane, he has mobility, and you can dome mid boss or your own Patron. [33:51]
- High rank: Infernus (rated high B on his last list) is the best gun carry in the game and best late game carry, better than Wraith because he comes online right after lane though he farms worse. Play gun Infernus with Rapid Recharge; perma dash Infernus is probably not good now. [35:23]
- High rank: Ivy is high B tier. Her full carry build with the highest gun damage was nerfed, so she is played more as a support (spirit) or a supportive gun style still farming Capacitor, getting much of her value from abilities; still crazy for utility support. [35:54]
- High rank: Lady Geist is B tier, better after the patch; okay but not crazy in lane since high rank players know how to play against her, always solid, can go hybrid, tanky front line, and does a lot of damage. [36:57]
- High rank: Lash is low C tier: everyone buys Counterspell, which leaves his ult doing nothing; he is not good in lane and his slam was nerfed. Bad but not unplayable. [37:28]
- F tier in high rank means unplayable: if you have one on your team you have nearly lost; seeing an enemy Seven makes a win very likely. [37:58]
- High rank: McGinnis is worse than Lash with no identity: turret and ult builds are not playable, her gun is outskilled by every gun carry and her support by every healer like Kelvin, Ivy and Dynamo. [38:29]
- High rank: Mina is broken: second highest pick and ban in Night Shift that week (banned three times, picked five), and played well she kills everyone. [38:59]
- High rank: Mirage gets an upgrade; good Mirages are scary but he is really hard despite his simple kit. His edge is a global ult to show up in any team fight or leave whenever he wants; placed in A tier. [39:29]
- High rank: Mo & Krill is a solid A tier, a bit weaker than before because the nerfs to his Burrow resistances make him easier to kill, but he still does not die; not Abrams level tanky. [40:00]
- High rank: Paige is B tier (playable, picked more in pro play): still a heal bot with an identity crisis who does a lot if she heals well, but Eternus 6 is solo queue only right now, no stacks, and Paige needs a competent teammate. [40:32]
- High rank: Pocket is S tier, unchanged from last patch: a budget Mina who presses Affliction and does insane damage. [41:02]
- High rank: Shiv is A tier; broken in some comps, especially with Kelvin, because he likes another close range hero; without Kelvin he is super solid but not amazing. [41:33]
- High rank: Sinclair is F tier: if the enemy has no good ult to steal he is a nonfactor. He got nerfed indirectly because Seven and Warden, two of the best ults to steal, were nerfed and nobody plays them. [42:04]
- High rank: Victor is top of A tier (low S or high A, S if fed). He is stronger than before because he can show up in early and mid game fights, which happen a lot in Eternus, instead of AFK farming for 20 minutes. [42:34]
- High rank: Vindicta is A tier (chat said S), below Grey Talon. She sees a lot of pro play; the build is gun with Rapid Recharge and a few more spirit items for a full nuke: throw your abilities, then snipe. [43:37]
- High rank: Viscous is low A tier and actually overpowered: there is a one shot combo players run, and he can support, nuke, live forever, save himself and his team, and go in and out fast. [44:37]
- High rank: Vyper is low B tier, better than before and scary enough as a damage dealer, but not seen much. [45:07]
- High rank: Warden goes below B: he has no identity, still an ult bot but the ult is mid, the ult build is not good anymore, and he is boring and not good. [45:39]
- High rank: Wraith is A tier, around Haze: if your team creates space for her to farm she can 1v6 late, but her lane is bad. [46:10]
- High rank: Yamato is B tier; he does not know how to rate her anymore. [46:41]
- Main difference between the lists: harder to learn heroes become a lot stronger at high rank, and super hard carries become a bit weaker because Eternus enemies shut them down before they reach their late game threshold (for example Wraith, S tier in low rank). [46:41]
- Unless you are about Ascendant or higher, the Initiate list is probably the one that applies to you. [48:12]
