# The Most Broken Hero Ever Released | Baba Analysis
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-10-06
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ws2OywOYNKM

## Covers
First look at the new hero Baba on release day (2026-10-06): all four abilities with their numbers and level ups, combos, ability order and the starting item core.
For anyone picking up Baba in the first days, framed as a spirit frontline disruptor compared to Celeste.

## Claims
- Baba is a spirit nuker who also deals damage with the gun, has a strong melee ability and is tanky; closest comparison is Celeste, a frontline disruptor who weaves in and out of fights to deal big damage and disrupt. [0:31]
- Ability 1 (Thread Slap) sends a thread that follows your crosshair, pulls enemies toward you and steals their spirit power; you gain 175% of what you steal, so stealing 10 gives about 17 or 18 spirit power. [0:31]
- Thread Slap also makes your bullets deal bonus spirit damage, 13 spirit damage per bullet at base, lasting 22 seconds. [1:03]
- Thread Slap level 1 doubles the spirit damage per bullet, level 2 lowers the cooldown, level 3 adds a charge and pulls enemies on hit. [1:03]
- Thread Slap looks underwhelming alone but is a setup tool: every hit steals spirit power, and the per-bullet spirit damage scales with spirit power. [1:33]
- Ability 2 gives long legs: more movement speed and melee debuff resist. [1:33]
- During ability 2, light melee kicks the enemy far away, and heavy melee slams the ground in a big AoE (even at base) that slows, knocks up and deals spirit damage. [2:05]
- The ability 2 light melee can be parried like normal and you get stunned, but the heavy melee is an AoE and does not stun you when parried, so spam the heavy since there is no risk. [2:36]
- Ability 2 also has a hold-crouch charged jump that works like Bebop's; a short charge only jumps a little. [3:06]
- The charged jump takes so long to fully charge that it is situational and nearly useless as an escape, since you are probably dead by the time it charges. [3:37]
- Ability 2 is your tool to get in and out of fights; the heavy melee is what you spam because it scales with spirit, deals spirit damage and disrupts. [3:37]
- Ability 2 has a low cooldown, 23 seconds at max level; its level ups add movement speed and cooldown, and the max level gives 50% life steal, which heals a lot in team fights with many enemies around. [3:37]
- In team fights not everyone parries, which is why the ability 2 heavy melee seems broken. [4:08]
- Ability 3 brews a flask with three rotating effects and you can hold it as long as you want before casting. [4:39]
- Flask effect 1, Soothing, gives you and allies in the AoE a barrier. [4:39]
- Flask effect 2 silences enemies for 3 seconds at base and deals no damage. [4:39]
- Flask effect 3, Scalding, is the big damage: a burn that also lowers the enemy's spirit resist. [5:09]
- While holding the flask you cannot shoot, only melee, so you can farm a sinner's sacrifice with melee and throw a potion if someone comes. [5:09]
- Pre-plan a gank by holding the silence flask and silencing a key target (example: Mina) the moment you come around the corner. [5:09]
- The flask is a very high skill ability, but its level ups are weak: level 1 adds radius, level 2 lowers cooldown to quite low, level 3 only gives 33%; it is probably the weakest at max and the one you level last. [5:39]
- The ultimate covers the floor in bird seed that stacks over a channel; when the channel ends a flock of pigeons bombards the target for spirit damage and hexes survivors into pigeons. [6:10]
- The ultimate is hard to dodge in real fights because the channel is long and you can keep spreading seed across spread-out enemies to hit the whole team. [6:41]
- The ultimate's spirit scaling is already high at base and goes up by 1.5 at max, to about 3.1x. [7:13]
- With a normal default build and stolen spirit power from Thread Slap, the ultimate did 1,700 damage to everyone. [7:45]
- You can use ability 2 during the ultimate to chase or escape, ability 3 during ability 2, and ability 1 as well. [8:16]
- The ability 2 heavy melee does not hit the Rejuvenator, so you do not have to be there for it. [8:16]
- In close range fights like the pit, sit in the middle and spam heavy melee; maybe three enemies parry but the other three miss it and die. [8:48]
- The per-bullet spirit damage scales with spirit, so later in the game with stolen spirit you deal about 125 bonus damage per bullet, and with items you have four Thread Slap casts. [9:18]
- You can build fire rate, but you definitely want spirit. [9:48]
- Spirit Snatch will be hard meta and a no-brainer, because ability 2 functions as a melee. [9:48]
- Ability order: take Thread Slap first in lane because base bullet damage is low and the first point doubles the spirit damage per bullet. [10:19]
- Put the second point in Thread Slap too, since it lengthens the debuff (more spirit damage time) and lowers the cooldown; two points in ability 1 is a no-brainer. [10:19]
- Thread Slap's max level is not crazy because Extra Charge or Rapid Recharge, which you buy anyway, give the extra charge; the pull on hit is nice but not game breaking. [10:49]
- After the two points in Thread Slap, max ability 2, because 50% melee life steal on an ability you spam nonstop is game breaking. [10:49]
- Ability 3 points are only radius and cooldown early and you mostly use it on yourself or one enemy, so level it later. [10:49]
- After ability 2, level the ultimate (cast range, 55 cooldown reduction, huge damage), then about two points in ability 3 to lower its cooldown, then max ability 1 and ability 3. [11:21]
- Extra Charge is a no-brainer item. [11:51]
- Radiant Regeneration is great because Baba deals AoE spirit damage and every bullet procs it for each hero hit; melee also procs it. [11:51]
- Rapid Recharge in lane for sure, and Spirit Snatch for sure. [12:23]
- Fire rate items look good because of spirit damage per bullet, and melee items look good because ability 2 heals you. [12:23]
- Many of her abilities are AoE, so range and radius items are core (Thread Slap range gets very long). [12:23]
- After the ultimate fully charges you can walk out of its radius and it still casts automatically; only the buildup needs you there, so you can charge it and then back off. [12:54]
- Her spirit resist scales with spirit power at 0.12: with four items she already had 27% spirit resist (Greater Expansion gave some), so you do not need to buy Spirit Resilience. [13:25]
- Stealing spirit power with Thread Slap also raises spirit resist; one pull took it to 48%. [13:56]
- Surge of Power will be really good on her because of the fire rate she builds. [13:56]
- Verdict: insane, very high skill ceiling, likely to be nerfed soon; max ability 2. [13:56]
