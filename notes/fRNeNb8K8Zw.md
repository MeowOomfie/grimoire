# Lots of Changes in Today's Patch | Deadlock Patch Overview
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-05-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRNeNb8K8Zw

## Covers
Midknight's overview of the 5/1/26 minor patch: vitality and weapon scaling nerfs, tougher Guardians and Walkers, balance pad knock-off, earlier max boons, a new tier four green anti-CC item, item reworks (Opening Rounds, Shadow Weave, Chronos Pendant, Reactive Barrier, Veil Walker, Inhibitor) and hero changes.
For players wanting a verdict on each change and who will buy the new items.

## Claims
- Vitality changes make heroes a little less innately tanky as they scale, and investing in the green tree a bit more valuable. [0:00]
- Weapon scaling nerfs are a step in the right direction but not enough; the fundamental issue is scaling by percentage off the base. [0:00]
- Gun nerfs to Pocket, Bebop and Vyper, a smaller nerf to Victor, increases to Mo & Krill; Pocket and Vyper both get 10% less growth, hopefully making their guns less oppressive. [0:30]
- Guardians and Walkers get more bullet resist, a great change; previously Valve lowered their spirit resist when guns did too much to Walkers. [0:30]
- Being knocked off balance pads now sends you downward quickly, so panic-hitting a balance pad to eat the stun and escape no longer works; it CCs you and makes you easier to catch. [1:00]
- You hit max boons 9k souls sooner: a big buff to gun heroes since scaling is easier, and hopefully lower level games end quicker. [1:00]
- New tier four green item (formerly in Street Brawl): on a 55 second cooldown, when hit by CC you gain a barrier instead of being CC'd, and all your ability cooldowns are reduced by 20%, which is crazy on high cooldown ults like Dynamo's. [1:31]
- He expects the new item to be bought more for the cooldown reduction than the CC block. [1:31]
- The new item's CC block can be beaten by things like Arctic Blast, and it doesn't block Dynamo's ult since it doesn't give unstoppable; it blocks a single instance like Mo & Krill's. [1:31]
- The new item suits very squishy spirit heroes, later game M1 heroes hard-targeted by someone like Krill, and heroes like Lash whose ults get cancelled by a stray Haze sleep or Vyper bola. [2:01]
- Opening Rounds: less spirit power, its active does 15% less weapon damage, but it now gives High Velocity stats (flat 7% bullet damage and 65% bullet velocity); a good change and an overall buff for gun heroes, but less relevant since its slot was for spirit hybrids. [2:31]
- Another item got a damage and stacks nerf; its core issue is that you don't have to aim on heroes balanced around aiming, and this doesn't fix it. [3:01]
- Shadow Weave: weaker but now tier three; essentially a Haze invis, powerful, and he expects heroes like Krill to buy it. [3:01]
- Shadow Weave lacks Veil Walker's heal; if you're good with Veil Walker (Holliday) keep buying it since the green tree is more valuable and the heal keeps you on map; offensive heroes like Wraith who want spirit and fire rate should consider Shadow Weave. [3:32]
- Heroic Aura and Blood Shroud got buffs he disagrees with; both are already overpowered. [3:32]
- An item now gives spirit lifesteal on active instead of ability duration; a good change since stacking lifesteal beats duration you already have innately, but it's very expensive and won't reach relevance. [3:32]
- Frenzy now gives debuff resistance instead of spirit resist below 50% HP; cool, but won't raise its buy rate. [4:32]
- Crystal Aegis: 4 less HP heal per stack but 5 extra max stacks, and less stamina; spirit resist untouched. Small changes to meta-defining tier twos tend to knock them off, so expect it bought less, though 10% spirit resist keeps it decent. [4:32]
- Return Fire: returns a bit more damage and gives a bit more bullet resist. [5:03]
- Reactive Barrier: no stamina on trigger (doesn't matter), 6% spirit resist (matters), now triggers on silences (more procs), but 15 seconds more cooldown and 0.3 less spirit scaling; still overtuned at 1.8 spirit scaling and may push toward Transcendent Cooldown builds. [5:03]
- Chronos Pendant: worse stats but now tier two; no longer a troll item, fairly easy to stack since 1,600 souls is far less than 3,200; expect it consistently on Krill. [5:33]
- If a support buys Chronos Pendant, they take comeback souls from their carry or feed the enemy carry more; expect it more on front liners and brawlers like Silver or Apollo at high level and pro play than on Paige. [6:04]
- Another Dispel Magic nerf; heavy buying of it reflects the item being overtuned rather than a CC problem (said sarcastically). [6:34]
- Veil Walker now upgrades from Sprint Boots: a bit less health, but sprint speed and out-of-combat regen; sprint speed is lost on wall bounces and is a mediocre stat. [7:04]
- Small Counterspell nerf; it still does its job. [7:04]
- Colossus is moving toward a tank item and will be bought on squishy heroes for the bonus health. [7:04]
- Inhibitor weapon damage nerfed 15%, so it can't simply be bought over Glass Cannon; it still suppresses weapon damage 40% like Silencer and is insane late game utility, so he still recommends it. [7:04]
- Suppressor now gives 8% bullet resist, good against bullet heroes, but Rusted Barrel is still very overpowered and remains the go-to bullet counter in lane. [7:36]
- Scourge now grants debuff resistance. [8:06]
- A bunch of lifesteal nerfs. [8:06]
- One hero gets a faster time to fire and a small ult buff. [8:06]
- Drifter gets small buffs on his 1 and 3, which is where he'd buff him; Drifter is very slept on. [8:06]
- Dynamo: 0.15 scaling reduced on his kick (still needs more of a nerf) and +1 second on Quantum Entanglement cooldown; that ability is disgustingly good, so Dynamo stays meta. [8:36]
- Haze buffed again for the 50th patch in a row; it won't make her playable in high elo, just more annoying in low elo. Rework her or cement her as a pick hero. [8:36]
- Holliday's lasso: 20 seconds less cooldown and a bit more damage, but that isn't what she needs; she hits a wall mid to late game and becomes cosmetic going spirit (like Apollo). He pivots to support to counter this. [9:06]
- Spirit needs an item to counter spirit resist, or spirit resist needs a nerf so it isn't a complete wall for heroes like Holliday and Apollo; remove Armor Piercing Rounds if they go that route. [9:37]
- Infernus Flame Dash spirit scaling reduced 0.3 (significant) and Flame Dash now deals half DPS to objectives, so he stops one-shotting them. [9:37]
- Another small Mirage nerf, putting him in his place. [10:08]
- Paige moved to a lower bucket (slower dash) but given one more stamina, plus small cooldown buffs. [10:08]
- Paradox gets 20% air dash distance with Carbine charged; she doesn't need more movement. [10:08]
- Paradox wall changes are fine; the wall should be pure team utility with the silence removed. Her kit is miserable to play against and may need a rework. [10:38]
- The meta is mostly pickless; in solo queue pick is still meta, but half your games have no pick hero on your team. [11:09]
- Pocket got buffed, which he can't understand. [11:09]
- Shiv knife scaling reduced; he thinks Shiv needs fewer, stronger knives on lower cooldown, or many small knives, so they aren't hard-walled by Dispel Magic. [11:09]
- Silver: -1% health as damage on her 1; he wants it nerfed down to zero over time. [11:39]
- Vindicator: -0.1 scaling on gut shot and a wall-hit nerf; disappointing, it shouldn't do damage on wall hit at all since it already gives 400 damage, a guaranteed melee, a stun and movement. [12:09]
- Victor tier two aura +6 DPS (significant); he thinks Victor is overrated and spirit Victor is fine. [12:40]
- Victor tier three ult buffed, encouraging the wall breaker Victors who max ult first and run at objectives; he is not a fan of that build. [12:40]
- Vyper: Venom cooldown a bit higher, gets it back at tier two, cast range bigger and scales with boons; less oppressive in lane but she needed more of a nerf. [13:11]
- Fixed: Armor Piercing Rounds going through Petrify, but it still goes through Kelvin's dome and McGinnis's wall. [13:11]
- Vyper can still hit barriers of people inside her Petrify, which matters more with the new item. [13:41]
- Street Brawl changes are not enough; the mode is very unbalanced. [13:41]
