# Massive Balance Patch Analysis (Shadow Weave NERFS)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-11-08
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7sd04L47oxY

## Covers
Metro reads through a large early November 2024 balance patch line by line with his first reactions: soul sharing and kill gold, Guardian aggro, walker armor, many item changes (Shadow Weave, Rebirth removed, Metal Skin, Slowing Hex, Toxic Bullets) and hero changes (Grey Talon, Vindicta, McGinnis and Mirage nerfs, Haze and Warden buffs, Yamato ult rework).
For players tracking how the meta shifted. This is a late-2024 patch and nearly all of these numbers have changed since; the transcript begins partway into the notes.

## Claims
- Bots now attack Shrines and the Patron more aggressively when pushing into the enemy base; a major change that helps a big pushing wave, since before they ignored them. [0:00]
- Fixed being able to gain regen when crossing the veil of the enemy Fountain; good change. [0:00]
- Hero Labs gets another hero and Hero Labs is now supported in private lobbies. [0:00]
- McGinnis' Heavy Barrage explosion now shows its edge better, making it easier to dodge. [0:00]
- Troopers no longer change soul sharing rules after the laning phase ends, so they no longer split souls and the person who shoots them gets them; he dislikes it, expecting more farm stealing and toxicity. [0:30]
- Troopers no longer increase their bounty by 20% at 8 minutes: less money in the lane. [0:30]
- Hero kill gold increased from 150-1,400 to 175-2,000; he likes the small early increase but doubts the late game needs more and expects more snowball. [1:00]
- Neutral creeps give 5% fewer souls; good, players who only farm neutral camps will drop. [1:00]
- Troopers take 28% more damage from Guardians, another Guardian buff that makes walkers harder to push. [1:31]
- Walker damage versus players increased by 10%; not a big deal. [1:31]
- Guardians now focus their attack on an enemy player who shoots a hero while within range; the biggest change so far. [1:31]
- He dislikes the Guardian aggro change: it encourages camping your tower and stops rewarding lane control and pushing, which took skill; a player who is harassed out of lane should not be rewarded. [2:02]
- Walker armor loss over time now happens at 16 minutes instead of 14, a walker buff; he thinks walkers did not need it but were too easy to kill early. [2:34]
- Spirit resist goes to 30% instead of 40% at 25 minutes. [2:34]
- Mid boss Rejuvenator health increased; a Rejuvenator buff, though it was already very strong. [2:34]
- Warehouse interior redesigned to allow entry from the front of the Tenements and Music Hall buildings, and all railings removed from fire escape stairs. [3:05]
- Close Quarters no longer grants 85 bullet health but grants 5% bullet resist; probably a nerf at least early. [3:05]
- Restorative Shot no longer grants 7 bullet resist but grants a bullet shield, and now procs on orb shots for the NPC heal value; a buff that makes it a better defensive option, so he will run more Restorative Shot and less Extra Regen. [3:36]
- Melee Charge no longer grants 1 HP regen and its weapon damage is reduced; he approves. [3:36]
- Long Range weapon damage reduced from 40% to 30% but it now grants 10% weapon damage, still 40% overall, so a buff to long range. [3:36]
- Berserker bullet resist increased from 7 to 9. [4:06]
- Active Reload grants 50 health; a small buff, unsure it makes the item worth it. [4:06]
- Slowing Bullets spirit power increased from 5 to 6; super small. [4:06]
- Sharpshooter long range weapon damage reduced but it is still a buff overall, more universal, which he finds odd since it buffs sniper heroes. [4:06]
- An item captioned "chemical fire" (name unclear) grants +1 sprint; a needed buff to a weak item. [4:36]
- Heroic Aura cooldown reduced. [4:36]
- Intensifying Magazine time to reach max damage reduced; more overall damage, it might be good again. [4:36]
- Toxic Bullets damage changed from 5% current health to 2.5% max health; it did high damage early and nothing late before, now it can kill low health targets; maybe a small nerf overall but still a really good item, bought mainly for the healing reduction. [4:36]
- Shadow Weave nerfed, smaller than he expected; it should give enough time to spot Shadow Weave users running at you. [5:08]
- Glass Cannon buffed quite a bit; the 3% health adds up at 1,000 to 2,000 HP, and it needed buffs as a weak item. [5:39]
- Frenzy low HP bullet resist increased; even stronger. [5:39]
- Lucky Shot fixed causing proc effects to roll twice; this likely makes Lucky Shot useless and it will no longer be in his build. [6:10]
- Silencer grants 12% spirit resist; a big buff to a previously useless item that might make it valuable. [6:10]
- Vampiric Burst cooldown change of 4 seconds barely matters, since you usually only get one off in a team fight. [6:10]
- Extra Health buffed; Extra Weapon damage increased; Healing Rite duration increased; Bullet Armor buffed. [6:42]
- Return Fire nerfed; he approves but thinks it is not enough and should be 4 seconds. [6:42]
- Combat Barrier buffed. [6:42]
- Debuff Reducer buffed; makes sense, it was a really bad item and is now more valuable. [6:42]
- Healbane healing reduction increased; buff. [7:13]
- Reactive Barrier restores one stamina on proc; a buff to an already really good item. [7:13]
- Spirit Armor buffed; he thinks it is already very good. [7:13]
- Divine Barrier no longer grants 8% ability range; pretty big, it was too good. [7:13]
- Restorative Locket no longer grants 5 spirit power but grants 25% melee resistance; much tankier, a net buff. [7:13]
- Metal Skin no longer blocks melee damage; a really big nerf, since much of the damage taken while Metal Skin is up was melee. [7:44]
- Debuff Remover buffed; he said it was a bad item. [7:44]
- An item captioned "the deaths agree" (name unclear) cooldown reduced; maybe buildable now since it was really bad. [7:44]
- An item captioned "bail Walker" (name unclear) gets more ammo and weapon damage; Fortitude health increased from 300 to 400. [8:15]
- Lifestrike bullet resistance increased. [8:15]
- Rebirth removed from the game; he agrees, it was inherently broken because two lives is overpowered late game and it could not be balanced even with a 5 minute respawn. [8:15]
- Inhibitor slow reduced; already niche on Wraith, he will not buy it anymore. [8:45]
- Siphon Bullets buffed with 15% weapon damage; it might be back on the menu. [8:45]
- Phantom Strike now grounds any flying target on impact; bad news for Vindicta and Grey Talon. [9:16]
- Ammo Scavenger stack duration increased a lot; you only lose stacks by dying or being AFK, so it is back on the menu. [9:16]
- Mystic Burst now triggers on your base damage rather than how much the target receives, ignoring resist; he sees a new build in it. [9:47]
- Slowing Hex now uses Enduring Spirit as a component, grants 10% spirit lifesteal and 75 bonus health, spirit power reduced, no longer grants +1 sprint, damage reduced; a nerf he agrees with since it was too good. [9:47]
- Withering Whip bullet resist reduction reduced, cooldown reduced, now deals 50 spirit damage; many new options, and many of these are Wraith items. [10:17]
- Decay now grants 1.5 HP regen and 8% ability duration, healing reduction increased, bleed damage reduced, cooldown reduced; a nerf overall because the 3% bleed was big, but now a better anti-heal item and good into Shiv. [10:17]
- Improved Cooldown cooldown reduction reduced; another spirit item (captions garbled) now grants 10% ammo; Superior Cooldown now grants 15% ammo; 10% ammo is quite a bit. [10:49]
- Improved Burst max HP damage reduced from 9% to 8%. [11:20]
- Abrams: faster melees; Shoulder Charge can again strafe to slightly change direction and now carries momentum at the end; Siphon Life spirit scaling increased; Infernal Resilience damage regen reduced but duration increased and its T3 reduced; Seismic Impact fixed to travel toward the facing direction. Overall he reads it as slight nerfs. [11:20]
- Bebop: Sticky Bomb damage gain per hero impact increased and per kill reduced, a buff overall; Sticky Bomb T3 now has a 30% damage penalty on allies he approves of; it calculates damage at detonation time and has 2x cast range on allies. [11:53]
- Bebop Hook damage reduced from 40 to 10 but now scales at 2x spirit, slightly more damage. [12:55]
- Bebop Hyper Beam now goes on cooldown for 3 seconds when canceled during precast. [12:55]
- Dynamo now gains 1% bullet resist per boon; a big tanky buff. [12:55]
- Dynamo Singularity now deals 1.5% max HP in the base ability and its T3 rises from 3.8 to 4; base DPS reduced, but the percentage matters more, a big buff to Black Hole. [13:28]
- Grey Talon bullet damage reduced with growth per boon increased (same late damage), falloff range rescaled from 22-58 to 18-54, strafe distance with stamina reduced 30%, and charge hitbox reduced; nerfs he welcomes. [13:58]
- Haze: Bullet Dance target changes (captions unclear) and it now allows using items during it; Sleep Dagger T3 makes the target wake up with 40 less of something (caption cuts off); Fixation also stacks faster, which he estimates as about a 60% early ult damage buff on a single target, making Haze's ult way more deadly early. [14:31]
- Infernus: Catalyst slow reduced and Afterburn buildup per shot reduced quite a lot (10 to 9 and 16 to 14); some last bullet burns will no longer happen; he thinks Infernus needed nerfs. [15:03]
- Ivy: Watcher's Covenant fire rate and bullet lifesteal spirit scaling increased; buffs to a weak ability. [15:34]
- Lash: Ground Strike can be cast on the zipline and now pulls enemies to the ground; buffs, though he thought Lash was already really good. [16:04]
- McGinnis mini turret DPS and health reduced; about a 33% damage nerf, a big one he welcomes. [16:04]
- Mirage: bullet size reduced (harder to hit), Tornado speed and lift duration reduced, Fire Scarabs T2 upgrade reduced, all nerfs; still not enough, good players will still bully you in lane; the Fire Scarabs change matters because Mirage players level it to at least level two. [16:37]
- Paradox bullet velocity increased; Time Wall no longer has charges and no longer deals 10% max health damage; Kinetic Carbine max damage increased; Paradoxical Swap completes faster, a nerf because Paradox is invulnerable during the swap. [17:09]
- Pocket barrage spirit scaling increased; light melee damage increased. [17:40]
- Vindicta bullet damage reduced with growth per boon increased (same total damage); Stake duration increased; Flight T2 upgrade reduced; Crow Familiar tick rate changed and starts at 25% power instead of 50%; nerfs to Vindicta he welcomes. [18:10]
- Viscous: The Cube health regen now scales with spirit at 0.2; Goo Ball down dash moved from tier three to base; Goo Ball size grows with range upgrades. [18:10]
- Warden: +1 sprint, a big buff in lane; bullet velocity greatly increased, removing his one lane weakness; Last Stand goes on cooldown if you die (slight nerf), T3 no longer grants Unstoppable but Last Stand grants 50% spirit resist during the channel; the biggest hero change, Warden might be S+ and the most broken hero now. [18:40]
- Wraith Telekinesis cast time increased; a nerf he would rather have seen elsewhere since it is already easy to escape. [19:41]
- Yamato Shadow Transformation rework: affects grapple, duration from 4 to 6 seconds, no longer prevents death, grants 6% bullet and spirit resist, heals 30% HP, and extends 2 seconds per kill. [19:41]
- The Yamato rework changes play style: casting the ult when already very low (how most Yamatos used it) now gets her killed, so cast it early; at full HP during the ult she is probably very hard to kill. [20:12]
- Overall verdict: sniper nerfs, Paradox nerfs, a scary Warden buff and really big item changes. [20:44]
