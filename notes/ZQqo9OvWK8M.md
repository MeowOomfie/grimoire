## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream, early July 2026, on the patch that added the Urn plus Rift objectives and reverted the Shiv changes. He coaches four players: a Lash (Ascendant, E3 or E4 before the patch), a Mo & Krill (Ascendant) into a Bebop, a Paige (Oracle) and a Vindicta (Ascendant) on a 2-max build, then gives a quick look at a Viscous build.
Themes: agency and making calls, trophy collector syndrome, resetting instead of greeding, overstaying after picks, shooting and confirming orbs, timers (buffs, tier one camp, Urn), positions 1 to 6, Divine Barrier and Dispel Magic into CC comps, Metal Skin versus Plated Armor, and tilt queueing.

- Pos 1 gets the most farm, pos 6 the least. Pos 1 is your true carry who is always weak siding and perma farming, a gun carry that scales well like Seven or Wraith. [150:01]

- Positions are malleable in solo queue but real; they ground where you should be and how much farm you take. A Rem should not take his carry's camps. [154:46]
