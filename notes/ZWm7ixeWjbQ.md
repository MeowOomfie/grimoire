# Explaining Deadlock's Most Significant Balance Patch In A Year
channel: HERESY
date: 2026-09-17
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWm7ixeWjbQ

## Covers
HERESY explains the mid September 2026 balance patch: the comeback economy nerfs (rift, bounties, tick souls to the two poorest players), respawn, parry, stun-reload and slow-affects-air-drag changes, then the item and hero changes with his verdicts. His thesis: the anti-tempo meta is over, tempo and snowball heroes are up and scalers and AFK farmers are down. For players deciding what to pick and buy after the patch.

## Claims
- This is the most significant balance patch since the hero reworks about 6 months earlier, arguably bigger. [0:00]
- Before the patch the game was in an anti-tempo meta at every rank: lane phase and early game barely mattered, comebacks were so strong that snowballing was almost impossible, and winning early had low correlation with winning the late game. [0:00]
- The Golden Goose Egg meta happened because early tempo didn't matter, not because the egg itself was overpowered. [0:31]
- The most significant changes are to the economy, not heroes or items. [1:03]
- Unstable comeback rift: the old mechanic that made the first, maybe second, rift almost impossible for the winning team to contest is gone. Comeback resistances for the losing team still scale linearly and over time; the cap is now 40% (was 35%), so the rift is a bit stronger for the losing team very late but much weaker at the first rift. [1:03]
- So if you win the lane phase you can now use your soul lead to contest the rift; even fights are not likely won, but they are winnable, and taking it removes a big comeback soul source from the enemy. [2:03]
- Extra bonus souls for being barely behind on kill bounties and the rift are removed (they were also multiplied by the general comeback buff), so comeback souls are heavily nerfed: kills, camps, troopers and especially objectives give the losing team less. [2:03]
- Underrated change: a portion of comeback souls earned by players above the enemy team's average net worth now goes through the tick system to the two lowest net worth players on the team, as a constant trickle based on team income. [3:03]
- Before, the losing team's AFK power farmer (for example a Wraith letting the team die) got boosted camp souls and often out-farmed the winning team's carries, even in pro play (one player at 40,000 souls while the rest of the team had 22,000). That boosted players bad at lane and map play. [3:34]
- Now an AFK farming Wraith on the losing team is mostly feeding comeback souls to her poorest teammates (for example Paige and Kelvin); HERESY calls this much healthier, especially for high elo. [4:35]
- Bounty for permanent objectives is increased, making them better compared to Rift and Urn; a good change. [5:06]
- Respawn time is increased only after the 20 minute mark, with the same late game values; picks at 20 minutes are worth more and Mid-Boss is easier to take between 20 and 30 minutes, which helps lower elo games too. [5:06]
- Parry now pauses reloading, and dashing into a parry cancels all momentum. Parrying while getting on a bounce pad sends you straight up like an elevator. [5:36]
- The parry nerf is good: parries were abusive in lane, punching was too risky (enemy parries and their team dives), and dashing away while parrying shut down melee attempts in close quarters. [6:06]
- Getting stunned now pauses your reload instead of restarting it; Seven's Static Charge no longer cancels your reload in lane, which HERESY links to Seven's long-running 54% win rate. [6:06]
- Breakables spawn later; not an enormous nerf to Rem, maybe more to Calico. [6:36]
- Slows now affect air drag, restricting air movement under any hero or item slow: a nerf to flyers, to projectile heroes like Celeste and to movement globally. [7:06]
- Movement slow is now a genuinely important stat; before, the better everyone was, the worse slow was. [7:06]
- Dashes and light melees no longer interrupt your gun cycle time, so Abrams and Silver can weave dashes and punches between shots and feel much more fluid in lane; it feels especially good on Silver, whose cycle time is worse than Abrams'. [7:37]
- These system changes usher in a tempo meta: carries and scalers are heavily nerfed relative to heroes that build early leads and snowball. [8:08]
- Warden is nerfed mainly by his direct nerfs; Celeste and Wraith are heavily nerfed because snowballing is more prevalent and they can't rely on comeback power farming. [8:39]
- Tempo heroes rise: those that win lane and mid game, capture center sacrifices, duel people and take early Walkers, and heroes with high flat numbers (big Paige shields, high damage ultimates unlocked early). [8:39]
- He expects Venator's 45% win rate to rise, compared to Warden's 55%. [9:09]
- Items: some shred item changes; Hollow Point buffed again and good, perhaps slightly under bought; Toxic Bullets a bit better; Shadow Weave buffed but probably still awful. [9:39]
- Cultist Sacrifice, Trophy Collector and Golden Goose Egg have their soul generation reduced, likely to keep economies in check now that tempo is buffed. [9:39]
- "Lustrous Enchantment" (caption spelling): its duration increase is significant, making it far more likely to fully stack off enemy players and more viable on Vindicta. [10:09]
- Spiritual Overflow is still viable but much worse, contributing to the death of the long-meta Mercurial Magnum plus Spiritual Overflow build. [10:09]
- Restorative Locket is still viable; the heal reduction is fine because people mostly buy it for the extra stamina, and without that stamina nobody would buy it at these numbers. [10:09]
- Veil Walker is nerfed but still very viable: lots of healing and movement speed with invisibility; still good on heroes with hard CC where pick potential matters, just not spam bought on every hero. [10:40]
- Fortitude got a slight buff, about the same. [10:40]
- Lifestrike: the extra melee heal makes it more viable early, but the big deal is the slow change; its 48% slow (after the slow nerf) now also hits air drag, making it a very good CC item, especially on Billy spamming slows. [11:10]
- Leech is still generally a terrible item, a little better now. [11:40]
- Plated Armor is fixed to block on-hit effects as its description says; it used to be one of the worst items, nearly worse than Bullet Resilience. Buy it against mixed gun and spirit damage heroes; it is now an anti-Vindicta, anti-Wraith, anti-Vyper item. [11:40]
- Diviner's Kevlar is a net change, maybe nice on Dynamo or even Paige. [12:11]
- Golden Goose Egg is killed: the tempo changes make it nearly unbuyable on their own, and it is also directly nerfed; the biggest nerf is that stored souls now count toward net worth. HERESY calls it by far the worst item in the game and unviable, not even situational. [12:11]
- Slowing Hex cooldown is nerfed, but it is a better item than before because its slow now affects air drag. [13:12]
- Radiant Regeneration healing tuned down a bit; good. [13:12]
- Tankbuster damage tuned down; good, still buy it for the same reasons. [13:12]
- Decay becomes more of an anti-heal item and less of a damage item; good, and it scales better into the late game when healing goes up. [13:43]
- Mercurial Magnum damage is severely tuned down and it won't be bought on so many heroes; it was only ever good on high base DPS heroes, always bad on Haze. [13:43]
- Warden probably stays viable, but his build is no longer Spiritual Overflow plus Mercurial Magnum; expect more traditional gun items. [14:14]
- Focus Lens damage and cast range increased, still a bad item because it is so cleansable; viable in niche situations. [14:14]
- Apollo has no changes but is one of the best heroes now: an early game monster who can gank, win lane, kill jungle instantly, escape death, with high flat damage and healing, and early tempo now matters. [14:46]
- Abrams: tweaked a little, same hero. [15:17]
- Celeste: nerfed about seven different ways; more important are the egg nerf, item nerfs, the scaling nerf (she usually loses the early game and is now punished more) and slows affecting air drag. [15:17]
- Graves: same hero. [15:47]
- Haze: gun Haze is better, especially with good aim, an appreciable damage increase since her damage comes from Fixation stacks; viability still to be seen. [15:47]
- Holliday: more of a gun hybrid with a Lasso buff; expect her back in the meta with hybrid gun and spirit builds, getting picks around 20 to 25 minutes now that post-20 respawns are longer. [16:17]
- Ivy: slight nerfs, good because she is overpowered. [16:17]
- Kelvin: Frost Shelter (caption: frozen shelter) finally has innate spirit power, so it may be usable late without maxing; worth testing a 1, 3, 2 build that doesn't max the ult, though you typically had to max it. [16:47]
- Lady Geist: slight buffs, and relatively stronger because heroes she competes with are nerfed. [16:47]
- Lash: gun Lash is almost completely dead in favor of Ground Strike (stomp) Lash; healthy for the game. [17:18]
- Paige is extremely buffed and one of the biggest winners: direct buffs, tempo mattering, and flying being nerfed (less gun Lash, less Celeste, Vindicta countered by heroes like Silver). Watch out for Paige. [17:18]
- Paradox: slight nerf to Kinetic Carbine quick scoping, same hero more or less. [18:19]
- Rem: bug fixes. [18:19]
- Shiv: knives no longer ricochet and instead do more current HP damage, giving more anti-tank ability, probably healthy; alt fire now actually damages heroes after being over-nerfed, not a huge deal. [18:19]
- Silver: big buff; Weighted Bola now acting like Phantom Strike is incredible, the cycle change makes her lane and mid game slapping and dashing feel better, and she has better scaling and sprint speed. Her abundant CC is more valuable now and she is re-emerging, picked in the Night Shift tournament. [18:49]
- Venator: his ultimate now works with Ricochet and Split Shot at the same time, a buff, but the main buff is tempo being up and super scaling being down; he is already a high tempo early game gun carry thanks to his ultimate and will be very good if he wins lane and bullies people off center sacrifices, while Wraith and Celeste struggle to compete. [19:20]
- Phinn (caption spelling, hero unclear): nerfed across the board, every ability did too much damage; same identity and reasons to pick, but no longer top damage while building half support items. [19:50]
- Vyper: slight nerf, same hero. [20:20]
- Warden: nerfed in several ways; the ult damage per boon hurts a lot (amplified by the Mercurial Magnum change), the Alchemical Flask range and speed reduction feels terrible, and Willpower's debuff resistance is reduced. With his items (Veil Walker, Mercurial Magnum, Spiritual Overflow) nerfed and carries down, he is more balanced, not gutted, and stays viable as long as he has his cage ult; expect neither 55% nor 45% win rate. [20:20]
- Wraith: nerfed as a scaler; her healing, armor shred and slow were brought down, her items nerfed slightly, and Plated Armor was buffed as a great anti-Wraith item. She will be much less oppressive mainly because of the tempo and scaling changes. [21:22]
- Yamato: buffed again and will be very good due to the tempo change; she is not picked in Night Shift mainly because players haven't practiced her while she was bad; expect a big uptick like Silver. [21:53]
- Summary: play tempo, steal enemy jungle camps, run the Urn, fight the rift when winning; AFK farming carries no longer take over the game. [22:23]
