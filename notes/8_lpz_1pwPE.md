# This Build Turns McGinnis Into an Artillery Cannon
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-06-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8_lpz_1pwPE

## Covers
McGinnis, played with a turret and ultimate (Heavy Barrage) focused build, by aquatiq.
Commentary over one full game, no coaching. Item picks and a few reasons are given along the way.
Patch of 2026-06-16.

## Claims
- The build is turret and ultimate focused, with turrets placed everywhere. [0:31]
- Buy Monster Rounds early in this build. [1:04]
- He held off a turret upgrade to buy his healing item first. [1:04]
- Enemies in lane die to the turrets; put up as many turrets as you can in a fight. [1:36]
- McGinnis basically plays the game for you. [2:07]
- Get the ultimate as soon as possible. [3:08]
- The wall is the speaker's favorite part of the kit. [3:39]
- Because the turrets do most of the damage in lane, buy Tesla Bullets in lane, or Toxic Bullets for the gun. [4:43]
- Tesla Bullets plus Healbane wins the lane. [7:49]
- With barely any gun investment the build still does a lot of damage; Tesla Bullets is a weapon item that deals spirit damage. [7:49]
- Mystic Slow is a good buy here, especially for the turrets. [9:54]
- Escalating Exposure makes the turrets do huge damage. [13:56]
- Pressure a different lane to pull enemies toward it. [14:28]
- Her full counter is an enemy standing right next to her and hitting her from behind. [15:29]
- Buy Spirit Resilience early when the enemy has heavy spirit damage (Infernus here). [15:59]
- McGinnis is a very forgiving hero. [17:01]
- Build a lineup of turrets to hold a lane; you can also drop turrets in a mid fight and leave. [17:31]
- Pressure every enemy wave so they spend their time going back and forth clearing waves instead of farming. [18:02]
- She needs Unstoppable later in the game. [18:35]
- An enemy with Ricochet counters her slightly. [19:06]
- Losing one walker to take another (yellow for green) is a fine trade. [20:39]
- Lightning Scroll is on the list of items to buy. [20:39]
- Her ultimate lasts a long time. [20:39]
- She moves slowly and cannot really escape when enemies walk up on her. [21:43]
- Heavy Barrage can be used on a walker. [22:46]
- Buy a duration item so the ultimate lasts as long as possible; with Unstoppable the enemy cannot do much to her while ulting. [24:49]
- The ultimate is very strong at mid boss, the enemy can do little to her there. [25:19]
- Use the wall to block one of the enemy's entrances at mid boss. [26:22]
- Spirit Burn is a later buy. [28:26]
- Pop Unstoppable before the ultimate so you do not get knocked down. [29:31]
- Late, he chose a cooldown item over Lightning Scroll as better for that moment. [31:02]
- In this game the turrets did about 90% of his damage. [32:02]
