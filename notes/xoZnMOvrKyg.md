# Win more Deadlock games - The Winning Trifecta (TM)
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-05-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoZnMOvrKyg

## Covers
How to turn a small or neutral lead after laning into a win: the "winning trifecta" layers (0-15 min guardians, stolen farm and first Urn; 15+ min second Urn or a walker plus a won fight; then a pick or won fight into Mid Boss and the base push), spiking between layers, and the "losing square" of distractions.
Shown on an Oracle-level coached game (Warden, Seven, Holliday, Paradox, Wraith). For players whose games drag on with no plan to end them.

## Claims
- This is not a comeback guide; it is how to grow a small lead after laning into a bigger lead and a win. [0:00]
- Games at lower skill take long only because the community doesn't know how to end them; the game is a lot easier to end. [0:00]
- Layer one, 0 to 15 minutes (mostly 10 to 15): take their guardians, steal their farm (Sinner's Sacrifice, boxes, jungle) and do the first Urn; if winning, do all three. That usually gives a 0 to 5,000 soul lead, sometimes up to 10,000 when really snowballing. [2:02]
- Before 15 minutes concentrate fully on layer one. [2:32]
- Once you have taken the guardians, stolen their farm and done the first Urn, relax: don't die at their walker, don't take pointless fights in their jungle, just push your waves and farm your jungle. [3:02]
- Layer two, from 15 minutes, for a 10 to 15k lead: either do the second Urn and win a fight, or take a walker and win a fight; these usually come with a fight, so win the fight and turn it into the walker or the Urn. [3:02]
- With a 5k lead you are stronger, so doing the Urn is usually the logical move: they fight you on it and you use your abilities. [3:33]
- Even if it is a comeback Urn for them, still pick it up and try to do it. [4:04]
- Between layers you want to spike, usually by buying items; before the 15 minute Urn spawn, spend your money. [4:04]
- Layer three, at 15+ minutes with a 10 to 15k lead: win one fight, get a pick, or have a significant advantage (for example all your ultimates against none of theirs, or a strong Mid Boss lineup), then do Mid Boss. [4:34]
- Strong Mid Boss heroes include Yamato, Dynamo, Abrams, Viscous and Warden. If your lineup is bad at Mid Boss you probably need to win a fight first; if it is good, a pick or even positional advantage with good lanes is enough to take Mid Boss. [5:04]
- With a 15 to 25k lead plus Mid Boss you win about 90% of the time; the game essentially plays itself. [5:04]
- Pushing the base is much easier now because the troopers in the enemy base get 40% resist, making them nearly impossible to kill, so you can usually end within a few minutes. [5:36]
- In the coached game, pushing the guardian with an ult while Holliday ran the Urn did two layer-one things at once, taking a 2k lead to about 4k. [6:38]
- Mistake: from 12 to 15 minutes the team tried to force walkers and died at them, giving up most or all of their lead. Dying at walkers before 15 minutes is very bad. [7:38]
- Pushing walkers before 15 minutes is the exception, not the rule; most players should follow the rule, not look for exceptions. [7:38]
- Warden popped his ult to take a walker (part of layer two) while Seven picked up the Urn. Running the walker there was a bit greedy; they should have focused on the Urn. [8:09]
- Mistake: Seven got distracted, dropped the Urn and popped his ult. Don't get excited; keep your eyes on the prize and stay on fundamentals. [8:39]
- After the Urn turn-in the team was up 11k (layer two reached); all they needed was to win a fight, then Mid Boss and end the game, not wait until 35 minutes. [9:09]
- Mistake: with the lead in hand they forced the mid walker and then the right walker, and got Black Holed and all died. Don't force walkers too hard; with the lead, look for a good fight. [9:40]
- A good fight is one on ground that is not advantageous for the enemy, such as any pick in their jungle; check whether your team has pick heroes (here Paradox) and play around them for a pick in their jungle or a lane, then force Mid Boss. [10:12]
- That lineup was strong at Mid Boss: Warden and Seven ults, Holliday to answer the enemy Black Hole, Mo & Krill to ult Kelvin or Dynamo; one pick in the jungle and they can Mid Boss together. [10:42]
- If teammates won't play for a pick, win an actual fight: get a pick, then go to a walker; if they defend it you should overwhelm them with the lead and numbers. [10:42]
- Mistake: after reaching layer two, the Warden took a side lane 1v1 with a Wraith, and when she ulted he stayed to fight instead of running, spending resources on a 1v1 he could never win. Look for the fight with your team instead: if you win mid you can Mid Boss even if you lose a side walker. [11:12]
- Past 20 minutes you can skip layer two: you don't need a lead, winning one good fight gives you the lead, then Mid Boss and the game should usually end. [12:43]
- After winning a fight and taking a walker, go Mid Boss as soon as possible; a roughly 13k lead plus Mid Boss is GG about 90% of the time. [13:13]
- Carry the Urn to the Mid Boss, take Mid Boss as a team, then deposit the Urn and look to end. [13:13]
- Push the base with all three lanes pushed. Warden ult works for pushing, but Deathy would keep it for when they are down in the base. [13:44]
- Troopers inside the enemy base are unbelievably tanky and deal a lot of damage to the Patron. [13:44]
- Kill the guardians in front so your troopers walk into the base while it is transforming; you have about 10 seconds to get your troopers in, and a big wave in the base makes the game very easy to end. [14:14]
- Following these principles raises win rate even for Eternus players, many of whom don't follow them. [15:15]
- Losing square, one: hyperfocusing on builds. Builds matter, but when following a build it is rarely why you lose (for example Opening Rounds versus Mystic Shot at 13:15 doesn't matter unless you are Eternus 6). Brand new players should follow builds. [15:45]
- Losing square, two: random fights in the jungle around 12 minutes with zero advantage; it is fine not to show up and to push lanes instead. Pro teams take good jungle fights, but that is past the fundamentals. [16:16]
- Losing square, three: pushing the walker before 15 minutes; it is bait most of the time. With a big wave and confidence they can't defend, a walker is a huge advantage, but this is where people usually lose a 4k lead, getting Lashed onto the walker. [16:46]
- Walkers are tanky to heroes before 15 minutes, and still somewhat tanky at 15, but never tanky to minions, so a big wave can kill one even though you yourself do little damage to it. [17:17]
- Losing square, four: a teammate (the Wraith "Timmy") begging for help in the worst fight ever, unrelated to the Urn, a walker, a guardian, stealing farm or a power spike; ignore them. [17:48]
- In about 15 community games reviewed on stream, nearly every game that ended under 30 minutes followed this structure; longer games had more back and forth and throws. [18:48]
