# How to Build and Play Victor | Arcanist Victor Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-09-01

## Covers
Midknight (former top 10 Mirage, free coach) reviews an Arcanist-rank Victor laning with a Lash into a Drifter and Billy, then walks through ABL's Victor build.
Coaching review. Themes: Aura of Suffering (3) usage and ramping damage, Jumpstart (2) timing, raw spirit items, melee and parry habits with a parry training drill, laning into Drifter, boxes and soul min-maxing, running the Urn, splitting and map awareness.
Recorded early September 2025, shortly after Victor's release.

## Claims
Player 1 (Victor, Arcanist):
- Starting Pain Battery (1) is good: you farm up its damage from the poke you take, though his early regen was nerfed so you get less value. [0:30]
- Victor has an easy time confirming souls, so you are in little danger of losing the soul duel; melee into the enemy's face is not worth it. [0:30]
- If you rush Extra Charge, play aggressively and below about 50% HP, since the 2 gives much more value the lower your health. [1:01]
- Do not auto-queue your build at the start of a game; think about every item when you buy it. [2:03]
- Before you have spirit resist the 3 is bad to use: you trade nearly equal HP with yourself. Use it only in dire situations or to finish someone, and turn it off the second they die. [3:36]
- Around 30% spirit resist the 3 starts getting much better, because your own spirit resist applies to its self damage. [4:07]
- Raw spirit is very good on Victor: more damage on the 1 and 3 and more healing on the 2, the best offense and defense at once. [7:43]
- Have the 3 up when you are significantly healthier than the enemy and know they will recast onto you. [7:43]
- Torment Pulse is a mistake item on Victor: raw spirit power gives comparable damage on the 3 plus extra healing on the 2. [8:15]
- Use Cold Front at the very least when you go in. [8:47]
- With ult up, do not take free trades and die; without kill potential there is no reason, because the res takes a few seconds and they can space you or kill your teammate. [9:18]
- Do not try to 1v1 the Drifter after you revive at low HP; with no spirit resist and no usable 3 you cannot win it at this stage. [10:50]
- Stop meleeing the Drifter: he parries off audio, so only light melee him from behind. [3:36]
- Drifter's biggest weakness is getting spaced in lane: your 1 outranges his Rend, so avoid the close gun-damage part of Rend and space him. [5:09]
- When Drifter hits his 2 on you, do not stand on your tower; stand just far enough away that if he teleports to you he cannot parry the tower. [13:52]
- Turn on the 3 the second the Drifter teleports onto you. [13:22]
- React to bridge buffs whenever the wave is shoved and you have a window. [9:48]
- When the lane is shoved and you have no kill potential under tower, do boxes or a camp instead of overextending; min-max your souls. [11:20]
- Only siege a tower when you can do a lot of damage to it or have kill potential under it. [12:21]
- Enduring Speed is a good buy: movement speed keeps enemies in the aura. [14:23]
- Do not die for your teammates' mistakes; back out of fights you do not want. [14:23]
- Scourge is solid (40% spirit resist active for 10 seconds) but not a rush item; regular spirit resist like Spirit Resilience comes first. [14:53]
- Spirit Resilience plus Rapid Recharge lets you live almost infinitely below 25% HP. [14:53]
- When farming with the 3, focus the big creep and make the creeps die at the same time; spreading it out wastes time and costs extra self damage. [15:59]
- Do not use the 2 for marginal healing (about 80 HP on a 17.4 second cooldown) while waiting on a charge; use it when you are at max charges or low, because it heals more when you are low. [17:01]
- Use the 2 to cleanse anti-heal; with three cleanses in his kit he is very hard to anti-heal, unlike Abrams whom you can answer with Decay. Healbane is the best anti-heal into him even at only 35%. [17:33]
- Victor scales: weak early, but at 30 to 40 minutes with 50 to 60% spirit resist and a full HP res he becomes unkillable. [19:35]
- Do not run the Urn when all your waves are negative and the enemy is pushing; ideally your lanes sit near your tower with two teammates about to shove them. [18:33]
- When you are boxing, also farm the nearby camps. [20:38]
- Shove the wave and pressure a lane instead of walking to a fight you cannot reach; worst case you trade Walkers. [21:39]
- Do not cancel the 3 when someone leaves its radius: it starts at about 11 damage a tick and has to stack, so canceling loses the ramp. [22:40]
- The aura damage ramps on you too, but with Scourge (40%) plus Enchanter's Emblem and Cold Front (another 20%) your spirit resist is around 52%, so the ramp matters much more on enemies. [23:10]
- He turned the 3 off to chase, which was bad; keeping it on with Cold Front might have killed. [23:40]
- Using the 2 just for movement speed to chase is fine. [24:11]
- With a small soul and kill advantage, run the Urn; your ult should be back before the next team fight. [25:45]
- Walkers and all objectives have no melee resist: bait the stomp, melee it, walk out when it stomps again. Higher elo players add a melee to Guardians after parrying them. [26:15]
- Do not turn off the 3 between camps; keep it ramped, farm the next camp and pop Infuser if low. [26:45]
- Push the Walker instead of rotating back to a fight: if your team wins you can run the Urn, if it loses you still get a Walker. [28:18]
- Healing Tempo is not worth it, especially before Rapid Recharge, Boundless Spirit or Spirit Resilience; Ethereal Shift outclasses it. [28:18]
- Do not use confirm cast; instant cast is quicker. [30:20]
- Either rotate to a teammate's 1v1 sooner or not at all; assume an enemy forcing a 1v1 has backup nearby. [30:50]
- With a three-kill advantage and the mid wave crashing, go mid or push the Walker instead of the fight. [31:22]
- Upgrading Cold Front to Arctic Blast is for when you have no slots; at this point he could have sold Enchanter's Emblem instead. [33:26]
- A melee punch cancels momentum abilities, like a Bebop uppercut. [33:26]
- High elo Victors turn on the 3 a few seconds before the fight; keep it up, use Arctic Blast to pin the target, and use the 2 to get on top of them. [33:56]
- Better than six going mid: four push and take Patron while Mina ults the mid boss with one other to secure Rejuvenator, or push every lane first so they cannot contest mid. [36:29]
- After Rejuvenator, siege with your team; farming is bad. [37:29]
- Boundless Spirit is a great item on Victor but came out too late: lots of regen, lots of healing on the 2, damage on the 1. [37:29]
- When inbound and losing, press 2 and hold W to line out, using air dashes if you have them. [38:32]
- Against gun heroes he needed bullet resist like Bullet Resilience sooner; Plated Armor is only okay into Mirage and not great into Holliday and Billy. [38:32]
- Build fixes: bullet resist sooner, definitely Spirit Resilience, cut Healing Tempo, Arctic Blast a little later, sell Enchanter's Emblem for Juggernaut; Torment Pulse can be cut for more resists. [40:04]
- Do not use the 2 for jump-starting at full HP; use it when lower. [41:07]
- Radiant Regen is a lower-level item: rushing Boundless Spirit is better for defense, and Radiant Regen does not get its flat 70 heal off the 3. [41:37]

ABL's recommended build (code in the video description):
- Standard start, rushing Enchanter's Emblem before Cold Front, Extra Regen before Extra Charge, and Extra Spirit early for spirit power. [42:39]
- Rush Rapid Recharge, then Healing Booster for healing, then mobility so you can keep your 3 on enemies. [43:10]
- Spirit Snatch is great: parrying Victor is risky because a whiffed parry locks you for about 1.5 seconds inside his aura, so people avoid parrying and his light melees feed it. [43:10]
- Spirit Lifesteal and Spirit Resilience (30% conditional) are core; they let you play for your 2s at low HP. [43:40]
- Infuser: when you get low, pop it and you are immortal. [44:11]
- Warp Stone to stay on the enemy; Counterspell for defense into heroes like Shiv. [44:11]
- Escalating Exposure (best guess at a garbled caption) gives spirit resist and amped damage. [44:11]
- Ethereal Shift is optional and goes after Boundless Spirit: use it with your 3 at max ramp to stall without taking damage and force enemies to space. [44:11]
- Boundless Spirit and Arctic Blast are good late game items for keeping enemies in the aura. [44:42]
- Situational: Plated Armor; Phantom Strike to reach flying heroes like Vindicta; Spell Breaker into heavy spirit like Grey Talon, Lash or Paradox; Juggernaut when bullets poke you but Plated Armor is not justified. [44:42]

Summary notes:
- Train parry: in the practice range gym, parry the meleeing bot, then turn around and parry off audio, then parry to melee sounds while a stream or video plays in the background. [45:45]
- Stop over-meleeing: once an enemy raw parries you, fake melee or look for light melees only. [46:15]
- In lane against Drifter, space him; his laning stage is his weakest point. [47:17]
- Before every team fight, check the map: split or defend an objective with a crashing wave if that matters more. [47:50]
- Run the Urn on small advantages when it is neutral: one kill with a 30 second respawn, key enemy ults down, or a small soul lead with two teammates about to shove waves into Walkers. [48:21]

General advice:
- Lane differences between ranks are mostly melee and parry: early ranks over-melee, Archon learns to parry and stops meleeing, Oracle and Phantom start fake meleeing again; freezing waves starts around Phantom. [6:09]
- In low elo, shouting "hit the Patron" on mic hugely raises your team's chance of ending. [39:03]
- Know how long your comp takes to break a base and whether you have time to end. [40:35]
- Sinclair can steal your ult. [35:27]
