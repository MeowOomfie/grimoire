# How to Build & Play Solomon | Coaching a Phantom Solomon
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-10-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfYVhDHtKPg

## Covers
Solomon coaching session by Midknight over a Phantom-rank player's game (lane with an Infernus against Silver and Rat King).
Covers itemization, aggressive laning, playing for a fed teammate, routing and farming, height positioning and ability usage (Bishop, Knight/King shield, Rooks, Queen).
Coaching, patch of 2026-10-16.

## Claims
- Good Solomon pattern: when you back up and your move is off cooldown, pull your Bishop back so you can reset, then back. [0:30]
- When you put spirit into the Bishop you have to replace it to actually get the value. [0:30]
- He prefers Extra Spirit first; the high velocity burst item is not bad at that point, it is preference. [1:01]
- Using your 1 to clear the wave is great. [1:01]
- Whether your second ability point goes into 2 or 3 depends on how the game feels. [1:01]
- With a lot of lane pressure and no plan to dive, the shield (2) is not very meaningful; take the root (Rooks) for more harass, which pairs really well with an Infernus. [1:31]
- The shield feels good if you are getting real trades or laning with a melee hero; the only guaranteed value would come from diving, which you do not want to do. [1:31]
- Kick the grenade away from your wave when you get the chance; it slows the enemy's clear. [2:01]
- He does not like Mystic Burst on Solomon: the Bishop move is on about a 3 second cooldown and hits for around 100 damage, so Extra Spirit is better, and spirit also scales his bullet damage a little (about 1.4 per bullet). [2:32]
- Be careful slow-playing a lane you should win: if it turns into a stall where everyone boxes, the enemy wins because they outscale you. [3:03]
- Matchup read: Infernus and Silver are tempo heroes, Rat King outscales Solomon, Solomon is a better laner than Silver and Infernus is better than Rat King, so your lane should win early. [3:33]
- You were accidentally grabbing your Infernus a lot; be more deliberate with your moves. [4:08]
- Playing in the air helps when using the low grab. [4:08]
- He does not like Long Range or Sharpshooter: Solomon's gun does a lot early (why people buy a gun spike first) but its base damage scales poorly with boons, so flat weapon damage and range feel great for about 15 minutes, then feel bad by 15 to 20 minutes. [4:08]
- You can start speeding up your wave clear; good micro on the 1. [5:09]
- Earlier coached Solomons were not microing their 1s enough; on an aggressive Bishop placement, pull it back a little and rotate. [5:39]
- Perma boxing feels bad in a lane you should win; stalling is not great, though boxing for a Long Range spike is acceptable. [5:39]
- Do not buy Titanic Magazine; buy Kinetic Dash instead, which gives ammo, a lot more fire rate and extra stamina, and your weapon damage falls off anyway. [5:39]
- He is the biggest Kinetic Dash hater (and hates Arcane Surge), so if he recommends Kinetic Dash for a hero you should buy it. [6:10]
- Use your 2 shield while farming Sinner's Sacrifice; you took 320 damage there that the 2 would have fully blocked. [6:45]
- If you do not have your 2, it is worth using Grit to farm a Sinner's Sacrifice. [6:45]
- After winning a fight you should pressure their Sinner's Sacrifices; once your Infernus loses 34 spirit power you no longer win a fight, so chill out. [6:45]
- Use your ult to win the fight: the second the enemy presses 4, you press 4 and stun her so she cannot get high value; sitting on the ult too long cost a lot. [7:16]
- The last hit, where the polymorph marks you, does the most damage; dodge that one rather than the minor hits. [7:16]
- Baiting at the Sinner's Sacrifice is good: start hitting it, the enemies will run at you, then recast your Queen; recasting the Queen early shows your hand. [7:46]
- The biggest issue in that fight was overholding your abilities. [8:17]
- He now likes a spirit cooldown rush over rushing Greater Expansion for the spirit spike, because cooldown reduces the time between rounds of abilities. [8:17]
- Running into the Silver's face and wasting the recast on your 2 lets her get on you and removes your ability to stall and survive the dive longer. [8:17]
- You were playing the back of the fight on the ground; Solomon needs height, so come into fights from up high. [8:48]
- Treat Solomon like a Lash: on that Rift he always jumps up to the crane and does everything from there while they just watch; be in the air controlling the fight. [8:48]
- If they can commit to you on the ground with a lasso it can be really scary. [8:48]
- Aim for about 25 to 30% of your damage from both Bishop damage types combined. [9:18]
- Side laning feels fine on Solomon. [9:53]
- For movement buy Extra Stamina or Warp Stone; stamina is the easiest to pilot and most reliable, Warp Stone is not bad at all. [10:53]
- After your team gets kills, enemies very often try to trade kills back somewhere else on the map, so watch for that. [10:53]
- Use your stamina more to roll the rats off. [10:53]
- Fortitude or Warp Stone and Dispel Magic are options there, though his new-hero takes are very opinionated. [11:23]
- Being the one to split push on Solomon does not feel good; his split potential is not M1-hero good. [11:23]
- Call your team off the Rift when your 30k-soul Haze is not in the team fight; there is no way that fight goes well. [11:23]
- Be careful tanking the fireball (captioned "locker fireball"). [11:54]
- Dying for the Walker there is correct, but killing it frees the Silver to chase; if you leave it at about 10% and run, she has to clear the wave or lose the Walker. [11:54]
- Tankbuster is good on him, especially against a comp of Rat King, Silver and Baba with healing. [12:25]
- Using your 2 as a shield for farming, especially on Sinner's Sacrifices, was a common miss across all the Solomons he coached that day. [13:29]
- After the fight win with a mid boss opportunity, you should not have continued into their base; go mid instead, and without a wave you cannot take base guardians. [13:59]
- With that many souls just buy; your lack of damage is coming from not having damage items. [14:30]
- Against a comeback Rift with Rat King, do not fight in it; give them the Rift and kill them all after, since they have 40 to 60% resistances in it and you save your Rejuvenators. [15:00]
- On this Rift the tree is a great place for Solomon to play; position like a Lash. [15:31]
- Giving up that Rift and killing them after would have had them patron-knocked with the game ending at 30 minutes; fighting it slowed the game by five minutes. [16:31]
- Do not run boxes when there are camps on the map; with pressure you should be stealing enemy farm, especially on the right side, otherwise secure your own. [17:07]
- He would want Magic Carpet there but you cannot buy it yet without expansion and spirit cooldown. [17:07]
- Divine Barrier rush when your Haze is fed, and hard funnel her. [17:42]
- You slow yourself by backing far off camps for information; on an open camp, farm and move to the left to watch the hallway where you would be contested. [17:42]
- Magic Carpet is really good on him and is his own first tier 4; he is unsure if it is a crutch but it feels good. [18:13]
- If the enemy turtles forever your team wins; the way you lose is by making a play. [18:48]
- Baiting mid boss is good, do not force it; forcing was not bad there given their map positions and your information. [18:48]
- Not jumping into the pit was good. [18:48]
- Ethereal Shift is not bad in theory but is glitched on him: entering it breaks your pieces (not patched as of the day before). [19:50]
- With a hyperfed teammate you have to play for him more; funnel the Haze, and your Divine Barrier should never go on anyone besides the Haze. [20:20]
- The combo of Rook into ult is really important: the root lets you land the full ult and stun. [20:50]
- Itemization summary: too many greens, too much gun, not enough utility, and do not sleep on Magic Carpet. [21:20]
- Your damage was very low because of the build and missing the last fights, but Bishops at 27 to 28% of damage was right where he says to aim. [21:51]
- Lane note: do not AFK in a lane you should win or on strong laner heroes; play aggressive. [22:22]
- Mid game note: farm more; side laning was okay (Lash is the better side laner for that comp) but too much time went to boxing instead of hitting camps. [22:22]
- Play for your hyperfed teammate; some fights were taken without the Haze, which was really scary. [22:54]
- Route out to heights; you got caught by the Silver at the left Walker without routing out to height. [22:54]
- Builds: his own build centers more around Magic Carpet; Lightbringer's build is the everything-on-the-screen option; Osis is a good simple left-to-right build, but its ability order needs two points in Bishop earlier. [23:24]
