# Ranking Every Hero In Deadlock From Easiest To Hardest
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-09-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmcxEQ38Uto

## Covers
Metro Deadlock ranks every hero by how easy it is to pick up (not to master), on the patch live 2025-09-01, after the six new heroes.
Tiers used: very easy, easy, mid, hard, very hard. Gives each hero's placement and the reason.
For new or returning players choosing a first hero, or players picking a hard hero to learn.

## Claims
- The list rates how easy a hero is to learn on a base level for a new or returning player, not how hard it is to master; every hero has its own skill ceiling. [0:31]
- Abrams is placed among the easiest heroes: very tanky and forgiving, a passive that just makes him tankier, and not aim intensive, so you can play him like a melee hero. [1:02]
- On Abrams you mainly need to learn Shoulder Charge, buffering a heavy melee after the charge to guarantee it at level two, and using the aura when close to the enemy. One of the easiest tanks to get into. [1:33]
- Bebop is mid: sticky bomb, Uppercut and the laser ult are easy to land, but there is nuance in when to ult, and the hook is a projectile harder to hit than most abilities. [1:33]
- Bebop stays no higher than mid because he is tanky and brawls well; caught in melee range out of position he can still Uppercut and bomb for value. [2:33]
- Billy is hard (demoted from very hard): deceptively difficult because he is a gun hero, not a melee hero. [2:33]
- On Billy you never want to heavy melee; light melees are faster and let you keep moving at the same speed, so shoot constantly and weave in light melees mainly to reload. [3:04]
- Many players play Billy too aggressively, melee too much, get parried over and over and die. [3:36]
- Billy's toolkit is fairly easy to understand at first, but he is one of the hardest heroes to master and, among the new heroes, the second hardest to play after Mina; very rewarding once you learn his game flow. [3:36]
- Calico is mid: her toolkit is straightforward, but her main burst comes from leaping in and hitting Gloom Bombs, which are not trivial to hit (Arctic Blast freezes targets to help). [4:06]
- Calico's ult is deceptively hard: use it too early with no escape and fail the burst and you die easily. [4:37]
- Calico's roaming needs decent map knowledge, such as where the boxes are. [4:37]
- Drifter is not put in hard only because he is still overtuned and does very high damage after multiple nerfs (placed mid). [5:08]
- Drifter wants isolated fights, but Deadlock is a team fighting game and especially in lower ranks people stack in fours, fives and sixes, so isolated targets are hard to find and his early damage without them is weak. [5:41]
- Drifter can still AFK farm, come out of the jungle rich and 1v6; he is a hard carry who can roll the lobby with a lot of money. [5:41]
- Dynamo is easy: Kinetic Pulse and his self-healing are easy; the only high skill ceiling ability is the teleport, which can save teammates from abilities. At a base level you walk in and Singularity the enemy team. [6:43]
- Grey Talon is hard: a very different, very squishy playstyle; new players who stand at the same range as other heroes die easily, so stay in the fight hitting arrows but out of the front line. [7:15]
- Grey Talon's skill shots need base practice, and his owl ult takes thought about when and on whom to use it. [7:46]
- Haze is very easy, the easiest hero to learn if you come from shooters like Overwatch or Apex: a medium range aim hero whose damage scales with hits, where you only need to hit Sleep Dagger and the ult needs no aim. [8:19]
- The top tier is renamed from "close your eyes and win" to very easy, because you still have to play the game. [8:49]
- New player Haze flow: Sleep Dagger to start the fight, then shoot and press 4; Smoke Bomb to walk around until you see someone. Maxed invisibility can dodge things later. [10:21]
- Holliday is very hard, one of the hardest for a new player: she needs very good aim, Cassidy style headshot value with a slow fire rate in lane, and every ability needs a lot of practice. [8:49]
- On Holliday it takes practice to hit barrels, more to learn jump pads, and more to combine them, including using jump pads to escape; her ult is a skill shot. [9:20]
- Infernus is easy, not quite Haze level: knowing when and where to Flame Dash is not trivial and he has more active abilities, but with aim and a gun build you get value easily. [9:20]
- Infernus needs more game knowledge than Haze to know when to use spells; Metro struggled on him when very new while Haze was easy. [9:50]
- Ivy is hard: Kudzu Bomb on waves is simple, but when to trigger the healing tether with teammates and whether to use the ult to go in and silence everyone or save it to escape (for example after pushing a Walker) are choices to learn. [10:51]
- Kelvin is very easy: more active abilities than Haze, but they are easy to use; Arctic Beam cannot be missed up close and freezes the enemy. [11:53]
- At a basic level, when low in a 1v1 Kelvin presses his ult and will most likely win because it heals so much; it can also dodge things like a Lash ult. [11:53]
- Kelvin's snowball (Frost Grenade) is not hard to hit, Ice Path makes escapes easy, his gun does a lot of damage, and he is very tanky, so he is rarely punished for mistakes. [12:23]
- Lady Geist is low mid, almost easy: very forgiving, since Life Drain is auto aim that heals you and damages the enemy as long as you stay in line of sight, and dashing around makes you hard to kill. [12:23]
- On Lady Geist, when low hide behind a corner and press 4 (Soul Exchange); Malice is spam, Life Drain auto aims, swap is simple, and a leveled Essence Bomb with items is an easy AoE nuke. [12:54]
- Lash is hard: without good movement you do little; higher Ground Strike slams do more damage, his escapes rely on movement, and many things cancel his ult. [13:27]
- On Lash you need map knowledge about where to engage; fighting inside he does nothing, which most other heroes on the list do not suffer from. Grey Talon has the same issue. [14:28]
- McGinnis is easy: pretty weak right now, but a straightforward Torbjorn-like kit: throw turrets, place the healing pad when low, then shoot. [14:28]
- McGinnis is rated slightly harder than Kelvin and Haze because full value is harder: as a support you think about more things, and as a spirit nuke you need decent positioning with the ultimate. [14:59]
- Mina is very hard, by far the hardest hero in the game: on paper the kit is simple, but she is a burst hero who must hit bullets fast to proc her 3, get into melee range as the squishiest hero in the game, land her 1, and still teleport out. [15:29]
- Mina's value is like Pocket's: bait enemy abilities and dodge them frame perfect with the teleport, ideally two abilities with two teleports; with 3 seconds between teleports she has huge potential, such as teleporting in, nuking and teleporting out, but only with a plan. [16:32]
- Without a plan, Mina teleports in with no escape route and dies instantly; she is "Pocket on crack", since Pocket's abilities are nearly unmissable in mid game and Barrage is easy to set up with Majestic Leap. [17:02]
- Mina's difficulty shows in her win rate: low even in high rank, and she keeps being buffed though she is not technically weak, just very hard. [17:34]
- Mirage is easy: his passive stacks damage the longer you shoot, so stay mid range and shoot, finish or escape with Tornado, and throw Scarabs whenever you can; hitting one or two in the cast window is enough. [17:34]
- Mirage's only complication is the teleport ult, which has huge potential because it crosses the whole map, letting you split push and then join a fight. [18:05]
- Mo & Krill is very easy, probably the easiest hero in the game: spam Scorn on creeps and enemies to heal, the ult is just walking up to someone, Burrow takes no damage to escape, and Sand Blast stops multiple shooters; he is giga tanky. Recommended for tank players from other games. [18:36]
- Paige is hard: her main playstyle is support; her 1 needs you to predict where the enemy will be, her 3 is not trivial to hit but gives huge value, and her global ult allows trick shots into other lanes on a very long cooldown. [19:36]
- Paige's kit seems simple but the abilities are hard to hit, and as a support she will not carry; shielding teammates feels useless if they do no damage. [20:06]
- Paradox is on the harder side (hard) but easier than Holliday: an initiator who can swap to start fights, with an easy 1v1 combo of wall, grenade and Kinetic Carbine; the carbine needs aim but has a forgiving hitbox. You can pick her up and do well fairly fast. [20:36]
- Pocket is hard to pick up: a unique toolkit with combo decisions like satchel then teleport out, or teleport out then satchel where you want to burst, and misused abilities get the squishy Pocket killed. [21:07]
- Once learned, Pocket plays himself and is easier to master than most heroes: Majestic Leap into Barrage, press 4, satchel out; hitting multiple people with Affliction in a team fight does your job. [21:38]
- Seven is easy, at the bottom of easy: stun then ball, Power Surge is basically an always active passive, and press 4 when in danger. He is below Kelvin and Mo & Krill because he is squishy and weaker in lane and can die easily there. [22:38]
- Ult Seven is very easy, but it is not very valuable anymore after nerfs; Power Surge Seven is by far better. [23:08]
- Shiv is lower hard, harder than other tanks: there is movement tech with his right click, the knives are not trivial to hit and ideally you delay them to stack bleed, and after his changes you must use Bloodletting at the right time to negate damage. His ult is simple: press 4 when they are low. [23:39]
- Sinclair is very hard, probably the second hardest hero: his ult has huge potential but you must know when, where and from whom to steal. [24:10]
- Sinclair's Rabbit Hex is weaker now and can backfire, since a hexed rabbit runs very fast and can escape; Metro has escaped this way himself. [24:40]
- Sinclair's bolt wants a long travel time for more damage, and his 2 forces a choice: teleport aggressively after the enemy, keep it up for damage, or save it to escape. [25:12]
- The Doorman is hard to learn but incredibly hard to master: the bells are easy to hit and the hitboxes are big, but his ult can backfire, letting an enemy's teammates save them or letting a low target heal in the hotel. [25:12]
- At a basic level, The Doorman places a door backwards, jumps through when dived and closes it behind him. [26:12]
- Victor is easy, not very easy: you can kill yourself by keeping his 3 on too long or run out of 2 charges by spamming them early, so he is easy to feed on, but his 1 is easy to hit, the 2 is a self heal, and he is very survivable. [26:42]
- Vindicta is lower mid: like Grey Talon she must stay away, but when jumped she can Stake and fly away instantly; you place one massive unmissable AoE, then another, then an unmissable Crow, debuff the enemy and stay away. [27:12]
- Viscous is very hard, probably the hardest support: ball movement tech and control are hard, The Cube has huge potential with choices of whom and when to save, Puddle Punch can be used to escape or aggressively, and even Splatter can bounce for extra damage. [28:16]
- Vyper is mid: as a new player her required movement is not trivial, and knockups, knockdowns and stuns hard counter her; she is not aim intensive because of her big spread, so slide and shoot. [28:47]
- Vyper's ult can save an enemy, because targets cannot take damage from most things during it, so she can screw over her team. [29:17]
- Warden is easy: super tanky with the best ult in the game. Play ult Warden: learn Majestic Leap tech, jump in and ult when you have Lightning Scroll, no aim needed since the ult heals you and kills them. [29:47]
- Warden is very forgiving to start: Alchemical Flask in lane is easy to hit and he has a lot of base regen. [29:47]
- Wraith is mid, almost hard (Metro's main): the cards are less consistent than most abilities and her teleport is less consistent too; mess up the teleport and you are instantly dead. He would rather have low ranked teammates on other heroes than Wraith. [30:18]
- Yamato is lower hard: very strong in lane and her 1 is easy to hit, but her 2 (the grapple) has movement tech such as canceling, sliding past the enemy and choosing whether to melee on arrival. [31:22]
- Yamato's ult is simple, but players still fail to use it in time when low and die; her 3 is not aim intensive but can carry fights. [31:22]
