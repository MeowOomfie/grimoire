# Silver is Broken Again...
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-09-19
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3e8m-Utr3M

## Covers
Silver, aquatiq, commentary over one of his own games on the day of a patch that buffed her.
He reads out Silver's patch changes, tries a build copied from a Silver one-trick and gives his verdict on how strong she is now. Patch of 2026-09-19.

## Claims
Patch changes to Silver:
- Her reload no longer pauses or gets interrupted during melees or dashes. [0:31]
- Her sprint speed was changed and she is way faster; he reads it as going from 1.5 to 2.5. [3:04]
- Weighted Bola now increases gravity on the target. [3:04]
- Weighted Bola can interrupt flying abilities. [3:04]
- Boot Kick and Entangling Bola got more restricted targeting. [3:34]
- There were global movement changes for every hero, and they benefit Silver dramatically. [3:34]
- Her health per boon was also changed. [0:31]
- Her pellets seem to fly in more of a straight line than before. [7:12]
- Bolas no longer seem to do damage to towers; he tested it and it did not hit. [8:53]

Build and items:
- Restorative Shot is a definite early buy on her. [1:02]
- He used a build copied from a Silver one-trick. [1:02]
- Berserker is good on her. [2:02]
- Phantom Strike is really good into an enemy team with flyers (Holliday) and Abrams, because of his ult. [2:02]
- He bought Stalker and found it very nice in that game. [2:34]
- Her bullets are slow compared with fast-bullet heroes like Venator. [2:34]
- With her extra sprint speed, Trophy Collector is worth considering; afterwards he said he should have bought it (8 kills, 20 assists). [3:04]
- He bought Slowing Bullets even though he thought of it as a bad item, and Slowing Hex, which paid off in a fight. [5:08]
- He considered Reactive Barrier and plated armor later in the game. [11:59]
- He was unsure between Tank Buster and Berserker as a buy; afterwards he thought Tank Buster may have been right that game. [15:35]
- Veil Walker was briefly locked in the shop during the game and could not be bought; it later became buyable again. [14:00]
- Warden can still disable your sprint speed. [5:08]

Other patch notes he mentions:
- Venator's ult can now split shot and ricochet, so Venator players buy Ricochet for the ult. [14:00]
- The comeback system and the Rift were changed a lot this patch. [6:11]

Verdict:
- He thought Silver was awful for a long time before this patch, but now she feels very strong and fast. [9:54]
- Silver is strong again, "freaking OP". [23:18]
