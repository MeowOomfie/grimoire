## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (patch of early September 2026) with four sessions: a Graves (Seeker), a Celeste (Ascendant 6), a second Graves (Phantom) and a Celeste (Sentinel, a former Holliday one-trick).
Themes: why spirit Graves beats gun Graves, root and gravestone placement, Restorative Shot when losing lane, respecting execute lanes, comeback souls, Rift and urn macro, playing to scale on Celeste, mental, soul awareness, dead time and Rejuvenator usage.
Side notes on the meta and tech: Street Brawl for parry practice, Split Shot, Rebuttal, a damage amp stacking test, Spirit Rend, dash hops versus short hops, and a tier-list rant (Celeste, Warden, Pocket on top, Graves at the bottom).
