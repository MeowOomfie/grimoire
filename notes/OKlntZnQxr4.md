# You're Itemizing Wrong | Oracle Warden Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-06-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKlntZnQxr4

## Covers
Warden, VOD review coaching by Midknight of an Oracle 5 Warden on a flask (Alchemical Flask) build, laning into Paradox and Kelvin. Mid-2025 video, patch around June 2025, shortly after Warden's Willpower became a full barrier.
Themes: last hitting safely with heavy melee, cover and bridge control, resetting and defensive itemization at low HP, the faster mid lane roll out, leveling abilities with hotkeys, not fighting Kelvin's beam, baiting with Willpower, boxes, Urn calls, ult timing, and the flask build's item gaps (Superior Cooldown, base gun damage, Fortitude, Slowing Hex).

## Claims
Player 1 (Warden, Oracle 5):
- Walking up slowly to light melee a minion risks a lot of poke; from behind cover just heavy melee to get the last hit. Melee-ing the second minion was risky too, walk back. [0:00]
- That cover is only good for shooting minions; if the enemy has bridge control you get shot in the head with little counterplay. Don't stand behind it post wave. [1:04]
- Going to the sign at low HP was ego; walking out the other way was much safer. [1:34]
- At very low HP, full reset and buy healing. Buying an offensive item instead of resetting just gets you killed, especially into Paradox and Kelvin, who both have abilities that burst you from under 100. At minimum buy defensive, like Extra Regen or Restorative Shot. [1:34]
- Faster mid lane roll out: jump off the zipline, diagonal air dash, double jump, land and grab the zip. Always do it on mid lane, even on first spawn for practice. On Dynamo it is great when they siege the Walker because you come in behind them with ult. [2:37]
- Don't click to level abilities; use the tab or controller binds so you can level an ability mid-fight and use it, here giving more HP from your shield. [3:38]
- Throw a flask when the enemy is in range; you needed to drop a flask there. [3:38]
- Never fight Kelvin while his beam is up; you don't win a DPS fight against the beam, even if he is maxing grenade instead of beam. With three stamina bars, just roll backwards. [4:09]
- Buying Quicksilver Reload under 100 HP is troll; keep itemizing defensively when low. [4:09]
- Itemization is the biggest weakness this game; Greater Expansion now is troll. Buy Extra Regen and you would be in a much better spot. [5:09]
- When pushing in, don't pop Willpower before going in. Bait that you are one HP so they walk up, then pop the shield. Since the patch Warden's barrier is fight turning, and many players have not adjusted. [5:39]
- That box out was bad; you lose minions for it. Boxing out is good when Healing Rite is running, so you are not wasting the regen. Statistically you throw about three minions. [6:11]
- Ult there; they cannot burst you down while you are in Last Stand, and with ult you kill both, even through Kelvin's dome. [6:42]
- Good that you box route, above this elo; Oracle players undervalue boxes. But be at this wave, or you lose tower damage. [7:13]
- Always slide after you roll. Chase Kelvin up his Ice Path instead of rolling elsewhere. [7:43]
- Roll toward shop or another piece of cover; you cannot outspace Kelvin's beam in the open. [8:49]
- Over half the game was spent under about 400 HP; Extra Regen or Healing Rite would solve that, and green items also give max HP from investment. [9:19]
- When the enemy is short three on a lane, shove the wave and probably take tower; when you see three pushing your Walker, it is not the time to farm. Don't fight someone just returning from a fight with teammates reinforcing. [9:19]
- If you see someone saving a buff, run at them: you either force them to take it at a bad time or steal it, since people often do not melee it. [10:24]
- Ult the moment Kelvin presses his dome; it came out a little late. [10:24]
- When the enemy runs the Urn, at minimum call and ping it to draw your team's attention. [10:54]
- You were the only one who could stop the Urn; after Paradox swapped you next to the carrier, back up and melee it off him. [11:24]
- Getting Majestic Leap is fine for Warden. [11:24]
- Your shield sat unused for no reason; use it sooner. [11:58]
- You were sitting on Fleetfoot; pop it when running from Infernus or chasing Dynamo. [12:30]
- At 100 HP with no defensive items, go to base instead of ego fighting. [13:02]
- Consider teleporting to the secret shop instead of walking. [13:33]
- Letting natural regen carry you back up is bad, because you cannot support your team if a fight or bridge buff fight breaks out. [14:06]
- Don't run into three enemies at 500 HP; jump into their team at full health, not greeding at one HP. [14:37]
- Spellslinger stacks last about 10 seconds; once at max stacks you don't need to spam the flask off cooldown, you are using it two to three times as often as needed. [16:41]
- The build has high fire rate but its only base gun damage is from investments; it needs base gun damage such as Intensifying Magazine, Titanic Magazine or Point Blank. Surge of Power and Extra Spirit were troll buys here. [17:12]
- Pop Fleetfoot when being chased by Kelvin; it gives slow resist. [17:12]
- Confirm your soul orbs. [17:12]
- With Kelvin holding mid and no objectives to split, grab the Urn after the Infernus kill. Grab the Urn whenever you have a small advantage and nothing else (objective, Walker) to get from it. [18:12]
- If going a full flask or ability build, why only Compressed Cooldown and not Superior Cooldown? [19:14]
- Route tech: after hitting the vent, hold only D (not W and D) to make it onto the roof, a much better spot. [19:45]
- Throw a flask before you grab the zip. [20:46]
- Stay to full heal at fountain; three more seconds is about 800 HP. Doing Rejuvenator while three of you are hurt is even worse. [20:46]
- Spellbreaker would be very strong into this comp; you are dying mostly to spirit damage, and Paradox is a spirit build. [21:46]
- Trophy Collector is both overrated and underrated: extremely good in about 20% of games and trolling in about 80%. [22:16]
- Your ult came way too late; not pressing it may have lost the game. [22:46]
- On a flask build you are not playing around Binding Word, so skipping Slowing Hex is fine, but it would have been good into Pocket (and Dynamo, Shiv) to suppress him when he jumps in. [23:16]
- Slowing Hex alone does not guarantee Binding Word hits; Binding Word is hard to land against thinking players. [24:18]
- Summary: in lane, overaggression on minion melees put you in dangerous spots you should have been punished for even at Oracle 5. [23:47]
- Summary: you need to buy defensively when you are one HP under tower, and reset instead of egoing at one HP. [25:20]
- Summary: buy Fortitude on Warden; it gives base gun damage and keeps you topped off, fixing both of your problems. [25:20]
- Summary: mid game, press ult more and sooner; it is very strong even on a flask build. [25:50]
- Summary: upgrade Compressed Cooldown to Superior Cooldown on a flask build; Compressed is 1,600 souls for 22% off one cooldown, Superior gives a bit more on it plus 25% off every other ability, great on Willpower and Last Stand. [26:22]
