## Covers
Violet, the new ink and paint hero, aquatiq channel.
Short ability showcase: each ability read from the tooltip and shown in a sandbox, with first impressions.
Video dated 2026-10-19.

## Claims
- Violet's right click is very strong right now: you can pin people and box them in so they cannot move. [0:31]
- The right click is not a passive; it comes off your 1, so your 1 must be active to draw. [0:31]
- You can step on the paint. [0:31]
- Ability 1 brings your next three bullets to life so you can steer them as they travel; they deal increased bullet damage and splash spirit damage in a small area. [1:01]
- Each successful hit on a hero reduces your ability cooldowns, and item cooldowns by half that (as read off the tooltip). [1:01]
- The right click paints a solid stroke of ink that blocks projectiles and characters from passing through. [1:01]
- The stroke can be destroyed with a heavy melee, but that deals spirit damage to the attacker; it is like McGinnis's wall except it damages you. [1:01]
- You can delete your own stroke too. [1:31]
- The empowered bullets are really strong. [1:31]
- Ability 1 has charges, so Rapid Recharge will be very useful on her. [1:31]
- Apparently the build goes Radiant (as captioned). [1:31]
- Ability 2 is an incline you can basically swim on; it gives movement speed while you are in it, and you can go slightly outside it. [1:31]
- Ability 2 (dragon brush) keeps traveling forward, leaving a trail that grants bonus slide distance to allies and a fading slow plus spirit damage to enemies it passes through. [2:03]
- The brush travels pretty far, about 23 m, and stops if it hits a wall. [2:03]
- Reactivate ability 2 to flick the brush forward, ending it early but dealing spirit damage in a small area; ending it stops the trail so you cannot go as far. [2:03]
- The upgrades read include flick range and gaining damage amp over time while sliding. [2:33]
- The flick does a lot of damage. [2:33]
- Ability 3: choose a hero to be your subject; they take spirit damage over time as long as you keep looking at them (line of sight). [3:04]
- Every hero has a different portrait. [3:04]
- A completed sketch is a full stun, so you can heavy punch right after. [3:04]
- While you are drawing, the subject deals 35% less damage of every type. [3:04]
- A completed sketch does extra damage. [3:36]
- The subject drops a sketch on the ground that you pick up for a buff; each hero gives a different buff (listed on the wiki): Abrams gives health, Haze gives speed, Vindicta maybe weapon damage. [3:36]
- Each sketch collected is a permanent buff, shown above the 3; go around and collect buffs from everybody. [4:06]
- Ability 4, Abstract Landscape, creates a circular sphere of ink that blocks vision and grants allies spirit. [4:06]
- Enemies inside the ult are revealed; leaving it deals spirit damage and silences and slows them. [4:36]
- He thinks the ult is one of the most OP in the game; enemies inside cannot see, but unlike Mordekaiser's ult in League they can leave. [4:36]
