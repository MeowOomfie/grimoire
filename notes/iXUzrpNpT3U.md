# Deadlock's First TRUE Balance Patch | Reworks, new item & more
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-03-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXUzrpNpT3U

## Covers
A walkthrough of the March 2026 balance patch with sandbox demos: the new Golden Goose Egg item and the biggest hero changes, with the coach's early verdicts.
Heroes: Abrams, Apollo, Billy, Celeste, Calico, The Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo, Haze, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash, Magnus, Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Rem, Seven, Shiv, Silver, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous, Warden, Wraith, Yamato. For anyone catching up on what changed.

## Claims
- Theme of the patch: many base abilities lost power while tier three (max) upgrades got massive buffs, so a lot of strength is now locked behind maxing an ability. [7:41]
- Golden Goose Egg (new, 800 souls): -10% damage penalty on all damage you deal (items, abilities, gun), plus 1 m sprint speed and 1 out of combat regen; the stats are almost nothing and the penalty outweighs them. [1:03]
- Golden Goose Egg starts worth 400 souls (the sell value of any 800 item) and grows by 75 per minute, so after 10 minutes it holds 750 souls; hatching gives the souls plus one golden statue buff per 200 souls. [1:34]
- Verdict on Golden Goose Egg: everyone will first try to be greedy and hold it 20 to 30 minutes, but the -10% damage is a big drawback; long term it is likely a support item, or it gets nerfed if everyone buys it. [1:34]
- Abrams was nerfed overall (less damage), but Shoulder Charge stuns longer with two points in it, and much shorter with zero points. [2:35]
- Abrams' Siphon Life has more scaling when maxed but gives even less value without items. Infernal Resilience now gives debuff resist when maxed, an incentive to max it. [2:35]
- Abrams' ultimate has a very long radius and a 2.2 second stun; with two points in it and duration it stuns for 2.82 seconds. The coach expects Abrams to still be good. [3:06]
- Apollo changes are mostly a nothing burger: Flawless Advance casts slower, Repulse is basically untouched, and the ult lost base damage but gained spirit scaling, so past roughly 40 to 50 spirit it does more damage than before; not a late game nerf. [3:38]
- Billy's Rising Ram now reduces its cooldown by 50% on impact at base, but base duration dropped, so you need two points for the long Rising Ram you used to get. [4:09]
- Billy's Bashdown has a smaller base radius but a bit more radius on the second upgrade. Blasted has less spirit scaling until maxed, when it jumps to 1.2 scaling. [4:09]
- Billy's Chain Gang: the first upgrade gives 40% spirit resist and 40% bullet resist; the max upgrade gives 1.3 seconds of Unstoppable per hero pulled, so a big ult rewards you with a long Unstoppable. The coach likes the Billy changes. [4:39]
- Celeste got a light nerf: slightly less acceleration but even lower gravity so she stays airborne longer; Shining Wonder linger duration went from 4.5 to 3.2 seconds and its bounce range was reduced a little. [5:40]
- Calico now gets 300 bonus souls for any kill on a target hit by Leaping Slash; the coach calls it insane and almost certain to be nerfed. [6:10]
- Calico's Leaping Slash is far less clunky: it goes where you look (look up to go up), and works at close range, even right on top of a target. [6:40]
- Calico's Ava (cat form) now ramps up speed the longer you stay in it, from 30% move speed to max in about 4 seconds; she can no longer escape instantly. Movement items like Enduring Speed make it much faster. [7:10]
- Maxed Ava now gives 20% damage amp when you come out of it; that damage amp was taken from her ult. [8:11]
- Calico's bombs at two points deal bonus damage to barriers, making her a good pick against heavy barrier heroes like Paige. [8:11]
- Doorman ult rework: with the max upgrade you are Unstoppable while someone is in the hotel, and if they fail to check out you can ult again within 10 seconds, so it has almost no cooldown. [8:42]
- Doorman's Call Bell no longer explodes when it passes through targets; only activating it does damage. Max upgrade bells last about 20 seconds, so you can stack them for a big explosion, and they keep slowing move speed in the area for area control. [9:14]
- Doorman's two point ult stun went from 1.25 to 1.5 seconds; returning ult targets get a time slow like Paradox's carbine, and failing to check out also stuns them, making a failed Doorman path very punishing. [9:45]
- Drifter's passive now always gives the bonus weapon damage (3%) when an isolated target dies near him, no last hit needed, so he gains more weapon damage on average. [10:16]
- Valve is being more lenient on stacking bonuses in general: Grey Talon's bird has a linger duration, Calico's Leaping Slash and Vindicta's snipe count if the target dies within a few seconds. [10:16]
- Drifter now heals for a percentage of missing health on kills (previously kills reduced his cooldowns). [10:46]
- Drifter's ult now makes every target affected by it count as isolated, so his passive works in teamfights; the coach calls this a fantastic, wanted change. [11:16]
- Drifter's maxed Rend now silences close range targets; base Rend damage is now spirit and the close range damage is melee. Maxed Stalker's Mark now doubles the bleed (4%) and adds healing reduction. [11:16]
- Dynamo's Quantum Entanglement no longer reloads guns or gives fire rate to him or teammates, pushing him toward spirit damage support. [12:18]
- Dynamo's Kinetic Pulse now reduces bullet resist by 18%, an extremely significant amount. [12:18]
- Dynamo can now jump while healing without cancelling it. Singularity base no longer does percent damage, but the max upgrade does more percent damage than before. [12:48]
- Haze's max Smoke Bomb lost its invulnerability but now removes debuffs like Victor's max 2, and gives 30% bullet lifesteal; with Rapid Recharge you get four debuff removes on a 5 second cooldown. It solves disarm, slow, stun and knockdown problems for a carry. [13:18]
- Haze's Sleep Dagger no longer drains stamina, but maxed it sleeps longer and on wake up gives -50% move speed and dash speed for 2.5 seconds. [13:49]
- Haze's max ult again gives one additional target; the coach expects ult build Haze to be good, especially late: farm into Ricochet and Unstoppable, then kill everyone with two targets plus ricochet. [14:21]
- Infernus was not good last patch and is hated when good; maxed Afterburn adds base and max burn duration: 3 seconds base, extendable to 6 seconds by shooting more bullets into a burning target. Without max Afterburn you do not get this mechanic. [14:51]
- Infernus' Flame Dash was buffed (trail lasts longer) to make the Flame Dash build more viable; Napalm only got small rebalances. [15:52]
- Infernus' max ult with Greater Expansion is ridiculous: it covers as far as the eye can see and stuns the entire mid boss pit. [15:52]
- Afterburn is now applied by melee, so when you need to reload you can melee to proc it and then Flame Dash. [16:52]
- Ivy is now a spirit demon and a potential gun demon. Max Kudzu Bomb is now Entangling Thorns: an enemy that stays in it for 2 seconds becomes entangled (immobilized like Graves' wall, no movement abilities). [16:52]
- Ivy combo: Kudzu into Stone Form to stun them inside, then they get entangled. [17:24]
- Ivy's max 2 (formerly the tether, now Kudzu Connection) stays on permanently; at max level with Boundless Spirit it gave the coach 40% fire rate and 43% bullet lifesteal all the time, also given to the linked ally with healing replicated. [17:55]
- Ivy's max Stone Form gives +1 second duration and more spirit damage with 2.8 spirit scaling, enough for giga nukes. [18:26]
- Ivy's ult no longer knocks up, is a bit bigger, and cannot be cast while you take damage, so it is no longer a free escape or the old walker cheese; it has no cast time on yourself anymore. [18:57]
- Kelvin's Frost Grenade now heals by default; one point makes it heal 90 and deal 90, two points freeze the target's stamina regen briefly, and max adds scaling to heal and damage (about 300 heal and 308 damage with decent spirit). [19:28]
- Kelvin's Ice Path now gives 35% bullet resist while on it at one point, and at max 35% spirit while on it, roughly another Boundless Spirit: the coach went from 166 to 209 spirit. It lingers only a few seconds after leaving the path. [20:29]
- Kelvin's beam was reworked in scaling but plays the same; you can now move, cast abilities and use items such as Warp Stone during it to stick to people. [20:59]
- Kelvin's ult can now be targeted on a specific enemy so they cannot escape the dome, or alt cast on yourself. [21:29]
- Lady Geist's Essence Bomb self-damage is now 30% of its damage (previously self-damage scaled 1.8 against 1.2 damage), so spirit and bomb builds, possibly with Boundless Spirit, might now be viable. [22:00]
- Lady Geist's Life Drain got buffs; maxed it has more spirit scaling and now +2 charges instead of +1, so with Rapid Recharge you have five Life Drains. [23:01]
- Lady Geist's Malice is basically unchanged; her ult at two points silences enemies in an AoE, at three points gives 60 spirit power, 40% fire rate and 50 spirit resist for 8 seconds, and has no delay after casting (was about 0.7 seconds). [23:31]
- Lash is eating good: Ground Strike untouched; Grapple base unchanged but the second upgrade gives a bit more weapon damage and the third gives +1 stamina on cast and 60% air control, so you can stay in the air almost forever and strafe like Celeste. [24:02]
- Lash's Flog attack angle no longer scales with spirit, you need to max it; maxed it now heals 70% versus heroes (was 60%), a small buff overall. [25:03]
- Death Slam throw distance no longer scales with spirit: one point gives 12 m, more only from Greater Expansion, so you can no longer throw people from the pit into your spawn unless very close. [25:03]
- Death Slam's max upgrade gives 10 m extra cast range, enough to grab everyone pushing your base; it may reduce the need for Greater Expansion. [26:34]
- Magnus' wall is destructible: two heavy melees break a portion, so do not assume you are walled off at mid boss. [27:05]
- Magnus' max turret upgrade deploys two mini turrets; turrets last 35 seconds base and 47 when maxed, have very little health but very high damage resistance right after spawning. [27:37]
- Magnus' Medicinal Specter now gives 40% spirit resist at the first point and stamina recovery at the second, pushing an area control support identity beyond split pushing. Her ult was buffed and does an insane amount of damage maxed. [28:07]
- Mina is nerfed overall: early numbers dropped and the heal on rake now needs two points instead of one, so she must choose between damage and tankiness in lane; Sanguine Retreat cooldown cannot get as low and her ult damage dropped a little. [29:08]
- Mina compensation: maxed Sanguine Retreat now gives three recasts instead of two. She will still be very strong. [29:08]
- Mirage's scarabs are now an instant charged cast that steals all the HP immediately, and give damage amp instead of bullet shred: 10% on the target, 20% maxed, with 2.0 spirit scaling and two charges. [30:09]
- Mirage's tornado is now Dust Devil: base lift is only 0.3 seconds, so it is more defensive than CC; it gives 30% bullet evasion, 60% with two points, and maxed you can recast it within 5 seconds. [30:39]
- Mirage's Djinn's Mark now stuns at max marks, and marks are simpler: up to five marks, each adding a fixed damage amount. [31:09]
- Mirage's ult can now teleport literally anywhere, not only to structures or teammates; two points give a 400 barrier that scales with spirit. With instant scarabs and double tornado, a frontline Mirage might appear instead of the Ricochet backline Mirage. [31:39]
- Mo & Krill can now burrow through the map's tunnels with increased movement speed, which is very fast and good for box routes; but burrow does not last long, so do not get stuck. [32:09]
- Mo & Krill's Scorn has much better spirit scaling: Mystic Burst can be a first item in lane, and with enough spirit Scorn can proc Tank Buster (needs 165 damage) for big healing. [33:11]
- Mo & Krill's Combo is shorter with a longer cooldown unless you invest points, but does very high damage and gives 100% lifesteal at one point, making solo kills easier; pure CC Combo is nerfed unless you invest. [33:41]
- Paige got mostly number changes; she is functionally the same hero, with an attempt to make her spirit damage build more viable. [34:11]
- Paradox's max Paradoxical Swap can now swap a whole group of people at once, the biggest change, enabling many combos. [34:41]
- Paradox's wall no longer does damage; it amplifies bullets that pass through it, slows enemies that touch it so it is very hard to move through, and maxed it becomes a charged ability again. Pulse Grenade now has a time slow like the carbine. [34:41]
- Paradox may shift from pick hero to teamfight CC hero, but her early combo does much less damage since the free 9% health damage wall is gone; Kinetic Carbine got a small damage buff, so you need to invest more. [35:11]
- Pocket's max Affliction now prevents all healing, making Pocket an extremely good answer to heavy healing heroes like Kelvin or the previous bruiser meta; the rest of his kit got small number changes. [35:42]
- Rem got small number changes; the big one is that two points in his ult no longer give Unstoppable, so his ult is no longer guaranteed through things like a Lash ult. [36:13]
- Seven's move speed no longer scales with spirit, and Power Surge duration no longer scales with spirit: maxed it lasts 22 seconds, and with duration plus cooldown items it still reaches 100% uptime. [36:45]
- Seven can now move slightly from the start of his ult, and much faster when it is maxed; otherwise he is fundamentally the same hero. [37:15]
- Shiv's Killing Blow is now a skill shot: press it, it sends you forward and hits only one person, can miss, and is not AoE. [37:45]
- Shiv's max ult no longer fully refreshes the cooldown on an execute; it gives a recast window, and each further execute gives another window, so no infinite executes and no full refresh for the next fight. [37:45]
- Shiv's knives lost impact damage but have much more bleed damage, especially maxed; stacking knives is the game. Four maxed knives with no spirit items did about 100 DPS. [38:16]
- Shiv's Slice and Dice now reduces spirit resist, by 14% at two points, and the coach believes the echo dash also applies it, possibly stacking linearly (unverified chat claim). He expects Bloodletting to still be a problem. [39:16]
- Silver's ult form no longer gives base resistances, gives less health, and has a 50 second cooldown; her ult form is nerfed while human form is still very strong, balancing power between the two forms. [39:46]
- Victor's Pain Battery now always starts charged when you spawn, a big deal for laning; maxed it gets 2.2 spirit scaling. His 2 is about the same with more scaling when maxed. [40:17]
- Victor's Aura of Suffering reaches max DPS much faster, about 6 seconds (shown by the bar), but the max DPS was reduced. [40:47]
- Victor's ult max upgrade: on death the Shocking Reanimation radius is huge, especially with Greater Expansion, and stuns for 3 seconds (3.84 with duration), possibly the longest true stun in the game. [41:17]
- Vindicta's fire rate no longer scales with spirit but her weapon damage does, double dipping with the weapon damage from her ult. Her crow is now an AoE of several crows. [41:47]
- Vindicta gets extra item range during Flight, maxed Flight refreshes its duration on a kill, and she can now parry during Flight, so she can Counterspell (for example a Lash ult or a knockdown) without cancelling Flight. [42:17]
- Viscous' Splatter now bounces once by default and two more times maxed, four times total; the goo on the ground gives more slide distance. The Cube got a cooldown and regen change. [42:48]
- Viscous' Puddle Punch has an insane radius, especially with Greater Expansion. Base Goo Ball now lets you use abilities right away, and maxed it gives more duration and a longer stun; expect Goo Ball builds and a very strong Viscous. [43:18]
- Warden's Willpower max now gives 40% debuff resist. [44:19]
- Warden's Binding Word (cage) max now silences enemies instantly until they escape the range or are immobilized, with -18 seconds cooldown, so you have it often; it catches people by surprise. [44:19]
- Warden's max Last Stand gives Unstoppable while channeling, so you can channel in front of people or stall for your team. [44:49]
- Warden's Alchemical Flask has more power behind its max upgrade (cooldown reduction, radius, fire rate) and is weaker before; his gun damage was slightly nerfed, but maxing the silencing cage is a good tool even for gun Warden. [45:19]
- Wraith's cards were reworked into types: spade, diamond, club and heart each do something different, and the joker (one in nine chance) does all of them: reduces bullet and spirit resist, slows, does bonus damage and heals you. [45:49]
- Wraith's ult is reworked: you reposition the target while ulting and cannot shoot during it; it is faster, no longer silences or disarms, and only blocks stamina and movement items and abilities (for example Mina's bats or a Sinclair teleport). [46:19]
- Wraith's Full Auto no longer buffs teammates, but maxed it gives unlimited ammo for its duration. [46:50]
- Yamato's max Power Slash now has 8 m cast range and goes extremely far. [47:20]
- Yamato's Flying Strike with two points: grappling gives 40 spirit for 6 seconds; with Power Slash's 2.4 scaling that is about 100 extra Power Slash damage, very significant for burst. [47:20]
- Yamato's Crimson Slash first point now gives more melee damage for 4 seconds; maxed it also improves damage and healing scaling instead of only cooldown. [47:51]
- Yamato's max Flying Strike gives charges and can grapple to ally heroes and structures (walkers, guardians), allowing crazy escapes by cancelling onto them. Her max ult gives even more resist than before. [48:21]
