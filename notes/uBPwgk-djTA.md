# This Vindicta Gun Build Should Be Illegal
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-05-13
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBPwgk-djTA

## Covers
Vindicta gameplay commentary by aquatiq, a Vindicta main, May 2026 patch.
A played game with a spirit-to-gun build (High-Velocity Rounds, Enchanter's Emblem, Opening Rounds, Spiritual Overflow, Leech, Toxic Bullets, Crippling Headshot) and some macro takes on wave priority, Urn and mid boss.

## Claims
- Opening items: High-Velocity Rounds first, then Enchanter's Emblem, then Opening Rounds. [1:02]
- Once he has his snipe (Assassinate) he expects it to be over for the enemy lane. [3:35]
- Build path: start with spirit, then about halfway through the game switch to gun. [6:13]
- An enemy Billy with Recharging Rush and Extra Charge one-taps him if he gets hit, so he left that lane. [7:15]
- Vindicta dominates on a good player because she is a gun character and her snipe now scales with her gun. [12:59]
- Gun characters are typically a little stronger than spirit characters because of what they do to towers, and this is a tower-oriented game. [13:33]
- Vindicta's snipe no longer gives money, so she no longer gets ahead from it like she used to. [14:35]
- The Urn is more important than the walker. [14:35]
- Check whether the enemy has anyone who buys Knockdown early. [15:05]
- Always wave prio: ignore killing people until the waves are prioritized, then go on a killing spree. Crashing waves let the enemy roam and make plays. [18:13]
- When the enemy sends a group to your side of the map while you take a walker, counter push another lane. [21:51]
- Letting the enemy get the Urn made them much richer than his team. [22:58]
- With two enemies dead, mid boss was free; the team was just too slow to take it. [26:06]
- He buys Spiritual Overflow for its stats. [27:08]
- Late game he sells Opening Rounds and buys Leech to live longer. [28:43]
- He buys Toxic Bullets and then Crippling Headshot; he calls the Toxic Bullets and Crippling Headshot combo pretty crazy. [30:53]
- When you have mid boss, run in and take the enemy's farm and vending machines; one walker plus their vending machines would have been far more valuable than what his team did. [34:07]
