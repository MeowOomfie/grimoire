## Covers
Vindicta on the max snipe damage spirit build, from aquatiq.
Commentary over a ranked game from a hard Bebop and Paradox lane through a comeback, with the item order called out as he buys.
Recorded 2026-09-25.

## Claims
- The build is max snipe (Assassinate) damage: a copy of Cory's build but with a lot of spirit items. [1:08]
- Against Bebop he must never get hooked, so he stays behind walls all the time. [0:36]
- He planned Healing Rite for a losable lane. [0:36]
- In a 1v2 lane he chose to greed damage instead of buying healing. [2:12]
- Once he has Escalating Exposure the snipes should do huge damage. [1:38]
- On his usual build he gets around 80 stacks and just shoots people to death; maxing the snipe gives extra stacks, so he does not care about stacking with the snipe. [1:38]
- On this build he snipes rather than shooting; the snipe should do around a thousand, about the same as a Grey Talon arrow, so any enemy visibly at about 1000 HP gets sniped. [2:43]
- Against Bebop and Paradox, sit behind the tower the whole lane and wait for them to make mistakes. [3:44]
- In that lane, hard clear the wave every time, because minions pushing on the enemy make it hard for them to play. [4:15]
- First item: Mystic Burst. [4:15]
- He clears the wave to get the healing from it. [4:46]
- Next: Extra Charge, timed with getting the ultimate. [5:16]
- Rapid Recharge is a big spike because it gives lots of charges on the ultimate. [7:22]
- Against hooks he needs Reactive Barrier and Indomitable. [7:54]
- He also wants Spirit Shielding. [8:25]
- After that comes Escalating Exposure, which he had never used on Vindicta before. [8:58]
- After Escalating Exposure he gets green items: Spellbreaker, Counterspell or Debuff Remover. [11:37]
- Spellbreaker is bad in this game; Debuff Remover is better because the enemies have so much to remove (Mirage's mark, Viper, Lady Geist's bomb, scarabs). [14:10]
- With escalation stacks on an enemy the snipe does enormous damage. [13:40]
- With the snipe build and stacks you do not need a gun; you kill people anyway. [16:19]
- Getting hooked is an instant kill on this hero, so buy Indomitable to never get hooked. [16:52]
- If he gets hooked the game is over, so he will not greed damage over Indomitable. [21:03]
- Later options: Surge of Power or Boundless Spirit. [20:32]
- He needs Tank Buster for a lot of the enemy team. [22:04]
- Buy Spirit Shielding when almost all the damage you take is spirit, even with Spellbreaker. [23:08]
- When the enemy has Rejuvenator, back off and wait it out instead of fighting. [23:08]
- When you are 50k down, the Rift is your comeback, so the team has to go for it. [25:41]
- Indomitable paid off: it stopped him from being hooked or swapped in a fight. [28:32]
- He picked up Counterspell later. [29:02]
- After winning a fight, take the enemy's farm. [29:02]
- Spellbreaker felt useless; he still got one-shot with it. [33:53]
- After taking shrines, dropping the Patron and backing gives the best chance of winning. [35:29]
- When you get a pick, focus that one target down. [37:00]
- Late he bought Lightning Scroll and skipped Debuff Remover because he already had Indomitable. [37:00]
