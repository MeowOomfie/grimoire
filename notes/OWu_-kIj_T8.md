# Is Veil Walker Dead? Deadlock Hotfix Dec. 21st
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-12-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OWu_-kIj_T8

## Covers
A walkthrough of the December 21st 2024 hotfix: slow fixes, tier four cost, shield regen delay, Ethereal Shift, Knockdown, Veil Walker moving to tier four, and hero changes to Infernus, Mirage, Vindicta, Warden and Yamato.
For players who want each change explained with the coach's verdict and what it does to builds, especially shield builds.

## Claims
- Slows were fixed so they can no longer drop your speed below 2 m/s, and slow percentages are now calculated assuming a minimum speed of 2 instead of 0; this is a nerf to slows, though they stay very strong. [0:00]
- Tier four items cost reduced from 6.2k to 6k; they cost 6.5k back in May, and the trend has always been downward. [0:30]
- Verdict: the cost cut is meant to make early tier four power spikes more viable, because builds that stack 500 cost items get more power per soul; the coach likes it. [1:00]
- Shield "do not take damage before regenerating" increased from 18 to 30 seconds: before, a shield not fully depleted regenerated to full if you took no damage of its type for 18 seconds. [1:30]
- The old high-level NA and EU meta was rushing a barrier as the first lane item, e.g. Combat Barrier first, trading about 200 damage, hiding 18 seconds and coming out 200 health up; the coach calls this unintended and the change fixes it. [2:01]
- Ethereal Shift now cancels abilities like flight: Vindicta using Ethereal Shift in flight starts falling. [2:33]
- Before the fix, Vindicta could Ethereal Shift to keep flight and nullify Knockdown, then buy a debuff remover against a second Knockdown, so you needed about three Knockdowns to get her out of the sky; the coach expects her to feel more balanced and calls her quite overpowered before. [2:33]
- Not in the patch notes: Ethereal Shift now also fully cancels Infernus's Flame Dash (before it kept going slowly), a slight nerf to the dash Infernus build. [3:04]
- Various Arcane Surge bugs were fixed (possibly not working on certain abilities); report any you find on the forums. [3:35]
- The soul ("gold") steal value was increased from 2% / 1% to 2.5% / 1.5% because it felt insignificant. [3:35]
- Restorative Shot cooldown increased by 1 second; the coach says it was bought in almost every lane and he favours nerfing lane healing so laning is more calculated. [4:05]
- Spellslinger Headshots cooldown is now per target instead of on the item, so you can headshot several different people, and spirit amp per headshot increased by 1%; the coach expects it to be bought more. [4:05]
- Knockdown stun duration on ground targets reduced from 0.75 to 0.5 seconds, with airborne values kept similar; it is meant as an anti-air counter for Lash, Vindicta, Grey Talon and similar, not ground CC. [4:35]
- Knockdown is still viable to interrupt a channel such as Dynamo's ultimate, but the ground stun is very short; it used to be about 1.25 seconds a few months back, and the coach welcomes the repeated nerfs because chain CC from three or four Knockdowns is not fun. [5:07]
- Big change: Veil Walker is now a tier four item; shields 250 to 300, invisibility duration 5 to 7 seconds, initial fade out time reduced from 1 second back to the previous 0.25. [5:37]
- Veil Walker cost doubled from 3k to 6k; the coach calls it a huge nerf and likes it, because invisibility (no footsteps) and shields are very strong and having it on a 3,000 item was unhealthy. [6:09]
- He expects Veil Walker to still see play eventually, but expects nobody to buy it at first out of pessimism. [6:40]
- Toxic Bullets base DPS reduced from 55 to 50 (caption says "chemical fire"); he expects little change because the item is bought for its stats and extra weapon damage against structures, Mid Boss and heroes, letting a team take Mid Boss very quickly. [6:40]
- Infernus Flame Dash charge time increased by 3 seconds, so charges come back slower; good change, Infernus is not particularly overpowered but many people struggle against the dash build. [7:10]
- Mirage Fire Scarabs T2 reduced from -10 to -8; a slight nerf, fine because Mirage was probably overpowered in the hands of top players. [7:10]
- Vindicta Assassinate base damage reduced from 120 to 100 and bonus damage also reduced by 20, about 40 less damage with the same scaling; combined with the Ethereal Shift fix she should still be played but be less impressive. [7:40]
- Warden bullet damage growth reduced (from 0.94 to 0.9) and Last Stand now does 40% less damage to objectives, so he can no longer run down a Walker alone with his ultimate and suicide for it as was common last patch. [7:40]
- Yamato Shadow Transformation resists reduced by 5% while the T3 gives 5 more, delaying a bit of her power to later. [8:10]
- With the new Veil Walker you stay invisible for a long time after passing a veil, longer with Duration Extender, so it is a useful mobility tool. [8:41]
- Shield builds get significantly worse: before you bought Combat Barrier and Enchanter's Barrier and only 3k more for Veil Walker to refresh all shields through a veil for huge mid game effective HP; now refreshing shields costs 6k and a shield build essentially requires Veil Walker. [8:41]
