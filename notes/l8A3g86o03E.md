# The BEST Seven Build In Deadlock - META Build Update
channel: Metro Deadlock  date: 2025-06-14  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l8A3g86o03E

## Covers
Seven Power Surge hard carry build after the item rework (Cultist Sacrifice, Superior Cooldown, Suppressor, Infuser, Juggernaut), investment thresholds, fire rate, skill order, lane plan, Infuser timing, why no Surge of Power, situational items, Pocket, Lash, Wraith, Haze, Infernus

## Claims
- Seven is still insanely strong, arguably one of the best heroes on ladder; many players now run him as an ultimate bot, but this Power Surge build is still the best play style to hard carry. [0:00]
- In the old item version you ran Surge of Power on Power Surge, but this build no longer needs it. [0:31]
- Seven is somewhat weak early: low regen, easy to hit, massive head hitbox, so build for survivability and avoid trading with the enemy while creeps are active. [1:01]
- Lane plan: buy Monster Rounds, then Compress Cooldown on Power Surge, put two levels in Power Surge and nuke the wave, then either break boxes or trade and deal tower damage. [1:01]
- Skill order: one point in Static Charge for a faster stun cooldown, max Power Surge, one point in the ball, then max the ultimate; it is still a hybrid build that makes both Power Surge and the ultimate strong. [1:31]
- Extended Magazine for more ammo: the build runs no reload items, so you want to empty a full magazine and kill someone without reloading, which is also why Titanic Magazine comes later. [1:31]
- The speaker changed his mind on Cultist Sacrifice: it now scales with boons, so once you have enough boons you get the old stat bonuses or more. [2:02]
- Ability range is good on everything for Seven: a bigger ball, a bigger stun radius, a bigger Power Surge radius and a bigger ultimate radius, so the 50% ability range from Cultist Sacrifice is very good. [2:32]
- Raw spirit is still the number one stat on Seven, and fire rate is the most underrated: Power Surge procs on every burst you shoot, so Power Surge damage is directly tied to fire rate. [3:03]
- Health is the most valuable stat right now because the early barriers from before the item rework (Divine Barrier, Enchanter's Barrier, Combat Barrier) are gone; raw HP early and mid game also makes resistances worth more later. [3:33]
- Rushing Battle Vest or Enchanter's is also fine and strong, and they get better the more HP you have. [4:36]
- Cultist Sacrifice early is a greedy choice that makes you weak when you buy it, so play passive; ideally have it at 8 minutes when tier 3 camps spawn and sacrifice a tier 3 creep. [4:36]
- Cultist Sacrifice gets stronger over time and is kept all game; it is really a mid to late game item, so you can move it later in the build. [5:08]
- Superior Cooldown, upgraded from Compress Cooldown for 1,600, is a no-brainer: it makes Cultist uptime 100%, and every Seven ability benefits from cooldown reduction. [5:39]
- Superior Cooldown also gives the first spirit investment bonus and 4 out of combat regen, which helps sustain while farming early mid game without going back to base; Fortitude is an alternative here. [6:11]
- Rapid Rounds is bought on purpose: it is one of the last orange items in the build (only Titanic Magazine and Fleetfoot follow), and it pushes orange investment past 4.8k for 29% weapon damage. [6:41]
- With Rapid Rounds, 800 souls buys 10% fire rate plus the next weapon investment threshold of another 11% weapon damage, which makes aiming smoother and raises Power Surge damage. [7:41]
- Buy Rapid Rounds in lane before Titanic Magazine; buying it after Titanic Magazine and Fleetfoot is also fine. [8:45]
- Enchanter's Emblem is great value for 1,600: spirit, cooldown reduction and 18% spirit resist. [8:45]
- Titanic Magazine and Fleetfoot because Seven wants to be fast, kite, shoot nonstop and stay outside the enemy's effective range while dealing big damage. [8:45]
- Buy Spirit Lifesteal fairly early because there are mid game fights you want to be in, and without lifesteal you die easily, while with it and a fat ultimate you are very hard to kill. [9:16]
- Suppressor is the most broken item in the game besides Rescue Beam: it cuts the fire rate of enemies hit by your spirit by 35%, which in the ultimate is everyone, and Power Surge also hits several. [9:47]
- Suppressor helps even against spirit burst heroes like Lash or Pocket, who still finish with their gun; the speaker has won 1v1s against a Pocket whose shotgun fired very slowly. [10:18]
- Up to Suppressor (about 15k invested) you need no flex slot; by then you should have one, and the worst case is selling Rapid Rounds later. [10:48]
- Then Improved Spirit, which adds damage and so more lifesteal to everything, and Enduring Speed, which with Fleetfoot works like Unstoppable for movement so you still kite through heavy slows. [11:20]
- Infuser is the big power spike where you kill everyone; before it, play passive as a farmer, split push and join team fights once the ultimate is maxed. [11:51]
- Popping Infuser before Power Surge extends Power Surge duration because the duration scales with spirit; popping it during Power Surge adds damage but not duration. [12:21]
- Popping Infuser at full HP wastes its 80% lifesteal, so the speaker usually engages without it, uses ball, stun and Power Surge, and pops Infuser when low to regen back to a huge amount of HP. [12:53]
- The same works with the ultimate: when low with Infuser up, pop Infuser and the ultimate and you are instantly back to full HP, especially with several enemies around. [13:56]
- The new Infuser is different from the old 500 one you used every time before Power Surge; it is the core item for Seven for both damage and sustain. [14:26]
- After Infuser: Unstoppable first if the enemy has a lot of CC that stuns you out of the ultimate, Boundless Spirit if you need more damage and movement speed. [14:26]
- Juggernaut is slept on for Seven: it lowers the fire rate of enemies shooting you on top of Suppressor, so even a richer Wraith and Haze cannot kill you in the ultimate; it needs no extra slot because it builds from Enduring Speed. [14:56]
- Most Seven deaths in the ultimate come from enemies taking cover and slowly shooting him with their gun. [15:26]
- Then Lightning Scroll, selling Rapid Rounds since gun damage no longer matters, and Escalating Exposure as an absolute luxury for more damage. [15:57]
- Surge of Power is no longer worth putting on Power Surge: its cooldown is low but the 25 imbued spirit is not high, and you want it on an ability you spam, while Power Surge stays up for 20 seconds and is cast once or twice a fight. [16:28]
- You can still imbue Surge of Power on the ball, but going straight to Infuser, Boundless Spirit or Silence Wave gave more overall spirit power. [16:58]
- The build keeps the name Metro Shock 7 because it plays the same and feels as fast and strong as the old Surge of Power version, only a tiny bit squishier early. [17:29]
- Mystic Slow is situational and upgrades into Lightning Scroll; the speaker only buys it sometimes because Seven already has a lot of movement speed. [17:29]
- Silence Wave is insane against some heroes, such as silencing and bursting a Pocket, and it also lowers enemy spirit resist. [17:59]
- Escalating Resilience is run a lot: with fire rate from Rapid Rounds and Cultist Sacrifice (35% when active) it stacks very fast, about 2 bullet armor per bullet, and it is the best value bullet resist in a meta with a lot of bullet resist. [18:29]
- Upgrading Rapid Rounds into Burst Fire is a desperation, glass cannon buy for more fire rate and movement speed when you cannot afford the late game spikes; Infuser still comes first. [19:29]
- Debuff Remover is still an amazing item. [19:29]
- Spirit Resilience suits Seven because with Infuser he can let himself drop below 35% health and lifesteal back up, which most heroes cannot do. [19:59]
- Spellbreaker is a super luxury that gives resist on both and saves you against spirit burst. [19:59]
- Spirit Burn is the best anti-heal in the game against heavy healing such as Infernus; Healbane also fits, but the build needs a lot of slots late. [20:30]
- With Superior Cooldown the Infuser (caption says "power surge" here, described as giving more spirit than Surge of Power plus lifesteal) has only 13 to 14 seconds of downtime, so it feels always up when you need burst or lifesteal. [21:01]
