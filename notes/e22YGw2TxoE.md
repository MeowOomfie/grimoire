## Covers
A 3-minute Mirage guide (April 2026): pros and cons, the four abilities, lane, mid game around the Urn, late game positioning and recommended builds.
For players new to Mirage.

## Claims
- Mirage is a gun carry (M1) that carries well and, unlike other M1s, has a lot of early game strength and does very well in lane, with lots of outplay potential. [0:00]
- Cons: very slow wave clear and camp farming, so he can't scale as far as Wraith or Seven and has slightly less carry potential than the average M1. [0:00]
- Slowing Hex shuts down his Tornado and leaves him very vulnerable. [0:00]
- Fire Scarabs has two charges, four with two points in it; a hit applies a DoT that heals you for the damage dealt and currently also applies an inhibitor-like damage reduction to the enemy. [0:00]
- The Scarab DoT can't stack, so don't use two on the same enemy. [0:31]
- His 2 (Tornado, called "Dust Devil" in the video) dashes forward invulnerable, briefly roots anyone hit, then grants bullet evasion; use it mainly for the i-frames to dodge important abilities and ultimates. [0:31]
- Djinn's Mark stacks on enemies you shoot after a brief charge; it detonates for a lot of damage when you recast it or when it reaches max stacks (four, five when maxed). [0:31]
- Traveler (ult) teleports him anywhere on the map after a channel; taking any damage during the channel cancels it and puts it on a brief cooldown. [1:01]
- Lane: you generally have the advantage, so just harass the enemies. [1:01]
- If you have lots of pressure before 5 minutes but no kill potential under the tower, box route as well. [1:01]
- Against two very strong laners, play the inverse: clear the wave fast, play safe and box route between waves, since he scales better than the average hero. [1:31]
- Mid game: sit in the side lane opposite the Urn cash-in and pressure the enemy objective. [1:31]
- If they rotate one or two players onto you and you have enough wave priority, teleport into the Urn fight to give your team a one-man advantage. [1:31]
- If they send nobody to you, tell your team not to cash the Urn and to play slow, take a free walker, then possibly teleport over. [2:02]
- When the Urn isn't up, farm a side lane; with a better scaling M1 on your team (Wraith, Seven, Venator), let them farm the weak side lane while you play aggressively or invade from the strong side lane. [2:02]
- When your ultimate is up you generally want to be on the weak side. [2:02]
- Late game: shoot the enemies; with Tornado up play more aggressively in their face, with it down stand behind your front line like a standard M1 and don't let enemies engage you. [2:02]
- Your primary objective is staying alive, your secondary objective is doing damage. [2:33]
- Build: the Lightbringer Mirage build (Scarab build) is easy, and you can auto-queue it, though he doesn't recommend buying builds left to right. [2:33]
- For a non-Scarab build, Prayan has the best Mark one-shot build, but the Scarab build is by far better right now. [2:33]
