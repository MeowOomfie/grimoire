Three-step counter itemization process, counter items that synergize with your hero, fitting counter items into a build (laning, core, situational, late game sections), the trap of buying defensive items when behind, shred vs resist value, counter item categories (anti-heal, movement, pick, debuffs and CC, gun, abilities, damage reduction, shred, miscellaneous), Healbane, Toxic Bullets, Slowing Hex, Knockdown, Warp Stone, Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Divine Barrier, Reactive Barrier, Debuff Reducer, Unstoppable, Suppressor, Juggernaut, Phantom Strike, Disarming Hex, Spirit Sap, Silence Wave, Silencer, Spirit Shielding, Weapon Shielding, Witch Mail, Metal Skin, Escalating Resilience, Plated Armor, Escalating Exposure, Tankbuster, Armor Piercing Rounds, Rebuttal, Extra Stamina, Enduring Speed, Cursed Relic, Holliday, Mina, Wraith, Seven, Lash, Ivy, Vindicta, Grey Talon, Pocket, Paradox, Haze, Dynamo, Abrams

## Claims
- Counter itemization is problem solving; step one is identifying the specific problem, not "their Holliday is a problem" but what about her is the problem: her ult targeting you, her barrel damage, or her escaping with bounce pads. Enemy items can be the problem too, for example the whole enemy team stacking spirit resist items against your Mina, or slowing hexes stopping you from using your movement ability; each problem has different item solutions. [0:30]
- With limited slots and souls you cannot counter everything, so counter your biggest problems and adjust your gameplay for the rest or just deal with them. With experience you should anticipate problems and be proactive with counter items rather than reactive. [1:00]
- Step two is listing all potential solutions, which is a knowledge check of every counter item, what it does and which heroes it is good on; against a Holliday who keeps ulting you, look at pick counters, and against her barrel damage, look at spirit damage counters. Ideally your counter items also synergize with your hero: tanky heroes with lots of health per boon benefit more from percentage resistances, squishy heroes should prefer barriers or mobility to avoid damage in the first place. [1:30]
- Witch Mail on Mina against constant spirit damage is a poor fit because Mina should not be tanking abilities, and she gets less from its percentage spirit resist than someone like Abrams. Seven does so much constant AoE spirit damage that he can almost permanently apply spirit debuffs, so Suppressor and Healbane are pretty much always bought on him. [2:00]
- Because people buy slowing hexes against Mina and Wraith (captioned "Rave") to shut down their movement abilities, Dispel Magic (captioned "the spell magic") is a standard core item on those two. [2:30]
- Step three is picking the item that counters the most problems on the enemy team and fits your build at the current stage of the game. Slowing Hex bought only for an enemy Lash, when no one else has movement abilities, is somewhat useless in every fight without the Lash. [2:30]
- If the enemy also has a Seven or Ivy, save for Knockdown instead: it still counters Lash and its stun cancels the Seven or Ivy ults, so it is worth far more despite costing twice as much as Slowing Hex. [3:00]
- He keeps laning and core items (bought in about 90% of games) within the nine starting slots and about 30k souls total, which at about 1,200 souls per minute finishes around 25 minutes, when one or two Walkers are usually down and extra slots are unlocked for counter items. [3:30]
- Optional tier one laning counter items are easy to slot in: 400 souls to counter your lane, and you can sell for half when needed. If you buy Rebuttal and get a single kill with it in lane it has paid for itself; extra regen or healing rate that helps you survive a bad lane is worth it. [4:00]
- In the mid game you pick up tier two or three counter items; 1,600 soul items you do not sell until very late, and tier three items you pretty much never sell, so leave open slots for them or accept that they delay core items. [4:00]
- Whether delaying a core item for a counter item is worth it depends on hero, build, game and item; try it, then evaluate afterwards whether it was worth it. Late game tier three and four items sit at the bottom of his builds and he picks from them as needed, sometimes selling a tier two in very long games. [4:31]
- Utility and support heroes tend to have more room for counter items, while carries and damage heroes need certain items to hit their power spikes. [5:01]
- Common trap: buying defensive items when behind; rule of thumb is defensive items when ahead and damage items when behind. Defensive items are a luxury when ahead to keep your lead, because a far-ahead death means a longer death timer and a soul bounty for the enemy. [5:31]
- When behind you want the enemy to throw and need damage to punish them; filling slots with green items means you probably still die and cripple your damage, so buy damage to shut down a fat Wraith when she oversteps. This does not mean ignoring green items when behind; it depends how far behind you are. [6:01]
- Resistances are percentage based and get more value the more health you have (as do some barrier items that scale with boons), so they are literally less valuable when you are behind. Shred gets more valuable the more resist the enemy has, and an enemy that is ahead probably has resist, so when behind prioritize shred items over resistance items. [6:31]
- Counter items are tricky when behind because the enemy can counter your counter: Warp Stone against a Paradox swap is wasted 3,200 souls once she buys Slowing Hex. Slowing Hex or Disarming Hex for a Wraith 10k up on everyone is easily answered by Dispel Magic, which she probably already has. [7:01]
- Be proactive with counter items when even or ahead; shut down a Wraith early by buying Slowing Hex before she can afford Dispel Magic. [7:32]
- Exception: if Pocket afflicting you has been the sole reason for your last two deaths, buying Counterspell while a little behind can be correct, because that one item decides whether you can play the game. [7:32]
- Another exception: as a struggling brawler with a Haze who is popping off, build utility to peel for her and tank items to stay alive a few seconds longer, since she has the damage to kill everyone. [8:03]
- Stalker gives bullet resist shred, but it is not a counter item you buy because the enemy has bullet resist; likewise items with 5 or 10% spirit resist like Cold Front are not bought to counter spirit damage. [8:33]
- Against healing items or built-in healing (Abrams, Victor, Haze), anti-heal is a great idea; there are six items that reduce healing by a percentage. Healbane applies a 35% healing reduction on any spirit damage and heals you if a target dies while under it. [9:03]
- Toxic Bullets is a build-up item that cuts healing by 30% and deals percentage max health damage over time, so it is better the more health the enemy has; build-up differs per hero, so some apply it more easily. Anti-heal from different items stacks diminishingly (Healbane plus Toxic Bullets gives 55%, not 65%), but the same item does not stack with itself, so diversify anti-heal across your team. [9:33]
- Healbane procs on any spirit damage, but Silver lacks the reliable multi-target spirit damage of Seven, so it is a worse item for her. [10:03]
- Toxic Bullets damage scales with spirit power, making it slightly better on heroes who invest in spirit. [10:34]
- Four items stop movement abilities; Capacitor also disables stamina, but it is a non-targeted projectile with good synergy on only a few heroes; consider these against Apollo, Calico, Infernus (captioned "Furnace"), Mirage or any hero with movement abilities. [10:34]
- Knockdown stuns for half a second, but on airborne targets the stun increases (he believes to 2 seconds), so it counters Vindicta and Grey Talon, plus Ivy, Seven, Bebop and Lash with airborne ults, and Celeste and Holliday who spend a lot of time in the air. [11:04]
- Stuns interrupt channeled abilities, so Knockdown can cancel a Haze or Dynamo ult if your team has no other way to. [11:34]
- Against pick heroes like Bebop or Holliday use the pick items: Warp Stone to avoid a Paradox swap, Counterspell to parry a Holliday ult. You can Counterspell literally anything, but it is most valuable on important high damage or crowd control abilities. [11:34]
- Dispel Magic removes a Vindicta stake, Slowing Hex or Silence Wave, which are pick setup tools. [12:05]
- Reactive Barrier procs a barrier on hard CC (stuns, immobilizations), so it is great against teams with Vindicta stake, Abrams charge, Haze sleep and Seven's stun. [12:05]
- Debuff Reducer, Weighted Shots and Blood Tribute give debuff resist, reducing the duration of all negative effects. Dispel Magic and Divine Barrier remove negative effects (not hard CC); Dispel Magic only removes non-ultimate debuffs, so it cannot remove a Pocket or Wraith ult, but Divine Barrier can, and it can be cast on teammates at a cooldown cost. [12:36]
- Unstoppable makes you immune to CC, but like all actives it cannot be cast while stunned or slept, so use it beforehand; buy it to commit to a dive or to a Dynamo or Lash ultimate so it is not canceled. [12:36]
- Against gun heroes like Wraith or Mina there are fire rate slows and disarms: Suppressor slows fire rate when you deal spirit damage, so it suits heroes with consistent AoE spirit damage. Juggernaut slows the fire rate of people who shoot you, so it makes more sense on frontliners; Phantom Strike and Disarming Hex fully disarm for a few seconds. [13:06]
- Against ability-reliant heroes like Yamato or Pocket, reduce their spirit power with Spirit Sap (captioned "spirits up") or silence them with one of three items. Silence Wave sends a wave that silences and damages people in front of you; Silencer reduces the target's outgoing spirit damage by 25% on hit and silences when fully built up. [13:36]
- Barriers are temporary health that cannot be healed but benefit from resistances and work against all damage; Spirit Shielding procs on lots of spirit damage in a short time, Weapon Shielding on lots of weapon damage. [14:08]
- Spirit resist and bullet resist stack diminishingly, so the more resist items you stack the less effective they get. Counterspell lets you fully avoid spirit nukes, great against Lash or Yamato; Metal Skin is the bullet equivalent, making you immune to bullet damage for a few seconds. [14:39]
- Escalating Resilience gives stacking bullet resist when you shoot, so it is great for a fast-shooting gun hero against bullet-heavy teams. [14:39]
- Plated Armor gives a 30% chance to deflect bullets and 50% to prevent on hits, so it counters on-hit build-up heroes like Mina, Haze (captioned "Ace") or Infernus, and build-up items like Toxic Bullets and Inhibitor. [15:09]
- Shred reduces resist and is essentially a damage multiplier for you and your team, among the most valuable stats in the game; the more resist a target has, the bigger the proportional gain (6% shred adds 6 on top of 100 at 0% resist, but 6 on top of 70 at 30% resist). [15:39]
- Shred stacks diminishingly and, except Rusted Barrel and Crippling Headshot, the same source does not stack with itself, so diversify shred across your team. Escalating Exposure is not shred and does not shred more than Mystic Vulnerability; it gives spirit damage amp, which resist still reduces and teammates do not benefit from. [16:10]
- Shred items are rarely counter buys; people put whichever shred items synergize with their hero into core builds. Tankbuster deals percentage current health damage that bypasses spirit resist, and Armor Piercing Rounds directly counters Plated Armor with a 50% chance for bullets to be unavoidable and bypass resists. [16:41]
- Rebuttal is a melee counter: some melee resist but mainly a massively reduced parry cooldown, great for baiting melees when enemies think parry is on cooldown. [17:11]
- Extra Stamina counters Warden's flask stamina drain so you can escape his cage; slows do not slow dashes unless stated, so stamina helps against slows, and Enduring Speed specifically gives slow resist. [17:11]
- Cursed Relic counters anyone and removes all non-ultimate buffs, at the cost of 10% less outgoing damage. [17:42]
- One Grey Talon arrow easily procs Spirit Shielding, so it is a good item against him. Wraith's ultimate is hard to react to with Counterspell and disables stamina and movement abilities when it hits, so you cannot dash or Warp Stone out, but you can still use Ethereal Shift (captioned "e-shift") after it hits to wait it out. [18:13]
- Use critical thinking rather than blindly following a chart. [18:43]
