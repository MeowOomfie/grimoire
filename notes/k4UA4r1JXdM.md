## Covers
Graves, aquatiq, commentary over a won game the patch after her buff.
Covers her strength in the meta, her trap, item picks and Urn versus mid boss priority.
Patch date: around 2026-05-31.

## Claims
- Graves is S tier with about a 58% win rate; he plays her because she is overpowered. [1:04]
- Her trap (road spike style) takes a little while to activate after you place it. [2:36]
- The Urn is now separated from the buffs, which he likes. [9:23]
- The Urn fight timer is around 35 seconds now, which is short. [11:24]
- You can now place her ult down ahead of a fight. [11:24]
- He would take a death for the Urn pretty much any day. [11:54]
- Enemies will use two ults on Graves; expect to be focused. [7:49]
- Items he picks up: Stamina Mastery, then Echo Shard. [14:25]
- Get Boundless (as captioned) when you just want more damage on your skulls. [20:08]
- Mid boss now feels secondary; the game revolves around the Urn more than mid boss. [18:37]
- When the enemy takes the Urn, push their base instead. [19:38]
