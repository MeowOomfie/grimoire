## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream right after the new ranked system and MMR reset (early August 2026 patch), with long rants about the rank system in between.
Four ranked games reviewed: a Shiv learning frontline, a high Ascendant Wraith in a losing lane with Lash, an Ascendant Calico laning with Mina, and an Eternus 6 Calico aiming to go pro.
