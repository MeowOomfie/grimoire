# How to Build & Play Baba | Coaching an Emissary Baba
channel: Midknight

## Covers
Baba, the new tempo brawler, coached by Midknight over an Emissary player's replay.
Coaching: lane vs Sinclair, brew choice, buy timing, farming Sinner's Sacrifice, wave and rotation mistakes, how the ult stacks, then recommended builds.
Recorded right after Baba's release, patch of 2026-10-07.

## Claims
- Sinclair is a miserable lane into Baba because she takes all his spirit power and he can do nothing. [0:31]
- Rat King's massive hitbox makes your 1 easy to land. [0:31]
- With stolen spirit power and the bonus damage on your M1 you can outgun DPS heroes in their face, but without souls and spirit power your gun is cosmetic. [0:31]
- Throw your 1 before the wave arrives; a common Baba mistake is clearing the wave first and then looking for the 1, when the 1 is what gives you the damage to clear. [1:01]
- You basically have to hit your 1 on a hero for wave clear; when you miss it you do almost zero minion damage. [1:01]
- Buy Extra Charge first or second, because missed 1s leave you with no wave damage. [1:01]
- If enemies walk up to hit your tower, spend the extra charge of your 1 on them. [1:01]
- Mistake: playing too safe in lane; walk up more to hit the wave. [1:01]
- Against Sinclair the scary part is his Spectral Assistant; find cover that lets you hit the wave and zone the assistant (around the taxi), or clear from further back if the assistant is hitting you hard. [2:03]
- Early vs Sinclair, angle yourself against his assistant, not him; the assistant is the bigger DPS check because it auto targets. [2:03]
- Do not fish for scalding brew poke early; it does little. A good Baba instantly pops the soothing brew to clear the wave, like a Paige barrier. [2:35]
- Stand on top of your lane partner, shield both of you with soothing, walk up and clear the wave; it keeps far more HP up in lane. [3:05]
- Think of the scalding brew more as resist shred than damage (about 90 damage at that point); the shred is what wins trades when your 1 is up. [3:05]
- The silence brew is an insanely strong utility to throw when you are with teammates. [3:36]
- Brew choice: if you have wave priority and are pressuring, brew scalding for poke; if they have wave priority and you just need to clear, brew soothing; if teammates are ganking and you want utility, brew silence. [3:36]
- When looking for picks in the jungle you will mostly be throwing silences. [4:06]
- When fed, call it with your partner, pull out silence and jump the Sinclair to one shot him before he can use his assistant. [4:06]
- The 1 is dodgeable and people will get better at rolling out of it, so place it smartly. [4:06]
- Heroes with blinks or dashes, like Sinclair's teleport, are hard to land the 1 on. [4:36]
- A guaranteed time to land the 1 is when the enemy is meleeing a minion. [4:36]
- Skip Arcane Surge: stamina items feel needed on paper but she has her 2 for movement and barely dashes, so it is an inefficient crutch that hurts when you take it away. [5:06]
- Baba is a tempo brawler, played like a Silver or Yamato style hero: brawl and play actively. [6:09]
- She has zero clear speed without her 1, which is one reason Radiant (a classic tempo item) feels good on her. [6:09]
- She gets so much spirit power from her 1 that broad spirit power items like Arcane are less worth it, though you want some balance for percentage spirit later. [6:09]
- Mistake: over fishing for poke when you could have dropped the scalding pot and hit the wave when no one showed up. [6:41]
- Broad spirit items mostly add to your 2 and your ult, so they are low value. [6:41]
- Early in lane the player threw the 1 only when it was a reliable hit (good); mid lane they forced 1s, so the on hit bonus was never up and damage dropped. [7:11]
- If you know Sinclair's assistant is down, look for scalding; if it is up, look for silence. [7:11]
- Right click pauses brewing, and you can hold the pause indefinitely. [8:12]
- Make sure you hit Sinner's Sacrifice when clearing; Baba's own clear is poor. [9:18]
- Be careful staying in one spot for long; the enemy is unlikely to be farming alone, and a Sinclair fully committing with his ult could be scary. [9:49]
- "Tempo" means you need to be fed and constantly fighting, not playing to scale and perma farm. [10:20]
- Good play: brew a silence and come in on the target with the ult. [10:53]
- Later in the game it is really important to land your 1 before ulting for the extra spirit power. [10:53]
- Ability order: one point each in 1, 2, 3 (captioned "2 one two three"), then probably two points in your 2, then max the ult; skipping the second point in 2 until after the ult is maxed is also fine. [11:24]
- After the ult, max the brew (3), then the last one, probably the 1. [11:24]
- The legs upgrades on the 2 are not needed for a long time. [11:24]
- Her ult feels like a teamfight ability but is really a 1v1 tool. [11:55]
- Mistake: rotating out of a lane you were winning; ahead in farm and kills you should stay and pressure the tower. Rotating sacked the tower and split the wave between three people, which is very soul inefficient. [11:55]
- Melee builds are not real on heroes that cannot guarantee melees; Abrams's "melee" build is really gun with guaranteed melee off his charge. [12:28]
- Hitting yourself with the soothing brew is good play. [12:28]
- How the ult stacks: the notches on the bar are stacks, and each stack adds more damage, so the more stacks the more damage. [13:29]
- The last bar of the ult adds roughly another 150 damage and about one more second of pigeon time, so do not end the ult early; stay on the target and get every bar. [14:00]
- In the reviewed fight the ult did 369 of a max around 500, losing about 150 to 180 damage by ending it early. [14:31]
- Melee trades are fine at this rank, but trade with light melee so the enemy cannot light melee and then parry you. [14:31]
- Mistake: sitting on 4k souls and then buying Radiant (a tier 3) is a badly timed spike; do not fight while sitting on unspent souls, and the player missed the Rift too. [15:01]
- Do not forsake ult max. Maxing the 3 into the ult is fine; maxing the 1 or 2 first is not. [15:31]
- Delegate the side wave to a teammate: Baba feels bad in side lane because she has zero wave clear without hitting her 1 on a hero. [16:03]
- When you have a lot of stolen spirit power, go steal enemy jungle farm; it is one of the few times you can farm efficiently. [16:33]
- Not buying hurts your healing and damage across the board; that is not the time to fight. [17:03]
- Baba is very strong in the mist with her 2 up, while everyone else has limited stamina there. [17:33]
- With teammates dead and the enemy team likely running at you after a kill, break off: two negative waves and a wave crashing into your Walker make the fight bad. [18:04]
- Fight for the Rift; missing it repeatedly is a mistake. [19:04]
- You cannot 1v2 after using your ult. [19:35]
- Spirit Snatch is a good item on her. [20:05]
- Do not buy Healing Booster just to build Radiant; the numbers do not make it worth it, even if good players buy it. [20:05]
- Do not sideline on Baba: you have zero split push and farm potential, farm at about 50% efficiency, and do around 77 DPS to objectives vs about 180 on a real side laner. [21:05]
- When alone at an enemy Walker, just hit the Walker and leave instead of cancelling it. [21:35]
- Hitting Sinner's Sacrifice is really important on Baba; her 2 heavy melee is AoE, so in spots like the bell tower she can farm several at once. [22:37]
- When multi farming Sinner's Sacrifice, melee one first in normal mode, then use the AoE melee, so they do not sync up and you clear the first on its light without breaking the second. [22:37]
- Use the soothing brew as a shield while farming Sinner's Sacrifice. [23:08]
- In your 2, control jump to height makes you nearly uncatchable, so you can dive enemies in their face. [23:08]
- Mid was the right call, but finish the fight before disengaging instead of waffling. [23:38]
- You should not be able to full tank the whole enemy team; buy yourself space. [24:08]
- Clear the wave before walking up to mid boss, so you have wave pressure. [24:38]
- Use the scalding brew on the mid boss because its shred works on it. [24:38]
- If a fragile teammate like Vindicta dies, mid boss is undoable, so peeling for her is fine; otherwise go boss with the team. [24:38]
- Do not put Compressed Cooldown on your ult unless the ult is on cooldown; you will upgrade it to Superior soon, so put it on your 1 instead. [25:41]
- Ult with your 2 active, because you are passive while ulting and the 2 gives debuff resist and melee resist. [26:22]
- You cannot parry during your ult, and smart enemies will heavy melee you if they cannot CC you out of it. [26:22]
- The ult hitbox is a cylinder with no height limit, so use your 2 jumps to stay in the air around the fight and collect bird stacks without real danger (tip from Risen). [26:52]
- During the ult you can be stunned but not parried. [27:22]
- Mistake: repeatedly dropping waves. Push waves, including neutral waves, before rotating; otherwise you lose pressure and two full waves of souls. [27:52]
- Use your light melee during your 2; the displacement is very strong. [28:25]
- The heavy melee in your 2 is very easy to parry; strafing A/D then heavy makes it a little less parryable. [28:25]
- Light melee to bait the enemy parry, then once their parry is down rip heavies freely. [28:25]
- Reactive procs when you get parried, which is one reason to buy it. [28:57]
- Look for base guardians when you crack the base. [28:57]
- Baba excels at 1v1s; she beats any spirit hero 1v1 by taking all their spirit power and has spirit resist scaling. [29:28]
- Her ult needs line of sight, which is awkward with multiple targets in teamfights. [30:00]
- In 1v1s where you are ripping heavies, brew scalding for the minus spirit resist. [30:00]
- Stealing around 103 spirit power from someone (before it is multiplied on yourself) is why Baba is so strong. [31:00]
- Max ranging your 1 is good, because a 1 thrown up close gives enemies time to react and dash out; just not so far that they end up outside it. [31:38]
- The player's late game damage problem came only from missing 1s. [32:38]
- Mystic Expansion feels good, at least tier one: on the 1 it makes the AoE giant, it increases how far your light melees displace people, and gives bigger bird range. [32:38]
- Spirit power stripped from you lowers your max magazine but does not unload the current one. [33:15]
- Into heroes with i-frames like Mina or Mirage, time a silence pot with your ult so they cannot dodge it; otherwise do not toss birds on people who have i-frames up. [33:47]
- People will get better at buying Counterspell and Ethereal Shift against Baba; they are underrated counters now. [33:47]
- Most people are not buying dispel, which you definitely need against this hero. [34:17]
- The default build is not bad as a simple left to right build. [34:49]
- Spirit Sap feels amazing into a spirit lane or any spirit nuker like Sinclair. [35:20]
- Overflow is the one item in the build he never wanted. [35:50]
- His game build: Counterspell and Reactive (Warp Stone if Counterspell is not good), then cooldown, Boundless Spirit, then duration. [35:50]
- Magic Carpet is not good on her; it is better for Sinclair. [36:21]
- The Fake Idol build is decent but its ability point order is atrocious. [36:52]
- Alternative build: Piggies; skip Echo Shard unless you want to play true support spamming shields. [37:22]
- Buy Rapid Rounds in lane and upgrade it to Swift Striker later. [37:22]
