# Deadlock Is Heading In The Wrong Direction
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-08-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eQrK5gJ5jOg

## Covers
Opinion video on four problems with the game before the delayed big patch: ranked matchmaking (demote protection shields, win streaks, duo queue at high rank), one-dimensional macro (AFK farm or deathball, Rift too strong), spirit-dominated itemization (Warden's Boundless Spirit build, weak 6k gun items) and movement power creep.
Mostly opinion, but it carries concrete facts about the rank system, the item meta and movement that a player can use.

## Claims
- The big patch expected in late August was delayed until the end of September. [0:00]
- Demote protection plus win streaks mean everyone climbs in the long run; by one calculation you can reach Eternus from any rank with about a 38% win rate if you play enough games. [1:01]
- There are win streak bonuses but no loss streaks, which is a net positive rating gain for everyone, so ranks inflate as a season goes on. [1:01]
- There are two demote protection shields for each sub rank and five for each major rank. [1:32]
- After reaching a new major rank you can lose five games in a row before dropping, and one win puts you straight back with five fresh shields, so you can hold a rank with about a 20% win rate. [2:03]
- Because a lucky win streak gets locked in, it is nearly impossible to derank and stay deranked; the longer the season runs, the more players of very different skill share a rank. [3:05]
- Under this system grinding many games climbs you as much as winning does, so a player with lots of free time ends up the same rank as a better player who plays less. [3:35]
- The coach expects player quality at a given rank to keep dropping through the season until the season reset, with about a week of balanced games after the soft reset. [4:36]
- Personal performance does not affect rating: the best and worst players on a winning team get the same rating change. [5:37]
- His fixes for ranked: remove the shields, nerf win streaks slightly, and consider personal performance to some extent. [6:08]
- At high rank, duo queues face solo queue players at random, which gives the duo side an inherent advantage; top players show much higher win rates in duo than solo, which is how they reach 60 to 80% win rates. [6:08]
- In Eternus and other high rank lobbies you can often tell the winner from the start when one team has three duo stacks and the other is six solo players. [7:09]
- He wants stacking removed at Ascendant and above (ideally Phantom and above), or duo stacks matched only against other duo stacks; solo queue is mathematically disadvantaged. [7:09]
- At high rank the current meta has two modes: everyone AFK farms safely for about 20 minutes, or one team deathballs as six and fights constantly. [8:11]
- The team that deathballs almost always wins in random queues; split pushing or safe farming against a deathball usually gets you killed. [8:42]
- Rift fights feel mandatory: if you do not contest the Rift you lose, and the team that shows up with more players usually wins the first Rift fight and snowballs; if not the first, then the second decides it. [8:42]
- There are a few niche cases where split pushing is better, but usually the choice is join the Rift fight or lose. [9:13]
- The Urn is a lot of souls if one team gets it consistently, but it rarely decides games. [9:13]
- Stealing enemy jungle, including enemy Sinner's Sacrifices and camps, gives much less of a lead than winning a Rift fight. [9:43]
- In pro games macro matters more, because one player's misstep can end the game. [10:13]
- A big item rework patch has been announced by the developers. [10:44]
- The Warden build that made him meta (credited to Lightbringer, then Zergi at Night Shift) uses Boundless Spirit so spirit investment scales Mercurial Magnum's damage, even on a gun carry Warden. [10:44]
- On that Warden build, the bonus Mercurial Magnum damage from Boundless Spirit's spirit is higher than buying Spellslinger, Lucky Shot, and often even Crippling Headshot, so spirit outdamages 6k gun items while also scaling his other abilities. [12:17]
- Many spirit and vitality items are must-buys right now, for example Slowing Hex and Veil Walker (which also gives spirit). [13:19]
- Night Shift buy rates show the meta has been spirit for over two years. [13:19]
- The best gun-category items are bought by spirit heroes: Mystic Shot (spirit damage), Opening Rounds, Spirit Shredder Bullets on carries, and Tesla Bullets, which deal mostly spirit damage. [13:49]
- The best 6k gun item is Spiritual Overflow, and most buyers take it for the spirit power and spirit lifesteal, not the fire rate; raw gun items like Lucky Shot are barely bought. [14:20]
- About 80 to 90% of the roster deals more spirit damage than gun damage. [14:50]
- The main gun heroes are Venator, Haze and Vyper; Warden and Lady Geist also do a lot of gun damage; Wraith deals most damage through her cards and abilities, not her gun. [14:50]
- Full spirit heroes include Apollo, Celeste and Calico, Graves turned into a spirit hero, Mirage does 80 to 90% of his damage with spirit, and Seven, Pocket and Mo & Krill are spirit heroes; Abrams and Lash mix gun or melee with spirit. [15:20]
- Half the 6k gun items are not worth buying even on a gun hero, and half the 3k gun items are niche or bought by spirit nukers. [15:51]
- A movement tech, an edge boost directly off a corner, gives a large amount of distance with zero stamina and is not hard to pull off; even an enemy out of stamina can escape with it. [16:21]
- Movement slow items such as Slowing Bullets and Mystic Slow do little now, because they slow ground movement while heroes are in the air, and air strafe speed is not affected by movement slow. [16:52]
- Most meta heroes have strong movement abilities, and many bucket one heroes (the fastest movement speed group) are meta, Celeste being the example. [17:54]
- Tell-tale of a spirit meta: everyone buys spirit resist and nobody buys bullet resist or even Metal Skin. [18:56]
- Metal Skin is very strong against gun damage: if one enemy deals a lot of gun damage, buy Metal Skin and you are fine. [19:27]
- The coach buys some spirit resist in nearly 100% of games but often skips gun resist even against one or two gun heroes. [19:27]
