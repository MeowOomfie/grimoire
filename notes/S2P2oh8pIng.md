## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream on the December 2025 patch (before the January patch). Three players are coached: Arcanist on Victor (a scrim game), an Infernus player laning with Billy, and an Abrams player.
Themes: fighting only over objectives, side lanes over mid mosh pits, urn and comeback urn decisions, Infernus ignite-before-dash, Abrams light melee and leading with Shoulder Charge, denies, wave pressure and farming efficiency. Side talk on Wraith spirit spike builds, Sinclair orb timing and the tilt mindset.
