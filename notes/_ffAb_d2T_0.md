## Covers
Graves, coached by Midknight: a VOD review of an Ascendant-rank Graves player (one student), posted 2026-02-23.
Themes: Restorative Shot being a bad buy, overextending as a two-stamina hero, running at towers and walkers before they can be broken, updating the urn call when the team is missing, confirming urn souls, Echo Shard versus Refresher, flex slots and tempo items, and taking game-deciding fights inside your base on height. Side notes on Haze, McGinnis, Silver itemization and draft bans.

## Claims
Graves, lane and items:
- Never try to parry a Haze melee off Sleep Dagger. [0:32]
- Haze is a really bad matchup for Graves: stealing her gun damage doesn't matter because her damage comes from Fixation. [1:03]
- Going back in at half HP with little stamina is the issue. [1:03]
- Sitting on 800 souls means you are sitting on a buy; you also died with 800 and didn't buy while dead, which is a really bad habit. [1:03]
- He hates Restorative Shot here: there is no world you can go within 17 m of a McGinnis or Haze and not take more than 40 damage. [1:33]
- Shooting McGinnis is much higher priority than Haze, because Haze's damage comes from Fixation while McGinnis' gun does a huge amount. [2:35]
- Don't shoot Haze under her tower there; if you don't shoot her, the tower won't aggro you. Once it has aggroed, reset behind the wheelbarrow. [3:05]
- He likes Rusted Barrel a lot and hates Restorative Shot. [3:05]
- If you were going for a Restorative Shot proc, you overextended without your Celeste nearby for no reason, and that is exactly why you don't buy it. [3:36]
- With 13 stacks of Fixation on you, walking to the tower was a zero pressure death. [4:05]
- Parrying the tower is good, but not when both enemy laners are next to it. [4:36]
- Extra Regen is his preferred defensive item on Graves. [4:36]
- Spirit Lifesteal won't provide enough value at this point in the game; it is good, but not yet. [5:06]
- Just hit the wave instead of dying on tower for fun. [5:06]
- He doesn't buy Recharge Rush until after at least one spike, realistically after two. [6:07]

Graves, fights and objectives:
- You are not down much in souls because of the tower, but you shouldn't run at them: McGinnis can walk over, they have both ults, your Celeste is not there, and you have zero siege potential on the walker. [6:37]
- Grab their Sinner's Sacrifice right off spawn. [7:07]
- The issue was trying to shove the wave, which let them rotate in. [7:07]
- On the Sinner's Sacrifice, once you get it to full, light melee it to break it and leave. [7:07]
- That is a bad fight: you run at them for an even 2v2 at best, their mid can rotate over easily, and you are so far behind you don't even win the 2v2. [8:07]
- He thinks you need Split Shot on Graves because your farming is too slow without it. [8:37]
- Melee farming the small denizens saves you some creep damage, but the melee travel time slows your farm too much to be worth it, since the small creeps don't do much damage. [8:37]
- He recommends Lightbringer's Graves build or Leviathan's Graves build. [9:38]
- You are drastically overstaying: you can't kill the walker and you have zero kill potential on them. [9:38]

- At 12 minutes there is no way you kill the walker, and you have zero kill potential on a McGinnis under her walker, so don't push into it. [12:40]
- Your parries are insane. [13:11]
- People trade melees at this level; people don't learn not to trade heavy melees until Eternus, where they trade back with light melees. [13:11]
- That 2v3 fight was caused by running at the walker. [13:41]
- Taking a vent in front of a Haze shooting you is the main mistake there. [15:12]
- When she is rooted, just leave. [15:12]

- No Haze will melee you with 40 Fixation stacks on you, since one bullet does as much damage as their melee, so baiting a melee there is not the play. [15:42]

- When they have Superior Stamina, just get out. [16:42]

- Contesting the urn with two really bad waves and two teammates missing means a 2v3 or 2v4; just give the urn. [18:14]

- Buy Echo Shard or Refresher, not both, since they give very similar value. He would go Echo Shard, but better coaches would tell you Refresher. [22:20]
- Echo Shard gets better the lower the Elo, because it is better the less people melee the skulls. [23:23]
