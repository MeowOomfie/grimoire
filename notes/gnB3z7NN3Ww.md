# Eternus 6 Seven Build Guide | Best Build for the New Map!
channel: Natan  date: 2025-03-14  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gnB3z7NN3Ww

## Covers
Seven, new map update, skill order change (Storm Cloud max second), Cold Front dropped, Slowing Hex earlier, Monster Rounds, Superior Stamina air dashes, Ethereal Shift, flex slot plan, Shadow Weave, Spellslinger bug

## Claims
- Skill order changed: max Storm Cloud second instead of stun second, but still put the second point in stun for the AoE; Storm Cloud is maxed at 19k souls. [0:00]
- Cold Front is no longer bought anywhere, because the patch removed its double damage to creeps so it no longer one shots waves and camps, which is why it was broken. [0:30]
- Cold Front can still do a lot of fight damage, helped by Power Surge shredding spirit resist, but it is too inconsistent; you are not guaranteed value from it against heroes like Lash, Pocket, Yamato or Holliday. [1:00]
- Slowing Hex now goes in the spirit slots earlier, where Cold Front used to be, with Improved Cooldown also moved earlier. [1:00]
- Improved Cooldown in that early slot is a bit better if you want to farm more, since it shaves Power Surge cooldown. [1:30]
- Mystic Vulnerability is optional: its damage is not great for Power Surge but it is pretty good for the ult, so take it in games where you will get a lot of ult value, when they cannot really cancel it. [1:30]
- Monster Rounds is now bought because early on most of your damage is from your gun. [1:30]
- On the new map you cannot really be ganked in lane anymore since it is an isolated 2v2, so hard pushing and shoving the Guardian is no longer punished; be as strong as possible in the 2v2 and take the Guardian if you can. [2:00]
- Superior Stamina is "completely broken" and stronger on the new map, because there is more verticality and fewer Guardians and Walkers to run to, so its escape gives safety when you get caught. [2:00]
- Use Superior Stamina by air dashing, which moves you much faster; touching the floor resets your dashes. [2:31]
- Superior Stamina also helps with Storm Cloud: double or triple jump for height to get the ult angle, then escape with two air dashes when done. [2:31]
- Ethereal Shift is broken and likely to be nerfed: about a 28 second cooldown with a 3 second duration, so a 25 second panic button to dodge a Lash ult or a charged Yamato Power Slash and stall for your team. [3:03]
- With Infuser, Slowing Hex and Fleetfoot as actives (plus Healing Rite until Debuff Reducer), you still have an active slot open now that Cold Front is gone, making it easier to fit Unstoppable or Warp Stone. [3:35]
- Buy Healing Rite as soon as you need it in lane, even as the first item after a bad level 1 trade; it keeps you on the map, still gives sprint speed mid game, and lets you out heal jungle camps. [4:07]
- All slots are filled at 12k; with no flex slot yet for Improved Cooldown, just skip it. [4:07]
- Sell order for upgrades: Monster Rounds for Soul Shredder Bullets, Healing Rite for Debuff Reducer, Hollow Point Ward for Fleetfoot; Improved Spirit is an upgrade using its own slot. [4:07]
- If you randomly have 3k souls after Healbane and cooldown, you can pick up an item early; Superior Stamina goes in a second flex slot or replaces Extra Health, and Ethereal Shift can replace Infuser. [4:38]
- At 24k the build needs only one flex slot. [4:38]
- After that choose freely: Bullet Armor, Mystic Vulnerability, Superior Cooldown upgrade (nearly 100% Power Surge uptime, only 2 seconds downtime), Boundless Spirit, or Mystic Slow against fire rate and gun heroes such as Haze, Infernus and Wraith. [5:08]
- If you want a lot of value from the ult, pick up Unstoppable. [5:08]
- The double stun direction is possible but the stun is only maxed at 33k, late in the game. [5:38]
- Shadow Weave (untested by him) may be good on the new map because there are fewer safety points for enemies, so catching someone from invisibility more likely gets a kill. [5:38]
- Spellslinger is currently bugged and gives double the intended value (about 30% lifesteal); he is not a fan of exploiting it. [6:08]
