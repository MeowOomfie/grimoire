## Covers
Three minute Dynamo guide (May 2026): why to play him, abilities, combos, lane, mid and late game plans, recommended builds and a Quantum Entanglement tip. For players new to Dynamo as a support.

## Claims
- Pros: a support who can turn the game around on his own with his ultimate, and his 2 gives him a huge skill ceiling. [0:00]
- Cons: good enemies double jump his kick, his abilities (especially the ult) are very telegraphed and need planning, and he is fat and easy to hit. [0:00]
- His 1, the kick, sends a pulse that damages enemies in front of him and slightly knocks them up; with a couple of points it also reduces bullet resist and movement speed. [0:00]
- His 2, Quantum Entanglement, makes him briefly intangible and teleports him where he looks; at base it restores one stamina. [0:30]
- Maxed, Quantum Entanglement also removes all ultimate debuffs and replenishes one charge of charged abilities for allies he uses it on. [0:30]
- Normal cast Quantum Entanglement to go alone; alt cast it to take nearby allies with you. [0:30]
- His 3, Rejuvenating Aura, heals in an area around him; at first he cannot dash or use other abilities in it, but maxed he can act freely. [0:30]
- His ultimate, Singularity, is an area stun around him; it is a channel, so any stun or curse on him stops it. [0:30]
- Bread and butter combo: Warp Stone into ult. Waiting to Warp Stone gives more distance and more information, and catches enemies more by surprise. [1:00]
- Item combo: when a teammate is mispositioned or about to die after a debuff, Rescue Beam them in, then cast your 2 to keep them alive. [1:00]
- Lane: he is a weak laner, so mostly survive, farm boxes and use your heal to keep you and your partner healthy; kills are hard before ult. [1:00]
- In lane, find angles where your kick hits both enemies and the wave; that greatly increases your poke and your wave priority. [1:30]
- Mid game: he is a good roamer, especially with Rescue Beam; the alt cast of his 2 saves allies and his ult turns fights. Sit on roofs near your carry or where a fight might break out. [1:30]
- Always play for Urns. [2:00]
- A solo ult for a pick in the mid game is worth it if you use the resulting priority to run Urn; it is not worth it if the victim respawns while your ult is still on cooldown. [2:00]
- Late game: look for a big ult; your main goal is to catch two or more enemies, ideally more. Minor goals are healing allies and keeping them alive with your 2. [2:00]
- Simpler build: Hydration's build, fairly left to right, maxes heal second instead of the 2 for a much lower skill floor. [2:30]
- Harder build: Piggy's build has many options and maxes the 2 before the 3; 2 max is very strong if you are confident on the hero. [2:30]
- Bonus tip: you can alt cast your 2 to save allies behind walls. [2:30]
