## Claims
- Seven is not as strong as he used to be but is not a bad hero; he has been reduced to an ultimate bot. [1:03]
- His 3 (Power Surge) is good, but you do not really build it; if you build it you are building your ultimate anyway. [1:03]
- Buy Battle Vest in lane against Paige, because her gun does a lot of damage. [1:03]
- In lane he was taking more damage from troopers than from heroes; kill the troopers. [2:36]
- Power Surge is useless at the start; Seven is naturally bad early but gets good later. [4:07]
- After lane he planned to sit on green (vitality) items for most of the game, with Healbane as a possible buy. [9:21]
- Once he had Toxic Bullets his damage was very high. [10:56]
- Without Unstoppable he can be knocked out of his ultimate; Paige had Knockdown, so she had to die before he could ult. [19:49]
- He has to be inside his ultimate to do damage with it. [19:49]
- The enemies are slowed while taking damage in his ball (Lightning Ball). [21:23]
- Build summary: the only way to win on this hero is to build up your Escalating Exposure stacks on people. [22:25]
- Use the ball in small areas like corridors. [22:25]
- Stun people (Static Charge) before you ult, then press 4. [22:55]
- The stun lasts 1.24 seconds and the ball slows them while they take damage, so ball plus stun is basically a 2 second stun. [22:55]
- An upgrade shown on screen gives the stun a much bigger radius, and the ball's radius is also large; together they are really strong. [23:27]
