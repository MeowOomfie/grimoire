# Ranking Every Hero In Deadlock From Strongest To Weakest - Halloween Patch
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-10-28
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYsMES2qT_c

## Covers
Metro's hero tier list for high rank (Eternus) on the Halloween patch of late October 2025, going through the roster alphabetically from Abrams to Yamato, with a "void" tier for the few balanced heroes.
For high rank players and anyone wondering why heroes that win in lower ranks fail at Eternus. Main theme: spirit nuke heroes and tanks dominate, gun carries are weak, and the patch gave slaps on the wrist and compensating buffs instead of real changes.

## Claims
- The tier list is for high rank (Eternus); heroes that feel strong in your games may not be doing well in high rank. [0:00]
- The Halloween patch gave small buffs to the D tier heroes of last patch and nerfed some S tier outliers, but left many heroes unaddressed. [0:31]
- In high rank half the roster feels insanely strong and half insanely weak; the "void" tier holds the few actually balanced heroes, and there are not many. [0:31]
- Abrams: A tier, not S. If played well he never dies, has great initiation and can steal mid boss with his ultimate; his only weakness is needing close range to kill. His only hit this patch was the Spirit Snatch nerf, an item he uses. [1:01]
- Bebop: A tier, same as Abrams. Tanks are very good now because they are the only heroes who survive all the incoming spirit nuke, and the high rank meta is mostly spirit nuke. [1:31]
- Billy: void tier (later grouped with B tier heroes), only mediocre even though he looks very strong on paper, which shows the balance is off. He was hit by the Spirit Snatch nerf but is otherwise unchanged and solid. [2:02]
- Calico: S tier, even stronger than before in Eternus despite the nerf. Gloom Bombs base damage and scaling went down, but every bomb after the first now hits for 65% instead of 50%, so landing four or more from level two Gloom Bombs does more damage than before. [2:33]
- Calico may feel weaker for an average Archon player who misses Gloom Bombs, but in Eternus she is "like Thanos", absolutely broken. [3:04]
- The Doorman: still insanely strong and very playable. His wall-pin headshot gun build was nerfed, but his Belt scaling got a compensating buff, so gun is a bit weaker and hybrid or support Doorman is even stronger than before. [3:04]
- Valve seems scared of over-nerfing, so compensating buffs leave strong heroes the same strength or, like Calico, stronger. [3:34]
- Drifter: still very bad in high rank. People group up in Eternus so isolated targets are rare and one of his abilities barely works; his small gun base damage and scaling buff came with a big passive nerf (4% per kill, and an assist now gives 1% instead of 3%), so he did not get better. [4:04]
- You cannot balance a game for low rank, because low rank players play suboptimally. [5:05]
- Dynamo: bad, mediocre at best. His tiny buffs (5% more fire rate after the teleport, slightly lower heal cooldown) changed nothing. [5:05]
- Seven: still one of the worst heroes. The small ball scaling increase did not help and ball builds feel awful; he only does well in low rank because players there do not move out of his ultimate, which is misplay, not strength. [5:37]
- Grey Talon: still bad, mediocre at best; nothing changed about him. [6:40]
- Haze: B tier, somewhat playable as a pick hero in high rank: run Silence Wave and Surge of Power, go in with Sleep Dagger, Silence Wave, then ultimate. [6:40]
- Haze is not viable as a carry in high rank because the meta is burst and she takes time to build damage, and mainly because she is one of the least tanky heroes, so even with all the farm she dies in one shot to spirit nuke. [7:10]
- Holliday: almost S tier (A tier last patch). Paradox got a bit weaker and people figured out Holliday is a "budget Paradox": crazy gun damage and more mobility, but not as tanky. [7:40]
- Infernus: A tier, not S anymore, a hot take that he is overrated now. He is one of two gun carries you can still play, and effectively the only left-click hero still playable, though 99% of his damage comes from spirit and his gun was nerfed quite hard. [8:11]
- Ivy: not strong in ranked and not a crazy carry. In pro play, where a team can funnel resources into her as the late game hard carry, she would rank higher; that does not happen in ladder games, even in Eternus. [8:43]
- Kelvin: still the most broken hero in the game. He was first pick or first ban for months and banned every game in Night Shift last week; the ultimate cooldown nerf (about 20 seconds) and one less regen in lane were a tiny slap on the wrist. [9:13]
- Lady Geist: B tier, viable in a hybrid role that tanks while dealing damage, not a hard carry; playable in comp without feeding, but far weaker than the meta heroes. [9:43]
- Mina: top tier, "Thanos in Eternus". She never dies, one-shots you at any point, is the best laner and wins lane and mid game for free, and has the most farm in the lobby nearly every game because she farms faster, sustains longer and kills faster. She has no weaknesses. [10:14]
- The Ethereal Shift nerf barely matters on Mina: she teleports out, Ethereal Shifts once, and has teleport again. [10:44]
- The overall trend: spirit nukes are better than anything else in the game, it is getting worse, and gun heroes fall behind. [10:44]
- Wraith: low, one of the weakest laners if not the weakest. She does insane damage after about 30 minutes, which is why she performs well up to Ascendant, but most high rank games end in 20 to 25 minutes, so she gets bullied all game and never gets the farm. [10:44]
- Lower rank players should learn to end games faster; dying to a Wraith after minute 30 to 35 means you failed to end the game, not that Wraith is overpowered. [11:44]
- Lash: D tier in Eternus, with no buffs. When the enemy has a Lash you buy Counterspell, which counters his slam and his ultimate; he is bad in lane and useless once the enemy has Counterspell. [12:15]
- Counterspell is a skillful item and should stay this strong; the problem is the lack of Lash buffs. [12:46]
- McGinnis: rated D tier last patch and only a tiny bit better now that Medicinal Specter lingers a second; still way below playable. [13:17]
- The gap between the void and B tier heroes and the top heroes is massive in high rank: a team of six top tier heroes beats a team of six from the lower tiers every time in Eternus. [13:48]
- Pro play is a different game; a pro tier list would rank Ivy higher but otherwise look very similar. [13:48]
- Mirage: somewhat playable but worse than the S tier heroes. Most top heroes win the lane or are very tanky like Abrams; Mirage used to be one of the best laners and is now mid. [14:19]
- Mo & Krill: A tier. You get value by pressing a button and you do not die; he has one of the best carry pick-offs in the game and gets value regardless of his money, with Sand Blast and Scorn keeping him alive. [14:50]
- Paige: A tier, performing very well in Eternus and in pro play, maybe better than Mo & Krill and Infernus. Her impact is hard to notice (overshields, Guardian Ward, lockdowns that stop chases), but in the replay she is insane. [15:21]
- Paige is one of the best laners in the game; paired with a Yamato or Mina who can sit in front and tank, she gives them overshield and higher fire rate. [15:51]
- Paradox: absolutely broken, the most broken hero other than Kelvin (third most broken before the patch). The tiny gun nerf was a slap on the wrist; she has about 2,000 HP at minute 10, more than about 80% of the roster and a wall. [16:21]
- Pocket: broken, close to Mina, a spirit nuker who does not die. He is a bit weaker in lane than Mina; with her lane he would be even better, since Mina must hit shots while Pocket presses an AoE button and has several free escapes. [16:51]
- Pocket has been broken for months, even before Mina came out, and is not addressed because he is harder to learn and loses in low rank; in Eternus he does the most damage nearly every game just by pressing his ultimate. [17:23]
- Shiv: absolutely broken despite a below 50% win rate, which comes from him being hard to learn. Good Shiv players one-shot you, do not die, can execute anyone in a team fight at a third health, and have some of the highest mobility in the game with the right click. [17:54]
- Sinclair: poor in ladder, but should not be buffed because he is a counter pick; once the game has draft he will do better, since against heroes with strong ultimates to steal (for example Warden or Seven) he gets a lot of value. [18:25]
- Queuing Sinclair on ladder is a coin flip, and many of the heroes whose ultimates he wants are not meta now, so he is doing even worse; he sees some pro play in comps with good ultimates to steal. [18:56]
- Victor: at least mid A tier, maybe not S. He was nerfed quite hard and fell out of pro play, but he still does not die. [18:56]
- Vindicta: crazy hero, first pick or first ban in most pro games, rated a bit lower for ladder because she needs a team that plays around her. Each nerf comes with a buff elsewhere: after Stake was nerfed, Assassinate and Flight were buffed, so she is back to sniping with Rapid Recharge and flying. [19:28]
- Viscous: B tier in ranked, playable but very hard to learn, and even in Eternus most players do not reach his potential; the only viable play style is the one-shot build, and for one-shotting Mina and Pocket are just better. [20:00]
- Vyper: C tier, a "dark horse" pick but no higher. After her buffs she is a lane bully with a lot of lane damage, but she falls off hard after lane; neither carry nor spirit Vyper shows up much, she is unpopular in high rank, and she probably needs more reworks. [20:30]
- Warden: B tier after his buff; gun Warden is a bit more playable again, but gun carries in general are weak, and ultimate-focused Warden is about as bad as gun or worse. [21:00]
- Yamato: one of the few viable carries, rated too low in the last tier list. She is a spirit carry who wants a lot of farm: build spirit and Spirit Snatch, deal spirit burst damage, and survive with her ultimate. [21:32]
- Gun carries do not feel playable in Eternus right now other than maybe Infernus. [22:02]
- The heroes broken for months, especially Mina, Paradox, Kelvin and Pocket, plus Shiv, got no real nerfs while the weakest heroes got no meaningful buffs; the meta is the same as before but spirit nukers are even better. [22:34]
