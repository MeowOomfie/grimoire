# The REAL Way To Get Ahead In Deadlock (New Macro Guide)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-09-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DCO1AQQjZG0

## Covers
Jungle and farming changes after the map rework patch: speed Sinner's Sacrifice and how to spot it, new gold statue buffs, crit spots on jungle creeps, parrying tier three creeps, moving creeps and melee farming.
Best farm routes on the Hidden King and Archmother sides, the purple pit and the Hidden King tower at 8 minutes, escape routes to the secret shop, and where to gank enemy carries.
For players who want to farm faster in the new map and know when jungling is worth it.

## Claims
- Sinner's Sacrifice now has a speed version and a normal version; which one spawns is random. [0:30]
- To tell a speed Sinner's Sacrifice: heavy melee it three times; if orange lights glow on or before the fourth punch, it is a speed one. [0:30]
- On a speed Sinner's Sacrifice the timing is tighter: punch between the middle light and the fourth light instead of on the third to last light. [1:00]
- A speed Sinner's Sacrifice gives no more souls or buffs than a normal one, it is only faster to farm. [1:00]
- If no orange lights show by the fourth punch it is a normal Sinner's Sacrifice: heavy melee on the third to last light to get the buffs, as before. [1:32]
- Gold statue buffs now include bullet resist, spirit resist, ability range and movement speed, on top of the old spirit power, weapon damage, fire rate, cooldown reduction and health. [1:32]
- Movement speed is the rarest and most broken gold statue buff; spirit power is still really good; the bullet and spirit resist buffs are minor. [2:02]
- Track your buffs through the game; Sinner's Sacrifices are worth even more farming than before. [2:32]
- Every jungle camp now has different creeps, and every creep has a crit spot (for example the glowing eye in the middle of the trash barrels, a small eye on the bottom of the jars). [2:32]
- New hit feedback: a body hit glows red, a crit spot hit glows yellow/orange; if the creep glows orange while you shoot, you are hitting the crit spot and farming as fast as possible. [3:04]
- Tier three creeps have new, dangerous attacks, for example glowing mushrooms on the ground that deal a lot of damage if you step in them. [3:04]
- You can now parry jungle creeps: go to melee range, shoot one, wait a second, then melee, and when they are stacked you can get a triple parry so they stop attacking you for a long time. [3:34]
- Ranged-only creeps such as the lanterns have no melee animation, so parrying them is pointless; big floating creeps are usually ranged, the small plants do have a melee animation. [4:05]
- Do not bother parrying on tier two camps, they die fast anyway; parry is only really good to avoid damage on tier three camps. [4:05]
- Jungle creeps now move around somewhat randomly; on a melee hero like Billy only melee when two or more are close together, because a spread-out camp means hitting just one. [4:36]
- Melee-attacking tier three creeps tend to bunch up, so you can sometimes melee two or all three at once like the old camps. [5:06]
- Soul value per creep is comparable to before, but some camps got better, for example the tier three camp on the dock side now has its three creeps closer together, where it used to be the worst tier three camp. [5:06]
- Almost every creep camp is now worth farming and most die faster, so souls per minute from the jungle are a bit higher; it is worth farming the jungle more. [5:37]
- Route one, Hidden King side in Chinatown: tier three camp, straight up to the next camp, then the next tier three camp (the old Hidden King tier three, now lanterns), with almost no downtime between them. [6:08]
- That route continues camp to camp, to the high ground camp, down to the next tier three camp, then down to the next tier two camp; the coach calls it probably the best route, and camps are more stacked on the Hidden King side. [6:38]
- Route two, also Hidden King side: a camp into three tier three camps in a row, all very close together. [7:09]
- Archmother side camps are further apart and harder to get value from, by design, because Valve wants an asymmetric map. [7:09]
- Archmother has always had a slightly lower ranked win rate than Hidden King. [7:09]
- Once draft mode comes, the team with first hero pick will probably not get Hidden King, so you trade better farm routes against the better first hero. [7:40]
- Archmother route: the camps beyond the bottom ones, then up, then drop down to the next two camps, which are close together. [7:40]
- The old double Sinner's Sacrifice farmed at 8 minutes, once the best farm in the game, is now outclassed by two other spots. [7:40]
- Archmother side has a "purple pit of doom", the riskiest camps on the map: gold satchels, boxes, two creep camps next to each other and two Sinner's Sacrifices. [8:11]
- Inside the purple pit you drain stamina and cannot hear; once out of stamina you cannot wall jump out easily. [8:11]
- The purple pit connects directly to the secret shop, which is the escape route if you get ganked: leave, spend your souls, then escape or die with them spent. [9:13]
- The purple pit spawns at 8 minutes; even if you cannot yet farm its two creep camps, it is worth going in for the double Sinner's Sacrifice and the boxes for their buffs. [9:44]
- The best farming spot in the game is the new tower in the Chinese section on the Hidden King side: three Sinner's Sacrifices, four lock boxes and two gold statues, with two sets of boxes on the way in. [9:44]
- The tower has only one way in and out (by zipline); jumping off is blocked. [10:15]
- As soon as you hit anything on the tower a gong plays that is heard far across the map, at least as far as someone near blue lane. [10:45]
- Because the only entry is the zipline, enemies can catch players going up; the coach killed a Haze on the zipline while farming it. [10:45]
- You lose a lot of health to the Sinner's Sacrifices in the tower, especially if you take them at 8 minutes. [11:16]
- To escape the tower, air dash down and go through the door, which puts you right at the secret shop to spend your souls. [11:16]
- The purple pit and the tower are for carries at 8 minutes, or for a player who is far behind after lane and needs to catch up; a support farming them leaves the team for a long time. [11:47]
- These two spots are also the best places to gank: at 8 minutes a Calico, Mina or Pocket can wait until the enemy carry has farmed it, then come up and kill them while they are low on HP. [11:47]
- The richer jungle also encourages more ganking, not only AFK farming. [12:17]
- Box spawn locations changed a lot; in mid lane boxes are further apart and harder to collect in bulk. [12:17]
- The basements are basically unchanged and still worth running; heavy boxes, worth double, sometimes spawn there. [12:47]
- Learn the three fastest farm routes: two on the Hidden King side, one on the Archmother side. [12:47]
- Creep waves are still king: they push the map and give the most farm on the map. [13:18]
- Push your lanes first and do the jungle route after you push a wave; do not AFK jungle the entire game. [13:50]
