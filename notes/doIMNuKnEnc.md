# Deadlock's Newest Patch Changed a Lot...
channel: Midknight
date: 2024-12-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doIMNuKnEnc

## Covers
Patch reaction by Midknight to the early December 2024 Deadlock patch: the new hero Raven, matchmaking options, item changes and hero changes, each with his verdict.

## Claims
New hero and systems:
- New hero Raven has a slow firing umbrella gun; his 2 launches him into the air and a recast shoots an exploding AOE shot like Grey Talon's arrow; his 3 is basically Wraith's Full Auto. [0:00]
- Raven's ult curses anyone who looks at him too long. [0:00]
- Raven's 1 passive does 40% extra damage (70% at max) to enemies with their back turned, gun and melee included; the active grenade stops enemies turning, locking them into the bonus damage. [0:00]
- The turn speed lock is a hard cap; raising your DPI does not let you turn faster. [0:30]
- New competitive preference setting is a band-aid for low player counts; he thinks it will push players away over time. [1:02]
- Party queue now has lane preferences. [1:32]
- Matchmaking now tries to give each team at least two front liners; a good start before draft. [1:32]
- Ability range now stacks diminishingly, so fewer extreme Dynamo or ability range maxing builds; good for game health. [1:32]
- Patron and its down state start with less HP that grows over the game, making it harder to burst in 45 to 50 minute games. [2:03]
- You can only get four soul orbs per wave, ending the double soaking meta in competitive play; normal players are not affected. [2:03]

Items:
- Berserker now builds into Frenzy, and Frenzy is a little more expensive; feels good. [2:33]
- Toxic Bullets bleed went from 2.5% to 2.75%; it was already a really good item and is now even better. [2:33]
- Restorative Locket cooldown went from 30 to 25 seconds; already very popular in high ELO and now even nicer. [2:33]
- Sharpshooter now lowers fire rate by 10% and adds 10 more weapon damage, so DPS is unchanged but you reload less; harder to confirm walker souls or many separate targets. [3:03]
- Sharpshooter now reduces movement speed by 0.5, which may nerf Sharpshooter Mirage builds and push it toward heroes like Vindicta. [3:03]
- Leech no longer gives cooldown reduction; it builds from Healbane and gives 60% anti-heal for 8 seconds on spirit damage. [3:35]
- Infuser lasts 2 seconds longer and swaps plus 50 health for plus 70 spirit shield; a decent buff, already very good on Infernus, especially in Enchanter's Barrier games. [3:35]
- Ethereal Shift now lets you float at 2 m per second; a slight but meaningful movement gain since you control when you enter it. [4:05]
- Silence Glyph now pierces all enemies in its path; after a grouping ult like Lash's you can silence several and they can do little; he expects it to be very powerful. [4:05]
- Arcane Surge got another needed nerf, its plus 20% values dropping to plus 18%. [4:35]
- Knockdown's stun is shorter on the ground but longer the higher the target was, so it counters snipers and flyers instead of being bought to cancel every channel. [4:35]
- Refresher only got 3 seconds added to its cast time; he calls it a dysfunctional item and expects a real nerf soon. [5:05]

Heroes:
- Bebop uppercut now locks enemies out of air control for 0.5 seconds instead of 1, a big anti gun Bebop change if it works, though in his testing he still could not move after being hit. [5:36]
- Grey Talon's trap is now Spirit Snare: it curses for 1.75 seconds and he can trigger it instantly with a charged shot; could be a problem in the hands of good Grey Talons. [6:06]
- Haze Sleep Dagger no longer stops movement, you walk at 2 m per second while asleep, and there is no falling asleep delay, so you can no longer cleanse it in the few frames after being hit; he does not expect a big difference. [6:38]
- Haze ult got 12 more seconds of cooldown and plus 6 instead of plus 7 spirit damage; he calls these placebo nerfs since Valve balances for Eternus where she sits near 50.3% win rate. [7:08]
- Infernus afterburn spirit scaling went from 0.5 to 0.6, about 20% more afterburn damage; a bigger buff than people think. [7:38]
- Kelvin's Frost Grenade now applies minus 40% stamina regen, strong in a duo lane with someone like Warden. [8:08]
- Lady Geist's Life Drain no longer silences at tier 3 but now has charges and can drain several people; her ult tier 2 gets the old tier 3, and the new tier 3 is a 15 m AOE silence for 3 seconds. [8:08]
- Lash Death Slam channel is 3 seconds longer, which helps targets slip away, but has 2 m more range and a wider cone; tier 2 cuts more cooldown and tier 3 now makes the impact area stun for 1 second. [9:08]
- McGinnis turrets now must be alive 4 seconds before they can die; turrets killed earlier are marked for death and explode after 4 seconds; turret fire rate cut 20%. [9:39]
- Mirage got a new model; Fire Scarabs can hit more than one person, the debuff drains health over time, re-hitting extends it, and the launch window is longer and works with Duration Extender. [10:09]
- Pocket can only stay in the suitcase 1.5 seconds instead of 2; Affliction DPS is cut a lot but it now applies 18% current health on hit (more at tier 3); overall a Pocket nerf, better only at fight starts. [10:40]
- Seven's ult at max level now lets him move and left click to fire a lightning strike that knocks people back from the center without canceling channels. [11:11]
- Viscous Cube cooldown 6 seconds lower; Puddle Punch is bigger, slows more and at max counts as a heavy melee, greatly increasing its damage. [11:11]
- Warden has the highest win rate in high ELO; spirit power scaling on fire rate went 25 to 22, and ult resist went 60% to 30% but max level adds 50%, making him weaker early and stronger late. [11:43]
- Wraith ult now lifts and slams enemies, silencing and disarming for 2.5 seconds; at max it bounces to one enemy within 20 m instead of a big AOE, a near guaranteed double without four man ult potential. [12:15]
