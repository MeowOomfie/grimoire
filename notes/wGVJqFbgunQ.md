# So, I Tried Silver After Gun Nerfs...
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-05-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wGVJqFbgunQ

## Covers
Silver, aquatiq. Commentary over a full Silver game (a loss) after gun nerfs, laning into Viper and Paige with Mirage, and Shiv winning mid.
Item path from extra regen and Close Quarters to Silencer, Inhibitor and Phantom Strike, the Phantom Strike plus boot kick combo, how Silver scales, and farm notes. Patch date 2026-05-20.

## Claims
- Silver has very high impact; he rates her near the top of the tier list even after the gun nerfs. [0:37]
- Against a Viper in lane he wants Battle Vest as soon as possible. [1:08]
- Build order shown: extra regen, then Close Quarters. [1:39]
- Hit the wave so the enemy cannot crash it under your tower; otherwise you take the damage. [1:39]
- Silver does not confirm souls well. [2:10]
- He first thought Viper was too strong in lane for Silver, who is stronger late; later in the same lane he changed his mind and thought Silver might be overpowered in lane. [2:41]
- Silver's gun is what makes her scale: the damage later is insane and she does not fall off. [2:41]
- Her bola slow is very strong. [2:41]
- Mirage is one of the top three laners in the game because his mark and scarabs keep him alive so long. [3:45]
- Got Trophy Collector early. [5:17]
- He did not want to be in Silver's other form at that moment because he could not shoot anything in it. [6:20]
- Bought Stalker (as captioned) in the early mid game. [8:28]
- Healing Booster lets him stay at full health at all times. [9:29]
- Took Express Shot next in the mid game. [11:39]
- Bought Restorative Locket and planned Hunter's Aura next. [13:45]
- Trading teammates getting killed for two enemy walkers is usually fine. [15:48]
- Even when not far ahead, check whether you can take the enemy's double camps without dying. [17:21]
- An enemy with zero spirit resist took 2k damage from three knives; heroes like that should buy Spirit Resilience. [17:52]
- Fighting for a team fight is better than going to save a walker you cannot reach in time. [18:25]
- After the enemy loses three players, look for mid boss, but heal first if you are low. [18:55]
- Item plan: Silencer, then Inhibitor, then Phantom Strike or Berserker; Berserker's bullet resist could also be useful. [19:25]
- Combo: Phantom Strike onto someone, then boot kick, and they die instantly. [19:25]
- Silencer did not stop one enemy getting her ult out; Inhibitor is what he needed against that hero. [23:06]
- He thinks one breakable box plus a vending machine gives more souls than one tier three camp enemy. [23:36]
- When there is a lot of farm on the map and the enemy is not far ahead, farm instead of fighting. [28:19]
- Later option: sell Slowing Hex or Stalker for Toxic Bullets, or take Unstoppable instead of Stalker. [28:19]
- He thinks Toxic Bullets is weak right now but wants it to bleed out healing heroes; with teammates on Decay too, the team has a lot of anti-heal. [28:49]
- Verdict: a pretty good Silver game with 43,000 damage despite the loss. [31:23]
