## Covers
Calico, Midknight, coaching review of a Phantom-rank Calico player.
Themes: ganking and invading, the 2-parry combo to farm kills on sinners, hitting Gloom Bombs (aim at feet, do not rush them), itemization (gun spike versus rushing Arctic Blast), wave clear, shrine calls and picks. Patch period of mid September 2026.

## Claims
Player 1 (Calico, Phantom):
- Be a little less afraid to commit after your 2 when the enemy has isolated himself; stay on top of him and punish the missed parry. [0:30]
- Putting an early point in your 2 for its healing is a waste: it is less healing than your tier 2 cat and only better for 3 to 4 minutes of lane, and it slows down your AP spikes since late game you put AP in it last. [1:01]
- When the tier one camps spawn at 2 minutes, look for a gank, on either the enemy's tier 2 side or yours. [1:33]
- When mid looks gankable, box farm on the roofs above it so you are already there if they stay. [2:04]
- Even without a kill, ganking mid relieves pressure that would otherwise lose your tower at about 5 minutes. [2:34]
- Teleporting to gank is good on Calico, but you need a good lane anchor; Mina is one of the better anchors. In solo queue you do not know if your laner can anchor reliably. [2:34]
- Greeding while the wave is actively hitting your tower costs tower damage you did not need to take. [3:36]
- Diving mid through the vents is very strong for free kills, which is why he prefers left lane on Calico: you can fight for the enemy mid tier one or gank mid through the vent. [3:36]
- Do not commit to a target you have no kill potential on; finish the farm instead. [5:08]
- An Ivy with two points in Stone Form is effectively unkillable for Calico, since she can Stone Form every Gloom Bomb. Ignore her unless you buy Silence Wave, or unless she is very low and you can finish with gun and 2 damage. [5:39]
- The sewer box run is fine but gives an awkward buy timing. You want to come out of the sewers at 8 minutes to invade and kill an enemy sinner camp. [5:39]
- If you are coming from behind the enemy, do not lead with your 2; a light melee is enough, or wait and keep the 2 as a gap closer once they create space. [6:09]
- After buying Cold Front you are strong while sinners are up: shove the wave and then look in their jungle. [6:40]
- Shoving the wave gives your teammates space to secure your sinners while you look in the enemy's. [7:11]
- You are missing a lot of Gloom Bombs because you aim at the top half of the target. Gloom Bombs throw a little higher than your crosshair, so aim at their feet. [7:11]
- After a kill, leave instead of lingering; staying is a waste of your time. [8:13]
- Silence Wave is good in a Victor, Dynamo, Ivy comp, arguably better than Focus Lens there, and it is also really good into Lash. [9:18]
- Change your zip grab bind away from the default space bar so you do not grab ropes by accident and lose momentum. He uses C. [9:49]
- Classic mistake: waiting too heavily for the comeback Rift when the enemy knows you are going for it. [10:20]
- A shrine at 12 minutes makes a game genuinely unwinnable. The enemy should have taken it even dying for it, since early respawn timers are short. [10:50]
- He does not like buying Arctic Blast next. You need your gun spike first; go Arctic Blast when you need your green spike. Buying it early is a crutch for not hitting Gloom Bombs and is not valuable before Gloom Bombs are maxed. [11:20]
- Good micro: using your 2 and then Gloom Bombs. Many Calicos, including him, lead with Gloom Bombs when they do not need to, which makes them harder to hit. [11:50]
- Run the other half of the tunnels when box farming; the boxes on the side you ran last time have not respawned. [13:06]
- Farm the trophy room in general, but not when the wave is about to be neutral and you are sitting on a 1k bag near your spike; path to the shop through the tunnels instead. [13:36]
- The 2-parry is very strong against everyone except Ivy, who can melee-cancel it. Look to 2-parry enemies on their sinners as much as you can; stare at the map and wait for them to pop up. [14:12]
- You missed the 8 minute and 13:30 sinner respawn timers. [14:12]
- He likes how you commit with your ult now. [14:42]
- Viper is one of the most slept-on heroes. [14:42]
- You cannot hit Gloom Bombs on Viper from low ground; slow down instead of rushing them. [15:13]
- Getting parried there was predictable. Either stay on top of her for a few seconds before the light melee, or disengage. [15:13]
- Do not focus the tank unless he is at 10 to 20% HP; focus the target without his 2 up. [15:48]
- When you are on full cooldowns, cat for a second; you should have used 2 on him and then ulted. [15:48]
- Defensive tech: Arctic Blast as you run away so it hits anyone on top of you, then slide downstairs. He does it as or before he rounds a corner, when chasers are closest. [16:19]
- Calico's melee shred makes mid boss much easier. [16:19]
- Most Calico builds go gun spike second. Mystic Shot has a ton of damage and extra shred on a hero that has amp and wants to one-shot. [16:50]
- Getting your gun back also significantly amps your melee damage, which helps your 2-parry potential. [17:58]
- With a 1k bag and your item nearly ready, do not get aggressive. [18:58]
- If six enemies defend the mid Walker, do not force the fight; shove the waves and take the base Guardians on the side lanes. [19:28]
- Rejuvenator can cycle your bag, and sometimes it just drops it, so be conscious of that. [19:28]
- You are underusing Arctic Blast. Use it off cooldown in team fights, for as many Gloom Bombs as you can. [20:29]
- Arctic Blast plus 2 on a Viper is worth it even if you die, because she is the main threat. She keeps sliding while blasted and often buys debuff resistance, so keep tracking and leading her with Gloom Bombs. [20:59]
- That loss snowballed from running at a mid Walker defended by six instead of taking everything on the side lanes. [21:29]
- Skipping the gun spike to get spirit items earlier is a nice idea in theory, but the gun spike is too much early tempo. [22:31]
- You are not light meleeing enough for Spirit Snatch. Proc Spirit Snatch with a light melee before throwing Gloom Bombs for an extra 25 spirit power on them. [22:31]
- Definitely buy Mystic Shot and Spirit Shredder Bullets. Hunter's Aura is also tempo but not that good. [23:01]
- Dying for a shrine is worth it; good call taking it. [23:31]
- Arctic Blast people who are about to mantle something so they fall back down. [23:31]
- Clear all base Guardians and knock the Patron while you are there when you have time. [24:01]
- It is better for Calico to jump past base Guardians, since she does much more damage to shrines than to base Guardians, especially without a gun spike. [24:31]
- He believes in clearing the tier 2 creep: you take more damage and make more noise by not clearing it, and the enemy will likely check their doubles soon. [24:31]
- Invading as your team backs out is good, but someone needs to split the sinners with you; if everyone leaves, take one sinner camp and leave. [25:01]
- To survive that dive you had to break line of sight on Viper: 2 or down dash down and go into the tunnels. [25:31]
- Spirit Rend is very good at that point in the game, but buying Mystic Shot earlier gives much more early tempo, scales, and covers shred you lack. [25:31]
- If you skip the gun spike, greed more purple damage instead, but not Arctic Blast: a tier 2 like Improved Spirit, or Spirit Sap building into Focus Lens late. Silence Wave could be the tempo item in this game. [26:33]
- If you are looking for a pick and they are grouped as six, do not force it. [27:38]
- When the enemy disengages you can disengage too. [28:09]
- Calico should not buy Tank Buster until very late: against about 30 average spirit resist you need about 180 damage per Gloom Bomb for it to give value, less if the enemy has little spirit resist. [28:39]
- You are using your ult for movement instead of good movement; corner boost or edge boost to make space. [29:11]
- Viper and Lash are the only heroes that can run you down when you fix a wave like that. [29:41]
- How to clear a wave like that: cat for amp, then at max amp Arctic Blast one-shots the wave and you are out before anything happens. [30:11]
- Disarming Hex feels fine to buy on Calico into a Viper with no dispel, though ideally your Graves or Haze buys it. [30:41]
- Calico is a hero where it feels fine to buy utility like Knockdown and Disarming Hex. [31:12]
- Counterspell would have done wonders that game against Lash, Drifter, Ivy's Stone Form and Victor. [31:12]
- Playing Calico into a fed Viper is hard: she one-shots you and is hard to Gloom Bomb. Slowing Hex is almost core into Viper, and it is great into Lash and good into Dynamo. [31:42]
- Summary notes: look for 2-parry kills on sinners, invade more and live in their jungle whenever you are on a spike, Arctic Blast first is a crutch, do not rush Gloom Bombs and aim them at feet, use Arctic Blast whenever it lets you land Gloom Bombs, and do not engage with your 2 from behind. [32:12]
- He is starting to think Trophy Collector may not be the best on Calico; a build going boots instead of trophy is interesting. [33:12]
- He prefers builds that go Spirit Rend late game. [33:42]
