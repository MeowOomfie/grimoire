## Covers
Drifter gameplay with commentary by aquatiq on the Wanderer Drifter build, in a game against Holliday, Sinclair, Vindicta and Mina where his team was behind.
Commentary over one game, no coaching. Recorded 2026-04-24; no patch date given.
Topics: lane sustain items, Holliday in lane, playing from behind, Fortitude, Debuff Remover, and a talk on losing streaks.

## Claims
Drifter build and play:
- He uses the Wanderer Drifter build, which starts with Long Range. [1:05]
- Extra Regen is a good early item when you need healing in lane. [4:09]
- After that he goes Extra Stamina and then Stalker (as captioned), which he really likes on Drifter. [4:40]
- Get the healing minion when you are low in lane; blind the enemies first so you can grab it. [6:44]
- Extra Health helps against burst from Holliday's barrel and Sinclair's Vexing Bolt; he also considers a shield item. [7:15]
- With Extra Health he heals to 1,500 at base, then clears his lane and goes to the farm. [9:19]
- He buys Toxic Bullets. [12:59]
- With no free slots he upgrades Sharpshooter. [25:28]
- He wants Fortitude for healing out of combat so he does not keep going back to spawn; it was nerfed recently but is still strong. [25:28]
- Once the enemy has hex and dispel effects, buy Debuff Remover so you cannot be dispelled or hexed. [27:03]
- He wants an ability duration item next. [27:03]

Matchups:
- Holliday is a very efficient laner because of her barrels and is insanely strong early, but weaker late; do not feed her early. [8:19]
- When Holliday ults, sit back and wait for it to finish. [8:49]
- An enemy with Superior Duration makes their effects last much longer than normal. [27:36]
- The enemy bought Metal Skin because he had 8 kills. [28:54]

Macro:
- If three enemies are on one side, go the other way. [14:31]
- Paige can use Captivating Read, Bookworm and her ultimate together to stun and displace a group. [15:33]
- Do not run into a fight when the enemy has more people there; they gave up the urn that way. [17:06]
- A teammate who is 0 and 7 needs to farm. [17:06]
- When your team is behind, fights are pointless; farm, and fight alongside your richest teammate. [18:40]
- When down on money, try not to fight and take souls instead. [21:20]
- He would accept losing his yellow walker if his team takes green, so he weighs helping the push over holding. [23:56]
- When enemies are separated, pick off the lone one; low-HP targets near their team are too risky, so leave. [28:16]
- When the enemy takes mid boss and money is close, push all the waves instead of contesting. [26:33]

General:
- Everyone has losing streaks, even top players; do not get discouraged, just go next. [1:35]
