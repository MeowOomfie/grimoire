# The BEST Wraith Build In Deadlock 2.0
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-05-10
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xxSDxd0zC7c

## Covers
Metro's "spirit shred" Wraith build for the May 2025 item overhaul (Deadlock 2.0, eight slots plus four extra, category investment bonuses). For Wraith players: two lane openings (Cultist Sacrifice or Headhunter), the core Mercurial Magnum card build, ability order, Boundless Spirit sustain with no lifesteal items, and the flex and situational items. Numbers are from the patch of 2025-05-09 and will go stale.

## Claims
- Wraith is back, one of the best heroes in the game: weak early, but from mid to late game she kills everyone and is probably the highest DPS hero other than Lady Geist (caption "Guys"), who is also busted. [0:30]
- 800 souls items are now half the price of a 1,600, so you can't spam them anymore, and you only have eight slots plus four extra slots instead of 16. [1:01]
- Have at least some items of every category, because each category's investment bonus has diminishing returns, usually above about 10k invested in orange. [1:32]
- Up to 9.6k spent on orange gives 52% weapon damage; spending 30k (three times as much) only adds roughly another 40%. [2:04]
- The build is a gun build that does mostly spirit damage, and it revolves around Mercurial Magnum, probably the most broken item in the game right now, insane on heroes that spam abilities fast like Wraith with cards. [2:04]
- Mercurial Magnum is an upgrade to Quicksilver Reload: casting the imbued ability reloads your magazine and gives bonus spirit damage based on your base gun damage, which grows as you level and scales insanely on Wraith. [2:35]
- Mercurial Magnum is a mid to late game item, bought around 16k net worth, when more than 50% of Wraith's maximum gun damage scaling is already reached. [3:06]
- With Mercurial Magnum each card gives a max reload, bonus spirit damage on the card, 30% fire rate, and base bullet damage as spirit damage on every bullet; it scales with spirit, so it is like a second Full Auto without the lifesteal. [3:36]
- Lane option one: buy Monster Rounds and rush Cultist Sacrifice as the second item (3.2k item, only 2.4k more since it upgrades from Monster Rounds). Monster Rounds lets you shove creeps fast so the enemy can't harass you as much. [4:06]
- Wraith is very weak in lane right now; you only need to survive lane without being far behind in souls, then scale and win. [4:37]
- Cultist Sacrifice: consume a creep for 150% bonus souls, and while you stay alive for 180 seconds you get 25% fire rate, 350 bonus health and 15% ability range. [5:07]
- Eating one lane creep gave about 200 bonus souls and took Wraith from about 700 HP to about 1,050, roughly 40% extra health. The ability range part looks bugged but doesn't matter much on Wraith. [5:37]
- Cultist Sacrifice also helps you tower dive (35% resist as an upgraded Monster Rounds) and gives 4 HP per second out of combat regen. [6:08]
- Consume a lane creep the first time, because lane creeps give the most souls other than tier three creeps; on later uses (cooldown about 260 seconds) consume a tier three creep every 240 to 260 seconds to keep the buff. [6:08]
- You currently lose the Cultist Sacrifice buff when you die, which he thinks is a bug; if intentional the item is very risky. [6:38]
- Lane option two: skip Monster Rounds and Cultist Sacrifice, rush Headshot Booster into Headhunter. [7:09]
- Headhunter: headshot an enemy hero every 6 seconds, which is easy on Wraith with her fire rate, to heal 6% health and deal 150 bonus headshot damage. If you live long enough to get it, you win the lane. [7:09]
- Both openings are viable; he prefers Cultist Sacrifice, and Headhunter is the safer choice if Cultist Sacrifice is too risky for you. [7:39]
- Rush a 3k upgrade instead of several 800s (Extended Magazine, Headshot Booster, Extra Regen) because the eight slots fill fast and 800 bonuses aren't worth it when double the money buys items like Fleetfoot. [8:10]
- His only 800 items are Extended Magazine and Extra Charge, plus Extra Regen only if he really needs it; then go straight into the main build, since the earlier your big power spike items come online the stronger you feel. [8:40]
- Quicksilver Reload is even better now because it builds into Mercurial Magnum; it got nerfed (fire rate 10% instead of 15%) but is still insane. Put it on the cards. [8:40]
- Next buy Opening Rounds: 40% extra damage against targets above 50% health plus spirit power, a more consistent pick than Mystic Shot, which does little on Wraith if you miss the one shot. [9:12]
- Then Tesla Bullets: the build has lots of spirit power so Tesla damage scales, and with limited slots it is good because it upgrades into Capacitor later. [9:44]
- Then Mercurial Magnum. There is no bullet lifesteal in the build; early sustain is Cultist Sacrifice regen or Headhunter plus Full Auto. [10:16]
- Ability order: two points in Card Trick, two in Full Auto, one in Project Mind (teleport), one in Telekinesis (ult), then max Full Auto, which should come around Tesla Bullets and before Mercurial Magnum. [10:47]
- Skip Bullet Lifesteal: it got nerfed hard to 16% on bullets, and with so much spirit damage per bullet from Tesla and Mercurial Magnum it isn't worth it. [11:18]
- The meta is about bursting the enemy down: don't rush healing early, stay full HP and proc Full Auto in fights. Defensive green items and armor are weaker right now and fights are shorter, so sustain later. [11:49]
- After Tesla Bullets the farming cycle is complete: Cultist Sacrifice on camps plus Tesla and 35% bonus weapon damage against NPCs clears camps and waves insanely fast, enough for tier three camps at about 10k net worth. [12:19]
- Mercurial Magnum (re-select the cards when you upgrade) gives 50% of each bullet's damage as extra; for 4,800 as an upgrade it gave 52% bullet damage at that point, 30% fire rate and 60 extra damage per card. [13:19]
- Its reload is diminishing: the longer you don't shoot, the closer to 100% reload you get. In a fight pop Full Auto, shoot and throw cards; as long as you hit shots you rarely reload. At 16k it was about 1,000 DPS with headshots. [13:50]
- The meta is "if I kill you first I'm fine", and Telekinesis means lifted targets die before they can act, so survivability can wait. [14:21]
- Next Enchanter's Emblem, a mix of the old Spirit Armor and Enchanter's Barrier: above 65% health you get bonus spirit power, cooldown reduction, and a flat 18% spirit resist, plus it starts your vitality investment (10% extra HP). [14:52]
- Then Escalating Resilience: 30% bullet resist, more health, more weapon damage and more ammo, so you basically never reload. [14:52]
- Then Surge of Power, put on the cards, not Full Auto as in his last five Surge builds: every card thrown gives ammo back and keeps the Mercurial Magnum buff up. [15:25]
- Start fights by throwing a card so the Mercurial Magnum bonus is active right away, then keep spamming cards to keep getting the diminishing reloads. [15:56]
- Surge of Power also brings purple investment past 10k, giving base 35 extra spirit, and adds damage to cards. Damage tested about the same on cards or Full Auto, but on cards Surge is active about every 8 seconds instead of once per fight. [16:26]
- Surge of Power got nerfed (less imbued spirit power) but is still good because it gives overall spirit power; the core build needs no extra slot. [16:56]
- Boundless Spirit (now only 6.4k) gives spirit power and 100 healing every time you cast an ability, even if it misses. If you have spare slots you can buy Extra Spirit or Improved Spirit early since it builds from them. [17:27]
- Boundless Spirit is best on heroes with the most casts: Lady Geist's Malice, Wraith's cards, and Mirage (Scarabs is four scarabs so it heals four times). He expects it to get nerfed like Mercurial Magnum, so abuse it now. [17:59]
- Boundless Spirit raised the Mercurial Magnum bonus from 68% to 98% of bullet damage as spirit, Full Auto from 12 to 17 per bullet, and about 40 extra damage per card. [18:32]
- Then Healing Booster, 25% more healing, so each card heals 125, combined with Full Auto's bullet and spirit lifesteal. [19:02]
- The full build is about 30k, which is reasonable since souls are easier to get this patch (creeps are worth a bit more). [19:33]
- After Full Auto, max Card Trick next, before the teleport upgrade and the ultimate, so you always have a card to throw even if you miss shots. [19:33]
- Metal Skin doesn't counter this build: you keep shooting, get cards back, and the cards with Boundless Spirit and Surge of Power kill through it. [20:04]
- The build has no lifesteal items, only Full Auto lifesteal and healing on cards; it can be countered by anti-heal, which got nerfed a bit. [20:34]
- Throwing cards with Boundless Spirit also gives 2 m/s movement speed, stacked with Surge of Power's movement speed, so you can kite in and out. [21:04]
- You don't need ammo items like Titanic Magazine (caption "dying magazine") or Intensifying Magazine because the build has so much ammo. [21:34]
- Next Toxic Bullets as the anti-heal (sell Extra Charge if you need the slot, since maxed cards come back fast). Healbane works too, but Toxic Bullets is easy to apply with Wraith's fire rate. [22:05]
- Extra slots are hard to get in an average game until very late, so every slot must be worth it; the optional upgrades below need no extra slot. [22:35]
- Capacitor upgrades from Tesla Bullets (6k item, only 3.2k more): a skill shot for 200 spirit damage that slows up to the maximum and removes every non-ultimate buff on the enemy. [23:05]
- Capacitor removes Viscous's cube on an ally, Seven's Power Surge, Warden's overshield, any barrier, Pocket's Divine Barrier (not his ult), Vindicta and Grey Talon flight, and stops an Infernus mid Flame Dash. [23:37]
- Capacitor setup: initiate with Telekinesis and fire the skill shot as they land from the lift. [24:07]
- He often buys Healing Tempo even before Capacitor: it upgrades from Healing Booster, raising each card or ability heal to 130 and Full Auto lifesteal to 90%. [24:37]
- Healing Tempo: whenever you heal yourself you get 40% bonus fire rate and 1.5 m/s movement speed for 7 seconds, so it is always on while you keep throwing cards. [25:10]
- Buy Healing Tempo if you have no extra slot; otherwise buy Ricochet or Unstoppable. [25:40]
- Unstoppable is more valuable now with so much stun in the game, because Debuff Remover got nerfed a lot; Ethereal Shift is really bad. [25:40]
- Counterspell is a situational defensive item that can technically parry any ability in the game. [26:10]
- Spellslinger: casting an ability gives stacks (six) of fire rate and reload time, about 60% faster reload and 60% more fire rate on Wraith; about 2k DPS, great for single target damage. [26:10]
- Ricochet got buffed to 25% fire rate, the second highest fire rate of any gun item after Burst Fire, and applies all bullet procs including Mercurial Magnum and Full Auto; insane if the enemy team clumps up. [27:10]
- Flex picks: Ricochet for AoE, Healing Tempo if no extra slot, Capacitor to shut down buff heroes like Viscous and flyers, Unstoppable if you get stunned a lot, Siphon Bullets to be more survivable, Spellslinger for single target. [28:11]
- He would put Spellslinger in the main build and buy it more often than Ricochet. [28:42]
- Optional defensive items he rarely buys: if they die in one second he doesn't need heal, and Unstoppable is the only thing he really needs to live long enough. [29:12]
- Spirit Resilience and Bullet Resilience are bad: 30% resist for 3.2k, 60% only below 30% health, when you are basically already dead. [29:12]
- Against lots of debuffs and spirit damage, Spellbreaker is great: a big investment that reduces 75% of a big spirit hit and gives spirit resist and debuff resist. [29:42]
- Plated Armor is insane against bullet damage: 30% of bullets do no damage and 60% of bullet effects are prevented (Haze's Fixation, Seven's Power Surge, Wraith's Full Auto), but it is more a tank item and Wraith plays as a glass cannon. [29:42]
- Curse is for shutting down one specific target. [30:14]
- Armor Piercing Rounds makes 65% of bullets ignore enemy resist; buy it against tanky targets like Viscous's ball, Mo & Krill's burrow, or enemies building lots of bullet armor. [30:14]
- Spirit Burn is the best anti-heal in the game (80% healing reduction plus AoE damage over time if you deal enough spirit damage) but very expensive and late game, since Wraith wants intrinsic damage on her bullets. [30:45]
- Blood Tribute is a good late game choice for debuff resist: toggle it for debuff resist and fire rate at the cost of a life drain you won't notice late with so much lifesteal. [31:15]
- Debuff Remover is still situationally good. [31:45]
- He says this build was rank one on the leaderboards at the time. [31:45]
