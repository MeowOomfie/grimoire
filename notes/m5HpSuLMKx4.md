# A Matchmaking Update is Coming. Here's What It Should Fix.
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-07-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m5HpSuLMKx4

## Covers
Opinion piece ahead of an announced matchmaking update: the coach's wish list for ranked (progress display, smurfs, draft, bans, accept button, placements, solo queue, strategy phase, hero MMR, Eternus leaderboard numbers, region choice).
Mostly suggestions, with a few facts about how the current ranked system works. For players curious why ranked feels the way it does.

## Claims
- A developer said on the Discord that a matchmaking update, an item update and a big content update are being worked on, with the matchmaking update hoped for in the coming weeks. [0:00]
- "Matchmaking sucks" is a scapegoat for any frustration; it is easy to blame and hard to pinpoint what is actually wrong. [0:30]
- The badge system is percentile based, so climbing gets harder the higher you go, and there is no display of how close you are to ranking up. [1:01]
- There are known big walls between ranks, for example Eternus 5 to Eternus 6 and Ascendant 6 to Eternus 1, and none of this is communicated in game. [1:31]
- Suggested fix: show an MMR number like Dota, or a progress bar to the next rank. [1:31]
- Reports like "won five, ranked up once, lost two, ranked down" are probably part memory bias and part real cases where rank gained or lost does not match the situation. [2:32]
- The coach does not think performance based MMR is the solution. [2:32]
- Smurfing is already against the terms of service and can be reported in game, but he has not seen anyone banned for it. [3:02]
- Suggested smurf measures: phone number verification to queue ranked, and clear bans, especially for people who stream or post videos of smurfing. [3:32]
- Smurfs already rank up decently fast; Valve could detect them faster from inputs, since a brand new account doing instant air dashes and edge boosts is very unlikely to be new. [4:03]
- A data mine about 6 months earlier showed the game tracks movement counts such as number of wall jumps. [5:04]
- Right now ranked and unranked are effectively one queue and every game counts as ranked. [5:35]
- Suggestion: draft only in a separate ranked queue; unranked keeps free hero picking. He expects draft would not change much because people still want to play one or two heroes. [5:35]
- Only two bans per game makes a ban feel like it never matters; with 38 heroes, four bans still leaves 34 playable and would cut how often broken heroes appear. [6:05]
- Suggestion: team-voted bans on load-in, as in Marvel Rivals; the current ban UI is so small he often forgets to adjust his ban. [6:36]
- Suggestion: an accept match button, so you can queue and step away, and so there is less pausing 30 seconds into a game for AFK players. [7:36]
- Suggestion: if ranked becomes its own queue again, about 10 placement matches worth two to three times a normal match. [8:37]
- A true rank reset would be a terrible idea because brand new players could be matched against Eternus 6 players and quit. [8:37]
- Solo players can currently be matched with and against duos and six stacks; a solo-only queue option would help, at the cost of longer queues. [9:37]
- Laning is always 2v2, so a solo player in lane against a duo is at a disadvantage: the duo is more coordinated, has a game plan and may run a strong lane pairing. [10:07]
- Suggestion: a pre-match strategy phase of about 60 seconds for bans, possibly draft, and above all lane assignments, since almost every game someone pauses at the start to swap lanes. [10:37]
- Lane pairings set in a strategy phase would be intentional rather than accidental and would help drive meta innovation. [11:09]
- Hero MMR shows as arrows on your profile per hero: two green, one green, one red or two red. A green arrow means the matchmaker thinks you are better on that hero than your rank and puts you in slightly higher lobbies. [11:40]
- Example: as Eternus 6 the coach has one green arrow on Victor, so he gets slightly higher ranked lobbies on Victor. [12:10]
- Hero MMR feels nebulous; a rank per role does not fit because Deadlock has no fixed roles. [12:10]
- Suggestion: once in Eternus, show a leaderboard number instead of Eternus 1 to 6, like Dota's Immortal rank, with roughly 4,000 to 5,000 people per region in Eternus. [12:41]
- There is no in-game region choice; the coach uses third-party software to block European servers so he gets North American games. [14:15]
- Post matchmaking feedback on the official forums; the developers are active there and read them. [14:45]
