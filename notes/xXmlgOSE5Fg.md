## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Doorman-only free coaching stream on 8/25/25, a few days after Doorman released. It predates the 2026 map and item changes, so items, camps and map spots may have moved.
He first analyzes a Hydronation scrim VOD of Lome (a strong Doorman), then coaches two Doorman players: Player 1 (Ritualist rank, into Mo & Krill, Warden, Seven, Infernus, Geist, Bebop) and Player 2 (Archon rank, into McGinnis, Vindicta, Ivy, Pocket).
Themes: door usage (escape, poke angles, buffs, chase, tower doors), the Knockdown into Luggage Cart combo, ult targets, Extra Charge and Mystic Burst, Cultist Sacrifice buff uptime, what goes through doors, a double Sinner's Sacrifice melee tech, and a first look at Paige's kit on her release.
