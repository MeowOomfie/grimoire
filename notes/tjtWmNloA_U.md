## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (VOD reviews of submitted matches), patch of mid May 2026.
Four players coached: Zach (Victor, Ascendant, on a wall breaker build), Move (Paradox, Oracle), a Calico player (Oracle) and a Pocket player (Oracle).
Themes: farming between waves and box farming, sieging walkers on a split-push build, playing for picks around farm, Swap placement, playing for spikes and soul awareness, Urn and Rejuvenator macro, lane locking, and hitting objectives instead of fighting in base.
Side opinions on the patch: Opening Rounds now includes bullet velocity, Haze's fixation lasting too long in lane, Ivy's Stone Form being overtuned, and Kinetic Dash on medium-magazine heroes.
