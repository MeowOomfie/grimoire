## Covers
Drifter gameplay with commentary by aquatiq, played on a published build (the "ABL build") that adds Shadow Weave for invisible flanks.

Topics: Trophy Collector stacking, Toxic Bullets and Slowing Bullets, Shadow Weave ambushes, and complaints about Doorman.

## Claims
Drifter build and play:
- He plays Drifter on the "ABL build" from the in-game build browser. [1:00]
- He considers Silence Wave (as captioned) a strong pickup against this lineup. [1:31]
- Use your blind to take enemy boxes and breakables while they cannot see you coming. [5:09]
- Trophy Collector is still a broken item. [5:41]
- Rush Trophy Collector before your first kill; he lost a stack because he already had a kill before buying it. [6:41]
- He gets about 48 gun damage per headshot at this point, which he calls a lot. [6:11]
- He buys Toxic Bullets mid lane phase even at low HP. [9:51]
- He uses Slowing Bullets so the targets he is shooting are slowed. [10:53]
- Engage when the enemy Doorman does not have his ultimate up. [10:22]
- When it is a 2v1 he does not think he wins, he backs off to another lane instead of fighting. [11:54]
- Do not walk into three enemies at low HP; push another lane instead. [16:00]
- Throwing his 2 into the wrong spot cost him in a fight; place it carefully before going in. [13:26]
- Shadow Weave plan: wait for its cooldown (about 20 seconds), then use it to sneak up on a target such as the Doorman. [19:08]
- Shadow Weave ambushes landed kills because enemies did not see him coming; he calls being sneaky Drifter fun and interesting. [21:14]
- His next 6k target is Crippling Headshot. [23:17]
- He drops Stalker (as captioned) because he does not really need it and wants the flex slot that comes from taking the walker. [23:17]
- He uses Debuff Remover to clear enemy effects in fights. [24:51]
- Trophy Collector stacks are worth it on Drifter because the extra sprint and especially the extra range are really good; he ended with about 15 stacks and 37,000 player damage. [27:29]

Matchups and reads:
- He thinks Doorman is ban worthy, while he does not consider the other hero he faced ban worthy. [3:05]
- Doorman threat: he can Luggage Cart someone, place doors behind his own Walker and use his ultimate to send them away or under the Walker. [3:37]
- A Mirage showing up in another lane very early probably means a lane swap, since Mirage could not teleport that early without a fully upgraded ultimate. [7:11]
- An enemy Doorman building spirit will not be shooting you, so he is less of a gun threat. [25:23]
