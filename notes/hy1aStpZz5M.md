## Covers

## Claims
- Teammate advice against Infernus: shoot him with your ranged gun, which applies a passive, and stop throwing heavy melees that get parried. [3:28]
- In a bad lane he buys extra health so he does not get one shot. [3:59]
- On Paradox you are supposed to poke, not run at people. [4:38]
- With Trophy Collector he roams to collect stacks. [7:38]
- Paige can hold a lane by herself pretty easily. [9:26]
- Voice chat callout: when the team all rushes out, save the ult for it instead of going in first. [11:02]
- Double pump Paradox was hard nerfed and is not as good as it used to be, but it is still very strong; her whole kit is strong. [15:46]
- He prefers carbine Paradox and finds bomb Paradox boring. [16:56]
- If you buy Counterspell or a knockdown item, use it; otherwise do not buy it. [18:35]
- He says this carbine Paradox build is back and very strong. [24:04]
