# Deadlock's Open Beta is Getting Closer | Early Patch Preview
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-01-23
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nnJOUczIldc

## Covers
Overview of the late January 2026 Old Gods New Blood update: hero voting and release schedule for six new heroes, the Street Brawl mode, base reworks, tunnels, UI changes and two new items (Recharging Rush, Ballistic Enchantment).
No balance changes in this patch. For returning and new players.

## Claims
- This is the first update posted on the playdeadlock.com website, which suggests the game is getting closer to open beta, possibly in 2026 and if not 2027. [0:00]
- Six new heroes are not out yet; they release one per day, Mondays and Thursdays at 2 p.m. PST (5 p.m. EST), not two at a time. [1:00]
- Every game you play earns a vote for which hero releases next; the first hero releases January 26 and all should be out about 3 weeks later. [1:30]
- You vote in game at the voting booth by pressing E. [1:30]
- Street Brawl is a new fast-paced, roguelike-style mode with rare, enhanced and legendary items. [2:01]
- Before each Street Brawl round you get three item picks, each from three options, with one reroll. [2:31]
- Street Brawl has at most five rounds; early rounds are won by killing the Guardian. [3:02]
- The Street Brawl Guardian has resistance while players are around, so you usually have to kill at least some players to take it. [3:02]
- After a team loses two rounds its Guardian is gone and it defends at its Walker, which is much stronger, making comebacks easier; reverse sweeps from 2-0 down happen, though 3-0s still happen too. [3:32]
- You can now queue as a random hero, including in Street Brawl. [4:02]
- Street Brawl is like League of Legends' ARAM done the Deadlock way, and it suggests more game modes in the future. [4:33]
- He will not make educational content on Street Brawl and advises not optimizing it too much; learning it yourself is part of the fun. [5:04]
- Street Brawl matchmaking is based on your rank but he believes it does not affect your rank, so it is a good place to learn a new hero's mechanics and combos (for example The Doorman or Mina) without risking rank; it does not teach macro. [5:34]
- Bases were reworked: the Hidden King base (formerly Amber Hand) looks very different and plays differently. [6:04]
- The Patron now has a transform phase and an AoE attack that curses people; the Shrines also have an AoE attack that he believes curses, so bases defend themselves more. [6:34]
- Troopers were visually reworked; some players have clarity complaints. [7:04]
- The six upcoming heroes are Graves, Apollo, Silver, Rem, Venator and Celeste; their kits are only known from leaks, which he does not cover. [7:34]
- Silver will transform between two forms; Rem has something to do with sleep and is small. [8:04]
- New tunnels in the map are accessible only to small heroes; right now only Calico can use them, and Rem likely will. [8:04]
- Graves looks like a necromancer, possibly with area denial or a tombstone; Apollo looks like a mobility hero, his guess a high skill healer type; Celeste is unclear (disruptive performer); Venator looks like a gun character, an arms expert. [8:34]
- UI changes include bars that show channeling times, stylized ability effects, new portraits, an objective health bar, an updated scoreboard and completely different settings; many map areas were updated. [9:34]
- New item Recharging Rush: recharges each of your charged abilities after you deal 200 weapon damage within 3.5 seconds, at most once every 20 seconds, and gives 20% max ammo and 10% weapon damage. [10:05]
- On Infernus missing a charge on both Flame Dash and Napalm, Recharging Rush would recharge both, so it has potential for him. [10:36]
- New item Ballistic Enchantment: imbued on an ability, gives 30% ability range; for comparison Greater Expansion is 35%. [10:36]
- Ballistic Enchantment combined with Greater Expansion or Mystic Expansion reaches huge ability range, for example on an ultimate Warden build; people may underestimate the range. [11:07]
- His first idea is Lady Geist using Ballistic Enchantment on her grenade (Essence Bomb). [11:38]
- There were no balance changes in this patch, which he considers normal while Valve focuses on delivering and polishing content. [12:08]
- The in-game learn to play section was not revamped and is completely outdated. [13:09]
