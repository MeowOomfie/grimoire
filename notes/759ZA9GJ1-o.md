## Covers
Pocket, Tonydadof4: commentary on why his Pocket fell apart after the map update and what he learned from watching the top Pocket player's replays.
Personal learning story with a short win montage, not a full guide; recorded after the 2026 map update.

## Claims
- After the map update he went from MVP or key player most games to negative KD, even though tier lists still rated Pocket S tier for survivability and damage. [0:00]
- His main problem was not knowing the new map, so he got caught in the open and died; just playing more did not fix it. [1:01]
- What fixed it: find the top Pocket player on Statlocker, watch a full replay, take notes and compare what they did with what you would do in the same spot. [1:31]
- He kept switching between builds (suitcase as main damage, or cloak as main damage) and could not settle on combos. [2:02]
- His old combo was to get behind the enemy, cloak, then suitcase, then teleport out, but without Majestic Leap or Stamina Mastery he lacked the mobility to get behind enemies in early and mid game. [2:02]
- The top Pocket player used the cloak mainly as an escape and rarely to engage. [2:32]
- The top player's pattern: prefire the cloak, then do the suitcase combo, then instantly pop Cold Front for a big burst, then peel out as soon as enemies retaliate, all fast enough to still have time to teleport. [3:03]
- Pocket's play style is get in, deal damage, get out; copying this immediately improved his games. [3:03]
- Game awareness made the difference: read the map, who is where, who has ultimate and how much money they have before making a play. [3:03]
- He was focused on the wrong things (his combo, where camps were) instead of map and hero information. [3:33]
- Before the update he knew all camps and timers by heart, so he could focus purely on piloting Pocket; the map change took that away. [4:03]
- He thinks a hard, high-skill-ceiling hero with a low pick rate like Pocket is a good climbing pick, because few players know how to play against him. [6:17]
- Sticking only to top meta builds without experimenting left him without the knowledge to adapt when the game changed. [6:47]
