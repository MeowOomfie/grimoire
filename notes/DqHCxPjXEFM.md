## Covers
Part 5 of a counters series (March 2026): tech, counter items and the key ability cooldown to track for the new heroes Apollo, Celeste, Graves, Rem, Silver and Venator.
Cooldowns are tracked against the 30 second wave timer.

## Claims
- Track key ability cooldowns with waves, which come every 30 seconds. [0:00]
- General: the bullet and spirit resilience items are very good into their damage types; bullet resist is slightly worse because Armor Piercing Rounds goes through it, but it also applies to melee damage. [0:31]
- Apollo tech: use walls or pillars to space his 3, since he outpaces you if you run in a straight line. [0:31]
- Apollo's parry (2) gives only 0.3 seconds of invulnerability when it procs, so shooting him right before he parries something big, like a Bebop bomb or an Infernus ult, usually lets it land. [1:02]
- Apollo items: Counterspell, which counters his 3 and, after he ults you, still lets you deny the damage completely. [1:33]
- Slowing Hex shuts down all his abilities except his parry; many Apollos panic and parry immediately, so hex, wait a second, and catch him with nothing up. [1:33]
- Wait a couple of seconds after his first 3 before hexing, and the hex lasts until his 3 expires, removing the later two uses. [1:33]
- His 3 heals him a lot, so anti-heal is very strong into him. [2:04]
- Curse is specifically very good into Apollo, a squishy hero who plays in your face, so your team can collapse on him. [2:04]
- Apollo key ability: his 2, 22 second cooldown (about 2/3 of a wave, think one use a wave), 15 seconds with one AP, a threat multiple times a wave; it gives movement, dodges anything and applies CC. [2:04]
- Celeste tech: when ulted, don't run straight back down the lane; teammates do the same so the ball ping-pongs between you, and the wave can bounce it back to you. [2:35]
- When Celeste ults you, walk left or right, splitting away from your team, the lane and the wave as quickly as possible. [3:06]
- Celeste is very overtuned, so few items counter her. [3:06]
- Her auto harassment mostly comes from her 1's on-hit extra damage; Debuff Remover cleanses that extra damage and the spirit lifesteal she gains on hit. [3:06]
- Curse into Celeste removes her shield, removes the amp from her 3 and stops her abilities, cutting her spirit lifesteal, so your team can shut her down. [3:36]
- Celeste key ability: her 3, 33 second cooldown (one a wave), 18 seconds with two points (two a wave); more important is tracking her amp stacks. [4:06]
- Celeste's horns glow when she has amp; when they glow, back off and space her until they stop, then engage. [4:06]
- Graves tech: her gun has only 17 meter range and she has only two stamina, so when she goes on you she is immobile and committed; push her back or run her down, and punish her when she comes in range (for example with Restorative Shot). [4:37]
- Graves' skulls die to one light melee; melee or shoot them, always clear them, or she feels super oppressive. [4:37]
- Graves items: Disarming Hex is the best; good Graves build debuff items (Toxic Bullets, Split Shot, Inhibitor) and it stops her spreading them and her lifesteal, including her innate 8% bullet lifesteal; it also works on a gun-built solo queue Graves. [5:07]
- More selfishly, Debuff Remover, now renamed Dispel Magic, cleanses the debuffs she applies. [5:38]
- Graves key ability: her 1, 13 second cooldown (about two a wave), but what matters is how many charges she holds; she gets about 80% of a charge per wave collecting enemy and own minion souls, and spawns with two charges. [5:38]
- Rem tech: hard focus him like a Paige; unless he has Echo Shard he gets one 2 per fight and a long downtime (about 30 seconds, reduced by pillow hits). [6:09]
- Whenever Rem pops out near you, especially in lane, and his 2 is down, hard focus him. [6:39]
- Rems love tunnel box routes and ganks; if he is shot between tunnels the vents shut down and you can one-shot him, or hard commit and 2v1 his lane partner while he is gone. [6:39]
- Rem items: Slowing Hex shuts down his 2 and he can't really buy Dispel Magic, giving about three seconds of free hits. [7:10]
- Counterspell is very good into Rem's ult, which is easily countered with a little timing practice. [7:10]
- Monster Rounds is good into Rem, who buffs minions and takes wave priority to box route; it helps kill his super minions. [7:10]
- Rem's 2 heals his teammates a lot, so any anti-heal is good into him. [7:10]
- Rem key ability: his 2, 40 second cooldown, about 32 once he puts a point in quickly, one a wave; if he used it this wave, all in him, he is very vulnerable without it. [7:40]
- Silver tech: punish her when her ultimate is not online, because it just ended or your team all ins her before she builds it. [7:40]
- Silver items: Warp Stone, since in her ultimate her only mobility is her 2 and her melees have no range, so spacing or outmaneuvering her limits her damage. [8:10]
- Denying her werewolf form is key, so Metal Skin is strong: if she can't damage you she can't stack her ult; it also blocks her autos during the ult. [8:10]
- Disarming Hex is very strong for the same reason; hex her, call it on mic, and your team focuses her. [8:10]
- Silver key abilities in lane: her 1 (21 second cooldown, 15 with the tier two cooldown upgrade, -7 s) and her 2 (22 seconds, 15 with the tier one upgrade, -6 s). Pressing 2 and 1 on you takes half your HP with essentially no counterplay. [8:40]
- The counterplay is the window when those are down and she is weak: trade back then; don't run away at half HP after her burst, that is the time to trade. [8:40]
- Venator tech: his traps die to one bullet; shoot any trap he places, especially in the open, so he can't set up on you. [9:10]
- Venator items: Metal Skin blocks almost all his damage including his ult; Disarming Hex shuts down his gun and his ult; Plated Armor procs deny damage from his gun, ult and grenade. [9:10]
- Venator key ability: his 2, 23 second cooldown, 13 seconds with two AP; it does huge damage followed by a melee, so while it is up don't go near a wall, and punish him when he uses it. [9:40]
