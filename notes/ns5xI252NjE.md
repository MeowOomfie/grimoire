# The ULTIMATE Deadlock Build Crafting (and Item Shop) Guide
channel: Natan
date: 2025-08-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ns5xI252NjE

## Covers
Build-crafting guide from an Eternus 6 player: shop basics (tiers, upgrades, slots, flex slots, actives, investment bonuses), the seven requirements of a good build, ability point order, and how to read a hero's kit and stats to pick items.
Hero examples: Lash, Haze, Calico, Billy, Seven, Lady Geist, Viscous, Pocket, Grey Talon. The second half builds a Mina build from scratch, judging nearly every item in the shop for her and laying out laning, core and situational sections and buy order.
For beginners through experienced players who want to judge builds and make their own.

## Claims
- Items are in three categories: weapon (better gun), vitality (survivability), spirit (better abilities). [1:02]
- Within each category items are split by price tier: 800, and each next tier doubles (1,600, 3,200, 6,400). [1:02]
- Some items build into others, and owning the component discounts the upgrade: Headhunter is usually 3,200 but 2,400 if you already own Headshot Booster. [1:02]
- Extended Magazine builds into Titanic Magazine and Escalating Resilience. [1:02]
- Everyone starts with eight item slots; any tier takes exactly one slot, and you can buy as many items from any category as you want (even all green). [1:33]
- Four extra (flex) slots unlock by destroying enemy objectives, e.g. one when you destroy three enemy guardians and the fourth when you destroy a set of base guardians. [1:33]
- Most items give base stats plus a unique passive: Enduring Speed gives movement speed and out-of-combat regen plus a slow resist passive. [2:03]
- Some items only give base stats (Extra Health), and some only a passive with no base stats (Mystic Regeneration). [2:03]
- Active items like Cold Front take a regular slot and one of four active slots; they are basically an extra ability any hero can buy. [2:03]
- Cold Front is a small expanding ice blast around you that slows and deals damage; Warp Stone is a short teleport that gives bullet resist for some seconds after. [2:34]
- Investment bonuses: weapon investment gives bonus weapon damage, vitality gives a percentage increase in base health, spirit gives extra spirit power. [2:34]
- Reaching an investment bonus gives stats even if the item has none of them: Compressed Cooldown gives no spirit itself, but bought and imbued it gave 11 spirit by reaching two investment bonuses. [3:05]
- Investment bonuses are not linear; the most valuable checkpoint in each category is the first, at 800 souls (one item). [3:05]
- Investment value falls off after 9.6k souls in a category (e.g. a 6.4k item plus two 1,600 items). [3:36]
- For most heroes it is worth investing at least a little into each category, because the first checkpoints are so valuable. [4:06]
- Example: Restorative Shot on a spirit hero gives 6% weapon damage plus 7% from the investment bonus, 13% weapon damage for 800 souls. [4:06]
- Don't obsess over investment bonuses: a common mistake is breaching 9.6k in every tree when it makes no sense for the hero. [4:36]
- Most heroes have one or two categories they synergize well with and a third they don't; buy items your hero does well with and reorder them to line up with the bonuses. [4:36]
- Lash has great synergy with spirit and with some vitality items (Warp Stone, Majestic Leap, Stamina Mastery). [5:06]
- Lash has some of the lowest base gun DPS in the game and a burst gun; fire rate only shortens the time between bursts, not between bullets in a burst, so building gun on Lash is really bad. [5:06]
- An inventory of only green and purple on Lash is normal; Mystic Shot seems fine on him but isn't worth the slot over his spirit and vitality items. [5:36]
- On Lash you can buy Restorative Shot, Headshot Booster or Monster Rounds in lane and sell them later for slots. [5:36]
- Buying cheap early items and selling them later is normal; Healing Rite is a very common sustain item usually sold after the laning phase. [5:36]
- Don't overdo early items: usually buy two, maybe three, that you sell later, not a full inventory of early green items. [6:07]
- Take the time to read every item in the shop; it is overwhelming but worth it. [6:07]
- Requirement 1 of a good build: an ability point order that prioritizes abilities worth prioritizing and is efficient with points. [6:38]
- Requirements 2 and 3: it amplifies or works with the hero's strengths, and minimizes the hero's weaknesses. [6:38]
- Requirement 4: it doesn't require too many slots or selling a bunch of items mid-game. [6:38]
- Requirement 5: it doesn't take too long to come online or need too many expensive items. [6:38]
- Requirements 6 and 7: it can be adapted to every game with situational items, and it keeps investment bonuses in mind. [7:08]
- Ability point order is under-discussed but really important: decide which one or two abilities the build focuses on. [7:08]
- You usually can't max a third ability until very late (about 35k souls), and you never get enough points to max all four. [7:08]
- Some heroes are flexible: Viscous can focus on maxing pretty much any ability and run a solid build; Pocket has only one or two viable skill orders. [7:38]
- Some heroes want one ability maxed asap because its tier 3 is a big spike: Grey Talon's Charged Shot and Seven's Power Surge. [7:38]
- Pocket puts two points into Flying Cloak and Enchanter's Satchel (big early spikes), then goes tier 3 on Barrage or Affliction depending on play style or the game. [8:08]
- Usually copy a high-level player's skill point order for the hero and adjust a few points to taste. [8:08]
- Be cautious spending ability points in random places, even cheap one or two AP upgrades; every AP delays your other power spikes. Even high-level players make this mistake. [8:08]
- Example: rushing one ability to tier 3 gets it maxed at 7.5k souls with eight AP; one point elsewhere first delays it to 8.4k, and another one or two push it to 9.4k or 10.6k, several minutes later. [8:38]
- Pick items that amplify the hero's strengths and minimize weaknesses, judged from abilities, ability spirit scalings and base stats. [9:08]
