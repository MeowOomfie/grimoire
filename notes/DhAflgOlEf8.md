channel: aquatiq

## Covers
Drifter, aquatiq, commentary over one game of spirit (Rend) Drifter (patch of late July 2026).
Mostly gameplay; useful parts are the spirit item path and a few item verdicts (Trophy Collector, Mystic Reverb, Veil Walker).

## Claims
- He starts the spirit build with Mystic Burst; it was already one-shotting low targets with almost no items. [2:03]
- Hit the wave first before trading, or you take too much minion damage. [2:03]
- If he lands a Rend on a low target, she is dead. [3:37]
- Points go into Rend, since it is a spirit build built around it. [4:41]
- He takes Spirit Snatch. [5:12]
- Crushing Fists is a bit of a meme on Drifter; not useless, but there is no real point to it. [5:12]
- He bought melee for more sustain in a hard lane. [11:00]
- He went Improved Spirit, then Veil Walker; he loves Veil Walker on Drifter. [15:04]
- He bought Surge of Power mid game. [21:18]
- He wanted souls for Escalating Exposure. [22:20]
- A teammate's tip: when Drifter is in his ult, press Unstoppable; Lightning Scroll is another good item because it stuns. [23:22]
- Spirit Burn was his next planned item. [24:24]
- Silence Wave plan: walk up on someone, Silence Wave them, then kill them. [27:00]
- He tried Mystic Reverb on his 1; it did nothing that game. [30:04]
- Trophy Collector is very good, but only with max stacks. [31:06]
- He sold Trophy Collector for Mystic Reverb and regretted it; he should have kept it. [33:17]
