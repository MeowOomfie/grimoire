## Covers
First look at Solomon, the new chessboard spirit hero: Setup Bishop, the Knight/King promote, the Rooks and the Queen ultimate, how each moves and levels, combos, and early build direction.
For players picking up Solomon on release. No ability order or matchups given.

## Claims
- Solomon is the newest hero, a chessboard caster who combos enemies with heavy spirit damage; the coach calls him the most complex hero ever added, with many buttons (Invoker players from Dota will like him). [0:00]
- Ability 1 Setup Bishop: place a bishop anywhere; placing it does nothing and does not even hit the enemy. [0:31]
- Move the bishop by right clicking where you want it to go; you can move it multiple times, and anyone it passes through takes impact damage and a movement slow. [1:02]
- After moving, the bishop gets the diagonal buff: when you next move it diagonally, anyone on the diagonal square takes bonus spirit damage. [1:02]
- Enemies not on the bishop's diagonal can't be hit by the bonus, so plan your first placement so the target sits on a diagonal, then wait for the diagonal buff before moving it onto them. [1:32]
- Ability 2 on an ally turns them into a Knight: a shield plus one stamina at base, two stamina at level two, and you can then move your ally into or out of the fight. [2:02]
- You can destroy and reset the Rooks, which have two charges, but each can only be moved one time. [2:02]
- Self-cast ability 2 to promote yourself to King: you can move anywhere and give yourself a barrier, so you can move yourself out of or into a fight. [2:33]
- Ability 2 level ups lower its cooldown and add more barrier. [2:33]
- Self-casting ability 2 is a free dispel that removes all non-ultimate debuffs (for example Infernus burn); it does not remove ults despite the tooltip. [3:03]
- Ability 3 the Rooks immobilize, which is not a stun: the enemy can't move or use movement abilities but can still shoot and use their other abilities. [3:03]
- The rook moves in a straight line and bounces back off a wall; base level gives one wall reflect, max level gives two reflects, lower cooldown and longer immobilize. [3:34]
- Alt-cast the rook to send it sideways; you can bounce, sideways cast, then sideways cast again. [4:05]
- Treat the Rooks like a Vindicta stake: cast them against a wall so they come back. [7:38]
- Ultimate the Queen: crashes down anywhere on the board for massive AoE spirit damage. [4:05]
- At ult level two, enemies below 200 health are executed; the threshold scales with your level, up to 375 health at max level, so use it as an execute. [4:05]
- The Queen has two moves at base: after it charges, you can replace it anywhere in a big radius and hit again; at max level you can do this four times, stunning enemies again and again. [4:36]
- Weave other pieces between Queen moves (rook into a wall, knight yourself, bishop moves); Solomon is a massive AoE team fight hero. [5:37]
- Expect a very low (around 10%) win rate at release while people learn him, unless the numbers turn out overtuned, which apart from the ult they don't seem to be. [6:07]
- Build for duration: rook immobilize, Knight shields, ult stun and other pieces all last longer; duration and expansion items look really strong on him. [6:07]
- No good build is known yet and it will take a while for people to figure one out. [6:38]
- His gun is reportedly the best in the game and shreds, so a gun build may work, though it will probably be nerfed. [6:38]
- Solomon is easy to misplay: with multiple pieces out, aiming toward an ability stops you self-casting, so you must aim away; do things in the right order or you lose the fight. [7:08]
- Solomon floats, giving him a free magic carpet. [8:08]
