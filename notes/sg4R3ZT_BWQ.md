# A Complete Deadlock Guide for BEGINNERS - (OUTDATED)
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-01-26
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sg4R3ZT_BWQ

## Covers
A first-steps guide for brand new players: stamina and map movement, ziplines, jump pads, teleporters, hero choice, laning and souls, boxes, camps, bridge buffs, center sacrifices, Mid Boss and Rejuvenator, Urn, Walkers, shrines and the Patron.
Marked OUTDATED by the channel: it describes the invite-only era with nine starting item slots plus three Walker flex slots, shrines and the two-phase Patron in the base, so check timings and slot numbers against the current patch.

## Claims
- Deadlock has no mana; abilities are cooldown based, with a few exceptions charged by criteria like damage dealt or received. [0:31]
- You win by killing the enemy Patron. [0:31]
- Out of combat you get bonus sprint speed, which goes away when you take or deal damage. [1:02]
- Stamina options are the air dash, regular dash, dash jump, wall jump and down dash. [1:02]
- As a new player don't use the down dash, it is extremely rarely useful. [1:02]
- Learn the dash jump first; it is mostly timing based and is your go-to way to move around the map. [1:02]
- After the dash jump, learn air dashes; they become incredibly important at a higher level. [1:33]
- Don't spam stamina mindlessly; it is a resource to use with purpose. [1:33]
- The zipline is the primary way to traverse the map since there are no TP scrolls; always use it when one lies between you and where you are going. [2:04]
- Let go of the zipline with crouch (Ctrl or whatever crouch is bound to) instead of jumping off; you keep more momentum and get propelled further. [2:04]
- Always use the jump pads scattered over the map to rotate when you can. [2:04]
- If you get shot while taking a jump pad or getting on the zipline, you are stunned and take significant damage. [2:35]
- Teleporters are extremely underused even by experienced players; learn their locations and make a habit of using them. [2:35]
- Pick whatever hero looks cool to you; don't overthink hero choice. [3:05]
- Heroes with simple kits like Abrams, Mo & Krill or McGinnis are new player friendly; complicated kits like Pocket or Mina are not. [3:05]
- Try a bunch of heroes and stick with the ones you enjoy. [3:36]
- The item system lets you adapt a hero to the playstyle you want. [3:36]
- Deadlock has no traditional roles; you don't queue as mid laner, side laner or carry, so don't worry about the meta or roles yet. [3:36]
- The first 8 to 10 minutes are the laning stage, and your main concern is getting souls by killing troopers and enemy heroes. [4:06]
- Objectives and boxes also give souls. [4:36]
- A killed trooper spawns a soul in the air that you must shoot to secure; enemies can shoot it first to deny it, and you can deny theirs the same way. [4:36]
- Part of the soul also drops on the ground and comes to you automatically when you are near it. [4:36]
- The healing trooper drops a med pack when killed that heals you and nearby teammates. [4:36]
- Be aggressive and don't play scared; making mistakes teaches you the game much faster than trying to play optimally from the start. [5:07]
- Rule of thumb: always push the wave into the enemy tower; pushing the wave is generally rewarded in Deadlock, unlike more passive MOBAs. [5:07]
- Boxes (brown crates) spawn around the map at 2 minutes; hit a few any time you are between waves, the souls add up over the game. [5:37]
- Golden statues near the boxes give permanent buffs instead of souls and are good to get. [5:37]
- You can parry the Guardian. [6:07]
- For items, follow a popular in-game build from the build section and buy roughly in order. [6:07]
- Builds per hero and a tier list are on the channel's site deadlockmeta.com. [6:38]
- You start with nine item slots and unlock three more over the game; each of the three Walkers you kill grants an extra slot. [6:38]
- At 2 minutes a small jungle camp (tier one camp) spawns in some lanes; camps always show on the minimap. [7:08]
- Farm the tier one camp as fast as possible; it gives you extra souls over an opponent who has none. [7:08]
- At 5 minutes the bridge buffs spawn, one on each side; reach them by the rope or the jump pads and claim one with a heavy melee. [7:08]
- There are four bridge buffs: movement, vitality, spirit and weapon; they last 160 seconds. [7:38]
- Bridge buffs respawn every 5 minutes: 5, 10, 15, 20 minutes and so on. [7:38]
- At 6 minutes the medium jungle camps (tier two camps) spawn; most heroes can clear them pretty efficiently. [7:38]
- Tier three camps are the hardest to clear; only a few heroes can clear them efficiently the moment they spawn. [8:09]
- Center sacrifices spawn at 8 minutes; you punch them for souls and they damage you each hit, so always heavy melee them because it takes fewer hits than light melee. [8:09]
- Time the final heavy melee on a center sacrifice to start when three lights at the top remain unlit; done right ("the jackpot") it gives four golden statue buffs, which are permanent. [8:39]
- Always farm the center sacrifices when you can, ideally right at 8 minutes; they give a ton of souls and the permanent buffs are huge. [9:09]
- Ideally steal the enemy center sacrifice, or farm your own if you can't. [9:09]
- Jungle farming gives unsecured souls that drop for the enemy if you die; bank them often. [9:09]
- At 10 minutes the Urn and Mid Boss spawn, which starts the mid game. [9:09]
- Mid game priority one: push the trooper waves as much as you can everywhere on the map; it gives you freedom to do other things. [9:40]
- Only fight with an advantage: more souls, a 2v1, a 4v3. [10:10]
- Don't stay in the same lane all game unless your hero just wants to farm; push a wave, then move around the map and do something. [10:10]
- Defeating the Mid Boss drops the Rejuvenator, which you secure by heavy meleeing it; it can be hit three times, each hit giving one Rejuvenator buff. [10:41]
- Each Rejuvenator buff is an extra life: a dead teammate uses one and respawns at their death location after a few seconds; each player can only benefit from it once. [10:41]
- Rejuvenator buffs can be split between teams, for example two hits for one team and one for the other. [10:41]
- Mid Boss spawning at 10 minutes doesn't mean you should take it; past 15 to 20 minutes, consider Mid Boss after winning a big team fight to push your lead. [11:11]
- The Rejuvenator buff lasts 4 minutes, so you don't need to act right away after getting it. [11:11]
- The Urn is carried from one side of the map to the other like capture the flag; while carrying it you are silenced and can't use weapons, abilities or items, but can use stamina and melee. [11:42]
- While carrying the Urn you can't use ziplines or teleporters. [11:42]
- Picking up the Urn highlights its deposit area: on the enemy side means your team is winning, a neutral middle spot means the game is even, on your side means you are losing; candles around the Urn show which before you pick it up. [11:42]
- The Urn is a comeback mechanic, worth a lot of souls, and massive for your team whether winning, losing or even; do it if you realistically can. [12:12]
- Don't go 5 minutes without touching the Urn. [12:42]
- Good times to run the Urn: when a few enemies are dead, or when all the waves are pushed up. [12:42]
- Walkers are the second line of defense after the Guardians. [12:42]
- Inside a Walker's inner circle it stomps; inside the outer circle it generally uses fireballs. [13:12]
- Walker stomp stuns everyone in the area and does massive damage; step out of the inner circle when you see it stomping, or dodge it with an ability. [13:12]
- Walker fireballs do heavy damage over time; move to another spot in the outer circle instead of standing in them. [13:42]
- Walkers are hard to kill early; don't force them much before 13 or 14 minutes. [13:42]
- Killing one Walker gives the other Walkers slightly more health. [14:12]
- Each Walker gives a flex slot and a lot of souls. [14:12]
- Dying for a Walker early is almost always worth it: losing one person for a Walker is worth it, losing two or three usually is not. [14:12]
- Take Mid Boss before trying to end the game; the base is hard to push without the Rejuvenator and an advantage in the tens of thousands of souls. [14:43]
- You must destroy both shrines (one left, one right) before you can hit the Patron. [15:14]
- Shrine attack: a circle that shrinks onto the shrine and explodes, cursing you (no abilities or items) and doing significant damage; back up when the circle starts shrinking. [15:14]
- Melee the shrines as well as shooting them unless you are a full gun hero; it speeds things up a lot. [15:44]
- Patron phase one can be shot from outside the ritual circle; at half health it transforms for about 10 seconds into phase two. [15:44]
- In Patron phase two you must be inside the ritual circle to deal damage, and it casts an expanding circle that does massive damage and curses you; step out, then step back in right after. [16:14]
- Nearby defenders (inside the circle) give the structure resistance, and two defenders make it invulnerable, so fight the enemy team before shooting the Patron. [16:45]
- With only one enemy defender you can usually ignore him and hit the Patron; with two or more, fight them. [16:45]
