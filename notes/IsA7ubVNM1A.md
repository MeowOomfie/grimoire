## Covers
First-look analysis of Apollo, a new frontline movement hero from the Old Gods New Blood update, tested in the sandbox on release.
Every ability with numbers and upgrades, the Riposte parry tech, Flawless Advance timing and direction chaining, ultimate behavior, skill order and a first spirit/tanky build.
For players picking up or playing against Apollo in February 2026.

## Claims
- Apollo is a frontline movement hero who disrupts the front line and counters enemy heroes with Riposte; the speaker sees him as a spirit nuker and close-range counter hero. [0:00]
- He calls Apollo the hardest hero in the game with an insane skill ceiling, and says the numbers seem overtuned. [0:00]
- The weapon is a sword that shoots projectiles with fairly far range, can headshot, has 15 ammo and quite high damage. [0:33]
- Ability 1, Disengaging Sigil: draws a sigil in front, damages in front and leaps backward. [0:33]
- Disengaging Sigil upgrades: +30 damage; then 20% fire rate and bullet speed for 10 seconds after; max level restores two stamina and resets your air dash limit. [0:33]
- Sigil gives no instant movement speed, so dashing away after is slow, but at max level you can use all stamina, cast it, and dash again; you can dash in or out. [1:03]
- Ability 2, Riposte: a deflect against any melee, gun or ability attack that makes you invulnerable for 0.3 seconds, then you dash forward, stun the enemy and lower their bullet resist. He calls it a better Counterspell. [1:33]
- Riposte upgrades: more bullet resist reduction, more cooldown reduction, and more stun duration at max level. [2:03]
- Riposte can parry anything in the game, including Seven's stun while it is being cast or at the end of it; you do not have to time it perfectly against Seven's stun. [2:03]
- You can turn 360 degrees while performing Riposte, so you can counter something like an Abrams charge and then turn 180 to stun behind you; this needs near frame-perfect timing. [2:33]
- The Riposte timing is forgiving, about the same duration as Counterspell; the hard part is hitting the enemy after countering, since many countered abilities come from enemies not in front of you. [3:35]
- Ability 3, Flawless Advance: a series of three lunges in any direction stabbing in front; hold the key and release in a timing window for bonus perfect damage. [4:05]
- Flawless Advance upgrades: level one adds a heal; level two increases lunge speed so you reach the perfect lunge faster; max level increases perfect damage and spirit scaling. [4:05]
- Flawless Advance is probably what you max first because of its max-level scaling and damage, and it is not hard to hit: release when the circle reaches the green circle for full damage, heal and bonus damage. [4:36]
- When you start each lunge you can hold a direction key or jump to aim it, so you can chain lunges in any direction and dodge enemy attacks during the downtime before the perfect lunge. [5:08]
- At max level Flawless Advance has 1.9 spirit scaling on perfect damage and 0.8 spirit scaling on the heal, so you will 100% build raw spirit. [5:38]
- Ultimate (name heard as "Itani loan"): charges up and performs a long-range slash; struck enemies are slowed, and when the effect expires they take devastating damage, doubled (100% bonus) if the enemy is below 50% health. [6:08]
- You can hold the ultimate; its range is very long, you are untargetable after casting, you always end at maximum range (even aiming down), and pop out of the shadows about a second later. [6:39]
- The ultimate can hit multiple enemies and has insane spirit scaling, so spirit plus tanky is king on Apollo. [7:09]
- Torment Pulse in the default build works with the ultimate: your shadow pulses during the cast and after. [7:39]
- Apollo wants close range: ability 1 is basically melee range, Riposte is best used on close-range heroes so you can stun them after, while Flawless Advance is deceptively long range. [8:11]
- After the first Flawless Advance lunge you have several seconds to cast the second, which resets the timer for the third, so you can weave other abilities (like a counter) between lunges. [8:41]
- He calls Apollo the hardest hero to master, like a harder Yamato, and likely overtuned. [9:11]
- Item first impressions: Torment Pulse is a no-brainer, as are Improved Spirit or any raw spirit; Mystic Burst or Mystic Regeneration early; movement items; Stamina Mastery is an insane item. [9:42]
- He thinks this is the best default build Valve has put out for the new heroes; he is unsure about Close Quarters and may skip gun damage or just go Monster Rounds. [9:42]
- Mystic Regeneration is very good because you use many abilities and can chain them during the long Flawless Advance window. [10:12]
- The Flawless Advance window counts from when the animation ends, not starts, so start the next lunge a bit before the window runs out to land the perfect lunge and reset it. [10:42]
- Build him tanky with several vitality items: his base HP is low, more than Celeste at max level but not tank HP, and he must be close to use his abilities. [11:12]
- Having Counterspell and Riposte at the same time would be very strong. [11:12]
- His first-game plan, which he thinks may be meta: an early 4.8k spirit investment in lane for damage, possibly Improved Spirit in lane. [11:44]
- Skill order: one point in Disengaging Sigil for the extra damage (fire rate and bullet speed matter less since you mostly use abilities). It does 130 damage on base on a 12-second cooldown, one of the highest early damage abilities. [11:44]
- One point in Riposte to have the counter up more often; it counters everything, so shooting him lets him counter and stun you. [12:16]
- Max Flawless Advance first: sustain at level one, cooldown reduction to use it every 16 seconds, faster charge to the perfect lunge, and crazy damage at max with early 4.8k spirit investment. [12:48]
- The ultimate's base range is already very large, so its extra dash range upgrade is not needed early. [12:48]
- In a sandbox test with early 4.8k spirit (Mystic Burst, Mystic Regeneration) Flawless Advance did crazy damage even against Abrams. [13:18]
- Verdict: insanely high skill ceiling, probably insanely busted and likely to be nerfed in the first big balance patch expected within days; abuse him while you can. He may be more broken than Celeste. [14:18]
