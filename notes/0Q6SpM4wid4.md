## Covers
Drifter, aquatiq, commentary over one game of spirit Drifter against Graves, Shiv and Seven (patch of late July 2026).
Mostly gameplay; useful parts are the spirit item path and why he picked each item.

## Claims
- Normally he gets Stalker as the first item; in this spirit build he starts with Mystic Burst and gets Stalker a bit later. [1:38]
- If your lane partner will not give you solo, buy Extra Regen in lane rather than Melee Lifesteal. [2:09]
- Spirit Strike only procs when you melee or use a melee ability, so remember to actually hit someone with Rend (or your fist) to proc it. [2:39]
- Play passive when you are not full HP against Shiv, since his knives can one-shot you. [4:10]
- The Extra Regen kept him alive with four Shiv daggers in him. [8:51]
- He bought Healbane against the enemy healing. [8:51]
- He bought Trophy Collector and stacked it while ganking. [6:15]
- He bought Slowing Hex. [12:38]
- Veil Walker is very good on Drifter, not just for invisibility but because it heals you when you walk through a veil; he walks through veils a lot. [15:22]
- Because Veil Walker gives sustain, he did not need Fortitude. [17:59]
- Surge of Power is a must; he would never skip it. [17:59]
- Tank Buster is better than Mystic Burst but more expensive. [17:59]
- Toxic Bullets was for Billy and Shiv, who heal too much. [18:29]
- He would never chase a Shiv. [22:43]
- Spirit Burn came next; he sold Stalker and regretted it. [23:15]
- He realized he needed Counterspell. [24:47]
- Verdict: spirit Drifter feels broken and fun. [19:30]
