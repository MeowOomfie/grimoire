# The Deadlock Hero Tier List - Worst Patch Ever?
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-06-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KTJ0sUZV8o

## Covers
Hero tier list for the patch current on 2025-06-24, rated for high-rank solo queue ladder viability and carry potential, not pro play.
Explains how that patch (more gold from kills and objectives, harder flex slots, lane creep gold nerfed) created a lane bully, perma team fight meta, then places every hero.
For ranked players choosing a hero; main advice is pick lane bullies.

## Claims
- The list is rated on high-rank ladder viability and carry potential, not pro play. [0:00]
- In the latest patch kills give more money and objectives give more money to whoever takes them. [0:00]
- Flex slots are harder to get: you need three walkers or three guardians for the first, and three walkers for the third. [0:00]
- Lane creep gold was nerfed, so team fighting becomes exceptionally important. [0:32]
- In practice this promotes a lane bully meta: heroes strong in lane or early mid game become too strong, like before the item rework patch. [0:32]
- Split pushing is risky now, and jungle and lane creeps give less money, so farming and farming or picking off in the enemy jungle are worth less. [1:03]
- High-level play, including Deadlock Fight Night, is now perma team fighting; he argues this makes the game one-dimensional and unhealthy. [1:34]
- Calico got nerfed on paper (harder box breaking, less Leaping Slash scaling) but is still S tier, as strong or stronger, because she is great at winning mid game fights early. [2:35]
- Dynamo is still okay and solid in team fights; his win rate is above 50% in Eternus and Ascendant. [3:05]
- Grey Talon is still bad: squishy, not good in team fights, and a pickoff hero when pickoffs matter less, so even worse than before. [3:05]
- Haze is easy A plus: get an early silence wave item, walk up to an enemy, silence wave and ult them; she is a pickoff hero who can also start fights, and kills are valuable. [3:35]
- Holliday had no direct changes but went back to domination tier in high rank because Rescue Beam's cooldown was doubled. [4:07]
- Rescue Beam was the hardest counter to Lasso; fewer people buy it now, so Lasso gets free kills again, and Holliday is a strong laner and the best pickoff initiator. [4:07]
- Infernus is completely broken, probably top three, maybe the best for ladder; he was not nerfed at all. [4:38]
- Late game Infernus with Ricochet, Inhibitor and Leech can't die and kills your whole team; since the item rework he is also strong mid game because he puts out a lot of damage with few items. [4:38]
- Infernus is a hard carry like Wraith, but also solid in lane and strong mid game. [5:09]
- McGinnis gets stronger because every other left clicker was nerfed; she is solid in team fights and high-rank players run gun on her and hard carry. He places her B, later says he rated her a bit too high. [5:09]
- Mirage is not balanced: insane before the patch and not nerfed; his only weakness, slow farming, does not matter when money comes from kills. [5:39]
- Mirage is the best or top two laner in the game and probably the best early mid game hero, doing big damage at every stage. [6:12]
- Mo & Krill is better again because kills are good and he wants kills. [6:44]
- Pocket is easily A if played well; he falls off past 30 minutes but until then can one-shot anyone, is great in team fights and is the most survivable hero if played properly. [6:44]
- Seven is A in ranked, almost S: he scales well into late game and does a lot in mid game fights if built for his ultimate. [7:14]
- Seven ultimate build: Suppressor, Healbane and Spirit Lifesteal, then fight under the Urn; the more people in a fight, the better he gets. [7:14]
- Shiv is back to broken, S tier: the best mid game hero, possibly second to Mirage in the first Urn fights; he doesn't die, one-shots almost everything and is the tankiest hero. [7:44]
- Sinclair is truly broken but hard to learn, so it takes the average player a while to get value from him. [8:14]
- Sinclair: mass rabbit AoE melee and Vexing Bolt AoE, and with Mirage the best laner in the game, winning every lane alone; he may need a hard nerf. Placed "easily a tier" while he is filling the top tiers. [8:46]
- Viscous is A in ladder; in pro play he is first ban or first pick, but much of his value comes from cubes and a disrupting ultimate, so he needs a team that does well. [8:46]

- Bebop is coming back and was undervalued: still a lane bully though weaker in lane because everyone has more HP; it is hard to lose lane on him and the hook is a free kill later. [10:19]

- Ivy is mid: still a support, slightly better because other left clickers were nerfed if she gets money, but play her as a support; good but not insane. [10:50]
