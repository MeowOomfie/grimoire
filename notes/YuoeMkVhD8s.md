# How To Carry | Deadlock Role Guide
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-06-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YuoeMkVhD8s

## Covers
Role guide for the position one gun (M1) carry: the hero with the most farm and the best scaling, whose job is to deal the most damage in fights without getting caught.
Which heroes fit, lane anchoring and box routes, jungle timers (with a note that it predates the latest urn and macro changes), spikes, split pushing, farming rules and fight positioning.

## Claims
- Carries generally have the most farm, so they output the most damage, and most carries scale best because gun naturally outscales spirit. [0:00]
- A carry's main goal is to do as much damage as possible in team fights without getting caught out or focused. [0:00]
- Drifter is a gun carry, but unlike other gun carries he wants to brawl and hunt stacks rather than farm. [0:30]
- Playing a hero captioned "Get Grays" as a gun carry is trolling and completely bad. [0:30]
- Haze is definitely a gun carry in average solo queue, but in high elo she becomes more of a pick hero. [0:30]
- Infernus and Mirage are gun carries; McGinnis can be played as one; Lady Geist can too, though her current meta build drifts into spirit. [0:30]
- Seven, Venator, Vyper, Warden and Wraith are more classical gun carries. [0:30]
- Silver is kind of a gun carry but sits with Drifter, wanting to brawl more; Ivy can be played as a carry though it isn't her meta build right now. [1:01]
- In lane, carries have the easiest job: stay in lane and get as much farm as possible; at high level this means anchoring while your lane partner roams and ganks. [1:01]
- Don't just sit in lane; if you have space, run a box route to maximize souls, and farm camps right off spawn, since more early farm snowballs into the mid and late game. [1:01]
- Timers after the latest changes: tier one camps at 2:00, breakables at 3:00, tier twos and buffs at 5:00, tier threes and Sinner's Sacrifices at 8:00, with buffs continuing to spawn at every 5 and 10 minute mark. [1:31]
- Staying alive in lane is very important, especially if your partner is roaming; if you struggle to kill the wave or are threatened with dives, Monster Rounds is a good buy for extra damage to lane troopers. [2:01]
- If two enemies run at you after 8 minutes when towers are squishy, or a roam gives them a big numbers advantage, back off and give the tower if you can't defend it without dying. [2:01]
- Watch the minimap: if enemies roam, you killed one or both laners, or many are fighting across the map, use that space to threaten the lane objective for team value and a ton of souls. [2:31]
- You usually don't want to siege walkers before about 12 minutes, though it is doable if the enemies leave it completely unguarded. [2:31]
- In the mid and late game, play around your spikes; most carries want to farm until 20 minutes, because fights before then have little weight without mid boss on the table. [2:31]
- If you aren't already in a side lane around 8 to 12 minutes, transition to one for solo farm without splitting a wave and to threaten enemy objectives. [3:01]
- When you hit a spike, look for a fight or brawl while strong, then cycle back to the jungle. [3:01]
- The biggest spikes are 4.8k souls in any investment tree (vitality, spirit or gun), ability maxes, and big items: early tier threes and especially your first tier four. [3:01]
- For Warden, maxing the flask lowers its cooldown, which also lets him farm much faster. [3:32]
- People overcomplicate spikes; generally a spike is just when you buy a big item, though some heroes have specific strong spikes. [3:32]
- Exception: Infernus is so countered by items that his strength is the mid game, so unlike other gun carries he wants to fight from 12 to 20 minutes using strong tier three afterburn damage. [4:02]
- A hero captioned "Vex" is also strong in that period but scales well, so he is usually played farming, and fights more in the mid game only if the comp calls for it. [4:02]
- While the rest of your team runs the urn, stay on the weak side to farm; if the enemy doesn't leave their gun carry in that lane, take a walker for a ton of souls. [4:33]
- Split pushing is insanely strong on gun carries, especially at lower elo, because they have a comically low time to kill on objectives: one wave of shove or one enemy too many on the far side lets any gun carry delete the walker. [4:33]
- If your teammates take a bad fight over nothing and you can't rotate in time, use the attention they draw to get real value on the map instead of joining a coin flip fight. [5:04]
- Watch your bag: do not fight while sitting on a 1 to 2K unspent bag. [5:34]
- Aim to be top souls on your team; being hyper fed is important in solo queue on any hero, especially gun carries. [5:34]
- Because of increased respawn timers it is not ideal to be insanely fed; if you are pulling far ahead, join important team fights over objectives to use your lead and raise your team's souls. [5:34]
- As a split-pushing gun carry, prefer the side lane that still has a walker so you apply more threat. [6:05]
- Hybrid camps (the side camps by your walkers and the basement camp) are very good for souls, because small creeps give almost as many souls as medium ones while large creeps are much worse per clear. [6:05]
- When ahead, farm riskier, aggressive farm first; if you leave lane very fed, invading and stealing enemy farm is very important. [6:35]
- Ideal pattern: acquire a bag in the enemy jungle, walk back with the returning wave farming your own jungle, then ping-pong the wave back; never acquire a bag in your own jungle and carry it into the enemy's. [6:35]
- On your own side, secure the aggressive camps that could be invaded before the safe camps by your walkers. [7:06]
- Prioritize Sinner's Sacrifices when farming, because their permanent buffs are hidden souls that don't raise the enemy's comeback mechanic, especially valuable on large HP heroes like Warden. [7:06]
- Heroes like Seven farm normal camps more efficiently, so on a quick timer or a safe invade it can be better to take a big tier three camp, and to leave Sinner's Sacrifices to someone else. [7:36]
- Fights on gun carries are simple: hold M1; the biggest difference maker is how efficiently you farmed before the fight. [8:06]
- Position behind your front line, avoid being hard focused or overextending, and gun down anyone who commits on you, especially squishies. [8:06]
- Avoid shooting the front line when you can; look for an enemy squishy who mispositions in front of their front line. [8:06]
- Staying near your supports so they can peel for you is valuable, though solo queue fights are more of a blender. [8:37]
- Always have an exit from a fight; with a teleport like Wraith's, position where you can use it, and on slower or bigger heroes know your exit before you get jumped. [8:37]
- At mid boss, the most fed player should just gun people down rather than go for melees on the Rejuvenator, because getting parried and giving a free kill loses the fight. [8:37]
