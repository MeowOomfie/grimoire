## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream recorded 1/21/26, the week before a big patch, on the patch that changed jungle camps and the Urn.
He reviews four student replays: a Pocket (Ascendant, on Cosmetical's build), a support Sinclair (Oracle), a Calico (Ascendant) and a bomb Bebop (Alchemist).
