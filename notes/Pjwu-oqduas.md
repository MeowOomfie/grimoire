## Covers
Violet movement tech, Eido channel.

## Claims
- The brush (flow state) lets you slide infinitely as long as the brush is out. [0:06]
- Do a little 180 to keep the brush closer to you. [0:06]
- The brush slide scales with your movement speed: faster down ramps, slightly slower up ramps. [0:36]
- The left click (with the 1) just shoots a ball that does damage; it is your main DPS skill. [0:36]
- The paint acts as a wall that bullets cannot go through; currently the only way to break it is a heavy punch, which damages the enemy who does it. [0:36]
- The paint creates hitboxes that are perfect squares; as your stroke continues it adds more squares to fill the new area, which is why you cannot move through it. [1:06]
- A console command (captioned "cl_n_v collide_wireframe") shows the paint's hitboxes. [1:06]
- Because the paint is squares, you can use it like any other terrain: edge boost by jumping off its corner, or wall bounce off a higher part. [1:36]
- Edge boosting is better than a wall bounce because it gives more vertical height and horizontal distance. [2:07]
- S-shape tech: start slightly back and draw an S; the corners and edges of the S hitboxes are where your feet meet for the boost. [2:07]
- Sometimes you get an edge boost with noticeable distance, but most of the time, especially when starting out, it is a wall bounce. [2:38]
- Chain a second boost off the top of the S. [2:38]
- You are placing the squares at your feet so you can press space and jump again to carry your momentum and velocity. [3:09]
- The S-shape double boost is very important because it helps you escape. [3:09]
- Because you draw while boosting, chasers get stuck behind your paint and must stop and heavy punch, by which time you are far away. [3:40]
- If Lash (captioned "Flash") is about to ult from above you, draw a stroke above and hide under it. [3:40]
- Block enemy attacks with paint, including towers: draw a stroke up and you can break line of sight with the tower completely, run up to it, and it will not hit you. [3:40]
- You can draw an L shape and boost off it, angling the squares, but that is very niche. [4:11]
- The S-shape boost and blocking are the most valuable techs in both comp matches and standard games. [4:41]
- Medium range (about 10 m) shots with her 1 are hard to land; a teammate compared them to Wraith cards. [19:36]
