## Covers
A 3-minute Calico guide (January 2026): pros and cons, abilities, the Arctic Blast full combo, escape tricks with the cat cooldown, lane, mid game invading and sinner steals, late game flanks and builds.
For players learning spirit Calico.

## Claims
- Pros: very mobile, escapes bad positions, lots of burst damage, and a really good box farmer. [0:01]
- Cons: poor sustained and anti-frontline damage, and heavily shut down by anti-mobility items like Slowing Hex. [0:01]
- Gloom Bombs (1) throws four bombs, seven when maxed, that explode after a couple of seconds for a lot of damage; they stick to targets, so landing them on the enemy makes them much easier to hit. [0:01]
- Leaping Slash (2) dashes forward and slashes enemies around her for a little damage and heals her. [0:31]
- Her 3 turns her into a cat: melee breaks boxes, great for box farming once it has two points, plus percentage movement speed; getting shot puts it on a 6 second cooldown until you are out of combat. [0:31]
- Ult (Return to Shadows) deals AoE damage around her, makes her briefly invincible, then deals another AoE burst; maxed, it refunds all your cooldowns. [0:31]
- Most Calico builds buy Cold Front and later upgrade it into Arctic Blast. [1:02]
- Press Arctic Blast then 2 forward and the blast follows in front of you, so you can cast it before the combo and still get the extra damage and damage amp. [1:02]
- Full combo: Gloom Bombs, Arctic Blast, Leaping Slash, then ult, done very quickly; the Arctic Blast damage amp applies to the whole combo. [1:02]
- When close to an enemy, the 2 is hard to hit, so jump and look down to land the slash much closer to you. [1:32]
- Escape trick: since the cat goes on a 6 second cooldown when shot, kite about 3 seconds, ult, then go straight into cat when the ult ends. [1:32]
- Or use Ethereal Shift, wait for it to expire, then go into your ult; you are invulnerable in both, which brings the cat back online for escaping. [1:32]
- Calico is a decent laner: against weak laners or with a strong lane partner, play aggressively and keep looking for trades. [1:32]
- She wants to break as many boxes as possible around the map. [2:02]
- Mid game: invade enemy boxes, the cat form is great for it; if caught, use your 2 to gain height and juke. [2:02]
- When invading, steal sinner's sacrifices: lots of souls, great buffs, and her mobility gets her out. [2:02]
- Late game team fights: get around the enemy front line or come from an off angle onto the backline or carries and full combo them from 100 to zero. [2:32]
- Builds: the "Deerra Bomb Calico" build (caption spelling) by one of the top EU Calico players, or "Ido Calico" for a different spirit Calico. [2:32]
