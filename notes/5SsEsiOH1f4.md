# The BEST Warden Build In Deadlock - META Build Update
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-11-30
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5SsEsiOH1f4

## Covers
Metro's gun Warden build: High Velocity Mag and Mystic Reach in lane, Last Stand maxed early, a run-and-gun movement speed core (Fleetfoot, Slowing Hex, Slowing Bullets, Heroic Aura), Frenzy, and late game and situational options.
For Warden players who want an early ganker that turns into a team fight carry. This is a late-2024 video (November 2024 patch): the item shop, item stats and the map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Metro switched to Warden because Wraith felt weak; he wanted a tanky 1v1 killing machine that can hard carry late, and found Haze strong but a glass cannon. [0:00] [0:31]
- He calls Warden arguably one of the best pub stompers in the game right now; he took a new account to Ascendant and played him in Eternus on his main. [0:31]
- He tested many builds, and credits the European Eternus Warden player Misclicked, whose build his is very similar to. [1:02] [27:09]
- This is a gun Warden build; he thinks gun is the strongest way to carry on Warden in the current meta. [1:02] [1:33]
- Warden plays like an early game ganker who can win any 1v1 early and is very good at ganking, and late he becomes a team fight monster. [1:33]
- Warden's biggest weakness is the lane: his gun is still slow despite a recent buff, and bullet travel time adds about a 200 to 300 ms delay that costs last hits and damage. [1:33] [2:03]
- At long range, aiming for the head misses most bullets because of the slow bullet velocity. [2:34]
- High Velocity Mag is a must item and almost always his first buy; at the same range you hit far more bullets. [2:34] [3:06]
- Then Hollow Point Ward for more lane damage and spirit power, making the flasks and Binding Word hit a bit harder. [3:06]
- Then Mystic Reach, rushed in lane, mainly for Binding Word range so the enemy must run much farther to escape it. [3:06] [3:36]
- Lane kills and harassment: hit the flask to slow them, then Binding Word right after. [3:36]
- Mystic Reach is a must buy, probably the best 500 item on Warden, and usually the last item he sells. [3:36]
- Then Basic Magazine as quality of life; Warden's magazine is small early and you miss kills by having to reload. [3:36] [4:06]
- Then Mystic Burst: once Alchemical Flask is level two (+50 damage per flask), each flask also triggers Mystic Burst, cheap harassment and damage in lane. [4:06]
- Then Enduring Spirit; it is not necessary, but you always buy Slowing Hex later and Slowing Hex builds from it, so get it early for life, spirit power and regen. [4:06] [4:36]
- Then Extra Stamina; the extra dash helps secure kills and escape, and he calls it probably the best 500 green item for all heroes. [4:36]
- Then Quicksilver Reload, imbued on the flask (Fleetfoot first is also an option): it makes farming much faster and boosts fire rate in early engagements. [4:36] [5:08]
- In an early engagement still initiate with the flask, which triggers Quicksilver Reload, adds flask damage and fire rate, then burst them down. [5:08]
- Before Quicksilver Reload, Warden is not very good at clearing creep camps; with it he clears tier one and tier two camps very fast. [5:38]
- Skill order: Alchemical Flask to level two first, then one point in Binding Word, then one point in Willpower, then max Last Stand (radius, damage, then cooldown) before anything else. [6:09] [8:10]
- The single Binding Word point adds one second of immobilize, which secures many kills because you trap enemies often early with this build. [6:09]
- The Willpower point gives move speed to chase down enemies. [6:09]
- Max Last Stand early even on a gun build because it adds a lot of early fight damage and survival; use it to initiate or reactively, backing behind a corner while chased, popping it and turning the fight. [6:40]
- Without leveling Last Stand you tend to die, because many enemies run anti-heal and its lifesteal is not significant until level two; he calls it probably Warden's best skill, especially early. [7:10]
- He tried maxing Alchemical Flask first (its -6.5 second cooldown clears camps even faster), but it lacked kill potential and survivability; switching to Last Stand first got him more kills and survived engagements. [7:10] [7:40]
- After Last Stand level anything: he tends to go Willpower next for survival and a low cooldown, or you can max Alchemical Flask first. [8:10]
- Level Binding Word last: you only use it about once per team fight so the cooldown does not matter, and maxed Flask and Willpower do more in fights than its +20% bullet damage to trapped heroes. [8:40] [9:10]
- Fleetfoot is core because this is a run-and-gun Warden; with items he has about the highest move speed in the game, to the point that jump dashing is no faster than running with Fleetfoot and Willpower active. [9:10] [9:41]
- Later Divine Barrier or Heroic Aura add even more move speed; then only jump dash to escape, perhaps after using your cooldowns. [9:41]
- Start the engagement, run in, pop Fleetfoot and chase; after Binding Word the enemy must run, and even if you miss the trap you are fast enough to chase the kill. [9:41] [10:12]
- Fleetfoot also lets you run away, get behind a corner and pop ult. [10:12]
- Then Combat Barrier: makes Warden even tankier and speeds up farming jungle and clearing waves. [10:12] [10:42]
- Then Slowing Hex, a core item you should always buy: initiate with Slowing Hex, flask, then Binding Word and almost no one gets away. [10:42]
- Slowing Hex disables movement abilities so Wraith, Infernus, Abrams and Shiv cannot dash out of Binding Word. [10:42] [11:12]
- Then Bullet Resist Shredder for this gun build; at that point you have four purple items. [11:12]
- If you have no flex slot yet, sell Mystic Burst, or skip Bullet Resist Shredder for now and buy Slowing Bullets first; you can also sell Hollow Point Ward for a slot. [11:12] [11:43]
- With Slowing Bullets the enemy cannot run: even if they leave Binding Word you keep chasing at full speed with Fleetfoot and reapply the slow. [11:43]
- Then Heroic Aura for even more move speed; Burst Fire is the alternative since both give fire rate and move speed. [12:14]
- Burst Fire gives 30% fire rate and 2 m/s move speed on hitting an enemy; Heroic Aura procs whenever you want with the same move speed and 25% fire rate but lasts longer, 6 seconds. [12:14] [12:44]
- He prefers Heroic Aura: Burst Fire's slide distance is wasted because Warden's running is usually faster than sliding, while Heroic Aura gives health, bullet lifesteal and a flat 1 m/s move speed at all times. [12:44] [13:15]
- When he buys Heroic Aura he sells Hollow Point Ward, since he usually has no more than two flex slots. [13:45]
- Then choose Pristine Emblem or Intensifying Magazine, close to 50/50 and up to preference; try both. [13:45] [15:17]
- Pristine Emblem upgrades from High Velocity Mag so it saves a flex slot and keeps the bullet velocity, adds weapon damage, spirit power and spirit resist, and bonus damage against enemies above a health threshold. [14:16]
- Intensifying Magazine usually means selling High Velocity Mag; it does more in prolonged fights since your large magazine easily reaches the 60% max ramp. [14:16] [14:47]
- He usually takes Pristine Emblem, trading some potential damage for consistency and a bit of tankiness. [15:17]
- Everything up to this point is core and bought in the same order. [15:17]
- Then Bullet Lifesteal: Heroic Aura's 18% and Bullet Lifesteal stack with diminishing returns to 39% total (about 21% more, not the full 26), which he still finds worth it for sustain on gun Warden. [15:48] [16:18] [16:48]
- Then Enduring Speed, sometimes skipped: being slowed is Warden's main enemy, and most enemies have some bullet slow, so slow resist keeps you fast. [16:48] [17:19]
- Speed is king on Warden: harder to catch when chased, easier to catch enemies, and in team fights with all cooldowns popped you are hard to hit. [17:19]
- Then Frenzy, which he calls the best item for Warden and slept on: ammo, about 30% weapon damage plus 18, and health. [17:50]
- Frenzy's passive below 50% health gives 40% fire rate, 4% move speed and 55% bullet resist, so heroes like Haze or gun Wraith cannot kill you in a 1v1. [17:50] [18:21]
- With a slot, buy Spirit Armor too, then Improved Spirit Armor for 52% spirit armor; with Frenzy procced you also have about 50% bullet armor, half damage from everything. [18:53] [19:23]
- Average late game has two flex slots. [18:53]
- After that you usually have enough damage; he likes Siphon Bullets for another source of healing and tankiness. [19:23]
- Without a flex slot, upgrade Slowing Bullets to Glass Cannon; it lowers HP a bit but takes no flex slot and is a good choice on Warden now if you need damage. You can buy Siphon Bullets, Glass Cannon or both. [19:54]
- If you still need flex slots, sell Extra Stamina. [19:54]
- Lucky Shot is an alternative to Glass Cannon: more slow, ammo and weapon damage. Inhibitor reduces enemy damage output, and Vampiric Burst is a great option for maximum lifesteal. [20:24]
- Improved Reach upgrades from Mystic Reach without a flex slot, greatly increasing ult range and Binding Word size and duration. [21:29]
- Arcane Surge is also really good with the Warden ult: jump dash for ability range, duration and spirit power on it. [21:59]
- Once you get Frenzy you can sell Basic Magazine since Frenzy gives ammo. [22:31] [23:01]
- If you cannot afford Frenzy and want damage, sell Basic Magazine and buy Berserker late; it is a 1,250 item many Wardens buy early, but he treats it as a cheap late pick. [23:01] [23:31]
- Berserker gives up to 60% weapon damage after taking about 600 damage; early it often gives only 6 to 18%, but late you always take that damage, so it is free value. [23:31] [24:02]
- His philosophy: an item that is not always active is situational and gives less value than one that always works, like Slowing Bullets or Fleetfoot. [24:02] [24:34]
- Situational: Unstoppable when you are the farmed hard carry being focused by ults like Mo & Krill's or Dynamo's Black Hole; he calls it insane on Warden late. [25:05]
- Silencer recently became passive, silencing on shots; it is an anti-Pocket item, so after Frenzy fire rate initiate, Slowing Hex him, and he cannot Satchel out of Binding Word. [25:05] [25:36]
- Buy Toxic Bullets or Healbane if the enemy has a lot of healing and sustain. [25:36]
- Mid game against a farmed Haze or gun Wraith dealing lots of bullet damage before you reach Frenzy, buy Escalating Resilience, the best value bullet resist item that also gives damage and fire rate. [26:08]
- Divine Barrier can replace Heroic Aura if you need more survivability: shields and move speed, a more defensive version. [26:08] [26:38]
