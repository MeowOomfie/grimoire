# The BIGGEST Macro Mistakes In Deadlock (Why You Can't Win)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-05-29
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kmpRSlo3cOM

## Covers
Replay review of a high-rank Wraith game used to teach the macro meta of the box patch: shove the lane first, farm boxes, rotate and gank, prioritize lanes and objectives over your own jungle.
Covers when to push Walkers, split pressure with the Urn, Sinner's Sacrifice timing, and why proactive play beats waiting for fights.
For players who AFK farm their jungle and never cross the map, any rank.

## Claims
- Wraith can almost never realistically win the lane; he trades very poorly until about 2.2k to 3k souls, when his cards get better. [1:03]
- In the current meta health matters a lot in lane because regen is harder to come by, so avoid taking damage early. [1:34]
- On most heroes, shoot the creeps, secure your last hits, then farm boxes; only play to win the lane with a very aggressive lane such as Calico plus Abrams, or Kelvin. [1:34]
- "Shoving the lane" means securing your last hits without worrying about denying or harassing; only shoot the enemy when they are out of position and you can trade favorably. [2:04]
- In a lane meant to lose, shoot the enemy creep waves and then go farm boxes. [2:34]
- Boxes start around 2:00; before farming boxes, get every single creep in the wave first, or you fall behind in money. [3:04]
- Split lane money 50/50 with your partner; only leave creeps to a teammate if you are a support who wants to give them the money. [3:34]
- Only shoot the enemy laner when they are in a spot where you can deal damage; force an aggressive enemy out when you have to. [3:34]
- As soon as a creep wave spawns in a losing lane, focus on shoving it as fast as possible and avoid the enemy; once you shove fast enough, go to the basement or boxes for money, then come back for the next wave. [4:04]
- Stand where you can shoot the creeps without taking damage. [4:34]
- Push when you and your partner are healthy and you have four creeps alive versus their zero; creeps deal decent damage. [5:05]
- Shoving the lane while your lane partner is dead is not very hurtful anymore, because they compensate with boxes and creep sharing exists; it is net positive for the team. [5:05]
- Letting the enemy push into your lane while you do nothing means tower damage, easier kills for them, and more time for them to farm boxes. [5:36]
- If boxes did not exist you would wait for your teammate before shooting the wave, but with boxes on the map it is better to shove no matter what. [6:06]
- If an enemy laner has been missing from lane for about 30 seconds they are probably doing a box run, so the risk of dying is lower. [6:06]
- Pushing is safe while you have four creeps in front of you; stay behind them and the tower will not shoot you. With only two creeps the enemy can nuke them and the tower kills you. [6:36]
- Once all nearby boxes are farmed the choice is push or do nothing, so push for tower damage and denies on your own creeps rather than looking for a kill. [7:38]
- Having four creeps active versus the enemy's zero is always an advantage; if you don't shove, the enemy's four creeps tank the tower and kill it. [7:38]
- Check enemy health and whether they have their ultimate before diving; a low Calico without ult under her tower was a free kill. [8:08]
- After trading HP, play safe again, especially at half HP on Wraith without ult. [8:40]
- Your number one lane priority is killing the creeps; damage the enemy only when they peek. [9:13]
- Losing your tower early is not that important in the current meta unless you lose every tower, as long as you don't fall far behind in money. [9:44]
- When the enemy is ahead in money with higher kill potential, the best choice is to shove the wave, then do something useful elsewhere on the map. [9:44]
- After losing your tower, shove the wave and rotate to another lane instead of playing passively under the Walker, where you are likely to die and won't get ahead. [10:14]
- Early Wraith with ult up should rotate, shove, and look for a kill on a defender. [10:44]
- Three heroes on one lane lose money through creep sharing, but it is worth it for a tower and a kill. [11:14]
- Do not rotate to a lane if you would arrive after losing another wave there. [11:46]
- Tier two camps are not worth it on any hero right now unless the hero farms very efficiently early, maybe Seven; even Infernus and Wraith are slow. [11:46]
- Without an efficient camp, the choices are sit on a lane and farm (lanes still give money), gank, or do boxes. [12:17]
- Rule out a lane if the wave is near the enemy tower or Walker and you can't get there in time; go to the lane whose wave is close to your tower, where worst case you still get creep money. [12:17]
- With three on a lane, either shove or leave; two on a lane can stay money-efficient until later in the game. [13:17]
- Past 8 minutes the first efficient farm besides quick camps is Sinner's Sacrifice; at that point no hero gets money fast from even tier three camps. [14:18]
- Early gank heroes such as Paradox, Abrams or Shiv can be more active and get more kills; a farm hero that already helped two lanes should then focus on its own money. [14:49]
- Running your jungle boxes is only reasonable when your lanes are pushed out by your team; if lanes are pushing into you, be on a lane. [15:20]
- The mistake 99% of players make, even in Eternus: farming jungle when there is more money or an objective on the map. If there is an objective to kill or money on a lane, always be on the lane. [15:20]
- Start farming jungle only once you have the damage; for this Wraith build that is Titanic Magazine and Quicksilver Reload, and definitely Tesla Bullets. Before that avoid creep camps unless they are on the way. [15:50]
- A teammate baiting a 1v1 while you arrive works against good players who know you are coming; against bad players they may just back out. [16:21]
- When your team is fighting, go take the tower. [16:51]
- Use Sinner's Sacrifice when it is up, even if it means stepping away from a push. [17:23]
- Stop AFK farming jungle when there are lanes to push, more efficient farm, or objectives; if your team is fighting, join the fight or push an objective. [17:23]
- The only jungle farming to defend is farming the enemy side, because you take money away from them and effectively get twice as much. [17:53]
- Do not sit in your own jungle at 11 minutes farming when there are other things to do. [17:53]
- Even once you can farm faster, back out of a camp you are not killing efficiently, and look for the lane first. [18:29]
- After shoving a wave, go forward and look for the enemy instead of backing out to your own jungle, even on a farm-hungry hero. [18:59]
- You never get ahead by farming your own jungle unless the enemy is really bad; push the wave first even with multiple tier three camps up. [19:32]
- A fight that trades one for one or gives the enemy a good chance to die for nothing is a bad play. [20:03]
- You can get your full build while doing things on the map; you do not need to be fully online before fighting. [20:34]
- Killing enemy Walkers opens the map: more access to the enemy jungle, more kill opportunities, bonus money and bonus flex slots. [20:34]
- If a creep wave is already hitting an enemy Walker, expect an enemy to show up immediately and push the wave back before you arrive. [21:05]
- Walkers gain more resistance the more people push them, so ideally have two people max pushing a Walker; don't send a third. [21:36]
- If no lane is a good target, options include farming tier two, sneaking the Urn, farming tier three, or helping a teammate in trouble. [22:07]
- Help your team first even with Sinner's Sacrifice off cooldown; AFK farming instead trades your money for your team's chance to come back. [22:07]
- After helping, then farm; go back to heal so you can be useful again. [23:07]
- Four enemies spending their time for two kills and a Guardian while your team takes a Walker is a good trade for you; the Guardian barely matters. [23:07]
- The middle Walker is the most important because it is the hardest to kill. [23:07]
- With lots of jungle up, push a lane first; if your team is fighting elsewhere, pressure a lane when you are strong in money. [23:38]
- Dying after getting a Walker and a kill, while pulling three enemies onto you, is a perfect play: it gives your team gold and a flex slot and wastes the enemy's time. [24:08]
- If everyone waits to join fights only after they start, the game never resolves; the team that aggresses first, looks for picks and objectives first, and pressures the map wins. [25:11]
- Refusing to take a fight until you are already up one is exactly the wrong way to play. [25:42]
- Selfish play to protect your KD or your own money is bad; if you want to carry, be proactive. [26:13]
- A forward Urn is risky when only up about 15k. [26:43]
- Pressure a Walker while your team holds the Urn so the enemy must choose between defending the Urn and the Walker; their attention splits and you usually get one or the other. [27:14]
- With waves pushed and map control, farming the enemy boxes is another decent play, but the proactive play is pressuring the Walker. [27:44]
- When the Urn is undefended, drop it at the drop spot instead of dropping it in your base. [28:44]
- Proactive gameplay is always better than reactive: when the enemy has to react, you get time to shoot towers, base Guardians and Walkers. [28:44]
- Down two, crossing deep to the enemy side can still be right: the enemy will not send more than one person to a far side lane with nothing there. [29:44]
- This only works if you know you can win the 1v1; being up 6k on the enemy means you can on any hero. [30:15]
- Let the closer teammate defend a lane you cannot reach in time. [30:15]
- Pushing a far side lane after a kill forces the enemy to back out and defend their base; lane control is really important. [31:17]
- Farm camps when lanes are pushed and you have map control in middle. [31:17]
- When you are strong and ahead, look for an early kill near the Urn. [31:17]
- With all lanes pushed at 5v5 and even numbers, pushing and Mid Boss are risky; look for a kill or farm more, and look for kills if you are ahead. [32:17]
- Boxes in the enemy jungle often sit up all game while the average player AFK farms their own jungle; going for a pick instead wins games. [32:49]
- Backing out of a winning fight at full HP lets the enemy chase and turn it; keep pressuring when the fight starts well. [33:19]
- A team with Yamato can almost always secure Mid Boss; six enemy deaths for Mid Boss against your three is still a good trade. [33:51]
- Later in the game it is more important to keep the Sinner's Sacrifice buff up than to get the money, so sacrifice it even with no tier three nearby. [34:21]
- AFK farm when your team is dead for longer than the enemy team. [34:21]
- With Superior Cooldown, using your ult freely is not a waste. [35:25]
- Bait out enemy abilities first, then fight while they have nothing; a fight right after forcing an enemy ultimate is a free kill. [35:25]
- Think of jungle as something you do when there is nothing else to do, even on Wraith, probably the fastest mid to late game farmer. [35:58]
- An even trade while you are ahead is not great, because the enemy gets more comeback money. [36:58]
- When you push alone, tell your team ("I'm pushing this walker, pressure the other lanes") so they use the moment to push elsewhere; even if 30 to 40% of games they don't listen, it is still correct. [36:58]
- With your team dead and the enemy pushing, pressure a different lane and push into their base instead of joining a losing defense; it forces enemies back and splits their attention. [38:31]
- Split pressuring alone is safe when you have your teleport and are the strongest player, so you win any 1v1. [39:32]
- Wraith can solo the Patron once it is exposed, so an undefended base can be backdoored. [40:04]
- Turning a 4v6 defense into a closer fight by drawing defenders away gives your team a fighting chance at the Walker. [40:04]
- The respawn timer can show 0 seconds when it is really about 70 seconds; long enemy respawns give enough time to end the game. [40:34]
- If your team never pushes you must farm more, but still look for objectives you can sneak, like a side Walker alone. [41:07]
- Stop being scared of crossing the middle of the map; push objectives and be confident taking at least 1v1s. [41:37]
- If you are even in money with the enemy and run away whenever you see someone, only fighting when up numbers, you are playing incorrectly. Far behind, you can't fight 1v1. [41:37]
- Prioritize the enemy jungle over yours. [42:07]
- Early game, prioritize boxes and lanes over tier two camps; even later, prioritize lanes over jungle camps. [42:07]
- Farmers with space on the map should be the ones taking Sinner's Sacrifice. [42:07]
- If a lane is being pushed and there are also boxes or Sinner's Sacrifice to take, take the lane first. [42:07]
