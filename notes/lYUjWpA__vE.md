# The Biggest Winners And Losers In The New Deadlock Patch
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-04-19
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYUjWpA__vE

## Covers
The coach reads the April 2025 patch: lane souls no longer split evenly (60% each in a duo, 100% solo), longer wave interval, Guardian range, medic trooper, Urn changes, big nerfs to green survival items (barriers, Healing Rite, Fortitude, Restorative Locket, Ethereal Shift to tier four), the Knockdown buff, and hero by hero verdicts.
For players planning lanes, early ganks and builds in that patch.

## Claims
- He calls it by far the best balance patch in a long time; the biggest change is to lane soul sharing, which adds many choices back to the game. [0:00]
- Until now everyone in a lane got the same shared souls as long as two or fewer were there; that system is gone. [0:30]
- Now two players in a lane each get 60% of the souls, a solo player gets 100%, so a duo gets 120% total. [1:01]
- If your lane partner dies in a 2v2 you do not lose much, because alone you get almost double the souls. [1:01]
- Two in a lane is still useful early to kill enemies and push towers, but a solo laner loses only 20% of the souls, not 50%. [1:32]
- A solo laner who survives and does not get denied will get more souls than anyone else in the game. [1:32]
- New strategy: pair an early ganker with a hard carry that can survive; the ganker leaves at 3 or 4 minutes, the carry plays safe on tower and stalls, and if it works the carry gets so far ahead nobody can catch up. [2:04]
- He predicts Dota or League style strategies, like sending three players to one lane at minute one or two for a very early tower while a solo laner stalls another lane. [2:35]
- That works best with an AoE deny hero that can survive long, like Mo & Krill or Warden; a Warden pushed 2v1 can throw a flask, hit both and stall. [3:05]
- You no longer need to sit in lane until 8 minutes; you can gank. [3:05]
- He played Warden, left lane at 4 minutes, got kills ganking another lane, and his solo lane partner came back far ahead of the other two; the tower took some damage but it was worth it. [3:35]
- This also reduces the lane bully problem: early ganking is viable again, and heroes that want lots of souls and heroes that roam are both good picks again. [4:05]
- Wave interval increased from 25 to 30 seconds, giving a few more seconds to rotate, so a gank from one lane to another costs at most about one creep wave. [4:36]
- Guardian range was reduced, maybe too much: you can walk up the stairs and shoot the Guardian without it shooting back, and tower divers often go unhit, which makes pushing aggressively better. [4:36]
- The medic trooper is reverted: it heals every 6 seconds with no cooldown, so keeping it alive is free regen. [5:07]
- This combats lane freezing: the team pushing in sustains enough as long as its healing trooper lives, so you cannot stall or freeze the lane. [5:37]
- You can no longer parry troopers, which further weakens the lane freeze meta. [6:08]
- With no sharing, a deny gives the denier all the souls, so denies are effectively worth double and players good at last hitting and denying can get far ahead in lane. [6:08]
- The Urn now reveals you immediately, so you can no longer rush it. [6:38]
- The first Urn now counts for comeback mechanics: before it always spawned mid and was a free Urn for the team ahead; now a team far ahead cannot take it for free. [6:38]
- Warden is no longer a bad early laner; he does not die, though last hitting is still somewhat hard; his build is back to High Velocity Mag. [7:10]
- The Urn now has a visual indicator on the model showing whether it will be a forward or mid Urn when stationary, a good quality of life change. [7:40]
- Heavy melee cancel momentum is significantly reduced; in practice the tech no longer works. [7:40]
- Trooper behaviour bug fixes (troopers stuck, not attacking buildings) will not change much but make the game feel better. [8:13]
- Hollow Point Ward nerfed: bonus weapon damage reduced to 18% and spirit shield reduced; part of its strength was stacking with Enchanter's Barrier for a bigger, longer barrier. It is still a great item. [8:44]
- Healing Rite nerfed: it was objectively a better choice than Extra Regen in most games; the nerf rewards staying in lane below full HP and punishes trading to 1 HP then hiding 20 seconds. He thinks it should be removed. [9:16]
- Healbane heal on kill nerfed to 275: it hurts heroes like Shiv who used it for sustain more than those buying it for anti-heal; still great on Wraith with Tesla Bullets. You can no longer kill one person at 1 HP and be back to full. [9:48]
- Enchanter's Barrier and Combat Barrier shields each reduced by 50 HP. [10:50]
- That is a double nerf: the barrier bonuses (fire rate and damage on Combat Barrier, cooldown and spirit power on Enchanter's Barrier) only apply while the shield is up, so smaller shields drop earlier in a fight and the bonuses are active less. [10:50]
- Divine Barrier was nerfed but not enough; bought early, especially in lane, it basically doubles your HP on a button press, and you cannot win a fight against it unless you have one too. He wanted a 50 second or 1 minute cooldown or lower base numbers. [11:50]
- Now you can bait the Divine Barrier: go in hoping they use it early, then 40 seconds is a reasonable window to go in again while it is down. [12:20]
- Restorative Locket range to gain charges reduced from 50 to 35, roughly half the area since it is squared, plus less healing per stack: a big double nerf that makes it situational at best, the right way to nerf it. [12:20]
- Debuff Reducer nerfed by 50 less health and 4% less debuff reduction; people rushed it early because it works against almost everything (stuns, poison ticks). [13:23]
- Debuff Remover was not nerfed and is now probably the best 3k item and the go-to 3k survival item, replacing Ethereal Shift. [13:23]
- Fortitude is gutted and now bad: weapon damage is weaker and the healing now takes 13 seconds to start. [13:55]
- Fortitude's point was fighting until low, hiding about 10 seconds and returning without going to base; from mid map spawn is about 10 to 15 seconds away, so at 13 seconds you can often just go back to base and get the same effect; do not spend 3.5k on it. Its main remaining use is movement speed, which other items also give. [14:25]
- Phantom Strike cooldown increased slightly; it will not matter since you usually get one, sometimes two, per fight. [15:57]
- Spirit Strike nerf: it was stupid on Pocket, where one light melee could nuke everyone; the nerf also hurts Calico, Bebop, Abrams and other melee heroes. [17:30]
- Decay nerfed (less spirit power); he wanted its health regen removed since people still run it in lane. It is the highest DPS 1k item in the game, especially early, because it is free damage, and it gave the most spirit power of any 1,250 spirit item; still very good. [18:00]
- Knockdown buff: it now removes all momentum and brings the hero straight down, so a fleeing Grey Talon or Vindicta can no longer glide away using momentum; a massive nerf to flyers. Buy Knockdown against Grey Talon and Vindicta. [18:30]
- Ethereal Shift is now a tier four item, getting the Veil Walker treatment; he worries it is over nerfed and will be bad on everyone except Calico, and maybe Pocket. [19:31]
- Unstoppable is now the better choice for most heroes: more survivability, more HP, and you play while immune to stuns, whereas Ethereal Shift dodged everything (Wraith, Lash, Bebop bombs). [20:02]
- Ethereal Shift stays good on Calico because she can stall longer while her Torment Pulse keeps ticking. [20:32]
- Expected meta: Debuff Remover as the 3k survival item and Unstoppable as the 6k item if you want to dodge stuns; for raw survivability Divine Barrier beats Ethereal Shift. [21:02]
- He thinks the rumored full item rework is still coming, so treat these item changes as a fix for now. [22:04]
- Echo Shard cooldown increased by 2 seconds, not a big deal. [22:35]
- Bebop got gutted, not buffed despite the weaker Ethereal Shift: at level one he effectively has a negative Extra Stamina and negative half Extra Regen versus before, and at 20 minutes he no longer has a free spirit armor. [22:35]
- Buying Extra Stamina and Extra Regen to make up for it costs 1k you do not have at level one, two 500 items. [23:39]
- You can now dash away from a Bebop at level one when he rolls into you; Crayon escaped a Bebop on Abrams at 1 HP because the Bebop ran out of stamina. [24:09]
- With Ethereal Shift weaker, Bebop's bomb may be more viable again; he will just run Debuff Remover. [24:09]
- Calico gutted: three fewer bullets (you need about an extra Berserker's worth to match level one) and growth reduced by 25%, which matters because she got even stronger late with items like Lucky Shot. [24:39]
- Calico's Gloom Bombs tier one and tier two upgrades were swapped; he thinks it changes little since almost nobody leveled it past one point, though spamming is weaker in lane. She is still a great early game hero that can one-shot with her nuke, just not insane late. [25:11]
- Grey Talon HP regen nerfed (he had 2.5 at level one) and move speed spirit scaling reduced a lot; the move speed nerf barely matters because Talons sit in the air. [26:13]
- Grey Talon base damage reduced 10% and early spirit scaling worse, a lane damage nerf, but most Talons max the arrow ability early, which restores the scaling; he still hits like a truck. [27:14]
- Every hero not in the patch notes is effectively buffed, since lane bullies and lane bullying were nerfed and the soul changes help hard carries, unless they relied on the nerfed items. [27:45]
- He thinks Wraith needs buffs too. [28:16]
- Haze Sleep Dagger now stops the target sliding (before you could keep sliding after being slept). [28:46]
- Haze Sleep Dagger T2 now also reduces stamina regeneration by 60% for 6 seconds, roughly the whole fight, so a slept target gets about three dashes and you will catch them if they run: a massive buff. [28:46]
- Haze ultimate got 5% more fire rate and a tier two cooldown buff; Haze is now definitely better than Wraith and looks very scary. [29:17]
- Holliday base gun damage reduced by 3 with gun damage growth increased, so less early and about the same late; he wanted it cut by about 20. [29:49]
- Holliday's Lasso duration was cut by half a second, still too long; about 90% of the time a grapple still kills you. [30:21]
- The patch notes say melee while casting Holliday's Lasso was fixed, but it still works, giving about 200 free damage per lasso. [30:21]
- Ivy's headshot hitbox is smaller and no longer covers the ears, about half the old head area, a big survivability buff that may make Ivy a support main pick. [31:22]
- Kelvin got a big nerf (a value cut from 15% to zero, tied to boons); after checking in game he concludes boons cap at 15, so Kelvin can never go past that, which he calls a massive nerf. [31:53]
- Kelvin's Frozen Shelter cooldown increased (he wanted about 180 seconds) plus a further dome change he expects to do little, since most Kelvins level the dome anyway. [33:26]
- Lady Geist got a small bug fix, a minor buff to an already really good hero. [33:57]
- Lash Death Slam lock-on time increased by 100 ms, a real nerf, but with Ethereal Shift gone Lash is still better than before, since everyone ran it and you cannot Debuff Remover out of the Lash ultimate. [34:28]
- McGinnis turrets buffed: tracking speed increased so you can no longer dodge them by strafing, bullet velocity increased, and duration increased (to 18 seconds), so a turret build can set up a turret farm. [34:59]
- Mirage nerf: base health reduced 10% (the bigger deal), and a slow reduced from 80% to 60%, which roughly doubles your speed while slowed (5 m/s becomes 2 instead of 1), so you can run from him; Fire Scarabs bullet resist reduction reduced from 10 to 8. Still probably good. [36:00]
- Mo & Krill Burrow T2 reduced from 140 to 100 damage with spirit scaling increased, and combo DPS reduced with spirit scaling increased; overall a nerf but not enough, and Mo benefits from the lane changes since roaming early is good. [37:02]
- Many Mo & Krill players run utility like Toxic Bullets and Phantom Strike rather than spirit items, so the nerf affects them little. [37:32]
- Holliday and Paradox still need much harder nerfs, as hard as Calico and Bebop got. [38:03]
- Pocket base damage reduced a lot with bullet damage growth increased, a nerf because his power spike is early; Enchanter's Satchel damage reduced from 100 to 70, more than a 30 damage nerf in practice because a Barrage hit amplifies damage. [38:34]
- Seven got another nerf, a big one to his lightning ability (caption says "lightning strike"), but its range increased; he will still be great. [39:05]
- Sinclair nerfs: lower bullet damage, Hex does less damage and you move faster in the bunny form, which lost its 80 base damage, ally amp reduced by 10% with lower scaling, and Vexing Bolt base and max damage nerfed. [40:09]
- Vindicta nerfs (crow and tier three spirit scaling) are not enough, though the Knockdown buff also hurts her. [40:40]
- Warden base health and regen increased, so he survives early lane much more easily; playable again, not hard meta. [41:12]
- Biggest winners: anything not nerfed; top three are Haze (biggest buffs, no nerfs), Lady Geist and Warden; Infernus and Wraith got a bit better but are still somewhat weak. [41:42]
- Green items got worse, which makes orange items better, so heroes that deal a lot of damage without being tanky are better. Divine Barrier is still too strong. [42:12]
- Denies now give double souls to the player, a buff to good denying heroes like Abrams or Wraith. [42:44]
