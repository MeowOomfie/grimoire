## Covers
Paige, Midknight. A coaching review of one game by an Oracle-rank Paige player.
Topics: trooper damage, when to buy Golden Goose Egg, levelling abilities with the hotkey, box maxing, root and dragon usage at choke points, using the ult to cancel enemy ults, farm targets in the mid and late game, calling mid boss, back-line positioning and not dying for teammates. - Use your root around choke points in general, and especially when enemies are locked into an enclosed area. [1:30]

- You did no box routes in the laning stage before 6 minutes, which is the important part to box during. [2:35]
- Paige save order: throw Guardian Ward on the teammate first, then your ability barrier, so the Guardian Ward's shield goes off first. [2:35]
- Your box route was inefficient; if you struggle with box routes, study them. [3:37]
- You sat on your root in a fight where using it would definitely have saved your Vindicta. [4:08]

- Do not circle back to a tower that is pretty much dead. [4:39]
- Buying Knockdown here was good timing. [4:39]
