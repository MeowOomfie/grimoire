## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Baba-only free coaching stream the day after Baba's release. Before coaching he tests her kit in the sandbox and reviews community Baba builds, then coaches three Baba players (none named, no ranks given).
Themes: Baba as a tempo brawler, Thread Slap (her 1) before the wave and before the ult, Extra Charge early, which Brew to throw (Soothing, Scalding, Silence), Granny Long Legs (her 2) for height and escape, Feed the Birds (ult) stacks and its cylinder hitbox, light melee to bait parries, farming waves and Sinner's Sacrifice instead of side lanes, dead time, and swapping off Baba into gun comps.
Ability names follow the game (Thread Slap, Granny Long Legs, Baba's Brew, Feed the Birds); the coach mostly calls them 1, 2, 3 (brew or "tea") and ult.
