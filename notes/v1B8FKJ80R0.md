# The state of Deadlock's Jungle - Too much?
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-03-10
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1B8FKJ80R0

## Covers
Five points on the jungle after the three lane update: punishing junglers is a skill issue, farm items like Cold Front and Monster Rounds, no extra objective between Mid Boss and Walkers, uneven jungle speed across heroes with no draft, and Sinner's Sacrifice statue buffs.
For players who feel games are a farmfest and want to know how to punish teams that only jungle.

## Claims
- The three lane update added a lot more jungle camps, and many players complain games are now a farmfest. [0:00]
- Point one: the jungle being a problem is partly a skill issue; with 1,000 more hours on average it would matter much less. [0:30]
- Every time you jungle you give up something: space, fighting for the lanes, and pressure on objectives; jungling by itself does not win games. [1:00]
- If both teams jungle well neither gains an advantage, as in Dota, which has much more jungle. [1:31]
- In regular matchmaking players are uncoordinated, so invading the jungle is less viable, and everyone just farms because farming gives souls. [2:01]
- How to deal with teams that jungle too much: pressure objectives, pressure the lanes, invade their jungle, catch people and kill them to secure a lead. [2:32]
- Jungling your own side does not give you a lead unless you clear much faster than the enemy. [2:32]
- Seven has a high win rate because he jungles so fast, an inherent advantage over most of the cast. [3:02]
- To counter Seven: invade his jungle and put pressure on him; he is not that strong early. [3:02]
- Point two is itemization: unlike Dota, where supports cannot clear the jungle effectively, items like Monster Rounds, Cold Front, Torment Pulse and others make farming extremely easy for anyone. [3:32]
- With 12 slots and no flex slots needed, these farm items are easy to fit and very cost effective. [4:03]
- Cold Front costs 1,250, gives 10% spirit resist, deals 75 base damage and 2x damage to non-heroes, so 150 base damage to neutral creeps and troopers with zero spirit, with a big spirit scaling (he guesses around 1.5). [4:03]
- With Cold Front almost anyone one-shots most camps (a Viscous and a Pocket in the spectated game), though not tier three camps. [4:33]
- Pocket is inherently good at clearing camps with all of his abilities, but the Cold Front purchase is what makes the instant clears; Cold Front at 1,250 is too affordable and too strong. [5:04]
- Cold Front and Monster Rounds are the biggest culprits; Tesla Bullets also contributes. [5:04]
- Point three is the lack of another objective: there are only Walkers, Mid Boss and the Urn, so after Mid Boss there is nothing to do but Walkers. [5:35]
- A team only up about 5k souls cannot force a Walker. [6:06]
- An allied Walker gives nearby heroes 25% bullet resist and 25% spirit resist, like free bullet and spirit armor worth about 2,500 net worth with no slots used, so fighting enemies under their own Walker is hard. [6:06]
- The buffs spawn every 5 minutes, usually around the same time as the Urn, too rarely to count as a regular objective, leaving long windows with nothing to contest. [6:37]
- In the spectated high ranked NA game the 6v6 Walker fight happened only after the entire jungle was cleared; lower ranks would be even slower. [6:37]
- Adding another objective would give a reason to be active and punish teams that jungle too much, since they could not contest it and would lose tempo. [7:08]
- Point four: some heroes clear the jungle much faster than others, and with no draft your team may get none; e.g. facing Wraith and Seven with Kelvin, Mirage, Abrams and Vindicta, who can clear but do not excel at it. [8:08]
- Point five: Sinner's Sacrifice giving three golden statue buffs is too much value; the stats are permanent and stack, so clearing them is extremely high priority. [8:38]
- Triple health from a Sinner's Sacrifice is 60 total health, almost a tier one item's worth, since Enduring Spirit gives only 75 bonus health. [9:09]
- Each spirit statue buff gives 2 spirit, so seven of them equal Extra Spirit's 14. [9:09]
- Golden statues are also scattered across the map. [9:39]
- His proposal: Sinner's Sacrifice should give two buffs or maybe one, and only if you hit the heavy melee timing, zero otherwise. [9:39]
- TL;DR: the jungle is not a huge issue by itself, but poor punishment of junglers, one-shot camp items (Cold Front most of all), no other objective, and strong Sinner's Sacrifice stats make games feel drawn out at lower levels. [10:40]
- He would not nerf the jungle as a whole; add a new objective and nerf Sinner's Sacrifice instead. [11:10]
- Be more active, look for plays and pressure, so constant junglers get punished instead of reaching 50,000 net worth as Seven. [11:41]
