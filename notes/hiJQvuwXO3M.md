## Covers
Metro's "Metro Surge" Wraith build after the Telekinesis rework and the Full Auto buff: a gun build where Full Auto, imbued with Surge of Power, makes about half the damage spirit, with stacked lifesteal and slows to stop silenced targets running away.
For Wraith players and anyone wanting the item order, skill order and late game options. This is a late-2024 video (December 2024 patch): the item shop, item stats and the map have changed a lot since.
