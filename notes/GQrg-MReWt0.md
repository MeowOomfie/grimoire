## Covers
Abrams game plan and key items by moe9times: lane timings, power spikes, roaming, mid game role, objectives he is good and bad at, and item reasoning.
A guide (not a full build). Patch around 2025-03-06, after Abrams nerfs that weakened his early game.

## Claims
- Recent nerfs made Abrams notably weaker in the early game. [0:00]
- Abrams' fast bullet velocity shotgun gives him an advantage securing and denying souls in lane, combined with an easy stun and built-in sustain. [0:30]
- Hit a Shoulder Charge timing at 400 souls: you can surprise enemies with a quick charge as the second minion wave arrives. [1:00]
- Tell your lane partner about the charge timing; charge into a double heavy melee for a free kill. [1:00]
- After that timing, gank nearby lanes using items like Extra Stamina or Sprint Boots to surprise pushed-up enemies with CC and burst. [1:00]
- Next power spike: the third Shoulder Charge upgrade at around 7 to 9k souls, which makes your shotgun hit much harder after a charge; you can two or three shot people. [1:30]
- The third charge upgrade plus a 3k gun item leads to early kill streaks as you roam lane to lane around 10 minutes. [1:30]
- Use the 10 minute spike for an early Urn run, either to turn around a losing game or to snowball a winning one. [2:00]
- From around 20 minutes, transition to a tankier supportive playstyle; you are not trying to be the top damage dealer. [2:00]
- In the mid game Abrams is the prime target and a prime user of disabling items: Slowing Hex, Decay, Knockdown, Curse. [2:31]
- Be patient with stuns and save them to interrupt key abilities like Dynamo's ult (with your ult's third upgrade) or Haze's ult. [2:31]
- You are limited by shotgun range, so barge into fights with your ult, shut the fight down with CC, then finish with the gun. [2:31]
- Summary: be a big tanky boy, shut down the enemy carry and absorb aggro for your team. [3:02]
- When behind, group with one or two teammates and burst people who are farming; charge and ult can be used from a silent crouch to set up a double stun. [3:02]
- Abrams escapes death well when behind by using charge, ult or heavy melee cancel. [3:02]
- Abrams is good in mid boss and Urn fights and steals; the free Unstoppable on the ult's third upgrade makes it a strong steal tool by stunning then securing the crystal. [3:33]
- Parry while Unstoppable: you cannot be stunned, but you can still be parried, which stops your melee and your Rejuvenator steal. [4:04]
- Abrams is very good at defending walkers and base: hide (for example on the high ledge at the left side walker or the map edge at the right side walker), stay crouched and charge enemies into the objective. [4:04]
- In end game base fights, his ult on a group can turn a team fight alone, enabling aggressive plays like staying after knocking the enemy Patron. [4:34]
- Abrams is bad at splitting walkers: he needs to be close and land the charge bonus, and getting close means getting stomped; let a better-suited teammate split. [4:34]
- With the third charge upgrade, pair Fleetfoot and Melee Charge for big mobility from heavy melee canceling around the map. [5:35]
- Adding a 3k weapon item like Hunter's Aura makes you a hypermobile ganking machine with insane burst. [5:35]
- Focus on maximizing usefulness in team fights with counter items rather than top damage. [6:07]
- Debuff Remover is a counter item and a second wind: it purges Affliction, Decay, bleed, bomb and others, and the purge gives HP and temporary move speed to finish targets. [6:07]
- Debuff Remover's passive reduction acts like armor against effects such as Afterburn, and it gives 20% weapon damage. [6:38]
- Armors are strong: they raise effective health by 45 to 50%, and with his passive's healing Abrams should almost always buy some armor. [6:38]
- Knockdown interrupts key abilities like Grey Talon's and Vindicta's flight, Mo and Krill's burrow or Dynamo's ult on a fairly short cooldown, and has a passive +1 stamina. [7:08]
- Curse removes a hero from the fight for about 3 seconds, enough to kill someone; buy it if one enemy keeps destroying you, and time it well because of the 53 second cooldown. [7:38]
- Frenzy is insane on Abrams: lots of ammo for a shotgun, bonus health and bullet resist, a stacking damage passive, and move speed, fire rate and bullet resist below half HP. [7:38]
- Against bullet-reliant heroes Frenzy single-handedly makes Abrams a pain to kill late game. [8:08]
- Colossus is a 6,000 soul ticket to being unkillable for 7 seconds: nearly as much resistance as both upgraded armors, a movement slow to keep enemies close, the most health of any item and 20% melee and weapon damage. [8:08]
- Colossus' short cooldown lets you absorb damage at both the start and end of long team fights. [8:38]
