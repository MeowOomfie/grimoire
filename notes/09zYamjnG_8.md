# The BEST Wraith Build In Deadlock - I Have Become What I Hated
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-11-14
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09zYamjnG_8

## Covers
Metro's Shadow Weave "scumbag" Wraith build: every item in order with why it is there, when to sell, the invis, Slowing Hex, Telekinesis, Full Auto pick-off combo, late game and situational items, and why the honest gun Wraith fell off after the patch's Telekinesis nerf and soul changes.
For Wraith players at mid to high rank. This is a late-2024 video: the item shop, hero kits and map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- The honest out-aim-the-opponent Wraith play style is, in his opinion, no longer doable at high level this patch, so he switched to a Shadow Weave build. [0:00]
- The build is a glass cannon, one-trick, one-shot combo build; he calls it the strongest build in the game right now and the only viable build against anything. [1:01]
- Start with Basic Magazine: a bigger magazine on Wraith is always worth it, almost a must pick; you could sell it later but he usually builds it into Titanic Magazine. [1:31]
- Monster Rounds is back in the build when there is an orange slot for it: it pushes and kills towers early, pushes Walkers, farms the jungle very fast for cheap, and helps win lane by shoving the wave and going to the jungle. [1:31]
- New addition: Hollow Point Ward, bought because of Shadow Weave. The build needs fewer orange items than his others (no Fleetfoot, no early Swift Striker rush), so there is a slot for another early orange item. [2:01]
- Hollow Point Ward is good in lane as long as you stay at high health, and you usually keep it until Shadow Weave: think of it as 22% extra damage for free in a fight. [2:31]
- With this play style you should never drop below 60% health in a fight unless you misplay badly, because fights last about two seconds. [3:01]
- Ammo Scavenger is back, probably the best purple spirit gain item in lane, but only in a solo lane; in a duo lane replace it with Extra Spirit. [3:32]
- Ammo Scavenger now needs 20 stacks (20 soul last hits) and you lose all stacks if you die, which is hard to keep up in a duo lane. [3:32]
- Ammo Scavenger now lasts 45 seconds instead of 35, so if you keep farming actively it never drops unless you die; it gives more ammo and +20 spirit for early card damage, and you will sell it later. [4:02]
- Extra Charge is a must have on Wraith: an extra card means about 33% more card damage, you hold more and farm better; he calls it the best 500 item in the game for Wraith. [4:32]
- Extra Stamina: Wraith has low stamina; it helps you survive, get in and out of fights, and move faster for jungling, farming and rotations; the build doesn't need many green slots so it is a no-brainer. [5:03]
- Extra Health is optional for survivability; it was just buffed to 160 health and later upgrades into Fortitude. [5:03]
- Restorative Shot and Extra Regen are only if you need regen; if you play the lane properly you may need neither. [5:33]
- Rush Tesla Bullets: probably the number one item for Wraith right now, it speeds up farming by an insane amount and lets you snowball into the Shadow Weave plus Slowing Hex or Silence Glyph combo. [5:33]
- Quicksilver Reload comes with Tesla Bullets to reload and farm the jungle faster. [6:04]
- Fleetfoot is still a great item and a good option, but it didn't fit in this build. [6:04]
- Next Bullet Lifesteal or Combat Barrier first: Bullet Lifesteal keeps you from dying while farming and heals in fights; Combat Barrier raises your damage and fire rate while its bullet shield is up. [6:34]
- Combat Barrier is amazing on gun Wraith because once your 2 is at least level two you get bullet shields back, so you can lose the barrier in a fight, use your 2, and get the Combat Barrier bonus back. [6:34]
- He still thinks gun is better than cards on Wraith; this is still a gun build. [7:04]
- After that, Bullet Resist Shredder to lower enemy bullet resist. [7:04]
- Then Burst Fire, the big change from his usual builds: the goal is to catch someone off guard and kill them in the lift before they reach the floor. [7:04]
- His older builds used defensive and sustain items (Escalating Resilience, Spirit Armor, magazine items); Burst Fire is a purely offensive choice. [7:34]
- Burst Fire's bonus fire rate and movement speed start when you shoot an enemy hero and last only 4 seconds, enough to burst anyone in the game as long as you get the lift off. [8:07]
- Slowing Hex gets the lift off: it slows the enemy and disables movement abilities, which stops escapes from your ult such as Mirage's Tornado, Infernus's run and Dynamo's teleport. [8:40]
- Telekinesis was nerfed this patch: it takes another 100 ms to cast, so at high level enemies escape too often, even through Slowing Hex, dashing just around a corner where you can't shoot them. [9:11]
- Wraith was nerfed quite hard this patch; she is not weak and is still insanely strong with this build, but the upfront honest build no longer works as well, especially at high level. [9:42]
- Even in sustained fights with a mega tanky build he couldn't stay alive and lost 1v1s to Bebops at his soul level or lower, though Wraith should be the 1v1 carry. [10:43]
- Why Wraith feels weak: the lift is easier to escape, and the soul change means staying together in lanes after 8 minutes now gives 75% of souls (after a hotfix; it used to be 50%) while jungle farm was nerfed. [11:14]
- Result: more team stacking and grouping, so fewer 1v1s and fewer chances to split push past single defenders, the two things Wraith is good at. [11:45]
- Shadow Weave is what lets Wraith still perform: it gets her into position to land a lift and catch anyone not with their team. [12:15]
- Before Shadow Weave you mostly farm and split push, join team fights when needed, and kill anyone dumb enough to 1v1 you; after Burst Fire, and especially Slowing Hex, you can kill a lone Infernus on a lane. [12:46]
- After buying Shadow Weave your play style changes: pop it and hunt for people out of position, alone, or in the jungle. [13:16]
- With this build he maxes Telekinesis first because the mass lift is so valuable. [13:16]
- If a pick works you get a free kill and go back to farming; if not, teleport out (or die) and go back to farming. Once Shadow Weave and Telekinesis are both up, look for kills. [13:16]
- Sell order: at Burst Fire with no flex slot, sell Monster Rounds; at Slowing Hex, sell Ammo Scavenger; don't sell Extra Charge. [14:16]
- By Shadow Weave (mid to late mid game) you should have a flex slot; he has never reached Shadow Weave without one. [14:16]
- By the time you can afford Shadow Weave you have at least two points in your key abilities and enough to put a level five on something; for this build's damage, what you level first doesn't matter much. [14:47]
- You don't need the lifesteal upgrades because the target should be dead before they land; he levels the lift first. [15:17]
- The combo: go invisible, find someone, pop out of invis by Slowing Hexing them, lift them immediately, press Full Auto, and only shoot. [15:17]
- You can throw one card very early to get Quicksilver Reload's fire rate bonus, but then you must reload later because you procced it early; it is an experience call, roughly 50/50. [15:48]
- It is always better to only shoot while they are lifted; throwing more than one card loses DPS. Start throwing cards when they hit the floor, after which you reload. [16:18]
- Throw cards instead when there are multiple targets in front of you (splash damage) or when the target uses Metal Skin and guns do nothing. [16:18]
- After this combo anyone close to you in souls is dead unless they have a way out; for most heroes the only two are Metal Skin and Ethereal Shift, which people often don't use in time if caught off guard. [16:48]
- Pro tip: you can see your own detection state; while fully transparent the enemy can't see you, but once you become slightly visible good players can see your shadow, so approach from the side rather than head-on. [17:19]
- Combo recap: Slowing Hex, lift, Full Auto, shoot the head, and when they drop throw cards while staying where you are. [17:49]
- Many players lift and then spam cards; in a 1v1 that does less damage, because cards were nerfed so hard that Shadow Weave's 55% spirit ambush power doesn't make up for the fire rate damage you give up. [18:52]
- If the target has a lot of bullet resist (for example Improved Bullet Armor), throw cards instead; it is case by case. [18:52]
- Weakness: after spending your ammo on one target you must reload, and you are weak with no Full Auto, no lift, no Slowing Hex, maybe one card, only a disengage tool and your normal fire. [19:24]
- Burst Fire procs every 8.5 seconds for 4 seconds, so after the kill it is on cooldown too. [19:55]
- At this point you are not tanky (no Titanic Magazine spirit and melee resist, no Escalating Resilience 40% bullet armor, no Spirit Armor, no Siphon Bullets) and the magazine is smaller than in his usual builds, worse in long sustained fights. [20:26]
- After Shadow Weave buy Improved Cooldown; he tried Duration Extender (slightly longer lift and Full Auto) but thinks it is worth less. [20:56]
- Improved Cooldown cuts ability and item cooldowns, taking the lift from 70 to 60 seconds, so you go for kills every minute instead of every 70 seconds; the ult is the limiting factor since Shadow Weave comes off cooldown sooner. Worth it at 1,250, but skippable. [21:26]
- In one game he sold Extra Charge here for the first time, since the build is mostly gun damage, and thinks it is worth it at this point. [21:57]
- Next Fortitude: if you are not far behind, the first person you pick with Shadow Weave should always die (except heroes like Pocket), but you are weak afterwards, so now you build sustain. [22:28]
- Fortitude's passive regen when not taking damage means fewer trips to base: farm or engage down to half HP, leave, go invis and rejoin the fight. [22:59]
- Fortitude also gives movement speed above 75% health, which the build lacks after dropping Fleetfoot and Kinetic Dash, plus 27% weapon damage. [23:30]
- You are the one engaging out of Shadow Weave, always at full HP, so Hollow Point Ward and Fortitude bonuses are both active; by the time you fall below 75% the lifted target should be dead. [23:30]
- Then Titanic Magazine, or from the situational tab Escalating Resilience if you need bullet resist, or Silence Glyph. [24:31]
- Silence Glyph is great against Pocket, who can still dodge the combo by jumping into his Satchel even through Slowing Hex, and against Debuff Remover users. [24:31]
- Yamato is the biggest Silence Glyph case, probably the best hero in the game this patch: with Slowing Hex and lift out of Shadow Weave she can still pop her ult, but Silence Glyph plus lift kills her before she can ult. [25:01]
- He usually takes Titanic Magazine because the build shoots fast and has few ammo items, and it adds spirit resist for tankiness. [26:03]
- Then Spirit Armor, because the meta is still 95 to 100% spirit damage; gun builds are rare apart from Haze, Wraith and maybe Vindicta. [26:03]
- Late game items don't have to go left to right; pick what's needed most. [26:33]
- Siphon Bullets is a late game pick after its rebuff (now gives weapon damage): once you can kill the lifted target, it helps sustain long fights with the big base health and 55 HP stolen per proc, plus bullet resist and weapon damage. [27:05]
- Improved Spirit Armor is a self-explanatory late pick. [27:38]
- If you still lack damage, Crippling Headshot: anything that enters your lift drops instantly. [27:38]
- Diviner's Kevlar if you die too much in team fights after lifting two or three and killing one: lifting with it lets you live much longer through its big spirit shield, and with Shadow Weave the extra spirit power makes cards hit hard enough. [27:38]
- Unstoppable if you get stunned out of the combo. [28:08]
- Curse is the ultimate one-target pick, for example against a Pocket with Ethereal Shift after you already bought Silence Glyph: Slowing Hex, Curse, then lift out of invis and they can't do anything. [28:08]
- Situational items: Escalating Resilience against a lot of enemy gun damage (probably better than Bullet Armor); Silence Glyph is the first situational pick and really good in this build. [29:10]
- Toxic Bullets is arguably better than before against heavy healing, but rarely needed here because targets die before they can heal. [29:10]
- Enduring Speed doesn't matter much either, because the enemy should be dead before they can slow you. Magic Carpet is for memes. [29:42]
- Inhibitor is still great if fights turn into long extended late game team fights; Lucky Shot is really bad now, so Inhibitor is probably the best item to be tankier, deal more damage, slow them and cut their damage. [29:42]
- Mystic Slow still has a place because it procs on Tesla Bullets, which hit the target and people around them, giving an AOE movement and fire rate slow without buying Ricochet and Inhibitor. [30:12]
- Refresher for a second ult; he bought it maybe once in this build and thinks the other late game picks (Siphon Bullets, Crippling Headshot) are more valuable. [30:43]
- He would still like a tanky, long-fight, tracking ADC-style Wraith build, but says that is not a thing right now because every non-rat play style is weak. [31:44]
