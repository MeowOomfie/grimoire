# Every New Item In The BIGGEST Deadlock Update Explained
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-05-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orI-u4l2MBk

## Covers
Metro's live first read of the whole shop on the day of the May 2025 item overhaul (Deadlock 2.0): how category investment bonuses work, then item by item through weapon, vitality and spirit with numbers and first-impression verdicts, plus Capacitor testing on what buffs it removes. First impressions only, many changed his mind in later videos; numbers will go stale.

## Claims
- Items no longer give the category bonus themselves; the bonus grows with souls invested in that category. 800 invested gives 6% weapon damage (more than the old 4%), but it diminishes: 10k gives 50% and 30k only 88%. [0:32]
- Full gun might be viable, but scaling is good until about 10 to 15k in gun items and then falls off, so there's still reason to mix categories. [1:03]
- Close Quarters looks pretty bad unless you play melee heroes like Abrams. [1:03]
- Extended Magazine (reworked basic magazine) gives 25% max ammo (was about 26%) for 800 and upgrades into Titanic Magazine, now 1,600, with 90% max ammo and 15% weapon damage. [1:03]
- Titanic Magazine is weaker than the old one (old was about 120% max ammo plus melee and spirit resist and more bullet damage) but being an upgrade makes it worth it, definitely better than before. [1:33]
- Headshot Booster is 50 headshot damage, which he thinks is a buff, though 800 items are more expensive; it may be worth saving for 1,600s. [2:04]
- Monster Rounds 25, small changes. Rapid Rounds got nerfed: it used to give about 9% fire rate plus sprint speed. [2:35]
- Rapid Rounds upgrades into Swift Striker or Burst Fire. Swift Striker doubles the fire rate and gives sprint speed back for only 800 more as an upgrade; think of Swift Striker and Titanic Magazine as 800 upgrades, which makes them viable. [3:05]
- Active Reload: 20% max ammo, 25% fire rate, 18% bullet lifesteal; little changed, might be back on the menu depending on how green items were nerfed. [3:36]
- Backstabber: footstep sound distance cut 50% and 75 bonus health, possibly OP for ganking since audio matters a lot. Hitting enemies from behind with gun or melee opens a wound with spirit damage over time and reduced bullet resist, reveals them through walls to you and nearby allies, and gives almost 2 m/s movement speed for 5 seconds. [3:36]
- Backstabber is gimmicky (useless if the enemy notices you), but if you land it it's insane value for a cheap item; seems strong for Haze, maybe Shadow Weave builds. [4:07]
- Fleetfoot is stronger than before. [4:39]
- Intensifying Magazine is now 1,600 with 20% max ammo and up to 40% weapon damage; weaker than before but about half the price. To run it early you probably need Titanic Magazine early too. [5:10]
- Extra Stamina now upgrades into Kinetic Dash for 800 more, adding 30% fire rate (more than before) and bonus ammo. [6:41]
- Long Range lost its many stats; bonus weapon damage beyond 15 m, a lot weaker and pricier than before. [7:13]
- Melee Charge (bullet resist, melee damage, 50% heavy melee distance) seems a bit better than before. [7:13]
- Mystic Shot looks stronger. [7:43]
- Opening Rounds (new, 1,600): bonus weapon damage against enemies above 50% health, the Pristine Emblem effect in a cheaper, weaker item that builds into nothing; good for hybrid builds on Warden or Wraith. [7:43]
- Slowing Bullets got buffed: movement slow up to 30% instead of 25%. [8:47]
- Spirit Shredder Bullets is basically the old Soul Shredder with no stats (reduces target spirit resist and gives you and allies spirit lifesteal against them), so weaker. [8:47]
- Spirit Shredder Bullets upgrades into Spirit Rend: headshots reduce spirit resist further, up to four stacks, 8 second debuff, 2 second cooldown. The effect changed from spirit amplification to spirit resist reduction, an important change; he's not impressed. [9:17]
- Split Shot (1,600): your weapon fires multishots, and hitting more than one hero per attack grants stacking weapon damage up to six stacks; a budget Ricochet that is awkward to angle, and he doubts you often hit multiple heroes in fights. [9:49]
- Weakening Headshot reduces the target's bullet resist on headshot (12%) and upgrades into Crippling Headshot. [12:27]
- Crippling Headshot is much weaker but now applies healing reduction, and it now has a cooldown; easier to apply than old bullet resist shredding since it needs headshots, but gives less, which is fair because it upgrades. [12:58]
- Alchemical Fire got nerfed by quite a lot. [13:29]
- Berserk is the old Berserk plus bullet resist at roughly double or triple the price, a nerf, and it no longer builds into Frenzy. [14:00]
- Blood Tribute: a toggle with no cooldown that drains 50 health per second and reduces healing received, for 35% fire rate, 50% debuff resist and movement speed; you can't die to it. [14:30]
- Blood Tribute is situational and high skill: turn it on to initiate or chase when you're not being shot; it might be an OP item people don't realize yet. [15:33]
- Burst Fire no longer gives bonus damage; it gives 45% fire rate for 4 seconds, and its cooldown went from 6.5 to 8 seconds. [16:05]
- Selling an item returns half its cost. [17:05]
- Cultist Sacrifice upgrades from Monster Rounds: 4 out of combat regen, 35% weapon damage and 35% bullet resist against NPCs; consume an enemy NPC for 150% bonus souls and a buff lasting 180 seconds on a 260 second cooldown. [17:05]

- Escalating Resilience: 30% bullet resist (was 40%), 30% max ammo, bonus health and weapon damage; better stats than before apart from the resist, seems pretty good. [19:39]
- Express Shot upgrades from High Velocity and replaces Pristine Emblem as that upgrade: after 4 seconds without attacking, your next attack fires twice with increased damage and velocity. Yamato can buy it in lane; he thinks it's better on Shiv because it works on his right click. [19:39]
- Headhunter (from Headshot Booster) is basically the same item. [21:46]
