# You'll Master Deadlock, Just Give Me 40 Minutes
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-05-15
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dUWP1ORnSxo

## Covers
A long all-round improvement talk built from 30 hours of VOD reviews: settings, melee and parry habits, playing every hero, laning (waves, souls, healing trooper, timers, spikes), aim and health habits, mid game (pushing waves, Trophy Collector, Urn, Walkers, Mid Boss), building, late game and the Patron.
For new and mid-rank solo queue players; names Lady Geist, Kelvin, Mo & Krill and Wraith as the easiest heroes to climb on.

## Claims
- Do not try to apply everything from a guide at once; pick one focus per day (a movement day, a building day and so on). He went from terrible to Ascendant 1 in about a month and a half this way. [1:01]
- Set every item and ability to quick cast in the settings: tap to cast instantly, hold to see where it goes. It removes the wait between pressing and confirming and makes ability use more fluid. [1:31]
- Stop meleeing low-health enemies you could kill by shooting; everyone desperation parries, and two or three more bullets would have killed them. [2:01]
- You can weave light melees into a fight, but do not spam light melee when the enemy is one shot from death. [3:02]
- Do not stand still to parry. Dash or air dash past the enemy and parry while moving away (a "drive-by parry"): people often throw a light melee as you pass, and if you miss the parry the dash momentum usually carries you out of heavy melee range. [3:02]
- Play every hero a few times, even in Street Brawl so your rank is safe; you learn what each hero is good and bad at. Example: after playing Bebop you learn that a hooked target can run Bebop down and kill him, especially if he misuses his uppercut. [4:04]
- In lane, kill the wave first in most fights, especially the first wave; otherwise you take free damage and the enemy can zone you off the souls. [6:08]
- Always confirm your souls and deny theirs. Shooting the soul orb gives you 40% of the minion's souls; the other 60% is in the orbs that drop, so skipping the confirm hands the enemy a repeated 40% swing. [6:38]
- Do not kill the healing trooper too early or you have nothing to heal off. [6:08]
- Killing the healing trooper heals roughly 15% of missing health plus some base HP, about 25% of your health in total, split between the players near it (about 12.5% each in a duo lane). [7:10]
- Solo laning while being dived under tower, ignore the divers and kill the healing trooper to heal about 25% and possibly turn the fight. [7:40]
- Below Archon players take the zipline back to spawn far too often; unless you cross the map, jump pads are faster for rotations. Killing two waves basically gets you to full health, so you rarely need to back for HP, and your side's shop means you never need spawn to buy. [8:10]
- Use Explore NYC from the play menu to learn the jump pads, wall jumps, basic air strafing and the map. [8:40]
- From a side lane to mid lane you never need the zipline; jump pads or rooftops are faster. [8:40]
- Grab a few boxes on every rotation; below Phantom nobody grabs them and it costs little time. [9:10]
- Respawn timers are much shorter early, so do your risky plays and limit testing before 10 to 15 minutes. If you die early and your lane partner does not kill the wave, you lose almost nothing, maybe three boxes' worth, which you make up by running boxes. [9:40]
- Waves spawn every 30 seconds; sync your play to them (kill the wave, then go do something) and always be doing something. Not being active enough on the map is a common pitfall. [10:10]
- Farming alone as a carry (he names his own Vyper and Wraith games) can leave you on 30k to 40k souls while the enemy team earns triple by fighting your teammates. [10:41]
- Denied souls matter as much as your own: invading enemy camps steals souls from them, and keeping enemies dead denies them farm, effectively removing a player. [11:11]
- If you are 30k but your teammates are 18k while the enemy team averages 25k, you will not win. In matchmaking with randoms you must be active and be the deciding factor rather than only farming. [11:43]
- Help struggling teammates (his example a 0 and 7 Drifter) so they get a kill or live instead of feeding further. [12:44]
- Look up a lane matchup guide (strong laners vs weak laners) and learn why each hero is strong in lane. Apollo, Vyper and Mina are strong laners. [12:44]
- Vyper is a weak laner apart from her 2, which executes enemies under about 30% HP and does around 250 damage at 5 minutes with tier two spirit investment. [13:16]
- Apollo is strong because with two points in his 3 he can do a whole health bar in one attack and executes under half health. [13:47]
- Mina is a good laner because she heals so much she is very hard to kill and does a lot of burst damage. [13:47]
- Against a strong lane like Apollo and Abrams, staying at tower is correct; leaving just gets you killed. [14:18]
- At 2:00 boxes and your first tier one camp spawn. From then on, never stay in lane after killing minions: kill minions, run boxes or a camp, repeat. [14:48]
- At 5:00 the bridge buffs spawn (both sides of mid lane, to the right of the left lane, to the left of the right lane) and respawn every 5 minutes; take them off cooldown when you can. [15:19]
- At 8:00 the Sinner's Sacrifice machines spawn. Punch until the top lights go out, then when only two lights remain charge a heavy melee and hit it for permanent buffs. [15:19]
- Sinner's Sacrifice gives more souls than anything except the wave, so make it a high priority, and stealing the enemy's is high priority too, but do not die for it. [15:50]
- If you lack time to charge the heavy on an enemy Sinner's Sacrifice, light melee it to break it: fewer buffs, but the enemy is denied. [15:50]
- Learn your hero's power spike, often a third point into an ability. His Vyper spikes on the second point into her 2 with Extra Spirit and goes for kills; on Apollo he maxes his 3 or waits for his ultimate before going for kills, and only pokes before that. [16:20]
- If you get fed in lane, rotate and kill people, since randoms will throw otherwise; take healthy fights for your losing lanes, but do not throw the lead diving Guardians and Walkers. [16:50]
- If you lose lane, let them take the Guardian; it is only about 500 souls and dying for it puts you on a long timer and costs about a thousand souls of minions. [17:20]
- When defending a Guardian, shoot minions, use cover, kill the healing trooper and weave in and out; kill minions so the Guardian targets them, rather than shooting the enemy heroes. [17:50]
- You can parry the Guardian: when rushing an enemy Guardian, dash jump past it and parry as you go by so it melees into your parry. [17:50]
- If your melee troopers walk up and get meleed by the Guardian, its melee goes on a different timer, so it can melee you right after your parry; only go in if it has not meleed in a while and is using its laser beam. [18:20]
- Hold Tab all game to see enemy items, souls, stacks (for example Bebop's bomb stacks) and ability points, so you know when enemies hit their damage spikes and when to avoid them. [18:52]
- Bullet travel time barely matters; he only thinks about it when shooting someone on a zipline. Most fights are at ranges where you aim directly at the enemy without leading, except for a few heroes like Paige. [19:22]
- Opening Rounds now builds from High Velocity Rounds and is an amazing item, so everyone should effectively be building a hit-scan hero. [19:53]
- The game is balanced around average aim and abilities, so good aim (he has about 65 to 70% accuracy) gives a free advantage, especially on heroes like Haze. [20:54]
- Aim is hand-eye coordination; treat it like any other skill. [21:24]
- Take care of health: no phone or game straight after waking, get sunlight and move, drink water in the morning, get 8 hours of sleep. He says a poor night's sleep (5 to 6 hours) can cost up to 100 ms of reaction time, more than the roughly 40 to 50 ms gap between a 20 and a 60 year old (caption says "6 year old"). [21:55]
- Rest days matter more as you practice more; grinding without breaks causes plateaus. [22:58]
- In mid game one good fight decides the game: one Mid Boss, or one good Mid Boss steal, usually wins it, so do not give up early. [23:58]
- Before every rotation, every Mid Boss and every push, kill the wave in front of you and the next wave, then leave; if something is urgent, kill at least one wave. Otherwise you lose objectives to minions. [25:29]
- In the current meta Trophy Collector is amazing; buy it and fight, since its extra souls (and extra range) keep you ahead of an equal opponent once you have about 10 stacks. [26:00]
- In mid game rotate after killing the wave, look for a fight, return to your wave, and grab boxes and camps along the way. [26:30]
- After dying two or three times in mid game (his example Mo & Krill), either stick to a strong teammate or play safe and farm lane, camps and boxes for a while instead of chasing more fights. [26:30]
- Avoid the spiral of repeated bad fights then overextending to reset an enemy-side wave; it leaves you thousands of souls behind and useless for the game. [27:02]
- A Mo & Krill should not take a hyper carry's farm to catch up, so buddy up with someone strong or farm safely. [27:32]
- Think of box routes as circles: go into the jungle, loop back to your zipline and zip back to lane. [28:02]
- Usually one or two enemy players carry their team; hard target them with Curse, Phantom Strike or a Mo & Krill ultimate, especially when they get overconfident. [28:32]
- Be the in-game leader: call Mid Boss, ask teammates to push out waves or reset a wave, because most players do not watch the map. [29:02]
- Buy spirit resist (even Dispel Magic) against spirit-heavy teams; most heroes deal spirit damage, and since most players aim badly you often do not need gun protection unless they are ulting. [29:32]
- Mid game objectives are Walkers, Guardians, the Urn and the Mid Boss. Objectives spawn with innate defense, so in the first few minutes inside an enemy Guardian you do marginal damage; leave and backdoor it later when stronger. [30:02]
- When either team loses a Guardian, call it: the players in that lane will probably start rotating. [30:32]
- Never walk past the midpoint of the map without vision on enemies, or you get ganked; do something on your side instead. [31:03]
- If the enemy is running the Urn to the opposite lane, push up your Walker since enemies will be at the Urn fight; if the Urn is coming to your side, push up only as far as where the Urn is going, never farther. [31:33]
- Past about 20 minutes do not die for a Walker; timers are 60 seconds and one death can lose the game. [32:04]
- Pick up the Urn almost every time it spawns, to run it or to deny it. [32:04]
- Use the Urn as a distraction: run it to a safe area on your side to draw enemies while someone like Wraith solo pushes a Walker in the opposite lane. [32:34]
- The Mid Boss is the biggest difference between low and high rank; low-rank players do not know how to end games. His games went from 50 minutes to almost never over 35 to 40. [33:04]
- General Mid Boss rule: take it after at least three kills with your waves pushed out, then push an objective or into their spawn, playing somewhat safe so the Rejuvenator is not wasted. [33:34]
- Even with three kills, watch for enemy ultimates (for example a Dynamo ult) and have someone ready to shut that hero down. [33:34]
- Judge your team's damage: with something like Vyper and Wraith the Mid Boss dies almost instantly, so one kill can be enough. [34:05]
- Do not try baiting the Mid Boss to force fights until at least Ascendant; he has never seen it done right. [34:05]
- The first player to heavy melee the Rejuvenator crystal heals to full; if you are not rushed, let the lowest-health teammate hit it first. [34:35]
- Have someone parry for whoever melees the crystal so it is not stolen, and keep some players outside the pit or in the veil watching for divers and making calls. [34:35]
- On Lady Geist he ults whoever is nearby during the Mid Boss to silence them, shutting down threats like an Apollo or Dynamo ult. [35:05]
- Stop auto-buying; open the shop and read every item so you understand what each does and why builds use them. [37:06]
- He dislikes most top builds; instead use Statlocker's top items for your hero sorted by rank, set a minimum (only items bought over 500 or 700 times), sort by time, and see when people buy them and how they affect win rate. [37:36]
- Do not buy defense items when losing; the enemies are so much stronger it does not matter. Buy damage to try to kill them first. [38:36]
- Build around hitting your 4.8k investment spike in each category (shown by hovering a category in the bottom left of the shop); a big buff comes at 4,800 souls spent. [39:06]
- Hitting your spike is a good signal to fight: if you hit your spirit spike and someone else has not, go fight them. Try to hit spikes earlier rather than later. [39:37]
- Late game is about taking favorable fights, since one loss means 60 second timers and the enemy takes Mid Boss and wins. [40:37]
- Favorable fights include outnumbering them or using combos, for example baiting them into the open for a Lash ult, or Lash with Dynamo. [40:37]
- Against Lash and Dynamo together you can buy Unstoppable and still lose to their ultimates. [40:37]
- In late game do not push too far, or too little; both get you or your teammates picked. [41:08]
- Once the enemy Patron is taken to half, all enemy respawn timers are cut in half (a 60 second timer drops to about 35), so leave after halving it if their timers are close. [41:38]
- Alternatively, after halving the Patron, hide nearby in cover; enemies often push too far thinking you left, and you jump them for a numbers advantage. [42:09]
- Best heroes to climb on, which he expects to stay true: Lady Geist, Kelvin, Mo & Krill and Wraith. [42:09]
- Lady Geist heals so much and stays in fights so long that she guarantees taking someone to 30% HP with her ult, and forces enemies into early anti-heal, making them weaker overall. [42:41]
- A good Kelvin tops damage, healing and distance covered; he rotates fast with his 2, slows enemies and removes stamina with his 1 and 3, and his ult protects objectives, isolates targets or saves the team from ultimates. [43:12]
- Mo & Krill and Wraith have a guaranteed kill button in their ultimates, so they can pick off the one or two strong enemy players and you do not need to rely on your team. [43:43]
