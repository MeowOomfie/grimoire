## Covers
Shiv, Midknight. Coaching an Ascendant Shiv player who is new to the hero, with great micro but weak macro.
Themes: respecting trooper damage, forcing abilities, over meleeing, right-click movement, dropping waves, playing for the Urn and objectives, itemization into a spirit lobby, and invading when ahead. Recorded around the 2026-06-24 patch.

## Claims
Player 1 (Shiv, Ascendant):
- He bought late; instead of finishing the last wave he could have bought on a similar timer earlier. [0:31]
- Do not "click the level" (as captioned); it cost a lot of damage he did not need to take. [1:01]
- Stacking knives on Pocket puts in a lot of work because of the spell resist reduction. [1:01]
- Shiv right-click movement: right click at a 90 degree angle, then flick backwards to get maximum momentum. [1:31]
- Stop burning and forcing knives, and do not drop knives at low charges when you cannot stack them. [1:31]
- He fights in the wave too much and does not respect trooper damage; troopers were his top damage taken by miles. [2:33]
- Buy timings are often off when you are new to a hero because you do not know what to buy or when you spike. [3:05]
- Going for a preemptive wave before the bot timers is good. [3:39]
- He ignored a full wave sieging his guardian and ran past it. [4:09]
- Weave right clicks (melee) into combos: right click, knife, right click, dash, right click, knife; buffer the downtime before abilities, especially before dash. [4:09]
- Playing Street Brawl is the best way to practice micro. [4:09]
- The early roam was good. [5:10]
- Stop forcing abilities; the forced dash felt bad. [5:10]
- He should have just one shoved, or rotated with Haze for more kills even at the cost of the guardian, then traded it back. [5:46]
- Kill with your dash instead of heavy meleeing for no reason. [5:46]
- He did not need to dash into the parry; light melee and then dash would have been better. [5:46]
- Hold your dash for a threat like Apollo; if your teammate dies there it is because you did not hold dash. [6:16]
- Over meleeing: light melee is strong, heavy melee for fun is bad; he made the same mistake against Pocket. [6:16]
- His biggest issue by far is ignoring waves: push the neutral wave so it gets pressure, then rotate, which takes about 4 seconds. [7:46]
- Suppressor is a questionable pick on Shiv because he struggles to apply it and the enemy has little fire-rate base damage. [8:48]
- His movement is really good, but he needs more edge boosts for efficiency, and could have wall bounced or right-click wall bounced. [8:48]
- His earlier ganks worked because he came from behind the enemies; ganking from in front lets someone like Seven just back up. [9:18]
- Using the ultimate for movement is good. [10:29]
- Into heavy spirit damage (Holliday, Abrams, Apollo), his item gives too little; Battle Vest is strong early when enemies go gun spikes, but it is only 1600, so pivot to spirit resist when they pivot to spirit. [10:29]
- He should have dashed instantly after the charge to avoid the stomp. [11:30]
- Play for the Urn, especially the comeback Urn. [11:30]
- Two major mistakes: dropping waves, and not playing for or calling Urns and objectives; great micro gets suffocated when the enemy calls objectives. [12:01]
- Against a cloaked Pocket, watch the cloak and wait to knife; having the knife ready almost guarantees the kill. [13:32]
- Not having any spirit lifesteal really hurts him long term; build Spirit Lifesteal into Infuser or similar instead of double shielding. [14:02]
- Heavy melee is only safe on someone who will not parry; the timing is tight, and heavy meleeing someone's back gets parried off audio. [15:04]
- Against a Seven ultimate, take the fight and keep stacking knives on him (porcupine him), saving knives to stack during the ult for amplified damage. [15:36]
- He kept walking into Seven's ult instead of respecting it. [16:06]
- Down dash at that spot is free movement and good for juking; build it into muscle memory. [16:38]
- Take the fight when your team has rejuvenator and the enemy is mispositioned outside their base, even if it costs a rejuv. [17:11]
- He was leading early but lost it by dropping waves and not invading; invading is how you push a lead. [18:15]
- His heavy melee on Pocket was an ego play that could easily be parried. [18:45]
- Counterspell is very strong; learn it, since countering ults like Pocket's or a Holliday lasso becomes more instinctual than it seems, though it is hard. [19:46]
- Respect enemy intelligence; do not try to 1v1 when the target can easily back up. [20:47]
- Send the wave ahead of you so the walker has no backdoor protection, instead of wasting four knives on it. [21:18]
- When a strong enemy ignores you, you can fight him and call your team to turn on him before rejuv drops. [22:57]
- When the enemy Seven ults, split from each other; you had three Healbanes, so you could have fought him. [26:35]
- Do not give up the Urn there. [28:05]
- He showed up about 30 seconds late to the fight that decided the game: they won it, took the Urn and Mid-Boss, going from neutral to about 70% odds or more. [29:07]
- Instead of forcing a solo contest on mid, split the mid walker or find value elsewhere. [29:38]
- No reason to fight when the map shows you are outnumbered; even greeding one knife is not worth it. [31:10]
- If you are dead to rights, doing as much damage to the walker as you can is fine; ult into dash, air dash diagonally, wall bounce to the vent and you might live. [31:40]
- Summary: took too much trooper damage, do not click the level, stop forcing abilities, stop over meleeing, stop dropping waves, play for Urns and objectives, and invade, especially when ahead. [32:10]
