## Covers
Coaching review by Midknight of one Phantom-rank Billy player (unnamed, in one of their first games on the hero) in a lane against Bebop and Abrams.
Themes: not dying for teammates' bad fights, when to join a fight versus split or take objectives, ult and ability awareness, minimap habits, and Billy's 2-max build, item choices and combos.
This is a 2025 video from Billy's release week (August 2025 patch), so builds and numbers may be out of date.

## Claims
Player (Billy, Phantom):
- Rebuttal as an early item is overcorrecting; you do need defense, so he would go Melee Lifesteal, or Extra Regen, Restorative Shot, or any healing. [0:30]
- In general Melee Lifesteal is better on Billy, but into Bebop (and to a degree Abrams) Extra Regen is better because it is very hard to even melee the wave for healing without getting bombed. [2:34]
- When you miss a parry, trade melees back; for a melee hero into another melee hero it is basically the only way to deal with it. [2:04]
- Going in when the enemy misses their dash is a good play, since they are vulnerable. [3:38]
- Pro players bunny hop while chasing more than he expected; test whether bunny hopping beats walking in those spots. [3:38]
- When Abrams charges you, sprinting out is better than trying to melee your way out. [4:40]
- Using melees to clear corners is a good habit. [5:11]
- He was too afraid of Bebop: with a full combo (2, 1, ult) you probably kill him if he stays in the radius, though an Abrams charge could displace you. [5:42]
- Map awareness: whenever you are not first in line, look at your minimap, because nothing will engage you until you turn the corner. Here it would have shown the Bebop running away, so you back off instead of chasing. [6:44]
- Tower melee seems changed this patch: on the previous patch he would have been meleed by the tower there. [7:48]
- When the enemy's only way to live is to parry, they will throw a panic parry, so do not melee; just shoot them. [8:52]
- Max your 2 second. [9:23]
- You can cancel the uppercut knockback with your 2. [9:23]
- Reactive Barrier is fine into both Bebop and Abrams, and into a double CC lane it gives a lot of health (325 base plus scaling). [9:55]
- Buy Reactive Barrier more into Bebop; in a Bebop lane you will get hooked if the Bebop is competent, so it is not only a skill issue. [11:25]
- With the 2-max build, rush Compress Cooldown for your 2; hold Superior Cooldown until you have more points in your ult (for its resists), since your other abilities do not justify it yet. [10:25]
- Melee Charge is needed for a play style that melees this much. [10:55]
- Pressing 2 and then 1 immediately gives a guaranteed 1, because there is a small window where they cannot air dash; the player confirmed it is impossible to dodge out. Normally 2-1 is not the best combo, except on a spirit build. [12:36]
- Your 2 does not knock up troopers or work on camps, so it is not worth using on camps. [13:07]
- Split the walker when the map shows four or five enemies elsewhere; you can chunk it to about half HP before anyone arrives, and only one person can come, who loses the 1v1 to you. [13:38]
- If a defender does respond (here Lash), you get about 20% walker damage and back off. [14:09]
- Arriving late to a fight already lost means jumping alone into three enemies; wanting to be a difference maker does not help when the fight is already over. [14:39]
- When your team is already there, focus on CCing the enemies for as long as possible. [15:09]
- Whenever you see a fight on the minimap, decide if you can get there in time (under about 10 seconds); if you cannot, ask how to use the fight instead. [15:09]
- At minimum steal enemy farm (their Sinner's Sacrifice); if you have a wave pushing into an objective, take that objective. [17:12]
- Do not run it down because you fear teammates blaming you; a little ego is healthy, and mute anyone who flames you for making the right call. [15:40]
- With 2 maxed, use your ult to set up your 2; ult into 2 is your biggest group damage. [16:10]
- To hit several enemies with your 2, pull them in with the ult and line them up so they come from the same direction. [19:46]
- If two of you split a walker, you are giving up the Urn; tell your team to give the Urn and not fight it, or the trade turns into a walker for Urn plus deaths. [18:15]
- After splitting, shove another wave; you had 10 more seconds to siege before the enemy could arrive. [18:15]
- When surrounded with no way to live, do not think about surviving; use your 2 first for damage, then spam parry to stay alive as long as possible and peel enemies in one direction while your team comes. [20:18]
- Popping your 3 for movement and using wall bounces to escape is good. [22:49]
- Pulling an enemy into your walker with the ult is a good Billy play, just not into a Bebop. [22:49]
- Parry after an Abrams dash even if it misses, because Abrams players almost always melee after the dash. [23:21]
- When your team shoves mid, go Mid Boss; a Haze will not run the Urn onto your side. One person (last in line) pushes mid while the first three rush Mid Boss; here it could have been taken before Dynamo respawned. [23:21]
- Billy's ult only makes you unstoppable once it pulls, not when you break the chain (tested in the replay). [25:28]
- About 0.5 seconds out of line of sight breaks the chain (his feel, not a measured number). [32:19]
- Do not chase there; popping the ult for its resistances and jumping out the window would have kept you alive. [32:50]
- Always play as if Lady Geist has her ult up unless you have checked that she does not; space around it. [33:20]
- Dynamo players telegraph their ult: they switch from backpedaling to dash jumping in. [33:20]
- On the build: he does not like the item captioned as "E"; it is an eighth-slot item for when you are out of items. Transcendent Cooldown is better (more Torment Pulse ticks, and the build has more cooldown items), and an item captioned "Arctic Blast" would be stronger. [34:52]
- Fight read: jumping to trade the Geist was fine while only two enemies showed, but the second three show and you only have four, back off and let your Viscus die. [36:54]
- Do not engage there without a piece of utility like Viscus's ball; down a player with Abrams rotating, it is a 4v5 to 4v6 and it cost the game. [37:27]
- Homework: work out where dying after your team comes from (fear of being flamed or blamed for the wrong call) and fix it. [38:59]

General advice:
- Before each queue pops, decide what you want to work on that game and put most of your focus on it. [30:16]
- At load in, look at the enemy comp and pick the ults that matter (Geist, Dynamo, Mo & Krill, Kelvin's dome, Holliday's lasso), and check if they are up before you enter melee range. [31:48]
- Against Grey Talon's bird you cannot stay around at 1 HP, so reset sooner than you would like. [40:29]
- Do that ult check every game for a week; he expects big improvement from it. [40:29]
