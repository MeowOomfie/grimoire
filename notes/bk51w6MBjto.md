# The Best Wraith Build In Deadlock Just Got Better...
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-03-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bk51w6MBjto

## Covers
Metro's Surge V4 Wraith build (Surge of Power, Tesla Bullets, Sinner's Sacrifice farming), every item explained for the new map with three Walkers and dual lanes, March 2025 patch era. Includes lane kill combo, sell order, a fast jungle route, late game swaps and situational items. For Wraith players who want a split-push hard carry build; items and numbers are from early 2025 and may be stale.

## Claims
- Wraith is back and stronger than ever; Surge V4 is the strongest version of the build, and this is the first update for the new map. [0:00]
- Lane changed least: still start Ammo Scavenger even in dual lanes, though your teammate may take souls and cost you stacks. [0:31]
- Wraith is one of the best last hitters with the new soul system: souls pop up just above the creep, so keep shooting after the kill to pre-fire the soul and guarantee last hits and denies for your teammate. [0:31]
- Ammo Scavenger also gets stacks from jungle camps later, and at max stacks gives 2 m extra sprint speed. [1:33]
- Sprint speed got a big buff: after about half a second of not dealing or taking damage from creeps you start sprinting, so outside full team fights you are almost always sprinting, which is great on Wraith. [1:33]
- 500 soul gun items were nerfed a lot and the meta is people spamming green items; Hollow Point Ward is still the best 500 gun item besides Rapid Rounds, giving 20% weapon damage while healthy plus a spirit shield and spirit power. [2:04]
- In lane Wraith still needs card damage, which Hollow Point Ward's spirit power helps. [2:34]
- Next buy Infuser and Extra Charge; at 2.2k souls you have two points in Card Trick and your lane kill combo comes online. Before 2.2k Wraith is weak. [2:34]
- Lane kill combo: when they engage, pop Infuser, pop Full Auto and throw three cards quickly. [3:05]
- Rapid Rounds next: the best 500 gun item, fire rate is strong on Wraith, and it gives another meter of sprint speed. [3:05]
- Then Enchanter's Barrier: barriers are still king for early survival; it adds spirit so cards and Full Auto hit harder; at that point you have 465 spirit shield and keep its bonus while the shield holds. [3:36]
- Then Sprint Boots, skipping Extra Stamina, which was nerfed and no longer gives fire rate; you can still run Extra Stamina instead or both. [3:36]
- Sprint Boots let you relocate very fast in lane after breaking damage; keep them until very late game, then upgrade to Enduring Speed. [4:06]
- Back to buying Tesla Bullets first after lane (last version bought Swift Striker and Quicksilver Reload first). [4:36]
- At 8 minutes almost every game, leave lane and farm Sinner's Sacrifices; Wraith benefits from every stat they give, even improved cooldown, since Tesla's damage and proc chance improve with cooldown reduction. Wraith is the number one hero who wants to farm them. [4:36]
- Because you are punching Sinner's Sacrifices rather than jungle camps early, skip Quicksilver Reload and Swift Striker until later; by the time you have punched all of yours (and some of the enemy's) you have Tesla and can farm fast. [5:07]
- Quicksilver Reload goes on Card Trick as always; cards no longer deal much damage and are mostly a reload tool, so pop one for an instant reload. [5:38]
- Fleetfoot comes earlier in this version because getting around the new map is more valuable. [6:08]
- Swift Striker can come early if you plan to farm T3 camps; then Combat Barrier, which you can buy first if you have no flex slot yet; sometimes you also have Extra Regen at this point. [6:08]
- New: Improved Cooldown instead of Duration Extender (credited to a friend's testing). [6:39]
- Improved Cooldown cuts Tesla's passive cooldown from 0.35 to 0.30 seconds, adding a lot of Tesla damage and faster camp farming. [7:09]
- Improved Cooldown also drops Full Auto from about 34-35 seconds to 29, enough to get Full Auto twice in drawn out mid game fights. [7:09]
- Before Surge of Power, most of your damage still comes from your gun, but Tesla damage is not to be underestimated in the early mid game. [7:39]
- Bullet Lifesteal next: there is no Heroic Aura in this build and most damage is bullets. [8:10]
- Surge of Power is the big power spike; if very far ahead you can skip Bullet Lifesteal and Improved Cooldown and go straight to it. [8:10]
- By Surge of Power, Full Auto is maxed; engage, pop Infuser, pop Full Auto and Surge and you can win any 1v1 in about two seconds, probably the highest early DPS of any hero. [8:41]
- Then Healbane, probably the best item in the game right now and underbought: almost every hero has healing (Calico melee lifesteal, Kelvin, Abrams' aura, Shiv's spirit lifesteal). [8:41]
- Healbane cuts enemy healing to 40% and procs passively from Tesla Bullets on your target and anyone near them, even without cards or Full Auto; the heal on kill helps clutch 1v2s. [9:42]
- Then upgrade Sprint Boots to Enduring Speed. [9:42]
- Sell order: always sell Extra Charge first, since after lane you do not need three cards and they are just reloads; then Rapid Rounds or Hollow Point Ward. [10:14]
- With only one flex slot, worst case skip Enduring Speed and sell Sprint Boots; flex slots come easier on the new map since you only need to kill one Walker for the second. [10:46]
- Realistic mid game: around 10 minutes with about 12k souls you have enough levels for Full Auto. [11:16]
- Bottom right farming route: four nearby creep camps including a tier two give roughly 1,750 souls and can be done in about a minute with tricks like sliding down the stairs. [11:46]
- On the new map Tesla is even stronger because there are many small creep camps close together to keep proccing it. [12:47]
- Until Surge of Power, focus on farming; still defend objectives when the enemy pushes a Walker, and pressure towers and Walkers if the enemy is not defending, but less than on the old map. [13:17]
- Pushing Walkers very early on Wraith is now risky: only three Walkers means defenders show up more, and Walker HP went up. Wraith is still the best split pusher but needs to farm longer. [13:48]
- Once you have Surge of Power, start forcing fights with the team; every Sinner's Sacrifice from then on is another power spike. You can show up to team fights and solo Walkers easily. [14:19]
- Crippling Headshot replaces Lucky Shot: the meta has lots of green items, resists and HP (Fortitude is hard meta), and Crippling Headshot gives your whole team bullet and spirit resist shred from one headshot every 4 seconds. [14:49]
- At around 25k souls roughly 35 to 40% of your damage is spirit, which makes Crippling Headshot even better. [15:51]
- Always pop Infuser before Full Auto: Full Auto's duration scales with spirit and only extends if Infuser is active first; the reverse order still adds damage but not duration. [15:51]
- After Full Auto ends your damage is mostly gun plus Tesla; Tesla scales into late game with Improved Cooldown and Sinner's Sacrifice cooldown buffs, and is never sold. [16:52]
- Lucky Shot is still an option if you struggle with ammo, but Sinner's Sacrifice ammo buffs usually give enough ammo by then, to the point you can sell Ammo Scavenger and later even Infuser. [17:24]
- Crippling Headshot is slightly harder to play since Lucky Shot's 30% movement slow is gone; Mystic Slow, bought later anyway, covers that. [18:25]
- Then Siphon Bullets: more damage, more tankiness and another layer of lifesteal; with everything popped the build had 45% bullet lifesteal and 42% spirit lifesteal, so 2,000 damage dealt is about 1,000 HP back. [18:25]
- Then Leech: even more lifesteal, HP and anti-heal on the enemy. [18:57]
- Late game sell barriers first, Combat Barrier then Enchanter's Barrier, because 300 bullet shield goes down very fast late. [19:28]
- After Leech, sell Bullet Lifesteal and buy Healing Booster: healing stacks diminishingly, and Healing Booster raises your healing and your resistance to enemy healing reduction. [19:28]
- Before late game, buy left to right; late game items are annotated in game with when to buy and what to replace. [19:58]
- Utility as needed: Escalating Resilience against heavy gun damage, Spirit Armor against heavy spirit damage, Ethereal Shift against things you can dodge. [20:28]
- Ethereal Shift dodges Lash's ult and Bebop's bombs (no Debuff Remover needed) and Pocket's ult, and lets you stall for 3 seconds when jumped. [20:58]
- Against a gun Bebop, buy Withering Whip: 45% fire rate slow; wait until he hooks you, then cast it so his hook and uppercut combo cannot kill you. [21:29]
- Slowing Hex is for catching escapers like a Rapid Recharge Infernus, but rarely needed since Surge of Power and Fleetfoot give enough speed to catch anyone. [21:59]
- Wraith's sprint speed scales with spirit, so spirit from Sinner's Sacrifices makes you very fast late game; do not buy Extra Spirit or Boundless Spirit just for speed. [22:30]
- Debuff Reducer or Debuff Remover against Bebop, Infernus burn, Mo & Krill and Pocket; Mo & Krill's blind leaves Wraith doing nothing because even Full Auto's spirit damage comes through the gun. [23:00]
- Burst Fire is a late upgrade replacing Enduring Speed (it also gives movement slow resist) or Swift Striker (Burst Fire takes fire rate from 20% to 40% while active; losing Swift Striker's 10% ammo does not matter here). [23:33]
- Late game fights do not last long enough for two Full Autos, and with enough spirit Full Auto lasts the whole fight, so sell Improved Cooldown for Duration Extender and Superior Duration on Full Auto, around 25 seconds active (the tooltip says 15 but Infuser's spirit extends it). [24:35]
- Super late game buy Colossus for more HP. [25:37]
- Situational: Toxic Bullets if the enemy heals a lot and you cannot afford Leech early; it stacks with Healbane to about 75-80% anti-heal. [25:37]
- Situational: Intensifying Magazine if behind and unable to afford Crippling Headshot; good in longer fights, but going straight to Siphon Bullets then Crippling Headshot is usually the bigger spike. [26:08]
- Bullet Armor only against a massive amount of bullet damage; Escalating Resilience is usually enough. [27:09]
- Unstoppable is bought a lot (should move earlier): as a fed late game carry with Leech and Siphon Bullets only hard stuns kill you (Mo & Krill, black hole, Lash ult), so pop it and kill someone. [27:09]
- Curse is a big investment to shut down one person; better for a teammate to buy it so you, the main damage, stay focused on damage and sustain. [28:09]
- Superior Stamina is a broken movement item: jump then sideways dash or double dash covers more distance faster than jump dash; dash sideways at a 45 degree angle even when you want to go forward. [28:09]
- Farm Sinner's Sacrifices until they get nerfed; they are overpowered on Wraith. [29:11]
