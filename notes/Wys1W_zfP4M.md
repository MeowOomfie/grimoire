# Stop Blaming Your Teammates | Coaching an Oracle Shiv
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-08-22

## Covers
Midknight reviews a replay of an Oracle-rank Shiv player (free coaching from his Discord).
Themes: the tier one camp at 2 minutes, using wave priority to secure farm in lane, ganks and the element of surprise, playing for item spikes, coin flip fights in mid, Shiv's right click movement, objectives with Dynamo, itemization, and tilt.
Patch of late August 2026.

## Claims
General and laning:
- Before 3 minutes, the only thing that matters is taking your tier one camp off spawn at 2 minutes; dying or losing trades before 3 minutes barely matters (about 200 souls to the enemy, one healing creep). [0:00]
- When you miss the melee on the first trooper, shoot the enemy hero to sync up the trooper instead of shooting the other creeps. [1:01]
- Your lane partner pushing the wave is good for you; take wave priority, there is no reason not to. [1:01]
- Wave priority is only good if you use it, for example to go hit boxes, not to stand and shoot the enemy. [2:34]
- Shotgun heroes have to kiss the wave to do damage to it, so they take trooper damage; it is hard to avoid. [2:02]
- With your partner on a box route, do not walk up so far; hit boxes and save for your big item spike. [2:02]
- Do not trade away your HP while full HP just to bleed denies. [2:34]
- Use wave priority to secure the buff (sinner box) in lane; get priority before going for the buff, or the enemy can contest it while your partner clears the wave. [3:04]
- Do not chase-engage an Infernus while you are burning; a dash and more shooting would have killed you. [3:04]
- Suppressor is very strong in a lane against two M1 (fire rate) heroes. [4:36]
- When setting up lanes, do not put two M1 heroes together: they are weak to Suppressor and even more to Rusted Barrel. [4:36]
- Two spirit nukers in one lane is great: one-shot the wave, one-shot the enemies, then roam. [4:36]
- Once you show a dash melee, the enemy is far more likely to parry the next one; under the enemy tower a parry is much more punishing. [5:07]
- Do not leave a winning lane to force a lane swap; you throw your lead into a lane where the enemy is hard winning. [6:08]
- If a teammate is losing, teleport over to help rather than walking your lead away. [6:41]
- As Shiv, push your lead in your lane, get fed, steal sinners, then go kill everyone. [6:41]
- When both teammates are in their lanes (you are strong side), you are the bonus player: steal farm rather than securing your own. [7:12]
- Do not enter a gank with one stamina; you cannot stay on the target. [7:43]
- A gank works off the element of surprise; once you spend it and they know you are there, leave. [8:13]
- Once you get parried in a gank, your teammates cannot help you. [8:13]
- It is very hard to dive a McGinnis who has her heal up. [9:16]
- As Shiv you are hard to punish alone because right click gets you away, unless the enemy has Slowing Hex or three show up. [9:46]
- Remember you can buy at the Guardian shop. [9:46]
- Never type to your team; say it to yourself. There is no constructive way to say most of it. [10:17]
- Arriving late to a Rift because you caught the mid wave and bought is correct; trading the Rift for a walker was a good play overall. [11:17]

Playing for spikes and itemization:
- Shiv, especially a Radiant buyer, wants to be fed; at 8.4k with Radiant he kills everyone. [8:44]
- Leaving lane too long cost a spike: his Dynamo was 8.4k while he was 6.8k. [8:44]
- Always take buffs; being greedy with buffs is good. [11:47]
- Do not fish for fights while sitting on a big bag; play for your spikes. He calls not playing for spikes a relatively major mistake. [11:47]
- Sell Extra Regen to buy a tier two or tier three item instead of saving for it, then rebuy healing later. [12:17]
- Against a heavy spirit comp, Spirit Resilience is good into all of them, and he was overthinking the itemization. [18:41]
- Carries should buy Unstoppable against an enemy Dynamo, or Dynamo lets the enemy take Mid Boss for free every time. [16:21]
- With Dynamo on your team, Mid Boss is overpowered. [29:58]
- Against a double flyer comp with a fed Vindicta, Phantom Strike is better than Unstoppable: more utility and team value, and it cancels Vindicta's flight. [30:29]
- Phantom Strike is about pulling flyers out of the air, not dashing on them; canceling Vindicta's flight removes a huge amount of her value. [31:32]
- His late items (Debuff Remover, Dispel Magic, Debuff Reducer, Unstoppable) were too many anti-debuff and defensive items; he had no damage after 27 minutes. [29:27]
- For damage, buy Phantom Strike or an offensive item like Infuser over Unstoppable. [34:42]
- This knife build depends on a soul lead to generate rage; when behind, it does not work. [28:26]
- Decay helps rage generation, and you get rage on execute. [28:56]

Shiv right click movement:
- Right click at a 90 degree angle to your movement; he was right clicking at about 110 degrees, too far back. [14:20]
- Air strafe in the air between right clicks. [14:20]
- You need momentum and to be on flat ground sliding for the next right click; doing it on a slope loses the movement. [14:50]
- Jump coming out of each right click. [14:50]
- Right clicking upstairs gives no movement; wall bounce around stairs instead. [14:50]
- Never right click twice in one slide unless you have a lot of fire rate; jump between slides. [19:12]
- Right clicks push you sideways because they cap move speed only in the direction you are looking. [16:52]
- For deeper movement detail he points to another creator's right click guide. [15:50]

Fights, macro and objectives:
- Do not force your way into mid with four enemies there; you spent too much time mid. [16:52]
- Take wave first before fishing for fights in mid. [17:22]
- In a fight he missed abilities, fought too early, and had too much downtime; stay on a target with your team. [19:42]
- Target priority: go on the Grey Talon who cannot use Veil and his bird; force his bird out or force him to waste it on you at full HP. [20:13]
- At 1,000 HP you are two snipes from death; leave instead of staying in. [24:18]
- Trading walkers is fine if the enemy is going to brute force yours anyway. [24:18]
- Heavy meleeing people caught in Holliday's lasso is very strong. [24:50]
- Do not chase a target whose teammates are about to arrive. [25:52]
- Fighting at the second shrine would have been better than at the first. [27:23]
- Staggering into the enemy one by one let them nearly end the game. [27:56]
- The walls on the left and right sides of the Patron area are fake: you can shoot, use abilities and see through them. [27:56]
- Shiv should not solo frontline or initiate; let the Abrams or Holliday's lasso start the fight, finish with your ult, then you are strong to continue. [29:27]
- Invade the side of the map where the enemy farms sinner camps; those are more valuable, and enemies inside them are isolated with no information and losing HP. [22:14]
- Splitting one player to Rift while the rest do Mid Boss risks a contest; if you give up the Rift you can lose the game to the wave. [31:01]
- Hit shrines faster; put the shrine behind the fight and melee it. [32:02]
- Not hitting objectives at the end was the team's biggest problem; they could have knocked the base. [33:08]

Mental:
- His mental is weak; take a second, breathe, drink water, then focus on what you can do to win. [21:14]
- A trick: remember you are happier than everyone else in your lobby. [21:14]
- Recognizing that tilt is coming from outside the game is good; the best mental coaching is therapy. [23:15]
- Losing a game where you played your heart out tilts more than one where you were chilling; being counter-called after correct calls is what tilts him. [25:21]

Final notes to the player:
- Stop sitting AFK in lane; use wave priority to secure farm. [33:40]
- Play for spikes and fish for fights off those spikes. [33:40]
- Do not look for coin flip fights in mid lane. [33:40]
- Hit the objectives. [34:11]
- Fix itemization so you still have damage late. [34:11]
