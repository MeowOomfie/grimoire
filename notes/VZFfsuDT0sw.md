# Find Your Perfect Deadlock Hero (Based on What You Play)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-10-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZFfsuDT0sw

## Covers
Hero picks for new players mapped from the games and roles they played before: pure shooter aimers, MOBA carry, mid, jungle and offlane players, and hero shooter tank, support and flex DPS players.
Each pick gets a short kit walkthrough and why it fits. For brand new players choosing a first hero.

## Claims
- Shooter players with zero MOBA experience who mainly hit heads: play Haze, a straightforward hero to learn. [0:00]
- Haze's passive Fixation is her main damage and you don't have to manage it: it ramps up over time, more stacks means more damage per bullet. [0:30]
- Haze was recently buffed so headshots apply triple Fixation stacks, so hitting headshots does a ton more damage. [0:30]
- Haze's Sleep Dagger is an easy-to-hit initiation tool to start on an enemy or get away if chased. [0:30]
- Haze's Smoke Bomb lets you run around invisible, and leveled up it gives bullet lifesteal, so as long as you hit bullets you will live. [1:01]
- Haze is the hero played by cracked aimers, some with thousands of hours on nothing but Haze. [1:01]
- A bit more complex aim pick: Infernus. His passive Afterburn applies burn stacks; at max stacks the enemy burns for a long time, and more bullets hit extend the burn. [1:01]
- Infernus's ability 1 (Catalyst) amps your damage on the enemy and reduces their healing; ability 2 (Flame Dash) gets you in and out of fights; his ult (Concussive Combustion) channels for a few seconds then explodes and stuns everyone around you. [1:32]
- All of Infernus's abilities extend the burn, so when you run out of bullets or have to reload, use your 1 or your 3 to reapply the burn and keep the enemy burning. [2:03]
- Infernus needs more finesse than Haze: he is a disruptor who goes in, gets the burn off, runs away and repeats, rather than shooting all the time. [2:03]
- Harder aim pick: Wraith, who like Haze is rewarded for hitting bullets, especially headshots. [2:34]
- Wraith's card meter fills the more bullets you hit, and headshots double the charge rate. [2:34]
- Wraith's thrown cards heal you, debuff and slow the enemy and do a lot of damage; they are her main damage tool, her bullets themselves do not do much. [3:05]
- Wraith's game flow is transitioning between shooting and throwing cards, or doing both at once. [3:05]
- Wraith has an easy teleport escape, and her ult lifts the enemy out of the fight and movement locks them for a while for easy 1v1 kills. [3:05]
- Coming from a MOBA, most Deadlock heroes are fairly complicated because you also need to learn shooting. [3:36]
- MOBA carry players (farm lane then jungle, become strong late): Victor is super simple to learn and does a lot of damage late game, though like MOBA carries he is weak early and needs a lot of souls to get online. [4:06]
- Victor's aura damages himself, but enemies in it take more damage than you, and his other abilities heal you; one main play style is running after the enemy so they die faster than you, with lifesteal from items keeping you up. [4:38]
- Victor's Pain Battery is the only ability in the game that recharges from taking damage rather than over time; it builds naturally from his self-damage and also when enemies hit you. [5:09]
- Victor's Jumpstart drains health then gives you more back, so it is poor to use when high and good when low; it is his healing tool for staying in longer. [5:09]
- Victor's ult is Skeleton King's ult from Dota: when you die you come back with full life and extra buffs, so you don't need to think about it. [5:39]
- Play Victor by running in, getting low, then pressing your Infuser for a lot of lifesteal. [5:39]
- Victor suits players whose aim is not great; he has a good gun and a gun build exists, but the most common build is spirit: farm the side lane all game, come out of the jungle and 1v6. [6:09]
- Victor loves brawling 1v6 because more enemies in his aura means more damage dealt and more healing for him. [6:09]
- Second carry pick: Seven, simple for MOBA players learning to aim, because Power Surge procs as long as you hit one of the three bullets in his burst and the aftershock jumps from enemy to enemy, so precise aim is not essential. [6:39]
- Seven's Static Charge is a point-and-click stun: after its timer, everyone in its radius is stunned. [6:39]
- Seven's Lightning Ball moves fast, slows down once it hits an enemy and deals a lot of damage to people who stay in its radius. [7:09]
- Put Lightning Ball on the floor among creeps to farm camps fast; Seven is the best and fastest jungle farmer in the game. [7:09]
- Seven needs a lot of souls to get online and is weak in the early game; pick him if you love jungle and safe lane carry. [7:39]
- Seven's ult Storm Cloud is a no-brainer and super powerful, especially in low ranks: you go up in the sky, anyone in the cloud takes heavy damage, and you are more resistant while in it. [7:39]
- Against Storm Cloud enemies often choose wrong: they ignore you and stay in it, take cover and die to it, or shoot you and find they can't kill the Seven. [8:09]
- MOBA mid lane players who like casters and ganking after lane: Pocket or Calico, neither super easy to learn. [8:39]
- Pocket is Puck from Dota: his Flying Cloak is like Puck's orb (it moves forward and you can teleport to it any time), and his satchel hides you inside it. [9:09]
- Pocket combo: throw the cloak, fight, use the satchel, deal damage from the satchel, then teleport out. [9:09]
- Pocket's ability 1 is a big damage amp on all your damage. [9:09]
- Pocket's ult Affliction is a big, easy-to-hit AoE that damages enemies over time down to 1 health, does a ridiculous amount late, and at max level stops all enemy healing; one of the most broken ultimates in the game. [9:39]
- Hitting Affliction on multiple enemies in a team fight almost always ends the fight: they tick down and die or must abandon the fight. [10:09]
- Pocket has a really good lane and afterwards roams with his mobility to one-tap people on the side lanes. [10:09]
- Calico is solid in lane, then roams into enemy jungles to kill people, preferably 1v1. [10:39]
- Calico is melee range: her gun does little damage, and her slash moves you forward, damages and heals you on hit. [10:39]
- Calico's Gloom Bombs (her 1) sends out a bunch of bombs; her cat form moves fast, gives a damage amp and lets her travel through the map's vents, where there are boxes to farm. [11:09]
- Calico assassin combo: run around in cat form, find your victim, press your 2 then your 1 so all the bombs attach to one person. [11:09]
- Calico's ult Return to Shadows takes you out of reality, explodes once, brings you back and explodes again, and resets all your cooldowns so you can repeat the whole combo. [11:39]
- Calico gets bonus souls from kills if you hit your 2 and kill the target within 3 seconds. [11:39]
- There is no classic jungler; everyone lanes first. For jungle players, Billy has the jungle feel. [12:10]
- Billy's Blasted: while active, meleeing enemies heals you and extends Blasted, a great tool for farming jungle camps while staying healthy, then leaving the jungle to gank a lane. [12:10]
- Billy reloads automatically after a melee, so if you melee you basically never have to reload. [12:41]
- Billy's ganking tools: a ram that knocks enemies up, and Bashdown; with Blasted active you get an immediate Bashdown recharge, so bash down, Blasted, bash down again. [12:41]
- Billy's ult chains enemies and pulls them to you at the end of the duration; you are very tanky during it and chained enemies can't escape with movement abilities, so it is a great team fight initiator. [13:12]
- Offlane players: most disruptive stay-alive heroes are advanced; Baba (just released) and Celeste give the weave in and out, stay alive disruptor feel but are quite difficult for new players. [13:42]
- Hero shooter tank players: Abrams is the most straightforward tank, similar to Reinhardt in Overwatch; he charges you into walls and punches you. [14:12]
- Abrams's activated aura heals you while enemies are in its radius and damages them, needs no good aim, and is very strong. [14:43]
- Abrams's 3 is a passive you don't need to manage: more HP, more lifesteal on melees and more regeneration on damage taken. [15:14]
- Abrams loop: charge them into a wall for the stun, melee, activate the aura to drain them. [15:14]
- Abrams's ult flies up and drops in a big AoE that stuns everyone, then you melee them; it also travels far, so it is a good escape when chased. [15:14]
- Drop Abrams's ult on the Rejuvenator in the Mid Boss pit to stun everyone and secure it for your team. [15:46]
- Tank players wanting something harder: Bebop, very tanky, basically Pudge from Dota or Roadhog from Overwatch: a hook, a sticky bomb that explodes over time, and an uppercut that knocks the enemy back, resets your hook and heals you. His ult is a laser beam. [16:17]
- Bebop is probably harder to learn than Abrams. [16:49]
- There is no perma-heal support; it would be game-breaking because you could heal yourself and your partner forever in lane and never lose. Team heals and shields are on cooldowns. [16:49]
- Support players: Paige, who sits behind the team; her shield on an ally gives an overshield plus extra fire rate and damage, and can go on yourself. [17:21]
- Paige's 1 is an AoE burn on the floor to deny area, stop pushes, chase and clear waves; her 3 spawns a sword that roots enemies under it once it lands. [17:51]
- Paige commonly builds Knockdown: it stuns the enemy after a second, so you can't miss the follow-up 3. [18:22]
- Paige can buy support items like Guardian Ward for more shields on teammates, or Rescue Beam to pull an ally in trouble out of harm's way. [18:52]
- Paige's ult Rallying Charge sends horses across the map in a line: allies hit get a big heal, enemies hit are knocked up and stunned; amazing for team fights. [18:52]
- Second support pick: Rem, one of the easier heroes. His main heal Tag Along jumps you to an ally and heals them, and you can jump to every teammate once as long as they are in range and line of sight. [19:22]
- Later-game meta Rem buys Echo Shard to do the full Tag Along chain heal on the team twice in a row. [19:52]
- Rem's Pillow Toss lowers his cooldowns when it hits an enemy and applies other debuffs. [19:52]
- Rem's Little Helpers on teammates give spirit resist and move speed; in lane put them on your creeps to make them stronger and heal over time for an easy push; also put them on Sinner's Sacrifice or boxes to farm them. [20:24]
- Save yourself as Rem by putting a helper on a creep, then Tag Along to that creep to heal and get away, or jump to the next one. [20:24]
- Rem's ult Nap Time is a big cone that slows enemies and then puts everyone in it to sleep for a long time; they take damage on waking. Use it to let your team chase, or to save your team by sleeping chasers and running. [20:54]
- Flex DPS players: few heroes have projectiles to lead (Danny not out yet); Yamato is akin to Genji, with an aimed slash and two fire modes: a fast near-hitscan left click and a slow AoE right click for lane harass. [21:26]
- Yamato's Flying Slash grapples to enemies to chase, and with advanced movement cancels can also escape; it is her gap closer. [21:57]
- Yamato's 3 (Crimson Slash) is a big cone slash that heals you, lowers enemy fire rate and boosts your melee damage. [22:28]
- Yamato's ult makes you invulnerable, shifts you out of reality, then gives a huge amount of resist and resets all your abilities. [22:28]
- Almost every hero is not super hard to learn; pick what you enjoy, since the heroes you enjoy most you play best. Try heroes in a bot match if you're nervous about real players. [22:58]
