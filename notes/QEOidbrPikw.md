## Covers
Venator with an unusual "spirit Venator" build built around Tesla Bullets and sustain items, aquatiq, commentary over a full duo queue game.
No one coached. Patch of early September 2026.

## Claims
- This Venator build does little damage early but is very strong late game; you have to pick your moments to strike. [1:01]
- Item order: Extended Magazine next, then straight into Tesla Bullets. [2:35]
- Grenades are very hard to hit on such a vertical map. [3:06]
- His trap is weak: enemies just shoot it. Its only real use is knocking people into it, since they cannot shoot it while they are already in it. [5:42]
- Healing Tempo, Radiant Regeneration (captioned Radiant Regen) and Mercurial Magnum (captioned Merk Mag) are core items in this build; Mercurial Magnum works basically as a gun item. [17:15]
- With the build online his fire rate is very high and he shreds. [18:18]
- Consider Mystic Slow to deal with fast enemies. [13:37]
- An enemy Warden with Veil Walker escaped by going into a veil. [16:45]
- He bought Indomitable (captioned indom): it gives a lot of resist out of combat and protects against a stun or immobilize, so you can be the only one not caught in a group lockdown. [19:22]
- He wanted Toxic Bullets next. [19:53]
- Juggernaut was his next buy after that. [23:03]
- With this build his ultimate does nothing because he is not building for it; just shoot people. [22:02]
- He ended the game with MVP on this build. [24:42]
