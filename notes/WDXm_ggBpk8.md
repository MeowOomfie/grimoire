# Deadlock MAJOR UPDATE Hero Tier List
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-05-13
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WDXm_ggBpk8

## Covers
First hero tier list after the major item rework update (video dated 2025-05-13), rated for ranked rather than pro play, before Fight Night had played the patch.
Argues inherent tankiness and inherent lifesteal win now because defensive items got weaker, and burst ("kill them faster") is the meta.
Places most heroes with new-item notes (Mystic Regeneration, Healing Tempo, Plated Armor, Rebuttal, Capacitor, Arcane Surge) and counters for Viscous and Wraith. The transcript ends mid-sentence on Wraith.

## Claims
- The list is rated on ranked, not pro play; pros had not scrimmed the patch yet. [0:00]
- McGinnis is high C: much better this patch, her turrets are tankier, and split pushing is viable again because side lane walkers die fast. [0:00]
- Split push as one or two: the more people show up, the more resist the enemy heroes and the walker get. McGinnis is good at this alone or with one partner. [0:30]
- Haze went from awful to solid, B tier: a good assassin pickoff hero, good but not OP. [0:30]
- The silence item he calls Focusing Lens helps Haze: it silences, then after 4 or 5 seconds deals 30% of the damage you dealt as extra damage; walk in, use it, ult, and they hopefully die. Arctic Blast is also great on Haze. [1:00]
- Grey Talon was nerfed (lower spirit scaling) after being S tier last patch, but is still A: some of the highest DPS in the game while safe in the backline. [1:31]
- He rates Haze above McGinnis because Haze can guarantee kills if played correctly. [1:31]
- Calico is balanced, high B, above Haze: a brawler, tank, initiator and delayer who no longer does insane damage but sustains long and disrupts fights. [2:02]
- Calico's role now: dive the backline and pin a glass cannon like Wraith so she can't deal damage, even without killing her; tanks are strong right now. [2:34]
- Abrams is absolutely busted, low S: the level-up values differ per hero and Abrams scales very high with lots of HP, works well with new items, and is the best frontliner after Lady Geist. [3:04]
- On Abrams you still run mostly spirit with some gun items, and his 1 gives a lot of survivability. [3:35]
- Bebop is D: everyone starts lane with more HP so hooks no longer guarantee kills, and securing souls is easier for the securing team with the new soul pop-up, so he denies less. [4:05]
- Bebop always had to win lane hard and snowball; Paradox and Holliday now do the hook-style pick better. [5:08]
- Dynamo is mid A, maybe high A: much better because full healing support builds got a lot better, his healing is insane, and late game he can run Healing Tempo to buff his DPS teammates. [5:40]
- Healing Tempo is an insane item on supports. [5:40]
- Holliday is weaker than before but not lower than A, behind S tier Paradox: both have incredibly low cooldown initiation that gets free kills; she still does big lane damage, has insane mobility and survives almost anything. [6:42]
- Supports buy more Rescue Beam now, which counters Holliday's Lasso (and somewhat Paradox's swap), but if the enemy is out of position and the support is slow, she still gets kills. He predicts pros will ban Paradox and pick Holliday anyway. [7:44]
- Infernus is high C: he trusts other Infernus mains; tank Infernus got better because Inhibitor and Ricochet improved, so players build full tank to slow and debuff everyone rather than DPS. [8:44]
- There are much better DPS options than Infernus, but he is playable. [9:14]
- Ivy is A and the second best support after Kelvin; Healing Tempo works very well on her and her ultimate is one of the best. [9:14]
- Gun Ivy is weaker now; build her as a support, and support Ivy is much better than Gun Ivy. [9:44]
- Kelvin is the best support: Arctic Beam with Infuser does some of the highest damage in the game plus a slow, he is strong in lane, and the dome is the best defensive ability in the game. [10:44]
- He first puts Kelvin in S, then moves him to A because he is not a hard carry, not game-definingly broken, and S is too full. [12:45]
- Lady Geist is the best hero in the game and needs more nerfs even after a hard hotfix nerf: everyone else has less lifesteal now, but she has inherent built-in lifesteal. [11:14]
- Mystic Regeneration, the new 800 soul item, is the best early item for Lady Geist: constant healing that scales with her spirit; she is unkillable with insane DPS. [12:14]
- Bound Spirit (Boundless Spirit) was nerfed mostly because of Lady Geist, since it heals per cast and she casts nonstop. [12:45]
- Yamato is top three: she buys Spirit Burn so every ability applies it to everyone, and she has inherent tankiness when armor items are much weaker. [13:17]
- Yamato can be played different ways, including spirit or gun with a damage aura; her strength comes from inherent lifesteal and tankiness, which is what the S tier heroes share. [14:17]
- Paradox is S. [14:48]
- Lash is good: Tankbuster is a strong item, better than Mystic Burst used to be, with insane burst damage. [14:48]
- It is hard to get spirit armor late game, so Lash can nearly one-shot anyone; the meta is kill the enemy faster than they kill you, and players who build defensive tend to die. [15:19]
- Only inherently tanky heroes are tanky because you can't build tankiness: there is no Improved Spirit Armor anymore and the new 3.2k defensive items are weak versions of the old improved armors, with some good ones later like Spellbreaker. [15:19]
- Mirage is low A, no longer S as he was for half a year; he benefits from Mystic Regeneration and can now put Mercurial Magnum on his 3, but his damage is not Wraith's. [16:19]
- Mercurial Magnum was nerfed and is less broken, no longer a must-buy on every hero. [16:50]
- Mirage no longer runs the lobby but is solid because he can split push and then teleport into fights. [17:21]
- Shiv is top three, consensus of five high-level players he asked (Lady Geist, Shiv, then Yamato): he has the most inherent tankiness in the game, and tank heroes are like Overwatch tanks now since no one else can build tanky. [17:52]
- Shiv is insane with Boundless Spirit, Leech or Spirit Burn and has many build options; Unstoppable is also a better item now. [18:23]
- Mo & Krill is A: if set up properly he gets a free kill every time but not at Paradox or Holliday level; very strong, not meta defining. [18:54]
- Pocket is high B: he dies fast because spirit armor is hard to get and the meta is spirit burst; a card with Mercurial Magnum or a Lady Geist nuke kills him. Debuff Remover no longer removes his affliction. [19:25]
- Seven is very weak, not quite D: a shell of himself, the new Boundless Spirit is weak on him; play him for mass silence and debuff with the new silence item, shred spirit resist and stun. [19:55]
- Seven is still great in team fights but dies to everything 1v1 and is not great in lane. [20:25]
- Sinclair is mid B by guess, he admits he doesn't know the hero; ultimate and AoE rabbit still make him a great support utility hero. [20:57]
- Vindicta is surprisingly weak: Alchemical Fire got a hard nerf, killing her Stake plus Alchemical Fire combo, and damage is so frontloaded that she kills too slowly. [21:29]
- The game right now is be very tanky, kill fast, or both. [21:59]
- Rebuttal, an 800 soul item, heals you when you parry; buy it and parry Puddle Punches and Viscous becomes useless in lane. [22:31]
- Left clickers can buy Armor Piercing Rounds, which ignores armor, so a farmed enemy kills Viscous in ball form. [23:01]
- Capacitor is the hardest counter to Viscous because it removes the cube; Capacitor is good in general for removing buffs, and the cube is the best buff to remove. [23:32]
- Shiv can currently execute through Viscous's cube, reportedly a bug. [23:32]
- Viscous starts at C, then is moved to B on a trusted player's take, no higher; he is weaker than before. [24:05]
- Vyper is A once a current bug is fixed: very fast, hard to kill, high damage, survivable, but not S and already nerfed at patch launch. [24:05]
- Warden is A and got a lot better: he builds the most like before, is scary mid game, can solo push walkers or solo gank the enemy jungler, and the patch created more small skirmishes he excels in. [25:08]
- Arcane Surge is now a 1,600 soul item; rushing it makes Warden's ultimate strong early, which makes him scary for ganks and split pushing. [25:08]
- Wraith is the biggest noob stomper in the game: one of the weakest laners, needs money, and is very strong late game if not behind, though not as strong as Yamato or Lady Geist with equal money. [26:11]
- Wraith has the highest late game damage output in the game right now, but high-level Lady Geist players still beat her. [26:42]
- Unstoppable is a great item and he rushes it every game. [27:12]
- A new defensive item that he says nobody talks about is a hard counter to left clickers that people don't buy enough; he has seen it on Shiv. [27:12]
- Plated Armor is an insane counter to Wraith that nobody buys: its 60% on-hit prevention works on what the captions call "material", plus Full Auto and Tesla, cutting a meta Wraith build's damage by about 50%; run into her as a tank and she dies. [27:43]
- Wraith will probably get nerfed because lower-rank players let her free farm and then don't focus her; she is easier to play in mid and low rank than Yamato, Shiv or Abrams. [28:14]
- To beat Wraith: punish her in lane, stop her farm for a while, and build properly. [28:14]
