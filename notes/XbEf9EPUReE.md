channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-11-08

## Covers
A short end-of-stream patch reaction: Metro goes through the patch's item buffs and nerfs and what each means for his Wraith build (Ammo Scavenger, Restorative Shot, Extra Health, Fortitude, Siphon Bullets, Leech, Withering Whip, Mystic Slow), still untested.
For Wraith players following his build. This is a late-2024 video: the item shop, hero kits and map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Mystic Burst got buffed, so it stays in the build. [0:00]
- Ammo Scavenger is actually good now with its 45 second duration: if you don't die you never lose the stacks; in a duo lane it is probably not as good because you need the last hits. [0:00]
- Restorative Shot was buffed: it now triggers on orbs, so shooting orbs no longer wastes it, and it now gives a bullet shield; in his opinion that makes it better than Extra Regen. [0:00]
- Extra Health is good again (it gives more weapon damage) and goes in the build. [0:30]
- Fortitude may be good later at 400 health: above 75% health it gives bonus weapon damage and move speed, and lifesteal should keep him above that threshold, especially while farming. [0:30]
- Fleetfoot and Quicksilver Reload are still both amazing items. [1:00]
- Combat Barrier got a 1% buff, small but welcome. Lucky Shot is bad. Vampiric Burst got a small buff and he is running it anyway; he still likes it a lot. [1:00]
- Inhibitor got nerfed, so he drops it. [1:00]
- Healbane got buffed and is probably a situational item now, likely run over Toxic Bullets, because gun Wraith's orange slots are so full. [1:00]
- Siphon Bullets got massive buffs, including 15% weapon damage, which might put it back on the menu, along with going tankier with more lifesteal. [1:31]
- Leech might now be broken: +50 damage, bullet shield, health, spirit power and sprint speed, and it stops the enemy fighting back; he will try it, though it costs the Bullet Resist Shredder slot. [2:01]
- Debuff Reducer got buffed; buy it against Infernus and Pocket. [2:01]
- Slots are the problem: there is no room for another purple item; Intensifying Magazine got buffed but goes to situational for now. [2:01]
- He may replace Bullet Resist Shredder with Withering Whip. Ammo Scavenger is back on the menu. [2:32]
- Start the build in the normal order and farm the jungle, since camps were weakened a bit. [2:32]
- Glass Cannon goes to situational for now; it may be good if Siphon Bullets is back. [3:03]
- Mystic Slow might be worth buying now since it is the only item that slows; he also considers Crippling Headshot, and says this all still needs testing. [3:33]
- Unstoppable got nerfed, which he says doesn't matter much. [3:33]
