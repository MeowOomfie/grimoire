# HOW TO COUNTER SEVEN - A Deadlock guide
channel: Deathy  date: 2024-08-28  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgVcYiag1bE

## Covers
Countering Seven in lane, mid game, team fights and late game: Power Surge, lightning ball, Static Charge stun, Seven's ultimate, jungle tracking, unsecured souls, Knockdown, Curse, Unstoppable, Debuff Remover, Restorative Shot, Extra Regen, Healing Rite, Headshot Booster, Close Quarters, Rapid Rounds, Mystic Shot, Warp Stone, Superior Stamina, Phantom Strike, Improved Spirit Armor, Decay, Healbane, Toxic Bullets; good picks into Seven (Lash, Vindicta, Wraith, Paradox, Pocket, Viscous, Dynamo, Ivy); Warden win rate; Grey Talon movement demo

## Claims
- Seven is not that good a hero at the moment; he put Seven in D tier. [0:00]
- Seven has the highest win rate on the stat-tracking websites, but those sites are not fully accurate (only about 50 of his 600+ games were listed and it is unknown how far back they go), so do not fully rely on them. [0:00]
- There is a clear trend that Seven and Warden have really high win rates, but he does not think they are broken, because they do not have a high win rate at the high MMR bracket and Seven is very rarely picked in featured matches. [0:30]
- The featured matches in the Watch tab are always the highest MMR matches (developers confirmed this), so they are good matches to watch. [1:00]
- Seven is a decent laner who pushes the lane: lightning ball and Power Surge are both really good at shoving the lane, and his laning stage is not the weakest part of him. [3:02]
- About a month to a month and a half earlier Seven was largely considered the strongest laner; after a couple of nerfs he is a pretty decent laner but nothing crazy. [3:34]
- Counter: put the aggression on him and shove the wave against him; if his lane is always shoved to his Tower he cannot harass you or do much. All the fundamentals of laning still apply against Seven. [3:34]
- If Seven does shove your wave, which he often will, focus on denies: with Power Surge and lightning ball he kills creeps he may not have vision of, so he cannot secure those last hits and you get free denies on creeps behind walls. [4:34]
- Do not fight him while Power Surge is active (around 10 seconds at the start); it gives him a lot of bonus damage and pretty much every character loses the trade, so hide and wait it out. [5:05]
- Seven has no movement ability, only dash, and no escape; if he overextends you can absolutely go on him, and he is fairly easy to kill once you are on top of him. [6:05]
- His stun has a 3.5 second delay, so you get 3.5 seconds to kill him before he stuns you, which a dual lane can often do depending on the heroes. [6:05]
- A lot of the time there are no specific items to counter specific heroes in lane, because those items come later in the game. [6:35]
- Against Seven in lane, get some form of regen, because Power Surge and lightning ball harass you: Restorative Shot, Extra Regen or Healing Rite, depending on how much regen you need. [6:35]
- Restorative Shot is not much regen but gives some bullet resist and weapon damage, the greedier, more offensive choice; Extra Regen is in between, with consistent health regen plus some ammo and bonus health; Healing Rite is the most defensive, a big healing burst but you have to stay AFK. [7:07]
- If Seven has a bad laning stage he will have a bad game, because he relies on a good start so he can farm the jungle early. [7:38]
- Offensively in lane, buy gun items that pressure him, ones good on almost every hero: Headshot Booster, Close Quarters, Rapid Rounds, and among the 1250s Mystic Shot, which newer players underrate and is a lot of laning harass if bought early. [8:09]
- You cannot counter pick for a lane or a game right now, but heroes good against Seven are ones that punish him for overextending or catch him out: Lash, Vindicta, Wraith, maybe Paradox. [8:39]
- Heroes that can deal with his stun (Static Charge) are also good against him: Pocket can suitcase it, Dynamo can use Quantum Entanglement, Viscous can use his cube, and Ivy can use Stone Form. [9:10]
- In the mid game Seven is a farming hero: his third ability and lightning ball power farm creep waves and jungle camps very fast, and his plan is pushing waves and then going to the nearest jungle camps. [10:11]
- Pushing waves gives him space and map information, which lets him farm the jungle safely. [10:11]
- You can see jungle camps disappear in real time on the minimap; if you saw Seven in a lane and a camp near it disappears, you know he is jungling there. [11:11]
- Counter his jungling by shoving lanes yourself and pressuring objectives so he has to respond, or his team loses Walkers and falls further behind. [11:41]
- Always pressure objectives early against Seven: he scales decently once allowed to farm, but his biggest weakness is that he takes time to get online, so keep up the tempo. [12:11]
- Seven is much like Anti-Mage in Dota: do not let him jungle for 20 minutes and come online with his items. [12:11]
- His win rate is high at lower levels partly because newer players do not apply that pressure. [12:11]
- Try to gank him in the jungle or while he pushes lanes; by watching the lanes and camps disappearing on the minimap, you and a teammate can intercept him in his own jungle even when he jungles correctly. [12:41]
- Buy Knockdown in the mid game, a really good item against Seven specifically: +1 stamina, +200 spirit shield health and spirit power, and an active that knocks down a target after a 3 second delay and stuns them, interrupting any action including Seven's ultimate. [13:12]
- For 3,000 souls Knockdown cancels Seven's ultimate; Curse also interrupts it but costs 6,300. [13:43]
- Seven's only answer to Knockdown is Unstoppable, because he cannot use an item such as Debuff Remover during his ultimate; Unstoppable costs 6,300, so you spend 3,000 to force him to spend 6,300. [14:13]
- Jungle creeps give unsecured souls, which drop on the ground if you die with them; killing Seven after he clears camps means he effectively farmed the jungle for you, and he has killed Sevens carrying 1,000+ soul bags. [14:44]
- Always look for opportunities to kill Seven in his own jungle: it stops his jungling and you get his unsecured souls. [15:14]
- Team fights against Seven are simple: if he ults, run away; if you have Knockdown, use it on him when he ults. [15:44]
- Buy Debuff Remover to counter his stun if you have nothing else. [15:44]
- A lot of people find Seven's ultimate really hard to dodge because they do not buy enough movement items. [15:44]
- With only three stamina, escaping his ultimate takes a dash jump plus another dash, using all your stamina bars; with Superior Stamina and Warp Stone you can get out instantly, so his ultimate is not a threat with these items. [16:14]
- Warp Stone mobility and Superior Stamina are good on almost every hero (Grey Talon may need them less as he usually stands far back). [16:44]
- Reaction drill (shown on Grey Talon, a hero with limited mobility): when Seven uses Majestic Leap and ults near you, cast Knockdown immediately, jump dash away, and Warp Stone if needed; this cancels his ult with very little damage taken, and works on any hero. [17:46]
- In the late game against a farmed Seven, buy items that remove him from the game or greatly reduce his ability to contribute. [18:48]
- Phantom Strike (Vitality slot) disarms Seven for a period of time and is a movement slow, so you can catch and disarm him, but it does not deal with his ultimate. [18:48]
- Curse interrupts his ultimate like Knockdown and also silences, disarms and prevents item usage; if he uses Majestic Leap, throw Curse and if it hits before he casts his ultimate or Unstoppable, he cannot ult. [19:19]
- Moving away from his ult is usually the safest thing; a late game Seven with a lot of spirit items does a lot of damage in its range, so have Warp Stone and Superior Stamina and position so you can run. [19:50]
- If none of that is possible, buy Improved Spirit Armor: it counters most of Seven's damage, which is pretty much all spirit, and there are very few ways to reduce spirit armor (Escalating Exposure and Crippling Headshot, as far as he knows). [20:20]
- Buy anti-heal: Seven's ult feels oppressive because he buys spirit lifesteal, Enduring Spirit and the like for 30 to 40% spirit lifesteal while ulting; Decay, Healbane or Toxic Bullets give him 80 to 90% reduced healing and make him much more killable during his ult. [20:50]
- He would not recommend fighting Seven during his ultimate, but if you have to, anti-heal is the best course of action. [21:20]
- Seven is not a completely useless character but does not feel very impactful compared to other kits; patches come every two weeks, so his power may change (he expects Seven might be buffed), though the ways to play and items against him probably will not. [21:20]
