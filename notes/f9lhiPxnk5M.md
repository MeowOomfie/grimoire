## Covers
A mostly unedited stream reading of every hero change in the 3/6/26 patch, hero by hero, with the coach's reaction, quick sandbox tests and a verdict on who got stronger or weaker.
Also touches the new Golden Goose Egg item and the Life Strike nerf. For players adjusting builds and hero picks right after the 3/6/26 patch.
