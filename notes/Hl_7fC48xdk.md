# 5 Takeaways from the New Patch(es) + TIER LIST
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-12-19
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hl_7fC48xdk

## Covers
Meta takeaways from the three updates in the week of 2025-12-19: the vitality (green) investment change to flat HP, why rushing greens is not meta, bigger solo and duo kill bounties and why this is a roaming meta not a jungle meta, McGinnis and Mina as outliers, patron and shrine changes, and the buffed Urn.
Also points to the coach's updated tier list (Haze, Mina, The Doorman named). For ranked players adjusting to the patch.

## Claims
- The green (vitality) investment category now gives flat HP instead of percent HP, which favors heroes with low base HP and low HP per boon (Vindicta, 29 HP per boon, is buffed) and nerfs heroes with the highest base HP and HP per boon (Mo & Krill). [1:02]
- The flat HP change evens the field between tanky bruisers, which were really strong last patch (Abrams, Billy), and squishier damage dealers; the coach thinks it is a good change. [1:33]
- Rushing greens is a lot better than before but far from meta, despite videos saying otherwise. [2:04]
- Myth debunked: Extra Health does not add about 100 HP of healing from the healing trooper; tested at 1 HP the extra healing was about 45 to 50 (295 base heal), and at half HP it is only about 20 to 25 more. [2:34]
- Extra Health is a fantastic item worth buying in certain lanes, but it will not become meta because of the healing trooper; if that worked it would have been meta last patch. [3:05]
- Rushing greens means you do not output damage; Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem give offense only above their HP threshold, and in lane, especially an aggressive one, you are often below it. [3:35]
- Rebuttal is one of the better aggressive green items because hitting a parry gives crazy value, but 1,600 soul greens have little aggressive potential, which is as it should be. [4:06]
- Kill bounties were hugely buffed: solo kills (one attacker) give 25% more souls and duo kills 15% more, and every lane kill is a solo or duo kill, so this heavily incentivizes killing people in lane. [4:06]
- Never play the lane just to survive; play to win because that is how you improve and how you win. Turtle only in lanes where you have no aggressive potential. [5:08]
- Before, rushing greens was not an option because percent HP on low early base health gave almost nothing; now it is one option among many, for a lane where you really cannot interact, but never default to it or treat it as meta. [5:38]
- Lane troopers are worth 12% less, and the jungle is less healthy and easier to take in the first 15 minutes; both incentivize jungling. [6:08]
- Kills are incentivized more than jungling was buffed: a solo kill on a jungler gives more souls than before plus the unsecured souls, which also take longer on average to become secured, so solo killing junglers is huge value. [6:38]
- The coach rates Haze probably S tier, at least A tier; in one Haze game he got 13,000 souls from kills by 30 minutes with 16 kills, and a Mina game showed about 10,000 souls from kills. [7:09]
- Previously an AFK jungler (example Ivy) often ended up richer than the kill-getter; now the hero who gets kills usually becomes the richest. [7:39]
- Heroes with a reasonable chance to solo kill (Lash, Haze, Calico, Mina) are buffed by the bounty change, and gankers that join two-man kills (Paradox, Holliday) are hugely buffed. [7:39]
- In the highest rank game the net worth was much more even than before; this is not a jungle meta, roaming is very heavily incentivized, even in the laning stage, though three-man kills pay a bit worse than two-man kills. [8:42]
- McGinnis is a big winner: her heal is insane, especially for base pushes, which matter more now that pushing the base is harder; her percent HP heal gains value with squishies having more early HP (one 47 minute game had over 90,000 healing). [9:13]
- Mina is probably S+ tier, mainly because every hero's stamina regen was buffed and she already had the fastest: 3.64 seconds base versus 4.55 for Lady Geist. [10:15]
- With Stamina Mastery Mina's stamina cooldown drops to about 3 seconds, effectively infinite stamina, so even Slowing Hex or a silence on her often fails to kill her. [10:46]
- Shrines are tankier and the 30% HP big troopers appear, but this does not change the meta much; shrines still die easily and the patron still dies extremely easily. [11:49]
- Because a winning team's waves are always pushing, it misses trooper waves on the enemy side and earns fewer souls, so the winning team is on a timer to close; in exchange it gets wave pressure every wave. (Credited to Lightbringer.) [11:49]
- Patron second phase in the pit: as a default, assume you must kill the enemy team to win; the patron is invulnerable while two enemy players are there, so do not try to ignore the enemies and burst the patron like before. [12:49]
- The coach moved The Doorman up to S+ tier because of the Urn. [13:20]
- The Urn was buffed significantly: the base Urn gives 600 extra souls and the comeback Urn was also buffed by an unknown amount; huge soul gaps are being created through the Urn, so always do it. [13:51]
- Viscous and The Doorman are the two best heroes at running and contesting the Urn and gained a huge amount of value this patch. [13:51]
