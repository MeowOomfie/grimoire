# Melee in Deadlock: Everything you need to know
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-09-15
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3LfEIC6clg

## Covers
A short 2024 explainer on melee: light versus heavy melee, parry timing and rewards, dash versus parry, parry mind games, and melee for last hits in lane. For any player who wants to melee or deal with melee heroes like Abrams. This is from September 2024; melee and parry numbers have changed since.

## Claims
- There are two melee attacks: light melee, and heavy melee, which is charged. [0:00]
- You can land about two light melees in the time it takes to charge one heavy, so light melee technically has higher DPS if you ignore other factors. [0:00]
- Heavy melee lets you travel with it and does more damage per punch, which is why people use it more, but both get used. [0:32]
- Light melee's main advantage is that it comes out almost instantly and the opponent can't react to it; you must be right beside them, which makes it predictable. [0:32]
- Light melee's disadvantages: less damage per hit and no travel. [1:02]
- Heavy melee's biggest advantage is travel: you can charge it around a corner and strafe with it. [1:02]
- Heavy melee's biggest disadvantage is that it is extremely predictable, so people have time to react and parry. [1:02]
- Parry is on F by default and counters any melee attack. [1:34]
- A successful parry does not go on cooldown, so you can parry repeatedly. [1:34]
- A parried player is stunned and takes bonus damage while stunned. [1:34]
- After a parry you can land two charged heavy melees before the opponent can act, usually around 300 damage with the parry damage amp, so a parry is usually a guaranteed kill in lane. [1:34]
- A missed parry goes on a 5 second cooldown. [2:04]
- Dashes have some lag: dashing away at the last second from a heavy melee often still gets you hit, so against a heavy melee you are always better off parrying than doing anything else, especially since a parry gives you two heavy melees back. [2:04]
- Practice parrying in the training range area where a bot repeatedly heavy melees you. [2:36]
- Parry drill: try parrying early and as late as possible, and try it with your eyes closed going off sound. [3:07]
- Good players bait out parries: heavy melee toward someone, then turn around. [3:38]
- Another bait: charge a heavy melee and cancel it with an ability (for example Abrams's Siphon Life); when they parry, their parry is on cooldown for 5 seconds and you can punch them as much as you want. [3:38]
- When parrying, hold it until the very last moment before the hit lands so they can't play mind games; parry too early and they turn around and punch you repeatedly while your parry is on cooldown. [4:08]
- When you chase a very low target and get close for a light melee finish, expect them to parry, especially when parrying is their only chance to escape. [4:39]
- If you are already winning the fight, don't go for melees; usually the only way to lose that engagement is by meleeing and getting parried. [4:39]
- Example: you have 500 health chasing a 100 health Infernus, you dash in and punch, he parries, double heavy melees you, Flame Dashes away, heals, and you are dead. [5:10]
- Only melee when it is unexpected; when you dash into someone they often expect a melee and parry, so dash in and shoot instead to bait the parry. [5:10]
- Against a light melee the opponent can't react in time and has to guess, so they will eventually have to parry to survive; once they have parried, go for the melee. [5:41]
- Nearly all melee mind games come down to baiting out the parry, either heavy melee then turn, or getting very close without meleeing to see if they parry. [5:41]
- At a high level melee is not all that good, though situationally it is very good. [5:41]
- Melee is very good against unsuspecting opponents because it does a lot of damage, especially around corners; punching around corners is much harder to react to. [6:11]
- The current lane meta is to secure as many last hits as you can with heavy melees. [6:11]
- If one of your creeps is hurt and the opponent is going to melee it, dash in front of it and parry; this gets easy early kills, especially against unsuspecting opponents. [6:11]
- Sometimes you will dash and parry with no one meleeing, take a lot of damage and have parry on cooldown; do it anyway, because making these mistakes is how you learn. [6:41]
