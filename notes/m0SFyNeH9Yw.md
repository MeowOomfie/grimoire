## Covers
Full VOD review of a Wraith player newer to the hyper carry role, on an Asia server, who later reached Eternus 6. Lane trades vs Grey Talon and Vyper with The Doorman, securing every soul, converting a won lane into kills, blue lane against Pocket, carry macro with a big bag (invading, split pushing, when to stop side laning), calling plays for the team and a decision flowchart for after clearing a wave.
For Wraith and other farming carries who play too safe once they are ahead.
