# Mirage is Secretly a Raid Boss
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-06-17
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKZLpYZuFBg

## Covers
Mirage, aquatiq, commentary over a pub game on "Mark Mirage" (the Djinn's Mark max build), laning with a Lash.
Lane sustain from Fire Scarabs, skill order, items considered mid game, and his verdict that the Mark build is still very strong.
Patch around 2026-06-17, right after a Holliday buff.

## Claims
- Mark Mirage means maxing Djinn's Mark; he calls it still good and wonders why he ever plays anything else. [9:24]
- Fire Scarabs heal you off the enemy, so in a good lane you are almost never low HP. [1:35]
- The lane gets much better once Djinn's Mark is online. [1:35]
- Mirage has very little wave shove, so he needs someone else to shove. [2:07]
- He wanted to grab the breakable boxes once they spawn. [2:39]
- He had Djinn's Mark maxed around 10 minutes. [9:58]
- Mirage cannot leave a fight easily when out of stamina. [8:21]
- Losing a walker at 12 minutes is bad; walkers usually fall around 15, and nobody should lose one that early. [12:05]
- He thought about buying Suppressor. [12:05]
- For a long time he ran another build (captioned "the Basil build"): it still did a lot of damage but he never got kills and felt he had no impact; the Mark build fixes that. [13:36]
- After getting hit by Healbane, Debuff Remover is a must in that game. [15:09]
- Disarming Hex does not do much against him once he has marked someone: he presses 3, hits his Fire Scarabs and they die. [19:19]
- He maxes Tornado over the ult; he does not care much about the teleport. [19:19]
- He put a point in the ult by habit and did not want to. [19:51]
- Plan from then on: max the ult last. [25:41]
- Against fast-moving enemies Fire Scarabs are hard to land. [17:45]
- In big fights with many targets he wanted Ricochet so his damage spreads while he focuses one target. [20:54]
- He planned Escalating Exposure next. [21:25]
- Marked enemies keep bleeding, so once you land Fire Scarabs they just die. [23:01]
- Considered Mercurial Magnum because it resets his ammo, and wanted it on his 3 (Djinn's Mark). [24:40]
- Inhibitor is really good in that game, but he went Mystic Reverb instead. [25:11]
- The Mark does so much damage that you should take advantage of it until Valve nerfs it. [16:43]
