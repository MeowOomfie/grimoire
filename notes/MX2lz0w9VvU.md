# This is the ONLY Infernus Build You Need
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-17
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MX2lz0w9VvU

## Covers
Infernus afterburn build, aquatiq, commentary over one game laning against an early Rusted Barrel Dynamo.
Item counters, farming and why he dislikes dash Infernus. Patch of March 2026.

## Claims
- He used Lightbringer's build, a normal afterburn build. [1:08]
- Infernus is not as strong as he was but is still very strong; his afterburn is not great now. [1:08]
- Get Titanic Magazine on Infernus; his magazine is so low that missing shots means no burn. [1:39]
- Titanic Magazine is needed, you do not burn people without it. [17:54]
- Against an early Rusted Barrel he considered Rusted Barrel back or Debuff Reducer, and bought Extra Regen. [4:46]
- Dash Infernus is not good: enemies jump over the dash and run away; Warp Stone does not fix that. [7:35]
- Flame Dash is not good on Infernus; he can only really do afterburn. [26:09]
- Infernus has one of the best farms in the game: afterburn plus Flame Dash can hit two camps at once. [8:37]
- Geist (because of her bomb) and Wraith also have really good farm. [8:37]
- Duration Extender for his 3 is a must. [9:07]
- Goal: reach your first 10k souls. [9:07]
- Losing one walker while taking two of theirs is a trade worth taking. [11:13]
- Slowing Bullets was bought mid game, later sold for Unstoppable. [11:13]
- Golden Goose Egg in the build is bad on Infernus; you do not want to reduce his already low damage. [13:17]
- Against several gun heroes, buy Escalating Resilience, then Escalating Exposure. [15:19]
- Escalating Exposure is the more important power spike for Infernus. [24:06]
- Debuff Remover lets you remove Disarming Hexes. [23:04]
- Take the enemy's camps and money when you can. [24:06]
- Kill the mid boss fast; once the enemy has no rejuvenators left you are not going to lose. [21:33]
- Against a team of heavy abilities he should have bought Counterspell instead of his dispel item. [25:08]
- Unstoppable should be bought from the start; he swapped Slowing Bullets for it. [26:39]
- Pop Unstoppable when you ult so the enemy cannot interrupt you. [28:46]
- Once he had Unstoppable he never needed Counterspell. [31:21]
- Infernus is very fun to play but bad right now. [32:22]
