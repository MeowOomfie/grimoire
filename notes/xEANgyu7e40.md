## Covers
Quick guide to item counters for things new players find overpowered: mobile heroes, flyers, debuffs, heavy healing and burst damage.
moe9times, educational guide, no one coached. Patch of late October 2024.

## Claims
- Almost everything in the game has one or more counters; if you lost to a "broken" hero, you probably did not know what to buy. [0:00]
- Against very mobile heroes who move with spells (Pocket, Lash), buy Silence Glyph and Silencer to stop them pressing buttons; then they only have their normal dashes. [0:31]
- Silencer is also great against heroes who cast spells near death; it stops Geist and Yamato from getting their ultimates off. [1:01]
- Against flyers (Grey Talon, Vindicta), buy Knockdown to bring them back to the ground after two seconds. [1:01]
- Knockdown also counters long channeled ultimates (Seven, Bebop, Haze): cast it just before their ult and they only get a tiny duration before it is canceled. [1:31]
- Against debuffs you have to wait out (Pocket's Affliction, Bebop's bombs, Seven's stun with Shiv's bleed), buy Debuff Remover. [1:31]
- Debuff Remover immediately removes things like Pocket's poison, Infernus's burn, Vindicta's trap and Shiv's bleed. [2:01]
- Against heroes who out-heal you (Shiv, Abrams), buy anti-heal: Healbane and Decay are cheap tier 2 items with large healing reduction. [2:32]
- Decay is great for shutting down a single target and also does 30% of the target's HP. [2:32]
- Healbane is great if you reliably deal spirit damage; with AOE spirit damage its healing reduction lands on everyone you hit. [2:32]
- Toxic Bullets reduces enemy healing by 65% for as long as you keep shooting them; good to weave into gun builds, but it needs shots to build up before it procs. [2:32]
- Against heavy burst (Wraith's lift, a Haze chasing you), Ethereal Shift makes you untargetable for 3 seconds, enough to dodge Wraith's lift entirely. [3:02]
- Warp Stone lets you get out of range quickly and gives a good chunk of bullet resist. [3:02]
- A cheap Extra Stamina to dodge away is also a valid option against burst. [3:33]
- Nothing in the game is ridiculously overpowered; there is always an option for every situation. [3:33]
