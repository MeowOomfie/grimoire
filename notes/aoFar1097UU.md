# The Problem Every Deadlock Player Has
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-02-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoFar1097UU

## Covers
Coaching review on players being chained to their lane and tower: when you cannot kill your laner, steal their camp or gank another lane instead.
Covers gank signals (enemies pushed up and around half HP), early tower safety before 4 minutes, and examples on Drifter and Rem. For low and mid rank players who stay in lane for 10 to 15 minutes.

## Claims
- Most players are chained to their towers and do not look at the bigger picture of the game. [0:00]
- In the first two minutes, generally all that matters is playing for wave pressure and getting an initial advantage. [1:00]
- A common mistake is tunnel visioning on keeping pressure on a laner you have an early advantage on; a hurt Haze can simply buy healing, skip the lane for 20 seconds and be healthy again. [1:00]
- Haze buying Extra Regen is a decent buy; if she plays passive at her tower she is very hard to kill, because diving this early is very difficult. [1:30]
- If you cannot kill someone, your next options are to steal their resources or to gank. [1:30]
- Example: instead of keeping pressure on a passive Haze, move to the enemy small camp so you are there when it spawns, take it instantly and secure a small lead. [2:03]
- A Drifter hitting Stalker's Mark on the Haze and parrying the tower might kill her, but that is a high risk play he would not necessarily make. [2:03]
- Good gank indicators: the enemy laners are pushed up at their tower, and their health is around half HP. [3:05]
- At about half HP enemies are very killable: one or two Drifter abilities do most of the damage, then a Shiv knife or dash, or Graves shooting or using Jar of the Dead, finishes them. [3:35]
- Before 4 minutes your tower is almost immortal; very few heroes, if any, can take a tower before 4 minutes as long as someone defends it. [4:06]
- McGinnis and Rem are the biggest exceptions for early tower pressure; Rem applies a lot of tower pressure early with his third ability. [4:06]
- Haze and Sinclair are probably not able to take a tower before 4 minutes as long as anybody defends it; leaving it with no defender is bad. [4:06]
- Only one laner should leave to gank; both players cannot leave. [4:06]
- You do not need to be a ganker hero to gank; almost any hero, including Rem, can do it. [4:37]
- Rem example: after clearing the mid wave and judging the tower would not fall, he went to a lane where both enemies were about half HP. [4:37]
- Read enemy HP at the top of the screen when health bars are bugged and show the wrong max HP. [4:37]
- More signals the gank is good: your own team is healthy and no ultimates are in play. [5:07]
- Parrying the tower during a dive can be enough to start it; teammates sometimes just need a little kick to be more aggressive. [5:07]
- Rem and McGinnis are two of the best heroes in the game at taking towers, so a won gank with them can turn straight into a tower. [5:38]
- Always clear the wave before leaving to gank. [5:38]
- You can rotate mid without a super obvious signal when you lose nothing by going, since you can come back to your lane in time if the gank fails. [6:09]
- After the gank, return to your lane if you are not a hero like Rem who can take the tower. [6:40]
- When an enemy follows your rotation, their lane partner is left alone, which can open a 2v1 for your lane. [6:40]
- At lower ranks people do not match your rotation, so they foolishly push your tower, and if your gank is early and quick they get little tower damage, which makes ganking a powerful option. [7:11]
- There are always many options: gank, farm boxes, do a camp; leave your lane once in a while to experiment with ganks instead of staying there for 10 minutes. [7:42]
- Losing a tower is not the end of the world, especially if you trade it for another tower elsewhere. [8:12]
- Creating chaos very often benefits the person creating it more than the person responding to it. [8:12]
