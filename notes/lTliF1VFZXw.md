# How Saint Controlled The Map |  Support Sinclair Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-06-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lTliF1VFZXw

## Covers
Sinclair, Midknight. Analysis of Saint's support Sinclair in a DLNS money match (Buff Enjoyers vs Abrams), who opens with a gun build and pivots to support.
Covers camp angles, Spectral Assistant tech, rotations before the Urn, wave cutting, stealing ultimates and how solo queue Sinclairs should pick a style. Recorded around the 2026-06-27 patch.

## Claims
Sinclair:
- Heroes with throwable abilities that do a lot of damage or apply multiple times should take an angle and throw them into a camp from range instead of fighting it face to face; Sinclair can clear the tier one with two bolts and a couple of follow-up autos. [0:30]
- A good Viscous can do the same with his spider and one shot a camp from range; Holliday's barrel will not one shot it. [1:02]
- Rush Opening Rounds on Sinclair: the bullet velocity fixes his worst weakness and the +7 spirit power makes his bullets stronger. [1:39]
- Sinclair does a lot of gun damage early off Spectral Assistant, and with high velocity his gun is surprisingly good secondary DPS. [2:10]
- Clear waves as fast as possible so the enemy gets little siege. [2:41]
- Monster Rounds is a core item on Sinclair because it gives him the wave clear he really needs. [3:12]
- Buff contest tech: place Spectral Assistant so you can get on top of the buff, melee immediately so they cannot parry, then use the second recast to teleport out. [3:42]
- Farming the tier twos off spawn is extremely important for farm; lower-elo players fight instead of taking it. [4:13]
- Holding the bridge in lane is very strong on Sinclair: press Assistant as the wave comes close to one shot it, and throw bolts at enemies standing on the stairs. [6:17]
- Taking an enemy ultimate with Audience Participation is fine when it is free damage, even if it is not the ideal ult. [6:48]
- Saint commits to gun after opening; the most common solo queue build pivots to spirit after Opening Rounds, and some Sinclairs disagree on which is right. [7:18]
- Midknight is not a big Long Range fan on Sinclair: it gives harass but no farming ability, which gun items should give. [7:48]
- Restorative Shot over Headshot Booster at that point: headshots are easy with high velocity, but Headshot Booster is flat damage you have outscaled, while Restorative Shot gives sustain and 6% weapon damage. [8:19]
- Going gun early makes sense when your lane opponents have gun-based ults to steal. [8:49]
- Chip enemies with bolts right before the Urn spawns: they cannot reset, so the damage is meaningful even if it is greedy. [10:22]
- Engage with Spectral Assistant left behind you as an exit; when a trade is short or you cannot keep chasing, press 2 to teleport far back, which keeps you safe from overchasing. [16:04]
- Solo queue Sinclairs are too afraid to steal gun ults; steal them even with some spirit damage. [17:37]
- Greater Expansion on Rabbit Hex is huge when the fight is going to be mid. [19:10]
- As a support, sometimes you are the one forced to catch the mid wave and play janitor, which can make you late to the Urn fight. [23:49]
- Shoot the walker while throwing bolts for extra damage; you can time it without losing an auto and gain a free ~100 damage. [24:19]
- Echo Shard is a natural buy for the bunny. [25:21]
- Solo queue advice: decide your Sinclair style in the lobby. Need a support (for example against a Vindicta): support Sinclair with bunny max. Team lacks spirit burst or the enemy has a big spirit ult to steal: damage Sinclair, maybe early Majestic Leap. Enemy only has gun ults: gun Sinclair. [28:43]
- In a losing base fight with Refresher available, refresh for a Hail Mary; sell a useless economy item like Golden Goose Egg for a tier three before a base-ending fight. [29:43]

Other heroes and macro:
- Decay rush on Viscous is very strong into Abrams: it keeps him around 80% HP and the anti-heal denies his healing on tight engages. [4:44]
- At high level Viscous's punch is used as utility to force a parry, which griefs the target's movement, like baiting a parry with a melee. [5:14]
- Viscous's ult now gives only 35% resist, so it is more a rotation tool than anything, and good players melee cancel it with a heavy melee. [6:48]
- If giving up the Urn, play for the enemy mid tower instead. [10:54]
- Cut the enemy's waves before the Urn and then rotate to it, so if they commit to the Urn they cannot siege your guardian; this is common at high level and rare in solo queue. [11:55]
- Contesting from bad map positioning just because you are already there is sunk cost fallacy. [13:29]
- Seven rushing Escalating Exposure means playing purely for his ultimate, so Unstoppable is the natural follow-up. [16:37]
- Escalating Exposure is the only spirit item that does much against resist. [28:11]
- Hunter's Aura rush means the hero is looking for 1v1s. [16:37]
- Duo queue with voice comms with your lane partner is very strong, and he thinks frequent duos should get an MMR boost. [17:37]
- Break off a fight when your team is low and the enemy starts responding. [21:14]
- An enemy shrine falling is very impactful: guaranteed prio for two waves and -15% souls in both lanes for the team that lost it. [24:49]
- Back off after cracking base in case the enemy looks to take your shrine. [24:49]
- Do not waste dead time hovering; use it for a box route or something useful. [25:56]
- Baiting with one hero in view while the rest wait to engage works when the enemy has no map information. [26:57]
