# Biggest Winners and Losers of the recent patch - Patch Highlights
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-10-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9aK1ZB_VEmI

## Covers
A 2024 patch review (the game has changed a lot since) of the late October 2024 patch that added six experimental heroes to Hero Labs. Winners: Grey Talon, Vindicta, Paradox; losers: Shiv and Lady Geist. Lists each change with the coach's verdict, plus items to buy against Grey Talon.

## Claims
- The patch added six experimental heroes playable in Hero Labs. [0:00]
- Grey Talon is the biggest winner despite only four lines of patch notes. [0:00]
- Grey Talon's base regen went from 2 to 2.5. [0:00]
- Grey Talon's gun spirit scaling went from 0.1 to 0.3, so buying spirit adds much more damage: at level one with Boundless Spirit his arrows hit about 105 on a headshot. [0:00] [0:32]
- Grey Talon's movement speed scales with spirit again; with a stack of spirit items plus Enduring Speed he reached about 17 m/s. [0:32]
- Grey Talon's air strafe speed while using stamina was increased, so he covers a lot of distance in the air, especially with Superior Stamina for more air dash distance. [0:32] [1:03]
- Expect a Grey Talon meta, especially at lower MMR where he will be a menace; at higher ranks he is still squishy and easy to jump on, but viable and probably still good. [1:03]
- Against Grey Talon buy Knockdown to knock him out of the sky. [1:35]
- Against Grey Talon consider Improved Spirit Armor, since the popular builds do a lot of spirit damage. [1:35]
- Against Grey Talon buy Slowing Hex to stop him going into the air and escaping when caught on the ground, though it may not be enough if he already has a lot of movement speed. [1:35] [2:06]
- Vindicta's gun cycle time was improved, which he believes gives slightly more DPS since the notes do not say "DPS unchanged". [2:06]
- Vindicta's bullet damage growth per boon was increased, giving more left-click DPS at max level. [2:06] [2:36]
- Vindicta's fire rate now scales with spirit power, so with just Boundless Spirit she already shoots fairly fast. [2:36]
- Vindicta's Assassinate no longer splits souls to allies: a buff to the selfish play style, but stacking souls on one hero means giving more souls to the enemy through comeback mechanics if she dies. [2:36] [3:06]
- Vindicta's base flight duration went from 8 to 12 seconds and the tier two upgrade from 6 to 8 seconds, so with both upgrades flight goes from 14 to 20 seconds. [3:06]
- Assassinate bullet radius went from 3 to 4 inches, so near misses still hit. [3:36]
- Vindicta can now zoom in; he would probably never use it. [3:36]
- Vindicta is stronger overall but keeps a sniper's weaknesses and is very weak to Grey Talon, so she may struggle if Grey Talon is very meta. [3:36] [4:07]
- Paradox was buffed even though she was already first pick or first ban in many tournaments and very strong in Eternus ranked, played mainly for her wall, Kinetic Carbine and swap. [4:07] [4:38]
- Paradox's Pulse Grenade pulses are now expressed as duration, so Extended Duration gives a very long grenade with many pulses. [4:38]
- A maxed Pulse Grenade hitting every pulse on one target does about 800 damage. [5:08]
- Each pulse stack gives 6% damage amp; hitting 7 pulses gives 42% damage amp, more than Pocket gets. [5:08]
- Kinetic Carbine now deals more damage on headshots than body shots (68 vs 57), a slight buff. [5:39]
- Kinetic Carbine can no longer miss against evasive targets, so an evading Mirage is hit guaranteed. [5:39]
- His verdict: Paradox did not need buffs; grenade builds with Echo Shard look good. [5:39]
- Shiv is the biggest loser: almost everything was nerfed and nothing buffed. [6:09]
- Shiv's knife collision box was reduced, though it is still pretty big. [6:09] [6:39]
- Bloodletting's base cooldown went from 21 to 50 seconds, so he can activate it only about once per fight to clear damage; even the improved tier two upgrade leaves it higher than the old base. [6:39] [7:09]
- Bloodletting's base duration went from 10 to 13 seconds, but base damage deferred is now 22% instead of 30%, and you need one point to get 30%. [7:09]
- Most Shiv builds should put at least one point into Bloodletting fairly early. [7:09] [7:40]
- Shiv's ultimate killing blow range is now only 13 m, so you must be much closer to execute. [7:40]
- Rage buildup from heavy melees was nerfed a lot, hurting melee Shiv; heavy melees on heroes still build a lot of rage, but on troopers and neutrals very little. [7:40] [8:11]
- Rage buildup against creeps was reduced 50%, a huge nerf to Shiv's farming: it takes about 20 heavy melees on creeps to reach max rage, so you must shoot heroes to build rage. [8:11] [8:41]
- Shiv's rage now starts decaying after only 9 seconds, so shoot a target every 9 seconds to stay at full rage. [8:41]
- His verdict: Shiv is still okay, especially at lower ranks, gun Shiv may be back, and melee Shiv stays strong from damage amp and tankiness; his melee build needs no change, just a different play style and harder farming. [8:41] [9:11]
- Lady Geist is also a loser: base Malice is now only 8% damage amp, so you need two points to reach 15%, which matters in lane because many players max the bomb first. [9:41]
- Malice's slow was nerfed from 20% to 15% and its debuff duration from 13 to 9 seconds, making it harder to keep Malice stacks on people in lane. [10:11]
- Mystic Reverb now applies its slow only to the primary target, not in the AOE, which probably kills the Mystic Reverb Malice build. [10:41]
- His verdict: Lady Geist is still very strong and oppressive in lane, just nerfed. [9:41] [10:41]

## Disagrees with
