## Covers
Lash coaching by Midknight: one Oracle-rank Lash player reviewed over a full game, patch of 2026-09-23.
Themes: Ground Strike on the wave instead of sitting on height, punishing wasted parries, where to slam people, split pushing against a deathballing enemy, picking good fights and rifts, mid boss and pit play into Dynamo, and common Wraith (M1) mistakes in the lobby.

## Claims
Laning (Lash, Oracle):
- You are not punishing enemies for wasting their parries, which is the recurring lane problem. [0:30]
- The right way for the enemy to play into you is to walk up and be ready to double jump your Ground Strike; when they hide instead, your Wraith gets full wave priority. [1:31]
- You need to Flog sooner, it would have mattered a lot for her not being able to outpace the tower. [2:01]
- Look for denies; you are right not to hit the wave, but there are lots of orbs floating up you are not taking. [2:31]
- You are playing height too much. Jumping on height is great at first, but once they know or see you are up there, just Ground Strike the wave. [3:36]
- Ground Strike has an 8 second cooldown and does at least half of two minions' HP; if you don't use it, you leave your Wraith to solo hit the wave. [3:36]
- Knowing buff wave timers would be great, even though he does not expect it at this rank. [4:07]
- Be more conscious when an enemy is staring at you the whole time, it means they are waiting on your move. [4:07]
- A good-aim Haze hit 33 bullets on you while you were in the sky; hanging in the air against good tracking is dangerous. [4:39]
- Going for a box your Wraith already ran is inefficient routing. [5:16]
- When you get lifted, walk to the corner and Ground Strike out. [5:47]
- Ring around the rosie more to dodge the Haze sleep. [6:18]
- On Lash, do not slam people on top of or next to the tower; when they land next to it they just parry instantly. Throw them to the side, out of melee range. [6:18]
- Once you hit your spirit spike you are really strong. [6:48]

Mid game and macro:
- When you siege, preemptively ping that the Haze is missing, since the only reason you get away with it is that she is gone. [8:06]
- Give the bag to your Wraith; the souls are worth much more on her than on you. Greed is fine in solo queue, but she is already fed. [8:36]
- Being fed and pushing your lead by fighting outside the rift is good, but two of you walking right side to fight under their walker left side is too aggressive. [10:28]
- You threw the target too close to the bridge for the Abrams. [10:28]
- Do not fight a 4v2 rift with your team out of position. Even far ahead, you are past the maximum comeback bounty, so keep your ult: once the Shiv dies they all run at you and your 4 can throw them all out while you hold the rift at 99%. [10:28]
- Standing in one spot keeps you in the rift while a step away takes you out; watch your footing on the capture. [10:58]
- Fight the rift if your whole team is there; if they are not, once the enemy contests, just give it up. [11:30]
- Giving up a walker puts the enemy fully back in the game; the Wraith farming instead of pushing mid and the team staggering to defend the walker was a dead fight. [11:30]
- Ground Striking waves is fine when your team is not around. [12:00]
- You can clock a player at minute one who has great aim and no macro (lots of deaths). Do not head-on fight him or he out-micros you; out-macro him and you win. [12:00]
- If an enemy taunts you, just send the commend back; it shows you don't care. [12:30]
- The enemy team is over-deathballing. Tell your Wraith to split, and every time you see one of them, split the other side of the map. [13:01]
- You should have pushed blue much earlier; waiting let the Haze respawn and the fight was over. [14:01]
- Your Wraith not split pushing enough is what hurts against a team that groups like this. [14:01]
- You engaged with really low stamina. A BM war with the Haze makes her overchase you, which is great with teammates nearby, but not with zero stamina and nobody around. [14:31]
- The Haze is probably in a veil; check your veils. [15:02]
- With the Dynamo dead, two picks and a fed Wraith, take mid boss. [15:02]
- Your Wraith just needs to stand at the side of the pit and ult the Dynamo; Dynamo's ult does not matter much into a hero with ranged instant CC like Wraith, even if he has Refresher. Your Wraith jumped in the pit, so you lost. [15:35]
- An M1 should never jump in the pit, especially when it has something that can cancel a Dynamo ult. [16:06]
- Help your Wraith on the rift sooner rather than chasing enemies mid; fighting without your 30k Wraith is really bad. [16:38]
- Your team is letting the Haze get in their heads. [17:52]
- Box farming is fine, but hitting real camps is better; walking to the right side of the map where there is nothing to farm feels bad, unless you are invading the enemy's farm. [18:26]
- You are overplaying your hand for that Haze. [18:59]
- That fight was a forced coin flip into Dynamo with all their ults up. They have good micro but bad macro, so back off and split push instead of fighting while both walker waves crash into you. [19:40]
- You griefed by not trying to ult the Dynamo out of his ult from range; the enemy was just slow to capitalize. [20:41]
- At this elo, do not respect a Haze sleep or other ult counter on your Lash ult until you actually see it; Dynamo 2 is the other counter. Your main goal there is getting the Dynamo out of his ult, and even if you get canceled it is still value. [22:13]
- Against Counterspells you want to out-hold your ult. Jumping out and ulting when they don't know you are there is not telegraphing it. [22:43]
- Someone told him (unverified) that shrine resist shred is glitched when more people are around, which would explain why the second shrine feels immortal; he planned to test it. [23:19]
- Looking for an ult while enemies are over-positioned staggers them further; you won that game almost solely off that ult. [23:57]
- Respect a Dynamo ult in the pit; do not all stand on top of each other. [23:57]
- Do not over-focus the Patron. When enemies jump into the pit, focus the player standing in the pit; you have a big player advantage. [24:30]
