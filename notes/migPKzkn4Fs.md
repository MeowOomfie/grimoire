channel: Midknight

## Covers
Apollo coaching by Midknight: he reviews one game of an Oracle-rank Apollo laning with Yamato against Seven and Paige.
Themes: soul awareness and spending, AP order and maxes, Headshot Booster rush, using the ult as an escape rather than an engage, Metal Skin over Bullet Resilience, mid boss and urn calls, parrying CC, and recommended builds.
Video date 2026-02-14. A few side answers to chat cover Shiv.

## Claims
Apollo tech, lane and build:
- People misuse the ult as an engage or damage tool; one of its biggest strengths is how far it displaces you, so if you mis-position or jump into the enemies it can get you out, and ulting in puts you in a bad spot. [0:00]
- Tech: after using your 1, flick your camera before it expires to change where it sends you; even if you cast in front of you, flicking behind sends you flying with the momentum in the new direction. [0:31]
- Apollo and Yamato is a good lane, two strong laners. Seven and Paige is a bad lane for them: Paige is strong but mostly amplifies her partner, and Seven is a mediocre laner. [0:31]
- Rush Headshot Booster on Apollo: he has no weapon damage falloff, so it always does max damage. [1:01]
- Almost always buy Headshot Booster into Seven and Mo & Krill, whose heads are so big headshots are easy to farm. [1:01]
- Extra Regen is fine on the hero but you don't need to start it. [1:31]
- Early roaming and box routes into ganks are super meta in pro play; in solo queue it is still good in theory but harder below very high elo, because teammates fight and die, skip Monster Rounds or don't anchor correctly. [1:31]
- In a Seven and Infernus lane, Seven roams since Infernus can clear with his dash and Seven has okay setup with his stun. [2:32]
- In an Apollo and Yamato lane, whoever has ult roams since both can roam and anchor, though Apollo only anchors okay. [2:32]
- Point order: he doesn't like the 2 second. Put an early point into the 1 (very low cooldown, 30 extra damage, feels good in lane), then a point into the 2, then max the 3. [2:32]
- Melee the wave instead of using the 1 when you are in their face and nobody is shooting you. [3:02]
- When the enemy sits under tower and you have no kill potential while they clear from far away, go on a box route; before 5 minutes towers don't take much damage anyway. [3:33]
- Letting your souls get very high is only acceptable if you are deliberately saving for a rush of Torment Pulse, which is not meta but very strong. [4:04]
- Stealing the enemy tier one camp is good, but first get wave priority and then call your Yamato over to steal it with you. [4:04]
- Pay more attention to your souls so you aren't sitting on a spike while saving for two tier ones. [4:35]
- If you didn't buy another tier one, you are saving for a tier two, so just buy the tier two (Cold Front) and buy Extra Spirit after. [5:06]
- Ulting into the enemies is a bad ult; the ult is fine when used to get out. [5:36]
- He would buy Mystic Burst before Torment Pulse so you aren't waiting so long for your spike. [6:06]
- Don't buy a tier two when you already have that many souls. [6:06]
- Mystic Expansion on the 3 is good; Compress Cooldown on the 3 is a waste. Utility items scale multiplicatively with how much the ability already does, so buy damage first, then more uptime on a stronger 3. [6:06]
- He puts a point in the 1 and the 2 first (the 1 early, the 2 before maxing the 3); a pure rush of one max is fine but extremely greedy. [7:08]
- Don't take fights while sitting on a tier three spike; if you lose your tower you have to run to the secret shop to buy. [7:38]
- Apollo can parry minions and towers. [7:38]
- The ult does more damage to targets below 50%, so don't lead with it. Its biggest value is the gap close, then the finisher, and dashing through the whole enemy team to do damage and escape is huge. [8:13]
- You don't want to M1 farm, but you still want to hit camps. [8:49]
- Doing both center camps at once is only worth it if you sync up the machines: melee the second one while the first is ticking, then go back, otherwise you save no time. [8:49]
- When you are low on HP with a big bag (1.2k), TP back and buy; even slightly aggressive routing risks losing the bag if anyone sees you. [9:19]
- After lane you shouldn't put more points into the 1; it is only really good in lane as flat damage on a low cooldown. [9:49]
- Solo queue will be an ult-max-second meta; at super high levels and pro play you may see more 1 maxes second, which has a much higher skill ceiling. [9:49]
- Reactive Barrier is solid into a comp with Warden cage, Seven stun, Paige stun and Graves. [10:19]
- Leading with the ult when you shouldn't engage with it means that when you are trapped in the middle of them you have no way out if the fight goes south. [10:50]
- Your waves are really bad while you take these fights. [10:50]
- A 2 max is not bad but worse than his other maxes: extended stun duration matters less because you use the 2 mostly to cancel channeled abilities or for a light poke or melee, not for a long stun like Abrams. [10:50]
- You can light melee off the 2. In theory a maxed 2 allows a heavy melee, but an animation buffer after the 2 prevents it at base; you can only heavy melee off the 2 if it is maxed. [11:22]
- Slow playing, securing centers and hitting waves is good. [11:52]
- With priority on the left side and nothing to do, grab the urn and start running it. [11:52]
- Don't fight while sitting on two tier threes worth of souls and a 700 bag; you are at a weak point, so buy at the secret shop before coming in. [12:24]
- Hitting the walker after picks while sitting on lots of souls is the most common way to throw a lead; you should be running urn. [12:57]
- The single biggest thing holding him back is not spending his souls. [13:28]
- When sitting on a lot of souls, playing safe can be better than walking to the secret shop. [13:28]
- Colossus gives little value and its slow hurts; for a purple tier four take Mystic Reverb on the 3 for lifesteal, Echo Shard on the 3 for uptime, or Boundless Spirit for pure damage, which is probably core. [13:58]
- Apollo is not a tanky bruiser; you play him less like Shiv and more like Mina. [14:28]
- Not rotating into a bad mid fight and catching the side wave instead was perfect. [14:59]
- He would have put two points into the 1 before maxing the ult. [14:59]
- Apollo is very strong but will have a low win rate because he is hard. [14:59]
- In scrims he would tell his team to max the 1; for a general solo queue player he says max the ult, since most solo queue players won't use the 1 well. [15:31]
- Itemization was way too defensive; more purples would feel better, and Boundless Spirit over Colossus. [16:01]
- Metal Skin is much better than Bullet Resilience right now because Armor Piercing Rounds fully counters Bullet Resilience, and Apollo's HP pool is too small for percent resistances to do much. [16:32]
- Slide after every dash; he dashed twice without sliding. [16:32]
- Disarming Hex if you are a support; Metal Skin if you are a carry, aggressive or just squishy. [17:02]
- Counterspell feels good in this game since there is a lot to counter; doubling up Counterspell with his parry is not bad at all. [18:38]
- Knowing you can shoot Venator's traps to clear them is huge. [18:38]
- You can parry Drifter, Paige root, Seven stun and Warden cage, and a good player can parry or Counterspell Silver's bola. [19:08]
- With four of you sieging into three, go mid boss instead; a better split is four on mid boss, one catching the wave and one walking the urn over, since Seven ult and Drifter are very good anti-siege ults. [19:38]
- Nobody caught the side wave, so they took your walker. [20:42]
- Pick up the urn after clearing. [20:42]
- Until around Ascendant, people don't think about mid boss as a feature. [20:42]
- Boundless Spirit came a little late, but better late than never. [21:12]
- With Rejuvenator on your team it is a good fight, so rotate in. [21:12]
- Parrying or Counterspelling Drifter's pounce is easy once you lock in; he learned to do it on instinct after about 10 Street Brawl games against Drifter. [21:12]
- He tunnel visioned and chased way too hard into their spawn; a huge overchase that luckily went unpunished. [21:43]
- Use the vent on the way to the shrine for a much faster rotation; it is worth it even if you get vent-stunned. [22:14]
- You can parry the Patron's curse (like Counterspelling a walker stomp), though he heard it is bugged. [22:44]
- Build: his own Apollo build with Counterspell added and spirit shred swapped in because you need shred. [23:45]
- He also recommends Lightbringer builds: buy items worth 4.8k in the spike categories, prefer the items on the left of each category, and greed an extra tier three in the most useful category; Lightbringer now includes Opening Rounds. [24:15]
- Once very fed, add defensive items like Counterspell before moving to the later categories. [24:45]

Side answers on Shiv:
- Shiv has a low win rate because solo queue players play him as a brawler who runs in; he should farm rage and then execute with the ult, which cuts about 28% of HP (25% plus an invisible 3% buffer). [17:33]
- Decay is a crutch on Shiv, not needed if you hit your knives, but nice if you don't. [18:38]
- Shiv's rage is based on damage. [19:38]
