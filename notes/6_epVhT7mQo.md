## Covers
Deadman Danny, aquatiq channel, his second game on the hero.
Commentary over a game: overheat and flight, lane plan against Vindicta and Shiv, items, and a verdict that the hero is weak even after buffs.
Video dated 2026-10-18.

## Claims
- Danny has an overheat: overheating costs you a lot of your own health. [0:03]
- He has a charge up, flight and a lot of other things, but is very weak. [0:33]
- He used Zukee's build (as captioned) from a Danny video. [0:33]
- Danny is very bad early; his late game is not too bad. [1:04]
- Play super passive in lane. [1:04]
- He needs Extra Health and Extra Regen in lane. [1:36]
- Recent buffs: health, regen, gun damage on the charged and single shots, and the ult's finish (execute), which should make the ult much better. [2:08]
- You cannot play forward in a lane against Vindicta. [2:40]
- His gun still feels bad even after the buffs. [3:10]
- His 3 puts marks on people; the marks do extra damage and scale a little with spirit. [3:42]
- His ult is basically an execute. [3:42]
- He takes damage from his own abilities and Shiv knives; Vindicta hard counters Danny. [5:16]
- Many players go 0 and 10 on him; if you just stay alive and don't die, you are fine. [5:16]
- The fly button is space, the same button as getting on the zipline, which is annoying. [5:16]
- Do not build gun on him. [5:47]
- His farm is terrible. [5:47]
- Express Shot is really good because it basically doubles up his shot. [6:19]
- People say the big heavy crates are broken; he disagrees, they are very valuable but not broken. [6:19]
- He bought Toxic Bullets to start procing, and Reactive Barrier (captioned "reactive") is a necessity. [9:23]
- Consider putting the ult on instant cast; it is hard to aim because you go straight down to the ground. [9:23]
- When the Rift spawns, the enemy pushes into your lanes because they expect you to go for it when down on money; that is the correct play. [11:06]
- Using Cold Front in the air decreases your heat bar. [13:12]
- Overheating automatically ejects you from the zipline. [13:45]
- He needs survivability and more shielding because his own abilities damage him, like Bebop bombs. [15:16]
- He can execute well, but his participation in fights is low; most of his kills come from the execute. [15:46]
- You can rocket jump on him, but it is hard to do. [18:23]
- When the enemy takes mid boss, taking a walker in return is a fair trade. [19:30]
- Item ideas later: Mystic Shot, or straight cooldown so everything is always up. [20:32]
- Combo with Bebop: if Danny grabs someone, Bebop can hook them, or if Bebop hooks someone, Danny can grab them. [23:08]
- Verdict: Danny is very bad even after buffs, close to unwinnable; fun but weak. [23:38]
- Even Escalating Exposure on his 3 does basically nothing. [24:08]
