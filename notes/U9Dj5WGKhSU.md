# HOW TO PICK YOUR DEADLOCK MAIN
channel: moe9times

## Covers
Hero picker guide by moe9times: groups the roster into archetypes (carry, tank, annoying, elusive, support, sniper, limit tester, others) and compares them to heroes from Valorant, Overwatch, Rivals, League and Dota.
Guide for new players choosing a main. Patch around 2025-10-16.

## Claims
- Carries who can take over a game when far enough ahead: Wraith, Haze, Seven, Mirage and Infernus. [1:01]
- If you are less of an aimer, Seven is easier to execute; the other carries reward strong tracking aim. [1:01]
- The carries all have tools that win 1v1s if you are better than your opponent. [1:01]
- Haze builds damage the more shots she lands (like Ursa). [1:01]
- Wraith has easy-to-execute lockdown. [1:32]
- Mirage makes opponents miserable with HP steal, building stacks and the tornado. [1:32]
- Infernus is a damage over time specialist with a straightforward kit: damage amp, dash and AoE stun; he rewards good aim and positioning. [1:32]
- Tanks: Abrams, Mo and Krill. Abrams is a teamfight disruptor who lands a stun combo with heavy melees mixed in. [1:32]
- Mo and Krill weave in and out of fights with built-in movement; both Abrams and Mo and Krill have spells that heal them and reduce damage taken. [2:03]
- Viscous is a tank with more finesse and flashy plays but needs practice to play well. [2:33]
- Billy is a melee specialist who gains max health for every melee he lands, with movement and lockdown to stay in enemies' faces. [2:33]
- Victor balances incoming damage with massive regen; his ult respawns him doing big damage and stunning nearby enemies, so running it down can help the team. [2:33]
- Annoying picks: Bebop puts someone where they should not be and explodes them himself (like Pudge, Blitzcrank, Roadhog). [3:03]
- Holliday and Paradox set up teammates' kills; Paradox is like Vengeful Spirit and Holliday like Batrider. [3:34]
- Pocket deals huge chunks of damage and gets away without a scratch; good Pocket players are a nightmare to catch (like Puck). [3:34]
- Calico is mobile with big burst, a bit tankier and more in your face than Pocket. [3:34]
- Lash is great at gap closing and escaping, with big crowd control from Ground Strike and Death Slam; he is the ego movement hero. [4:04]
- Drifter thrives at hunting isolated players and punishing poor positioning, snowballing from there. [4:04]
- Support is not a strict role; every hero can carry a match, so do not force a full support build if you can take over. [4:35]
- Main supports: Ivy, Kelvin, Dynamo and Paige. [4:35]
- Ivy has huge AoE area denial and silence, constant healing and a potent stun, and can carry people in and out of fights; take advantage of her ult. [5:06]
- Dynamo has two of the most impactful spells: Quantum Entanglement saves teammates and Singularity is a massive AoE shutdown that can bring back big deficits. [5:06]
- Kelvin is the laning king: Frost Grenade's burst heal and damage wins trades, and Arctic Beam punishes anyone too close. [5:36]
- Use Kelvin's Frozen Shelter to space teamfights, secure an objective, trap an opponent or save teammates. [5:36]
- Paige is the most traditional support: big AoE damage, shields and a global stun and heal combo, with surprising damage. [5:36]
- Sinclair is harder to execute but has big burst with his bolt, outplays with his assistant teleport, oppressive Rabbit Hex control and spell stealing. [5:36]
- Sniper players should try Vindicta and Grey Talon, who play high up and do chunky damage from range; Vindicta's hitscan ult does more damage to low enemies, Grey Talon's ult is a guided missile. [6:08]
- Limit testers: Shiv deals damage with his dash and bleed and clears self-damage with Bloodletting. [6:39]
- Lady Geist's spells hurt herself, and her ult swaps health with an enemy, so you must know how low you can go. [6:39]
- Yamato grapples to enemies for close range damage; her ult makes her tanky, gives debuff immunity and refreshes her abilities. [7:09]
- McGinnis is the turret pick: turrets, heals, fight-isolating walls and a long-range big damage ult. [7:09]
- Warden is a tanky hybrid whose ult heals him for damage dealt in a cone; his Flask and Cage keep enemies from escaping, best with a crowd-controlling teammate. [7:40]
