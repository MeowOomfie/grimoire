# What Does Midboss ACTUALLY Give, And When Should You Do It?
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-04-19
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BiZ29Dtc-Wo

## Covers
Mid Boss facts (HP, shield, resists, scream, regen, Rejuvenator rules, souls and permanent buffs), then game examples and a whiteboard for when to take Mid Boss, when to contest it, when not to do it, and how to play the parry game on the Rejuvenator. For players whose teams rarely take or contest Mid Boss.

## Claims
- Killing Mid Boss gives the Rejuvenator crystal, souls and permanent buffs for everyone on the team. [0:00]
- Mid Boss spawns at 10 minutes with 13,000 HP that scales with time; you rarely see anyone take it at 10 minutes but you can. [0:00]
- Mid Boss has 15% bullet resist, no spirit resist, and debuff resistance. [0:31]
- Mid Boss has a regenerating shield that starts at about 85 HP at 10 minutes and scales with time, so if your team does less than about 85 DPS you do no damage at all. [0:31]
- At 50% HP Mid Boss screams once, heard by everyone on the map; it does not scream again if you leave and come back. [1:02]
- Mid Boss regenerates 15 HP per second, so if you leave and do not come back it heals up. [1:32]
- The Rejuvenator takes 7 seconds to drop from where it spawns to the ground. [1:32]
- Only the first punch on the Rejuvenator heals you (to full HP); later punches do not heal. [1:32]
- Mid Boss gives 3,000 souls split across the team plus two permanent buffs to everyone, 12 permanent buffs total. [2:03]
- The Urn gives four permanent buffs to the deliverer and one to everyone else, nine total, so Mid Boss gives more; a center sacrifice gives four, so Mid Boss equals three of them in buffs and more souls. [2:03]
- The Rejuvenator buff shows a timer at the top and an "instant revive" buff bottom left, and a golden bar under a player's net worth means they still hold a Rejuvenator charge; use the bar to see who on either team still has one. [2:33]
- Rejuvenator charges are used by the first three people on the team to die, with no other criteria; a team can hold three, two or one depending on how many punches it secured. [3:03]
- Around the 26 minute mark is a realistic time to take Mid Boss in many games. [3:34]
- Take Mid Boss when you are confident you win the fight even if they contest, for example right after getting two kills. [3:34]
- Mid Boss should almost always be done before the last Walker; if you won a fight at 20 minutes, go Mid Boss instead of the last Walker, and if you took all three Walkers before Mid Boss you most likely did something wrong. [4:05]
- Minimize risk by checking which enemies have big ultimates and identifying who is dangerous in the pit. [4:35]
- In the pit you mostly fear AoE stuns that stop you hitting the crystal, plus Kelvin's dome, which freezes the crystal. [4:35]
- Victor is dangerous in the pit: AoE stun ult, big damage, can sit on the crystal, and if killed he revives, stuns everyone and steals Rejuvenators. [5:05]
- Sinclair is dangerous in the pit: his Bunny, plus he can steal your team's ultimates (dome, Infernus ult, Celeste ult). [5:05]
- Ivy's Stone Form is a 1.75 second stun (longer with duration) that can secure her team one or two revives while everyone is stunned. [5:36]
- Lash's ult can be a problem in the pit, but not if you can cancel it (dome, Vyper ult) and he has no Unstoppable. [5:36]
- Look at who you killed: two kills on low threat pit heroes (Mina, Shiv) do not make Mid Boss safe, while kills on Sinclair and Ivy do. [6:06]
- From the 15 minute mark, always keep in mind which enemies are problematic if you go Mid Boss. [6:06]
- Contest Mid Boss in close games (example: 136k vs 127k souls); it is better to take the risk than let them have three revives uncontested, especially when enemies are hurt. [6:37]
- Always try to contest if you are in position; do not contest only if you are completely out of position or completely outnumbered with their whole healthy team there. [7:07]
- Even if you cannot win, getting one revive and delaying them buys time for your team to arrive and contest the Rejuvenator (a lone Mirage stalled with a stolen revive, Echo Shard and his tornado). [7:38]
- It is better to be dead for 40 seconds than to give the enemy three Rejuvenator charges. [7:38]
- The earlier in the game, the more worth it a contest is, because respawn timers are shorter. [8:08]
- The Rejuvenator gives about 4 minutes of map control (or until the charges are used), because every fight is favorable with extra lives; that duration does not change through the game. [8:08]
- At 35 minutes a failed contest likely loses the game because respawns are around 90 seconds and the enemy has time to end. [8:38]
- Between 15 and 25 minutes you can be quite liberal contesting Mid Boss; from 30 minutes on be much more cautious. [9:08]
- If you cannot steal a Rejuvenator (for example they have Dynamo and you will all get black holed), play for kills instead and try to take at least one charge from them. [9:38]
- If your team truly cannot contest, look for counter play: a Walker or the Urn while they are busy with Mid Boss. [9:38]
- Example of what not to do: a team down 15k souls went Mid Boss after two kills without the key ultimates to secure it (no Rem sleep, no Paradox swap, Infernus ult not ready); they would have been better getting more picks or finishing a Walker, and when it failed they went from 15k to 25k down. [10:08]
- Yamato is a good kill before Mid Boss because of her ultimate; Calico is decent in the pit but nothing crazy. [11:09]
- Counterspell is an item that makes you very strong in the Mid Boss pit; the coach's Celeste ran Scourge, Torment Pulse and Counterspell. [11:09]
- A coin flip desperation Mid Boss can get you back into the game, but losing it costs all momentum; the losing team's only consolation is the permanent buffs. [12:10]
- Generally do Mid Boss past 15 minutes; pros sometimes do it earlier but that is rare. [12:41]
- Generally want at least two kills before Mid Boss; sometimes one is enough, and kills can be replaced by a big soul lead or a comp advantage (for example Dynamo and Kelvin into Haze and Wraith). [12:41]
- Haze is now mid in the pit since her old build is gone. [13:11]
- Mid Boss can sometimes be done before the first Walker, but in most games you take one Walker first as the natural flow. [13:42]
- Mid Boss is worth trading any number of Walkers for; ignore bad side waves, only the mid wave matters, so get mid wave priority by pushing it forward. [13:42]
- Two Walkers are only 8,000 souls, so Mid Boss only needs to earn more than an 8,000 soul advantage to be an even trade, and it adds permanent buffs while Walkers only give flex slots. [14:14]
- Use Mid Boss to force an advantage when you are ahead but their Walker is too hard to siege; surprising the enemy with it at 15 or 17 minutes works well. [14:45]
- Always contest before 25 to 30 minutes; later, only contest if the game is hopeless without stealing charges or there is a decent chance of winning the contest. [15:16]
- The main reason to avoid late contests is longer death timers. [15:46]
- Good poke and AoE abilities make contesting much better, so the more you have, the more you should contest. [15:46]
- Do not contest wildly outnumbered (one or two vs six); three or four vs six is manageable. [15:46]
- The three goals when contesting are stall them, kill them, or steal a Rejuvenator charge. [16:16]
- Stalling works best when wildly outnumbered: poke from the stairs, make them chase you, kite, come back, stay close enough to bother them but not so close they run you down. At lower ranks stalling may not work because enemies ignore you. [16:16]
- Aim to kill when you have more people, for example four vs six with enough damage to kill one before they finish. [16:47]
- Ideally start the contest and poke while they are still doing Mid Boss; contesting after it dies is also fine. [16:47]
- Go for the steal when you have a good chance: an AoE stun or a tool like Counterspell or Warp Stone to get in and parry. [17:18]
- When stealing, start with the parry unless you are at a rank where nobody parries; then you can just swing a heavy melee. There is no golden rule, it is situational. [17:48]
- Do not do Mid Boss when you are unsure you win the fight; a 50/50 coin flip can be okay only in a dire situation. [17:48]
- Do not do Mid Boss into scary steal tools without an answer: Ivy Stone Form, Kelvin dome, Victor ult, Infernus ult, Sinclair; anything AoE with a stun. [18:19]
- Answers are items or abilities that counter them: Lash ult can be cancelled by Vyper's bola or Bebop's hook; Sinclair's Bunny by standing on it with Counterspell; or Unstoppable. Have an answer for every threat. [18:49]
- Even with one or two picks and a lead, skip Mid Boss if their comp is much better in the pit. [19:19]
- Dynamo is nearly uncounterable in the pit once he has Unstoppable; the only answer is your own Unstoppable so you can punch the crystal through his ult, otherwise he can steal every crystal with Warp Stone into ult. [19:50]
- In a party, stagger parries: one person parries when the Rejuvenator drops and others take second and third parry, so one enemy cannot parry everyone repeatedly and steal it. [20:21]
- Most Rejuvenator steals happen when one player stands on it and parries again and again while three to five people get parried to the ground. [20:21]
- Always have at least one person parrying, and call whether you will punch or parry. [20:51]
- If a teammate calls an instant parry, delay yours by about 0.7 seconds to catch someone who punches late after the first parries are used. [20:51]
