## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream on the 6/12/26 patch that brought Holliday back with the stun on her Bounce Pad. He opens by updating his Holliday build from what top players buy, then coaches four players: a Seven (Ascendant), a Calico (Ascendant), a Vindicta (Phantom 6 to Ascendant 1 lobby) and a Viscous (Oracle), with a short Vindicta build review between the last two.
Themes: playing for urns, not mindlessly running at objectives, fights without an advantage or purpose, trooper damage, secret shop use, tilt and flaming teammates, picks with your team, vitality (green) spike, melee and parry micro, Opening Rounds being broken.
