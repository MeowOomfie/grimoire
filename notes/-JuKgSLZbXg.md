## Covers
Deathy reviews one of his own Eternus 6 games on Warden (MVP), mid lane with Holliday against Mina and Mo & Krill, explaining his thought process decision by decision.
A close game behind from about 12 to 29 minutes: lane power spikes, resets, itemization (Opening Rounds, Battle Vest, Frenzy, Siphon Bullets), Rift and Urn decisions, playing from a soul deficit, buying time around Mid Boss, the shorter Rejuvenator, and mistakes he would change. For mid to high rank players.

## Claims
- In lane, first think about the power spikes of each hero: when is each one strong. [0:30]
- Mina and Mo & Krill are not strong early: Mina depends on Rake or Love Bites for damage, usually takes Love Bites at level one, which does no damage early, and her gun is really bad. [1:01]
- Warden's Alchemical Flask is a much better starting ability: 12 second cooldown and it reduces enemies' weapon damage. [1:01]
- Mo & Krill depends on Scorn, which does no damage early until he has at least Mystic Burst and one AP in Scorn, and at level one he has a really big head hitbox. [1:01]
- Knowing he was stronger than both early, he took a lot of trades with Mo & Krill whenever he walked up. [1:31]
- With wave priority and Mo & Krill overextending aggressively out of the veil, he called his Holliday over to fight him. [1:31]
- Execution mistakes in that fight: he wasted a parry against Mo & Krill, went a little early for his Holliday, and missed shots; the play itself was correct. [2:01]
- Going back to base is more viable in the mid lane than other lanes because the path is shorter, especially with the shortcut; taking it properly you miss no troopers. [2:01]
- Resetting to full HP let him buy High Velocity Rounds and rush Opening Rounds instead of extra regen or healing, an aggressive itemization path that helps win the lane. [2:32]
- With an item like Opening Rounds you do a lot of poke under tower, especially to targets at full HP; Mo & Krill is a big body to shoot. [3:03]
- When ahead in lane, keep the aggression up but don't take too many risks. [3:33]
- Light melee mind game: he had to reload, so he threw three light melees, more than Mo & Krill expected. [3:33]
- With the enemies backed, take as much tower damage as possible, then back up once they return. [4:03]
- After clearing the wave, go farm your small camp, or steal their small camp if you feel confident. [4:03]
- If an enemy laner hasn't shown himself, take advantage and push the tower. [4:03]
- Parrying the tower with no troopers remaining was a mistake. [4:33]
- Seeing on the minimap that Celeste was leaving mid, he took the trade aggressively; with his barrier up he hard won Mina's trade. [4:33]
