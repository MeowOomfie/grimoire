# Deadlock's latest patch loves Charged Abilities - Patch Highlights
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-12-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bytdF6lX7o4

## Covers
Deathy's walkthrough of the December 6, 2024 patch: matchmaking options, system changes (diminishing range and duration, fire rate cap, sprint, Urn speed cap, double soaking removed), item changes and hero changes, with his verdict on each.
For players catching up on that patch. This is a late-2024 video; most of these numbers and kits have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- A new hero, Raven, was added to Hero Labs only; there are about eight Hero Labs heroes and none has joined the roster since Mirage almost two months earlier. Raven's kit is far from finished; view it via the hero tab, show experimental heroes. [0:00]
- New queue option "extra competitive" signals you want competitive, coordinated, communicative matches with like-minded allies; it may cut down on leavers, but if everyone selects it, it is worthless. Enable it in queue options, top right. [1:02]
- The matchmaker now tries to give each team at least two frontliners when it can; Valve has not said which heroes count as frontliners. [1:32]
- Ability range and ability duration now have diminishing stacking, so stacking many sources (for example Kinetic Dash plus Superior Duration) is worth less; a nerf to Dynamo and Viscous in particular and to any hero that cares about duration and range, which should bring Dynamo down. [2:03]
- Fire rate now has a cap: at most -50% can be applied (Deathy is not 100% sure of the wording). [2:33]
- Sprint boots are effectively nerfed: all heroes get a base 0.5 sprint (+0.5 on top for heroes that already had sprint), and Sprint Boots and Enduring Speed give a little less, so they are less mandatory. [2:33]
- Carrying the Urn now caps your movement at a fixed 13 m/s that cannot be raised or lowered, even with abilities, Haze invisibility, or Warden with move speed and Veil Walker. [3:03]
- Spawn points are no longer random; not a big deal, but Deathy likes it. [3:03]
- The Patron is weaker earlier and a little tankier later; Deathy doesn't expect it to affect the game much. [3:33]
- Tick-based souls: the bottom two players on a team now get some souls; not expected to change gameplay much. [3:33]
- Double soaking is officially dead: after four soul orb share events per trooper wave, extra participation shares the souls instead of duplicating them, which only griefs your teammate. [3:33]
- The earlier anti double soak changes (guardians dealing more damage to troopers, troopers dealing more damage to players and each other, reduced ranges) were all reverted. [4:05]
- All 500 soul weapon items were nerfed; they were extremely valuable and made gun builds very strong even with little investment. [4:35]
- Berserker now builds into Frenzy; Frenzy costs more but has the Berserker passive. [4:35]
- Spellslinger Headshots now gives 3% spirit amp instead of 3% spirit power per stack, 36% spirit amp at max stacks: a huge deal, since amp multiplies hundreds of spirit damage while the old version gave at most 36 spirit power. [5:05]
- Sharpshooter now reduces fire rate by 10% and move speed by 0.5 but gives a total of 100% damage with its regular plus far range bonus; it might be very strong. [5:36]
- Veil Walker was nerfed: 4 seconds more cooldown, and after being shot it takes 1 second instead of 0.25 to go invisible again; right direction but not enough, it will still be very strong. [6:06]
- Metal Skin was buffed. [6:38]
- Debuff Remover's heal changed from 10% max HP to a flat 300 HP, making it stronger early. [6:38]
- Leech now upgrades from Healbane and no longer gives cooldown reduction. [6:38]
- Cooldown reduction was reduced across the patch, including ultimate talents that cut seconds off cooldowns; Deathy says this follows his feedback and hopes it continues. [6:38]
- Silence Glyph now pierces through all enemies in its path and its projectile and collision size were buffed a lot, so it should be easy to hit; seems completely insane. [7:09]
- Refresher's cast delay went from 0.3 to 0.6 seconds, making it much harder for Dynamo to land two Singularities in the same spot, since people have time to Warp Stone or dash away. [7:09]
- Abrams and Bebop got only slight changes. [7:39]
- Dynamo's ultimate cooldown was nerfed by 24 seconds; with the item nerfs this may take him out of double S tier into very strong but not the most oppressive. [7:39]
- Grey Talon's Immobilizing Trap is now Spirit Snare: with no upgrade, once armed it curses whoever is in it for 1.75 seconds. [8:10]
- Shooting the trap's center with a Charged Shot triggers the curse early; throw it from the air and instantly charge shot it. [8:41]
- This makes Grey Talon a very potent counter to big channeled area ults like Dynamo's and Mo & Krill's: trap plus arrow curses and interrupts them. Probably enough to bring him back into the meta. [8:41]
- Haze's Sleep Dagger no longer disables the target; sleeping targets keep moving slowly, and their hitboxes are no longer messed up, so tracking is easier but you get fewer headshots. Overall probably a nerf. [9:12]
- Sleep Dagger now procs sleep instantly, so it can no longer be dodged with Stone Form and the like. [9:43]
- Haze's Smoke Bomb move speed and Fixation damage (tier three only, so early damage is the same) were nerfed, plus a slight nerf to ult spirit damage; may move her from one of the best carries to slightly worse. [9:43]
- Infernus Flame Dash tier three now makes it charged; between dashes there is a 3 second cooldown, and the charge cooldown is 15 seconds. [10:13]
- With Rapid Recharge (60% faster charges) the charge time is about 7 seconds, and it starts when you begin the dash, so it works out to about a 3 second cooldown between dashes; Deathy calls it ridiculous and expects a nerf. [10:43]
- Ivy and Kelvin changes are not very interesting; the notable one is Kelvin's Frost Grenade now also slows stamina regeneration by the same amount as its slow, which Deathy thinks is 40%. [10:43]
- Lady Geist's ultimate tier three now silences enemies in a 15 m radius for 3 seconds: very strong, a buff she did not need since she was already one of the strongest, if not the strongest, heroes. [11:13]
- Maxed Life Drain now has charges, so with Rapid Recharge you can Life Drain four people at once; needs more testing. [11:44]
- Lady Geist lost the silence on maxed Life Drain, so fewer people may max it; silence is now only on her ult. Deathy doesn't think these changes are good for the game. [12:15]
- Lash's Death Slam got buffs: tier three now stuns in the impact area for 1 second, guaranteeing follow-up, so enemies can no longer Tornado out (Mirage), ult out (Yamato) or Quantum Entanglement out (Dynamo) after being lifted. [12:46]
- Death Slam's cone angle and cast range were also increased; the Lash ult build is back to its former glory and investing in it should be rewarded. Lash should be pretty strong. [13:17]
- McGinnis turrets lost the 33% HP per second damage cap, so they last a minimum of 4 seconds and die after that if killed; fire rate down, HP gain up. Unclear how strong, but good changes. [13:48]
- Mirage's Fire Scarabs now drain over 4 seconds instead of front-loaded, and hitting the same target again refreshes it, so at level one with no points you can steal about 218 health from one opponent; extremely strong, probably too strong, expect a nerf. [14:19]
- Mo & Krill and Paradox got only number changes. [14:49]
- Pocket's ultimate Affliction was reworked: it immediately deals 18% of current HP on application (23% with tier three), but its DPS was gutted to 12 base, +15 at tier three (the old base 27), and base radius is 9 m with tier three needed for the extra 5 m; spirit scaling was also nerfed. [15:20]
- Since Pocket's ult is an initiation tool used on near full health targets, 23% of current HP is a big instant nuke (about 500 on a 2,000 HP target); Deathy doesn't see it as a nerf and Pocket is still likely one of the strongest heroes. [15:51]
- Seven's ultimate adds a new ability, Storm Cloud: an AOE that does damage and knocks people away, useful to force enemies out of cover and disrupt; it adds burst and a reason to ult. Probably a good change. [16:21]
- With Seven's ult maxed you can float and move slowly, so you are not a completely stationary target. [16:52]
- Vindicta's changes are mostly number changes. [17:22]
- Viscous's Puddle Punch tier three now turns the punch into a heavy melee, giving a ton of damage; Deathy expects a nerf and thinks Viscous is probably broken, after already being one of the best heroes. [17:22]
- Warden's ult tier two and three changed: Last Stand resistance was reduced (a nerf) but it only goes on a 20 second cooldown when interrupted instead of the full cooldown (a buff). [17:53]
- Warden's spirit power scaling on fire rate dropped from 0.25 to 0.22, which barely matters for gun Warden; Warden is still one of the strongest heroes and high priority. [17:53]
- Wraith's Full Auto tier three now gives spirit lifesteal and bullet lifesteal, and with tier two Full Auto she has default spirit scaling. [18:23]
- Wraith's Telekinesis was reworked: it lifts and slams the target, an instant interrupt (still cancels Mo & Krill's grab or Singularity) instead of a long stun, then silences and disarms them; two points add 0.75 seconds, and maxing it bounces to a second target. [18:54]
- With duration items (for example Decay and Duration Extender) Telekinesis silences and disarms two targets for nearly 4 seconds; the bounce range is fairly generous. Seems very strong. [19:24]
- Yamato can grapple to ally heroes with tier three Grapple, not a big deal since few max it early; the big buff is a faster ult cast with no post-cast delay, letting her go onto someone much quicker. She will be very strong again, maybe not OP. [19:24]
- Leaderboards in the main menu now show ranks and players' main heroes, for example 48 Eternus 6 players in North America vs 74 in Europe. [20:25]
