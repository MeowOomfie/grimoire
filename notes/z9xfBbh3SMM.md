# The BEST Infernus Build In Deadlock - META Build Guide
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-12-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z9xfBbh3SMM

## Covers
Metro's "Perma Dash" Infernus build: maxed Flame Dash charges with Extra Charge and Rapid Recharge, the skill order, the full item path with reasons, lane play, and the burn-then-dash fight loop.
For Infernus players who want a mobility spirit build rather than a gun build. This is a late-2024 video (December 2024 patch): the item shop, item stats and the map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Metro calls Infernus the most broken hero in the game if played correctly, despite a below 50% win rate on Tracklock; he says he can 1v6 and is near unkillable on this "Perma Dash" build. [0:35] [1:07]
- In the last patch Flame Dash gained ability charges when maxed, so with Extra Charge and Rapid Recharge you get multiple dashes on near-instant cooldowns, plus many Catalysts. [2:07]
- Skill order: one point in Afterburn first, then put points into Flame Dash right away (level two, then the max upgrade). [2:07] [2:37]
- He used to max Afterburn first; this build changes that and plays the lane differently. [2:07]
- Buy Ammo Scavenger first in lane and build up its spirit stacks. [2:37]
- Play the lane fairly passive until 2.2k souls, when you can put Flame Dash to level two; he calls this a huge power spike. [2:37]
- Flame Dash level two adds 45 DPS, which more than doubles its DPS; do not put early points in any other ability. [3:07]
- At 2.2k souls you should have about four lane items, with Ammo Scavenger near max stacks (about another 20 spirit power) and Infuser. [3:07]
- Infuser was buffed in the last patch and he calls it the most broken item on Infernus. [3:07] [3:37]
- Lane combo: get the burn on the enemy, then pop Infuser, Flame Dash into them and hit them with Catalyst. [3:37]
- Even with basically zero Ammo Scavenger stacks, burn plus Flame Dash does about 600 damage, and adding Catalyst (which extends the burn and adds damage) makes about 950 spirit damage; in lane nobody has more than 1,000 HP, so it is an instant kill. [4:07] [4:38]
- Play Infernus in a solo lane: he excels 1v1 and is a lot weaker in duo lanes when he has two enemies to worry about. [4:38]
- If the enemy trades damage with you, get the burn off and Dash them and they die; he says maybe only Mirage can stand up to Infernus 1v1 in lane, and every other hero loses to it if played properly. [5:09]
- When low in lane, pop Infuser and Flame Dash through the enemy creeps to heal a good amount, even without a burn on the enemy hero. [5:39]
- Lifesteal stacks with diminishing returns, so with Infuser, then Spirit Lifesteal early and Soul Shredder Bullets later, you have enough lifesteal and skip Enduring Spirit to save a slot. [5:39] [6:12]
- This is a mobility Infernus: start a fight by shooting to get a burn, then Dash around nonstop and only shoot during the roughly 3 second Flame Dash downtime. [6:12]
- Buy Soul Shredder Bullets next for survivability and damage. [6:43]
- Then go Extra Charge, then Rapid Recharge; the 3k for Rapid Recharge lines up with having the ability points to max Flame Dash, because souls earned track ability points. [6:43] [7:14]
- Maxed Flame Dash with Extra Charge and Rapid Recharge is, he now says, the biggest power spike in the game: you can farm anything instantly and nobody can kill you if you play it properly. [7:14]
- Chain dashes: as one dash ends, jump Dash and start the next Flame Dash; use the 3 second downtime to re-burn, shoot, heal back, or jump Dash for distance to escape. [7:45]
- At this point you can play passive and clear jungle camps very fast (apply the burn and run through; three camps quickly) or go for aggressive kills. [7:45] [8:15]
- Then buy Spirit Lifesteal, then Mystic Vulnerability for more damage. [8:15]
- Next choose Healbane or Toxic Bullets; both cut enemy healing, and Healbane is better in most scenarios for this build. [8:15] [8:46]
- Toxic Bullets applies for 3 seconds from bullets, while Healbane applies on any spirit damage and lasts 7 seconds, so with constant burn and dash damage it stays up forever. [8:46] [9:48] [10:18]
- You only need to start the burn once; Catalyst and Flame Dash charges keep extending it without shooting again, so you can keep running and the enemy keeps burning. [9:16] [9:48]
- Then buy Duration Extender, which lengthens Catalyst, Flame Dash and most importantly Afterburn. [10:18]
- After maxing Flame Dash, put points into Afterburn (level one, two, then max). [10:18]
- Full skill order: max Flame Dash, then max Afterburn, then max Catalyst, then max Concussive Combustion. [10:48]
- Maxing Catalyst before Afterburn is also fine (max Catalyst gives 60% damage amplification and more healing reduction), but he prefers Afterburn because it is more solid damage and Catalyst can miss when you cannot close the gap. [10:48] [11:18]
- Burst Fire is next and he calls it a core item people sleep on; it got a movement slow resist buff in a recent patch. [11:18]
- Movement slow, especially Slowing Hex, is what can ruin Infernus; Burst Fire helps negate it so you can skip Enduring Speed. [11:49]
- Enduring Speed gives 30% movement slow resist, but its move and sprint speed are wasted because Flame Dash already puts Infernus at his max speed (about 20 m/s), so other green items are worth more. [11:49] [12:20]
- Burst Fire's fire rate and move speed let you build the burn very fast with body shots, even at long range when you arrive at a fight or spot someone farming. [12:50] [13:21]
- Once enemies are burning you can Dash in aggressively even into two or three enemies, because the lifesteal plus the slows from Flame Dash and Catalyst keep you alive. [13:21]
- Multiple Slowing Hexes are the main threat; Slowing Hex disables Flame Dash, but if you cast Flame Dash before the hex lands, the dash stays active. [13:53]
- Against Warden, who is scary for Infernus with Slowing Hex and Binding Word, engage, start the burn and Dash in first so his hex and Binding Word do not trap you. [13:53]
- Loop: burn, pop Infuser, run in, Catalyst them, keep the pressure up, go back in, and Flame Dash out to escape at any point. [13:53] [14:26]
- Unlike old Infernus, there is no cost to using the first Flame Dash aggressively because you have more charges right after. [14:57]
- Then Healing Booster for even more healing, then Spirit Armor. [14:57]
- When short on slots, sell Extra Stamina, or sell Ammo Scavenger if you only have one flex slot; with two flex slots keep Ammo Scavenger. [15:27]
- Then Improved Spirit, which builds from Extra Spirit so it takes no extra slot, and boosts Catalyst, Flame Dash and Afterburn damage and so your lifesteal. [15:27] [15:57]
- Then Mystic Slow; most Infernus players buy it after Ricochet or Leech, but he buys it late mid game because it synergizes with Flame Dash fire on the ground and Catalyst's 35% slow. [15:57] [16:27]
- With Catalyst, Flame Dash's fire rate slow and Mystic Slow on them, enemies can barely move or shoot back (fire rate around 50%). [16:27] [16:57]
- After that your damage is enough to kill everyone, so the rest is survival: almost always buy at least one item from the survivability tab, sometimes before Improved Spirit or Mystic Slow. [16:57] [17:27]
- Ethereal Shift is the most solid survival option overall, protecting against multiple Slowing Hexes or Knockdown. [17:27]
- With Ethereal Shift: engage, start the burn, Dash in, then pop Ethereal Shift while they burn from Catalyst and Flame Dash; you keep lifesteal inside it, and its roughly 3.5 seconds covers the Flame Dash downtime, so you have zero downtime. [17:27] [17:59]
- Slowing Hex and Knockdown are the two items that counter Infernus and this build. [18:29]
- Buy Metal Skin instead if the enemy does a lot of bullet damage and little spirit damage; you can also run both. [18:29] [18:59]
- Against heavy bullet damage (for example Wraith or Vindicta gun builds) in longer fights, Escalating Resilience is a great choice: 2% bullet resist per stack for 20 seconds, and with Burst Fire's fire rate the first burn nearly maxes it (36 to 40% bullet armor shown). [18:59] [19:29]
- Debuff Remover is also great and can replace Ethereal Shift; it works against Warden's Binding Word, Slowing Hex and many abilities. He buys it less as personal preference. [19:59] [20:30]
- Late game Leech: alone it is not great because spirit lifesteal has diminishing returns (27% spirit lifesteal, 46% with Infuser, plus 22% from Soul Shredder on the target, about 55% total), but it builds from Healbane so it takes no flex slot and adds base health and bullet lifesteal. [20:30] [21:03]
- He would not buy Leech if it did not build out of Healbane. [21:03]
- If you are already doing enough damage, Leech is a good 6k upgrade; otherwise upgrade Improved Spirit to Boundless Spirit (no extra flex slot) for bonus health, much more spirit power and weapon damage. [21:33]
- Usually you only have two flex slots at this point in the game. [21:33]
- Escalating Exposure deals about the same damage as Boundless Spirit in his testing (more the longer the fight), but he finds Boundless Spirit more solid for the bonus health and weapon damage; Escalating Exposure only gives 15% spirit resist. [22:04] [22:36]
- Spiritual Overflow is a solid choice if you need more damage, but Boundless Spirit is usually better because you want raw spirit power and the extra fire rate is redundant with Burst Fire. [22:36] [23:07]
- Pick Spiritual Overflow if the enemy is so scary that you die every time you Dash in after starting the burn. [23:07]
- Silencer is another great option if enemies escape; it builds at about the same rate as the burn, so you silence them around when the burn pops, and silenced enemies deal less spirit damage to you. [23:37]
- Silencer takes a tiny bit longer than the burn to apply: watch the icons above the enemy and fire one or two extra bullets after the burn to get it. [24:09]
- Buy Unstoppable if the enemy has so much CC that they stun you after your Ethereal Shift. [24:41]
- Inhibitor works beautifully on Infernus: more movement slow, and with their fire rate at 50% they also deal 35% less damage, so they cannot fight back. [24:41] [25:11]
- If rich, buy Spellslinger Headshots; he thinks it is still weak, but it builds from Soul Shredder Bullets so it is a cheap upgrade, a luxury item and last on the late game list. [25:11]
- Ricochet is only situational here: it is good on Infernus but this build spends more time dashing than shooting, so it only shines when enemies stack up; Silencer, Boundless Spirit or Leech are worth more for the price. [25:11] [25:43] [26:15]
- He almost never sells Basic Magazine: Infernus's magazine is small, and without it you may fail to burn someone in one magazine. [26:15] [26:46]
- If you do not get the burn, you have no lifesteal and dashing does almost no damage; the main damage is Afterburn, and Flame Dash and Catalyst keep you alive and extend it. Make sure one magazine gets a burn before you engage. [26:46] [27:18]
- If you struggle to get the burn off, upgrade Basic Magazine to Titanic Magazine. [27:18]
- Superior Duration is a luxury upgrade from Duration Extender; it only adds one more burn tick, which is easy to keep up anyway, so 3k buys little. [27:18] [27:49]
- The build takes practice: the timings for Metal Skin and Ethereal Shift and knowing when to Dash in and out are essential. [28:50]
