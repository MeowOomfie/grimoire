## Covers
First-impressions breakdown of Venator, the gun carry hero released 2026-02-05 as the fourth hero of the Old Gods New Blood update.
Every ability with its upgrades, pure and blessed damage, skill order, early and 35k item build, playstyle around cover and objectives. For anyone picking up Venator on release.

## Claims
- Venator is a raw gun carry and the first hero with multiple abilities that scale with gun damage. [0:00]
- His gun is auto fire with very high bullet velocity, easy to hit at long range, treat it as a hitscan gun; velocity feels slightly slower than Wraith's and the fire rate feels like Wraith's, with a lot of bullets in the mag early. [0:30]
- Consecrating Grenade (1): fires a grenade that bounces off walls, burns enemies, reduces their healing and deals pure damage in an area. [1:01]
- Pure damage is a new term: resistances and Metal Skin do not matter, you deal full damage however tanky the hero is, so Venator should be very good against tanks stacking resistances. [1:01]
- Consecrating Grenade upgrades: cooldown reduction, then more explosion and burn radius, and at max level it gets charges (more healing reduction plus charges). [1:31]
- Consecrating Grenade is easy to hit even at long distance with direct hits, a fast and forgiving projectile that leaves a burning area; very good in lane. [1:31]
- Do not max Consecrating Grenade first, since most of the kit scales with gun and the charges would need something like Rapid Recharge; put one point in early, maybe two. [2:01]
- Gutshot (2): a shotgun blast that works like Ash's coach gun, pushing enemies away; pushing them into a wall stuns them. [2:32]
- Gutshot upgrades: more damage, lower cooldown, more range, and at max level the wall stun makes your next melee blessed. [2:32]
- Blessed means true damage; a wall-stunned enemy cannot parry, so your next heavy melee does a lot of damage. [3:03]
- Melee damage scales directly with gun damage, so weaving in melee items on Venator is very good. [3:03]
- Hexline Snap Trap (3, caption spelling): a bear trap that immobilizes enemies who step on it; it takes a second to arm. [3:03]
- Snap Trap upgrades: cooldown reduction, longer immobilize, and at max level 20% more damage to the trapped target; it feels close to Warden's cage. [3:33]
- Combo: Gutshot an enemy into a wall for the stun, then drop the trap next to them to guarantee the immobilize. [3:33]
- The trap is hard to place because it bounces off walls; you slide it like a hockey puck, but its radius is forgiving. [4:04]
- Ultimate (caption "era Dominique"): loads a crossbow with three stakes that deal massively increased weapon damage; the final stake is blessed, dealing pure damage and executing enemies below 8% health. [4:35]
- Ultimate upgrades: level 1 more movement speed, level 2 lower cooldown and more damage, level 3 makes all stakes blessed, so every stake does pure damage and executes. [4:35]
- The ultimate stakes can headshot for massively increased damage and they pierce. [5:06]
- Three abilities scale only with gun damage; only Hexline Snap Trap scales with spirit. [5:39]
- Venator's bullet resist and spirit resist both scale with spirit power, so hybrid items like Surge of Power or Rapid Recharge are worth it: they keep synergy, give the 4.8k spirit spike later and raise resistances without buying many green items. [6:10]
- You must build a lot of orange (weapon) items on Venator because all abilities get better with gun. [6:10]
- The default build has troll picks; Titanic Magazine will not be bought. [6:41]
- Fleetfoot is the number one item every game because it lets you shoot without the movement slow. [6:41]
- Damage falloff is minimal for an auto fire gun; at about 30 m he still does a lot of damage, so Point Blank or Sharpshooter are not needed. [7:11]
- He is a mid-range hero who plays around walls and baits enemies into him. [7:11]
- Gutshot is not an offensive tool: when the enemy dives you, push them into a wall and punish. Hold corners strategically to get stuns, shoot from afar and punish close enemies with the trap or a stun. [7:11]
- Consecrating Grenade is a harassment tool and works great against healers; 50% healing reduction at max is huge. [7:41]
- One point in Consecrating Grenade gives a 20 second cooldown. [7:41]
- Gutshot has a x1 scaling, so put one point in it for value. [8:12]
- One point in Snap Trap takes its cooldown from 30 seconds to 18 seconds, almost halving it. [8:12]
- There is a melee route that maxes Gutshot, but as a straight gun carry max the ultimate first for lower cooldown and go for kills and executes every time it is up. [8:12]
- Max Consecrating Grenade second; it has the highest gun scaling and its burn damage per second scales with his level, from 10 at base to 37 at max. [8:43]
- An Extra Charge for one more grenade is probably worth it; whether Rapid Recharge is worth it is still to be tested. [9:13]
- Fire rate is great on gun carries; prefer Active Reload over Quicksilver Reload because the grenade cooldown is still 20 seconds at max and no ability is fast enough for Quicksilver. [9:13]
- Core early items: Active Reload, Fleetfoot, then Intensifying Magazine. [9:13]
- Buildups proc insanely fast on Venator: Toxic Bullets and Inhibitor are strong. [9:45]
- Verdict: insane gun carry; buy gun items, play cover, and play close to objectives so a diving enemy can be pushed into a wall or into your trap. [9:45]
