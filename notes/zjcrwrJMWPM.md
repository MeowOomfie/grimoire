# How Good is Metro's Builds in Deadlock?
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-06-02
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zjcrwrJMWPM

## Covers
Wraith, aquatiq trying Metro Deadlock's Wraith build in an Ascendant lobby, laning with Paradox.
Commentary over his own game, no coaching. Patch around 2026-06-02, shortly after a Graves nerf and the urn change that makes holding it lethal.

## Claims
- Two early upgrades into Card Trick (cards) feel really nice; the cards home in so you don't need to aim them. [2:05] [3:06]
- Paradox with Wraith wins this lane most of the time. [3:42]
- You can still parry minions, but parrying a minion no longer resets your cooldown or rewards you. [4:17]
- Many Calicos go Leaping Slash, then Gloom Bombs, then a light melee, so expect the light melee after that combo. [4:47]
- As Wraith, whenever you are going to fight (or shoot the tower), always turn your 3 on first. [5:53]
- Tesla Bullets is really good right now; he buys it as his next item. [5:53] [6:24]
- When your team is a bit behind, go for the urn at 12. [10:07]
- He takes Extra Regen as his next item. [10:07]
- Max cards after the early points. [11:09]
- Metro's build is a bit more tempo: you can start playing earlier and don't have to farm as much. [11:09]
- Forcing the enemy Yamato to use her ult early means she doesn't have it when the fight that matters comes. [12:45] [13:15]
- You can't hold the urn for longer than about a minute any more, the urn now damages and can kill the carrier. [13:45]
- He plays Graves more like a support, and it works. [14:16]
- He buys Spirit Shielding, and later also Weapon Shielding, to have both. [14:47] [17:27]
- Get ready for the next urn: keep the lane on the urn's spawn side pushed at all times, it is a tug-of-war over where the urn spawns. [15:50]
- Don't die when the urn is up. [16:57]
- Urn plus one walker is probably better than two walkers. [19:19]
- Against a team full of heals (Rem, Abrams, Calico, Graves, Yamato, likely Leech), he farms for Ricochet and then buys Toxic Bullets for the anti-heal. [20:49]
- Let a low-HP teammate punch the mid boss (rejuvenator) for the last hit. [22:58]
- He buys Spirit Lifesteal next and wishes he had Fleetfoot. [22:58] [23:33]
