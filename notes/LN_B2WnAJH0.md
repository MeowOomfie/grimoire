## Covers
General itemization guide, moe9times: build to win the game, not the best version of your hero.
Defensive and counter items, when to leave a preset build. Patch of October 2025.

## Claims
- Build to win the game, not to build the best version of your character; test dummy damage does not win games. [0:30]
- Following the same all-gun build every game gets you hard stuck. [0:00]
- Builds are still useful to learn a hero's strengths, for example almost every Haze build has Burst Fire and Bullet Lifesteal, Dynamo usually has Arcane Surge plus melee items. [1:01]
- Know when to leave the build: ask what stops you playing your game and how you stop the enemy playing theirs. [1:33]
- What usually stops you playing is CC and high damage; DPS does not matter if you are dead or walking back to heal. [1:33]
- You make around 1,000 souls per minute on average, so a 1,600 soul item that stops you dying twice is a profit. [2:03]
- Compromise your DPS build: think about what you are dying to, or will die to. [2:03]
- Dedicated resist items: Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem at tier 2, Bullet Resilience and Spirit Resilience at tier 3. [2:33]
- Restorative Locket gives an instant big chunk of HP and can be used to bait enemies in. [3:04]
- Bullet and spirit shield items are great on low HP heroes. [3:04]
- Warp Stone gives mobility to start and escape fights, and its on-use bullet resist is stronger than you think. [3:04]
- Plated Armor and Juggernaut make gun heroes miserable. [3:04]
- Spellbreaker and Witchmail are strong against high spirit burst. [3:35]
- Against lockdown: Reactive Barrier early, which scales nicely with spirit. [3:35]
- Debuff Reducer builds into Debuff Remover and Unstoppable, both huge for staying alive. [3:35]
- Unstoppable counters every stun in the game for 5 seconds: dodge a Dynamo Singularity, waste a Holliday lasso, stop Mo & Krill grabbing you. [4:05]
- Unstoppable is almost mandatory on hard carries; one chain stun at 45 minutes can throw a game you were dominating. [4:05]
- Killing an enemy at 19 minutes takes them off the map for about 40 seconds, costing them souls and freeing space for your team to farm or take an objective. [4:36]
- It is fine to leave your build path to shut down one enemy; only killing the Patron matters. [5:06]
- Ask what the enemy is good at: heavy healing, evasiveness, big spells, big gun damage, and when they are strong. [5:37]
- Slows, stuns and silences answer mobile heroes like Calico, Pocket, Yamato and Lash. [6:08]
- Against big healing (Shiv, Abrams, Warden) buy healing reduction. [6:08]
- Rusted Barrel, Suppressor, Disarming Hex and Inhibitor cut enemy damage, which helps your whole team survive. [6:39]
- Do not buy Toxic Bullets or Crippling Headshot if you are not a gun hero. [6:39]
- Do not buy Spirit Burn on heroes with no way to deal 500 spirit damage to proc it. [6:39]
- There are no hard hero limits on items: if nobody buys Slowing Hex and the Pocket keeps escaping, buy it. [7:09]
- Your build should solve problems, so it should almost never look the same between games. [7:09]
- Staying alive usually matters more than directly countering enemies; being alive is map pressure. [7:39]
- Never buy Trophy Collector; it is by far the worst item in the game. [7:39]
- Too many players have no green items at 40 minutes. [8:09]
