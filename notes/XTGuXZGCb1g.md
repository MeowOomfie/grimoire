## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream recorded 1/20/26, on the last build before the hero patch, with a small patch dropping mid stream (respawn timers, faster neutral camps, urn and down dash changes) that he reads and reacts to.
He coaches three replays: a Paradox (Phantom, mid with Shiv), a Holliday (Alchemist, lane with Ivy into Warden and a fed Mina) and a Lash (Seeker, Trophy Collector build), then live-coaches a South American pro team scrim (about E5 average, two subs) on lane assignment, weak-side farming, urn and Rejuvenator macro and base racing.
Side topics: chat build questions (Mina, Surge of Power), a Quicksilver Reload test on damage-over-time abilities, the mid skip movement route, laning into Lash and Vyper, and the most common mistakes across his students.

- Respawn timers at 19 minutes reduced from 35 s to 30 s, and at 30 minutes reduced by 5 s; he dislikes making the game longer. [132:33]
- New: you respawn 6 to 22 s slower when you die while meaningfully richer than the enemy team's average net worth (15 to 30% richer, game time 10 to 25 minutes); nothing happens under 15% or before 10 minutes. He likes it as a comeback mechanic, though comeback mechanics risk feeling like punishment for doing well. [133:03]
- He likes that killing a fed carry is now more impactful and punishes M1 carries who win through farm. [195:25]

It also puts more value on Sinner's Sacrifice. [223:03]
- Urn: no drop-off channel time, knocked out of your hands by a light melee, pickup channel reset by a light melee, can no longer parry while channeling the pickup, carrier resist reduced from 50% to 30%. [134:03]
- He first read "no drop-off channel" as only dropping the urn, then saw in the scrim that the urn now deposits instantly; he calls it the worst change they have ever made, since there is now no counterplay to Doorman running the urn. [217:12]

- With instant deposit, he thinks you just run the urn straight in instead of playing slow. [241:05]
- Down dash stamina cost reduced from 1 to 0.5; he calls it the best change they have ever made. Stamina regen in the healing zone is dramatically increased. Kinetic Dash ammo reduced from 8 to 6. [134:33]
