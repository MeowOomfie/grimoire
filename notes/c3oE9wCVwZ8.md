# Infernus Is Still Broken...
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-12-23
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3oE9wCVwZ8

## Covers
A full replay walkthrough of Metro's Flame Dash Infernus build (lane vs Yamato, then mid and late game teamfights) showing the burn-then-dash gameplay loop, item order and when to fight, farm or pressure.
For Infernus players and anyone facing him. This is a late-2024 video (December 2024 patch); the game, items and Infernus kit have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Metro calls Infernus the hardest 1v6 character he has seen in any patch and completely broken. [0:42]
- Yamato is one of the best laners in the game but Infernus is even better in lane. [1:13]
- Play passive in lane until 2,200 souls; the build revolves around Flame Dash and the big lane power spike is Flame Dash at 2,200 souls. [1:13]
- Going aggressive early was fine because his wave pushed faster, winning lane control, and Yamato came close to melee a creep, giving a free Afterburn. [1:45]
- Infernus can trade efficiently with Yamato early: as long as you get Afterburn on, you come out at least even on trades against any hero 1v1. [1:45]
- After the trade, slow the pace a little; once above 500 souls with the lane pushed, buy the first item, Ammo Scavenger, for more spirit damage on the burn and on Flame Dash. [2:15]
- Until 2.2k souls Infernus is weak: play back and don't go for kills unless the enemy is badly overextended. [2:46]
- Keep lane control by taking last hits and pressuring the enemy creeps; while your creeps push into the enemy you can trade damage onto him without him damaging you. [2:46]
- If the enemy peeks for too long, pressure them so they must back out or take a burn. [3:16]
- Secure every soul last hit, because Ammo Scavenger stacks off soul last hits. [3:48]
- Tower damage for two souls was probably still worth it, but then you have to back out. [3:48]
- At 1,000+ souls buy Mystic Infuser (Infuser) and Rapid Rounds next; the build is public in game under "Metro Dash Infernus". [4:19]
- The first kill window comes at 2.2k souls, when Flame Dash goes to level two; at that point there are no points in Afterburn or Catalyst. [4:49]
- Max Flame Dash before levelling anything else. [4:49]
- From 2.2k, look for a burn and then a dash into the target; the enemy only lives if far enough away that you can't dash straight into their face. [4:49]
- Connecting Flame Dash extends the burn and does insane damage, around 800 to 900 total with Infuser popped. [5:19]
- If the target is too far (you would only reach them at their tower), don't dash: that is a dive, an overcommit and probably your death; pop Infuser for damage and lifesteal and keep lane control instead. [5:19]
- Played properly, Infernus is very scary in lane and the enemy can't do much. [5:50]
- With an Afterburn applied you could possibly tower dive, but at low health it is risky. [6:20]
- With a burn on the enemy, landing Flame Dash means a 100% kill; without Infuser you have no lifesteal, so don't chase too far when low. [6:51]
- Lane items done: Extra Spirit (to upgrade later), Extra Stamina and Basic Magazine; everything after is the mid game build. [7:24]
- He won lane without big outplays: 3.6k vs 3.1k souls and far more tower damage. [7:55]
- When an enemy Yamato pops her ult, the burn extinguishes, so Flame Dash no longer does the extended burn damage. [8:25]
- Mistake: he should have parried the tower to avoid its melee damage while diving. [8:25]
- Next buy Soul Shredder Bullets, the first lifesteal that is always active, which he calls insane on Infernus. [9:26]
- Gameplay loop: dash in, get behind the target, apply the burn immediately, pop Infuser and run after them; once the burn is extended the target is dead, often without needing the ult. [9:56]
- Save ability points to max Flame Dash; buy Rapid Recharge at the same time, since 3k souls for Rapid Recharge lines up almost exactly with level five Flame Dash. [10:56]
- Slashing at Infernus in lane is dangerous for Yamato: he gets the burn, uses Infuser and Catalyst and runs into her. [10:56]
- Run slightly past the target so they stay in the fire (Flame Dash trail) as long as possible, instead of turning early to shoot. [11:27]
- Once you have Rapid Recharge and max Flame Dash, consider helping your team rather than only pressuring the walker. [11:57]
- Backing out with Flame Dash as soon as Yamato ulted would have been the more solid choice; part of becoming very good is knowing what you can get away with. [13:31]
- With Rapid Recharge every creep on the map dies in about a second and nobody can catch you unless several people jump you. [13:31]
- Infernus is trickier into comps with many hard stuns (here: Dynamo's Singularity, Mo & Krill's ult, Warden's Slowing Hex and trap, Seven's stun). [13:31]
- When the creeps push into your tower, leave the tier three camp and go back to lane immediately: four creeps are about 400 souls that disappear forever, while the camp can be finished later. [14:32]
- With this build tier three camps go down very fast, so every camp on the map is efficient for Infernus to farm. [15:02]
- At 10 minutes grab the bridge buff if nobody took it; push the lane out and last hit souls to keep Ammo Scavenger stacks up. [15:33]
- Farming cycle: burn the camp, Flame Dash through it, then take the golden statues. [16:03]
- When ahead and nearly unkillable, he could have looked for kills more instead of farming; farming vs ganking has no right or wrong answer, it comes down to experience. [16:35]
- He chose to keep farming and pressure the left side of the map, and ended highest net worth in the game. [17:05]
- Getting the burn on Yamato just for lifesteal and forcing her ult is a good trade; his own ult matters little since most damage comes from Flame Dash and the burn. [17:36]
- The only way to learn what you can get away with is playing the character a lot. [18:07]
- Keep your guardian (tower) alive: while you hold three guardians the enemy gets no flex slot, so it is essential to keep it safe; pressuring their walker diverts attention. [18:37]
- Walker trick: bait the walker's first melee smash, then run through it; this does a lot of damage to the walker. [19:07]
- Mistake: running through the walker with Yamato behind him was greedy; he should have backed out the other way. [19:07]
- A Bebop that looks safe behind his tower can die easily: apply the burn from range, then pop Infuser, Flame Dash and Catalyst, and make sure the flame trail hits him. [20:09]
- When your team is far behind, you as the highest net worth must not die; still, pressure the enemy rather than AFK farm, because making multiple enemies rotate without dying is already a win and gives your team space. [21:10]
- If behind as a team, look for pickoffs, kills, teamfights and objectives; playing safe and only farming your side of the jungle is the biggest mistake he sees up to Eternus, because the enemy gets more map control. [21:40]
- He is not saying run into the enemy when behind; but the farmed player has to act, the game is not won by sitting and getting more money. [22:42]
- Even when ganked by three, the first essential thing is to get a burn on one person: the burn gives lifesteal, and Catalyst and Flame Dash only do big damage when the burn is active. [22:42]
- After getting the burn, immediately use Catalyst and Flame Dash. [23:14]
- Warden looks scary for Infernus on paper but Slowing Hex doesn't matter if he doesn't land it before you dash; the dash lasts long enough to get out of Binding Word, and burning while dashing through enemies gives huge lifesteal. [23:45]
- After escaping Binding Word there is about 3 seconds where you can't dash; use it to reapply the burn, then dash again. [24:15]
- This way he won a 3v1 under the enemy tower; keep reapplying the burn. [24:46]
- A won fight while 10k behind brought the game back to even. [25:51]
- Infernus basically can't die unless the enemy catches you off guard, for example Slowing Hex plus Binding Word before you get Flame Dash off; avoid that. [26:21]
- If two teammates are already in a lane, go elsewhere: farm the tier three camp on the side away from your ally, then defend the threatened walker. [26:52]
- It is deceiving for the enemy how fast they die; a target near his Warden still dies to the combo. [27:23]
- Metro thinks the build is too strong and needs a fix: it gives four or five Flame Dashes and Catalysts with almost no cooldown. [28:24]
- When you see an enemy, burn them then run into them; he dodged Dynamo's stun this way. [28:55]
- After using four dashes in two seconds the dash is on cooldown, so with three enemies around he backed out instead of committing. [30:28]
- With no downtime, go straight back to farming; a tier three camp dies in about one second. [31:00]
- If you run out of bullets, run through the target while reloading; the burn can still kill. [31:30]
- Even as the richest player, if every teammate is behind you need to do more: look for kills, fix lanes and help the team. [32:02]
- Dashing into walkers does insane damage to them, and lifesteal heals back the stun damage. [32:34]
- Mistake: with a burn ticking on Warden he should have kept shooting Warden and run through him; switching to an ulting Yamato meant he missed both and died. [33:06]
- With so much lifesteal he would have lived against four or five enemies without the Dynamo Singularity (black hole). [34:07]
- Dying on Infernus gives the enemy a lot of souls, but trading his death for the enemy's black hole and two walkers was still worth it. [34:38]
- He upgraded to Boundless Spirit early instead of something else because he had no flex slot; once the flex slot came he bought Metal Skin for survivability. [35:08]
- He took Toxic Bullets instead of Healbane this game because the enemies (Warden, Mo & Krill, Dynamo) had a lot of lifesteal and he had no slot for Healbane; both are great choices. [35:38]
- If both enemies heavy melee you, parrying would kill them both; he misplayed by not parrying. [36:41]
- As long as you keep burns up you survive: starting the fight with a burn and dash on Warden gave enough lifesteal to survive both of their big cooldowns. [36:41]
- Infernus is the fastest at fixing lanes, so he fixed lanes while the team took mid boss. [37:11]
- With Metal Skin on, the enemies can't kill you during the dash, so burn, then shift and run through them. [38:16]
- After a kill, immediately look for the next target to burn and extend the burn with Flame Dash. [38:47]
- With the Rejuvenator, don't chase a kill too close to the enemy spawn; burn the guardians/shrines to lifesteal back to full and play safe until an enemy steps too far forward. [40:17]
- Getting hooked into the enemy spawn while his team backed out was his fault for going too aggressive. [40:48]
- He finished his slots with Mystic Slow and Extra Health, planning a 6k item next. [41:19]
- The playstyle is simple and not hard, but overpowered; he encourages trying the Flame Dash builds. [42:23]
