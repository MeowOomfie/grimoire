# Deadlock for Beginners in 20 Minutes - Updated Late 2026
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-10-08
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7L-j1mjASk0

## Covers
Full beginner primer for the late 2026 patch: game access, souls, movement and stamina, zipline and jump pads, melee and parry, hero choice, laning, boxes, builds and item slots, ability points, the timer schedule (camps, boxes, bridge buffs, Sinner's Sacrifice, Urn, Rift, Mid Boss), Walkers, the bell tower and sunken plaza, and ending the game at the Patron.
For brand new players.

## Claims
- To get the game, someone who owns it must invite you; ask in his Discord's dedicated channel or other Discords and Reddit threads. [0:00]
- Souls are the money: with more souls than your opponents you can kill them and buy more items, then progress on the map, siege their base and win by defeating their Patron. [1:02]
- There is no mana; all abilities run on cooldowns, with a few exceptions based on damage dealt or received. [1:02]
- Out of combat you get bonus sprint speed that varies by hero and goes away when you take or deal damage. [1:33]
- Stamina options: air dash, regular dash, dash jump, wall jump and down dash. [1:33]
- As a new player, wall jump as much as possible, even when walking around the map; it is critical to getting better at movement and good players do it a lot. [2:05]
- Dash jump: when you dash, your stamina turns blue briefly; jump right then to dash jump. It is the second most important movement to learn, arguably more important than wall jumping early, but wall jumping carries you further as you climb. [2:05]
- Don't spend stamina mindlessly; it is a resource, so show up to fights with some stamina left. [2:35]
- The zipline is the "move faster" button; when going somewhere, check if a zipline gets you there. [3:05]
- Get off the zipline with crouch (Ctrl) to keep much more momentum, then immediately air dash forward; one of the fastest ways to cross the map. [3:05]
- If a zipline isn't available, use jump pads to rotate. [3:35]
- Getting shot while on a jump pad or zipline stuns you and deals a lot of damage. [3:35]
- Teleporters are extremely underused even by experienced players; learn their locations and get in the habit of using them. [3:35]
- All melees can be parried, but melee abilities cannot; the only parryable ability is Viscous's Bottle Punch (Puddle Punch). Counterspell changes this. [4:07]
- Light and heavy melees have similar DPS, heavy slightly higher, but heavy is always reactable with a parry while light must be predicted. Avoid heavy melees unless you know the opponent has no parry; prioritize light melees. [4:07]
- After a successful parry you always have time for two heavy melees if you start them right away. [4:37]
- A successful parry refreshes your parry cooldown; a missed parry puts it on a 5 second cooldown, which Rebuttal reduces. [4:37]
- Parrying some things, such as towers, does not refresh your parry. [5:08]
- Six new heroes arrive this patch (including Rat King and Solomon); pick whatever is fun. [5:39]
- Play a bot game or two with heroes that look cool, find two to three you vibe with and stick with them; the game is fairly balanced. [6:10]
- There are no set roles right now; any hero can have carry potential or do some supporting, though some can't frontline. [6:10]
- The laning stage is roughly the first 10 minutes (the game counts 9; some say 8, when Sinner's Sacrifices spawn), the period where you unlock abilities and get first items. [6:41]
- In lane your main concern is souls, from killing troopers and enemy heroes. [7:11]
- A killed trooper spawns a soul in the air you must shoot to secure, or enemies can deny it; a second soul spawns on the ground and comes to you if you are near. [7:11]
- Confirm your souls, and deny the enemy's souls when you notice the chance; it adds up very quickly. [7:41]
- The healing trooper drops a med pack on death that heals you, nearby teammates and your own troopers. [7:41]
- His best general advice: be aggressive. Playing scared slows your learning tremendously; you need to make mistakes to understand. Try to trade with enemy heroes. [7:41]
- Pushing the wave into the enemy tower is almost always the right play, unlike other MOBAs with more wave manipulation. [8:11]
- Boxes spawn at 3 minutes; regular boxes break with a light melee or by sliding through, heavy boxes need a heavy melee. [8:11]
- If you are not actively killing enemies between waves, run boxes extremely frequently. [8:41]
- Golden statues near boxes give permanent buffs instead of souls. [8:41]
- You can parry the Guardian to stun it for a few seconds when you want to dive. [8:41]
- Don't worry much about items; follow a popular in-game build that makes sense, in the item order shown, and don't deviate much at first. [8:41]
- You start with nine item slots and unlock three more as the game goes on; each Walker kill grants a slot. [9:11]
- Ability upgrades cost 1 point for the first, 2 for the second and 5 for the final; follow your build's ability point order. [9:41]
- Most builds go for a power spike by maxing one ability first; some heroes prefer spreading points, so follow the guide. [9:41]
- At 2 minutes a small (tier 1) jungle camp spawns in some lanes; farming it the second it spawns is highly beneficial. [10:12]
- At 5 minutes the bridge buffs spawn, one per side, reached by rope or jump pad; claim with a heavy melee. The four buffs are movement, vitality, spirit and gun, lasting (he believes) 150 seconds currently, and they are very strong. [10:12]
- At 5 minutes the medium (tier 2) camps spawn; much harder than small camps, but after the jungle update many heroes can farm them with any AoE ability. Don't miss waves for them early; do them between waves. [10:43]
- At 8 minutes the strong (tier 3) camps spawn; almost no hero can farm them at spawn, but they are a huge source of souls. [11:13]
- At 8 minutes Sinner's Sacrifices spawn: slot machines you punch for souls, which hurt you on every hit, so always heavy melee them to take fewer hits. [11:13]
- Sinner's Sacrifice timing: start your final heavy melee when three lights remain; on the faster red flashing type, start at four lights remaining. [11:43]
- Timing the final hit correctly gives four golden statue buffs, permanent for the game. [11:43]
- Sinner's Sacrifices give some of the most souls in the game plus massive permanent buffs; almost always farm them, steal the enemy's if you can, otherwise farm your own. [12:14]
- Jungle kills give haunted souls; die holding them and you drop a soul bag that enemies secure with a heavy melee. [12:14]
- Spend souls as often as possible, buying your next item as soon as you can afford it, to shed haunted souls. [12:44]
- The Urn spawns at 10 minutes. The Mid Boss now spawns at 0 minutes (it used to spawn at 10). [12:44]
- Mid Boss is the map's final boss, the pinnacle objective after winning a big team fight, like Roshan or Baron. [12:44]
- The Mid Boss drops the Rejuvenator; heavy melee it up to three times, each hit one Rejuvenator buff. [12:44]
- Each Rejuvenator buff is an extra life for anyone on your team who dies: they respawn where they died after a few seconds; a player can only benefit once, and buffs can be split between both teams. [13:14]
- Mid Boss is usually not done before 15 to 20 minutes; very high ranks sometimes take it at 12, low ranks may not until 25 to 30. At 20 to 25 minutes it is almost always a good thing to do. [13:14]
- Past 10 minutes with the Urn available, you are in the mid game. [13:45]
- Mid game: the number one focus is pushing your waves, your default chore. Before doing anything, ask whether you can push a wave; if yes, push it, then do the other thing. [14:15]
- Take fights when you have any advantage: position, mechanics, more souls, more teammates nearby. If it seems even, still limit test to learn. [14:45]
- Avoid staying in the same lane all game unless you just want to farm; moving around teaches you the map. [14:45]
- The Urn is carried from one side of the map to the other like capture the flag; carrying it gives slightly more stamina regen plus extra buffs depending on how far behind your team is. [15:16]
- While carrying the Urn you can do most things except take teleporters and use the zipline. [15:16]
- The Urn is very often the highest souls per minute on the map, so pick it up whenever it spawns. [15:16]
- The Urn spawns every 5 minutes on a fixed schedule (10, 15, 20, 25...) whether or not the last one was done. [15:16]
- The first Urn spawns yellow, the second green, then alternating: even tens (10, 20, 30) spawn yellow, odd fives (15, 25, 35) spawn green. [15:46]
- Depositing the Urn gives permanent buffs, and the souls go into the air for anyone nearby to claim, including enemies, so secure them. [15:46]
- The Unstable Rift first spawns between 11 and 13 minutes, then respawns 6 to 8 minutes after each one is done, so it is hard to predict. [16:17]
- A map indicator appears when the Rift is 60 seconds away, and an on-screen "unstable rift approaching" warning at 20 seconds. [16:17]
- The Rift is king of the hill: progress rises for both teams while both are in the circle; the team left alone captures it at 99%. [16:47]
- Capturing the Rift gives souls and Rift troopers, stronger troopers that push objectives. [16:47]
- Don't worry much about the first Rift; when your team is winning it can be dangerous because of a comeback mechanic giving the losing team bonus resistances up to 40% depending on game time. [17:17]
- The first Rift is almost never worth all six of your team fighting for; split push if your team over-groups. [17:17]
- If your team is behind in net worth, fighting the Rift is almost always worth it; it is one of the main comeback mechanics. [17:47]
- Walkers are the second line of base defense, with two attacks to watch: the stomp and the fireball. [17:47]
- Inside the Walker's inner circle it stomps, stunning everyone there for massive damage that can kill you; step out of the circle when you see it, dash back, let it stomp, then go back in while it is on cooldown for a few seconds. [17:47]
- The Walker sometimes pump fakes the stomp and currently seems buggy, so dodging it is not fully reliable. [18:17]
- Walker fireballs do heavy damage over time; keep moving and don't stand in them. When committed to a Walker push, stand away from your troopers so fireballs don't hit both you and them. [18:17]
- Don't try too hard to kill Walkers before about 13 or 14 minutes; they have a lot of resistances early. [18:47]
- Killing a Walker gives the other Walkers slightly more health; each Walker kill gives a flex slot and a lot of souls. [18:47]
- Dying for a Walker in the first 20 minutes is generally worth it from a souls perspective. [18:47]
- Chinatown bell tower: a rope up to three Sinner's Sacrifices and heavy boxes; punching either rings a loud bell the whole map hears, and the only exit is the rope, so if enemies come you are dead. [19:17]
- When you hear the enemy's bell, go check it out; you can often kill them for juicy soul bags. [19:47]
- Sunken Plaza: the purple fog on the other side of the map; inside you are kind of invisible from outside and your sound is not heard, but your stamina drains. [19:47]
- The Sunken Plaza is less risky than the bell tower because it has multiple escape paths, but if jumped at zero stamina you are almost certainly dead; both hold a lot of souls and can be worth it at the right time. [20:17]
- Ending the game: each base has two Shrines, protected until you kill the base guardians; killing both base guardians in any lane makes the Shrines vulnerable. [20:48]
- The Patron is invincible until both Shrines are dead. [20:48]
- Killing a Shrine permanently buffs your troopers in that lane; the first Shrine buffs two lanes, middle and the Shrine's lane. Buffed troopers keep pushing and drop fewer souls for the enemy. [20:48]
- Pushing the base before killing the Mid Boss is almost always a mistake; after two, three or four kills consider doing Mid Boss, which drastically reduces the risk of throwing. [21:19]
- Patron first phase barely does anything; in the second phase you must stand in the circle to damage it. [21:50]
- In the second phase the Patron charges an area attack that curses you (no abilities, very high damage); step out of the circle when it charges, then come back in. [21:50]
- One enemy in the Patron circle gives it significant damage resistance and two or more make it fully invulnerable, so kill the defenders first; if no enemies are in the pit, just kill the Patron. [22:21]
