# Deadlock has a BIG problem, and it's not what you think.
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-12-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2eoETHLPSgY

## Covers
A 2024 opinion video (the game has changed a lot since) arguing that cooldown reduction is too easy to get and ultimates are too short, so ults are up every fight. Mostly design criticism, but it lists the 2024 cooldown reduction sources and concrete cooldown numbers for Dynamo, Pocket, Wraith and others. Useful for anyone judging how often an enemy ult will be back.

## Claims
- Deadlock has no mana, so abilities are balanced only through their cooldowns. [1:02]
- Cooldown reduction sources: golden statues, the bridge buff (the casting buff gives 15% cooldown reduction), many items, and minus-cooldown ability upgrades. [1:02] [1:33]
- Bebop's first ultimate upgrade is a minus cooldown on his ultimate. [1:33]
- You usually get about 1,000 souls per minute, so Superior Cooldown (4,200 souls) takes about 4 minutes or a little less to farm. [2:03]
- Superior Cooldown gives 32% cooldown reduction to the one ability you choose and 24% to all your other abilities. [2:33]
- By comparison Dota's only cooldown item (Octarine Core) gives 25%, costs a little over 5,000 gold and takes 6 to 7 minutes to farm at 600-700 gold per minute, so cooldown reduction is much cheaper in Deadlock. [2:33]
- Spiritual Overflow gives 15% cooldown reduction, Diviner's Kevlar 12%, and an item the captions call "Lee" 12%. [3:05]
- Rapid Recharge gives 30% cooldown reduction on charged abilities, on top of the other recharge effects. [3:05]
- Late game, with several items, 10 to 15% from golden statue buffs and the bridge buff, reaching 50% cooldown reduction is not hard. [3:35]
- Dynamo felt like he always had Singularity in North American pubs because his ult easily gets to about a 90 to 95 second cooldown with modest cooldown reduction. [3:35] [4:05] [4:35]
- Long ults are meant to be punished by a higher-tempo lineup taking fights while the ult is on cooldown. [4:05]
- At 20 minutes players are dead for about 40 seconds, so after a won fight the losing team has only 50 to 60 seconds to punish a 95 second ult, and 30 to 40 seconds once they are out of spawn and in position. [4:35] [5:05]
- Pocket's ult is 127 seconds base; the first upgrade is -28 seconds, bringing it to about 100. [5:35]
- With Superior Cooldown and Diviner's Kevlar, Pocket's ult is about a 60 second cooldown and is up every fight, so he can throw it out freely, even on one target. [5:35]
- Pocket's Satchel is about 17 seconds base, which is long enough to punish (catch him with CC or silence). [6:06]
- The Satchel upgrade gives -4.75 seconds (about 12 seconds), and late game with cooldown reduction it is easily an 8 second cooldown; the Satchel itself lasts about 2 seconds, leaving maybe a second to punish. [6:36]
- He played against a Wraith whose ult got down to 35 seconds, usable twice in one fight. [7:06]
- Infernus, Wraith, Bebop, Pocket and Abrams all have a minus cooldown as their first ultimate upgrade; almost every ult has a big minus cooldown upgrade for one point. [8:38]
- Warden's ult gets -56 seconds at its max upgrade; Paradox has a -15 second cooldown upgrade. [9:38]
- His view: ults should be strong, but missing one or using it badly should leave a 2 to 3 minute window where you are less impactful, adding a layer to macro. [9:08]
- His suggestion: if minus cooldown stays, put it on the last upgrade so it costs full investment, not on the first point. [9:38]
- His suggestion: remove cooldown reduction from golden statues (keep health, spirit and ammo), especially since center sacrifices now also give golden statue buffs. [10:09]
- Rapid Recharge gives 60% faster time between charges and extra charges; he would replace its cooldown reduction with flat spirit for charged abilities, like Surge of Power. [10:40] [11:10]
- Enchanter's Barrier got cooldown reduction in an earlier buff to the barrier items; he would remove it. [11:10] [11:41]
- Type "cooldown reduction" in the shop search to see every item that gives it. [11:41]
- His suggestion: Superior Cooldown should be the only cooldown item; 24% on a 100 second cooldown already saves 24 seconds. [11:41] [12:11]
- Heavy cooldown reduction mainly enables builds focused on spamming ultimates more often. [12:11]
