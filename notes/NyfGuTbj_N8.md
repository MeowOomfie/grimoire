## Covers
Macro guide by moe9times: the three things to do on the map, early rotations, wave pushing, staying alive, mid boss timing and steals, reading the enemy in neutral states, and which fights to join.
General guide, no specific hero. Patch around 2025-07-01.

## Claims
- Good Deadlock in one sentence: be in the right place at the right time. [0:00]
- At any time, pick the one of three things that helps your game state most: progress the game (guardians, walkers, mid boss), farm efficiently (shove lanes, take camps fast, box runs), or disrupt the enemy (ganks, pickoffs, team fights, invading their jungle). [0:31]
- Do not rotate out of your lane before 5 minutes: you leave your lane partner alone, miss wave farm and let the enemy pressure your tower even if you get the kill. [1:02]
- If an enemy leaves lane to gank too early, punish by pushing the wave and pressuring the solo laner. [1:02]
- If your wave is under their guardian you have more wiggle room; taking the guardian early opens up where you can position, farm and rotate. [1:32]
- Just before 10 minutes, while the lane phase is still on, invade the enemy jungle to disrupt their farm; best on heroes with fast camp clear and good mobility. [1:32]
- Push your own wave before going to steal jungle, or your guardian takes unnecessary damage. [2:02]
- Almost every play should be preceded by pushing waves; pushed waves protect your objectives and force the enemy onto defense. [2:02]
- Minion waves alone do massive damage to objectives, and one extra player on top of two waves can take a walker alone. [2:02]
- Do not let waves hit your walkers freely; when choosing between joining a fight and stopping a walker split push, usually defend the walker. [2:32]
- Pushed waves deny information: creeps deep on the enemy side cannot spot heroes, so shove and then sneak across the map. [2:32]
- When enemies are defending waves at their guardian or walker, use that time to steal their jungle or punish players with no backup. [3:02]
- If the enemy commits to one side for mid boss or the Urn, take guardians, walkers, base guardians or even a risky shrine on the other side. [3:32]
- Do not die: if your team has already lost a fight you are not obligated to join; use the space elsewhere by split pushing or stealing jungle. [3:32]
- Dead players have no map presence: they cannot farm, rotate, gank or take objectives. Rare exception: trading one death for a flex slot. [4:02]
- Never go for mid boss if the whole enemy team is alive and your objectives are being pushed. [4:33]
- Mid boss is risky when the enemy has a net worth lead, has abilities that guarantee the Rejuvenator steal, or when you cannot kill it fast. [5:03]
- Ideal mid boss: around 20 minutes onward, when death timers open wide windows, and with enough damage to kill it before the enemy can counterattack. [5:03]
- Only try a mid boss steal from behind with a way to win the crystal: an ability that knocks heroes off it (Bebop's uppercut, Viscous's puddle punch), a wide AoE stun like Seven's, or items like Warp Stone or Unstoppable. [5:33]
- Do not go for a steal if the enemy has an ability that basically guarantees securing the crystal. [5:33]
- High impact team fighters like Dynamo or Lash should stay alive for the next fight rather than staggering their death on a steal; instead grab a flex slot by beelining to a walker. [6:04]
- In neutral mid and late game states, watch enemy neutral camps disappearing to locate them, and think about what threats they are responding to. [6:34]
- A double-stacked creep wave going deep into enemy territory can bait out a greedy hyper carry for a pick. [6:34]
- If the enemy jungle is entirely empty, be careful; you may be out of position and next. [6:34]
- Before joining a fight ask: can you make it in time? Do not show up to fights that will be over when you arrive. [7:05]
- Ask whether the fight secures an important objective (guardian, walker, mid boss); lower ranks take many fights for no profit, so farming or split pushing is often better. [7:36]
- In a late game fight that decides the match, join even a bad full-team send; you cannot win a 1v6 if they all die without you. [7:36]
