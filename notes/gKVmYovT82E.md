# What is Going On With Deadlock's Ranks?
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-05-13
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gKVmYovT82E

## Covers
Deathy's opinion on rank movement in May 2026: his leaderboard drop despite a winning record, his guess that Valve added performance-based MMR, smurf detection and matchmaking quality.
Mostly speculation; the few game facts are below. For players wondering why their rank moves oddly.

## Claims
- Rank is shown under skill rating, with global leaderboards; Valve does not disclose how the rank system works. [0:00]
- Deathy fell from about NA 34-35 to NA 57 in roughly two weeks while holding a 59% win rate over his last 200 games at an average match rank of Eternus 6. [1:02] [1:32]
- One page of games with high Statlocker performance grades took him from rank 60 to 48; a page with a similar win-loss record but poor grades took him from 48 to 59, fully reversing the MMR gained. [2:02] [2:32] [3:02]
- His best guess is that Valve has added some kind of individual performance tracking to MMR, and Valve has far more metrics than any third-party site. [3:32]
- Valve has tracked metrics like wall jumps per minute for a long time; he doubts they affect rank and thinks they are for catching smurfs, since a genuinely new player does not spam slide, dash jump or edge boost. [4:03] [9:06]
- He thinks performance metrics only make sense on fresh accounts, to rank up an obvious smurf (for example a 30 and 0 first game) quickly. [4:33] [5:04]
- Stats miss impact: in a Grey Talon game he was losing, one key late kill on Mina kept her dead for 90 seconds and let his team turn the game and take mid boss. [5:04]
- A common complaint is being stuck in a rank such as Archon for 500 games; under the old system a positive win rate still got you out, just very slowly. [6:35] [7:05]
- There is no separate unranked queue; every match is ranked, since the game is still a playtest. [7:05]
- His two guesses for Valve's reasons: move players with strong individual performances out of the wrong rank faster, and push smurfs out of low ranks faster. [7:35] [8:36]
- Smurfing is reportable, but he has never seen anyone banned for it, even players smurfing openly on stream. [8:06]
- When judging matchmaking quality, only consider the last week or two and be objective, since players complain when matchmaking is bad and never say when it is good. [10:36] [11:06]
- Faster rank movement may add more variance to match quality. [11:06]
- Yoshi posted in the Discord that the team was working on long queue times; forcing players into matches faster could make matchmaking worse. [11:36]
