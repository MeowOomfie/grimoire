channel: Midknight

## Covers
Midknight coaches one Phantom-rank Pocket player through a full game, in March 2026 shortly after a Pocket buff.
Coaching review: Flying Cloak and Barrage use in lane, confirming orbs, lane start and items (Extra Regen, Mystic Burst, Mystic Shot, Opening Rounds, Kinetic Dash), gun Pocket into a spirit comp, Silence Wave vs Spellbreaker, Majestic Leap vs Superior Stamina, Ballistic Enchantment, Sinner's Sacrifice syncing, invading with a bag, answering Drifter's ult, overstaying and over fighting.

## Claims
Player 1 (Pocket, Phantom):

Lane:
- Using Flying Cloak (2) and missing feels bad, and it means you don't have cloak to clear the next wave or for a fight that breaks out. [0:30]
- Shoot the orbs; orbs are key. In the summary: you were not hitting enough orbs, and denying is OP. [0:30]
- Don't start using cloak when you are already at half HP; same problem, you won't have it for the next wave. [0:30]
- With a point in cloak it is nuclear and can basically one-shot a wave; rush Mystic Burst and it literally one-shots the wave. [1:01]
- He would have preferred an Extra Regen start: you need the HP into lane bullies like the "Impaler" (as captioned) and Drifter, though Drifter takes a little longer to come online. [1:01]
- Barrage (1) is the better start from a pure fight perspective and was started back in solo lanes, but wave clear matters most, so most heroes start an ability that hits both the wave and the enemy; that is why Pocket starts cloak. [1:31]
- The first three waves of a lane really dictate how it goes, even when people are busy on boxes. [2:01]
- When a quick recall was not an option, teleport; one box is not worth the time you lose by not teleporting. [2:01]
- Pocket mains tend to get locked into fixed itemization; he liked this item choice. [2:34]
- Shooting that creep in the open let Drifter mark and kill you. [2:34]
- He hates using your 1 (Barrage) for wave clear, even though focusing the healing creep is good; he also hated that teleport, which got you mobbed with no teammate nearby. [3:08]
- Parry timing: the enemy was only in heavy melee range, not light melee range, so you didn't need to predict a melee. [3:38]
- He would prefer a spirit item over Mystic Shot early: Mystic Burst gives your 2 so much more wave clear and he likes Mystic Burst on Pocket in general. [3:38]
- Mystic Shot is good on Pocket because it gives one-shot potential, a gun tier two like Lash buys, which suits spirit burst heroes. [6:12]
- Opening Rounds would be really nice, especially after its buff: an insane amount of weapon damage and spirit power. [5:41]
- An item captioned "Arcane dash" over Mystic Shot is fine too. [5:41]
- The second you miss the Mystic Shot, you can't chase. [5:11]
- Don't ego with your 2 down; flying is super important. [5:11]
- Holding your cooldowns in that fight was mechanically fine. [5:41]
- Using cloak to steal that was too risky: you end up in their back line with nothing else, and if they are near and predict it you are just dead. [6:43]

Gun Pocket and items:
- Gun Pocket ("Glocket"): never play it if you have a choice, but if your comp is full spirit, gun Pocket is better than another spirit hero. [4:10]
- In this game, gun Pocket is significantly better than gun Calico, which is bad; if your team asks who should go gun, say so, or volunteer. [22:56]
- A 4.8k gun spike on Pocket: Opening Rounds plus Point Blank (Point Blank is insane on the zero). Some players like an item captioned "alacrity" on Pocket. [8:46]
- He prefers Point Blank with Kinetic Dash over Point Blank with Swift Striker. [9:30]
- Kinetic Dash gives infinite value on gun Pocket: more fire rate when up and it is great for farming without reloading. The cost is spending two stamina to pop it, which is expensive in a fight. [9:30]
- Silence Wave is a go-to item people underestimate (caption says "Paladin", context is Pocket). Rushing it into this comp is a no, but into a Yamato or Lady Geist he could see it; after the changes you have enough base damage to rush pure utility. [10:32]
- Silence Wave is a great anti-Spellbreaker item: if enemies build Spellbreaker, throw Silence Wave first, then do your full combo for all your damage. [11:03]
- Majestic Leap is so good on Pocket for setting up Afflictions and Barrages; not having it, or even Improved Spirit, feels bad. [17:38]
- Pockets dropping Majestic Leap and calling it bad are wrong; the real argument is that Superior Stamina is disgustingly broken right now, so he can't tell you not to buy it. [19:14]
- Not having Majestic Leap probably hurt your Afflictions; you were not getting as much value out of Affliction as you should. [29:35]
- Majestic Leap also lets you bail from a bad engage and re-engage, though here the real issue was going too far behind the enemy. [30:09]
- He would personally cut Warp Stone from Cosmedical's core of Warp Stone, Superior Stamina and Majestic Leap; he recommends Cosmedical's Pocket build as the best. [31:09]
- Fortitude on Pocket: he doesn't like it, though Fortitude is not bad after its buffs. [21:23]
- Ballistic Enchantment on Barrage: not really for normal games, though some high-level Pockets like it. It pays for itself only when Barrage hits an extra person you would not have hit, which is niche. It also gets stacks from creeps. [26:29]
- Casting Barrage while reloading is good; technically better is the reload tech of reloading then using Enchanter's Satchel to finish the reload. [29:05]

Satchel, Affliction and Drifter's ult:
- You died with Enchanter's Satchel up; Satchel his last three hits, or Satchel right before he goes in and come out after it hits, and he dies. [8:46]
- Blocking yourself into a corner with your 1 gave the enemy kill potential. Either 1 backwards then diagonal dash (new tech) or just go backwards and 1. [13:03]
- As Pocket you should always answer Drifter's ult; build the muscle memory, it is barely harder than reacting to a Lash ult. [4:41]
- You don't have to answer Drifter's ult instantly; you can wait, and it takes getting used to how far away it sounds. [15:04]
- Try to pop the last Satchel window like a cloak so you are inside Satchel when the effect pops and don't get silenced. [15:35]
- Every time you got Drifter ulted this game you had Satchel up and died with it, at least twice; you popped it for fun earlier. [23:56]
- Using Affliction before the Urn fight left you without it there. [25:28]

Movement:
- Bad stamina use: to go downstairs, dash preemptively so the dash ends at the top of the stairs, then slide down them. [13:33]
- Hug walls as Pocket to avoid Apollo's 3. [20:53]

Farming macro:
- Being half dead with a 40-second respawn timer at minute 15 feels bad. [13:33]
- With nothing time-pressuring you, heavy melee instead of light melee; rule of thumb, if you would walk 3 seconds to grab five golden statues, take the time. [14:04]
- Don't farm your own jungle and then invade the enemy's: you carry your bag into their jungle. Steal their farm first, then come back to your side with the wave. [14:04]
- Syncing camps: you are getting too fancy waiting a full rotation either way; only sync them if you farmed the initial one. [16:35]
- Sinner's Sacrifice pairs: if one needs a full rotation, heavy melee the other while it cycles, then come back and finish the original. [16:35]
- The effort to manually sync camps might save you once a game; the melee-the-other-one method saves 3 seconds or 0, an expected 1.5 seconds, so it is better. [22:25]
- A 19-minute rush of an item captioned "Bang Buster" is early but fine since you mostly use it to farm. [17:38]
- Looking for a pick in mid lane was bad even though the Mid Boss after it worked out; you should have all folded. [27:30]

Fighting and positioning:
- Trying to gank a Viscous with three players and no pick hero means he just leaves; ganks like that need a pick hero. [18:43]
- You could ask teammates to pick up the side lane rather than leaving one to anchor, but it can tilt them, so it is a little risky. [19:14]
- You are playing really active; you should leave now, because if Toxic re-procs you die. [20:22]
- You had a massive bag and needed to reset; there was no reason to overstay. [20:53]
- Chasing the Apollo too deep, especially after your Calico died, was the mistake. [21:55]
- Fighting a Drifter under his walker with no map information while Apollo is respawning is bad; he can beeline you and, with boost and his stun, you die. [24:27]
- Retaking the Urn space was the correct play; be around the Urn with your team instead of off alone, where two enemies can collapse on you. [25:28]
- After drawing the enemy onto shrines, kill the target and leave instead of getting cursed. [31:39]
- Summary: hit orbs, deny, don't take your bag into the enemy jungle, stop overstaying, and stop over fighting. [31:39]
- Not every fight was advantageous, and you win a MOBA by taking the most advantaged fights. Before every engage check the minimap and ask: do we have an advantage, or is this over an objective? If neither, back off. [32:40]

General:
- Apollo with his 3 maxed punishes Barrage-ins; he didn't know Apollo's stun could hit two people. [12:03]
