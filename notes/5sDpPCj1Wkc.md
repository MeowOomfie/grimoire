# How to Carry in The Midgame | Coaching an Oracle Wraith
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-09-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5sDpPCj1Wkc

## Covers
Wraith coaching by Midknight; the student is an Oracle-rank Wraith laning with a Yamato into Abrams and Infernus. Video dated 2026-09-01.
Themes: parrying and parry drills, mirroring your lane partner's aggression, farming outside lane and while rotating, card stacking (Spades, Jokers, Diamond), Rift and walker macro, AoE items for farming, and getting side-lane solo kills or ganks in the mid game.

## Claims
Lane (Wraith and Yamato into Abrams and Infernus):
- Going into the lane, mirror your Yamato's aggression; if she plays slow, take farm outside of lane like a normal M1 hero, and if she plays aggressive and you see a chance to kill on your tier one or tier two cards, go for it. [1:02]
- Once your lane partner is at 1 HP at 30 seconds, it becomes a play-slow-and-farm lane. [1:02]
- On a dive, switch to the mispositioned enemy rather than over committing to the one who is lower; using the last cards on the Abrams would have killed him. [1:32]
- Trading a low HP hero for a high HP hero is an advantage for the side that traded away the low HP hero. [2:03]
- Confirm your orbs. [2:03]
- With an insignificant HP advantage you can still win a fight, but don't force anything. [3:04]
- In solo queue it is very strong to mirror your lane partner's aggression. [3:04]
- If your partner is an over aggressive mediocre laner (example: a Shiv), still support him but from a safer distance rather than fully mirroring. [3:34]
- Almost every solo queue lane is winnable; how much to mirror depends on how strong the enemy lane is. [3:34]
- Against a spam-meleeing Abrams, press your parry key instead of melee trading; he is a melee boxer and wins if you get pulled into "monkey mode". [4:04]
- Biggest lane mistake: no farm outside of the lane itself; take tier two camps once they are up at 5 minutes and box run when you have space. [5:04]
- Don't panic dump cards; dumping Hearts is not a big deal, but keep the Diamond because it is your shred. [5:04]
- Don't overfish for a Restorative Shot hit when you should just shoot the minion. [5:34]
- When a Yamato parries or is ulting, the one realistic way to live is to parry her: roll on her and parry the second she ults, since Yamatos usually spam an ability right off the bat. [6:05]
- When the enemy lane has a soul and HP advantage, concede contested tier ones rather than contesting. [6:37]

Mid game farming and cards:
- After winning a fight on their side, push the neutral wave first before going back to your mid; leaving it lost minions. [8:40]
- Wasting a Joker on random farm is really bad; always stack cards. [9:12]
- Avoid wasted routing time: farm a nearby tier two, basement or any camp on the way back, and at minimum farm boxes while rotating. [9:42]
- Wraith players who take Restorative Shot usually do it over Extra Regen or for an earlier gun spike. [10:12]
- Farming for an item spike before Rift is very important; with Rift spawning you should have farmed an extra 90 souls to complete a spike. [10:42]
- Ideally be on the weak side farming and pressuring the enemy walker while Rift happens; don't switch sides if there is no time. [10:42]
- If your team leaned gun before a big Rift fight, pick up BRS as a supportive teamfight tool; extra spirit was not the play here even though the team leaned spirit. [11:12]
- Losing mid walker for a Rift the enemy did not contest is not worth it; if they don't contest, assume they are trading walkers instead and fall back to defend. [11:12]
- Don't solo greed a walker. [11:43]
- Mid walker is the most important walker to keep up and to take; if mid walker goes tier one first it is really bad. [11:43]
- Right before your cooldowns and ult spike come up, slow play: farm, get an item spike and a card stack spike, then look for a fight; let a dying teammate die rather than fight then. [12:17]
- Cycle Clovers so you hold as many Spades and Jokers and a Diamond as possible for raw damage. [12:17]
- Use dead time to farm: the tier two next to your walker instead of running back. [12:47]
- When the enemy has wave priority on many waves they steal your farm; a close soul count can still favor them because they have more farm left to fall back on. [13:18]
- Your team needs to keep the mid wave and shove it away from the walker, but the Wraith should not be the one sitting mid. [13:48]
- Don't get a Club stacked between a Spade and a Joker you want to keep. [14:18]
- Card stacking: early you want as many Spades as possible plus a Diamond for shred; late the same plus as many Jokers as you can; keep a Diamond or Joker on the top or bottom so you can lead with shred. [14:18]
- 100% don't use Jokers to farm except niche cases; Spades generally not either. [14:48]
- When farming, the primary goal is farming efficiency and the sub goal is building a stack of Spades and Jokers. [15:19]
- With Rejuvenator up and all walkers up, put pressure on every walker and see what is weak; if you can choose freely, take mid walker. [15:19]
- Tier one mid walker is ridiculously valuable; strong teams do an early fight, then immediately run back to take the mid walker. [15:49]
- Wraith is not meant to have big impact this early; the real issue is inefficient farming, so the farm lead from AFK side laning never appears. [15:49]
- With ult and a good card stack you can get side-lane solo kills, or ping a teammate (Lash) to gank a pushing Abrams with you. [16:21]
- Abrams is a victim of ganks but hard to solo kill. [16:52]
- As a team, get priority on every lane, see where they defend, then all commit to one walker; don't commit to walkers individually. [17:22]
- Farming priority is wave, then camp, then boxes. [17:52]
- Tier twos are a strong farm target, but the most soul-efficient camps are the hybrids such as basements and the side walker camps; with Ricochet, tier threes are good too. [18:24]
- Wraith's job during Rift is to be the one on the side lane; if Rift is coming and you have a chance to swap sides, take it, but don't force a swap without the opportunity. [18:54]
- Losing a walker hurts much less than losing mid boss. [18:54]
- When the enemy splits between walker and mid boss too hard, go in early and cooldown dump to punish. [19:25]
- Without Tesla Bullets or Ricochet you have no AoE, which hurts your farm; either one gives AoE. [19:58]
- For a Ricochet rush build, Parzival's is good. [20:31]
- Don't sit on an ability (captioned "Cold Stare") for a long time; even using it on a lane creep is better than holding it. [20:31]
- Defending from height is good. [21:31]
- Lacking Ricochet or Tesla Bullets hurt your teamfight damage. [21:31]
- Losing your shrines early is very hard to come back from. [22:01]
- Itemization order: get base damage and AoE such as Tesla Bullets first, then a mark into Spiritual Overflow; Overflow loves the mark and is overtuned. [23:07]
- If your team won't move for a Rift, don't position aggressively; the enemy wants to kill you before the Rift, so give space and look for priority on other lanes instead. [23:37]

Parry drills and summary:
- Parry drill: in Sandbox stand by the melee bot without looking at it and parry off the audio cue. [24:39]
- Once you are decent at it, do the drill in the background while watching a TV show, for about 30 minutes, until parrying the melee sound is muscle memory. [24:39]
- Summary: mirror your lane partner's aggression, and once your partner is very low it becomes a chill farm lane. [25:09]
- Summary: in lane, farm tier twos off spawn and box run whenever you have space. [25:39]
- Summary: stack Spades with Jokers, or Spades with a Diamond. [25:39]
- Summary: the two deaths were a bad teleport and not respecting an overextension. [25:39]
- On an M1 scaling hero, if your team loses before 20 minutes there is not much you can do, but you still must farm efficiently: more camps between waves and boxes while rotating. [26:09]
- Summary: stack cards so you can play for your ult and get side-lane solo kills, or ask a teammate to gank when you have a soul lead. [26:39]
- Play aggressively when you have ult and a good card stack, or when a teammate is hovering you. [27:10]
