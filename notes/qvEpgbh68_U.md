# Stop Building Seven WRONG | Cold Front 3 Max Seven Build Guide
channel: Natan  date: 2025-01-19  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qvEpgbh68_U

## Covers
Seven, Power Surge max first, Cold Front farming, skill order, lane and mid core items, flex slots, late game situational items, Slowing Hex with Static Charge, Ethereal Shift, Mystic Slow

## Claims
- With this build you do not need to AFK farm for 20 minutes to be relevant; you can 1v1 people with more souls than you and kill two people in one magazine before the 12 minute mark, while staying active in the mid game without losing farming speed. [0:34]
- After the 20k souls mark all further items are optional and situational. [1:04]
- Seven's Lightning Ball and Power Surge are heavily gated behind their tier 3 upgrades, so rush maxing one of them ASAP; maxing Power Surge is much better. [1:34]
- Maxing Lightning Ball first as a farming tool is outdated and fails higher in rank, because you are very weak in player combat before about 20 minutes, when the game is often already decided. [1:34]
- Maxed Lightning Ball does about 150 DPS (maybe 200 with items), which is easily avoided by dashing out, and a missed ball leaves you without one for a few seconds, so it is inconsistent. [2:04]
- Maxing ball first gives its tier 3 at 8 minutes, but tier 2 camps (which tier 3 ball one shots) spawn at 5 minutes, take 6 minutes to respawn, and you clear them from about 5 to 7 or 8 minutes, so the ball upgrade arrives when there is nothing left to farm. [2:34]
- Maxed Power Surge gives roughly 300 to 400 targeted DPS that only relies on hitting your shots: about 200 without the tier 3, about 300 with it, and about 400 with Surge of Power at 11k souls. [3:05]
- Farming without ball is why the build takes Cold Front: buy four 500 items, then Cold Front, which you should have at almost exactly 5 minutes if you farm properly, right when tier 2 camps spawn, so you clear them faster. [3:38]
- Basic Magazine gives three more bursts per magazine, so three more Power Surge procs, since Power Surge procs every burst. [4:08]
- Hollow Point Ward gives gun damage for lane poke, stays strong in the mid game so you need not sell it early, and its spirit shield blocks important abilities such as a Yamato 1 or a Vindicta crow; staying above 65% health is easy because of Healing Rite. [4:08]
- Buy Healing Rite as early as you need, sometimes first if you have lost a lot of HP trading; pop it, use the two sprint speed to grab boxes, and Hollow Point Ward is back up when you return. [4:38]
- Extra Health is a no-brainer early because Seven is very squishy; it gives over 200 health. [4:38]
- Extra Spirit and Enduring Spirit add sustain and health; Extra Spirit builds into Improved Spirit and Enduring Spirit into Slowing Hex. [5:09]
- Mystic Shot gives weapon damage, spirit power and a bonus spirit damage bullet every 5 seconds. [5:09]
- Slowing Hex combos with the stun: Static Charge takes 3.5 seconds to proc and Slowing Hex lasts 3 seconds, slowing and silencing movement abilities so Wraith cannot card away, Mirage cannot tornado dodge, Vindicta cannot fly, Abrams cannot charge and Shiv cannot dash, while you hit them with 400 DPS. [5:39]
- Enchanter's Barrier makes you tankier and, while active, gives spirit power and cooldown reduction for Power Surge uptime. [6:09]
- After these items you are at exactly 8k souls, when tier 3 Power Surge arrives; before 8k you are weak and should farm rather than fight, after 8k you start taking fights. [6:09]
- Buy Surge of Power (11k) before Improved Spirit: tested on a full combo it still does more damage, even with the Lightning Ball on the target the whole time. [6:39]
- Fleetfoot is essential ("completely illegal" not to build) because shooting costs 30% movement speed and Seven's movement speed scales with spirit, so he loses more flat speed than others; Fleetfoot removes that penalty. [7:10]
- Take cooldown over duration: cooldown reduces all the many active items (Healing Rite, Cold Front, Mystic Shot passive, Slowing Hex, Fleetfoot, Divine Barrier) and aims for 100% Power Surge uptime for late farming and long fights. [7:10]
- With Improved Spirit, Power Surge lasts 18 seconds on a 33 second cooldown; Improved Cooldown plus Superior Cooldown imbued on Power Surge leaves only 3 seconds of downtime, while Superior Duration leaves 7 seconds (5 even with Boundless Spirit), so cooldown is straight up better. [7:41]
- Soul Shredder Bullets gives 8% spirit amp and spirit lifesteal; when you buy it you can sell Hollow Point Ward. [8:42]
- Improved Cooldown and Soul Shredder Bullets are the first two flex slot items; with no flex slots yet, skip Improved Cooldown and go straight to Improved Spirit. [8:42]
- Buy two of the situational vitality items before 20k souls, that is before Superior Cooldown; usually Healbane (healing reduction, easy to spread since Seven deals spirit damage all the time) and Debuff Reducer (tankier, shortens all negative effects). [9:13]
- Divine Barrier is preference; Reactive Barrier is situational, for example against Bebop, Mo & Krill and Lash; Enduring Speed is good against many movement slows (30% slow resist); Bullet Armor is better a bit later; Suppressor builds into Mystic Slow. [9:44]
- You can sell Healing Rite around this point, and Bullet Armor or Spirit Armor can replace Extra Health. [9:44]
- The core finishes at 23k after selling two items and needs only one flex slot, so it still works when your team is behind. [10:14]
- Ethereal Shift should be bought almost every game: a 24 second cooldown, usable several times a fight to dodge things like a Spirit Lash or a Dynamo flying in to ult you. [10:44]
- Mystic Slow (built from Suppressor) should be bought almost every game; it reduces fire rate and slows, and wrecks fire rate reliant heroes such as Wraith, Haze, Infernus, Mirage, gun Ivy and gun McGinnis. [11:14]
- Boundless Spirit is a no-brainer, slot efficient upgrade from Improved Spirit; sell for it late game if needed. [11:14]
- Skill maxing in this build: Power Surge maxed at 8k, Static Charge at 16k and Lightning Ball at 30k, so picking up Rapid Recharge around 30k gives a useful late game ball. [11:44]
- Silence Glyph cannot miss after the stun (Slowing Hex, then a nearly 2 second stun, then Silence Glyph); Curse is similar but targeted, pricier and blocks item use; both are good against casters such as Pocket, Lash and Yamato. [11:44]
- Echo Shard is for a double stun; Escalating Exposure is a very late game damage option. [12:14]
- Late game take both Improved Bullet Armor and Improved Spirit Armor; with Improved Spirit Armor you can sell Enchanter's Barrier. [12:45]
- Debuff Remover cleanses things like Pocket ult, Infernus burn, a Mo & Krill arm or a fire rate slow; Leech is a near every game upgrade from Healbane; Metal Skin against bullet heavy comps; Unstoppable against lots of CC; Colossus against constant divers such as Yamato, Lash, Shiv and Abrams. [12:45]
- Spiritual Overflow is the most consistent late weapon option for a big DPS gain. [13:16]
- Sharpshooter also extends Power Surge's falloff range, good if you hit from long range all game or your team is behind and you must play at range. [13:16]
- Burst Fire is a good late game option; Alchemical Fire is currently "broken" and chains with the stun, longer with Echo Shard; Crippling Headshot reduces bullet and spirit resist on headshots, which helps the team. [13:46]
- Spellslinger Headshots is kind of bad, never prioritize it; Toxic Bullets is fine against HP stackers that heal, such as Lady Geist and Abrams; Tesla Bullets is not recommended. [14:16]
- If you dislike Cold Front, take Tesla Bullets right before Slowing Hex instead, since it has good DPS with Power Surge active and also farms. [14:47]
- His usual late picks: Ethereal Shift, Mystic Slow, Boundless Spirit, both armors, sometimes Sharpshooter or Rapid Recharge, then Leech and Spiritual Overflow; sell Cold Front late, or much earlier if you need the slot, since it is only for early farming. [14:47]
