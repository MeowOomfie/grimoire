## Covers
Wraith coaching by Midknight: one Mystic-rank (mid-low) Wraith player reviewed over a full game, a few days after the big map and jungle patch (2026-10-06).
Themes: stacking Card Trick cards (spades and jokers) instead of poking, the Wraith lane plan, farm efficiency and the wave-camp cycle, doubles and permanent buffs, green spike and not over-buying greens, when an M1 rotates to fights, ult usage and fight range, and item micro (Counterspell, dispel, Silencer).

## Claims
Lane plan (Wraith, Mystic):
- Wraith lane in theory is a slow play lane where you stack cards: mostly spades, maybe a joker or a diamond for shred. Jokers are king, but you don't get many early. [0:30]
- Look for trades when you have a stack of spades, ideally off your tier 2 AP spike (more damage and scaling) or off your ult spike, or punish an enemy who mispositions. [1:01]
- Wraith being a weak laner is propaganda; she is just not a strong laner in a meta full of strong laners. At mid-low ranks enemies troll enough that slow playing for cards does not lose you the lane. [1:31]
- Don't waste cards; save card stacks for your tier 2 AP spike and your ult spike. Using cards on your ult spike there was a waste because they did no meaningful damage. [2:01]
- Card poke matters less now that heal snacks are on the map, even if low-rank enemies don't yet know the routes. [2:01]
- You are overusing cards in lane; stack them more. [2:31]
- Teleporting in (Project Mind) is fine if you have cards stacked; without cards your kill potential is really low and it is a big negative trade. [3:04]
- With cards stacked you could get kills yourself instead of needing a support's gank. [3:36]
- Box starting in lane is great early, especially once your tier 2 camp is farmed; generally box start before camps are up on the map. [3:36]
- On this side there are more tier 2 or hybrid tier 1/tier 2 camps close by to farm after the construction site camp; one looks much further than it is. [4:06]
- The most important thing an M1 does in lane is optimize farm. When you have wave pressure, be on the 5-minute timer farming your tier 2 camps. [4:37]
- Giving up a tower instead of dying for it was right. [4:37]
- Your main goal is soul acquisition. Creating side lane pressure instead of joining that fight was perfect. [5:08]

Fights and rotations:
- An M1 generally does not rotate to fights until about 22 minutes in an average solo queue game, when fights start ending in mid boss; at high level (Eternus) it is more like 18 minutes. The M1 still avoids the small one-pick or two-pick fights. [5:39]
- Spades do double damage. Without cards your ult does nothing; instead ask for a gank, and when someone arrives, press 4. [6:09]
- You showed the Silver too much respect when your 2 was up, then too little when you walked into her face. [6:40]
- After ulting the Silver, shoot her head while she can't use her hammer or movement; you gave her far too much distance. [7:12]
- Play a mid distance shooting her, and if she engages on you, teleport away; you should not be trying to fight Silver head-on. [7:43]
- Green spike: a very common mid-low mistake is skipping it. Getting 4.8k souls in green items for the extra 40% HP is insanely strong; don't over-itemize defensively, but get the spike. [8:17]
- You need to be farming for your next big spike, not looking to fight. [8:17]
- Farm doubles: permanent buffs are strong on heroes that scale a lot, and with HP to spare you should take them. You never took your theater doubles and the Rem took your mid doubles twice. [8:47]
- Keep splitting while both walkers in that lane are up; if your walker were down, shove the wave and rotate instead. [9:48]
- You were too scared of the Apollo: you are 19k to his 14k, and unless you badly mess up he can't 1v1 you, so don't forfeit wave pressure the second he shows up. [10:18]
- Pulling an enemy into your side lane gives value similar to joining the fight, since you take one enemy off it. [10:18]

Items and build:
- In this comp Counterspell should come out over the dispel, and the green spike should have come before your offensive item. [10:50]
- Fortitude's value is staying on the map while you are being poked; buying it late loses the mid game value, where small trades and short respawns happen most. [11:21]
- Fortitude is better when you are ahead; don't buy it when behind. [11:52]
- Your AP order was not the build you said you copied; that build puts a point in the ult before card max. [12:22]
- When a Lash groups you, your instinct should be to teleport out instantly, since their whole team will be looking there. [12:52]
- You should have ulted the Lash rather than the Silver. [13:23]
- Some players only ever heavy melee (common on Abrams and Billy players); after one interaction you can tell and stop trying to parry a light. [13:54]
- Farm rhythm: shove one wave, shove two waves, go farm all the camps, repeat. Your map position was right but your souls didn't reflect it because you farmed inefficiently. [14:55]
- Your build is too green. Two greens is fine (dispel and Counterspell here, or Fortitude and dispel depending on comp), but three is too many and you stop doing meaningful damage. [14:55]
- A hand of two hearts and a diamond is trash; look for spades and jokers. One diamond is okay for shred. [15:56]
- Risen has a good Wraith build, which he believes is the top build. [16:26]
- A fight can be good and a mid boss can be good, but you can't take both at the same time. [16:59]
- Coming from behind a staggered enemy is good but risky when the fight is staggered. [17:32]
- Wraith does the most damage at mid range, but not if that range gets you hooked or hit by an ability that kills you. [18:33]
- In a 4v1 it is right to play far back even though you do less damage. [19:04]
- Don't dive three low enemies alone unless you are confident in your micro with Counterspell and dispel up; when your team is winning by a lot there is no reason to force a risky play. Knowing your own limits and backing off is fine. [19:36]
- Silencer is the spirit counter and is insanely broken: one bullet gives the target minus 25% damage for 7 seconds, before even counting the silence. [20:38]
- Witch Mail is wrong on Wraith because she is too squishy. Spellbreaker is for spirit nukers like Grey Talon, Lash and Vindicta. [21:08]
- When enemies stagger hard, just kill them all and then take mid boss. [21:39]
- Your dispel and Counterspell were barely used; an unused Counterspell is about 6k wasted. [22:11]
- To learn item micro, play Street Brawl and take the item every time, or buy it every game and focus on it. [22:41]
- At this level Counterspell for the Lash ult is fine, but at high level good Lashes wait it out; there go Unstoppable or dodge, depending on the enemy comp. [23:11]
- Start calling mid bosses earlier; getting your team onto what counts as an early mid boss at this level (about 22 minutes) wins a lot of games. [24:12]
- Super rift minions aggro on Venator traps and just stand staring at them. [24:12]

Summary notes:

- Your permanent buffs are far too low compared with the Rem; take them before he does. [25:42]
