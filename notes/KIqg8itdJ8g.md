# Everything You Need To Know About Deadlock's New Map
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-02-28
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KIqg8itdJ8g

## Covers
Guide to the new map (Midtown, as captioned) on the patch that removed purple lane: Sinner's Sacrifice heavy melee timing, mid lane layout, mid boss routes, vents, box and buff spots, secret shop, camps, flex slot rule changes.
Midknight, fast-paced map guide, no one coached. Patch of late February 2025.

## Claims
- The map now has only three lanes; purple lane is gone and all three are duo lanes. [0:00]
- Sinner's Sacrifice (vending machine) now shows lights on top as its HP bar; when one light is left it starts an animation. [0:00]
- When the last light flashes you can light or heavy melee it; light melee awards one buff, heavy melee awards three, so always try to heavy melee it. [0:30]
- Timing: the second that light lights up, start holding heavy melee; practice it a couple of times and it becomes consistent. [0:30]
- The tall arch in mid lane makes flyers like Vindicta and Grey Talon significantly worse in mid, because you can hide behind it when they go in the air. [1:01]
- The arch makes Lash extremely strong in mid, since a couple of wall jumps give a big Ground Strike. [1:01]
- Lane trade advice: give your Lash the mid lane, and move your Vindicta or Grey Talon out of mid. [1:01]
- To get on top of the mid arch fast, stand on the red sign, do a corner boost and you get up quickly. [1:32]
- Small alcoves with veils near mid are good for ganks: wait in the veil, then go in with something like Dynamo's ultimate. [1:32]
- Routes to mid boss: the subway station, a zipline elevator to an underground area, or a hole that drops you right into the mid boss area. [2:04]
- When heading to mid boss, go through a veil and up the rope instead of the stairs, because you cannot slide up stairs and burn more stamina. [2:04]
- The underground boxes and buff pickups near mid spawn at 10 minutes, so have at least a couple of teammates rotate to mid lane at 10 minutes to fight for them. [2:35]
- Bridge buffs are more important than the mid box room. [2:35]
- After clearing the boxes your wave will probably push back over you; a safe exit is dropping down past more boxes, through a veil and up a rope into your back line. [3:05]
- The box room is not worth rotating your whole team for, but adding one more person to mid for it can be important. [3:05]
- Bridge buffs moved up to a higher bridge, which gives Lash a decent spike and makes things harder for flyers, though slightly harder for Lash to ult. [3:05]
- To reach the bridge: go up the rope in the Jazz Records building, or bounce off a wall into one of the two vents. [3:36]
- Wall jump route to the bridge vent: wall jump off the small corner, melee cancel, then hit the vent. [3:36]
- The map is bigger without the fourth lane, so rotating is slower; use vents and slides to rotate and gank faster. [4:06]
- Behind your mid Guardian, slightly to the right, a building has two vents for quick rotations and box farming, plus a camp. [4:06]
- Left of your Guardian there is still a medium camp, and that area is a good entrance into the subway for farming boxes and getting back to the teleporters. [4:36]
- The secret shop moved: drop into the sewer grate between your mid lane and side lane; there are boxes there and a teleporter back to the subway. [5:08]
- From the other side of the map you can teleport to the secret shop in an emergency, buy, and teleport back; another route is next to the mid boss entrance, marked with a teleporter sign. [5:08]
- Dropping off the zipline at a certain spot is about half a second faster to a fight. [5:39]
- Boxes moved around and are now mostly outside; there is a good chunk on the side of the map by the bull and bear areas where you can farm small camps quickly. [5:39]
- There are more hybrid camps (two medium and one big minion, or one of each) and more camps overall, which makes hyper farming heroes like Infernus and Haze better. [6:09]
- A camp was added where purple lane used to be, next to another medium camp and the City Garage. [6:09]
- You now only need to kill two Guardians for your first flex slot, and only one Walker for your second flex slot. [6:39]
- The basement camps are no longer a dead end; you can escape down into the subway to the teleporter and boxes. [6:39]
- Many buildings have accessible roofs with lots of boxes; grab as many as you can. Roofs are why the speaker expects Lash to dominate. [7:10]
- A vent on top of the City Garage takes you toward mid lane, good for ganking from an off angle. [7:10]
- In bases you can no longer go through the glass window; you must go around to fight the enemy sieging. [7:10]
- Cover in front of Guardians was made taller and a wagon was added to hide behind while sieging; the other side's wagon is harder to hide behind. [7:41]
