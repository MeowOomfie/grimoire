## Covers
Patch reaction by aquatiq to the 2026-08-01 ranked update: new rank names and sub-ranks, ranked eligibility, seasons, calibration, rank points, loss protection, hero pre-selection, then a game on Seven.
Mostly reading and reacting to the ranked rules, with gameplay commentary afterwards.

## Claims
- Ranks are renamed and split into main ranks and sub-ranks; the new names include Initiate, Seeker, Acolyte, Sentinel, Mystic, Ritualist, Emissary, Oracle, Phantom, Ascendant and Eternus. [0:00]
- To queue ranked you need at least 60 wins in the standard mode. [0:31]
- Ranked expects you to know the heroes you play: if you have very few games on a hero the game will not let you play it in ranked. [0:31]
- Ranked has seasons; the first started on this patch day and ends October 7. [0:31]
- The highest rank you reach in a season is saved permanently on your rank history page. [1:02]
- Each season starts with 8 calibration games and a soft reset that keeps some history of your past performance to seed you. [1:02]
- You can exit calibration at most at Oracle 6, and you cannot party during calibration matches. [1:33]
- After each match a progress bar shows your rank points; a win or loss is about plus or minus 250 points on average, varying with matchmaker expectations and win streaks. [1:33]
- Each sub-rank promotion needs 1,000 points, except the last sub-rank before a new rank (for example Mystic 6 to Ritualist 1), which needs 2,000. [2:04]
- From Eternus 1 up there is no fixed point amount; thresholds update daily on a percentile basis. [2:34]
- Rank loss protection: you get five games of protection on the boundary of a full rank, so at Mystic 1 with zero points you keep Mystic until you lose five games. [3:05]
- Hero selection now happens before the match is formed; the matchmaker uses the queue to give players their preferred heroes and build reasonable team compositions. [3:35]
- Where the queue is thin, the matchmaker may not manage this, so you can also change your hero in a new pregame waiting area and adjust your starting lane there. [4:36]
- Zipline lane swapping is no longer possible. [4:36]
- Pause is disabled until 2 minutes into the match. [4:36]
- He saw a large performance and frame rate improvement this patch, though late game in base still feels a bit laggy. [5:07]
- On Seven, the plan is just to farm after lane. [6:42]
- Should you get 15 wins on every hero? No need; get good at one carry, one support and one damage hero. [17:25]
- Fighting on the enemy walker while far behind is a mistake; he called it out on his team. [17:58]
- When the enemy is mostly dead and you are ahead, go mid boss; he could not believe the enemy team did not go mid when they had the chance. [19:01]
- Against a team with knockdown, he bought Unstoppable even though he did not want to spend the slot. [20:35]
- Against a hero doing huge gun damage he wanted Metal Skin. [21:38]
