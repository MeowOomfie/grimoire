## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (streamed 12/9/25, before the delayed December patch). Three free replay reviews: an Oracle Abrams (lane with Viscous into Haze and Lash), an Archon Victor (lane with Pocket into Haze and Shiv) and a Phantom Paradox.
Then live coaching of his South American pro team (captioned Colossus) in two scrims, covering drafts, Doorman tech, sieging after ganks, urn and Rejuvenator play, and lanes.
Recurring themes: Trophy Collector is a tempo killer, don't ego fight when behind, farm instead of joining pointless fights, siege rules, and the mid boss flowchart.
