# How To Play Around Objectives | Emissary Bebop Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2024-11-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6tKY5BN6yPA

## Covers
Midknight coaching review of an Emissary-rank Bebop in a duo lane with Infernus against Geist and Vindicta; the player asked for help with post-laning and late game macro.
Themes: Healing Rite and teammate HP, respecting poke, ulting from height, roaming after pushing a wave, autopiloting down a lane, taking fewer disadvantaged fights, playing around the last Walker and Rejuvenator, secret shop routing.
Late-2024 video (patch of early November 2024, game played on an older patch); the game has changed a lot since, so treat numbers and item names as dated.

## Claims
Bebop laning (Emissary, duo with Infernus):
- When rolling in to place a bomb, roll in to your max bomb range and roll away, rather than rolling between both enemies. [1:01]
- He was on autopilot in lane, just hitting minions; look for more hooks and rolling bombs. [1:01]
- Buying Healing Rite for a teammate who does not buy it is good, but wait a moment before casting it in case the enemy still has a shot ready. [1:31]
- When your laning partner has Healing Rite on him, do not walk past your stairs; he cannot peek with you. [1:31]
- At this point in the game do not save for a tier 2 item; buy two 500-soul items instead. [2:03]
- Level abilities with Tab-click or Alt-click instead of clicking them, so you can level mid-fight and use the new point right away (a hook on Geist could have become a kill). [2:03]
- Get in the habit of breaking every box you slide past; there is no downside. [2:33]
- If you skip extra regen (Restorative Shot or another defensive item) because you have Healing Rite, play at your Guardian, or behind it against Geist, until Healing Rite is off cooldown. [3:03]
- He was not respecting the Vindicta tether: if Vindicta hits a tether, Geist cannot miss her bomb. [3:03]
- Use the "I can heal you" ping (or voice) so your teammate knows to come back for Healing Rite. [3:33]
- Track your partner's HP and position; once you put Healing Rite on Infernus he will tuck back, so you must play safe. [4:04]
- Grab a 200-soul bag even if it costs an Essence Bomb hit. [4:04]
- If you walk in intending to Hyper Beam, jump on top of the bridge first so you can see behind cover. [4:34]
- Do not dive when your partner is at 10-15% HP at the Guardian; a good team collapses on you and you die for nothing. [4:34]
- Once you have the souls for your tier 2 item, buy it right away. [5:36]
- Enchanter's Barrier is a good buy against Geist and Vindicta; it blocks her snipe. [5:36]
- Turning to knock a hooked enemy back with the uppercut is good. [6:06]
- Ulting from the bottom of stairs while looking uphill is much weaker because Bebop's beam cannot hit the ground; jump, double jump or walk up a bit (out of Guardian range) so you can see the ground on top. [6:06]

Roaming and mid game macro:
- He was lane locked; fine in this rank, but to climb (around Oracle) roam to an enemy lane whenever you push your wave, using the hook's gank potential. [6:37]
- Past 8 minutes with the enemy Guardian down, staying lane locked is less excusable. [7:08]
- Patch note: after 8 minutes, one hero farming a lane gets 100% of minion souls and two heroes in a lane each get 75% (50% extra total, down from 100%), so it is worse to stay in lane as two. [7:39]
- Instead of sieging a high-armor Walker at 10 minutes, roam, look for ganks and hooks in other lanes and take bridge buffs. [8:10]
- Patch note: two patches ago Walker armor fully dropped at 12 minutes; on the latest patch it drops later (captioned "60", likely 16 minutes), so early Walker sieges are even worse. [8:41]
- Bridge buffs are valuable; take them when you are close. [9:12]
- A defensive green build (Healing Nova, barriers) is fine but makes Bebop's ult weak. [9:43]
- The biggest issue was running mindlessly down his lane after laning; roam, farm camps and show presence in other lanes. [9:43]
- By 12 minutes there are no set lanes; play for objectives. [10:13]
- Hook teammates out of bad spots; many lower-rank Bebops do not. [11:14]
- Once a caught teammate escapes, walk away instead of taking the leftover fight. [11:44]
- Let go of right click (zoom) when tracking a moving target. [11:44]
- Do not put yourself in fights you only survive if the enemy misplays, such as fighting a Vindicta who may have ult up. [12:15]
- Do not chase too deep after a kill. [12:47]
- Do not rush hooks: take an extra second to line up the hook; this builds better fundamentals than flashy quick hooks. [13:17]
- He was in too many deathball fights; instead of autopiloting to the next fight, farm, split and take objectives. [13:47]
- Your match timeline should not be wall-to-wall kill and death dots; it should have breaks where you catch waves or farm. [14:48]
- In this rank, split pushing is the strongest thing in the game and will rank you up fast, even though Bebop is a teamfight hero. [14:48]
- In mid ranks the upper camps rarely get taken; farm them. [15:19]
- With a player advantage, pushing into mid boss is good; taking the fight before finishing mid boss was the right call. [15:19]
- Use the bomb to clear and siege waves faster (bomb plus minion punch); a bomb would have saved the base Guardian. [16:20]
- After missing a hook on a fleeing target, check the map, notice your team is not with you and back off. [16:50]
- Use the secret shop instead of the base shop when it is nearby: in the same time you get the item plus about 400 souls from boxes and a medium camp, about 2,000 souls over five resets a game. [20:24]

Objectives and the last Walker:
- When your team has one Walker left, keep that lane pushed as far toward the enemy as you can without overextending; never let it pass about the 40% line, or they can brute force it. The enemy should do the same with their last Walker. [17:52]
- With your last Walker as the only objective anyone can take without winning a fight, most of your team should be on that side of the map; if four of you fight on the other side, the enemy sends three to kill it and get their flex slot. [18:52]
- After getting kills with no objectives up, take the enemy jungle farm and shove the wave out as far as you can. [19:53]
- Do not let a wave get deep, especially after you got kills in that lane. [20:24]
- Winning a fight but losing your Walker is a lost trade; the side fight risked the only objective. [21:24]
- With four enemies dead, push base Guardians or at minimum steal their farm to starve them, instead of resetting. [21:54]
- Fighting over your mid boss is a good fight. [22:56]
- React to audio cues like Haze's; turn your game audio up if you cannot hear them. [22:56]
- When the enemy has Rejuvenator, wait it out and farm instead of fighting, unless they force a fight down a lane. [23:27]
- Rotating to a fight is good when you are farming and react to a bad fight your team is already in; it is bad when you autopilot toward the group preemptively, which encourages fights and costs farm. [24:27]
- Starting pointless fights with your hook is especially bad. [24:58]
- Do not walk into an enemy ult that gives more lifesteal than your DPS; hook from outside it. [25:30]
- His macro became good once the enemy got Rejuvenator (past 20 minutes): farming, skipping bad fights, showing up only to hook teammates out; that switch should happen around 12 minutes. [26:32]
- Knockdown is one of Bebop's favorite items, especially with Enchanter's Barrier; buy it to shut down the fed Vindicta. [27:02]
- Do not fight in the mid boss pit while the mid boss hits you; kill mid boss, then push a lane to knock Patron. [27:33]
- Mid boss has no weak spots, so never right click (zoom) there; zooming cut his vision and Haze and Vindicta burst him unseen. [28:03]
- When two players down, do not take a fight; spam ping Retreat, run, and wait for respawns instead of staggering them. [29:37]
- Bomb a contested wave and run if you want it; the bomb should full clear it. [30:07]
- Ult from height: walk around and up the stairs or onto the bridge and ult from above. [30:07]
- You win MOBA games by taking as many fights as possible where you have the advantage, usually a player advantage, sometimes a Rejuvenator advantage. [31:09]

Notes summary:
- Pay attention to teammate HP, especially when you used Healing Rite on them. [31:39]
- Respect enemy poke below 300 or 200 HP, especially Vindicta tether into Geist bomb. [32:09]
- Most Bebop ults should be in the air; keep a few emergency ground ults for the spirit lifesteal. [32:09]
- After pushing your wave, roam to the other duo lane or the solo lane for a hook, combo and kill to spread your lane advantage. [32:40]
- From 8 to 14 minutes after taking the Guardian, stop thinking of it as your lane and look at the whole map. [33:10]
- Take half a second longer to aim hooks instead of copying flashy quick hooks. [33:40]
- Play around objectives: do not posture on the opposite side from your Walker or Urns, and after kills take objectives or steal farm. [34:10]
