# 3 tips for every hero - Part 1
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-08-17
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x_ISyG5yvFg

## Covers
Three quick tips each for the first row of the hero roster: Abrams, Bebop (tips from a guest high rank NA Bebop player), Calico, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy and Kelvin.
For players who already play these heroes and want small tech and habits they may be missing.

## Claims
- Abrams: you can cast Siphon Life while charging (Shoulder Charge), rather than after the charge, for extra DPS. [0:00]
- Abrams: both Shoulder Charge and your ultimate can be used to escape as well as to initiate; do not only use them to go in. [0:30]
- Abrams: you need some mobility to set up your charge, so get Warp Stone or Phantom Strike and target backliners you can one-shot, such as Wraith. [1:00]
- Bebop (guest tip): with the ability at level two, holding the button still has a wind-up; time it properly and it fires instantly instead of bugging out. New players hold the mouse button down through the whole thing, which is really bad. [1:31]
- Bebop (guest tip): Sticky Bomb has more range on allies than on enemies; on enemies you now need actual melee range, so the enemy bomb is about reading their movement, while on allies (especially ones punching) you can cheese it from far away. [2:01]
- Bebop (guest tip): no one is scared of Bebop until he has his hook, so you can legit run at them; he used to rush Fortitude and roam, running straight at enemies in mid or a side lane, and they would mess up from the threat alone. [2:31]
- Calico: with the max ultimate upgrade, the ultimate resets cat form, so ult and then spam 3 to always come out in cat form, even if you took damage (which normally puts cat on cooldown); useful for escaping. [3:01]
- Calico: do not aim Gloom Bombs at the top or middle of the body; aim at the lower body, legs or feet, and you hit them far more consistently. [3:01]
- Calico: Leaping Slash is a low cooldown mobility tool; use it after a wall jump to climb onto heights and escape chases. [3:31]
- Dynamo: Quantum Entanglement can reach people through walls, so you can save allies who seem out of reach. [3:31]
- Dynamo: use Quantum Entanglement to secure the Urn when a teammate goes to deliver it. [4:03]
- Dynamo: he is incredible at sieging walkers, since he can dodge the walker stomp with Quantum Entanglement and stun the walker with his Singularity ultimate, which makes walker dives trivial. [4:03]
- Grey Talon: do not be afraid to use the owl (Guided Owl) to clear waves, especially when someone is split pushing on the other side of the map and nobody can go back. [4:34]
- Grey Talon: use Spirit Snare on objectives or doorways; on the Urn, an enemy who walks near it drops the Urn, immediately giving your team the edge in the Urn fight. [4:34]
- Grey Talon: use your second ability as an escape rather than always for damage; cancel it and go around roofs with it, which matters on spirit Grey Talon whose escapes are otherwise limited. [5:06]
- Haze: you can slide while ulting; sliding down stairs as you go makes you significantly faster and takes you much further than walking. [5:06]
- Haze: you can use Sleep Dagger and items while invisible, such as Silence Wave, Cold Front, Knockdown and Slowing Hex, to set up ganks or even clear waves (Cold Front near a wave clears it). [5:37]
- Haze: in your match history, Haze's top damage type is always Fixation, because Fixation is most of Haze's damage; do not build gun damage on Haze, build fire rate, which gets Fixation stacks faster. [5:37]
- Holliday: to lasso someone without flying too far or too high, lasso them, place the Bounce Pad, get on it and dash forward right away; you can do the same with Warp Stone right after taking the Bounce Pad, so teammates can easily shoot the target. [6:08]
- Holliday: with max Crackshot and Ricochet, the multiple headshots from Ricochet, on minions or heroes, all reduce the Crackshot cooldown, making Crackshot almost permanent. [6:08]
- Holliday: the barrel (Powder Keg) sets up the rest of your kit, so use it before you lasso and to set up Crackshot; barreled targets are fairly easy to headshot. [6:38]
- Infernus: every one of your abilities refreshes Afterburn, even the ultimate, so space out your abilities to keep refreshing Afterburn on the target. [6:38]
- Infernus: you can use items and abilities during Flame Dash, such as Cold Front, Slowing Hex, Napalm or your ultimate. [7:09]
- Infernus: if you need to reload during Flame Dash you can start the reload while dashing; with most abilities you have to reload before, but Flame Dash lets you do it during. [7:09]
- Ivy: using your ultimate as you come off the zipline keeps your momentum, allowing very quick rotates; it also keeps your position in the air during escapes. [7:09]
- Ivy: in the laning stage, throw Kudzu Bomb when the wave drops to hit all the minions and push the wave further. [7:39]
- Ivy: use the Ivy ultimate to set up Stone Form. [7:39]
- Kelvin: when you are in or about to be in an engagement and several teammates near you are low, use the Dome (Frozen Shelter) to reset them and get them healthy for the fight. [7:39]
- Kelvin: Ice Path is a great escape; keep climbing and take sharp turns around buildings to be as hard to catch as possible during the laning stage. [8:09]
- Kelvin: always try to hit both the enemy heroes and the troopers with your grenade, or both heroes. [8:09]
