# How to Transition to The Mid Game | Deadlock Guide
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-10-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7aH1OFo2xxY

## Covers
A short flow chart for the lane-to-mid-game transition (8 minutes to the first rift around 11 to 13 minutes), after the map change that hurt Calico.
Sinner's Sacrifices at 8:00 (behind vs ahead), invading doubles, 10:00 buffs and urn, the pre-rift spike window, and setting or slow pushing waves before the rift. For any rank, with notes on what changes by elo.

## Claims
- The mid game transition runs from 8 minutes to the first rift, roughly 11 to 13 minutes. [0:00]
- Sinner's Sacrifices spawn at 8:00; set up the 7:30 wave ahead of time for what you want to do on them. [0:32]
- If you are behind, look at the map and go secure the closest Sinner's Sacrifice on your side, because an enemy team that is ahead with wave priority should be trying to invade them. [0:32]
- The single Sinner's Sacrifices sit at the edge of every lane and to the right of blue lane, and they are very free to invade. [1:03]
- The indoor Sinner's Sacrifice he shows is the hardest in the game to farm (maybe excluding the Sapphire side doubles): it limits your information and your route to safety is long, so be very careful on it and have a lot of mid lane priority. [1:33]
- The other indoor Sinner's Sacrifice is safer because you can check the long corridor and you are next to your guardian if jumped. [2:03]
- If you are ahead, get priority on the 7:30 or 7:45 wave, shove as fast as you can, then go invade the enemy Sinner's Sacrifices and take them. [2:03]
- Sinner's Sacrifices matter because they give a lot of souls and permanent buffs that are not offset by comeback souls or comeback mechanics. [2:34]
- Taking permanent buffs from Sinner's Sacrifices and statues is a key way for tempo heroes to push a lead without one mistake throwing it away; spending the first 20 minutes invading their Sinner's Sacrifices makes a late comeback much harder. [3:06]
- After single Sinner's Sacrifices, graduate to invading the doubles, such as the theater doubles, which are very easy to invade because the camp is open, you have information and you can route out to the roof (Rem players know this). [3:06]
- At Oracle or below, and especially Mystic or below, you can invade the double Sinner's Sacrifices right off spawn because nobody will contest you. [3:37]
- At higher elo, only invade the doubles off spawn with priority in the corresponding lanes, especially mid lane, because shoved waves force enemies to respond and keep them out of their jungle. [4:07]
- Pretty much never invade double Sinner's Sacrifices alone unless you are an assassin looking for a kill; to steal and secure them, invade with teammates, generally three people including yourself. [4:38]
- After taking an invaded Sinner's Sacrifice, run away; whoever has the biggest bag should be kept alive, and someone without a bag should die for them. [5:09]
- If you are dead to rights while holding a bag, path to the secret shop and spend it quickly so you don't drop it when you die. [5:09]
- While invading at 8 minutes, look for kills on enemies coming to or already hitting a Sinner's Sacrifice, since they will be lower HP. [5:09]
- Even with dash parrying removed, dashing or simply walking onto a Sinner's Sacrifice the enemy is hitting and parrying nets far more kills than you expect, because players hitting it are often looking at the minimap or the shop; it is pretty much a free kill. [5:39]
- In high elo the Sinner's Sacrifices are normally all off the map by about 9 minutes; in average elo it takes noticeably longer. [6:10]
- As a team, aim to have all Sinner's Sacrifices off the map by 10 minutes at the latest, because buffs spawn at 10:00. [6:10]
- At 10:00 secure the buffs off spawn, especially stacking the yellow side, because that is the side the first urn spawns on. [6:41]
- Urn side rule: if the minute ends in 5 the urn is green side, if it ends in 0 it is yellow side. [6:41]
- Ideally secure both buffs, then immediately have at least one teammate grab the urn; in low elo, if nobody grabs it, it is better you run it than no one, whatever hero you are. [6:41]
- An urn is worth about 60 to 70% of the souls of a rift, yet far fewer people fight over it than over rifts. [7:12]
- Around 9:30 angle yourself for a power spike: spend the bag from farming Sinner's Sacrifices, reset your HP if needed, and be strong, healthy and holding as little unspent souls as possible when the buffs spawn. [7:12]
- His high-rank Calico flow: fight and invade at 8:00, the Sinner's Sacrifices are gone by 8:30 to 9:00, then heal, run tunnels, take a minute to reach a spike, then be on the buff and ideally the urn. [7:42]
- From about 10:45 to 12:00 is the simplest part of the mid game: pause and decide what you need before the rift (a big item, a big spike, cycling your bag, healing, or splitting during the rift as a gun hero) and get it from camps, waves or remaining Sinner's Sacrifices. [8:13]
- Have teammates scout the side lanes and call when the rift is spawning so you get a heads up. [8:43]
- When any objective is happening (rift spawning, urn being run, mid boss being taken) you need to be on the map creating pressure; you pretty much can't be farming. [8:43]
- Exception before the early rift: if you are at about 2,800 souls and want a 3,200 item, farm a camp to reach that spike, because a big spike is very important for the rift. [9:13]
- One of the best things before the rift, especially in mid lane and the matching side lane, is to set the wave: at the 20 second rift-spawning timer, shove the wave, then rotate to the rift. [9:44]
- If teammates spot the rift spawning 40 seconds early, push the first and second waves to create more tempo and pressure that forces enemies to respond. [9:44]
- Advanced (around Oracle and above): with the rift about 40 seconds out, let the enemy shove the wave into you and soft freeze by your walker, then shove the next waves immediately. [10:14]
- Or slow push: kill only the healing trooper of a wave so it pushes up slowly; by the time you rotate to the rift the wave reaches about mid lane, so an enemy must leave after the rift opens to clear it. [10:14]
- Don't hold a freeze for more than one wave, or you forfeit a lot of jungle. [10:44]
