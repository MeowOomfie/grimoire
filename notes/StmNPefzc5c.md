# Why You Feel Weak in Deadlock (Even When You’re Ahead)
channel: Metro Deadlock

## Covers
Power spikes: what they are (items, 4.8k investment bonuses, maxed abilities) and why you must fight on them instead of farming.
A hero-by-hero walk through the roster with each hero's spikes, when they are strongest and whether to fight or farm.
For players who treat every hero like a carry and keep farming after their peak.

## Claims
- Power spikes are points in the game where your character is at its strongest, and every hero has them at different points. [0:00]
- The three main sources of a spike are items, investment bonuses and leveling an ability to a strong point. Item example: Dynamo buying Refresher gets two Black Holes and becomes very strong in fights. [0:30]
- The 4.8k investment spikes (marked by a star in the current meta) are by far the strongest spikes because they give the most value per soul. [1:00]
- Example investment spike: Warden buying his first 4.8k in gun and suddenly doing 49% extra gun damage. [1:00]
- Ability spike examples: maxing Infernus's Afterburn takes base burn damage from 12 to 35; maxing Yamato's 1 adds damage and spirit scaling. [1:30]
- If you do not fight on your power spikes you neglect your hero's potential and lose your team the game. [2:01]
- Think of a match as roughly 30 to 40 minutes in which different heroes are strong at different points; a hero strongest in the early mid game (for example Billy) who keeps farming then removes their biggest advantage, and that time never comes back. [2:01]
- Many Deadlock heroes peak in the early mid game; if you drag the game out and don't capitalize on your spike you fall behind. [2:32]
- Not every hero should fight early mid game: hard carries need a lot of farm and spike in the late game or late mid game. [3:03]
- Abrams is not the strongest in lane and not very lethal there, but he really can't die. [4:04]
- Abrams's first spikes come early when you max his 1 (Siphon Life) or 2 (Shoulder Charge); good builds line up the max with a 4.8k investment spike and good items. [4:04]
- Abrams is not great at farming jungle; spending the mid game farming on him lowers your chances to win. [5:05]
- Once Abrams has his first and especially second ability maxed, look for fights constantly: max stun duration, multiple Shoulder Charges per fight, a big Siphon Life. [5:36]
- Abrams is an early mid and late mid game fighter who should be active on the map, not AFK farm; he falls off a little late game because he lacks late game carry potential, though he still provides value. [5:36]
- Bebop is a tanky disruptor right now with bomb back to being meta, doing a lot of damage over time. [6:07]
- Bebop is very strong in lane and can win it if he hits solid hooks. [6:07]
- Bebop is not great at farming; be active and look for fights, especially in the early mid game once you get Majestic Leap, with your bomb leveled decently. [6:40]
- The longer the game goes the worse Bebop gets, but he is solid at all points and not weak at any stage, nor the best at any stage. [6:40]
