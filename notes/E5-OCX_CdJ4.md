# Graves is the BEST Support Character in Deadlock
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-07-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E5-OCX_CdJ4

## Covers
Graves, aquatiq commentary over one of his own games, playing a farming spirit build as a support.
Covers lane play against Grey Talon, ability priority, item notes and farming, patch of 2026-07.

## Claims
- He runs a farming spirit build; he says the game is in a spirit meta where spirit heroes are exploding people. [0:31]
- On Graves you cannot play forward in lane; play behind walls and respect the enemy. [0:31]
- After getting poke damage off, use Restoration Shot to heal. [1:05]
- Grey Talon is a bad matchup: Graves cannot hit him while he is in the sky and Grey Talon will do a lot of damage to you in lane. [1:37]
- In no world can Graves fight Grey Talon. [10:52]
- Graves is very easy to use; he picks him when he does not want to tryhard. [2:37]
- Without skulls up he could not get rid of an enemy trap and had to leave it. [3:09]
- Go do boxes when they are up, even if it means leaving the wave to your partner briefly. [3:09]
- He plays Graves more as a support than going for kills, but the hero can kill people. [4:10]
- The skulls and the ghouls together do about the same damage as a Bebop bomb, though it takes two abilities. [4:42]
- Mid has some of the best camps; mid lane often ends with the most money because it starts with so much, even though it gets ganked the most. [5:13]
- You can throw ghouls up onto spots enemies will not reach. [5:43]
- Poke with Mystic Shot is very strong on him. [8:20]
- Rapid Recharge gives you many more ghouls. [8:20]
- He maxes ability 3 first: it looks useless but works in the background, stealing weapon damage and spirit resist over time, raising how many targets you can steal from, giving +1 max steal to summons, and making summons apply the essence theft. [8:50]
- Because of the steal and Restoration Shot, always be shooting. [9:20]
- Enemies must shoot the ghouls; they are lethal if ignored. [9:50]
- He buys Toxic Bullets once ahead. [11:23]
- Get Spirit Resilience soon or you will die. [13:59]
- Walk to the yellow camps for the skulls and the money (about a 400 soul stack). [15:02]
- Do not waste the ult where it does nothing; put it down on a fight or the mid boss area where enemies group. [18:40]
- Echo Shard is on his buy list. [18:40]
- Spiritual Overflow has great life steal and great stats; he puts it on his E ability. [19:42]
- Graves gets high objective damage (about 10k by 22 minutes). [22:18]
- Haze is not a hard carry; even with 30k souls she cannot carry a team alone. [24:21]
