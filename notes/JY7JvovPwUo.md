# How to Monitor Your Enemies | Archon Calico Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-09-16
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JY7JvovPwUo

## Covers
Calico, Midknight. Coaching review of an Archon-rank Calico laning with Grey Talon into The Doorman and Vindicta (enemies later include Shiv, Abrams, Paige and Wraith), patch of September 2025.
Themes: tracking enemy abilities in lane, playing with a passive lane partner, box running, invading with wave states, the 22-minute mid boss call, soul splitting, and focusing the backline.

## Claims
Lane (Calico with Grey Talon into The Doorman and Vindicta):
- Lane revolves around Vindicta's Stake: when it is online you can't go in or she stakes you and takes your whole HP bar; when it is offline, look for guerrilla trades. [0:32]
- With a safe poking Grey Talon you'd rather not go in and let him poke, but as Calico you kind of have to. [0:32]
- Standard Calico combo: throw your 1, then use your 2 to proc Spirit Strike before the 1 bombs explode, so they have reduced spirit resist when the bombs nuke. [1:03]
- Your 2 does apply Spirit Strike. [3:45]
- When the enemy plays safe, hard shove waves and run boxes. [1:36]
- You can parry Viscous' Puddle Punch, but you cannot parry any other melee skills. [1:36]
- Don't waste your cat form jumping around; keep it online for boxes. [2:13]
- You don't have to be scared like Vindicta has snipe up when she doesn't. [2:45]
- Extra Regen is worse than Restorative Shot right now; Healing Rite would feel crazy, including its 2 m sprint speed for box routing. [3:15]
- A bad ult: going in on The Doorman when you have ult and they don't is a good time for a trade setting up your Talon, but even with full combo The Doorman stays above 20% so Talon can't kill him. [3:45]
- Walking up the stairs into The Doorman when he has ult is terrifying: he ults you, goes behind you and carts you into his tower, and without your ult you are guaranteed dead. If you have ult you trade ults. [4:16]
- Rather than sieging a tower when both enemies are healthy and have Stake, box out instead. [4:47]
- Once The Doorman is unhealthy, he can't go for you because your Talon would one-shot him. [5:49]
- Whenever a game starts, assess your lane and both team comps: team fight potential versus pick potential. This game the enemy had pick potential (Wraith, Vindicta, The Doorman) more than team fight. [5:49]
- Holliday, Mo and Krill and Paradox are the three best pick heroes in the game. [6:49]
- Reactive Barrier is crazy into a CC-heavy comp: Vindicta Stake, Doorman cart, Abrams charge, Paige root, Wraith ult. [7:19]
- When running away, light melee a soul machine to deny the souls even though you don't get them. [6:49]
- Calico's ult one-shots a 130 HP Vindicta after a melee. [7:19]
- Farm camps efficiently: melee both medium creeps at once with a melee lineup. [8:21]
- Two points in Ava, then buying Fortitude to max out box running makes sense, since Calico is always healthy. [8:21]

Invading and escapes:
- Stealing the enemy centers while both your waves are crashing is very risky; you only get away with it in lower elo. Invade when your waves are positive, or take one camp and leave. [8:52]
- When both enemy waves are crashing toward you, the players in those lanes have nothing better to do than look at their jungle. [10:22]
- As Calico you are good at invading because you have ult and cat to escape with. [10:22]
- Cat before you take damage and can no longer cat. [9:22]
- When escaping, wall bounce around the corner to break line of sight and put space between you and Vindicta's snipe, then escape through the vent. [9:52]
- Ult before The Doorman's ult ends, to block his damage and the burst they throw when you exit. [11:23]
- Don't partly farm an enemy T3; you just put the camp on cooldown for them. [13:24]
- To dodge damage while reloading, go behind a wall instead of jumping off and mantling, which slows your reload and farming. [13:24]

Fighting:
- Spam light melee on a melee hero, at random times, not only after your 1; it is hard to parry without a read. Light meleeing after your 1 is a common, predictable Calico habit. [14:25]
- With a lot of defensive (green) items you can't look for fights unless your Grey Talon is right there; the build is fine for box routing but neutered for one-shots. [12:53]
- Don't grab the Urn on their side when the enemy is being rolled; it reminds them it exists. [14:25]
- Against a hard spirit lobby, buy simple spirit resist like Spirit Resilience; Spellbreaker is only good into The Doorman and Vindicta here; Counterspell is also strong. [15:58]
- When they are running the Urn with both Walkers up and you can't contest, pressure the mid Walker with one person showing as bait. [16:30]
- Grab the next Urn off spawn and drop it so it stays neutral, even if you don't run it. [17:30]
- Don't rotate somewhere you can't get the useful camp while your lane partner falls back and a wave pushes into your tower; farm your own map instead. [18:00]
- For a camp, push another wave with your lane partner first for 20% extra souls, then come back. [19:33]
- If you get two picks after about 22 minutes, go mid boss. Sieging the mid Walker instead is the classic throw: you sit in a choke and eat a big AoE like Paige's ult. [20:34]
- Soul splitting lasts the whole game: 2 in lane each get 60%, 3 get 30%, 4 get about 18%, 5 get 15%, 6 get 10%. Two people farming a wave is best. [21:06]
- A good fight: every wave positive, up 30k, you know at least a 4v4, and the Urn coming. [22:38]
- Use your full burst combo on the backline (Wraith, Paige, The Doorman, Vindicta), not on Shiv or Abrams, whose job is to tank you; a full combo on Shiv does about a third of his HP but one-shots Paige. [23:08]
- Calico is assassin-like; always focus the backline when you can get on it. [24:40]
- When ending, clear base guardians while the Patron is knocked instead of shooting minions. [25:11]
- A team that is far behind gets back in by turtling, waiting for an overextend, and getting the Urn. [24:10]

Summary notes:
- The big gap between Archon and Oracle is monitoring enemy abilities and ults: Vindicta Stake, Doorman cart and door, his ult. If The Doorman wastes his cart (which has the stun), he is in a bad spot. [26:12]
- Don't look at kills, look at kill participation (kills plus assists); 30% KP is low and means you should be in more fights. [27:13]
- Always keep wave states in mind when invading; don't invade when both waves are shoved into you. [28:15]
- If you can get onto their backline you one-shot everyone except Shiv and Abrams; The Doorman only if he has no cart. [28:46]
- He recommends Idos' Calico build, which is close to what the player had. [29:18]
