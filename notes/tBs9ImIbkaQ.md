## Covers
Grey Talon, aquatiq. Commentary over one of his own ranked games (a lopsided win, MVP), not a structured guide.
Mostly about using the bird ultimate (Guided Owl) for stacks, plus a few lane and item notes. Video dated 2026-06-27.

## Claims
- Grey Talon's lane was strong against Abrams and Seven: Abrams could not do much to him and Seven could not really use Static Charge on him. [2:39]
- The curse trap (Spirit Snare) helps a lot in lane for securing kills. [3:40]
- Turn off instant cast on the trap; it went down instantly when he did not want it to, so he changed the setting. [4:41]
- His charged arrows and left-click gun damage were very high in this game. [7:15]
- Only send the bird when it will get a kill, because the stack only comes from a kill. [7:45]
- Do not bird a low enemy far away who will die to teammates before the bird arrives. [5:12]
- He maxes the bird to see how many stacks he can get, aiming for as many stacks as possible by 15 minutes. [9:50]
- Keep the bird on cooldown as much as possible to get value out of it. [14:01]
- Heroes with big healing (a Mo and Krill with Mystic Regeneration who is scorning people) heal too much to be good bird targets. [13:31]
- Do not bird Ivy: she will just use Stone Form and survive it. [15:05]
- He gets more proactive with birds once stacked; he watches enemy health bars very closely when playing Grey Talon to find bird targets. [17:09]
- Buy a cooldown item before using the next bird so it comes back faster. [20:21]
- Bird into a fight you are already winning to collect stacks from several kills at once; he birds a won fight for +80. [20:52]
- Against enemies that will not die, he wants bleed or Spirit Burn items. [23:54]
- With no vitality (green) items, he cannot just run at people. [25:32]
