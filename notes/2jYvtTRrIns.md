# URN REWORKED? Balance Changes + More! New Patch
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-05-23
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2jYvtTRrIns

## Covers
Stream read-through of the big patch released 2026-05-23 (the notes are dated May 28, which the coach thinks is a typo): the melee-claim Soul Urn deposited on the mid bridge, base HP and Guardian changes, shrine and backdoor nerfs, movement nerfs, investment spikes, the new Grit item, a long item list and almost every hero.
For players who want every change with a quick verdict. Winners named: Yamato, Rem, Paradox, Grey Talon; nerfed: Apollo, Celeste, Mina, Doorman, Dynamo, Victor.

## Claims
- The new urn is always deposited in the middle; after depositing you defend it while a meter fills, and the enemy must heavy melee it to contest. Probably the most major change of the patch. [0:00]
- Biggest hero winners: Yamato, Rem, Paradox, Grey Talon; Graves is a question mark but got changes that should make her more consistent. [0:00]
- Apollo, Celeste, Mina and Doorman all got nerfs that should make them less annoying and more manageable. [0:32]
- Base HP reduced by 10 for all heroes and HP per boon reduced by 3, likely because time to kill was too high. Flat HP cuts hurt squishy heroes more than tanks in percentage terms, so this is a bigger nerf to Mina than to Mo & Krill. [1:03]
- Guardian resistance scaling changed from +75% to -50% over 12 minutes to +50% to -50%, so Guardians take more damage early and the first four or five minutes of lane matter more. [1:33]
- Medium neutrals initial spawn time reduced by 1 minute; breakables spawn time increased from 2 to 3 minutes. The aim seems to be to push the tower in the first minutes instead of running boxes. [1:33]
- Durations are now affected by debuff reduction; the coach calls this significant. [2:04]
- Shrines are invulnerable until one pair of base Guardians is destroyed, so wall breaker Victor will struggle more. [2:04]
- Backdoor removal linger duration cut from 20 to 14 seconds, another wall breaker nerf: your wave must have been there more recently to deal full damage. [2:34]
- Two jump pads around the middle of the map that pointed outward left and right were removed, a mobility nerf. [2:34]
- Sliding no longer resets sprint speed. [2:34]
- Stamina regen is reduced by 25% for 5 seconds after a wall jump while in combat; it nerfs every hero's movement, Celeste most of all. [3:05]
- Bullet velocity now stacks additively rather than diminishingly. [3:05]
- Medic trooper heal duration increased from 2 to 4 seconds and multiple orbs now add duration (four in a row heal over 16 seconds instead of 4); heal raised from 12 to 13. Good change: the medic trooper is meant to keep you on the map, not save a dived player. [3:35]
- Parry effects not always matching state were fixed (parry could still hit after it looked over). [4:05]
- Base Guardian bullet resistance raised from 10 to 20, plus Patron and tier 2 bullet resistance increased: all buildings are harder to take, a nerf to wall breaker Victor and objective-melting builds. [4:05]
- Mid boss now spawns at the start of the game; the coach doubts zero-minute mid boss strategies are possible because it sits in the middle of the map. [4:35]
- Total damage reduction debuffs (such as Inhibitor and fire effects) now stack diminishingly instead of additively, which removes some overpowered interactions. [4:35]
- Move speed diminishing point raised from 11 to 12 m/s, so you can stack slightly more move speed. [5:07]
- Investment spikes now come at 6.4k, 8k, 11.2k and 16k for weapon, vitality and spirit (five steps instead of four), so you do not have to reach 16k for results and are not forced into big 6.4k purchases. [5:07]
- Walkers interrupt their rocket barrage during the 1-second windup if stomp becomes an option. [6:09]
- Ropes now follow the zipline keybind and the zipline delay with alternative binds was removed. [6:09]
- Many items and abilities were fixed to work through the veil (Heroic Aura, Rescue Beam and others). [6:09]
- Urn rules: light or heavy melee the urn to pick it up; it is always dropped on top of the mid bridge; depositing lasts a set time depending on which team it favors; the enemy heavy melees it to claim it, adding 1.25 seconds to their timer. Like King of the Hill. [6:39]
- The favored team gets 50% bullet and spirit resistance in a 60 m radius around the urn; the coach thinks fighting into that is nearly unwinnable unless far ahead. [7:09]
- After 35 seconds of aggregate hold time per team the carrier takes 5% max HP per second (was 45 seconds and 0.15%), and urn damage is lethal. [7:39]
- A fumbled urn waits 13 seconds and then quickly runs back to spawn and cannot be picked up on the way; these are anti-hostage changes. [8:09]
- Carrying the urn no longer silences you, only disarms and movement silences, so you can fight a bit with it (Dynamo can ult). It no longer grants bullet or spirit resist while carried. [8:40]
- The area around the depositing urn is revealed, the runner is revealed as usual, souls are instant so they cannot be denied, and the last person to contest gets three Golden Idol buffs (falls back to the carrier). [8:40]
- New tier 1 vitality item Grit: 200 barrier and out-of-combat regen. Weak by itself, but it builds into Guardian Ward, Reactive Barrier, Spirit Shielding, Weapon Shielding, Divine Barrier and Indomitable, so buy it if you are building barriers anyway. [9:10]
