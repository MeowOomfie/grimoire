# Watch This To Win Every Solo Queue Match
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-06-10
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rc7HCrGVS6U

## Covers
A solo queue carry plan from lane to late game: pick a hero that can kill Walkers, shove early, run box routes, farm T2 camps and Sinner's Sacrifice, split push Walkers while the team fights, then Mid Boss, shrines and the Patron.
For solo queue players who want to win games their team is losing, with Seven and Grey Talon named as strong picks right now.

## Claims
- To carry in solo queue, play a hero that can kill a Walker easily or somewhat easily; it doesn't have to be a gun carry like Vyper, Haze or Seven, and even Pocket is decent because of the scaling on his 2. [0:30] [1:01]
- Bad teammates create a lot of opportunities for a hero that can take Walkers. [1:01]
- In lane, shoving the wave for the first three minutes is all that matters: kill the wave as fast as possible, push it into the enemy tower, then leave, buy or poke. [1:01]
- Since the change to objective damage you can take a Guardian at five minutes. [1:32]
- Early lane: poke the enemy a little while standing behind your creeps, and only think "I do more damage to them than they do to me". [1:32]
- Buy Healing Rite or Extra Regen almost every game; it's a free lane-winning item, and since you are farming out of combat (boxes, rotations) you get a large amount of free HP. [1:32] [2:02]
- Around 4:30, run a box route. Think of box routes as circles that loop back to the zipline; the exact route doesn't matter as long as you run the boxes, and three or four routes clear them all. [2:02]
- Most teammates don't run boxes, especially the Mid Boss boxes, so they are free money. [2:33]
- End the 4:30 box route at the bridge buff and wait there around 4:50; if nobody comes it's a free buff, if they come shoot them off the bounce pad or parry. [2:33]
- Never die for the bridge buff; if two enemies are coming, leave and let them have it. [2:33] [3:04]
- 6 minutes is when the game really starts and decides whether you win, so be alive and ready for it. [3:04]
- It's okay to lose or go even in lane for the first 5 minutes; running boxes, centers and camps means you almost always outscale your lane opponent unless they are high rank and know macro. [3:04] [3:34]
- By 10 minutes you can walk down their tower and kill them, so don't overvalue getting the Guardian or picks early. [3:34]
- Waves spawn every 30 seconds. [3:34] [4:05]
- At 6 minutes, after killing the wave, take the T2 camp on your lane's roof, leave, repeat, and also take the T2 camp near the Walker; doing this you come out with 8k to 9k souls while the enemy laners are at 6k. [3:34] [4:05]
- After lane, ask your team for a side lane; if they won't give you one, keep showing up in their lane and split the farm with them. [4:05] [4:35]
- Take Sinner's Sacrifice at 8 minutes; as the carry, always be the one taking the doubles and your lane's Sinner's Sacrifices. [4:35]
- When you take the doubles, ask a teammate to hover near you, because dying with Sinner's at 200 health drops a 1k soul bag and can end the game. [4:35]
- Many players can't play from behind: after dropping a 1k bag they fight the same enemy who took it and die again. [5:06]
- Your job is to scale; don't go to the first Urn. If your team fights it, push out two or three waves instead. [5:06]
- Use the HP bars on the top bar to track who is in vision: a hero's bar pops up when someone on your team sees them. [5:06] [5:36]
- Example: if blue wave was just reset and pushing, you are in yellow, and the Urn is in green, use the Urn as the moment to run down yellow. [5:36]
- Until about 25 minutes, your job is to bait your team, take Walkers (dying for a Walker is fine) and scale. [5:36] [6:07]
- Taking a Walker during the Urn fight pulls enemy resources toward you, gives your team a better chance at the Urn, and if they lose it you still have the Walker and don't get slot locked. [6:07]
- Always babysitting waves makes the enemy afraid to go for your Walkers. [6:07]
- Learn to rotate without the zipline: use one jump pad or one jungle route (zip halfway, then through the mini camp to arrive behind the blue Walker). [6:37]
- The zipline is slower than everything else you can do, good only for healing or with boost; walking is faster half the time. [6:37] [7:08]
- Good rotations let you babysit two waves at once and get so far ahead (20k) that the enemy's play doesn't matter. [7:08]
- Seven and Grey Talon are busted right now and force the enemy to buy every counter item, which still doesn't stop them. [7:38] [8:09]
- Scale constantly: when your team is fighting, throwing, or you have vision on four enemies, go hit a Walker. [8:09] [8:40]
- When taking a Walker, stand behind it, not in front where the minion wave is; if someone dives you, circle around the Walker using it as cover so they can't hit or see you, and save stamina to dash out of the slam. [8:40]
- Judge your own damage on the Walker: if one ability took half its health, stay and wait for the next one to come back up; if a whole clip did a quarter, leave. [9:11]
- In every game he describes he took every Walker, with 28k to 40k objective damage; you must take the Walkers because your team doesn't know how. [9:11] [9:42]
- Start fighting with your team once you have your first Walker or a 5k to 10k lead (10k if possible), or when there's no Walker left for you to take, such as an Urn on a lane whose Walker you already killed. [9:42] [10:12]
- With that lead you should win at least two or three picks in a fight as long as you don't run it down and give free kills. [10:12]
- If around three enemies have respawn timers over 30 seconds, take Mid Boss as fast as possible. [10:42]
- After Mid Boss, your job is to get a shrine; with base Guardian broken and both shrines down, the enemy team gets about 20 to 25% fewer souls and your waves always push into them. [10:42]
- Around 30 minutes, try your best to get the first or second shrine. [11:12]
- Players don't value "halfing" enough (caption term, meaning unclear); you don't have to stand in the pit to do it, back up about 30 m and do it from range. [11:12]
- Killing enemies after you half is better than before, because their respawn timer is not reduced by you having the Patron: a free kill and a 70 second timer, enough to win off one good fight. [11:42]
