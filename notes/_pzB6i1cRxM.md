# Valve is listening. But is it in the right direction?
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-11-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_pzB6i1cRxM

## Covers
A reaction to the November 2025 patch and the hotfixes in its first 48 hours: stamina revert, Warden and Drifter nerfs, kill bounty split change, Guardian buff, Trophy Collector and Focus Lens nerfs, the gun buff and falloff bug, tanky Walkers, the death ball meta and soul splitting. Mostly opinion, with patch facts and a few itemization and macro points for players in that patch.

## Claims
- The first hotfix reverted the stamina change, so stamina regenerates quickly again. [1:01]
- The first hotfix nerfed Warden's bullet damage growth and Drifter's base HP. [1:01]
- Neutrals were made to give slightly more souls because they had become harder to kill. [1:01]
- Hero kill bounty split efficiency drops the more players share the kill, hit hardest at four or more players (90% down to 70%), to nerf the death ball meta. [1:32]
- Guardians were buffed with more HP because they were falling over very quickly. [1:32]
- Every hero got 50 more base health. [2:02]
- Trophy Collector was nerfed on both its health and its soul generation. [2:02]
- Focus Lens got a slight cooldown nerf. [2:02]
- The 4.8k investment categories push you to diversify builds into a hybrid rather than going all in on spirit or all in on gun. [2:32]
- The healing trooper gives a big burst of healing and is worth a risky play in close lane situations, for example at 50 HP. [3:02]
- Walkers are very tanky now and don't feel pushable before about 18 to 20 minutes; even in Eternus 6 lobbies every Walker is often still up at 18 or 20 minutes. [3:33]
- Walker fireballs now deal a lot of damage; the stomp barely changed, but most of the damage comes from the fireballs. [4:04]
- The mid game objectives are the jungle, Mid Boss, the Urn and the Walkers. [4:35]
- The Urn is in play once every 5 minutes, and after an Urn fight it isn't back until it respawns (for example at 15 minutes), so it can't fill the mid game. [4:35]
- Jungle respawn times are long, so around 17 to 18 minutes most of the jungle is farmed and the Walkers are still too strong to take, which feeds the death ball. [5:05]
- The old answer to a death ball was to split push Walkers; now split pushing a Walker is very hard before 20 plus minutes unless you play a hero with strong gun damage to Walkers like Wraith or McGinnis. [5:36]
- Before the patch most heroes dealt 70% plus spirit damage, with gun only 20 to 30% of output, especially late game. [6:38]
- Many "gun" builds were fake gun items like Capacitor that did mostly spirit damage, and bullet resist items like Metal Skin went essentially unbought. [7:08]
- A damage falloff bug (since fixed) left heroes like Drifter with basically no damage falloff, so Drifter's gun did more damage at range than intended. [7:39]
- Gun was nerfed by 5% before the falloff bug was fixed. [8:09]
- Answers to gun exist and were rarely bought: Suppressor, Metal Skin, Warp Stone and Return Fire. [8:39]
- Metal Skin is an incredible answer to gun, even better now that Capacitor no longer removes buffs; to strip it, gun heroes now need Curse, which most of them won't buy. [8:39]
- In his lobbies gun is not a problem and the game is already back to a mostly spirit meta; Deadlock will always lean a little spirit because of movement and players preferring abilities. [9:10]
- Under the soul splitting change a team of four in a lane gets nearly the same total souls as two players there, which is a big reason death balling started. [9:40]
- The soul changes free players from the old 2-2-2 split where it was optimal to always have two people catch every wave. [10:10]
- Valve has addressed every death ball meta quickly; more mid game objectives would also help against it. [10:41]
