# How To Build and Play Doorman | Pro Doorman Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-08-26
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQWFBn8UFmo

## Covers
Doorman guide by Midknight, done as commentary over a Hydronation scrim game by a pro Doorman (captioned as Lain), recorded on stream with chat questions.
Covers his gun-then-spirit build, ability order, door usage (poke, chase, escape, shields, team combos), cart and Knockdown, positioning and squishiness, and some macro.
This is a 2025 video from Doorman's release week (August 2025 patch), so builds and numbers may be out of date.

## Claims
- Doorman's gun DPS is not high (about 43, while Viscus has 49), but the hitbox on his gun is so big that headshots are very easy, which is why he can win gun trades anyway. [0:00]
- The pro's build: gun for the first three or so items, then pivot to full spirit (build linked in the description). [0:31]
- He does not like buying Extra Regen on Doorman; he only bought it this game because the Holliday and Viscus lane had so much pressure. [0:31]
- Use the door to get into fights quicker; most players use it far too frugally, but it is fine to use it for minor things like saving one second to get one more bullet in. [1:34]
- Suppressor counters a gun build Doorman so hard mid and late game that a full gun build is not viable; Debuff Remover is the main answer and Spellbreaker is very situational. [2:05]
- Poke door: open an aggressive door, throw your poke through it, then close it, when the enemy has shoved the wave and will not look for another trade for the next cooldown (13 to 16 seconds). [2:35]
- He took an aggressive door peek because Viscus had no Splatter and trusted himself to parry Puddle Punch. [3:36]
- Use your door when escaping to walk back through it and get to lane much quicker. [3:06]
- Zip tech: when routing under that bridge, getting off the zipline early is slightly faster (under half a second) than riding it all the way, useful for defending your walker or throwing a door. [3:36]
- Every time you place a door, call to your team exactly where it is and what it is for; later in the game he places doors just as shields for teammates and calls "shield to your right". [4:07]
- Against his ult in this clip, high elo players often jump off right away and tank the extra damage so the enemy team cannot set up on them. [4:37]
- Holliday's Barrel is better than Doorman's Bell; he would take Barrel over Bell 100%. [5:08]
- Doorman will always be very strong in pro and coordinated play because of the portal; maybe strong in solo queue too. [5:08]
- Build update: the pro now buys Extra Charge, and Healing Rite to get a green item early, because the build is weak on greens and you are very squishy as a front liner. [5:40]
- After that he goes Rapid Rounds, then usually Swift Striker, or rushes Long Range if he has the money on reset, with Long Range after. [6:11]
- The cart is especially strong when you group people with it, but it is a bit hard to hit. [6:41]
- Buying Knockdown on Doorman is insanely good because it gives you free carts, the same way Knockdown sets up Bebop hooks. [6:41]
- The cart has a lot of range but is a very slow projectile; if you get hit by it from 30 to 40 m away, that is your misplay. [7:11]
- Level order change: he no longer puts two points in the door early; he maxes the cart and then puts points into the door, because they kept getting shoved in. [7:44]
- Hitting carts gets easier at higher elo because pathing is more predictable; once people get used to Doorman they will intentionally make wrong movements to throw off your carts. [8:14]
- Use the door for bridge buffs; you can instantly snipe a bridge buff, and he is easy to react to bridge buffs with, which matters most where people do not pre-take them. [9:16]
- He bought Cultist Sacrifice for its health, but has moved more toward Battle Vest for defense, because Cultist gives max HP but none of the weapon investment or percentage bonus. [9:16]
- Cart shines point blank; if you space about 10 m from Doorman, he has to read your movement and you can avoid it with a dash (and maybe a double jump, untested). [11:19]
- Use doors for chasing; lower elo Doormans are too stingy with the door. [11:52]
- The door feels like it should have a minute long cooldown, but it is about 40 seconds before cooldown reduction and ticks down while your doors are up, so it effectively feels like 20 to 30 seconds. [11:52]
- When sieging, walk through a door to bait Walker Stomp so a teammate (like a Billy) can dive or melee the walker. [12:54]
- While sieging, create off-angle doors onto the Walker for your teammates to push through, instead of only placing an escape door behind yourself as lower elo Doormans do. [13:24]
- Doors can shove a wave quickly because of the low cooldown, but without exact lineups you do not get much distance. [13:54]
- Knockdown into Luggage Cart is a combo he uses a lot. [14:25]
- Door combo with Yamato: put one door next to your Yamato, tell them to start Power Slash, then throw the other door behind the enemies so the Power Slash comes through. [14:25]
- Same idea with Holliday: tell her to get her lasso ready, put the door behind the enemy, she walks through and lassos them back, doing what the cart could do from behind. [14:56]
- In lower elo, where people notice doors less, you can sneak behind enemies with doors and throw a cart from behind; niche, maybe once a game. [14:56]
- Tower combo: put a door at your tower, cart the enemy, and as the cart pushes them toward a wall place a door behind them so they go through the door into your tower, then close it. He was surprised the pro never tried it. [15:28]
- Holliday's lasso does not go through doors (chat tested); she has to step through first. [18:00]
- Bebop's hook does go through doors (he was 95% sure and chat confirmed). [18:30]
- When he knocked down an Ivy who did not use Stone Form, he wanted the whole team to dive the micro stun so he could cart off it. [16:28]
- Doorman's 1v1 potential is not insane, so do not stay alone to siege a walker when two enemies can jump you; go back with your team. [16:58]
- Doors on walls are janky and sometimes fail, which can get you killed. [17:28]
- Crippling Headshot is in his build: the cart stun gives you about four headshots, like old Mirage tornado. [18:00]
- Coming from behind with the cart lets you stall or take the enemy backline out of the fight. [19:05]
- Ulting an Ivy is not the best use since Ivy does not have to cancel her ult when hit by it. [19:05]
- Buy Counterspell because Doorman is a pseudo front liner with little tankiness; it lets you parry major burst like Geist bomb, Viscus Splatter, Holliday Barrel or Lash's Ground Strike. [19:05]
- When down a couple of players, high elo teams almost always try to trade something for the enemy's Mid Boss; he held a lane while his team went mid, and they traded Urn for mid. [19:36]
- The Guardian's hitbox stops you from placing a door even when the UI says you can, the same way player hitboxes do. [21:12]
- Positioning: play about 10 m behind your front line, close enough to land abilities but able to cart away a diver, strafe back and ult defensively. Many players wrongly go in first or second. [21:42]
- Without Counterspell and Cultist up he is extremely squishy, and an Abrams can run him down when his cart or door is down. [21:42]
