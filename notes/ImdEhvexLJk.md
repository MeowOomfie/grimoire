# How to be Malleable | Phantom Bebop Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-10-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ImdEhvexLJk

## Covers
Bebop, Midknight. Coaching review of a Phantom-rank Bebop player's game (lane into Viscous and Pocket, team with Vindicta and Ivy, enemies include Paige, Victor, Mina and Holliday), patch of October 2025.
Themes: holding your first level, escape movement and verticality, playing around a roaming lane partner, itemization (Capacitor, Cultist Sacrifice, Veil Walker), target prioritization, and taking pointless fights.

## Claims
Laning and leveling (Bebop):
- Bebop is the one hero where you really want to hold your first level, because an early hook point achieves little. [0:31]
- Only put your first level into hook if you are hooking them near your tower or into your lane partner. [0:31]
- Hold the level until you see a situation: they walk up so you can hook, or they walk into you so you can bomb. If nobody walks near you, level your 1 in wave and uppercut minions at them. [1:02]
- Pre-leveling hook risks wasting the level if the enemy never walks up. [1:02]
- If you see no early hook, put the point into uppercut instead and use uppercut for poke, especially if the enemy team plays passive or your laner is AFK. [36:36]
- After a hook, if the enemy walks away, push up to apply pressure. [1:32]
- Restorative Shot is a little hard to pilot on Bebop, though it is the best healing item in the game when you actively proc it. [2:04]
- Don't heavy melee a Viscous off a hook near your tower: a decent Viscous can cube to cancel the parry. Light melee him instead; light melee still moves him and Viscous will not cancel a parry against it without a big read. [4:09]
- Light meleeing someone who is jumping away will never get punished at this rank. [7:16]
- If your lane partner roams at 6 minutes (which a Vindicta should never do), stop looking for hooks or anything aggressive: bomb the wave, hide behind your tower and play slow. [5:44]
- Bad habit: hooking while you are alone in lane. Even if it hits, you probably take a big chunk or die. [5:44]
- If you are aware enough to lane switch, Viscous is the worst lane for Bebop; anyone else on their comp is better. Pocket is also bad but can only Suitcase away from your bombs. [6:14]
- Into i-frame heroes or anyone with Counterspell or a debuff remover, time your hook with a big CC like the Walker stomp, so they are stuck in the hook animation when the stomp lands and cannot dodge it. [9:47]
- Bomb the wave for quick wave clear so the guardian or Walker can shoot the enemy. [9:47]

Itemization:
- The more strictly meta Bebop build is a double vest rush and getting Tesla Bullets sooner, though his build was not inherently bad. [7:46]
- Capacitor goes insane this game: use it to remove Viscous' cube, Paige's barriers, Victor's 3 and Infuser. You should buy it in general on Bebop right now. [7:46]
- Capacitor is so good on Bebop because if anyone uses a buff or self-peel after you hook them in, you just toss it on them. [8:46]
- Capacitoring an uppercut target resets your hook, so when he gets Rescue Beamed out you can counter hook him back in. [31:47]
- You cannot Capacitor Ivy's stone form; you used to be able to remove Pocket's suitcase, but that was patched out. [32:17]
- He believes Capacitor cancels a charged shot (carbine) and has tested it within the last month. [33:27]
- Cultist Sacrifice is OP right now and he will never tell someone not to buy it: it gives so much soul value, and because boons are tied to souls, reinvesting souls into yourself scales your boons too. [12:58]
- Cultist Sacrifice is a significantly better "trophy collector" than Trophy Collector. [15:36]
- Pop Cultist Sacrifice when it is off cooldown; going into a fight it is fine on a T2 camp, ideally on a T3 camp early when many T3s are up. [20:44]
- Use Cultist Sacrifice to finish off a camp. [10:51]
- With Cultist Sacrifice you don't need extra fire rate, so skipping Kinetic Dash is fine. [14:01]
- Veil Walker is very good on Bebop and great for setting up hooks; if you buy it, buy it much sooner when you need the sustain. [21:14]
- Veil Walker is a band-aid for the real issue of constantly fighting and pathing to fights. [38:09]
- He doesn't love Burst Fire here because you already have a lot of fire rate. [23:21]
- Crippling Headshot is a good later buy, especially into Victor. [33:27]
- Slowing Hex would be good this game (into Pocket, Holliday and Mina), or the classic silence item, which is also very good into Victor. [37:36]
- Taking a ton of damage when you are not a tank usually means you are overfighting. [21:47]

Farming:
- Don't farm your defensive camps first; farm the more aggressive camps first, since they give more soul value (three centers up) and you secure farm that could be stolen. Camps at the back will never be stolen. [9:16]
- Using the Hyper Beam AoE on T3 camps is good because the beam has relatively low value otherwise. Don't walk into a 500 HP enemy, though. [10:20]
- Heavy melee the soul machine (vending machine) instead of light melee: 3 extra seconds for 3 extra golden statues' worth is worth it. Only light melee it when you are actively needed somewhere. [22:17]
- Sweaty trick: while the lights rotate, melee the other machine; after you finish the first it is synced, so you just hold melee to finish the second. [22:47]

Escape movement:
- Grabbing a ledge does not need a melee; wall jump or hold W to grab it. [3:38]
- Hold Ctrl while you grab the ledge to mantle slide for more distance. Get in the habit of mantle sliding. [3:38]
- When you hit a vent, hold A to air strafe out; a good player can wall bounce off a sign to get away. [3:38]
- When escaping, go up the rope and seek verticality. [4:09]
- Verticality is one of the best ways to escape: roofs have vents that give mobility, and height gives the runner more tools, like fake jumping down. [18:40]
- If Viscous Puddle Punches you onto a roof, stay up: he already gave you about a 3-second lead (the time it takes them to scale the rope); going back to the ground throws that space away. [19:41]
- When escaping someone, always make your way toward a vent. [19:41]
- When grabbing a zipline to escape, grab it, wait about half a second, then jump off, so the enemy wastes his attack knocking you off and you are not on the zip to be hyperlined. [30:11]

Macro and fights:
- Bebop generally wants to be strong side, looking for picks with hook and playing around one of your carries. [12:25]
- Being in the side lane is fine when your team should keep two there to defend and pressure the guardian; it is better for you to look around mid. [12:25]
- Don't push mid when an enemy is about to shove a wave into you and no one on your team is responding; don't give free siege. [13:28]
- When down in farm with your farm on Vindicta and Ivy, hover your Ivy: don't steal her farm, let her farm, and feed her kills with hooks. You can split with her and she can ult you away. [14:31]
- Against a Paige, hook where she is going; she walks in straight lines and will not react to you. [14:31]
- Rather than a random skirmish, the team should be pressuring; when you win the space after a fight, cash the Urn. [16:06]
- Don't lose two Walkers to a fight that achieves nothing; if you want a fight, pressure, or run the Urn. Don't brute force push a Walker. [16:37]
- Don't boost back to defend a lane that is already lost when they won't push base guardians; you are worth more in the fight with your hook. [16:37]
- Four in one lane while two lanes are crashing loses a ton of souls; the enemy sees both lanes shoved and collapses with six. [17:39]
- Contest the Urn when it is being run instead of giving it away. [20:44]
- Constantly going to base and zipping back means you need sustain and are overfighting. [21:14]
- Hyper Beam DPS (263) only slightly beats your gun (179) before headshots, the uppercut buff, hook buff and hunter buff, so just shoot instead; staying in the beam makes you easy to hit and gives free headshots. You can reload during the beam like a gun Kelvin beam. [24:28]
- Play around your Walker's resist buff and stay in range of it when fighting near your Walker. [25:29]
- If someone Rescue Beams after you hook, rehook: whatever happens second takes priority. [25:59]
- Don't spam hook on the tank just for the damage buff. [25:59]
- Don't buy in the enemy secret shop with zero wave pressure and both waves folding in; you get collapsed on weak side. Buy it when your whole team is there and running the Urn. [28:07]
- Don't base race when you already have your flex slot from guardians; Bebop is very useful in base with hooks that split the enemies. [29:09]
- A 1v1 with Hunter's Aura against a Pocket while their team is in base is fine, but don't get greedy killing guardians you don't need. [29:40]
- When mid is under attack and a side wave is crashing, fix the side wave; the Patron can be backdoored with only a few shots. [32:50]
- Sweaty option after killing two: split, one knocks the Patron and others go mid boss; safe option is just mid boss. [32:17]
- Target priority this game, from most to least: Paige and Mina equal, then Holliday, then Pocket, then Viscous, then Victor last. [37:36]
- Don't hook the front line (Victor) into your team; hook the backline. A hook on a no-mobility, low-HP Paige gets a kill when your team collapses. [35:00]
- Don't always path to the immediate fight, especially when other waves are crashing into your Walkers; call your team back instead. Sitting mid around a strong carry to look for a hook is good. [38:43]
- Watch your HP and play around it better. [39:13]
