## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Celeste-only free coaching stream right after her release patch (February 2026), calling her overtuned. He first reviews viewer and top public Celeste builds, then coaches four Celeste players: Player 1 (laning with Pocket into Lash and Silver), Player 2 (on a gun build), Player 3 (Archon, laning with Rem into Wraith and The Doorman) and Koala (Archon, laning with Venator into Lash and Seven).
Themes: ground-shooting for the ricochet auto aim, playing around Light Eater (her 1) for amp and spirit lifesteal, amp stacks from the 3, ult usage and holding it, max order, spirit over gun builds, Superior Stamina by 22 minutes, movement (wall bounces, mantle slides, instant air dashes, air strafing), "at best" fights, soul awareness and urn decisions.
