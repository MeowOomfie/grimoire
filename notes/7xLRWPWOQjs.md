# This Is Going To Be An Interesting Deadlock Patch...
channel: Midknight
date: 2024-11-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xLRWPWOQjs

## Covers
Patch reaction by Midknight to the November 21, 2024 Deadlock patch: ranked removal and hero MMR, walker and objective buffs, bridge buffs, three new items (Arcane Surge the big one), item and hero changes with his verdicts.

## Claims
Systems and map:
- Ranked was removed; your rank now shows from normal games, and hero MMRs were added so picking an unfamiliar hero feels less rough. [0:00]
- The console command mm_prefer_solos_only matches you only with other solo queuers. [0:00]
- A lane swapping feature mostly fixes end of game lane stats when you switch lanes. [0:00]
- Post lane soul sharing was cut again: two people splitting a wave now give the team only an extra 20%; he predicts it will be walked back to no bonus within a month. [0:31]
- Hero kill souls slightly lowered in late game; a good change. [0:31]
- Walker stomp now does 10% max HP, its beam does more damage, and walkers give nearby allies 25% bullet and spirit resist, so diving under a walker is much harder. [0:31]
- Walkers gain spirit resist per nearby enemy and middle lane walkers have about 35% more HP, so they are harder to brute force. [1:01]
- You get the flex slot from one shrine again, not all shrines, and need two base guardians down, which only affects cheesy backdoor strats. [1:01]
- Patron laser now does 3% max HP, the first mid boss buff is weaker, and respawns are slightly shorter; Valve seems to want more base fights and longer games. [1:31]
- Knocking the enemy patron down now takes 20 seconds off enemy respawn timers, to a minimum of 10. [1:31]
- Medic troopers now have three heal charges instead of two, so hugging your medic trooper in lane may get you extra HP. [1:31]
- Air drag when leaving zip lines with control is higher, toning down wall jump and dash movement off zip lines. [1:31]
- Bridge buffs now must be meleed to take; they are weaker early and grow stronger up to 40 minutes. [2:01]
- The urn has a slightly lower initial bounty and a 5 second longer reveal; that weekend only, its drop off is on top of the mid temple (mid boss) for fewer souls. [2:01]
- Healing and anti-heal were both generally nerfed. [2:01]

New items:
- Spellslinger Headshots (tier 3 weapon): weapon damage and bonus health; headshots raise your spirit power and give spirit lifesteal. [2:33]
- Spirit Snatch (tier 3 spirit, builds from Spirit Strike): melee damage, health, spirit resist and sprint; melee hits on heroes add spirit damage and steal spirit resist and spirit power; may bring melee Shiv back. [2:33]
- Arcane Surge (tier 3 spirit, builds from Kinetic Dash): stamina, health, slow resist, a free dash jump like Kinetic Dash, and the next ability gets 20% duration, range and 20 spirit power. [3:04]
- Arcane Surge works on ults, so Dynamo, who already buys Kinetic Dash, gets a gigantic ult; also strong on Seven ball and Viscous ult; he expects Dynamo meta for a week or two and a nerf. [3:04]

Item changes:
- Glass Cannon now builds from Slowing Bullets and gives its slow; harder and more expensive to get. [4:06]
- Silencer reworked: a slower build up like Toxic Bullets, then a 2 second silence and minus 40% spirit damage for 4 seconds. [4:06]
- Lucky Shot buffed; he expects it to be really good this patch. [4:06]
- Small Heroic Aura buff; it was already pretty good, try building it. [4:37]
- Return Fire now builds from Extra Regen, making it less of a must buy. [4:37]
- Divine Barrier gets 8% ability range back instead of 6 spirit power. [4:37]
- Debuff Remover now also heals 10% max HP, making it even stronger. [4:37]
- Metal Skin now slows you 1.5 m per second, so it no longer counters Haze as hard; with Haze's buff he expects Haze to be strong. [4:37]
- Superior Stamina gives an extra wall bounce for big air time. [5:07]
- Phantom Strike and Knockdown ignore vertical height for range, so they counter snipers harder. [5:07]
- Ammo Scavenger gets more spirit at max stacks and Infuser 5 seconds less cooldown; both were already decent and are now better. [5:07]
- Silence Glyph is now an aimed fast projectile, not tracking: overall a nerf, but harder to react to with Ivy Stone Form, Mirage Tornado or Yamato ult. [5:07]
- Ethereal Shift gives 20 spirit power when it ends; already good, now really good on some heroes. [5:37]
- Refresher cooldown up 30 seconds; does not matter much. [5:37]

Heroes:
- Bebop tier 2 uppercut now does melee damage, and a hooked ally can cancel the hook with space bar, like Viscous Cube. [5:37]
- Dynamo's Kinetic Pulse can go around corners and is blocked less by geometry, which good Dynamos will abuse. [6:07]
- A trap ability (captioned "Raijin Trap") is now a tether like a Vindicta stake: you can move inside the radius but not leave, and it lasts longer. [6:07]
- Haze Smoke Bomb now allows items, and casting it phases you out for 0.5 seconds to dodge abilities like Wraith's ult. [6:07]
- Haze ult no longer gives plus 5 bullet damage but plus 6 spirit damage with spirit scaling, so Return Fire and Metal Skin work less well against it. [6:38]
- Ivy gets small bullet damage buffs and in her ult is affected by movement and sprint speed out of combat, so crossing the map is very fast. [6:38]
- Lash's Ground Strike and ult now break Kelvin's ice path. [7:08]
- McGinnis turrets have less health but take at most 33% of max HP per second, so they need at least three seconds to kill, and are very hard to kill with her heal on them. [7:08]
- Mo & Krill can burrow under McGinnis's wall, and his ult now gives 70 health instead of 30 when someone dies in it. [7:08]
- Shiv gets 3 seconds off his knife cooldown and early game buffs; with Spirit Snatch he expects Shiv to be very strong. [7:08]
- Viscous Puddle Punch can be parried: no stun on Viscous, but you take no damage or knockback. [7:40]
- Wraith Telekinesis now bobs targets up and down so she and her team cannot easily headshot them. [7:40]
- Yamato Power Slash should hit through cover less; her ult no longer heals, but she heals 15% max HP on a kill (captioned "when she gets killed"). [8:11]
