## Covers
Three minute Mina guide (June 2026): pros and cons, abilities, combos, lane, mid and late game plans, recommended builds and a tip against Slowing Hex. For players new to Mina.

## Claims
- Pros: very high damage and burst, very high mobility, and she escapes bad situations quickly and easily. [0:00]
- Con: she is very squishy, so if you misuse your mobility or get caught out you explode. [0:00]
- Her 1 sweeps a rake that deals more damage the more health the enemy is missing, and heals her a flat amount if it kills anything. [0:00]
- Once the rake is unlocked she can hold right click to hover; she glides down slowly, and hovering more than 2 seconds makes her accelerate downwards rapidly. [0:30]
- Her 2, Sanguine Retreat, dashes to a location in front of her twice and she is invincible during the dash; maxed, it gets an extra recast. [0:30]
- Her 3, Love Bites, is a passive: her damaging abilities and gun deal a little extra damage on hit and apply a debuff that bursts when fully stacked, with an individual cooldown per target. [0:30]
- Her ult (Nox Noctura in the captions) hovers her and releases bats where she looks that deal small damage, silence and apply Love Bites; maxed, the bats also deal 1% current health damage. [1:01]
- With the ult unlocked she gains a passive: every Love Bites trigger on an enemy hero adds two permanent bats to her ult. [1:01]
- Combo: stack Love Bites almost fully on camps or troopers, then finish them with the rake to trigger Love Bites and heal a lot in lane. [1:01]
- Sanguine Retreat can give her a lot of height so she can ult from a safer distance. [1:01]
- Lane: she is a strong laner, so harass the enemies and proc Love Bites as much as you can. [1:31]
- Her rake is very strong for stealing enemy jungle; invade the enemy tier one camp as much as you can for healing and farm denial, but call your lane partner over so you are not caught alone. [1:31]
- Mid game: with burst and mobility she likes to invade and steal important enemy farm such as sentinels or smaller camps; she loses HP on sentinels and heals it back on a camp. [1:31]
- Late game: burst down squishy enemies, especially mispositioned ones; with no cover nearby one ult can kill them, and with cover nearby one Love Bites proc does half their HP once your items are online. [2:02]
- Because she is squishy, staying alive is a primary goal. [2:02]
- Builds: Shyro's build is simple, strong and fairly left to right; Cosmedical's build has more options and items that need more game knowledge. [2:02]
- When running away, use the ult to keep your height. [2:33]
- She is heavily countered by Slowing Hex, so many builds make Dispel Magic core; if you dispel a Slowing Hex, cast your 2 immediately afterwards so you are not hit by another one. [2:33]
