# DEADLOCK - Shiv is BROKEN - How to win with Shiv (showcase)
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-08-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VdQhXgTKKUI

## Covers
A 2024 commented Shiv game: ability order, lane approach, power farming with a maxed Slice and Dice, why his deferred-damage passive is strong, counters, and the gun (Berserker) build around Siphon Bullets and Leech. For Shiv players and those facing him. This is from August 2024, when Shiv was considered broken; he and his items have been reworked a lot since.

## Claims
- Shiv is extremely broken right now; he has already been nerfed twice and Deathy expects one or two more nerfs. [0:01]
- The way to play Shiv will probably stay similar despite nerfs, so the video focuses on strengths and weaknesses more than the item build. [0:31]
- Shiv is fairly strong in lane: his gun does a lot of damage, his knife (Serrated Knives) is good harass, and his 2, Slice and Dice, gives kill potential. [1:01]
- At level one you are not super strong; just get poke with the knife. [1:31]
- Level the knife first; that will pretty much always be right because it is reliable harass with bleed. [1:31]
- Early in lane focus on last hits as much as possible; experienced players can play aggressive, but last hits matter most, so avoid missing them or getting denied. [1:31]
- Creep advantage matters, like in Dota, so try to have more creeps than the enemy. [2:01]
- Pushing the lane is better in Deadlock than in Dota because lanes are close together, rotating is easy and people are easy to kill with a quick rotation, so a pushed lane gives you the chance to gank. [2:01]
- After the knife he maxes his 2 (Slice and Dice) and puts no points into anything else. [2:32]
- Lash with Lady Geist is a decent laning combo; Lady Geist in particular has very good harass with her bomb and a pretty good gun. [3:03]
- Get melees whenever you can, but not melees that make you take a lot of damage (he took 200 for nothing). [3:33]
- Shiv tends to want to go in; pay attention to when your lane opponents have their ult, and don't go in when they do. [3:33]
- Always farm boxes when there is nothing else to do. [4:33]
- On a rotation where you can't kill the target (a Kelvin), kill their creep wave and then return to your own wave; because of how soul sharing works you get more souls, which took him from behind his Grey Talon to even. [4:33]
- Around the 6 to 7 minute mark the laning stage becomes very dynamic, so look for plays; Shiv is very strong at looking for plays. [5:06]
- Shiv gets kills easily when ganking, especially with his ult, which lets him clean up anyone; without the ult it is harder. [5:06]
- With the second point in Slice and Dice (+100 damage) it is about a 235 damage nuke, and with max rage it echoes (casts again). [5:06]
- Follow where the enemy Lash goes and go there too. [5:36]
- One of Shiv's greatest strengths is farming: with a maxed 2 he is an insane power farmer, so farm the creep camps between plays. [5:36]
- Shiv is very hard to kill because of his passive, which is the main reason he is broken; continued passive nerfs would make him feel fairer. [6:37]
- Heroic Aura is a very good early item on Shiv: movement speed, lifesteal, fire rate and bonus health, and the active gives 25% fire rate and +3 movement speed. [6:37]
- A more spirit-oriented Shiv build exists (Deathy used to play it), but right now the weapon build is better because of certain weapon items and the damage you get; that may change with item changes. [7:08]
- Shiv clears camps very fast even at 10 minutes without many items, and is one of the few heroes that can farm tier three camps. [7:38]
- Warp Stone is a really good item on Shiv. [8:08]
- Don't overcommit: even when it feels like you can 1v5, wait for the opportune moment; he died defending a tower when he could have played passive. [8:38]
- At this stage Shiv's priority is to get waves pushed and get farm; be willing to show up to fights, but Shiv only comes online with a few items. [9:09]
- Farming tier three camps is easy on Shiv, especially with max rage, since his 2 echoes and destroys camps. [9:39]
- Always push out waves whenever you can: it applies map pressure, gives information, and waves give more souls than creep camps. [9:39]
- A common newer-player mistake is hitting the jungle way too much; there is a time and place for neutrals, and Shiv is particularly good at them. [9:39]
- Position your 2 to hit every creep in a camp; that is how Shiv gets ahead (he was 3k souls ahead of everyone with only three kills). [10:10]
- Especially at lower elo, people think they can fight Shiv, but if you build and understand him there is pretty much no hero that can 1v1 you unless they are far ahead. [10:41]
- The best counters to Shiv are damage-over-time heroes: his passive defers damage and lets him clear a percentage of it, so it is very good against burst but feels weak against constant damage over time. [11:11]
- Shiv is very good against Lash, who has no damage over time and wants to kill instantly with a combo; even with ult and all abilities Lash won't kill you unless he is far ahead. [11:41]
- Siphon Bullets is still broken on Shiv even after its nerf: 45 max HP steal per bullet (a maximum frequency he describes as roughly every two shots on Shiv), an extra flat 45 damage that also heals you. [12:11]
- In a fight Siphon Bullets gains you roughly 500 to 800 health, and you permanently steal one stack of health when you kill someone. [12:11]
- Shiv is a kill-heavy, snowbally hero; over 30-something games Deathy averages 14 kills a game, so you stack many Siphon Bullets stacks and become very tanky. [12:42]
- Siphon Bullets also gives 40% weapon damage plus 18% from the tier bonus, almost 60% weapon damage, not far from Glass Cannon (88 total), while making you tanky, which is everything Shiv wants. [12:42]
- Shiv gets +25% all damage at max rage, which makes his shotgun hurt a lot. [13:12]
- When you can take an objective, push the Walker before farming a big jungle camp; objectives matter more than jungle, and you can farm the jungle on the way back. [14:13]
- The items that enable Shiv most right now are Siphon Bullets and Leech; whatever build you play, even spirit, you want Leech. [14:46]
- Because Shiv defers damage he still takes a lot of it and needs a way to regen; there are no really good life regen items (the best gives about 15 regen, which is about 300 health in a 20 second fight), while Leech easily gives 40 to 50% lifesteal and also works as anti-heal. [15:16]
- Shiv's ult can be used as a gap closer, for example onto a flying Vindicta; killing with the ult gives nothing extra, so there is no reason to save it only for kills. [15:47]
- Example of why Shiv is broken: getting dived by four, he turns, uses his 2 through everyone, uses it again, and they all die; no other hero would even have a chance there. [16:17]
- Summary for lane: you have some kill potential, but focus on not getting behind; Shiv has little recovery potential and plays badly from behind, so you pretty much have to get ahead. [17:50]
- Summary after lane: push out lanes, max your 2 for its cooldown reduction to farm fast, and hit the tier three camps as soon as you can, especially the ones on the side lanes (one on each side lane). [17:50]
- Once ahead, keep pressure on the enemy team. [18:53]
- Current build summary: Warp Stone, Siphon Bullets, Superior Stamina, Leech, then supporting spirit items such as Bullet Resist Shredder, Suppressor, Mystic Burst, Improved Cooldown, and Improved Burst most games. [18:53]
- His in-game guide is for spirit Shiv; he plans an in-game guide for gun Shiv (Berserker Shiv). [19:25]
