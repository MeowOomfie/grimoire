## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Solomon-only free coaching stream on 10/12/26, while Solomon and the other new heroes are fresh. He coaches three Solomon players: one whose rank is not stated, an Emissary and a Phantom, and mixes in his own Solomon build and opinions (Magic Carpet early, Swift Striker over Mystic Shot).
Themes: micro on the pieces (Bishops on 1, Knight barrier on 2, Rook on 3, Queen ult on 4), recasting and breaking pieces, aiming for 25 to 30% of damage from Bishops, using the 2 to farm, AP order, gun items falling off after 15 minutes, playing in the air and on height, resetting when low, and macro (setting mid, Rift, walkers, funnelling a fed teammate).
Side notes on the meta: Sinclair, Rat King and Baba strong, Radiant, Enchanter's Emblem versus Spirit Shielding, and mid lane anchors.
