# Eternus Viscous Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-11-06
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQuZ-AoT7fA

## Covers
Viscous, Midknight. Commentary over a pro Eternus game by Goober on Viscous (laning with Mina against Pocket and Ivy), no student coached.
Themes: Viscous tech and bugs (Split Shot double hit, skybox and vent Puddle Punch, wall bounces), Express Shot spike, Healing Rite value, stealing Sinner's Sacrifice, the floater role, Majestic Leap rotations, and why Midwalker is a death trap. Video dated 2025-11-06; the Split Shot bug was patched after the VOD.

## Claims
Viscous:
- Split Shot bug: with Split Shot on Viscous or Yamato, right click at an enemy's feet to hit them with two projectiles for double damage. It was patched after this VOD, but Split Shot still looks viable on Viscous without the bug. [0:00]
- Only Viscous and Yamato can do the Split Shot trick, since they are the only heroes with circle right clicks. [17:32]
- He thinks the double Split Shot should be nerfed, but the other bugs (skybox punching, meleeing in ult) are fine; playing Viscous means knowing about eight different bugs. [23:41]
- Cube defensively, especially when the enemy has a pocket combo into a one-shotable teammate like Mina; cubing the combo can turn a fight. [0:31]
- For a good Viscous build, use Goober's Leap Viscous build. [1:01]
- If Viscous has bridge control he is much more oppressive, so the enemy will pressure him for it. [1:01]
- Always try to switch into mid lane on Viscous: the map geometry gives almost no cover, so he can poke you with Splatter and right clicks. [1:31]
- In any lane against Pocket, target Pocket. Ivy's Stone Form lets her dodge a lot and gives her sustain. [2:34]
- When healing, do a box route; it is not worth peeking. [2:34]
- Play around Spirit Shielding by bursting it out, then cube and wait for the shield to go away. [3:36]
- Express Shot is an insanely overpowered item on Viscous because of how it interacts with his right click; Goober saved for it instead of buying green investments like Healbane. [5:11]
- Use Express Shot almost only for the right click. [7:11]
- Getting Express Shot is a very massive spike; play passive before it, then the second you have it, start looking for fights and damage. [6:41]
- Movement tech: Puddle Punch yourself as you hit a vent to get sent flying. It works on most vents but is only useful on a couple because of the walls. [11:52]
- Puddle Punch off the skybox: very useful against flying heroes. On the vent he shows, a skybox Puddle Punch with the right timing knocks an escaping enemy off the vent and down. [11:52]
- Buy Majestic Leap on Viscous because Puddle Punch into Leap gives a ton of movement. [12:53]
- Majestic Leap Viscous has the fastest rotation in the game; use it to react to fights and catch people out when your team cannot get there in time. [17:01]
- Not having stamina to double jump off Leap slows him down significantly. [20:03]
- Tech: Majestic Leap with a teammate on Rescue Beam slingshots them across the map. [20:03]
- If Viscous gets caught out, his ult can escape a lot of dangerous situations. [19:02]
- Viscous is great with Infernus: Infernus is squishy and has to go in, and the cube keeps him alive in the front line when he gets focused. [14:57]
- Viscous is very good in pit fights; spam damage there when Rejuv is falling. [15:27]
- Floater role: in coordinated play Viscous does not side lane or sit mid, he hovers lanes, pokes and stays scary while giving farm to his carries. [11:22]
- In solo queue, play Viscous more farm heavy: still float, but farm camps and eat souls yourself, because you need to carry in solo queue. [13:23]

Laning and economy:
- Healing Rite is a great item: buy it to top yourself off any time you can instead of resetting. At about 250 souls of value per use and 400 back on sale for an 800 cost, it pays for itself in about three uses. [4:41]
- No other item lets you avoid resetting at 50% or 20% HP the way Healing Rite does; Healbane is more conditional. [4:09]
- Healing Rite is a fine buy if you are not buying a green investment; other heroes would get an early Battle Vest, Enchanter's Emblem or Healbane. [5:11]
- In a side lane, once Healing Rite is on cooldown, back off and reset; mid lane allows some cheeky resets. [6:11]
- Pop Healing Rite even on the way to base: the second or two of ticking healing saved gets you out of base quicker. [10:20]
- Stay in soul range of the wave without farming it so souls split between you and your laning partner. [10:50]
- After a lane kill, immediately go steal the enemy Sinner's Sacrifice: each hit is roughly a 130 soul gain for you and 130 denied, a net 260 from two melees. [7:11]
- In solo queue, call it on mic ("I'm stealing the Sinners, can you support me?"); if your partner is not covering you, the enemy gets a favorable fight. [8:12]
- Every rank, even the top, takes too much damage from troopers. [8:46]
- Right clicking on top of a vent there was to get the new stun mechanic. [8:46]

Movement:
- Wall bounces are overpowered, especially edge boosting: wall bounce on the end of a wall for a horizontal corner boost and more momentum. [9:17]
- Easy corner boost: jump into a wall, put your back to it, then jump; nine times out of ten it corner boosts, and otherwise you still get good momentum. [9:48]

Macro:
- Walker stomp has a 10 second cooldown between stomps; once it is baited, the walker is not that scary when there is a wave. [12:53]
- With a wave proxy, thinning the wave before it reaches the walker keeps the walker's reinforcement and wastes the enemy's Rejuv time. [16:29]
- When an enemy buys from somewhere they cannot be in base, they are in a secret shop; check the other one too if you need to buy anyway. [16:29]
- After two picks, go mid; keep to the formula. [14:57]
- Do not siege Midwalker early, even with Rejuv: the geometry, walker resist and its extra 3 to 4k HP over side walkers make it a death trap. This game ended without anyone killing Midwalker. [20:33]
- Around 23 to 24 minutes, Midwalker is scarier than anything in the game; he would rather coin flip a six-man mid boss than six-man their Midwalker. [24:41]
- Getting shrines does nothing at that point, so take your slot and then the base guardian. [20:33]
- Solo queue mistake: grouping mid to look for a fight when you have far more farm. Secure your camps, wait for the next urn, spike off camps and buffs, then use the urn to force the deciding fight. [21:39]
- Comeback souls are at most 25% more, but if you have 300 to 400% more camps you still gain much more. [21:39]
- When you have more flex slots than the enemy, extra neutral farm favors you because they are slot capped and that cap weighs more the more souls they get. [23:41]
