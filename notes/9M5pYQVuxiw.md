# The Ultimate Guide To Billy - DPS Monster Tank
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-09-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9M5pYQVuxiw

## Covers
A full Billy hero guide: his abilities and their tech, why he should be played as a gun brawler that rarely melees, the skill order, the Spirit Snatch and Lifestrike into gun items build, the lane, jungle-stealing and mid game plan, Chain Gang at Mid Boss and walkers, and how to counter him.
For Billy players at any rank, and for players trying to beat Billy.

## Claims
- Billy is a DPS tank hybrid: weak in early lane, but very solid once Blasted (his 3) reaches level two, with an insane mid game and good late game scaling. [0:00]
- Billy is great in 1v1s and team fights, benefits from all three item categories evenly and is versatile in builds. [0:30]
- Billy is a gun brawler who wants to get up close; most of his damage comes from his gun, and he has the fourth highest gun scaling in the game. [0:30]
- Most Billies, at every rank up to Eternus, play him strictly as a close range melee hero, and the main way you die on Billy is getting parried. [1:02]
- Billy's number one rule: do not melee until you bait out an enemy parry, and even after it do not heavy melee, spam light melees instead. [1:02]
- Billy's passive lets him melee faster and walk while meleeing with no slowdown, unlike Abrams, who nearly comes to a halt on every light melee. [1:33]
- A heavy melee does twice the damage of a light melee and, for every hero except Billy, one heavy is faster than two lights; Billy is the exception. [2:05]
- Billy's light melees are less predictable, so harder to parry, and let him stay in melee range at all times. [2:36]
- Bashdown (1) slams the ground in front of you; Rising Ram (2) rams you into an enemy and, on hit, carries you both up into the air. [2:36]
- Blasted (3) marks targets you hit with a melee: it lowers their fire rate, gives bullet amp, heals you on every melee and gives movement speed; light melees are half as effective, which does not matter because Billy's light melees are fast. [3:07]
- Blasted is the first skill to take in lane, and it is what wins, or at least does not lose, Billy's lane, since Billy is not a very good laner. [3:37]
- Chain Gang (ult) is one of the best ultimates in the game: it chains everyone in an aura around you and at the end pulls them all towards you. [3:37]
- The only way to break Chain Gang is to get out of line of sight and stay out for about a second; it can take up to a second to break, so late line of sight breaks often fail. [4:07]
- Chain Gang is really strong at Mid Boss and in team fights. [4:07]
- Bashdown tech: you can hold the bash in the air before slamming; it adds no damage but lets you turn in the air to readjust if the enemy doubles back. [4:37]
- Bashdown movement tech: jump dash in, then hold the 1 for a tiny moment, and you travel a lot further. [5:09]
- Bashdown deals melee and spirit damage and displaces the enemy, knocking them to the floor like a Lash slam. [5:09]
- Rising Ram tech: do a simple jump before casting Rising Ram to get directional control, letting you catch people in the air (a Vindicta) or on a bridge in the middle of a lane. [5:41]
- Blasted melees give over health, which is not healing and cannot be anti-healed, and grows the more you melee the enemy. [6:12]
- Every melee hit extends Blasted by 4.5 seconds, so a few melees in a fight make it last a very long time. [6:12]
- Avoid starting a fight on Billy if you do not have Blasted up; it gives the healing and the bonus bullet damage that make him strong. [6:12]
- Do not run full spirit or full melee Billy: enemies expect the melee for health and get ready to parry, and a parry is often a death sentence for Billy; the higher the rank, the more they parry. [7:13]
- Heavy melees are very easy to predict and counter (a loud sound and a long cast time), so basically never press heavy melee on Billy, even though new players can get away with it. [7:43]
- Even after an enemy parries, do not heavy melee: two quick melees do almost the same damage, and good players who miss a parry jump or dash out to dodge the heavy. Keep running into them with quick melees instead. [7:43]
- Think of Billy's melees as a reload tool: his passive reloads him whenever melees hit enemies. [8:44]
- Jungle farming: hold mouse one, and when low on ammo heavy melee the camp to go straight back to a full magazine. Two quick melees work too (each refills about half the magazine, same total time) but are more spammy. [8:44]
- Start the lane with Blasted and put two points in it; at two points, casting Blasted instantly gives a Bashdown charge back, and Bashdown counts as a melee, so it heals from Blasted. [9:15]
- Billy becomes strong in lane with two points in Blasted. Lane combo: Bashdown, proc Blasted, get the Bashdown charge back, Bashdown again for a double Bashdown that heals, then shoot or quick melee only (1-3-1). [9:45]
- Most players then add value points (Bashdown cooldown, Rising Ram cooldown or an ult point) and max Blasted for its +50 melee bonus health; a better plan in many cases is to max Bashdown after lane. [10:16]
- Maxed Bashdown ("deal 60% heavy melee damage") turns it from a light melee attack into a heavy melee attack, so it gets double the Blasted healing: 80 healing per slam with Bashdown maxed vs 65 with Blasted maxed. [10:46]
- Spirit Snatch and Lifestrike give insane value on Billy: they are melee items, and because Bashdown counts as a melee you get them without the risk of meleeing. [11:46]
- Both items have twice the cooldown on light melee hits: with an unmaxed Bashdown, Spirit Snatch goes on a 12 second cooldown and Lifestrike on 8 seconds. [12:16]
- Proc these items as often as possible, especially Spirit Snatch: every hero you hit gives you spirit resist and takes spirit away from them; hitting three heroes with Bashdown took him from 0 to 45 spirit resist. [12:16]
- Hit multiple targets with Spirit Snatch; three is ideal, more is possible with the ult, and even two is value. [12:46]
- The main reason to max Bashdown before Blasted is that it halves the cooldowns of the two melee survival items. [12:46]
- Skill order: two points in Blasted in lane, one value point in Chain Gang, then max Bashdown. [13:20]
- Following his build left to right, you buy Lifestrike at about the same point Bashdown is maxed, and that is Billy's first power spike, where you can fight and probably win a 1v1 against anyone unless you hard lost lane. [13:20]
- At that first spike, with double melee items, he sat at 18% bullet armor and 45% spirit armor without buying a single spirit resist item. Enchanter's Emblem comes next. [14:20]
- Play the lane passively until Blasted is level two, then start fighting with the 1-3-1 combo. [14:51]
- Use Rising Ram mainly to escape or to catch the enemy. [14:51]
- Against Infernus, Rising Ram him immediately after he dashes to keep him in the air, wasting his Flame Dash, then Bashdown him so he cannot get out. [14:51]
- Rising Ram is on a very long cooldown and is often needed to get out; Bashdown and Blasted are the main fighting tools, Rising Ram is for finishing a kill, catching someone off guard or leaving a fight. [15:22]
- Even when low on health and tempted to melee for Blasted health, hold off, because it often backfires into a parry; Bashdown already gives the melee item value, and a maxed Bashdown procs Lifestrike as a heavy every time. [15:52]
- Fight flow: 1-3-1, then shoot the enemy; they panic, get low and start parrying, and right after their parry you can smack them with up to four light melees. [16:22]
- Bashdown also reloads your entire magazine, since it counts as a heavy melee, so use it to reload mid fight without meleeing. [16:53]
- Buy the two melee items early for damage, survivability, early spirit resist, healing and slow on the enemy, then go straight gun items: Fleetfoot, Intensifying Magazine, Escalating Resilience, then Point Blank. [17:25]
- Billy's gun falloff is big, so he wants to be as close as possible; Fleetfoot is the chase and escape item that keeps you close while shooting. [17:56]
- Intensifying Magazine fits because Billy's base ammo is quite low; if you keep shooting and only quick melee to reload, it stays at max damage amplification nearly all the time, which also makes his farming speed insane. [18:26]
- Billy's high fire rate makes Escalating Resilience amazing: a few bullets put him at 40% bullet armor, and with Spirit Snatch procs he sits around 40% bullet and 40% spirit resist for the whole fight. [18:57]
- Fury Trance is optional but he loves it: Billy uses few abilities, so the silence costs little, while it adds fire rate and 40% spirit resist, taking him to 64% spirit armor depending on Spirit Snatch hits. [19:58]
- Combo with Fury Trance: 1-3-1 (or 1-1-3-1), then pop Fury Trance and run them down, light meleeing to reload, and use spells again when unsilenced. [20:30]
- Chain Gang puts a Slowing Hex effect on every enemy it hits for its whole duration, so they cannot use movement abilities: Mina cannot teleport, Mirage cannot use Tornado, Wraith cannot teleport, Infernus cannot Flame Dash. [21:00]
- Ult combo: go in, Chain Gang, wait until they get pulled into you, then Bashdown. [21:30]
- Silencer reduces the outgoing spirit damage of targets hit by your bullets, and this also works on melees, including Bashdown, so everyone you slam deals 25% less spirit damage for the full 6 seconds, and you can keep reapplying it. [22:00]
- Start a fight with Bashdown, 3, 1, then hunt one target; with Billy's high fire rate they get the Silencer debuff instantly and cannot get out, then proc Fury Trance. [23:02]
- Rapid Recharge can be bought earlier as a quality of life upgrade, and is a good late game one; two Bashdown charges are usually enough (one to start the fight, one ready to reload, heal or apply Spirit Snatch and Lifestrike). [23:02]
- In lane, do weave in light melees, because your gun does not do much damage yet. [24:03]
- Blasted works on creeps and on Sinner's Sacrifice, so Billy is amazing at stealing enemy jungle farm: proc Blasted and hit enemy sinners, and the over health keeps your HP pool rising. [24:03]
- Farming the enemy jungle keeps Blasted up, so if someone contests you in the enemy double sinner room you are ready: Chain Gang them, Bashdown and kill them. [24:34]
- Billy is a great jungler in general and gets faster with gun items, and he is the best hero in the game for stealing enemy jungle, since he stays near full HP where other heroes end up low. [25:04]
- As long as Blasted stays on and you keep extending it, you will not die in the enemy jungle. [25:34]
- At 8 minutes, whether he won or lost lane, he goes to the enemy jungle and steals as many sinners as possible; after lane he is usually at or near Spirit Snatch, and the sinners take him to Lifestrike and a maxed Bashdown, ready for team fights. [25:34]
- If you are getting parried a lot on Billy, melee less and just shoot; most players up to Eternus eventually panic parry, and then you smack them down. [26:34]
- Billy gains money from fighting and wants to fight a lot in the mid game, where he is really strong; every time Chain Gang is up, look for something. [27:05]
- After maxing Bashdown, max Blasted, then put a second point in Chain Gang (30 extra resists, around 80% on both if played perfectly, so near unkillable in the ult), then two points in Rising Ram for weapon damage. [27:35]
- You can max the ult first because it also gives Unstoppable at the end; his order is then max the ult, then max Rising Ram. [28:06]
- Mid Boss tech: when the Mid Boss dies, dash in and Chain Gang in the pit; enemies there usually cannot break line of sight in time, and if they try they must leave the pit, so you pull them away from the Rejuvenator. [28:06]
- Your job at Mid Boss is to take the enemy out of the pit so your team can secure the melees or parries; he has pulled six out in one fight. [28:37]
- Even without Rising Ram you can go in, ult and run out, closing enough distance in time to drag the enemy out of the pit. [29:07]
- Billy is great at initiating when the enemy pushes your walker: come in on the zipline, jump off, ult, pull them into your walker and Bashdown. [29:07]
- Even without the ult, fight whenever Blasted is up; without it you lack the bonus weapon damage and the melee and Bashdown healing and are significantly weaker. [29:39]
- With Blasted up and somewhat healthy, Billy is one of the tankiest heroes: the base build reached 72% spirit resist and 46% bullet resist after a Bashdown or two, and 80% spirit and 60% bullet resist with the ult on. [30:09]
- If you do not like Silencer, Rapid Recharge earlier is an option; Rapid Recharge can also replace Fury Trance. [30:39]
- Vampiric Burst is another amazing option, especially with Fury Trance: when you cannot reach melee range and are running out of ammo, pop it to refill ammo, and with Billy's gun damage the lifesteal is huge. [30:39]
- Transcendent Cooldown is super luxury and not needed; Superior Cooldown is the better pick, cutting Chain Gang from 122 to 92 seconds and giving more Bashdowns. [31:40]
- The main gain from cooldown reduction on Billy is having Blasted more often; without much cooldown reduction it is about a 26 second cooldown during which Billy cannot do much. [32:11]
- Countering Billy: do not panic parry; wait until he is about to run out of ammo, since his manual reload is one of the slowest in the game, and when he is low he will almost surely start meleeing for health. [32:11]
- Do not fight Billy at close range: inside his Bashdown range his combo leaves you very low. [33:15]
- In lane you can punish Billy by keeping your distance and shooting him at medium range, where he cannot trade; Billy has to get into you to do damage. [33:15]
- At the time of the video Billy is S tier, but not the best new hero: Drifter is surely better and Victor probably better. [33:15]
