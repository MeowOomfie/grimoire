## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (December 2025 patch) with three replay reviews: a Phantom Yamato (Yamato Dynamo into Ivy Calico), an Oracle Pocket (Paige Pocket into Bebop Lash) and an Emissary Warden on Metro's gun Warden build (Warden Mina into Yamato Abrams).
Themes: fighting in front of your wave, orb confirming on high ping, green spike at 4.8k, coin flip mid fights versus urn and invading, Pocket cloak placement and resets, side lane farming as a carry, and when to run urn.
Side notes on Sinclair and Abrams strength, melee conditioning, dash parry tech and Mina's ult.

## Claims
General and side notes:
- Sinclair is a lane bully that does nothing outside lane unless the enemy has a Dynamo or Pocket ult; he has the lowest win rate in the general population at 47%. [4:34]
- Sinclair's best lane partner is any strong laner (Paradox, Holliday, Yamato, Doorman), and you want to be into heroes with good ults to copy; into Haze it feels bad because her ult is useless on you. [6:05]
- Abrams is broken right now: in lane, if he plays around the veil, enemies can never win a trade up close. [4:34]
- Buying a lot of Slowing Hex is the way you deal with Mina. Silence Wave is no longer the item, but still worth buying. [16:29]
- Don't heavy melee: it is complete bait. Light melee only. [70:18]
- Condition opponents: do a light melee pattern twice and then stop, and don't open with the common pattern a hero is known for (for example a light melee right after Billy's 2). [70:49]
- To dash parry, air dash and then parry; a ground dash then parry doesn't work for most heroes. You can bind parry to mouse scroll to make it easier. [70:49]
- Early, melee does significantly more damage than shooting; it falls off unless you build melee. [71:51]
- Per the movement wiki, heroes with a unique dash animation cannot ground dash parry but still can while airborne; Yamato and Holliday can do it on the ground. [77:17]
- Mina's ult cleanses debuffs on cast but she can still be debuffed during it; the tooltip saying she is immune to negative status effects is misleading. She is immune to CC but not to small debuffs or slows. [58:00]
- Express Shot nerfs don't fix the core issue, which is that all fire rate is overtuned. [145:25]
- High-Velocity Rounds don't add numbers, but they make your gun close to hitscan, which makes shots significantly easier to hit. [144:22]
- Gun Warden builds generally skip Majestic Leap, which is for spirit Warden playing for the ult; gun Warden maxes the ult second to last. [46:37]
