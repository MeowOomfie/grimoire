# Meta Changing Patch Today | Deadlock Patch Overview
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-01-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CsbexJsua8

## Covers
Patch reaction by Midknight to a short hotfix he calls bigger than any patch of the previous two months, mainly a global 10% gun damage nerf.
Covers Calico, Holliday, Mirage, Shiv, Magician, Vindicta, Viper, Wraith and Yamato changes, the Sharpshooter nerf and an item nerf captioned as "chemical fire", plus a quick recap of how the four newly released heroes changed since Hero Labs.
Patch of 2025-01-20.

## Claims
- Calico got a decent buff: more movement speed in cat form, and her slash got buffs; he agrees with the cat form speed buff. [0:00]
- Verdict on Calico: she is already very strong in bullet damage and people just have not learned her yet, so this buff will make her a real problem once players learn her. [0:30]
- Holliday got a nerf overall, though it is fairly neutral: lower bullet scaling, Crackshot reduced a bit with better spirit scaling, but she does not build many spirit items. [1:00]
- Holliday's Powder Keg was reduced by a lot. [1:00]
- Holliday's headshot now triggers on minions, so she clears a little faster and can get Crackshot off cooldown sooner by shooting minions. [1:00]
- Holliday got a small buff to a tier 2 upgrade giving back damage from earlier, and her lasso tier 2 got a little more duration; she had been very oppressive in lane. [1:00]
- Mirage's Scarabs got 25% less max health stolen but now scale better with spirit. [1:31]
- Verdict on Mirage: as a Mirage main he says Scarabs are very overtuned; a flat nerf would have been fine, but the spirit scaling buff may make him even stronger in the end game. [1:31]
- Shiv's Slice and Dice was nerfed; good nerf, it was doing a lot of damage and rough to play against. [2:01]
- Shiv's Bloodletting got a longer cooldown and changes to how long it defers damage; verdict: no amount of tuning will fix it, it will keep making him feel too tanky. [2:01]
- Magician got a pretty big, deserved nerf: Vexing Bolt damage, how long his Spectral Assistant lasts and the Assistant cooldown. [2:31]
- Rabbits from his Rabbit Hex got a little more movement speed to run away, and a bug letting him teleport with the ear was fixed. [3:01]
- Magician's gun needs a buff because its bullet velocity feels bad, and his ult needs longer cast range or longer than 10 seconds of holding a stolen ult. [2:31]
- Vindicta got a small nerf: a little less movement speed and a little less bullet velocity; he says the game is better when Vindicta is in a bad spot. [3:01]
- Viper got a gun damage nerf of -2 bullet damage, on top of the global gun nerf; she does a lot of damage with a lot of sliding movement speed, especially late game. [3:31]
- Verdict on Viper: the nerf is significant but may not be enough; she still feels very strong and he expects more nerfs. [4:02]
- Wraith's Card Trick got a pretty big nerf and her ult got a small, deserved buff; overall still a nerf but not a major one. [4:02]
- Wraith's ult had felt weak since it changed to silencing and disarming instead of holding targets up; Card Trick at three stacked cards did too much damage. [4:02]
- Yamato got another bullet damage nerf; he says a Yamato gun build with her ult will never be healthy for the game, so expect more nerfs while it works. [4:33]
- The big change: every hero's base gun damage and gun damage growth was reduced by 10%, on top of the per-hero numbers. [5:33]
- Because weapon damage from items is a percentage of base damage, this is effectively a flat 10% nerf to nearly all bullet damage. [5:33]
- Verdict on the gun nerf: heavy-handed but expected after a gun-heavy meta; every gun build is about 10% worse, so spirit may come back into the meta, and it should be meta-defining for this patch. [6:33]
- An item captioned as "chemical fire" was nerfed; he expects it nerfed further, since on lockdown heroes like Warden or Kelvin it gives about 40% extra damage and also destroys objectives. [6:33]
- Sharpshooter got a very big nerf: 10% less damage and a .5 larger movement speed penalty; it had been very strong, especially on long range Mirage builds. [7:03]
- Magician now steals the enemy ult: a very short range (about 10 meters) narrow cone, and he keeps the stolen ult only 10 seconds, which gives little flexibility. [8:33]
- Calico's release version: she turns into a cat that moves a little faster, keeps her slash, throws sticky bombs, and her new ult makes her invulnerable for a second then does big damage around her; good for dodging Warden cage, Infernus ult and similar. [9:03]
- Holliday's only real release change is that her ult is now a skill shot; he likes it because he dislikes point and click CC in MOBAs. [9:33]
- Viper did not change much on release; her shank may be new but is basically Shiv's shank. [9:33]
