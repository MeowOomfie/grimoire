# Massive Deadlock Changes Pt. 1 Items | Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-03-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1KPA7ct4uJQ

## Covers
Patch reaction by Midknight to the large early March 2026 patch, part 1: map, objective and trooper changes plus the item changes, with a verdict on each.
Hero changes are left for a separate video. Patch date 2026-03-07.

## Claims
- Jumping during a slide was changed; he dislikes it because it was high-skill movement tech that raised the skill ceiling. [0:00]
- Dash jump now grants a brief period of increased air control. [0:00]
- Shrines (captioned "friends") are now easier to kill for the first one and harder for the second; he likes it. [0:00]
- Shrines no longer hit heroes standing under the shrine; big change. [0:00]
- Super trooper bonus DPS increased; he thinks they did not need a buff since they already felt oppressive, especially with no shrine respawn. [0:31]
- Mid lane troopers now upgrade when either shrine dies instead of both; he hates it. [0:31]
- Killing a pair of base Guardians now spawns a zipline-boosted trooper wave to reinforce your push. [0:31]
- Troopers now regenerate out of combat; a wave buff. [1:01]
- Trooper wave interval goes from every 25 to every 20 seconds starting at 35 minutes, so very late games close out; makes sense. [1:01]
- Guardian bounty increased so Guardians actually matter. [1:01]
- Walker bounty increased; he does not love it and calls it a gun buff. [1:01]
- Walker health rescaled: they start lower and end about the same. [1:01]
- Walker armor now decays from +65 to -65 over 18 minutes instead of 20, so they want Walkers sieged mid game; by his math Walkers become killable around 13 to 14 minutes. [1:32]
- Base Guardian bounty increased and a shrine bounty added; he doubts killing shrines needed a buff. [2:02]
- Mid-Boss base HP increased. [2:02]
- Mid-Boss global announcement threshold moved from 70% to 50% health; he dislikes it because teams can now burst the Mid-Boss faster before anyone is told. [2:02]
- Mid-Boss debuff resistance removed and Rejuvenator drop duration reduced; he thinks this rewards forcing the Mid-Boss. [2:33]
- Rejuvenator duration no longer refreshes when picking up the crystal later; good change. [2:33]
- The minimap now shows whether an objective (captioned "earnest") is favored, neutral or unfavored; good for low elo and it hurts mod abusers. [2:33]
- A neutral camp was added near the Hidden King and Archmother Walkers, on the left and right sides. [2:33]
- Zero-way veils (block vision both ways) added under the shrine in each base, adding counterplay. [3:05]
- Mid lane bridge above the Hidden King base entrance moved more to the middle; mostly visual. [3:35]
- Subsequent CC reduction was increased (captioned 8% to 24%), so chained CC is easier to deal with; he finds it odd alongside the debuff-remover nerfs. [3:35]
- The Walker camps from the four-lane map are back. [3:35]
- Fix: melee abilities now use conditional base damage based on enemy state; he says it feels bad. [4:05]
- Streetball removed the item slot limit; sounds like a fun change. [4:05]
- New spirit item Golden Goose Egg: -10% damage, gains soul value over time (captioned 75% per minute), and its active hatches it to turn the stored souls into a permanent buff per 200 souls. [4:05]
- Golden Goose Egg verdict: Trophy Collector done right; it handicaps you but does not push you to fight, which is what makes it viable; he will buy it, unsure if it will be meta. [4:35]
- Extended Magazine now gives more weapon damage. [5:06]
- High-Velocity Rounds no longer grant fire rate, they grant weapon damage instead; a big buff, keeping in mind Slowing Bullets is bought mainly for its 16% weapon damage at tier 2. [5:06]
- Long range falloff increased (on the High-Velocity item). [5:36]
- Active Reload can now reload while already full; good quality of life, but he thinks the item is overtuned and is disappointed it was not nerfed. [5:36]
- Opening Rounds spirit power buffed; spirit heroes like Rem already buy it as a tier 2 gun spike, so it did not need a buff. [5:36]
- Swift Striker fire rate buffed; people were not buying it. [6:06]
- Mystic Shot spirit scaling brought back; it is mainly bought for no damage falloff and infinite lane poke, and this is a decent scaling buff. [6:06]
- Backstabber renamed to Stalker. [6:37]
- Split Shot enabled for Celeste, now supports custom angles per hero, and its cooldown was reduced; he questions the cooldown buff. [6:37]
- Stalker no longer needs proximity (the 8 m check) for its overtime bonus; he is surprised by this. [6:37]
- Melee Charge heavy melee bonus now works with heavy melee abilities; he questions buffing melee abilities. [7:07]
- Spirit Shredder Bullets spirit lifesteal increased; people already buy it, so he does not see why. [7:37]
- Ballistic Enchantment weapon damage per stack up slightly, ability range decreased (he likes the range cut), non-hero weapon damage increased. [7:37]
- Hunter's Aura solo multiplier reduced from 3 to 2 and its bullet resist reduction increased: more value in general fights, less in 1v1s; good change. [7:37]
- Weighted Shots now builds from Slowing Bullets and has the Slowing Bullets passive. [8:08]
- Weighted Shots no longer grants spirit resist (good) and its downside is now -movement speed (good) instead of -bullet velocity, which was not a real downside. [8:08]
- Weighted Shots debuff resist and weapon damage increased, stamina reduction decreased; he thinks it is already a stat stick and dislikes it getting the Slowing Bullets passive on top of high flat weapon damage. [8:39]
- Blood Shroud (as captioned) no longer has healing reduction, self-damage raised a bit; he predicts it will run lobbies because the one thing keeping it in check is gone. [8:39]
- Spirit Rend cooldown buffed; it did not need a buff. [9:10]
- Crushing Fists heavy melee change, okay. [9:10]
- Frenzy base fire rate increased and conditional fire rate reduced; unsure it needed it. [9:40]
- Spiritual Overflow power increased; makes sense, no one buys it. [9:40]
- An item now gives +20% ability duration while shooting enemies (unclear if only in combat); he thinks it seems strong, could fit Dynamo, and wants to see where it lands. [9:40]
- Extra Health buffed; he thinks it is already overpowered. [10:41]
- Restorative Locket buffed. [10:41]
- A stamina item now always restores at least one stamina and its max stamina regen increased; he still will not buy it. [10:41]
- Return Fire now always hits the target's body so it cannot miss, but its damage was reduced. [10:41]
- Weapon Shielding damage threshold and cooldown nerfed even though people are not buying it. [10:41]
- A low-health resilience item's threshold raised to 50% while its resist dropped from 22% to 15%; he sarcastically says bruisers needed a buff. [11:11]
- Lifestrike heal on melee hit reduced from 160 to 100 and now scales, so it is no longer rushable; good change. [11:11]
- Trophy Collector souls per minute buffed again; he says this does not fix the item's core issues. [12:13]
- Majestic Leap buffed; the item needed it. [12:13]
- An item captioned "No magic" (50 second cooldown after its rework) had its cooldown reduced; he still will not buy it. [12:13]
- Spellbreaker damage reduction reduced; good, it felt like a complete neuter to some heroes. [12:44]
- Glaive lord (as captioned) cooldown decreased; he does not think it needed it or that buyers change. [12:44]
- Inhibitor damage penalty reduced and weapon damage increased; you should already have been getting this item. [12:44]
- Cheat Death no longer reduces movement speed; he likes it, though he will only buy it in streetball, not normal games. [12:44]
- Colossus active now grants +30% melee damage; he questions why. [12:44]
- Siphon Bullets now steals 2.5% of the enemy's max HP per bullet instead of a fixed amount; with its short cooldown that is like old Tankbuster every couple of seconds, which he thinks is insane for late game gun heroes. [13:15]
- He fears Lucky Shot plus Siphon Bullets plus some bullet lifesteal on a late game gun hero being near immortal. [13:46]
- Mystic Regeneration buffed (+10 HP per proc); a fair buff that should make the item better; buying it on Apollo is griefing. [14:16]
- Rusted Barrel cooldown cut by about 20%; he calls it the dumbest change in the patch because the item was already insanely good. [14:16]
- Arcane Surge gives more spirit power; makes sense, people stopped buying it. [14:47]
- Slowing Hex now sets the target's gravity scale, making normal heroes heavier and slower (neutral on Celeste); this is what he suggested and he likes it. [14:47]
- Slowing Hex projectile speed increased and cooldown reduced; he thinks it was already plenty good and that it hurts Rem players hard. [15:19]
- Suppressor (captioned "Depressor") debuff duration increased; he still thinks the item is bad, but people stopped buying it. [15:50]
- Spirit Sap spirit power loss raised from -12 to -30 and cooldown reduced; people skip it because it builds into bad items, not because its spirit strip is weak; he might start buying it. [15:50]
- An item captioned "Atlas" got more damage spirit scaling; you do not buy it for the damage, but it may suit Rem with high spirit. [16:20]
- Knockdown gravity increased from +50 to +100 so airborne targets fall faster; he reads it as actually a nerf. [16:20]
- Radiant Regeneration buffed (healing on cast, boon scaling increased); he thinks it might be decent now and will try it, still not on Apollo. [16:50]
- Tankbuster current health bonus buffed; makes sense. [17:20]
- Torment Pulse spirit scaling nerfed by .03 (about 3 damage a tick, around 4 DPS at Rem's 120 late game spirit); not enough, Lash and Holliday style heroes will still buy it. [17:20]
- Torment Pulse no longer wakes sleeping enemies, so on Rem you can light or heavy melee sleeping targets again; huge. [17:50]
- Magic Carpet buffed; huge. [18:22]
- Ethereal Shift cooldown raised by 10 seconds (35 to 45); bad change, since that is the difference between one and two uses in a fight. [18:22]
- An item's damage penalty increased; he likes it and thinks it should be more. [18:52]
- Vortex Web now has a unit target option that vacuums enemies around that target; seems strong but fun. [18:52]
