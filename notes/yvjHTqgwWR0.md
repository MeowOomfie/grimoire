# The Most Intense Game On Vindicta

## Covers
Vindicta, aquatiq commentary over one of his own games (about 400 games on her) using the "Aiden Never Lose Spirit Hybrid 6.0" build.
Lane against Calico and Doorman, item choices and why, March 2026 patch.

## Claims
- Build used: "Aiden Never Lose Spirit Hybrid 6.0", a spirit hybrid build. [0:34]
- Vindicta is surprisingly hard to carry on if you like a carry play style. [0:34]
- Her crow used to be single target and now works kind of like an AoE; very strong. [1:04]
- When Calico uses Leaping Slash she will probably follow with a punch, so be ready to parry. [3:07]
- You can parry midair now. [3:07]
- In lane avoid Doorman when he has his ult and Calico when she has hers, since she can press 4 and one-shot you, especially with Stalker (formerly Backstabber). [4:07]
- This build takes Rapid Recharge very early, which is unusual. [5:11]
- He saved ability points to get the snipe (Assassinate) next rather than upgrading other abilities. [5:11]
- Vindicta has to stay far from enemies to have an effect; pairing her with a close range hero like Drifter makes the team comp work. [8:50]
- Counterspell is really good on her in this game. [10:23]
- Her ult is clunky to cancel: you seem to have to wait for the charge meter before you can deselect the scope. [11:24]
- Only snipe targets low enough to be killed ("snipeable"); wait for teammates to bring a target low enough. [15:03]
- Maxing the snipe gives a lot more weapon damage per kill. [17:43]
- If you buy Blood Tribute you have to get Leech for the synergy. [17:43]
- Toxic Bullets is a must on her in this game and easy to forget. [21:32]
- She lacks AoE, so when the enemy pushes with several heroes she has little she can do; a hybrid build helps. [23:07]
- Order he settled on: Toxic Bullets, then Leech. [24:08]
- With the build there are no points in her 1. [27:14]
- With more money than the enemy, push down the lane instead of being scared; clear your lanes, bad waves let enemies walk into your base. [26:43]
- Boundless Spirit adds damage on her flight, crow and Assassinate. [28:16]
- Late game, heroes with gun damage have the most impact going into the enemy base. [31:49]
