# Deadlock's BIGGEST PATCH EVER | Patch Overview
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-01-23
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMPZJ1IfiD8

## Covers
Live first reaction to the big 2026-01-23 update: six new heroes, the Street Brawl game mode, the Patrons and team renames, UI and quality of life additions, new enchantment items, and a walk through the reworked bases (base guardians, shrines, removed vents). For players catching up on what changed; light on numbers.

## Claims
- The update adds six new heroes, among them Venator, Celeste, Graves and Rem. [0:01]
- New game mode Street Brawl: an all mid 6v6 like League's ARAM, you pick your hero, it is a short round based format with streamlined shopping where you get a roll of items instead of buying them. [0:01]
- Street Brawl has special custom items, for example one that drops a piano on someone's head. [0:32]
- The map is reworked: a new Patron on the other side with two new zips (if unchanged since testing), and troopers got a visual overhaul. [1:02]
- Hero previews: Apollo looks like a demon; one new hero is a support with an AoE sleep on her ult (Rem); one summons minions (Graves); one is a fencer style front line M1 with tankiness built in and no on-hit. [1:02]
- New heroes now release two per week instead of three; he hates this, because you don't get to play the new content, even though it is better for him as a content creator. [1:33]
- New scoring system with an MVP. [2:04]
- The teams are renamed Hidden King and Archmother. [2:04]
- UI improvements: heroes look beaten up at low HP (McGinnis shown), new chat, new kill streak display, reactive portraits. [2:34]
- Players now have animated custom channel bars for each of their channeled abilities, and damage numbers are cleaner. [2:34]
- The advertised replay upgrade is only Steam game recordings, not a replay system update. [3:05]
- Objective health bars and the scoreboard are reworked, mostly visual. [3:36]
- A built-in mid boss timer is added; it lowers the skill ceiling but means you don't need mods, and he thinks it is overall a good change (he had hoped they would ban the mid boss timer mod). [3:36]
- A random hero button and a Rejuvenator duration timer are added; many former mods are being built in. [3:36]
- Visible outlines are added (something many players already did in their config file), plus a hole punch view that lets the camera clip through objects behind you. [4:06]
- Neutrals near walls fix, settings rework with the same settings laid out better, and a custom crosshair maker. [4:06]
- New item type, enchantments: one imbues an ability with increased range, and dealing damage with that ability grants increased weapon damage per target hit; gun heroes needed more fun items. [5:11]
- He suggests that enchantment on Vindicta with one point into her crow (Crow Familiar) could be very strong. [5:11]
- Recharge Rush: dealing significant weapon damage replenishes a charge on each of your charged abilities. He thinks it seems insane, probably not great on Wraith (better to buy Wraith damage items), but imagines it on gun Viscous for infinite punches. [5:41]
- The new base has a lot of height to fight around; most base defense fights will happen from that height, and there is a veil pocket in the truck and an under area to wall bounce up. [6:12]
- The side vents in the base, which he used as an escape and outplay spot, appear to be removed. [6:43]
- One base nook can only be entered by heroes with small hitboxes (he names Rem), which leaves room for future small heroes. [7:13]
- The middle of the map looks generally the same, with visual upgrades and a newly lit path. [7:43]
- The base went from two zips to three in this layout, but bases look like they will play generally the same; the other base has an extra jump up instead of the small-hero cubby. [8:13]
- Base guardians can no longer be parried. [9:13]
- Base guardians no longer have a headshot hitbox, so together with no parry they are significantly stronger, a big change for gameplay. [9:44]
- Shrines are still very tanky. [10:14]
- Shrines now have an attack that curses you and throws you back, so you have to space shrines now; you have to be up on the high ground to see it. He assumes it can be countered with a spell break item but could not check. [10:45]
- Patron knock looks cooler; the downed Patron now has a laser attack and curses anyone inside its pit. [11:47]
- He plans to research each new hero on release day and coach every one the day after. [12:17]
