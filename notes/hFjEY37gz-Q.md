# Rank 2 Victor - Macro & Build Guide
channel: HERESY
date: 2025-12-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hFjEY37gz-Q

## Covers
Victor from a top 50 NA, near rank one Victor: the Pain Battery and Jump Start health mini game in lane, tower dives, the 4.8k Torment Pulse spike, invading jungle, ultimate usage (proactive plays, cleansing Curse, tactical death at mid boss), fight positioning and target choice, Aura of Suffering ramp and Infuser timing, then a full spirit build with flex items and the items he calls traps (Healing Tempo, Cultist Sacrifice). For players who know Victor's kit and want to climb on him.

## Claims
- Victor's lane phase is deceptively complicated and is the number one thing holding Victor players back in lower elos; his late game and team fighting are relatively simple. [0:31]
- His whole lane hinges on efficient use of Pain Battery, which has no time cooldown: it charges when Victor takes, by default, 40% of his maximum health in damage. The more damage he heals over the lane, the more damage he can do. [0:31] [1:01]
- Reworked healing minions heal a percentage of missing health, so the lower Victor's health, the more they heal him. [1:01]
- Jump Start damages you for a percentage of current HP, so the lower Victor is, the more Jump Start effectively heals. Victor is technically strongest at trading when relatively low on HP. [1:32]
- Don't get so low you can be bullied out of trading (at 200 HP you can't fight anyone), but don't sit at full HP either: walk the line between high enough not to be bullied and low enough to heal effectively. [2:03] [2:35]
- With Pain Battery charged and Jump Start coming up, if you plan to fight the wave, use Pain Battery first and only then cast Jump Start. [2:35]
- When you level up mid lane, upgrade Jump Start before casting it; it gets you 50 extra health. [2:35] [3:05]
- Exception: at about 250 HP against a burst threat like Grey Talon's arrow, use Jump Start purely for the heal right away, because going in for a Pain Battery trade could get you killed. [3:05]
- Early in the game Victor has one of the highest DPS weapons in the game, so he is well equipped to punish enemies who overextend or blow their core abilities. [3:36]
- Aura of Suffering hurts Victor less than it hurts the enemy, and the self damage charges Pain Battery. If everyone in a fight has used their abilities, Victor is the strongest person in it because he always has Aura of Suffering and his gun. [3:36] [4:07]
- Victor is good at tower diving: use a Pain Battery, then dive expecting to take enough damage to cast another within a few seconds; few heroes have a high damage skill on a 3 to 4 second cooldown in lane. [4:07] [4:37]
- His ultimate adds safety to spend HP as an offensive resource; dive towers when you have the ult advantage (both of you have ults and both enemies don't). [5:08]
- Mastering the Jump Start, Pain Battery and health bar mini game is mastering Victor. [5:42]
- Victor gains a lot from the 4.8k spirit investment spike. You will likely be saving for Torment Pulse around the 5 to 6 minute mark: accept you are weaker while saving, then play very aggressively once you buy it, since you are likely stronger in that window. [5:42] [6:14]
- Throw Pain Batteries preferentially at Pocket rather than another target, because Pocket has a weakness to spirit damage. [6:14] [6:44]
- Pocket is one of Victor's hardest counters; when the enemy has a Pocket, try lane swapping into his lane, since Victor's lane matchup into Pocket is very favorable, and shutting him down in lane makes the end game much smoother. [6:44]
- Don't rotate to an important fight with unspent souls; don't linger at a walker you can't push when you could be spending your souls in case a fight starts elsewhere. [7:15] [7:46]
- Small increases in Victor's damage give much more survivability, because more damage means more healing and more time alive to cycle Pain Battery and Jump Start. [7:46]
- Stay aware of fights around the map; don't push a lane or kill a camp with a big bank when something may happen. Victor is a skirmisher, not an AFK farmer. [8:17]
- Spirit Victor has extraordinarily high health regen from many Jump Starts, so he excels at invading the enemy jungle and taking lots of fights: trade both players to half HP and Victor heals back fast while the enemy doesn't. [8:17] [8:48]
- Victor should generally not AFK farm his own jungle; whenever you can get away with it (especially with ult up), invading the enemy jungle is almost always better than taking your own. [8:48] [9:18]
- Take your own jungle when you can't invade safely: no ultimate, Jump Start not maxed (so no CC cleanse to escape), or souls not spent. [9:18]
- Victor is very good at clearing center sacrifices, because he can heal through their damage. [9:18]
- When you know you're weak, prioritize your own jungle; when strong, get into the enemy's jungle, or you are wasting your power. [9:50]
- The ultimate is also a proactive play-making tool: he used it to defend the blue guardian, the most important guardian, against four attackers by bombing the wave in Aura of Suffering, since staying meant getting stunned by the ult. [9:50] [10:20]
- By the 11 minute mark guardians are easily divable by two, maybe three players. [9:50]
- Victor's ult effectively cleanses Curse and all CC: being cursed inside four enemies late would kill most heroes, but Victor can respawn and keep fighting while Curse is on cooldown. [10:20] [10:50]
- At mid boss Victor can fight normally with massive AoE damage, or tactically die to use the ultimate's stun to secure the area and fight afterwards. [11:51]
- When the enemy engages and your team isn't grouped, retreat toward your teammates rather than charging; he played the space next to his Kelvin to use the healing of Kelvin's ultimate to generate more Pain Batteries and use his third ability aggressively. [11:51] [12:22]
- Target priority: chase down low mobility targets with bad positioning (for example a Paige caught out), and counter and harass enemy carries. [12:53]
- Victor likes fighting heroes with cleansable on-hit status effects: Toxic Bullets, Healbane, Infernus's burn, Mirage's Djinn's Mark, Seven's static. Each game note which enemies you counter and run them down in fights. [12:53] [13:26]
- You are the front line, but you don't have to stand next to your team: his movement speed, Aura of Suffering's on-hit slow and his cleanse let him charge ahead, create space and force carries out of position. [13:26] [13:57]
- Wraith is not a good target for Victor: no on-hit effects to cleanse, just raw upfront damage. He ran from the Wraith and left her to his Haze, then charged the Mirage he knew he could beat. Calculate these matchups in team fights. [13:57] [14:29]
- Aura of Suffering ramps over 10 seconds and does four times the damage at the end of the cast compared to the start, so in late game team fights cast it before the fight begins (about 280 instead of 130 damage per second when you jump into three people). [15:00]
- Early, on a single target, pre-charging doesn't matter much because self damage rises with damage dealt; late, hitting multiple targets, you deal much more than you take. [15:32]
- On Statlocker, Infuser sits at the bottom of Victor's highest win rate items, likely because players pop it before fully channeling Aura of Suffering, losing two to three times the AoE spirit damage and hundreds of HP of healing per fight. [15:32] [16:03] [16:34]
- Dive with aura fully charged, then use Infuser; that is how he practically solo wiped a team. [16:34]
- The build was mostly solved by Low Main and Listic months ago, with adjustments for the new patch and 4.8k breakpoints. [17:05]
- Early items: in most cases save to hit Torment Pulse as fast as possible; it gets you to the 4.8k spike and gives a huge spirit jump that adds damage to Pain Battery and sustain to Jump Start. Raw spirit is Victor's best stat, especially early. [17:05] [17:35]
- Opening 800s: either rush Extra Spirit into Improved Spirit, or Extra Spirit and Extra Charge. Improved Spirit gives more damage; Extra Charge gives about the same healing plus an extra Jump Start cast, so if you're low on HP buy Extra Charge instead of a default healing item. [17:35] [18:05]
- In a close lane it's standard to buy one supplementary item like Monster Rounds or Extra Regen while saving the full 3,200 souls, and play weak while saving. [18:05] [18:35]
- Track enemy purchases: if it's 6 minutes and you sit on 2,500 souls while the enemies have spent theirs, don't get aggressive or fight them on even terms. [18:35]
- After Torment Pulse most Victors go into green items; that's reasonable, but Victor doesn't need it, because his effective HP comes from constant healing, not max health. Spirit items make him tankier by improving Jump Start healing and camp clear speed. [19:06] [19:37]
- The 7.2k spirit breakpoint is now very efficient too (14 spirit for 2,400 souls), so after Torment Pulse he goes straight into Improved Spirit and Compress Cooldown on Jump Start. [19:37] [20:07]
- Enduring Speed is still a core item even after the movement speed nerfs, since Victor doesn't stack multiple speed sources and isn't firing his gun; it helps him track people with his third ability. [20:07] [20:38]
- Don't rush the full Healing Booster upgrade in lane: it doesn't boost heals from heal minions and only adds about 1, later 2, health regen per second through Jump Start. It becomes truly effective once Pain Battery is maxed, since it boosts the missing health heal from Pain Battery on heroes; get it then. [20:38] [21:09] [21:39]
- Default to Spirit Lifesteal over the barrier items. Max health, armor and barriers all make it harder to charge Pain Battery (damage to a barrier doesn't count), so Victor wants effective HP from regeneration instead. [22:09] [22:40] [23:10]
- Spirit Lifesteal also builds into Infuser, one of Victor's three strongest items. Spirit Shielding or Reactive Barrier can be better in early fights, but they hurt your end game; if you take a barrier, drop Spirit Lifesteal for it and pick Lifesteal up later. [23:41] [24:13]
- Don't spend more than the 4.8k breakpoint in greens; move on to more important items. [24:13]
- The first purple upgrade should be the cooldown upgrade (Superior Cooldown): with Pain Battery maxed it makes batteries easier to charge, since cooldown speeds up Pain Battery generation when you take damage, and it lowers the ultimate cooldown. [24:13] [24:43]
- Treat the ultimate as a play-making tool: with it up you can skirmish in the enemy jungle confidently, push risky waves, and are much harder to kill when ganked in your own jungle. [24:43] [25:13]
- The mid core order is situational game to game. [25:13]
- Rapid Recharge is a late game must but is only worth rushing when there are DoTs to cleanse. [25:43]
- Greater Expansion's range helps Aura of Suffering hit multiple targets and Pain Battery reach far targets for heals; it also gives spirit resist, so it's better when enemies buy spirit damage. [25:43] [26:14]
- Warp Stone is Victor's default bullet resist item, but the teleport alone isn't worth 3,200; don't default it until there are skills to dodge or enemy gun damage scales up. [26:14]
- Mystic Vulnerability is an excellent DPS increase that also makes him tankier, and it doesn't raise self damage: Aura of Suffering always hits you for 70% of its tooltip and can't be armored, while Mystic Vulnerability and Escalating Exposure make enemies take more instead of raising your outgoing damage. [26:44] [27:15]
- By this point sell Improved Spirit for Mystic Vulnerability, since you no longer need the flat spirit for Jump Start healing; it's basically a flat 8% damage increase. [27:15] [27:46]
- The 6k core power spikes are Infuser and Escalating Exposure. Into high spirit damage or very tanky targets (like a burrowed Mo & Krill) go Escalating Exposure first; in most cases go Infuser first. [27:46] [28:17]
- Escalating Exposure is 4,800 souls for its passive alone (the 8% spirit resist shred is the same as Mystic Vulnerability), and the passive isn't improved by Transcendent Cooldown. Infuser can mean a thousand or more HP of healing in a team fight. [28:17] [28:49]
- Use Infuser only after charging Aura of Suffering at least 5 seconds (triple damage, quadruple at 10 seconds): at 250 damage per target into three people, Victor instantly heals about a thousand HP. [28:49] [29:20]
- Finish the core with Transcendent Cooldown: you have many item cooldowns, so cycling Infuser and Warp Stone and boosting Torment Pulse damage is great. [29:50]
- Early flex: Monster Rounds in lane is both offensive (clear waves faster to take better trades) and defensive (keeps the wave off your guardian and cuts the time you expose yourself to poke while clearing). [30:20] [30:50]
- The barriers are generally suboptimal but usable in high chaos games with lots of skirmishing in the enemy jungle and around towers rather than hard farming. [30:50] [31:21]
- Suppressor can be rushed as item one when laning against high gun damage (two gun heroes), but not against Mirage, whose damage comes through Djinn's Mark. It gives less spirit than other 800s but scales well from mid game, and it also lowers jungle camp damage since it slows their fire rate. [31:21] [31:51]
- Late flex: Ethereal Shift had its cooldown practically doubled; it's now situational, bought to dodge AoE ultimates and stuns (for example Lash, Infernus, Wraith, Pocket), and realistically you only get one use per fight. [32:21] [32:51]
- Pocket's Affliction is the hardest counter to Victor; Counterspell is a cheaper way to dodge it if you pay attention. [33:21]
- Cauterize is Victor's best anti-heal, but don't default to being the team's anti-healer: buy it only from about 20 minutes on when your teammates haven't bought enough anti-heal. [33:21] [33:51]
- Return Fire is a very good late game hybrid item against the enemy gun carry; popped when a Wraith uses Full Auto on you, she can deal a thousand damage to herself. Buy it only as a late slot filler, don't sell a core item for it. [34:21] [34:51] [35:21]
- Late game you drop Improved Spirit; a spare late slot can hold Return Fire or Suppressor for the final fight. [35:21]
- With an open flex slot and money before the decisive fight, buy Rebuttal: its damage bonus applies to everything, so one parry gives about 30% more total damage, which is absurd in the mid boss pit after channeling aura, Warp Stone in and popping Infuser. It is better late than early. [35:52] [36:22]
- Capstones: most games buy Juggernaut, because enemies have gun carries and someone on your team should have it; if a teammate already has it, buy Plated Armor, since its effect doesn't stack. Spellbreaker is the spirit-oriented defensive option. [36:52] [37:22]
- Frenzy alone gets you the 4.8k gun breakpoint; only in super late game consider Berserker after it, and usually not. [37:22]
- If no situational counter item is needed (Ethereal Shift or Counterspell to dodge an ult, Juggernaut or Plated Armor for a gun carry), finish with Boundless Spirit as the last item: maximum spirit investment, and Aura of Suffering near 400 DPS with max boons. [37:22] [37:53]
- Never buy Healing Tempo on Victor: its stats only shine on teammates (McGinnis, Dynamo), the heal effectiveness gain is about 5% over Healing Booster, the regen is meaningless late, and it's bad even on gun Victor. [38:24] [38:56] [39:28]
- Spirit Victor is much better than gun Victor at every stage of the game in practically every circumstance. [39:28]
