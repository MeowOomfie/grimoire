## Covers
First look at the 2026-09-30 major update by aquatiq: new map tour, HUD and build menu changes, breakables, the Broker, mantling, then a Street Brawl game on Vindicta.
Patch reaction and map tour with light commentary, no coaching.

## Claims
- Six new heroes are up for a player vote this season (names in the captions include Rat King, Violet, Danny, Baba and Solomon). [0:00]
- Your stats are no longer small bars in the bottom left; they are now shown prominently, which makes building easier. [0:30]
- The build menu now shows the whole build when you click one, popular items change per hero, and there are quick gun, vitality and spirit investment options. [1:02]
- The whole map is restructured: overall lane layout is the same, but structures and paths to areas changed a lot, so map knowledge has to be relearned. [1:34]
- Some jungle camps now move around. [2:06]
- Unsecured souls are now called Haunted Souls. [2:36]
- Boxes (breakables) can only be broken with a heavy melee, not by shooting. [3:06]
- Golden statue buffs now give small item-like bonuses, for example +3 weapon damage; a vitality statue also exists. [3:06]
- Power-up buffs are gained the same way as before. [3:38]
- Some camps can no longer be sniped from the old lined-up positions. [4:39]
- Big boxes give much more souls, about twice a normal box (67 souls from one), so going for boxes is now worth it. [5:10]
- You can now mantle backwards. [5:41]
- Jungle camps are labelled with their tier. [6:13]
- A room on the Archmother side has two Sinner's Sacrifices in it; he wonders if that makes the Archmother side better. [6:44]
- The Broker lets you corrupt a high tier item you own into a stronger version that carries a random downside. [7:15]
- There is a new auto mantle setting. [19:12]
- Walker health is now visible on the HUD. [20:14]
- Vindicta Street Brawl build notes: bullet lifesteal with a small spirit investment for her snipe. [13:02]
- He went back to Alchemical Fire on Vindicta, an item players used to run on her, and needed a vitality investment too. [15:07]
