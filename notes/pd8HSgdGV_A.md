## Covers
Grey Talon, aquatiq commentary over one of his own games on a spirit build (Spirit Gun Shot as captioned).
Hard lane against Infernus and Geist, how to use the Guided Owl (bird) for stacks, items, March 2026 patch.

## Claims
- Build used: a spirit build named "Spirit God Shot" in the captions. [0:32]
- Grey Talon is very long ranged, but has poor wave clear; a lane with Ivy and no wave clear is not fun. [1:03]
- He calls Grey Talon an underpowered hero that needs a redesign. [1:34]
- He does a lot of damage, but none if you cannot hit people; enemies hiding behind boxes make it hard. [1:34]
- Early on his 1 does little until it is upgraded; he takes extra charges on it. [1:03]
- Gun Grey Talon is plausible and not too bad, though he would not build it. [2:36]
- Do not throw the bird from too far away or it will not reach the target. [6:17]
- Bait out the enemy ult (here Geist's), then back to heal. [6:49]
- Lane against Infernus and Geist is very hard on him; play for stacks to come back later. [9:23]
- Throw the bird every time it is up so it is on cooldown as much as possible, even if that sounds odd to new players. [9:23]
- He cannot take tier three triple camps, and has some of the worst camp and wave clear in the game; the only tool is shooting his 1 at camps, so he falls behind on souls. [9:54]
- The bird stuns its target. [12:01]
- Surge of Power gives his 1 a big burst, close to 500 damage. [14:06]
- When you get kills, the bird does a lot of damage, around 400, more than his 1. [14:06]
- He buys Tank Buster. [15:38]
- With the bird maxed, enemies at about a quarter HP should just die to it. [18:12]
- Superior Stamina is very good on him in his flight mode (ult). [19:52]
- Boundless Spirit next; his spirit keeps stacking ("infinite spirit damage"). [20:24]
- At 26 minutes and ahead, the team should go take the Mid-Boss rather than idle. [21:25]
- Use the bird on low HP enemies so you get the kill and not just the assist, since kills give stacks. [22:31]
- Verdict: he is weak in normal situations, which is why he is not picked. [24:33]
