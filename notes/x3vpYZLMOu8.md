# The Best Build for Every Hero in Deadlock
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-11-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x3vpYZLMOu8

## Covers
Winter 2025 hero-by-hero build picks, each credited to a top player for that hero, with why the build is best right now and how to play it.
Builds were picked on ladder and pro win rates and ease of use, meant for every rank from Initiate to Ascendant.
Heroes: Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Calico, The Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash, McGinnis, Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Seven, Shiv, Sinclair, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous, Vyper, Warden, Wraith, Yamato.

## Claims

- Abrams: later on he does massive damage with Crushing Fists and Frenzy; the build is super tanky, gets in and out fast and disables enemies. [3:02]
