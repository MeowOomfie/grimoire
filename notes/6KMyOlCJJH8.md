## Covers
Billy, Midknight, coaching review of one game by an Ascendant/Oracle Billy player (Cardiac).
Covers respecting trooper and tower damage, ability start and max order, light melee, movement, bad fights after objectives, slot lock and itemization.
Patch date: early June 2026.

## Claims
Player 1 (Billy, Oracle/Ascendant):
- Start Blasted, not Bashdown; Bashdown start is only fine if you will not use Blasted for early meleeing. [0:31]
- Blasted would have given a lot of extra HP on his early melee, plus the debuffs and more damage. [0:31]
- As Billy you should be light meleeing more early; Blasted accentuates how broken light melee is. [1:02]
- Pressing his 2 into the enemy cost him 200 HP; that was his fault. [1:02]
- He prefers a melee lifesteal start, then Extra Regen, over the shown order. [1:32]
- He was giving a lot of potential denies. [2:36]
- Play to steal the enemy tier one camp when you are already there. [2:36]
- When a lot of minions push to your tower, let them aggro the tower instead of standing in them; he took 400 avoidable minion damage. [3:36]
- At Ascendant he expects you to know buff timers. [4:07]
- On paper his lane should win; the enemy duo (Venator and partner) are good laners and Lash is a poor laner. [4:42]
- Pull trades further back into tower range; he took 600 tower damage that two steps back would have avoided. Respect trooper and tower damage. [5:13]
- Respect the enemy Venator's boost or he mugs you; use your 2 for movement speed to get out. [6:14]
- Positioning with a wall behind you is a coaching point, though it is hard to avoid. [6:45]
- Close Quarters makes sense and is a broken item. [7:18]
- Use your 3, then another ability, to clear a camp faster and get the HP back. [7:18]
- He bought Close Quarters at 1600 and then saved for another 1600, which is inefficient; buy the other 1600 first, then save for Close Quarters to top off the spike. [7:48]
- When you are strong and the enemies have used their ults, play offensively in that 20 to 50 second window. [7:48]
- Track ability usage: if Venator already used his boost on a dash, you do not have to respect it. [8:18]
- His movement is inefficient; watch a movement guide and practice in the practice range. [8:49]
- When an enemy is dead to rights, there is no reason to toss in a melee; the only way he lives is a parry, so do not feed him one. [9:19]
- He took a lot of damage from not rolling to cover quickly enough, a result of sloppy movement. [9:49]
- He missed the 10 minute buff. [9:49]
- He played too aggressively while the enemies had ults and he did not; you should not win that trade. [10:19]
- If you go neutral while hidden MMR pulls you down, you are slowly raising hidden MMR. [10:49]
- Every dash forward is the same: respect it and use 2 for movement. [11:19]
- If you are tilt queuing, take a break and come back tomorrow; your skill did not drop, it is mental. [11:19]
- He overextended mid when the team should have been playing for Urn. [11:52]
- If the enemy puts five on the Urn and your team puts three, you have to give it; try to recontest on the cash in. [12:22]
- While the team fights, a Seven putting pressure on a walker is good; the team should have traded a walker for the Urn. [12:54]
- Seven should have been in that fight since his ult is huge value in team fights. [12:54]
- Carry rule: play behind your front line, and if you have a teleport or save ability, do not overextend when it is not up. [13:25]
- When an enemy dives your Lash, kite away from your Lash instead of dying for him. [14:31]
- Throwing Hail Mary melees to buy time for teammates before dying is fine. [15:01]
- Slowing Hex (as captioned "slowing that") should do wonders this game against dash Infernus, Yamato and Celeste. [17:03]
- The main reason to buy it was neutralizing Celeste's grab, and it also shuts down Paradox's Carbine movement. [17:33]
- He fought sitting on 6K souls; buy before fighting. [17:33]
- Do not fight after objectives; back out, farm the camp you need and go buy your tier four. [18:03]
- Do not go into an enclosed space against a dash Infernus. [18:33]
- Weighted Shots is a good stat stick; Spirit Resilience was an insane item that game. [18:33]
- Securing sentries (camps) as Billy is good only when teammates cover you; without cover it is a free kill when they come to invade after mid. [19:03]
- Light meleeing a camp just to break it and leave because you are scared is great. [19:33]
- Fight near your walkers to use their resistances instead of fighting away from them. [20:03]
- In fights, stay near the walkers and actually do something in the fight. [20:35]
- He is glad he did not max his 1. Maxing the ult is good in a competitive landscape; in solo queue he prefers a 3 max. [21:09]
- You cannot cancel the 3 or the 1, but holding it keeps you in it for a second longer. [21:09]
- Countering a spell (Counterspell) could do wonders. [21:39]
- Point Blank is an item you only buy when you are slot locked, and he had four slots of upgradeable or sellable items. [22:09]
- He arrived at the Urn very late. [22:39]
- His 2 movement is insane but his general movement holds him back; do movement drills. [22:39]
- Before every fight check if you have an advantage or it is over an objective; if neither, invade the other side or split another objective, or ping fall back. [23:39]
- As a front line he had too many counter items: he would have bought only Spirit Shielding, not Spirit Shielding and Counterspell. [24:10]
- Securing aggressive camps is good, but not with a 700 bag when the enemy has Rejuv and Celeste could run you down. [24:40]
- He strongly dislikes Plated Armor; if you want a gun counter, take Metal Skin. One dash Infernus alone is not enough of a problem for it. [25:41]
- Chasing outside your base into an enemy Rejuv is very risky. [26:13]
- Silencer would not hurt: another anti-spirit item that is also strong, gives weapon damage and is a utility counter, good into dash Infernus. [27:15]
- He never used his Counterspell; to get value, practice it in Street Brawl or 1v1 a friend, for example parrying Viscous Puddle Punch. [28:16]
- Dumping everything to kill as fast as possible is right. [29:17]
- He went way too defensive with Spellbreaker; Indomitable is fine if you go that route, but he needed an offensive item. [30:52]
- Summary: respect trooper and tower damage, start the 3, watch the movement guide and do drills, do not fight after objectives, buy Point Blank only when slot locked, and do not buy Plated Armor or Spellbreaker that game. [30:52]
- Do not join bad fights; always ask if a fight has a purpose. [31:22]
- With the updated build he recommends a more offensive item, such as the item captioned "R2 blast" and Heroic Aura. [31:52]

General:
- Graves is weak and he would put her as an off carry. [16:33]
- Urn is very strong: a Shiv who keeps getting caught and is not winning fights is still at that rank because he understands Urn equals good. [30:17]
