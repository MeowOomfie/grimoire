# The New Meta In Deadlock (Movement Is Dead)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-12-17
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3m2VXxdQWV8

## Covers
A rundown of the December 2025 Christmas patch and how to build in it: movement speed and sprint nerfs with diminishing stacking, flat vitality boons, the weaker spirit investment, cheaper jungle, shrines that buff creeps, and hero nerfs (Billy, Lady Geist, Mirage, Shiv, Vindicta, Wraith, Seven). For players adapting their builds to that patch; it argues for an early 4.8k vitality rush, which Deathy disputes in XNR6JjHe9y0.

## Claims
- Getting hit now slows you by 20% of base movement speed instead of 10%. [0:30]
- Every movement item was nerfed, base movement speed was reduced, and movement speed bonuses now stack diminishingly, so the classic Fleetfoot plus Enduring Speed combo moves you much slower than before. [1:00]
- Still buy movement items, but stamina items like Kinetic Dash (and what he calls stamina mastery) got relatively stronger. [1:31]
- Even with stacked movement items you often aren't fast enough to slide and shoot, so ammo matters more and starting slides with a dash matters more. [2:03]
- Sprint now accelerates over a longer time and base sprint speed was nerfed on every hero; every sprint speed item was nerfed too. [2:03]
- Seven got a 40% nerf to his movement speed scaling; Sinclair also has movement speed scaling. [2:33]
- The other patch changes should make Seven better overall despite the movement nerf. [2:33]
- Enduring Speed and Fleetfoot give less slow resist now. [3:04]
- Bonus health on all weapon and spirit items was cut by 25%. [3:04]
- Vitality investment now gives a flat amount of HP per level instead of a percentage; until 4,800 souls in vitality every hero gets more health. [3:34]
- Beyond 4.8k in vitality the gains are more diminishing for heroes with a high base HP pool, so spamming green on tanks is not as strong as before. [4:04]
- Fragile heroes like Mina, Haze and Wraith, and middle heroes like Seven or Infernus, get a lot more health at 4.8k vitality. [4:04]
- Example: Seven has 700 base HP; before, the 4.8k vitality investment gave 34% extra health (340 on a 1,000 HP hero); now it gives a flat 525, about 200 to 250 more HP on an average hero at 4.8k. [4:35]
- Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem got a lot better again because they give percentage resists, so more base HP means they absorb more, and they now beat shielding items or items like Enduring Speed. [5:36]
- Rushing an early 4.8k vitality investment in lane makes you nearly unkillable, especially with both vests. [6:06]
- Build it with Enchanter's Emblem and Battle Vest if they suit your hero, plus Extra Health and Healing Rite; Healing Rite helps throughout the game. [6:37]
- The spirit investment bonus was nerfed from 45 to 38 spirit. [6:37]
- Surge of Power and Rapid Recharge, the items people rushed to reach 4.8k spirit (Surge of Power on Wraith, Rapid Recharge on Dynamo and Grey Talon), were also nerfed, so the early 4.8k spirit rush in lane is far less viable, including on heroes like Mirage. [7:07]
- There is now an argument for an early 4.8k in vitality or weapon instead of spirit, depending on hero. [7:38]
- Rushing double barrier vests to an early 4.8k vitality, even alongside Enduring Speed, is the strongest lane build right now: you won't die and you trade really well. [7:38]
- Trooper bounty was reduced by 12%. [8:08]
- Neutrals were buffed for farming: they are much more killable until 15 minutes, then have more HP after 15 minutes. [8:08]
- Fast jungle clearers like Seven and Infernus, and Pocket or Ivy who nuke tier two camps well, farm the jungle more easily early. [8:38]
- Souls from Sinner's Sacrifice were reduced. [8:38]
- Cultist got a slight nerf. [9:09]
- Large unsecured soul totals (a thousand plus) now dissipate slower; if you carry that many you should go back to base and buy anyway. [9:09]
- Shrines have more than double HP and are much harder to kill. [9:40]
- Killing both enemy shrines gives your creeps 30% more HP and damage, like Dota mega creeps; waves keep pushing into the enemy base and the losing team struggles to push out. [9:40]
- Downing both shrines gives a huge amount of map control, and split pushing is more encouraged. [10:10]
- Kill bounty was increased on solo kills and a bit on dual kills, to encourage smaller skirmishes and catching people in the jungle with unsecured souls. [10:40]
- Expect more small skirmishes and split pushing, and guard your shrines: losing both makes the game very hard to win. [10:40]
- Medic troopers were changed, with little impact overall. [11:10]
- Billy's light melee healing effectiveness was cut from 50 to 40, about a 20% nerf; Billy light melees almost all the time and rarely heavy melees. [11:41]
- Billy's Chain Gang disconnects faster out of line of sight. [12:11]
- Dynamo got a tiny nerf. [12:11]
- Lady Geist moved to bucket three, so she is treated as a tank, and she takes more damage from her own Malice and Essence Bomb, which makes her less oppressive in lane. [12:11]
- Mirage's Djinn's Mark procs slower and at a shorter distance, and his bullet damage and move speed were nerfed; he is still very playable. [12:41]
- Shiv's nerfs are small: Bloodletting heals or defers less at early levels, rage buildup was slowed 6%, and Killing Blow range dropped from 15 m to 13 m, so stay out of that range. [12:41]
- Vindicta's Assassinate lost 10 max damage at its second upgrade, which is irrelevant. [13:11]
- Wraith was nerfed harder than anyone: about 20% less bullet damage growth over the game, and less barrier scaling on her teleport. [13:42]
- Winners of the patch: Battle Vest, Enchanter's Emblem and Toxic Bullets; Toxic Bullets is one of the best items in the game now. [14:12]
- Orange and purple items that gave health, like Backstabber, Suppressor and Mystic Slow, are a bit worse; non-green items that gave no health got relatively better. [14:43]
- Siphon Bullets is the biggest loser: its proc went from every 0.8 to every 1.2 seconds, cutting its effective damage and self healing by 33%. [14:43]
- Don't buy Siphon Bullets on any hero anymore; it is now the second worst item in the game after Cheat Death. [15:14]
- Anything giving resists instead of base health got better, since vitality now gives a decent base HP pool; shielding is a bit less valuable and barriers and resists more viable. [15:44]
- Heroes that need movement speed should still run one movement item; he still runs Fleetfoot on Wraith and Seven but stopped also running Enduring Speed. [16:15]
- Buy only one movement item, Enduring Speed or Fleetfoot, not both; one or two movement items in a build is fine, anything beyond that is bad. [16:46]
- Slow items got even better: Slowing Hex, Mystic Slow and Slowing Bullets, because people can now mostly escape only by dashing and these reduce dash distance. [17:16]
- Gun builds got relatively stronger because slower targets are easier to hit with body and headshots, while spirit abilities are often AoE and always hit; this will show most in low ranks. [17:16]
- You no longer have to rush 4.8k spirit on every hero; before, early 4.8k spirit was best on about 90% of the roster, now there is good reason for early 4.8k vitality or gun. [18:17]
- Squishy heroes gain the most power this patch because they benefit more from vitality. [18:48]
