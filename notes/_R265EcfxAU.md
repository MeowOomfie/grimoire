## Covers
Midknight Vods co-coaches with PeerContent in a Rem-only free coaching stream right after Rem's release (stream of 1/27/26). Three Rem players are coached (a low Ascendant South America player, an Emissary player in a Dynamo lane, and Skeleton Honey at Eternus), plus one Yamato player at Ritualist when Rem VODs ran out.
Themes: Rem point order and builds (Guardian Ward, Healing Booster, Enchanter's Emblem, Torment Pulse, Echo Shard, Compress Cooldown vs Extra Charge), putting the 3 on healer troopers and other lanes for pressure, setting waves, box and tunnel routes, stealing centers, 2 routing (front line to back line) and recasts, Urn play, and new scroll-wheel movement tech.
Yamato session: hitting Flying Strike and mantle 1s, ult buffering, parrying Haze, melee discipline, Counterspell and Metal Skin, Urn rules, fighting around spikes, hero pools and limit testing in lane.
