# How to Build & Play Celeste | Archon Celeste Coaching
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-02-11

## Covers
Celeste coaching by Midknight: he reviews one game of an Archon-rank Celeste laning with Rem against Wraith and Doorman.
Themes: respecting trooper damage in lane, soul awareness and fighting off your spikes rather than while saving for them, respecting enemy ults, box routes before 5 minutes, movement (mantle sliding, instant air dashing, Superior Stamina), "at best" fights, ult and Light Eater usage, AP order and builds.
Video date 2026-02-11.

## Claims
Lane:
- Giving two denies on the first wave means the enemy gets their ability point and level before you; a good enemy kills a minion off the wave and then runs at you. [0:30]
- Be careful using stamina to dash in for souls; souls on the ground last a long time, you can wait a whole wave rotation and then pick them up, so you don't have to take damage to grab them. [1:00]
- With Rem you basically can't lose wave priority, as long as he puts his helper on the heal creep rather than a melee creep. [1:30]
- When the enemies hide under tower and you can neither poke them meaningfully nor siege the tower, shove the wave and do a box route instead of wasting time. [2:00]
- Fight aggressively while Rem is getting buffs, but backpedal once the wave is pushing into you; don't fight inside the wave. [2:30]
- He took almost 600 trooper damage, around 100 a minute, which is highly preventable. [2:30]
- With that much incoming damage, buy Extra Regen or another regen item. [3:00]
- Don't look for an aggressive fight when you are 11 souls away from your tier two item. [3:00]
- He doesn't think you need Extra Charge. [3:30]
- He picked the one moment when the enemies had ult on him; it was also a bad fight because it was in front of the wave, and killing those minions first would have given him his own ult. [3:30]
- Once you are losing ults on the enemies, they have a decent fight on you; shove the wave and box route, or take their left center camp, or wait for buffs. [4:33]
- After a good trade you have used your full combo, so box routing is the play, especially because Rem can send super minions while you box. [5:04]
- The biggest issue in lane is how much trooper damage you ignore. [5:34]
- Fighting aggressively while sitting on a tier two item is bad again; box route instead. [5:34]
- Trading while the enemies have no ults is fine, but the second they get ult back they will do exactly what they did before. [6:05]
- Once they have ults and spikes (Doorman has his spirit spike) and you are not on your spike, put a ton of respect on them or they run at you and one-shot you. [7:09]
- If you keep missing your shots, hold the wave and the targeting prioritizes enemy heroes. [7:39]
- A melee under the enemy tower is insanely risky: if she parries you she lives and you die parried under tower. [7:39]
- Good: using the ricochet a lot and shooting the enemies' feet. [7:39]
- Clear the wave first before walking forward; you can't fight in front of a massive wave. [8:09]
- He should have cleared the wave and looked for their tower rather than going that aggressive, though the micro in that fight (getting damage off before dying) was good. [8:39]

Movement:
- He should have mantle slid there. Celeste needs good movement understanding: mantle sliding and wall bouncing are even more valuable on her because of her minus 20% grab skill. [9:10]
- Mantle sliding is overpowered: press control as you mantle and you go very far. [27:06]
- You should have Superior Stamina by 22 minutes on Celeste; it gives a comical amount of value if you instant air dash and air strafe. [15:51]
- Instant air dashing means jumping and then instantly dashing for the diagonal air dash. [15:51]
- Be perma wall bouncing and air dashing on this hero; with Superior Stamina instant air dashes give huge distance. [27:06]

Ult, abilities and AP:
- Counterspell against Celeste's ult only stops one tick of damage; it keeps bouncing to someone else. A dash (E shift) can fully dodge the initial ult. [9:40]
- Her ult doesn't bounce off objectives. [10:43]
- You can hold the ult by holding left click (someone says the ult key works too): channel it while they are in the air and release the second they hit the ground. [14:50]
- In a 1v1 where the ricochet doesn't matter, shoot at their feet so the bounce hits them and auto-aims to them. [18:24]
- Toss your ult before you die. [18:55]
- People sleep on the spirit lifesteal from her 1 (Light Eater, around 35%); use it before your combo and you heal a lot because you do so much damage. [19:25]
- If you are jumping into their face anyway, throw the 1 first for the spirit lifesteal; he was sitting on it far too long. [23:00]
- You are sitting on your ult for way too long in fights. [21:29]
- Consistently missing the ult is often a macro issue, not aim: take an extra half second to line it up instead of forcing it, and throw it from about 10 meters away with good line of sight instead of jumping into all of them first. [21:29]
- If you keep missing the ult, throw it at minions near the enemies and it bounces to them. [25:01]
- Ideally get a few amp stacks before throwing the ult, but if you are jumping into a fight throw it first and farm amp stacks while it bounces. [22:30]
- Amp gained while the ult is bouncing should add to its damage as it lands; he is pretty certain but plans to test it. [23:31]
- AP order: put points into the 2, max the 1 first, then probably ult, then 3, then 2. All her maxes are strong and he won't blame other orders, but 1 max first is the play. [21:59]
- Celeste is not a 1v1 hero unless you have your 2 maxed. [11:14]

Items and builds:
- Buy Ballistic Enchantment only after Greater Expansion: you buy it for ability range on the 3, and Greater Expansion gives more range to every ability. [13:19]
- Her gun damage comes from on-hit effects; base weapon DPS is only about 90, though Ballistic Enchantment still has value because you stack it often with your charges. [13:50]
- You don't need a 4.8k gun spike on Celeste; if you want one, Toxic Bullets is probably best, Ballistic Enchantment is fine, plus Kinetic Dash. [14:20]
- Lightbringer says Active Reload is viable; he hasn't tried it. [15:20]
- Healing Banner, spirit lifesteal and Stamina Mastery seem like all the greens you need. [17:53]
- The only major build mistake was having no Superior Stamina. [25:01]
- Builds: Lightbringer's is solid if you know what you are doing (his weakest, he thinks); Wick's is the best simple left-to-right build; for fire rate, Lain's or Metro's are okay solo queue builds. [20:29]
- How to read a Lightbringer build: buy 4.8k from the categories, grade a second 3.2k item in the most meaningful category (spirit for her), and prioritize the items on the left of each category. [27:36]

Macro and fights:
- An "at best" fight is one where you know you have no teammates to reinforce you but you only see some of the enemies, so at best it is a 1v1; don't take it, because he could easily have help. [11:14]
- He took another aggressive 1v1 with no info on reinforcements while sitting on a tier three (green spike), which is doubly bad; backing off to the secret shop was right. [12:18]
- When bored with good wave priority and no camps up, run the urn; box routing gets fewer souls than running urn. [13:50]
- Don't take an aggressive fight where you know the enemy's two fed heroes are; back up. [14:20]
- Don't reset to heal while waves are crashing into you. [16:22]
- Don't tunnel vision chase the Calico when you know their whole team is there. [18:24]
- If you see Doorman jump into a pit and ult, he is animation locked: you get one free melee and then parry. [19:56]
- A late but fundamentally good call is better than no call at all. [19:25]
- Focus the Patron instead of fishing for a stack of your 3. [24:01]
- After taking their whole base, even with a big souls lead your team is probably sitting on unspent souls, so use a stagger to back out and farm; staying is only okay because nothing is on the map, and if mid boss were up it would be a bad fight. [24:01]

Summary notes:
- Respect trooper damage much more in lane. [26:04]
- Play around your spikes: fight off your spike, never while saving for one. [26:04]
- Respect enemy ults and track their cooldowns; all three lane deaths came from a double ult. [26:04]
- Box route, especially before 5 minutes, when they play hard under tower and you can't siege or poke. [26:36]
- Stop taking at best fights. [27:06]
