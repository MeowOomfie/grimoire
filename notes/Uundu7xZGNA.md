## Covers
Warden, aquatiq, commentary over one ranked game right after the big rank update, on a build that maxes Alchemical Flask.
Notes on the flask, targets and late game calls, plus a word on the new rank system. Recorded 2026-08-03.

## Claims
- After the rank update, Oracle 6 is now a high rank. [0:31]
- People underestimate how much damage Alchemical Flask does; a direct hit on the head hurts a lot. [4:04]
- If he is taking that much damage in lane, he has to buy Extra Regen. [6:13]
- Warden's barrier (Willpower) is very good. [7:44]
- Shooting Vindicta is mostly pointless because her flight lasts so long and she flies away; he shoots the other target instead. [7:44]
- Do not buy Knockdown against Vindicta this early in the game. [8:17]
- Paige's gun has poor bullet velocity so she is forced to buy High-Velocity Rounds; with good velocity she hits like a truck. [8:48]
- This build maxes Alchemical Flask (build ID shown on screen). [9:51]
- When the enemy is hitting him with spirit damage he buys spirit armor, even though he would rather keep High-Velocity Rounds. [13:26]
- A movement speed powerup is perfect for Warden because he is so slow. [13:56]
- With the enemy grouped elsewhere, he takes the walker for free and pushes the wave in. [14:28]
- Alchemical Flask takes away stamina, cuts enemy weapon damage, gives Warden weapon damage and applies minus fire rate, which is good against almost every hero. [16:36]
- Do not underestimate the flask; he calls it the strongest ability in the game. [16:36]
- He pushes the left side alone to take pressure off his teammates and draw an enemy to him. [17:36]
- He goes back to save a walker to keep the enemy starved, since a big reason they are losing is that they have no money. [20:43]
- Once his build is basically done, he buys Fortitude mainly for the bonus health. [23:18]
- Late game with a big lead, mid boss is better than the Rift; Rift minions do little at that point. [23:48]
- He forgot Fleetfoot this game and says with it he would have been much stronger. [24:19]
- In the game before, against five gun heroes, Plated Armor, Metal Skin and two Knockdowns for Vindicta were not enough; the only thing missing was Phantom Strike. [25:52]
- Win streaks give almost no rank points, so do not worry about them. [26:52]
