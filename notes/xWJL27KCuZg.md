# They Can't Find a Place For Victor | 3/25/26 Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-03-26
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWJL27KCuZg

## Covers
Patch reaction to the 3/25/26 Deadlock patch by Midknight, a Deadeye coach: each hero change with his verdict.
Heroes covered: Bebop, Calico, Celeste, Doorman, Graves, Holiday, Infernus, Kelvin, Geist, McGinnis, Mirage, Rem, Silver, Venator and Victor.

## Claims
- Bebop (captioned Pip) gets a small health regen buff to strengthen his lane; verdict: disagrees, his lane is already extremely strong and his hook needs at least double the cooldown during laning. [0:00]
- Calico gets more base damage on her slash; verdict: not needed, she was meta defining last patch and should be left in the dirt for a while. [0:30]
- Celeste gets laning stage nerfs and the silence on her maxed 2 is reduced; verdict: good, he thinks the shield ability should not silence at all since it already does everything. [0:30]
- Doorman's bell gets some radius back plus a small slow; verdict: makes sense, but one or two more meters would make him very OP, since bell size is what keeps him in check. [1:02]
- Doorman gets door distance back; verdict: not needed, his doors were absurdly long for a long time. [1:33]
- Doorman's cart tier two now deals 75 bonus damage with 0.06 spirit scaling when it hits a wall, with slightly less sustain on the cart tier two and three to compensate; verdict: still an overall buff, a lot of extra damage and scaling on a hero that already builds spirit. [1:33]
- Graves's 2 does less damage; verdict: good, the damage was absurd, though it still pulls from very far and high. [2:04]
- Graves's tier three now summons only one ghoul but gets -10 seconds cooldown; verdict: this stops the Echo Shard two build, but she may be stronger for it with a very valuable low cooldown utility. [2:04]
- Graves's goons get 20 less base HP, 4 less HP per boon and 15 less damage; verdict: good, she needs fundamental changes before she is allowed to define a meta. [2:35]
- Holiday's balance pad now gives allies air control and stomp damage; verdict: insane, huge rotational value, she can move her whole team if they follow her pathing, likely to show up in pro play when teams coordinate. [3:06]
- Holiday's Crack Shot tier three gets a lower cooldown on headshots against heroes or minions; verdict: fine, neither loves nor hates it. [3:36]
- Infernus Afterburner gets about 4 total DPS at tier one, and his ult gets more lifesteal and a cooldown decrease; verdict: fine changes that will not move him on a tier list. [3:36]
- Kelvin's spirit scaling is reduced, including the tier three scaling (0.2 off at max); verdict: good but way too little. [4:06]
- Geist gets reduced gun falloff and reduced Life Drain spirit scaling; verdict: she needed it, but she ends at the same strength when maxed, so the drain ("suck") build is still as strong late; he thinks that build should not be viable. [4:36]
- McGinnis's turret and wall each last half a second longer; verdict: fine, but the hero needs a rework. [5:07]
- Mirage's scarabs lose 2 DPS and 0.06 spirit scaling; verdict: probably not enough, the scarab build has been nuclear, wait and see. [5:37]
- Mirage's Tornado gets a higher cooldown that he gets back at tier two, and tier three adds 0.3 seconds of lift; verdict: insane, since he can cast it twice for about 0.6 seconds of extra CC. [5:37]
- Mirage's Mark spirit scaling is reduced a bit; verdict: good. [6:08]
- Rem gets unstoppable on his ult; verdict: welcome. [6:08]
- Silver (captioned Silas) is a little weaker early and scales a little better, likely ending at the same point. [6:08]
- Silver's Slam Fire (her 1) gets a cooldown increase that she gets back later; verdict: only a laning nerf, the real issue is that it does half of anyone's HP bar. [6:38]
- Venator (captioned Vanders) gets a grenade nerf and his Gut Shot wall stun goes from 0.65 to 0.6 seconds; verdict: huge, because a heavy melee takes 0.64 seconds to connect, so he can no longer stun then heavy melee at base. [6:38]
- The same timing is why Abrams, whose charge stuns for 0.6 seconds, cannot heavy melee after it until he has two points in his charge. [7:08]
- Most Venators were not using the stun then heavy melee tech, so nerfing it will not change that he is already good; his damage scaling is also reduced, but his core issue is what he does with Echo Shard. [7:08]
- Victor's built-in Healbane on himself is walked back: Jump Start no longer cleanses debuffs, and instead cuts debuffs in half (he read it as 50% debuff resistance while his 2 is up); verdict: good change. [7:38]
- Before this, Victor was a knowledge check: he permanently cleansed Spirit Burn and similar, and the answer was Crippling Headshot (Healbane somewhat, Toxic Bullets a little); now anti heal and debuffs keep some effect on him. [8:08]
- Victor's stink is a little shorter. [8:38]
- Verdict on Victor overall: the devs reacted too quickly to Victor mains calling him unplayable; coaches agreed he was worse but not dead, and he was never really a pro play hero. [8:38]
