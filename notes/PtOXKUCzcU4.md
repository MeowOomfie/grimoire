# You Don't Understand Objectives | Arcanist Shiv Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-06-24
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PtOXKUCzcU4

## Covers
Coaching review by Midknight (former top-10 Mirage) of an Arcanist-rank Shiv player's game, a 2025 video on a June 2025 patch.
Themes: objective focus inside fights (Rejuvenator, Urn), mid boss calls, pressing kill advantages, Shiv dash use and vertical escapes, rage management, item choices against this comp (Warden, Abrams, Haze, Ivy, Bebop), right-click combos.

## Claims
Shiv laning and dash use:
- Before he gets his dash Shiv is very immobile with a shotgun, and the first wave is rough; once you have the dash, be more aggressive. [1:02]
- The quickest path to losing lane is not shooting the wave and letting it crash into your tower repeatedly, because minions do a lot of damage to objectives right now. [1:33]
- Reposition with a movement dash-jump (shift dash) instead of spending the ability dash. [1:33]
- Do not look for overly aggressive trades in lane. [2:04]
- Using the dash correctly is key: without it Shiv is hyper immobile, and dashing behind enemies at 300 HP strands you and gets you killed. [2:35]
- Unless you have kill potential, poke with the dash in and dash back out, like a Pocket going in and out. [3:06]
- Slide after your dashes. [8:16]

Shiv items:
- If going double defensive on Shiv, prefer Extra Regen and Mystic Regen; Mystic Regen gives a flat ~40 heal every knife. [3:06]
- Restorative Shot gives ~40 healing per shot but is hyper micro intensive: you must throw it at enemies, not minions. [3:37]
- Knife is on a 16 second cooldown, so you need to land roughly every other Restorative Shot on an enemy for it to out-heal regen. [4:38]
- Mystic Shot procs Mystic Regen, and Backstabber also procs it on every backstab (around 30 HP, versus ~38-40 on a knife). [5:09]
- With Backstabber in the build, Mystic Regen is better than Restorative Shot because it is less micro dependent. [6:12]
- After a lane where you took a lot of damage, go Extra Regen then Mystic Regen; Backstabber is better on paper than Mystic Shot but finicky, so Mystic Shot is a preference. [7:15]
- Backstabber gives damage, chase and regen; the chase is very valuable on Shiv, who only has his dash. [8:48]
- Into Warden he always buys Restorative Locket, which he calls super slept on: two stamina in your back pocket. Not as a first item. [6:44]
- Buy Restorative Locket instead of Cold Front here; in the ~400 souls before you have it, play safer when Warden has ult. [7:46]
- Restorative Locket is bought for the two stamina, not the healing; it also helps get out of things like Haze's ult. [8:16]
- Kinetic Dash is very good on Shiv: it keeps you on top of enemies and the fire rate feels nice; Arcane Surge is also very strong. [11:27]
- Decay is good on Shiv: half your rage on one button, and its anti-heal is very strong into Warden and Abrams, pretty good into Haze, decent into Vindicta. [11:57]
- Decay is even more beneficial in middling elos because players manage rage less well. [12:30]
- Correction from chat: Decay no longer gives ability duration, but is still valuable for its other effects. [13:01]
- Opening Rounds is strong on gun Shiv, but not good if you are not a gun build. [14:32]
- Weighted Shots is a strong item, great into Abrams, and he likes it into Vindicta, Haze sleep, Ivy stun and Warden cage. [17:35]
- Debuff Remover gives a lot of value into Abrams (parry his charge even when he has duration) and removes Bebop bombs; buying it and not removing them is bad. [19:36]
- Pop Debuff Remover before you die; dying with it up is never good. [33:22]
- Debuff Remover can remove Warden's cage before it hits you. [44:14]
- Berserker is a pretty standard gun item. [23:12]
- Warp Stone on gun Shiv: Stamina Mastery is much better at the same price, giving more chase and movement; it is also a good crutch for stamina management. [30:52]
- Warp Stone is defensible into Paradox or Holliday, because you can warp onto someone after Paradox swaps (carbine) or Holliday barrels to dodge the ult. [51:54]
- Do not Warp Stone into the things you are attacking and get lost; if you do, be ready to flick back. [41:37]
- Return Fire farms a lot of rage on Shiv and is very good into a fed Haze who is nearly one-shotting you; buy it sooner, then sell it later. [45:18]
- Selling for active slots to get Colossus is fine. [46:20]
- Bullet Resilience plus Plated Armor only worked because neither fed enemy carry had Armor Piercing Rounds; normally they buy it and bullets go straight through bullet armor. [49:21]
- Plated Armor is good into Lucky Shot because it denies lucky shot procs. [48:51]
- Echo Shard gives bullet resist (new to him). [49:51]
- Stacking resistances is not always best against fed gun carries; Disarming Hex (purple) is very strong into two fed gun carries, cheaper than Curse, though Curse is never bad. [50:21]
- Arctic Blast on gun Shiv: freeze them, then right-click their head for the burst and the extra 15% damage, ideally while Opening Rounds procs; if going Arctic Blast, consider Express Shot for double right-click burst. [42:38]
- Arctic Blast does not cancel Haze's ult, and she had Unstoppable. [48:21]
- You are meleeing too much for having no melee items. [41:37]

Shiv rage, ult and combos:
- When resetting, jump off the rope for a split second and throw a knife into a camp so you walk out of base with 50-70% rage, and throw another on the way back. [9:52]
- When rage is bleeding, put even one bullet into a wave or camp so you do not lose it all. [14:32]
- Farming a T3 camp with double melees gives full rage; stand at the very edge of the hitbox and double melee in succession. [15:33]
- Shiv is the most important hero to know double melee spots on camps. [17:35]
- Gun builds max the ult first. [18:35]
- Cold Front before executing to be extra safe; a painful thing on Shiv is enemies healing up during the execute animation. [18:05]
- Combo: knife, right click, dash, right click, knife, right click; using abilities after right clicks hides the right-click downtime. [52:54]
- Always right click when you have one bullet left in your mag; right click more on Shiv. [52:54]
- Use right clicks while running away for the movement, and right-click a camp while moving away from it since it barely slows you. [40:34]
- On the ground the dash only goes horizontal; jump or wall jump first, then dash upward. [45:49]
- One of Shiv's best escapes is jumping and vertical dashing on top of something, or wall jump then vertical dash in the air; think of the dash in 3D. [43:42]

Movement and positioning:
- Mid jump out of base: sideways air dash then double jump; it saves about 3 seconds every rotation if you are mid lane. [10:22]
- Do not stand where the enemy can hit you and your one-HP Wraith at once; at least throw a knife at the attacker. [9:20]
- With a teammate nearby, hug the wall, and when the enemy walks across, jump out and dash so she is hit by both dashes. [9:20]
- Path behind enemies in enclosed areas; Shiv loves flanking and a double dash. [13:32]
- When chasing, stay between the enemy and his escape and do not dash yet; wait until he gets past you, then dash to keep chasing. [17:05]
- Only light melee the vending machines when you need to get somewhere immediately. [17:35]
- Versus Bebop's bomb, stand still behind a pillar instead of moving. [20:06]
- For quick zip grabs, drop off, slide, jump, slide and grab again instead of spam grabbing, using momentum on the ground. [41:05]
- Map knowledge comes with time; the only way to speed it up is time in the practice tool. [4:08]

Fights and objectives:
- Do not farm while your team is fighting. [15:33]
- Run away with the Urn instead of dropping it where it gives them an Urn cash. [15:33]
- When two of your team split, you are down two in the fight; play keep away. [16:05]
- Do not chase; chasing is very bad. [16:35]
- Back off early when you are too deep into a Bebop. [19:05]
- Do not chase two healthy enemies into an at-best-neutral fight when their walker is about to get resistances. [21:09]
- Whenever you get kills, look at the map and press the advantage: run Urn if it is up, mid boss later in the game, or split objectives. [21:39]
- After two or three kills you squandered the advantage; at least take a walker or run Urn. [27:18]
- With a two-player advantage, rush mid boss. [26:48]
- Do not steal a teammate's (Holliday's) bridge buffs; the risk to her mental outweighs getting three buffs. [22:41]
- Once a walker is already lost, boosting back is a classic mid-to-low elo mistake: it wastes the boost and puts two people on one lane; push mid or steal their camps instead. [23:44]
- Do not overstay a siege with no map awareness, especially into Ivy who has one of the strongest wave clears. [25:47]
- Always make sure you overkill; do not sit on your dash in a fight. [26:48]
- The mid boss call came too late; it needed to happen when Abrams was down or had no ult. [28:50]
- Better to make bad mid boss calls than no mid boss calls at this elo. [29:51]
- Good mid time: one player catches the wave while high objective-DPS heroes (Wraith, Infernus) and Holliday go mid. [31:22]
- A mid call 30 seconds late is awful, and this team took 30 seconds to kill mid boss. [32:52]
- In any fight around mid boss your mind should be on getting the Rejuvenator; it sat for minutes while you dashed past it. [33:22]
- In a team fight over the Urn, focus on picking it up; cashing the Urn is super valuable. [34:28]
- Keep shooting and knifing Ivy while she is teleporting. [35:29]
- With five enemies dead, knock the Patron; do not reset, be in their base with your team. [35:59]
- Shoot the shrine before the enemy shows up. [36:30]
- Drop off a fight when your teammates have not arrived or most of the team is at half HP or less, especially into base healing. [37:30]
- Take the enemy's lotto (as captioned): it gives three buffs, denies them from the enemy and takes a long time to respawn. [38:31]
- Hybrid camp rule of thumb: before 25 minutes always leave one alive on their side; after 25 minutes full farm if winning; after 35 minutes in a neutral game leave it unless it is in their back 30%. [40:04]
- Always leave their basement camps, because it takes them a while to see they are unfarmed. [40:34]
- 1.5k damage in a whole fight is sloppy. [39:33]
- Your team running in one at a time loses; group for a real team fight. [43:11]
- Stall waves but respect Bebop hook while waiting for teammates to respawn. [44:14]
- Do not walk up when all your survivability items are on cooldown. [48:51]
- Do not take an Urn fight two players down. [46:50]
- Summary: more objective focus in fights, Stamina Mastery over Warp Stone, Return Fire sooner, Disarming Hex, mid boss calls 20 seconds earlier (you win this game with that), and vertical dash movement. [52:24]

Practice:
- "Ankle weight training": play games focusing on only one thing, for example a game using only right click, to learn its movement; bot games are a gentler start. [54:57]
- If you know a tool but forget it, loop a YouTube timer that says "reminder" every 30 seconds to prompt yourself. [55:27]
