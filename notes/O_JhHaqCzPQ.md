## Covers
Midknight Vods reviews two replays on a short free coaching stream: a Violet player (rank not said) and an Oracle Danny, then reads the kit of the newly released support hero (captions: Haro) live and tests it in the sandbox.
Themes: wave freezing and lane swapping on Violet, buying regen on time, tempo versus scaling items (Radiant, Cultist, Trophy Collector, Fortitude), shielding for squishy spirit heroes, playing Rifts and not giving them up, shoving mid before rotating, shrine deficits, target priority and calling targets on mic.
Also Q and A on Close Quarters versus Rebuttal and Solomon, plus his view that a third of the roster is overtuned this patch.

Q and A after the reviews:
- Support Solomon is a good support build, but Solomon's numbers are so strong that playing him as support is inefficient. [93:13]
- He doesn't like Close Quarters as a gun spike unless you need melee resist. [93:44]
- Rebuttal is for when the enemy team has many melee heroes. Close Quarters is for when one lane opponent does steady melee damage, like a Billy, and the rest of their team doesn't. [94:48]
- If you face only a little melee, buy Close Quarters for its multi-purpose stats. If you face a lot, buy Rebuttal because it is overtuned. The question is whether you value the lower parry cooldown or more close-range gun damage. [95:48]
- Placing a right click out of range normally goes to max range, but on Solomon, if a wall is in the way it defaults to minimum distance by the wall. To place it through a wall, move it very unaggressively. [96:49]
- Solomon's bishop: use it for priority on wave one, level it, and break it right after wave one when you go to buy, so you get the slow reset. [99:23]
