Vindicta, aquatiq, commentary over one ranked game (OCE) on a team with Holliday, Shiv, Mirage and Ivy against Bebop, Drifter, Warden and Lash.

## Claims
- Vindicta is very strong right now and the speaker only uses one build on her. [0:31]
- Bebop is a direct counter to Vindicta in lane; unlike Mirage she cannot dodge his stuff. [1:33]
- Against an aggressive Bebop lane, play super safe. [2:37]
- Do not fly in a lane with Bebop; if you fly you get hooked, so fly rarely. [3:38]
- A missed Bebop hook is a big window for your lane. [3:38]
- You need Assassinate (snipe) early; not having it yet is bad. [5:15]
- After Assassinate, Extra Charge is the next buy. [5:15]
- A pick made possible by the extra charge is "why you get Extra Charge". [10:31]
- Greedy option while already getting assists: Rapid Recharge, then Trophy Collector early. [8:25]
- Start Trophy Collector stacking early. [8:56]
- Against a team that takes little gun damage, the speaker considers an item captioned "Indom" a super good buy. [15:29]
- Later item goal: Burst Fire, but get the magazine item first. [18:08]
- Cut out side items and go magazine then Spiritual Overflow; the speaker does not even want Sharpshooter. [19:09]
- When snipes are not landing, jump straight to Spiritual Overflow and just shoot people; he skips Blood Tribute that game. [22:43]
- When neither team is doing anything and the enemy sits in base, take mid boss to force a fight. [19:39]
- Teammate plan agreed by the speaker: let the enemy take mid boss and kill them off after. [20:41]
- When the enemy is on high souls, go for mid boss; going mid is all it took for a fight to happen, like Urn or Rift. [21:41]
- Vindicta can fly and headshot enemies inside their own base and kill them, which the speaker says no other hero can do. [24:24]
