## Covers
Grey Talon gameplay with commentary by aquatiq against a mostly spirit-damage team (Pocket, Sinclair, Holliday, Mo & Krill).
Mostly banter; a few notes on bird stacks on the new map, Pocket, spirit resist items and staying back.
Commentary over a game, patch of 2026-10-17.

## Claims
- Bird stacks are much harder to get on this map because there are so many buildings to traverse; he got 60 to 80 a game on the last map and about half that now. [0:37]
- Minus 15 spirit resist makes enemies take a lot of damage. [1:38]
- When he aggros a bird onto a target it is very easy to get stacks. [10:01]
- Stay out of Pocket's range; a Pocket running at you can do 3,000 damage. [6:56]
- Do not shoot your arrow at an enemy while he is burrowed or at a clone; it does nothing. [8:28]
- Solomon does not do much to you while you are in the air. [11:06]
- Against an all-spirit team he considered spirit shielding and then Spirit Resilience. [11:36]
- Getting one-shot by a Sinclair, he doubted Spirit Resilience alone would be enough but bought it and his green items before anything else. [16:52]
- Solomon cannot fight up close. [17:23]
- Rat King's banner is something he is kind of forced to use. [20:32]
- When the enemy uses all their ults early, fight them right then. [25:51]
- Leave when your team is low and mid boss is off; stay alive rather than die there. [25:20]
- As Grey Talon, stay back; he is not playing a carry. [28:30]
