# The OFFICIAL Deadlock Hero Tier List
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-08-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCFFWt1t5yU

## Covers
A ladder (not pro play) tier list for every hero, recorded about a day after the late August 2025 hero update (the 2025-08-19 update: Billy, Doorman, Mina and Paige out, Drifter and Victor not yet released). Ratings average across all ranks, with notes for new players.
Main theme: the meta is spirit burst, resist items were nerfed, and the new heroes are strong, with Doorman a tier above everyone. Also covers why win rates mislead.

## Claims
- This is an early tier list, made with Drifter and Victor not yet out and the patch only about a day old, so the meta may change. [0:00]
- Ratings are an average across every rank and keep low-rank strength in mind for new players. [0:31]
- Spirit damage is king: everything does spirit damage except Haze and Vyper, and all the new heroes are spirit or hybrid (Billy's melee counts as bullet damage). Mirage does gun damage to an extent. [1:02]
- Spirit Resilience got nerfed and is now bad, so don't buy it. The meta is burst spirit heavy. [1:33]
- Vyper: C, almost D. She feels very weak after being nerfed hard a few patches ago. [1:33]
- Having one or two gun damage heroes on a team has value just by existing, since nobody counters gun damage directly when everyone builds spirit armor: the enemy either buys bullet armor or takes your damage. That is the only reason Vyper is C and not D. [2:03]
- Haze: high B. Solid in lane and good against most new heroes, including Billy. [2:33]
- Haze is very good against Mina: Mina wants to pick Haze for Love Bites stacks, and a Haze with good aim can poke her out, since Mina has even less HP than Haze, one of the lowest HP heroes. [2:33]
- Play Haze as a spirit and silence hero: initiate with Sleep Dagger, Silence Wave, then ultimate; Fury Trance is a great item on her. [3:04]
- Mirage: a shell of his former self. He used to be insane in lane and is not scary there anymore; his global teleport needs team coordination that ladder players rarely use, and his split pushing is mediocre since he doesn't kill towers or walkers very fast. Better in pro play than on ladder. [3:34]
- Mirage was first put low A, then moved down to B after chat pushback; the coach agreed B makes more sense next to the others. [4:05]
- Abrams: low A, a bit above average. He is scary, okay in lane, and a hybrid build (a bit of everything) is really strong right now. [4:05]
- Abrams is not S tier, but he is one of the few very tanky heroes who can live through the spirit burst meta. [4:36]
- Bebop: B, below Haze but not as bad as Vyper. The new heroes, who are in every game, do very well against him: Billy is great into him, Mina can dodge the bombs, and if Bebop comes close the Doorman gets a free ultimate and can bully him. [5:09]
- Billy: the highest rank (S), "absolutely broken". New heroes feel weak at first only because players have few games on them; Billy is hard to play. [6:09]
- Billy wins a 1v1 against basically any hero as long as he has Blasted up and plays it properly. [6:39]
- Billy's ultimate is an insane teamfight ult: chain the whole enemy team, slow them so they can't escape, pull them to your team. He is an insane disruptor. [7:11]
- Billy can build very tanky or very high damage, and has about the fourth highest gun scaling in the game, so he does insane gun damage late. [7:11]
- Bad Billys melee a lot and get parried. Good Billys avoid being parried, use their 1 to reset Blasted, rarely melee, and shoot or use rapid recharge spirit abilities. Once players reach that level, Billy will 100% get nerfed. [7:41]
- Calico: below average. She is not as tanky as other tanks and not the best at burst (Pocket, Lash and Mina burst better); the main way to play her is still burst: Arctic Blast, leap in, press 1, then ult. A hero of all trades who does nothing amazing. [8:13]
- Doorman: by far the best hero in the game, given his own tier above S. A good Doorman maxes his card (Call Bell). [8:43]
- Doorman's card stun moves you away or into the enemy team while you are stunned the whole ride; hitting a wall stuns for another 1.5 seconds, and with Echo Shard he gets another card, so by combining ultimate and two cards he can lock your movement for about 10 seconds. [9:14]
- Doorman is an S tier laner, and his gun does a lot of damage; gun Doorman also hurts a lot. [9:14]
- Doorman's doors can save your team, let your team recontest mid boss, and in lane let you get behind the enemy to shoot them. Players will only get better at them. He will need hard nerfs. [9:45]
- Doorman's ultimate is the best solo ult in the game, better than Mo & Krill's: you can't move for a few seconds and take a lot of damage while the enemy team sets up and bursts you. [10:15]
- The one counter to Doorman's ultimate is jumping down inside it and using Counterspell, but you are probably dead after anyway. If you don't make it out of the hotel it does roughly 500 to 1,000 damage. [10:15]
- So far Valve has balanced the new heroes well: all strong but, other than Doorman, not completely broken, unlike the previous four-hero update where Calico and Holliday were broken. [11:18]
- Drifter: not out yet, no rating. [11:18]
- Dynamo: solid, not bad and not good. He is weak in lane, loses a lot of lanes and must play passive, and does little damage outside his ultimate. [11:49]
- Dynamo's Singularity is probably the strongest ultimate in the game and the best AoE teamfight: even 20-30k behind, a big Singularity can turn the game. You can also go the 1 max (Kinetic Pulse) build. [11:49]
- The Hero Labs heroes are on ice; Valve doesn't like them enough to release them now. [12:23]
- Grey Talon: A, not Vindicta level broken. With a frontline he does insane damage, and he is one of the highest spirit burst heroes in the game. [12:55]
- Grey Talon's Guided Owl was recently buffed and has a very low cooldown, so you can use it a lot in a fight; it is an amazing teamfight tool. [13:26]
- Grey Talon is very squishy and needs a lot of souls to come online, but is good in lane. If he catches you out mid game and hits about three arrows, you're dead. [13:26]
- Holliday: S, not quite DLC level. Her Lasso is still the best initiation in the game, she does insane lane damage with her gun, has some of the best movement (Bounce Pads to get in and out), and her barrels are really strong. [13:57]
- Holliday has been busted for a while and is unfun to play against; the coach hopes Valve addresses her. [14:27]
- Infernus: high B, almost A, a very good carry. Most players run a dash (Flame Dash) build. [14:27]
- The coach's theory: gun build Infernus is viable again because his gun has decent matchups into the new heroes. [14:27]
- Gun Infernus beats Mina: when you burn her she has to leave, and she is nearly killed every time because her base HP is so low. [14:58]
- Infernus has a decent matchup into Billy: keep your distance and burn him, since Billy must be in your face to do damage; if Billy doesn't have his charge (his 2), Infernus can dash away and kill him. [14:58]
- Ivy: mid to high A. She is a support who works really well with heroes like Vindicta because Kudzu Bomb is really annoying. [15:30]
- Ivy's ultimate (Air Drop) is one of the best in the game but goes unnoticed: it lets you escape or get in and silences the entire enemy team, stopping them from playing for a few seconds, and nobody credits the Ivy afterwards. [16:00]
- Kelvin: high A, not S because he isn't game-breaking like the new heroes or Holliday. He has some of the best gun scaling in the game. [16:31]
- Kelvin's ultimate (Frozen Shelter dome) is insane: it can save your team, stop the enemy team or just kill someone. [17:03]
- Lady Geist: higher B, mid. Good in lane and has a decent matchup into Billy, but nothing outstanding and can be outplayed a lot. [18:06]
- Doorman kills Lady Geist: she must get to melee range and wants to be low to Soul Exchange you, so a good Doorman waits until she is low, cards or ults her, and nukes her. [18:36]
- Lash: solid A, very strong. Still one of the best mobility heroes, has some of the best spirit burst, and one of the best teamfights in the game. [19:08]
- Lash benefits from the map changes and this meta: he buys Tankbuster, Mystic Reach and Superior Stamina, gets value, and Ground Strikes you. [19:40]
- McGinnis: below average ("okay is below average"), suffering like the other gun heroes, about Vyper's level. She was nerfed, can't tank through the spirit damage, and her turret build is not good, the turrets don't do enough. [19:40]
- Mina: mid A, not on the level of Doorman or Billy. She is a Pocket-style burst hero with one of the best escape abilities: go in, bait an ability, dodge, bait another, dodge, live, come back and burst more. [21:11]
- Mina doesn't burst as hard as Pocket but can stay in a fight longer. [21:41]
- Mina's ultimate is not that good on its own: break line of sight and you're fine, and it doesn't do much damage. It is crazy when combined on targets who are already stunned. [21:41]
- Mina is so squishy that when she ults you, you can often turn around, trade and kill her first. [22:12]
- Mina's ultimate is good against objectives like walkers, but you need to max it for big objective damage and most Mina builds don't put many points into it. [22:12]
- The average Mina player is bad; she is maybe the hardest hero in the game to play, other than Magician, with infinite potential. [22:43]
- Gun heroes like Mirage and Haze are rated where they are because the meta is 90%+ spirit burst and nobody buys gun resist, not because they are necessarily good. [23:14]
- Mo & Krill: S tier, easily the best tank in the game. [23:45]
- Resist items (Enchanter's Emblem, Battle Vest, and especially Spirit Resilience) were nerfed very hard and are often not worth buying; almost nobody buys Spirit Resilience anymore. [23:45]
- Mo & Krill is tanky through inherent resistance: he takes far less damage while burrowed, has a way to stop everyone from shooting, Scorn heals him a lot every few seconds, and his ultimate is a free kill with a teammate nearby. [24:16]
- Billy may be the better tank and counters Mo & Krill: if Krill burrows, Billy melees him on the ground and has about a thousand more HP when he comes out because of his passive. So Mo & Krill is not rated above Billy. [24:46]
- Paige: at least A, possibly stronger; the hardest to judge from too few games. She is a support (some play her with gun or books). [25:18]
- Paige's 2 is an insane shield, about 200-250 at level one in lane; maxed it shields three teammates, and on a carry it also gives more gun damage and fire rate. [25:18]
- Paige's ultimate is on a long cooldown for a reason: a map-wide AoE that hard stuns and damages the enemy team and heals your entire team. [25:48]
- Paige's 3 is a better Warden cage: a slowing field, and anyone still inside when the sword lands gets rooted at max level; maxed it also shreds 18% spirit resist, which matters in a spirit meta. [26:18]
- Play Paige as a debuff support: buy Rescue Beam, max her 3 and 2, shield everyone and debuff enemies. Hitting someone with the 3 is like a Vindicta tether with more debuff. [26:18]
- Paige's books do a lot of damage in lane. Her 1 is underwhelming: an AoE deny like Kudzu Bomb without the slow, easy to dodge and slow to cast, so not useful in lane. [27:19]
- Vindicta: S, the second most broken hero, probably above Billy. She Stakes you so you can't move, Crows you to debuff (mostly spirit resistance), then Alchemical Fire, and her spirit damage team nukes you. [27:49]
- Vindicta has no risk because she sits flying at the back of the map where you can't kill her; she needed a nerf a few patches ago. [28:20]
- Debuff Remover alone doesn't beat Vindicta: most Vindictas run Echo Shard for a double stake, and even if you cleanse the stake she then Crows you; Stake's cooldown is too low. You still kind of have to buy it against her. [28:52]
- Every Vindicta runs Suppressor, so your fire rate is also slowed; she is the most oppressive hero in the game. [28:52]
- Paradox: high A, back to strong, the second best initiator after Holliday and slightly weaker than her. Time Wall sometimes gives insane value; her laning is okay, and after lane she does really good damage, initiates and lives. [29:22]
- Pocket: S tier, the highest spirit burst in the game in a spirit burst meta. He flies in, Barrages a teammate so you don't notice the stacks, then flies into you and you're dead before you can play; even if you survive, Affliction leaves you at 1 HP and back in base. [30:23]
- Pocket needs skill to learn his game flow but no aim: the shotgun has a big spread and his 1 and 2 are massive AoE. Affliction is weak in lane but huge later after items like Mystic Expansion. [30:53]
- Seven: high A, no longer S. The new heroes are strong at all points, so ladder games are more fighting and less farming, and Seven needs a lot of souls and is weak in lane and early mid game. [32:23]
- Seven is still the best carry in the game once he gets farm, and may return to S once players stop forcing fights so hard. [32:54]
- Win rates alone don't show hero strength: popular heroes get skewed win rates, and Shiv had a low win rate even in patches where he was insane. [33:24]
- Heroes that die easily (Mina, Grey Talon) show win rates below their strength because they do nothing if played badly; Vindicta at a 43% win rate is still S because played properly she kills everyone. [34:25]
- Shiv: C. He is tanky and can execute you but doesn't feel scary; in about 150 games since the patch no Shiv carried. For a tank pick Mo & Krill, Billy or Abrams; for a killer pick Pocket. [34:55]
- Sinclair: fell off. His melee build has been gone for a while, and Rabbit Hexed targets can now run away fast, so the only real way to play him is 1 nuke (Vexing Bolt) and ultimate. [36:25]
- Sinclair is still insane in lane damage-wise, then falls off into a support, but has insane playmaking potential if he steals something like the enemy Singularity or Warden ultimate. [36:25]
- Victor: not out yet (expected Friday), likely a tank. [36:57]
- Viscous: very solid on ladder, not S. Cube, ultimate and Splatter are good, Puddle Punch not so much; spirit nuke Viscous and his ultimate are very annoying to play against. [37:28]
- Warden: only playable as an ultimate Warden, and ultimate Warden is broken: get Lightning Scroll and spirit items, jump in with Majestic Leap, and they all die. [37:58]
- Warden has the best mid boss contest damage in the game if you want to kill the enemy team rather than steal. His Flask is good in lane and his gun okay in lane, his 2 and 3 are weak, but Last Stand is a top three ultimate. [38:29]
- Warden's ultimate plus Lightning Scroll means nobody can move and they all die, and he has insane lifesteal so he survives. [38:59]
- Wraith: very weak right now, though not as low as Vyper. She got weaker for the same reason as Seven: she needs even more farm and is even worse in lane. [38:59]
- Wraith is a noob stomper: watch for her ultimate; Counterspell removes it completely, and even if you get lifted, dash behind a corner and you're fine, not even movement slowed. She does no damage until she has a lot of farm. [40:30]
- Wraith is better in lower ranks, and her win rate drops the higher the rank as players learn to counter her. She is very squishy, and even farmed must choose between damage and tankiness. [41:01]
- Yamato: A, very solid. [42:01]
- Expect the new heroes to only get stronger over time as players learn them. [43:34]
- Meta summary: spirit burst. Don't buy Spirit Resilience, it doesn't give enough value; buy Spellbreaker late game, play aggressive and try to kill the enemy before they kill you. [44:04]
