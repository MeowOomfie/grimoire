# 5 MISTAKES DEADLOCK NOOBS MAKE

## Covers
Five common new player mistakes: standing in front of the creep wave, not last hitting and denying, chasing kills instead of farming, ignoring the Urn, and not healing.
moe9times, beginner guide, no one coached. Patch of early September 2024.

## Claims
- Mistake 1: not standing behind your creep wave. Creeps do a huge amount of damage early; 5 seconds of a wave attacking you can take half your HP. [0:00]
- Behind your own wave you take no damage from enemy creeps. [0:30]
- Let your creeps hit the tower first so it targets them and you can shoot safely; the tower can kill you very quickly. [0:30]
- Do not walk close to towers; it triggers a very painful melee attack. [0:30]
- Mistake 2: not focusing on last hits and denies. In lane your priority is getting more souls than the enemy. [1:00]
- Last hit creeps, shoot their soul orbs, and deny the opponent's orbs by shooting the orange ones. [1:00]
- Harass and kill attempts are fine, but you should almost never miss creeps to get a bit of damage off. [1:00]
- You can see creeps through walls; watch all their HP and miss as few as possible. [1:30]
- If you reload too much in lane, you are either shooting heroes too much or shooting creeps too early; you do not need to shoot them down from full. [1:30]
- Go for the last hit when the creep's health bar starts turning white. [1:30]
- Mistake 3: chasing kills after lane. Do not fall into a deathmatch mentality; whenever you are not fighting, farm. [2:02]
- Clear jungle and lane creeps, and kill crates and golden statues; they give souls and permanent buffs. [2:02]
- Two weapon damage buffs from golden statues equal a 500 soul item, and they stack up over a game. [2:02]
- When a vending machine (Sinner's Sacrifice) is uncontested, melee it until the souls run out; each hit costs a little HP but gives souls, and finishing it gives a big chunk. [2:33]
- Mistake 4: forgetting the Urn. It gives your whole team lots of souls, and the runner a 25% bonus plus a free skill point; it is almost always worth running if safe. [2:33]
- The first Urn spawns at 10:30, then every 5 minutes. Taking it also denies the enemy the chance. [3:04]
- While carrying the Urn you cannot use items, abilities or shoot; if a fight breaks out, drop it and fight back. [3:04]
- Mistake 5: not healing. Sitting on low HP in lane is almost always wrong; at 150 HP under tower, use the speed boost to go back to base and return full. [3:35]
- If you speed boost back, you still have the boost to return to lane; time it between creep waves and you barely miss creeps. [3:35]
- Coming back full lets you contest and threaten kills instead of sitting behind your tower taking damage or feeding. [3:35]
