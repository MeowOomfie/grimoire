# How To Play Wraith (Best Method) | In-Depth Game Analysis (Deadlock)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-09-04
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yg-9GvaP04U

## Covers
A Wraith game review from September 2024 by a former Overwatch pro: winning a solo lane against Vindicta through wave pressure and denies, buy timing, Card Trick bursts, then turning the lead into a lifesteal hard carry (and the greedy deaths that nearly threw it).
For Wraith players and anyone wanting to learn solo lane pressure and playing from ahead. This is a 2024 video: Wraith's kit, the items named and the map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Wraith is a very solid laner: very good at getting last hits but not great at securing kills; many heroes do better in lane, Kelvin especially is insane right now. [0:30]
- Wraith struggles against a lot of tanks and loses lane to a really well played Haze, but Vindicta is a pretty even matchup. [0:30]
- Start pushing the lane really early with melees and last hits to push the enemy back and secure your last hits early, so you can then focus on denying the enemy creeps. [1:01]
- A common mistake is playing very passive in lane and not pressuring the enemy creeps, ending up at your own tower; pressuring forces the creeps onto the enemy tower and costs them creeps. [1:01]
- Avoid peeking out on the wrong angle (the right side). [1:33]
- By 2 minutes, aggressive pressure had him at triple Vindicta's souls. [1:33]
- Skill order (early statement): max Card Trick early; if you feel you can't survive, go Project Mind (the 2), otherwise go Full Auto (the 3); against Vindicta there is no need for an early escape. [2:03]
- First buy was High Velocity Mag, which he really likes on Wraith; with it you can secure almost every creep if you play well. [2:03]
- Stick around your healing creep; it is super essential and few players do it. [2:35]
- When the enemy is under pressure and needs healing, kill their healing creep first; the healing creep carries the lane for you. [2:35]
- Card Trick tracks to your crosshair, so you can slingshot cards around corners to hit enemies behind cover; it also tracks to enemies near it, which can be wonky; it takes practice. [3:06]
- Card Trick is Wraith's strongest ability in the early game. [3:06]
- Whenever your creeps are not in danger of dying in the next few seconds, attack an enemy creep with a melee or take the last hit with your gun; keeping the pressure up wins the lane. [3:36]
- He stayed in lane on 2,000 souls with only one item because keeping pressure up was worth more than going back. [4:06]
- Go back to shop right when your new wave has just landed at your tower: you can buy and return before any creep on either team dies. [4:37]
- Time your buys to when you have finished off the enemy creeps (the four that spawned), or go back after all the creeps are dead. [4:37]
- Basic Magazine is a must for Wraith: she has a really fast fire rate and needs a bigger magazine to sustain; he also loves Titanic Magazine on her. [5:39]
- He buys Sprint Boots, and Extra Charge is almost a must on Wraith because one more card charge has insane damage potential early. [6:09]
- Playing greedy at low HP when you are winning the lane hard can bait the enemy into over-committing; check the enemy has no ult first. [6:09]
- Kill combo: card, Telekinesis to lift her, second card, then slide behind a wall and card again; most of the damage was cards, and the kill needed Extra Charge. [6:39]
- If you are low after the enemy laner dies early, back right away, and back out as soon as you see her coming. [7:11]
- When your lane is going well, the enemy tends to call for ganks, so always keep an eye on the minimap. [7:41]
- With denies he was farming the creeps of two (his and the enemy's). [8:11]
- He bought Bullet Lifesteal earlier than usual because he was low and knew he could sustain in lane without going to spawn. [8:11]
- Once you have Bullet Lifesteal, shoot creeps more and melee them less to get more regen out of them. [8:43]
- No reason to leave the lane if it is going that well and the enemy is giving you farm for two. [9:43]
- He starts roaming either when doing really badly and needing help, or when he can farm the jungle, typically once he has Tesla Bullets, his main farming item for rotating. [9:43]
- Lifesteal lets you stay in lane at 200 HP; one creep shot gets you back to 300. [10:14]
- Active Reload is still a really good item on Wraith though it was just nerfed and he is starting to lean away from it; far ahead, he took it to snowball more lifesteal and fire rate. [10:14]
- You don't have to spend cards right away; hold all three for openings when enemies get close, a three-card burst nobody expects and that is hard to dodge since it follows your crosshair. [11:15]
- Always secure the souls that come out of a tower; they are shared by the whole team, and if the enemy denies them they hand souls to every player on their team. Always deny or confirm. [11:46]
- After Tesla Bullets he is set up for the farming stage and can rotate and gank. [11:46]
- Wraith's ult is really good for ganks if the team needs it, but he likes using Telekinesis defensively: lift the ganker and trade the kill. [12:17]
- When far ahead in farm, a 2v1 gank on you can fail and hand you free souls, so you can stay in lane. [12:49]
- Count the enemies visible on the minimap (two dead, three elsewhere) to know when you can safely push a tower. [13:19]
- With Bullet Lifesteal and Active Reload (double lifesteal when it procs) he could stay at 400 HP, as long as every enemy is visible on the map. [13:52]
- Mistake: losing track of one enemy (Bebop) leaving to gank, from being cocky. [13:52]
- If it takes four enemies to kill you in one lane, it is not awful. [14:22]
- Many players in lane focus on killing the enemy when farming is far more important: money equals levels and items, which equals power. [14:52]
- You can go 0 and 4 in lane and still end with more souls than the enemy if they chase kills and you take all the last hits and denies. [14:52]
- If you are already farming another lane far from a team fight, don't rotate (even by teleporter); stay in farming mode. [15:25]
- If you're ahead, try to get more ahead. [15:25]
- When your team doesn't need help, farm; dying is the one way a fed carry throws, because they get a lot of souls from you. [15:58]
- Golden statues are really important. [15:58]
- The biggest difference between a starter player and one becoming good is finding the right farming route: souls as efficiently as possible without dying to the enemy team. [16:30]
- Far ahead, he bought both bullet armor and spirit armor; he would not buy these randomly if he were not that far ahead, because the only way to lose is dying. [17:00]
- Bullet Resist Shredder is a must on Wraith: Full Auto applies spirit damage and the cards are spirit damage, making your bullets more deadly. [17:00]
- Dynamo's ult is the best ult in the game; Dynamo is considered one of the best heroes and most of his strength is the ult, especially early. [17:31]
- Mistake: pushing into the enemy tier 2 at 400 to 500 HP because he felt ahead, and getting punished by a Dynamo black hole; the souls went back to even. [18:02]
- If you are the only fed player on the team, you must be the anchor and not die a lot, or the game is over. [18:32]
- With enough lifesteal and sustain you can survive solo against four when the team doesn't follow. [19:34]
- Headhunter is insane on Wraith (possibly broken): more lifesteal, bonus headshot damage and movement speed. [20:35]
- The one thing Wraith lacks is good movement, which is why he is experimenting with more stamina on her. [20:35]
- Project Mind is one of the best tools in the game for getting in and out. [20:35]
- Skill order: one point in Card Trick, one in Full Auto, two in Card Trick, one in Telekinesis, then always max Project Mind first. [21:06]
- Maxed Project Mind cuts the cooldown by 30 seconds (from 45 to 15 seconds as he says it), so you can go in aggressive and get out again, and you get a bullet shield when you teleport. You also farm faster and team fight better. [21:06]
- There is no reason to max Card Trick: summon rate doesn't matter because Wraith's high fire rate summons cards fast anyway. [21:36]
- Full Auto is the second ability to max: 35% extra bullet lifesteal. [21:36]
- He loves playing lifesteal and sustain on Wraith: keep shooting and never die. [21:36]
- Mistake: pushing into the enemy spawn at 17 minutes while barely ahead and with everyone alive; that is greed. [22:38]
- Wraith is not a tank or a full glass cannon like Haze; you can build her tanky, but you want to sit on the side and flank, or stay behind your team, dealing consistent damage and lifestealing. Don't front-line against a tank. [23:08]
- His score was only 8 and 3, but the game felt dominant because of the souls farmed in lane. [24:10]
- Headhunter is on about a 6 second cooldown; it is always worth triggering, even just for the movement speed and the healing. [24:40]
- You can shoot the enemy base Guardians to lifesteal back HP, with Full Auto's extra lifesteal; teleport to the side, shoot them and get back to full health. [25:13]
- The more souls you have, the more the enemy gets for killing you, so the further ahead you are, the safer you should play. [26:14]
- When it is safe, farm the middle until your team is back up. [26:44]
- Mistake: fighting an Abrams instead of backing out with Project Mind ready, expecting the team to show up. [27:14]
- At 6.3k souls he bought Healing Booster and Healbane; Healbane is insanely good against Abrams or tanks that regen a lot, worth it in a free flex slot. [27:45]
- Healing Booster adds healing to his triple lifesteal (Bullet Lifesteal, Active Reload, Full Auto). [28:17]
- Crippling Headshot is his favorite first 6.3k item on Wraith; he rates its spirit and bullet resist reduction above Lucky Shot's slow, and Siphon Bullets got nerfed. [28:17]
- The enemy contested mid boss with only one farmed player (Abrams) and lost; Kelvin's Frozen Shelter and Seven's ult saved the team. [29:19]
- Main lesson: dominate the laning stage and transition into an unstoppable DPS hard carry on Wraith. [29:49]
- He is experimenting with Titanic Magazine and skipping Active Reload since its nerf; his build is in game as "Metro Wraith". [30:53]
