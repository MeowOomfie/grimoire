## Covers
Warden gameplay commentary by aquatiq, May 2026 patch.
A played game focused on wave priority in lane, buying Spirit Resilience and Debuff Remover into a spirit and debuff comp, max Binding Word plus Knockdown as a lockdown combo, and late-game item choices.

## Claims
- In lane, wave priority matters more than doing damage to the enemy heroes. [0:35]
- Lesson from watching pro play: push your wave forward, then attack people; you get far more out of pushing the wave to their side of the map. [3:38]
- The game works like tower defense: push the wave away from your tower so the enemy cannot freely hit your tower. [3:38]
- Most of his lost lanes come from ganks from mid; sometimes it is simply that he has no wave prio. [5:39]
- Warden can kill tier 3 camps at minute 11. [10:52]
- In a spirit-heavy game he wants Spirit Resilience early so he can tank everything. [11:23]
- He needs Debuff Remover against Love Bites, Shiv and Stake; he rates Debuff Remover as more important than Spirit Resilience but wants both. [13:30]
- Next buys: Debuff Remover for two of the enemy heroes, spirit resist because it is always nice, and Weighted Shots; Spell Breaker could come later. [17:04]
- Once he has max Binding Word (cage) he goes on a rampage; that is his spike. [17:36]
- He needs to buy Knockdown this game. [19:36]
- Knockdown plus the silence on Binding Word means the enemy cannot do a thing; he wants to start knocking enemies down while they are caged. [23:12]
- He calls Knockdown overpowered because it is up every 20 seconds. [24:14]
- He buys Metal Skin and then Mercurial Magnum. [25:45]
- When the rest of the team is dead and the enemy has no mid boss, clear the waves and avoid dying so they cannot take mid boss. [26:16]
- Use Knockdown on Vindicta and the silence on Dynamo. [26:46]
- Juggernaut is decent in general when the enemy has gun damage. [30:01]
- The team should have chilled and taken mid boss a minute later instead of forcing a fight. [31:03]
- Late item choice: over Colossus or Headshot Booster he picks Crippling Headshot because the enemies have a lot of resist and are tanky. [32:38]
- When pushing into the enemy base, watch for backdoors; Vindicta can walk in and shoot your base, so push up the waves before fighting. [32:38]
