# This One Mistake Lost Them the Game
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-02
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qE-8gOFts4s

## Covers
Paradox on a mostly-gun hybrid build, played and commented by aquatiq, laning with Rem.
Commentary over a game with ability explanations, item reasoning and a closing lesson on an enemy throw (staying in base too long).
Patch around 2026-03-02.

## Claims
- Paradox has turned into a gun character; this build is mostly gun with some spirit items to choose from. [0:34]
- Pulse Grenade (1) pulses and hits anyone in its radius, applies a slow and gives a stacking damage amplification for Paradox against victims; he uses it mostly for the slow. [1:05]
- He did not want to upgrade the grenade early and preferred swap (Paradoxical Swap) next. [2:46]
- He likes using Time Wall aggressively, to stop people walking toward him. [2:46]
- Kinetic Carbine charges a shot that applies a time stop on hit; the longer you let it charge (bar in the middle of the screen), the more damage it does. [3:47]
- A carbine time stop sets up teammates' skillshots, such as Rem's pillows. [3:47]
- He considered Disarming Hex against Billy so Billy cannot shoot. [3:17]
- With a sustain laner like Rem you do not need sustain items; buy damage instead. [5:20]
- He rushed Headhunter (Headshot Booster builds into it), then Backstabber. [5:20]
- Backstabber is good on Paradox because a lot of enemies will be looking away from you, so you shoot them in the back. [9:29]
- He tried Veil Walker early instead of Fortitude; it is still very good sustain. [6:21]
- Swap when the enemies are pushed up. [4:49]
- Leaving the lane to farm is fine if the enemy wants the tower badly; just give it to them. [12:32]
- Maxing Time Wall is strong because it silences enemies who walk through it, and any silence is ridiculous. [12:02]
- Assists are basically kills: an assist means you took part, and kills only go to whoever last hits. [16:16]
- Last hits do not matter for jungle camps; whoever hits the camp splits the money. [16:48]
- Do not go mid boss when you are not grouped with your strongest player. [17:18]
- Knockdown is valuable against heavy melee and dangerous ults (Lash, Seven). [18:20]
- He skipped Crippling Headshot because he has little spirit resilience to justify it. [19:24]
- When two enemies are down and your team has five, fight. [20:54]
- Too many active items is uncomfortable to play. [20:54]
- Plan Knockdown on the enemy ults: wait for Seven or Lash to ult, then knock down. [25:01]
- Check whether Seven has Unstoppable or Refresher before relying on Knockdown. [25:01]
- At 10,000 souls he took the grenade upgrade for a second bomb, Transcendent Cooldown and more wave clear. [23:59]
- Glass Cannon for more gun damage late; Echo Shard; Warp Stone was his best item. [26:05]
- Double grenades on a shrine do a lot of damage. [26:37]
- Against Infernus he wants Debuff Remover more than Spellbreaker. [28:40]
- He wants to give his barrier item to a teammate going in (about 600 barrier and movement speed) rather than use it on himself. [29:11]
- Main lesson: when your whole team is up on the enemy in their base, do not stay too long; leave, farm, come back stronger and fight. [33:14]
- The enemy lost because they stayed after a winning push instead of backing; one mistake can end the game. [33:45]
