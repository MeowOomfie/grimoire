# How we ALMOST beat the #2 Team in NA - Fight Night Analysis
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-11-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tjiTmJi-vKg

## Covers
Deathy reviews his team's 36 minute NA Fight Night tournament loss to Lama Lords from his Mirage perspective: draft limits, lane rotations, Mirage's Traveler global teleport and side lane split pushing, Urn macro, a 30k comeback through enemy staggers, and the positional mistakes that lost the last fight.
For players who want team-level macro around objectives and Mirage. This is a late-2024 video and the game, Mirage and the objectives have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Lama Lords had the better draft on paper (Wraith, Dynamo, Warden, Yamato, Infernus), a good mix of damage and crowd control; his team's draft was worse mainly because of limited player hero pools. [1:32]
- They picked Mirage because they needed a hyper carry and Mirage was the only viable one left; Lady Geist fit a player's pool and added damage the team lacked. [3:02]
- Mirage versus Infernus is Mirage favored, but a good Infernus is hard to dominate; little happens early because Mirage is not super strong early. [3:34]
- Mirage spikes when he gets two points in Djinn's Mark, because at that point the stacks are extremely hard to avoid. [4:05]
- Freeze the lane on your side with Mirage and stand on the bridge so an enemy who peeks for last hits gets shot; abuse the bridge and positioning. [4:05]
- Being active between lanes and rotating is very important in the laning stage at every skill level; staying in your own lane for 10 minutes is not the way to win. [4:35]
- Rotations have always been strong in Deadlock despite many changes to the meta and soul sharing; they let you snowball and create map pressure (an enemy got two kills just from rotations). [5:06]
- Early on, Mirage's ult Traveler can be used to apply pressure in a part of the map where you had none, and his zipline speed boost then lets you go somewhere else. [5:36]
- He teleported onto the Wraith's lane knowing she could not 1v1 him because of Tornado, so he could defend that tower while his team took a 3v2 nearby; the idea was fine even though the 3v2 was lost. [6:07]
- There is always a strong side and a weak side: with four players on one side and two on the other, the two weak side players just hold and delay. [7:08]
- On the weak side, a player who cannot stop a 1v2 push should only delay the enemy from taking the tower. [7:39]
- In a favorable 1v1 matchup (Mirage into Wraith) apply as much pressure as possible. [7:39]
- In the laning stage towers matter a lot; be very active around the map creating counterplay and opportunities. [8:40]
- Moving lane to lane and creating imbalances in the laning stage is a way to get ahead; play around your ability and item timings. [9:10]
- Early in the game, trading one death for an Urn is probably worth it in most situations: the Urn gives roughly 4k, and the death costs less. [10:12]
- When the enemy pushes walkers, punish a lone player who is too far up (here the Warden). [10:43]
- A player defending a walker alone should play very passive under the walker, throw grenades and wait for help instead of dying. [11:13]
- His early items mixed active fight items (Reactive Barrier, Return Fire, Slowing Hex) with damage scaling, because his team lacked damage and he needed to scale. [11:44]
- As the team's damage carry he must prioritize his own life; getting caught on the map early was bad. [11:44]
- Mirage is a high priority tournament pick because Traveler, a global teleport to any allied hero, gives him global map presence. [12:14]
- As Mirage, split push a side lane and hide to see who shows on the wave: if only one enemy comes, solo kill him; if two come, stay hidden and leave. [12:46]
- Split pushing pulls enemies; if they answer with one person, the rest of the team can take an engagement anywhere else and Mirage can teleport in. [13:17]
- An enemy should not be allowed to keep pushing alone when Mirage can teleport into the fight at any time; his team should have been more aggressive on the other side playing around Paradox. [13:17]
- As long as his team takes fights, they should be advantageous because Mirage can teleport to them. [13:48]
- When playing weak side, do not die trying to defend a walker you cannot hold; just delay. [13:48]
- Mirage wants to live in the side lanes, as far away as possible on the map, since he can teleport to his team at any time. [14:18]
- With a 7k enemy lead that is not enough to end, expect a long game and save up for Ricochet. [14:48]
- Ricochet helps Mirage's damage by spreading Djinn's Mark (his 3) stacks to multiple people, works with Toxic Bullets later, and greatly helps his farm, since Mirage struggles with wave clear early except with Fire Scarabs. [15:18]
- Once someone picks up the Urn, its delivery side is locked: an Urn picked up while on your side stays on your side and never becomes neutral again. [17:20]
- Getting the Urn while 8k behind would erase the whole lead. [17:20]
- Mirage is really bad at holding the Urn; someone else should carry it so Mirage is free to do other things. [17:53]
- While the team holds the Urn, Mirage splits the map: if the enemy does not answer he gets a free walker, and if they answer he teleports and the team fights for the Urn. [18:23]
- When answered on a split push, his number one priority is not dying: run away, teleport to the other side and call the Urn. [18:23]
- Mirage is so picked partly because he is insanely strong around the Urn. [18:53]
- In the current meta trading one death for one walker is usually not worth it because respawn timers are long and it can give the enemy a free mid boss. [16:49]
- They lost a 3v2 walker fight because they used their abilities poorly. [16:49]
- Do not make plays when your waves are bad; a risky fight with bad waves can lose your whole base. [18:53]
- Ricochet Mirage is really good at defending, since he can shoot attackers for a long time. [19:24]
- Buy Toxic Bullets against a team with a decent amount of healing that you need to out-damage. [19:24]
- With a Rejuvenator but a lead too small to end, the enemy should use it to farm, invade the enemy jungle or farm their own to extend the lead rather than force fights. [19:55]
- In the current meta, past 20 minutes team fights are everything; if you lose them it is very hard to win. [20:25]
- Past 20 minutes itemize to fight: he filled his slots with Spirit Armor and Bullet Resist Shredder because one fight could decide the game. [20:56]
- Teleporting to a Lady Geist on the high ground and spamming damage from above is the dream position for Mirage. [20:56]
- A lost fight came from a bad call: one player said in comms he was not ready and nobody registered it or counter-called, and the team had mixed plans (some wanted a pick, some wanted to bait and protect a player). [21:57]
- He called not to show too many people on the left side so the enemy would think only two or three were there and take the fight; the enemy instead showed with their entire team. [22:27]
- Farming alone after a won fight gave the enemy a lot of value, reaching 14k ahead at 25 minutes. [22:58]
- A bad side wave should be fixed; Mirage probably should have been in that lane. [23:29]
- Ballsy call: go mid; if the enemy goes for the Urn the team gets a free mid boss, and if not, they get the fight. [23:29]
- Grouping up allowed a six-man Dynamo Singularity (black hole); they should have expected the Dynamo and not been grouped that tight. [24:00]
- After that wipe the enemy took mid boss, the Wraith took a Shrine alone and the kill souls (significant nowadays) pushed the deficit to 30k. [24:32]
- Being down 30k with the Rejuvenator against you is not a guaranteed loss: you still have the defender advantage of holding your base and win conditions for fights. [25:02]
- Win conditions while behind: a good Paradoxical Swap, silencing the Yamato when she cannot be saved by the enemy's one Rescue Beam, Mirage's damage, Lash's ult and Lady Geist's damage. [25:33]
- When far behind, the plan is to deal with waves, mostly stay in base and not die; one pick at this stage is basically game over. [26:03]
- Going for a wave far out in that state was probably too risky. [26:33]
- When defending base as Mirage, the goal is just to spam out as much damage as possible. [26:33]
- Kiting perfectly made the enemy use all their ultimates (Warden ult, the grab, black hole) to take a Shrine and the first Patron phase without killing anyone; Kelvin's dome stopped the black hole in their spawn. [27:03]
- Once the enemy has used their team fight winning ultimates and you still have abilities, take 1v1s and clean up; he popped Return Fire. [27:33]
- After picks, the calls are: fix the waves, catch other people if you can, otherwise get the Urn. [28:03]
- Teleport to a teammate who calls an enemy nearby to fight together; Tornado can hit two enemies at once. [28:03]
- Keep staggering the enemy (killing them one at a time so they respawn apart) and take objectives with each stagger; this turned a 30k deficit into an even game and then a 12k lead. [29:03]
- Taking both walkers at once also earns a flex slot. [29:03]
- The comeback was partly the enemy's mistakes in giving staggers, not only good play. [29:34]
- With a key enemy down for 30 seconds, take the base Guardians; but at 6v5 against a team with a Dynamo black hole, back off, since a big black hole wins even a 6v5. [29:34]
- After backing off with a lead, scatter and collect as much farm as possible to extend it; he calls this 100% the correct call. [30:05]
- At the highest level late in the game, jungle farm is not focused on one player; anyone can farm it, and the goal is clearing it as efficiently and fast as possible, then looking for another advantageous engagement. [30:36]
- Contest the enemy's remaining jungle rather than let them farm it for free. [31:07]
- Before the buffs spawn, stack the side you want to fight for. [31:07]
- The decisive fight was lost on positioning: they should have been on the high ground expecting a contest, ready for the engage, not easily split, and with counterplay ready for the black hole. [32:07]
- They probably needed a Curse on the team against Dynamo, though his Unstoppable means it probably would not have mattered. [32:07]
- Tournament games have fewer kills than ranked or unranked games because they are more methodical. [32:38]
