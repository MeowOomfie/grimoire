# Mina Is Still OP? Massive Balance Patch Breakdown
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-10-03
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N19iXuIIe_M

## Covers
A live read-through, with quick in-game checks, of the early October 2025 balance patch (released around 2025-10-02): general and movement changes, objectives, every hero change and the item changes, with a verdict on each.
For players wanting to know who and what won or lost the patch and how builds shift (Mina, Calico, Vyper, Victor, Seven, Warden, Veil Walker, Cultist Sacrifice, Diviner's Kevlar).

## Claims
- Weapon investment bonus tiers were raised slightly; verdict: irrelevant, about 2% at 9.6k invested. [0:00]
- New CC diminishing returns: a CC applied to an enemy lasts shorter for each other CC (stun, sleep, immobilize, tether, the same set as Reactive Barrier) applied in the past 7 seconds, 7% each up to 21%. Verdict: a good change that removes chain stunning, but not a big deal from the numbers. [0:32]
- Matchmaking now considers a balanced roster beyond only front liners; if done right, this is big. [1:02]
- Shooting lockout after jumping reduced from 0.15 to 0 seconds, and shooting while jumping gets a reduced inaccuracy period; he likes it. [1:33]
- Wall jumps after the first in a sequence now cost 0.5 stamina (first one free). Verdict: a nerf to good movement; he dislikes lowering the skill ceiling. [1:33]
- Corner boosting velocity reduced by 20%. [2:33]
- Mid Boss base HP increased by 500 and its damage resistance growth reduced; verdict: won't change anything, it may die easier later. Rejuvenator bonus HP reduced and second Mid Boss Rejuvenator credits reduced: welcome, though Mid Boss is still overpowered, just less. [2:33]
- A dropped unsecured soul pack now needs a heavy melee to secure instead of standing on it; about the same speed, but it adds counterplay (parry the person collecting) and raises the skill ceiling. He likes it. [3:04]
- The Soul Urn carrier now gets 30% bullet resist but a smaller move speed bonus, and damage for holding it too long rises from 1 to 1.5; overall a buff to the carrier. [4:04]
- An extra boon added at 12k souls. [5:06]
- The neutral shop is no longer disabled when an enemy is nearby; a buff for him, though he liked punishing people that way. [5:06]
- Distance at which walkers attack reduced from 35 to 34 m, which makes walkers a bit stronger. [5:06]
- Negative bullet resist from bullet procs now applies before the procs' bonus damage, so shred applies first; effectively a small buff to items like Headshot Booster and the headshot items, but it won't change much. [5:38]
- Abrams: Siphon Life damage and spirit scaling reduced by 10%, heal ratio increased by 10; Shoulder Charge tier one changed from 25% weapon power to 20% bullet damage per shot, a weaker follow-up melee. It might make Abrams weaker; maybe you run gun. [6:40]
- Billy: base move speed reduced, Blasted move speed increased, footsteps added to out-of-combat run. Not a nerf, he is still insane and moves faster while Blasted. [7:43]
- Calico: base bullet damage, Gloom Bomb radius and duration increased, Leaping Slash height increased, Return to Shadows cooldown reduced; six buffs at once to a hero who was already good. [8:13]
- The Doorman: only Doorway placement improvements (can be placed through veils again); no nerfs, which he finds wrong given how strong gun Doorman is. [9:14]
- Drifter: Stalker's Mark ambush cast delay increased; a nerf to a hero who is not even that good. [9:14]
- Dynamo: Kinetic Pulse height increased and Rejuvenating Aura spirit scaling increased. A single jump probably won't clear the stomp anymore (a double jump might); a pretty big buff. [9:44]
- Grey Talon: Charged Shot spirit scaling reduced, including tier three; Rain of Arrows no longer keeps its air benefits (unlimited air dash) after it is canceled or ends, a quite big nerf to an escape. Good nerfs; he may no longer be Thanos on ladder. [10:16]
- Ivy: bullet damage growth reduced from 0.15 to 0.11 (massive), spirit scaling per boon increased, Kudzu Bomb DPS reduced including tier three, Stone Form damage spirit scaling increased (massive), Air Drop no longer grants allied bullet resistance. Valve wants her as a spirit support; probably out of the meta as a hard carry. [11:19]
- Gun Doorman on ladder is crazy; he is fine with nerfing support damage but wants real gun heroes buffed. [12:20]
- Kelvin: base damage and clip size reduced, spirit power per boon increased; makes sense, he was a top three gun hero. Valve wants Ivy and Kelvin as support heroes. [12:51]
- Lady Geist: Essence Bomb spirit scaling increased. [12:51]
- Lash: Ground Strike range reduced, cooldown increased, damage reduced from 70 to 60; pretty big nerfs. [12:51]
- Mina: new heavy melee (a kick) and she can float in the air with the umbrella for up to 2.5 seconds, which is more movement but makes her easier to hit. [13:24]
- Mina: Rake heal spirit scaling reduced; checked in game, the early heal is about 46 instead of 80 (0.5 scaling instead of 1.5), a bigger early-level nerf than he expected. Sanguine Retreat cooldown increased by 3 seconds. [13:55]
- Mina: 50% less damage against objectives, and the tier three objective damage cap cut from 50 to 20 per bat, so she can no longer instantly nuke objectives; Mid Boss does not count as an objective. Love Bites was not nerfed. [14:26]
- Verdict on Mina: still Thanos. Early sustain is weaker, so buy one green item in lane; you still need Love Bites at level two before you do lane damage. [17:02]
- Mirage: Djinn's Mark is now affected by cooldown reduction, so cooldown is now worth buying on him. [18:06]
- Mo & Krill: bullet damage growth per boon increased, Burrow resistance reduced from 80 to 60 (a big nerf), Combo duration reduced by 0.1 seconds and tier two by 0.05. Verdict: still way too good, maybe killable a bit more in Burrow. [18:36]
- Paige: bullet projectile speed +15%, bullet growth per boon reduced, her shield (overshield) lasts longer but has a longer cooldown, her area ability gets lower cooldown and bigger radius, Conjure Dragon now burns properly below high dragons. [19:09]
- Paige's ult Rallying Charge: base damage and healing reduced but both grow up to 100% as the horse travels, horse faster, knockup speed reduced, spawn time reduced, dead zone in front removed. Valve wants a global ult, but it is too inconsistent to hit globally. [19:42]
- Verdict on Paige: not enough to make her playable, though the longer overshield (it was 5 seconds), the faster projectile and the radius increase help her in lane. [20:43]
- Seven: Static Charge tier two radius from 8 to 7 m, Power Surge spirit scaling reduced, Storm Cloud lightning damage reduced. "Seven is dead"; he was already B tier. Maybe run gun Seven. [21:44]
- Victor: base health 680 to 720 with lower per-boon growth, tankier early, a little weaker late, overall almost nothing. Pain Battery now needs damage equal to 40% of max health to charge instead of 50%, quite a big buff, plus a fix for its balls hitting world geometry. [22:15]
- Victor: Aura of Suffering DPS spirit scaling reduced but self-damage reduced, overall a buff to aura Victor. His ult gets longer stun and higher damage scaling; tier one now gives a 3 m radius and 100 damage instead of 10 spirit damage per bullet and 25% fire rate. [23:18]
- Victor ult tier three now gives 10 spirit damage per bullet and 20% fire rate while the ability is on cooldown, permanently once you have it; crazy, but hard to max since Victor wants his 1 and 2 maxed, and you must die to trigger the ult. Overall more playable. [24:54]
- Vindicta: base bullet damage, flight duration, flight tier two and flight spirit scaling increased, Assassinate bonus damage scaling increased and cooldown reduced; a lot of damage buffs, and he worries she becomes a one-shot combo hero again. [26:59]
- Viscous: alt fire base damage reduced with higher growth (overall a nerf), Splatter damage reduced and cooldown changed; his right click is weak early, so you don't right click in lane anymore. [27:29]
- Vyper: Screwjab Dagger reworked to stack damage and slow per dagger on the same target (max three stacks shown overhead, like Shiv's knives), charge based with 2 charges and 4.5 seconds between charges, slow 35% plus 15% per stack, slow duration 1 to 2 seconds, cooldown 9 to 10, faster projectile. [27:59]
- Vyper Screwjab Dagger upgrades: tier one is now +1 charge (three charges), tier two is now bullet resist reduction per stack, tier three is now 40% cooldown reduction on hit instead of 2 seconds slow and -1 stamina. He calls it crazy damage. [28:32]
- Vyper: Lethal Venom can now be cast on non-trooper enemies, neutrals and objectives, and its tier three triggers on all enemies including troopers, which speeds her farming; tier three build up was nerfed. [30:44]
- Vyper: Slither no longer starts leveled; Slither tier three changed from 20% resist to a 180 health barrier on starting a slide (0.6 spirit scaling, 5 seconds, 8 second cooldown), and slide leaves a green trail. Good early, falls off hard, so hard carry late game Vyper is weaker. [30:44]
- Verdict on Vyper: a hybrid nuke hero now, not a hard carry left clicker; you still need bullet damage because the dagger reduces bullet resist, with items like Fleet Foot and maybe Tesla Bullets. [31:44]
- Warden: Last Stand DPS spirit scaling reduced and fire rate spirit scaling increased. It doesn't fix him, you still only run spirit ult Warden; he wanted gun Warden buffed. "Seven dead, Warden dead." [33:16]
- Wraith: base bullet damage increased, bullet damage growth per boon reduced, Card Trick delay reduced so you throw cards faster. Overall a buff: stronger early, even with the old scaling around level 15, and she does little gun damage late anyway. [33:47]
- Headshot Booster damage reduced from 50 to 45; it was the best early item and is still great. High-Velocity Rounds bullet velocity increased. [36:59]
- Backstabber cooldown increased; a slight nerf, still an insane item on the heroes who run it. Mystic Shot damage reduced. [36:59]
- Cultist Sacrifice: fire rate 10 to 8, fire rate boon scaling 0.8 to 0.7, health boon scaling 7 to 4, health 100 to 75, cooldown increased by 10 seconds. Maxed it is about 200 health instead of 300 and 31% fire rate instead of 35%. No longer an item for every hero; he likely won't run it on Wraith. It did need nerfs. [37:31]
- Toxic Bullets burn increased. [39:04]
- An item whose heal reduction drops from 40 to 30 (caption "lab to", likely Blood Tribute) is a big winner of the patch, already slept on and now better; run it on gun heroes and Wraith. [39:35]
- Tesla Bullets: fixed its proc rate being improved by general cooldown reduction (a two month bug); not really a nerf to Capacitor unless you abused compressed cooldown. Capacitor is very good. [40:07]
- Shadow Weave spotted radius reduced; Valve may want the Shadow Weave meta back, and with Veil Walker nerfed there may be a reason to run it now. [40:37]
- Spiritual Overflow now grants 20% cooldown reduction on proc instead of 20% ability duration: a massive buff and big winner. Duration often missed the ability you just used, cooldown is always useful; massive on Wraith and Seven, lets them skip cooldown items, not that good on Haze. [41:07]
- Extra Health bonus health increased. Sprint Boots sprint 2 to 2.5, which also means 2.25 m move speed on Enduring Speed, a buff. Trophy Collector buffed. [42:43]
- Rebuttal now grants 18% melee resistance, which might make it more playable. [43:14]
- Spirit Lifesteal now gives 6 spirit power, so you can buy it early; he had been asking for lifesteal buffs. He notes Valve buffed more spirit items after three months of a spirit meta. [43:44]
- Restorative Locket slight buff; Weapon Shielding damage threshold reduced and move speed increased, Spirit Shielding move speed increased; he will run shielding even more. Reactive Barrier cooldown increased, a reasonable nerf. [44:14]
- Veil Walker: heal reduced from 150 to 85 (massive) though heal scaling per boon increased, cooldown +3 seconds, reveal on damage 0.25 to 0.5 seconds, longer initial invis fade and longer reveal when spotted. Expect it in 10% of games instead of 100%; it goes back to its purpose of roaming for ganks and stays good on pickoff heroes. [44:44]
- Counterspell parry duration reduced; makes sense, it was too good. [45:45]
- Majestic Leap barrier reduced and now scales with boons, so you can't really rush it as a first lane item. Rescue Beam cast range increased. Fury Trance cooldown increased, still an insane item. [45:45]
- Spirit Resilience is back to viable on every hero, not just Victor; before, there was no 3k option to reduce spirit damage. [46:16]
- Diviner's Kevlar duration 13 to 20 seconds and spirit power 25% to 35%: a double buff. That is about Boundless Spirit's spirit power plus a roughly 1,000 barrier and ability duration, and 20 seconds is enough for a fight; possibly playable on Wraith, likely crazy on Paradox, maybe broken. [46:47]
- Big winning items: the "lab to" item (likely Blood Tribute), Spiritual Overflow, Spirit Resilience and Diviner's Kevlar. [48:50]
- Cheat Death damage output reduction 70 to 60 and healing reduction reduced; still bad, won't change anything. [48:50]
- Inhibitor build up rate increased to match Slowing Bullets and it is no longer hero-only (doesn't matter). Unstoppable buff duration and Colossus changes. Spellbreaker now triggers on fewer small hits, a buff because spirit damage is so strong. [48:50]
- Rusted Barrel cast range 28 to 32 and bullet resist reduction 6 to 8; Valve wants it as a lane item. Mystic Burst cooldown 12 to 14. [49:51]
- Quicksilver Reload bonus damage reduced; doesn't matter since you upgrade it anyway, but combined with his ability nerfs Lash is dead. [49:51]
- Smaller item changes: an item read as "confront" gets less bonus damage and more cooldown, Mystic Slow now reduces dash distance by 10%, Disarming Hex cast range increased, a silence item's silence duration reduced, Spirit Snatch melee damage 10 to 7, and an item's spirit resistance raised from 8 to 10. [50:21]
- Big losing items: Veil Walker (much less healing, seen far less) and Cultist Sacrifice. [51:21]
- Hero winners and losers: Calico a massive winner and going to be Thanos; Billy unaffected and just as strong minus Cultist Sacrifice; Abrams slight nerfs, still very good; The Doorman no nerfs; Spirit Lifesteal and Spirit Resilience buffs are huge for Victor. [52:25]
- Mina is still very good though she can't nuke towers as fast; Mo & Krill still really strong; Paradox got no changes but Diviner's Kevlar may be crazy on her. [52:55]
- Seven is a big loser; Kelvin, the third strongest gun hero before, moves toward support; Warden is bad; gun Ivy is probably gone. [54:27]
