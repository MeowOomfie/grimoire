## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (patch of early January 2026) reviewing four student games: a Mina (Arcanist), a Seven (Ascendant), a Paige (Archon) and a Paradox (Archon, coached before, reviewed for YouTube).
Themes: buying on time and soul awareness, resetting, not fighting with a big bag, giving up urns and objectives you cannot contest, playing around walkers when the enemy has Rejuvenator, support hovering versus doing nothing, Curse and anti-Curse items, Seven as a weak laner and Lightning Ball max, Paige barrier order, Paradox wall timing and Carbine pressure.
Side notes on his own Pocket build and on several builds he recommends (Shyro's Mina, Osis's Seven, Division's Paige, Taz's Bebop).
