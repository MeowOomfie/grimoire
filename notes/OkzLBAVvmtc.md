# Making the other team rage as Apollo
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-04
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OkzLBAVvmtc

## Covers
Apollo, commentary over one game with item choices against specific enemies.
aquatiq, lane with Grey Talon against Rem and Haze, Archon lobby. No one is coached. Patch before the early March 2026 update.

## Claims
- Buy Monster Rounds first item against a Rem in your lane, every game; his troopers have so much HP. [1:00]
- Clear the high HP minions quickly or they reach your base much faster. [2:34]
- Apollo does not have much range, so a Vindicta in lane would be a bad matchup. [2:02]
- Get Headshot Booster for more gun damage. [3:06]
- Extra Regen over Healing Rite when you are not taking much damage in lane; Healing Rite is for lanes where you take a lot. [3:06]
- Clear the lane first, then go for the buff. [4:38]
- Get Extra Spirit to boost the 3, then Rusted Barrel if the Haze is still a problem. [5:08]
- Rusted Barrel in lane against any gun hero makes their gun useless. [11:59]
- One of his abilities destroys camps. [6:09]
- When poor, lay back and do not fight. [6:09]
- Max the 3 in lane. [10:56]
- Cold Front is a power spike on most spirit heroes; it is basically a fifth ability, use it all the time. [9:53]
- Headhunter is a good gun sustain item on Apollo, who does not have a lot of HP. [10:24]
- Check the map often so the enemy does not collapse on you. [12:30]
- Battle Vest would have been better than a spirit item here, but against Rem's pillow damage spirit was fine. [12:30]
- Teammates should call out where enemies are on the map. [13:35]
- Do not go for a buff if you are getting killed for it; stay, clear the lane and push. [14:07]
- Buy normal items first, then start building armor when taking a lot of gun damage. [15:48]
- Drop Monster Rounds once you are out of lane. [17:54]
- Torment Pulse is good on Apollo because it is constant damage. [22:06]
- Buy Healbane against Billy, who lives too much. [21:35]
- Mystic Expansion goes on the 1; a bigger field on the 1 is very important. [23:40]
- Lash is scary even when poor; buy Counterspell against him. [24:10]
- Slowing Hex is a better pick than Rusted Barrel later on while the Haze exists. [24:42]
- Return Fire can be used when Haze ults. [26:51]
- Check what you take damage from; against spirit damage buy Spirit Shielding. [26:51]
- Arctic Blast is a very strong item; do not underestimate it. [26:51]
- Apollo is easy to play, you hardly need a crosshair. [31:01]
