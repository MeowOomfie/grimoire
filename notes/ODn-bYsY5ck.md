## Covers
Breakdown of the urn-only patch released around 2026-06-04 that replaces the melee-claim urn with a King of the Hill capture circle, plus bounty, timer, damage and comeback changes and a live spectated capture.
For players who want to know how the new urn capture works and how it changes snowballing and comebacks.

## Claims
- This is a very minor update, almost only urn changes, with no hero or balance changes. [0:00]
- Delivering the urn now starts a King of the Hill capture point instead of meleeing to flip it; the coach conceptually prefers this to melee though it still has issues. [0:31]
- Both teams can progress at the same time, but the urn is only fully claimed once only one team is in the circle; with one player from each team on it, the moment one leaves the other caps instantly, which feels a bit janky. [0:31]
- The urn runner now gets their normal 35% bonus bounty and stats immediately on deposit, even if the team does not win the capture, which incentivizes running the urn. [1:33]
- Urn overall bounty reduced 20%; together with the urn moving from every 5 minutes starting at 10 to every 6 minutes starting at 12, there is one fewer urn by 30 minutes, roughly 5,000 to 6,000 souls or more removed from the game. [2:03]
- The aim is to cut the snowball where winning one urn fight put a team up 10,000 to 15,000 souls in a close game. [3:05]
- The bounty is unreduced for the trailing team, so the comeback urn is a stronger comeback mechanic relative to the regular urn; the coach calls it a good change. [3:05]
- The carrier no longer gets resistances while carrying, but still gets them while turning it in. [3:35]
- Progress radius is 20 m and the progress rate is fixed regardless of how many allies are in the area (unlike Team Fortress 2). [3:35]
- Progress duration is 6, 12 and 18 seconds for favored, neutral and unfavored; 18 seconds when you are ahead gives the losing team time to set up a fight. [4:06]
- If the urn has been in progress for over 75 seconds it gives up and throws all souls as orbs into the sky; good, since hostaging the urn is one of the most anti-fun loops in the game. [4:36]
- Time allowed to run the urn before taking damage increased by 10 seconds, so runners have leeway to take safe paths. [5:06]
- Trailing team sprint speed bonus slightly increased. [5:36]
- The urn comeback resistance now adds debuff resistance on top of 35% bullet and spirit resist, which helps against stuns such as Abrams and Victor's ult in the AoE and CC heavy meta. [5:36]
- Stuns matter less now because standing in the area counts as contesting; before, you only contested while meleeing. [6:06]
- The comeback resistance aura shrank from 60 m to the 20 m progress radius. [6:36]
- The comeback urn is quite powerful when down about 10% souls but much weaker when down 20%; the coach would like comeback strength to scale with the deficit. [7:06]
- The drop-off location on the side lanes is now above the bridge rather than below; the coach thinks this matters little now that you contest a large area. [7:36]
- In a live capture both progress bars filled while players from both teams stood in the circle, and the urn was captured the moment the last contester left. [8:06]
- The coach hated the melee urn and thinks most players did; he is glad Valve is listening and expects them to try something else if King of the Hill fails. [9:06]
