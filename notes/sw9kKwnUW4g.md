## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream, recorded the week of the urn rework patch (May 2026). Four VOD reviews: a Celeste (Eternus lobby), a Seven (Docs, Archon), a Seven (Eternus, not an M1 main) and a Grey Talon (Eternus).
Themes: fighting over objectives instead of for fun, playing for the urn and buying spikes before fights, wave and jungle farming efficiency, bag awareness, ignoring bad teammates, instant cast, shrines over walkers, Grey Talon trap and bird usage, routing out of line of sight.
Side notes on the new urn, breakables timing, Rem and Graves being underplayed, Cultist Sacrifice timing, Extra Regen versus Extra Spirit and community builds.
