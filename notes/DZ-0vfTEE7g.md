# Ranking Every Hero In Deadlock From Strongest To Weakest - New Patch
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-10-04
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZ-0vfTEE7g

## Covers
A tier list of every hero for ladder play, averaged across ranks, made about a day and a half after the early October 2025 balance patch (around 2025-10-02) and minutes after the Victor hotfix.
Heroes are taken alphabetically with what changed and why each lands where it does. For ranked players picking heroes in the new patch.

## Claims
- The list is an average of all ranks; hard carries like Victor do well in low rank because low rank games last longer and are very bad in high rank. [0:00]
- Abrams: low S tier at the lowest, meta defining. He got a slap on the wrist: a bit less damage on the melee after Shoulder Charge and on Siphon Life, but more healing from Siphon Life. [0:31]
- Tanks are really strong this meta, even stronger after the patch, because Spirit Resilience was buffed and is an amazing anti-spirit item on tanks. [1:01]
- Bebop: top of A tier, unchanged and maybe a tiny bit better. Play him as a gun hero: the double bomb spirit build is not viable because everyone runs Debuff Remover and Counterspell. Gun Bebop is insanely tanky with insane damage and lane, though not easy to play. [1:31]
- Billy: easy S tier, arguably better. Base move speed was nerfed slightly but Blasted move speed buffed more, so he is faster than before while Blasted is on. His usual items are barely touched; Cultist Sacrifice was gutted but you can play him without it. [2:03]
- If you struggle against Billy, it is probably a skill issue; get good at the character first. [2:33]
- Calico: S tier, the biggest winner of the patch. Leaping Slash height and Gloom Bombs are more consistent, and her ult and cat cooldowns are lower. [2:33]
- The Doorman: S tier. Gun Doorman kills you; he does more gun damage than most gun carries and is a support with insane utility and one of the best ults. [3:35]
- Drifter: below average, unplayable if this were an Eternus-only list. Stay with your team and never fight him 1v1; with one teammate nearby he is very weak. He is bad at farming and only scary once he gets late game money. [4:06]
- Dynamo: middle of the pack, maybe low B. Stomp (Kinetic Pulse) height increased so it is harder to jump. Singularity is the best ult in the game and always gives a comeback chance, which keeps him viable on ladder. [5:08]
- Support Dynamo got better: Rejuvenating Aura and his heals got small buffs and none of his items got worse. He is still one of the weakest laners in the game. [5:40]
- Grey Talon: A tier, down from absolute S before the patch. Charged Shot spirit scaling on tier one and tier three was reduced by 0.2, and Spirit Resilience is usable again to counter him. [6:11]
- Grey Talon needs a team that plays around him or he dies, so he is not S for ladder; in pro play he may still be Thanos. [7:12]
- Haze: high B tier, untouched and a tiny bit better since spirit heroes were nerfed on average and gun got a little better. Blood Tribute is crazy as an item now and does a lot of damage on Haze. Not a hard carry, but a super playable pickoff hero. [7:46]
- Holliday: easily top of A tier. Not S only because Paradox does what she does but better. Still the best pick character, with insane laning, insane nuke potential and escape with the jump pads; possibly the fastest vertical movement in the game, other than Mina. [8:17]
- Infernus: top of B tier, unchanged. He got relatively better because the gun-heavy supports (Ivy, Kelvin) were nerfed; he is probably the most viable gun carry other than Mirage, slightly above average. [9:20]
- Ivy: A tier, no lower. Her gun scaling was nerfed very hard and her ult got a bit weaker, but Stone Form spirit scaling got a huge buff plus spirit quality of life upgrades. Full spirit Ivy with Kudzu Bomb and support or spirit items nukes waves, and her silencing ult is insane. [9:51]
- Kelvin: A tier, maybe too low. Gun Kelvin was nerfed nearly as hard as Ivy, so you don't run gun Kelvin anymore, but he is still the tankiest support and Frozen Shelter is one of the most broken abilities. Play him support or spirit: beam max, or more commonly Rapid Recharge plus spirit items to make his 1 (snowballs) strong. [10:52]
- Lady Geist: A tier. Her bomb (Essence Bomb) scaling was buffed and it nukes hard again; she is not broken but definitely stronger. [11:55]
- Lash: C tier, below average, from high A; one of the big losers. Ground Strike range reduced, cooldown increased and damage cut about 13% at base, so he is much worse in lane and the slam is easier to dodge. His items were hit too: Quicksilver Reload (commonly rushed, healed him with his 3) and Mystic Burst. He may have been hit a bit too hard. [12:26]
- McGinnis: below average, untouched. Since the six hero patch he has not seen a scary McGinnis as support, split pusher or gun carry. She lacks an identity: turret spam builds are not a thing, and full gun is underwhelming next to Haze, Infernus or Mirage. Gun carry Wraith is better. [13:58]
- Mina: S tier, still Thanos and maybe the best hero. Instantly blowing up walkers and objectives is removed, she does much less objective damage, and her early Rake heals less, so she may need one green (healing) item in lane where she used to buy none. [15:02]
- Mina's Rake scaling at level one or two is the same as before, Love Bites was not touched at all, and the teleport nerf of 3 seconds still leaves a very fast cooldown with Superior Cooldown. [15:33]
- Mirage: middle A, maybe below Bebop and Grey Talon. Djinn's Mark is now affected by cooldown reduction, so with cooldown on a hybrid or spirit build you collect stacks faster and do a lot of nuke damage. He may be the best carry for pro play since the other carries are weak. [16:35]
- Mo & Krill: A tier, out of S. Burrow takes more bullet damage, and his items (Quicksilver Reload) were nerfed, but he still doesn't die; if you want to shut down one specific enemy, he is your hero. [17:05]
- The new CC diminishing returns (each stacked stun lasts a bit shorter) barely affects Mo & Krill, because the grab usually comes first and the target is usually dead after it. [18:07]
- Paige: C tier, below average but not unplayable. Mostly quality of life buffs to her 1, 2 and 3; her ult now heals and damages more the farther it travels to push cross-map sniping, but its hitbox is too small to hit reliably across the map. [18:38]
- Paradox: S tier easily and realistically probably the best hero. She was already insane and got no nerfs at all; she doesn't die, has insane HP and the best initiation and pick in the game. [19:09]
- Pocket: S tier easily, not nerfed at all, maybe stronger: Diviner's Kevlar got an insane buff and is really good on him. He and Mina are the two best spirit nukes; Spellbreaker and Spirit Resilience buffs counter him a tiny bit, but you will still probably die in one shot. [20:12]
- Seven: unplayable tier, maybe the worst hero right now. He already fell out of the meta (not seen in pro play in 3 weeks) as Capacitor, which shuts down Power Surge, became common; hit by Capacitor, Seven does no damage. [20:43]
- Seven keeps a decent low rank win rate because low rank players don't walk out of Storm Cloud, but he had the lowest pick rate in Eternus of any hero a day and a half into the patch. His stun, Power Surge and ult were nerfed, his spirit scaling was nerfed and Cultist Sacrifice, great on him, was hard nerfed. In low rank he might be C or low B. [21:46]
- Shiv: A tier, placed below Kelvin, almost S. He benefited because he got no nerfs, tanks got better and many of his counters were nerfed. Run spirit Shiv: knives, dash, ult. [22:48]
- Sinclair: untouched, very hard to play, maybe one or two good Sinclair players on ladder; a very mediocre support if the enemy has no good ult to steal. He is a counter pick for specific ults (he countered Warden and stole Seven's ult, both now unplayed), so he is best as a last pick. [23:19]
- Victor: C tier after the hotfix (he was S, Thanos, before it). Aura of Suffering self-damage can no longer be reduced by any spirit resistance (Enchanter's Emblem, Spirit Resilience), even though the self-damage was cut from 85 to 80. [24:21]
- Victor used to run Spirit Resilience and stay at about one third HP for around 60% resistance, taking only 40 to 50% of Aura of Suffering's self-damage; now it is a minimum of 80 at all times, so with aura on you die much faster. Jumpstart and Pain Battery were also nerfed. Spirit Victor is back to very weak; he will try gun Victor. [25:23]
- Vindicta: B tier. The debuff play style was nerfed over the last patches, but her gun, snipe and flight got big buffs (flight lasts longer, more damage in flight), raising her left click, Assassinate and ult potential. She is "budget Grey Talon": he kills faster and his ult can hit you cross-map, hers only in line of sight. [26:25]
- Viscous: high A tier. His right click took another big damage nerf, so the right click spam in lane is weaker and his laning is worse, but he is the best support in the game other than The Doorman: good ball, can't die, great ult. [27:58]
- Vyper: A tier, maybe even a hard S. Her 1 was completely reworked and she is a lot stronger; the slide now gives an overshield instead of resistances, a bit weaker late but more damage until then. [28:59]
- Vyper prediction: hybrid builds, still mainly gun because her 1 now shreds a lot of bullet resist, plus some purple items to add spirit damage and strengthen her 1. [30:01]
- Warden: C tier at best, below average. He was an ult one trick, and Majestic Leap, his ult scaling, Quicksilver Reload and Cultist Sacrifice (a must-have on him) were all nerfed; the only buff was a little fire rate from spirit, so gun Warden still does nothing. Only one Warden seen in about 15 games since the patch. [30:32]
- Wraith: high B tier, big buffs. Base bullet damage increased (so Mercurial Magnum, which scales off base bullet damage, also does more), Spiritual Overflow now gives cooldown reduction for more card spam, and cards throw faster. She is a solid late game carry but still needs a lot of money to get online. [31:34]
- Yamato: B+ tier. Few changes and spirit Yamato is arguably a bit worse; for spirit nuke Pocket and Mina are better, but Yamato is also a tank who can tank massively. [33:05]
- Patch verdict: good and bad changes; he is happy Veil Walker and Cultist Sacrifice were nerfed, but Abrams was barely touched and Warden and Seven were gutted undeservedly, so the meta will not change much. [34:05]
