## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream (patch of late March 2026, replays from early March) with no analysis session. He reviews four players: an Apollo (Archon, Central US, with a Vyper lane partner into Grey Talon and Drifter), a Calico (Oracle, duo lane with a Bebop into Lady Geist and Lash), a Mina (Oracle) and a Graves (Oracle 3, duo with a high Eternus Bebop).
Themes: wave priority and box routes, fighting after objectives, invading enemy Sinner's Sacrifices, coin-flip fights mid, running the Urn, playing for spikes, aim and sensitivity, itemizing against spirit, melee and Lash.
Side talk: what new players should learn first, Victor's design, Fortitude versus Veil Walker, Restorative Shot, and why duration belongs on Mo & Krill.
