# How to win more ranked games AND enjoy it.
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-08-02
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KmeUtYBtGVM

## Covers
Five ranked tips from two years of coaching: an improvement mindset over chasing wins, not fearing early deaths (four criteria for a good or bad death), letting waves dictate plays, not jungling during fights and objectives, and avoiding large early engagements (kill soul split numbers).
For solo queue players climbing after the ranked update, shown on his Lash games.

## Claims
- Do not chase wins; playing only to win locks you into the same loops that worked before and stops long-term growth. [0:30]
- With enough time and games you almost always reach the rank you deserve, so focus on improving gameplay rather than winning. [1:01]
- You will lose about 40% of your games however well you play; a win rate above 60% is very hard. Accept losses. [2:03]
- Start every session with a specific goal to work on, so each game is progress whatever the result. [2:03]
- Easy concrete goals are timers: be on time for the buffs, the Urn, the Rift and other objectives, and be at the small camp when it spawns at 2 minutes. Check after each game whether you did it. [2:33]
- Do not play scared in ranked; Deadlock rewards aggression and many deaths do not matter much. [3:04]
- Example: at 12:22 on Lash he was 1/6/5 but had the same souls as the Shiv and the Bebop and was only 1.6k behind a 4/2 Haze; one play can win back 1.6k souls. [3:34]
- Judge a death on four things: the time in the game, its location, how many enemies were involved, and whether you had unsecured souls. [4:35]
- Dying before 20 minutes is not a big deal because respawn timers are very short. [4:35]
- Dying while playing for an objective, like stalling the Rift for your team, can be worth it. [5:05]
- Die past the halfway point of the map if you must; dying on their side gives them less than dying on yours. [5:05]
- Prefer to die to three or more enemies, since the soul split then punishes them; two killers still each get a lot of souls. [5:35]
- A death before 20 minutes on the enemy side, with no unsecured soul bag and three or more enemies nearby, is usually fine and sometimes good, especially if you killed a wave and created space. [5:35]
- Risk is rewarded, especially with Trophy Collector or other items that reward early fighting; a Walker or solo kill from a risk before 15 minutes gives a massive reward. [6:06]
- Most players do not push waves enough. Before taking any fight, push the wave if there is one to push. [6:36]
- At very high level (Eternus) there are situations where you skip the wave to keep the element of surprise, but for most players pushing first is sound. [7:07]
- Clearing a wave buys about 30 to 45 seconds: about 30 seconds for the next wave to spawn and about 15 more for it to reach your Walker. Use that time to gank, look for a kill or push a Walker. [7:07]
- In late game (25+ minutes) you usually want a pick to take Mid Boss; before trying, make sure two of your waves are in a good position. [8:08]
- One of the most reliable ways to get a pick, especially at lower ranks, is to set up and hide on the lane that is pushing aggressively and wait for whoever comes to clear it, then collapse on them. [8:38]
- Ensuring a good wave state before a play means that if the play goes even, you can keep going or back off without losing much on the map. [9:39]
- Getting a lead in souls is one of the most important things, but do not jungle when any objective or fight is happening on the map; if you are not adding pressure then, you are helping lose the game. [10:10]
- If a carry cannot reach a game-deciding fight in time, the correct plays are to apply pressure on their lane (push the wave, threaten the Walker) or try to reach the fight, not to farm. [10:40]
- Holding a high net worth enemy in a side lane by keeping the wave shoved is a win for the lower net worth player; he did not try to kill the Wraith, only to keep the wave out so she could not join. [11:11]
- Solo Walkers and solo kills are among the fastest ways to get a big soul spike; always poke holes in the enemy's defense when things are happening elsewhere. [11:42]
- Avoid large engagements in the first 20 minutes unless you have Trophy Collector; with it he took calculated early fights to build stacks despite many deaths. [12:12]
- Hero kill soul split (from the wiki): one killer gets 125% of the kill value, two killers 57% each (114% total), three killers 84% total; with three or more involved you get less than the full value. [12:42]
- With six players involved a kill is worth about half value, so three kills in a 6v6 are worth almost one solo kill. [13:13]
- One kill with four people gives 68% of the value, and one kill with two people 114%, so a two-person kill is worth almost two four-person kills. [13:44]
- Big group fights also mean everyone is farming the same area, missing waves and jungling inefficiently. [13:44]
- Prefer smaller fights: your individual skill decides a 2v2 far more often than a 4v4. [14:14]
- At lower ranks people over-group in mid, so limit your time in the middle lane. [14:44]
