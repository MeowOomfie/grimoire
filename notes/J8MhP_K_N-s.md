# I Turned Grey Talon into a Gun Carry
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-09-10
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8MhP_K_N-s

## Covers
Grey Talon, aquatiq, commentary over one standard match (win) on a gun build, his first time trying it.
Item choices against a tanky, low bullet resist enemy team, on the 2026-09-10 patch.

## Claims
- On a gun build, Charged Shot does almost no damage, so he does not use it. [2:14]
- Early buys: Rapid Rounds, then Extra Regen. [2:46]
- Against this enemy team it is a Reactive Barrier game. [2:46]
- Guided Owl kills are hard against a team of three tanky, high HP heroes (Venator, Bebop, Warden). [6:25]
- Use the Owl for its stun even when it will not kill. [11:32]
- Owl stacks matter less when you are not building spirit damage, but he still goes for them. [12:03]
- Duration Extender is for his 2 (Rain of Arrows); he is in flight mode half the time. [13:36]
- The edge boost tech is very useful, especially at the blue wave. [14:36]
- Raising your field of view to about 2.2 or 2.3 lets you see much more of the map. [15:38]
- Against a Kinetic Carbine Paradox he buys Spellbreaker. [17:10]
- The gun build works: decent, and good if you can aim. [19:15]
- Reactive Barrier helps against Bebop's hook and chain into trap; he is glad he bought it early. [19:45]
- He wants Lucky Shot. [19:45]
- He buys Glass Cannon, then says Fortitude would have been better and Fortitude is bad with Glass Cannon, so he should have skipped Glass Cannon. [21:16]
- The enemy team had no bullet resist, which is why the gun build did so much. [24:24]
- He buys Indomitable over Lucky Shot because his damage was already high and he had no resist. [26:54]
- Push one shrine at a time, because the shrine heals. [26:54]
