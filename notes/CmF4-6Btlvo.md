## Covers
A method for making a build for any hero from Statlocker purchase data at Ascendant and Eternus, shown on Vyper.
For players who find existing builds confusing or bloated and want a simple build to copy every game.

## Claims
- Copying a YouTube build on Vyper went badly (4 and 4, 19k damage); the build made with this method went much better, even with a support item and a spell bought. [1:00]
- You need only two tools: the Statlocker website and the Windows Snipping Tool. [1:30]
- On Statlocker pick your hero, set the rank filter to Ascendant and Eternus, and hover over the most bought item to see its purchase count (around 5,000 to 6,000 in his example). [1:30]
- The general rule: take about 10% of the most bought item's count (600 in his example) as the cut-off; he personally prefers a bit above 10%, around 700. [2:02]
- Set the chart to souls, not time; souls takes a bit more thinking but makes more sense. [2:02]
- Screenshot the chart with Snipping Tool, put it side by side, and underline every item bought more than the cut-off. [2:02]
- Put all the underlined items into a build in order, left to right as they appear on the chart. [3:04]
- Then compare with the time view to decide build order; newer players can use the time view to learn basic build order. [3:04]
- Put support items (Healing Rite and similar), and even Fortitude or Extra Health, in an optional tab instead of the main build. [3:34]
- High rank Vyper players build Extra Spirit, then Improved Spirit, then Close Quarters, then Opening Rounds, sometimes with Rapid Rounds in between for a gun spike. [4:05]
- Skip items under the cut-off: Mystic Burst fell under 700 and he doesn't like it, so it is out (add it if you like it); Extra Health also would not make it at 700. [4:05]
- The general Vyper build includes Intensifying Magazine, which he personally buys. [5:06]
- The souls view teaches tier four order best: players rush Vampiric Burst, then go to Ricochet, then Lucky Shot and Silencer. [5:06]
- The time view helps build order for tier one to two items; the souls view helps for tier fours. [5:06]
- His finished Vyper build: Extra Spirit, Close Quarters, Rapid Rounds, Opening Rounds, Bullet Resist Shredder, Intensifying Magazine, Quicksilver Reload and a few more, then green items, then Point Blank and an item captioned "Mark Mag", then tier fours. [5:37]
- He keeps both Leech and Vampiric Burst in the build and picks one depending on what he wants to do. [5:37]
- Below Ascendant (and usually in Phantom) your build mostly doesn't matter; you can build the same thing every game and win most games. [6:07]
- Counter building matters little at those ranks; basic counters like Dispel Magic (against Infernus or Mirage) or Spirit Resist against a heavy spirit team are all most heroes need. [6:07]
- Buying Spirit Resist is good in general too, because most heroes in the game are spirit. [6:37]
- His builds made this way are published under his name (search "Shotty") in the in-game build browser. [7:08]
- Ability point order is personal; most players max their 3 or their ultimate first depending on playstyle, then max their 2. [7:08]
- Bullet Resist Shredder is an insane item on Vyper; he added it because the build he copied lacked it. [7:38]
- To make the build in game: copy a build you like, delete every tab, add a category, add every underlined item, order them by comparing the time and souls views, then test it in a game. [7:38]
- The method shows what better players use; use your own discretion on which items to keep, you don't have to buy every underlined item every game. [8:08]
- It cuts out about half the items most builds list, because you only keep the most played ones. [8:40]
- If several gun items (for example five) are all bought at the same time on the graph, they are probably alternatives, so buy one or the other, not all. [8:40]
