# Every NEW Deadlock Hero Explained
channel: MartinHitsRecord  date: 2026-10-23  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D3Pptd6R5z0

## Covers
The six newest heroes in release order, abilities and team role of each: Rat King, Baba, Solomon, Deadman Danny, Violet, Nurse Harrow. Lore segments skipped. No lane, farming, spike or build advice in this video.

## Claims

### General
- Six new heroes were added; most information comes from the Deadlock wiki, covered in the order they were released [0:02]

### Rat King
- The first hero added in this update; a tanky frontline brawler who controls areas, takes damage and helps teammates in fights [0:32]
- Scrap Grenade bounces around and explodes multiple times, dealing bullet damage and slowing nearby enemies [0:32]
- Rat Swarm sends rats that leap on enemies and bite repeatedly, dealing spirit damage and reducing their bullet resistance with each bite [0:32]
- Royal Pestments gives him a barrier based on his maximum health; while it is up, incoming bullets have a chance to be reflected back at the shooter [1:03]
- His ultimate (captioned "Roolratania") charges forward with a flag, giving nearby teammates movement speed and protection; planting it makes the sewers erupt and release more rats [1:03]

### Baba
- Frontline brawler who deals spirit damage, disrupts enemies and supports teammates with magic [2:36]
- Her first ability (captioned "Threat Sat") sends a magical thread that follows her crosshair and bursts along its path, dealing spirit damage and stealing spirit from enemies [2:36]
- Granny Long Legs extends her bench's legs for movement speed, higher jumps and melee resistance; light melee becomes a knockback kick and heavy melee a ground and pound [2:36]
- Baba's Brew cycles through three effects: a barrier for Baba and teammates, a silence on enemies, and a burn that reduces enemy spirit resistance [3:07]
- Feed the Birds covers enemies in breadcrumbs, then pigeons attack them for spirit damage and turn them into pigeons; the longer they are covered, the more damage and the longer the transformation [3:07]

### Solomon
- Spirit-based support and area-control hero who positions pieces around the map to damage enemies, protect teammates and control where opponents can move; one of the most interesting kits [4:38]
- Set Up Bishop places a bishop that stays until he recalls it or is eliminated; he moves it to damage and slow enemies it passes, and after a delay it creates diagonal squares that deal extra spirit damage [4:38]
- King-side Knight turns a teammate (or himself) into a knight with a barrier and extra stamina; he can then move them, and they are invulnerable while being moved [4:38]
- Rook Battery sends two rooks forward that deal spirit damage and immobilize enemies they hit [5:09]
- Develop Queen drops a queen that deals spirit damage on impact, then releases damaging squares that can stun; he can reposition it multiple times to repeat the attack [5:09]

### Deadman Danny
- Mobile damage dealer who flies around dealing explosive damage and getting up close to enemies [6:41]
- His first ability (captioned "Strict Salvo") fires missiles that stick to the ground and explode after a short delay or when enemies get close [6:41]
- Chimera Mark 1 flies him around with his jetpack; when it overheats he crashes down in an explosion that damages and launches nearby enemies [6:41]
- Jettison Fuel sprays burning fuel that deals damage over time and reduces bullet resistance, then an explosion deals extra spirit damage and knocks Danny back [6:41]
- Close the Deal grabs an enemy hero, flies up and crashes down; both take damage, and it deals even more to enemies already below half health [7:12]

### Violet
- Spirit-based damage dealer who controls enemies and supports teammates with her artwork [8:43]
- Living Ink empowers her next three bullets so she can steer them in flight; it can also paint walls that block enemies and projectiles [8:43]
- Flow State sends a paintbrush forward leaving an ink trail; Violet and teammates slide much farther on it, and enemies in its path take spirit damage and are slowed [8:43]
- Still Life starts drawing a portrait of an enemy hero, dealing spirit damage while she draws and stunning them when it is complete [9:13]
- Abstract Landscape creates a ring of ink that blocks enemy vision, reveals enemies inside and gives nearby teammates spirit power; enemies who try to leave take spirit damage and are slowed and silenced [9:13]

### Nurse Harrow
- Support who heals teammates, weakens enemies and controls areas [10:14]
- Leech Therapy throws a jar of leeches that deal damage over time; when done they crawl back and collecting them gives her permanent health and regeneration [10:14]
- Wretch Vapors on herself or a teammate increases movement and action speed and delays some of the damage received [10:45]
- Healing Needles loads her weapon with syringes she fires at teammates to restore their health [10:45]
- Lurking Terrors: enemies looking directly at her build terror, which stops stamina and movement abilities; at maximum terror they are forced to run away [10:45]
