## Covers
Midknight Vods, a Calico main, plays three ranked Calico games and calls out his own mistakes as he goes (roaming, box routing, waving, over-rotating with the team, item activations).
Mid-stream the 9/16/26 balance patch drops and he reads the notes live with a verdict on each change, then plays some Bebop in Street Brawl to practice tracking.
He ends by analyzing an EU Calico player's VOD (name unclear in the captions), mostly for the mid game: tunnel and box routes, roams, combos and item choices.

Patch reaction (9/16/26 balance patch):
- Comeback resist now scales as 10 plus 1 per minute instead of a 35 max; good, less impactful. [133:38]
- The fixed extra boon souls for being slightly behind are removed; more comeback souls go to the lowest two net worth players through the gold tick. Makes sense. [134:09]
- Objective bounty shared to nearby heroes drops from 30% to 25%, so less reward for splitting. [134:39]
- A regular dash now cancels a parry, so dash parrying is gone; wall jump parries are now the meta. [135:10]
- Getting stunned now pauses your reload instead of restarting it; a very good change he has wanted for a long time. [135:40]
- Tunnel breakables now first spawn at 5 minutes instead of 3 and respawn every 5 minutes; this will hurt Calico's win rate, more so if more tunnel heroes are added. [136:10]
- Whether the box change is good depends on how many tunnel heroes come next patch; right now tunnels are overpowered for the few heroes that use them. [174:25]
- All movement slows reduced by 20%, dash loss reduced; slows now also affect air drag. [136:10]
- The air drag slow change will hurt Celeste a lot. [141:47]
- Hollow Point and Toxic Bullets buffed. [136:40]
- Spiritual Overflow nerf is decent, but it still gives four flat stats, so the Warden build stays viable. [137:10]
- Veil Walker no longer builds from Sprint Boots, loses +2 sprint and out-of-combat regen, and its movement bonus ends when invis ends: a very good change, and it should still feel good on Holliday. [137:41]
- Lifestrike heal increase is not a big deal. [138:41]
- Leech buff misses the point: it is a burst meta, so nerf burst heroes and items instead. [139:11]
- Diviner's Kevlar buff is unneeded; it was already good. [139:41]
- The egg changes won't stop solo queue teammates from buying it. [139:41]
- Radiant reduced and Tank Buster current health bonus reduced: fine. [140:12]
- Decay DPS reduced 25% with longer duration: still a significant nerf. [140:45]
- Focus Lens is slept on; buffing it is risky with Abrams already using it. [140:45]
- Later he calls the Focus Lens change close to a placebo: people just realize it is good. [180:47]
- The Celeste nerfs are not that big; she is still Celeste. [141:47]
