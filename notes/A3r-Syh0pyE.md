## Covers
Pocket on an all-gun build, played and commented by aquatiq in an Ascendant lobby.
Commentary over a game, no coaching; mostly gameplay with a few reasons for lane, farm and item decisions.
Patch around 2026-04-27.

## Claims
- Fire rate from a box (breakable) is useful on a Pocket going all gun. [2:05]
- When a lane is lost to a strong laner (Haze here) and you are too poor to go elsewhere, play passive and consider leaving the lane to gank another lane instead. [4:40]
- Pocket on this build cannot really poke from far away in lane, so get money from a side camp instead. [5:12]
- Your cloak (Flying Cloak) clears an entire wave, which gives Pocket good lane push. [5:43]
- Gun Pocket can take jungle camps that a normal Pocket build could not. [7:15]
- Gun Pocket does not take long to get online; at 10,000 souls he is already killing people. [10:54]
- Leave one camp up so it does not reset the respawn timer while you take the others. [10:54]
- Item path he considered after 70% weapon damage: Battle Vest, then Hunter's Aura. [9:21]
- Pocket needs Spirit Resilience no matter what. [9:52]
- If the enemy team buys Juggernaut, it would shut this build down. [17:23]
- Check enemy resistances before buying: the enemy had only one player with bullet resilience, which favors gun. [16:51]
- His job on this build: sponge damage, left click and kill everyone, trading his death for an enemy walker is fine. [14:12]
- Use Affliction (ult) on enemies to take them out of a fight; afflicted enemies tend to leave. [17:58]
- Save Affliction for the key enemy (Doorman here) when you can. [25:27]
- He considered Titanic Magazine and wanted Lucky Shot later in the game. [15:15]
