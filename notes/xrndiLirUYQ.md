# How Map Vision ACTUALLY Works in Deadlock
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-04-10
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xrndiLirUYQ

## Covers
Tested rules for when you show on the enemy minimap: gun shots, line of sight range, abilities and items, the Urn, trooper and objective vision, invisibility (Smoke Bomb, Veil Walker, Shadow Weave), summons, and niche cases (Stalker, Calico cat form, Djinn's Mark). Ends with practical tips for rotating unseen, rooftops and hunting heroes. For players who want to rotate and gank without being seen.

## Claims
- Using abilities or items on enemies does not reveal you on the minimap. [0:00]
- Shooting a hero, trooper or objective with your weapon always reveals you on the minimap, whether you are right next to it or 70 m away. [0:00]
- You stay revealed for about 2 seconds after an action that reveals you. [0:30]
- An enemy hero with line of sight on you reveals you on the minimap only within about 50 to 60 m; further away, line of sight does not reveal you. [0:30]
- Being shot by an enemy hero from beyond that 50 to 60 m range does not reveal you, but if you shoot them you are revealed. [0:30]
- Using abilities on troopers or heroes does not reveal you, for example Alchemical Fire, Ivy's Kudzu Bomb or Lady Geist's Essence Bomb thrown onto a far wave or hero. [1:01]
- Items like Cold Front or Alchemical Fire used on troopers or heroes do not reveal you by themselves, though Cold Front needs you close, where enemy heroes may see you. [1:01]
- Carrying the Urn always reveals you on the minimap. [1:32]
- Troopers have no proximity vision: they reveal you only if you shoot them with your gun or they shoot you, so you can use Cold Front, Alchemical Fire or abilities on them without showing. [1:32]
- Objectives work roughly like troopers with no inherent proximity vision, but Walkers and Guardians usually attack you when you are near, which reveals you. [2:02]
- There are three sources of invisibility: Smoke Bomb, Veil Walker and Shadow Weave. [2:02]
- Buildings currently do not reveal invisible heroes, do not target them and do not break invisibility. [2:33]
- Invisibility breaks only when you use abilities, shoot your gun, or come near an enemy hero; the proximity check needs line of sight, so a wall between you should keep you hidden, though very close around some odd walls it can break anyway. [2:33]
- Haze's Smoke Bomb invisibility lets her use items and abilities without revealing herself; otherwise it behaves exactly like Veil Walker and Shadow Weave. [3:03]
- Summons (Graves' ghouls and skulls, McGinnis's turrets) largely follow the vision rules: being hit by a summon counts as an ability and reveals neither the caster nor the target, and shooting summons does not reveal you. [3:03]
- Graves' ghouls chase invisible heroes but do not explode on them, so they can stack up on an invisible hero; likely a bug. [3:34]
- The Stalker item gives you vision of the hit hero through walls but does not reveal them on the minimap for you or your team. [4:05]
- Calico in cat form cannot be revealed on the minimap no matter what shoots her (heroes, troopers, Guardians, Walkers); this is in her ability description and intended. [4:05]
- Mirage's Djinn's Mark reveals the target on the minimap when activated, even if invisible, with just one stack; base reveal is about 6 seconds and scales with items like Duration Extender. [4:35]
- When rotating to a fight, clear the wave with abilities or items and leave to give the least vision; for example Ivy can Kudzu Bomb the wave and ult away, so the enemy neither sees the rotation nor expects that lane to push. [5:06]
- Do not shoot the last troopers when a teammate is already clearing the wave next to you; you get the souls anyway by being nearby, and shooting only gives the enemy information to speed the push by about a second. [5:37]
- Rooftops are great for vision and usually put you beyond the reveal range, so stand on them to watch rotations and spot people out of position; in matchmaking few players will call your position. [6:08]
- The downside of rooftops is that you are also too far to reveal enemies on the minimap for your team. [7:10]
- On hunting heroes like Mo & Krill, Paradox or Holliday, be mindful of showing on the map, especially with your ultimate up: showing makes enemies feel safe, while a missing Mo & Krill (who could Warp Stone around a corner and ult) limits how aggressive a Wraith can play. [7:10]
- This hunting-hero tip matters mostly above Archon; below Archon you probably do not need to worry about it. [7:40]
