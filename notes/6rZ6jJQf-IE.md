# The REAL Way To Climb In The New Deadlock Ranked
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-08-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6rZ6jJQf-IE

## Covers
How the new ranked system (August 2026, first season) works after two days of play: eight solo placement matches, 250 point wins and losses, favored-team adjustments, win streak bonuses, stacking rules, demote protection, how long the climb takes, and which hero types climb fastest.
For any ranked player, from a player placed Oracle 6.

## Claims
- You start with eight placement matches, and they matter more for your rating than any game after them. [0:00]
- After placements you gain or lose about 250 rank points per game on average; how much placements give is not known. [0:00]
- A Statlocker rank distribution shows where people were placed relative to their old ranks; so far only 8.8% of players have finished the eight placements. [0:30]
- Placement can land you anywhere: an old Archon 6 who plays well probably lands Emissary 6 (the equivalent rank after the renumbering), but playing poorly can drop you to Mystic 6; the average is around Ritualist. [0:30]
- An old Eternus 6 is placed in Oracle 6 or Oracle 5; lower ranks spread wider, so even Ascendants can reach games with Eternus players, and some high Phantom players were placed in high Oracle. [1:00]
- Placement matches can't be stacked; you must solo queue them. [1:31]
- If your team is favored to win and you lose, you lose up to 50 extra points (he lost 275, others 300). [2:01]
- If the other team is favored and you win, you gain up to 50 extra, up to 300. [2:01]
- Win streaks start at three wins: wins one and two give the normal 250, wins three and four give 50 extra (300), and every win from the fifth on gives 75 extra (325). [2:31]
- The absolute max per win is about 375, on a long win streak against a favored team; it will be rare. [3:02]
- Duo queuing drastically increases your chance to win: you can rely on one person, win your lane together and carry as two. [3:02]
- Stacking matters more right now because placements were volatile and your games mix players far above and below your skill (an old Archon could see old Phantoms, even Ascendants, and also Arcanists). [3:32]
- Yoshi said in the patch notes ranks will be volatile for a while as people find their place; expect players who are not your skill level in the first days or weeks. [4:02]
- A duo partner around your rank helps control the pace of the game and make up for one feeding teammate. [4:32]
- You can only stack within one rank: a Ritualist can stack with an Emissary or a Mystic, not a Sentinel or an Oracle. [4:32]
- Ranking up one sub-rank takes 1,000 points (four average wins); at the threshold to a major rank you need 2,000, which he describes as eight wins in a row without a loss. [5:32]
- Demote protection: after dropping to zero points in a rank you can lose two games without being demoted. [5:32]
- An estimate for climbing from Oracle 4 to Eternus 6, counting expected win streaks: about 670 games at a 55% win rate and 370 games at a 60% win rate. [6:03]
- Even the top 10 players do not average a 60% win rate. [6:35]
- A season lasts 70 days (68 left), so reaching Eternus 6 from Oracle would take at least 10 games a day at a 55% win rate, more than a full-time job by his maths. [6:35]
- It is unknown whether later seasons reset everyone to Oracle again. [7:06]
- Personal performance does not affect points; he wishes it did, like Statlocker's rating, which uses objective damage, player damage, assists and healing, not only KD. [7:36]
- Two hero types shorten the grind. First, hyper or hard carries who can 1v6 late: Lady Geist is very strong, also Venator (caption), Wraith and Seven. [8:06]
- Second, early roamers and gankers with lots of early spirit damage, mainly Lash and Pocket: win your lane, or if lane is tough with a random, roam and control the map. [8:37]
- Controlling the map early and being the playmaker at all points in the game is very valuable because everyone is trying harder in ranked. [9:08]
- Supports and tanks have a harder time because they rely on their team for value and rarely get solo pick-offs. [9:08]
- His recommended roster leaves out mostly tanks, supports or weak heroes; one hero (caption "Kace", name unclear) is not good right now. [9:39]
- If you are an insane Lash, pick Lash right now; the hero feels really strong. [10:11]
- The only way to climb faster is keeping a win streak, so stay locked in; after about 8 hours of play he lost focus, started losing and stopped. [10:11]
- You don't have to grind now: the longer the system runs the more accurate ranks become and the easier it is to climb, since everyone in Oracle, high Oracle or low Phantom is currently an old Eternus 6 player. [10:42]
- An old Ascendant placed in low Oracle reaches full Eternus-level lobbies after a few wins, because nobody is above yet. [11:13]
- The first two weeks of a rank reset are the most hardcore; waiting until things stabilize probably makes your climb easier. [11:44]
- If you can't play 10 to 20 games a day, don't expect to climb fast; the system is slow right now. [11:44]
- If a teammate AFKs and abandons, the game is canceled and you lose no rank. [12:14]
- He thinks larger stacks (three or four) should come back for ranks up to Oracle, and that duo queue should not be allowed in higher ranks because it is the number one thing being abused to climb. [12:45]
