## Covers
Pocket coaching by Midknight: one Phantom-rank Pocket player reviewed over a full game, two days after the big map and jungle patch (2026-10-02).
Themes: trading with your lane partner, Enchanter's Satchel and Flying Cloak as outs, heal snack routes, Barrage max versus ult build, Swift Striker gun spike, building defensively when ahead, mid boss pit protocol, walker trades, Broker items, and invading to push a lead.

## Claims
Laning (Pocket, Phantom):
- You were late jumping on the bridge to help your Paige; that trade should have been very negative for her. [0:30]
- You sat on your case (Enchanter's Satchel) holding it for reload tech and gained only about 30 damage from reloaded bullets. Casting it earlier does more damage to the Victor and the wave and has the cooldown up about 3 seconds sooner, exactly in time for the camp. [0:30]
- The biggest lane mistake on both sides is one player trading without their lane partner. Mirror your lane partner and know where they are as much as you can; even top 10% players make this mistake. [1:34]
- Learn heal snack locations and build them into box routes; this lane has several and each is a quick 30% max HP of passive healing. [2:05]
- You traded without your Paige; the fight would not have been close if you had picked up the 30% max HP heal first. [3:07]
- The engage was fine; the problem was the ability dump. Dump abilities so your cloak goes backwards and you keep an out and recast: toss your Satchel, and if it doesn't hit, cloak out with no danger. [3:07]
- Carry Pocket is fine. Barrage (1) max is better than it looks because it gives much more side lane fight potential, which is what carries. [3:38]
- The Paige wasted her ult on a meaningless trade, so the enemy then sunk-costed. [4:08]
- If your Drifter is under tower, follow up and join him. [4:40]
- How to play with your lane partner in solo queue: watch your minimap. If your partner backs up, don't trade or go in; if your partner is getting jumped, reinforce. [4:40]
- When the Calico shows up, cloak to height and then case; save your case for Calico's Gloom Bombs. If you case Gloom Bombs every time, Calico does almost zero damage to you. [5:42]
- Parrying a melee camp gives about 30% damage amp on the parried target, so parrying camps is offensive too: it clears the camp about 30% faster. [6:13]
- You are overextending; be ready to use abilities defensively. Calico has no slowing hex, so you can leave whenever you want if you use your escapes. [6:44]
- Calico staying in lane 700 souls off her ult is flawed: you can just box out, run your whole tunnel route and hit your Sinner's Sacrifice timer. [7:14]
- At higher ranks the bell tower is probably not contestable: two or three players farm it through a choke, the way high-level players already hold hands farming doubles. [8:15]
- The ult-bot AP build is fine and much easier to pilot, but he prefers the build centered on Barrage max with two points in cloak instead of two points in case early. [8:46]
- Engage with your leap: Barrage him, throw your cloak slightly defensively, and you take half his HP or more with no danger. [8:46]
- When your Drifter wants to gank, let your leap come up, then leap, Barrage, ult. [9:16]
- Don't crash behind cover there; you should have left. [9:46]
- Tell your Drifter to catch a couple of side waves; the urn is a lot of free souls. [9:46]
- Keep wave priority so you can still farm; when a Haze is overextended and mispositioned, call the gank on mic. [10:19]
- As good as side laning is on Pocket, at rift time with Affliction up and near max Affliction you want to be in the rift fight, not the one side laning. Ask someone to swap side lane with you. [10:49]

Rifts, build and items:
- Rift resists only start when the rift opens. A 10% comeback is about 8% resist, which is still contestable depending on positioning. [12:22]
- With the new rift resists, ults matter about as much as the soul deficit when deciding to contest. [14:56]
- AP order: don't put two points into Affliction before Barrage; finishing 1 max before the rift gives side lane pressure and a better AP spike. [13:23]
- You are very green and dodged your spikes. Building defensively when ahead is not a bad thought, but you need your first damage spike (gun or spirit) first. [13:55]
- The early gun spike is only insane once you have two points in cloak; otherwise it is good, not ridiculous. [14:56]
- Escaping the hotel: with a dispel item, the slowing hex should not scare you. Case out, you are not combat tagged, then leap. If you get hexed, dispel and recast your 2. [15:26]
- Good Pocket mindset: when you are about to die, toss an Affliction to help your team first. [15:57]
- Gun spike is normally Swift Striker. Pocket does not lack raw damage, it lacks fire rate, so Swift Striker finishes the spike; Close Quarters is fine only in a melee-heavy lane. [16:30]
- Barrage max is broken. People prefer cloak max with ult bot because it is easier and nearly unpunishable, while Barrage max does huge damage but punishes mistakes and has a much higher skill ceiling. [17:30]
- You could and should have gone mid boss there. [18:32]

Mid boss pit protocol:
- Grabbing a camp on the way to mid boss helps clear speed. [19:05]
- Even when a contest is unlikely, don't have the whole team on the pit with nobody parrying. Pocket has i-frames: stand on the Rejuvenator, case into parry. [19:05]
- When a contest is unlikely (one or two enemies), case and parry and say on mic that you are parrying so others don't over-parry; defer to a Counterspell player or an Ivy going Stone Form into parry. [20:07]
- When a contest is likely (say 5v6), position outside the pit and don't play for crystals; your goal is a hidden Affliction, maybe a parry, then teleport out. [20:37]
- Meleeing is situational: as the defending team take a good melee (only one enemy there, or they mistimed key CC), but if they have four in the pit immediately, don't risk it. [21:07]
- As the contesting team, almost never play for melees; play for a big Affliction. Look for parries, because on an AoE hero like Pocket or Calico a parry amps everyone parried and your Affliction, 1, 2 combo kills them. [22:39]
- He thinks Sinner's Sacrifice camps may still be syncable as a 50/50, since there seem to be only a fast and a slow version; unverified. [24:15]
- Missing Swift Striker is killing you; you desperately need your gun spike. [24:46]
- Typing in the enemy secret shop is dangerous. [24:46]

Macro and trades:
- Common mistake: the enemy takes your walker and pushes yours, and your team zips back to defend a walker that is already dead. Take their base Guardians instead, and a shrine if it takes them long enough. [25:16]
- All five need to stay for that trade; two alone can do it but the immobile Paige would probably die. [25:47]
- If you didn't use your mic to call the push, your teammates resetting is your fault. [26:20]
- With your dispel up you should not get kidnapped by the Doorman's cart; dispel it. [26:52]
- He thinks Trophy Collector is getting worse on Calico and Enduring is also fine, but on the new map there is so much fighting that Trophy has felt good. Trophy sacks tempo, and the meta moved from scaling to tempo. [26:52]
- You are forcing your Affliction too hard into enemies with Rejuvenator; they will soon be forced down a choke to hit your base Guardians or walker, which gives a better Affliction. [27:54]
- Solo queue players don't reset into a Pocket Affliction even though they should. [28:24]
- Ethereal Shift is good into this comp: great into Kelvin's dome, great into Infernus ult, decent into a Doorman kidnap, good into Haze when your dispel is down, and good into Calico Gloom Bombs. [28:54]
- During the Broker event, always broker an item; for Pocket the best may be Tankbuster, Spirit Burn, a duration item or Transcendent Cooldown, depending on the downsides. [29:24]
- To climb fast while Broker is live, spend 10 minutes on stat sites learning which brokered items are overpowered for your hero and just buy those. [29:58]
- On the new map one of his games had 60% of net worth from neutrals; it is easier to catch up when behind by taking time to farm. [30:29]
- A brokered Divine Barrier is also very good: about 30% ult cooldown reduction and a barrier that nearly doubles to about 1,900. [31:30]
- Forcing mid boss in a winning position was an unforced error; the enemy still had a strong pit fight. If you want to force that fight, bait the mid boss instead of going full. [32:15]
- Your cloak in that coin-flip mid boss was fine; throwing it through the pit and out could add damage but risks dying when you are this fed. [33:48]
- You should always have a brokered item; even with the worst downsides one of the options is good. Avoid downsides like minus ability duration or cooldown on Pocket. A brokered item is worth roughly double the item's power. [34:20]
- Brokering Refresher with minus ability range was a poor choice; there was probably a weapon damage downside available instead. [36:22]
- You engaged with only one Rejuvenator; you had over a minute and a half left on it, so set your other waves first. [37:31]
- When you start selling early items, Overflow (as captioned) is the common tier 4 buy for the spike; he has seen Spellslinger but thinks Overflow is better. [38:03]
- You should not have re-engaged; set your waves before sieging. [38:38]
- Commit your abilities more to players. Too much of your damage went into the Victor and the player next to him instead of jumping their backline or a squishy. [39:44]
- Summary: trade with your lane partner, always place your cloak as an out, get your gun spike and early item spikes, and look for side lane 1v1s, where a Barrage max Pocket can one-shot or whittle people. [40:14]
- When fed, invade: you stole only about two camps all game and were not denying enemy farm. [40:44]
- The core losses were the awful mid boss call and teammates wasting your Rejuvenator on the second one. [40:44]
