# How To Carry Bad Teammates In Deadlock (It's Easy)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-09-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XM7SOBSoAf0

## Covers
Replay review of a 2024 Wraith game where the rest of the team feeds and Wraith carries by farming safely.
Aggressive lane start vs Infernus, Healing Rite, Tesla Bullets before jungling, passive Wraith play vs ganking Haze, bridge buffs and Sinner's Sacrifice at 10 minutes, where to go after base, solo Mid Boss, not dying when richest.
For players whose teams fall behind early, especially Wraith players.

## Claims
- In Deadlock more than other MOBAs you can hard carry your team, because souls are often shared and any hero can be built like a carry. [0:30]
- Five teammates who are AFK and keep dying cannot be carried. [1:01]
- In lane Metro took a lot of damage but used it to push onto the enemy tower and play aggressively, getting a kill around 1:40. [1:31]
- At the start of lane, check your lane opponent; Infernus cannot do much damage to you at level one or two. [2:02]
- Look for creeps you can kill fast early without taking much damage, play behind cover, and burst down two creeps instantly. [2:33]
- Bursting the enemy creeps early puts you on their tower while they must deny and last hit under pressure, and your creeps shoot the tower and the enemy while you shoot the enemy. [3:05]
- Willingly take damage from the enemy in order to get ahead. [3:05]
- With two cards stored as Wraith, kill the enemy as soon as they overstep: hit the first card, then the second with the tracking. [3:05]
- When low after a lane win, buy Healing Rite and pop it immediately instead of going back to base, which costs a lot of time; Metro also bought Basic Magazine to stay ahead. [3:36]
- Play safe behind cover until Healing Rite has almost run out. [4:06]
- When the enemy has twice your creeps coming (eight vs four), do your best not to let eight creeps run into your tower. [4:06]
- Early lane is too early to gank or help your team. [4:36]
- When the enemy snowballs (a Bebop at 4.5k vs your Vindicta at 1.5k at 5 minutes), there is not much you can do yet; keep farming. [5:06]
- Wraith is a defensive hero in the early and mid game; a common mistake is hitting level six, getting Telekinesis and roaming to gank like a Haze. [5:36]
- Wraith ganks often fail because Telekinesis has a travel time, so a good player with stamina saved reaches cover or gets out of range. [6:07]
- Played defensively, Wraith farms and the enemy comes to punish her; Telekinesis then lands easily because they spent resources getting close. [6:38]
- Metro gets far ahead when he plays defensively and farms, and loses the games where he tries to do too much; do not panic. [7:09]
- Stay in lane until you kill the enemy tower and get Tesla Bullets; without Tesla Bullets Wraith does not do enough damage to farm camps or other lanes. [7:40]
- After Tesla Bullets, push the lane out once more and farm the enemy creeps when you have seen where the enemy heroes are. [7:40]
- If you only showed in lane, the enemy probably thinks you are back at base, so their jungle is safe to farm. [8:11]
- The first thing to do after Tesla Bullets is farm camps; take the closer enemy camp if it is safe. [8:42]
- With the lane pushed to their tower and an enemy close and defensive, take the opportunity for a free kill. [8:42]
- Early on, Wraith only wants 1v1s (she almost always wins them, even behind) or 2v2s, not full team fights; a Haze would instead look for ganks. [9:42]
- As the soul leader on your team you must be the one not dying; go back to heal instead of jungling at low HP, even with Bullet Lifesteal. [10:13]
- Every time you go home, check if you can buy items, then pick which lane to go to; Metro joined a Warden being attacked by Haze and got the kill. [10:44]
- The important part of a kill is not wasting time getting it; take kills on the way when you already had to go back to base, not by running between lanes. [10:44]
- After the kill, take the free tower if the lane is empty, then go straight back to farming. [11:15]
- At 10 minutes two buffs spawn on the two bridges (left and right); few players take them. [11:15]
- Bridge buffs last about two and a half to three minutes and give insane regen, faster cast time, or gun damage, which is the best one for Wraith. [11:15]
- The gun damage buff is free money: you farm creeps faster and win 1v1s easily, so be near one of the two bridge spawns at 10 minutes. [11:48]
- After 10 minutes the Sinner's Sacrifice (slot machines you punch) spawn and give insane souls, especially early. [12:18]
- Metro tries to be at one of the bridges and then farms the Sinner's Sacrifice, since he will be his team's main farmer. [12:18]
- An enemy Bebop with Echo Shard can double bomb for about 1,000 damage in one combo. [12:48]
- After surviving a gank, go right back to farming rather than overcommitting. [12:48]
- Fleetfoot is insane on this Wraith build. [12:48]
- The most important skill in Deadlock is farming efficiently while staying alive: know which camps are safe, because dying with lots of unsecured souls makes the game easier for the enemy. [13:18]
- Pure jungle farming has a big risk: you lose the souls if you die. [13:48]
- The Sinner's Sacrifice room is insane money if safe; many players skip it because punching them is boring. [13:48]
- As the highest farmed player, do not join a 2v3 that already lost a teammate; dying to turn it around is not helping your team. [14:19]
- Wraith's play style is passive: sit in your territory farming, and if someone comes in, kill them for free; the opposite of Haze, who goes invisible to gank farming enemies. [14:49]
- Keep Telekinesis up almost always while farming. [15:19]
- Use Project Mind to backdoor and take an enemy tower, then teleport out before enemies arrive. [15:19]
- From a zipline perch, any enemy coming up solo dies easily to Telekinesis; wait there for cooldowns. [16:19]
- When enemies push deep into your side and you have teammates near, the worst case is the enemy escapes and you get the farm. [16:19]
- When you are the richest player on the map, you should get at least one kill from a fight unless you misplay completely. [16:49]
- Do not go for proactive aggressive plays; look for opportunities within your play style. [17:20]
- Playing in your comfort zone is worth more than panicking to make something happen because your team is behind, as long as you are confident you can survive and carry in the long run. [17:20]
- Do not scream at teammates who keep dying; keep farming what is safe. [17:51]
- After finishing the mid game build, Metro planned Intensifying Magazine next. [17:51]
- Do not go in with 4.5k unspent souls; Metro broke his own rule and tried to Telekinesis a Mo & Krill who also had an ult. [18:23]
- Even when farmed enough that the enemy cannot punish you, play it safe and do not overcommit; make sure your team does not die. [18:23]
- Metro bought Lucky Shot right away, the more aggressive choice; Intensifying Magazine then Spirit Armor for more tankiness may have been more correct. [18:55]
- Escort your teammate delivering the Urn so no one contests it. [19:27]
- Every time you leave base, look at all the lanes and decide where to go; this is the number one thing to learn. [19:27]
- Go to the side where your team most needs reinforcement, for example a lone Lady Geist without cover, rather than a side that already has three teammates or a Dynamo safe near his Walker. [19:57]
- If all six enemies are missing they may be setting up a team fight or a catch; as Wraith you do not want to be the one caught out. [20:58]
- When you are far ahead in souls and the team is grouped, a team fight should be won unless a teammate gets caught early. [21:28]
- Wraith's Telekinesis is very auto aimed and locks onto the closest target, which can grab the wrong enemy. [21:28]
- Once up a player, you can play more aggressively. [22:00]
- Mo & Krill soloing you with his ult while other enemies are around can stun lock and kill you; play it safe and leave even if you could probably win the 1v1. [22:32]
- When your team is winning a fight deep in enemy territory that you cannot reach in time, push out the blue wave and take Mid Boss alone. [23:02]
- With two enemies dead (Haze and Infernus) Wraith can solo Mid Boss easily; it secures the win even if your team overcommits and dies. [23:32]
- When soloing Mid Boss with Telekinesis up, a single contester like a Kelvin cannot do anything if you play it correctly. [24:02]
- Not every game works out: sometimes your team keeps feeding and you fall further behind. [24:02]
- One player can turn a game around by being in the right place at the right time; a few key pickoffs while farming can be all it takes. [24:33]
- A big mistake is forcing team fights over and over; even if net positive, you give the enemy souls, and farming efficiently earns more. [25:03]
- With Rejuvenator up, dying is less costly, so you can let your team initiate and go in. [25:33]
- Main key when behind: be safe and reduce your deaths as much as possible. [25:33]
- The more souls you have relative to your team, the worse it is to die, because the enemy gets more souls from you. [26:03]
- Metro finished 14 and 1 while his teammates were 1 and 4, 1 and 4, 1 and 1 and 1 and 7, showing the soul swing from safe farming. [26:03]
- Even when behind on Wraith, the same safe play style works: unless you have half the enemy's souls, you win most 1v1s because of Telekinesis, and you become insane in team fights later. [27:04]
