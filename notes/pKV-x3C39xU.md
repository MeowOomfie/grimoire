# 22 Minutes of Brutally Honest Deadlock Advice
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-10-29
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pKV-x3C39xU

## Covers
A talk-over list of the mistakes the coach sees in hundreds of VOD reviews: farming balance, spending souls before fights, all-in then all-out fighting, limit testing aggression, rotations with bounce pads, aim, mic use, blaming teammates, minimap checks and unspent souls. For new to mid-level players.

## Claims
- The most common mistake is farming too much: players sit in the jungle all game while their team dies, then miss the first three Rifts, the Mid Boss fight, the Walker fight and the fights leading up to them. [0:30]
- By the time an over-farmer has the souls, the rest of the team is so far behind that it does not matter. [0:30]
- Instead of blaming teammates, ask what you could have done; there is always something, and it keeps control of your games and your improvement in your hands. [2:01]
- Example from his own game: buying Indomitable first instead of Inhibitor at the very end would have kept him alive to win the last fight. [1:31]
- Balance farming by going for one or two items; if you are close to an item, finish it before you show up for a fight most of the time. [2:31]
- Exception: if you are about 3k souls behind, do not hold back from fights to farm. [2:31]
- Spend as many souls as possible before heading to a fight; if your team is fighting, ask on mic to back out for a minute so you can finish your item (Silencer, Ricochet, Inhibitor). [2:31]
- When the enemy deathballs six heroes mid, call your team into a 2-2-2 split, defending Walkers or grouping in twos, so a single responder to your Walker push loses a 2v1. [3:01]
- Two defenders under a Walker, especially CC heroes like Solomon (with ult) or Paige, can hold it against four pushers and benefit from the extra resist from standing under it. [3:32]
- Keep thinking about numbers around the map the whole game. [3:32]
- Before 10 minutes, do not go to lane fights or bridge buff fights if the enemy is fighting a lot; instead get lane priority by killing the wave first and pushing into the Guardian to kill it as early as possible. It still depends on the game. [4:03]
- Fight all-in then all-out: a high-rank Ivy he played with did nothing (did not even shoot) when his cooldowns were down, used his cooldowns, usually got a pick, and left. [4:33]
- Many players force fights and kills they do not have the strength for; the longer a fight draws out, the more risk someone comes to pick you. [5:03]
- Drawn-out fights give enemies more chances to react; full dive, execute, leave gets picks without the whole enemy team arriving. [5:34]
- Bait the over-rotation: after a pick mid, if the enemy over-rotates toward you, call for everyone to push out the side lanes and use the distraction. [6:04]
- Two players jumping one target quickly with a stun (Ivy, or Haze's Sleep Dagger) kills him before he can do anything, and then both leave. [6:04]
- While farming, leave the camp and take the wave when you see one coming to your Walker or your side of the map, because waves give more souls than camps and boxes. [6:34]
- He thinks a slower wave timer (40 or 35 seconds) is not a bad idea because the new map has so much to do: about 17 camps, 43 heavy boxes, 45 golden statues and 17 Sinner's Sacrifices. [6:34]
- Players are not aggressive enough; running at people you think you are better than is a powerful tool in every game. [7:37]
- Limit test: run it down to see what you can get away with, so you learn your hero's and your own limits. [7:37]
- On Lady Geist he could 1v5 with good cooldown management, thinking about his ultimate and timing a swap to dodge big attacks. [8:07]
- Against early lane bullies like Grey Talon and Mina, going in with confidence (backed by mechanics) and baiting light melees for parries can beat them, rather than accepting you will lose. [8:37]
- To win more lanes, do not melee in lane phase unless you have already baited out the enemy parry; many lanes and kills are lost to a parried light melee for about 50 damage. [9:07]
- Lower-ranked players are either too afraid or too gutsy to push Walkers; if you watch the map and no one is there, go for the objective. [9:38]
- You can only find the line between too aggressive and too passive by testing both boundaries and learning from each game. [10:08]
- After a run of deaths, take time out: tell the team you will be back, farm half the map for a few minutes, come back with two tier 4 items and win fights again. [10:38]
- Every new player is too slow on rotations; ziplines without Zip Boost are very slow. [11:09]
- Rotate with bounce pads instead: jump on a bounce pad, bounce off a wall before you hit the ground and air dash once, about four buttons, and you are across the map; there is a matching bounce pad for the other direction. [11:39]
- To learn the map, use the in-game table button "Explore NYC" and walk from place to place over and over. [12:10]
- Deadlock is very aim intensive and most players miss too many shots; even Billy needs aim, and Nurse Harrow needs aim to hit headshots with the heal gun. [12:41]
- Aim training is not only about hitting basic attacks or ultimates; better mouse control keeps track of your crosshair and the target after fast flicks and air strafes, which makes everything more consistent. [13:42]
- Use your mic to delegate: ask a Vindicta to do something, an Abrams to push, a Billy to front line for you. It takes pressure off you. [14:43]
- Call missing in lane phase, even spam it; hold Tab or Alt and click the enemy's portrait (he thinks middle click) until your hero calls the enemy missing. [15:13]
- Climbing has a social side: make teammates laugh, praise their plays and raise their mental to get them working with you and listening to you. [15:44]
- Do not be toxic while the game is still winnable; encourage and guide teammates, since most players do not know what they are doing. [16:46]
- You are the common denominator every match; take as much accountability as you can and use every match as a learning experience. [17:47]
- If you start tilting, mute the person tilting you, or get off the game after a match. [18:19]
- Most players do not look at the minimap enough and farm Sinner's Sacrifices and tier 3 camps while their team fights and dies mid. [19:51]
- With the QOL Lock mod you can increase the minimap size, which forces you to look at it; he found that one of the biggest helpers when starting. [19:51]
- Build a scan loop like checking car mirrors: enemy, ammo, time, objectives (Mid Boss), minimap, and repeat about every 20 seconds until it is second nature. [20:51]
- If you have a bag of unsecured souls, spend them or stop fighting; having to fight with a bag usually means it is late in the game or your positioning was bad. [21:21]
