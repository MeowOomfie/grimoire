## Claims
- Her 1 weaves a thread that follows your crosshair then bursts along its path, dealing spirit damage and stealing enemy spirit; while holding stolen spirit, Baba deals bonus spirit damage with every bullet. [0:00]
- The 1 travels straight out and comes straight back like a boomerang, and you can steer it only a tiny bit. [1:01]
- Her 2, Granny Long Legs, extends her bench for bonus speed, resistance, higher jumps and melee resistance; it is a movement ability and you can still dash during it. [1:01]
- Her 3 brews a pot of tea that, when thrown, applies an area effect cycling over time between a soothing barrier, silence, or a scalding burn that deals spirit damage over time and reduces spirit resist. [1:31]
- Press mouse 2 to pause or resume brewing, so you do not need to time the click; just wait for the brew you want and pause it. [1:31]
- The soothing brew heals teammates, so she has a healing tool. [2:04]
- The silence brew lasts a fairly long time. [2:34]
- Her ult, Feed the Birds, channels bread on targets in front of you; when the channel ends pigeons bombard the target for spirit damage and hex them into a pigeon, with damage and duration increasing. [2:34]
- Her heavy melee during the 2 is a kick that knocks enemies back. [3:06]
- Press control during the 2 to jump, and you can ground pound people with the chair. [3:06]
