# Why Gun Heroes OUTSCALE Spirit Heroes
channel: Deathy
date: 2026-03-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DMAVxcaYdoQ

## Covers
Theory video on why gun heroes (Wraith, Warden) scale into the late game better than spirit heroes (Apollo, Lash): jungle speed, percent weapon damage that grows with boons vs flat spirit, the multipliers each side can buy, and the counter items and resist reduction each side has access to.
For players choosing heroes and items who want to understand early vs late game power.

## Claims
- Wraith and Warden scale well while Apollo and Lash fall off later, largely because of how gun vs spirit scales, how the shop works and the answers each damage type has. [0:00]
- Gun-focused heroes generally farm the jungle faster, so they get more souls and scale better. [0:31]
- Gun always has higher DPS than spirit, while spirit has more burst; consistent damage is better for farming the jungle, especially tier three camps. [1:01]
- Spirit heroes can farm tier two camps extremely well, so they are good at snowballing, while gun heroes are good at farming the harder camps; this is less true now that the jungle is easier for everyone. [1:01]
- Gun heroes more typically play side lanes, which gets them more money. [1:01]
- Gun items give a percent multiplier on your base bullet damage, so they get better with boons: buying 4.8k in gun items (for example Kinetic Dash and Headhunter) gives 49% weapon damage, 54% with Headhunter's extra 5%. [3:03]
- At low level that 54% only gave 76 DPS: base bullet 8.46, shop bonus +4.15, Headhunter +0.42; at max level the same items gave +10 bullet damage from the shop bonus and +1.02 from Headhunter, twice as effective. [3:34]
- Spirit items and spirit investment are flat, except Boundless Spirit, whose 15% spirit power scales with boons because every hero gains spirit per boon (Lash gets 1.1 spirit per boon, a little over 38 at max level). [4:04]
- Improved Spirit gives the same whether bought early or late, which makes spirit much more valuable early and much less valuable late compared to gun. [4:35]
- Ways to raise spirit damage: more raw spirit or Boundless Spirit, scaled by each ability's spirit scaling (comparable to each hero's gun growth). [5:05]
- Second way: cast more often; Superior Cooldown is effectively 20% more damage if you use spells off cooldown, which rarely happens but is realistic on Lash and some other heroes. [5:36]
- Third way: resistance reduction, for example Mystic Vulnerability at -8 spirit resist, which can go negative; it gets better as your damage grows and as enemies stack resist, since 50% to 42% is worth more than 0 to -8. [6:06]
- Fourth way: flat extra damage items such as Silence Wave, Arctic Blast and Thermal Pulse; Echo Shard counts as cooldown, and Mercurial Magnum is a unique case. [6:37]
- Spirit's only real multipliers are resist reduction and cooldown if you can use it; otherwise you buy flat spirit that doesn't scale well. [7:07]
- Gun scales through reload speed (the hardest to solve: Active Reload, Spellslinger, ammo from items like Escalating Resilience and Titanic Magazine, or Quicksilver Reload, a spirit item), fire rate, and resist reduction. [7:07]
- Fire rate multiplies all your damage: going from 0 to 20% fire rate gives 20% more damage whatever your weapon damage, and it lets you dish out damage in shorter bursts late game, which is more applicable than spirit's cooldown. [7:37]
- 100% fire rate and 100% weapon damage multiply on top of each other, something spirit does not have. [8:38]
- Lucky Shot is another damage multiplier on top of everything, last checked around 1.27x on all your damage; with a full build gun reaches damage spirit builds can pretty much never achieve. [8:38]
- Metal Skin is insanely powerful against gun, basically making them do zero damage, and is an incredible counter in the early to mid game. [9:40]
- Curse removes Metal Skin; once Curse is in play, Metal Skin gets removed and gun becomes strong. [9:40]
- The video says gun scales better, not that gun is stronger than spirit. [10:10]
- Bullet resist options: Bullet Resilience (buffed recently, all green items got buffed), Escalating Resilience, a little on Berserker, Battle Vest (a bit more than Enchanter's Emblem), and Plated Armor late, which is very strong against gun and on-hit items because it multiplies existing weapon resist. [10:40]
- The gun answer to stacked bullet resist is Armor Piercing Rounds, a 50% chance to ignore all of your resistances. [11:11]
- Disarming Hex is a fantastic mid game option, but late game the gun hero buys Unstoppable and disarm and CC stop working. [11:11]
- When Deadlock is centered on the mid game, gun heroes perform poorly because counters to them are plentiful then and their team lacks Curse yet; in a late game meta (which the game was in until recently, games are now getting shorter), having one or two gun heroes matters or you fall off. [11:11]
- The common spirit counters are Spellbreaker, Counterspell (which can parry several spells) and Spirit Resilience. [11:42]
- A spirit hero facing Spellbreaker plus Spirit Resilience generally has no answer; the only trick is to pop their Spellbreaker with Mystic Shot at range. [12:43]
- Bullet resist reduction options are many: Weakening Headshot, Alchemical Fire, Hollow Point, Hunter's Aura, Crippling Headshot, Rusted Barrel, Disarming Hex, Bullet Resist Shredder, and Armor Piercing Rounds, which bypasses resist rather than reducing it. [13:14]
- Spirit resist reduction options are far fewer and mostly upgrades: Spirit Snatch, Escalating Exposure, Spirit Sap (cooldown based, one target, hard to reapply), Spirit Shredder Bullets and Spirit Rend. [13:46]
- Myth: Escalating Exposure does not reduce spirit resist more than Mystic Vulnerability; both reduce it by the same amount (captioned "80%"). [13:46]
- Because spirit heroes have few answers to resistance stacking, stacking spirit resist against them late game is extremely effective. [14:17]
- It is good design that some heroes are strong early and fall off: Apollo wins lane extremely easily but falls off hard when everyone buys Spellbreaker, Counterspell and Spirit Resilience, while many gun heroes feel underwhelming early and extremely strong with full items. [14:17]
- Ricochet, which lets gun heroes damage multiple targets and apply debuffs more easily, is another big reason gun scales better. [14:48]
- Right now gun heroes are not that good overall, though some are; Deadlock has had few gun metas because it has historically been won in the first 15 to 20 minutes, especially at high level. [15:20]
