## Covers
Release-day quick start guide for Violet, the painter hero: each ability and upgrade (empowered brush shots and ink walls, the slide brush, Still Life, Abstract Landscape), combos, a slide exploit and first build ideas. For players picking her up on release.

## Claims
- Violet is a very complicated hero with a high skill ceiling: four abilities that work more like eight. [0:00]
- Her normal left click is a fairly fast projectile. [0:32]
- Ability 1 (left click part, Living Ink): empowers your next three shots with bonus bullet and spirit damage, and they track and can be curved like Wraith's cards, so you can hit enemies behind corners with tight timing. [0:32]
- Empowered shots deal AoE damage to anyone close to the target. [1:03]
- Ability 1 is on a charge meter with 2 charges at start; levelling it lowers the cooldown. [1:03]
- Ability 1 (right click part) draws on the map: a solid wall nobody can pass, which you can also draw below you to climb to high ground. [1:34]
- Draw a wall behind enemies at max draw distance to stop them escaping and imprison them. [2:05]
- One heavy melee breaks the entire ink wall. [2:05]
- Drawing and empowering share charges: choose between a wall (one charge) or bonus damage shots. [2:05]
- Ability 1 level 2 adds a stacking fire rate buff. [2:35]
- Every empowered hit lowers all your ability cooldowns by 1.5 seconds and your item cooldowns by half that, so active items are great on her. [2:35]
- Ability 1 is her core: you want as many charges on it as possible; it is her main damage tool and main disruption tool. [3:05]
- Ability 2: a brush stroke that travels across the map; sliding on it gives increased slide speed. [3:05]
- Enemies the ability 2 brush passes through take damage, and you can explode it at any time for a bigger AoE hit on several enemies. [3:38]
- You steer ability 2 with your mouse, so you can choose a creative path. [3:38]
- Probably unintended: cast ability 2 while sliding and keep steering it under you to slide almost indefinitely; the tooltip says a 10 second maximum, which is still plenty to escape a team fight. Movement speed makes it easier. [4:08]
- Escape line: jump dash, start the slide, and ride the trail out; it is hard because you control your movement and the brush at once. [4:38]
- While sliding on ability 2 you get 150 stamina regen, so you can burn your stamina and get it back quickly. [4:38]
- Ability 2 level 2: lower cooldown and longer duration; level 3: a damage amp that stacks to 20% over 6 seconds of sliding and amplifies all your damage (gun, melee, spirit). [5:10]
- Ability 2 is your tool both to get into and out of fights and has a low cooldown. [5:10]
- Combo: start the fight on ability 2 to build the damage amp, cancel it (about a 10 second cooldown), then use ability 1 hits to bring its cooldown back. [5:40]
- Ability 3, Still Life, is in his view the overpowered ability: pick a hero and they take spirit damage over time while you look at them; when the sketch finishes they are stunned and drop a buff you can pick up. [5:40]
- The Still Life buff is like a golden statue buff: spirit power on some heroes, weapon damage on others, health on tanks. [6:11]
- Still Life level 1 upgrade: you heal while casting (15 health per second for 4 seconds), and picking up the buff extends the heal by 4 more seconds, up to 8. [6:41]
- After casting Still Life you can walk away and it still lands; the only stun condition is staying in line of sight for about 3 seconds. [7:12]
- Still Life level 2: the target deals 35% less damage to you while being drawn, like a free Inhibitor or Mirage's Fire Scarabs on steroids, so in a 1v1 they barely damage you. [7:12]
- Combo with a teammate like Wraith on the side: go in, paint the target, they are stunned and could not damage you, then shoot them and they die. [7:43]
- Still Life max level: movement speed while casting, lower cooldown and longer stun. [7:43]
- Still Life has a long base cooldown of 35 seconds, 24 seconds at max level. [8:13]
- Still Life is a candidate for Echo Shard because a free stun plus damage plus damage reduction shuts down one person; it feels close to an ultimate like Wraith's. [8:13]
- Ultimate, Abstract Landscape: a big AoE circle that gives your team spirit power, slows enemies inside, and silences and damages enemies who try to leave. [8:44]
- Abstract Landscape is a massive AoE even without Mystic Expansion. [9:16]
- Abstract Landscape upgrades: spirit lifesteal for your whole team in the circle (25%), more spirit power, and it reveals all enemies, giving full info like wall hacks. [9:16]
- Cast Abstract Landscape at Mid Boss or in Rift fights to see the whole enemy team at all times. [9:47]
- Enemies can melee through the Abstract Landscape barrier at base, but at max level the ink no longer breaks when they step out. [9:47]
- Violet does very well in spirit heavy comps because her ult gives the whole team spirit power. [10:18]
- Build: Radiant Regen and Rapid Recharge are no-brainers; every ability of hers deals spirit damage, so Radiant Regen procs easily in team fights. [10:48]
- Team fight pattern: go in with the ability 2 path to hit several people, then use ability 1 shots on the others, and Radiant Regen gives near endless healing. [11:18]
- Playstyle is in and out like Mina or Celeste, but without teleports: use the brush to get in, then your recovered stamina to get out. [11:50]
- She is hard to build because ability 1 lowers every cooldown, making active items strong. [11:50]
- Kinetic Dash is very good: walking onto ability 2 starts a slow slide, but jump dashing then starting the slide is faster. [12:21]
- Fire rate helps her because more fire rate applies Living Ink more often; ability 1 stacks fire rate up to five times, for up to 50%. [12:21]
- Spellslinger, not in the default build, should be crazy on her for the same reason it is on Celeste: she casts a lot, so by the time she shoots she already has many stacks. [12:52]
- Reload during Still Life: when you run out of bullets, cast your 3 and reload while the target is being stunned. [13:22]
- After the base build go full spirit power; get some fire rate, Kinetic Dash, more stamina, and eventually your spirit spike. [13:53]
- Ink walls have very high range and last a long time; they can be heavy meleed, and you can likewise double heavy melee McGinnis's wall. [14:24]
- Use the ink wall by waiting until enemies come in, then walling them in. [14:54]
