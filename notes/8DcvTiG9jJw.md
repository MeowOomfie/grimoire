## Covers
Wraith coaching by Midknight: he reviews an Eternus Wraith's game (laning with Lash into Viscous and Pocket), posted 2026-08-14.
Themes: farming camps in and out of lane as a scaling gun hero, card stacking and card spikes, playing off the ult cooldown, looking for picks in side lanes not mid, respecting ganks as the pick target, item choices, and baiting mid boss.

## Claims
Card spikes and stacking:
- With a Joker at your tier one card spike you are very strong; look for an all-in on the Pocket, especially if Lash goes in on the same target with all your cards. [0:30]
- Your next spike is at 2 ability points in the card ability, with increased spirit scaling and damage, so you have time to farm cards until then. [0:30]
- You saved the Joker for a long time and then spent it at a suboptimal time, essentially just for damage. [2:01]
- Your card stack was bad (one spade, two hearts and a club is one useful card), so you could not fight there. [5:40]
- With a Rift coming up, dump the club before you get the Joker so there is no club in the middle of your stack. [9:50]
- When clearing creeps in a panic, alt cast so you do not waste the Joker. [10:21]
- Wraith ult plan: farm cards around your ult, then look for a 1v1 or ask a teammate (like Holliday with Lasso) to gank; your ult is close to a free kill when cards are stacked. [11:28]
- Consistently having clubs in the middle of your stack is hurting you; dump the club. [11:28]
- Extra Charge is not a big deal on Wraith; its spirit power is small, you mostly buy it for cooldown, and it is a crutch for inefficient card stacking. [12:31]
- He prefers maxing the 1 over the 3. [21:23]
- Use a card to hit two creeps at once when farming camps; he loves that trick and most Wraiths he coaches do not do it. [21:23]
- Wait out your ult cooldown before looking for a pick with your Joker stack. [23:58]
Lane:
- Against Viscous, parry Puddle Punch, and play around Splatter and Cube cooldowns: all his damage relies on Splatter and all his defense on Cube. [2:01]
- If you are getting bogged down by Viscous, buy Restorative Shot, but it is still about playing safe. [3:03]
- After giving up a kill, chill: get the wave piled and use it to farm instead of fighting. [3:33]
- The most important thing for a weaker scaling gun laner (not Mirage, who wants to win lane) is farming camps off spawn; leaving both nearby tier 2 camps up is really bad for soul efficiency. [4:03]
- When the enemy has Vyper's ult on you, play hyper safe; they could run at you and kill you while you stand on the stairs. [4:34]
- Restorative Shot or Extra Regen into Viscous is fine; a top player favors Restorative Shot on Wraith because it gets you closer to your gun spike. [5:07]
- Vyper could have taken you from 100 to 0 if Pocket walked up before Lash arrived, so wait for your laning partner. [6:14]
- Buy Healing Rite only if you absolutely need the HP to farm; if you have enough health for a different regen item, that is better. [6:44]
- Biggest lane issue: min-max souls and farm more outside of lane; you fought too much as a gun carry. [7:18]
- Zero boxes and a first camp at 9:20 is really bad. [7:48]
- Know when your mid is broken (map awareness) so you do not get ganked. [7:48]
Items:
- Most players like rushing gun spike on Wraith, but some E6 players rush spirit spike first; commit to one, and finish gun spike before starting spirit items if you are already deep into it. [8:18]
- Ricochet is fine instead of Tesla Bullets, but it specs you toward hyper farming and team fights, not 1v1s; it pulls you into Rift fights and gives no extra split potential, so respect that you are weak in 1v1s with it. [16:10]
- Intensifying Magazine gives a lot of weapon damage and is stepping up now that Slowing Bullets no longer overshadows it. [17:42]
Map and macro:

- Tell your team the Rift is coming; your team should not be four-manning mid with a Rift up. [9:50]
- Do not jump into the wave to parry; clear it from a safe distance. [10:21]
- Teleport out the second you hear Vindicta flying. [11:59]
- Once you know they are hunting you, ask teammates for help and come with your stack ready. [11:59]
- You were positioned way in front of your front line while the enemy routed the Urn, so they ended up behind you. [13:03]
- With only two contesting the Urn, play for denies more than the Urn steal itself, and do not stand in front of the Urn. [13:03]
- Ulting to confirm a kill on Pocket is better safe than sorry, since Pocket can Satchel out of a Walker stomp and the cloak was not seen used. [13:34]
- You needed to buy a while ago; sitting on a massive unspent spike is bad. [14:04]
- With souls unspent, no card stacks and no ult, you should chill and farm instead of fighting. [15:09]
- You could have ulted the Dynamo and killed him since your teammate was coming. [16:10]
- When a kill is possible if anyone shows up, call for help. [17:42]
- You are not playing around your build. [19:17]
- Low HP with no ult and no cards: just run the Urn. [19:52]
- Running the Urn immediately would also have given a window to force while you knew they were on the right side. [20:23]
- As a gun carry, if your team overfights and over-rotates, you just farm; with no mid boss up you do not need to be in those fights. [21:53]
- If you are only about 10% ahead of the average teammate in farm as the gun carry, that is a huge issue. [22:57]
- Look for picks in the side lane, never mid lane, where the whole enemy team can rotate in. [23:58]
- Central issue: you were poor because you fought too much; spend more time in side lanes generating souls. Everyone on the enemy team out-farming you means a gun carry gets run over. [25:00]

Summary notes:
- Farm more: farm the tier 2 camps off spawn and box more; in lane your goal is as many souls as possible when you cannot kill them. [27:40]
- Stack cards for the key spikes: the one-point card spike, the tier 2 card spike, and your ult. [28:10]
- Play off your ult's cooldown: you fought when it was down, or had it up with no card stack. [29:10]
