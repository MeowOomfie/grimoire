## Covers
Deadman Danny first-game gameplay with commentary by Eido on release day.
First impressions of his grab slam, jetpack, missiles and death explosion, plus a few item thoughts.
Commentary over a game, patch of 2026-10-14.

## Claims
- His grab slam does self-damage, and you stun yourself if you do not hit someone. [0:30]
- He seems to be a counter to flyers: a point-and-click grab that slams them to the ground. [1:09]
- He can rocket jump quite high. [1:09]
- He explodes on death. [1:40]
- He felt you should only left click; the other shots do little damage by comparison, about 50 versus 18. [2:45]
- The slam does not curve around walls; aimed into a wall it lands you in the wall. [6:28]
- He kept dying to his own damage early; later he noted he no longer had self-damage, which is good. [7:43]
- Slowing Hex fully cancels his ability, which is really bad. [9:58]
- Sharpshooter seems fine on him. [11:38]
- He wanted Warp Stone; he felt he should have gone Mystic Shot and spirit. [15:57]
- Backstab does 10% bonus damage and the hit applies a mark; he only got 18 mark damage. [17:34]
- One point in that ability is fine. [18:05]
- One left click used all his ammo. [23:35]
- Warp Stone was clutch; he liked it a lot. [25:45]
