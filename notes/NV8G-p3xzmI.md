# The Deadlock Item Guide: What’s Actually Worth Buying
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-10-17
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NV8G-p3xzmI

## Covers
Counter itemization: reading the enemy comp in the loading screen, early, mid game and luxury counter tiers, why shielding items beat Reactive Barrier, the 4.8k green rule, Counterspell vs Dispel Magic, and telling a counter-item death from a positioning death.
For players who over-buy green items or buy the wrong defensive item; examples on Wraith, Dynamo, Apollo, Rat King and Baba from Eternus games.

## Claims
- Always check the enemy team comp in the pregame lobby; it takes about 10 seconds and lets you plan counters before the game starts. [0:30]
- Example spirit-heavy comp: Calico, Infernus, Wraith, Pocket and Paige; Infernus is mostly spirit damage because of afterburn even with gun items, Wraith does most damage with her cards, Pocket is a full spirit nuker, so plan on several spirit counter items. [1:02]
- Example gun-heavy comp: Haze, McGinnis, Bebop and Drifter; McGinnis, Bebop and Drifter can pivot to spirit but always do some gun or melee damage, and you can pivot away from gun resist later if they go spirit. [1:33]
- Check early because you want to avoid the very first death, which is the highest value one; knowing the enemy was gun heavy, he built a very early Battle Vest to survive early engagements. [2:34]
- Counter items come in three tiers: early game counters (800 or 1,600 souls) bought in or right after lane, mid game counters in the 3,200 category, and late game luxury counters at 6.4k. [3:04]
- The most common early game counters are Spirit Shielding, Weapon Shielding, Reactive Barrier, Battle Vest, Enchanter's Emblem and Healbane. [3:34]
- Treat Healbane as a counter item: many heroes put out a lot of healing and without anti-heal you cannot kill them efficiently. [3:34]
- Rusted Barrel is an anti-gun item worth buying early on many tanks and supports because it upgrades into Disarming Hex, one of the strongest items to shut down one specific player. [4:05]
- Resist items give an overall damage reduction against one damage type; Spirit Shielding reduces all enemy spirit damage. [4:35]
- Bullet resist reduces both bullet and melee damage, so against a melee-heavy comp consider bullet resist as a counter too. [4:35]
- Reactive Barrier is a niche item that is not great in the current meta, misunderstood up to the highest level. [4:35]
- Reactive Barrier gives a barrier when you are stunned, chained or immobilized, which makes people think it is the top choice against Bebop's hook. [5:06]
- Worked example: at 3,000 HP, 48 spirit and 20 boons, Reactive Barrier gives roughly 411 barrier (it scales with spirit), Spirit Shielding gives a little less (it scales with boons) but also grants 18% spirit resist when it triggers. [5:06]
- After a Bebop hook you usually get meleed and sticky bombed, so Spirit or Weapon Shielding triggers soon after Reactive Barrier would, and then its resist applies. [5:39]
- With damage split 50% bullet and 50% spirit, Spirit Shielding provides more effective HP than Reactive Barrier because of the resistance. [6:09]
- It does not matter whether 400 HP of barrier comes at the start of the fight or 2 seconds later after the sticky bomb; you lose the same HP, but shielding also gives resist. [6:40]
- Reactive Barrier's one big upside is upgrading into Indomitable, so buying it early is fine if you plan to buy Indomitable. [7:10]
- Reactive Barrier is also better on a hero with very high spirit such as Baba, since it scales with spirit while the shielding items only scale with boons. [7:10]
- For most heroes trying to stay alive early, shielding items are better: Spirit Shielding against a spirit-heavy comp, Weapon Shielding against a gun-heavy comp. [7:41]
- If you are already fairly survivable but want a green spike, buy Battle Vest or Enchanter's Emblem instead; they are the aggressive versions of the shielding items because they also add damage. [7:41]
- Rule two: do not overinvest in counter items, especially before your power spikes; carries like Wraith, Seven or Venator must deal enough damage first. [8:12]
- A common mistake is buying too many greens after getting ganked while farming in the mid game, then being unable to deal damage. [8:42]
- On Wraith he makes sure to get his farming item, Surge of Power and Tesla Bullets first; without them he does no damage to heroes or creeps, which slows his whole progression. [8:42]
- Buying more greens or a Dispel Magic after Tesla Bullets on Wraith leaves you doing basically zero damage at 14k, slowing farm and power spikes. [9:13]
- A power spike is the item or items that let you start fighting and doing your job; on carries it is usually your first 6k item, on Wraith a 6k item he names "murial" (caption). [9:45]
- Never buy more than 4.8k in green investment before your first big power spike. [9:45]
- Dynamo support (Piggy's build) goes to the 4.8k green spike, then the big items that let Dynamo do his job, which is landing a big Black Hole in a team fight. [10:15]
- Piggy's "big three" for Dynamo are items that stop enemies cancelling your Black Hole (stun immunity) and Refresher so you can double Black Hole; never buy two more green items before them. [10:15]
- Apollo is a spirit nuker who becomes strong very early; meta builds (ABL's) skip extra greens after the 4.8k investment and keep buying damage, because killing is worth more than staying alive without dealing damage. [10:45]
- Low-level trap: die to spirit, buy Enchanter's Emblem; die to Haze, buy Metal Skin; get stunned by Lash, buy Counterspell; at 30k to 34k you have no damage. [11:16]
- If your mid or late game investment is mostly vitality, you are probably doing it wrong. [11:46]
- Exception: tanks like Abrams whose goal is to stay on the front line and disrupt can spam green items. [11:46]
- Rule of thumb: get your 4.8k green spike, then consider only one extra counter item. [11:46]
- The two most commonly bought mid game counters are Counterspell and Dispel Magic. [12:16]
- Counterspell is probably the most universal counter: it negates any big stun or big damage source you can react to, such as Infernus's ult explosion, Lash's ult or Yamato's slash. [12:16]
- Dispel Magic is a tool against damage over time abilities like Infernus burn, Shiv knives and Haze Fixation. [12:46]
- Buy Dispel Magic over Counterspell on movement heroes like Apollo (three of four abilities are movement) when the enemy buys Slowing Hex, because you cannot react to a Slowing Hex with Counterspell. [13:18]
- Buy Dispel Magic when your hero has an escape or movement ability essential to the kit and the enemy has movement slows or damage over time buildup like Shiv knives. [13:48]
- You will die in Deadlock; if you counter everything with greens you will never kill the enemy and still die. [14:18]
- After a death ask whether you died for lack of resist or counters or because you were out of position; most of the time you do not need Metal Skin. [14:49]
- Rat King example, 10k behind with Spirit Shielding but no bullet resist, pushing mid with no map info, killed by a flying Vindicta with gun damage; Metal Skin might have saved him, but buying it then would have delayed all his damage and farm. [15:20]
- Vindicta is a great carry against Rat King. [15:20]
- A lot of deaths are avoided by playing better, not by counter items; do not over-spam counter items. [15:50]
- The further behind you are, the less you should buy counter items, because the richer enemy already out-damages you and you need damage to get back in. [16:20]
- Over-spamming greens against a fed Haze or fed Victor probably loses the game; instead buy a disable or damage and catch him off guard with multiple people or punish his positioning. [16:20]
- The further ahead you are, the more you should think about staying alive, because enemy kills on you give them comeback gold. [16:51]
- Baba is great at countering spirit: she steals enemy spirit power to gain spirit resist and lives through her abilities (spamming flask and Granny Long Legs to get in and out). [17:22]
- Focus Lens is the big counter item against Baba; a Calico with Focus Lens caught him and he died instantly because he did not track her buy. [17:53]
- The big counter to your hero varies: Slowing Hex against movement heroes, Unstoppable in the late game against stun-heavy heroes. [18:25]
- Track enemy items through the game; he bought Dispel Magic right after that death, and having it instead of Spirit Snatch earlier would likely have won the mid boss fight. [18:55]
- Save Dispel Magic for the one thing you know can kill you (here the Focus Lens); doing so won him a kill and a much better trade later. [19:26]
