# The Best Deadlock Hero for YOU (Based on What You Play)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-01-28
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m5_o7MhK2CA

## Covers
Starter hero picks for new players by the game and role they came from (shooter DPS, hero shooter support and tank, MOBA carry, roaming support and mid), with a short kit summary for each.
January 2026, just after Street Brawl and the new heroes arrived. Heroes: Haze, Paige, Kelvin, Abrams, Mo & Krill, Seven, Victor, Dynamo, Paradox, Holliday, Pocket.

## Claims
- The newest heroes are fairly complex, advanced characters; Rem is one of the easier new ones, but there are easier heroes to start with. [0:30] [1:00]
- To try a new hero without risk, play against bots or play Street Brawl, which is unranked, so you can try any hero instantly. [1:00]
- Coming from shooters (Call of Duty, or DPS in Overwatch or Marvel Rivals): play Haze. [1:30]
- Haze has few abilities to manage: Fixation is a passive that stacks bullet damage the longer you shoot a target, her ultimate is an AoE around her with no aiming, plus invisibility and a sleep dart. [1:30] [2:01]
- Haze does an insane amount of gun damage, so a player with cracked aim performs well almost instantly; headshots double the Fixation stacks applied. [2:01]
- If you are new, the top builds on the build page are usually all solid; pick a streamer's build and try a few. [2:32]
- Metro started on Haze when he came from Overwatch. [2:32]
- Coming from hero shooter support: there is no true full healer in Deadlock, but Paige is the closest to a classic healer. [3:02] [4:04]
- Paige's main ability, taken first in almost every build, gives an ally a barrier (extra HP) and buffs their damage and fire rate. [3:33]
- Paige also has an area denial ability that deals heavy AoE damage, an AoE lockdown that stops enemies moving toward you, and a map-wide ultimate charge that stuns enemies and heals allies in its line. [3:33] [4:04]
- For a more proactive support who still heals a lot: Kelvin. Frozen Shelter is one of the strongest ultimates in the game, arguably top two; it heals teammates inside and slows enemies. [4:35]
- Kelvin's Frost Grenade damages enemies and, once leveled, heals allies, and it works on charges so you can throw several; Ice Path gets him around the map and is not hard to use; his beam slows enemies. [4:35] [5:06]
- Coming from tank (for example Reinhardt): play Abrams, probably the face of the game; simple to start, very high skill ceiling. [5:06] [5:37]
- Abrams has a tanky passive, a Shoulder Charge that can pin an enemy into a wall for a follow-up heavy melee or gunfire, an aura that drains enemies and heals him, and an ultimate where he leaps up and lands in an AoE stun. [5:37] [6:07]
- An even simpler tank is Mo & Krill: Scorn damages everyone around you and heals more the more enemies it hits, Sand Blast disarms enemies, Burrow makes him very tanky, and his ultimate locks one enemy in place, stunned, for your team. [6:07] [6:39]
- Coming from MOBA carry (safe lane, bot lane): play Seven. He starts weak, his abilities have very strong max-level upgrades, and he scales into a late game monster that can fight 1v6. [7:10] [7:41]
- Seven's kit: Power Surge does AoE damage, an AoE stun, a lightning ball that damages anyone standing in it, and a Storm Cloud ultimate that zaps everyone below. He is one of the easiest heroes in the game, like Anti-Mage or Terrorblade in Dota, but still needs a fair amount of aim. [7:41] [8:12] [8:42]
- A carry for players with no aim: Victor. His gun is not what makes him strong; Aura of Suffering is, which damages enemies around him and himself, while his other abilities heal him back. [8:42] [9:12]
- Victor's Pain Battery is easy to hit and, at max level, gives health back; Jumpstart heals him and removes debuffs; his ultimate is a passive that brings him back to life when he dies. [9:12] [9:43]
- Coming from MOBA roaming support (position 4 or 5): play Dynamo. His ultimate is like Enigma's in Dota, a black hole that full-stuns everyone in a big area for several seconds, which Metro calls the best ultimate in the game. [9:43] [10:17]
- Dynamo can teleport himself or teammates to a new point on the map, which also dodges stuns and spells; Kinetic Pulse knocks enemies up and lowers their fire rate; he also has an AoE healing aura. [10:47]
- Dynamo does not need much farm; wanted items are Refresher for a double black hole later and Unstoppable, which works like BKB in Dota. He needs little aim. [11:17] [11:47]
- Deadlock has no classic support role and no defined roles yet. [11:17]
- Coming from MOBA mid (mage, caster, disruptor): play Paradox, who has a lot of utility and a really good gun. [11:47] [12:18]
- Paradox's Kinetic Carbine charged shot slows enemies to a halt; Time Wall stops enemy projectiles passing through it and acts like an invulnerability shield while you A-D strafe in it. [12:18] [12:49]
- Paradox's Pulse Grenade pushes waves, farms camps and debuffs enemies, dealing more damage the more debuffs are on them; her ultimate swaps places with an enemy, like Vengeful Spirit in Dota, so you swap an enemy into your team and then place a Time Wall. [12:49] [13:19]
- Another hero for mid players is Holliday, a very similar design. [13:19]
- From any other game: try every hero, using bots or Street Brawl if you are worried about real players or losing rating. [14:20]
- Metro's own pool is gun heroes (Haze, Seven, Wraith, Warden); many heroes can build full gun and rely on aim and positioning. [14:20] [14:50]
- Pocket plays like a spellcaster that does not need great aim, close to Puck in Dota: he teleports around, dodges spells and gets in and out. [14:50]
- Do not be discouraged by heroes that look hard on paper; play what you enjoy, which he calls the easiest way to climb. [15:20]
