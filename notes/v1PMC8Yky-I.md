# The Worst and Best Items - NEW Deadlock Patch
channel: Natan
date: 2025-07-05
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1PMC8Yky-I

## Covers
Item winners and losers from the early July 2025 patch (the one that split cooldown reduction into ability and item cooldown), from an Eternus 6 coach.
For players adjusting builds after that patch: Transcendent Cooldown, Cultist Sacrifice, Fortitude, Counterspell, the shielding items and Slowing Hex.

## Claims
- New item Transcendent Cooldown is basically what Superior Cooldown used to be: cooldown reduction is now split into ability cooldown reduction and item cooldown reduction. [0:00]
- Enchanter's Emblem now gives ability cooldown reduction and no longer reduces item cooldowns. [0:30]
- Compressed Cooldown works the same as before: 22% ability cooldown reduction on the ability you imbue. [0:30]
- Superior Cooldown no longer reduces item cooldowns, so item cooldown reduction costs another 3,200 souls; item cooldowns were effectively nerfed across the board. [0:30]
- You need a good reason to buy Transcendent Cooldown: Pocket, who builds four active items, still wants it, but it takes longer to reach. Unless you have three or four active items or items like Mystic Shot that benefit from item cooldown reduction, avoid it. [1:01]
- Cultist Sacrifice looks buffed (active cooldown 260 to 250) but is nerfed significantly because the 3,200 cooldown item no longer reduces item cooldowns. [1:01]
- Cultist Sacrifice has about 90 seconds of default downtime after it runs out, during which it only gives some out of combat regen, basically a glorified Monster Rounds. [1:31]
- Cultist Sacrifice is no longer worth buying early; if you still buy it, get Transcendent Cooldown soon after. In general it is not a great item anymore. [2:02]
- Arcane Surge does not add duration to Cultist Sacrifice (tested); Arcane Surge gives ability range and duration and changes nothing about items. [2:02]
- Loser: Fortitude lost its weapon damage (it used to give 27% weapon damage above 75% health) and now only gives the movement speed; its regen was nerfed from 3% max health to 2%. [2:33]
- Fortitude is kind of dead; there is no real reason to buy it. [3:03]
- Test: healing from 400 health to full took about 45 to 50 seconds with Fortitude, while going back to base took about 35 seconds, 10 to 15 seconds faster. [3:03]
- Instead of Fortitude rely on out of combat healing from items like Monster Rounds, Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem. [3:03]
- Healing Rite is still an amazing item: a flat 300 heal scaling with spirit power and 2 sprint speed while active. [3:33]
- Worst case when solo and low, just go back to base, which is faster, especially with a zipline boost. [3:33]
- Going back to base on the zipline objectively beats buying an item like Fortitude even though it feels worse. [4:04]
- Winner: Counterspell got 100 bonus health and 8 spirit power, and now heals 150 when you successfully counter a spell; the cooldown buff roughly cancels out with the item cooldown changes. [4:04]
- Counterspell has a 1 second spell parry window; any spell that hits you in it grants the buffs: 150 heal, 20 spirit power and 2 movement speed for 6 seconds. [4:35]
- Counterspell is probably not a must buy on every hero every game, but expect to see it a lot more. [4:35]
- Winner: the shielding items, especially Spirit Shielding, keep getting buffed every patch; they now give movement speed, the movement speed got buffed again, and the cooldown got buffed again. [5:05]
- Spirit Shielding is very easy for enemies to proc continuously: a Yamato slash, two Wraith cards, Pocket, Seven, Shiv dashing through you, Sinclair's bolts, Holliday's single barrel, a single Grey Talon arrow, Calico's Gloom Bombs or Cold Front with Spirit Strike or her ult, or a single Geist bomb. [5:05]
- Despite the name, Spirit Shielding's 400 barrier is universal: it works against weapon and spirit damage, and it benefits from resist (for example from Enchanter's Emblem). [5:36]
- Spirit Shielding's barrier has 1.1 spirit scaling, so it gets better on heroes that buy spirit items. [6:06]
- Weapon Shielding is decent, but Spirit Shielding is much easier for every hero to proc; even Dynamo or Kelvin will proc Spirit Shielding but struggle to proc Weapon Shielding. [6:06]
- Both shielding items give out of combat regen, which is nice in the mid game. [6:06]
- Bonus pick: Slowing Hex is underbuilt though really good; it got a small buff of 0.5 sprint speed. [6:36]
- Slowing Hex reduces dash distance, slows, and silences movement abilities and movement items, for example Warp Stone. [6:36]
- Slowing Hex stops Infernus's Flame Dash, Calico's ult and dash, Abrams's Shoulder Charge, Vindicta's flight (little effect if she is already airborne), Wraith's teleport, Mirage's Tornado, Lash's Grapple and Shiv's ult and dash. [6:36]
- Slowing Hex is a component of Vortex Web, which was also buffed with a slightly larger capture radius. [7:06]
- Slowing Hex and Vortex Web are good on anyone but situational; support players especially should consider them. [7:06]
