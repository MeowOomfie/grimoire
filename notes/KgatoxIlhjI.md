## Covers
Five free settings and mods that give an edge without practice: the Topbar Plus mod, the console command for the newer health bars, instant cast for abilities and items, dash on an alternate key for instant air dashes, and auto queue / auto buy at the shop.
For any player who has not set up their client yet.

## Claims
- The Topbar Plus mod shows your soul advantage or disadvantage against the enemy team at all points in the game. [0:00]
- Comeback Urn starts when a team is behind 10% or more souls, except the first Urn where it is 15%; with Topbar Plus you can glance at the top of the screen before picking up the Urn to tell whether it is comeback, even or forward. [0:31]
- With Topbar Plus, pressing Tab shows every build on every character, souls per minute per character, and unspent souls. [1:02]
- An enemy with a lot of unspent souls is likely weaker than they look on paper, or may have unsecured souls you can take if you kill them. [1:02]
- Topbar Plus also shows each character's skill level ups, resistances, maximum health and objective damage. [1:02]
- In a fight Topbar Plus greys out enemy characters that are not showing, so you can tell how many are missing (for example before pushing a Walker) without looking at the minimap. [1:33]
- Topbar Plus gives timers for Mid Boss, Urns and buffs, and a countdown of the seconds left on your Mid Boss buff, which makes the push base or farm jungle decision easier. [1:33]
- Every mod in the game is legal right now, though Valve might disallow them later; more info is better than less. [2:05]
- The easiest way to install it is the Deadlock Mod Manager, a separate program; setup takes about 2 minutes. [2:05]
- The newer health bars are in game and enabled with a console command; they take effect the next game you load. [2:36]
- The new health bars show whether an enemy or ally has their ultimate: if the small icon on the left is lit, they have it and you should be careful; if not, you can engage. [2:36]
- You can still read overall enemy HP from the new bars by looking at them. [3:07]
- To keep the new health bars on permanently, put the command in Deadlock's Steam launch options (right click, Properties, Launch Options). [3:07]
- Turn on instant cast for abilities on almost every character; ability default needs a second click to confirm, which costs seconds you could spend shooting or using the next ability. [3:37]
- Very few abilities are better on ability default; once you know where an ability goes you do not need the cone indicator. [4:08]
- Set items to instant cast too: aim at a teammate to cast Rescue Beam instantly, and self-cast items are one button press. [4:08]
- Quick cast is the middle ground: hold the button and it casts on release on the target you aimed at, useful if instant cast makes you hit the wrong target (for example a Focus Lens on another enemy). [4:38]
- Keep essential escape tools that can bug out on a non instant setting, for example Wraith's teleport, where a bad placement can get you stuck in a wall and killed. [5:08]
- Bind dash to an alternate key, usually V, to perform instant air dashes, which are faster than a normal dash. [5:38]
- An instant air dash is pressing Spacebar and then immediately dash; with V just above the Spacebar you roll your thumb from Spacebar onto V, which makes it consistent and is not a macro. [5:38]
- Many pros on stream use this binding for their impressive movement. [6:39]
- When chaining instant air dashes, go 45 degrees to the side and alternate left and right instead of going straight forward to keep velocity. [6:39]
- Enable auto queue and auto buy at the start of the game; once enabled it stays on, and walking to the shop buys your queued items one by one if you have the souls. [7:39]
- Queue items manually rather than the whole build, since you sometimes need Extra Regen or situational items. [7:39]
- Auto buy is useful when chased with unsecured souls: run to a shop such as the secret shop with items queued and get the buys off before you die. [8:09]
