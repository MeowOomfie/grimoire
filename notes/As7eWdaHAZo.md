## Covers
Part 1 of a four part counter guide (September 2025), heroes A to G: Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Calico, The Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo and Grey Talon. Each hero gets counter tech, counter items, and the key lane ability whose cooldown to track. For any player who wants to know how to play against these heroes.

## Claims
### General rules
- Track key cooldowns in waves: waves come every 30 seconds, so a 40 second cooldown means the hero is punishable until a little after the same point in the next wave. [0:00]
- Rebuttal lowers your parry cooldown, gives a buff and heals you when you parry, and reflects damage; it is good into any hero or player who melees a lot. [0:30]
- Bullet resilience and spirit resilience are always good into their damage types, but bullet resilience is slightly worse because Armor Piercing Rounds cuts its value by about 50%. [1:01]
- Curse counters every hero; buy it if one enemy is running the lobby or trying to 1v9, great on supports. [1:31]
- Anti-heal choice depends on your own hero; any of the anti-heal items works when anti-heal is called for. [1:31]
- Close Quarters gives 20% melee resist, good into melee heroes. [2:02]

### Abrams
- Space him: he has a shotgun and does much less damage at range, and wants to be in your face to melee. [2:32]
- Use verticality: double jump his charge and you stay above him. [2:32]
- Before two points in Shoulder Charge (longer wall stun) he can only guarantee a light melee off it, so a heavy melee off charge before then is a free parry. [3:02]
- Reactive Barrier is great into him, especially on spirit heroes: he cannot kill you without dashing and proccing it. [3:02]
- Close Quarters (upgrade to Point Blank for more melee resist) is a good alternative to Rebuttal if you are meleed a lot. [3:32]
- Any anti-heal is very good; he loves to heal. [3:32]
- Slowing Hex completely denies his charge, so hex him when he is about to charge so you can space. [3:32]
- With 40% or more debuff reduction (Debuff Reducer, Debuff Reducer plus Weighted Shots, Weighted Shots plus Spellbreaker, Spellbreaker plus Debuff Reducer, or Blood Tribute) you can parry his heavy melee off a charge unless he has duration extension. [4:02]
- The same 40% debuff reduction means he cannot heavy melee you off his ult unless he has duration extension on it, so look for the parry. [4:02]
- Key ability: Shoulder Charge, 37 second cooldown in lane, one wave and a bit; after he uses or misses it he is much less threatening until the same point next wave. Maxed it drops toward 15 seconds. [4:33]
- 99% of mid to low elo Abrams put two points in charge first; in higher elo, when winning lane, some put one or two points in the passive for sustain. [5:03]

### Bebop
- Always assume he has hook: never stand in the open, stay next to cover and tuck when he peeks out or jumps looking for a hook. [5:03]
- If hooked and he turns to pull you into tower range, punch in the direction you want to go to cancel the momentum; after an uppercut, punch to stop being displaced. [5:33]
- Reactive Barrier is good into him: he usually needs to hook you to kill you and it pops on each hook. [6:04]
- Debuff Remover cleanses his bombs, and Counterspell at the last second blocks all their damage. [6:04]
- Knockdown on him when he ults cancels the ult and knocks him out of the air, stunning him. [6:34]
- Key ability: hook, 23 second cooldown, a little less than a wave; without hook he is much less scary and you can farm in peace. [6:34]
- Space him: he starts with only two stamina; if he rolls in for a bomb stack and rolls out, he has no stamina and no bomb (18 seconds, 10 when leveled), so punish him hard then. [7:05]

### Billy
- Do not let him sit in your face; he wants to threaten light melees and his SMG does a ton of damage at close range even without a gun build. [7:35]
- When ulted, break line of sight to break the chain; get into an enclosed space with a wall between you so if you are tugged you hit the wall. [7:35]
- Reactive Barrier triggers on his 1 if he pulls you out of the air, but not if he uses it while you are on the ground. [8:06]
- Counterspell just before his ult ends stops you being tugged out of position. [8:36]
- Close Quarters for melee resist; Rebuttal matters more on Billy than any other melee hero because he loves light melees. [8:36]
- Anti-heal denies the big healing he gets from Blasted when he melees your teammates. [9:06]
- Key ability: his 2, Rising Ram, 30 second cooldown, exactly one wave, halved if it hits someone (most Billys level it early). If it misses he cannot get on you or run away for a whole wave. [9:06]

### Calico
- In lane, hard shove waves into her tower so she never has time to go cat form and farm boxes, denying her a soul lead. [9:37]
- Close Quarters melee resist cuts her 2, which does melee damage. [10:07]
- Counterspell is great into her: it denies her bombs and Leaping Slash, and her burst combo is easy to predict. [10:07]
- Slowing Hex completely denies her 2, so you can space her or stop her escaping. [10:07]
- Ava matters mostly for box farming; in team fights know whether she has cat up to slip away or split. [10:38]
- Key lane ability: Leaping Slash, 11 second cooldown; when it is down she cannot use it to escape or hit you. [10:38]

### The Doorman
- Jump or change your momentum to dodge his cart; from range he must predict where you go. [11:09]
- When he ults someone, CC him out of it immediately if you have quick CC; before two points in his ult (most only after maxing cart and Doorway) he is not unstoppable. [11:09]
- Reactive Barrier triggers both on a cart hit and on his ult. [11:39]
- Counterspell the second he ults to deny all its damage, and you leave the ult inside your Counterspell. [11:39]
- Debuff reduction is very important against an Echo Shard Doorman to escape chained carts. [12:10]
- Mobility (boots, Warp Stone, stamina items like Arcane Surge, Kinetic Dash, Stamina Mastery) gets you through his ult and back to your team faster. [12:10]
- Key lane ability: Doorway, 40 second cooldown; know when it is up so he cannot poke you. [12:41]
- Once his cart is maxed (most rush it early mid game after points in the bell) the key ability becomes Luggage Cart, 30 seconds or about 23 with cooldown items; if he misses it he is in a bad spot, as his ult is then his only self-heal. [12:41]

### Drifter
- Space him in lane: the claw marks in front of him do a bonus 40 damage, while at the edge it only does 60; stay out of melee range so he cannot keep landing the inner claws. [13:12]
- Against his ult, use your mic: if ulted, call it so your team tells you where they are; if not ulted, tell teammates which way the team is. [13:43]
- Debuff Remover right as Stalker's Mark hits cleanses its current health bleed and stops him recasting the teleport behind you. [14:13]
- Divine Barrier cleanses his ult; supports should throw it on ulted teammates. [14:13]
- Disarming Hex is very good when he jumps or teleports on you for a quick burst. [14:43]
- Key lane ability: Stalker's Mark, his 2, 20 second cooldown; without it he loses much of his mobility and survivability. [15:14]

### Dynamo
- Space from your teammates so he cannot hit both of you, in lane and later; do not group in choke points and do not clump at mid into his ult. [15:45]
- If he buys Refresher, track that cooldown too. [15:45]
- Double jump his kick; it is telegraphed, especially when he rolls at you. Double jumping it is a reason Dynamo is falling out of the meta. [15:45]
- Slowing Hex denies his 2, so you can land Bebop bombs, Lash ult or Mirage mark that he would otherwise dodge. [16:46]
- Spellbreaker is great into the full kick build, denying his first kick and his long range poke. [17:16]
- Warp Stone gets you away from his ult and out of chained kicks once he has Rapid Recharge. [17:16]
- Supports can Rescue Beam people out of his ult. [17:16]
- Knockdown on him as he starts ulting stuns him out after about 2 more seconds, most useful against duration extension builds (Arcane Surge, Superior Duration) with the 3.5 second channel. [17:46]
- Key ability: Quantum Entanglement, his 2, 16 second cooldown; while it is down he is very vulnerable and jumpable. [18:16]

### Grey Talon
- Wait out his traps: they only last 20 seconds. Bait them only in a choke point and only when he cannot see you or the trap. [18:46]
- Spellbreaker is amazing into him: it blocks much of his arrow damage and his owl, and cuts the on-hit of Spirit Burn and Tankbuster by 75%; before the owl is maxed you basically cannot die to it. [19:16]
- Knockdown takes him out of the air; staggering multiple Knockdowns beats his Counterspell. It can also stop his ult so the bird does not reach a teammate. [19:46]
- Counterspell can block his owl and, with practice, his arrows; at higher levels players do flybys to bait it, so expect more flybys after one. [20:17]
