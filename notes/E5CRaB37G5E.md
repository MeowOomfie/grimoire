# Why You Should STOP HOLDING W | Deadlock Movement Essentials Guide
channel: Natan
date: 2026-01-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E5CRaB37G5E

## Covers
A zero to hero movement guide from an Eternus 6 player, for beginners through players with a few hundred hours: sprint and move speed, sliding, stamina, dash cancels, dash jumps, mantle slides, super glides, wall jumps, air strafing without W, diagonal air dashes, instant air dashes, edge boosts, the one-stamina mini dash jump and corner boosts.

## Claims
- Every hero has a base movement speed; Calico's is 7.2. [0:31]
- A few seconds after leaving combat your hero starts to sprint, which increases your speed. [0:31]
- Sprint Boots adds 2 sprint speed, which you lose in combat; its upgrade Enduring Speed adds 2 move speed, which you keep in combat. [0:31]
- Moving faster than 8.9 meters per second lets you crouch to slide while running. [0:31]
- Shooting while sliding consumes no ammo. [1:01]
- You can slide on any ramp or stairs. [1:01]
- In lane, combine sliding with the free ammo to poke enemies under their Guardian. [1:01]
- Double jump, ground dash and air dash each cost one stamina bar. [1:01]
- Each time you leave the ground you get one air dash and one double jump, and must touch the ground again to get them back. [1:32]
- Double tap crouch in the air to dash downwards; it costs only half a stamina bar and is useful on jump pads to stay closer to the ground. [1:32]
- Heroes start with two, three or four stamina bars; items give extra bars or faster stamina regen. [1:32]
- A dash makes you fast enough to slide, so slide almost every time you dash. [1:32]
- Cancel a dash early by sliding to sidestep abilities, get behind cover quickly, or close distance for a quick melee or parry. [2:02]
- Dashing while holding M1 interrupts your firing, and canceling the dash with a slide does not shorten that delay. [2:02]
- Work around the firing delay by releasing M1 briefly before the dash and pressing it again once you are sliding. [2:02]
- The stamina bar turns blue briefly during a dash; jump in that window to dash jump, a big speed boost that costs two stamina. The window is generous. [2:33]
- You are fast enough to slide right after a dash jump. [2:33]
- Slide bunny hop: cancel the slide with a jump, then slide again. [2:33]
- Mantle by jumping and holding W and space; hold crouch while mantling to do a mantle slide right after. [2:33]
- Jump right after a mantle slide to do a super glide, which leaves you fast enough to slide or slide bunny hop after. [3:03]
- Wall jump by pressing jump while in the air near a wall, whichever way you face. [3:03]
- Your first wall jump in the air currently costs no stamina, so use wall jumps to gain height and distance around the map for free. [3:03]
- You can glide along walls and jump at any point during the glide to wall jump. [3:03]
- Wall jumps carry momentum, so a dash jump before a wall jump sends you much further. [3:33]
- To climb, a forward wall jump then mantle is more efficient than a double jump then mantle, since it uses only one stamina. [3:33]
- Wall jumps let you avoid mantling, so you don't lock yourself in the mantle animation and you move less predictably. [3:33]
- If you accidentally mantle when trying to wall jump off something small or a low ledge, stop holding W and/or look away from the surface. [4:04]
- A few spots on the map allow a mantle slide into a wall jump. [4:04]
- Heavy melee charges you forward like a dash; use it as a last resort to get around a corner when out of stamina, or to reach further without spending stamina. [4:04]
- Air strafing: in the air hold only A or D (not W) and move the mouse slowly in that direction, A with mouse left, D with mouse right. [5:04]
- Stop holding W almost always while in the air; you already carry forward momentum, and releasing W gives you far more control. [5:04]
- Alternate air strafing left and right to work around obstacles or go further. [5:34]
- Crouch off ziplines instead of jumping off to keep your momentum. [5:34]
- Dash diagonally in the air instead of straight forward: forward gave a velocity of 732, diagonal almost 900. [5:34]
- Instant air dash: press jump then dash in almost instant succession; you are fast enough to slide after, and diagonal is a little faster than forward. [6:04]
- Unlike ground dashes, you are actionable during instant air dashes and can melee, parry or shoot; release M1 at the start to get around the short shooting delay. [6:36]
- Well timed, you can chain instant air dashes and slides together while shooting. [6:36]
- Edge boost: wall jump parallel to any corner right as your hero is about to pass it; more speed going in gives a bigger boost. [6:36]
- Edge boosts need precise air strafing and the timing window is small, so the tech is difficult. [7:06]
- In a few spots you can combine an edge boost with a mantle slide. [7:06]
- Single stamina mini dash jump: bind crouch to scroll wheel up or down, then dash jump but scroll during the dash before you jump. [7:06]
- If you scroll after jumping, or are still scrolling during the jump, you dash straight back down to the ground. [7:36]
- The mini dash jump is a good setup for edge boosts, especially off a door inside a building. [7:36]
- You can quick melee while holding W and pressing space to climb over fences; not faster than jumping or the mini dash jump, but a good setup for corner boosts. [7:36]
- Corner boost: wall jump right as you touch the corner of anything for a vertical boost; very tricky and very niche. [8:06]
- To learn movement, practice 10 to 20 minutes a day alone on the map and watch good players to copy the tech they use in each area. [8:37]
- Learn one tech at a time, for example day one slide on every slope, day two mantle slide every time you mantle, so you can still focus on winning games. [8:37]
- The Deadlock Movement Wiki has more on movement. [9:08]
