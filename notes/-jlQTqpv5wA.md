# Pocket is very strong, you're just using him wrong
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-03-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jlQTqpv5wA

## Covers
Pocket played and commented by aquatiq, who explains each ability during the game and gives item and farming reasoning.
Commentary over a game with light guide content, no coaching. Patch around 2026-03-11.

## Claims
- Flying Cloak (1) is a cloak that does damage when it hits people, and you can teleport to it. [0:31]
- The cloak only travels in the direction you are looking. [0:31]
- He uses the cloak for wave clear more than anything. [1:03]
- Enchanter's Satchel (3) lets you escape into your suitcase and avoid pretty much any ability used on you; when the duration ends it deals spirit damage to nearby enemies. [3:37]
- You can end the satchel early by pressing 3 again, which deals the damage instantly. [3:37]
- Barrage (2) sends projectiles that deal spirit damage, apply a slow and briefly amplify all damage you deal to the target, including gun damage. [4:08]
- You can be airborne with Barrage, so it doubles as a movement ability, like the cloak. [4:39]
- Put Barrage on the main threat; he put it on Mo when he should have put it on Drifter. [3:37]
- He used to think Barrage was trash, but it is really good: the damage amp makes you do a lot of damage. [9:46]
- Affliction (ult) poisons enemies so they take tick damage slowly, and it is really strong. [6:40]
- Affliction is on about a 70 second cooldown. [21:45]
- Afflict an enemy who might teleport away so a teammate can catch him. [20:15]
- Early sustain: Restorative Shot, and Healing Rite to back to full. [1:33]
- Minions and camps do not interrupt Healing Rite, so you can farm while it is active. [6:10]
- Pocket starts with minus 15% spirit resist, so you need Spirit Resilience almost every game. [8:43]
- Drifter with melee lifesteal heals off his Rend because Rend is melee damage, which makes him hard to trade with. [2:34]
- Drifter's Lifestrike is good mainly as a healing item. [9:46]
- A Drifter can kill Pocket if he isolates him, but a poorer Drifter cannot do enough damage. [9:46]
- Pocket is strong off spawn but you have to work for it and get your money up; his damage is not free. [10:16]
- Drifter, Calico and Seven are good right off spawn; Wraith, Infernus and Haze need money to get anywhere. [10:46]
- Clearing the wave can matter more than killing an enemy (Kelvin here). [11:19]
- Pocket's farm is solid; he prefers it to sitting in lane for 25 minutes on Wraith. [11:19]
- Matchmaking tip: players get high priority on heroes by picking their favorite plus the heroes with high pick rates. [12:20]
- Mystic Expansion gives abilities a lot of range; he wants it on the ult. [14:25]
- He shoved the wave while the enemy team was grouped elsewhere, calling it really good at that moment. [14:56]
- Swift Striker, bought by the build he followed, is a really good item on Pocket, especially for taking mid boss. [15:26]
- He keeps Mystic Burst and buys Greater Expansion next. [15:59]
- He skips Cheat Death. [15:59]
- An enemy ult (not named) can no longer be counterspelled, and Pocket can no longer dodge it in his suitcase. [16:34]
- Side laning the whole game earns a lot of money, though it is boring. [17:05]
- Cold Front is basically his fifth ability. [18:39]
- Do not let two of you die to a lost fight; leave rather than stagger deaths. [21:45]
- Warp Stone is needed on Pocket. [24:48]
- Pocket's wave clear is very good; use it to push lanes up to the enemy base. [24:48]
- He tries to damage objectives when teammates are not. [28:57]
