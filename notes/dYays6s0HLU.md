## Covers
Midknight Vods coaching stream from 1/12/26, recorded while waiting for the next hero patch. He first reviews two Calico games with the streamer Skeleton Honey (Eternus lobbies), then free sessions on a Victor (Phantom) and a Shiv (Arcanist 4), with quick build reviews for Paradox and Victor in between.
Themes: when to gank and how to come back from a lost lane, box routing, Calico point order (Ava max versus Gloom Bombs), Counterspell and Curse as situational buys, perma fighting mid while behind, playing into Dynamo's ult, Victor's self-damage and Extra Regen, Infuser versus Escalating Exposure, "at best" side-lane fights, overstaying in a broken lane, Shiv right-click combos, defending base from height, and movement tech for Doorman and vents.
