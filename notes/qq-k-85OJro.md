## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream from 12/11/25, four replay reviews: a Mina (Ascendant) laning with Warden into Victor and Calico, a Vindicta (Ascendant) into Infernus and Viscous, a Mina (Phantom) laning with Holliday into Victor and Haze, and a Lash (Emissary) on Deathy's build.
Themes: playing around Mina's 2, target priority, invading instead of brawling, splitting while your team takes bad fights, Vindicta positioning and flicking, support Vindicta pivot, Lash air play, urn and mid boss calls.
Side notes: a Spirit Holliday build review, anti-heal into Victor, Sinclair stealing Mina's ult, a mid jump boost test, and his views on wall bounce changes, Refresher and Trophy Collector.
