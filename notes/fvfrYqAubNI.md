# Best SOLO CARRIES in Valve's Deadlock - Tier List
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-08-27
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fvfrYqAubNI

## Covers
A solo queue tier list (S, A, B, C, D) ranking every hero of the August 2024 roster by how well it can take over an uncoordinated game alone. This is a very early build of the game (2024, original 20-hero roster) and much has changed since.
For lower MMR players who want a hero to climb with when teammates do their own thing.

## Claims
- Most high MMR tier lists assume a fairly coordinated team; in lower MMR games people are uncoordinated, so this list ranks which hero is best to take over a game by yourself. [0:31]
- Tiers: S and A can take over a game alone (A viable but not the absolute best), B can solo carry if you are really good but is not recommended, C and D struggle the most to solo carry. [1:01]
- Shiv is S tier and without a doubt the best solo carry in the game, partly from his design and partly because he is very broken right now. [1:34]
- Shiv is one of the few heroes who can 1v6 team fights; once he has Leech, Siphon Bullets or Unstoppable he can kill everyone with little counterplay, and he has a fairly high skill cap. [2:04]
- Lash is S tier, second best; the lower your MMR the easier he is to solo carry with because people misunderstand his kill potential. [2:34]
- Lash is extremely fast around the map, can kill people from 100 to zero very quickly, and has a high impact ultimate that can get multiple kills. [3:05]
- Infernus is S tier, third: his laning stage is one of the strongest in the game and he can easily run away with the game. [3:05]
- Infernus is extremely hard to catch with obscene DPS and lifesteal (build Spirit Lifesteal, Soul Shredder Bullets, Infuser), which you need to take 1v2s and 1v3s when solo carrying. [3:36]
- All three top S tier heroes have ways to sustain themselves: Lash his Flog, Infernus lifesteal, Shiv a passive that sustains very well. [3:36]
- Paradox is S tier, fourth: her damage is absurd at lower MMR when ahead, with a guaranteed kill combo of the rail gun (Kinetic Carbine) into the ultimate swap. [4:07]
- Paradox has a very strong laning stage: the grenade for wave clear and her 3 for heavy harass. [4:38]
- Vindicta goes at the bottom of S tier because she is the definition of a snowball hero: kills with Assassinate grant her bonus souls. [5:09]
- Since the Slowing Hex changes you can no longer easily take Vindicta out of flight; you need knockdown or similar, and she can buy Debuff Remover to stay in flight. She has a lot of DPS and a strong laning stage. [5:39]
- Pocket is top of A tier and could have been S: high impact, Barrage damage, good escape, survivability, split push and solo kill potential. [5:39]
- Pocket's weakness is that his ultimate falls off hard late game when everyone has debuff removers. [6:09]
- Four stacks of Barrage on someone and they might as well be dead, especially when people don't know how Barrage works, with a high damage amp on them. [6:09]
- Wraith is A tier: not incredible but pretty good, with a lot of solo kill potential from her ultimate, good farming and survivability. [6:42]
- Warden is A tier: good solo kill potential, tankiness, DPS, and extreme mobility if you build movement speed items like Fleetfoot or Heroic Aura; rotating is very beneficial when solo carrying. [7:14]
- Grey Talon is B tier: very strong with insane damage output, but squishy and hard to keep alive when your team is behind; he lacks Vindicta's faster flight and survivability. Arguably A tier. [7:46]
- Lady Geist is bottom of A tier (moved up from B after thinking): ridiculous damage especially when people don't understand that her 3 amplifies her damage the more you hit it, she can swap health, and she has really good wave clear. [8:49]
- Bebop is B tier because his kit revolves around having a team: hooks create opportunities for teammates; strong Bebop players can follow up their own hooks, but for the average person he is B tier. [9:19]
- Haze is B tier: she used to be S tier but her damage is not good enough right now and she offers little utility; you would never prefer her over an Infernus or even a Wraith. Her ultimate is still very good for killing at lower MMR. [9:51]
- Yamato is B tier: she used to be A or even S tier for solo carry but feels underwhelming in the current meta. [10:22]
- Abrams is bottom of B tier: you can solo carry with him to an extent because he can become a killing machine fairly easily. [10:52]
- Kelvin is bottom of B tier: lots of rotational power, and guaranteed solo kills with his ult once he builds Alchemical Fire. [10:52]
- Mo & Krill is C tier: does some damage but is mostly utility, quite hard to solo carry with. [11:22]
- Seven is C tier: just not a good hero right now, period. [12:25]
- Dynamo and Ivy are C tier: both can be built to do a lot of damage, but their kits are inherently team dependent. [12:25]
- McGinnis is D tier, one of the two worst solo carries: her kit doesn't offer much, a farmed McGinnis gets shut down quickly, and unless you are a McGinnis one trick he does not recommend her. [11:53]
- Viscous is D tier: he doesn't do enough damage to solo carry, the hero is 100% utility. [11:53]
- To climb out of lower MMR, pick S or A tier heroes from this list; he climbed out of low MMR mostly with Lash, Infernus and Paradox, which work well against inexperienced players. [12:55]
