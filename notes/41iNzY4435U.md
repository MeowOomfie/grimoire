## Covers
First-impressions breakdown of Silver, released 2026-02-02 in the Old Gods New Blood update: a long range shotgun form that transforms into a tanky melee werewolf.
Both ability sets with upgrades, the transform timing, skill order, items (Kinetic Dash, Lifestrike, Fury Trance) and the combo. For anyone picking up Silver on release.

## Claims
- Silver is the first hero that transforms between a long range high damage form and a tanky melee range werewolf form, and the first with a permanent passive ultimate, giving her seven abilities. [0:00]
- Her ultimate is a passive meter that fills as you deal damage in normal form; when it is full you transform into the werewolf. [0:00]
- Her gun looks like a shotgun but is very precise with a very long falloff range, good close and long range. [0:31]
- Her weaknesses: only five bullets in the mag, a really slow fire rate, and only two stamina at base. [0:31]
- Slamfire (1): instantly reloads your gun and gives 300% fire rate. [1:01]
- Slamfire upgrades: lower cooldown, more weapon damage, and at max level hitting all your pellets deals 10% of the enemy's max health as damage. [1:01]
- Dealing damage and getting kills fills the meter toward the werewolf form. [1:01]
- Boot Kick (2): similar to Billy's 2; leaps you forward, deals melee damage on hit, and marks the enemy so your next shot deals bonus spirit damage. [1:32]
- Boot Kick upgrades: lower cooldown, level 2 restores stamina on hit, and at max level the enemy deals 25% less damage to you, like an instant mini Inhibitor. [1:32]
- On hit Boot Kick bounces you backwards; it can be used in the air after a jump and air dash. [2:03]
- Billy's tech works: Boot Kick and instantly light melee to land a light melee while stuck to the enemy in the animation. [2:03]
- The melee abilities themselves probably cannot be parried, but the follow-up melee can. [2:03]
- Entangling Bowler (3, caption spelling): entangles an enemy, slowing them and stopping movement abilities, stamina use and jumping, like a free Capacitor. [2:33]
- Entangling Bowler upgrades: more movement slow, longer debuff, and at max level it ricochets to two more targets. [2:33]
- Entangling Bowler has a very big, forgiving range; hitting three targets in team fights with the slow and movement lock seems broken on its own. [3:03]
- The ultimate charges faster when you get kills; during the transform animation window press 4 to transform instantly. [3:34]
- The werewolf form raises max health, gives bullet and spirit resist and heals 30% of missing health. [3:34]
- In werewolf form the whole ability set changes and primary fire becomes a very close range, basically melee, attack. [4:05]
- Getting kills in werewolf form resets its timer, so as long as you keep killing you stay in werewolf. [4:05]
- Go for the Throat (werewolf 1): deals melee damage and heals you; very low cooldown, about 5 seconds, use it when low or about to transform back. [4:41]
- Go for the Throat is a small cone in front of you, like Mina's Rake; hitting multiple targets gives multiple heals. [5:12]
- Go for the Throat upgrades: more damage, more lifesteal, and at max level bonus damage based on the enemy's missing health. [5:44]
- Mauling Leap (werewolf 2): a long, forgiving leap; the first enemy hit takes melee damage, spirit damage over time and reduced bullet resist. [5:44]
- Mauling Leap upgrades: +10 spirit damage per second, cooldown reduction, and more bullet shred, 20% bullet resist reduction at max level. [6:14]
- Tail Whack (werewolf 3): a big AoE smash that disarms everyone around you, slows them, deals spirit damage and pushes them away. [6:44]
- The first thing to learn on Silver: press 4 the moment the transformation starts; if you do not activate it manually you lose the bonus effects (healing, movement speed and stacking fire rate). [6:44]
- There is a sound cue for the transform and a prompt in the bottom right. [7:14]
- Verdict: seems broken; she disarms, stops movement on multiple enemies, is tanky, deals high damage at long and short range, and will be a gun hero. [7:14]
- Take two points in Slamfire early; it adds a lot of lane damage with its low cooldown. [7:44]
- Boot Kick and Entangling Bowler upgrades are strong but not as powerful as Slamfire and the ultimate; maybe one point in Boot Kick for the 6 second cooldown cut, maybe two if you use a lot of stamina. [8:15]
- Do not level Entangling Bowler early; its base 20 movement slow and movement lock are already a free Slowing Hex. [8:15]
- One point in the ultimate (Lycan Curse, caption "Lyk and curse") raises resistances from 20 to 35 instantly. [8:45]
- Maxed ultimate refreshes everything and heals you again on any kill during the form, so in team fights you can stay in werewolf forever. [8:45]
- Skill order to test: two points in Slamfire, then max the ultimate or max Slamfire, never 2 or 3 first, then max Entangling Bowler for a three-person lockdown. [9:16]
- Silver has the lowest base bullets in the game, so Kinetic Dash is a must buy; it more than doubles her base ammo and is better than Titanic Magazine on her. [9:16]
- She wants fire rate, so Kinetic Dash is the first item; fire rate stays slow even with it, which Slamfire helps. [9:46]
- Melee Lifesteal into Lifestrike is item number two because many of her abilities trigger melee damage; Lifestrike was recently buffed and even abilities counting as light melee bring it back fast. [9:46]
- In base form Boot Kick deals weapon damage and triggers Lifestrike easily when you are getting low. [10:16]
- In werewolf form both Go for the Throat and Mauling Leap count as melee damage, which makes Lifestrike even stronger. [10:16]
- Fury Trance is an off-build pick: in werewolf form you have no ammo to reload, so its downsides do not punish you. [10:54]
- Combo: deal damage to fill the meter, transform, then Mauling Leap in, Go for the Throat, and maul the enemy. [11:26]
- Build her very tanky; the lifesteal heals off many sources. [11:59]
- Valve's default build is good this time, including Extra Stamina. [11:59]
