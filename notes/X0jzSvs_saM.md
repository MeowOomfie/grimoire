# WIN every lane - A top 0.01% Laning Guide
channel: Deathy  date: 2024-09-03  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X0jzSvs_saM

## Covers
Laning basics (2024 map): pushing creeps towards the enemy, melee on creeps, denies, speeding back to base, shooting under the enemy tower, itemizing by lane type (winning, even, losing), Headshot Booster, High Velocity Mag, Close Quarters, Hollow Point Ward, Rapid Rounds, Extra Regen, Healing Rite, Restorative Shot, Extra Health, Extra Stamina, Melee Lifesteal, Health Nova, heal creep, solo vs dual lanes, ganking pushed lanes, speed zip, jump pads, early towers, buying at 500, Vindicta, Shiv, Haze, Paradox, Kelvin, Seven, Warden, Wraith

## Claims
- With creeps, you generally want to apply pressure and push the creeps towards the enemy most of the time, because then you can put pressure on them, shoot them and go for denies (captioned "kniv"), none of which you can do when creeps push into you. [0:31]
- This is counterintuitive coming from MOBAs where you keep the lane near your tower, but in Deadlock the side whose wave is at the enemy tower is usually the one applying pressure. [1:01]
- Do not idle in lane and always push the wave, but do not sacrifice souls for it: use melee a lot and watch the souls so they are not easy denies for the enemy. [1:01]
- With the wave at their tower he got Shiv to half health just by clicking on him as Vindicta. [1:01]
- Because the wave is at their tower, he can overextend a little and still back up safely. [1:31]
- When you cannot push the wave (for example at 100 health), focus on denying creeps as much as possible, especially on a shotgun hero, so you get some value despite being pushed back. [1:31]
- Speeding back to base is something most players do not do well; if you are extremely hurt you only have two options, go back to base or buy healing. [2:02]
- If you have creep advantage and your creeps are under their tower, you can just run at the enemies and shoot them the whole time, especially with a health advantage; he got nearly 200 damage on the Shiv this way. [2:02]
- This works because towers do not automatically aggro to you, unlike Dota, so you can sit right there and shoot your enemy. [2:02]
- Shooting under their tower is particularly effective with a poke item such as Headshot Booster for extra headshot damage. [2:32]
- Many players itemize wrong because they follow guides without understanding why the items are in them. [2:32]
- There are two to three lane types: even lanes where you trade farm and either side could win, favourable matchups you are expected to win, and losing matchups where there is not much you can do and you should focus on farm; itemize slightly differently in each. [2:32]
- The only way to know which matchup is which is practice; he will not label matchups because the meta is evolving fast. [3:03]
- Feel out the first wave or two and decide whether you are winning, trading farm, or losing. [3:34]
- If you are winning, buy items that let you pressure even more: Headshot Booster, High Velocity Mag, Close Quarters (depending on the hero) and Hollow Point Ward; Rapid Rounds can be good on heroes that need fire rate. [3:34]
- Those four pressure items have some of the highest damage in the game: Headshot Booster gives 45 bonus damage on a headshot every 7 seconds, Close Quarters +25% weapon damage within 15 meters, High Velocity Mag bullet velocity plus 14% weapon damage, and Hollow Point Ward 20% weapon damage while above 60 health, which you mostly should be when winning. [4:07]
- With pressure items you must shoot the enemy at every opportunity when you are not shooting creeps; shooting is Deadlock's equivalent of left-click harass in Dota, as long as it does not draw creep aggro. [4:38]
- If you are losing, consider Extra Regen or Healing Rite for sustain; Restorative Shot gives sustain plus bullet resist and weapon damage and is a slightly more aggressive regen option; Extra Health or Extra Stamina help you survive burst. [5:08]
- In an even matchup go the aggressive route, meaning more gun items, to push your advantage further. [5:39]
- If you are ahead, your first two items should be gun items, perhaps even your first three. [5:39]
- Even in an even lane, if you are not taking a lot of damage, get as many gun items as possible early to enable pressure; people often buy too many vitality or spirit items early when these give less value than weapon items on most heroes. [6:09]
- A few heroes buy spirit items first and that is fine, for example Wraith right now, whose damage is based on one spirit ability. [6:09]
- At the moment orange (weapon) items apply the most pressure, spirit items some pressure depending on the character, and green (vitality) items are mostly survival with very little pressure, except Extra Stamina gives a little fire rate and Melee Lifesteal gives some pressure if you are punching. [6:39]
- Stay near your own heal creep whenever you can and kill your opponent's heal creep as early as possible; you can sometimes get two heals per wave of around 40 to 50 health each, which adds up to a very large difference. [7:09]
- If your heal creep always lives and you always kill theirs, you get very far ahead very quickly. [7:39]
- Solo and dual lanes are similar, but dual lanes let you focus one specific person and create moments of numbers advantage (two vs one). [7:39]
- When one enemy laner is dead, the two remaining push the wave and shoot the lone player the whole time, then dive him. [8:10]
- Dual lanes allow more pressure; a teammate going aggressive in a 1v2 gets shot by two people. [8:41]
- After taking a tower early you can keep going (as his Seven did) or check the minimap for lanes pushed up near you and gank them. [8:41]
- Seeing the Haze extremely pushed up on the minimap, he went straight for the gank, and after the failed gank he stayed to push that wave. [9:13]
- Once you have won your lane, go win other lanes too; objectives give souls, so take as many objectives as quickly as you can and ruin the other player's game. [9:13]
- If you are losing your lane, have the same mentality: look for another lane that is pushed up. [9:43]
- A good gank target is an enemy past the middle of their lane; generally do not gank people sitting at their tower, as it is very hard. [10:13]
- The basic formula to win lane: get aggressive on the creeps right away for creep advantage, get them to half health then heavy melee them while avoiding damage, push them under the enemy tower, then go for denies and harass the enemy heroes. [10:44]
- If you do not manage to push the creeps it is a more neutral laning stage; if you do get the wave onto their tower you can be the aggressor. [11:15]
- When there are no opportunities under their tower, go for denies. [11:15]
- You should usually buy whenever you have 500, because every item is a significant power spike. [11:15]
- You should not necessarily leave lane right away to buy; if staying applies more pressure than the item would, stay. [11:46]
- Once the wave is under their tower, nuke the enemies and try to deny them; they cannot be aggressive because they have to worry about creep damage. [11:46]
- He died diving a Kelvin who bought Health Nova (a smart buy), but with the lane still pushing into a very hurt enemy and him returning at full health, the situation was still better for his side. [12:16]
- When you die in lane, look at the other lanes for anyone pushed up; he took the speed zip straight to a Paradox pushed on his Haze's lane and got the kill. [12:16]
- That rotation cost him little, made his Haze's lane much easier and the Paradox's game much harder. [12:47]
- The same holds for enemies: when an enemy dies, especially their first death, be wary that they could gank you with the speed boost. [12:47]
- Being far ahead lets you dive the tower. [12:47]
- If their wave has never been at your tower and yours has always been at theirs, you win the lane 99% of the time as long as you do not overextend. [13:17]
- Keeping the wave at their tower gives constant tower pressure and an early tower; this one died at 5 minutes and he has taken towers much earlier. [13:17]
- The early tower gave a 1.5k to 1.8k soul lead (Warden at 2.3k, him at 4.1k). [13:48]
- Jump pads are very strong ganking tools; if your lane has one, always use it to gank the other lane when you can, especially if you are winning. [13:48]
