# How to Build & Play Silver | Phantom Silver Coaching
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-02-04
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55n3B8dWMP0

## Covers
Silver guide plus coaching, Midknight reviewing a Phantom-rank Silver player's game (lane into Viper, a Bebop with hooks on the enemy team).
Opens with Silver tech (kick swap, kick-up, hidden melee in the 2), then covers item mistakes (Point Blank, Kinetic Dash, gun items), shred and melee items, AP order, how to fight Silver, map awareness and her role as a position 3 brawler.
Ends with two recommended builds: Pure Content's for Phantom and below, Lightbringer's for Ascendant and above. Patch date 2026-02-04.

## Claims
Tech:
- Kick swap, like a Paradox swap: right after your 2 connects, flick your camera around and the enemy is kicked behind you, so you trade places. [0:00]
- Do not start flicking the mouse before the 2 hits or you will miss; flick right after the hit. [0:00]
- You can also do the kick at about a 90 degree angle depending on where you want to move them. [0:30]
- Main uses of the kick swap: kicking enemies back into your team, or swapping places to escape when you are in trouble. [0:30]
- Kick and look straight down and you go flying up, which is useful as escape movement. [0:30]
- If you melee right after your 2 you can hide a melee inside the 2 for extra damage. [0:30]
- The kick swap is essentially a Paradox swap on a 14 second cooldown. [33:23]
- Kicking while looking down pushes the enemy back, but they do not go as far. [34:23]
- You can manually recast (end) wolf form early by pressing 4. [5:34]

Build and items:
- Point Blank does not affect her wolf form: she does 30 damage per auto with Point Blank and still 30 after selling it for a gun item with no weapon damage. It seems good but is not worth buying. [0:30]
- Many players start Monster Rounds; not bad if you expect to lose lane, but its minion damage reduction rarely matters since your gun barely hurts minions. [1:32]
- Headshot Booster is a good early item; the next item should be Melee Lifesteal. [3:03]
- Kinetic Dash looks good (low ammo, lots of gun damage) but is bad on her: once you have the ult your 1 reloads your gun, and you rarely fire 10 bullets in a hero fight without going into wolf form. [4:33]
- Do not buy Kinetic Dash on Silver. [5:34]
- In wolf form you heal and deal damage off your 1, so build a lot of shred and spam the 1 off cooldown rather than building fire rate. [6:05]
- Her autos in wolf form do a lot, but shred is more valuable than fire rate. [6:36]
- Building for her gun is bad: her gun tickles, her damage comes from wolf form and the 1. Melee items are much better. [8:37]
- The 1 scales directly off gun damage, but it is still better to build melee. [9:08]
- Buy Melee Lifesteal every game: three of her abilities can proc it. [10:39]
- If you want movement, go full stamina items rather than boots. [10:39]
- Extra Health is strong, more so in lane, and makes sense since she has low max HP. [12:10]
- Do not go Colossus on her; shred is her best offensive and defensive tool because shred makes her wolf form 1 heal more. [14:45]
- Toxic Bullets is good on her; she likes several tier 3 gun items. [15:16]
- Headhunter is good but not crazy; buy it if you struggle to get into wolf form, though a good Silver should not need it. [15:16]
- Express Shot is good for adding to your combo and has no real downside on her: it only uses two bullets because of her low magazine. [15:46]
- At 6k souls with your spikes done, good options are Crippling Headshot, Crushing Fist (three abilities trigger it, plus the hidden melee) and Weakening Headshot for the resist shred. [16:47]
- Phantom Strike is solid for staying on people in wolf form. [16:47]
- Ricochet currently works in her ult and is very broken, but he expects that to be patched. [17:19]
- Inhibitor is very strong and an excellent 1v1 item; look for 1v1s on this hero. [17:19]
- He does not like Berserker on her: once you have taken sustained damage you are either in human form, where gun DPS does not matter, or in wolf form, where you already kill them. It fits true tanks better. [18:24]
- Melee Lifesteal gives much more healing than Fortitude. [27:41]
- Shred gives your 1 more damage and more healing; it is very strong on her as long as you headshot before going into wolf form. [27:41]
- Pop shred items like Bullet Resist Shredder before the fight. [28:42]
- Her default spirit items should be Bullet Resist Shredder and Duration Extender, or Slowing Hex and Duration Extender. [21:33]
- Spirit Snatch is buyable into hard spirit comps; treat it as defensive, since her spirit scaling is subpar. [22:03]
- Torment Pulse has some logic, but duration items are better. [25:41]
- Against a hooking Bebop, buy Reactive Barrier and respect his hooks more. [5:34]
- Her gun is very weak, about 200 damage a shot at one bullet a second, so do not build full gun. [34:23]

Ability order and use:
- General AP order: max the 1 first. [8:07]
- Into Viper, put a point into the ability that slows: she does nothing while slowed. [8:07]
- Pop wolf form before killing minions, because its fire rate lets you kill minions and confirm the orb. [5:34]
- Her fire rate is too low to confirm orbs on minions she kills; get minions to 1 HP and confirm after other minions finish them, or walk up and melee. [2:03]
- Against Bebop's bomb, pop wolf form after the bomb hits: the 30% missing HP heal is worth more than taking 20% less damage from the bomb. [6:36]
- When you hit your 2 on someone, follow with a heavy melee. [9:39]
- Use the 1 (bite) to finish kills when you can; it heals about 100 HP. [9:39]
- Throw your bola at fleeing targets; it would have secured the kill. [11:40]
- Use the tail for its disarm. [35:28]
- When you cannot escape, commit into wolf form, since it is the only way to live. [22:33]

How to fight against Silver:
- Best counter: Metal Skin her 1. If she uses her 1 on you and you Metal Skin it, she cannot go into wolf form. [15:46]
- When she is in wolf form, kite her with good movement or items like Superior Stamina or Warp Stone; she has to be on top of you. [15:46]
- If you kite her she cannot hit you off a zipline in wolf form; kited Silvers often start buying Phantom Strike. [10:39]

Laning and macro:
- Silver is a subpar laner before her ult, so she can play a stalling lane. [1:32]
- You are fighting too aggressively before your ult; fine if you are confident, but do not get hooked. [7:06]
- Without green items you will get harassed under tower. [7:36]
- Play her like Yamato: invade enemy jungle camps and look to brawl. [12:10]
- Do not extend on your weak side when the enemy shows no map presence and most of your team is on the other side; you will get collapsed on. [13:12]
- Identify both teams' strong sides; when five enemies are on your side, leave instead of chasing. [14:14]
- That chase was too greedy; with no kill potential in a 1v1, a 4v1 is worse. [14:45]
- You keep chasing too deep into the jungle; check the map, and if no enemies show, assume they are waiting there. [20:58]
- Fighting far in front of your wave is risky. [22:33]
- In a neutral game where the enemy has no jungle farm, you can slow play and farm your own jungle, or deny the urn rather than run it. [24:37]
- Taking a walker opens that side of the map for invading their jungle. [25:08]
- When a bad player jumps around in front of you, spam melee to force a parry; against a good player that is predictable. [25:41]
- If Silver wins lane she runs the lobby; he thinks she is overtuned but hard to play while people are still learning her. [29:43]
- Route behind targets for catches instead of routing too aggressively. [29:43]
- Do not look for fights while sitting on a lot of souls; head to the shop. [29:43]
- She is a position 3 hero (bruiser), like Shiv or Yamato, not a position 2 off carry like Mirage, Pocket or Mina. [30:46]
- You are reacting to the map instead of predicting it: set your pathing so you are already next to a walker when a wave reaches it. [31:16]
- Position 3 brawlers should steal enemy farm and Sinner's Sacrifices using mid game pressure, like an Abrams. [32:20]
- Bringing the urn to mid would have felt great if you did not want to go mid yourself. [32:52]
- When there is a kill to secure but the enemies are healthy and stay up for a while, play safe, back, and use your wave pressure to run the urn. [34:54]

Recommended builds:
- Phantom and below: Pure Content's build. It buys Rusted Barrel nearly every lane (bonus health, resist shred, fire rate reduction that helps you get into wolf form); simple left to right; maybe add Weakening Headshot after Stamina Mastery. [35:59]
- Ascendant and above: Lightbringer's build (not his Darkbringer troll build, which teaches the hero wrong). Spend 4.8k souls across the categories to reach the 4.8k spike; top row is tier 2, bottom tier 3, left is bought more often. [36:29]
- In Lightbringer's gun category you mostly buy Weakening Headshot and Hollow Point, sometimes Superior Duration and Bullet Resist Shredder; for 1v1 games, Weakening Headshot and Hunter's Aura. [37:00]
- When ahead, itemize more defensively (more greens), such as an extra Stamina Mastery. [37:00]
- Mystic Shot over Weakening Headshot, because Mystic Shot procs Bullet Resist Shredder for your opening combo. [37:30]
- Late game items: Inhibitor is very strong, and Crippling Headshot also does a lot. [37:30]
