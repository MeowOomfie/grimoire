# HUGE Nerfs to Jump Pads | Deadlock Patch Notes
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-10-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWMdouDC4AE

## Covers
Same-day breakdown of the 2025-10-25 balance patch (Halloween masks back in the jungle), recorded minutes after release.
Jump pad stun, item changes (Mystic Shot, barriers, Fortitude, Trophy Collector, Spirit Snatch, Ethereal Shift) and hero changes for Calico, Doorman, Drifter, Infernus, Kelvin, McGinnis, Paradox, Seven, Shiv, Victor, Vyper, Warden.
For any player wanting the coach's verdict on what the patch actually changes.

## Claims
- New jump pad mechanic: shooting someone as they take a jump pad stuns them. [0:31]
- In testing the stun only happens if you shoot them as they are taking the jump pad; shooting them right after they are in the air did not stun. [1:02]
- He suspects the stun is bugged and the intent is to stun if you shot them within 6 seconds, as the patch notes say; working as intended it would be a massive change, in its current form it is not a huge deal. [1:02]
- The change is meant to counter players who take the jump pad and strafe away to escape very far; it nerfs jump pad mobility only when you get hit. He thinks it is potentially a good change. [1:32]
- Mystic Shot base damage nerfed and scaling increased; it should not change much. He would have preferred damage falloff on Mystic Shot. Valve keeps nerfing every source of flat damage in the early game. [2:03]
- Warp Stone bullet resist nerfed a little. [2:33]
- Spirit Shielding, Weapon Shielding and Reactive Barrier base barriers nerfed a little, and Reactive Barrier's cooldown nerfed; Reactive Barrier looks like an even better option comparatively. [2:33]
- Fortitude buffed: regen now kicks in after 13 seconds instead of 14; the item was already very strong and did not need it. [3:04]
- Trophy Collector buffed from 25 to 30 souls per minute per stack: at max stacks (16) that is 80 more, 480 souls per minute instead of 400. [3:34]
- Trophy Collector will still be rare at high levels, but it might become broken at lower levels; if your games last about 35 minutes on average you might want it most games. [4:05]
- Spirit Snatch nerfed significantly: duration 10 seconds instead of 14 and bonus damage down 25; it was overperforming and should be more balanced. [4:05]
- Ethereal Shift base cooldown now 45 seconds instead of 20; with Transcendent Cooldown it is 32 seconds, still 12 above the old base. [4:35]
- You can no longer cube (Viscous) or Rescue Beam an Ethereal Shifted hero. [4:35]
- Calico Gloom Bombs base cooldown nerfed by 2 seconds, which is significant: no more spamming Gloom Bombs in lane. [5:05]
- Calico Gloom Bombs base damage nerfed by 10 and scaling from 0.74 to 0.64, but enemies hit by multiple bombs now take 65% damage instead of 50%; hitting all bombs is about the same as before, hitting one gives less free damage. [5:36]
- Calico's Ava no longer replenishes stamina, so the stamina cheese (use all stamina, then Ava) is gone. [6:08]
- Doorman gun nerfed: 25% less headshot damage and lower bullet growth per boon, so gun Doorman should be much less aggressive. He thinks the nerf is good because gun Doorman was against the spirit of the character. [6:08]
- Doorman compensation: Call Bell explosion damage scaling up to 1.7 (from about 1.5). [6:38]
- Drifter's weapon damage per stack nerfed to limit snowball, but base bullet damage up from 20.9 to 21.3 and bullet growth from 0.52 to 0.56, roughly a 7 to 8% gun DPS increase at max level. He thinks Drifter is not that good. [7:08]
- Infernus hit hard: bullet growth from 0.12 to 0.08, second Flame Dash point +30 DPS instead of +35, Afterburn tier one -20% spirit damage instead of -25%, tier three +26 instead of +28 DPS. Much worse but should still be fine. [7:38]
- Kelvin base regen halved to 1, which he believes makes him the only hero with 1 base regen. [8:08]
- Kelvin headshot reduction increased from -20 to -25, and his dome (Frozen Shelter) timer increased by 20 seconds. [8:38]
- The Kelvin nerfs are good but not enough to bring him in line; Valve should have been harsher and more nerfs may come. [8:38]
- McGinnis Medicinal Specter now has a 1 second linger after you step out, which is not shown in the ability text; a huge deal that makes the heal feel much better. [9:09]
- In testing the Medicinal Specter seemed to keep healing longer than the linger, possibly a new bug. [9:09]
- Paradox gun damage growth from 6.48 to 6.15 and Paradoxical Swap cooldown from 70 to 75 seconds; not enough to matter, she is probably still going to dominate. [9:39]
- Seven Lightning Ball scaling from 0.43 to 0.55, roughly a 20% increase; a Lightning Ball build could be better but he is not convinced. [10:41]
- Shiv's alt fire movement nerfed by 10%; still really good but you cannot travel as far, especially with walls. [10:41]
- Victor Aura of Suffering max DPS from 80 to 70 (about a 10 to 12% nerf) and its tier two upgrade max DPS down 10 and min DPS down 2 (about 20%). [11:11]
- Victor was an Ethereal Shift abuser, so Valve avoided nerfing him too much. [11:11]
- Victor Pain Battery tier one and tier two swapped, so +40 damage now needs two points; good change because wave one Victor with one point was dealing 200 damage. [11:42]
- Vyper got spirit scaling on her dagger to make spirit Vyper more viable; her lane was already insane and she might be a lane dominator now, but she struggles mid to late game because she is squishy. [12:14]
- Warden base bullet damage from 15 to 16.2 (about 8%) and Binding Word base radius 20 m instead of 19 m, easier to hit in lane. [12:45]
- The Warden buff brings his gun DPS closer to Kelvin's: Kelvin base DPS 68, Warden 62. [13:16]
