# This lash has lost 15/16 games. Why?
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-08-01
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QVJyCI1wGDM

## Covers
Unprepared VOD review of a viewer's Lash who won lane but kept losing after the patch. Main lesson: create map pressure and use it, cross onto the enemy side after power spikes and wave pushes, farm efficiently (Sinner's Sacrifice, buffs) instead of drifting back to your own jungle.
For players who win lane then lose momentum, especially on active heroes like Lash.

## Claims
- In any game, always ask what the macro situation is overall: towers taken, soul lead on your lane opponents. [1:01]
- After winning your lane, try to steal the enemy's resources and impact other lanes; stealing the enemy Sinner's Sacrifice when it spawns, then doing your own, is fine. [1:32]
- Mistake: in a 1v2 defending tower against Haze and Dynamo, Lash ulted the Haze right away; against a good Dynamo, Dynamo would have saved both himself and Haze, so don't ult right away. [2:03]
- When you are outnumbered on a side of the map, your goal is not to kill them but to keep your tower and make it as hard as possible to take. The Lash's fight was a waste of time: he had already damaged both and they would back off, and he had a big unsecured bag he handed them, losing nearly his whole lead. [2:33]
- Recognize when you are outnumbered on a side of the map and shift your play style to the situation. [3:03]
- Mistake: doing an inefficient jungle camp. Sinner's Sacrifices are generally more efficient; he should have finished the one he started, gone to the double Sinner's Sacrifice, and more importantly gone to the buff earlier. [3:03]
- What Deathy wants to see: clear the Sinner's Sacrifice, hit a power spike, then get active. [3:33]
- After pushing a wave you either keep going or you don't; with an 800 unsecured bag close to a 3,200 item, and a max Ground Strike build, save for Majestic Leap. [4:03]
- If your team is pushing mid you don't need to show up; after pushing a side wave, do your double Sinner's Sacrifice on your side, buy, then show up to a fight. [4:03]
- Mistake: about 30 seconds wasted wandering toward the left side and backing up, then hitting two boxes instead of the Sinner's Sacrifice; boxes are okay, but Sinner's Sacrifices give far more value, especially now that they give four buffs. [4:34]
- Whenever you push a wave you buy time to do something on the map; use it for the most efficient thing, measured by souls and also by map impact. [5:06]
- Buying Cultist Sacrifice there is fine though not amazing; don't tank the tier three camp. [5:06]
- Mistake: going back to base on 1,900 souls with a 4k lead right after hitting the max Flog spike. Spend your money and go fight; you are at your strongest until your next significant item or max Ground Strike. [5:37]
- Healbane is a great item and fine to buy, though by then Lash should probably have Cold Front; items are not the reason he loses. [6:37]
- The reason he loses: every time he pushes a wave he goes back to his own side to clear a jungle camp, and not even the most efficient ones, so his map impact is basically zero. A top Lash copying those map movements could not carry the game. [6:37]
- In the new meta big fights are heavily disincentivized: with four or five players mid, souls get split many ways, so it is often better to make plays on the side lanes or in smaller groups. [7:08]
- Movement: instead of using grapple to move forward, use the zipline. [7:39]
- When you are very close to 3,200, save up for a 3,200 item. For Lash, options include Tankbuster, Majestic Leap or Warp Stone (Deathy values mobility a lot on Lash), or a cooldown item. [8:09]
- Lash can do fine against a fairly strong Warden on a side lane if careful in the approach; being aggressive on a side lane is much better than passive play. [8:41]
- Mistake: after trading with the Warden, getting his abilities out and half his health, and with Shiv arriving for a numbers advantage while enemies were elsewhere, Lash made another passive decision. [9:11]
- Count how often you cross the middle line onto their side of the map; apart from right after laning, this Lash almost never did. Use pressure to create something; don't be too afraid of dying if it creates pressure on objectives. [9:11]
- No walker having taken damage at 15 minutes is a symptom of low rank play. [9:41]
- If you see a lot of enemies mid as Lash and Shiv, keep pushing the side lane; it is okay to trade objectives and create counter pressure, because teams at this level are bad at pushing objectives and won't coordinate on a walker. [9:41]
- Instead of doing a box, walk forward to a position to help: with Shiv you can kill the Warden, or at the very least push the wave. Arriving 5 to 10 seconds late wastes it. [10:11]
- Grouping up four players on one side tends not to be great, though defending your last guardian is fair. [10:42]
- Going to boxes on your own side while being the richest player in the lobby on a team that is ahead creates no pressure. [10:42]
- Pressure on two fronts (Lash's group on the enemy side while Mirage pressures mid) is good. [11:13]
- When down two players you can't push further because they only want your objective; stealing their jungle is the best option. [11:43]
- Picking up the Urn is a good option when your team has pressure on the other side of the map, since you can often run it over for free; but watch for enemy Black Hole and Warden ult coming back. Running the Urn would have been far more efficient than the fight on McGinnis. [11:43]
- To push walkers you need aggression at multiple spots so the map feels pressure; pushing their walker while down numbers is easy for them to defend. Before fights ask: are we up numbers, do we have pressure? [12:13]
- After the enemy used big cooldowns to get kills, take the Urn while they lack their big ultimates; the wave they must deal with also gives you time to do something else. [12:43]
- The core principle for winning Deadlock: create pressure on the map and use it to make things happen. Pushing a wave, creating something on the map and then doing nothing with it is why this player loses so many games. [13:13]
- Check the damage breakdown: doing more damage to troopers than players is normal because pushing waves is the essence of the game, but doing more damage to neutrals than players should never happen, especially on Lash. That may be okay on Wraith or Seven, but Lash wants to be active; 10,000 player damage at 20 minutes should never happen. [13:43]
- Whenever you hit a power spike or push a wave, ask: can I invade their jungle, look for a pick in another lane, keep pressuring this lane, do I have an advantage? Defaulting to your own jungle is a last resort, or a way to reach a power spike before getting active. [14:13]
- Sinner's Sacrifices are incredibly efficient: quick to do, then fight right after. Each gives four golden statue buffs, so the double gives eight, plus a good amount of souls; spend them right away to hit a mid-game power spike, which is what Lash and many other heroes need to win. [14:45]
