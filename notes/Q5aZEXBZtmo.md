## Covers
Mirage gameplay with commentary by aquatiq on a Fire Scarabs focused build (he calls it a Scarabs build).
Commentary over one game, no coaching. Recorded 2026-05-06, a few hours after a large nerf patch.
Topics: ability priority, item choices, the patch nerfs, and a skins performance tip.

## Claims
Mirage build and play:
- He is running a Scarabs build and likes Scarabs builds on Mirage; they are very strong. [1:32]
- He puts no points into Djinn's Mark this game, since the build goes for Scarabs, not marks. [3:36]
- He buys Extra Charge for his 1. [3:03]
- From a powerup he hoped for a casting buff, and took the gun buff when it came. [4:06]
- Arcane Surge is his next buy. [5:16]
- He wants Recharging Rush when he has the money. [9:34]
- He almost always gets Rapid Recharge on Mirage these days. [12:09]
- He maxes the ultimate second, which he calls strong, almost too strong. [13:12]
- Before mid boss he buys Fortitude to survive longer and heal out of combat, plus Improved Spirit, which took his spirit power from 125 to 133. [18:27]
- An ultimate upgrade takes 80 seconds off the cooldown of Mirage's ultimate, which he calls ridiculous. [23:37]
- He recommends the item captioned "siphon" on Mirage as always good; it gives near infinite health. [24:07]

Patch and matchups:
- A big patch a few hours earlier nerfed a lot of heroes, including Infernus's Flame Dash and Viper. [2:32]
- Denying seems to do nothing: the enemy missed many souls but had the same money. [3:03]
- McGinnis's ultimate radius is huge and hard to sidestep. [12:09]
- He thinks unstick does not work while someone is in the sewers, because the game still treats them as on the map. [17:57]

General:
- He turned off cosmetic skins because their effects make the game lag; plain retexture skins like his Yamato one are fine. [1:02]
