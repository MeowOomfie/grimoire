## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream on the late June 2026 patch, which he calls bad for macro players. Four students: a Sinclair (Ascendant), a Lash (Oracle, Archon), an Ascendant player new to Shiv, and a Wraith (Arcanist).
Then an analysis of a Sinclair from a Deadlock Night Shift league game (Abrams' team into Buffendor): gun versus spirit Sinclair, Spectral Assistant tricks, farming camps from range and cutting waves before the urn.
Themes: urn play, dropping waves, invading, bag awareness, Rejuvenator hygiene, splitting against a deathball, and the meta favoring M1 and AoE heroes.
