channel: aquatiq

## Covers
Paige played as a gun carry, commentary over one game by aquatiq (about his third game on the hero).
Golden Goose Egg first item, then gun items and a stack of actives (Knockdown, Phantom Strike), playing from behind teammates.
Patch of late August 2026.

## Claims
- Gun Paige is the build: Golden Goose Egg as the first item, then build into her gun; he says it looks like a troll but works. [0:34]
- He cites Paige at about a 54% win rate and says players have been using her wrong; she should build gun. [0:34]
- Paige's gun does a surprising amount of damage per bullet, about as much as Vindicta's; with one gun item she was killing people. [7:51]
- Bind the Golden Goose Egg active to a key you will not press by accident (he moved it to T) so you never pop the egg early. [5:42]
- He planned to pop the egg at around 18 to 22 minutes, after buying Heroic Aura. [4:10]
- He popped it early when he had many assists and needed money, then sold the egg to make room for items. [12:00]
- From the crates he picked Healing Rite over Restorative Shard because it also heals a teammate. [3:10]
- After Healing Rite he rushed Express Shot, then moved into movement items. [7:19]
- At 11k souls he bought Fleetfoot and an early Knockdown. [13:05]
- Knockdown is a strong pick on Paige: with her own stun plus Knockdown he got repeated double knockdowns on the enemy team. [19:15]
- Against an enemy Suppressor slowing your bullets, he switched to Split Shot that game. [17:43]
- He also wanted Toxic Bullets, Mirage and Phantom Strike in this build. [15:09]
- Silencer is good when nobody on your team has one; he bought it late. [27:12]
- Ricochet lets her clear waves easily late game. [28:16]
- He held good Trophy Collector stacks from assists. [16:41]
- Paige's stun and an ally's stun can be chained for long lockdown. [3:40]
- Paige farms very well. [6:45]
- Stay behind your teammate at almost all times so you do not get one-shot. [5:42]
- Shield (barrier) teammates when the enemy goes to attack them. [5:11]
- In fights, put the barrier mainly on your carries and tanks. [26:10]
- With all the actives, play the back line and shoot from range while your frontline fights. [20:18]
- Lash has near infinite movement; Knockdown on him does not keep him grounded for long. [21:18]
- At 9.6k souls he decided to stop fighting and farm a little instead. [13:05]
- When ahead after a won fight, go take Mid Boss. [22:55]
- Apollo is very strong early; when behind early, scale your money instead of trying to fight. [1:35]
- He finished 6/7 with 20 assists in a win. [29:19]
