## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream from 3/3/26 (patch era with Silver, Graves, Venator and Celeste new and a Silver nerf expected that week). After a quick proxy question on a Victor game, he coaches five players: two Silver players, Scout on Rem (Archon 6), Shadow Visions on Graves (Ascendant 5) and an Oracle Holliday.
Themes: hitting waves and wave priority, box routes and tunnel routes before 5 minutes, roaming and ganking mid, running urn and confirming orbs, his mid boss flowchart, bag awareness, ARAMing mid as the classic misplay, Melee Lifesteal and light versus heavy melee, Superior Duration value (and the "Palmer principle" on Mo & Krill), Crack Shot on minions, Restorative Shot and Trophy Collector as bad buys, and anti-spirit itemization.
