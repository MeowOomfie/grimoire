## Covers
Grey Talon, played on MikaelS's spirit build, by aquatiq.
Commentary over one full game, no coaching; items and ability points are named as he follows the build.
Patch of 2026-06-08.

## Claims
- Since his buffs Grey Talon is roughly S tier and constantly does around a thousand damage. [0:30]
- Shoot the wave first before shooting enemies, to get wave priority. [1:34]
- Buy Opening Rounds early in lane, then get the 1 (Charged Shot). [3:07]
- The build goes Mystic Shot in lane; Grey Talon is a little weak in lane without it. [3:07]
- The build is basically the same as ABL's. [3:07]
- When you cannot help a teammate, cut the wave. [3:39]
- Put two points into the 2 early. [4:10]
- Buy the Mystic Shot upgrade after that. [4:40]
- Battle Vest is probably not needed later; he had Spirit Shielding and considered Trophy Collector. [8:45]
- Build order continues: Extra Charge for Mystic Shot, Extra Stamina, then Tank Buster. [10:18]
- Stamina Mastery is a very good item. [10:18]
- Pocket is very good right now with a 9 second cloak cooldown. [12:55]
- The owl ultimate needs a kill or assist within a window to give the +10 stacks; MikaelS often throws the bird at enemies right in their face. [14:29]
- With 8K: Tank Buster and Stamina Mastery. [14:59]
- Do not hunt for bird stacks; kill people with your arrows instead. [16:07]
- Against a team with knockdowns, buy Counterspell, and maybe a debuff remover. [16:38]
- Recognize a bad fight and leave. [20:17]
- Have two snares (traps) ready before mid boss. [22:51]
- The build says Boundless Spirit first, but he bought Mystic Reverb first because the item is so strong. [22:51]
- Improved Spirit only added 10 spirit power at 164 spirit. [23:22]
- Grey Talon is not supposed to frontline. [24:00]
- Spirit Burn is a late buy if the game goes long. [26:08]
