# How to Manipulate Deadlock's Matchmaking
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-08-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eIi7dARGT4I

## Covers
How to raise your odds of getting a contested hero (Billy at his release, August 2025) through the hero queue: greying the most picked heroes, how the queue favours heroes you have played, and queue length vs match quality. Ends with a few Billy tips. For players who want a specific new hero.

## Claims
- Queueing with three friends who each queue Haze, Infernus and Lash while all of you grey the same heroes locks each other out, but only gives about a 25% chance of getting the new hero if your friends also want it. [0:00]
- Basic method: put the three most popular heroes on grey; at the time Haze was on about 85%, Infernus 80% and Lash 64% pick rate. [1:00]
- Greying those three gives roughly a 20% chance of getting Billy, versus about 8% queueing normally if all 12 players in the lobby want him. [1:00]
- The queue system wants to give you heroes you have played, and probably heroes you do well on, so with many games on Haze you have a decent chance of popping Haze. [1:30]
- Check the pick rate column on Statlocker's general hero tier list to find the current most popular heroes; if you have many games on one of the top three, grey the next most popular (then Seven, fourth) instead. [2:01]
- Once you get some games on the wanted hero, your chance of getting him snowballs because the game favours heroes you have played. [2:31]
- If you main the most popular heroes yourself, grey the next three most popular heroes you have no games on. [3:02]
- The method works for any hero: with Infernus on grey but many Infernus games, the speaker was filled onto Infernus about 50% of games despite Dynamo being high priority. [3:02]
- Tell other players in your games about the method, which gives them a bigger chance and evens out the odds you take from them. [4:02]
- The longer you wait in queue, the wider the rank gap allowed and the worse the match quality, so for a high quality match cancel and requeue about every four minutes. [5:02]
- For a higher chance of the wanted hero, do the opposite and sit in long queues. [5:02]
- Billy tech: from a dash jump, holding your 1 instead of tapping it carries Ground Strike further, good for catching people; holding too long freezes you in the air. [5:32]
- Billy ult: do not chain and stay on top of the target; chain them, then run as far as you can while keeping line of sight to pull and misposition them, like Holliday's lasso. [6:03]
- Used that way Billy's ult is pretty strong, especially in lower elos where enemies do not break line of sight. [6:03]
- Use Billy's 2 as movement, like Shiv's dash, to get in the air and dodge things like an Infernus ult or an Ivy Stone Form landing on you. [6:34]
- Billy is not as good as Mina but has real validity; he was judged on Fight Night games where he was not played right. [7:05]
