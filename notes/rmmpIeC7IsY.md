## Covers
Midknight (a Calico player) coaches a Celeste player in an Eternus lobby (he guesses at least Eternus 4 to 5) through a game their team was losing badly.
Coaching VOD review, uploaded 2026-08-18. Themes: hitting the wave and orbs in lane, denies, punishing a roaming lane by shoving the tower, not meleeing to kill, Hail Mary plays only in side lanes, timers and urn side, the comeback Rift, Celeste movement (gravity scale, edge and corner boosts), Ethereal Shift timing and Calico counters.
