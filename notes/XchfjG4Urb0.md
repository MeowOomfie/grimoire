# Eternus Seven Build Guide Update
channel: Natan  date: 2025-02-01  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XchfjG4Urb0

## Covers
Seven, skill order change (Storm Cloud third instead of Lightning Ball), Power Surge and Static Charge, when Storm Cloud works and who cancels it, Unstoppable vs Ethereal Shift, split pushing, soul benchmarks

## Claims
- The skill order changed from Power Surge, then stun, then ball, then ult, to Power Surge, then stun, then ult instead of ball. [0:00]
- A tier 3 ult late game is useful for Patron fights (defending or attacking), Mid Boss fights, and late team fights with many people to hit. [0:31]
- Power Surge maxed at 8k souls is the most reliable and strongest damage for early fights, which are usually 1v1 or 2v2 rather than four people at 10 minutes, so it is the best ability to max first. [0:31]
- Static Charge maxed at 16k souls (roughly 14 minutes), with Superior Cooldown imbued on Power Surge, is reliable targeted AoE CC that often hits several people, stuns almost 2 seconds and is up every 18 seconds, multiple times a team fight, which is more impactful than Storm Cloud. [1:01]
- For roughly the first 25 minutes you play the reliable Power Surge build; the tier 3 ult at 30k souls is a bonus late spike to close games and defend your Patron. [1:32]
- 1,200 souls per minute is reasonable or standard farming for Seven, which puts 30k souls at 25 minutes; some games never reach it, which does not matter because you are already functional. [1:32]
- He looks for the most consistent and reliable way to have impact, so he does not max or build for Storm Cloud first. [2:03]
- Storm Cloud is only really good if you hit three or more people, in an open area where they cannot reach cover quickly, and who cannot cancel it: not Lash, Vyper, Holliday, Dynamo or Wraith (and others), and no knockdown. [2:03]
- Unstoppable is a 6k item, so relying on it means walking around for minutes with unsecured souls; you do not want a build that relies on tier 4 items. [2:33]
- Building around a 200 second cooldown ultimate that needs two or three people hit is not as good as building around the abilities, the same lesson Dynamo players are learning about their ult versus their stomp. [2:33]
- Power Surge and Static Charge need far less setup and commitment; if you mess up Storm Cloud with no other abilities maxed, you are useless for 2 minutes. [3:33]
- In lobbies where everyone fights in one lane at 12 minutes, you are better off playing Power Surge Seven and split pushing, because people do not punish split pushing well, you can 1v1 anyone who comes, and if they send several your team wins with the numbers advantage. [3:33]
- The core finishes at roughly 21k, after which you are a functional hero running around killing people with Power Surge and stun, with Power Surge up almost all the time. [4:04]
- To make Storm Cloud good at 30k: Spirit Lifesteal (not mandatory but makes it much better), Healing Booster (optional, for when enemies buy healing reduction, which lower ranks often do not), Suppressor (reduces fire rate of people in the ult) and Spirit Armor. [4:34]
- Unstoppable is not mandatory for ult Seven because you are functional without the ult; Ethereal Shift is much better, a free panic button every 24 seconds, for example dodging a Lash who comes to cancel the ult. [4:34]
- Unstoppable is great when the enemy has several cancels, such as Holliday, Vyper, Lash and a knockdown, because it guarantees 6 seconds of ult and baits them into wasting abilities. [5:05]
