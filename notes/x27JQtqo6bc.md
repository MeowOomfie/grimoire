## Covers
Macro after the 8-minute lane phase: when to farm, fight, split push, push Walkers and take Mid Boss.
Covers trooper sharing math, Sinner's Sacrifice, unsecured souls, side lane farming, a Seven jungle route, roamers and gankers, flexible roles (Ivy), power spikes, playing safe when ahead and risky when behind, and the comeback Urn.
For players who end up poorest on their team or want to get richer.

## Claims
- Macro is every map choice (when to farm, fight, take a fight, go back to base); in-fight choices like which ability to use are micro. [0:31]
- Always be fighting, farming or pushing; if you are doing none of those you are doing nothing. [1:03]
- Have a plan for getting from point A to point B efficiently; time wasted deciding what to do next accumulates and is a big reason some players end up richer. [1:03]
- Always think about the next thing you will do (for example, after killing this Walker, go back or keep pushing). [1:34]
- Whether you farm or fight depends on your hero: Seven needs money to come online, while Abrams, Dynamo and Ivy are strong early without much farm. [2:04]
- Always prioritize lane troopers first: they give more souls than anything else at almost every point; only very late, when you clear jungle very fast, do lanes become less efficient. [2:36]
- Lane troopers also give map control, which earns more farm and wins games. [3:07]
- Trooper sharing: alone in lane you get 100% of the souls; with two people each gets 60% (120% total), so two farming a lane is actually more efficient. [3:07]
- With three people in a lane the total drops below 100%, so you lose souls, plus other lanes and jungle go unfarmed. [3:39]
- Rule of thumb: when farming, at most two people in a lane. More is only for a fight or gank, pushing a Guardian, Walker or base, a team fight, or defending an objective against the whole enemy team. [4:11]
- A third player who sits in lane neither pushing nor looking for a kill loses the team souls every wave. [4:41]
- From 8 minutes, win or lose lane, start taking Sinner's Sacrifices; they are the second highest income in the game and, done fast, slightly better than lane troopers. [5:12]
- Do not sacrifice the lane or your Guardian to go for a Sinner's Sacrifice. [5:12]
- Sinner's Sacrifice costs you HP on most heroes (Billy is an exception, healing with every melee hit). [5:42]
- Unsecured souls are everything except lane troopers: hero kills, camps, Sinner's Sacrifices and boxes; you drop them when you die, a risk against AFK farming that encourages ganking jungles. [5:42]
- Unsecured souls tick down over time to zero, but the best way to clear them is to spend them; unsecured souls are spent last when you buy. [6:42]
- Once you have enough for an item and carry unsecured souls, go to base or a forward shop and spend so you do not drop them in a fight. [7:13]
- Give the Sinner's Sacrifices to carries who need farm, such as Victor, Seven, Haze, Wraith and Infernus; Abrams can also use money well. If a teammate needs the souls more, give them the Sinner's Sacrifice. [7:43]
- Side lane farming: a carry or a player behind in souls takes a side lane alone (100% of trooper souls) and farms nearby jungle between waves; this is usually the fastest way to get souls. [8:43]
- Push the side wave into the enemy side, then farm your own camps, or if ahead go into the enemy jungle (for example the church camp on the left side) while the wave comes back. [9:15]
- Only farm the enemy jungle when you are ahead, can escape a gank, or can win a 1v1 or get out of a 1v2. [9:47]
- Even when behind, if the enemy team is fighting on the other side of the map, push your wave further (even to the Walker) and steal their farm; the more enemies you see elsewhere, the more freedom you have. [9:47]
- Look at the map a lot and plan your next move while farming. [10:17]
- Farming the enemy Sinner's Sacrifice while your wave is at their Walker is worth double, because you gain the souls and take them from the enemy. [10:48]
- Who takes the side lane: usually the carry who needs farm, or whoever is behind after a bad lane. [11:18]
- Great side laners win 1v1s, push the enemy back easily, have escapes and are usually long-range carries: Mirage (Tornado) and Infernus (Flame Dash). [11:49]
- Abrams is a poor side laner: he wants to fight, is a close-range tank, farms troopers slowly and has to get close, which raises his risk of dying. [12:20]
- Side laners usually have decent movement so they can rotate to mid fast; most action revolves around mid because it is the fastest point to reach from anywhere. [12:50]
- There is no dedicated jungler role; every hero farms jungle, but some are faster. [13:20]
- Seven is probably the best jungler now, the fastest in the game once he has max Power Surge and a few items; Wraith is very good, and Pocket and Ivy are also great. [13:20]
- Ivy clears camps very fast with Kudzu Bomb; Pocket nukes camps fast in the early game. [13:50]
- If walking to and clearing a camp feels too long, it probably is; build an inner clock for efficient jungling. [13:50]
- Seven route once he has Power Surge (and perhaps Spirit Lifesteal): start in the right-bottom jungle, which is usually untouched because it has no Sinner's Sacrifice and sits deep in your territory; the left jungle with Sinner's Sacrifices is easy to invade. [14:52]
- On that route, use Cultist Sacrifice on the tier 3 camp if up, clear it, go straight to the tier 2, and keep going with the next camp already in mind. [15:23]
- Slide on the hill and stairs between camps to clear that whole side of the jungle very quickly on Seven. [15:53]
- After the route, if low on HP with many unsecured souls, go back to base picking up boxes and spend; if healthy or a fight is breaking out nearby, buy at the forward secret shop and join the fight. [16:24]
- Farming is just getting from one soul source to the next as fast as possible, while staying ready to fight. [16:24]
- Carries should usually avoid big team fights unless the fight is close, very one-sided for your team (to clean up), or you are far ahead in souls. [17:26]
- Being a carry does not mean never joining fights; join safely. Do not join with 2k unsecured souls at half health; buy at the forward secret shop first. [17:56]
- Lane over jungle always; players in every rank get lost in jungle farming and deprioritize lanes, but lanes give faster souls most of the game, push the wave for map control and force the enemy to react. [17:56]
- Never let your Walker take heavy damage or fall because you are finishing a tier 3 camp next to it. [18:28]
- Grab boxes by walking or sliding past them on your routes; they add up. Calico is very fast at this. Do not usually go out of your way just for boxes. [19:01]
- The middle (Mid Boss area) box farm spawns at 10 minutes with Mid Boss and then about every 2 minutes (he is unsure of the exact interval); it is the highest souls per minute on the map and gives many golden statue buffs. [19:31]
- Roamers and gankers: Abrams, Ivy, Dynamo, or any aggressive player, even Mina, can roam for kills as a play style choice. [20:01]
- Roaming is rotating lane to lane to pressure objectives, look for kills or help teammates; ganking is going to a lane or jungle solely to find and kill an enemy. [20:01]
- Roamers and gankers spend a lot of time around the middle of the map because it reaches everywhere fastest; with a carry on the side lane, an Abrams stays around mid, farms some camps and joins many fights. [20:33]
- Roaming suits pick-off heroes and supports that need little money: Holliday, Paradox, Dynamo, Kelvin. [21:03]
- There is no point ganking as a Seven with no farm; enemies run away and his stun has a delay. [21:35]
- Ivy can play support (save teammates with her ultimate, silence everyone in fights, AoE damage) or farm gun items and become a gun carry; both are strong currently, though balance changes this. [22:06]
- Adjust play style to your team: an Ivy with a Wraith and a Seven on her team should give them the farm and Sinner's Sacrifices, let them side lane, and run the Urn, look for kills, help fights and push or defend objectives. [23:08]
- Wraith and Seven are bad if they do not get money and cannot play support. [23:08]
- If no one on your team benefits from money (for example Ivy, Paradox, Abrams, Kelvin), play Ivy as the carry, take the side lane and farm; late game needs a reliable damage dealer, while early fights depend less on comps. [24:10]
- A great time to fight is right after a big power spike: an item or level up that gives a big damage boost. [24:40]
- Example: Haze buying Siphon Bullets as her first big item around 6.4k souls is a massive spike; she is weak before and strong after. [24:40]
- Levelling your first ability to its big upgrade (when it gains a major bonus effect) is almost always a power spike, as is buying bigger items. [25:10]
- At equal 12k net worth, having 3k unspent makes you potentially much weaker; buy before taking a fight, and spend before you know big fights are coming. [25:40]
- If your support picks up the Urn and your team rotates, decide whether to join the Urn fight; if so go to the shop right away, buy, and show up, or buy and split push the other side. [26:11]
- Unspent souls are no reason to skip a fight right next to you at full HP; a 3v3 beats letting your team lose a 3v2 while you go back to buy. Joining a nearby fight is almost always better than not joining. [26:42]
- If you skip a fight, still use the map state: do anything except farm your own jungle, because farming your own jungle gives up the advantages the fight creates. [27:13]
- When four or five enemies are fighting on the far side, push the Walker on your side; at worst one defender shows up for a 1v1 and you can back off safely. [27:43]
- During fights or skirmishes, split push or steal the enemy jungle; even pushing out a wave to force an enemy to rotate beats farming your own jungle, the biggest beginner mistake. [28:45]
- Walkers are very hard to crack while defended: they give passive bullet and spirit resistance to nearby allies, and they shoot, laser or stun you up close. [29:16]
- Randomly pushing a Walker as two with all enemies unseen almost always draws one or two defenders and risks death or wasted time. [29:47]
- Push Walkers when you have an advantage, such as after killing one or two enemies with two lanes pushing into their side. [30:17]
- Ideally push multiple Walkers at once (for example two left and four right) so the enemy splits attention and makes mistakes. [30:17]
- A big team fight on the far side of the map is a good time to push a Walker alone, especially on a hero with high Walker damage. [30:47]
- After winning a team fight up at least two players (3v1, 4v2), always push an objective; after about 15 minutes, take Mid Boss instead. [30:47]
- Around 15 minutes, respawn times are long enough that after a won fight you can kill Mid Boss before the enemy reacts with enough people. [31:17]
- Call Mid Boss for your team: with enemies down two or three for 20 to 25 seconds, send five or six to Mid Boss. [31:48]
- Mid Boss is by far the strongest buff in the game; a team 10k to 20k behind that wins a fight and takes it can come back or reach even. [32:19]
- Up 10k to 20k, winning a fight and taking Mid Boss means you cannot lose in about 80% of games if you play correctly after. [32:19]
- After the second Mid Boss the team has four Rejuvenator lives, effectively fighting 10 versus 6. [32:50]
- If the enemy is down many players they rarely steal more than one of the three or four Rejuvenator buffs. [33:20]
- Consider Mid Boss after 15 minutes and more and more as the game goes on, because respawn timers grow; two kills at 25 minutes give plenty of time. [33:20]
- Advanced: bring Mid Boss to the 60% health threshold where it screams, then leave as a group of six to look for more kills before finishing it, if unsure you can secure it. [33:51]
- Yamato and Lash have ultimates that can steal Mid Boss; Yamato goes Unstoppable in his ultimate and perma-parries to stop your heavy melees on the Rejuvenator. [34:21]
- Mid Boss should be your top thought after 15 to 20 minutes when you win a big team fight. [34:21]
- Be ready to contest Mid Boss: if you lose a fight at 15 minutes but four of you are alive and alert, gather and contest, especially with stun ultimates around the Rejuvenator (for example Ivy). [34:52]
- In public matches the enemy rarely sends all six to Mid Boss, often making it a 4v4 you can punish. [35:23]
- If up, get the whole team to do Mid Boss; if behind, be aware of the threat. [35:23]
- The first Mid Boss buff lasts 4 minutes: dead teammates respawn up to three times, and the team gets fire rate, more HP and more spirit damage. [35:53]
- Do not rush objectives right after Mid Boss or act invincible; pushing their last Walker as six lets all six defend with the defender's advantage, which is risky. [36:23]
- With the 4 minutes of buff, take more space, go deeper into their side, steal their jungle, and gankers look for another kill, then push objectives or the base. [36:54]
- The enemy base is the hardest thing to crack and close to their spawn (Lash's ultimate can throw you into their spawn); unless about 40k or more ahead, farm their jungle and look for a kill or two first, then take shrines and the Patron. [37:24]
- If you are ahead play safe, and the further behind you are the riskier you must play. [38:24]
- Comeback souls: the team behind earns more from sources like the Urn and hero kills, and the team ahead earns less, so do not take even trades when ahead. [38:54]
- When behind, sitting in base loses; leave as six to look for a kill, maybe pop a Rejuvenator early or kill the same person twice (a Rejuvenator cannot revive the same person twice), making it 6v5. [39:25]
- Every fight a team that is behind does not take is one step closer to losing; comebacks do not come from sitting in base. [39:55]
- Sitting in base gives roughly a 5% chance to win; looking for a kill might raise it to 20% or 30%. [40:25]
- Behind, take smart fights: do not push objectives, look for kills on greedy enemies farming your jungle. [40:55]
- If the enemy hyper carry is on one side lane, go as six and fight the enemies on the other side of the map. [41:25]
- The Urn has three drop spots (forward toward the enemy, middle, and your side); if you are more than 10% of souls behind when you first pick it up, the drop spot is on your side. [41:56]
- Comeback Urn: wait for it to spawn, ideally control that lane, pick it up and deliver it deep in your own territory; it is fairly safe and gives comeback souls. [42:26]
- Even fights are good when behind: dying while getting one enemy kill helps because you get more souls from their death than they get from yours. Do not worry about K/D. [42:56]
- When behind, taking a Walker is worth dying for. [43:29]
