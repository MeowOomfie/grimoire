# Deadlock Ranked Has a Big Problem
channel: Natan
date: 2026-08-18
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CKSjFQJG2Us

## Covers
Opinion and feedback on the first ranked season's system: how demotion shields and win streak bonuses make rank depend on the order of wins and losses, the harsh MMR reset and grind, long queues at the top, and suggested fixes (fewer shields, recent win rate bonus, longer seasons, no duo queue at Eternus).
Little direct gameplay advice, but it lays out the exact point and shield math, useful for anyone trying to understand their rank changes.

## Claims
- About 50 days were left in the first ranked season at the time of the video. [0:00]
- In the current system you can climb with a negative win rate; rank rewards luck in the order of results and play time over skill. [0:00]
- A win or loss is worth about 300 points, but what a result does depends on when it happens because of demotion shields. [1:02]
- Example from Oracle 4 at zero points, losing seven in a row: the first two losses only use up Oracle 4's two protection shields, the third demotes to Oracle 3 at 700 points, then 400, then 100, and the sixth and seventh use up Oracle 3's two shields, so seven losses cost about 1,000 points instead of 2,100. [1:02]
- Win streak bonuses: a three win streak adds 70 points and a four win streak adds 90, so four straight wins from there give 1,200 plus 160 for 1,360 points and promotion back to Oracle 4 with two new shields. [1:32]
- Following that pattern, 10 losses and five wins can still leave you 300 points above where you started. [2:03]
- Same record, different result: at zero points in a rank, loss, loss, win, win, win reaches 970 points, while win, loss, win, loss, win reaches only 300. [2:33]
- Practical consequence: losses spent on fresh demotion shields after a rank up are effectively free, while losses in the middle of a run cost full points. [3:03]
- Players placed above their skill drop far more slowly than they should, and may hold the rank with a negative win rate. [2:03]
- Over the season the whole population drifts upward, so you meet the same players at a higher rank a month later without having improved. [3:33]
- There are six sub ranks per rank, which rewards playing lots of games. [4:04]
- The system's upside: a new rank feels like a safe checkpoint, and short-term win streak points feel good. [4:04]
- Suggested fix: one demotion shield per sub rank and two for the lowest sub rank of each division. [4:34]
- Suggested fix: replace win streak bonuses with a bonus for recent win rate, for example extra points for winning 60 to 70% of your last 20 or 30 games. [5:04]
- At the time there were fewer than 30 Eternus players worldwide; Phantom 4 was about top 350 in Europe and top 1,000 worldwide. [5:35]
- At about three games a day even with a 60% win rate, getting back to Eternus takes a very long time, so players are gated by play time. [5:35]
- The grind pushes players to spam the one hero they think climbs fastest instead of trying new heroes in ranked. [6:05]
- Top Ascendant and Eternus players have very long queues, because many former Eternus players are still spread across Phantom, Ascendant, Eternus and even Oracle; some Eternus players use alt accounts for shorter queues, which unbalances games further. [6:35]
- Statlocker has a page showing where players landed after placements; both former Eternus and Ascendant players were placed in high Oracle, making high Oracle very unbalanced. [7:05]
- Suggested fix: seasons at least twice as long; the matchmaking update patch notes said future seasons are likely to be longer. [7:35]
- Suggested fix: do not reset players three whole divisions lower, or give increased gains until a player returns to their previous season's rank. [8:05]
- A soft reset is fine in principle, since without one players who peak and then play rarely never drop, but this season's reset was too harsh. [8:35]
- Heroes with game-breaking bugs should be disabled in ranked until fixed; a Doorman was abusing a bug to push players through a door into tunnels that regular heroes cannot get out of. [9:05]
- Duo queue should be removed at least at Eternus: a top duo with comms can pick strong meta lane heroes, swap to the mid lane, win every lane and get far more agency over the game. [9:35]
- Giving extra points or smaller losses for MVP or key player is a bad idea, because any performance score can be gamed; Statlocker's performance scores heavily favor carries over supports for MVP. [10:05]
