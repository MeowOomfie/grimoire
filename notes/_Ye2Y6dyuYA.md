- Drifter does a shocking amount of damage, even very early. [0:31]

- He bought Healing Rite in a lane where he was taking heavy damage. [2:04]
- Rem's luggage carts were made much bigger, which makes them easier to hit and the hero more accessible. [3:05]
- He bought Stalker (previously named Backstabber). [4:07]
- An enemy Rem stops your lane from clearing waves fast; Rem is one of the worst heroes to face in lane and great to have on your team. [4:37]
- He went in once Healing Rite had him topped up enough to risk the trade. [5:40]
- Spirit power is mostly useless on Drifter; his damage feels mostly gun. [6:42]
- Items bought after lane: Point Blank, Sprint Boots, Enduring Speed. [12:05]
- Even his level one ult gives a huge burst of movement speed. [13:39]
- Drifter is extremely fast with his ult buffs, though you do not have them for long. [16:48]
- He wanted to try Veil Walker on Drifter but was unsure it would be good. [16:48]
- He bought Toxic Bullets because the enemy had an Abrams. [17:18]
- Plan to hold an objective: take the urn and blind (ult) everyone who comes to contest it. [18:56]
- He tried Ricochet on chat's suggestion; he had never used it on Drifter. [19:57]
- He considered Duration Extender for his ult, or for his 2. [20:27]
- Mid boss is not a good take while the enemies are alive. [22:03]
- Paradox's swap can now pull up to five people out of mid boss. [22:03]
- Fortitude is a weak item in general but decent on Drifter, because he spends a long time out of combat. [22:03]
- Fortitude's heal stops if you take a single tick of damage, so avoid damage while relying on it. [24:43]
- Against enemies with little resist, he chose Headhunter over Crippling Headshot to fill a slot with extra weapon damage, while admitting it is not very good. [23:38]
- His damage scales off assists too: Drifter gets stacks whether he kills or assists, so kills do not matter on him. [25:13]
- He ended with 5 kills and 21 assists and still had his stacks, which he says shows kill count does not matter on Drifter. [28:55]
