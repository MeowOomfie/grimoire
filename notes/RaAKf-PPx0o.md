# Complete Guide to Climb With Seven Beginner Friendly | Deadlock
channel: Tonydadof4  date: 2026-08-31  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RaAKf-PPx0o

## Covers
Seven beginner guide in five pillars: Statlocker build, Power Surge, Lightning Ball, Static Charge, Storm Cloud, stun-surge-shoot-ball combo, ult in team fights not solo, Fleetfoot slide farming, Unstoppable vs Indomitable, farm priority (waves, Sinner's Sacrifice, jungle, boxes and golden statues), Escalating Exposure, stealing enemy camps, positioning in the back, Infuser, Arcane Surge, weaknesses (mobility, squishy, cooldown reliant)

## Claims
- There are five pillars to learning Seven, from the most basic to the most advanced. [0:00]
- Pillar one, the build: he uses Statlocker's top daily build, which has the meta items, counter buys and actives, updates regularly, and lists the buy order left to right. [0:00]
- Pillar two, abilities: Power Surge (the 3) is his main damage dealer; it makes his weapon stronger with free ricochet that bounces between enemies, is really good for clearing waves, and should be up almost all the time. [0:30]
- Lightning Ball can be thrown at people or placed on the ground, where it stays in one spot. [1:00]
- Static Charge (the 2) stuns the target a few seconds after you put it on them, for about half a second. [1:00]
- Storm Cloud (the 4) is a big area that constantly does damage over time, but enemies out of line of sight take no damage. [1:00]
- Combo that wins most of his fights: initiate with the stun (enemies back off and do not chase), pop Power Surge, shoot them as they get stunned, then throw the balls on them. [1:31]
- Use the ult in as many team fights as possible; the more enemies the better, since you deal more damage, can push them out of areas and win objectives more easily. [1:31]
- Do not solo ult most of the time; doing it is desperation when you are dying anyway, so take the death and keep the ult for when you are back. [2:01]
- Fleetfoot removes the movement speed penalty while shooting; pop Power Surge and slide, and while sliding with the ability up you get (in his words) infinite powered bullets with ricochet, farming faster and dealing more damage without reloading; it has a short cooldown so you can spam it. [2:01]
- Unstoppable is really good for the ultimate: knockdowns, stuns and other CC can end Storm Cloud early, and Unstoppable nullifies all of it for a short duration (he thinks about 9 seconds) on about a one minute recharge. [2:31]
- Indomitable is a passive that blocks only the first stun that would knock you out of the ult; a second one still works. [3:31]
- He recommends Unstoppable over Indomitable, Indomitable for newer players, and Unstoppable when the enemy has multiple ways to get you out of the ult. [4:01]
- Pillar three, farm, is the lifeblood of the hero: Seven has the fastest farm in the game and clears jungles in seconds. [4:01]
- Farm priority one is waves, the most souls you can get; clear them easily with the balls or Power Surge. [4:32]
- Priority two is the Sinner's Sacrifice (captioned "center sacrifice"), which spawns at 8 minutes and is very easy to farm between waves. [4:32]
- Priority three is jungle camps, which is what Seven is really good at. [4:32]
- Priority four is boxes, taken while rotating; the golden statues have a chance to give one permanent buff each, and the more buffs you get the stronger you are regardless of money. [5:03]
- You usually have a 30 to 60 second window before waves come back, enough to do Sinner's Sacrifice, jungle and boxes in between. [5:33]
- With this farm you should have the most souls on your team or in the game; Seven has a lot of expensive items and needs it. [5:33]
- Rush Escalating Exposure; it is one of the last buys in the build but a late game power spike, and once you have it look for more team fights. [5:33]
- When up in money and the waves have pushed past you, steal enemy camps, which is double the money because you gain it and deny it; farm backwards, enemy camps first then your own. [6:04]
- If you are down in souls and losing lane, do not feed in the enemy jungle; farm your own jungle to have a chance of coming back. [6:35]
- Pillar four, positioning: in fights stay back, use abilities to help with damage and stay alive; do not stand in the middle like a tank, since Seven is squishy with little base HP until he gets sustain items. [6:35]
- Seven is a high priority target because of his team fight ult, so stay in the back, more hidden, behind your tanks and aggressive teammates. [7:05]
- The only time to get aggressive is the late game once you have Infuser, an active that basically gives spirit lifesteal, so your abilities heal you and you are basically unkillable in your ultimate. [7:05]
- To reach Infuser you must stay alive and play in the back for the first half of the game; be patient and do not feed. [7:36]
- Pillar five, weaknesses: Seven has really bad mobility; most builds include a stamina item, and Arcane Surge helps both spirit damage and mobility, so play passively before you have it. [8:08]
- Seven is very squishy early and mid game, so farm the jungle after you have some spirit lifesteal, and never go into the Sinner's Sacrifice super low, where you cannot get out and enemies can kill you and steal your bag. [8:38]
- Seven is mainly a spirit damage hero and his gun alone is kind of bad, so do not rush into a fight right after using all abilities on a wave or jungle; wait until they are back up. [9:08]
- Even fully maxed, after spending all cooldowns in a fight, hang back until they recharge: get in, burst, back off, give cover fire, then go back in, and repeat. [9:38]
