## Covers
Educational commentary with live comms from an Eternus solo queue game on the new patch (2026-10-03), playing Billy with Haze in lane against Vyper and Holliday, enemy team with Rat King.
Winning a weak lane through the new box system and healing packs, rift and urn play, blue wave priority, playing around the Rat King ultimate, and shot calling to win a late game despite a draft disadvantage.
