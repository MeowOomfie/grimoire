# The Metro Deadlock Patch (Eternus vs Initiate Testing)
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-11-18
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wtiK_9ejiQc

## Covers
Metro writes his own (unofficial, modded) balance patch in two hours, then casts custom lobbies: Eternus players first, then a mixed low Ascendant lobby with an Initiate in it.
Most numbers below are the mod's changes, not the live game; the real game facts and advice are his commentary on the meta, items and play.
Main thesis: number changes matter a lot at high rank and much less at low rank, where misplays decide games.
For players curious about balance, item value (Improved Spirit, Active Reload, Counterspell, anti-heal) and why low rank balance complaints are mostly misplays.
