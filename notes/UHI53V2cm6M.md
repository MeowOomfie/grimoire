# Roles in Deadlock Explained
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-10-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHI53V2cm6M

## Covers
A 2024 explainer (the game has changed a lot since, including drafting) on the roles emerging in tournament play: support, carry, tank and initiator, which heroes filled them, how to build a hero pool, and how to itemize around your team's draft. Breaks down two tournament drafts (NA and EU). For players wanting to think about team composition.

## Claims
- Most team compositions have a carry, a tank and a support, but itemization lets many heroes become tanky or deal lots of damage, so roles are more flexible than in other MOBAs. [1:02]
- Team composition gives an advantage between equally skilled teams: a team with Kelvin and Abrams has it easier than one with Haze, Infernus, Wraith and Seven who all just want to farm the jungle. [1:34]
- The two roles found in every tournament draft are support and carry. [2:05]
- The support farms less than teammates and buys items like Divine Barrier, Healing Nova and above all Rescue Beam. [2:05]
- Rescue Beam is mandatory on any support-archetype hero in its current state; it is extremely strong. [2:05] [2:35]
- Heroes most often seen as support: Kelvin, Ivy, McGinnis, Bebop, Viscous and sometimes Dynamo, who fits less because he tends not to buy Rescue Beam. [2:35]
- Support Mirage and support Vindicta have also been played. [2:35] [3:05]
- Carries are good at farming the jungle and output tons of damage with farm; currently mainly Haze, Infernus, Seven and Wraith, who dominate the late game if allowed to farm. [3:05] [3:35]
- His team makes sure to draft at least one fairly tanky frontliner and at least one initiator, such as Paradox with her swap or Lash with his ultimate. [3:35]
- Always draft at least one support-like hero who buys Rescue Beam and at least one hero who farms the jungle well and scales into the late game. [3:35] [4:06]
- Do not draft too many heroes who just want to farm the jungle; you want heroes who can be active early in the current meta. [4:06]
- Build a hero pool of heroes that serve a similar purpose: an Abrams main could add Mo & Krill and then Lady Geist, all tanky control heroes. [4:06] [4:37]
- Be able to play at least one hero outside your main role: an Abrams player might add Paradox for initiation, utility and damage; a Seven carry player might add Abrams. [4:37] [5:08]
- If role queue comes, as in Dota, you will still have to play your off role sometimes. [5:08]
- In a draft of Kelvin, Shiv, Abrams, Pocket, Wraith and Lady Geist, Kelvin is the support (Rescue Beam, support items, moving around) and usually one of the lowest net worth players; Wraith is the carry. [5:39] [6:09]
- In a draft of Yamato, Warden, Infernus, Viscous, Lash and Paradox, Viscous is the support who picks up Rescue Beam and Infernus is the carry, likely the highest net worth. [6:09] [6:43]
- Lady Geist and Shiv are flexible and can itemize for a balance of tankiness and damage depending on team needs. [6:43]
- If your team already has several naturally tanky heroes, Shiv should itemize more aggressively; watch how teammates such as Lady Geist itemize and adjust. [7:14]
- As Shiv you can choose a tankier melee build or a higher DPS bleed build with the knives, depending on what Lady Geist buys. [7:44]
- Warden, Yamato, Lash, Infernus and Viscous's ball are decent teamfight ultimates. [7:44]
- As Warden in a teamfight-ult draft, building around your ultimate makes sense; but if your team lacks weapon damage (Infernus is mostly spirit), a gun build makes your team harder to itemize against. [7:44] [8:15]
- In the current spirit-heavy meta most people build Spirit Armor and underestimate Bullet Armor, so having both spirit and bullet damage on your team makes you harder to itemize against; this is underused. [8:15] [8:45]
- In a draft of Wraith, Yamato, Lady Geist, Warden, Seven and Kelvin, Kelvin is the support and either Wraith or Seven can carry. [8:45] [9:15]
- With two jungle carries, the Wraith should itemize more aggressively to be useful in fights, for example skipping Tesla Bullets since Seven already farms the jungle well with his kit; get your power spike earlier. [9:15] [9:46]
- In a draft with Haze and Infernus as carries, Ivy is probably the support; Dynamo could also buy Rescue Beam but is more of a control hero. [9:46] [10:16]
- With two carries, one must be less greedy because the map does not have infinite farm; Infernus is the likelier one to play active since he can do it with fewer items, though Haze can itemize as an invisibility assassin going for solo kills. [10:16] [10:47]
- If your team already has three jungling heroes (for example Infernus, Haze and Seven) and you are also a jungler, itemize differently that game. [11:17]
- Think critically about what role your team needs rather than just following trends and builds. [11:17] [11:47]
