## Covers
General guide to power spikes: what they are, example spikes for Wraith, Haze and Paradox, how to play around your own spike and how to shut down or survive the enemy's.
moe9times, educational guide, no one coached. Recorded around the 2025-03-01 patch.

## Claims
- Heroes like Haze are not overpowered all game; they are overpowered at a certain point in the game, and so are you. [0:00]
- A power spike is a specific point in a match when a hero becomes significantly stronger, usually from a skill point or an item purchase. [0:30]
- Every hero has one to three power spikes; some come early, some mid game, some late. [0:30]
- Wraith early spike: at 22,000 souls (as captioned) she can put two points into her cards; with Extra Charge and Infuser she has three cards and gets a fourth while shooting. [0:30]
- Going all in with cards while shooting, that Wraith does about 800 damage in a very short time, enough to take almost anyone from full to zero at that point. [1:00]
- Late spike example: Haze with max ultimate, Unstoppable and expensive gun items feels uncontrollable. [1:00]
- The 3,000 soul mark matters: getting your ultimate before your opponent is huge, especially in a solo lane. [1:31]
- If you have your ultimate and they do not, press the advantage and try to kill them; a kill widens the soul gap, usually gives a free wave, and the item lead keeps them out of lane. [1:31]
- If you are behind and the opponent will reach 3K first, either try to kill them immediately or play the lane safer so you cannot be dived when they get their ultimate. [2:03]
- Know your hero's biggest power spike so you can get active around the map when you hit it, and force the game into the state where you are strongest. [2:03]
- Paradox: get active at around 9 to 10K souls, because Headhunter is an early core item that gives a decent spike; roam between lanes looking for picks by forcing two-on-one fights with a teammate. [2:33]
- Haze: get very active after buying something like Burst Fire, around 15 to 20K souls when ahead; force quick fights with your team, burst enemies and disappear. [3:03]
- A good Haze comes online and pressures the map well before late game if she is ahead; no hero should feel safe sitting solo against her. [3:33]
- Decide whether to play safe or aggressive early by identifying your strengths and the enemy's weaknesses. [3:33]
- Against late game scaling heroes, create pressure with split pushing: the carry cannot be everywhere at once. [4:04]
- If a fight breaks out across the map and you will not make it in time, pressure enemy objectives harder and steal enemy camps on the way out instead of scrambling over. [4:04]
- If you can force the enemy to chase you and get out before they kill you, you create map advantage for your team. [4:34]
- It is fine to deviate from your build to shut down an enemy; winning is about killing the Patron, not a perfect DPS build. [5:05]
- Enemy strong through spells: buy a silencing hex (as captioned). Elusive and mobile enemy: buy Slowing Hex or Knockdown so they cannot get away. [5:05]
- Buy anti-heal against a lifesteal power spike (Geist given as the example). [5:35]
- Curse is the expensive option to shut down a carry; with Soul Rebirth removed, a cursed ultra carry can only sit and wait. [5:35]
- Surviving a few extra seconds can decide a fight; dying without making any impact is one of the worst things you can do. [6:05]
- Against a snowballing Lash with big Ground Strikes or other high burst and stuns, consider Ethereal Shift. [6:05]
- Against a gun Haze shredding you with bullets, consider Metal Skin; both buy around 4 extra seconds for your team to connect with you. [6:36]
- Against damage over time from Infernus or Pocket, buy Debuff Reducer and later Debuff Remover. [6:36]
- Debuff Remover's 40% base resist means Affliction and burn do 40% less damage and stuns and disarms are 40% shorter, on top of the active purge. [7:06]
- Debuff Remover works on almost everything except Curse (you cannot use items while cursed), but it still shortens Curse by 40%. [7:06]
- There are not many games where the debuff items are bad. [7:37]
- During downtime (shopping, ziplining to base, dead), check enemy items and skill points: knowing they have a Silence Glyph or that their carry just hit a key skill point changes how to fight and who to use actives on. [8:08]
- Summary: maximize your power spikes, prevent or minimize the enemy's, create pressure away from the enemy carry, and buy to disable enemies or survive. [8:38]
