## Covers
Deathy coaches Zyzx_, a long-time streamer with about 50 hours in Deadlock, through an Initiate 1 lobby replay on Venator (lane partner Seven).
Light, fundamentals-only session meant to show to new friends: the trooper rule in lane, trooper damage, parrying the tower, following the build, Fortitude as a crutch, minimap habits, Sinner's Sacrifice at 8:00, Cultist Sacrifice, the Urn, side lanes and solo kills, and wave pressure past 20 minutes.
