## Covers
A 3-minute Rem guide (March 2026): pros and cons, abilities, combos, early game wave priority and tunnel box routes, mid game sinner steals, Urn runs, team fights and builds.
For players picking up Rem as a support.

## Claims
- Rem is the most active support, with the highest skill ceiling, carry potential and map impact of any support. [0:00]
- He is the only hero that can use the tunnels without an ability, giving him a unique way to create farm. [0:00]
- Cons: he blows up quickly when focused, needs competent teammates, can't solo carry, and needs more itemization knowledge than any other role. [0:00]
- His 1 throws a pillow that, on hitting enemies, takes 5 seconds off all his active cooldowns; great for getting the ult up quickly, and the refund procs on camps and minions too. [0:30]
- His 2 jumps to an ally, making him briefly invulnerable and healing himself and the ally for missing health plus flat healing. [0:30]
- At tier two his 2 also grants bonus item duration, range and barrier effectiveness; maxed it gives a temporary Boundless Spirit for +15% spirit. [0:30]
- His 3 sends a helper to an ally or trooper: troopers get resist and damage, allies get spirit resist and movement speed. [1:00]
- Helpers can also steal sinner's sacrifices, secure bridge buffs, farm boxes and buy from the shop. [1:00]
- Recast his 2 to hop to another ally, refreshing the duration and the heal. [1:00]
- Ult: a 2 second channel that slows and stops movement abilities of anyone he looks at, then puts them to sleep; they wake when the duration expires or when damage passes the sleep threshold. [1:00]
- Combo: when on top of an ally who is being chased, pop off behind them and ult; the momentum from your 2 gains a little more distance. [1:30]
- Combo: use your 3 on a minion, then 2 onto it to become invulnerable, dodging things like Bebop's bomb or buying time when ganked. [1:30]
- Early game: play active; force wave priority by using your 3 on the heal trooper. [1:30]
- Use that lead to run tunnel box routes, giving your lane partner solo waves if they are comfortable anchoring. [2:00]
- Or use the lane pressure for a box route ending between your lane and an ally's lane, then look for a gank. [2:00]
- Mid game: be on the enemy double sinner's sacrifices at 8 minutes to steal them, then spend the mid game stealing as many enemy sinners as possible. [2:00]
- Pretty much never use your 3 to farm your own sinners; that griefs your team, since that farm should go to your carries. [2:00]
- Late game: run Urns off cooldown, using the fact that you can cash the Urn and immediately 2 away to secure many Urns. [2:31]
- Team fights: spam your 2 on everyone in the fight to heal as much as possible. [2:31]
- Builds: Lightbringer's for advanced players who know how to itemize, PeerContent's for a simple left to right build, or his own for a bit of both. [2:31]
