# The Best Build for Every Hero in Deadlock (And How to Play It)

## Covers
One recommended community build per hero, picked from hundreds of high rank games, grouped by role (off carries, carries, supports, initiators and hybrids), with the core items, skill order and how to play each. Build authors are named as captioned; the build IDs are in the video comments. For any player picking a build for their hero in the current meta.

## Claims
- Some of the top listed builds on the build page are made up and will lower your win rate; these picks come from hundreds of high rank games and both work in and counter the current meta. [0:00]
- Apollo (off carry): Rumples' melee build, a hybrid focused on Riposte instead of the old full spirit build that did its damage with the 3. [0:31]
- Apollo: with Riposte at level two you get a free heavy melee on the enemy every time, which also procs Spirit Snatch and Lifestrike. [0:31]
- Apollo: go in and Riposte, then use your 3 (or the other way round), and use your 1 to escape or finish kills; incredibly strong mid game. [1:02]
- Calico: Reichke's build. Go in from the side with the cat, proc your 2, proc Spirit Snatch and Stalker, throw Gloom Bombs and hit a Mystic Shot for one big burst combo. [1:02]
- Calico: later go in with Arctic Blast; practice the combo, then you are a one-shot machine. [1:33]
- Calico is not very meta because everyone is tanky with big HP pools, so even this one-shot build will not get one-shots every time. [1:33]
- Celeste: Deuce's build, the best Celeste player; classic Radiant Regeneration and Suppressor, go in and never die with Torment Pulse. [1:33]
- Celeste: follow the build left to right, maybe add a counter item like Counterspell if you need it; it can carry you to Eternus. [2:04]
- Danny: played as a spirit one-shot nuker like Calico: hit your 1, then 2, then 3 and the enemy is usually dead. [2:04]
- Danny: shoot your gun only once to proc Express Shot, hit all your bullets, hit your stun and enemies blow up; later buy Toxic Bullets to finish them, since the 3 procs damage over time effects. [2:36]
- Danny: do not go too gun heavy; full gun Danny is not how Eternus players play him. [2:36]
- Danny: go in, regenerate with your 2, go out, in and out, play hyperaggressive at all times; the best 1v1 nuker in the game. [3:06]
- Drifter: AL's very heavy melee build that maxes the 1 first for an early silence. [3:06]
- Drifter: once you have Spirit Snatch and Hunter's Aura you can 1v1 anyone inside the isolation radius. [3:06]
- Drifter: win the lane, get some money in the mid game, then run around getting stacks nonstop and snowball; do not AFK farm, play him aggressive. [3:36]
- Grey Talon: played as a hybrid; you definitely want Sharpshooter plus a lot of spirit items for damage with your 1 and 4. [3:36]
- Grey Talon: sit on a lane, usually holding mid or a side lane, and use the bird whenever it is up to get stacks; Michael's build, with Stamina Mastery to get around faster. [3:36]
- Grey Talon: play him as a glass cannon with some of the highest DPS in the game. [4:06]
- Lash: Pazilian's Flog Slam build, a hybrid with an early Headhunter but mostly spirit damage. [4:06]
- Lash: hit your slam, then proc Flog, proc Quicksilver Reload, hit your shots with Mystic Shot and Headhunter for heavy burst; late game go either damage over time or more front-loaded damage. [4:06]
- Mina: Cosmetical's build, unchanged for months; lots of spirit burst and insane survivability, and with Diviner's Kevlar late game you cannot die. [4:36]
- Mina: Spirit Burn; do not build too many gun items, raw spirit is the way to go. [5:06]
- Pocket: Cosmetical's build; buy a bit more gun early since his gun does a lot of damage if you hit your Barrage stacks. [5:06]
- Pocket: Cultist Sacrifice for the extra range, then the classic spirit combo, then items to stay alive forever: Warp Stone, Ethereal Shift, Diviner's Kevlar. [5:06]
- Vindicta: Cashcory's rank one build; played like Grey Talon with some gun and some spirit, since the snipe is your main execution and damage tool while the gun still hits hard. [5:36]
- Vindicta: Counterspell, Sharpshooter and Rapid Recharge are necessary; late game the build goes more gun, left click and debuff heavy. Going more spirit than this is overkill. [5:36]
- Violet: Metro's Hyper Carry build; you have to go Radiant Regeneration and Healing Booster, then double shielding and Warp Stone, since her cooldowns drop every time you hit your 1. [6:07]
- Violet has insane damage scaling into late game and can hard carry from a good start. [6:37]
- Violet: after maxing the 1, max the ult (first or second, second in this build), at least by the time you fight the first Mid Boss; it is maybe the best ult in the game right now. [6:37]
- Vyper: T-Bird's build; mostly gun damage with Split Shot as a core item. [7:07]
- Vyper: roam in the mid game for pickoffs; do not play her as a hard carry that only farms. [7:07]
- Vyper struggles in this meta because she is squishy and everyone has a lot of HP, so play a bit more passive; buy the mid game items left to right (the mid game section displays too small). [7:07]
- Yamato: ABL's hybrid melee build with Mystic Regeneration; grapple in with Flying Strike, proc your melee items and play brawly front line where you never die. [7:37]
- Yamato: be careful with heavy melees; skip Melee Charge and Crushing Fists if you get parried too much. Go in with your 2, bait out the parry, then heavy melee and the enemy is almost always dead. [8:08]
- Graves (carry): resurging because the meta is slow and in long drawn out teamfights she steals a lot of spirit resist and max weapon damage; a sustained damage over time character, played as a carry. [8:08]
- Graves: O's build; early Heroic Aura, late game Echo Shard on your wall (or on your skulls), and Refresher. [8:39]
- Graves: if you are your team's hard carry, go more AoE left click items late game: Inhibitor, Ricochet, Silencer or Toxic Bullets. [9:09]

- Lady Geist: same build for over a year with Radiant Regeneration core; early spirit spike, stay alive early with Life Drain and your 1, transition to a full gun carry late. [9:40]

- Rat King: farm the jungle nonstop until your late game items, then become the 1v6 monster. [11:11]
- Seven: great in low rank, not in high rank; Shaw's build, an ability range item is core since every ability benefits from range. [11:11]

- Seven: once you have Escalating Exposure start taking fights; before then avoid fights unless it is a Rift fight and your ult is up. [11:41]
