# How to Support | Deadlock Role Guide
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-05-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPFxSm60VFg

## Covers
Role guide for supports: low income box-maxing heroes with utility items and kits. Which heroes fit, farming without starving carries, counter itemizing, lane box routes and ganking from the bridge, roaming etiquette, urn tricks, vision, and how to fight (peel, punish squishies, melee on cooldown).
The coach argues support is the second best role to climb on, and lane is its most important stage.

## Claims
- Supports are the heroes who box max and play a lower income position, and buy utility items like Rescue Beam, Healing Nova or Heroic Aura while their kits give out more utility. [0:00]
- Supports: Dynamo, Paige, Rem, The Doorman, Viscous, Kelvin, Ivy on the current meta build, and at a very high level Sinclair, though average players rarely see support Sinclair. [0:00]
- You can climb on support; in his opinion it is the best role to climb on after gun carry, at least on Kelvin, Rem and Dynamo, though there is an argument against Paige, who mostly places barriers. [0:30]
- A big mistake is supports playing too greedy; you can farm a bit more in solo queue than in coordinated play, but never deprive your team of Sinner's Sacrifices (Rem should never use his 3 to take your own). [1:00]
- If you are doing well and want to take more farm, steal the enemy's farm instead, by invading yourself or with your pick hero or a strong mid game hero. [1:00]
- Box maxing is great on support and the most impactful farm you can have; when losing or unable to invade, farm boxes, which creates farm out of nothing without hurting teammates. [1:31]
- Exception: Paige can take the first doubles that spawn using her barriers, but no later doubles. [1:31]
- At low elo, when your Sinner's Sacrifices (especially doubles) are up, tell a teammate to farm them; if the camp is still up after about 2 minutes, take it yourself. [2:01]
- Farm is only about a third as valuable on a support as on a Wraith, Seven or Yamato who wants those Sinner's Sacrifices. [2:01]
- Same rule for waves: ask a teammate to catch a wave pushing into you, and if you are catching a side lane always ask someone to take it; still, a support catching a side lane is better than nobody. [2:31]
- Support is the role where items matter most, should constantly change, and should counter itemize the most. [2:31]
- Into Dynamo, rush Curse; into Seven, Grey Talon or Vindicta, get Knockdown. [3:02]
- Into grabs like Bebop and Paradox, or pick heroes like Holliday and a Lash ult, buy Rescue Beam. [3:02]
- If either team is gun heavy, buy Heroic Aura; if they are spirit heavy, buy Scourge to put on your carries. [3:02]
- Into ultimate debuffs like Pocket's ult, or debuffs in general, buy Divine Barrier; into gun heroes like Silver or Venator, buy Disarming Hex. [3:02]
- Until he makes a counter itemizing guide, look at the optional items in your build and buy them situationally; if unsure an item works into a hero, try it, trial and error is the best way to learn. [3:33]
- Before 2 minutes, boxes and tier one camps don't spawn: get lane priority, buy after the first wave if you're buying an 800 item, spar lightly if you want, and keep the wave shoved so you have priority at 2:00 to start box routing. [3:33]
- You must know your box routes; the most important ones end on or under the bridge between lanes. [4:03]
- From the bridge, look at the neighboring lane and ask if it is a good gank angle; playing active early is very important on support, and pre-8 minute impact is what lets you carry. [4:34]
- Ganking every lane and putting every lane in a winning position makes climbing extremely easy. [4:34]
- Gank check one, position: enemies pushed into your tower favors the gank; your team hard shoved into their tower makes it harder but not impossible. [5:04]
- Gank check two, HP: if the enemies are shoved in but both healthy and your teammates are at 1 HP, you can't force it; if your teammates are shoved in but both enemies are at 1 HP, force the gank and dive. [5:04]
- Gank check three, ults (later in lane): ganking is hard with your teammates' ults on cooldown, great with ganking or pick ults like Lash or Holliday ults, much harder into a defending Dynamo ult, and you are stronger with your own ult (Kelvin, Rem) up. [5:35]
- A gank doesn't need a kill to succeed: taking 40% HP off a Yamato at 2 or 3 minutes to give your lane an HP advantage is very impactful. [6:05]
- After a gank, once surprise is spent, go back to your lane with a box route; staying, eating waves and splitting farm three ways griefs your teammates. [6:05]
- Know who the better anchor is: don't leave a Rem to solo hold, but most heroes can anchor somewhat and there are very few lanes where a support shouldn't roam at all. [6:35]
- If put in a double support lane like Paige and Rem, switch out of it preemptively. [7:05]
- If you plan to play very active, ask your lane partner to buy Monster Rounds so they clear faster and avoid waves crashing into them. [7:05]
- Never stay away for more than two waves without asking your lane partner if they are fine, because two waves crashing into the tower often lets enemies dive your ally on the third; tell them when you roam. [7:05]
- An anchor like Lady Geist will often clear waves with no problem; if your partner says they're fine, they're fine. [7:36]
- If you did everything right and your partner keeps dying from overaggression, keep making the right plays: babysitting raises that game's win chance but slows your own improvement, and he recommends focusing on your own improvement. [7:36]
- Mid and late game: invade, calling over your front line or strong mid game heroes, getting your front line the Sinner's Sacrifices, and taking kills on enemies you find farming them. [8:07]
- Supports usually have a CC tool that, while not as good as a pick hero's, helps secure picks. [8:37]
- There is no reason not to pick up the urn; your enemies are in your elo too, and if you run it and call for help on mic, statistically more of your teammates will come. [8:37]
- If nobody reacts, hold the urn near the delivery point on that side, backed up near your walker so you can't be jumped, and tell teammates to pressure the opposite side; it forces the enemies to defend the delivery. [9:07]
- If two teammates split the walker and get jumped by four, the urn is free. [9:38]
- The urn's damage timer ticks while you hold it but not while you just stand next to it, so if you are waiting, drop it beside you, wait until it starts running away, pick it up, and repeat. [9:38]
- Keep box maxing for souls in the mid and late game. [9:38]
- If your carry keeps dying in a side lane, hover them from the roofs without showing on vision so you can defend when they are run at, especially with Rescue Beam; run boxes on the roofs above them to avoid dead time. [9:38]
- Shooting the wave or being hit by it shows you on the enemy minimap, but most abilities used on the wave do not. [10:09]
- Revealing a support's position tells the enemy how your team is weighted; supports generally want to be on the strong side making plays, invading or ganking with the pick hero. [10:39]
- In fights, peeling for your carry matters most: use CC to keep them alive, such as Rem ult to put a diving Drifter to sleep, a Paige root, or Divine Barrier on your Wraith to cleanse a mark or ult. [10:39]
- Next, punish squishies: if their Mina overextends, lock her down with Rem ult or Paige's hold, then scream on mic for everyone to focus her. [11:10]
- On full cooldowns (seen most on Rem), guerrilla light melee the enemy: baiting a parry locks them in about 0.7 seconds of self CC and puts their parry on cooldown for your allies to melee them. [11:10]
- Lane is by far the most important stage for a support. [11:41]
