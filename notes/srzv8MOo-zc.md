## Covers
A 3-minute Abrams guide (December 2025, gun meta): pros and cons, abilities, charge and melee combos, side lane play, mid game invades, team fights, builds and players to watch.
For new players and anyone learning Abrams.

## Claims
- Abrams is very tanky, which makes him new-player friendly since he can take a lot of poke in lane. [0:00]
- He deals a lot of damage, can carry, and provides value both when far ahead and when behind in souls. [0:00]
- Cons: slow with very low mobility, and he must commit to fights to have an impact. [0:00]
- Siphon Life (1) creates a damaging area around him and heals him for 66% of the damage dealt. [0:00]
- Shoulder Charge (2) drags enemy heroes it hits; hitting a wall at the end briefly stuns them. [0:00]
- Infernal Resilience (3) is a passive that slowly regenerates damage taken over time. [0:30]
- Seismic Impact (ult) leaps into the air; recast to slam down, stunning in an area. [0:30]
- Cast Siphon Life during the charge to set up when you connect with enemies. [0:30]
- After slamming someone into a wall, light melee during the stun. [0:30]
- With two points in charge (tier two), you can land a full heavy melee off the wall stun if you buffer it by holding melee during the dash; main combo then is charge, Siphon Life, heavy melee after the wall stun. [0:30]
- In this very gun Abrams meta, once you have Point Blank and around 7.2k, with 2k invested in gun, go back to light meleeing and guarantee a headshot off the stun instead of the heavy melee. [1:00]
- The ult's stun is only long enough for a light melee, so use it with the charge for a lot of damage. [1:00]
- Maxed, the ult makes him unstoppable, great for contesting and stealing the Rejuvenator. [1:30]
- Lane: play a side lane and play around the veil, which gives good shotgun trades and guaranteed dashes. [1:30]
- He is strong mid game: invade the enemy jungle, ideally with a pick hero, to steal jungle or get picks. [1:30]
- Play around the Urn and other mid game objectives. [1:30]
- Be on the strong side of your map, the side with the most teammates. [2:00]
- When invading, prioritize sinner's sacrifices for the most souls and the permanent buffs. [2:00]
- If caught out, his charge works as an escape tool. [2:00]
- In fights, get on the enemy backline and stun them for big combo damage. [2:00]
- Builds: Salty's Abrams for a more classical build, Listix's for a more aggressive gun build; Salty and Listix are good Abrams players to watch. [2:30]
- Bonus tip: with the right ability settings you can cancel the charge with the ability key to slide afterwards (cut off mid sentence). [2:30]
