## Covers
Paradox coaching VOD review by Midknight of an Archon-rank Paradox player, from a 2025 patch (July 2025 video).
Themes: farming boxes and camps in a winning lane, wave timing with a lane partner, Paradox as a pick hero (high-value swaps), mid boss calls and which ults steal it (Abrams first), map awareness and bridge buffs, mantle slides, and escaping the jail with Paradoxical Swap.

## Claims
General mechanics and tech:
- Paradox and Mo & Krill players love to melee for no reason; do not melee, it is extremely greedy because the enemy can parry. [0:31]
- Do not melee someone who is chain parrying minions. [1:02]
- Viscous leveling The Cube second was very clutch in this lane. [1:02]
- When Viscous cubes you, parry as soon as an enemy tries to melee you. People expect a self-cubed Viscous to parry, but not the cubed teammate, which makes it one of the most OP things you can do. [1:32]
- Do not level abilities for what you plan to build at the end of the game: level the best early ability first. On Viscous, Splatter is the best to level, so take it first no matter what, even on a punch build. [2:34]
- Mantle slides: every time you mantle, press Ctrl right after for free movement; condition your brain to do it on every mantle. [9:14]
- Haze and Holliday fill a similar pick role, but Haze is very countered if you just position near your team; she capitalizes on a large mistake, while Holliday can capitalize on a very small one (run at you, ult, run away). [14:52]
- Items now discount each other's price when you build up, which he likes. [20:03]
- He does not think Glass Cannon is worth buying if you are 0 and 10, but it is fine if you are doing okay and will get a couple stacks. [28:50]
- You can escape the jail with Paradoxical Swap because the swap goes through the gate; he tested swapping himself into the jail and back out. Waiting and swapping out is quicker than using unstuck. [35:59]
- A Mirage Tornado into the jail as it opens gets you stuck in there, and you have to ult out. [36:29]

Mid boss calls:
- Abrams has the best ult in the game for stealing mid boss. Before doing mid, check whether Abrams has his ult maxed (unstoppable); if he does, do not do mid into it. [32:26]
- Dynamo ult is also very scary to do mid into. Yamato and Lash ults discourage mid but are not make-or-break. [32:26]
- Infernus is a free steal ult but counterable with Paradoxical Swap or Holliday unless your stoppers misposition or he hits a pixel-perfect timing. [32:58]
- Abrams is scarier than Yamato or Infernus because his ult gives an AoE stun plus unstoppable, two in one; Yamato only has the unstoppable part, so a couple of people parrying and meleeing can still stop her off the rejuv steal. [33:59]
- The only counter to an unstoppable Abrams steal is parrying him, which is hard with only two people there. [34:29]
