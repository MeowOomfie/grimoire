## Covers
Midknight Vods runs four free coaching sessions on April Fools day (2026-04-01): a Mina (Ascendant), a Paradox (Emissary), a Doorman (Eternus) and a Lady Geist (Oracle).
Themes: playing and buying around the wave, box routes, overstaying ganks, fighting for no reason ("mosh pitting"), soul awareness, playing for spikes, urn running, mid boss macro, side walker trades, Counterspell and Curse checks, and movement tech (wall bounces, edge boosts, mantle slides).
Side notes on gun versus spirit scaling, Rem, Superior Stamina, Reactive Barrier and the item meta.
