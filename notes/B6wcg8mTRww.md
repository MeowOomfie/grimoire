# Ranking Every Ability In Deadlock From Strongest To Weakest
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-11-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B6wcg8mTRww

## Covers
Metro rates every hero ability in the game on an S to F scale (B = solid and almost always usable, the average), hero by hero from Abrams to Yamato, on the early November 2025 patch (after the Halloween patch of late October 2025).
For players who want to know which abilities make their hero strong or weak, how to use them and how to play against them. Recurring ideas: mobility is king, niche ultimates are bad ultimates, Counterspell and Slowing Hex counter a lot, and ability strength is not the same as hero strength.

## Claims
- Rating four of a hero's abilities S tier does not make the hero S tier; stats such as late game scaling (for example Wraith) also matter, but ability strength and hero strength correlate closely in most cases. [0:30]
- B tier is the baseline: an ability that is very solid and almost always usable but not broken. [1:32]
- Abrams Siphon Life: B+. It works, makes you tanky and does a lot of damage up close, but Abrams is not played as a full spirit hero in this meta and you can get away from it. [1:32]
- Abrams Shoulder Charge: S tier, one of the best abilities in the game. Without it Abrams would be nothing; it gets you in and out, guarantees the melee and close range, and adds bonus damage. [2:03]
- Mobility abilities are all going to rate well because mobility is king in this game; getting in and out fast is the best thing there is. [3:04]
- Abrams Infernal Resilience: B tier. It is an underrated always-on passive that keeps him alive in lane; many Abrams level it a few times early, and one or two points already give a lot of value. [3:04]
- Abrams Seismic Impact: C tier, a below average ultimate. It is good for mid boss contests, but Shoulder Charge is even better there; if you do not land it directly you often cannot melee and get parried, and it has a long cooldown. [4:05]
- Bebop Exploding Uppercut: an okay ability that synergizes with Bebop (first considered C, then taken back). It needs melee range, deals a lot of damage, gets you out of bad situations and relocates enemies, but it is not what makes Bebop Bebop. [5:38]
- Bebop Hook: crazy, broken, the defining ability; better than Shoulder Charge. It has a big hitbox, landing it in a team fight almost always gets a kill, it is like a Paradox or Holliday ultimate on a normal cooldown, and with Uppercut leveled you can do it several times a fight. [6:08]
- Bebop Sticky Bomb: low C tier (his hottest take). It is strong lane harass and scales, but Debuff Remover, Counterspell or staying out of range negate it; a full bomb build does less damage than hook, uppercut and a gun build. [6:40]
- Counterspell also works against a self-cast Sticky Bomb, so if a bomb Bebop gets close you Counterspell and you are fine. [8:12]
- Sticky Bomb is S tier only in very low ranks where people do not counter it and give free bomb stacks; higher up players keep distance and buy counter items. [8:44]
- Bebop Hyper Beam: first put in F as one of the worst abilities, later upgraded out of F because Drifter's Bloodscent is so much worse. It lasts long so it can deny an area from the back in niche fights, but it winds up slowly, does modest damage, turns slowly and leaves you exposed from the side. [9:14]
- A niche ultimate is a bad ultimate; the strong, hero-defining ultimates (for example Kelvin's dome) are usable all the time. [9:47]
- Bebop is in a gun meta right now, so Hyper Beam does not scale well because he will not have much spirit; it is solid in lane but not great even there. [10:17]
- Billy Bashdown: A tier. Weak at base and does nearly nothing in lane, but it scales very well with both melee and spirit damage and works with Blasted; its synergy with the rest of the kit makes it very good. In gun builds it does almost no damage. [10:48]
- Billy Rising Ram: low C tier, Billy's weakest ability. It is a movement ability with a very high cooldown (always 30 seconds if used to get out), and its small knock-up can be dashed out of. Billy would be playable without it. [11:49]
