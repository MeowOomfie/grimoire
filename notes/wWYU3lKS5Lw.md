# How To Get 3x The Souls of Everyone in Your Games
channel: Shotty
date: 2026-05-12
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wWYU3lKS5Lw

## Covers
Why players who farm well and die little still lose: role-based fighting, what to do after winning or losing lane, roaming and jungle invades, Trophy Collector and kill value, and the core mistake from two high Ascendant VOD reviews (farming instead of joining fights). For players who feel their games are out of their control.

## Claims
- Losses usually come from several smaller mistakes and missed opportunities, not one death; start by deciding your role and how to play it. [0:30]
- Seven's job is to reach the late game where he scales to 30k or 40k farm and wins; he is not very mobile, so he holds down a side lane, farms camps and survives. [0:30]
- Rem's job is to steal center sacrifices (centers), get farm and support the team. [1:01]
- How much you fight depends on your role: some heroes are meant to fight almost the whole game, rotating for boxes, killing someone in lane, then rotating for boxes again. [1:01]
- Mina can run around, blow up a health bar, steal centers, farm and camps, then leave; once she has her 2 she flies onto a roof and cannot be caught. [1:01]
- A big mistake, made most by carries, is never fighting; do not farm bot the whole game. [1:32]
- If you win lane, especially in public matches, it is your job to roam and do something with the farm, even as a carry; if you hit spikes ahead of the enemy and leave them alone while they fight a lot, you will get out farmed every time. [2:02]
- If you lose lane, your job is to macro properly: boxes, camps, lane farm, min-maxing rotations without putting yourself in danger. Losing lane is not a big deal if you do this. [2:33]
- Do not keep fighting the laner who already beat you, and do not push for the guardian at 6k farm when everyone else is at 12k; a guardian is only about 400 souls and not worth dying for. [3:04]
- After losing lane, farm up and catch back up; once caught up, look for opportunities. Continuing to only farm after catching up is a throw, especially in public matches. [3:34]
- With everything else equal, both teams get the same jungle and the same waves, so kills are the only way the other side out scales you. [3:34]
- A player with five kills and two or three deaths while the team has 15 deaths each probably avoided most team fights to farm. [4:04]
- When you win lane, roam, shut down other players and maintain your lead with smart fights; someone will always push too far or be in their jungle not paying attention. [4:34]
- Below Oracle, and honestly below Phantom, nobody invades the jungle properly. [4:34]
- Many enemies farm mindlessly, sitting in camps taking damage; that makes them a free kill for an invader like Mina, and many heroes can do the same, especially with a soul lead. [5:04]
- Help your teammates: there is a 100% chance they will lose lane and then run it down instead of farming back up, so you must be the scale tipper. [5:34]
- In the current meta it is pretty much buy Trophy Collector and win the game; even before it, well executed kills were extremely useful. [5:34]
- Fighting correctly nets more souls than farming; a hero like Mina can take a kill quickly and go back to what she was doing. [6:04]
- A kill on your Seven or carry gives the enemy maybe 400 souls depending on how grouped they are, plus a Trophy Collector stack. [6:04]
- With equal jungle and boxes, the team with more Trophy Collector buyers will pretty much always win because of the extra souls; higher ranked players buy it nearly every game. [6:36]
- The coach buys Trophy Collector every game and by 30 minutes has usually generated 7,000 to 8,000 souls from it, a whole tier four item, by fighting early and often. [6:36]
- If the enemy team fights a lot you have to be at some of those fights, but not right after you lost lane and need to farm back up. [7:07]
- If you are not at a fight, expect the enemy to win it; every team game feels like your team is doing nothing, so be at fights whenever you can. [7:37]
- If a fight is on the far lane and you would arrive after it ends, do not rotate; take a Walker or another objective instead, but always do something. [7:37]
- The big mistake in both high Ascendant reviews: good macro and positioning, two or three deaths, great souls per minute, but not in the fights; for every soul they farmed the enemy got about three. [8:07]
- If you do not fight you are at the mercy of how much the enemy fights and how well your team fights. [8:38]
- Many players treat Deadlock as only a MOBA and ignore shooter skills like positioning, fight hygiene and peeking. [9:08]
- High Eternus lobbies play corners and angles: right-hand vs left-hand peeks, side angles, off angles, high and low, rather than running straight down a corridor. [9:08]
- With all else equal, an enemy team that fights gets triple the souls you farm. [9:38]
- Example from the coach's own Vyper game: he came out of the jungle around 20 minutes, the enemy Seven was already 35k with 12 kills, and the team lost because he farmed too much and missed the fights. [10:08]
