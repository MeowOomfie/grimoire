# HMC is Gone?! Urn has new visuals? Patch overview - Deadlock
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-04-18
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CH3DcJgGpHw

## Covers
A short overview of the 2025-04-17 "minor" patch: soul orb sharing and denies before 8 minutes, the 30 second wave interval, Urn changes and its new comeback candles, the heavy melee cancel (HMC) removal, Ethereal Shift moving to 6k and the hero nerfs to Bebop, Calico, Holliday, Kelvin, Pocket, Sinclair, Vindicta and others.
For any player catching up on that patch. Early 2025; the game has changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Soul orbs no longer have special duplication rules before 8 minutes; soul splitting is the same before and after 8 minutes, trooper economy was adjusted to compensate, and there is no bounty bump at 8 minutes. [0:00]
- This makes funneling (feeding resources into one hero) possible in Deadlock: one hero alone in a lane gets more money than two heroes there. [0:32]
- From the 6 minute mark it now pays for one lane partner to lane and the other to jungle, but towers are still incredibly important: pushing the tower is still the number one priority, so don't just go into the jungle. [1:03]
- Denies are now much stronger: before, a trooper (say 80 souls) was duplicated for the last-hitting team (160 total) and a deny gave you half of 80 (40) while cutting the enemy to 80. Now the trooper is worth 160 undivided, a deny still costs the enemy 80, but you get 80 for it, double what it was. [1:33]
- Four or five denies can now be enough for a significant advantage, putting you multiple hundreds of souls ahead and reaching your timings faster (3k when they are 2.5k). He expects denies to be nerfed because they are probably far too strong. [3:05]
- The wave interval went from 25 to 30 seconds, with souls changed so souls per minute stay about the same; 30 seconds is easier to remember, as in Dota. [3:36]
- Players can no longer carry troopers, which makes it harder to freeze. [4:06]
- The Urn now reveals you immediately when picked up. [4:06]
- The first Urn now counts for comeback mechanics at a 15% net worth delta (other urns use 10%); 15% is roughly an 8,000 soul lead at 10 minutes, so it needs a very large lead, but it gives some recourse when you get dominated in lane. [4:37]
- The Urn now shows candles on its model: red candles mean it is a comeback urn for the enemy team, green candles mean a comeback urn for your team, no candles means neutral. Pick it up when it is a comeback urn for you and leave it when it is one for the enemies. [4:06]
- Heavy melee cancel momentum was "significantly reduced", but test it in the sandbox: it is essentially dead and gives no real momentum. He thinks it is a great change. [5:08]
- Several OP items got small number nerfs: the barriers, Hollow Point Ward, Divine Barrier and others. [5:38]
- Fortitude's out of combat time increased by 3 seconds. [6:10]
- Decay was nerfed across the board. [6:10]
- Knockdown now removes all momentum and brings heroes straight down, a nerf to Grey Talon and Vindicta. [6:10]
- The biggest item change: Ethereal Shift moved from tier 3 to tier 4, now a 6k item. It was incredibly OP at 3k, and the move is an indirect buff to heroes it hard countered, such as Lash, whose ult used to meet three or four Ethereal Shifts. [6:10]
- Bebop got one less stamina and less spirit resist; he no longer gains spirit resist per boon at all. [6:41]
- Calico's base ammo was reduced, her gun damage growth nerfed by 25%, and Gloom Bombs tier 1 and tier 2 upgrades were swapped; overall really significant nerfs that might leave her balanced. [6:41]
- Grey Talon's nerf is mostly a slap on the wrist: his laning stage is weaker, the rest is insignificant, and he is probably still OP. [7:12]
- Haze got some buffs, but probably not enough to bring her back into popularity. [7:12]
- Holliday got overall nerfs: no melee while casting Spirit Lasso, shorter Bounce Pad duration, lower base gun damage with gun growth buffed a little to compensate. She will still be strong but maybe more reasonable. [7:12]
- Kelvin's laning stage resistance was nerfed significantly: base spirit resist from 15 to 0, now gaining 1 spirit resist per boon. Frozen Shelter's cooldown went up a little, but you get it back with two points. [7:43]
- Lash was nerfed as a stray; he was problematic in pubs, so that is fine. [7:43]
- McGinnis's turrets were buffed, but playing her it doesn't feel like it changes anything; she is probably still really bad. [8:13]
- Mirage got nerfed but is probably still really good. [8:13]
- Mo & Krill's damage output was nerfed a little; it won't change much since he still has insane CC, and he shouldn't have both insane CC and damage. [8:13]
- Pocket got hit hard early: overall gun damage about 15% lower early, gained back through boons so his late game is barely nerfed. Satchel base damage went from 100 to 70, with +110 at two points, so you need two points in Satchel to recover the old damage. [8:44]
- Seven got some nerfs that probably won't matter much. [8:44]
- Sinclair's bullet damage was nerfed, though his gun is still really good. [9:15]
- Rabbit Hex's damage amp now affects allies' damage (before it only amped Sinclair's own damage, despite the wording), so it was nerfed from 25% base amp to 15% and its spirit scaling cut by about 40%. Maxing Rabbit Hex now gives some damage amp back, so there is more reason to max it. [9:15]
- Rabbit Hex no longer does damage and Vexing Bolt damage was nerfed, so all of Sinclair's lane damage sources are lower; but the bunny build is better than before, since with enough spirit you can give your whole team 25 to 30% damage amp. He is probably still really OP, especially in organized play. [9:46]
- Vindicta's tier 3 Crow upgrade went from -12 to -8, bringing the Crow's total shred from 18 to 14: more reasonable but probably still insane. [10:16]
- Warden got slight laning buffs; he wasn't that bad, his lane just sucked, and he should be much more pickable now. [10:46]
