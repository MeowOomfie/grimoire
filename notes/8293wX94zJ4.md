# You Fight TOO Much | Oracle Sinclair Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-03-31
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8293wX94zJ4

## Covers
Midknight reviews one Oracle-rank Sinclair game (the hero was called Magician at the time), against a Wraith, Warden, Seven, Lash and Paradox.
Free coaching review over a replay. Recorded March 2025, on an early-2025 patch; the game has changed a lot since, so check items, numbers and rank names against the current patch.
Themes: soul awareness and buying, diving when behind, farming instead of hunting fights, going back in after escaping at 1 HP, Spectral Assistant use, and why Sinclair's escapes hide mistakes.

## Claims
Player 1 (Sinclair, Oracle):
- When you see the Warden has Healing Rite, it is better to focus him first, and use Vexing Bolt to stop the healing. [1:32]
- Against a Wraith, one card plus a couple of gunshots can one-shot a squishy Sinclair, so be careful at low HP and throw your Spectral Assistant out before you bolt away. [1:32]
- Don't take a fight while sitting on a lot of unspent souls; go buy first. [2:02]
- Once Wraith has three cards (Extra Charge), it does not matter what HP you are at; she can jump on you and one-shot you. [3:02]
- Biggest repeat issue: not buying. He kept leaving shop with about 500 souls and letting souls climb to 2k without saving for a tier two item. [3:02]
- Diving when you are down on them, sitting on 2k souls, while the enemy has ult and you don't, never ends well. [4:04]
- When escaping, use the Spectral Assistant to get on top of a building for height so you can run away faster and reach the vent. [3:33]
- Rabbit Hex came out rushed; it would have been great to hex the Warden. [4:04]
- Pre-8 minutes, when your laning teammate is resetting, wait for them to return to lane before farming the minions; killing the wave alone before 8 minutes wastes about half the minion souls because souls are still split. [5:10]
- At 300 HP, don't dive; one Wraith card and you are dead. [5:40]
- When low, buy healing (Healing Rite) instead of staying low; Wraith will come back and jump on you with ult. [6:10]
- Drop back sooner when the enemy you just escaped is predictably coming back to finish you. [6:41]
- After getting one-shot while behind, take that as a wake-up call that you can't fight them; sit back and farm. [7:12]
- You can't really go in when your Haze is not there. [7:12]
- Don't waste the Spectral Assistant. [7:45]
- When behind, take a minute to farm the camps on the empty side of the map. [8:15]
- When farming a vending machine (box), shoot the medium creep in between vending machine melees instead of shooting it and then hitting the machine. [8:50]
- Slowing Hex disables Warp Stone. [9:20]
- Farming a secluded camp with no teammates nearby is fine, but be ready to escape. [9:50]
- The buy was fine assuming you are going to upgrade one of your tier one items (burst, reach, health, spirit). [10:23]
- When collapsed on, don't path back toward the enemy (Seven's ult); you have no out. Try a pull to one side and use the sign as cover. [10:54]
- You are using the Spectral Assistant too much for movement; it is a 30 second cooldown that literally doubles your damage, and you throw it for about a Warp Stone of movement. [10:54]
- Grab boxes and vending machines when you walk past them; he walked past many and looped the map for nothing. [11:25]
- Stop "Overwatch mode", pathing to the next fight; this is a MOBA, so mostly farm and do objectives, and be okay with not fighting. Archon players take a lot of stupid fights for no reason. [11:56]
- Pathing toward where you think a fight is, instead of to farm, keeps you falling further behind in souls. [11:56]
- After you fake toward one side and pull most of the enemy left, pushing the walker nearby is predictable and you get collapsed on. [12:27]
- When Seven ults on you as Sinclair, you have to run away; you cannot fight him. [12:27]
- When you are up a player, the fight is good; once it turns neutral and your team is chunked, back off. After Lash ults and you are down a player you have no chance, so run. [13:27]
- Biggest issue: you escape at 1 HP, your guardian angel saves you, and then you go straight back in and die. When you get away at 1 HP or are at 500 HP, go to base and heal, then come back. [13:58]
- Save the Spectral Assistant for running away in case you get jumped. [14:28]
- After you push the wave into a walker, expect one or two enemies to come push the wave back; don't go back there isolated. [14:59]
- When your whole team is fighting for the urn on the other side, it is right to grab urn, so teleport to them. [15:30]
- Splitting instead of teleporting is an understandable call, but the second you see Wraith you have no split potential and must teleport. [15:30]
- Look at the map while splitting so an enemy (Warden) can't surprise you from behind. [17:05]
- Recommendation: stop playing Sinclair for a while. He lets you make so many mistakes without being punished that you don't notice how often you overextend. [16:03]
- The same crutch happens to Abrams in lane (his healing passive hides damage taken), Infernus (dash away), and less so Calico (her escape needs some preemptive timing). [16:34]
- Play a hero with no get-out-of-jail-free card for about 10 games (not Sinclair, Infernus, or Wraith; Talon is fine), and also avoid Calico and Seven, which are broken right now. For every death, ask why you died and how to prevent it. [17:36]
- If you keep playing Sinclair, every time you slip away at 1 HP, ask what you did wrong and why you got caught out, as if you had died. [18:06]
- In laning stage he didn't buy enough; early soul awareness was near zero, though late game it was cleaned up. Go to shop more. [19:06]
- He reads the player as good micro, weak macro. [2:32]
- His laning was good: bolts on point and damage on point; only not buying made the lane go neutral. [19:37]
