# Metro is still wrong. Here's why. @MetroDeadlock
channel: Deathy
date: 2025-12-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XNR6JjHe9y0

## Covers
A response to Metro's claim that rushing the 4.8k vitality investment is broken, from the patch where vitality boons became flat health. Covers when green items beat the old percentage system, why lane wants burst and wave pressure, power-spike items from each shop category, and which heroes gain or lose from greens (Warden, Kelvin, Mina). For laners deciding their first items.

## Claims
- Below roughly 10k souls, vitality investment is better than it used to be on every hero, because of how boons, boon scaling and base health now work; the old system was a percentage and was weak early. [1:02]
- Going vitality is now an option but not the meta. [1:32]
- Extra Health is great value and Rebuttal is an extremely stat-heavy item. [1:32]
- Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem are good, flexible items for a variety of heroes, but not meta-defining. [2:03]
- Rushing Extra Health first is not necessarily good, because the first waves matter a lot in deciding how a lane goes. [2:33]
- Early, items like Monster Rounds or Headshot Booster, or Close Quarters depending on hero, win the first waves; on Abrams you often go Close Quarters first, or even Rebuttal first, to swing the lane. [2:33]
- Get wave pressure in the first waves; pushing the lane gives you agency to gank, do boxes and so on, and Extra Health does not help you do that. [3:04]
- Rebuttal early has a good chance of working, especially at lower ranks with chances to land a parry; even without parries its stats are strong depending on the enemy heroes. [3:04]
- If you build Battle Vest, Enchanter's Emblem, Extra Health and Healing Rite you are not really going to do much damage. [3:34]
- Standing still and trading for 10 seconds is a flawed test; lane is bursty, short interactions where people combo, then run away. [4:34]
- With Battle Vest, Enchanter's Emblem, Extra Health and Rebuttal, in a 1v1 from full HP where nobody moves, you will beat most item combinations; real lanes have high ground, cover, towers and cooldowns. [5:04]
- On Lash you don't trade for 10 seconds, you Flog, Ground Strike and run away. [5:35]
- On Wraith, wait until you have three cards and maybe your ult, burst someone with ult and cards, then back up when they are spent. [5:35]
- To win lane you need burst damage and wave pressure; Monster Rounds and Headshot Booster are the best items for that. [6:35]
- Rushing 4.8k greens means you always lose wave pressure to the player with Monster Rounds and lose early trades to the player with Headshot Booster. [7:07]
- Headshot Booster is inherently a better lane item because lane trades are short: a few seconds of shooting around a pillar or out of cover. [7:07]
- The 4.8k green investment items are good at stat-checking people, not at the fights that naturally happen in lane. [7:37]
- In live games at that time very few players were rushing the 4.8k vitality investment. [8:38]
- High level players said rushing greens can be decent on Shiv, and could have potential on Haze or Infernus, but on most heroes it is not great if you want to win lane. [9:08]
- In 12 of Metro's Warden and Wraith games rushing greens, judged by soul count at 9 minutes, the lane results were six losses, three ties and three wins. [9:39]
- Don't rush greens on every hero. [10:10]
- Early, everyone has more HP than before, which makes the percentage-based Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem stronger early. [11:11]
- Battle Vest and Enchanter's Emblem got buffed on squishier heroes and nerfed on tankier heroes; on Warden and other high HP-per-boon heroes both are worse than before after about 11k souls. [11:41]
- On Mina, Enchanter's Emblem and Battle Vest are way better than before, though she is frail enough that you still won't see them bought on her much. [12:11]
- Tier three camps are not relevant early because you won't be hitting them. [12:41]
- Doing the center sacrifices early is optimal. [13:11]
- For sustain you don't need the 4.8k green rush: Healing Rite alone gives a lot (300 HP), Sprint Boots is one of the cheapest sources of out of combat regen, or buy Extra Regen. [13:11]
- Regen is a little less important now that the healing trooper exists, though it still has value. [13:41]
- Rushing 4.8k greens loses to the player who has Cultist; rushing greens and then Cultist will probably leave you a little late for the 8 minute timer. [13:41]
- With these items you are basically playing to lose the lane in most situations. [14:12]
- Items that change short engagements: orange Headshot Booster, Monster Rounds, Close Quarters; green Rebuttal, sometimes Extra Health, or Melee Lifesteal depending on hero; purple Rusted Barrel, Spirit Strike, Mystic Burst. [14:42]
- Headshot Booster favors short engagements with about 45 extra burst damage. [15:12]
- Rebuttal favors any engagement with a hero in melee range through 18% melee resist, and a parry pretty much guarantees the kill. [15:12]
- Rusted Barrel lets you take one favorable trade every 20 seconds against one hero for 5 seconds, by slowing their fire rate and making them take more bullet damage. [15:12]
- If you are playing to win lane, try to get one of these power spikes from each category; the meta is no longer rushing one category and ignoring the rest. [15:42]
- On many heroes, get your first 800 in two categories (many heroes ignore spirit or ignore gun), then rush one category, most often spirit, sometimes vitality, sometimes a mix, or a Cultist rush. [15:42]
- Buy items situationally based on your lane rather than rushing the most broken investment category; spirit no longer beats every category the way it did. [16:12]
- Against a worse player you don't want much green; go all in on lane-winning items like Monster Rounds, Headshot Booster or Mystic Burst. [16:42]
- Never default to rushing green; more health only lets you take longer trades and survive big bursts, it does not give you agency in trades. [17:12]
- Each hero has a break point for vitality: below it greens are better than before, above it worse. On Warden, green is only better early and worse after about 10 to 11k souls. [18:12]
- Kelvin has high base health (850) and high HP per boon (58), so for Kelvin green items are strictly worse except in roughly the first 10k souls. [18:42]
- For Mina the green shop is better basically the whole game, until about 45k souls. [19:12]
- The real change is that squishy heroes can now consider green items to tank damage, where before they bought shielding or "fake green" dodge items like Warp Stone or Counterspell. [19:12]
- Rushing the 4.8k vitality investment is no longer the noob trap it was; it can do decently, but there are very few situations where you should make it your earliest investment. [20:14]
- Even carry heroes like Wraith should not play to lose the lane; top carry players try to win lane, and only if they lose it do they adapt toward survival and movement speed items to recover. [21:14]
