# WHY LOSING IN DEADLOCK IS GOOD
channel: moe9times
date: 2024-10-04
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUAjTPiX63E

## Covers
Mindset and learning guide for the early access game with no ranked ladder: learn from solo deaths, play every hero, try every item and actives, and accept losses while experimenting.
moe9times, commentary and advice, no one coached. October 2024, before ranked existed.

## Claims
- With no visible ranked system and patches every two weeks, losing in Deadlock is fine if you use games to learn. [0:00]
- Actively think about your wider decisions while playing instead of going on autopilot. [1:00]
- Positioning, knowing where to be at any point in time, is one of the hardest things to learn; players from traditional shooters often struggle with it even with good aim. [1:00]
- Learn from your solo deaths: usually it is not what you did that second but your decisions in the minute before that sealed it. [1:30]
- Do not beat yourself up over micro misplays like a late Metal Skin; reactive button play comes with time. [1:30]
- Ask yourself: why was I there, why did I die for being there, should I have been somewhere else? The answer is usually that you pushed too far without knowing where the heroes were. [2:00]
- If you think you died because a spell, hero or item is overpowered, you are going about it wrong; focus on yourself. [2:00]
- To improve, play every hero in the roster at least a little, to learn their game flow, power spikes, weaknesses and how they play in lane. [3:00]
- If you do not know what an ability does, you cannot expect to know what an opponent will do with it. [3:30]
- Buy every item and experiment with builds; do not be afraid to buy an item that sounds good for your situation, because it probably is. [3:30]
- Learning how and when to deviate from builds is core to the game. [3:30]
- Do not avoid active items; some of the best counters are actives, such as Knockdown for flyers and Silence Glyph for evasive heroes like Pocket and Lash. [4:01]
- Understanding what to buy in each situation makes you better than someone who just follows the top guide. [4:31]
- Expect to lose games while trying unfamiliar heroes and playstyles; it does not matter if you are serious about improving. [4:31]
- Be a nice teammate; focus on how you can play better rather than on teammates who are not playing well. [4:31]
