## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream from 12/17/25, during the winter holiday event patch (wall bounce stamina change, Stamina Mastery, flat-health green spike).
Three coaching sessions: an Ascendant Mina laning with a Pocket into Shiv and Ivy, a Ritualist Lash laning with Warden into Mina and Kelvin, and an Ascendant 2 Pocket (Marco) laning with Shiv into Mirage and Bebop. Then an analysis of a top-leaderboard gun Bebop (Together).
