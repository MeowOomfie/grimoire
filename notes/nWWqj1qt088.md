# Why The Worst Item In Deadlock Is Loved By Everyone
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-09-26
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nWWqj1qt088

## Covers
Why Trophy Collector is a trap: power spikes, soul inflation over a game, and stat-by-stat comparisons with Cultist, Mystic Expansion, Greater Expansion, Sprint Boots, Enduring Speed, Fortitude, Spirit Shielding and Veil Walker.
Ends with what to buy instead and a warning about Cheat Death. For lower rank players who buy Trophy Collector as their first 3.2k item.

## Claims
- Trophy Collector is a scam that deletes 3,200 souls and gives basically nothing back; it is one of the worst items in the game, though Cheat Death is worse, and it is bought way too much in low rank. [0:00]
- A power spike is a point where you spend most of your farmed money on an item or a level 5 ability and suddenly become a lot stronger; different heroes spike on different items or abilities. [0:31]
- Power spikes matter most early, because every hero starts with low base stats, so the first few items are the most decisive; your first 3.2k item should have a big impact on farming, pushing waves, moving around the map, getting kills and staying alive. [1:01]
- At base Trophy Collector does nothing: beyond its Sprint Boots component (2 sprint speed and 2 out of combat regen) the upgrade gives no bonus until you get kills or assists. [2:03]
- Trophy Collector's stacking stats are health, sprint speed, ability range and souls per minute; Cultist overlaps a lot with what it is supposed to do. [2:33]
- Ability range: Trophy Collector starts at zero and reaches about 15% after 15 kills; Cultist gives that same amount instantly on purchase for the entire game. [3:03]
- If you want ability range, Mystic Expansion costs 800 and gives more range on one ability instantly; Greater Expansion gives 35% on most abilities, more than double the 15%. Few heroes benefit from range on every ability. [3:34]
- Sprint speed is the worst stat in the game right now because sprint cancels on almost anything: shooting stops it for a few seconds, any damage stops it, hitting a box stops it. [4:05]
- In a fight you are shooting or taking damage 99% of the time, so sprint speed almost never helps you escape or chase. [4:36]
- For rotating, players use dashes and jumps (not affected by sprint speed), vents and wall jumps, so sprint speed adds little. [5:07]
- If you do want sprint speed, Sprint Boots into Enduring Speed is better: 2 m/s sprint speed instantly, while Trophy Collector needs 10 stacks to equal one Sprint Boots. Fleetfoot and Enduring Speed also have slow resist; Trophy Collector has none. [5:39]
- Health: at 10 stacks, which takes multiple minutes, Trophy Collector gives 250 health; Fortitude gives 375 instantly (and Fortitude itself is overrated right now). [6:10]
- Spirit Shielding gives a 325 barrier instantly at half the price, and most green items give more health instantly than Trophy Collector. [6:40]
- Veil Walker gives 175 bonus health instantly, heals you every time you walk through a veil, gives 4 m/s movement speed in a veil, and has a low cooldown; for being tankier and getting kills it is a better option. [6:40]
- The souls per minute is what makes Trophy Collector feel good, since games end with it showing 3k or 6k paid back, but that is misleading. [7:10]
- The longer the game goes, the more souls every source gives: jungle camps, boxes, lane creeps, hero kills and assists. [7:41]
- A tier three camp killed solo at 8 minutes gives about 672 souls; the same camp at 30 minutes gives about 900, roughly 25% more. [8:11]
- A lane creep at level one gives 120 souls if you are alone, rising to 160 at 30 minutes. [8:41]
- Every soul source also gets faster to collect over the game because every hero, even a support, gets stronger; a late Dynamo stomp kills a whole creep wave instantly. [9:12]
- Soul collection other than hero kills is at least three times as fast at 30 minutes as at 10 minutes; you can confirm it in your replays. [9:42]
- Deadlock has hyperinflation: souls are worth less the longer the game goes. A 3,200 item at 7-8 minutes is a big share of your net worth, while late game it is barely 10% of it. [10:12]
- Stop thinking Trophy Collector "paid itself back": 3k invested at 8 minutes is worth far more than 3k or even 6k returned at the end of the game. [10:45]
- Trophy Collector has to be bought early to get stacks, and whether you are ahead, behind or even, there is always a better option. [10:45]
- If you won lane and buy Trophy Collector, you give away your power spike for nothing; at 10k net worth against a 7k lane opponent you end up about equal in strength, making farming, wave clear and fights harder. [11:16]
- Example: on Lash, Improved Spirit, Mystic Reach, Majestic Leap or Tankbuster give a spike that makes it easier to kill the enemy and to survive the fight, unlike Trophy Collector. [11:46]
- An item that helps you win the fight might have turned one kill into two, and those extra kills give money that never shows in Trophy Collector's stats. [12:16]
- If you are ahead, you get further ahead by farming, shutting down the enemy, pushing objectives and fighting, all easier with items that give stats. [12:46]
- If you are even or behind and buy Trophy Collector while the enemy (a Paradox in the example) buys Cultist, they sacrifice a creep camp for instant souls (more than you earn for minutes), and instantly have more health, fire rate and ability range for the fight against you. [13:16]
- Every other item also gives value through faster farming, more kills and fewer deaths; it just doesn't show on the end screen. [13:46]
- Trophy Collector feels good because it is rewarding and a dopamine rush, but kills are always rewarding anyway: they give money, map openings and the next item, and even assists give gold. [14:49]
- Example: a Warp Stone on Mo & Krill gets the grab and the assist; with Trophy Collector instead, the enemy gets away because you lacked the Warp Stone to catch them. [15:19]
- Even at max stacks Trophy Collector does not give enough value; there are better items in all three categories. [15:49]
- 3.2k items are the best bang for your buck right now and the main power spike items. [15:49]
- Even if you are confident, there is a chance you won't get stacks soon after buying; Deadlock has enough randomness that 20 to 50% of the time you won't get enough stacks to make it worth it on paper. [16:20]
- Even the good case (bought at 8 minutes, 10 stacks by 15 minutes) is a bad item. [16:51]
- Even a flat version giving 250 health, 2 m/s sprint speed, 10% ability range and 250 souls a minute would be a bad item, because other 3k items do more. [17:22]
- For more damage, weapon items give more; for more souls, weapon and spirit items open more kills or speed farming beyond passive income; for health, cheaper items give more survivability plus useful effects. [17:22]
- To low rank players who say it guarantees souls in a bad game: to improve you need to learn when to fight and when to farm; casual players buying it for fun is fair. [18:22]
- The first step to getting better is to stop buying Trophy Collector. [18:52]
- Buy instead: Cultist is the number one choice, instant value, helps get kills, shove creep waves, and kill walkers and towers faster because it does more damage to them, plus ability range. [19:22]
- For health, Spirit Shielding is an insane item right now. [19:22]
- For initiators like Pocket, rushing Majestic Leap as first item is super common in pro play; it gives basically 350 health when used so you can initiate easily. It is also amazing on Warden, Lash and others. [19:52]
- Silence Wave is really strong right now, and Surge of Power is really strong depending on your ability and the enemies. [19:52]
- Counterspell and Debuff Remover are insane later 3k items, but don't rush them; you need some damage first. [20:23]
- In general your first 3k item should not be a green item, since most are defensive, unless it is Veil Walker, which is crazy. [20:23]
- Cheat Death is a meme: Metro has not seen a situation where it saved someone, and it is a waste of 6k when better 6k items exist. Stay away from it. [20:53]
- The longer the game goes the faster you get money, so be smarter with money spent early; later you can sell items that don't fit your build without being punished as hard. [21:23]
