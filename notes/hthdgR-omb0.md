## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream during the holiday patch (late December 2025, before the expected mid-January patch). He coaches three players: a Haze duo-laning with a Pocket, a Wraith laning with Mo & Krill, and an Emissary Yamato laning with Lash.
Themes: fight less and farm side lanes, invading Sinners and jungle, running the Urn, Rejuvenator use, over-respecting and over-chasing, base defense from height, Yamato right click integration and ability buffering, Teleport angles, Tank Buster timing.
He ends with an unfinished one-take guide covering combos and game-phase roles for Abrams, Bebop, Billy, Calico, Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist and Victor.
