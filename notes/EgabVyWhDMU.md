# The Ultimate Guide To Victor - Undying Carry Machine
channel: Metro Deadlock

## Covers
A full Victor hero guide: how Pain Battery, Jumpstart, Aura of Suffering and the revive ultimate work, the ability order, a gun-leaning build in buy order with sell order, the passive farm-then-carry playstyle by game phase, and fight tips.
For players learning Victor or wondering why the pure spirit/aura style is falling off.

## Claims
- Victor is a carry and tank hybrid: pretty good in lane, falls off massively in the mid game, needs a lot of money, and becomes the strongest character in the game late game and very strong in team fights. [0:00]
- He benefits most from vitality and spirit items, and also does strong gun damage late. [0:30]
- His kit is built around damaging himself to damage the enemy; Aura of Suffering (3) deals damage over time in a radius around you and to yourself, and the longer it stays on the more damage both ways. [0:30]
- Pain Battery (1) is the only ability in Deadlock that charges only when you take damage; it has no cooldown of its own. [1:01]
- Jumpstart (2) takes a percentage of your current health, then heals you back, with very high spirit scaling. [1:32]
- The ultimate is a second life: when you die you pop back out after a second or two with 60% health at base, stunning nearby enemies, with more damage after revive as you level it. [1:32]
- Do not play around the ultimate; treat it as a safety backup for a second life, nothing else. [2:06]
- Pain Battery level 2 adds a slow; maxing it heals you for 20% of your missing health when you hit an enemy, a major power spike. [2:36]
- Jumpstart at base heals almost nothing (about 20 to 25 HP net at 528 HP), but two early points and spirit items make the healing far bigger than the health it costs; its heal scales at 1.8 times your spirit power. [2:36] [7:11]
- Because Jumpstart costs a percent of current health, it is better the lower you are; at near zero HP it costs almost nothing and gives the full heal. [3:38]
- Rule of thumb: delay Jumpstart until you are around half HP; the lower you are, the more valuable it is. [3:38]
- Aura of Suffering reaches maximum damage, to you and the enemy, after 10 seconds; leveling adds a slow in a radius and raises min and max damage, and the top level makes enemies take 13% more damage. [4:08]
- Aura of Suffering is not very useful early compared to Pain Battery and Jumpstart. [4:08]
- The ultimate's upgrades look good on paper but are weak in practice: level 1 adds gun damage, spirit damage and fire rate after revive, level 2 revives at 100% health, level 3 increases the stun radius and damage. [4:38]
- The final ultimate upgrade (radius and damage) is a wasted point leveled at the very end, if ever, because enemies have time to leave the radius before you revive even at max level. [5:08]
- Victor is good in lane thanks to decent base regen and a spammable short-cooldown Jumpstart, especially once Extra Charge adds charges. [5:38]
- Ability order: one point in Pain Battery first (you don't need healing at level one or two), then two points in Jumpstart early, then one value point in Aura of Suffering, then max Jumpstart, then max Pain Battery, then max Aura of Suffering; ignore the ultimate (one or two points optional for quality of life).
