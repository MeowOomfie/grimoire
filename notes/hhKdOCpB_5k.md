# Macro Beats Micro | Oracle Shiv Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-06-20
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hhKdOCpB_5k

## Covers
Shiv coaching review by Midknight of an Oracle-rank gun Shiv (Backstabber rush) laning with Pocket against Vyper and Mo & Krill. The student's micro is strong, so the notes are mostly macro.
Themes: respecting Krill and enemy ults, wave management after lane kills, when to leave a camp minion alive, diagonal air dashes and slide chains, and taking trades only when they are positive.
A 2025 video; the replay was on the patch just before the latest one (June 2025).

## Claims
Laning (Shiv and Pocket vs Vyper and Mo & Krill):
- Don't let Vyper walk in and shoot you freely; that should have cost 100 HP. [0:00]
- Krills tend to go for a light melee on you; be ready to parry. [0:30]
- Going for a melee on the Pocket is fine; worst case you trade. [0:30]
- When your Pocket got poked and has to back off, he can no longer support you, so don't turn a positive trade into a negative one by chasing the Vyper down. [1:00]
- Dashing at a Vyper like that is risky. [1:31]
- When Krill is overaggressive, look for a parry, throw a knife and kill him. [1:31]
- After a kill with the wave neutral (two of theirs dead, one of yours), don't shove; slow push. Shoving eats all four minions onto yourself, gives your Pocket no farm, costs you about 20% souls net since a duo lane splits souls, and the Vyper is back before she misses more than a minion. [2:01]
- The Backstabber rush on gun Shiv is good and fits an early advantage. [3:01]
- After Backstabber go Mystic Regeneration for something defensive; Healing Rite or extra regen also work. [3:01]
- Respect Vyper's venom: if she gets a slide and a venom on you, you die. [3:32]
- When Krill starts backing up, remember you have Backstabber and walk back to poke him; he won't dive you there. [4:03]
- Backstabber is finicky and rarely procs in lane; he suspects it doesn't proc on headshots. [5:03]
- Good dash: use it both to escape and to full clear the wave at once. [5:33]
- The melee there was a little risky but fine, because you hadn't shown melee earlier, so she wasn't thinking about parry. [6:03]
- After shoving, let the wave crash and farm a camp rather than boxes, because you want rage; buy Healing Rite and hit the T2 camp. [6:03]
- What separates a good Shiv from a great one is not how they build rage but how they maintain it. [7:05]
- Don't greed a knife at that HP; you died by about 5 HP. [7:37]
- A good use of right click is right before you ult. [8:38]
- When your Pocket has left lane, stop greeding the wave; you are in danger. [9:45]
- Throw a knife at a burrowing Krill: it is easy to hit and gives a lot of rage. [9:45]
- Pushing up that far was a spot that ends in your death about 7 times out of 10. [10:15]

Mid game:
- Not chasing with your dash was understandable, but jump up onto the bridge to get information on where Krill is; you still have dash to escape. [11:48]
- Leave the last creep alive on an enemy-side hybrid camp: the longer it stays alive, the longer the camp takes to respawn. [13:52]
- Camp rule: on a very deep enemy camp always leave one alive, even a normal camp. [15:22]
- A plain three-minion camp on their side is fine to full farm. [15:52]
- The hybrid camp near secret shop before Guardians fall (about 22 minutes) is about 70 to 80% likely to be farmed next by them, so leave one alive; around 24 minutes it is closer to 60/40 and you can full farm it. [16:22]
- Simplest rule: leave one alive on hybrid camps, and on their basement leave a small creep alive about 90% of the time, since their hybrid farmer will come in later and get one minion. [17:23]
- Don't jump into that fight; they will see you and curse you or all focus you. [18:59]
- The fastest way to move is a diagonal air dash; work them in whenever you can, for example when crossing between roofs. Good players angle their camera to prep for it. [21:02]
- Diagonal air dashes are a minor min-max to lock down on the way to Ascendant. [21:32]
- When you dash jump, chain it: dash jump, slide, jump, slide, instead of a single dash jump. [23:07]
- When you have no kill potential on someone, your primary objective is building rage. [23:38]
- Walk out of a fight you would only trade in; hit a couple of camps or throw a knife to keep rage, then leave or take the rope. [24:09]
- His flaw: he "sees red" when an enemy is near a kill threshold and locks onto them. [24:39]
- Trading is good when your team is losing and you have fewer souls, since you get more for killing them than they get for you. [25:39]
- When the game is neutral or you are winning, don't look for a 50/50 trade unless it is their hard carry; you want to be the one with the game in your hands, and you lose more souls by trading when ahead. [26:10]
- Trading for Krill is not great because he does not need souls much; trading for a hyperfarming Haze can be nice. [26:41]
- Holding a door open and spam shooting through it leaves the enemy nothing to do about it; less efficient with Shiv's shotgun but still free rage. [27:11]
- Against a big wave, dash into it and dash back out. [27:41]
- Shoot for about 3 to 5 denies; Shiv has a shotgun without Krill's fire rate. [28:43]

His notes:
- Respect Krill's ult: give him space when he has Warp Stone or Phantom Strike. If he burrows at you, dash to avoid it; with good spacing he can't catch you. [29:14]
- Use diagonal air dashes and dash jump slide jump slide chains. [30:16]
- Wave management in lane: whenever enemies die, look at their respawn timers and ask how to deny the most minions. Sometimes that is shoving under their tower when they are out for 20 seconds, but usually it is not hitting the wave and letting the minions kill each other. [31:17]
- You may be a little over-chasey, such as pushing Krill into the Walker or staying stuck in lane. [32:18]
- If you lose a lot on Shiv without being the problem, focus on improving your own play and the rank will follow. [32:48]
