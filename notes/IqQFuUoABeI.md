# The Enemy Team Made 1 Mistake and We Won | Educational Eternus 6 Gameplay Commentary #5
channel: Natan
date: 2025-05-25
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IqQFuUoABeI

## Covers
Eternus 6 Seven game (0 deaths in 30 minutes) commented by the player, with Abrams in lane vs Ivy and Viscous, down three guardians at 10 minutes and won after an enemy mistake.
Covers wave first over boxes, holding waves to force answers, flex slots from walkers, Silence Glyph and spirit shred items, not staggering, Urn vs mid boss choices, and sliding to extend the magazine.
For Seven players and anyone learning comeback macro, May 2025 patch.

## Claims
- Their comp was much better: Lash can't really play against his team. [0:30]
- Seven and Abrams vs Ivy and Viscous is a lane neither side wins: Ivy wave clear vs Seven wave clear, and you'll never kill them with Stone Form, The Cube and Healing Nova. [0:30]
- Rushing Healing Nova is the current meta and he hopes it gets nerfed. [0:30]
- After the third wave, 2 minutes in, boxes spawn; stop looking for poke and grab the boxes. [1:00]
- His Abrams going too far for boxes gave the enemy wave priority and the first push with damage on your guardian; teammates should take the wave first instead of running for boxes. [1:30]
- Buy Monster Rounds and Extra Regen to stay healthy in this lane, keep clearing waves, and take boxes in between when possible. [1:30]
- An extremely low Abrams fighting while Seven is still dealing with the wave just dies, dropping a fat bag of souls from the boxes he ran. [2:21]
- He should have dashed and parried for his Abrams, and was slow to do it. [2:21]
- The bridge casting buff doesn't do much for him at this point, so he gives it to his nearby Abrams and takes the wave himself. [2:56]
- Boxes respawn after 3 minutes, so grab them again. [2:56]
- With Suppressor, Viscous can't do much damage to you. [3:30]
- Once Viscous has used The Cube and is very low, if he steps up it's a free kill. [3:30]
- Don't hit the tower solo while your Abrams isn't there; take a camp instead. [4:00]
- His Grey Talon roaming in to help when both enemy laners were full HP was a very big mistake: he shared the wave and lost his own green guardian. [4:00]
- He could have killed the Ivy by dashing in and parrying the guardian. [4:34]
- Pick up Healbane against teams with a lot of healing heroes. [4:34]
- He also bought Healbane because he expected the Kelvin to come gank after the green guardian fell, so the enemy Pocket could solo farm green. [4:34]
- When the enemy rotates in on you, keep clearing the wave and stall as much as possible. [5:06]
- A Kelvin without Frozen Shelter is killable, but Healing Nova kept him alive. [5:06]
- Without cooldowns, don't pointlessly shoot enemies; take the wave instead. [5:36]
- When your team leaves a 4v4 mid, back off and go for the center sacrifices while keeping a safe angle. [5:36]
- Check enemy items after a big hit to understand where the damage came from. [5:36]
- When Bebop is there and you can't walk up, use the time to go back to base if the wave isn't at your guardian yet. [6:07]
- Using the zipline boost to try a big ult off the zipline into a crowd can work if they have no way to cancel it, but here they canceled it. [6:07]
- Suppressor and Healbane still tag everyone even when the fight is lost. [6:38]
- Don't walk up into a spot where Bebop can hook you. [6:38]
- When behind and not strong yet, skip the Urn fight, push the blue wave and farm. [7:10]
- Carrying 600 unsecured souls, be really careful not to die. [7:10]

- With a lot of unsecured souls, spend them before joining a fight. [8:12]

- If it's too late to defend a lane, go back and push a wave instead. [9:21]
