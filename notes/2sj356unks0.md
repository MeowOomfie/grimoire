## Covers
Midknight Vods runs a Graves-only free coaching stream right after Graves released, coaching four Graves players (Emissary, Archon, Alchemist, and an Eternus 3 player in an Ascendant lobby) and reviewing several chat builds, including Lightbringer's and Wick's.
Themes: why gun Graves is bad and spirit is far better, the 1 (Jar of the Dead, "pickles"), the 2 root wall, the 3 weapon-damage steal, the 4 Tombstone ult for split and side-lane pressure, AP order, Split Shot, duration items, Restorative Shot versus Extra Regen, Echo Shard versus Rapid Recharge, and light meleeing the skulls as counterplay.
Ends with a patch reaction (the January 2026 patch with hero banning, many item changes, hero nerfs and Street Brawl tweaks) with a verdict on each change.
