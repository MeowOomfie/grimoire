- Baba seems to have a high skill barrier; you cannot just pick her up and destroy everyone. [2:43]
- This lane is probably the best lane for the healing pickups. [3:13]
- Grey Talon's playstyle is very close to Vindicta's. [4:44]
- Grey Talon's gun is really strong early, so he likes gunning people down at the start of the game. [4:44]

- Baba's early laning does not look good; she is now the second new hero with a really bad laning stage. [5:55]

- He wants his bird to start one-shotting people, so he needs many more bird stacks than 8. [9:27]
- Grey Talon does not get hit much because he roams and stays at the back of team fights instead of playing front of map, so he buys few or no green items, only Counterspell and Spirit Resilience. [9:58]
- He does a lot of gun damage with only two gun items; his gun is like a spirit item in disguise. [12:01]
- He bought Tankbuster and Indom (as captioned); he expects Tankbuster to be very strong. [12:01]
- Look to invade the enemy's farm when they are not paying attention. [12:01]

- Bird Victor whenever you can; he gives a free bird stack every time. [15:14]
- Getting a Walker is better than the Rift in that spot; let them have the Rift and fight them after. [15:45]
- The Stamina Mastery buff is very strong. [16:16]
- Bird stacks are shockingly hard to get; 24 stacks means 24 spirit power. [18:53]
- The bird does not work up at that high spot. [23:05]
- He thinks Grey Talon is better now than before the new map because the map is so open, letting him do much more. [23:37]
