## Covers
Minimap-only VOD review of a Lash game by supertf (Eternus 5, about rank 861 NA), going 14/10/12 against Pocket and Viscous in lane. Covers Lash laning, recognizing unwinnable situations, kill share, side lane pressure and wave states in the mid game, Rift timing, and how to fight on the current gun Lash build in late game teamfights, with ratings out of 10.
For Lash players and mid to high rank players who over-commit or wander mid.

## Claims

- Headshot Booster is one of the most common first items for Lash, giving a lot of damage and poke in lane. [3:04]
- Keep proc items like Headshot Booster on cooldown whenever possible; Viscous is particularly easy to headshot. [3:04]
- Playing defensively for denies is fine when your lane has pressure but cannot do much with it. [3:34]
- Be aggressive when the troopers are in the enemy tower. [3:34]
- Being careless with last hits and getting denied a lot is a problem because last hits are very important right now. [3:34]
- Getting the small camp the second it spawns is what separates good from great players. [4:04]
- Boxes spawn around 3 minutes; watch whether you are on time after that wave. [4:04]
- A good Lash tries to hit both laners with Flog. [4:04]
- As Lash, focus damage on Pocket, who has negative spirit resistance. [4:34]
- Going to boxes right away when very hurt is a good decision, since you cannot be aggressive. [4:34]
- Duo partners need to be on the same page: his Grey Talon pushed the wave while he ran boxes. [5:04]
