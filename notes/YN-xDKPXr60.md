# How to Carry as Mirage in Deadlock
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-10-07
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YN-xDKPXr60

## Covers
Mirage on the new map, aquatiq commentating over his own ranked game.
Gameplay commentary with reasons Mirage is strong now, farming on the new map (boxes, heavy crates, vents, walking camps), item choices and a build code.
Patch of early October 2026 (video 2026-10-07).

## Claims
- Mirage is very strong this patch because the new map is so open that it is hard for enemies to hide from Djinn's Mark. [0:31]
- Before the patch Mirage struggled to kill people because they could hide behind walls, so you rarely got more than one or two marks; now you can mark over and over. [8:45]
- The same open map reason is why Vindicta and Grey Talon are strong now too. [8:15]
- Mirage's old weakness was farm; before this patch you had to rush Ricochet to farm at all. [1:35]
- The new map has far more boxes and especially heavy crates, so Mirage can now farm like a tank. [4:41]
- Heavy barrels have a 100% spawn rate, so breaking them always gives souls. [6:12]
- Break every single heavy box you see. [7:13]
- Boxes gave around 200 souls each in the late game (207, 209), more than he thought a minion wave gives. [30:35]
- Camps now walk at you (like League of Legends camps), which makes Mirage's clear much faster; heavy punch them as they come. [5:11]
- A camp that used to be annoying to farm because the units were spread out is now easy. [6:43]
- On the fast moving target, wait until it hits the middle and then start the heavy punch; on the normal ones you still start the heavy punch three before the end. [7:43]
- The vents make you invisible while you sit on them and heal you about 5 HP a second; when the wave is not shoved, go sit on a vent to heal. [3:05]
- There are also healing snacks around the map and a statue with eyes where both eyes give the healing buff. [4:07]
- The bat spawns at about 5 minutes. [4:07]
- He maxed Djinn's Mark first and says he will max it every game until Mirage gets changed. [5:42]
- Four marks on a target means she is dead if she peeks. [6:12]
- The patch becomes a death ball meta around 20 minutes because there is so much farm: farm up and run at people. [11:51]
- Siphon Bullets vs Inhibitor: both are good, but he prefers Siphon Bullets this meta because everyone is fat with huge HP. [17:03]
- He bought Siphon Bullets so he can actually kill an Abrams in a 1v1, and hoped a teammate would buy Inhibitor. [17:34]
- When the lanes are bad, do not worry about killing people. [21:39]
- He planned to buy his vitality (green) items first, then go to the broker for a corrupted item. [22:10]
- Trading a mid boss for an enemy shrine is not a good trade; contest instead. [24:14]
- He needed spirit resist because the enemies had little bullet damage, so bullet resist did not matter. [25:49]
- Do not die while the Rift is up, or the enemy just takes the Rift; go to the shop for spirit resist, then farm, then teleport over. [25:49]
- Titanic Magazine paired with his high fire rate helps a lot. [26:21]
- Going for the Rift was more important than a Walker; if the enemy jumps the Rift and you kill them, mid boss is easy. [20:39]
- Buying too many items with stamina minuses can leave you with zero stamina. [29:02]
- Late game he enhanced Spirit Burn at the broker to see how much damage it does. [30:03]
- With spare souls late game, a teammate suggested Silencer and he planned to buy it. [31:36]
- If you can aim, play Mirage; he is very strong this patch. [33:44]
- Recommended build code 632506 (captioned "a 632 5 06"), a fan made build like Sire's: you can buy it left to right and add Spirit Burn late game for huge damage. [34:14]
