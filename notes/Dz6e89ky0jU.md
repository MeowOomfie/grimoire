## Covers
Mo & Krill, VOD review coaching by Midknight of an Oracle-rank Mo & Krill player in a FaceIt (coordinated) game, laning with Haze against Abrams and Seven. Mid-2025 video; the VOD itself is from about a week earlier, before the Healing Nova and Cultist Sacrifice nerfs.
Themes: taking negative trades and healing with Scorn, respecting Abrams' charge, shove and farm, instant cast versus confirm cast, Monster Rounds, enemy buffs and box clusters, Urn running with Burrow, pinging ult targets.

## Claims
Player 1 (Mo & Krill, Oracle):
- Intentionally take negative trades in lane, then press Scorn and heal it all back while they cannot. Wait until Burrow is leveled if you used Sand Blast early. [0:30]
- Headshot Booster and Monster Rounds are good early buys; Headshot Booster into a Seven feels great. [1:00]
- Look for the trade when Abrams' charge and Seven's stun are not up: jump in their face, take a negative trade, press Scorn. [1:00]
- As Mo & Krill you have an easy time getting denies; perma fish for denies. [1:31]
- Buying Extra Regen while full HP is autopilot. Seven is not a strong laner and you do not need Extra Regen into this lane. [2:02]
- Don't waste Sand Blast on someone already backing off; it saved maybe four bullets. [4:06]
- Once Seven has his ult, he goes from a weak laner to a weak laner with one button that can nearly one-shot you. [4:06]
- Pushing up while your Haze shows on the map going for the bridge buff is a very big misplay, especially in coordinated play; Abrams should charge you there. [4:36]
- Right after taking a negative trade, if Scorn just came online and Abrams' charge is down, run back at him and press Scorn. [5:11]
- Don't relax when Seven is stunned; it is a terrible habit. [5:11]
- Track Abrams' charge timer and be over-afraid of it coming back online. Don't dive into it. [5:41]
- Into Abrams and Seven you probably cannot dive because Abrams' charge is so scary (Seven's stun less so); shove the wave and then box route or farm a camp. [6:43]
- Healing Nova feels placebo nerfed, but Rescue Beam is now objectively better; in the current lobby everyone would have Rescue Beam instead of Healing Nova. [6:43]
- After a good trade that baits out Abrams' 40-second charge cooldown, don't dive, but hard shove the wave, stay and poke them under tower, look for a good Scorn, then hard shove the next wave and go farm. That wave without charge is a very positive window to poke under the Guardian. [7:46]
- Trying to siege the tower when Abrams has his charge is risky. [8:48]
- Sieging the tower when the bridge buff just spawned is reasonable because one of them may peel off for it, but it becomes troll when your Haze goes to shop and leaves you alone. [9:50]
- If you are ever getting shoved in, always buy Monster Rounds to keep your Guardian alive. [10:20]
- With Seven low and without ult, and with your ults up, pushing for a dive is fine. [10:51]
- Confirm the soul orbs; you hand-delivered that orb to them. [10:51]
- Stop using confirm cast. On instant cast you would have landed that ult. Confirm cast griefs you: you hold the ability while running at them, it telegraphs your intent (very obvious on Lady Geist), and by the time you left click they have walked out of range. [11:22]
- Instant cast shows a small icon under enemies when you are in range with an ability, which both tells you range and reminds you which actives (like Decay) are up. Instant cast is much better. [12:23]
- Quick cast is fine but does not show the icons; its upside is that holding the button does not throw the ability, so you can preview where something like an ice bomb lands while still learning the trajectory. [12:54]
- Cultist Sacrifice on Mo & Krill after the nerf would be pretty bad with no spirit damage; in the current meta Rescue Beam over Healing Nova and over Cultist Sacrifice, plus some spirit damage, would be significantly better. [13:27]
- With your team posturing elsewhere, grab the enemy's buffs; denying three buffs from the enemy feels great and is a real amount of power, beyond the souls. [14:32]
- There is a cluster of five boxes and an Urn-area box spot that people sleep on; grab those before taking the wave if you are not teleporting. [15:02]
- If you have Cultist Sacrifice up, beeline to the tier 3 camp to use it. [16:02]
- A fight taken 5v6 without your Haze was a bad fight even though you won it. [18:12]
- Use Healing Nova; one use from minute 10 to minute 24 is insanely bad. Refine item usage. [19:12]
- You missed the boxes and the Urn buff. [19:43]
- When the team calls Urn you can go back, but pushing base Guardians first and then going back for the Urn would have been better. [20:13]
- Burrow before you pick up the Urn: if you pick it up in Burrow you run a chunk of it with Burrow movement, which is faster. [20:13]
- In a 5v6 with all enemy waves negative and pushing into their base, an Urn fight is positive for you: one of them must catch the waves (making it 5v5) or you stall the fight while minions flood their base. Just play for time. [21:13]
- Warpstone on slot 4 is a psychotic keybind; bind your actives sensibly. [21:45]
- Don't back off from two enemies when your team backs you up; Seven's stun is 2 seconds of about 120 DPS, roughly 300 damage, which Krill eats for breakfast. [22:16]
- If you back off to read the minimap that is better than going in blind, but you should have glanced at the minimap while chasing. [22:46]
- Whenever you ult someone as Krill, middle click to ping them so your team focuses them; there is no downside and it is good muscle memory for solo queue. Midknight checks live and finds a ping now only highlights the target rather than giving vision through walls. [22:46]
- Summary: in lane, more aggression, take more bad trades when Abrams' charge is down so you can Scorn it back. [24:21]
- Summary: coordinate your ult with Haze's ult; pressing them together while Abrams' charge was down could kill either laner, even Abrams. Haze only ulted in lane once or twice. [24:51]
- Summary: use instant cast, use Fleetfoot more for chasing, be more aware of the minimap, don't be too scared, and ping your ult targets. [25:53]
