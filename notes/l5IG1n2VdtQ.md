# I Found The Most Optimal Celeste Build
channel: aquatiq
date: 2026-08-22
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l5IG1n2VdtQ

## Covers
Celeste, commentary over one game by aquatiq, with a few item choices named along the way rather than a full build walkthrough.
Covers her weak lane, strong late game, hard matchups (Vindicta, Apollo) and objective calls.
Patch of late August 2026, the patch that added a delay to Echo Shard.

## Claims
- Celeste is broken in the current patch. [0:00]
- Going against Vindicta and Apollo together as Celeste is torture; there is little you can do. [0:31]
- Her early lane is usually really bad; her late game is insane. [4:23]
- First lane buys: Extra Health, and he says he should have taken Extra Regen; he got Extra Regen next. [1:38]
- Enemy bullet velocity on many heroes is better than hers, which makes confirming souls in lane hard. [2:40]
- When you cannot do much in lane, just clear the wave and take the camp up the lane instead of trading. [6:05]
- Mid game buy path he chose: an item into Fortitude, then straight to Mercurial Magnum, skipping Healing Booster because he did not need it. [10:52]
- Toxic Bullets carried his previous Celeste game and is so good at finishing people off. [18:45]
- Stamina Mastery is close to a permanent buy on her, for reaching high places. [19:18]
- With a big soul injection late, his next item was Greater Expansion. [25:05]
- He wanted Restorative Locket in a fight against heavy ultimates. [15:29]
- A soul lead of about one walker is not much; do not let the number deceive you. [21:19]
- Celeste melts guardians, walkers and camps very fast. [21:19]
- Push with Rift minions after taking the Rift. [13:57]
- When three enemies are dead, take Mid Boss; skipping it then is a mistake. [16:06]
- Do not join a push you are sure will fail; farm your camps instead of dying again. [22:20]
- Late game she wants to stay up in the air. [22:51]
- Echo Shard was nerfed: there is now a small delay on the copy, mostly noticeable on heroes like Bebop doing double bombs. [28:14]
