# The BEST Wraith Build In Deadlock - META Build Update
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2025-02-11
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iMuQFhuarig

## Covers
Metro's Heroic Wraith build (Heroic Aura, double barriers, Tesla Bullets) compared to his Surge V3 build, February 2025 patch era on the old map. Covers lane cards, why Quicksilver Reload and Swift Striker now come before Tesla, one-clipping Walkers, late game 6k choices and situational items. For Wraith players who want an early split-push power spike; items and numbers may be stale.

## Claims
- The Heroic Wraith build (public as "Metro heroic wraith") is the best Wraith build right now; the Surge V3 build is still good and each is better in different situations. [0:00]
- Lane barely changed: cards got a bit weaker but are still the best tool to win lane, so in lane focus on card and spirit damage. [1:00]
- After lane, focus on farming speed, then killing objectives fast and winning 1v1s, then become a hard carry; many people struggle with Wraith because they misplay her. [1:30]
- Wraith is a solid 1v1 laner: not the strongest, but she cannot really lose 1v1 lanes either. [1:30]
- Lane order: Ammo Scavenger, Extra Charge, Infuser; spirit items first because last hitting is good anyway with a big magazine and lane damage comes from cards. [2:00]
- Get Infuser as early as possible, ideally first, for the extra 20 spirit on every card; Extra Charge gives three cards. [2:30]
- Start every lane by leveling Card Trick once, then a second time; at 2.2k souls (Ammo Scavenger, Extra Charge, Infuser plus another item) cards start doing huge damage. [2:30]
- With about 20 Ammo Scavenger stacks, pop Infuser, Full Auto and throw three cards to almost one shot an even-soul enemy; that is the entire lane kill combo. [3:01]
- Then Rapid Rounds and Monster Rounds: Monster Rounds farms and pushes lanes quickly early; Rapid Rounds over Basic Magazine because fire rate is king and Ammo Scavenger and Infuser already give extra ammo. [3:01]
- If lane is still going, buy Enchanter's Barrier: active while you have a spirit shield, and with the other items you have a 370 spirit shield, so cards hit even harder. [3:31]
- Then Extra Stamina, a must buy on almost every hero and the best item in the game: one extra stamina, fire rate and faster stamina recharge. [4:03]
- Sprint Boots early is a waste: sprint starts only after a while without shooting or taking damage, which rarely happens in lane or on an active farming route; it takes roughly 4 seconds to start. [4:34]
- Fortitude is overrated on Wraith for the same reason: its regen needs 10 seconds without taking damage. [5:06]
- Change: Quicksilver Reload and Swift Striker now come before Tesla Bullets (previous builds rushed Tesla after lane). Quicksilver gives instant reload, fire rate and card damage; Swift Striker gives ammo and fire rate, and together they raise farming speed enough to beat Tesla first. [5:37]
- At 1,250 souls in lane, buy Quicksilver, rotate to a creep camp, then get the next 1,250 item, then Tesla; tested over 20-40 games. [6:08]
- Exception: if you dominate lane, kill the enemy tower fast and get kills (3k early), rushing Tesla can be worth it. [6:39]
- Then Tesla Bullets and Fleetfoot; with no flex slot yet, buy Combat Barrier first. [7:09]
- Running both Combat Barrier and Enchanter's Barrier is the current meta: barriers give huge early effective HP plus offensive bonuses (Combat Barrier 22% weapon damage and 7% fire rate; Enchanter's spirit and cooldown), while Spirit Armor or Bullet Armor (6% weapon damage) add nothing offensive. [7:39]
- With both barriers you rarely die when ganked; if they get broken, chill about 40 seconds (50 second cooldown), regen and push lanes, and only re-engage once both barriers are up. Barriers do not go down to creep camps. [8:44]
- Barriers are probably overpowered and due a nerf. [9:45]
- By Fleetfoot you should have a flex slot; if not, you can sell Monster Rounds early, or buy an early Healbane, but do not sell before Heroic Aura. [9:45]
- For Heroic Aura, sell Monster Rounds if needed; Heroic Aura is the build's major power spike. [10:15]
- Surge vs Heroic: the Surge build (Surge of Power, Bullet Lifesteal, Burst Fire, later Duration Extender) is about huge burst during Full Auto; Heroic gives far higher early tower pushing because you choose when to pop its 6 second fire rate buff. [10:45]
- Burst Fire only procs on hitting enemy heroes and lasts 4 seconds, so you cannot choose when; Heroic Aura can be popped on a tier three camp or a Walker. [11:17]
- At 14-15k souls you can one-clip a side Walker: walk up, pop Infuser, Full Auto and Heroic Aura, empty the magazine, reload with Quicksilver, use Fleetfoot. [11:48]
- In team fights, initiate with Fleetfoot and Full Auto, or kite out with Heroic Aura's movement speed when chased. [12:19]
- This build has more actives; on average actives are better than passives in Deadlock if you use them properly, and it just takes practice (Infuser timing was hard at first too). [12:49]
- Heroic Aura gives an earlier spike because it lets you skip Bullet Lifesteal: Heroic Aura gives 16% bullet lifesteal versus 24% from Bullet Lifesteal, and saves a slot and money. [13:51]
- 16% bullet lifesteal is enough if you use Full Auto properly: Full Auto adds 20% bullet and spirit lifesteal, and with Infuser you reach about 33% bullet and 36% spirit lifesteal. [14:22]
- Tesla, Full Auto and card reloads all deal spirit damage, so spirit lifesteal stays useful; only engage while Full Auto is active and you will be fine. [14:53]
- Skipping Surge of Power, buy Bullet Resist Shredder for damage: it procs on everyone hit by Tesla since Tesla is spirit damage, and works on creeps and towers too. [15:24]
- Fleetfoot and Heroic Aura replace the movement speed Surge of Power gave, and Heroic Aura lasts 6 seconds; Project Mind is still there to escape. [15:54]
- Then Healbane: almost everyone has lifesteal or healing and anti-heal is becoming one of the best stats; it is always active through Tesla. Run it to stop enemy healing, the heal after a kill is a bonus. [16:24]
- Then Intensifying Magazine (also added to the Surge build): handled correctly, especially after Lucky Shot, you have enough ammo for value, and it adds ammo itself; a pretty small 3k investment. [17:26]
- By Intensifying Magazine you should have two flex slots; sell order is Monster Rounds, then Extra Charge, then Rapid Rounds. [17:26]
- Sell Extra Charge because cards do almost no damage later; think of cards as great in lane and a reload tool later with Quicksilver. Slides also extend damage. [17:58]
- Buy left to right until late game; in late game buy what you need, usually one of the first four late items first. [18:28]
- If you still lack damage, Lucky Shot: much more damage and a slow to chase or kite, with 30% extra ammo that suits the build; often bought eventually anyway. [18:59]
- Colossus is one of the strongest items in the game and underbought: not just a front line tank item, it gives 40% bullet and 40% spirit resist for 7 seconds, which is usually enough to decide a fight. [19:30]
- Colossus use: go in, pop it, burst one person down, then kite out or teleport and let your team finish. [20:01]
- Colossus is usually your last active; to fit it, sell whichever barrier you need least, since barriers break fast in late game. [20:33]
- Colossus against heavy burst; Siphon Bullets against teams that deal damage over time with little burst (example: Infernus, Haze, Kelvin, Lady Geist, no Mo & Krill or Abrams to grab and instakill you). [21:04]
- Siphon Bullets gets better the longer the fight goes and is the more offensive option compared to Colossus. [22:05]
- First 6k item rule: Lucky Shot for more damage, Colossus to be tanky against burst, Siphon Bullets to be tanky in sustained fights. [22:36]
- Mystic Slow against heavy gun damage (Haze, gun Vindicta, three or more fire rate gun heroes): 40% fire rate slow on everyone hit by Tesla and those near them; a cheaper multi-target Inhibitor, but it adds no damage or tankiness. [23:06]
- When actives are full and you want Unstoppable, sell Infuser; Unstoppable is the hard carry button when you are the most farmed, and counters ults focused on you (Lash, Mo & Krill, Dynamo). It is a luxury item. [24:06]
- Leech is a high value upgrade from Healbane, with no extra slot needed: more lifesteal, weapon damage and tankiness, and raises Healbane's healing reduction to 60% from 40. [25:36]
- With Leech, sell Combat Barrier for Healing Booster, which boosts lifesteal and reduces enemy anti-heal; you reach about 64% bullet and 55% spirit lifesteal with actives. [26:06]
- Almost never sell Extra Stamina, keeping it over both barriers into late game; Superior Stamina is a great hard meta choice. [26:37]
- Superior Stamina movement: air jump then immediately double dash covers more distance faster than dash jump. [27:07]
- Situational: Crippling Headshot if your team cannot burst a very tanky target with extra bullet armor and you need resist reduction. [27:38]
- Situational: Toxic Bullets in the rare case the enemy has so much lifesteal that Healbane is not enough. [28:09]
- Improved Spirit Armor is bought less now because Colossus already gives 40% spirit resist; Colossus gives 40% bullet and 40% spirit for a bit more than the 4,250 that 45% spirit resist costs. [28:09]
- Debuff Remover against Pocket, Bebop and Infernus; Curse if you really need to shut someone down. [28:39]
- Diviner's Kevlar removed from the build; not bought in a month and not worth it currently. [29:09]
- Surge V3 shares the lane and defensive items but uses Hollow Point Ward instead of Rapid Rounds and is more spirit focused around Surge of Power on Full Auto; you are strong while Full Auto is up and weaker at split pushing when it is not. [29:39]
- Best way to play Wraith now: take Walkers and pickups as early as possible; Heroic Aura does that better because it adds early movement speed and can be spammed, while Surge of Power is tied to Full Auto's long early cooldown. [30:09]
- You can mix and match, for example drop Bullet Resist Shredder and run both Heroic Aura and Surge of Power, but Surge slows your progression into the late game carry. [30:40]
- Follow the build one to one through Intensifying Magazine and your mid game build is done: you get every Walker for free, then worry about hard carrying. [31:12]
- Do not play Wraith defensively: she is the best split pusher in the game, kills towers, Walkers and objectives faster than anyone, so push objectives; he kills multiple Walkers around 15 minutes in most games, even Eternus. [31:12]
