## Claims
- Rat King is bigger than Abrams. [0:00]
- His gun feels weak without gun items, but it is not bad once you have gun items. [0:32]
- Scrap Grenade (1) detonates multiple times, four detonations, dealing bullet damage and slowing; it bounces in random directions and seems to attach to people. [1:02]
- The final Scrap Grenade detonation has a longer fuse but a bigger radius, more bullet damage and a stronger slow, like Vindicta's ult where the last shot hits harder. [1:33]
- Rat Swarm (2) sends rats that leap on the enemy for spirit damage over time, like scarabs, and reduce bullet resist with every bite; more attached rats bite faster. [2:04]
- Enemies can shake off a rat early with a ground or air dash. [2:04]
- Each Rat Swarm bite takes 2% bullet resist, so it should be good against gun heavy teams. [2:04]
- Royal Pestilence (3) gives a barrier based on max health and, while it holds, a chance to deflect bullets back at the attacker. [2:35]
- Colossus is very good on him because the barrier scales with max health; it can give around +1,000 HP. [2:35]
- Royal Pestilence looks like a hard counter to gun heroes like Haze. [2:35]
- The Royal Pestilence upgrade caps all damage at 250 per hit and adds 10% max health as barrier. [3:06]
- The build he looked at maxes the ult first. [3:06]
- The ult (Banner of Rattania) gives all nearby allies more move speed, slow immunity and reduced incoming damage while near the banner. [3:06]
- When you slam the banner the sewer erupts, dealing damage with a knock up and slow, so land it in a group of enemies like Abrams's ult; rats then swarm out applying Rat Swarm. [3:37]
- The banner turns very slowly while carried. [3:37]
- The banner lasts 23 seconds. [4:09]
- Save the ult for mid boss and the Rift: the move speed, slow immunity and damage reduction let your team take the pit and get out fast if the enemy contests. [4:09]
