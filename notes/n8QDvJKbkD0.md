## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream on the day of the 12/15/25 Christmas update. He opens with an Abrams tech, coaches a Haze (Ascendant) and an Ivy (Alchemist), checks the holiday update, then plays three games himself (two Pocket, one Lady Geist) duoing with another coach.
