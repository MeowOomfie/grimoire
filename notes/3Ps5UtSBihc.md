# Why I Play Gun Wraith Over Card Wraith.
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2024-10-06
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Ps5UtSBihc

## Covers
A 2024 ramble comparing card (spirit) Wraith and gun Wraith: shared lane start, how each build fights, scaling, farming, and a hybrid build the coach prefers. For Wraith players choosing a build. This is from October 2024; items, numbers and the meta have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- Both builds start the same: you win lane mostly with abilities, because Wraith's gun does very low damage early; use the gun mostly to secure last hits and charge cards, then throw the cards. [1:02]
- Lane builds for both start with mystic and spirit items to ramp up card damage. [1:02]
- Extra Charge is almost always bought because it gives one extra card, which is very valuable in lane. [1:33]
- Mystic Burst is almost a must have: even an unleveled level one card gets its bonus damage. [1:33]
- Some players go harder into cards in lane with Ammo Scavenger, Infuser or both, rushing four purple items very early. [1:33]
- Ammo Scavenger gives stacking spirit power as long as you secure or deny souls; kept active it lasts 30 seconds, up to 20 stacks of spirit power early. [1:33]
- Improved Spirit costs 3,500 for +23 spirit power (plus the 12 every spirit item gives), while full-stack Ammo Scavenger gives 20 to 24 spirit power for 500, so Improved Spirit costs about seven times as much for about 10 more spirit power. [2:04]
- Ammo Scavenger is an insane item for both card and gun Wraith. [2:35]
- The builds deviate once lane ends (their tower or yours is down): card Wraith buys items that raise spirit power overall, since card damage scales with spirit power. [3:05]
- Spirit power also scales Full Auto's cooldown and Wraith's movement speed, but most card players rush spirit for card damage. [3:05]
- Card Wraith has more burst in mid and late game: in the first one to two seconds of an engage with max cards it out-damages gun Wraith. [3:35]
- Once card Wraith runs out of cards, its damage drops sharply and it is less sustainable and less tanky. [4:05]
- Card builds invest mid game in spirit power: an extra card with Rapid Recharge, Surge of Power, and little lifesteal or defense. [4:05]
- Spirit Lifesteal on card Wraith is possible but not worth it in practice, because you don't throw cards non-stop. [4:05]
- Card Wraith fight pattern: engage with max cards, lift (Telekinesis) a target and burst it, or lift one person in a teamfight and dump cards, then sit back and try to sustain; damage falls off because recharging cards needs gun damage, which is low on a spirit build. [4:35]
- The mid game spike from Surge of Power, then Improved Burst and Rapid Recharge, is insane; card Wraith does more damage than gun Wraith in mid game. [5:06]
- Card damage falls off late because you can never hold more than four cards; once they are spent, no amount of spirit items helps until you recharge. [5:06]
- Ricochet is run on card Wraith to recharge cards faster and add sustain. [5:36]
- Card Wraith is probably the better play style in the current meta. [5:36]
- The latest patch nerfed bullet damage: items that gave spirit resist now give bullet resist (for example Frenzy), bullet armor was buffed and Improved Bullet Armor got a major buff, so the meta is focused on spirit damage. [5:36]
- Playing off meta is not a bad idea: high level players expect spirit damage and rush Spirit Armor, so his gun Wraith was rarely hard countered; good players check your inventory and then buy Metal Skin. [6:07]
- Gun Wraith is a much harder carry; if you want to be your team's main carry, gun is still the way to go, and if teammates can't carry, card Wraith looks rough. [7:08]
- In a mid-late fight, a three-man lift plus four cards kills one or two people and then you die or escape with little damage or sustain left; gun Wraith with the same lift kills one or two and sustains much longer with Vampiric Burst and Bullet Lifesteal while damage keeps ramping. [7:39]
- Gun Wraith has no single power spike in a fight; card Wraith's damage falls off if a fight lasts more than 5 to 10 seconds, and the longer the fight the worse it gets. [8:09]
- Card Wraith combo: rush Diviner's Kevlar then Refresher; open with a mass lift, throw four cards, Refresher, repeat, for two mass ultimates and eight cards. [8:09]
- Card Wraith is still an insanely powerful late game carry. [8:39]
- Gun Wraith scales to the hard carry role faster: less mid game kill and burst potential, but much higher farming potential, clearing creep camps insanely fast and spiking in souls. [8:39]
- Many pro players consider card Wraith broken right now, so he expects a nerf. [9:09]
- Play whichever build you like most. [9:40]
- Card Wraith's downside: if you spent cards farming jungle and get ganked, you have nothing; without at least two cards ready the enemy will probably kill you. [9:40]
- Card Wraith needs more micromanagement: keep cards ready (which slows farming), have cards before a fight, or open slowly shooting from the back to charge cards. [10:11]
- Gun Wraith is always ready: even with all cards used and caught out, lift them, point at their head and they die. [10:41]
- His recommendation is a hybrid build that focuses mostly on gun but keeps strong spirit damage: not the highest burst or the longest sustain, but you can adapt items to what you need. [10:41]
- Hybrid order: same card lane start, then farm items. [11:43]
- Fleetfoot is his number one item for Wraith; some prefer Kinetic Dash, but Fleetfoot is broken because you shoot with no movement penalty, not because of its active. [11:43]
- He still buys Tesla Bullets most games, sometimes skipping it for Intensifying Magazine, then farms the jungle. [11:43]
- Quicksilver Reload is one of the best items for Wraith because you can reload every time you throw a card. [12:13]
- Bullet Resist Shredder gives spirit damage and makes enemies hit by a card take more damage from your gun. [12:13]
- After Intensifying Magazine and maybe Enduring Speed, choose Surge of Power or Headhunter: Headhunter adds gun damage, while rushing Surge of Power suddenly adds card damage that enemies reading your inventory as gun Wraith don't expect. [12:44]
- Other surprise pivots from the hybrid build: rush Refresher or buy Diviner's Kevlar. [12:44]
- Ethereal Shift is an amazing Wraith item: very survivable and adds spirit damage; he buys it over Escalating Resilience or Spirit Armor because it actively saves him, and cards hit very hard after coming out of it. [13:14]
- The choice: gun for a fast, efficient farming mega DPS carry, card for a mid to late game ganker who initiates, bursts people and brings teamfight AOE stun like the mass lift. [13:45]
- Swap builds to counter the enemy: if they all buy Metal Skin against your gun, there is no reason after Intensifying Magazine not to go Surge of Power and more spirit damage, or Refresher for a double mass lift, or Diviner's Kevlar for resistance and spirit power while using your ult. [14:16]
