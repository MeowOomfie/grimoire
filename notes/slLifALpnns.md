# Top 5 reasons you are losing lane in Deadlock
channel: Deathy  date: 2024-08-26  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=slLifALpnns

## Covers
Five laning mistakes of new players: not hitting creeps, ignoring creep advantage (wave equilibrium), not buying lane items (Healing Rite, kill items, Lash sustain), not prioritising denies (soul swing math), missing rotation opportunities (gank solo lanes, soul sharing)

## Claims
- He has played Deadlock for about 3 months with almost 600 games and sees new players make the same lane mistakes. [0:01]
- Reason 1, you are not hitting creeps enough: new players treat the game like Overwatch or an FPS and brawl with enemies instead of hitting creeps; the game is a MOBA first and FPS second, and creeps are the most important thing in lane. [0:01]
- Prioritise hitting creeps and only be aggressive when you have a reason, such as a creep advantage. [0:33]
- Hitting creeps frees up opportunities: you can be aggressive and rotate; when creeps push into you, you have fewer opportunities and are usually the one receiving aggression. [1:03]
- Reason 2, you are not paying attention to creep advantage (wave equilibrium, whether the wave is pushing into you or against you); sometimes the enemy has better wave clear and the lane pushes into you, which is just how the game works. [1:03]
- If the wave is pushing into you (say they have three or four creeps and you have one or zero), be very careful and do not be aggressive: creeps do significant damage and tanking them makes the fight go badly. [1:33]
- Newer players take fights while down creeps, when it is not a good time for them. [2:04]
- Reason 3, you are not buying items for the lane: for example, newer players get really hurt and do not buy Healing Rite or go back to base. [2:04]
- You can follow a formula for lane items, but a lot of the time it will not work and you have to adapt. [2:34]
- On Lash he takes quite a bit of damage but has not bought healing, because Lash has good sustain with his whip; he mostly bought items that let him kill. [2:34]
- Killing in lane is one of the best ways to get ahead, so usually you want your lane items to be for killing. [2:34]
- Reason 4, you are not prioritising denies: every deny is roughly an 80 soul swing, since you get about 37 souls for a deny early and they lose about 37. [3:05]
- Kills are usually about a 400 soul swing (you get 200 or so and they fall behind), so denying an entire wave is about as good as a kill in most situations. [3:37]
- Deny tip: pay attention to the wave in general, which of your creeps are hurt or about to die, and what your opponent is shooting. [3:37]
- Reason 5, you are not paying attention to rotation opportunities: you push the lane so you can do other things, either pressure your opponent under Tower or rotate. [4:08]
- One way to win your lane is to go win another lane: if you can shove your lane all the time, go gank another lane. [4:38]
- Gank solo lanes, because of soul sharing: with two people in a lane souls are shared equally, and with three you get very diminishing returns, so you definitely do not want three in a lane early. [4:38]
