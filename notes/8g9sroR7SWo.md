## Covers
Natan (Eternus 6) commentates his own Pocket game, laning with Billy against Drifter and Victor, then playing the map after a won lane.
Lane pushing and Healing Rite, item choices (Monster Rounds, Cold Front, Majestic Leap, Spirit Burn, Tesla Bullets), playing around winning lanes, keeping waves pushed, Rejuvenator pressure and not getting staggered before mid boss.
For mid to high rank Pocket players and anyone learning the wave and map game, September 2025.

## Claims
- Start lane by pushing to get wave priority; here the enemies conceded the push immediately. [0:30]
- Don't stack up with your lane partner; standing together lets one Drifter slash hit both, which adds up in lanes against a lot of AoE or grenades. [0:30]
- On Pocket, take side angles whenever possible so your cloak (Flying Cloak) passes through both the minions and the enemy laners for more value. [1:01]
- Pick up Monster Rounds in lane: it helps shove the lane harder and builds into Cultist Sacrifice later. [1:32]
- Focus on pushing the wave first, then look to trade; an enemy who oversteps is an instant kill. [1:32]
- After pushing the wave, buy Healing Rite and heal back up; ping that you will be right back. [2:05]
- At 2 minutes the camp and some boxes have spawned, so grab them while healing. [2:05]
- Mistake by the Billy: trading 1v2 before his partner arrives, then going in again with a risky melee. [2:36]
- Grab your healing trooper's heal when you return to lane. [2:36]
- After hitting someone with the cloak, shoot them instead of meleeing, because a smart enemy will parry as soon as the cloak hits. [2:36]
- With a lead and ultimate up, heal back up and buy items before looking for an all-in; Cold Front was the buy here. [3:07]
- Check whether the enemy is about to get their ultimate (Victor) before committing. [3:37]
- The bridge buffs spawn at 5:00. At 4:30, don't look to trade; push the wave to get priority so you and your partner can take the buffs instead of fighting as they spawn. [4:11]
- Use the zipline to gain height for Barrage. [4:41]
- At 3k souls in lane with a big lead he tried Majestic Leap (it also gives a barrier); normally he would buy Enchanter's Barrier and then Cultist Sacrifice later. [5:11]
- Healing Rite is crazy valuable in lane; pop it and wait for cooldowns between engagements. [5:44]
- Mistake: using Barrage slightly before ultimate was up when it could have waited. [6:14]
- Mistake: backing off from a free kill on the Victor; he should have dived the tower and gone after him. [6:14]
- Don't chase bags (dropped souls) when the enemies are respawning with the zipline boost. [6:14]
- Mistake: not committing harder on the tower and not saving the cloak to escape, which led to an avoidable death. [6:44]
- A big lead and a pushed mid lane are always very valuable because mid push gives a lot of map control. [6:44]
- Don't chase a Victor in his ultimate. [7:14]
- After pushing mid you can roam to green or yellow or take jungle camps, preferably the enemy camps. [7:14]
- Play around your winning teammates, not your losing ones, unless you are very confident you can help the losing lane get back into the game. He went to the even or winning green side to take that guardian and control the entire right side of the map. [7:14]
- When far ahead, steal all the enemy camps; you are not scared of anyone. [7:44]
- Superior Cooldown is a greedy buy he could afford only because his team was so far ahead. [8:16]
- Don't bother contesting someone stealing your center sacrifices (vaults) if you are not confident you can one-shot them, but check who it is. [8:16]
- Don't leave a lane to chase a kill if doing so lets the enemy (The Doorman) keep pushing your blue lane and take the guardian; clearing the wave is the consistent decision. [8:46]
- When far ahead, take the mid push and walk up to the mid tower; fighting a single enemy there is pointless. Either help with the Urn or go to another lane. [8:46]
- Before rotating, check whether teammates are already heading there; if two are already on yellow, go back and hold the blue wave so you don't lose that guardian and the map control. [9:17]
- His role after winning lane: help the green side, steal enemy camps, keep the blue wave pushed so the enemy cannot take your guardian, and farm. [9:48]
- Tier 3 on Barrage is a big power spike that gives much more amp. [9:48]
- Push the wave before joining a fight. [9:48]
- When your teammates push hard on one lane, support them by pushing somewhere else; pressuring the blue wave keeps The Doorman interested in you so he can't help on green. [10:19]
- When your teammates back off, back off too; with no enemies seen on the map and teammates far back or in base there is no reason to walk up to the walker. [10:49]
- You can play the map game together with your teammates by making consistent decisions even when not physically with them. [10:49]
- Mistake: a showboat parry attempt that failed and got him punished. The Grey Talon one-shot him. [11:30]
- When a side is already lost, you can still go there for exit kills as enemies try to leave. [11:30]
- Going for a walker while your team fights is not wrong, but if another teammate (the Ivy) is already taking it and you are stronger with your ultimate up, join the fight to win it quickly. [12:09]
- If you find the Grey Talon, ult him instantly because he is a threat. [13:14]
- Mistake: using Barrage when teammates weren't there to support; he should have quickly gotten out. Overextending gets punished. [13:14]
- Teammates not following a fight is reasonable when wave states are bad; when ahead, your waves (yellow and blue) should be pushed up. [13:14]
- When you respawn and your teammates are fighting, push a wave first to get priority so that after winning the fight you can take the walker or the mid walls (Shrines); plan ahead. [13:46]
- Games end faster at Eternus because players are not afraid to take mid boss; even 17 minutes in you can take mid boss after a few picks while ahead. [14:18]
- Wait for teammates to trigger the Grey Talon trap so you don't get disarmed, then jump in; against a Lash, parry if he melees or punch after he parries. [14:18]
- With the Rejuvenator, don't pressure just one lane; pressure multiple lanes at once. [14:48]
- Use the cloak to keep enemies busy and give your team space. [14:48]
- Wait for Majestic Leap to come off cooldown to get its barrier before engaging. [14:48]
- Mistake: forgetting he had Rejuvenator and backing off; with the extra life he could have been more aggressive and died in a position that punished them. [15:21]
- Every wave you push forces someone to respond in that lane, so that person is not in another lane; push multiple waves to create pressure. Ping waves you want teammates to push. [15:21]
- While waiting for teammates to arrive, clear a camp. [15:52]
- With Rejuvenators left, play for the objectives. [16:22]
- When low but holding Affliction, look for a flank angle. [16:22]
- Stay on the zipline a little longer to close the gap before dropping on a target. [16:52]
- Once Rejuvenators are gone and you are low with someone dead, back off: keep waves pushed, take all the jungle on the map and expand the lead. [17:24]
- Spirit Burn is a great Pocket item for three reasons: it procs very easily, Pocket builds item cooldown (Transcendent Cooldown) so it procs more often than on most heroes, and it helps farm tier 3 camps. [17:55]
- Use your ultimate (Affliction) even if it hits only one person; killing anyone, even a 20k soul Drifter, gives an instant numbers advantage that wins the fight. Don't save it for the perfect five-man. [17:55]
- After dropping the patron, decide whether to end the game or wait; with the enemy Victor's ultimate coming up and teammates dead, back off and take all the farm. [18:26]
- At around 30k to 40k souls ahead, Tesla Bullets is an affordable buy. [19:00]
- Right before mid boss spawns is very dangerous: if you get staggered (die one by one), the enemies can still win the game. After showing two players on the map, back off and don't get caught. Getting caught before mid boss is the easiest way to throw. [19:34]
- If a teammate got caught before the mid boss spawn, the enemies could take a random mid boss on spawn while you can't respond, which could cost the game. [20:34]
- When only one more fight is expected, don't bother saving Cultist Sacrifice for tier 3 camps; use it on a trooper. [21:04]
- Push the wave, then use the zipline to rotate to the fight; Pocket generally wants to join fights late with Majestic Leap. [21:04]
- Use Satchel to dodge damage. [21:34]
- The game is really about waves: keep waves pushed, help get objectives and stop the enemies taking yours. Realizing this massively improves gameplay, especially without a MOBA background. [22:05]
