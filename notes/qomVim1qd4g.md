## Covers
Midknight Vods free coaching stream, no analysis segment, a lot of stream setup banter. He reviews four viewer games in the June 2026 patch era.
Players coached: a Seven (Ascendant) on an ult-max build, a Drifter (Emissary) laning with Abrams into Bebop and Silver, an Ivy (Alchemist) playing support with a Haze, and a Silver (Ritualist) laning with Ivy into Shiv and Mo & Krill.
Recurring themes: play for the urn and call mid boss off advantages, hit the wave, slide and mantle slide more, soul and map awareness, coin-flip and at-best fights, the support fallacy, plus side opinions on gun damage, Slowing Bullets and Shiv.
