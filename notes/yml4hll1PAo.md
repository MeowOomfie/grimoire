## Covers
Midknight coaches an Eternus-ranked Calico (patch of late July 2026).
Themes: the 3 minute wave decision (fight with Spirit Strike or box out for Stalker), bursty vs sustain trades, Gloom Bombs aim and combo order, Arctic Blast timing, box and tunnel routing, denying the urn, roaming for picks, Rift and mid boss timing, and overusing ult and overfighting.

## Claims
Player (Calico, Eternus), lane and trades:
- Invade the enemy tier one camp when you have space; Gloom Bombs on the tier one feels good. [0:33]
- The early build is solid; you could take Spirit Strike a little earlier for more tempo, given a Sinclair lane. [1:03]
- The 3 minute wave is the decision point: if you think you can kill them, buy Spirit Strike and fight the next wave or two; if not, box out, play low for Stalker, and fight after. [1:03]
- The best way to throw a lead is forcing a bad dive. [1:03]
- Against Sinclair's bolts: do not burn all your stamina before he recasts; walk out of the bolt path, and the second you hear the recast, dump all your stamina. [1:37]
- Ganking to give a low teammate breathing room is good, but once you have mag dumped the target, leave; shooting from 20 m does nothing and you died for 48 damage. [3:42]
- Fundamentals are right; the main problem is greed. [5:21]
- Do not fight with a full enemy wave crashing into your tower; shove it first, and that wave would also have given you ult time. [5:51]
- Using cat form puts it on cooldown and costs the option to cat out through the sewers for the next 30 seconds; that is the opportunity cost. [6:22]
- Forcing a second roam had an opportunity cost: stealing enemy boxes instead would have been more valuable. [8:00]
- Calico wants bursty trades, not sustain trades: trade off your 2 and the Melee Lifesteal cooldown, do your full combo with Gloom Bombs, then back up. [8:30]
- Her gun has high DPS, so in someone's face you can run them down, but do not take mid-range sustain trades. [8:30]
- Never miss Gloom Bombs on an enemy going up a rope: bombs stick to anyone who walks or rolls over them, and thrown slightly above someone on a rope or wall they still detonate next to him if they miss. [9:31]
- Respect gold leads: the Drifter was 1k up on you. [10:01]
- Drifter lowkey counters Calico. [10:01]

Roles, waves and kills:
- Rift timers this patch: first Rift spawns at about 12 minutes (give or take a minute), then about every 7 minutes after. [10:31]
- Leave waves for your carries as Calico, but like football roles, when a teammate dies or is overextended your role shifts and you must catch the wave; do not drop waves you need to pick up. [11:02]
- You keep burning your ult for no reason. [12:03]
- Do not try to kill full-HP enemies under a guardian in mid, where they can respond fast. [12:34]
- With Trophy Collector you want easy kills, not hard kills; there are many easy kills from constant mistakes, so take those when they appear. [12:34]
- You always lead with Gloom Bombs from range and miss a lot of them; Gloom Bombs are much easier to hit when you are in someone's face. [14:06]
- If the target sees you, lead with your 2 and then Gloom Bomb on top of them, because a target who sees you varies his movement. [14:37]
- If they do not see you, they run in a straight line: get on top of them and bomb. The melee shred is great but not worth missing bombs. [15:08]
- Do not crutch on always using your 2 before your 1: if they see you and you are a little far, or they already die to two or three bombs, bombing first can be better. [15:08]
- Arctic Blast came out way too soon; it is about guaranteeing bombs late game and is not needed before bomb max or about 25k to 30k souls. Many better utility options early. [14:37]
- Calico is the best hero in the game at denying the urn because of her high fire rate and bullet spread: post up on a nearby building or window, box route roofs near the urn, and deny it when it gets close. Giving it for free cost 500 souls or more. [15:38]
- Gun spike with Hunter's Aura is normally her second or third spike, around the 4.8k tier 4 spike. [16:09]
- Do not look for picks on front-liners; Shiv is the hardest hero in the game to pick because of his right-click movement and healing off Bloodletting. [16:41]
- If you get kills while roaming but die a lot, look for picks with teammates; most pick heroes cannot pick alone. [17:11]
- Dying a lot usually means getting caught from missing map information: work out the enemy strong side and weak side, invade the weak side, and look for picks around centers, which enemies gravitate toward. [17:41]
- The Gloom Bomb melee shred change is insanely strong for early mid bosses. [18:12]
- Dispel Magic over Counterspell against Slowing Hex, bombs, Shiv knives and Drifter's mark was a good choice, even though Counterspell is better against Lash. [18:42]
- Review your own deaths: for each one, ask if it was a valuable or effective death and why you died. [19:12]
- You jumped into the fray against a Drifter ult with no information. [19:42]
- Using Arctic Blast 2 seconds before your bombs came back up wasted it; save Arctic Blast for when bombs are ready. [19:42]
- Later in the game, spread your Gloom Bombs: throw them between two melee creeps or move your mouse while throwing. [20:43]
- Your team could have done mid boss off those picks. [20:43]
- Calico is the best hero (maybe with Dynamo) for early mid boss: mid boss takes increased damage from melee, and her 1 gives infinite melee shred. [21:13]
- People tuck into corners when ulting for a reason; the shop jump up gives a better chance of living. [21:45]
- Scourge before cooldown items this game, since they do a decent chunk of spirit damage. [22:15]
- You could have used your ult to dodge the Lash ult. [22:50]
- Dropping off a fight when it turned bad was fine. [23:21]
