## Covers
Midknight Vods plays six solo queue games on an alt account (around Ascendant, falling lower) on the day of the 12/16/25 patch, with commentary over his own play. Nobody is coached.
Heroes: Holliday, Warden, Grey Talon (owl max), Yamato, Haze and McGinnis, mostly off-main heroes played for the holiday ornament event.
Themes: lane space at minute one, picks with Lasso, item choices (Plated Armor, Glass Cannon, Reactive Barrier, Torment Pulse, Burst Fire), walkers versus fights, urn running, side lanes versus mid deathballs, learning the right lesson from a lost fight, and reactions to the patch (movement speed nerfs, wall bounce, shrines, bags, jump pad damage).
