# The Best Heroes To Climb In Deadlock Right Now
channel: Metro Deadlock
date: 2026-05-06
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYztvpQT12Q

## Covers
Solo queue hero picks for the patch where Trophy Collector became cheap and gun damage and carries were nerfed: Mina, Apollo, Warden (with Colossus), Wraith, Venator, Lady Geist, Silver as a dark horse, and why Seven, supports, tanks and several others are weaker picks. For players below Eternus who want easier wins.

## Claims
- New patch: Trophy Collector is overpowered, several items were reworked, Indomitable is a new item, carries and gun damage were nerfed (Vyper among them). [0:00]
- Carries are still very solid unless you are Eternus or higher, because up to Eternus the same misplays happen. [0:00]
- Games last longer on average in lower ranks because players do not capitalize on mistakes, which naturally skews performance toward carries. [0:30]
- Mina is strong in lane, becomes a monster in the early mid game, then gradually falls off; her strength is her one or two shot ability, and her ultimate still does a lot of damage late, but her strongest phase is the mid game. [0:30]
- Wraith starts weak and gets scarier the more farm she gets the longer the game goes; that is what a carry is. [1:00]
- Even with gun damage nerfed and spirit nukers made stronger early, carries are still performing really well. [1:00]
- Number one recommendation is Mina, a middle ground hero that does everything. [1:30]
- Winning the lane leads to about two out of three games won; laning is the most significant stage of the game, and Mina is great at winning lane. [1:30]
- Apollo got some spirit damage nerfs but a 10% gun damage buff. [2:00]
- Apollo is one of the best Trophy Collector users: after lane he wants to go around killing people non-stop, he benefits from the range Trophy Collector gives, and he does not want to farm much while the jungle is weak. [2:00]
- Last patch Trophy Collector was not bought because it was too expensive (giving up early power for money later); now it is cheap, so Apollo is probably the best Trophy Collector user in the game. [2:00]
- If you have a strong lane and mid game and gank a lot, Apollo and Mina give the highest chance to win right now. [2:30]
- For players who want to farm and carry, the number one pick is Warden, who benefited from the patch more than other heroes. [2:30]
- Warden has the fourth highest base HP in the game (only Kelvin, Mo & Krill and one other are tankier) and more base HP than both Abrams and Shiv. [3:01]
- Warden build idea: an early fighting style, max the cage first, early Trophy Collector, and "cultist" (as said) to double dip for lots of money. [3:01]
- Warden benefits from the new Colossus, which gives HP as a percentage of base HP; at 24k net worth it took Warden from about 3,300 to about 4,200 HP, around 900 HP from one item. [3:31]
- Colossus's active slows people around you, which makes it easier to land Warden's cage, and it also gives resist; tanky heroes are generally stronger. [4:01]
- Recommendation number two: one of four solid late game carries, Warden, Wraith, Venator or Lady Geist. Gun is not dead. [4:01]
- If you farm and play consistently, a carry will win the late game even 1v6 because players make mistakes; carries are prevalent because games go long, which may change as the community learns to capitalize. [4:32]
- Seven is not recommended: he got even weaker this patch and has very low base HP, while the meta wants you active early. [5:02]
- Do not sit in the jungle farming this patch; the jungle is really weak. [5:02]
- Best way to play: tell all five teammates to buy Trophy Collector and run around looking for kills; it is a death ball, perma team fighting meta. [5:32]
- In an even trade where five die on each side, a team with six Trophy Collectors collects stacks on everyone while a team with three collects half as many; this is why Trophy Collector was emergency nerfed 12 hours after the patch, and it is still crazy. [5:32]
- Everyone in Eternus games buys Trophy Collector right now. [6:02]
- The only time to skip Trophy Collector is on heroes that want to AFK farm, for example a Seven who is not good at early fights; on everyone else it is free money. [6:32]
- Any hero can climb if you are good on it and play your role, but supports and hard tanks like Abrams are team reliant, so with matchmaking this poor they are not good for easy wins. [6:32]
- Pick heroes that can do everything by themselves. [7:35]
- Solo carry ranking: number one Apollo, a top three laner with the best mid game who can 1v6, frontline, nuke, tank and survive alone without follow up. [7:35]
- How to win on Apollo: win lane, then roam the mid game with Trophy Collector killing everyone; he falls off like Mina, but enough mid game kills should leave your team far enough ahead to win. [8:05]
- Ranking: Apollo first, Warden second, third is a toss up by playstyle, Wraith, or Mina if you prefer the mid game nuking style. [8:05]
- Calico is in a really weak spot: a spirit nuker who does not do enough damage. [8:35]
- Drifter relies on getting kills but his lane is kind of weak. [8:35]
- Holliday and Paradox are not terrible, but a good lasso or swap means nothing if your team does not follow up. [8:35]
- Infernus and Vyper got gutted. [9:07]
- Dark horse: Silver, played well, is probably the best hero in the game right now; she is a tank that needs a team but transitions easily into a pick-off roamer who ganks and gets kills. [9:07]
- This patch has no standout broken hero other than Silver; it is more of an item meta where what you buy matters more than the hero. [9:37]
- Buy Trophy Collector, buy green (vitality) items which are really strong, and avoid buying a lot of orange (weapon) items. [10:08]
