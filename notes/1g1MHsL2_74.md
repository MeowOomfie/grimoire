# How to make MELEE Work - Deadlock Itemization lesson
channel: Deathy
date: 2024-10-09
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1g1MHsL2_74

## Covers
An itemization lesson on melee builds: the setup that guarantees a heavy melee, the three ways to scale melee damage (damage amp, bullet resist shred, gun damage), which heroes suit melee (McGinnis, Infernus, Pocket, Shiv, Lady Geist, Mirage) and the core melee items, plus parry mind games.
For anyone trying melee builds, especially melee Mirage. This is a late-2024 video: the item shop, hero kits and map have changed a lot since.

## Claims
- His in-game melee Mirage build is listed as "deathy melee Mirage". [0:00]
- High level players call melee builds a meme because parry hard counters them; mostly still true, but on some heroes melee is much less of a meme. [0:30]
- A good melee build has two parts. Part one is a reliable setup that leads into a guaranteed heavy melee. [1:00]
- McGinnis with a maxed wall (Spectral Wall) stuns, giving a guaranteed melee. [1:00]
- Infernus's ultimate (Concussive Combustion) stun lasts long enough for a guaranteed heavy melee. [1:00]
- Most setups come from hero abilities, not items; the only item setup he can think of is Knockdown, and its stun is short (about 0.9 seconds, still about 1 second even with Duration Extender), so you must be right next to them. [1:31]
- Part two is scaling melee damage. Melee is some of the highest DPS in the game, probably the highest, balanced by needing to be in close range and by a big punishment when parried. [2:02]
- Getting parried stuns you for a while and you take extra damage on the ground; early on a parry is basically guaranteed death. [2:33]
- Scaling method one: any hero with inherent damage amplification works well with melee, because the amp also applies to melee and melee items. [3:03]
- Infernus's Catalyst gives 25% global damage amp: Catalyst the target, then punch; you don't need many melee items. [3:03]
- With just Melee Charge, Melee Lifesteal and Spirit Strike (about 2,250 souls total), a Catalysted punch does around 250 to 300 damage. [3:33]
- McGinnis's wall also gives 25% damage amp on top of the stun, which is why she has always been a go-to melee hero. [4:04]
- Pocket has damage amp on Barrage, up to 40% when maxed and every barrage hits, so a full melee build hits very hard; but Pocket melee is a bit of a meme since he is squishy with negative resistances and it is hard to land. [4:04]
- Shiv at max rage, especially with two points in his ult, gets 25% all damage amp; that, plus Shiv wanting to be in melee range anyway, makes melee Shiv work. [5:04]
- Lady Geist's Malice gives damage amp per stack: with melee items and three Malice stacks on someone, they basically instantly die. [5:04]
- Melee still works on heroes without damage amp, through the other scaling methods. [5:34]
- Scaling method two: melee damage counts as bullet damage, so bullet resist shred boosts it. That is why melee Mirage works with no damage amp on his kit. [6:04]
- Mirage's Fire Scarabs with two points give -25% bullet resist for 14 seconds; with very few items and two points in Scarabs one punch did about 300 damage, so keep Scarabs on your target. [6:04]
- Any hero with inherent bullet resist shred is better at melee and at gun builds in general. [6:35]
- Items that shred bullet resist: Withering Whip, Bullet Resist Shredder, and Hunter's Aura (up to 27% bullet resist shred); stack them and you start one-shotting people. [6:35]
- Scaling method three, for any hero: gun damage scales melee damage, so buy the high-value gun items that fit the melee play style. [7:36]
- Almost every melee build has Berserker: 50% weapon damage at max stacks for 1,250, and melee players take lots of damage in their face, so they hit max stacks often. [7:36]
- Close Quarters is lots of weapon damage up close and you are always close when punching; upgrade it to Point Blank later for the same reason. [8:06]
- Always buy the melee items: Melee Lifesteal, Spirit Strike and Melee Charge are insanely cost-efficient and really good laning items. [8:06]
- Melee Charge, after its recent buffs, is incredibly powerful with lots of useful stats. [8:37]
- Upgrade into Lifestrike for more resistance, health and damage; it gives the most melee damage increase. [8:37]
- Later Colossus adds more melee damage and a lot of health, fitting a play style where you are in their face and must be tanky; for example tornado in, pop Colossus and melee everyone. [8:37]
- To build your own melee hero, look for heroes with bullet resist shred or damage amplification and try melee builds there. [9:08]
- Parry is the danger, so watch his "everything you need to know about melee" video for the mind games. [9:08]
- Don't always use the same melee pattern; it is like conditioning in fighting games such as Smash. [9:38]
- On Mirage he first always Tornados then punches right away; once opponents learn to parry after the Tornado, he delays the punch until they parry, and sometimes fakes to see if they parry. [9:38]
- Even top players are not good at parrying every time; melee them from behind or around corners and stay unpredictable. [10:08]
