# Your Macro is Holding You Back | Phantom Yamato Review
channel: Midknight
date: 2025-12-29
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ezk1pRe0ls

## Covers
Yamato, a Midknight coaching review of one Phantom-rank Yamato player's game (December 2025 patch).
Themes: respecting the wave and not fighting in front of it, rotating after winning lane in solo queue, expected value of fights on a soul bag, overstaying after an objective, managing HP, spending souls and the secret shop, good versus coin flip fights, plus item notes (Slowing Hex, Reactive Barrier, Counterspell, Spellbreaker, Trophy Collector).

## Claims
Lane:
- Harassing before waves arrive is fine, but once waves arrive hard focus the wave; poking while giving them full wave priority just gets the damage traded back. [0:00]
- Playing around cover (like the bridge) while hitting the wave is the correct lane play. [0:30]
- Do not stand vastly in front of your wave on an aggressive enemy at 300 HP: an enemy Mina who just runs at you with Love Bites and rakes kills you with zero counterplay. [1:01]
- Do not fight in front of your wave. Against Yamato he buys Monster Rounds first and full focuses wave, because Yamatos tend to tank the full wave while fighting. [2:03]
- Do not grapple in with no real kill potential in front of the wave; get wave priority first, then fight. [2:33]
- When both enemies are 1 HP and Mina has no 2, wait about 4 seconds for your kit to come up and then dive. [3:33]
- When you are 2k up on Mina, stand between her and her exit instead of grappling into a wall; keep your 2 up so you can 2 onto her if she walks back. [5:08]
- When you have this much lane pressure at 6 minutes, eat their tier two camp, one of the best camps to invade when winning: shove the wave, eat the camp, create another wave, then take their centers off spawn. [6:09]
- Playing selfishly is generally the better way in solo queue. [6:39]
- If your spirit spikes you are really strong; if Mina walks up, just kill her. [8:15]
- Slowing Hex is great when far ahead of a Mina mirroring you in a side lane: you can lock her out of the game until she buys Debuff Remover, which she should rush. It is less crazy against Abrams. [8:15]
- Small thing: melee for your free buff when it is there (about a 60% chance of a free buff). [8:45]

Rotating after lane:
- In solo queue, if you still have your tower up, wait until you see your opponent rotate before you rotate; otherwise one of them may stay and hard shove your tower. [9:15]
- When you first break tower, use that pressure to eat their jungle, then roam. [9:46]
- Invading the double camps after taking tower is great, but get a little more pressure mid before finishing them. [9:46]
- Rotating ASAP after winning lane is right only if the enemy laner rotates; leaving tower solo defended against two lets them trade towers, and equal value plays are how the losing team comes back. [11:18]

Fights and expected value:
- Do not actively seek out a fight while carrying a 1k soul bag. [9:46]
- Expected value: if kill souls are about 400, a 1k bag is about two and a half kills extra. In a 2v2 with maybe a 40% chance the enemy gets backup, they gain four and a half kills of souls if they win, while you only gain two kills of souls in the 60% case. Not worth the risk. [10:48]
- Fighting when you are extremely strong and not on a bag is a good fight. [11:49]
- A teammate who over-rotates into a fight has no impact, splits souls and loses mid wave priority; he should push mid instead. [12:20]
- When your whole team is not there for the Urn, you have to give it. [12:51]
- When your team is spread out, back up, hold the Urn from a roof and apply pressure while calling teammates to push a walker. If they mirror with two, you run the Urn; with one, you get the walker; with three, you get the Urn for free. [13:22]
- If you do not want to use your mic, spam ping fall back and ping the walker you want pushed. [14:24]
- Outside the mid lane, the lane guardian barely matters since the flex slot changes; a mid lane guardian matters because it gives them a shop in the middle of the map. [15:54]
- When you respawn, head to the lanes where you can put pressure and steal their camps and centers; those are more valuable than a side guardian. [16:25]
- Focus tower first; do not brute force a walker or guardian with no information, it turns into a coin flip brawl. [16:57]
- After taking the objective, do not chase a fight with no information about enemy backup; fall back. [17:27]
- While two teammates take a side objective, the third should keep pressure on the mid walker instead of rotating in late. [17:58]
- Up to about Phantom, climbing is mostly mechanical skill; climbing from there into Ascendant needs real MOBA skills. [18:58]
- Nine stacks of Paradox's pulse grenade on you is way too many in any context; the bomb is highly avoidable. [20:30]
- Do not stay to finish a low enemy when your pocket has left and you have no information on the other enemies; one more enemy trades you back. [21:00]
- After a pick with only two sieging, do not chase into the walker; get wave priority in your lanes and eat jungle, because the pick will not last long. [21:31]
- From where the fight happened it was too early for mid boss; do not overstay. [22:01]
- At around 40% HP, fully reset to base; that is not enough HP to keep playing. [22:32]
- At 50 to 60% HP you may not die, but your ability to fight is lowered, and engaging with no information at 60% HP is bad. [24:34]
- Confirm your soul orbs; otherwise you give the enemy denies. [24:34]
- Gun Warden is much better than Spirit Warden right now. [24:34]
- After your team loses two in a fight, the enemy four can collapse on a lone player in the jungle; do not extend there. [25:04]
- A 2v2 with one reinforcing and no player advantage is still not a great fight. [26:07]
- After harassing Mina low, turn on her with your 1 or 2 and kill her instead of tunnel visioning the Bebop. [26:42]
- Overstaying without information is the big issue in this game. [26:42]
- When escaping, hit the vent instead of trying to climb, even with the risk of a vent stun. [27:12]
- When there are many center camps alive and space in your jungle, shove the mid lane and eat your centers instead of joining a coin flip fight. [30:24]
- A walker that is already dead to their push does not need defending; keep farming your jungle. [30:58]
- Sitting on 11k unspent souls is really bad: buy at the secret shop instead of running at the Abrams. With that much unspent, the Abrams should be able to 1v1 you. [32:35]
- Walkers and guardians do not really matter for taking mid boss; wave priority does, which can be a consequence of objectives. [33:38]
- Never put four mid to coin flip for no reason; secure your many camps, run the Urn if you really want a fight, and check your waves, which were extremely bad for taking a coin flip fight. [35:09]
- With many stacks of Haze's Fixation on you, you should die; route out by the safest path that keeps you in the open for the least time, or the shortest path if you have space. [37:42]
- Since the patron rework, when defending you can play around the pit instead of being forced to fight. [38:45]
- The defender-in-pit change means a team with a rejuv that knocks your Patron no longer simply wins; ending now takes real skill. [39:17]
- Using Counterspell to keep it on cooldown is worth it when you get guaranteed spirit power out of it; 20 spirit power is significant. [38:45]
- If you right click after your slash and in between your 1 and 3, the fight is not close; integrate right clicks into all your abilities. [40:18]
- Even a split second of greed at the end of a fight is bad when you need to run. [40:18]
- When defending base, lead with your ult: keeping your ult online gives them about 3 seconds to hit the Patron, so put everything into stopping their brute force. [41:51]
- Do not chase while minions are clearing your base. [41:51]
- Do not brute force a walker with two players when only one of your waves is crashing; either wait until you have pressure in other lanes with your waves or eat their jungle and slow play it, otherwise they collapse and kill you. [43:01]
- Splitting with Seven on a side lane while creating space was a good call. [43:32]

Items:
- The default build in this game looked like Express Shot and gun spike or a full rusher spirit spike; he expected a spirit pivot. [4:37]
- Trophy Collector is bad on every hero; it is only not actively griefing on Bebop and Paige, who rely on teammates' power. Bebop values its ability range and sprint speed. [8:45]
- Mo & Krill does not want Trophy Collector: he only relies on teammates' power when his ult is up, and sacking souls matters much more on him. [14:24]
- Fred the Finch's build is what most Yamatos use; its greens are optional. [23:02]
- Reactive Barrier is pretty solid when five enemies can proc it, four off basic abilities. [23:32]
- Default green picks if unsure: Healing Booster, Healbane and Spirit Shielding; Reactive Barrier in a game like this. [24:03]
- Counterspell is solid this game, but you have to get the timing right. [26:07]
- Debuff Remover is better than Counterspell against Haze's Fixation: Counterspell denies the damage but Haze still gets the stacks. [31:35]
- Slowing Hex would have killed a constantly mispositioning Mina many times. [26:42]
- Spellbreaker is a good item, but it is not as good against Yamato as people think: Yamato leads with her 2 or 3, which breaks it, and a good Yamato who sees it will use her 3 to break it before she ults. [36:09]
- Counterspell is falling out of use but is still an extremely good item; it just takes a lot of micro. [39:17]
- Fury Trance lets an enemy only 4k up 1v1 you. [39:48]

Final notes:
- In lane, the main issue was fighting in front of the wave; respect the wave a lot more. [44:03]
- After the objective is gone, or after a fight where your teammates are low, back up instead of pushing to their walker. [44:34]
- There was no chance for mid boss in this game, because neither team was up two players before about 25 minutes. [44:34]
- Manage your HP: the lower your HP, the less your value on the map, because even if you cannot die you cannot help or reinforce a fight that breaks out. Healing off waves is stronger now that heal creeps heal a lot. [45:04]
- Do not overstay or go to the secret shop on 1 HP; reset instead. [46:07]
- Pay attention to your souls and use the secret shop; spending before that Abrams fight could have changed the game. [46:07]
