# A Really Good Deadlock Patch Just Dropped | 3/21/26 Patch Analysis
channel: Midknight
date: 2026-03-21
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DMvDNBNU9LQ

## Covers
Midknight gives a quick patch reaction to the 3/21/26 Deadlock patch, released about an hour before recording.
He goes hero by hero saying what was buffed or nerfed and whether he agrees: Abrams, Apollo, Calico, Celeste, Doorman, Dynamo, Graves, Ivy, Kelvin, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Shiv, Silver, Victor, Viscous, Wraith, plus general and Street Brawl changes.

## Claims
General:
- The vitality tree is back to percent-based HP instead of a flat number, which makes the green tree significantly weaker early and benefits tanks more. [0:00]
- Ability range was nerfed on Greater Expansion and by small 2 to 3% cuts on many abilities; the item changes are minor. [0:00]

Heroes:
- Abrams gets 10% more melee damage per boon plus Siphon Life and ult buffs, so a melee build scales a little better and his melees get scarier. [0:30]
- Apollo gets small buffs, more melee resist reduction and a little more gun damage, to push him toward building the gun spike. [0:30]
- Calico is nerfed: Leaping Slash gets a higher cooldown, worse scaling and fewer souls, and her ult a slightly higher cooldown and slower speed. [1:01]
- Celeste's gun gets falloff and its first ever damage buff; her real issue was how much on-hit damage her 1 gave her gun, so the gun changes are fine. [1:01]
- He dislikes the Celeste Light Eater spirit scaling buff and the Radiant Daggers damage buff, since she is already strong and the daggers should just be an amp tool. [1:31]
- Doorman's tier 1 Bell no longer gives 3 m radius; the radius moves to the max Bell, so you now want to max his Bell. [1:31]
- Doorman Bell poke in lane will be harder and easier to dodge, which he thinks is a good change; late game Doorman is barely affected except in pit fights. [2:01]
- Dynamo is overall nerfed (less Kinetic Pulse damage, his main damage), with a little strength back in ammo and his 1 max. [2:01]
- Dynamo's tier 3 Quantum Entanglement now gives allies half a charge back and himself a charge back, very useful in fights against heroes with long cooldowns or charge cooldowns. [2:32]
- Graves: the Grasping Hands max now adds two more ghouls instead of reducing cooldown; he thinks the ability was already strong and underrated when maxed. [2:32]
- Graves' ult max no longer summons three ghouls on cast, only about 1% more current health damage, while her base ult now summons two ghouls on cast: a big hit to the max and a buff to her early game. [3:02]
- Ivy's Stone Form spirit scaling reduction is a step in the right direction but not enough; Stone Form is absurd right now. [3:02]
- Ivy's ult lasts longer if she self casts it; on an ally it is about the same. [3:33]
- Kelvin's grenade scaling is reduced slightly and Ice Path is stronger with AP; +20 spirit power on tier 3 Ice Path is significant. [3:33]
- Kelvin's dome buff is not needed. [3:33]
- A hero whose ult build was overpowered got ult nerfs and a gun buff; he says her gun is already second only to Vyper as the most over-statted in the game, and she and Vyper need fundamental reworks, not number changes. [3:33]
- Mirage's 1 is reworked to drain life and heal him based on its DPS, and instead of giving him damage amp it reduces the enemy's damage by 20% (35% maxed), making him an anti-carry; he likes it. [4:04]
- Mirage's ult got real buffs, especially the minus 25 second cooldown. [4:34]
- Mo & Krill lose a lot of DPS from Combo, which he welcomes. [4:34]
- Mo & Krill's ult kill heal goes from 50 HP to 30 base plus 1 per boon (62 at all 32 boons), so early kills give less and late kills more. [4:34]
- Shiv gets his stamina back, which he does not think was needed; Bloodletting being affected by healing modifiers is a great and needed change. [5:04]
- Silver's 1 accuracy is cut 5%, which does not matter; her 2 is now affected by range, her spirit scaling is buffed and her ult gains more from points. [5:04]
- He thinks Silver is fine overall, but her human mode is too strong: she presses 2 then 1 and you die. [5:35]
- Victor gets 30% less effectiveness from lifesteal. [5:35]
- Victor's ult now has the bigger radius at base (the old tier 3 extra 6 m), tier 3 gives more damage instead, the base cooldown is higher and tier 3 cuts more: a nerf early and a buff late. [6:05]
- Viscous has the best changes of the patch: Puddle Punch windup goes from 0.15 s to 0.2 s, so it is no longer a ping check to parry; at 0.15 s it was mathematically impossible to parry above about 70 ping. [6:05]
- Puddle Punch also gets a little more damage and its placement is nerfed, both changes he has asked for. [6:35]
- Wraith's heart card heals less on secondary targets, which is good since it healed far too much, and her cards are a little less random. [6:35]
- Wraith's ult is nerfed: easier to counter spell, higher cooldown and a shorter debuff, all good; he would nerf it much harder. [7:05]

Street Brawl:
- Celestial Busting needed a buff, but Electric Slippers and Ruin Gauntlets did not. [7:05]
