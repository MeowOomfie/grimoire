## Covers
First look at the new hero Deadman Danny on release (2026-10-13): charge gun, jetpack and overheat, all abilities with numbers and level ups, combos, starting ability order, core items and counters.
For players trying the hero early, framed as a flying AoE DPS character, a mix of Pharah and Junkrat from Overwatch.

## Claims
- Deadman Danny looks extremely strong right now in 1v1s and in team fights: a character that flies and kills. [0:00]
- His gun has a normal left click you can tap to last hit creeps; holding left click charges a shot that auto fires when charged and hits enemies in an AoE radius. [0:31]
- Ability 2 is a jetpack float with a fuel meter, like Pharah: tap space in the air to float. [0:31]
- When the float maxes out you overheat, and if you do nothing you self stun and fall to the ground. [1:01]
- The active part of ability 2 flies you at an enemy for explosion damage, slamming into them and negating the overheat penalty. [1:01]
- Air dashes in the jetpack use fuel, and dashing during the air jump drains it faster, so you cannot stay airborne indefinitely. [1:31]
- He is a combo heavy character. [1:31]
- Ability 1 (Strix Salvo) fires a salvo of rockets forward that marks enemies; the next fully charged left click on a marked enemy deals bonus spirit damage, which is the main part of his damage. [1:31]
- A normal tapped left click does not consume the mark for bonus damage; you must fully charge the gun. [2:01]
- Strix Salvo level ups: cooldown, mark damage increase, then two extra missiles, longer range and improved spirit scaling. [2:01]
- Strix Salvo spirit scaling is strong at max but quite low at base, so early on it does little damage unless you level it. [2:31]
- The first Strix Salvo rocket lands at your cursor and you can hold ability 1 to steer the rest, but the timing is tight and very sensitive, hard to control. [2:31]
- Ability 3 (Jettison Fuel) is a flamethrower dealing weapon damage over time and shredding bullet resist: 15% at base (already high), 25% at max. [3:01]
- At max level Jettison Fuel also applies a mark when you left click. [3:01]
- Jettison Fuel ends with a spirit bomb on every target you hit for at least one damage, so in a team fight every hit enemy takes the spirit portion at the end. [3:31]
- Combo chain: mark them, charged left click, mark again with ability 1, charged left click again. [3:31]
- The ultimate grabs one enemy at melee range and flies up with them; both of you take spirit damage over time, then you slam them down, stunning them, and the slam also damages you. [4:01]
- The ultimate deals bonus damage when the enemy is below half health, killing from half HP even at base, like a single target Paradox style ult. [4:35]
- During the ultimate you control the trajectory, so carry the target away from their allies. [4:35]
- When Danny dies he blows up and damages enemies, scaling with your gun, so late game with gun items the death explosion does very high damage. [4:35]
- Split Shot works on his gun. [5:06]
- He is interesting to build: some abilities scale with spirit, but ability 2 and ability 3 deal gun scaling damage, so you will buy gun items. [5:06]
- Express Shot is the number one buy because his slow charging projectile makes it more than double your left click damage; rush it in lane as your first 3K item. [5:37]
- Combo Express Shot with ability 3 shred first for huge damage. [6:11]
- In lane put at least one point into Strix Salvo: its level 1 cooldown is 30 seconds, one point makes it 18 seconds, and the second point is worth it for more damage and mark damage. It is your main lane harassment tool. [6:11]
- Ability 2 has a value point at level 2 that recharges flight faster, but often you do not want that, because you want to go in, press ability 1 and overheat to use the ability 2 combo. [6:42]
- Overheating is not always bad; to kill or combo someone you want to overheat, and timing the overheat just as the enemy runs so you can use ability 2 is the hero's skill ceiling. [6:42]
- The other ability 2 upgrades (heal after hit, cooldown, spirit damage) are for later when you fly a lot; most enemies die after the combo and you do not fly much in lane. [7:12]
- Jettison Fuel upgrades are very good: cooldown reduction, more burst damage and higher fire rate on your gun after. [7:42]
- Go in with ability 3, then ability 1, and if they are not dead use ability 2. [7:42]
- 15% bullet shred at base is a crazy amount of early shred, more than any other base ability he knows of. [7:42]
- The ultimate upgrades are not strong: 50 damage is nice but below half health it already does huge damage; maybe one early value point for kill securing, like the Haze ult. [8:13]
- Starting order: two points in Strix Salvo for mark damage, then two points in Jettison Fuel; all the two point upgrades on this hero are high value. [8:13]
- If you build gun, max ability 3; he will probably end up a hybrid. [8:44]
- Greater Expansion works on his left click AoE and makes the gun more consistent; a must buy because he is all AoE. [8:44]
- Buy Ballistic Enhancements and put it on ability 3, since you get bonus gun damage for every hero hit and his abilities scale with gun damage. [9:44]
- Gameplay loop: shred with ability 3, ability 1, rockets, play up, and if they are still alive finish with the ultimate. [9:44]
- The ultimate dive can probably be blocked with Counterspell, but it cannot be parried because it is a spirit damage ability. [10:16]
- Do not buy Lightning Scroll on him, its effect applies to you. [10:16]
- Verdict: an insane DPS character, the AoE monster of the game, nasty in team fights; scaling still to be seen. [10:47]
